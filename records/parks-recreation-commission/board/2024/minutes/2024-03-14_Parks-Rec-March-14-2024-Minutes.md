    8. COMMITTEE REPORTS
    a) Darrow Pond (Todd) Mr. Lokken worked with the Planning Department to apply for a
    Recreation Trails grant from CT DEEP to construct trails at Darrow Pond. We have letters from
    various advocates regarding accessibility. The Goodwin Trail would have an added loop and
    existing trails can be improved. He informed the Commission there is a map of the area in the
    Parks and Recreation Office.


    b) Dog Park (Rob). Mr. Tukey reported we had a positive meeting. There was discussion on
    locations. The sub-committee is planning a site visit on the field on Roxbury Road and they wish
    to survey residents. The cost ofthe Dog Park is expected to be 525,000 to 550,000. The Dog Park
    Committee is willing to raise funds. The subcommittee also discussed possible features and
    rules in the park.

    Mr. Larkin asked have they looked at other locations? Mr. Lokken replied they discussed
    Darrow Pond property and an area near Care & Share. Veterans Field is also being considered.
    Mr. Larkin stated it already has parking. All that would be needed at Veterans Field is a fence
    and some ground cover. Veteran's Park is not used 60% of the year. Mr. Beebe added there are
    no homes at that location. Veterans Park also has restrooms and shade.

    9. NEW BUSINESS
    a. 2024 Fees
            a.)Vending in parks and events. Mr. Lokken reported two months ago Ms. Kumro
    discussed having vendors at concerts. We have a database of trucks and can invite them to
    submit a bid. We would like to have a vendor for Friday nights and if it goes well also have it
    next year on Wednesday nights. He asked if Commission members wish to consider requesting
    donations? Mr. Armstrong asked are you able to accept credit cards? Mr. Lokken agreed to
    check on that.

            b.) Proposed Pickleball Court Usage. Mr. Lokken stated a meeting has been scheduled
     with pickleball users to discuss plans for court use once they are open. We will discuss
     scheduling, rules, fees and other items.

              Mr. Larkin reported we charge 5385 for a child to come for summer programs for 7
     weeks and five hours per day and he wondered if that covered the cost. Mr. Lokken reported
     that it is close to break-even every year. Commissioners wondered why they have never voted
     on program fees. Mr. Lokken agreed to share additional information regarding the Special
     Revenue Fund in the next budget cycle so Commissioners can see whether programs and
     events generate excess revenue or not.

         Mr. Lokken reported that in terms of Special Revenue Fund the operations of the
     Community Center does not come out of that budget.
