    9. NEW BUSINESS
    a) Field Conditions and Park Resources. Mr. Lokken reported we have asked for additional
    resources and have been denied. We have several volunteers who have put much effort in
    sports. The problem is if it rains more effort is need and it takes a sufficient amount of time. The
    reason the fields are the way they are is we have been neglecting the fields. lf the fields are in
    good condition it takes less time to keep them up. The solution is to prepare the fields well and
    maintain them. Mr. Lokken stated we need to ask for new resources to maintain them.

    ln years past we have had prison help.

    Mr. Larkin asked Mr. Lokken why are we keeping lifeguards on and not keeping park attendants
    on when it rains. Mr. Lokken stated the lifeguards are a safety issue. The parking lot attendants
    control how many cars come in and where they can park. On a rainy day it doesn't pay to have
    parking lot staff. The lifeguards are needed for those individuals who want to swim.

    b) Discussion of Potentially scheduling a tour of the facilities with the Board of Selectmen. Mr.
    Lokken requested ARPA funds from the Board of Selectmen. There is erosion at McCook's and
    weeds on the Boardwalk. Ms. Kumro suggested inviting them before its dark and during the
    week. Mr. Lokken agreed to schedule a time with the Board of Selectmen.

    c) Park Rules and Regulations Review. Mr. Tuckey asked commission members to review these
    rules and regulations and give their comments to Mr. Lokken.

    Mr. Armstrong reported when we rent out the fields every trash can is overflowing. Mr. Lokken
    agreed this is an issue. We have had discussion with War at the Shore. We take a security
    deposit and charge them for things that need to be taken care of. We have a history of only
    charging for the fields he uses. Mr. Kumro suggested charging for a deposit and keeping it if the
    area is not in a condition as it was received.
