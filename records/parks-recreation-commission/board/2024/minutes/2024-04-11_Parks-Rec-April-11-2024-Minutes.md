        a. Budget updates. Mr. Lokken reported funding for the seawall at McCook's and the disc golf

court construction is progressing and we expect to have water fountains replaced in parks soon.
        c. Recreation Supervisor. Mr. Lokken reported Mike McDowell has resigned and is taking a
position in Deep River. We are working with Human Resources and the union to update the Recreation
Supervisor's job description and begin recruiting for candidates.
