        I.CALLTO ORDER Acting Chair Kumro called the May 9, 2024Parks and Recreation Commission

          2. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. Mr. Licatra, East Lyme President of Babe Ruth, suggested allocating
money to redo one of the fields. He would like to see Veterans Field redone and if funds remain, he
suggested Bridebrook. He added there is poor drainage, He felt the fields have been neglected for 5-6
years. He added the fields should be brought up to East Lyme standards. He has been involved in
baseball for over twenty years. Mr. Lokken agreed with him. At Veterans Field we need to take out the
infill, regrade and rebuild the mound in order to be proud of it. He added every couple of years we
should grade the fields. We need to impress on the Board of Finance and Board of Selectmen that we
need to maintain the fields. The cost for each field is anticipated to cost about $35,000. Mr. Donovan
added our fees did not change for a long time and fields are deteriorating. lf we put money into our
facilities, we could generate more revenue, since people are coming to East Lyme from other parts of
Connecticut and New York.

        Mr. Licatra stated that the Town at one time collected 525,000 from War at the Shore and some
people stay overnight at local hotels and support our restaurants. East Lyme is proud of our educational
system and our sports and we should keep up our fields. He would like to host a regional tournament
but decent fields are needed. He stated years ago when the fields were in good condition we didn't have
water problems. Volunteers are preparing the fields. Ms. Kumro reported a maintenance plan is needed.
Ms. Carlson reported the Board of Selectmen are forming a capital improvement committee and she
suggested that a member could potentially sit on the committee. She also stated that the Commission
may want to speak at an upcoming meeting to share recommendations. Mr. Donovan did not feelthe
Town realizes how much the Parks and Recreation Department does. He added the Town is growing and
we are still operating as in the past. Mr. Licatra stated this Town has had many volunteers. Now they do
not have time. Ms. Carlson suggested having the Board of Selectmen tour the fields.
