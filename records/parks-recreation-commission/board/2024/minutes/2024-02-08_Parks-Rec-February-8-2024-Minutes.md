 Ms. Carlson reported grants are available. She added a grant writer would be a worthwhile expense

         a. An email was received from a former resident of East Lyme. She asked that the ratio be fair

        7. UNFINISHED BUSINESS
      a. Filling Commission Vacancies. Mr. Armstrong was welcomed as a new member of this
Commission. We now have one more vacancy to fill.

       8. COMMITTEE REPORTS
        a) Darrow Pond (Todd) Mr. Donovan reported the Darrow Pond Subcommittee and Mr. Lokken
met with the Board of Selectmen. Penny Heller of the Commission for the Conservation of Natural
Resources suggested Marjorie Meekhoff, President of East Lyme Pollinator Pathways, as a member on
the Darrow Pond Subcommittee.

        b) Dog Park (Rob). Mr. Lokken reported he is waiting for the Dog Park Subcommittee to formerly
appointthe Dog Park Subcommittee. Mr. Tukey is presently liaison, but since he now Chairman of this
Commission he asked that a member of this Commission be liaison. A Dog Park was in the original
Darrow Pond plan. lt was felt it was not a good option. Ms. Kumro agreed to be liaison to the Dog Park
Subcommittee. Jay Ginsberg volunteered to be Chairman of the Dog Park Subcommittee. Mr. Lokken
stated there is a list of individuals who wish to be on the Dog Park Subcommittee. There are girl scouts
that are interested in volunteering. To be a voting member they would have to be 18 years of age. Ms.
Kumro will contact Dr. Ginsberg to set up a meeting with him. Mr. Lokken stated we will have to go to
the Town to request funding for it. Ms. Kumro suggested that the Dog Park Subcommittee consider
fundraising.

        9. NEW BUSINESS
        a,2024 Fees
        a.) Beach Parking Passes. Mr. Lokken distributed a list showing the 2023 current fees and fees
for a5% increase in2024. Tax will be added for parking only.

Mr. Armstrong asked when was the last time the fees were increased? Mr. Lokken replied they were
increased modestly last year. Mr. Lokken informed the Commission he has received a comment on the
fee for town employees and active military.

Mr. Tukey stated the Commission wishes to cover expenses as well as keeping the townspeople happy
Mr. Lokken added Youth Services is now a new department and we will no longer be subsidizing Youth
Services. Ourfund balance is less than 530,000. We need to build it up. Mr. Lokken reported now our
state beaches are free. Once Rocky Neck is full, they come to us. He added our beaches are neither
under-used or overwhelmed.

        b.) Beach Admission. Mr. Lokken reported we had three storms that destroyed our sea wall. A
temporary fix has been taken care of. Our options are to fix it now and have it ready for the season;
request money to engineer it over the next several months or wait until September and October to do a
well-planned project. Joe Bragaw, Mr. Lokken and Dan Cunningham feelthat is the best option. Ms.
Kumro asked will that create issues at the beach? Mr. Lokken did not feel it would affect our operation.
Mr. Tukey asked willwe lose revenue? Mr. Lokken felt we would lose very little revenue. There was a
consensus of the Commission to authorize Mr. Lokken to wait to do a well-planned project.

        c.) Pavilion Rental. No further discussion.

        d.) Vendor. No further discussion.

        e.) Athletic Field Rental. Mr. Lokken reported our fields need much work. He felt if an individual

        f.) Pickleball Court. No fees are charged at the present time.
