        I.CALL TO ORDER Chairman Tukey called the November 7 ,2024 Parks and Recreation
Commission Special Meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.

          2. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. Mr. Haylon reported the East Lyme Lacrosse Association (ELLA) has
three volunteers that they would like to honor for years of service to the league by having the
Commission rename facilities at Peretz Park for them. Mr. Haylon stated Tim Swansen, Bruce Cohen
and Rich McCafrey deserve this honor. Ms. Brothers suggested that the Fire House Field be renamed the
Founders Field. Adam Keefe, President of ELLA, wrote a letter of support for the three volunteers. Mr.
Tukey felt it is important to youth sports. Mr. Lokken added there is a practice in Town of naming
facilities after people. He stated it is within the commission's realm of responsibilities. lt was suggested
that since it is the 25th anniversary of ELLA Tim Swensen, Bruce Cohen and Rich McCaffrey should be
recognized. The honoring could take place in April 2025. This Commission will work together with the
ELLA to finalize details.


MOTTON (1):     Mr. Donovan moved to follow through with the President of
                Lacrosse to change the name of the Fire House Field to
                Founders Field and give them the authority to purchase a
                Plaque honoring Tim Swensen, Bruce Cohen and Rich McCafrey.
                Seconded by Ms. Kumro. (5-0) Unanimous.

         7. UNFINISHED BUSINESS
          a. ELLA proposal to rename facilities. This item was discussed under delegations.

        b. Review Park Rules and Regulations. Mr. Lokken suggested that some administrative changes
including changes related to the new Youth Service Department should be updated in the Park Rules.
There have been questions related to the rules, e.g. spear fishing, bikes on the Boardwalk and dogs at
the beach, but consensus is that no changes to rules related to those activities are necessary. Mr.
Lokken will update the rules accordingly and share them with the public.

         c. Filling Vacant Position. Mr. Donovan reported he has spoken to David Castro who works with
Public Works in Guilford. He is interested in joining the Commission. Mr. Tukey will contact the First
Selectman. Mr. Lokken informed the Commission that Mr. Larkin has expressed his plans to submit a
letter of resignation as of December.

        d. Tour of Facilities by Board of Selectmen. The Board of Selectmen have asked that the tour be
scheduled in early spring. Mr. Lokken suggested also inviting members of the Board of Finance and
adding a visit to look at our equipment.

          8. COMMITTEE REPORTS
          a) Darrow Pond (Todd) Mr. Donovan reported he has stepped down as Chair of the Darrow Pond
    Subcommittee. We did receive a grant for the trail design and plan, Mr. Donovan expressed
    disappointment that additionalfunds weren't allocated to completing projects at Darrow Pond given
    that 550 individuals filled out the survey. The Darrow Pond projects are listed among the capital
    funding requests for the upcoming year.

            b) Dog Park (Sue). The Chairman of the Dog Park Subcommittee resigned, because he moved out
    of town, Ms. Palumbo was elected Chair. They will begin fundraising. Locations have been discussed,
    and it was felt the best location was at Roxbury Road and Corey Lane. Residents are concerned about
    traffic. The next Dog Park meeting will be held on the November 18.
