2. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. Mr. Tukey invited guests to speak.

Cynthia Girgenti distributed copies of her presentation on McCook Park Labyrinth
lnitiative, located in Niantic, Connecticut.

The former Labyrinth was removed due to safety and maintenance concerns. Several
members of the public started an initiative to reinstallthe Labyrinth at McCook Point
Park. They prefer an option that uses Pavers, rather than stones, and only needs to
be cleaned every ten years. There is no additional maintenance for it.

Ms. Girgenti reported the Labyrinth is a toolfor relaxation, and meditation. She
asked for this Commission's approval. The Labyrinth group is interested in support
from the town, which could be equipment and labor. A kit with town labor would
cost 534,000 and without labor would be 565,000. She added the time-frame for this
project is contingent on funding and approval.

Diane Swan was interested in helping since her husband's was instrumental in the
original one being installed. She added Rob's brother would be willing to work on the
project. Niantic Community Church and the Rotary may be willing to make
donations. Ms. Swan has set aside 51,000 she received from donations at her

4. DIRECTOR'S REPORT
a. BOE Charging for Summer Camp Security in Schools. Mr. Lokken reported the
   Board of Education is charging Parks and Recreation $7,200 for security for the
   Summer Camp Program. Mr. Lokken stated this will add about S20 per camper
   participating in the program. The price for camp this year is approximately $495
   per camper, plus additional 580 per week for extended care for those who need it

  Ms. Lloyd stated the Board of Education chooses the school we can use. Mr.
  Lokken added the minimum wage has increased. Mr. Tuckey suggested informing
  families of why there will be an increase in the cost for Summer Camp. High
  School athletics uses Parks and Recreation sports fields without charge, but the
  value of that use is about $19,000 for the last year. Mr. Castro suggested charging
  them for the fields. Ms. Lloyd stated from a public health perspective it benefits
  school performance and test scores to have students occupied. Mr. Castro added
  individuals who can't afford the increase will suffer.

b. McCook Labyrinth. Mr. Lokken reported the park crew identified the former
   Labyrinth as a safety maintenance problem and a decision was made to remove it.
   Mr. Lokken added whatever is done, it needs to be as maintenance free as
   possible. Pavers make it much more accessible. Mr. Castro asked if it would be
   placed in the same area? Mr. Lokken replied discussion will be held on its
   placement.
