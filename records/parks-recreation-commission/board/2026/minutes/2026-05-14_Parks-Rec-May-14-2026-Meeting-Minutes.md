of the commission.

So, what can be done about it? Recusal. Recusal is when a commissioner (or any
decider really) refuses to participate in a debate or a vote because the public might
perceive that they have a vested interest in the outcome. Elected officials are
expected to do it. lt is an honorable thing to do and can go a long way towards
restoring public trust. You do not have to leave the commission or duck out of a
meeting. You can just sit it out.

Now, l'm not a sports guy. Furthermore, l'm not from this town originally. I am an
