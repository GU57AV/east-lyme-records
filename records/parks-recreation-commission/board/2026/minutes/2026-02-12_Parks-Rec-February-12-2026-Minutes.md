    b. Report on Nip and Cannabis Funds. Mr. Lokken reported nip money is given to
towns by the state. The Board of Selectmen will use the funds to clean out catch
basins. The funds can only be used for trash and recycling.

    Mr. Lokken informed the Commission that the Town has received approximately
575,000 for the Cannabis Fund. This fund can be used for streetscape improvement,
educational programs, for youth employment, services in the municipality and civic
engagement efforts. lf we are able to obtain some of this funding, we will need to
identify what it will be used for. Mr. Donovan suggested outdoor recreation. Mr.
Castro suggested focusing on the fields and seasonal staffing.

    c. Budget Update
             a.) SRF. Mr. Lokken informed the Commission the CIP Committee
reviewed our request. The Town has a spending and hiring freeze. We have a 5%
limit. CIP vehicles line item has been cut by 549,000, including a replacement blower
and utility vehicle. Permanent repairs to the McCook seawall will be delayed at least
another year. The CIP Committee might have funded restrooms at Bridebrook but
they deemed the overall cost to replace the garage also to be too high. He asked
Commission members to share what they feel is a priority with elected officials.
Budget meetings will begin next week. Mr. Lokken agreed to inform commission
members of the dates and times.

   The Special Revenue Fund is not affected because we charge fees
