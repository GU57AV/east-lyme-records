     1.. ATTENDANCE
           a. R. Tukey called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and the Pledge was recited.
         Present: Rob Tukey, Alicia Brothers, Sue Kumro, Dave Castro, Ben Armstrong, Jerry Lokken
         Absent: Todd Donovan, Kenzi Lloyd, Candice Carlson

     2. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS
         None

     3. MINUTES
          a. Sue Kumro made a motion to approve the minutes of May L4,2026, meeting. Seconded
                by Dave Castro. No discussion. Motion passed unanimously.

     4. DIRECTOR'S REPORT
          a. J. Lokken reported that the final FYE2027 budget included increases in operations to
                partially cover lost CIP projects. The only CIP project that was approved was the
                replacement of the large rotary athleticfield mower. The department also had a
                maintenance position cut. The position was vacaht, so no layoff resulted.

     5. EX-OFFICIO REPORT
         None

     5. COMMUNICATIONS
           a. J. Lokken reported that he received a letter from Kevin Marcoux, ELHS Athletic Director,
                requesting that the Vets Softball Field be renamed in honor of long-time ELHS Softball
                Coach, Judy Deeb.

     7. COMMITTEE REPORTS
           a. Darrow Pond (Todd) - J. Lokken reported that the Committee met at Darrow Pond and
                planned for restoration of the meadow on the south side of the parking lot to be a
                wildflower meadow. The Committee also passed a motion to name the meadow in
                honor of Bruce Dassinger in recognition of his efforts at the property, and the meadow
                in particular.
           b. Dog Park (Sue) - The proposed location of the dog park at the Roxbury Rd property is
                going before the Zoning Commission on June 18,2026.
           c, Multi-Sport Fields (Ben) - The next meeting is scheduled for September, and a vice chair
                was chosen. They still need to identify a chair. One member resigned and B. Armstrong
                is looking at a potential replacement.
     d. Playground Replacement (Alicia) - The committee met and selected two preferred
         vendors to work with. Each of the vendors will meet with the committee to propose
         conceptual plans. Following the presentations, the committee will select the preferred
         vendor to work with to develop a final proposal. No Budget has been set, rather the
         committee is choosing to develop a plan and then find out what it costs. Depending on
         what that is, they may recommend phasing the project, fundraising, or other means to
         make it more affordable and likely to be approved.

8. UNFINISHED BUSINESS
    a. Park Rules - S. Kumro made a motion to define in the Parks And Recreation Commission
        Policies And Regulations an Exempt Organization as "An East Lyme-based tax-exempt
        organization, per IRS 501(c)(3), that provides opportunity for members of the general
        public to participate in youth sports without regard to skill or ability to pay." Motion
        seconded by A. Brothers. Members discussed that this language best reflects the
        historical common understanding the department has been operating under. The
        members also agreed that they will modify it as needed in the future should the
        landscape of youth sports continue to evolve or if we find that organizations are taking
        unfair advantage of it. Motion passed unanimously.
     b. Facility Fee Discount Rubric - Commissioners asked the Director to revise the proposed
        rubric without any percentages listed. They felt that would lead to issues. lt was noted
        that the rubric says that anyone who disagrees with the Director's decision can appeal
        to the Commission for a final determination. The applicant has the duty of providing
        complete information to give them the best chance for a discount, Motion was tabled
        untilthe next meeting for action at that time.

9, NEW BUSINESS
    a. Fees for exempt/non-exempt collaborations - D. Castro made a motion to set fees for
         exempt/non-exempt youth sport collaborations to be half of the approved fees. S.
         Kumro seconded the motion. Members discussed that it was a compromise that was
         very clear and easy to implement. Commissioners also recognized that there is an
         opportunity to adjust in the future once we see how well the system is working, D.
         Castro made a motion to amend the motion to make the effective date January 'J-,2027.
         Seconded by S. Kumro. Members discussed that desire to allow league time to plan for
         the change, and that since it was mainly affecting baseball right now and the season was
         drawing to a close, the potential loss of revenue would be negligible and there was
         potential to harm youth sports if we implemented it without giving them a chance to
         plan. Motion to set fees for exempt/non-exempt youth sport collaborations to be half of
         the approved fees effective January 'J",2027 passed 4-0-1 with B. Armstrong abstaining.

10. ADJOURNMENT
     a. S. Kumro made a motion to adjourn, seconded by A. Brothers. No discussion and
        motion passed unanimously. Meeting adjourned at 8:40 p.m.
