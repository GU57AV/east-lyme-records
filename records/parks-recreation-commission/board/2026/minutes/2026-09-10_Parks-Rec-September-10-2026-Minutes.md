2. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. Mr. Tukey invited Ms. Booth to speak.

Barbara Booth of 34 Riverview Road asked that the Halloween event set aside hours
for children aged six and below in order that no horror is shown. She did not feel it is
good for children in that age group to see horror. She did not want images of horror
to remain in the mind of youngsters. Ms. Carlson informed her that Walk of Horrors
not Parks and Recreation put on that event and suggested that she express her
concerns to them.

She also expressed concern over the lack of a fence at McCook's Point Park. She
stated she has lived in East Lyme for fifty years. There is a steep cliff and she was
worried that a child could fall into the water. Ms. Booth stated in the lower part of
the park there are rocks with slime on them. When the tide comes, she felt a sign is
needed stating that when the rocks are wet, they can be dangerous.

She also was informed a fence is not allowed near the flagpole, because it might be
too high to obstruct views. She suggested that it be 4' or 4Tr'. She stated she is
willing to make a contribution towards the fence. Rather than town crew mowing
the area, she is also willing to do it as a volunteer.

She attended a Board of Selectmen Meeting and Joe Bragaw was present. Ms.
Carlson informed her that Mr. Bragaw, the Town Tree Warden, discussed trees.

9. NEW BUSINESS
a. McCook Labyrinth. Mr. Lokken reported the individuals who desire an improved
labyrinth gave their input at the August meeting. Since there is no money in budget
for it, they are interested in raising funds. He asked Commission members if they are
in favor of pursuing it. lt was felt many individuals appreciate it. The former labyrinth
was a safety hazard and created maintenance concerns so staff removed it. This
group desires a more permanent labyrinth using pavers. Material and labor will be
donated. lt will have less maintenance and mowing will be easier. The path is not
ADA accessible. However, there is a potential finger labyrinth that could be added for
accessibility. Mr.Tukey asked has the labyrinth been used? He added it is wonderful,
but there isn't much space. Mr. Lokken reported it will be located by the bandshell.
Ms. Kumro stated it seems to have much support. Mr. Armstrong asked is it in the
way? He was informed the area is unused space and not in a path of traffic. lt is in
the section of the park is quiet. This matter was tabled until Mr. Lokken is able to
have discussion with Town Attorney.
