         Present: Rob Tukey, Ben Armstrong, Dave Castro, Todd Donovan, Kenzie Llyod, Jerry Lokken,
         Joey DeFillippi
         Absent: Sue Kumro, Alicia Brothers, Candice Carlson

     2. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS
          a. Robb Dunn, Chairman of the Playground Replacement Subcommittee, spoke about the
              subcommittee's process and recommendation for including funds for replacement of
              playground equipment in the upcoming capital budget request. Members of the
              Commission were complimentary about the subcommittee's work. Casey Dixe and
              Amanda Jenkins, other members of the Subcommittee also spoke briefly in support of
              the need to replacement playground equipment at McCook Point Park and Peretz Park.
           b. Scott Todd shared a proposal to include lights at the Pickleball courts paid for by private
              donations. He stated he had a petition signed by over 200 people in support of lighting
              the courts.

     3. MINUTES
          a. Ben Armstrong made a motion to approve the minutes of the September 10, 2026
               meeting of the Parks and Recreation Commission, Motion was seconded by Kenzie
               Lloyd, there was no discussion and the motion passed unanimously.


     4. DIRECTOR,S REPORT
          a. Cini Kayak Launch - Jerry Lokken reported that our application for a SHIPP grant from
               the CT Port Authority was not approved so plans are being made to apply again next
               year, Thehopeisthatthisprojectcanbedonewithoutanyuseoftaxpayerfunding,
           b. Rec Trails Grant $405,000 for Darrow Pond - Lokken reported that the Town was
              awarded a grant that would cover construction of an accessible trail and boardwalk near
              the pond, plus a renovation for the parking lot. The town needs to identify 5gt,00O in
              matching funds to get the grant.
           c. Budget Update - Lokken reported that it is anticipated that the Budget situation forthe
              town will be very tight this year with only level of service funding and extremely limited
              capital funding. For operations the only proposed change will be to upgrade two parks
              maintainers to higher positions with no new positions added.
           d. Accessibility Audit of Parks - Lokken reported that he will be conducting an accessibility
              audit of town parks along with the budling inspector and town engineer,

     5. EX-OFFICIO REPORT
          a. No report.
6. COMMUNICATIONS
    a. Lokken reported that he attended a Board of Selectman meeting to explain the newly
        approve rubric for modifying field use fees. The Selectmen were complimentary of the
        effort and solution and expressed satisfaction with the results.
     b. Lokken reported that Joey DeFillippi, Recreation Supervisor, was attending the meeting
        in order to strengthen the department's ability to have continuity of operations should
        something happen to the director,

7. COMMITTEE REPORTS
    a. Darrow Pond - Todd Donavan reported the committee met and is seeing a bit more
        activity in terms of the effort to reclaim the wildflower meadow and work in anticipation
        of receiving the trails grant funding.
     b. Dog Park - no report
     c. Multi-Sport Fields - Ben Armstrong reported that the group met but that little progress
        was made. They still have yet to choose a chairman.
     d. Playground Replacement - no report
8. UNFINISHED BUSINESS
    a. McCook Labyrinth - the proposed labyrinth was discussed and it was reiterated that if
        the project were to move forward, external funding would have to be obtained for
        construction, an accessible path, and future maintenance.
     b. Charges from East Lyme Public Schools - Commissioners were informed that the current
        superintendent was not in favor of waiving fees charged for security in the schools
        during summer camp , but suggested that the Commission could take the issue to the
        Board of Education for a potential different answer, Since the schools will be hiring a
        new superintendent soon, it was suggested that we wait until after that has happened
        and broach the issue with the new person first.

9. NEW BUSINESS
     a    Meeting schedule for 2027 - Kenzie Lloyd made a motion to set the 2027 regular
          meeting of the Parks and Recreation Commission as the second Thursday of the month
          at 7:00 p.m. in the Community Center, except for the month of July. Todd Donovan
          seconded, there was not additional discussion and the motion passed unanimously.
     b.   2028 Capital Budget Request - Jerry Lokken presented recommendation for the 2028
          Capital Budget request including five pieces of equipment (replacement pickup,
          replacement utility vehicle, new tracked brush cutter, replacement beach groomer,
          replacement stand up blower)and four parks projects (Darrow Pond matching funds,
          Peretz restroom renovation, McCook Playground renovation, bleacher replacement).
          Todd Donvan made a motion to approve the capital request as presented, seconded by
          Kenzie Lloyd, no additional discussion and motion passed unanimously.
     c.   Pickleball lights - Scott Todd presented information to the Commission on a proposed
          solution for lights on the Pickleball Courts. The Commission understands that value of
          adding lights but questioned how it might impact department operations in terms of
          wind load, park closures, repairs, timeframe and other concerns. By consensus, the
          Commission asked that the Director work with Pickleball advocates to explore
          alternatives and develop a recommended plan including any cost impacts on the
          department's operating budget.
10. ADJOURNMENT
