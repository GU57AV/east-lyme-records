Collaborations
We are partnering with the Junior NBA to offer a youth summer basketball league for middle and high
school age kids at Peretz Park at Bridebrook this summer. We are also working closely with East Lyme
Youth Basketballto get coaches as well as other personnelto help make the league run smoothly. We

East Lyme Parks and Recreation also partnered with multiple SECT parks and recreation departments in
the area to plan a "Unified Night" that offerrs a variety of programs and adctivites for people with all
abilities. Partnering departments included New London, Norwich, Waterford, Groton, Salem, Ledyard
and the Miracle League of SECT. Eash agency planned one or more actvities for those in attendance to

people who have difficulty participating in many of our more typical programs.
