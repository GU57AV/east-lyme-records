                                 EAST LYME PARKS AND RECREATION COMMISSION
                                                  Special Meeting
                                                      Minutes
                                                   June 30, 2025
                                                      5:30 pM

Meeting location: Peretz Park at Bridebrook baseball field



       1. Rob Tukey called the meeting to order at 5:32 pm.
       2. Present: Rob Tukey, Ben Armstrong, Alicia Brother, Wick Haylon, Sue Kumro, Dave Castro,
             Todd Donovan, Jerry Lokken
               a. Members of the public: Jack Biggs, Scott Anderson, Adam Keefe
       3. Tour of baseball field and discussion of rehabilitation project
            a. Members discussed best uses of allocating funding in terms of rehabilitating the
                  baseball field. Consensus is that the best course of action is to rehab the full-size
                  baseball field, similar to the work that was done at Vet's Field, and to begin the process
                  of pursuing additional artificial turf multi-purpose fields at the Roxbury Road property.
               b. Jerry Lokken will contact the Public Works Department to explore possibilities for their
                  crews doing some of the work to save funds. He will also begin the process of
                      identifying a vendor to lead the renovations.

      4. Meeting adjourned at 6:09 pm
