Alternative Resources
The town obtained a compact sweeping machine from the State of
CT public surplus auction site for free. Parks staff members have
made a couple of small repairs to the unit and have tested it out. lt
is anticipated that the machine will be used to keep the Niantic Bay
Boardwalk and beach sidewalks and lots free of debris and sand.
The value of the machine is estimated to be about $25,000.

Park Projects
                                          Peretz Park at Bridebrook
                                          received two important
                                          services to maintain the
                                        property: irrigation well reconditioning and crack sealing the
                                        parking lot. Both of these projects were funded using the Park
                                        lmprovement CIP account. The parking lot required extensive
                                        crack sealing to prevent the asphalt from deteriorating and
                                        requiring a substantial investment to repave the entire lot,
                                        likely costing several hundred thousand dollars. The crack
                                        sealing of all parking lots is also being included in future budget
requests implementing a plan to keep them safe and usable, and preventing them from becoming so
damaged that they need replacement.

Over time, wells require reconditioning to maintain optimal flow rates, and this summer with the dry
weather, irrigating the fields at the park was critical. After consulting with Water and Sewer operations
professionals it was determined that the well should be reconditioned every three years. lt was last
done in 2020, Moving forward, funds will be requested to recondition the well when needed and to
perform annual maintenance on the pump to help ensure uninterrupted operation when water is most
needed.

Our own Parks crew trimmed all trees in Peretz Park,
and they look fabulous! ln the months ahead we
will have a commercial crew come in to do
additional work higher in the tree canopy to remove
dangerous limbs and to prune trees for health. At
McCook Point Park, we are having our stately beech
trees treated with a fungicide to improve their
overall health. These trees live for hundreds of
years and contribute greatly to the aesthetics of the
park, provide necessary shade, and stabilize the
ground from erosion.

Community Events
We're proud to look back on a Summer Concert Series season filled with great music, energy, and
attendance. The feedback has been incredible, especially about the bands and the sound on Friday
nights. We're excited to share that moving forward, we'll continue working with our professional sound
provider, Mineau Sound, to keep our Friday night concerts among the best around     !




This summer, our Wednesday concerts reached parking capacity six times, while every single Friday
night filled out the parking lot - a true sign of how
much these concerts have grown.

We were also thrilled to have food vendors at every
Friday concert and several Wednesdays, bringing
even more variety and fun to the evenings. A huge
thank you to Lobstah Dawg, Gennal- Jamaican, Rasta
Rant, Yummy Wagon, Tom's BBQ Pit Stop, and
Robin's lce Cream for serving up delicious options
and making the experience even better for everyone

What Participants are Saying
A survey of summer camp participants was sent
to parents for feedback on items such as camp trips, staff performance, activities and more.
We're proud to report that over B0 % of respondents felt the camp was affordable or very
affordable, reinforcing our commitment to providing a high-quality experience at great value.
Parents rated our morning care staff at an impressive average rating of 4.4 out of 5, while our aftercare
staff rated a strong 4.2.

We also received many open-ended responses from parents who took the time to share thoughtful
feedback. These insights are invaluable, and we'll be using them to guide improvements and new ideas
for next summer.
