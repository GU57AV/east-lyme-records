                                    East Lyme Board of Education
                    Finance, Facilities and Operations (FFO) Committee Meeting
                                      Monday, February 13, 2012
                                              9:45 p.m.


Present: Marlene Nickerson( Co-chair), Al Littlefield (Co-chair), Melissa Ford, Chris Sandford, Pam
Rowe, Jaime Barr, Dr. James Lombardo (Superintendent), Brian Reas (Assistant Superintendent), Nancy
Burdick (Business Manager), Don Meltabarger (Business/Facilities Director), Steve Hargis (ELHS Athletic
Director)

The meeting began at 9:45 PM

1. Review ELPS Special Revenue Accounts - Special revenue accounts were reviewed. These funds
   include the high school pool, before and after care programs, a preschool program and periodic funds
   for a specific special purpose. Questions arose on the specific policy regarding these revenues. It
   was suggested that the policy committee report back to FFO on the specifics in the near future.

2. Review Monthly Budget Summary - The monthly budget summary was discussed. Don Meltabarger
   reported that the instructional improvements of $80,990 should be back on track in March '12. FFO
   would like to make a motion to approve a transfer of $120,000 out of workers compensation account
   into non-instructional account at the next BOE meeting on 2/27/12.

3. Proposed EL Aquatic Center’s Fitness Room Floor Replacement Project - Steve Hargis spoke about
   the need to replace the aquatic floor as a means of updating obsolete equipment and obsolete
   flooring. The flooring will be replaced with an overlay on top. Bids are coming in at approximately
   $16,000 - $20,000. As part of a 3 way cost sharing model, ELHS, The Athletic Dept and The
   Fitness/Aquatic program would each pay $7,000. Jaypro's bid was for $21,000. A request will be
   made for $7,000 out of the capital reserve fund for flooring.

4. Proposed EL Aquatic Center Boiler Replacement Project - Discussion of the replacement of the
   aquatic boiler took place. Reportedly, the 13 year old boiler which recently sprung a leak, was
   repaired. However, due to corrosion, it was recommended that the entire boiler be replaced within the
   year. The estimated cost would be between $50,000-$60,000. Don will check into natural gas options.

5. Proposed Reduction in EL Aquatic Center’s Fitness Room Fee for ELHS Students - Opinions on
   student fees were shared. There was a consensus to reducing ELHS student fees from $4.00 to
   $2.00.


Meeting was adjourned at 10:31PM

Respectfully submitted,

Marlene Nickerson, Co-Chair
Al Littlefield, Co-chair
