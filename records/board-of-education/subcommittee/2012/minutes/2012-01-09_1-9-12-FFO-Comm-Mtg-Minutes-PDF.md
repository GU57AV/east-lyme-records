EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION
East Lyme, Connecticut
01/09/2011
Meeting Minutes
Finance, Facilities, and Operations (FFO) COMMITTEE MEETING

MEETING DATE: Monday, January 09, 2012

LOCATION: Room B102 (INTV Room), East Lyme High Schoo!

TIME: 9:35 PM

ATTENDEES: Marlene Nickerson, Al Littlefield, Pam Rowe, Chris Sandford, Jill Carini,

James Lombardo (Superintendent), Don Meltabarger (Business/Facilities Director)

Committee meeting called to order at 9:35! by Dr. Lombardo.

MEETING CONDUCT

1. Chairperson Election —

Dr. Lombardo presided over the selection/election of a committee chair. The result, by acclamation was selection
of Marlene Nickerson and Al Littlefield as Co-Chairs.

2. Discussion: Greenskies, Inc. (Solar Farm in East Lyme) —

The Committee members had just seen/heard the presentation of Greenskies, Inc. at the regular-stated Board
meeting. The Committee had no further questions. It was felt that the potential benefit in cost-of-energy savings
were well-worth the commitment. The agreement is at no-cost to the District. If Greenskies, Inc. is successful in
this endeavor, then it is a “Win Situation” and if the endeavor does not come to fruition, then the District wil!
continue on as we have been doing.

** The consensus of the committee was a RECOMMENDATION that the Board take the action and approve the
commitment/agreement with Greenskies, Inc.

3. Discussion: Proposed Carelot Enterprises, LLC Rental Agreement —

Prior to the meeting, Dr. Lombardo answered questions about the least/rental of space for Coastal Connections.
For the benefit of the Committee, he synopsized the status of the current lease for the old Alternative Education
building and the lease with Carelot Enterprises.

The current monthly rent on the old Alternative Education building, across from the High School driveway, is
$1950, plus utilities. We have 11 months remaining in this lease. The lease on the building does allow us to sublet
the space. At present, there are two possible groups considering a sub-lease. The District has a firm
commitment (to start next week) from Eastern Community Developmental Corporation (ECDC) to rent the upstairs
for $800 (pius a share of utilities) for at least the remainder of this school year. There is also additional interest in
a sub-lease of the downstairs but the arrangements are still being worked out.

The $800 sub-lease, plus utilities, will cover the lease cost for Coastal Connections at the Carelot Enterprises
building for the remainder of this school year. The rental agreement with Carelot Enterprises is $750 per month

plus $250.00 to cover utilities. We only pay rent for the time that school is in session. The lease runs from
August 29, 2011 through June 22, 2012. (During the summer months, Carelot Enterprises runs other programs

out of the building.)

The lease with Carelot Enterprises has options for two successive renewal periods, the 2012-2013 and 2013-
2014 school years. in these out years, the lease/rent payments will be approximately $1400.00 per month plus
utilities (as opposed to the current $1950 (plus utilities) that is paid for the old Alternative Education building).

* The consensus of the committee was a RECOMMENDATION that the Board take the action and approve the

2011-2012 lease with Carelot Enterprises.
