                                                                                    EL-BOE - FFO Mtg Minutes

                               EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION
                                       East Lyme, Connecticut
                                          Meeting Minutes
                 Finance, Facilities, and Operations (FFO) COMMITTEE MEETING


MEETING DATE:           Monday, July 9, 2012
LOCATION:               Room B101 (INTV Room), East Lyme High School
TIME:                   8:15 p.m. (Immediately following Board of Education Regular Meeting)
ATTENDEES:              Marlene Nickerson and Al Littlefield (Co-Chairs), Chris Sandford, Jill Carini, Dr. James
                        Lombardo (Superintendent), Don Meltabarger (Business/Facilities Director)



                                                 MINUTES

DISCUSSION

   1. FY12 Budget Update: The District will be returning $162,000 to the Town.

   2. East Lyme Athletic Complex Update: Derita and Sons has been selected by the District and the Town as
      the contractor. A groundbreaking ceremony is scheduled for Thursday, August 2 at 10 AM. The
       anticipated project completion date is November 2012.

   3. ELPS Facilities Vision Committee Update: Surveys of the District's 4th and 5th through 8th graders have
      been completed. Meetings with the elementary school staff and the architectural firm have been
      completed. A Community Forum is planned for late September. The results of the surveys, meetings,
      and forum will be vetted with the Finance, Facilities, and Operations (FFO) subcommittee prior to being
      presented to the full Board.

The meeting was adjourned at 8:30 P.M.


Respectfully Submitted,
Marlene Nickerson, Co-Chair
Al Littlefield, Co-Chair
