                          East Lyme Board of Education
                                 East Lyme, Connecticut

                                      Minutes of
    Academics, Athletics, and Activities (AAA) Committee Meeting
                                 Monday, April 2, 2012
                          Room B100 East Lyme High School


Members Present:           Laura Greenstein (Chair), Jaime Barr, Rich Steel, Melissa
                           Ford, Tim Hagen

Administration Present:    Dr. James Lombardo (Superintendent), Brian Reas
                           (Assistant Superintendent), Karen Costello (Administrator for
                           Program Improvement), Michael Susi (HS Principal)



The meeting began at 7:45 PM.

   1. Update on ELHS GPS and Class Rank Forums: The ELMS 8th grade forum and
      the Salem forum were well attended with no negative feedback. Other avenues
      for informing constituents were discussed including an insert in the high school
      handbook and a parent notice of this being on the next BOE agenda.

   2. Tentative enrollments for new HS pilot classes next year were discussed: Human
      Rights and Wrongs, Chinese 1A, Art Co-op, Math Topics, and transition
      Algebra/Geometry class.

   3. The ELPS Transition Academy was discussed. In the past, this was a fully
      contracted program. It is currently being transitioned into a local vocational
      program to include a job coach and life skills teacher. Anticipated benefits include
      more students staying in district and cost savings.


The meeting was adjourned at 8:20 PM.

Respectfully Submitted,

Laura Greenstein
