                             EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION
                                          East Lyme, Connecticut

                          MINUTES OF
  ACADEMICS, ATHLETICS AND ACTIVITIES (AAA) COMMITTEE MEETING
                                       Monday, February 13, 2012
                                    Room B100, East Lyme High School

Members Present            Laura Greenstein (Chair), Jaime Barr, Melissa Ford, Rich Steel

Members Excused:           Tim Hagen

Administrators Present:    James Lombardo (Superintendent), Brian Reas (Assistant Superintendent
                           for Special Education and Pupil Personnel), Karen Costello (Administrator for
                           Program Improvement), Michael Susi (High School Principal)

The meeting began at 6:00 PM.

    1. GPA and Class Rank: Michael Susi, Marci Cunningham, and Deb Kelly presented a report from The
       Program Review Committee (a post NEASC subcommittee), on the high school GPA and Class
       Rank. The current system was explained, other high school’s policies were reviewed, and research
       on college admissions was summarized. A recommendation was made to calculate both simple and
       weighted GPA but to no longer report class rank. A new weighted GPA calculator was presented and
       discussed. It was decided to present the report to the full board for review. After that forums will be
       held to get feedback from parents, students, and the community.

    2. Student Success Plans were reviewed. These are state mandated plans for all students from 6th
       through 11th grade. East Lyme Schools will be using Naviance to manage the plans. The committee
       requested regular updates on how the plans are being developed and monitored and with what types
       of mentoring, meetings, and record keeping are being put in place to support student success. ELMS
       and ELHS plans will be discussed next month.

    3. Safe School Climate Plans for the three elementary schools were reviewed. There are required by
       recent anti-bullying legislation. The schools are taking many steps to create a safe environment for
       students and teachers through a variety of policies and practices including Positive Behavior Supports
       and Responsive Classroom.

    4. A brief discussion of the new BOE member orientation took place, but more feedback and data is
       needed before it can be assessed.

The meeting was adjourned at 7:00 PM.

Respectfully Submitted,
Laura Greenstein
Chair
