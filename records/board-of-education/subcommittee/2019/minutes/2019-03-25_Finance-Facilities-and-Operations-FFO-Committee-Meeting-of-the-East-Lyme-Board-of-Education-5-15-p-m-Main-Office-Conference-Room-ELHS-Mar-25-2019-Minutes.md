2. Discussion Items
A: 2018/2019 Budget Update
-Continue to forecast being on budget but this is with freezing a number of budget items.
B. ELPS Food Service Update
-The Food Services Department is up 9% from last year, primarily due to the increased number of meals.
C. ELHS Aquatics Update
-The Aquatics Department is below budget due to the changes in membership structure and closing over the summer. They have added 2 months to every membership and are now offering monthly memberships.
-Working on forecast for the year but expect next year to be in much better shape.
D. ELHS Athletic Field Scoreboard
-Researching a partnership to install a new scoreboard which would include advertising. After scoreboard is paid for, a 50/50 revenue split.
