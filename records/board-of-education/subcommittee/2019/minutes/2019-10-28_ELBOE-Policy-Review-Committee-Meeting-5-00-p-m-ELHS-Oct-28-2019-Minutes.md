Second Review of New Policy 44118.114 Personnel: Reports of Suspected Abuse or Neglect of Children or Sexual Assault of Students by School Employees:
Shipmen recommends not subdividing into a regulation and keep all in the Policy. Question?  Why are there two policies?  It might be easier for locating with a different number and policy. Language has “technical school”, is it appropriate, or should it be tailored to public school.  It is probably part of the State Statute and should remain. The age requirement is under 18.  We would still call if it occurred if under our care.  Still mandated to call, they do treat it differently. Fine with all policy and no regulation.  Good to have it in the policy. Jeff Newton will check on language “technical high school system or local…..” with Shipman & Goodwin.
First Review of New Policy – Reports of Suspected Abuse or Neglect of Adults with an Intellectual Disability or Autism Spectrum Disorder:
Okay.  Needs a number and check to see if it has to be separated from the one above or if they can be combined into 1 policy.
First Review-
Policy 3291 Student Activity Account (Review):
Change statute due to transposition.  Finance Director is not comfortable being a treasurer of the accounts because she does not see everything.  She does see the reconciliation after it has been paid. At this time, the accounts are being overseen by the principal.   Her goal it to centralize it once MUNIS is up and running, a request will be made and approved before a check can be issued. Easier to have money all handled and centralized.  MUNIS will be phased in within two years.  The Superintendent will ask Shipman & Goodwin about leaving the last paragraph out by just listing the conn. Gen. Statute. Recommend bring to BOE the first meeting in November for second reading first of December.
First Review-Suggested New Policy Personnel: Volunteers in Schools:
Suggestion:  Replace Policy with Shipman & Goodwin and keep our regulation for first review.  Human Resource personnel is attending employment background seminar tomorrow and will bring any other changes to BOE if any.  As of now, 197 volunteer applications/background checks received.  Administration is working with PTA’s to reinforce and advertise volunteer requirements. Recommend bring to BOE the first meeting in November for second reading first of December.
First Review- Suggested New Policy and Regulation: Pool Safety Plan
:  We currently do not have a pool safety plan or policy because we didn’t own the pool program. We broke up into two plans:  Student and Community.  This has been approved and vetted by Shipman & Goodwin. We will use Waterford’s Policy and the other two as a regulation for our guidelines. Recommend bring to BOE the first meeting in November for second reading first of December.
Future Agenda Items:
Curriculum revisions for Latin American and African American studies. SERC is creating unit for middle and high school course requirement.  It is about one year away and as of now it is not mandatory for each student, but a requirement to offer it.
Homework discussion is needed. What is mandatory? Equitable? Is there consistency? Etc.
