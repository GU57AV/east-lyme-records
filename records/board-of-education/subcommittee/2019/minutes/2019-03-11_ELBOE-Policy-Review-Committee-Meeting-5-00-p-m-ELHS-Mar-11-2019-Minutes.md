EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION
East Lyme, Connecticut
POLICY REVIEW COMMITTEE MINUTES
March 11, 2019
East Lyme Public Schools will inspire, engage and educate each student to become a  contributing citizen
and a responsible, independent, and critical thinker.
Board of Education Members Present:
Jaime Barr Shelburn (Chair), Dr. Timothy Hagen, Leigh Gianakos, Barbara Senges
Administrators Present: Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Amy Drowne (Assistant Superintendent for Curriculum, Instruction and Assessment)
Meeting Time: 5:00 – 6:00 p.m.
BOE Policy 1250 Community Relations: School Visitors Policy
This policy was brought back to the Policy Review Committee for a second review.  The Policy Review Committee suggested the following changes:
Item 1:  Add “visitations that have been approved”
Change “Main Office” to “Main Office and Safety & Security Desk”
The revised policy will be brought forward to the Board of Education for a First Reading on March 25, 2019.
BOE Policy 6142.101(a) Instruction: Student Nutrition and Physical Activity (Student Wellness Policy)
Superintendent Jeffrey Newton informed the Policy Review Committee that the suggested additions to the current policy have been mandated by the State after the completion of the Child Nutrition Program Administration Review.   We are required to site partnerships that we have in place, document that we have student input on a regular basis, site that we comply with Connecticut Nutrition Standards, that we recognize the national program, and that we identify the tool we are using for the wellness self-assessment (WellSAT).
Assistant Superintendent Amy Drowne informed the committee that the Wellness Committee Agenda has been formatted to cover the three areas of this policy:  Nutrition Education and Promotion, Physical Activity and Other School-Based Activities, and Nutritional Guidelines for School Food.
The revised policy will be brought forward to the Board of Education for a First Reading on March 25, 2019.
Suggested New Policy: Personnel: Reports of Suspected Abuse or Neglect of Children or Sexual Assault of Students by School Employees
The Policy Review Committee reviewed the suggested policy from Shipman & Goodwin.  The district currently does not have a policy for personnel to cover suspected abuse or neglect.
The committee suggested making the following changes to the Shipman & Goodwin suggested policy:
In lieu of listing all staff types in the policy, state “Any employee or any other person who…”
Begin the Regulation with Item 4: Reporting Procedures for Statutory Mandated Reporters.  Superintendent Jeffrey Newton will consult with Shipman & Goodwin that moving this part of their suggested Policy to a Regulation is appropriate.
Superintendent Newton will check with Shipman & Goodwin if volunteers should be included in the policy.
The revised policy will be brought back to the Policy Review Committee for a second review.
Future Items
The following policies will be moved to the top of the Future Item List for review:
Policy 3219 Student Activity Account (Review)
Volunteers in School (New)
Policy 6154 Instruction: Homework will go to AAA Committee for review before being brought to the Policy Review Committee.
Respectfully submitted by
Jaime Barr Shelburn
Policy Committee Chair
