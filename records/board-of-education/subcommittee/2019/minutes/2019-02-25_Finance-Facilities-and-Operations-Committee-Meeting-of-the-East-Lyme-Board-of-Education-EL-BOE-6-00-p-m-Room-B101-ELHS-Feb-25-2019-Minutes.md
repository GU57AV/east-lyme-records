2018/2019 Budget Update
Due to unanticipated SPED tuition costs 2018-19 budget projection ends year with $1,249.47. Superintendent Newton, however, reports that Claims for Health Insurance are down the last two months and town is hopeful we will end the year without the previously anticipated shortfall of 1.5 million.

A meeting will be scheduled with the CPS to discuss a possible increase in rates for 19-20.
Review of Current ELPS Facilities Use Rates and Proposed Adjusted and Additional Rates Effective April 1, 2019
Committee discussed and supported proposed change from daily rates to hourly rates, security hourly rate change, and addition of grounds/maintenance hourly rate charge. Committee will continue to research and discuss changes in field rental charges.
