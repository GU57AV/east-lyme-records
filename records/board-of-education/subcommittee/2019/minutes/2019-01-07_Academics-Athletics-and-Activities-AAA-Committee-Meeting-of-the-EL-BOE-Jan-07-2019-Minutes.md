3. Activities
Discussion: A.
Discuss 50th Anniversary of ELHS Graduation Class
:
A very special 50th Anniversary Event will be coming to the East Lyme High School in June ... this may include special events within the week leading up to graduation, in addition to including everyone from that year to attend, walk with this year’s graduation class....
Also, we learned that a New Alumni Association is possibly being established.
ELHS Science Teacher Dan Nazzaro and/or ELHS Math Teacher Linda Johansen will be coming back to the AAA Committee later in the year to talk about the development of the Alumni Association and what it looks like.
4.
Athletics
Discussion: A.
Discuss specific items that Board members would like to have more information on for ELHS Athletic Director Steve Hargis to visit at a future AAA Committee meeting
A great conversation on the East Lyme Athletic Department. Looking at the structure of how the Athletic Department runs ... what The HR position looks like. Family ID, Communication / advertising, programs offered. The CIAC handbook is what governs the Athletic Department, however we will be looking at our internal processes to possibly include district specific regulations and or policies.
Other suggestions include Equity, Title 9, student attendance & performance along with Athletics (Requirement in order to participate.), training for coaches.
