Action, Discussion:
A. Discussion/Action: Reconciliation of Salem Tuition Rates for 2017-2018
East Lyme Finance Director Maryanna Stevens walked the membership through the reconciliation process of the Salem Tuition Rates for 2017-2018. The 2017-2018 audited overpayment of $161,899.90 by Salem will be applied to the current year, as the number of special education students reflects an increase for 2018-2019.
MOTION: Acting Chair Jaime Barr Shelburn (EL) moved to approve the reconciliation Salem tuition rates for 2017-2018 as follows:
1) Salem Base Tuition Rate: $12,473.38
2) Salem Total Premium Tuition Rate: $27,520.82
A total overpayment by Salem of $161,899.90 as presented; seconded by Chair Sean Reith (Salem)
VOTE: Unanimous    MOTION CARRIED
Action, Discussion:
B. Discussion/Action: Proposed Salem Tuition Rates for Salem Students Attending ELHS for 2019-2020
ELPS Finance Director Maryanna Stevens took members step by step through the calculation process used to determine the proposed Salem tuition rates for 2019-2020.
MOTION: Chair Sean Reith (Salem) moved to approve the Salem Tuition Rates for 2019-2020 as follows:
1) Salem Base Tuition Rate: $12,473.38

2) Premium Tuition Rate: $28,894.82 as presented;
VOTE: Unanimous    MOTION CARRIED
Information:
C. Discussion: Update on EL Elementary Alteration Projects
East Lyme Superintendent Jeffrey Newton stated the elementary alteration projects are approximately 35% complete, on budget, and on schedule. Currently in Phase II of Phase IV, the projects are scheduled for completion in late August 2019. Elementary redistricting will take place at the start of the 2019-2020 school year. Transitioning activities for students and their families continue. East Lyme enrollment is expected to rise (Milone & MacBroom Study) through 2027.
Information:
D. Discussion: Update on ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2019-2020
EL Superintendent Newton gave an overview of his proposed budget for 2019-2020 representing a 3.18% increase over the current year. The Public Hearing follows this meeting. Major budget drivers include Security/Safety, District Technology, Special Education, Certified Staff Retirements, and Health Insurance Increases. A budget freeze for 2018-2019 is currently in effect due to a 28% increase in medical insurance increase this year.
Information, Reports:
E. Discussion: Update on Salem Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2019-2020
Salem Superintendent Joe Onofrio stated his proposed 2019-2020 budget represents a 3.24% increase over the present year's budget. He is working with the Salem Board on possible reductions.
