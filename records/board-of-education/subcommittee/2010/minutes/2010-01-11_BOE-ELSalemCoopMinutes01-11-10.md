VII. Cooperative Agreement Committee
The Salem/East Lyme Cooperative Agreement Committee will meet to discuss the
following:
1) 2011-2012 Salem tuition rates; 2) tuition rates for part-time Salem students who
attend
offsite programs; 3) whether East Lyme should hire and pay for all staff; and 4)
ELPS
Futures Report. The minutes will be mailed to Salem and East Lyme Board Members.

VIII. EL Superintendent’s “State of the District Presentation”
Dr. Lombardo presented an overview of the following:
• ELPS Task Force Committees and asked for Salem representation
• 2010-2011 Superintendent’s Proposed Budget – 3.5% increase over current year
• Hiring process for High School Principal and Assistant Superintendent for Special
Education and Pupil/Personnel Services
He also stated that a review of the HS Alternative Education Program is being
conducted.
Dr. Lombardo thanked the Salem and East Lyme communities for their support and looks
forward to the remainder of the year.

IX. Next Cooperative Meeting Date
The next East Lyme/Salem Cooperative Committee Meeting is scheduled for Monday, May
17, 2010 at 6:00 p.m. in Salem.
