                                      AGENDA/PURPOSE

   The meeting was called to order at 8:00 p.m.

1. Review High School New Elective Offerings in Physical Education and Fine Arts by Principal
   John Sullivan and Curriculum and Instructional Leaders
   ELHS teachers Kim Thompson, Sue Johnston, Eloise Bottinelli, Fine Arts CIL Jim Warykas and
   Health/PE CIL Freda Gianakos presented course descriptions and rationale for the following four
   (4) proposed new courses for their departments:
          1. Yogalates – Length ½ yr, Credit ½
          2. Advanced Ceramics and Pottery III – Length ½ yr, Credit ½ - Grades 11-12
          3. Digital Piano I – Length ½ yr, Credit ½ - Grades 9-12
          4. Creating Music with Computers – Length ½ yr, Credit ½ - Grades 9-12
   All proposed courses are at no FTE increase and will replace existing electives based on student
   enrollment. Funding has been set aside for Creating Music with Computer software. No additional
   equipment is needed for the other courses.

2. Review Updated Proposed Procedure Re: Student Driving Policy
   Dr. Lombardo presented a proposed administrative regulation regarding Board Policy 5131.4
   Students/Transportation for Off-Site Instruction. Based on a legal opinion and discussions with the
   administrators, students and staff, the proposal ensures that the District can provide students with
   unique educational opportunities that can best be offered in settings outside of the regular
   classroom while ensuring the safety of students and minimizing the liability risk to the ELPS and
   its employees. No student will be denied access to a course or activity due to lack of personal
   transportation, at a cost to the District. The Superintendent will have the authority to make

     exceptions, within the law. The committee’s recommendation, to include a slightly revised policy
     and proposed regulation, will be presented to the full Board on Monday, March 22, 2010.

3.   Review Updated Proposed Procedure Re: Tuition Policy
     Dr. Lombardo is awaiting a legal opinion regarding differentiating the non-resident tuition fee for
     regular education and special education students. More discussion will follow.

4.    Review of Proposed 2010/2011 ELPS Calendar
     Dr. Lombardo presented the proposed 2010/2011 ELPS Calendar as recommended by the
     Superintendent’s Calendar Committee. The AAA Committee supported the professional
     development days but recommended a well-supervised and affordable afterschool childcare
     program be available to parents on those shortened days. Alternate shortened session dates for
     April 28 and 29, 2010 conferences/PD will be explored. The graduation date and snow days were
     also discussed. The proposed calendar will be presented to the Board on March 22, 2010.

5.   Report re: ASCD Conference Learning
     Information was provided by Dr. Lombardo during the Special Meeting.

     The meeting adjourned at 10:00 p.m.
