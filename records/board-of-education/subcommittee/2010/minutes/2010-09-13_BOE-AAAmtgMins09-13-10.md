                            EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION
                                           East Lyme, Connecticut

                           MINUTES OF
     ACADEMICS, ATHLETICS AND ACTIVITIES COMMITTEE MEETING
                      Monday, September 13, 2010
                   Room B101, East Lyme High School
                              8:45 p.m.
Members Present:           Mary Broderick and Laura Greenstein (Co-Chairs), Jaime Ellis, Melissa Ford,
                           Al Littlefield

Administrators Present:    Dr. James Lombardo (Superintendent), Brian Reas (Assistant Superintendent for
                           Special Education and Pupil Personnel, Dr. Karen Costello (Administrator for
                           Program Improvement)


The meeting began at 8:45 p.m.

1.   Review of ELPS Health and Balanced Living Guide – Dr. Karen Costello, Administrator for Program
     Improvement, presented the ELPS Health and Balanced Living Curriculum Guide (K-12). Middle school
     science teacher Joan Love and high school health/physical education teacher Kim Thompson provided
     additional information and addressed questions from committee members. The guide was compiled over a
     3-year period, with input gathered from staff and community members, the Chartwells Food Service
     Director and the Community Wellness Advisory Committee. A summary of the comprehensive guide will
     be presented to the full Board on September 27, 2010 as a discussion item and returned for action on
     October 26. The complete guide is available for review at Central Office. The committee thanked all those
     involved for their work.

2.   Update on New Special Education Programs – Mr. Brian Reas, Assistant Superintendent for Special
     Education and Pupil Personnel, provided a PowerPoint presentation of the new special education programs
     in the district including the Therapeutic Learning Program (FL), the Applied Behavior Analysis Programs
     (MS); and the Medically Fragile Programs (MS and HS). He explained that these programs reflect the
     recommendations of the Futures Committee and changes will continue to be made over time. The
     committee thanked Mr. Reas for his comprehensive review.

 The meeting was adjourned at 10:15 p.m.

 Respectfully Submitted,

Mary Broderick                                          Laura Greenstein
