2. Recommendations from the Administration Sub-Group on Special
Education Tuition.

Dr. Lombardo led a discussion of current thinking by Administration on Special
Education Tuition. The philosophical position is that there should be one single
tuition charge per Salem student attending East Lyme High School. This
philosophy is in recognition that Special Education services have evolved
considerably over the past 15 years, and it is impossible to separate the services
since they are now so integrated into the entire educational system and process.
There are two exceptions which will be cause for additional charges on a per
student basis. The first exception is for students requiring “exceptional” Special
Education Services, such as extended day services or partial day out-placement
programs. The additional costs for such programs would be billed as excess cost
over the per pupil tuition charge. The second exception is where there is special
equipment required for a student in which the cost exceeds a threshold of $1500.
For example, Salem would pay for an individual computer with special software if
the total price exceeded $1500. If the total cost was less than $1500, East Lyme
would pay for the equipment.
The Study Group discussed the proposal in depth and several points where
raised.

     •   The philosophical concept that all children are educated with equality in
         response to their individual needs is essential. Continuation of
         philosophy requires both school districts to be aligned and consistent at
         pre-school and grade levels K through 8 with identification and actions
         for addressing special needs. It will be important that both districts are
         moving in the same direction at all levels and that the dialogue and
         coordination is continuous.
     •   There are considerable opportunities for providing improved and more
         cost efficient services for children with Special Education needs through
         the development of in-house programs, such as ABA (Applied Behavior
         Analysis) Therapy. Both districts are working together to prepare for
         such opportunities.
     •   The Alternative Education Program may become a component this
         concept, and Salem is part of the re-engineering initiative that has
         begun.
     •   Further discussion and thinking is required for how to handle tuition
         charges for a child that is part-time at the high school and part-time in
         out-placement program.
     •   A “white paper” is needed to help define the elements that have led to
         the philosophy of “one tuition rate.” A graphical depiction of the
         philosophy and its implementation could be useful in explaining the new
         system.

The Study Group concurred with the recommendation of the Administration Sub-
Group on the philosophical basis for the proposal and recommended that it be
shared with both Boards at the next Joint Cooperative Meeting.

Dr. Lombardo presented at a high level two approaches for calculating the tuition
for a Salem student attending East Lyme High School. The first method uses the
total education cost from ED001 with transportation and non-high school costs
backed out. The second method starts with the high school costs and adds
districtwide allocations accordingly. The Study Group discussed the two
approaches and emphasized a commitment to “fairness” in which costs are
shared equitably. The committee did not reach a conclusion on a method for
tuition calculation.
