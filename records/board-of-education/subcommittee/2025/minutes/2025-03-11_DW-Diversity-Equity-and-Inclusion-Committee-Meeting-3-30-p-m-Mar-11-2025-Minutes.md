Districtwide Diversity, Equity and Inclusion Committee Meeting
Tuesday, March 11, 2025 - 3:30 p.m. via Zoom
Committee Members in Attendance:
Jeff Newton (Superintendent of Schools), Annaliese Spaziano (Assistant Superintendent), Claudine Kelly (ELMS Principal), Danielle Schoman (FL Principal),
Dr. Linda Darcy (Director of Teaching and Learning at LEARN), Jeannine Barber (ELHS librarian), Kim Buckley (ELHS Teacher), Meredith Coffey (Social Worker), Colleen Gresh (ELL Teacher), Lynn Sullivan (Elementary Teacher), Camille Sarna (Special Education Teacher), Sally Vernaglia  (ELL Teacher)
Committee Members Absent:
Henry Kydd (ELHS Assistant Principal), Amanda Cappon (Elementary Teacher), Sarah Zielinski (ELMS Teacher), Lori Kiely (NCS Secretary)
1. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 3:31 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
A. Discussion/Information: Student Shadowing Follow Up
Superintendent Newton & Assistant Superintendent Spaziano shared their experiences and the positive impact of student shadowing at the elementary and middle school levels. Student Shadowing work will continue in the next school year.
B. Discussion/Information: DEI School-Based Committee Updates
The school-based DEI committee representatives shared the work of the last few months and future plans for the spring.
C. Discussion/Information: 2025-26 School Year Planning
Discussions around transgender bathroom use and the availability and accessibility at the high school occurred.
Discussions on using EdCamps for DEI district initiatives occured and will be considered in planning next year.
The next meeting is scheduled May 6, 2025.
3. Adjournment
The meeting adjourned at 4:11 p.m.
