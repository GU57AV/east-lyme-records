3.    DISCUSSION: Culture & Climate

           A.     Update on Districtwide Culture & Climate October meeting
                  The Districtwide Climate & Culture Committee met in the
                  beginning of the month and reviewed the survey data from
                  the last school year and discussed different approaches to
                  increase engagement to gather comprehensive data. The
                  committee meets again in January.

        B.   Review of Satchel Pulse Survey Data from 2024-25 SY
             Committee members reviewed questions/responses from
             the survey subgroups (students, parents, staff). The pillars
             were displayed and reviewed in relation to the questions.
             Discussions ensued around the number of questions and
             types of questions that are being used. Other thoughts and
             ideas were shared as it pertains to data collection and share
             out. One idea was sharing student data ahead of time with
             parents to outline areas of focus based on the student's
             responses, while asking parent to reflect on those specific
             areas.

        C.   Discussion on Survey Question Selection from 2026-27 SY
             Additional comparisons continued to be reviewed and
             discussed between survey categories. Pinpointing areas of
             concern remains a focus to address and continue to discuss.
             The idea came forth that at this point we leave staff and
             student surveys/questions as is, but put more focus on the
             parent surveys, trying to tweak those questions to obtain
             additional data.

        D.   Discussion on Anecdotal Feedback Collection
             Additionally, committee members feel more strongly about
             leaving a parent question out that asks or allows parents to
             'opt out' or click that they are satisfied and do not wish to
             continue the survey at the start.
             Consensus came forth to continue to survey parents as is but
             reduce the number of questions.

4.   ADJOURNMENT

        A.   Adjourn Meeting
             The meeting adjourned at 6:28 p.m.
