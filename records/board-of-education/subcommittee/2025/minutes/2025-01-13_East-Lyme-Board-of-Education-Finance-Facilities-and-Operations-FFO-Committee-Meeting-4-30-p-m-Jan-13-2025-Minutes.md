Finance, Facilities, and Operations (FFO) Committee Meeting
Monday, January 13, 2025 at 4:30 p.m.
Minutes
Members in Attendance:
Jill Carini, Chair; Cate Steel, Jeffrey Newton, Superintendent; Annaliese Spaziano, Assistant Superintendent, Dr. Christian Lund, Director of Facilities, Diane Nazarko, Facilities Project Manager, Eric Bauman (4:58 – 5:03 p.m.)
1. OPENING OF MEETING
A. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 4:33 p.m.
2.  DISCUSSION ITEMS
Ms. Jill Carini requested to add an item “Discussion of Merging the FFO Committee & the Facilities Asset Management Committee” to the agenda. Ms. Cate Steel was in agreement.
A. Update on Aquatics Roof Center
Dr. Lund began discussions around the status of the roof replacement plans for the Aquatics Center. The amount originally budgeted for this replacement was significantly below the amount needed to complete this project. Through working with the town, available funds were found to allocate for capital projects in this fiscal year. Clarifying discussion included what the state requires to qualify for reimbursementThe project would take approximately 4-5 months to complete. Ms. Steel reiterated that the community uses the Aquatics Center and thinks it is appropriate that the town is contributing capital funds to this renovation.
The Aquatics Center Roof project will be brought to the Board of Education Regular Meeting for approval with this updated information on January 27, 2025.
B. Discussion: Remaining use of Capital Funds of $250,000
Discussion ensued on the best approach to utilize the remaining use of Capital Funds from this fiscal year. Plans include upgrading the boiler at Central Office and upgrading the girls’ locker room and facilities at the high school to meet ADA compliance. The remaining $100,000 of the funding can be allocated towards the Aquatics Center Roof project.
C: Discussion: Merging Finance, Facilities & Operations Committee and Facilities Asset Management Committee
Ms. Carini began the discussion around merging this committee with the Facilities Asset Management (FAM) committee in the spirit of efficiency and minimizing overlapping discussions. Dr. Lund shared the history behind forming FAM  and Superintendent Newton shared the importance of maintaining FFO and that removing that committee would involve revising Board policy. Discussion also included moving FAM to become a subcommittee of FFO. The committee agreed that members of the FAM committee (Bill Derry & Alisa Bradley) should be included and that further discussion on the subject should include all BOE members.
