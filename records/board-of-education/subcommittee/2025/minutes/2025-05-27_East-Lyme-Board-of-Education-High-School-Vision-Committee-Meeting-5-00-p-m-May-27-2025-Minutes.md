East Lyme Board of Education
High School Vision Committee Meeting
Tuesday, May 27, 2025 at 5:00 p.m.
MINUTES
Committee Members Present:
Marc Salerno (Vision Committee Chair), Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Maryanna Stevens (Director of Finance), Christian Lund (Director of Facilities),
Esteban Garcia (BOE member), Eric Bauman (BOE member), Deb Roselli-Kelly (Retired ELHS Principal), Lori Singer (ELHS Science Teacher), Michelle Williams (Town member), Diane Nazarko (Project Manager)
Committee Members Absent:
Henry Kydd (ELHS Principal), Brian Hendrickson (Salem Superintendent), Sue Spang (Salem Representative)
1. OPENING OF MEETING
The meeting was called to order at 5:00 p.m.
2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES
A. Minutes of the April 8, 2025 Meeting
Motion to approve the minutes of the April 8, 2025, High School Vision Committee meeting, as presented.
Motion by Lori Singer, second by Esteban Garcia
Final Resolution: Motion approved.
Pro: Jeffrey Newton, Maryanna Stevens, Christian Lund, Esteban Garcia, Eric Bauman, Deb Roselli-Kelly, Lori Singer, Marc Salerno, Diane Nazarko, and Michelle Williams
3. DISCUSSION ITEMS
A. Discussion: Feasibility Study
The committee discussed the feasibility study and since there is funding to complete Task 1 & 2 (Phase 1) over the summer months, they agreed to move forward with BOE approval. Phase 1 of the feasibility study totals $40,650, approval for the study will be added to the agenda in June.
Phase 2 (Task 3 & 4) of the feasibility study will be assessed after June 30, 2025. Utilizing the non-lapsing account to fund the second phase will be considered.
B. Discussion: Review of High School Building Feedback
The committee reviewed the high school building feedback from departments collected by the high school. Discussions ensued on the building size and student population moving into the future.
C. Discussion: Final Review of High School Tours
The committee discussed the high school tours completed this spring. If time permits the committee should complete the survey with feedback to Diane Nazarko on schools’ community members visited.
The committee will meet in the early fall to have the architects report their findings on the feasibility study.
Superintendent Newton will provide the committee with feedback on whether phase 2 is feasible to complete based on the final projection for the current year budget.
4. ADJOURNMENT
The meeting adjourned at 6:07 p.m.
