East Lyme Board of Education
Climate, Culture, & Innovation (CCI) Committee Meeting,
Monday, March 3, 2025, at 5:00 p.m.
Members present onsite:
Bill Derry (Chair), Olivia Fairchild
Members absent:
Leigh Gianakos, Vivek Purohit
Administrators present onsite:
Annaliese Spaziano (Assistant Superintendent)
1. OPENING OF MEETING
The meeting was called to order at 5:04 p.m.
2. DISCUSSION ITEM: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
The committee had exploratory discussions on feedback for the district Generative AI Guidance draft, the next steps for approval, and implementation with a unified approach. Discussions also included: how modern technology has shaped humanity and how to anticipate and prepare for how AI will shape behavior; human abilities that AI specifically cannot complete, plans for AI professional development & how AI should not supplant student learning.
Assistant Superintendent Spaziano shared through the support of the instructional coaches, teachers are guided to highlight what humans can do that AI specifically cannot and ways certain personal acts can have important contributions towards learning.
Assistant Superintendent Spaziano also shared there is a forum in April on AI featuring Matt Mervis, hosted by the Waterford Public Schools community. Detailed information will be shared with BOE members and East Lyme families.
3. DISCUSSION ITEM: CLIMATE & CULTURE
Assistant Superintendent Spaziano reviewed the climate survey results by school with the committee. Based on results, Satchel Pulse worked with Ms. Spaziano to adjust questions based on staff and student feedback in hopes to result in more student engagement with qualitative feedback. Staff survey questions will not be adjusted. The committee discussed that buildings will focus on fostering culture & climate work through the school improvement plans.
4. ADJOURNMENT
The meeting was adjourned at 5:56 p.m.
