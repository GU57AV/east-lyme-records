           G.     9000 Series - Bylaws of the Board

                  Order of Business - Bylaw 9325.2.pdf
                  Discussions began on the existing language in the bylaw and
                  the practice of reciting the mission statement during every
                  meeting. Discussions also included ways to incorporate the
                  mission statement within meetings, without it being
                  adhered to through policy.

                  Options of the revised bylaw were reviewed. The committee
                  will move forward a version of the bylaw without reciting
                  the mission statement, while remaining focused on its
                  importance and usage across the board goals. This will be
                  moved to the full Board in their next meeting.
