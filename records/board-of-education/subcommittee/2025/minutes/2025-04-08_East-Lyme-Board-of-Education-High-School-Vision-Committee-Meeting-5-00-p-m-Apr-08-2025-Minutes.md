3. DISCUSSION ITEMS
A. Feasibility Study & Associated Cost
There may not be enough funding in the current fiscal year to cover the study cost. The study will be reassessed at the end of the year. There are a number of capital projects the high school will need in the next 10 years and the district will need to weigh that against building a new school or renovating.
B. Update on High School Tours
The high school visits have gone well. The most recent was Guilford on Thursday April 3
rd
. The next tour is North Branford High School on April 23, 2025 at 4:00 p.m.
Ms. Williams presented the idea of gathering information (likes/dislikes) of the current building status and linking this with completing the feasibility study.
Ms. Nazarko will use her slide deck with pictures to allow us to write in observations/feedback on the tours. This will be a shared google document.
C. Open Discussion
During the next meeting feedback from various departments at the high school will be shared by Mr. Kydd pertaining to current building issues/concerns they see. Also, we will look at the fiscal state of the district and see if financially we are able to move forward with the feasibility study.
The next meeting will be held on May 13, 2025 at 5:00 p.m.
