School Reorganization Committee Meetings Minutes
Wednesday, October 22, 2025 at 4:30 PM
Central Office Conference Room

Committee Members Present: Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, and Jill Carini
Committee Members Present via Zoom: Leigh Gianakos, Chair
Administrators present: Jeff Newton, Annaliese Spaziano, and Kim Davis


1.    OPENING OF MEETING

           A.     Call to Order
                  A quorum was established and the meeting was called to
                  order at 5:02 p.m.

2.    DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS

           A.     Continued Discussion and Review of Varying School Models
                  and Reorganization Priorities.
                  The committee reviewed the graph data provided of varying
                  school configurations and achievements and concluded that
                  one configuration model is not better than another, as it
                  pertains to greater achievement for students. DRG
                  Performance Data 10.22.25.pdf
                  The committee discussed next steps and whether to move
                  forward with hiring a firm and engaging in a full study.
                  Additional discussions took place around modifications to
                  the current grade 5 structure at the middle school (i.e.
                  number of teachers with students) and would changed
                  models (4 teachers to 2 at gr. 5) help with transitions and
                  data points/achievement.
