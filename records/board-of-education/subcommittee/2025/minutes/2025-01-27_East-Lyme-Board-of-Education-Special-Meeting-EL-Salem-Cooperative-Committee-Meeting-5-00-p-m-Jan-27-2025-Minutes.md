3. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
A. Discussion/Information: Discussion: Update - Shared Services Programs
Brian Hendrickson spoke to the positive addition of security and facilities in shared services at Salem. The partnership has also been helpful with eighth grade students transitioning to the high school.
B. Discussion/Action: Reconciliation of Salem Tuition Rates for 2023-2024
Maryanna Stevens reviewed the tuition reconciliation calculation used for Salem high school students to attend East Lyme High School. Discussion ensued about the factors that contributed to the increase in tuition rates.
Motion to approve the reconciled Salem tuition rates for 2023-2024 as follows: 1) Salem Base Tuition Rate: $17,946.00; 2) Salem Total Premium Tuition Rate: $33,820.53; a total underpayment of $349,385.74, as presented.
Motion by Eric Bauman, second by Sean Reith
Motion passed unanimously.
East Lyme Pro: Eric Bauman, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini. Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, and Vivek Purohit
Salem Pro: Sean Reith, Sue Spang, Ryan Little, Jen Rucci, Krystal Hales, and Tiffany Cunningham
C.
Proposed Salem Tuition Rates for Salem Students Attending ELHS for 2025-2026
Maryanna Stevens reviewed the calculation process and shared the factors used to determine the proposed Salem Tuition Rates for the 2025-26 school year.
Move to approve the Salem Tuition Rates for 2025-2026 as follows: 1) Base Tuition Rate at $18,841.38; 2) Premium Tuition Rate of $35,509.63 as presented.
Motion by Eric Bauman, second by Sean Reith
Motion passed unanimously.
East Lyme Pro: Eric Bauman, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini. Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, and Vivek Purohit
Salem Pro: Sean Reith, Sue Spang, Ryan Little, Jen Rucci, Krystal Hales, and Tiffany Cunningham
D. Discussion: Update on ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2025-2026
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget for the FY
2025-2026
SY. The East Lyme Board of Education have held two budget workshops/meetings and will continue discussions at the next board meeting on February 10, 2025.
Proposed 2025-2026 Budget Details:
-Proposed 6.99% budget increase.
Major budget drivers circle around these areas:
Salaries & Benefits
Health Insurance
Bus Contract with First Student
Discussion ensued between the administration and East Lyme and Salem board members.
E. Discussion: Update on Salem Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2025-2026
Superintendent Hendrickson provided an update on the Salem Superintendent's Proposed Budget for the FY 2025-2026 SY. The Salem Board of Education has created a non-lapsing account this past year for unencumbered funds to provide a cushion for future unanticipated costs.
Proposed 2025-2026 Budget Details:
-Proposed 7.17% budget increase.
Major budget drivers circle around four areas:
Unanticipated Special Education Costs
Program Enhancements
Health Insurance
Next committee meeting is scheduled for February 3, 2025 to review the budget with the board of education members.
4. FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS
A. Future Agenda Items
Salem Board Chair Sean Reith shared that their BOE has been contacted by two other districts to form a cooperative agreement for their high school students. Salem anticipates forming an exploratory committee to review future options.
