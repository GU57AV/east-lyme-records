2. Policy Review in 2000 Series: Administration
A. Discussion: P2236 – Retention of Electronic Records and Information
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates and that changes to the policy were minor language revisions.

A. Discussion: P3160 – Budget Procedures and Line Item Transfers
Superintendent Newton requested to table this policy update to request clarification on conflicting language.

4. Policy Review in 4000 Series: Personnel
A. Discussion: P4111.3 – Increasing Educator Diversity – Discontinued
Superintendent Newton explained that Shipman & Goodwin removed their model policy based on recent changes to the law. Discussion ensued amongst all committee members on the desire to keep the policy active and how to maintain the contents of the policy in different mediums in the district.
Superintendent Newton will seek clarification from Shipman & Goodwin recommendations on how to move forward without a model policy and potential legal ramifications of keeping the policy active.
B. Discussion: P4112.5 – Employment and Student Teacher Checks
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates and that the changes to the policy were minor language revisions.
The committee agreed to move the revised policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
C. Discussion: P4118.112 – Prohibition of Sex Discrimination and Sexual Harassment in the Workplace
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest guidance from the Office of Civil Rights and the Shipman & Goodwin model update.
The committee agreed to move the revised policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
D. Discussion: P4152.6 – Family and Medical Leave
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates, which incorporate revised eligibility language based on new legislation.
The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.

The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.

A. Discussion: P5112 – Admission to Public Schools at or Before Age 5
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates and that the changes to the policy were minor language revisions.

D. Discussion: P5141.21 – Administration of Medications
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates.
The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.

The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates. Ms. Bradley asked for clarification around the district’s approach to student suspension and how that is reflected within the policy.
The committee agreed to move the revised policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
F. Discussion: P5145. 5 – Prohibition of Sex Discrimination and Sexual Harassment
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest guidance from the Office of Civil Rights and the Shipman & Goodwin model update.
