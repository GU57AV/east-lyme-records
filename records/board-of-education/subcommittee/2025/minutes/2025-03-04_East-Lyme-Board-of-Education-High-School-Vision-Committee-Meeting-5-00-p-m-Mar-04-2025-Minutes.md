3. DISCUSSION ITEMS
A. Debrief & Follow-up of High School Tours
Superintendent Newton began discussions on the three tours the committee completed: New London Magnet High School, Waterford High School, and The Morgan School.
Discussion ensued amongst the committee on aspects and highlights that would suit ELPS or detractors to be aware of in future planning. The committee discussed topics such as classrooms with moveable furniture; flexible classroom space to hold larger student groups for collaboration, guest speakers, special classes; keeping existing athletic fields; and server capacity and cloud-based storage.
Discussion also included partnering with area businesses or districts to capitalize on community resources and fostering community engagement and approval.
B. Committee Next Steps/Future Tours
Superintendent Newton will review the Feasibility Study at the next meeting as financial projections are being finalized.
The next tour is March 18, 2025 – Grasso High School at 4:00 p.m.
The next tours to schedule are Guilford High School and/or North Branford High School.
C. New Business
The next meeting will be held on April 8, 2025 at 5:00 p.m.
