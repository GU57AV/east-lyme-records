1. OPENING OF MEETING

The meeting was called to order at 5:02 pm. Introductions were made across the committee.

A. Minutes of December 2, 2024 Meeting
Motion to approve the minutes of the December 2, 2024 High School Vision Committee meeting, as presented.
Motion by Lori Singer, second by Esteban Garcia
Final Resolution: Motion approved.
Pro: Jeffrey Newton, Maryanna Stevens, Christian Lund, Esteban Garcia, Eric Bauman, Deb Roselli-Kelly, Lori Singer, March Salerno, Brian Hendrickson, Sue Spang
Abstain: Michelle Williams
3. DISCUSSION ITEMS
A. Final Review of Feasibility Study
The committee engaged in discussions on splitting the proposal of the Feasibility Study into two sections to see if it can be partially funded in this fiscal year. Dr. Lund and Ms. Nazarko will confirm the terms of the proposal and cost breakdown with the architecture firm. Discussions included factors to consider that were not included in the proposal and the possible timeline for the Board’s approval of the study.
B. Discussion: Review of High School Tour Options
Ms. Nazarko shared a list of high schools that have recently been built or renovated in the area and surrounding states. High schools presented were selected based on size, renovation type, infrastructure needs, style and design.
Discussions amongst the committee included sustainability, utilizing the existing landscape and natural light, similarities to East Lyme campus, cost, and factors that contribute to state reimbursement.
The committee discussed which schools to prioritize touring and agreed to reach out to the following schools: New London High School, Grasso Technical High School, Waterford High School, and The Morgan School.
The Superintendent’s office will coordinate the tour logistics and schedule the tours based on the schools’ availability.
C. Discussion: New Business
The next meeting is March 4, 2025, at 5:00 pm in room B101, ELHS.
4. ADJOURNMENT
A. Adjourn Meeting
Motion to adjourn.
Motion by Lori Singer, second by Deb Roselli Kelly
Motion Passed Unanimously.
The meeting adjourned at 6:24 p.m.
