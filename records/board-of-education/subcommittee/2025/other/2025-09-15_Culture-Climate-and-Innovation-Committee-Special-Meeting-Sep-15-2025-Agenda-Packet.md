Connecticut School Climate Law: Board Subcommittee Brief
Effective for Full Implementation: 2025–2026 School Year

Mandated by Public Act 23-167 / C.G.S. §§ 10-222aa – 10-222jj

Key Requirements for Districts & Schools
New School Climate Policy:

• Must adopt CABE-developed model approved by the SEL and School Climate Collaborative.
• Policy must define 'challenging behavior' and incorporate restorative practices.

New Required Roles:

• School Climate Coordinator (District-level): Superintendent or designee with 092.
• School Climate Specialist (School-level): Principal or certified/trained designee.

School Climate Committees:

• Required in each school with racially, culturally, and linguistically diverse representation.
• Must include students, families, teachers (union-selected), and staff.
• Required to meet at least twice per year.

School Climate Survey:

• Administered every two years to students, families, and staff.
• Must allow parent opt-out and be research-based and developmentally appropriate.

School Climate Improvement Plan (SCIP)
• Due annually to the School Climate Coordinator by December 31.
• Must include supports for challenging behavior, trauma-informed and SEL practices,
IEP/504 safeguards, and stakeholder feedback.
• Must also include a process for compiling an annual FERPA-compliant report to the Board
of Education on the number and type of tiered response incidents and supports provided.

Restorative Practices Response Policy
• Must be used for nonviolent, non-criminal student conflict or behavior.
• Excludes law enforcement involvement unless conduct escalates to violence or a crime.
