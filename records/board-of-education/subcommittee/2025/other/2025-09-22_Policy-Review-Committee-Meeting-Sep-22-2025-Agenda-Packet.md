Order of Business

Unless changed by a two-thirds vote of those present at each regular and special Board meeting, the order of business shall be
as follows:

I. CALL TO ORDER:
Read the East Lyme Public Schools Mission Statement at the start of each meeting.

follow-up by Board of Education members or staff.

Recommended approval of the minutes of the East Lyme Board of Education meeting.

VI. CONSENT AGENDA
Items in this section require Board approval and are usually driven by Policy or statute. These items require little or no
discussion. These include maternity requests, field trips, and other requests for normal operations for the district. All of these
items can be moved at once. If any Board member desires, an item can be moved from the consent agenda to the
Discussion/Action item section.
