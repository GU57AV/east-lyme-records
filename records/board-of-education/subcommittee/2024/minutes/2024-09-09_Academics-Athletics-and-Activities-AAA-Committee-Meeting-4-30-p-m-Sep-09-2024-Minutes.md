1. OPENING OF MEETING
The meeting was called to order at 4:39 p.m.
2. DISCUSSION ITEM: CULTURE
A. Rebranding and Focus
Mrs. Spaziano discussed rebranding the committee’s name to Culture, Climate and Innovation as athletics are no longer agenda items in meetings since a full-time administrator in athletics has been hired. Academics and activities are informed and improved by developing culture and climate within the district.
Discussion ensued on Artificial Intelligence and how it is used within the district. Topics included:
Utilizing AI to inform curriculum development
Software programs used in the district that currently utilize or have components of AI
Future Professional Development with Instructional Technology Coaches
Approaching the use of AI thoughtfully through maintaining effective district policies as the technology evolves and develops.
Not allowing AI to be a replacement for student work but used as a tool to assist in learning.
Instill in student values to produce their own work and voice and how that is linked to culture
Encouraging integrity within the teacher-student relationship and expectations of assignments
Mrs. Spaziano asked the committee members to read
the
AI Guidance for Schools Toolkit
in preparation for the next meeting.
B. Districtwide Culture & Climate Plan Draft
Mrs. Spaziano shared a draft of the
ELPS Districtwide Culture & Climate Plan
The District Culture & Climate Committee will review and work on the plan based on Satchel Pulse Survey results, throughout the year, but actions toward the plan should be a committee decision. Each school has their own team working to foster culture within their own building. The long-term goal is the committee serve as an umbrella connecting all the teams into same purpose and program with all staff serving as stakeholders in the process – each role in district counts. Discussion included looking for themes and patterns from quarterly survey results, adjusting future survey questions based on answers provided, and how the work can produce sustainable and long-lasting effects.
The next committee meeting will be rescheduled to Monday, October, 28, 5:00- 6:00 p.m.
3. ADJOURNMENT
The meeting was adjourned at 5:35 p.m.
