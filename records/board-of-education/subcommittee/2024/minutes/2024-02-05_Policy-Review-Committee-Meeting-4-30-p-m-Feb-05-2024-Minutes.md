East Lyme Board of Education
Policy Review Committee Meeting
Monday, February 5, 2024 at 4:30 p.m.
MINUTES

Alisa Bradley (Chair)
Administrators present:
Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Annaliese Spaziano (Assistant Superintendent of Curriculum and Instruction), Maryanna Stevens (Director of Finance)
1. OPENING OF MEETING
A. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 4:31 p.m.
2. Policy Review in 1000 Series: Community
A. Discussion: P1010 - Non-Discrimination (Community)
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to move forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
3. Policy Review in 2000 Series: Administration
Discussion: A. P2236 - Retention and Disposition of Records and Information
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to move forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
4. Policy Review in 3000 Series: Business
Discussion: A. P3160 - Transfer of Funds within Budget
The committee discussed the revised policy and agreed adjust the title to match Shipman & Goodwin’s model policy to “Board Budget Procedures and Line Item Transfers.” The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
Discussion: B. P3260 - Disposal of Obsolete or Surplus Equipment Materials
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to move forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
Discussion: C. P3440 – Inventories
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and discussed adjusting the title to “Fixed Asset Inventories” to ensure policy clarification. The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
5. Policy Review of 4000 Series: Personnel
Discussion: A. P4111.3 - Increasing Educator Diversity Plan
The committee discussed the revised policy based on state legislation and the Shipman & Goodwin model policy. Clarification on training requirements for hiring committees was discussed and requested. Superintendent Newton will confirm the language with Shipman & Goodwin and will report back to the committee.
Based on the state legislation deadline requirements, the policy was moved forward to the BOE meeting on 2/5/24 for review.
Discussion: B. P4118.11 - Non-Discrimination (Personnel)
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to move forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
6. Policy Review of 5000 Series: Students
Discussion: A. P5118 - Nonresident Students
The committee discussed the revised policy to include the company EF Exchange Programs in the list of approved programs vetted by Superintendent. The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
Discussion: B. P5145.41 - Non-Discrimination (Students)
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to revise the contact table to remove the principal name to ensure further approvals are not required based on personnel changes. The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
Discussion: C. P5112 - Admission to Public School at or Before Age 5
The committee discussed the revised policy based on state legislation and the Shipman & Goodwin model policy. The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
7. Future Agenda Items
Discussion: A. Future Agenda Items
The Policy Review Committee will meet on 3/18/24.
8. Adjournment
A. Adjournment
The meeting was adjourned at 5:08 p.m.
