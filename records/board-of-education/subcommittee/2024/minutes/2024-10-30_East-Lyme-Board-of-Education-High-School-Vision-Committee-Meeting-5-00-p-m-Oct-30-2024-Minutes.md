East Lyme Board of Education
High School Vision Committee Meeting
Tuesday, October 30 2024 at 5:00 pm
MINUTES
Committee Members Present:
Marc Salerno (Vision Committee Chair), Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Eric Bauman (Board of Education Chair), Maryanna Stevens (Director of Finance), Chris Lund (Director of Facilities) left 6:10 p.m.,
Esteban Garcia (BOE member), Deb Roselli-Kelly (ELHS Principal), Henry Kydd (ELHS Assistant Principal), Lori Singer (ELHS Science Teacher),
Brian Hendrickson (Salem Superintendent), Michelle Williams (town member) left 6:45 p.m.
Committee Members Absent:
1. OPENING OF MEETING
A. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 5:00 pm.
Introductions were made across the committee.
2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES
A. Minutes of the October 8, 2024 Meeting
Motion to approve the minutes of the October 8, 2024 High School Vision Committee meeting, as presented.
Motion by Lori Singer, second by Eric Bauman.
Motion approved unanimously.
3. DISCUSSION ITEMS
Mr. Salerno requested that Item C is moved to Item A to allow enough daylight for the walkthrough process.
A. Building Walkthrough
Mrs. Roselli-Kelly & Mr. Kydd provided committee members with a tour of the high school building, including athletic fields, several classrooms, administrative offices, media center, and restrooms. Discussions and topics considered were classroom size, desk types, the use (or lack of use) of hallway lockers, ADA compliance, and safety.
B. Discussion: Review of Feasibility Study
Discussion ensued around the proposed feasibility study submitted by Silver + Petrucelli Associates, including the proposal timeline, budgeting the study within this fiscal year, and the timeline of the study. The committee agreed to request S+P to see if the study can be phased out into stages to allow work to begin during the school year; to include adding a campus style option to the study proposal request; and the committee will schedule local high school renovations for research. The committee will invite Silver + Petrucelli Associates to the next meeting to further discuss the proposed feasibility study.
C. Discussion: Town Debt Services/Financial Overview
Maryanna Stevens initiated the discussion on the financial overview of town debt services, current bonding rates, the effect of interest rates and the market economy during project bidding.
Discussions also included the town building renovation schedule and needs, final renovation costs and build/plan timelines with area districts, classroom size standards and anticipating evolving student and classroom needs. The committee will create a list of questions to interview area schools that have completed renovations for feedback on what worked well and what would be completed differently.
The next meeting is scheduled for December 2, 2024 at 5:00 pm in the INTV room at East Lyme High School.
4. ADJOURNMENT
The meeting adjourned at 7:04 pm.
