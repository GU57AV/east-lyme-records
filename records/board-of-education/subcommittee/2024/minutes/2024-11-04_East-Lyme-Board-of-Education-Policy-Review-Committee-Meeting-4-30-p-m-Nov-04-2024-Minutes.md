East Lyme Board of Education
Policy Review Committee Meeting
Monday, November 4, 2024 at 4:30 p.m.
MINUTES
Members present:
Alisa Bradley (Chair), Esteban Garcia, Jaime Barr Shelburn
Administrators present:
Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent)
1. OPENING OF MEETING
A. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 4:36 p.m.
2. Policy Review in 1000 Series: Community
A. Discussion: P1010 – Non-Discrimination (Community)
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates. Committee members discussed whether the policy language is inclusive of Artificial Intelligence and clarification was sought on the inclusion of Boy Scouts of America language.
Mr. Newton will seek legal clarification on the concerns reviewed.
The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
3. Policy Review in 4000 Series: Personnel
A. Discussion: P4118.11 – Non-Discrimination (Personnel)
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates.  The committee acknowledged similar language and concerns in Policy 1010, which Mr. Newton will address.
The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
B. Discussion: P4118.112 – Title IX Sex Discrimination and Sexual Harassment (Personnel)
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates. Committee members discussed whether this policy language is also inclusive of Artificial Intelligence.
Mr. Newton will seek legal clarification on the concerns reviewed.
The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the next BOE meeting for review.

5. Policy Review in 9000 Series: Bylaws of the Board
A. Discussion: 9132.3 – Standing Committee: Academics, Athletics, & Activities (AAA) Committee
The committee discussed the revision of the committee title into Climate, Culture, and Innovation and discussed the how to ensure academics will continue to be included with the language change.  The committee agreed to move the bylaw and will provide feedback to Assistant Superintendent Spaziano & Chair of the Committee, Bill Derry.
The committee agreed to move the revised bylaw forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
B. Discussion: 9325 – Meeting Conduct
The committee discussed incorporating additional language in the public comments section, clarifying that the platform is not to be used to make inflammatory remarks towards staff or board members.
The committee agreed to move the revised bylaw forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
C. Discussion: 9325.2 – Order of Business
The committee discussed revising the bylaw to allow items not requiring discussion be included in items that can be approved through the Consent section to allow better efficiency during regular meetings. Any item within consent can still be removed by request to allow further discussion.
The committee agreed to move the revised bylaw forward to the next BOE meeting for review.
6. Adjournment
A. Adjournment
The meeting was adjourned at 5:35 p.m.
