East Lyme Board of Education
Policy Review Committee Special Meeting
Tuesday, March 26, 2024 at 4:30 p.m.
MINUTES
Members present onsite:
Alisa Bradley (Chair), Jaime Barr Shelburn
Members absent: Esteban Garcia
Administrators present:
Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Annaliese Spaziano (Assistant Superintendent of Curriculum and Instruction)
1. OPENING OF MEETING
A. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 4:35 p.m.
2. Policy Review in 1000 Series: Community
A. Discussion: 1250 – School Visitors
The committee discussed the existing policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model and agreed to revise the existing language regarding visitor approval for school entry. The revised policy will be moved forward to the 4/1/24 BOE meeting for review.
3
.
Policy Review of 4000 Series: Personnel
A. Discussion: 4111.3 – Increasing Educator Diversity Plan
The committee discussed removing antiquated language from a former version of the policy not included in the Shipman & Goodwin model update and expanding the training requirement for all members of the hiring committee. The revised policy will be moved forward to the 4/1/24 BOE meeting for review.
B. Discussion: 4112.5 – Employment and Student Teacher Checks
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to move forward to the 4/1/24 BOE meeting for review.
C. Discussion: 4118.114 – Reports of Suspected Abuse or Neglect of Children or Reports of Sexual Assault of Students by Employees
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to add “custodian” and “security staff” to the definition list of “School employee”.  The revised policy will be moved forward to the 4/1/24 BOE meeting for review.
D. Discussion: 4420 – Employee Use of the District’s Computer Systems and Electronic Communication
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to remove the clause “on Board premises” to improve readability. The revised policy will be moved forward to the 4/1/24 BOE meeting for review.
4. Policy Review of 5000 Series: Students
A. Discussion: 5141.21 – Administration of Student Medication in School
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to move forward to the 4/1/24 BOE meeting for review.
B. Discussion: 5131.9 – Bullying Prevention & Intervention
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to move forward to the 4/1/24 BOE meeting for review.
C. Discussion: Student Use of District’s Computer Systems and Internet Safety
The committee discussed the revised policy based on the Shipman & Goodwin model policy update and agreed to move forward to the 4/1/24 BOE meeting for review.
7. Future Agenda Items
Discussion: A. Future Agenda Items
The Policy Review Committee will continue to revise current policies based on 2023 legal updates provided by Shipman & Goodwin and will find a future date for a special meeting before the existing June meeting.
8. ADJOURNMENT
A. Adjournment
The meeting was adjourned at 5:24 p.m.
