East Lyme Board of Education
High School Vision Committee Meeting
Monday, December 2, 2024 at 5:00 pm
MINUTES
Committee Members Present:
Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Maryanna Stevens (Director of Finance), Chris Lund (Director of Facilities),
Esteban Garcia (BOE member), Eric Bauman via phone (BOE member), Deb Roselli-Kelly (ELHS Principal), Henry Kydd (ELHS Assistant Principal), Lori Singer (ELHS Science Teacher), Marc Salerno at 5:54 p.m. (Vision Committee Chair)

1. OPENING OF MEETING
A. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 5:05 pm.
Introductions were made across the committee.
2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES
A. Minutes of October 30, 2024 Meeting
Motion to approve the minutes of the October 30, 2024 High School Vision Committee meeting, as presented.
Motion by Esteban Garcia, second by Deb Roselli-Kelly
Motion approved unanimously.
3. DISCUSSION ITEMS
A. Review of Feasibility Student with Architect
Dr. Lund introduced the architects from Silver Petrucelli Associates and discussions began on factors to consider within the proposed feasibility study. Topics discussed included: planning around enrollment projections; reimbursement rates with the state; projection on costs and construction timeframes; areas of focus in the engineering study; communication plans; zoning regulations and ADA requirements; student educational needs based on existing course study and traffic patterns within existing and future programming.
B. Discussion: High School Renovation Tours & Dates
Diane Nazarko will compile a list of high schools to tour based on size, timeline completed, and renovation and infrastructure needs. Options will be shared at the next scheduled meeting.
C. Discussion: New Business
Discussion occurred about the feasibility study being completed split between two fiscal years and communication to the board on a status update. The goal is to bring the feasibility study to the Board for approval in January 2025.
The next meeting is January 6, 2025 at 5:00 pm in room B101, ELHS.
4. ADJOURNMENT
The meeting adjourned at 6:05 p.m.
