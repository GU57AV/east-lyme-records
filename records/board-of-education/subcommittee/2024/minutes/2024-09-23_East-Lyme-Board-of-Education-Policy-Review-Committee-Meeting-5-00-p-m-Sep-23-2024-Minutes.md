2. Policy Review in 6000 Series: Instruction
A. Discussion: P6110 – Parental Access to Instructional Materials
The committee discussed implementing this policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates.  The Board members discussed how this practice was already occurring in the district without a formalized Board policy and the ongoing goal to post current curriculum on the ELPS website to allow accessibility to all families. The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the 10/7/24 BOE Regular meeting for review.
B. Discussion: P6141.51 – Enrollment in Advance Course or Program & Challenging Curriculum
The committee discussed implementing this policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates. Prior to this meeting, Assistant Superintendent Spaziano & ELHS Principal Kelly reviewed the model policy to compare current ELHS practice to ensure that they align as presented.  The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the 10/7/24 BOE Regular meeting for review.
C. Discussion: P6146.11 – Weighted Grading & Calculation of Grade Point Averages
The committee discussed implementing this policy based on the latest Shipman & Goodwin model updates. Assistant Superintendent Spaziano & ELHS Principal Kelly reviewed the model policy to current ELHS practice to ensure the policy aligns to current HS protocol. The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the 10/7/24 BOE Regular meeting for review.
D. Discussion: P6172.6 – Credit for Courses Taken Online/Remote or Off-Campus
The committee discussed the revised policy based on a recent partnership with LEARN and the Regional Remote Learning Program to ensure they align with current practice and afford students to enroll in RRLP without disruption to their ELHS studies. The committee agreed to move the policy forward to the 10/7/24 BOE Regular meeting for review.
3. Future Agenda Items
Discussion: A. Future Agenda Items
The district is awaiting model policy updates from Shipman & Goodwin, which will inform future agenda items. The next meeting is scheduled on November 4, 2024.
