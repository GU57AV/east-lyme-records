                            East Lyme Board of Education
                          Budget Committee Meeting Minutes
                                    May 26, 2009
                                   Central Office

In attendance: Mary Broderick, Melissa Ford, Tim Hagen Committee Chair, Kevin Seery, Nancy
Burdick, Karen Costello, Paul Freeman, Don Meltabarger, Paul Smotas, John Sullivan

Excused: Marlene Nickerson

The meeting was called to order at 5:30 p.m.

Agenda Item 1. FY 09 Budget Status Summary

Dr. Smotas updated the committee on the overall status of the 2008/2009 budget and reported
that there were no new significant findings or changes. The estimated FY09 budget balance at
06/30/09 is $195,000, and Dr. Smotas provided a summary of the accounts with either surpluses
or deficits. One key point is that a surplus of $300,000 in the teachers salary account was
obtained from the breakage in hiring 29 new replacement teachers this year. For 2009/20010, we
do not expect to have any where near as large of a surplus due to reductions in staffing levels.
The committee was reminded that a virtual budget freeze has been in effect during much of the
year and that several purchase orders have been submitted to Central Office for text books and
replenishment of needed supplies. Dr. Smotas said that he would inform the Board of any major
expenditure for the remaining year.


Agenda Item 2. Continued 2009/2010 Budget Deliberations

The Town budget referendum was passed on May 22, and the Ad/Staff Team met on the
following Tuesday for final review of the teaching staff reductions for next year. The total number
of reductions remains at 7 FTEs and Dr. Smotas and Dr. Freeman will be meeting with the
teacher bargaining unit later this week. The goal is to finalize the staffing positions by the end of
the week such that communication may occur as quickly as possible. The Budget Committee
commended the Ad/Staff Team for their rapid follow-up in bringing the teacher staffing plan to
closure. At the last Budget Committee meeting, Dr. Smotas reported that there may be savings
in bus transportation for next year. He informed the committee today that they have concluded
that 2 buses can be eliminated for a total annual savings of $70,000. The committee discussed
the timing for bringing the 2009/2010 budget recommendations to the full Board and concluded
                                                                       th
that the Board should be able to adopt its final budget at the June 8 meeting.

The Committee briefly discussed timing and process for reviewing Capital planning in concert
with the Town. The Committee agreed that this should be a standing agenda item for the Budget
Committee and that in September along with Building & Grounds Committee, a Capital Plan
should be updated and presented to the Board of Education for review.

The committee was adjourned at 6:21 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,
Tim Hagen
Budget Committee Chair
