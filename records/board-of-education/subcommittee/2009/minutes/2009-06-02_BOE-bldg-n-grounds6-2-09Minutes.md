                             Buildings and Grounds Committee Meeting
                                            June 2, 2009
                                             Town Hall
                                              5:30 pm

Board Members Present: Jaime Ellis (Chair), Al Littlefield, Kevin Seery
Administration Present: Paul Freeman, Don Meltabarger

Meeting called to order at 5:30
                                            Agenda/Purpose

   1. Flanders Parking Lot: Paul Formica presented the committee with a plan to redesign the Flanders
      parking lot entrance/exit and also the entrance/exit to the high school along Boston Post Road. It
      would be an approximate 100 extra parking spots and a stop light to make it safer for everyone,
      including the fire department, to navigate entering Boston Post Road. This is in the initial stages
      and still requires more discussion by this committee on what steps to take next before coming to
      the full Board for discussion/approval.

   2. Artificial Turf at the High School: Jim Moran and Don Meltabarger presented the committee
      with a grant option from the Jeremy Worrell Foundation to get funding to pay for any consulting
      fees that would be involved in the study and designing of an artificial turf. The committee
      decided it was a good idea to pursue this grant in case the board and community decided to go
      through with the turf. The committee discussed the possibility of putting together a committee to
      research the pros/cons of artificial turf. We will meet again to discuss this before bringing it to the
      board.

   3. HVAC at High school/Middle school Gym floor: Paul Freeman met with the town Building
      Committee and they reported that the high school gym floor is good for now and they will
      continue to monitor it. He also reported that they looked into the middle schools HVAC unit and
      found that it is too big and that a smaller unit is needed to make it more efficient and this would
      also help to eliminate the “float” of the middle school gym floor. A request was made to that they
      be allowed to go to bid to look for this. This building committee agreed with this request.

   At Don’s request we have added high school boilers to the next agenda.

   Public Comment:
   1. Karen Rak: Would like us to enlist as many individuals as possible to provide due diligence
   because there are so many conflicting studies regarding artificial turf. She also suggested that we
   should take the time to do this the right way to determine the safety of our children/environment and
   the true costs involved.
   2. Bob Gadbois: Supports what Karen Rak stated and would like us to do research so that we do not
   have another Flanders Playground incident.
   3. Mike Shultz: Stated that the wooden bleachers are in poor condition, however every effort should
   be made to reuse the wood if at all possible.

Meeting adjourned at 6:40

Respectfully submitted,
Jaime Ellis
June 16, 2009
