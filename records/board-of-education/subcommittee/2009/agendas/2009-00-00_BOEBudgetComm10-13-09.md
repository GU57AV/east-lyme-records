                            East Lyme Board of Education
                          Budget Committee Meeting Minutes
                                  October 13, 2009
                          Room B100, East Lyme High School



In attendance: Mary Broderick, Melissa Ford, Tim Hagen Committee Chair, Marlene Nickerson,
Kevin Seery, Nancy Burdick, Karen Costello, James Lombardo, Don Meltabarger, Wayne
Sweeney

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m.

Agenda Item 1. Proposed 2010-2011 and 2011-2012 Tuition Rates for Non-Salem Students

Don Meltabarger provided the committee with background and past precedence for adjusting
future tuition rates for non-Salem students. Typically, the BOE has adopted a 5% increase in the
tuition rate on annual basis and has authorized the adjustment for two consecutive years. Don
also provided the committee with non-resident tuition rates for surrounding school districts. East
Lyme’s rates are comparable to other districts but could in the future be tracking to the higher end
of rates. Dr. Lombardo was concerned that the East Lyme did not have a clear set of guiding
principles for establishing the rates. The committee agreed to recommend to the full BOE that
the 2009/2010 non-Salem tuition rates be set at the 2008/2009 level with a 5% increase, and that
the tuition rate for the 2010/2011 school year be determined in the spring of 2010. In addition,
the committee agreed that a policy or guiding principles should be explored for determining the
tuition rates in the future.

Agenda Item 2. FY 10 Budget Discussion

Don Meltabarger presented to the committee an updated estimated budget for the 2009-2010
school year as of September 30, 2009. Overall, the budget is tracking at about $143,000 deficit
due to primarily additional needs in the Special Ed. services. Dr. Lombarbo has instituted a
purchasing freeze on educational supplies to assure coverage of the projected deficit. The
committee commended Don Meltabarger and Nancy Burdick on their diligence in tracking the
budget and noted that this is a particularly tight year with almost no flexibility in the budget to
cover unexpected deficits. Committee discussed the need to have the full Board informed on the
evolving situation in Special Education budgeting due to the impact of the Stimulus funding and
the shortfall that it will create for the 2010-2011 budget. Dr. Lomarbo offered to lead the
discussion at the BOE meeting on October 27.

The committee was adjourned at 7:30 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,
Tim Hagen
Budget Committee Chair
