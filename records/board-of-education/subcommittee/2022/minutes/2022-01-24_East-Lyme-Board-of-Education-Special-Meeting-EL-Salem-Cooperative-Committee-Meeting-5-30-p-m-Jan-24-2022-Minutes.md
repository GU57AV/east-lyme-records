Information: C. Discussion: Update on ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2022-2023
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget for the FY 2022-2023 SY. We have held two budget workshop/meetings with the Board of Education and will continue discussions at the next board meeting on February 7th.
Proposed 2022-2023 Budget Details:
-Proposed 4.59% budget increase
Major budget drivers circle around four areas:
Salaries: increase in salaries, contracts and minimum wage is up
Benefits: 5% for health insurance, in a state partnership plan hoping to see a drop
Contracted Services: additional nursing costs in special education, additional technology expenses
Utilities: natural gas and propane costs increase
-AARP/ESSER Grant funding supporting district budget
-Areas of focus:
Student support with math and technology coaches
Additional intervention support at the high school level
Pathways and career components in the high school Coastal Connections program
Technology advances
Additional social and emotional support with social work at the middle school level and psychology support at the elementary level
Discussion ensued between administration and the board members of East Lyme and Salem.
Information: D. Discussion: Update on Salem Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2022-2023
Superintendent Hendrickson provided and update on the Salem Superintendent's Proposed Budget for the FY 2022-2023 SY.
Proposed 2022-2023 Budget Details:
-Proposed 4.81% budget increase
-Health insurance is a moving number
-Nonbinding Declaration of Intent notice has been sent to families to gauge transportation needs, and thus, associated costs with bus contract due to increase in families picking up and dropping off students
-No early notification incentive to know which staff members are returning
-Next budget workshop is scheduled for January 31, 2022, to review budget with board of education members
Discussion ensued between administration and the board members of East Lyme and Salem.
Discussion, Information: E. Discussion: Update - Shared Food Services Program
Chris Urban, East Lyme Director of Food Services, provided an update on the current program:
-The supply chain is starting to get better, but pricing is still increased on produce and protein items.
-USDA announced that they will be increasing the reimbursement rate to schools.
-Lunch is going up to $4.56 and breakfast to $2.60 through June 30, 2021.
-No determination has been made for next school year in terms of rates.
-State of Connecticut was part of a Community Eligibility Provision study group and may have an additional year to serve breakfast and lunch meals at no cost to all enrolled students without collecting household applications.
