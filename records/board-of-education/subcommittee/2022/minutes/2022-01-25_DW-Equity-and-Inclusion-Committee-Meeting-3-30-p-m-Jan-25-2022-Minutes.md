One of the top choices is Diversity, Equity and Inclusion for facilitating difficult conversations.
We also wanted to share that last week some of our staff and administrators were involved in a training with Tony Ferraiolo to understand the terminology with gender identity and sexual preference. Many administrators and staff felt it was a valuable training.
Discussion ensued with committee members.

Superintendent Newton and Assistant Superintendent Spaziano shared an
article

New Haven school board adopts policy to protect, assist LGBTQ students, ‘it is literally life saving’.
Discussion ensued with committee members on how as a district we might be able to adopt a policy regarding transgender students. Superintendent Newton would like to read more information on the policy that New Haven BOE passed along with reaching out to Shipman & Goodwin.
Discussion, Information: C: Bias Response Protocol
Superintendent Newton discussed information regarding the bias response protocol with Mr. Garcia. We need to first have training before moving forward with starting a committee to create a bias response protocol.

Superintendent Newton and the districtwide and school-based committee continue to prioritize their efforts for the 2021/2022 school year. Mrs. Schoman (FL Principal), Mrs. Kelly (ELMS Assistant Principal) and Mr. Kydd (ELHS Assistant Principal) provided an update on the DEI school-based teams.
