Discussion, Information, Presentation: A. Discussion: Linda Darcy and DEI PD Work for this Year
Superintendent Newton introduced Dr. Linda Darcy, Director of Teaching and Learning at LEARN. This year, we will use Dr. Darcy as a consultant to support the school and district-level work. The following ideas came forth from the discussion:
Empower our staff with problem-solving skills in the classroom and/or team. Provide tools and strategies to help.
Role playing and how to respond – practice with our staff.
Dealing with courageous conversations within our class and scenarios. What should be handled in the classroom vs administration?
Continue focusing on being a proactive community and fostering the climate we strive for.
Put students in leadership roles with the help of Dr. Darcy and show how they can be a great voice for the school community.
Follow-up on the work of the Equity Institute training that previously occurred.
Provide an Ed Camp to offer different sessions based on the needs of the staff.
Best to survey or approach the staff for ideas and then provide workshop time.
Provide curriculum writing revisions to work cooperatively with Dr. Darcy.
Provide our students with the tools to be active participants in this work.
Jeff and Annaliese will work further with Dr. Darcy to plan the next steps.
