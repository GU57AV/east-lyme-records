1. Call to Order
Procedural: A. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 3:34 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
Discussion, Information, Presentation: A. Discussion: Review and Discussion of Survey Results
Superintendent Newton shared the survey results of the 2021/2022 ELPS Districtwide DEI Self-Assessment Summary.
Discussion ensued with committee members.
Discussion, Information: B. Discussion: Sister Mural Project Update
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the Sister Mural Project. On April 14th, students from elementary, middle and high school painted the three panels. Mr. Jason Deeble and committee have asked for help on when to unveil the Sister Mural Project to the community.
Discussion ensued with committee members but feel it would benefit the students and the community most if we waited until fall 2022.
Discussion, Information: B. Discussion: Restorative Justice Training
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the restorative justice training that is planned for June 20th and June 21st with Joanne Freiberg. We have about 60 staff members who will be attending. Our next step will be how we incorporate this training to the rest of the staff in the buildings.  It is the hope that the restorative practice training will help to build and restore relationships.
Discussion ensued with committee members.
Discussion, Information: D. Discussion: Update from DEI School-Based Teams and 22/23 Planning
Superintendent Newton and the districtwide and school-based committees continue to prioritize their efforts for the 2021/2022 school year. Mrs. Schoman (FL Principal), Mrs. Zielinski (ELMS Teacher) and Mrs. Petrillo (ELHS Teacher) provided an update on the DEI school-based teams. The committee members also discussed what are next steps will be for the 2022/2023 school year. The committee will reconvene meetings for districtwide and school-based sub-committees in the Fall 2022.
