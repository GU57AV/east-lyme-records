East Lyme Board of Education Facilities Asset Management Committee Meeting
Wednesday, April 13, 2022 at 3:30 p.m.
Via ZOOM
Minutes
1. Call to Order
Procedural: A. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 3:30 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Review Charter Tasking and Expectations, Ground Rules
Dr. Lund and committee members reviewed the recent approved charter for the Facilities Asset Management Committee and what expectations we plan to achieve with forming this committee.
Discussion ensued with committee members.
Discussion, Information: B. Discussion: Update on open/Pending CIP Projects (Approved in Prior and Current Fiscal Years)
Dr. Lund provided an update on open CIP projects.
Discussion, Information: C. Discussion: Baseball Field Status Report
Dr. Lund provided an update on the status of the baseball field reconstruction and discussion ensued with committee members. Irrigation line installation is occurring the week of April 18.
Discussion, Information: D. Discussion: Pool Roof
Dr. Lund provided an update on the pool roof.  The committee plans to bring the topic forward to the Board of Education meeting on May 23, 2022 for discussion. Committee members agreed to recommend to the FFO and the BOE that we move forward with design/engineering and roof repairs using the funds in the escrow account.
Discussion, Information: E. Discussion: Inflationary Impacts on Open Projects
Dr. Lund provided an update on the impacts of inflation on the current open projects for East Lyme Public Schools. Dr. Lund informed committee members that due to inflation and supply/demand issues, the HVAC work originally scheduled for ELMS this summer will be sliding at least one year.
Discussion ensued with committee members.
3. Adjournment
Procedural: A. Adjournment
The meeting adjourned at 4:05 p.m.
Respectfully Submitted,
Christian Lund
Director of Facilities
