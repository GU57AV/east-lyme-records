EL Parks & Recreation Director Dave Putnam will ask the Committee to recommend to the Board of Education to move forward in adding Kevin's Kourts to the proposed Miracle League Field at Flanders School.  Bankshot representatives have determined that Flanders School would be a suitable site for the courts and has pledged monies to pay for the construction of the courts.  The courts would be accessible for school recess and PE classes as well.  Six of these courts have been installed in CT.  This would be the first in SE CT.
