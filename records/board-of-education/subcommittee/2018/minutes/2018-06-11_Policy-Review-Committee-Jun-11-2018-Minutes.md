EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION
East Lyme Public Schools will inspire, engage and educate each student to become a
contributing citizen and a responsible, independent, and critical thinker.
Policy Review Committee Minutes
June 11, 2018
Members present:
Jaime Barr Shelburn, Leigh Gianakos, Timothy Hagen, Barbara Senges
Administrators Present:
Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Amy Drowne (Assistant Superintendent for Curriculum, Instruction and Assessment
Meeting called to order at 5:00
1. Call to Order
Superintendent Jeffrey Newton distributed Shipman and Goodwin's priority policy and regulation listing (most pressing, pressing, and in need of review).  The Board of Education has revised and/or reviewed the majority of the policies listed.  The remaining on list are in progress of being revised or are listed under future items to discuss.  Assistant Superintendent Amy Drowne noted the majority of the items on the list are mandated to be included in the student handbooks.
2. Review of BOE Policies in Order of Discussion:
Discussion:
A. Second Review - 4218.51 Personnel: Social Networking
This policy was brought back to the Policy Review Committee for a second review.   Topics discussed:
Item #4 of the Regulation (pg. 5) "...employees must refrain from social media that breaches confidentiality obligation of school district employees."
Should a statement pertaining to the confidentiality of students be included?
The Department of Education covers student confidentiality under the Connecticut Code of Responsibility.
We need to verify that the Board currently has a specific policy that addresses student confidentiality.
Teachers and staff should only use district or building-based social media and/or Facebook accounts to communicate with students.  Personal accounts must not be used.
The suggested regulation states that it is "not appropriate" to use personal accounts.  Jeffrey Newton will confirm with Shipman and Goodwin their specific language to use.
Is was noted that East Lyme students have district email accounts for communication.
It was noted that staff are currently using crowd funding to purchase additional classroom items.
Jeffrey Newton will check with the building principals their thoughts on crowd funding.  He will also confirm with Shipman and Goodwin their specific language regarding crowd funding and consequences if permission was not granted.
Permission must be granted before anyone crowd funds.   The building principal should give the initial approval with final approval being granted by the Superintendent.  An approval process needs to be established.
Someone will have to monitor all crowd funded donations and flow of funds.
Any items purchased or received through crowd funding should be the property of the district.
Who is going to maintain crowd funded items or provide the resources to integrate hardware and software if required?
Would the Town ever consider adjusting the school budget if crowd funding is used regularly?
The Policy Review Committee discussed district wide social media accounts.  Amy Drowne noted that some grant applications are currently asking for our district's social media accounts and how they are being used.
District wide social media accounts would be a great forum for getting information out to parents and the public.
Someone would need to monitor comments and maintain the accounts.
Will have to adhere to student data privacy mandates.
Other school districts are using social media to their advantage.
This Policy will be brought back to the Policy Review Committee for another review.
