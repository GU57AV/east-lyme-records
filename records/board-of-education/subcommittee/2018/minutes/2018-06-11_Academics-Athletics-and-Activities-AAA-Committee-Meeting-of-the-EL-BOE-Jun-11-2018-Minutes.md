Looping and Departmentalization at the Elementary Schools:
Amy Drowne updated the committee on Looping and Departmentalization at the Elementary Schools. Following discussions between Niantic Center School’s Administration , Teachers and Central Office along with review of the looping model it has been determined that we will no longer be utilizing a looping model at NCS. NCS was the only elementary school within the EL district still utilizing this model. Throughout discussions focus was on Curriculum standards , slide back, family dynamics , the concern for students becoming dependent and unable to adapt. Discontinuing the use of looping was met with overwhelming support.
In addition, The Constellation Program at Flanders was also reviewed as it is also a Program that Loops. Amy Drowne, Flanders Administration and Teachers discussed & reviewed this program in the same fashion and this program will end as well.
The East Lyme Middle School will continue with looping at this time however administration will continue discussions with Central Office (Amy Drowne) on possible Looping structure changes within the Kiva’s .  (See agenda attachment)
