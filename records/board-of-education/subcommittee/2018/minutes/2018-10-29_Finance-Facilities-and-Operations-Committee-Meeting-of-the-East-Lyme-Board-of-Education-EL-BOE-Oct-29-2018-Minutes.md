Finance, Facilities and Operations Committee Meeting
East Lyme Board of Education
Monday, October 29, 2018

East Lyme High School

MINUTES
Members Present: Eric Bauman (Co-Chair), Barbara Senges (Co-Chair), Tim Hagen,
Ryan Shrader
Administrators Present: Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Amy Drowne (Assistant Superintendent), Michael Susi (ELHS Principal)
1. Call to Order
The meeting convened at 6:00 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
A. Proposed Kevin’s Kourts Project to be Located at the Miracle League Field, Flanders School
Discussed accepting the donation from Kevin Ollie’s Foundation of $50,000 to the Miracle League to put in handicapped accessible courts. BoE needs to approve the use of the Flanders courts for this project. FFO proposes to move forward with the courts. Also, a discussion about advertising on the courts and any advertising would come to Superintendent for approval. BoE would be informed of advertising decisions.
B. Proposed East Lyme Fitness Center Equipment
Two proposals for new cardio equipment for Fitness Center. Reviewed quotes and discussed where funding could come from. FFO is generally in favor of the proposal, but would like more information from an expert in the fitness field to advise us.
C. Proposed ELHS Scoreboard
Discussed proposal for new scoreboard on turf field. Will discuss with Stonington who did this already. Also, see if other companies do this.
D. Monthly Budget/Budget Transfer Update
Reviewed current month budget and actuals. One transfer for administration otherwise on track.
E. Proposed Van Replacement (2)
Investigating options for Van replacement for AIM replacement and Mail Van
F. ELHS Student Activity Fund
First review of the ELHS activity account, further review at next meeting.
3. Adjournment
The meeting concluded at 6:55 p.m.
Respectfully Submitted,
Eric Bauman
Co-Chair
FFO Committee
