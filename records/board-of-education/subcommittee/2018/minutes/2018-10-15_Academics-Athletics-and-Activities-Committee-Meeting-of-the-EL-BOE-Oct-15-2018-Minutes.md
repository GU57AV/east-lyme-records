B.
Review and Discussion Regarding the “Choose Love” Curriculum
:
Brought forward in the Spring of 2018. Choose Love is an enrichment program focusing on Social Emotional Needs that was well received throughout our district. A wonderful curriculum that is not a mandated program in our district, however if each building would like to utilize resources from this curriculum model to meet individual needs they may. Currently Lillie B. Haynes (LBH) is piloting, and we are looking forward to reviewing their data at a later time.
Focus at the moment is with the “Dessa” assessment program, focusing on our children’s social emotional needs, looking at creating a tool box for teachers and enhancing resources by creating an on-line library.
*Amy Drowne and Kim Davis will come back in Jan/Feb 2019 to continue discussion in regard to the “Choose Love Curriculum” in more depth and review information from the LBH pilot. See the attached link from our Social Emotional Committee.
