1. Call to Order
The meeting convened at 6:00 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
A. Proposed East Lyme Aquatics and Fitness Center Fee Schedule Effective October 1, 2018
Reviewed new proposed pricing schedule for the EL Aquatic and Fitness Center and did competitor analysis. Recommended some minor changes and recommend moving forward with the proposal. Also discussed new equipment for the fitness center.
B. Proposed Funding for East Lyme High School Turf Field Replacement
Discussed different funding proposals for the turf field for a discussion on October 10
with the Board of Finance.
C. East Lyme Purchasing Policy
The Committee was made aware of the new purchasing policy for the Town of East Lyme. The Board of Selectmen will entertain action on October 4, 2018.
3. Adjournment
The meeting concluded at 6:55 p.m.
