Jaime Barr Shelburn introduced board attorney, Jessica Ritter for guidance on how to proceed with policies.
Board attorney, Mrs. Ritter recommended:
-
Comprehensive review and audit of all diversity anti-discrimination policies
-Create equity policy
-Provide Board of Education Training
-Provide professional development for ALL staff
-Establish a task force with community stakeholders
The Policy committee has agreed to board attorney's suggestions. Shipman & Goodwin will audit all polices that are related and create robust equity policy and bring back in 3-4 weeks.  The board attorney will present to Board of Education a review of suggestions on Monday, August 17th meeting.

A.  Discussion: Proposed Temporary Policies Related to the COVID-19 Health Crisis as a result of legislative and regulatory changes
Jaime Barr Shelburn began discussions on the temporary COVID-19 policies. As per our bylaws, the Board of Education can agree to circumvent 2 reviews. We should consider these policies as a supplement to our current policies in light of the current health crisis.  We will bring to Monday, August 17, 2020 meeting for approval. These policies have language that would only be used during the current pandemic and will be labeled as C-19 in our policy manual.
