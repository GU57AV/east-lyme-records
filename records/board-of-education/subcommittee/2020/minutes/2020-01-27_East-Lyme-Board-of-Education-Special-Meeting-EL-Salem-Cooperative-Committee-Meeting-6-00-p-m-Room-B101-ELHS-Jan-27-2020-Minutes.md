Information: E. Discussion: Update on Salem Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2020-2021
Save
-Superintendent Joseph O'Nofrio discussed the proposed Salem Public School's budget for the 2020-2021 school year. He stated it was the largest increase in 17years.
-Our original budget with all requests was $11,953,961 or 14.66% increase.
-We made reductions totaling $202,224. This brought the budget to $11,751,737 or 12.72%.  This is the budget originally presented to the BOE.
-Then we made further adjustments, including reconciliation, which brought our budget to $11,293,771 or 8.33% increase.
