 2.      PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
         Mr. Bauman led the Pledge of Allegiance.

 3.      PRESENTATION - Security Strategy & Deployment of Security Personnel
         Mr. Bauman thanked everyone for attending and the feedback received on this topic. Mr.
         Bauman provided a brief history of ELPS security and the current SRO vacancy.
         Mr. Newton & Mr. Hull presented an overview of a potential safety and security plan.
         Highlights included state legal requirements of school security personnel and a comparison
         of SRO and ARO roles.
         Mr. Newton emphasized this is one layer of safety and security, and the district priority
         remains on mental health support and ensuring students have "trusted adult" connections.
