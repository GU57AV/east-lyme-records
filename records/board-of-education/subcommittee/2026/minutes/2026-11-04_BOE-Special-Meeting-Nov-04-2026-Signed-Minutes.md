BOE Special Meeting Minutes

 1.     OPENING OF MEETING
        A.       Call to Order
                 A quorum was established; the meeting was called to order at 5:34 p.m.

 2.     DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
        A.       Discussion/Possible Action regarding the hiring of a consultant to assist with the
                 Superintendent search.
                 Eric Bauman provided an overview of the search committee’s approach and plan to engage
                 in a formal search for the Superintendent of Schools. Three different consultants presented
                 to the committee on Monday 11/2 and held further discussion on selecting the right
                 consultant for this process. Mr. Bauman opened the discussion and welcomed thoughts or
                 comments to the attending board members. Alisa Bradley shared that his summary was
                 reflective of the committee’s discussions up to this point.

                 MOTION:
                 Motion to approve hiring NESDEC with consultant Vonda Tecza, to perform a study
                 including a survey and focus groups to assist in the superintendent search, not to exceed
                 $6,000.
                 Moved by: Alisa Bradley; seconded by: William Derry
                 Pro: Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, William Derry, Esteban Garcia, Stephen
                 Luber, Jaime Barr Shelburn, and Catherine Steel
                                                                                 Motion Carried 8-0


                 Eric Bauman shared the consultant work will begin soon; the Board will notify and welcome
                 the community on opportunities to provide feedback, thoughts and insight. The Board
                 values community and staff participation and perspective on hiring the best Superintendent
                 for East Lyme Public Schools. No additional board members offered further comments.
