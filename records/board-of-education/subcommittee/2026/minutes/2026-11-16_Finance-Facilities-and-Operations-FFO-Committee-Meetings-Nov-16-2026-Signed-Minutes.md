 2.     DISCUSSION
        A.      Oil, Fuel, Water - Current Year Associated Costs
                Maryanna Stevens led discussions about the price of utilities this fiscal year and there was
                not enough funding set aside for water and diesel based on unanticipated increases to
                prices. She is considering purchasing through a consortium with other school districts but is
                hoping the price will continue to decrease. The price of heating oil was locked into a fixed
                price already.
                Maryanna also shared that the percentage rate of water has increased 12-13% which is
                significantly higher than the budgeted amount. The committee discussed planning for
                utilities purchasing through the remainder of this year and planning for the next fiscal year.
        B.      Update - Health Insurance Audit
                Maryanna Stevens led discussions about the health insurance audit. A few staff members
                have self-reported as a result of the audit. There are only three staff members that have not
                completed the audit.

                Dr. Christian Lund shared that NCS completed an air quality inspection this year based on
