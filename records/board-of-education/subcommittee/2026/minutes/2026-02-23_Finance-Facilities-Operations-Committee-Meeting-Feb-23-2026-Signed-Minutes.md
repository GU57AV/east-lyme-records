Finance, Facilities, & Operations Committee Meeting Minutes
Monday, February 23, 2026 at 5:00 PM
Zoom: https://us02web.zoom.us/j/87056647086

The FFO meeting was held virtually due to the high school building closure from the inclement weather.


Members in attendance: Catherine Steel, Chair; Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, Vivek Purohit (5:07 p.m.)
Administrators in attendance: Jeffrey Newton, Kimberly Davis (5:07 p.m.), Christian Lund, Maryanna Stevens


 1.     OPENING OF MEETING

              A.       Call to Order
                       A quorum was established and the meeting was called to order at
                       5:04 p.m.

 2.     DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS

              A.       Discussion: Non-Lapsing Account Follow up
                       Committee members engaged in discussions on strategy and
                       potential use for the BOE Non-Lapsing Account. Discussions
                       included the debt service with the town, current expenditures &
                       overtime status with snow removal, the operational logistics of the
                       account, and possible spending options.
                       The committee agreed to establish the account to be limited to
                       yearly deposits of no more than .75% of the budget, and the
                       cumulative account total would be capped at 1.25%.

                       2024.pdf
