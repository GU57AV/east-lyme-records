3.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
     A.    Discussion: East Lyme & Salem Partnership
           Mr. Bauman initiated discussion and recapped the partnership that has been in place
           and shared concerns and noted experiences in the last couple of years of distancing in
           terms of school collaboration and feedback between East Lyme & Salem.

           Moving forward the two boards will work to establish additional meeting structure,
           which would initiate further clarity and communication. The committee agreed to hold
           both cooperative meetings each year.

     B.    Discussion: Regional Bonus Funding
           Superintendent Hendrickson discussed the advocacy efforts the District has been
           making for access to Regional Bonus Money for high school tuition in ECS.

     C.    Discussion: Shared Services Program Update
           The Boards discussed the shared services partnership with Food Services (Chris
           Urban), Facilities (Chris Lund) and Security (Don Hull), and agreed that the
           partnership works well for both districts.


     D.    Discussion: Salem School Capacity - East Lyme Partnership Opportunities
           Superintendent Hendrickson shared that Salem School is designed to serve over 600
           students and Salem welcomes the opportunity to have an exploratory discussion
           about whether Salem could potentially serve as an option for East Lyme families
           should such an approach ever align with East Lyme’s planning considerations.
