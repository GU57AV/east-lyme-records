5.   DISCUSSION: COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT COMMITTEE
     A.    Committee Members and Purpose
           The Boards discussed Salem’s K-8 single-building model and busing logistics, as well as
           efforts to align meeting calendars to improve attendance. The current agreement runs
           through 2039, with a committee being formed for review and re-ratification by 2029.
           Financial reconciliation concerns were noted briefly. East Lyme has a subcommittee ready;
           Salem is drafting a list and will work to solidify that soon in hopes that a first meeting can be
           set up before the end of December.


6.   DISCUSSION: SHARED SERVICES
     A.    Departments Beyond Facilities, Food Services, & Security
           The new armed security initiative and window film installation at the high school have been
           positively received. The boards are exploring expanded shared services, such as
           transportation, for greater efficiency. The next joint meeting is planned for
           January/February, with feedback encouraged on the format.
