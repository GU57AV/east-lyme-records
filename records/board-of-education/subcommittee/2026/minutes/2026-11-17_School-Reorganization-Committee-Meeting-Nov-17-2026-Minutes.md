School Reorganization Committee Meeting Minutes
Tuesday, November 17, 2026 at 9:00 AM
Central Office Conference Room

Committee members present: Leigh Gianakos, Chair; Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley
Committee members absent: Jill Carini
Administrators present: Jeffrey Newton, Superintendent; Annaliese Spaziano, Assistant Superintendent;
Maryanna Stevens, Director of Finance

 1.     OPENING OF MEETING
        A.      Call to Order
                The meeting was called to order at 9:02 a.m.

 2.     DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
        A.      Discussion: School Rebalancing & Reorganization
                Mike Zuba of MP Planning provided a brief update an overview of where things stand with
                elementary enrollment numbers and what he can do in obtaining information about the
                middle school. This would include a full capacity study for the middle school building. Leigh
                Gianakos asked about how we might begin to include the public through stakeholder
                groups or committee work among focus groups. Eric Bauman asked about additional cost
                analysis and further discussions in this area with the BOE. He inquired about how to task a
                stakeholder committee with the work of school rebalancing. Alisa Bradley would like the
                Board to ground themselves in the work to build a Q&A component that links to forms and
                offered to start a FAQ on this topic to help guide the process and gather feedback along with
                promoting transparency.
                The committee will recommend the following to the BOE:
                   •   Consider January and/or March forums for open discussion with the public
                   •   Recommend on 12/7 to form a full stakeholders committee led by an outside
                       contracted individual and begin identifying forum dates.
                   •   Recommend funding the additional cost for the middle school capacity study to be
                       done by MP Planning.

 3.     ADJOURNMENT
        A.      Adjourn Meeting
                The meeting adjourned at 10:30 a.m.
