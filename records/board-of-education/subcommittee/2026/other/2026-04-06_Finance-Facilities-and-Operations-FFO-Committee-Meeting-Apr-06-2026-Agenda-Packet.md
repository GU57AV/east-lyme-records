                 Shared Service Agreement for Director of Facilities
THIS AGREEMENT is entered into by and between the Salem Board of Education, hereafter
designated as "Salem BOE," East Lyme Board of Education, hereafter designated as "East Lyme
BOE."

WHEREAS, Salem BOE and East Lyme BOE desire to enter into a mutual agreement for the
Director of Facilities to provide oversight services for facilities as outlined in Exhibit A, and all
parties are desirous of having the Agreement set out in writing; and

WHEREAS, Salem BOE and East Lyme BOE believe that such a mutual agreement is in the best
interest of their respective districts.

IT IS THEREFORE MUTUALLY AGREED by and between the parties that the following terms and
conditions shall govern this Agreement:

1. PURPOSE.

   A. This Agreement is a shared service agreement entered into by the parties pursuant to
      the provisions of Section 10-239k of the Connecticut General Statutes.

   B. The parties agree to share the services of the Director of Facilities. The Director of
      Facilities is Dr. Christian Lund.

2. TERM. The term of this Agreement shall be for the fiscal year July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026.
A program assessment will be done by the end of April 2026 to determine whether to extend the
contract for an additional year(s).

3. EAST LYME BOE RESPONSIBILITIES.

   A. East Lyme BOE has hired and will hold the individual employment contract for the
      Director of Facilities.

   B. The Director of Facilities shall be the sole employee of East Lyme BOE, which will be
      responsible for the Director of Facilities' employment. East Lyme BOE shall be
      responsible for all obligations relating to the employment of the Director of Facilities
      without limitation, wages, payroll, benefits, social security, workers' compensation costs
      and withholding or paying taxes.

   C. East Lyme BOE shall provide office space for the Director of Facilities when the
      Director of Facilities is working at East Lyme BOE facilities.

4. SALEM BOE RESPONSIBILITIES.

   A. Salem BOE agrees to pay annually to East Lyme BOE towards the expense of the Director
      of Facilities. The amount to be paid to East Lyme BOE for such services will be $42,000
      for the fiscal year 2025-2026. The payments shall be made to East Lyme BOE in two (2)
      equal installments, with the first installment due on September 1, 2025, and the second
      installment due on March 1, 2026.




                                                                                                        Page 2 of 13
   B. Salem BOE agrees to provide office space for the Director of Facilities when the
      Director of Facilities is working at Salem BOE facilities.

5. SERVICES TO BE SHARED BY PARTIES.

   A. The Director of Facilities will perform the specific duties and other Director of Facilities
      level services as per the job description of duties and responsibilities as outlined in
      Exhibit A, attached hereto and made a part hereof.

   B. The policies of Salem BOE as well as directions from Salem BOE's Superintendent or
      designee may also outline those services to be provided to Salem BOE.

   C. The Director of Facilities' services provided will be competent and professional in
      accordance with and subject to federal, state, and local laws, rules, and regulations.

6. INSURANCE AND INDEMNIFICATION

   A. East Lyme BOE shall be responsible for all insurance related to the Director of Facilities,
      including without limitation, workers' compensation insurance in accordance with the
      statutory requirements of the State of Connecticut.

   B. East Lyme BOE shall defend, indemnify, and hold Salem BOE, its officers, employees and
      agents harmless from and against any and all claims, liability, loss and expense (including
      attorneys ' fees) arising in connection with the employment contract of the Director of
      Facilities, the employment of the Director of Facilities, any payments to the Director of
      Facilities or any claim for workers' compensation benefits.

   C. This section shall survive the termination of this Agreement.

7. TERMINATION OF AGREEMENT. Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason
   on 60 days written notice to the other party. Such notice will be by registered mail or by hand
   delivery to the Superintendent of Schools of the other party. This Agreement will also terminate
   automatically upon the resignation of the Director of Facilities or termination of the Director of
   Facilities' contract with East Lyme BOE. In the event of a termination of this Agreement, Salem
   BOE's payment obligations shall terminate as of the last day of this Agreement and East Lyme
   BOE shall refund any portion of payments made by Salem BOE at the start of the month for a
   partial month.

8. EMPLOYMENT STATUS OF DIRECTOR OF FACILITIES. This is an agreement for shared
   services. The Director of Facilities shall be an employee of East Lyme BOE and shall be issued
   a contract through East Lyme BOE only, and shall not be an employee, or an independent
   contractor, of Salem BOE. For the purpose of this Agreement, the individual shall retain all
   rights and privileges as a full-time employee of East Lyme BOE. The actual contract shall be
   an East Lyme BOE contract, subject to the Board Policies of East Lyme BOE. There shall be no
   changes in the contract, except for changes approved in board policy or negotiations with the
   East Lyme Board of Education. East Lyme BOE shall be deemed the "Employer" of the Director
   of Facilities for purposes of rights and obligations under federal and state laws relating to
   employment, such as income tax withholding, FICA payments and withholding, unemployment
   compensation payments, workers' compensation payments, and group insurance eligibility.

DIRECTOR OF FACILITIES DUTIES:

   • Ensure facilities assets are operated, maintained, and repaired IAW appropriate
       standards, guidelines, manufacturer instructions, and regulatory requirements.
   • Ensure compliance with regulations and laws pertaining to school facilities.
   • Meet with the Board of Education, Parent Teacher Organizations and any other
     school- related organizations when requested.
   • Directs, schedules, and oversees external contractors (electricians, plumbers,
     excavators, roofers, painters, landscapers, etc.)
   •   Coordinate annual inspections.
   • Schedules and supervises maintenance workers engaged in building and grounds
     upkeep and repair; responsible for safety and security, electrical, plumbing, heating, air
     conditioning, and ventilation services
   • Develops and executes department budget.
   • Develops, implements, and supervises preventative maintenance and
     renovation programs for buildings, grounds, mechanical and electrical, utility,
     and safety and security systems
   • Ensures physical facility complies with security and safety plans
   • Prepares specifications for physical plant projects; estimates costs of equipment,
     materials, labor, and supplies; prepares bid specifications for projects, equipment,
     and contracted services; oversees site and building projects performed by outside
     contractors
   •   Monitors facility energy consumption and investigates/implements energy
       savings opportunities
   • Ensures the proper disposal of hazardous and controlled wastes in compliance
       with regulations and guidelines;
   • Arranges facilities related/physical plant training sessions (bloodborne pathogens,
     OSHA, MSDS, and other job-related training) both in-house and off campus
   •   Contributes to strategic/capital planning by evaluating and projecting future facility
       needs and proposing options to achieve them

Exceptions

   • Routine/daily cleaning assignments, supervision and evaluation of custodians will be
     the responsibility of the School Principal. The Facilities Director will provide additional
     consultation, direction, and assistance as needed for unusual tasks, repairs or other
     facilities related incidents as needed as well as general quality control.
   • Custodians shall be the first notified and first to respond to alarms and other
     incidents that occur at the school during the workday and after hours. The Facilities
     Director shall be called by responding to custodians if they are unable to address the
     situation.
