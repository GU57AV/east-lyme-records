3. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
A. Discussion/Information: Discussion: Update - Shared Services Programs
Brian Hendrickson spoke to the positive addition of security and facilities in shared services at Salem. The partnership
has also been helpful with eighth grade students transitioning to the high school.
B. Discussion/Action: Reconciliation of Salem Tuition Rates for 2023-2024
Maryanna Stevens reviewed the tuition reconciliation calculation used for Salem high school students to attend East
Lyme High School. Discussion ensued about the factors that contributed to the increase in tuition rates.
Motion to approve the reconciled Salem tuition rates for 2023-2024 as follows: 1) Salem Base Tuition Rate:
$17,946.00; 2) Salem Total Premium Tuition Rate: $33,820.53; a total underpayment of $349,385.74, as presented.
Motion by Eric Bauman, second by Sean Reith

Motion passed unanimously.

Maryanna Stevens reviewed the calculation process and shared the factors used to determine the proposed Salem

D. Discussion: Update on ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2025-2026
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget for the FY 2025-2026 SY.
The East Lyme Board of Education have held two budget workshops/meetings and will continue discussions at the next
board meeting on February 10, 2025.
Proposed 2025-2026 Budget Details:
-Proposed 6.99% budget increase.
Major budget drivers circle around these areas:
    •   Salaries & Beneﬁts
    •   Health Insurance
    •   Bus Contract with First Student
Discussion ensued between the administration and East Lyme and Salem board members.
E. Discussion: Update on Salem Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2025-2026
Superintendent Hendrickson provided an update on the Salem Superintendent's Proposed Budget for the FY 2025-2026
SY. The Salem Board of Education has created a non-lapsing account this past year for unencumbered funds to provide
a cushion for future unanticipated costs.
Proposed 2025-2026 Budget Details:
-Proposed 7.17% budget increase.
Major budget drivers circle around four areas:
    •   Unanticipated Special Education Costs
    •   Program Enhancements
    •   Health Insurance
Next committee meeting is scheduled for February 3, 2025 to review the budget with the board of education members.

                              Base Tuition Rate Calculation

                          Total Education Cost - Per ED001
                          Less: Total SPED cost
                Step 1    Total Base Enrollment Cost
                          X   % of HS Students in district
                Step 2    Total Cost to run HS
                          Less: Transportation Cost
                Step 3    Total Cost to run HS, less Transportation
                          Number of HS Students
                Step 4    Total Cost per Pupil , less transportation
                          + Surcharge - for services not included in ED001 (such as police, fire safety, etc.)
                Step 5    Total Cost per Pupil - Base Tuition Rate

                         HS Special Education Per Pupil Base Rate
                          District Wide (DW) - SPED Costs
                          Less: 15% of Admin & Non-Cert. Salaries, Purch Serv. & Equip.
                Step 1    Total DW Adjusted SPED costs
                                                                         = DW SPED cost per pupil
                          Number of Total DW SPED Students
