1. Call to Order
Procedural: A. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 3:36 pm.
2. Discussion Items
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Update from DEI School-Based Teams
Superintendent Newton along with the districtwide and school-based committees continue to prioritize their efforts for the 2022/2023 school year.
Mr. Kydd (ELHS Assistant Principal), Ms. Zielinski (ELMS Teacher), Ms. Sullivan (Elementary Teacher), Ms. Grava (School Psychologist) provided an update on the DEI school-based teams. The committee discussed challenges that some schools were facing in engagement in the committee by the end of the school year. The committee discussed incorporating questions focusing on engagement and inclusivity into climate surveys, adding cultural spirit days that focus on students having “pride in themselves”, and earmarking dedicated times for DEI within Professional Development days to allow reflection, guidance, or facilitation of action items within schools during next school year.
Mr. Kydd suggested moving this committee and/or school committees to quarterly meetings for the next school year. The committee discussed scheduling fewer meetings with confirmed agendas could be beneficial for engagement. Dr. Darcy shared scheduling meetings with focused agendas before school begins would help the trajectory of the committee’s goals and maintain thoughtful commitment to action steps. Mr. Newton and Dr. Darcy offered to work with the committee to assist with staff engagement in each school as needed.
Discussion, Information: B. Discussion: Courageous Conversations Training Follow-up
Mrs. Schoman (FL Principal), Mr. Kydd (ELHS Assistant Principal), and Ms. Zielinski (ELMS Teacher) participated in the Curious Conversations Academy: Exploration course on May 9
th
.
Mr. Kydd and Ms. Zielinski reported that the training was enjoyable and is a well-run program. Mr. Kydd shared that he believes psychologists/guidance counselors, ELA and Social studies teachers should attend this training as it would be very beneficial with their subject(s) and for their students. Ms. Zielinski was able to immediately implement one of the tools taught during the training in her classroom. It afforded her classroom to have a direct conversation in a recent lesson that incorporated behavioral awareness and humanity to the student perspective. Discussion during the Courageous Conversations training allowed participants to have direct vulnerable discussions without shame or defensiveness, which Mr. Kydd and Ms. Zielinski agreed was beneficial. The committee discussed the Courageous Conversations practitioner program and having specific staff take the training.
Discussion: C. Discussion: 2023-24 Meetings
Mr. Newton shared his focus next year is to work on restorative conversations and expanding Professional Development for this committee. Mrs. Spaziano will work to integrate the committee’s action steps into the Professional Development based upon the district’s goals. Mr. Newton discussed creating a core group for DEI that is thoroughly trained to create a tier level of support in each building. Mr. Newton and Mrs. Spaziano will work with Dr. Darcy on action steps and focus for next year.
Mr. Newton encouraged the committee to reach out to him to discuss future committee possibilities or share topics/concerns.
3. Adjournment
Procedural: A. Adjournment
The meeting concluded at 4:23 pm.
