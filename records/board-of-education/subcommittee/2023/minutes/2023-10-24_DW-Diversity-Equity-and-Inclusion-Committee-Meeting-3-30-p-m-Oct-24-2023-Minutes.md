1. Opening of Meeting
Procedural: A. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 3:30 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
A. Discussion/Information: Partnership with LEARN/Linda Darcy & Overview of DEI work
The partnership and planning with ELPS and Dr. Darcy on DEI training were reviewed and discussed. Beginning in January – June 2024, Dr. Darcy will provide full day training off site for committee members. The goal is to create a core group of individuals at each school that have participated in the training. Dr. Darcy shared the three-year cohort model plan, with a new group beginning training each school year to establish dedication and build common understanding on what equity means for the larger organization. Dates will be confirmed before the end of November.
Dr. Darcy shared utilizing short PD sessions and implementing learning opportunities as part of a daily routine.
https://www.learningforjustice.org/sites/default/files/general/speak_up_pocket_card_2up.pdf
B. Discussion/Information: Article – Speaking up without Tearing Down
The committee discussed the article
Speaking Up Without Tearing Down
and its insight into responding to bias, allowing the opportunity to reflect on word choice, the approach of “calling in” instead of “calling out” and how disciplinary conversations change the solution and doesn’t necessarily allow further learning. The committee shared conversation outcomes with group discussions on the article or plans to use the article as a learning tool in upcoming meetings.
C. Discussion/Information: DEI School Based Committee Updates
High School:
Mr. Kydd shared the ELHS DEI committee has had productive collaborative discussions around civil discourse, social justice standards and the framework of advisory content and ensuring that it is aligned to Vision of the Graduate. Mr. Kydd also shared the plans to rotate student representatives from various clubs and organizations in the school committee meetings and discussions to include student voice.
Middle School
: Mrs. Kelly shared her experience at the MLK Scholarship Dinner; the middle school was represented with a few students and teachers in attendance. The middle school team are using case studies to support teachers in handling the spectrum of conversations. Discussion ensued on forming connections with high school and middle school students to assist in future school transition with 8
th
grade Kivas visiting social studies at the high school to use as the catalyst for the discussions. Mr. Newton and Mrs. Spaziano would like to join as available for these conversations.
Elementary:
Mrs. Schoman shared discussions of the recent elementary level meeting. The team is working to create a collection of resources that can be accessed and utilized by staff or faculty at any time and is looking to create quick learning opportunities during staff meetings so that conversations continue as often as possible. Elementary schools are utilizing morning greetings as an opportunity to incorporate different languages inclusive for ELPS students and creating a specific DEI awareness bulletin board that is regularly updated.
D. Discussion: Open Discussion
Discussion occurred around the use and awareness of ELPS anonymous tip line and ensuring it is visible and easy to find. Mr. Garcia shared a reporting mechanism webpage within higher education that is effectively used by his employer
https://inside.southernct.edu/campus-climate-support
The idea around the possibility of students attending the next DEI meeting and options will be explored.
