3. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Update - Shared Food Services Program
Mr. Chris Urban, East Lyme Public Schools Director of Food Services, provided an update on the current program:
On Thursday, there will be a legislative discussion to extend free lunch to all students for the remainder of the 2022-2023 school year. There was a slight drop in the number of daily lunches when the program went back to having students/families pay full price. We have definitely noticed an increase over the last week. We will continue to update the families on any new changes.
Discussion ensued between the administration and East Lyme and Salem board members.
