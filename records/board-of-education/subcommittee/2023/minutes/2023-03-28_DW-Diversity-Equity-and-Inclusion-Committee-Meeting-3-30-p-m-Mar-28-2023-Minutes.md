2. Discussion Items
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: LEARN Collaborative for Improving Educational Diversity
Dr. Linda Darcy of LEARN provided an update to the Committee and highlighted the importance and impact of tools to have open, upfront conversations. Superintendent Newton and Dr. Darcy discussed plans for further collaboration and continued discussions to improve educational diversity and knowledge. Dr. Darcy recommends that the committee as a whole and school-based teams create attainable goals to measure the work for the upcoming school year. Discussion ensued among the Committee members.
Discussion, Information: B. Discussion: High School Advisory Lesson/Student Survey Results
Mr. Kydd (ELHS Assistant Principal) shared the presentation that accompanied Advisory Lesson for ELHS students in February for Black History Month. The purpose of the lesson was to provide students with an overview of the history of Black History Month, increase student awareness of the current state of Black history education in the United States, and provide them with an opportunity to give feedback as it relates to their own experiences with Black history in their educational careers. Discussion ensued among the Committee members.
Discussion, Information: C. Discussion: Update from DEI School-Based Teams
Superintendent Newton along with the districtwide and school-based committees continue to prioritize their efforts for the 2022/2023 school year. Mrs. Schoman (FL Principal), Ms. Zielinski (ELMS Teacher), Ms. Kelly (ELMS Assistant Principal), Ms. Thomson (ELHS Teacher), Ms. Petrillo (ELHS Teacher) and Mr. Kydd (ELHS Assistant Principal) provided an update on the DEI school-based teams.
