6. Culture
We reviewed and discussed the questions that were used in last year’s annual survey.
There was consensus that we should add some addition open ended questions including a question about what questions do we need to include.
These questions will be updated and revised for review at our next meeting.
We will continue to give students and staff time to complete these surveys and we are looking at ways to increase parent participation.
We also discussed which parts of climate and culture are most important…what should we focus on first. Morale was mentioned. This prompted a discussion how the budget process can affect morale. Finally, we discussed the recent act concerning the implementation of the Connecticut school climate policy. We are considering adopting the model Connecticut climate policy whether or not it passes.
