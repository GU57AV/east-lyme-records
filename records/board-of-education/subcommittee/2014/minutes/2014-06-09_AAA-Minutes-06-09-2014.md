East Lyme Board of Education
Academics, Athletics, and Activities (AAA) Committee Meeting
June 9, 2014
6:00 p.m.

Minutes

Members Present: Candice Carlson (Co-Chair), Tim Hagen, Rob Kupis, Jaime Barr
Shelburn

Members Absent: Joe Arcarese

Adminstration and Others Present: Barbara Senges, Brian Reas, Karen Costello, Dr.
Judy DeLeeuw, Carl Reichard, Lori Singer, Henry E. Kydd Ill, Pamela Nowak

The meeting was called to order at 6:00 p.m. by Candice Carlson.

Update of Professional Development: Dr. Karen Costello presented to the committee
a draft presentation for the full Board of Education on Professional Development within
the East Lyme Public Schools. In addition, a package of Professional Development
Plans for the Elementary Schools, ELHS ED Camp, Regional PD Groups, and multiple
example of PD days during 2013-2014 were included. The committee suggested that a
couple of slides be included in the presentation to show the linkage of the PD efforts to
the Long Range for ELPS. The Committee complemented Dr. Costello on her efforts
and recommended that the presentation be included at the next BOE meeting.

Proposed High School Textbook Adoption: Carl Reichard and Lori Singer presented
their recommendations for a new textbook, World of Chemistry, author/editor:
Zumdahl, Zumdahl, DeCoste, publisher: Brooks/Cole, copyright, 2013, to be used in
Honors Chemistry. The cost per book is $87.00. Major points for adoption were the
reading level is more consistent with Honors Level and the emphasis on problems
solving strategies with numerous examples worked out with explanation. The AAA
committee endorsed their recommendations.

Henry Kydd presented recommendation for a new textbook series, Magruder’s
American Government, Standard Version and Foundation Series, author/editor:
William A, McClenaghan, publisher: Pearson: copyright, 2013, to be used in the 10!”
Grade American Civics course. There are two texts with the same title, topics, and
organization. One text, Standard Version, is for A level students and has a higher text
complexity and the other text, Foundation, is for B level students. Mr. Kydd pointed out
that either text could be used, which provides flexibility for students. The cost per text is
$88.47. The committee endorsed Mr. Kydd’s recommendations.
Proposed High School Trade Book Adoption: Pamela Nowak presented four books
for adoption by the English Department. The books are as follows:

The Bonesetter’s Daughter, author Amy Tan
Paper Towns, author John Green

Twisted, author Laurie Halse Anderson

Pride and Prejudice, author Jane Austen

Ms. Nowak reviewed with the committee the process for Trade book adoption. Last
summer each of these books were read by the team of English Teachers and then were
piloted with groups of students during the past school year. Feedback was solicited from
students in the pilot groups. One book, Dreaming in Cuban by Cristina Garcia, was
not being recommended based upon the feedback from students. Ms. Nowak indicated
that they are still looking for a book that has a strong Hispanic voice. Jaime Shelburn
suggested looking at the book Red Umbrella by Christina Gonzalez. The Committee

endorsed Ms. Nowak’s recommendations.

Book Selection Process — Middle School Presentation: Tim Hagen apologized to
the committee for creating confusion on whether this topic was to be discussed at this
time. It was recommended that the topic be deferred to a subsequent AAA meeting.
The committee is looking forward to the ELMS’s return in August with policy
recommendations on future book selection.

The meeting was adjorned at 6:50 p.m.
Respectfully Submitted,

Candice Carlson
Co-Chair of AAA Committee
