East Lyme Board of Education
Academics, Athletics, and Activities (AAA) Committee Meeting
April 7,2014
MINUTES
FILED IN EAST LYME

Member Present: Candice Carlson (Co-Chair), Tim Hagen and Rob Kupis CONNECTICUT_

20Lt At 0115. Kasim
Member Absent: Jaime Barr Shelburn EASTALYME TOWN CLERK
Administrators Present: Brian Reas (Assistant Superintendent for Special
Education and Pupil Personnel), Dr. Karen Costello (Administrator for Program
Improvement) , Michael Susi (HS Principal), Chris Mountain (School Psychologist),

Chris Olsen (Director of Security), Dr. DeLeeuw (MS Principal), Pam Nowak (HS
English CIL/Teacher)

Meeting was called to order by Candice Carlson at 6:00pm.
Prescription and Non-Prescription Drug Use By Young People:

e Mr. Susi, Mr. Mountain and Mr. Olsen where present to discuss prescription
and non-prescription Drug use at the High School. Handout presented,
reviewed the number of cases per school and in the community. Discussion
led to how we approach drug use within the school & how we support our
kids internally. Information is disseminated through some of their existing
classes as well as team activities. Dr. Costello discussed what we teach
throughout the District as well. The Middle School follows the same path.
Both have dogs come in to do searches at times.

e Mr. Mountain thought that having a drug counselor at the school would
provide more continuity within the school.

e Candice Carlson questioned about the relationship between the District and
ASAP as Sarah Butterfield had given a presentation last year and had many
outreach ideas for the district. Sarah has since moved on and Dave Mcdowell
is the new Director. Everyone agreed that approaching Dave Putnam through
Youth Services as well as Dave through ASAP to reestablish a collaborative
effort would be beneficial in the hopes of establishing a community
connection to address a growing global problem.

o ELHS French Books: A brief discussion in regard to updating the French
Books and the different price ranges.
OVERVIEW OF BOOK SELECTION PROCESS:

e Discussion on how books are selected and piloted. ELHS CIL Pam
Novak was present and discussed the piloting process, how the books
are presented for approval and the process in which a parent can take
to request there child not read certain material due to sensitive
content.

e Mr. Susi suggested at the first of the year a letter go home to parents
to inform them of the books their children would be using throughout
the year and the content. Mrs. Carlson added that having the district
be proactive could possibly help parents with the questions of “Why”
is my child reading this book, “Understanding the context in which
questionable reading material is being read” and the opt out option
possibility. Sensorship was discussed briefly; however, continuing
that discussion would be beneficial at the next AAA Meeting.

e The committee was unable to hear the ELMS presentation due to time
constraints; however, the committee would like to have them back at
the next meeting to continue the discussion.

The meeting was adjourned at 7:00 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,
Candice Carlow
Candice Carlson
Co-Chair, AAA Committee
