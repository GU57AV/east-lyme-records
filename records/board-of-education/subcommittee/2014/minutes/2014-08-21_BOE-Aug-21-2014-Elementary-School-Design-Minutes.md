                                   East Lyme Board of Education
                  East Lyme Elementary Schools Design Steering Committee Meeting
                                          August 21, 2014
                                             4:00 p.m.

                                                MINUTES

Members Present: Dr. James Lombardo, Linda Anania, Candice Carlson, Melissa DeLoreto, Gil Gallant,
Dr. Tim Hagen, Rob Kupis, Don Meltabarger, Ray O’Connor, John Rhodes, Al Jacunski from Jacunski
Humes Architects, LLC

Members Absent: John Arnold, Dr. David Miko, Dr. John Whritner

The meeting began at 4:00 p.m. in the Central Office Conference Room

Update on Demographic Study: Dr. Lombardo reported that NESDEC is on track to present the
demographic study findings to the Design Steering Committee and the Board of Education as planned on
September 8, 2014. NESDEC will provide the Design Committee with a draft of the report for review by
September 1, 2014.

Community Survey: Candice Carlson reported that she and Dr. Lombardo have meet and that a Survey
Monkey survey will be sent out to the community after the three September forums so community
members will be better informed to respond to survey questions.

 Community Forums: Tim Hagen and Dr. Lombardo will begin drafting a brochure announcing the three
elementary design community forums scheduled for September 23rd, 24th, and 25th outlining key topics
that will be discussed. The brochure will be emailed to parents, broadcasted on channel 22, put on the
website, and made available at each school, Town Hall, Senior Center, and East Lyme Public Library. Tim
Hagen distributed and reviewed the draft of the PowerPoint presentation to be presented at the
community forums for comments and input from the Design Steering Committee. The presentation will
include all elementary school design options and will highlight that the quality of education in East Lyme
will always remain excellent no matter what design option is ultimately selected.

Design Committee Conceptual Framework: Al Jacunski presented the overall budget comparisons for
all elementary school design concepts. Al verified that the State will reimburse 20% of the demolition
cost of the Lillie B. Haynes wing under renovate as new if that option is selected. Al will update the
current budget comparisons to include the demolition reimbursement and to include commissioning
costs for all concepts. Al reviewed the picture overlays of each concept and will provide the committee
with pdf formats of each overlay. At this early stage, it was suggested to use percentages instead of
dollar figures at the community forums when discussing costs. Example: to renovate three schools as
new would be 30% more than to build one school or renovate two schools as new.

Next Meeting: The next Design Steering Committee meeting will be held on Thursday, September 4,
2014 in the Central Office Conference Room.

Respectfully Submitted,

Dr. Timothy Hagen                       Dr. James Lombardo
Timothy Hagen, Ph.D.                    James D. Lombardo, Ed.D.
BOE Chair                               Superintendent of School
