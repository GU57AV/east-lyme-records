East Lyme Elementary Schools Design Steering Committee Meeting
June 4, 2014

MINUTES

Members Present: Linda Anania, John Arnold, Candice Carlson, Melissa DeLoreto, Gil Gallant, Tim
Hagen, Don Meltabarger, David Miko, Ray O’Connor, Brian Reas, John Rhodes, Dr. John Whritner, Al
Jacunski from Jacunski Humes Architects, LLC

Members Absent: Dr. James Lombardo, Rob Kupis
The meeting began at 4:00 p.m. at Flanders Elementary School, Media Center

Update from Administration Regarding the First Draft of the Educational Specifications: Brian Reas
distributed a roster of past meeting dates and events of the ELPS Facilities Vision Committee and ELPS
Design Steering Committee for review. Mr. Reas and the elementary principals have met regarding their
individual educational specifications and have each done a site visit at Waterford Schools. In addition,
each elementary school principal is obtaining input from their teaching staff on ideas for the
specifications. A draft of the educational specifications will be shared with the Design Committee the
end of June. Al Jacunski would like a list of conceptual attributes from both staff and administration as
soon as possible, not August or September.

Demographic Study Feedback: Tim Hagen and Brian Reas talked to Dr. Chung from H. G. Planning
Demographer and Dr. Kennedy from NESDEC regarding a demographic study. Both organizations are
very qualified and the cost to perform the demographic study would be $14,000.00 from H.G. Planning
Demographer and $6,240.00 from NESDEC. H. G. Planning Demographer would start their study from
raw data where as NESDEC currently maintains a demographic database dating back to 1963. The
Design Committee will recommend to the Board of Education at their June 9, 2014 meeting to contract
NESDEC to perform a total demographic study to include Pre-K and to also include an interim report
with projected figures and a final report with October 1, 2014 enrollment figures for a total cost of
$6,240.00.

Al Jacunski will provide the Design Committee with comparative conceptual numbers for the next Design

meeting in preparation for future presentations to the Board of Education, staff and the community.

Community Forum Content and Planning: In September, the Design Committee will hold individual
forms at each elementary school to present to staff and community the progress to date. When the
dates of the forums are finalized they will be advertised on the website, newsletter and Channel 22. The
possibility of using Survey Monkey for community feedback was discussed.

Other: Al Jacunski noted that that if the final decision is to renovate existing buildings, the Design
Committee should contact our State Representative about our eligibility for additional reimbursement
with a Space Standard Waiver. The Space Standard Waiver cannot be initiated until the design project
has been submitted to the State.

Next Meeting: The next Design Committee meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, June 17, 2014 at 4:00
p.m. in the Library Media Center at Niantic Center School.
