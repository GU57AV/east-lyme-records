MINUTES

1. Continue Committee Work Regarding Additional Charge by Board of Education on April
28, 2014

a. Regarding the concept of flexing the need basis for having children go to a different
school including change to policies; the Ad Hoc Committee recommends that the
existing ELPS 5117 policy be reviewed by the Policy Committee and to make any
revisions to reflect present practice that has evolved over the years,

b. The student to teacher ratios in the elementary schools range from 1:13 to 1:23. There
is one principal in each elementary school.

c. Full equity in enrollment at the elementary schools will never be achieved. The Ad
Hoc Committee previously recommended and recommends now that no student be
moved at this time. To move students at the present time will be a short-sighted
move and will cause much disruption to students and families.
