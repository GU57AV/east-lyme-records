Steve Hargis, ELHS Athletic Director, presented the findings and recommendations of the East Lyme Tennis

Summer of 2014.

= Recommendation #1 — Post tension concrete surface to support six courts in a configuration similar to the
present layout, This type of surface has an industry-standard 25 year warranty. Estimated Cost of Court
Replacement: $400,000 (derived from 2011 & 2012 quotes)

* Recommendation #2 — Asphalt surface to support six courts in a configuration similar to the present
layout. This type of surface would require resurfacing every three years for 25 years to maintain court
quality. Estimated Cost of Court Replacement: $300,000 (derived from 2011 & 2012 quotes)

= Estimated Cost of Court Lighting: $180,000 (derived from 2011 & 2012 quotes)

Committee Consensus: Recommended to the Board that Recommendation #1 (Post tension concrete
surface and court lighting) be added to the District's part of the Town's Capital Improvement Plan.

These two items were subsequently added to the approved District's part of the Town's Capital
Improvement Plan.

Don Meltabarger, Business/Facilities Director, informed the Committee that natural gas prices are on the way
up because of the pipeline capacity capabilities in New England and the use of natural gas by electric utility
companies. He has been closely watching the cost of Natural Gas because the District's current contract is

expiring and we need to enter into a new one. The current cost is $1.284. The best we can now do is $1.154
(for a three-year) contract (which is still a substantial savings over the cost of fuel oil).

Committee Consensus: In light of the increasing costs of energy, especially fuel oil, we feel that the
savings of using natural gas will hopefully offset some of the fuel oil costs.
