Academics, Athletics, and Activities (AAA) Committee Meeting
May 13, 2014

MINUTES

Members Present: Robert Kupis (Acting Chair), Tim Hagen, Jill Carini
Members Absent: Candice Carlson, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Joe Arcarese

Administrators Present: Brian Reas (Asst Supt), Dr. Karen Costello (Admin for Program
Improvement), Michael Susi (ELHS Principal), Deb Kelly (HS Curriculum and Instructional
Leader - Counseling Dept)

The meeting was called to order at 6:00 p.m. by Rob Kupis.

Professional Development: The topic of how best to conduct Staff Professional
Development is a reoccurring topic when discussing and setting the annual school calendar,
Concerns are raised about the use of half days from a perspective of allowing staff to focus
fully on PD topics as well as educational challenge of half days for the children. Dr. Costello
led the discussion on how Professional Development is handled during the school year. A
minimum of 18 hours per year is needed for certification and the district delivers a range 3.5
days to 5.5 days of PD per staff per year. The use of half-days provides a means for
accomplishing the PD within the number of contractual days for the teaching staff while
assuring 182 student days.

Dr. Costello, Michael Susi and Deb Kelly described several examples of Professional
Development programming. Half sessions work very well for focusing on one topic of
development at a time. Special attention is given to building connectivity from one topic to
the next. Regional PD days also provide excellent opportunities for the special areas to
have engagement with other districts.

Overall, the committee was impressed with the creativeness in the PD programming and
recommended that Dr. Costello prepare a short presentation for the full Board. At the next
AAA meeting, the committee would like to review the presentation prior to presenting it to the
full Board.

Equity Budget Issues with Athletics and Extra Curricular Activities: In response to
budget discussions, the Board expressed interest in understanding the equity of funding
across athletics and extra-curricular activities. Mr. Reas and Mr. Susi led a short discussion
on the complexity of the task since each activity is unique and used the golf team as an
example. The Committee recommended the discussion of this topic continue at the next AAA
meeting.

Respectfully Submitted,
Robert Kupis
Robert Kupis
