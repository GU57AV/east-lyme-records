Approval of Minutes:
Motion made by Loretta McIlhenny to approve the minutes of the
March 31, 2014 meeting with a second by Jan Logozzo; motion
passed unanimously with one abstention.

Report of Robert Albright:

Bob Albright reported he spoke with Allison Woods regarding
fund raising of proposed renovations to the pool facility. She
participated in the original pool negotiations and is knowledgeable
about fund raising. She cautioned that that in order to be
successful not to rely on private money - not sufficient numbers
of “deep pockets". Over the summer Jen and Jack will work to

determine how much private funding is potentially available as
well as consulting technically based individuals. These people
include: Sean Farrel from the Cheshire Community Pool; Paul
Davis, the selectman of Woodbridge who developed, with the Red
Cross, pool policies for schools; Nick Nelson of Build a Pool USA.
They will report back at the September meeting. All of the
above people ‘are willing to attend a meeting if we feel it would be
beneficial as well as inviting the East Lyme First Selectman, Paul
Formica. It is noted that safety of students and adults in the
locker rooms is enough to justify making locker room changes -
bring them up to current standards. The Board of Education will
be kept informed of the findings of Jack and Jen.

Pool Update:

Jack presented the attached financial report with explanation.
It is noted that membership are up and finances are in good
shape, which is due in part to the efficient business management

of the pool staff.

Jack will talk to Steve regarding the timers of the parking lot
lights so they are on prior to 5:00 a.m. beginning in the fall.

Jack reported that the moss filtering system is working and has
cut down on chemical usage. There is an issue with the new boiler
with regard to temperature regulation. New England Mechanical
is asked to solve the issue so that the water temperature remains

at 80 degrees ona constant basis.

Boy's Swim Team had the best season ever and claimed second
place in Class M State Championships.

Smith: Per the agreement with East Lyme Community
Management Services, it is recommended that (1) incentive
payment of $4000 be paid if the Board of Education determines
satisfaction with the performance of East Lyme Community
Management Services and (2) incentive payment of $4000 be paid
if annual achievement of breakdown including a deduction for
$20,000 reserve for maintenance and this incentive payment -
for a total of $8000 - to be paid to the Pool Director. The
motion and financial statement will be e-mailed to Committee
members with a return e-mail vote. All votes are yes to paying
the incentive.
