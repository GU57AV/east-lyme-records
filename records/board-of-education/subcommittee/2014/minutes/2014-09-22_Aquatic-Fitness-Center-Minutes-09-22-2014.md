Welcome: Steve Hargis called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.
Public Comments: None

Paul Davis:

Paul Davis (see attached biography) spoke to the Committee
regarding consideration of various fund-raising methods for pool
improvements/renovations. His suggestions included: (1)
corporate sponsorship with one large corporate contribution (2)
State of CT funds, which are limited, but may be tapped into with
health and safety issues of students. Also programs for the
elderly and rehabilitation may be a qualification for state funding
(3) the community itself by explaining the reasoning behind the
need. Paul Davis concluded his discussion by inviting committee
members to contact him with any further questions and/or
concerns.

Approval of Minutes:

Motion made by Nancy Burkhardt to approve the minutes of the
May 19, 2014 meeting with a second by Jan Logozzo; motion
passed unanimously.

Pool Update:

Jack presented the attached financial report with explanation.
It is noted that rentals, memberships and day passes are down as
are swim lessons due in part to Waterford establishing their own
program. Summer sports camp went well. There is an insurance
issue regarding the programs of scuba and spring board diving.
Jack and Jan Logozzo will look further into this issue.

A question was raised regarding the difference in cost of a
summer out-of-town membership versus the daily rate, noting it
costs three times more to have a summer pass. Jack will ask
Steve Baker to look into the issue.

Jack spoke about the rust of the pool roof, which will become a
structural and health issue, The rust is due to humidity and the
air exchange system. He noted the moss system is working well.

Regarding the issues of rust, safety, and health, Steve Hargis will
be meeting with the Board of Education, Superintendent of
School, Business Manager, and the East Lyme Board of Finance to
discuss: (1) can we fit into town bonding (2) what grants are
available (3) could we start with $1.3 million and have a capital
plan to complete the work needed so it will not be a “band aid"
repair. Steve will also talk to Jen about ways to actually doa
capital campaign.

Memberships:

status. A notice announcing a vacancy on the Pool Committee will
be sent to the East Lyme Tymes seeking applicants.
