04-28-14 — EL-BOE FFO Mtg Minutes

East Lyme Board of Education - East Lyme, Connecticut

Finance, Facilities, and Operations (FFO) Committee FILED IN EAST LYME
Meeting Minutes

MEETING DATE: = Monday, April, 28 2014
LOCATION: Room B100, East Lyme High Schoo!

ATTENDEES: Al Littlefield (Co-Chair), Pam Rowe (Co-Chair), Barbara Senges, Brian Reas
(Assistant Superintendent for Special Education and Pupil Personnel), Don
Meltabarger (Business/Facilities Director), Linda Anania (Flanders Elementary School
Principal), Dr. David Miko (Lillie B. Haynes Principal), Melissa DeLoreto (Niantic
Center School Principal), Mike Susi (East Lyme High School Principal)

Committee meeting was called to order at 6:00! by Al Littlefield (Co-Chair).
MEETING CONDUCT
1. Discussion: FY 14 Budget Update as of 03/31/2014 --

Don Meltabarger, Business/Facilities Director, stated that to-date, that because of the colder spring, we are
still incurring fuel expenses. This has caused the District to use approximately $67,000 of the Instructional
Resources line item — the $379,000 that was frozen at the beginning of the school year and would have
normally been unfrozen in the January time frame. In light of the freeze still being in force, the school
principals are working through their expenditure priority lists. As we approach the end of the school year, it is
anticipated that there will be budget transfers to review at the next FFO meeting.

2, Discussion: Continued Discussion of Revenue Accounts --

Don Meltabarger provided the Committee with a detailed description of the Revenue Accounts as was
requested. Each of the school principals, in turn, walked the Committee through each Revenue Account line
item and provided additional explanation.

The only items that need to be resolved were the retained Kindergarten Enrichment Program (KEP) Revenue
Accounts that need to be removed and the funds transferred to other Revenue Accounts within each school.

4, Discussion: Update on FY14 Capital Project Completions --

Don Meltabarger presented the Committee an update on those Capital Improvement Projects that have been
completed during FY14. The items completed included the High School gymnasium bleachers, the fire
sprinklers for the High School A-wing, and beginning May 5 the High School gymnasium floor (that was
damaged by a water leak) will be refinished (with the cost borne by our insurance carrier) and the floor should
be ready in about six weeks (in time for an indoor high school graduation if so dictated by the weather).

5. Discussion: Update on High School Baseball Field --

Don Meltabarger informed the Committee that the High School Baseball Field water problem is corrected and
the field was available for use on opening day of baseball season. Further upgrades to the center field
portion of the field will be addressed in the near future.

6. Adjournment - The meeting was adjourned at 6:45&",

Respectfully Submitted,
Albert lL. Lithefila Pam Rowe
Albert L. Littlefield, Co-Chair Pam Rowe, Co-Chair
