Approval of Minutes:

Motion made by Jan Logozzo to approve the minutes of the

September 22, 2014 meeting with a second by Nancy Burkhardt:
motion passed unanimously.

Pool Update:
Jack reported that finances are about the same as they were in

September in that we are a little behind the previous year, but
still doing well. There is no change in equipment and the moss
system providing better air quality is working well. A heater
problem has been corrected. The Nutmeg Swim Team is doing
well as are swim lessons. Next year the doorways and lockers will
be renovated/repaired.
Pool Parking Lot Lights:

Steve Hargis will again talk to Maintenance regarding the lack of
lighting in the pool parking lot early in the morning. The lights
should come on at 5:45 a.m. It is noted they are not on by 6:00
a.m. when people have come and gone.

keep committee members informed as to the progress of the
renovations/funding, updates regarding this meeting and any

Membership Applicants:

After an explanation of the goals of the Pool Committee and the
responsibilities of its members, the committee interviewed Brett

making a final decision, Steve will contact them and forward
results to committee members.
