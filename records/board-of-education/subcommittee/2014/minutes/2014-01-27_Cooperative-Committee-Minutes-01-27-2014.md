C. Discussion re: Brief Update on ELPS District's Annual Initiatives
Assistant Superintendent Brian Reas provided a brief update on ELPS’s initiatives for the 2013/2014
school year as follows:

Administrator and Teacher Evaluation Plan implementation
Continuation of ELPS Elementary School Vision work
Reinstatement of Elementary School Global Language
Successful Transition Program implementation

ATLAS Curriculum Mapping Process software implementation

Concerns were voiced by both Salem and East Lyme board members regarding the administrative
challenges faced by the districts to efficiently manage the state-mandated evaluation plan for
administrators and teachers.

D. Discussion re: Update on ELPS and Salem Community Discussions Regarding Future Cooperative
Agreement

= The current 20-year Salem/East Lyme Cooperative Agreement expires on June 30, 2019.

* Both Superintendents, business personnel, and the Salem/East Lyme Cooperative Study Group
Committee are in the process of preparing a draft of a new cooperative agreement. This will
allow Salem students to continue to attend East Lyme High School for grades 9 through 12.

= A draft of the agreement is expected to be finalized by June 30, 2014 and available for the Boards
and the communities to review.
