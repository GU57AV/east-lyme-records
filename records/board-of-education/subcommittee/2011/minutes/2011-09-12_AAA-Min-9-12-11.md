1. Overview: ELPS Long Range Plan, Goals & Priorities 2011-2012 — Dr. Lombardo

Dr. Lombardo went over the ELPS Long Range Plan indicating that the plan would be a working
template to facilitate district efforts to achieve the goals. The committee felt that to work
towards achievement of the highlighted goals/objectives this year is a very ambitious
undertaking.

The understanding that the goals/objectives listed are not placed in any priority order and that
during the course of the year the established committees, working groups, and staff would be
providing periodic status to Dr. Lombardo who would in turn be reporting out to the Board and
as needed, the committees would also be reporting-out to the Board as well.

The goals/objectives would dovetail nicely with Dr. Lombardo’s Superintendent Evaluation
(which is usually done by the Board in June of each year).

2. Special Education Overview — Brian Reas

¢ Administrative Structure — Last year’s Special Education focus was broad, Pre-K to 12, but
this year it has been restructured into Elementary and Secondary coordinators.

+ New Program Development — Based on the recommendations of the Future’s Special
Education Task Force, the concept to “Bring Our Children Home” was supported by the
introduction of a pilot summer program of an Extended Day Program to serve those children
who were previously transported to a private agency after their regular school day. This pilot
program had parental support and met with great success.

In August, it evolved into a 2011-12 school year program based at the Middle Schoo! (which is
the school attended by those students who would be served). The program would be after
school, two hours per day, five days a week.

This program would have the ability to be programmatically linked to the present school-based
Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) program and be supervised by ELPS employees. Offering this
extended after-school day at the students’ regular school would make an easy transition for
these students.

By implementing this in-school Extended Day Program, the initial cost savings (after staff/facility
expenses) is approximately $15,000 with the potential for adding additional students at a
minimal cost.
