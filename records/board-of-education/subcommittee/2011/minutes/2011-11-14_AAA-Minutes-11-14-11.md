= New Courses - Presentation of the following four (4) new ELHS and ELMS courses for the
2012-2013 school year:

Passion to Profit (ELHS: Family & Consumer Sciences) Half Year

Course will provide students with the resource management skills needed to turn an idea
into a product or service. Students will generate an individual, original business idea
through which they will explore the course content including innovation, leadership,
communication, and problem solving through both classroom and experienced-based
environments.

The Committee questioned why this is in the Family & Consumer Sciences department
instead of the Business department. The response was that the idea for the course
originated within the Family and Consumer Sciences department (with Ms. Adams) and
that the Business department was closely consulted during the development of this
course. The course does provide the business instruction and knowledge using an
“entrepreneurial” methodology.

** AAA Committee recommendation: Approval of Course.
Creative Writing Il (ELHS: English) Half Year

The course is an open-workshop format in creative writing for those students who have
successfully completed the Creative Writing | course and wish to continue their
exploration of writing. This course will foster a writers’ community at ELHS.

** AAA Committee recommendation: Approval of Course.
Intermediate Algebra (Algebra/Geometry Transition) (ELHS: Mathematics) Full Year

The course will reinforce and build upon topics learned in Algebra | and will
introduce/teach concepts of geometry as a method to create a more solid foundation of
algebraic skills and better prepare students for additional math/geometry courses.

** AAA Committee recommendation: Approval of Course.

— Topics in Mathematics (ELHS): Mathematics) Half Year

The course will investigate a variety of topics that are not traditionally covered in high
school mathematics courses (e.g., voting, fair division, networking, Fibonacci numbers,
and fractals) and provide students with a working knowledge of contemporary
mathematics topics that will have broad applications to many future career paths.

This course answers the question: When in my life will | ever use all of this math stuff
that | am learning in school?

— ** AAA Committee recommendation: Approval of Course.
— Algebra! Part | (ELMS) Full Year

The course forms the basis of the necessary mathematical knowledge for all future math
classes. This course will provide that “extra margin” of time and mathematical
instruction that will better prepare students for future mathematics classes.

** AAA Committee recommendation: Approval of Course.
