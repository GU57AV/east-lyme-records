EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION
ACADEMICS, ATHLETICS, AND ACTIVITIES COMMITTEE MINUTES
Monday, May 2, 2011
Superintendent’s Office, Central Office

Members Present: Laura Greenstein and Al Littlefield (Co-Chairs), Jaime Barr
Members Absent: Mary Broderick, Melissa Ford
Administrators Present: Brian Reas (Assistant Superintendent for Special Education and Pupil Personnel), Dr.

Karen Costello (Administrator for Program Improvement), Mike Susi (HS Principal), Judy
DeLeeuw (MS Principal)

The meeting was called to order at 7:04 ™.

1. ELMS Gym Uniform Policy - Lisa Kriger, Josh Sheppard, Jim Cunningham, and Judy DeLeeuw
presented an updated PowerPoint on the rationale for uniforms, student and parent feedback, and the
uniforms/costs. The conclusion was this is a successful, positive policy with no problems in its
implementation.

2. Livestream Programming Plans - Robin McKenny reviewed a handout on the purpose, potential, and
cost of creating a live-streaming channel for the East Lyme Public Schools. This is a communications and
community-relations initiative to showcase and share events and activities of the district. Robin will
convene a representative committee to systematically move forward with the proposed idea. This
committee will develop an implementation schedule, review existing and develop necessary policies,
determine protocols for posting video, and consider overall management of the channel. Feedback,
garnered from various constituencies (e.g., PTAs, staff, and representatives of community government),
and additional information on development of a live-streaming channel initiative will then be presented to
the full Board.

3. Proposed Mandarin Course ~ Discussed a 0.2 certified Mandarin teacher to be shared with the Old
Lyme Public Schools. The committee requested more information on the teacher and the feasibility (space
and potential enrollment) of running a pilot class in the 2011-12 school year at ELHS. Karen Costello and
Mike Susi will follow up on this and bring to the Subcommittee/Board.

4. Review of BOE Policy and Regulation 6140 on Instruction and Curriculum - Discussion was
postponed until a review by the Curriculum Council over the summer, convening in September-October for
discussion, and then being brought back to the Subcommittee/Board in October-November.

5. Review of BOE Policy and Regulation 4218.51 on Social Networking - The committee felt that the
first step should be to develop a separate policy on Social Networking with input from multiple sources
such as legal, technology, personnel, and CABE. Then a regulation can be developed that supports the
policy. The process should include legal advice to ensure that the regulation reflects a balance between
safety and freedom of speech. It was noted that some of the proposed regulations require clarity of terms
and intent and also reflect the rapidly changing picture of social networking and should consider the use of
social networking by students and parents also. The end result was to send this back to the Administrative
staff for further work.

Meeting adjourned at 8:57 ™ Respectfully submitted,
Albert Littlefield and Laura Greenstein
Committee Co-Chairs
