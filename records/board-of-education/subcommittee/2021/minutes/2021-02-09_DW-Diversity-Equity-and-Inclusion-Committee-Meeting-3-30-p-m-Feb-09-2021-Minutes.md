DW Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Committee Meeting

MINUTES
1. Call to Order
The meeting convened 3:34 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
A. Discussion: Review Draft Executive Summary and DEI Strategic Plan
Superintendent Newton provided the draft executive summary and strategic plan as a pre-read for the districtwide committee. Mr. Newton reviewed the six action steps that the East Lyme Public Schools will focus on over the next five years.
Action Steps:
Action item 1: Public Declaration
Action Item 2: Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion, (DEI) Action Teams
Action Item 3: Institutional Audit
Action Item 4: Curricular Revisions
Action item 5: Hiring Faculty and Administrators from Non-white Populations
Action Item 6: Enduring Commitment
Discussion ensued with committee members.
B: Discussion: Black History Month
Superintendent Newton and committee members shared plans that have been happening during the month of February for black history month.
Elementary:
-Lessons in Seesaw
-28 days in black history that has changed things in the world.
-Specials teachers are incorporating through music/composers and library are using age appropriate books for lessons.
-Bulletin boards
ELMS:
-In flex time, choice boards of parts of history being introduced to students.
-Music teachers are incorporating music/composers.
-Counselors have sent out activities to partake in.
ELHS:
-Many student led activities through Viking Saga and Podcast Club.
-Lessons that are being incorporated into lessons.
-Student display cases
-
Mr. Kydd wanted to know how we are going to handle all the other months?
Our hope over the next five years, this will become integrated into curriculum and will not need to have months.
Discussion ensued with committee members.
C: Discussion: Equity Audit
Superintendent Newton provided update on the Equity Institute proposal and timeline. We have invited Karla Vigil to speak with the board at an upcoming board meeting. We hope to finalize by March.
D: Discussion: Appendix A
Superintendent Newton shared the updated Appendix A and opened for discussion on how to incorporate the Appendix A and Strategic Plan.
Future Agenda Items:
1. Discussion: Survey for Students
2. Discussion: Current Curriculum (How to embed diversity, equity and inclusion?)
3. Discussion: Appendix A and Strategic Plan Combine
3. Adjournment
The meeting concluded at 4:35 p.m.

Jeffrey R. Newton
