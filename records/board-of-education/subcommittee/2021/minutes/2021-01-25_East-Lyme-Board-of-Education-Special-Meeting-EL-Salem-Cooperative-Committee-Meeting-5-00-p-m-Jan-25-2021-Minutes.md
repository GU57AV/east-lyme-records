Information: C. Discussion: Update on ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2021-2022
Superintendent Newton shared the proposed East Lyme Public Schools budget for 2021-2022 school year. We have held two budget workshop/meetings the Board of Education and three Board of Finance members.
Proposed 2021-2022 Budget Details:
-Proposed 4.05% budget increase
-
1.0 FTE High School Social Worker​
-1.0 FTE Certified Occupational Therapy Assistant (COTA)​
-2.0 FTE Elementary Math Coaches​
-1.0 FTE Middle School Math Coach​
-6.0 FTE Paraprofessionals ​
-2.0 FTE Additional Grade K Teachers (based on enrollment)​
-2.0 FTE Additional Grade 2 Teachers (based on enrollment)
Discussion ensued between administration and the board members of East Lyme and Salem.
Information: D. Discussion: Update on Salem Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2020-2021
Superintendent Joseph Onofrio shared the proposed Salem School District budget for the 2021-2022
Proposed 2021-2022 Budget Details:
-Proposed 4.62% budget increase
-Additional Grade K Teacher
-Computer Software Specialist
-We did not include COVID-19 expenses in the budget
-We are working with CABE to start a Study Committee to include Mary Broderick and Jack Reynolds to help recommend an administrative structure for Salem along with beginning the search for a new Superintendent.
Discussion, Information: E. Shared Food Services Director Agreement

-Superintendent Newton provided an update on the ELPS Food Services. Chris Urban and his crew have been working very hard dispersing food to all families in the East Lyme community.
