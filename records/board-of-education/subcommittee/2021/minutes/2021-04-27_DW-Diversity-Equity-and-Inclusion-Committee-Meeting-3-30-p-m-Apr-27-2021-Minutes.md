1. Call to Order

2. Discussion Items
A. Discussion: Website Design
Superintendent Newton introduced Kristina Erinakes (Helpdesk/Website Supervisor) who provided an overview on the current website design and updates that she plans to bring forward. She also shared with the committee where the link for any Diversity, Equity and Inclusion efforts is located on the website. Superintendent Newton would like the committee to provide any feedback and/or links to share.
B: Discussion: Communication and Website Translation
Kristina Erinakes provided an overview of the new tools for the website.
East Lyme Schools is committed to equal access to our district website content, and two upcoming changes will contribute to this goal. We will add a translation feature that allows visitors to switch from English to a language of their choice, and it will be located in the upper right corner.
Another feature called
Page Assist,
will provide an accessibility toolbar to make the website more usable and visually accessible to people with disabilities. It allows visitors to customize the site in a way that meets their unique requirements, and it will be located in the lower right corner.
We look forward to providing a better user experience for our community members. You can expect to see these changes on Wednesday, April 28th
C: Discussion: DEI Strategic Plan and Appendix A
