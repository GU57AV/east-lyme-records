DW Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Committee Meeting
March 9, 2021
3:30 p.m.
Via ZOOM
MINUTES
1. Call to Order
The meeting convened 3:33 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
A. Discussion: Equity Audit with the Equity Institute
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the audit with the Equity Institute and their suggested timeline that will be brought forward.
B: Discussion: School-Based Survey for Students
Superintendent Newton and committee members shared plans on developing school-based surveys for staff and students.
Discussion:
-Committee members plan to survey staff members on diversity, equity and inclusion.
-Incorporate using padlet (anonymously) to staff with buckets so all staff can see responses.
-Committee members decided to wait on surveying students and collaborate with the Equity Institute.
-Discussion to survey Grade 2-12 or Grades 5-12.
-Incorporate SEL component in survey.
Discussion ensued with committee members.
C: Discussion: Current Curriculum (How to Embed DEI)
Superintendent Newton and Assistant Superintendent Drowne provided an overview on the expectations she plans to review with the Instructional Pathways committee. This work will begin once we receive results from the equity audit that is planning to take place.
Plans:
-Revise curriculum units
-Look at cycles and identify targeted work in May.
-Revise templates and see how it aligns with results of the audit and get a definitive direction.
-Use Great Schools Partnership as the framing for our educational thinking.
-Look to collaborate with this committee and Instructional Pathways committee to identify targeted stakeholders for the work.
D: Discussion: Appendix A and Strategic Plan (Combine)
Superintendent Newton provide an update that we are still working with our board attorney on the draft strategic plan. We plan to meet with DEI Ad-Hoc Committee on March 18, 2021 at 4:00 p.m. for the legal perspective.
Future Agenda Items:
1. Discussion: Equity Institute Timelines
2. Discussion: School-Based Surveys to Staff
3. Discussion: Communication and District Website on DEI
4. Discussion: How we orientate new students and staff to be more inclusive?
5. Discussion: ESL coordinator and translation services for students/families
3. Adjournment
The meeting concluded at 4:39 p.m.
Respectfully Submitted,
