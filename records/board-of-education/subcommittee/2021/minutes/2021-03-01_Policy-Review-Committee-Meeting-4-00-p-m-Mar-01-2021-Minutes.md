2. REVIEW OF BOE POLICIES TO DISCUSSION
A.  Discussion: Second Review-Suggested Revision of Policy - 6121.2 Equal Educational Opportunity
The Policy Review Committee brought back for second review on Policy 6121.1 - Equal Educational Opportunity to include our policy. There is no model policy from Shipman & Goodwin and most of the policy is embedded in other policies.
Discussion ensued between the committee members and will bring to the board on Monday, March 8, 2021 to suggest eliminating policy 6121.1.
B.  Discussion: First Review-Suggested Revision of Policy - 1411 Law Enforcement Agencies
The Policy Review Committee reviewed Policy 1411 - Law Enforcement Agencies to include our policy, CREC, and WPS as model policy suggestions.
Discussion ensued between the committee members and agreed to use the current language of WPS.
-Bring to the board for First Reading on Monday, March 8, 2021.
C.  Discussion: First Review-Suggested Revision of Policy - 5140 Student Welfare
The Policy Review Committee reviewed Policy 5140 - Student Welfare to include our policy and CREC as a model policy suggestion.

-Bring to the board for First Reading on Monday, March 8, 2021 to suggest eliminating policy 5140.

The Policy Review Committee reviewed Policy 5141.231 Psychotropic Drug Use to include our policy, CREC, Learn, and WPS as model policy suggestions.
