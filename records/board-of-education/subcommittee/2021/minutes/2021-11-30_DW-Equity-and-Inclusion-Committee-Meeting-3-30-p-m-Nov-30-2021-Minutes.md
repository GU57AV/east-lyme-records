MINUTES
1. Call to Order
Procedural: A. Call to Order
The meeting convened at 3:35 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
Discussion, Information: A. Follow-Up Discussion on High School Walkout
Superintendent Newton and Assistant Principal Kydd shared information on the recent high school walkout on November 16, 2021. The walkout led to more conversations with students and administration. Mr. Kydd shared that there are approximately 12 students working with administration to hold a student-led assembly in January 2022. The goal is to continue the conversations between students and staff.
Discussion, Information: B. Bias Response Protocol
Superintendent Newton and Esteban Garcia shared information on the bias response protocol that has been implemented at SCSU. Superintendent Newton would like to form a small committee of students, staff and administration to potentially create a bias response protocol for East Lyme Public Schools.
Discussion, Information: C. Translating School-Based Information and Communications
Superintendent Newton and Mrs. Thomson discussed information regarding translations and sharing of limited English proficient documents. We would like to discuss incorporating more training and time into translating for our ELL families. It will help to increase the communication with ELL students/parents.
Discussion, Information: D. Discussion: Prioritize Work Efforts for the Districtwide and School-Based committees
Superintendent Newton and the districtwide and school-based committee continue to prioritize their efforts for the 2021/2022 school year.
Districtwide:
Bias Response Protocol
Continue to work collaboratively between the districtwide and school-based committees
Continue to bring forth a sense of belonging for all our students
Continue to gather more information on communication efforts with our ELL students/families
High School:
Establishing school norms on inclusion
Engage in further training on restorative practices
Continue to work with students and administration on the student led assembly in January
Work collaboratively with high school students/administration and be mentors to our middle school students
Middle School:
Continue to work with students and administration regarding norms on inclusion and what we expect to accomplish this school year
Connect the middle school and high school students and work together on cultural awareness
Elementary:
Further support ELL students and families (Over 17 different languages across the elementary level)
Add additional professional development opportunities and continue throughout the school year
Bring forth a sense of belonging
Engage in schoolwide responsive programs
Future Agenda Items:
Report out on discussions from School-Based committees
Bias response protocol
Engage in breakout groups to further discussion
3. Adjournment
Procedural: A. Adjournment
The meeting concluded at 4:30 p.m.
