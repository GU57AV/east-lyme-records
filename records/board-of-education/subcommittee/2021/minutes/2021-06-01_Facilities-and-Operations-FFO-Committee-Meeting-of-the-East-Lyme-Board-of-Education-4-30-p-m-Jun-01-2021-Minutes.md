Finance, Facilities and Operations Committee Meeting (FFO)
East Lyme Board of Education
June 1, 2021
4:30 p.m.
Via Zoom
MINUTES
Members Present
: Eric Bauman (Chair), John Kleinhans and Jill Carini
Non-Member Present:
Timothy Hagen
Administrators Present:
Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Maryanna Stevens (Director of Finance, Marketing and Growth Management),  Christian Lund (Director of Facilities) and Robert Bouchey (Director of Aquatics)
1. Call to Order
The meeting convened at 4:30 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
A.
Discussion: Aquatics Membership Rates and Pool Planning
Reviewed revised rates based on feedback at the last FFO Meeting.  Finance committee recommends moving forward with the revised rates.
B:
Discussion: Aquatic Center Ceiling/Roof Investigation Proposal
Discussed proposal for investigating the roof issues at the aquatic center.  Finance committee recommends using the escrow funds that were originally established when pool was built.
C:
Discussion: ELHS Baseball Field Update
Field soil analysis is being performed.  Will find out results in a few weeks.
D:
Discussion: Substitute Rates
Reviewed proposed rates for next school year.  Finance committee recommends moving forward with the revised rates.
E:
Discussion: Proposed 2021-2022 ELPS Tuition Rate for Out-of-District Students (Not Including High School Students from Salem)
Reviewed proposed tuition rate which is determined based on policy.  Finance committee recommends approving revised rates.
F:
Discussion: 2021/2022 BOF Budget Allocation
Reviewed proposed savings to reach 2021/22 budget allocation.
G:
Discussion: 2020/2021 Budget Deficit
Reviewed current year budget deficit which is in line with projections.
3. Executive Session
A.
Move to enter into Executive Session
The committee will move into executive session and include Superintendent Newton for discussion on the proposed Non-Union/Unaffiliated benefit wage schedule
Motion by Jill Carini, second John Kleinhans
Final Resolution: Motion Carried   - Will review the proposal in executive session at BOE
B.
Move to Exit Executive Session
4. Adjournment
The meeting concluded at 5:45 p.m.
Respectfully Submitted,
Eric Bauman
Chair
FFO Committee
