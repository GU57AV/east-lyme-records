Discussed the State Black / Latino Course at the ELHS..This course will be offered as an elective to Juniors & Seniors. Amy will be coming back to the AAA Committee in May/June to go through the State Curriculum with a possible action to adopt.

Discussion on ways to connect with our teachers to see what is working and what is the most challenging during the Pandemic and how we as a Board can help. Discussed survey and or having teacher representatives visit the AAA committee also discussed  Amy providing  an update on a weekly basis so that we aware and understand the ongoing challenges. Mrs. Kelly & Mr. Kydd stars the loss of in person time with students has been most challenging and the constant changes with modes of instruction.
