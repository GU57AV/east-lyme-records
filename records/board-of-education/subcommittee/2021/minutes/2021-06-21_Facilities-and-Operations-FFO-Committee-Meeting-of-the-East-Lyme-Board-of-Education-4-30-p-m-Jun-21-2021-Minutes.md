MINUTES
Members Present:

Timothy Hagen
Administrators Present:
Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Maryanna Stevens (Director of Finance, Marketing and Growth Management), Kimberly Davis (Director of Student Services) and Amy Drowne (Assistant Superintendent)
1. Call to Order
The meeting convened at 4:30 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
A.
Discussion: Creative Playschool Tuition Update
The committee reviewed the planned tuition rates for the 2021/22 school year which was part of the original 3-year plan for tuition.  Creative Playschool is at full capacity for all three sections.
B:
Discussion: School Revenue Update
The committee reviewed the revenue chart for all revenue accounts.  In addition, the committee discussed implementation of the new controls for cash and other incoming revenue along with new controls for expenditures.
C: Di
scussion: COVID Relief Funds and Usage (ARP Update)
The committee reviewed the latest update for funds for COVID relief.  These funds remain in line with our prior discussions.
D:
Discussion: ELHS Grounds Security Cameras
The committee discussed adding a camera at the high school to cover the senior parking lot, the baseball field and outer parking lot.  Superintendent Newton will research the costs for this.
3. Adjournment
The meeting concluded at 5:33 p.m.
Respectfully Submitted,
Eric Bauman
Chair
FFO Committee
