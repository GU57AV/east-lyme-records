MINUTES
1. Call to Order
The meeting convened at 3:30 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
A.
Discussion: Pre-Read - Interview with Dr. Pedro Noguera
Superintendent Newton provided a preview of the pre-read and then committee members were assigned to breakout rooms to discuss the article. Discussion ensued with committee members upon return.
B: Discussion: MLK Plans for Schools
Superintendent Newton and committee members proposed suggestions for MLK plans in each school.
Committee Proposed MLK Plans:
Elementary:
-Sub-committee has put together information for each grade level (age-appropriate) to be shared out to students.
-Provide SWAY and videos to share out to families/community.
-Community bulletin boards at each grade level using key words regarding MLK and diversity.
ELMS:
-Met with two students from the high school (Encountering Differences) who provided a PowerPoint for discussion.
-Will deliver during Social Studies classes to all students.
-Language Arts will be doing a writing assignment.
-Virtual Interviews with high school students and members of our community.
-Possible Panel Discussions with the 8th grade students.
ELHS:
-Panel Discussions that will be viewed during Advisory. (Set-up similar to Veterans Day)
-Involvement with the Viking Saga staff and Encountering differences staff and possible alumni.
-Will present on the Wednesday after MLK day.
C: Discussion: Update on BOE Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Work
Superintendent Newton provided update on the work that the Board of Education has been working on through the school year.
Update:
-Recently formed a BOE Ad-Hoc Committee for Diversity, Equity and Inclusion to form strategic plan. (Public declaration, minority recruitment, strategic partnership, audit, climate, curricular changes)
-Looking to join with an outside vendor to come in district to do an audit of the East Lyme Public School district.
D: Discussion: Appendix A
Superintendent Newton looking for feedback on next steps for the Appendix A. Discussion ensued with committee members. Superintendent Newton and Mr. Kydd will work on creating bullet points to add to the Appendix A and bring back to committee.
3. Adjournment
The meeting concluded at 4:30 p.m.
