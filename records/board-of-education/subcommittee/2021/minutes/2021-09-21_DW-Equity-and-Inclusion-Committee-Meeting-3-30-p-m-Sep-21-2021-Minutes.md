DW Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Committee Meeting
September 21, 2021

Via ZOOM
MINUTES
1. Call to Order
The meeting convened at 3:31 p.m.
2. Discussion Items
A. Discussion: Follow-Up ELPS Root Cause Equity Analysis
Superintendent Newton provided the ELPS Root Cause Equity Analysis for review to committee.
Mr. Newton shared important focus points that were taken away from the analysis.
Important Focus Points:
Strong call for more mental health support and support for students struggling with mental health issues
Be clear with our commitment to equity and building a sense of belonging for students
Focus on ‘Pathways’ and supporting students who might be entering trades and not going to college
Not enough PD time.  Look for additional opportunities for staff
Further support our ELL students
Free/Reduced Population of students and families:  Identify improved methods to communicate out information and allow for opportunities in which families can privately share hardships and needs for financial assistance
Increased translation of information and letters should occur
Work to provide increased clarity around goals and values – norms
Identify additional hands-on experiences for students/staff
There was an over identification of black, indigenous students receiving special education services.  Statistics will be looked at in Infinite Campus and PSIS data
Health Components – Support engagement and participation of students in activities that promote positive outcomes
School Calendar – account for religious holidays
Continue recruiting efforts of minority staff from outside the district
Pace Ourselves in this work to ensure we are doing everything we can to support students
Our focus:  Bring forth a sense of belonging and acceptance for every student who walks through our doors.
Discussion ensued with committee members.
B: Discussion: Planning for the 21/22 School Year
Superintendent Newton and committee shared action steps to take for next meeting.
Action Steps:
Ensure School-Based committees dates are set and finalize committee members
Discuss using the professional development on March 8, 2021 for ED Camp
Future Agenda Items:
Prioritize important focus points and have discussions at the Districtwide and School-Based committees
Solidify meeting dates and committee members
Discuss resources to share
Discuss DEI Strategic Plan
3. Adjournment
The meeting concluded at 4:28 p.m.
Respectfully Submitted,
Jeffrey R. Newton
