2. REVIEW OF BOE POLICIES TO DISCUSSION
A. First Review - Suggested New Policy - 6172.6 Instruction: Virtual/Online Courses (New)
The Policy Review committee reviewed Suggested New Policy - 6172.6 Instruction: Virtual/Online Courses (New) to include LEARN and WPS as model policy suggestions.
Suggestions:
-WPS model policy was aligned most with ELPS.
-Reflect name of policy to say 6172.6 - Instruction: High School Virtual/Online Courses.
-First paragraph should reflect changes: The Board recognizes the importance of technology in education and the
growing popularity
opportunity to excel in
using
online coursework. In that regard, and in accordance with Connecticut General Statutes § 10-221a, the Board sanctions the receipt of online course credit to be used toward high school graduation requirements, in accordance with this policy.
Discussion ensued between the committee members and will bring back to the Policy Committee for review at the next meeting.
B. Discussion: First Review - Suggested Revision of Policy - 6141.32 Instruction: Technology and Instruction (Review)
The Policy Review Committee reviewed Policy - 6141.32 Instruction: Technology and Instruction (Review) to include our policy and LEARN as a model policy suggestion. Mrs. Drowne shared Stonington Public Schools policy on technology/instruction which aligned best with ELPS.
Suggestions:
-Will add in blended learning framework into policy provided from Stonigton Public Schools.
-Add in: The board is committed to provide technology and instruction in an equitable way.
Discussion ensued between the committee members and will bring back to the Policy Committee for review at the next meeting.
C. Discussion: First Review - Suggested Revision of Policy - 6154 Instruction: Homework
The Policy Review Committee reviewed Policy 6154 Instruction: Homework include our policy, LEARN, WPS, and Shipman & Goodwin as model policy suggestion.
Suggestions:
-Shipman and Goodwin policy most align with ELPS.
-Homework policy should be short and concise of what ELPS is requiring.
-Do not include make-up work in policy.
-Add in: The board acknowledges homework and the appropriate matter as an integral part of instruction.
Discussion ensued between the committee members and will bring back to the Policy Committee for review at the next meeting.
D. Discussion: Policy Series Review
The Policy Review Committee discussed reviewing policy by a series. We will start with the 1000 series in increments of five. Discussed ensued between committee members.
