1. Discussion/action re: Proposed Change to ELPS Calendar for 2010-2011 (April 1, 2011)
To date, the district has six (6) snow days to make up for the 182 day school year. Dr. Lombardo
recommended that the April 1, 2011 professional development day be changed to a regular school day for
students. If there are no further snow days, the last day of school for students will be Wednesday, June 22,
2011 (1/2 day). The professional development make-up day for certified staff will be the day after the last

day of school for students.

Mary Broderick moved to approve amending the ELPS 2010-2011 Calendar changing the April 1, 2011
professional development day to a regular school day for students and the teacher professional development
make-up day to be held the day after the last day of school for students, as presented; Beth Groeber seconded.

UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED

2. Discussion/action re: Proposed ELPS Calendar for 2011-2012
The proposed calendar was recommended for adoption by the Calendar Committee and Dr. Lombardo. Staff
and community input was considered and incorporated into the development of the calendar. The calendar
mirrors the proposed “regional calendar” in several ways: Common PD days on October 1] and Nov. 8;
common Thanksgiving, December and April vacation weeks; common start of the February break. The
mirror departs from the “regional calendar” in the following ways: school on Veterans Day; four (4) half
days for local PD; parent conference days; full week for February break. All half days have common times in
this calendar draft; there is no distinction between “half” and “shortened” days as in this year’s calendar.
Both concerns and support for the continued inclusion of Winter Break were voiced by the Board. Likewise,
concern over the inclusion of half days for PD and disruption of teaching continuity throughout the year was
expressed.

Marlene Nickerson moved adoption of the ELPS Calendar for 2011-2012 (draft 12/8/10) as presented; Jaime
Barr seconded.
