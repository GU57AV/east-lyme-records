Discussion re: Use of Breathalyzers at Extra~Curricular Activities

The Board discussed the district’s proposed use of breathalyzers to screen random individuals for
alcohol use at extra-curricular high school activities. HS Assistant Principal Jeff Provost and ELPS
Director of Safety and Security Chris Olsen reviewed alcohol and drug preventative type school
measures currently being used with additional measures to be explored. The intent of the school
administration is to give students a tool to help them make good decisions and to provide an
effective detourant for safety reasons. Discussion and debate ensued regarding how this would
change the school culture and the additional need to engage students in finding solutions to alcohol
and drug use. In general, board members desired alternative options but agreed to go forward with a
one time random breathalyzer testing at the rescheduled homecoming dance on Monday, November
7. Further communication, feedback and discussion will occur before proceeding with additional

breathalyzer use.
