1. Discussion re: FY 11 Budget Update as of February 28, 2011
The budget update as of February 28, 2011 was reviewed by the FFO Committee on March 14. A budget
freeze remains in effect and the district expects to end the year with a surplus. Special education
reimbursement for FY 12 was anticipated at 84%, but may be reduced to 78%.

2. Discussion/action re: FY 11 Budget Transfers in the Amount of $231,241.00

The budget transfer was reviewed by the FFO on March 14, 2011 and is recommended for approval. The
budget transfer will reallocate funds to cover natural gas at the high school, medically fragile room
renovations, feasibility study cost, unemployment, overtime and maintenance supplies.

Marlene Nickerson moved approval of the proposed FY 11 budget transfer in the amount of $231,241.00 as
presented; Melissa Ford seconded.

UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED

3. Discussion/action re: Process Plan to Address the Long Term Facilities Needs of the District

On March 14, Dr. Lombardo presented the FFO Committee with five possible options that the district and
town might choose for addressing the long term elementary school and Central Office facility needs. He also
presented a template illustrating how ELPS shall engage in a process whereby the community will complete
the analysis of each of the indicated variables and determine a long-range plan to address the facility needs of
the district. FFO Co-Chairs Marlene Nickerson and Beth Groeber explained the charge of the vision
committee. Conversation with ELPS teachers and staff members, the community-at-large, the Town of East

. Lyme leadership and 20/20 Vision Committee is essential in identifying a long term strategy for the district.
No action was taken.
