B. Instruction
1. Discussion re: Presentation on ELPS CMT, CAPT and Other Assessment Data

Dr. Lombardo presented assessment data for the 2010-2011 school year. The CT Mastery Test (CMT) is
administered in grades 3-8 in reading, math and writing; science in grades 5 and 8. East Lyme students
continue to do well with student achievement on the rise each year. The CT Academic Performance Test
(CAPT) is administered in grade 10 and tests students in the areas of writing, math, and science. A total of
166 students achieved goal (4) or advanced level (5) on all of the CAPT subtests. Percentages of students
performing at or above proficiency held the highest overall averages since the history of the test and are
representative of the work by students and staff throughout the district. Scholastic Aptitude Tests (SAT) are
taken in grades 11 and 12 and are used for college admissions to determine college readiness. The trend
continues with boys scoring slightly higher in math and girls higher in reading and writing. East Lyme’s
overall mean scores were significantly higher than the state and national averages. Five students from the
class of 2011 were National Merit Finalists. Also used for college admissions, the American College Testing
(ACT) showed a significant increase in student participation and scores were higher than state and national
averages. Advanced Placement (AP) tests scores were impressive with 237 students taking a total of 481
tests. The Board congratulated the staff and students for their tremendous effort.

1. Discussion/action re: Proposed Recognition of the American Institute for Foreign Study (AIFS) Foundation
as a BOE Approved International Exchange Program

In accordance with Board of Education Policy 5118, Dr. Lombardo requested that the American Institute for

September 27, 2010, the Board approved attendance of a single foreign exchange student through this
