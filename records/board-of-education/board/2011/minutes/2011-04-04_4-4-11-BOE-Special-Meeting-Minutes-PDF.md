EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION
East Lyme, Connecticut

Board of Education Special Meeting Minutes
Monday, April 4, 2011
6:45 p.m.
INTV Room (B101)
East Lyme High School

Board Members Present: Tim Hagen (Chair), Beth Groeber (Secretary), Jaime Barr,
Laura Greenstein, Marlene Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Rich Steel

Board Members Absent: | Mary Broderick, Melissa Ford, Al Littlefield

Administrators Present: Dr. James Lombardo (Superintendent), Brian Reas (Assistant Superintendent),
Dr. Karen Costello (Administrator for Program Improvement), Jeff Provost (HS
Asst. Principal), Jason Bitgood (Asst. Principal HS Alternative Education
Program), Don Meltabarger (Finance and Facilities Director)

I. CALL TO ORDER- 6:45 p.m.

Il DISCUSSION
1. Discussion/Action re: Set East Lyme High School Graduation Date for the Class of 2011
Dr. Lombardo presented graduation date options to the Board as follows: 1) Wednesday, June 22,
2011 which is the 1824 school day for the current school year; or 2) Monday, June 20, 2011
(waiving two days of the 182 school year calendar for seniors only).

MOTION: Kevin Seery moved to set the East Lyme High School graduation date for the Class of 2011 as
Monday, June 20, 2011 at 6:00 p.m. and to waive two days of the 182 school year calendar for
seniors only; Al Littlefield seconded.

VOTE: UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED

2. Discussion /Action re: Proposed Capital Plan Requests
Dr. Lombardo recommended the Board authorize him to send a letter to the Board of Selectmen to
include capital projects in the 2011-2012 Town of East Lyme Capital Plan. The projects are in
addition to the previous request regarding the ELPS Athletic Complex Repair/Replacement Project
of $2.3 million.

MOTION: Beth Groeber moved the inclusion of the following items in the 2011-2012 Town of East Lyme
Long Range Capital Plan, with the projects being in addition to the previous request regarding
the ELPS Athletic Complex Repair/Replacement Project of $2.3 million:

Replace roof on LBH Learn wing $300,000
HS Music wing wall repair 150,000
HS Baseball field repairs 80,000
HS Tennis court repairs 300,000
HS Culinary Arts Lab upgrade 400,000; Rich Steel seconded.

VOTE: UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED
4654

3. Discussion/Action re: Follow-up of Board of Finance Budget Review
The Board of Finance has indicated that the proposed 2011-2012 Board of Education Budget may
be significantly reduced. Dr. Lombardo and Tim Hagen presented a draft letter that described the
consequences and significant impact a dramatic budget reduction would have on the district. The
amounts indicated are placeholders, with the final plan and figures subject to Board of Education
approval. Input was considered and the final draft will be reviewed by Board members.

Ill. PUBLIC COMMENT - None
IV. ADJOURNMENT

MOTION: Al Littlefield moved to adjourn the meeting at 7:30 p.m.; Jaime Barr seconded.
VOTE: UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED

Respectfully submitted,
Beth Grocber

Beth Groeber, Secretary
East Lyme Board of Education
