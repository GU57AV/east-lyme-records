         B. Instruction
            1. Update re: Proposed Expansion of Coastal Connections Program for 2012-2013
               Dr. Lombardo formally requested the expansion of the Coastal Connections program. Sufficient out-of-
               district interest exists to generate tuition paying students. The Carelot building at 315 Flanders Road has
               room for expansion and no additional staff was requested.

MOTION:       Al Littlefield moved to approve the proposed expansion of the East Lyme Public Schools Coastal Connections
              Program for 2012-2013, enrolling up to five(5) tuition paying students as presented; Jaime Barr seconded.

              Discussion/action action was delayed to follow the VII.C.2. Proposed Transition Academy Expansion
              presentation.

              Coastal Connections discussion continued. Program Director Dr. Mike Sullivan and ELHS Principal Mike Susi
              emphasized the importance of housing the program in a separate and distinct location from ELHS and
              addressed questions related to program challenges. They also explained the internship process for students and
              mentors and how the program relates to the proposed expansion of the ELPS Transition Academy for 18-21
              year olds with special needs. Concerns were voiced that by accepting out of district students to the Coastal
              Connections program, East Lyme students might be turned away. Dr. Lombardo recommended the Board
              approve the expansion of the program to allow the program to move forward.

              The motion was amended as follows:

MOTION:      Al Littlefield moved to approve the proposed expansion of the East Lyme Public Schools Coastal Connections
             Program for 2012-2013 for East Lyme students, with the continuation of the Board’s close monitoring of the
             program, and giving Dr. Lombardo the option to return to the Board with future out of district requests; Jaime
             Barr seconded.

VOTE PRO: Tim Hagen (Chair), Rich Steel (Secretary), Al Littlefield, Marlene Nickerson, Pam Rowe, Chris Sandford

VOTE CON: Jaime Barr                                                                            MOTION CARRIED 6:1

             2. Proposed Expansion of East Lyme Public Schools Transition Academy for 18-21 Year Olds with Special
                      Needs for 2012-2013
                The district has experienced success with its local transition program for 18-21 year old special needs
                students by contracting the services of a job coach. Assistant Superintendent Brian Reas explained that the
                district now has the opportunity to better serve students and to generate additional savings by hiring a 0.5
                FTE special education teacher and a 1.0 FTE job coach to provide services to 5-6 students previously served
                by an outside vendor. The estimated cost savings is $95,000 after the additional staff expenditure and the
                Carelot rental expansion at 315 Flanders Road.

MOTION:       Al Littlefield moved to approve the proposed expansion of the East Lyme Public Schools Transition Academy
              for 18-21 year olds with special needs for 2012-2013 with the employment of a 1.0 FTE job coach and a 0.5
              FTE special education teacher as presented; Pam Rowe seconded.

VOTE:         UNANIMOUS                                                                         MOTION CARRIED

             3. Discussion: Proposed Recalculation of Grade Point Average (GPA) and Elimination of Class Rank for East
                      Lyme High School Students Beginning with the Class of 2016 (and Possibly the Class of 2015)
                The Board continued discussion on the recommendation to modify the Grade Point Average (GPA)
                calculation and eliminate class rank at East Lyme High School, beginning with the class of 2016. On April
                24, the Board asked the district to explore the possibility of implementing the proposal beginning with the
                class of 2015, and the potential to bring electives into the weighted system. Principal Susi reported on the
                positive feedback received from the recent informational meeting for parents of current grade 9 students. A
                proposal will be presented for discussion/action at the May 29 regular Board meeting.

           4. Discussion/action re: Proposed Safe Schools Climate Plan for East Lyme Public Schools
              Dr. Lombardo informed the Board that recent legislation requires all CT School Districts to adjust new anti-

              endorse the Safe School Climate Plan for each of its schools. The AAA Committee has reviewed the draft
              plan and recommends Board approval.
