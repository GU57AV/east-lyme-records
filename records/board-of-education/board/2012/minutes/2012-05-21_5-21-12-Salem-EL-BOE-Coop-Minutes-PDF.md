VI.     Report on SSD Long-Range Educational Planning Subcommittee re: Discussion on
        Future Cooperative Agreement
        Linda Robson reported that the subcommittee has been tasked with looking at ELHS and
        other area high schools with regard to course offerings, school climate, tuition rates, capacity,
        and distance from Salem. It was noted that this research is being conducted as a good faith
        effort to the Salem constituency. It was also noted that Salem is not in any way dissatisfied
        with the educational programming the students receive at East Lyme High School.

VII.     Report by Salem/East Lyme Study Group as to Questions to Consider in Preparation
         for Future
         It was noted that Kim Gadaree should be marked as excused in the March 5, 2012
         Salem/East Lyme Cooperative Study Group meeting summary. Pamela Munro made some
         notes to the meeting summary and copies were distributed during the discussion. The
         topics/questions that were listed on the meeting summary were reviewed and discussed.
         There were no additional items added to this list.

VIII.    Proposed Changes in Class Rank and Grade Point Average at ELHS
         Tim Hagen explained that the original proposal was to make this change for the incoming
         freshman class. There was a push to include the current freshman and sophomore classes to
         this proposal. The current proposal includes incoming freshman, current freshman and
         current sophomores. There were meetings held with the current freshman class about this
         proposal and there was a favorable consensus with starting this change for them as well next
         school year. It was noted that students would still be able to get their class rank calculated if
         another school required it. Mike Susi reported that once this proposal is approved by the
         Board of Education the high school will publicize that students can change courses if they
         would like to do so.

IX.      Report on East Lyme Initiatives
         Dr. Lombardo reported on the following items:
         A. Athletic Complex – Bids will be solicited this week and are due by June 4th. Work is
             anticipated to begin after graduation and the field will be available for play by October
             4th.
         B. Coastal Connections Program – An expansion to the program was approved for next
             school year and the program will allow up to 25 students to attend. (Currently have 16
             students.)
         C. Technology Hardware and Education Plan – The ELPS three year technology plan
             has been developed. A hardware upgrade is included in this technology plan that will
             allow computer replacements so that no computer will be older than 5 years old.
         D. Transition Program for 18-21 Year Old Special Needs Population – An expansion
             to this program was approved for next school year that will allow for an additional 6
             students to enter the program.
         E. Report on Progress of Facilities Vision Committee, Including Enrollment
             Projections – The committee will be looking at demographic trends and determining
             how to proceed with regard to their elementary schools.

X.      Timeline Review for Renewal of Cooperative Agreement
        The current agreement expires June 30, 2019. The consensus was to continue to move
        forward. Linda Robson will replace Pamela Munro as joint-chair of the Salem/East Lyme
        Study Group. Stephen Buck asked the Superintendents to coordinate four meetings for the
        Salem/East Lyme Study Group to meet next school year.
