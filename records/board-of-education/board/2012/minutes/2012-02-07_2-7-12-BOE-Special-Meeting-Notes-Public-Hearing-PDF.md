       A. Presentation of the Proposed EL Athletic Complex, Parking Lot, Traffic Light Capital Project
          First Selectman Paul Formica, BOE Chair Tim Hagen and ELPS Superintendent James
          Lombardo provided a detailed PowerPoint presentation of the $3.45 million proposed East
          Lyme Athletic Complex, parking lot, and traffic light capital project. Eric Roise presented
          the Gale Associates slides. Paul Formica followed up with details regarding refunding and
          existing and proposed debt service in the 5-year capital plan. The audience was invited to ask
          questions and comment throughout the presentation.

           Steve Harney – What is the life expectancy of the track?
           A – At least 20 years

           Denise Dinsmore – What is the annual maintenance cost for the new athletic field?
           A - $2,000

           Denise Dinsmore - Explain the water drainage of the field.
           A – Designed to capture and store significant water under the field and released slowly.
           Rinsing as needed.

           Denise Dinsmore - Is there a danger of static electricity on the field?
           A – Not an issue with the rubber and sand

           Jack Hogan – The facility usage will increase approximately 300%; therefore, restrooms must
           be a consideration.

           Lisa Picarazzi – 1) What is the cost of the traffic light?
           A - $150,000, possibly reimbursable by the State of CT

Lisa Picarazzi - Is there a plan to renovate the HS softball field?
A – Not at this time.
Lisa Picarazzi - Will the maintenance savings over 10 years pay for the replacement turf?
A – Yes, without taking into consideration revenue streams during non school/town use.
Lisa Picarazzi - Is there a plan to renovate the HS baseball field?
A – The HS baseball field is presently being repaired and is expected to be completed in
April. The cost of the project is on target at $80,000.

Art Carlson – This is a well designed project. Safety is an enormous factor. The facility may
provide revenue opportunities to the town.

Allison Woods - What is the timeline for the project?
A – Construction will begin immediately following graduation is expected to be completed in
September 2012.

Allison Woods - The project makes good sense for many reasons; the number of usage hours,
cost effective, plan reflective of community, marketability of town, and investment in
community. I encourage the Board of Finance to move the project along and I encourage
residents to vote for the project.

Steve Harney – How comfortable is Gale Associates with the numbers given?
A – Very comfortable based on recent project completion.

Citizen – In favor of community fundraising effort

Mark Nickerson – This is a great opportunity for our children and for our town, especially

Wednesday, February 8, 7:00 p.m., Town Hall. The meeting will be televised live.
