VII. _DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
A. Instruction

1, Presentation and Discussion re: ELHS Coastal Connections Program Update

Dr. Lombardo began the presentation by providing background information and a mid-year update on the
Coastal Connections Program. The program was implemented at the start of this school year and has reached
its enrollment goal of 15 students, Dr. Mike Sullivan (Assistant Principal) and Jeff Drew (teacher and Learning
Through Interests Coordinator) provided a comprehensive presentation on the school culture, student
expectations, learning goals, community service and internship placements. Two students spoke passionately
about their positive program and internship experiences and a parent thanked the Board for providing her son
the opportunity to be a successful student and citizen. Regular program and budget updates will be provided to

the Board.

2. Discussion/action re: Second Reading for ELHS Math Textbook Adoption 2012-2013 — Excursions in

B. Finance/Facilities/Community

1. Presentation/Discussion re: Greenskies, Inc. — Solar Farm Project in East Lyme

Dr. Lombardo asked the Board to explore the potential benefits of entering into a solar power purchase
agreement with Greenskies, Inc. to provide electricity for the Central Office, East Lyme Middle School and
the elementary schools. Greenskies, Inc. is preparing to build a solar photovoltaic panel farm on Grassy Hill
Road in East Lyme and has approached the town to determine the feasibility of this project. Robert Landino,
Chairman and Michael Silvestrini, President gave a brief overview of the project and outlined the terms of the
proposed 20-year agreement. Attorney Ted Harris and Greenskies Senior Vice President Andrew Chester
were also available to answer questions. Pending project approval by the Town of East Lyme, Greenskies
will install, service, maintain, and monitor a Solar Farm on Grassy Hill Road and in turn the town makes a
commitment to purchase electricity at a discounted rate. Further discussion will take place at the FFO
committee meeting following the Board meeting.
