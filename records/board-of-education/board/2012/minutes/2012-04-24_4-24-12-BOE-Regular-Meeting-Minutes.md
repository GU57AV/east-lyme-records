   VII. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
        A. Instruction
           1. Discussion re: Proposed Recalculation of Grade Point Average (GPA) and the Elimination of Class Rank for
                    ELHS Students Beginning with the Class of 2016
              The Board discussed the high school’s recommendation to modify the Grade Point Average (GPA)
              calculation and eliminate class rank, beginning with the class of 2016. Principal Mike Susi explained that a
              committee of over 25 faculty members has worked over a one-year period reviewing BEST practices of
              highly performing schools and researching how to best present our students to colleges and universities. The
              recalibrated GPA calculations will provide a more equitable system with the district reporting both a “simple”
              GPA, where grades in all classes are worth the same, and a weighted GPA. The simple GPA would include
              grades in elective courses that are not factored into the weighted GPA. The proposal has been presented to
              Curriculum Council, the BOE Academics, Athletics and Activities (AAA) Committee, the Salem Board of
              Education, the ELMS PTA and the parents of current grade 8 students in East Lyme and Salem. An
              informational letter was sent to parents of elementary and middle school students. Some Board members
              suggested the district explore the possibility of implementing the proposal beginning with the class of 2015,
              and the also the potential to bring electives into the weighted system. Principal Susi will brief the parents of
              the current freshmen class and collect feedback prior to the Board meeting scheduled for May 15.

         B. Finance/Facilities/Community
            1. Discussion re: Board of Education’s 2012-2013 Budget Reduction of $350,000 Directed by the Board of
                    Finance
               Dr. Lombardo presented his initial thoughts on areas that he may be able to reduce to reach the $350,000
               budget reduction directed by the Board of Finance. An additional cut of $90,800 was made by the Board of
               Finance at the April 23 public hearing, bringing the 2012-2013 budget to a 1.78% increase over the current
               year’s budget. Reevaluation of the budget will occur over the next few weeks to determine the final
               recommended reductions based on the most accurate end-of-the year financial information.

             2. Discussion re: Announcement of Board of Education Vacancy

                Committee will recommend a replacement candidate and the Board is expected to appoint her replacement at
                the next regular Board meeting on May 15. The Board passed along its thanks to Laura for her service and
                dedication to the Board.
