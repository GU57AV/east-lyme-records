             1. Update re: ELPS Kindergarten Study Committee Progress
                Assistant Superintendent Brian Reas provided a summary of the Full Day Kindergarten Study Committee’s
                work to date. The committee, consisting of parents, community preschool directors, Board of Education
                members, teachers, principals and interested community members, was tasked with researching the
                desirability and educational rationale for expanding our half day program to full day. Research has included
                visits to full day kindergarten programs, the review of dozens of research articles, and a review of our current

              programs. Common core curriculum was mentioned. The public is invited to attend the Community Forum
              on Tuesday, March 27, 2012, 6:30 – 8:00 p.m. in the Gerald M. Mistretta Commons, East Lyme High School.
              The Full Day Kindergarten Study Committee expects to present its recommendation to the full Board in May.

           2. Presentation/Discussion re: Proposed Changes to Grade Point Average (GPA) Determination and Class Rank
                   for ELHS
              ELHS Principal Mike Susi presented a recommended change to the way grade point average (GPA) is
              determined and the elimination of class rank at ELHS. Currently a simple, non-weighted GPA and a
              weighted class rank, based on the weighted GPA of a student’s leveled classes, is reported to students in their
              junior year and to colleges. By recalculating GPA and eliminating class rank, a more equitable process will
              be created and students will be presented in the best possible light as they move forward. Mr. Susi stated that
              class rank is not currently a true representation of student performance, and by not reporting class rank,
              colleges will look beyond the numbers to evaluate the individual student. The proposed change is supported
              by Curriculum Council, the AAA Committee and Dr. Lombardo. Parent informational sessions are
              scheduled.

           3. Discussion re: Proposed East Lyme School District Calendar for 2012-2013
              Dr. Lombardo presented the proposed ELPS Calendar for 2012-2013. The calendar mirrors the “regional
              calendar” in several ways: common PD days on Nov. 6 and March 28; common Thanksgiving, December
              and April vacation weeks; and a common start of the February break. The mirror departs from the “regional
              calendar” in the following ways: school on Veterans Day; four (4) half days for local PD; parent conference
              days; and a full week for February break. The ELPS Calendar Committee met on several occasions and staff
              and community input was considered. The Committee recommended an additional half day for parent
              conferences on May 1, 2013, but the recommendation of three consecutive half days was not supported by the
              Superintendent. Concerns were voiced regarding the number of half day sessions and the impact they have
              on students and parents. Contractual obligations do not allow for additional full days of professional
              development. The full February break was another concern. Dr. Lombardo will present an alternate calendar
              at the March 26 Board meeting.
