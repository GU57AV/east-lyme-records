         1. Discussion/action re: Proposed Sublease Between EL BOE and Eastern Community

VOTE:        UNANIMOUS                                                               MOTION CARRIED

           2. Discussion re: Proposed ELPS Budget for 2012-2013
              Prior to the meeting, Dr. Lombardo presented the Board of Education draft budget for 2012-
              2013 at the Public Hearing. The presentation will be posted to the district website along with
              questions and responses from Board members and the public. Dr. Lombardo responded to
              questions regarding unfunded state mandates, deferment of the full day kindergarten program,
              unemployment costs to the district, direct instructional categories, and special education
              related costs. A breakdown of technology purchases, special revenue accounts, and special
              education student population numbers will be provided. Regionalized and special education
              contracted services were reviewed. In response to the Board’s request, Dr. Lombardo
              provided possible areas to reduce the budget to 3% and noted the impact the reductions will
              have on the district. The next special budget meeting is scheduled for Monday, February 6,
              2012 at 7:00 p.m.
