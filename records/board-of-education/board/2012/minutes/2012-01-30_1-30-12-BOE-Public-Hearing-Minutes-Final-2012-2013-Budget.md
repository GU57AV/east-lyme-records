  III. ITEM ITEMS
       A. Presentation on the 2012/2013 Board of Education Draft Budget
          Dr. Lombardo gave a PowerPoint presentation of the draft 2012/2013 Board of Education
          budget. The total budget of $42,041,219 is a 3.82% increase over the current budget. Salary
          and benefit changes represent 3.2 of the 3.8% budget increase. The reduction of 4.0 teachers
          is due to a decline in enrollment and is expected to be absorbed through retirements. Some
          reallocation of funding is necessary to cover staffing positions funded by a two-year federal
          jobs grant which is no longer available. Major increases include salaries, custodial overtime,
          and unemployment. The increase in supplemental salaries is due to under budgeting in 2011-
          2012. Cost control and program modifications include reduction in special education tuition
          due to program changes, reduction in fuel costs due to natural gas conversion at HA, MS and
          HS, reduction in projects to reflect shift to service contracts and 2011-2012 work, and
          reduction in legal fees. Initiatives deferred include full day kindergarten pilot, new World
          Languages, and the development of funds/resources for the Capstone Project. The district has
          realized a steady cost savings in special education tuition by bringing students back from
          outside placements and providing successful programs in district. Dr. Lombardo noted the
          average increase in budget growth over the past four years is 1.69%. The Board will continue
          discussion and deliberations and has until February 27 to adopt a final budget proposal to
          forward to the Board of Finance. Dr. Lombardo and Tim Hagen invited the public to submit
