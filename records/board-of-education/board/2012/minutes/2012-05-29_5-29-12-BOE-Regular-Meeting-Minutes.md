          B. Instruction
             1. Discussion/action re: Proposed Recalculation of Grade Point Average (GPA) and Elimination of Class Rank
                      for East Lyme High School Students Beginning with the Class of 2016 or the Class of 2015
                 Dr. Lombardo’s and HS Principal Michael Susi’s recommended the modification of the Grade Point Average
                 (GPA) calculation and eliminate class rank at East Lyme High School beginning with the class of 2015
                 (current freshmen). Discussion has occurred at three (3) Board meetings and Mr. Susi reported on the
                 overwhelming positive parent response.

MOTION:        Al Littlefield moved to approve that the district modify the Grade Point Average (GPA) calculation and
               eliminate class rank at East Lyme High School, beginning with the class of 2015 as follows:

               Recalculation of Grade Point Average (GPA)
               Weighted Grade Point Average (GPA) will be based on a weighted scale that starts with the simple 4.0 scale
               for B/C level classes and multiplies grade x weight as follows:
                   Advanced Placement (AP)/ECE courses are weighted with a 1.15 multiplier;
                   Honors classes are weighted by a 1.1 multiplier;
                                                                                                                4779

                 A-level classes are weighted by a 1.05 multiplier;
                 B-level and C-level classes earn a weighted GPA equivalent to the simple GPA and are weighted by a 1.0
                 multiplier.
              Both simple GPA and weighted GPA will be reported.
              Simple/unweighted GPA will include all classes including non-leveled electives.

              Recognition of Top 20 Scholars
              Weighted GPA will be used to determine East Lyme Scholars (top 20) and students from that group would be
              given an opportunity to “audition” to speak, and all would be recognized, at graduation on the program and
              with Honor Stoles.

              Class Rank
              Class rank will not be reported; however, it will be calculated based on weighted GPA and kept confidential
              for colleges and scholarship applications that require it.

              Pam Rowe seconded.

VOTE PRO:     Tim Hagen (Chair), Jaime Barr, Melissa Ford, Beth Groeber, Al Littlefield, Pam Rowe

VOTE CON: Jill Carini                                                                           MOTION CARRIED 6:1

            2. Discussion/action re: Proposed Full-Day Kindergarten Study Committee Presentation
               On behalf of the Full-Day Kindergarten Study Committee, Assistant Superintendent Brian Reas reviewed the
               committee’s charge, and presented its findings and recommendation regarding full-day Kindergarten
               programming in East Lyme Public Schools. Based on community feedback, review of research, the district’s
               current Kindergarten experience, and visitations to other districts, the committee recommended the
               implementation of a full-day Kindergarten program starting in the school year 2013-2014 with an option for
               one half-day section. Mr. Reas and committee members responded to questions regarding educational
               benefits, transitioning, optional programs, academic equity, and costs associated with the program.
               Dr. Lombardo asked the Board to authorize the Superintendent to develop a timeline and fiscal plan to
               implement a full-day Kindergarten program starting in the school year 2013-2014 with an option for one half-
               day section. Dr Lombardo will update the Board early next fall. The Board thanked the committee for their
               work.

MOTION:       Jill Carini moved to authorize the Superintendent to develop a timeline and fiscal plan to implement a full-day
              Kindergarten program starting in the school year 2013-2014 with an option for one half-day section as
              presented: Al Littlefield seconded.

VOTE:   UNANIMOUS                                                                               MOTION CARRIED
