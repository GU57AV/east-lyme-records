III. DISCUSSION/ACTION

1.

Discussion re: Initial Presentation of the Proposed ELPS Budget for 2012-2013

Dr. Lombardo presented the proposed budget for 2012-2013. Budget priorities align with
district goals. The initial budget represents a 3.8% increase over the adopted budget for the
current year. Salary and benefit changes represent 3.2 of the 3.8% budget increase. The
reduction of 4.0 teachers is due to a decline in enrollment and is expected to be absorbed
through retirements. Some reallocation of funding is necessary to cover staffing positions
funded by a two-year federal jobs grant, which will not be available next year. Major
increases include salaries, custodial overtime, and unemployment. The increase in
supplemental salaries is due to under budgeting in 2011-2012. Cost control and program
modifications include reduction in special education tuition due to program changes,
reduction in fuel costs due to natural gas conversion at HA, MS and HS, reduction in projects
to reflect shift to service contracts and 2011-2012 work, and reduction in legal fees. Dr.
Lombardo noted the approximate 50% cost savings in special education tuition since 2008-
2009, primarily due to creating programs and bringing students back to the district.
Initiatives deferred include full day kindergarten pilot, new World Languages, and the
development of funds/resources for the Capstone Project. Budget discussion will be held on
January 23, 30, and February 6, with possible adoption of the budget on February 13, but no
later than February 27. A public hearing will be held on January 30. The PowerPoint
presentation is available on the district website. Questions were recorded and will be
responded to in written form. Dr. Lombardo encouraged Board members to submit further
budget questions by email.
