 VII.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
        A. Instruction
           1. Discussion re: Channel 3 Cool Schools Video Presentation
           Dr. Lombardo congratulated Dr. Miko (Principal) and Lillie B. Haynes School for being chosen as a Channel 3
           Cool School. The Channel 3 Cool Schools video, highlighting Lillie B. Haynes School, was played for the
           Board of Education.
        B. Finance/Facilities/Community
           1. Discussion/action re: Adoption of Board of Education Budget for 2012-2013
              Dr. Lombardo reviewed a list of potential reductions that would bring the original proposed 2012-2013
              budget, which included a 3.82% increase, down to a 2.69% increase over the current year. These possible
              reductions included health insurance revised rates ($177,000), workers compensation lower rates ($100,000),
              part-time and over-time for maintenance ($100,000), maintenance projects ($30,000), paraprofessional raise
              reduction ($17,000), 2.0 FTE paraprofessional reduction ($30,000), technology hardware ($40,000), and per
              pupil expenditures ($50,000). This proposal also included an increase of $60,000 to reinstate one teaching
              position due to a possible increase in kindergarten enrollment and $27,625 to cover tuitions payments for five
              Marine Magnet School students. Concerns were raised regarding the reduction of paraprofessional salaries,
              per pupil expenditures and a further reduction of $15,000 in oil and gas expenditures. Additional pros and
              cons were discussed by the Board including Pay for Pay, PTA subsidies and special revenue items. The
              Board would like clarification on a $90,000 increase in the clerical line item and heating oil expenditures.
