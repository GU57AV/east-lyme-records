   I. CALL TO ORDER– 7:06 p.m.

   II. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE – Led by Board of Education members

  III. DISCUSSION/ACTION
       1. Discussion/action re: Draft Budget for ELPS for 2012-2013
          Budget discussion continued. Dr. Lombardo responded to questions regarding Coastal
          Connections staffing, contingency funding, pay for play and grant opportunities. Pay for play
          was discussed; however, Board members were mindful that parents currently pay fees in
          many areas. Revenue accounts were seen as an area to review. The Board noted the
          importance of bringing forward a budget responsive to difficult economic times, while
          continuing to provide quality education for our children. Based on updated information, Dr.
          Lombardo presented additional reductions in the amount of $200,000, bringing the budget
          increase to 3.3% over the current budget, but noted that further reductions would most likely
          be in positions. Other areas of possible savings may be health insurance, presently budgeted
          at 5% increase, and employee groups that have not negotiated salaries for next year. Tim
          Hagen reminded the Board that the average budget increase over the last four years was
          1.69%. Budget questions and comments were encouraged and will continue to be posted to
          the district website. Further budget discussion and possible adoption is scheduled for
          Monday, February 13, 2012.
