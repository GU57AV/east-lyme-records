               The Board of Education presented the 2012-2013 adopted budget to the Board of Finance and the Board of

               Finance is expected to deliberate the BOE budget on April 11.

        B. Instruction
           1. Update re: Update on ELPS Full Day Kindergarten Community Forum
              Assistant Superintendent Brian Reas reported that approximately 89 persons were in attendance at the ELPS
              Full Day Kindergarten Community Forum on Tuesday, March 27, 2012, 6:30 – 8:30 p.m. in the Gerald M.
              Mistretta Commons, East Lyme High School. Small group discussion followed the presentation with large
              support in favor of full day Kindergarten. Information collected will be discussed by the committee in April.
              The Full Day Kindergarten Study Committee expects to present its recommendation to the full Board in May.
