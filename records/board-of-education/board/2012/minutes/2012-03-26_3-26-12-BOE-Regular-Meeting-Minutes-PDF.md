   VII. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
        A. Instruction
           1. Discussion/action re: Proposed East Lyme School District Calendar for 2012-2013
              On March 12, 2012, the Board asked Dr. Lombardo to resubmit an adjusted 2012-2013 school calendar that
              reflects a shortened February break. Two options were presented to the Board: 1) a calendar with a week
              long February break; and 2) a calendar with an abbreviated February break mirroring the 2012-2013 Regional
              Calendar.

MOTION:         Jill Carini moved to approve option 2 of the proposed draft #2 3-13-12 East Lyme School District Calendar for
                2012 – 2013 as presented (an abbreviated February break mirroring the 2012-2013 Regional Calendar): Pam
                Rowe seconded.

                Discussion ensued regarding sensitivity to the ELPS Calendar Committee’s recommendation and the ELTA
                poll results in favor of keeping the full February break. Half-day professional development day concerns will
                be addressed during contract negotiations next year.

VOTE PRO: Rich Steel (Secretary), Jill Carini, Laura Greenstein, Al Littlefield, Pam Rowe, Christopher Sandford

           3. Discussion re: Update on ELPS Facilities Vision Committee Work
              Dr. Lombardo reviewed a PowerPoint presentation made to the FFO Committee on March 12 regarding the
              progress of the ELPS Facilities Vision Committee’s work. Committee work has included the identification of
              desired architectural features of future renovation or construction, the development of plans to solicit input
              from parents, students, staff and community, and the identification of options for renovation and the
              advantages and economic impact of these options. Next steps are as follows: 1) conduct forums and surveys
              in May; 2) complete analysis of various renovation/construction options; and 3) analyze all input and
              complete report to the Board of Education in October or November 2012.
