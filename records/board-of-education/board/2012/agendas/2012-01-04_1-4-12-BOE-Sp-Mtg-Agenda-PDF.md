EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION
East Lyme, Connecticut

Special Meeting Minutes
Wednesday, January 4, 2012
4:00 p.m.
Superintendent’s Office, Central Office

Board Members Present: Tim Hagen (Chair), Rich Steel (Secretary), Jaime Barr, Jill Carini, Melissa Ford,
Laura Greenstein, Al Littlefield, Pam Rowe, Chris Sandford

Board Members Absent: Jaime Barr, Marlene Nickerson

Administrators Present: Dr. James Lombardo (Superintendent), Brian Reas (Assistant Superintendent), Don
Meltabarger (Business/Facilities Director), Michael Susi (HS Principal)

Others Present: Steve Hargis (ELHS Athletic Director), Carl Reichard (HS Coach and Teacher), Mike
Giannattasio (Public Works Director), Bill Scheer Jr. (Town Engineer)

I. CALL TO ORDER - 4:05 p.m.
Il. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE - Led by Tim Hagen

UI. PRESENTATION/DISCUSSION ITEMS
1. Presentation for Discussion re: Proposed High School Athletic Complex Project
Dr. Lombardo provided background information and an overview of the proposed ELHS Athletic
Complex Project. A tour of the athletic facility was conducted by Carl Reichard and Steve Hargis.

The Board is seeking a $2.3 million bond for the purpose of making necessary repairs and renovations
to the ELHS Athletic Complex.

IV. PUBLIC COMMENT -— None
Vv. ADJOURNMENT
MOTION: Al Littlefield moved to adjourn the meeting at 5:25 p.m.; Pam Rowe seconded.

VOTE: UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED

Respectfully submitted,
Kishard Steel

Richard Steel, Secretary
East Lyme Board of Education
