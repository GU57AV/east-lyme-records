1. OPENING OF MEETING
Procedural: A. Call to Order
Chair Tim Hagen called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.
Procedural: B. Pledge of Allegiance
Led by Chair Tim Hagen.
Recognition for Board of Education Members
Superintendent Newton presented the Board of Education members with cards from our three elementary schools for board appreciation month.
Recognition for ALLI Group
Jennifer Brush and the ALLI group from East Lyme High School presented a PSA on gambling. The club has been working with the youth coalition to promote healthy choices. They were at the PAWS convention at Manchester Community College recently to present.
Procedural: C. Public Comment
1. Dave Putnam, East Lyme Parks and Recreation Director, congratulated the Youth Coalition. He also reported that the Miracle League will have a spring installation in May of the Kevin’s Kourt. They also received a grant for $25,000 and they are currently nearing $400,000 for the Miracle League. They have numerous fundraisers coming up.
2. Korie Witcraft, 17 Heritage Rd, PTA FL, parent and PTA President who spoke regarding the communication between schools with redistricting and loss of programs (constellations, Nature's Classroom, winter concerts) that are so valuable to students. She feels that parents and students have lots of questions with the transitions to come and looking for help to assure that there is a successful transition.
3. Anthony Graesch, 51 Grouse Circle, parent who spoke regarding the specials that the students currently receive and the upcoming budget reductions of two music teachers from 1.0 to 0.8 FTE. He believes that making this reduction will make it a less desirable position which may result in the loss of valuable staff. He also spoke about how the arts are a core part of our students learning and these teachers could be used to integrate other new programs.
4. Rebecca Karun, 3 Aberdeen Court, parent of students at Flanders. She said her life was changed by special area teachers' and they set her up for success. She spoke of how her own children's highlight of the day is in one of their daily specials classes. She knows that budgetary cuts of special area teachers will be felt by each child. She feels this was never discussed in original plans of redistricting and feels the Board of Education should look elsewhere for cost saving.
5. Melissa Wallace, 34 Grouse Circle, parent of students at Flanders, she attended redistricting meeting about a year ago and was told changes would not affect loss of programs.
6. Scott Eident -4 Esther Pond Lane, parent of students at Flanders, he has concerns regarding the loss of programs. He believes that Nature's Classroom promotes good climate among the student body. As an educator in another district he attended Nature's classroom for a week and feels it is very valuable and aligns with the NGSS standards.

5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS -FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion: A. Discussion: School Project Update and Discussion
Superintendent Newton gave an update on the Elementary School Alteration Project.
-The remaining windows at Flanders have been ordered and the cost associated is less than the approved amount. Projects to end in August. Flanders roof project is in process at the state level. In May it should go out to bid with completion this summer. The Flanders roof was approved with entire school projects. This year (2019) it becomes 20 years old. -Technology for the projects has had an increase in cost due to Custom Computers having to move and re-hook servers. It has been very time consuming and costly and we are working with the building committee as it has cost $120,000.
-We have started Phase 3 moves at each school:
1: Flanders is underway with office and teachers.
2: Lillie B. Haynes classrooms and teachers are rearing to move.
3: Niantic Center School office will move on April 22, 2019.
Discussion: B. Discussion: 2019/2020 Board of Education Adopted Budget
Superintendent Newton reported that the Board of Finance budget presentation took place on March 18, 2019. We are currently working on the questions that the Board of Finance have asked. We will keep communication between all the members open. The Board of Education feels we are being fiscally responsible and hoping that we have increases Board of Finance representation at the budget workshops next year which will alleviate many of these questions. The enrollment is up 18 students since Oct 1.
-Tim Hagen commented that this was the best presentation given to the Board of Finance.
-Barbara Senges feels we should include the pie graph on fixed budgets. This will be helpful because 90% is fixed cost that we can't control.
6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
Discussion: A. Discussion: Proposed ELHS Graduation Date and Last Day of School
Superintendent Newton reported that Flanders and East Lyme High School need to make-up 3 days and other the schools need 2 days.
Suggestion:
-High School Seniors would complete 180 school days and they would come to school on May 24, 2019 for a 1/2 day for graduation rehearsal which will allow them to graduate on Friday, June 14, 2019.
-All other students would have their last day of school on June 18, 2019.
