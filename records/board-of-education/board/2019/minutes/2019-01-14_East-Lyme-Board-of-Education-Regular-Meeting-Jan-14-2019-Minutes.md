5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Presentation:
A. Presentation/Discussion: Progress Review of Niantic Center School Improvement Plan for 2018/2019
NCS Principal Jeffrey Provost walked the Board through the action steps being taken to address the 2018-2019 School Improvement Plan (SIP) (see SIP document attached to the BOE agenda). He noted that additional Math Consultant time is an important need. NCS Teachers
Kristine Dishaw, Kim Hill, Julie Horner, Christine Ollhoff, Michelle Palumbo, Amanda Roy and Diane Swan
were present to assist with the presentation and to answer questions. Principal Provost thanked the Board for the additional Social Worker and School Psychologist time this school year and noted NCS now has support staff coverage five days per week. He also noted how students have benefited from this increase. NCS now has support staff coverage for five days per week. Chair Tim Hagen thanked the NCS staff for their work and dedication.
Discussion:
B. Discussion: Superintendent's Proposed ELPS Budget for 2019-2020
Chair Tim Hagen led the budget discussion. Concerns were voiced regarding anticipated ECS numbers.
Presentation Suggestions for BOE Public Hearing:
-Articulate the reasons behind the increases as we go forward and show how we got to this point
-Identify important long-term needs
-Note the districts response to enrollment shifts
-Include ongoing things we are working on to provide more efficiencies in future budgets
Chair Tim Hagen and Superintendent Newton will continue to meet to finalize the budget presentation for the BOE Public Hearing scheduled for Monday, January 28, 2019 at 7:00 p.m.
Chair Tim Hagen thanked the community for investing in the elementary school alteration projects and noted savings will be coming in from efficiencies.
Discussion:
C. Discussion/Action: Proposed 2018-2019 ELPS Budget Transfers in the Amounts of $14,524.00 and $251,150.00
FFO Chair Eric Bauman discussed the reasoning behind the proposed budget transfers (see document attached to the BOE agenda). We will be looking for ways to prepare for the health insurance overages.
Recommend approval of the proposed ELPS budget transfers in the amounts of $14,524.00 and $251,150.00 as presented to the Board by Maryanna Stevens.

Information:
A. Public Comment
Mike Schultz, Lovers Lane, East Lyme, stated it is obvious the Board of Education is on top of its game and thanked the members for their service. He said it appears employment contract for the Assistant Superintendent and Finance Director have not been included in budget and asked what the salary increase will be.  He expressed concerns that no one has come up with a solution on how we are going to cover the health insurance shortfall.  He said that $54.19 million dollars is a lot for taxpayers to swallow.  In his opinion, significant salary increases are included for this year.  He recommended the $1.6 million health insurance shortfall be passed along to those enrolled in the health care program and suggested the Board and Town freeze COLA and step changes for 2019/2020.
The Board took a short break.
