2. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE
Action, Discussion:
A. Discussion/Action: Proposed Adoption of ELPS Budget for FY 2019-2020
Chair Tim Hagen polled Board members for their comments regarding the proposed budget:
-Administration has worked very hard to get the budget as low as they possibly could.
-Important items in line with the 5-year Strategic Plan have been eliminated in an effort to keep the budget down.
-US News and World Report – East Lyme is listed as one of the top 25 high schools in CT.
-EL taxpayers are getting a premier school district for less money than surrounding towns.
-Appreciate collaborative discussions with Administration and Board of Finance members during the budget preparation process.
-Concern regarding the number of staff positions we will not be filling.
-Must keep the addition of a math consultant front and center, concern it was eliminated.
-Huge compromise in instruction if we keep things as status quo.
-Imperative that we fix what is broken in technology.
-Must emphasize the compromises and tough decisions we have made to Strategic Plan.
-Important to show in presentation where cuts were made and at what stage of budget process.
-Great job getting to this number, collaborative effort. Appreciate having BOF members at the BOE budget workshops to show work being done.
-Start thinking about what’s next to drive Strategic Plan forward over the next few years and what savings can be found.
-Appreciate step-by-step, line-by-line, budget review and how it came to be.
Move to adopt the Superintendent's Proposed 2019/2020 ELPS Budget in the amount of $49,497,250, a 3.18% increase over the current year's budget as presented.
Motion by Barbara Senges, second Eric Bauman.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Jaime Barr Shelburn (Acting Chair), Candice Carlson (Acting Secretary), Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, John Kleinhans, Barbara Senges, Ryan Shrader
