Action, Discussion: A. Discussion/Action: Proposed 2019/2020 Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement-Healthy Food Option
Motion:
Moved that pursuant to C.G.S. Section 10-215f, the board of education or governing authority certifies that all food items offered for sale to students in the schools under its jurisdiction, and not exempted from the Connecticut Nutrition Standards published by the Connecticut State Department of Education, will comply
with the Connecticut Nutrition Standards during the period of July 1, 2019 through June 30, 2020. This certification shall include all food offered for sale to students separately from reimbursable meals at all times and from all sources, including but not limited to, school stores, vending machines, school cafeterias, and any fundraising activities on school premises sponsored by the school or by non-school organizations and groups.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Eric Bauman.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, John Kleinhans, Barbara Senges, Ryan Shrader
Action, Discussion: B. Discussion/Action: Proposed 2019/2020 Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement-Exemption for Food Items
Motion:
Moved that the board of education or governing authority will allow the sale to students of food items that do not meet the Connecticut Nutrition Standards provided that the following conditions are met: 1) the sale is in connection with an event occurring after the end of the regular school day or on the weekend; 2) the sale is at the location of the event; and 3) the food items are not sold from a vending machine or school store. An “event” is an occurrence that involves more than just a regularly scheduled practice, meeting, or extracurricular activity. For example, soccer games, school plays, and interscholastic debates are events but soccer practices, play rehearsals, and debate team meetings are not. The “regular school day” is the period from midnight before to 30 minutes after the end of the official school day. “Location” means where the event is being held.
Motion by Ryan Shrader, second by Jaime Barr Shelburn.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, John Kleinhans, Barbara Senges, Ryan Shrader
Action, Discussion: C. Discussion/Action: Proposed 2019/2020 Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement-Beverage Exemptions
Motion:
Moved that the board of education will allow the sale to students of beverages not listed in Section 10-221q of the Connecticut General Statutes provided that the following conditions are met: 1) the sale is in connection with an event occurring after the end of the regular school day or on the weekend; 2) the sale is at the location of the event; and 3) the beverages are not sold from a vending machine or school store. An “event” is an occurrence that involves more than just a regularly scheduled practice, meeting or extracurricular activity. The “school day” is the period from midnight before to 30 minutes after the end of the official school day. “Location” means where the event is being held, and must be the same place as the beverage sales.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Candice Carlson.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, John Kleinhans, Barbara Senges, Ryan Shrader

Superintendent Newton gave update regarding Board of Education budget for 2019/2020 and will work with administration and the Board of Education after town referendum on Thursday, May 23, 2019. He identified the five key areas that are untouchable are security/safety, technology, Special Education costs, certified staff retirements, health insurance increases, and Coastal Connections. Superintendent also identified possible budget reductions are late bus, Global Language program, Pay to Play-HS, Library Assistants, Secretarial Staff, Certified staff, Para Educators, Park and Rec Facility use, out-of-district bussing, health insurance waiver, other revenues, North Wing Space at LBH, cut or realign sports/activities. and possible Kindergarten teacher.
