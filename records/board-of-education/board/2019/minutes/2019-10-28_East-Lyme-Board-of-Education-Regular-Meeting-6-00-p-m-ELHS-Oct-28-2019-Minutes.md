5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Miracle League Update
David Putman, Director of East Lyme Parks and Recreation, updated the Board of Education on the Miracle League SECT Project. The Miracle League raised $16,000.00 towards the Miracle Field project during the Oyster Festival fundraiser held on Columbus Day.  To date, the Miracle League of SECT has raised over $500,000.00 for the project.  The project will go out to bid in November with hopes of construction beginning in the spring of 2020.
Discussion, Information: B. Vaping Concerns in School
East Lyme High School Principal, Michael Susi, reported on student vaping at the high school.  Every school district is struggling with vaping.  The devises are small, odorless, and is not easy to detect.  There are discipline consequences if a student is caught vaping at school, but educating students is the best preventative.  The 0.5 FTE Substance Abuse Counselor is very effective and meets regularly to educate students.   Students are also educated on vaping during Freshman Academy and Advisory Programs.  Mr. Susi thanked the Board of Education for supporting the 0.5 FTE Substance Abuse Counselor at the high school.  East Lyme Middle School Principal, Jason Bitgood, noted that students in grades 5-8 receive education on vaping during the DARE program and in Health/PE and Life Skills classes.
Board of Education Member John Kleinhans suggested that the Board of Education write a letter to the Connecticut State Legislature in support of a state-wide ban on vaping.
Assistant Superintendent Amy Drowne reported that the topic of vaping is embedded in the curriculum for grades 2, 5 and 8 and that parents should also be provided with educational resources. Parents should be notified of upcoming curriculum programming concerning vaping and should have the option to opt-out their child from the discussion if they feel it is necessary to do so.
Discussion, Information: C. Elementary School Projects
Superintendent Jeffrey Newton met with the Town Finance Director and is working to have O & G Construction release the remaining allowances.  The elementary Principals continue to update their punch lists.  The next Board of Finance Meeting is scheduled for November 13, 2019.
Discussion, Information: D. Exterior of Niantic Center School
Superintendent Jeffrey Newton reported that there has been discussion within the community regarding the removal of the fish sculptures on the exterior of Niantic Center School.  Due to their age, the fish sculptures crumbled upon removal during the building renovation.  Per the Town Fire Marshall, the exterior of the building cannot be covered with future sculptures.  The Board of Education commented that any future building planning should be equitable among the three elementary schools and should be determined by the Board of Education.
