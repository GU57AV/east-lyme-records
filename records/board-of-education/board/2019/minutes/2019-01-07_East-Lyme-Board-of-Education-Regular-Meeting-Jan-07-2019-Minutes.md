6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion:
A. Discussion: Presentation of Superintendent's Proposed ELPS Budget for 2019-2020
Superintendent Newton presented the proposed ELPS Budget for 2019/2020 of $49,497,250 representing a 3.18% increase over the present year.  He expressed frustration over increased costs not under his control (ie. contractual obligations, rising insurance costs) as well as presenting a budget that includes no new initiatives to increase student achievement.
Reductions include
:
7.0 FTE Certified teachers (3.0 FTE Elementary as a result of redistricting, 1.0 FTE Middle School and 3.0 FTE High School), absorbed through retirements
1.0 FTE Director of Technology (Technology Reorganization)
3.0 FTE Paraprofessionals (Redistricting)
Staffing Request
:
-1.0 FTE Certified Occupational Therapy Assistant (COTA) - $55,000
Need based on caseload numbers (Preschool/LBH)
Enrollment
Elementary - Near-term growth, stagnation in the mid 2020s, followed by strong growth in 2025-2026 and 2026-2027, Projected net change over from 2017-2018 to 2027-2018 projected between 19.7% to 36.9% (Milone & MacBroom)
Middle School - Continuation of Grade 5-8 enrollment decline to 2020-2021, followed by varying rates of recover to 2024-2025, Projected net increase from 2017-2018 to 2027-2028 projected between 4.7% to 16.2% (Milone & MacBroom)
High School - Declines through 2026-2027 before rising for a slight net gain (1.3% to 5.9%) by 2027-2028 (Milone and MacBroom)
Projected 8-Year PK-13 student enrollment from 2019 - 2027 shows steady increase from 2817 to 3237 students district wide (Milone and MacBroom)
Special Education - Director of Student Services Kim Davis
-Numbers on the rise
-More students with intensive needs
Major Budget Drivers Include
Salaries/Security
Health Insurance Increase
Special Education Tuition and Transportation Costs
Certified Retirements (appropriate budgeting correction)
District Technology Costs (Custom Computers extend 10 to 12 month contract)
Superintendent's Recap
-Frustrating budget
-No new initiatives
-Additional cuts will be extremely impactful
Board Comments
Stress the sacrifice we are making to be fiscally responsible to the Town
Concerns regarding no new initiatives and nothing in budget to increase student achievement
Technology increase is essential
Further budget discussion will take place at the next BOE Regular Meeting on Monday, 1/14/19, 7:00 p.m., B101, ELHS
