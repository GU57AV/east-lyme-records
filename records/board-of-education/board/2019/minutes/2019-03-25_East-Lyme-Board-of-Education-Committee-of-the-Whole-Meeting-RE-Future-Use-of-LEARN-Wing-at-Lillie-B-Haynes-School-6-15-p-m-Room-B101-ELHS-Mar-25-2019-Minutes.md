2. DISCUSSION
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion Re: Future Use of LEARN Wing at Lillie B. Haynes School
Superintendent Newton started the discussion regarding future use of the North Wing at Lillie B. Haynes School. He gave the Board of Education members maps of the Lillie B. Haynes School that were highlighted according to the three classrooms that are being currently used for Creative Playschool and the four classrooms that East Lyme Parks and Recreation would like to use.
East Lyme Parks and Recreation Director Dave Putnam spoke regarding his request for use of the classroom space. East Lyme Parks and Recreation would like to use classrooms for the  Regional Theatre program which includes students from all surrounding towns. He also spoke about expanding other Parks and Recreation programs for East Lyme students in town. He would need to use the space during day and evening.  They are currently in contract. Dave Putnam would like to utilize the space with possibility of contract beginning in October 2019.
Comments and questions:
-Principal Melissa DeLoreto would still like to keep a classroom as a "makers space room" where students can engage in technology and robotics.
-Chairman, Tim Hagen has suggested renting space for two years and then engage in yearly contracts.
-Board members had some questions regarding security as the main office closes at 4:00 P.M.
-How to determine costs at a minimal charge to cover custodial, depreciation, electricity, etc to go into operating budget?
-Suggestion made that the revenue made from the rental space to East Lyme Parks and Recreation being used to help pay for the turf field?
-Follow-up with FFO
