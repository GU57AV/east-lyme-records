6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS-INSTRUCTIONAL
Discussion, Information, Presentation: A. Discussion: Connecticut State Education Portal-EdSight
Ajit Gopalakrishnan, Chief Performance Officer from the Connecticut State Department of Education presented to the Board on EdSight. It is a public platform that shares overall data of the district/schools. Please see the attached PowerPoint Presentation attached.
-CT Report Cards Data is the key tool to see the overall picture of the district/school
-It also shows trends on enrollment, low income meals, % of student’s disabilities (IEP), English Language Learners, Chronic Absenteeism, Expulsion/Suspensions
-Ability to track Post-Secondary enrollment and progress
-Growth and Accountability Model
Discussion, Information, Presentation: B. Discussion: Project Oceanology Overview
Jim McCauley and Dr. Molly Jacobs presented an overview of Project Oceanology. Please see attached PowerPoint Presentation attached.
-Mission is nurture interest and inspire enthusiasm for science and our planet's marine environment.
-Curriculum Director gave insight on the program and focused on how it is much more than a field trip but a field experience. She works closely with directors/teachers to align the program with units of instruction to meet the standards in each school.
-Discussed current contract costs and experiences associated with the cost.
Board members have concerns how these experiences will benefit most students across the board. It primarily has a focus on Middle and High School students.
