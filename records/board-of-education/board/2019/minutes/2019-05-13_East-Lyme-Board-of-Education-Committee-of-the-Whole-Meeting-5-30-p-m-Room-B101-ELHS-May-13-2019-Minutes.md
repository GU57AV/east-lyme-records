Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Budget Adjustments for 2019/2020
Superintendent Newton gave an update on enrollment for 2019/2020 school year and some possible budget reductions.
Chair Tim Hagen verified that all Board of Education members are agreed
that the five key areas that are untouchable are security/safety, technology, Special Education costs, certified staff retirements, and health insurance increases.
Discussion ensued by BOE members and Administrators of
possible budget reductions:
1) Late bus
2) Global Language program
3) Pay to Play sports/activities
4) Library Assistants
5) Secretary
6) Park and Rec facility use fee
7) Out of district bussing and partnering with other towns
8) Health insurance waiver for staff
9) Other revenues to include parking passes, sports admissions, SAT/ACT proctoring, print shop
10) Coastal Connections
11) North Wing space at Lillie B. Haynes rental space
12) Cut or realign sports/activities programs
13) Paraprofessionals
Board members are very aligned on finding ways that do not directly affect the students. Will reconvene for more discussion on the $250,000 budget reduction.
