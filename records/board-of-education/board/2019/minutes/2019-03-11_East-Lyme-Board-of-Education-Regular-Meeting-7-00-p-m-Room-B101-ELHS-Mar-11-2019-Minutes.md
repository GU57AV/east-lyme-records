DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information:
A. Discussion: Continued Discussion Regarding LEARN Rental Space at Lillie B. Haynes School
Superintendent Newton and HA Principal Melissa DeLoreto met earlier this week with EL Parks and Recreation Director Dave Putnam regarding the vacated classrooms in the LEARN wing at Lillie B. Haynes for possible use by the EL Regional Theater. Approximately seven classrooms will be vacant next year.
Discussion ensued:
-Future expansion of the revenue based Preschool program was briefly discussed and will be added as a future agenda topic following completion of elementary school projects and redistricting implementation
-A Board Member asked for a written update on the status of the Planetarium Committee's request for use at ELHS.
-Support EL Regional Theater program, but concerns regarding displacing them after a short period of time if space is needed by the school district.
Dave Putnam will be invited to attend the 3/25/19 FFO and BOE meetings to discuss his request.
Discussion, Information:
B. Discussion: Coastal Connections Update Regarding Space and Cost
Interim Facilities Director will provide an update to the Board on 3/25/19 regarding the upcoming Coastal Connections Program move to Central Office.
Discussion, Information:
C. Discussion: ELPS Five-Year Coherence Plan Update
Superintendent Newton asked the Board for input regarding ELPS Five-Year Coherence Plan.
-How do the social emotional programs fit into the 5-year plan, committee update
-Consistency throughout district-data teams, assessment, and presentation student cohort data
-Clearly articulate to the parents the common practices throughout schools
-Quantify the benefits from additional Social Workers hired this year
