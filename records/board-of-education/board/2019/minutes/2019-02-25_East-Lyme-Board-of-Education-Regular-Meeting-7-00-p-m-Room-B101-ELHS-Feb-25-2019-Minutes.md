5.
DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information, Presentation:
A. Presentation/Discussion: Progress Review of Flanders School Improvement Plan for 2018/2019
FL Principal Linda Anania and staff members Melissa Ewers, Caryn Novick, Tracy Burns, Steve Kirsch, Gary Wight, Amanda Magleby, and Michelle Sweatland reviewed the steps being taken to address the 2018/2019 School Improvement Plan (SIP) (see SIP document attached to the BOE agenda). They noted the positive impact goals are having on students, the importance of cohesive framework, and the values of Responsive Classroom. Examples were given as to how setting expectations empowers children, builds social awareness, and gives students coping strategies to help them to become better flexible social thinkers. The Board thanked the FL staff for their work and dedication.
Action, Discussion:
B. Discussion/Action: Proposed Rate Adjustments and Additions for ELPS Facilities Use Effective April 1, 2019
Committee Co-Chair Barbara Senges presented the FFO Committee's recommendation to approve the proposed rate adjustments and additions for ELPS facilities use effective April 1, 2019 as presented. Finance Director Maryanna Stevens was asked to prepare a list of rentals that have not been charged fees.
Moved to approve the proposed rate adjustments and additions for the use of ELPS facilities to include: 1.) Security hourly rate change, 2.) Addition of grounds/maintenance hourly rate charge; and 3). Addition of an hourly facility rental rate) effective April 1, 2019 as presented.
Motion by Barbara Senges, second by Jill Carini.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, John Kleinhans, Barbara Senges, Ryan Shrader
Discussion, Information:
D. Discussion: Future Use of LEARN Wing at Lillie B. Haynes School
Superintendent Newton has been contacted by EL Parks and Recreation Director Dave Putnam with a proposal for use of the LEARN wing at Lillie B. Haynes School for the East Lyme Regional Theater. Future preschool expansion was discussed with a possible one year rental of space at Lillie B. Haynes School for the 2019-2020 school year only. Supt Newton will gather more information and have further discussion with Dave Putnam.
