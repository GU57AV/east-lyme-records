Discussion: A. Discussion: Salem Food Service Update
Report by Food Service Director, Mr. Christian Urban:
Salem Public School’s started using the Point of Sale system last week and their snack program was launched this week.  Lunch participation in Salem is on the rise.
Discussion, Information: B. Coastal Connections Program Update
Superintendent Jeffrey Newton introduced Coastal Connections Assistant Principal, David Fasulo.
David Fasulo presented on Coastal Connections - 21
st
Century Learning which is a non-traditional satellite program based after Big Picture Learning and is housed at Central Office.
David Fasulo thanked the numerous organizations and companies that provide internships for Coastal Connection students and thanked the Board of Education and the East Lyme Community for their support of the program.
BOE Comment:
Eric Bauman asked for enrollment numbers. (Current enrollment is 16, including one out-of-district placement, with a maximum of 26 potential students.)
Eric Bauman asked if Coastal Connections students could partner with programs such as the Robotic Team.
Candice Carlson commended the program and suggested that Coastal Connections participate in the public high school offering night that is held at the middle school annually.
Jaime Barr Shelburn asked when Open House is scheduled for Coastal Connections.
Discussion, Information: C. Update on EEE and the Sports Ban
Superintendent Jeffrey Newton reported that there has been no change in afterschool outdoor activities and that the 5:30 ban is still in effect until the first hard freeze of the season.  Mr. Newton thanked the all teams and activities for accommodating their schedules to keep the students safe during the ban.
Discussion, Information: D. Elementary School Projects
Superintendent Jeffrey Newton reported on the following:
The next Building Committee Meeting is on October 17, 2019.
Some security cameras are not working at their full capacity.  Required switches and ports for the upgraded security were not included in the elementary building project.  This will be discussed at the upcoming Building Committee Meeting.
The new shrubs are being cared for and watered during the recent dry spell.  There is a one-year warranty on all new shrubs.
BOE Comment:
Candice Carlson praised the ribbon cutting ceremonies at each elementary school and was pleased that the ceremonies were focused on students.
Barbara Senges inquired on the status of Town funds being returned.  Mr. Newton reported that they are currently working on finalizing the remaining contingency list and are still expecting 500K to come back to the projects for use from excess bonding money.

Discussion/Action: A. Proposed East Lyme Board of Education Goals for 2019-2020 School Year
Dr. Timothy Hagen presented the draft Board of Education Goals for the 2019-2010 School year.  The emphasis of the goals are to monitor and support the Long-Range Coherence Plan.
BOE Comment:
Leigh Gianakos suggested clearly mapping out how each goal will be achieved.
Barbara Senges suggested establishing a Goal Sub-Committee and commented that the focus and success of goals are variable.  The Administration’s goal is to put steps in place to accomplish the goal while the Board of Education’s goal is to help the Administration in their effort
Eric Baumam would like to see bullets to highlight objectives for each goal.
Ryan Shrader stated the Board of Education needs to be articulate regarding their goals and that teachers and staff should be clearly informed of the goals and on-board with them.

Superintendent Jeffrey Newton reported on the following:
The Superintendents’ Goals includes improving parent communication.  A Twitter account will be used to funnel information to parents and the community.  This account will be controlled through the Superintendent’s Office.
