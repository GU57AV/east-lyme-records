Information, Presentation:
A. Presentation re: Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2019-2020
Superintendent Newton presented the proposed ELPS Budget for 2019-2020 (PowerPoint presentation is attached to the agenda). The $49,497,250 budget represents a 3.18% increase over the current year.
Superintendent Newton reviewed the budget process and the work that was done between November and January to bring the proposed budget down from 5.56% to 3.18%. Budget reductions include 7.0 FTE certified staff and 3.0 FTE paraprofessionals due to redistricting and enrollment fluctuation and a 1.0 FTE Director of Technology due to an administrative reorganization for a total savings of $613,552 to include salary and benefits. Additional cost avoidance measures were reviewed. Additional funding slated to go directly to the Town as a result of Educational Cost Sharing (ECS) and tuition increase represents +$791,104 over the current year.
Elementary enrollment is on the rise, while Middle School enrollment continues to decline. The High School is projected to experience a slight decrease in enrollment, but the total district enrollment is projected to continue on an upward trend through 2027.
Five key budget cost drivers include Security/Safety, Technology, Special Education, Certified Staff Retirements and Health Insurance Increase. Issues and solutions were outlined (see presentation). Without the 5 key issues, the 2019-2020 budget increase would be 0.75%.
