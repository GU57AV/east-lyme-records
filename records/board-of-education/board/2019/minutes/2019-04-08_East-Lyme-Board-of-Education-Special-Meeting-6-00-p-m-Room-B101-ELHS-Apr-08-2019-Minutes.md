5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS- INSTRUCTION
Action (Consent), Discussion: A. Discussion/Action: Proposed ELHS Graduation Date for Class of 2019 and the Last Day of School for 2018-2019 SY
-Superintendent Newton proposed the ELHS Graduation for Class of 2019 date for June 14, 2019 and the last day of school for all other students would be June 18, 2019.
-Tammy Bogue, parent of a graduating senior and a member of the Project Graduation committee shared that they have secured the venue for Tuesday, June 18, 2019 and the venue does not have availability on Friday, June 14, 2019.
The Board of Education and Superintendent will discuss options as necessary when they reconvene at the next scheduled meeting.
Discussion: B. Discussion: Elementary Redistricting, Special Area Teacher Instruction, and Elementary Programming Update
Superintendent Newton discussed redistricting and the dates/events at the three elementary schools.  There is a scheduled PTA President's meeting scheduled on Monday, April 22, 2019 to discuss other redistricting ideas. He will continue working with administrators on mapping out redistricting ideas that will not upset our students.
Some Board Members shared a few suggestions:
1. Skype with other classrooms
2. Mixers on Saturday
3. Step-Up day
3. Pen Pals
Assistant Superintendent Amy Drowne presented a PowerPoint on Elementary Programming Update and Special Area Instruction Update
-PowerPoint is attached to the April 8, 2019 Board of Education agenda.
Full Discussion ensued regarding Elementary Programming and Special Area Instruction which included:
1. Nature's Learning
2. Field Experiences and aligning the standards to East Lyme Public Schools Curriculum
3. District vs. PTA funded Field Experiences
4. Roadshow to the Learning Community for all 2019-2020 parents which will be a Q & A to Curriculum and Student Services.
Discussion: C. Discussion: School Project Update
Superintendent Newton and Tim Hagen provided update on school projects. They met with Ray O'Connor and Maryanna Stevens recently and honed in on the contingency budget and allowances remaining. There is a Town Building Meeting on Thursday, April 18, 2019.  Mr. Newton and Mrs. Stevens will be at the State Review Meeting for Flanders Roof on Tuesday, April 16, 2019.
