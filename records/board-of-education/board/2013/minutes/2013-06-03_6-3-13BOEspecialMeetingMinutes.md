I. CALL TO ORDER – The meeting was called to order at 7:08 p.m. by Chair Tim Hagen.

II. PUBLIC COMMENT - None

III. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
     A. Discussion re: BOE Self Evaluation and Review of Goals for 2012-2013
        The Board reviewed their goals for the current year, the progress they have made so far, and
        identified a few key spots of interest to continue working on or adding as goals for next year. They
        include educating the Board on Common Core State Standards, having more Board attendance at
        CABE functions preferably the November conference, enhancing the drug and alcohol curriculum,
        and setting up a timeline for Facility Vision Committee recommendations. The Board also took a
        self-evaluation that Tim will put together.

    B. Discussion re: The Path Forward
       The Board reviewed our mission statement and a history of how we came to be faced with our current
       facility needs/problems. The Board had rich discussion on what they would need to make a decision about
       the facilities and how those decisions would affect the students, staff, parents, and community members.
       A discussion about a timeline for these decisions was also discussed.

IV. ADJOURNMENT
