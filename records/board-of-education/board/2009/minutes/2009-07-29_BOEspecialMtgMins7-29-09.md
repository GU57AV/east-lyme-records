                                  Board of Education Special Meeting Minutes

         II. PUBLIC COMMENT – None

        III. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
             A. Discussion re: Interview Flanders School Principal Candidates (Executive Session)

                EXECUTIVE SESSION

Board Members Present: Kevin Seery, Elizabeth Groeber, Mary Broderick, Jaime Ellis, Melissa Ford,
                       Laura Greenstein, Al Littlefield, Marlene Nickerson, Richard Steel

      MOTION: Jaime Ellis moved to enter into executive session at 6:40 p.m. for the purpose of interviewing
              the Flanders School principal candidates to include Interim Superintendent Paul Freeman and
              Incoming Superintendent Dr. James Lombardo; Laura Greenstein seconded.

      VOTE:        Unanimous                                                          MOTION CARRIED

      The Board exited executive session at 10:27 p.m.

                 B. Discussion/action re: Proposed Appointment of Flanders School Principal

      MOTION: Al Littlefield moved to offer Linda Anania the position of Flanders School Principal
              effective August 1, 2009; Mary Broderick seconded.

      VOTE:        Unanimous                                                          MOTION CARRIED

         III.     PUBLIC COMMENT - None
