             4. Proposed contract extension with Futures Health Core, LLC, specifically Dr. Connie Berglund, to provide
                services as Interim Director of Special Services
                Dr. Connie Berglund served as Interim Director of Special Services from January through June 2009. The
                proposal would extend her contract beginning August 20, 2009 and ending approximately after up to 90 days
                of service have been provided. Dr. Lombardo outlined his proposed short and long term plan for
                administrative staff within the district and stated that further discussion and planning will take place with the

                believes that it is most likely all key positions will not be filled until next summer.

MOTION:       Tim Hagen moved to approve the proposed contract extension with Futures Health Core, LLC, specifically Dr.
              Connie Berglund, to provide services as Interim Director of Special Services beginning August 20, 2009 and
