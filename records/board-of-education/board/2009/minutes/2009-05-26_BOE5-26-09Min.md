   VII. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
        A. Finance/Facilities/Community
           1. Discussion/action re: Proposed 2009-2010 Federal Special Education Grant Programs
              Dr. Paul Smotas presented the applications for 2009-2010 federal special education grants. Grants included
              the district special education grant and the preschool grant which totaled $492,595 and $12,251 respectively.
              Additional funds are expected from the stimulus plan.

MOTION:      Jaime Ellis moved to approve the applications for federal special education grants for the 2009-2010 fiscal year;
             Melissa Ford seconded.

VOTE:        Unanimous                                                                           MOTION CARRIED
            2. Discussion re: 2008-2009 Budget Update
               The Budget Subcommittee met prior to the Board meeting. Committee Chair Tim Hagen provided an update
               of the FY 09 budget status with an estimated balance of $195,000. It was noted that a savings of $300,000
               was realized as a result of unfilled positions, long-term substitutes and breakage fees from 29 new teachers
               being hired at the start of the school year. Currently, there is a projected deficit of -$13,031 in Special
               Education. Concern continues that the State of CT will reduce our excess cost reimbursement from 90% to
               75% given the State deficit. If that occurs, an additional deficit of -$135,000 is projected.

            3. Discussion re: 2009-2010 Budget
               The 2009-2010 Town Budget was adopted by an overwhelming margin. Adstaff met today to discuss staff
               adjustments and Dr. Smotas will consult with bargaining units this week before finalizing staffing. A
               concentrated effort is being made by the administration to provide speedy notification to all affected staff.
               As a result of the Futures Health Core evaluation, two school buses will be eliminated which will result in
               savings of $70,000. The Board will review and finalize the 2009/2010 Budget on June 8.

          C. Personnel
             1. Discussion/action re: Proposed Early Retirement Incentive Plan for ELPS Secretaries
                The 2009-2010 Budget reflects a reduction of four (4) secretarial/library staff. As an attempt to avoid an
                involuntary reduction in force, the Board of Education proposed an early retirement incentive plan to
                members of the secretarial bargaining unit and the Central Office confidential secretarial staff. Under this
                plan, secretaries would have the option of two (2) years of free, self-only medical insurance or a cash payout
                of $5,000 per year for two (2) years. Two (2) members of the secretarial bargaining unit applied for inclusion
                in the plan with a 2009-2010 approximate savings of $77,000. A middle school secretary, Rowena James,
                retired in January 2009 and will not be replaced.

MOTION:       Al Littlefield moved to approve the proposed ELPS Early Retirement Incentive Plan for two members of the
              secretarial bargaining unit, Carol Geluso and Linda Scacciaferro, effective June 30, 2009, as presented; Jaime
              Ellis seconded.
