   VII. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
        A. Finance/Facilities/Community
           1. Discussion re: Utilization of Stimulus Funding for the District
              Dr. Lombardo and Don Meltabarger provided a summary of how the stimulus funding was used this year. A
              total of $591,395 was received for the ARRA IDEA 611 grant for the award period 2/17/09 – 6/30/11. The
              majority of these funds were used for special education tuition for FY10 due to identification of additional
              special education students in the current fiscal year. The Special Education Coordinator salary and benefits
              for 2009-2010 will be fully funded by the grant for FY10, however will only be funded 50% for FY11. The
              FY10 ECS was funded 85.74% by the State of CT ($6,087,602) and 14.26% by the federal stimulus funds for
              a total of $1,013,009. Funding of the ECS grant for next year is unclear at this time due to the uncertainty of
              the State’s budget.

               2. Discussion re: Proposed HS Boiler Project
                  The Board of Selectmen proposed that the Board of Education ask the Board of Finance to appropriate
                  $10,000 to do an independent engineering study to determine whether the boiler replacement project should
                  be natural gas, dual fuel or repair the current boiler and make it a dual fuel boiler. Subsequently, the Board
                  of Finance denied the appropriation of $10,000 for the study. If East Lyme decides not to install the natural
                  gas boilers, the project would no longer qualify for the CL&P incentive of $90,493 and the no cost incentive
                  from Yankee Gas to install the gas line from RT 161 to the high school at an estimated savings of
                  $100,000+. Currently, only one of the three HS boilers is operational. Don Meltabarger will prepare a
                  presentation for the Board of Selectmen showing the rough cost to repair and maintain the boilers, buildings,
                  and stack as compared to the replacement of natural gas boilers. Upon positive action by the Board of
                  Selectmen, the request will be forwarded to the Board of Finance with a town meeting to follow. The bid
                  process will be follow town approval.

VIII.   ADMINISTRATION REPORTS
        A. Superintendent of Schools – Dr. James Lombardo
           1. H1N1 Virus District Update
                • Additional information is being sent home regarding the virus and our response to it.
                • To date, we are not experiencing the increase in absences as reported by some other districts.
                • Overall absenteeism average is 7% compared to 4% on a typical day.
                • Parents are encouraged to keep children home until they are fever free for over 24 hours.
                • Practice good hygiene.
           2. National ASCD Conference in Vancouver
               Dr. Lombardo’s recent visit included observations of several elementary and secondary schools, a visit to the
                Aboriginal Learning Center, discussions with educational leaders at the university, government and public
                school levels, and a day touring the Olympic venues.
               Themes that emerged during his visit included:
                • Leadership at all levels is supported by the public schools, particularly at the student level.
                • Positive behavioral strategies are emphasized at both the elementary and secondary levels.
              • Teacher professional development includes significant involvement and sharing of promising approaches
                  among teachers.
                • Special education services are provided to a much narrower proportion of the student population.
                • World language acquisition is emphasized at all grade levels.
           3. Later this week administrators will work to develop plans of action to follow through on the various
                activities required to fulfill our district goals and priorities for 2009-2010.
           4. We have completed the necessary cleaning of the alternative education building and various A wing
                classrooms that experienced mold growth earlier this year. Air testing has cleared these areas for use. Bids
                are being sought for the installation of dehumidifiers in affected spaces to ensure a more permanent solution
                to the problem.
