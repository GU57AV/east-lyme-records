     VII. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
          A. Finance/Facilities/Community
             1. Discussion re: 2009-2010 Budget Update
                Budget Subcommittee Chair Tim Hagen summarized the committee events over the past three weeks.
                Additional savings of $300,000 have been identified leaving a shortfall of $146,431 to reach the full
                reduction of $446,431 for the 2009/2010 Budget. Bus transportation is being assessed for possible
                reductions. As a result of Adstaff deliberations, the original reduction of 7 teaching positions has been
                redistributed to 4.0 FTE at the high school, 2.65 FTE at the middle school, and .35 - .5 at the elementary
                level. If further staffing reductions are necessary, Adstaff recommends reducing elementary specials in the
                following priority; 1.0 FTE Global Language, 1.0 Elementary Music, and 1.0 Kindergarten Specials. This
                was based upon an alternative of increasing elementary class sizes, however serious unease in reducing
                elementary school specials was expressed. The lower number of hours of instruction per year at the
                elementary level, as compared with our DRG and the State, was also of concern. It was clarified that
                although the adjustment will allow for more daily time in core curriculum courses, the proposed course
                reductions will not result in increases in our instructional hours. Board members stressed the importance of
                maintaining Global Language, Elementary Music and Kindergarten Specials for educating the whole child.
                Dr. Smotas raised concern for the 2010/2011 Budget. He noted that difficult choices will be imperative, and
                he emphasized the importance of engaging the community in preparation for the next budget. The Board
                discussed ways to ensure better involvement in the capital budget process to include early identification of
                district needs, better communication with the Town, and becoming more proactive. Dr. Smotas
                recommended the Board request a seat on the Capital Budget Committee. The Referendum Vote is scheduled
                for Friday, May 22, 2009.
              2. Discussion/action re: Renovation of Flanders and the High School Fields
                 Dr. Smotas and Kevin Seery will frame a letter to the staff clarifying the operating budget versus the capital
                 budget.
