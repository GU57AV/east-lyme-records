8. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
Discussion, Information: A. ELHS Enrollment/Course Offering Report
Principal Deb Kelly, School Counselor Lisa Belisle, Teachers Grant Place and Lori Singer, and Assistant Principal Henry Kydd shared a
presentation
on ELHS Course Enrollment and Offerings informed by the ELHS mission and the Vision of the Graduate, graduation requirements, career concentrations and the benefits, staffing and logistics behind course offerings and future planning.
BOE members praised the work around providing dynamic opportunities at ELHS. Discussion ensued on the best way to utilize ELHS staff members’ efforts to accommodate all students interests and needs at the high school.
Discussion, Information: B. Director of Student Services Report
Kimberly Davis shared updates in all areas of the Student Services department such as staffing new hires, transportation staffing and strategy, and school nurses. Important highlights include:
- Bridges Outreach program, work with elementary schools and 2 to 5 year-olds in the community outreach ensuring preschool experience is accessible if interested. Hosted a Backpack Drive in August and other community partnerships attended, the event was very successful.
- Creative Playschool, Integrated Preschool, Revenue programs are jointly participating with Kindergarten teachers on Play Based Learning Professional Development from EASTCONN.  This work entails integrating play with intention in support and development of executive function skills.
- State has officially changed the designation from English Language Learners to Multi-Language Learners. 108 students enrolled in East Lyme where English is not their first language. Language Line is available to all teachers, which is an interpretive tool to utilize for communication with families. The tool is available on demand without prior scheduling or permission.
- Physical therapist, Kate Holland, has been working with the PE department to make PE an inclusive environment with additional modifications and adaptive equipment, which has helped students be more engaged and enjoy Physical Education class.
- AIM is using Central Office two days a week to launch community outings as a home base.
- Therapeutic Learning Program moved from LBH to FLS and has been very successful in the transition.
- Two 5
th
grade special ed teachers are sharing their case load – each has a focus of reading and math instructional responsibilities
Discussion ensued between the Board and Kim Davis. Board members appreciate the hard work and inclusiveness to all.
