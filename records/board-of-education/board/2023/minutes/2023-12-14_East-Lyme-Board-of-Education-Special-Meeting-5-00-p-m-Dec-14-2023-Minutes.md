2. DISCUSSION/ACTION - OTHER
A. Discussion/Action: Proposed Appointment of Board of Education Members
Chair Bauman carried over the nomination of Vivek Purohit, made by Cate Steel, second by Jill Carini, from the December 4, 2023 meeting.
Chair Bauman requested any further nominations for the Board of Education member vacancies.
Jaime Barr Shelburn nominated Olivia Fairchild, second by Jill Carini.
Chair Bauman called for further nominations and closed nominations as no additional nominations were presented. Each nominee was invited to introduce themselves and share why they wanted to serve on the Board of Education.
Vivek Purohit
, thanked the Board for the opportunity and shared his background in pharmaceutical science and his qualifications that would contribute positively to the Board. As a parent of a recent East Lyme graduate, he believes he has a responsibility to serve the Board in return. Dr. Purohit shared he is a strategic thinker and team player and wants to focus on balancing budget needs and maintaining the exceptional programming at ELPS.
Olivia Fairchild
shared her background and educational experience growing up in Pennsylvania and graduating from Pennsylvania State University. In high school Mrs. Fairchild served as a student liaison at Board of Education meetings and has always been a passionate learner. Mrs. Fairchild is employed at Electric Boat and chose to reside in East Lyme because of the exceptional reputation of the school system and looks forward to serving the community through the Board of Education.
All Board of Education members asked questions and participated in comprehensive discussions with the candidates on their educational priorities; educational topics that excite or energize them; how to foster culture, climate and diversity, the boards’ role and the importance of sharing perspectives, and approaches to problem solving and working with others to find solutions for all.
Chair Bauman shared that Laura Greenstein resigned from the BOE on 12/9/23 and thanked her for her insights and keen understanding on education and shared she was valuable member of the Board of Education.
Chair Bauman explained the voting process and how to submit the ballot for each nominee.
