A. Discussion/Workshop: School Budget Planning for 2024-25 Fiscal Year
Superintendent Newton led the continued discussion on the budget building process and reviewed the preliminary budget
presentation
for FY 2024-25. A robust discussion between all board members occurred around factors contributing to the budget proposal to date, such as preliminary data on kindergarten enrollment, student services needs based on enrollment variants, state mandated reading program costs, insurance and benefits, retirements, and other various drivers that affect the preliminary proposed budget.
Board members shared topics that they would like to see addressed further in the Superintendent Budget Presentation on January 8, 2024.
