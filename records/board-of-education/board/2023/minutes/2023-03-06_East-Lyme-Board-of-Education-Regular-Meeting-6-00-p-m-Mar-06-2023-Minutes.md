5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion/Information: Update on the Pool Roof
Dr. Christian Lund, Director of Facilities, shared that bids for the pool roof repairs closed last Thursday, and no responses were received. Dr. Lund has reached out to contractors for additional information as to why they did not participate and will plan the next steps based on feedback received. Discussion ensued with the Board members and Administration regarding the status of the aquatic center facility and planning ahead for repairs.
Discussion, Information, Presentation: B. Discussion/Information: ELPS 2022/2023 Budget Deficit
Superintendent Newton shared that the budget deficit is down to $148,246.38, inclusive of the leftover LEARN funds. Consistent monitoring of the budget by the Director of Finance, due diligence by the departments and staff, going without necessary supplies and other items, and mild weather and lower utility costs have factored in the improvement in the deficit. Discussion ensued with the Board members and Administration regarding budget projections for the coming months.
Discussion, Information: C. Discussion/Information: Non-Lapsing Account
Mr. Bauman, Board of Education Chairman, reviewed that up to 2% of funds leftover at the end of each school year can be put into a non-lapsing account to utilize funds in the following school year. The Finance, Facilities and Operations (FFO) Committee reviewed and recommended the Board of Education members proceed with a non-lapsing account. The Board Members recommend that Superintendent Newton and Mrs. Stevens, the Director of Finance, investigate options with the Board attorney. Superintendent Newton will bring back an agreement as a Discussion/Action item at the March 27
th
Board of Education meeting. Discussion ensued with the Board members and Administration regarding limitations and options available with a non-lapsing account.
Discussion, Information: D. Discussion/Information: Student-Based Health Centers
Superintendent Newton shared that he has been in discussions with outside organizations and agencies to bring Student-Based Health Centers to the high school and middle school to support physical and mental health needs for students and families. Spaces at each building need to be identified for these centers. He continued that a grant application was submitted to the State of Connecticut last week for funding for this opportunity to support students and families. Discussion ensued among the Board members and Administration regarding previous efforts to bring Student-Based Health Centers to East Lyme Public Schools, possible timeline, and spaces under consideration.
6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTIONAL
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: East Lyme Board of Education Goals
Mr. Bauman, Board of Education Chairman and Superintendent Newton reviewed the Board of Education Goals:
The connections made through the Pathways Program has been positive and provided opportunities to the East Lyme High School students to get involved in career-based local businesses and trades.
Culture and Climate progression is the most challenging goal. Many factors are impacting culture, climate, and morale. Administration continues to explore various ways to improve culture and climate districtwide.
Assistant Superintendent Spaziano reviewed implementation, calibration, and utilization of high impact teaching strategies in the buildings with teachers and Administration.
Discussion ensued with Board members and Administration regarding marketing and showcasing East Lyme Public Schools’ offerings to the community through advertising and social media. This item will be added to the upcoming Finance, Facilities and Operations (FFO) Committee meeting agenda for further discussion.
The Board of Education will utilize planning goals for the 23-24 school year to help to provide a timeline to work towards achieving long-term goals.
