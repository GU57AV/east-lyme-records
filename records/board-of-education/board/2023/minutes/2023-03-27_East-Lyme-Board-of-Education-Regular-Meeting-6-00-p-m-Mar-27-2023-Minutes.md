Discussion, Information: A. Discussion/Action: Proposed Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement - Healthy Food Option
Motion to approve Proposed Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement – Healthy Food Option. Pursuant to C.G.S. Section 10-215f, the East Lyme Board of Education certifies that all food items offered for sale to students in the schools under its jurisdiction, and not exempted from the Connecticut Nutrition Standards published by the Connecticut State Department of Education, will comply with the Connecticut Nutrition Standards during the period of July 1, 2023, through June 30, 2024. This certification shall include all food offered for sale to students separately from reimbursable meals at all times and from all sources, including but not limited to school stores, vending machines, school cafeterias, culinary programs, and any fundraising activities on school premises sponsored by the school or non-school organizations and groups.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Candice Carlson
Pro: Eric Bauman (Chair), Jaime Barr Shelburn (Secretary), Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, and Catherine Steel.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Discussion, Information: B. Discussion/Action: Proposed Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement – Food and Beverage Exemptions
Motion to approve Proposed Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement – Food and Beverage Exemptions as Presented. The East Lyme Board of Education will allow the sale to students of food items that do not meet the Connecticut Nutrition Standards and beverages not listed in Section 10-221q of the Connecticut General Statutes provided that the following conditions are met: 1) the sale is in connection with an event occurring after the end of the regular school day or on the weekend; 2) the sale is at the location of the event; and 3) the food and beverage items are not sold from a vending machine or school store. An “event” is an occurrence that involves more than just a regularly scheduled practice, meeting, or extracurricular activity. For example, soccer games, school plays, and interscholastic debates are events but soccer practices, play rehearsals, and debate team meetings are not. The “regular school day” is the period from midnight before to 30 minutes after the end of the official school day. “Location” means where the event is being held and must be the same place as the food and beverage sales.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Candice Carlson
Pro: Eric Bauman (Chair), Jaime Barr Shelburn (Secretary), Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, and Catherine Steel.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Discussion, Information: C. Discussion/Presentation: Combined Heat and Power Proposal for ELMS
Dr. Christian Lund, Director of Facilities, introduced Mr. Adam Teff, General Manager of Titan Energy for a presentation to the Board of Education on a possible energy saving initiative. The presentation provided a detailed overview of the recommended installation of an energy saving combined heat and power unit at the Middle School. This equipment would combust natural gas to drive a turbine for electricity while the combustion heat is captured and used to help heat the building.  A Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with a twenty-year duration is being considered. In this model, ELPS would pay the gas utility for the natural gas, and the PPA contractor for the electricity produced. The overall cost of utilities is less expensive in the PPA model than the current process of buying the gas and electricity separately. The Board of Education recommended the Finance, Facilities and Operation (FFO) and Facility Asset Management Subcommittee discuss this proposal further and develop a recommendation whether to proceed further.
Numerous questions were posed, and discussion ensued among the Board Members, Christian Lund, and Adam Teff.
