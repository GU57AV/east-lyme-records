1. OPENING OF MEETING
Procedural:
A. Call to Order
Chairman Eric Bauman called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.
Procedural:
B. Pledge of Allegiance
Chairman, Eric Bauman led the pledge of allegiance.
2.DISCUSSION/ ACTION ITEMS -OTHER
A. Discussion/Action: Proposed Adoption of ELPS Budget for FY 2023/2024
Superintendent Newton and Chairman, Mr. Bauman reviewed the timeline and changes that had been made to the budget since its initial presentation on January 9, 2023. They asked for more Board discussions.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
Move to adopt the proposed 2023-2024 ELPS Budget in the amount of $58,914,852, an 8.8% increase over the current year's budget as presented.
Motion by William Derry, second by Jaime Barr Shelburn.
Final Resolution: Motion carried
Pro: Eric Bauman (Chair), Jaime Barr Shelburn (Secretary), Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, Laura Greenstein and Catherine Steel
B. Discussion: Next Steps for Budget Progression to Board of Selectmen and Board of Finance
The future dates to be aware of are March 13, March 28, and April 24, 2023. These are the dates the Board of Education will have discussions with the Boards of Selectmen and Finance.
