Procedural: E. Public Comment
Diane Swan, 7 North Beechwood Road, Niantic, President of STARS to STEM, Inc. Ms. Swan thanked the Board for the partnership with STARS to STEM and the ELHS Planetarium and is proud of the work of the Board of Directors, volunteers, and donors. Ms. Swan spoke of the fall programs popularity, the demand for additional shows and the partnership with regional organizations. She thanked all staff that contributed to accessibility for the planetarium. Ms. Swan submitted the minutes from the Planetarium Ad-Hoc Committee meeting on 10/24/23 and shared that she hopes the Board votes favorably for the continued partnership with STARS to STEM, Inc.
Jason Deeble, 5 Mackinnon Place, East Lyme, Math & Science Educator in Old Saybrook, spoke of favorable support for the planetarium and the agreement to continue the partnership with ELHS. Mr. Deeble shared his childhood interest in the stars and views the ELHS planetarium as a cultural resource with significant potential for the community.
Elise Piotrowski, 5 Barone Road, Niantic, spoke favorably of the STARS to STEM program and the partnership with the planetarium. Ms. Piotrowski shared she has two young daughters and attended several shows at the Planetarium and spoke of the positive impact of educational shows hosted by STARS to STEM, Inc.
Justin Daubar, 21 King James Drive, East Lyme, daughter East Lyme graduate. Mr. Daubar shared that his daughter took an astronomy course while attending ELHS and is now a PhD candidate and spoke of accomplishments and the planetarium’s influence had on her success.
2. SPECIAL RECOGNITION
A. ELMS – Grade 6 Student Author
Principal Claudine Kelly introduced Nadia Lira, grade 6 student author. Nadia began writing her book as a fourth-grade assignment. Her writing extended into a project that she wanted to continue after the school year was over. Her book “The Crystal Quest” has recently been published, she is working on the sequel and her long-term goal is to make it into a trilogy.
B. ELMS – Grade 8 Student Inventor
Principal Claudine Kelly introduced Sashrika (Sash) Das. Sash spoke about her experience competing in the worldwide contest Coolest Inventions. Sash shared one of her prototypes, that can detect gas leaks utilizing a multichannel sensor. The prototype is on exhibit in the Victoria & Albert Museum in London. Sash hopes to become a software engineer.

7. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – INSTRUCTIONAL
A. Lillie B. Haynes School Principal Report – Mr. Brian Kalkreuth
Principal Kalkreuth introduced Mrs. Rock, Literacy Consultant, Mrs. Abbott, first grade teacher, Mrs. Arnold, Math Coach and Mrs. Ellal, third grade teacher. The group presented their data-informed approach to literacy and math and their coaching cycle within the classroom. With this approach, teachers use feedback to strengthen student understanding based on assessments, develop routines that build strategies in students’ problem-solving skills, and improve classroom engagement. Students from Mrs. Abbott’s class participated in a demonstration of explicit instruction with vowel sounds.
Discussion ensued about staffing for coaches in all school buildings and how the investment has shown significant success for teachers and students.
