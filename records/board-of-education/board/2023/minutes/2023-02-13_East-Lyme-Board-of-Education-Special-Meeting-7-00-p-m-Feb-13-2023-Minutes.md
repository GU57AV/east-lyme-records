2. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - OTHER
Discussion:
A. Discussion: ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget for 2023/2024 FY
Superintendent Newton and Chairman, Mr. Bauman began discussions regarding the Superintendent’s proposed budget for the 2023/2024 FY that was presented on January 9, 2023.
Discussion ensued with the Board and Administration regarding the proposed budget for
the 2023
/2024 FY.  The Board discussed adding back the 18.5 FTE positions, while keeping the healthcare cost share at 12% and keeping facility projects in place. The Public Hearing is scheduled for February 27, 2023, at 6:00 p.m. with a Special Meeting for budget deliberations and
possible adoption
of the 2023/2024 budget.
3. PUBLIC COMMENT
Procedural: A. Public Comment
Timothy Dowd, 66 East Pattagansett Road, Niantic, is a parent and teacher at East Lyme High School who wanted to thank the Board for the support they are providing for our school community and the strength of our Coastal Connections program. Mr. Dowd emphasizes the importance of not downsizing and retaining our teachers as we have a variety of students with
different needs
. The school connections between staff and students are crucial if we want to maintain continuity.
Nicole Muscato, 7 Darrows Ridge, East Lyme, a parent who wanted to share her concerns with the Superintendent’s proposed budget. Mrs. Muscato moved to this district because of the great schools and sports/activities that were a part of the East Lyme Public School district. We need to look for other ways to make sure we do not have budget reductions of 18.5 FTE of staff and certain sports. It is crucial for our students and staff to maintain a positive morale and learning environment, especially following the pandemic. I urge you to move forward with a higher budget increase to the Board of Finance as it will be detrimental to our students.
