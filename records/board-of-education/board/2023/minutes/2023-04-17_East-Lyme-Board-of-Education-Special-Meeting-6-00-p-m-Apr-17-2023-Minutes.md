5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Update on the Miracle League Field Project - Dave Putnam
Executive Director Dave Putnam provided an update to the Board of Education members. The organization is expanding their programs. Phase one is completed with construction of the field. They are finalizing phase two for the construction of the concession, restroom, and storage building facility as well as a playground.
Discussion ensued with the Board of Education members, administration, and Mr. Putnam regarding work accomplished and next steps. Mr. Putnam will come back to the Board with the final plans for approval.
Discussion, Information: B. Discussion/Information: 2023/2024 Budget Update
Eric Bauman and Superintendent Newton summarized the proposed budget reductions to the adopted Board of Education 2023/2024 Budget based on the Board of Finance’s recommendations. Mr. Newton reviewed prospective areas of cost savings in addition to reductions in overall health insurance costs. Mr. Newton also reviewed projected class enrollment numbers for the 2023/2024 school year at the elementary level.
Questions were posed and discussion ensued with the Board of Education members and administration regarding the prospective budget reductions and class size projections.
Action, Discussion, Information: C. Discussion/Action: Non-Lapsing Account
Eric Bauman reviewed the Board’s consideration to approve transferring monies leftover at the end of a school year into a non-lapsing account in lieu of returning those funds back to the Town of East Lyme per the stipulations included under the “Use of Funds” description.
Motion by Leigh Gianakos to adopt the East Lyme Public Schools Non-Lapsing Unexpended Education Funds Account with the addition of the recommended adjustment. Second by Candice Carlson.
Pro: Eric Bauman, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, Laura Greenstein, and Cate Steel.
