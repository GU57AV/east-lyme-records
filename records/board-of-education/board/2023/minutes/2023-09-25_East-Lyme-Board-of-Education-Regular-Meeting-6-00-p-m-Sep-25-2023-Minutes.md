4. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
Discussion, Information, Presentation: A. Discussion/Information: Principal Report - Claudine Kelly, ELMS & Deb Roselli Kelly, ELHS
- East Lyme Middle School: Mrs. Claudine Kelly provided a report on the beginning of the school year and how BOE goals are integrated into the focus at ELMS. Mrs. Kelly spoke of fostering culture and climate for students and staff and utilizing restorative practices within the classroom. She also shared updates on the reconfigured school counselor suite, the learning lab, and peer mentor program.
- Mrs. Kelly also shared that 2 ELMS students have been invited to attend a local MLK Scholarship dinner in October.
- East Lyme High School: Mrs. Deb Kelly spoke about the beginning of the school year for ELHS including convocation, parent and student engagement and activities, educational opportunities such as dual credit classes and expanding Pathways programs, and collaborations with the Mystic Seaport to support therapeutic learning program students.
Questions were posed by the Board Members and discussion ensued.
Discussion, Information, Presentation: B. Discussion/Information: High School Student Report/Presentation
- Mingwen Duan, ELHS senior, was one of four students chosen to be a member in the United States team at the International Chemistry Olympiad this past July. The competition was held is Zurich, Switzerland. Mingwen shared his reflections on the experience that included over 90 countries participating.
5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Information, Reports: A. Discussion/Information: Facilities Update - Dr. Christian Lund
- Dr. Lund provided updates and timelines on facilities and operations across the district, including the arrival of the new tractor and anticipated arrival of the new plow truck in early December.
- The pool roof repair contract is posted online for bid, due 11/16/23, with an anticipated start of April 2024.
- Interviews for the part-time project manager position have been conducted, a candidate will be finalized soon.
- Dr. Lund will attend the next Board of Selectman meeting to discuss the MOU for the use of the former dispatch building.
- Dr. Lund shared probable future items on the CIP list such as replacing tennis courts. The roof at LBH has been scanned to check condition for leaks and is awaiting the results of that study.
