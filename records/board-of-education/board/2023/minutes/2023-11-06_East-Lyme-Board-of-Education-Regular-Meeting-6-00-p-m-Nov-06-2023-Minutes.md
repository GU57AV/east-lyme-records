7. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information: A. Food Servies Update – Mr. Christian Urban & Ms. Ashleigh Meek
Chris Urban & Ashleigh Meek provided updates for Food Services Department, such as kitchen equipment upgrades and new POS systems, heat lamps, and reach in coolers. The department shared interactive and engagement activities such as “Crunch Day”: all students received an apple and counted down to crunch at the same time to see how loud their crunch could be. They shared the work with farm-to-table initiatives, creating “Build your Own Meal” days with different themes for the high school, and supporting East Lyme sports teams by providing sandwiches and snacks for away games.
