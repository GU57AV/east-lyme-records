6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Action, Discussion, Information: A. Discussion/Action: Proposed Student Pricing for Food Services
Chris Urban, Director of Food Services, spoke to the updated state and USDA guidelines for public school food services programs. Governor Lamont has incorporated free breakfast for all Connecticut students for the 2023-24 school year into his budget, regardless of their eligibility. Lunch costs for all students that qualify for free and reduced meals will be covered by the state and federal allocations, which is approximately 19% of the ELPS population.
The prices have not been increased for ELPS students in over 10 years. The proposed price increases are approximately 25 cents at each building level, which would be revised to $3.00 at the elementary schools; $3.50 at the middle school; and $3.75 at the high school. This pricing is still below the average lunch cost compared to other school districts. This information update would be communicated to district families as soon as it is approved by the Board of Education.
Motion to approve to
increase the price for student lunches to $3.00 at the elementary schools, $3.50 at the middle school, and $3.75 at the high school in accordance with federal guidelines.
Motion Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Leigh Gianakos
Final Resolution:  Motion Carried
Pro: Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman,  Alisa Bradley, Candice Carlson, Jill Carini, William Derry, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, Laura Greenstein
