Ms. Steel initiated a discussion that the tennis courts at ELHS need repair. The courts are heavily used all day and there are cracks throughout the courts. The courts were last replaced over ten years ago. Superintendent Newton will review the space with Dr. Lund. Discussions ensued around conditions underneath the courts and the possibility of shared facilities between the Parks & Recreation department and the Board of Education regarding publicly used spaces on BOE locations. Further discussions will be held at the Committee of the Whole meetings.
