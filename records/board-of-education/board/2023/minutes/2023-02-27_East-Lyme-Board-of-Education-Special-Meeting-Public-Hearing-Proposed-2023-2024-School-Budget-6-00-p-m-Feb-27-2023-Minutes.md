2. DISCUSSION/ ACTION ITEMS - OTHER
A. Overview of the ELPS Proposed Budget for FY 2023/2024
Superintendent Newton and Chairman, Mr. Bauman reviewed the timeline and changes that had been made to the budget since its Initial presentation on January 9, 2023.
3. PUBLIC COMMENT
A. Public Comment Relative to the Proposed School District Budget for FY 2023-2024
Mike Shultz, Lovers Lane-We are all working hard to budget during these hard times . No one wants to see staff cuts as they are needed. We need to look at compensations and think about voluntary salary freezes for 2023-2024 for teachers and administration.
Christine Maxfield, 14 Greencliff Drive-Thank you for letting me a part of the discussions. I want to tell you today about my gratitude and for students, teachers, and community members. We have been working on SEL and restorative practices and with that in mind, I challenge you to choose humanity. Choose the human who Is with your children.
Fiona Samuelson, 18 Ledge Rd-ELPS does a great job hiring not only teachers but mentors. We are pushed to succeed, and I learned to develop a love of reading and writing because of our small class sizes. I can break out of my shell and do AP classes and The Saga. Our educators make us global citizens
Scott Mahon, ELTA President- I am here to express gratitude. We come from a place of excellence, and we are here to support you every step of the way.
Isabelle Georgian, 20 Bay View Rd-Our base line is not where It should be and that is why our % Increases are high. I am here as a parent, teacher, and a taxpayer. We need the basics to get our job done.
Matvey Sokolovsky, 26 White Birch Circle, I am a 10-year resident and every year my taxes go up 15%. This meeting Is a confrontation between teachers and the town. This is suffocating. The teachers deserve a lot of credit, but a lot of parents are dissatisfied with the schools. Every year it stays the same and nothing is ever done. The buildings are only used half of the day, why are they not used at night? We need to cut the superintendent and run independent schools. Norwich has a public and private collaboration. Old Lyme has robotics. We need more things. Jeff is falling us, and the Board of Education is encouraging it. We need to fire him and start again with all new people.
Ted Benjamin, 15 Fawn Run Road, What Is community? We belong and exist here to share and collaborate. We are fundamentally human. Budgets require formulas and structures and there is a need there to protect our community. I want to thank the Board of Education for doing the difficult job of figuring it out. We are a vast web of connections. As you do your job please continue to keep these 18.5 people connected. Thank you for thinking of our community. Together we thrive. Together we belong. You are here to protect our community. Every year is not the same. We are constantly adapting and changing.
