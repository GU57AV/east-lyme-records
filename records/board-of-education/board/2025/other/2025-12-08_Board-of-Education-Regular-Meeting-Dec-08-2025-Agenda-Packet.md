Policy: 9271
Section: 9000 Bylaws of the Board

Code of Ethics
An East Lyme Board of Education member should respect his/her relationships with
other members of the Board by:

     1. Recognizing that authority rests only with the Board in official meetings, and
     that the individual member has no legal power to bind the Board outside of such
     meetings.

     2. Recognizing the integrity of his/her predecessors and associates, and the merit
     of their work.

     3. Refusing to make statements or promises as to how he/she will vote on any
     matter which should come exclusively before the Board as a whole.

     4. Making decisions only after all the facts bearing on the question have been
     presented and discussed.

     5. Respecting the opinion of others and by graciously conforming to the principle
     of "majority rule".

     6. Complying with requirements of the Freedom of Information Commission in the
     holding of meetings.

     7. Recognizing that a Board member's responsibility is not to "run the schools,"
     but to see that they are well run through effective policies.

     8. Referring all complaints through the proper "chain of command" within the
     system, and act on such complaints at public meetings only when administrative
     solutions fail.

     9. Taking and complying with the East Lyme oath for newly elected officials.
