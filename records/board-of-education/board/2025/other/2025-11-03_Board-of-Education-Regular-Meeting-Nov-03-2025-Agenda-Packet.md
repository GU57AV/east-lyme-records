                            2025-2026 GOALS & OUTCOMES
                      Adopted by the Board of Education on September 15, 2025



1. Pathways Programming: The BOE will continue to support the expansion of the East Lyme
   Pathways Program including the district's further development of community partnerships and
   career/technical education learning opportunities for students.
     Outcomes:
     •   Continue to provide a broader range of Pathways opportunities that support varied
         experiences for student needs and interests. Create at least two of these new broader
         range opportunities

     •   Collect information on the impact of the pathways program on student college and career
         planning to create a baseline of the benefits of Pathways on student success

     •   Integrate and align the VOG (Vision of the Graduate) experience to the Pathways program


2. Culture and Climate Progression: The BOE will support the district and school initiatives to
   advance a safe and inclusive learning and working environment.
     Outcomes:
     •   Continue to invite district leaders, staff, and stakeholders (including community partners) to
         board meetings for engagement and recognition. Recognition of at least one (1) student,
         staff, or group will occur during no less than ten (10) meetings within the school year.
         Ensure broad recognition occurs across elementary and secondary levels

     •   By December 2025, the district and each school building Culture and Climate committee will
         develop Culture & Climate Improvement Plans that:
                 i. Align with district priorities for a positive, inclusive and supportive school
                    environment
                ii. Are informed by anecdotal feedback (focus groups, staff, student, and family
                    conversations) and survey data (satchel pulse)
               iii. Include measurable action steps and indicators of progress to be monitored
                    throughout the school year

     •   Foster a continued sense of belonging and a greater student experience for every child
         across the district. This entails supporting the continued partnership and engaged training
         we will partake in through LEARN




                                                                                               Page 24 of 28
     •   Between the months of October and June, the BOE student representatives will have the
         option to participate in at least one full board meeting a month.


3. High Level Instructional Practices, Assessment, Growth/Development for Students: The BOE will
   support the further achievement, development, and growth for all students.
     Outcomes:
     •   The BOE will form a committee to investigate a school realignment plan with a
         recommendation to the BOE by December 2025.

     •   Form a committee to assess the feasibility and resource requirements to reintroduce world
         language instruction into the elementary school environment and bring a recommendation
         to the BOE by December 2025, and if it can be incorporated into the school realignment
         plan.

     •   Support administration and staff with the further development and engagement of artificial
         intelligence across K-12 classroom and curricular domains. In addition, the BOE will have A.I.
         as an agenda topic on a quarterly basis during the course of this school year.

     •   In collaboration with administration the BOE will review and discuss the high school course
         offerings with the goal of establishing a median class size of twenty (20) for the 26-27
         school year. Maintaining a high standard while achieving efficiency remains the focus.


4. District Marketing/Branding/Social Media Strategy: The BOE and administration will strengthen
   district marketing & branding through increased revenue opportunities and implementing the
   use of social media strategies.
     Outcomes:
     •   At least $10,000 dollars in revenue funds will be generated through paid advertising by June
         30, 2026.

     •   Increase athletics and central office followers on Facebook and Instagram by 10% through
         the use of marketing strategies such as QR codes which will support further district
         messaging and branding.
