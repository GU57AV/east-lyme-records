East Lyme Public Schools Board of Distinction Application – Level Two

1. Board Leadership/Student Achievement
   1A. The Board of Education works to improve student achievement.

   -   High Level Instructional Practices, Assessment, Growth/Development for Students was one of the four East
       Lyme Board of Education goals for the 24-25 school year and continues to be for the 2025-26 school year.
       The Board continually uses state data and district reporting to inform and support the growth of all district
       students.

   -   SBA Review of Assessment Data Presentation BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 10.7.24.pdf

   -   East Lyme Board of Education 2025-26 Goals

   1B. The Board relates the mission statement and goals to agenda items.

   -   The ELBOE’s mission statement is a part of the Bylaw 9325.2 and is recited at the beginning of the meeting
       agenda and included on the meeting minutes.

   -   The Board agendas link speciﬁc BOE Goals to applicable agenda items.

   1C. The Board supports the appropriate use of technology in educational programming.

   -   The Board supports equitable access to technology tools for all students and integration of technology in
       district educational programming inside and outside of the classroom. Professional development and
       engagement of understanding Artiﬁcial Intelligence was a continued focus over the last school year and has
       been woven into one of the 2025-26 BOE Goals.

   -   AI Presentation on 10.7.24 & 6.2.25
       BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 10.7.24.pdf
       BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 6.2.25.pdf

   -   AI Guidelines Discussion on 11.18.24
       BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 11.18.24.pdf


   1D. The Board uses data to make informed decisions regarding student achievement.

   -   The Board invites district leadership to present at the Board meetings throughout the year to help understand
       district data and discuss action taken to guide and improve student achievement and learning. The Board
       invited all school levels to present on their schools focus and improvement plans based upon the Board
       Goals.
       Elementary Schools - BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 4.7.25.pdf
       Middle School - BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 3.3.25.pdf
       High School - BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 10.21.24.pdf; BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 5.19.25.pdf

   1E. The Board addresses issues of diversity, equity, and inclusion.

   -   The Board continues its’ partnership with LEARN to formulate professional learning cohorts that focus on
       diversity, equity and inclusion. The cohorts participate in a two-year long training that meets four times
       throughout the school year. The sessions focus on productive courageous conversations, reframing bias and
       building common understanding, social identity, culture and microaggressions, culturally relevant pedagogy,
       and facilitating equity conversations in the classroom. This year a third cohort will begin the training.

4. Community Relations
   4B. The Board has successfully worked with other community leaders

   -   In addition to regular meetings and working with the First Selectman, Town Finance Director, and the
       Emergency Management department, the Board also collaborates with the town’s Wellness, Prevention, and
       Mentoring Coordinator that oversees East Lyme Youth Services.

   -   The BOE continues their partnership with Community Health Center to host a student-based health clinic at
       East Lyme High School for the 2025-2026 school year.

   -   Formation of the East Lyme Education Foundation. The Foundation presented its mission and purpose to the
       Board during the February 10, 2025 meeting.


   4D. The Board works with other Boards of Education

   -   East Lyme Public Schools continued the shared services agreement with Waterford Public Schools for the
       Nurses Services Manager. This role serves as a health liaison to both districts equally, overseeing nursing
       operations within both districts’ school buildings.

   -   Continued shared service agreements with Waterford Public Schools and Lyme-Old Lyme Schools for the
       2025-26 school year. The agreement allows their students enroll and earn credit in Emergency Medical
       Technician (EMT) Courses at East Lyme High School.

   -   The Board has continued the shared service partnerships for the Directors of Safety & Security, Facilities, &
       Food Service departments with Salem Public Schools.


5. Related Organizational Leadership
   5A. Representatives of the Board presented a workshop related to a district initiative at the annual
   CABE/CAPSS Convention or NSBA Convention

   -   ELHS Assistant Principal David Fasulo and science teacher, Mary Turano, were selected to present at the
       CABE/CAPSS Convention this coming November. The presentation encourages learning outside of the
       classroom to increase and encourage Pathways Program (such as EMT certiﬁcation).

   5B. The Board has sponsored a Legislative Breakfast

   -   The East Lyme Board of Education hosted Area 9 Legislative Breakfast on January 10, 2025 at East Lyme
       Middle School.

   5C. The Board works closely with its local legislative delegation to improve the schools.

   -   The Board/Superintendent meets and works with State Representative Nick Menapace & State Senator
       Martha Marx regarding legislative bills and changes impacting education and they are regularly invited to
       school sponsored events. Since elected, State Representative Menapace attended BOE meetings on
       February 10, March 3, and April 7, 2025 to provide legislative updates and recognize outstanding students.
