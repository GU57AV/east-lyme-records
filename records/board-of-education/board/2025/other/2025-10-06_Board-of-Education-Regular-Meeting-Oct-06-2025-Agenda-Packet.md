Order of Business
Unless changed by a two-thirds vote of those present at each regular and special Board
meeting, the order of business shall be as follows:

I. CALL TO ORDER

II. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

III. SPECIAL RECOGNITIONS

IV. PUBLIC COMMENT
Provides citizens the opportunity to address the Board on any matter, see Policy 9325.
This section does not include question or debate and the public will have three minutes
to address the Board.

V. SPECIAL REPORTS
     A. Students' Representative
     B. Selectmen's Representative
     C. Other Reporters

VI. CONSENT AGENDA
Items in this section require Board approval and are usually driven by Policy or
statute. These items require little or no discussion. These include meeting minutes,
policy review approvals, out of country field trips, donations, and other requests for
normal operations for the district. All of these items can be moved at once. If any Board
member desires, an item can be moved from the consent agenda to the
Discussion/Action item section.

      A. Instruction
      B. Personnel

TRACK & FIELD REPLACEMENT

  FieldTurf USA, Inc. is pleased to present the following proposal. FieldTurf pricing is based on the Capital
  Region Education Council (CREC) program. CREC is a member of The Association of Educational
  Purchasing Agencies (AEPA) program. The AEPA is a purchasing co-op that provides member schools
  with pre-determined preferential pricing by approved vendors. Since the product has already been bid
  at the national level, individual schools do not have to duplicate the formal bid process. AEPA IFB #024.

  Project Description

  This proposal is based on existing site conditions, review meetings with the school, and a target
  construction period in summer 2026. The pricing assumes FieldTurf will provide a turnkey design-build
  delivery, including all necessary engineering, site construction, track surfacing, synthetic turf
  installation, and project closeout.

  A detailed breakdown of costs, inclusions, and exclusions is provided below. The scope includes
  removing the existing synthetic turf and installing a new surface. The existing track surfacing will be fully
  removed and replaced with a Beynon BSS-100 system, with the assumption that the underlying asphalt
  remains suitable for reuse. Several alternate improvements have also been selected including: end
  zone lettering, (3) grey exchange zones, shadowed football numbers, and performance and payment
  bonds.

  Construction is expected to take approximately three to four months, depending on field availability. It
  is assumed that an adequate staging area will be available near the site.
