EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In January of 2025, Silver Petrucelli + Associates (SP+A) was retained by East Lyme Public Schools to
conduct a facilities condition study of the existing East Lyme High School building located at 30
Chesterfield Road in East Lyme CT. The purpose of this initial Phase 1 study is to assess the current High
School’s existing architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire protection conditions,
in conjunction with a Phase 1 space needs assessment and future Phase 2 site and building expansion
study, to determine the feasibility of renovation, expansion and/or replacement of the existing High
School building.

All inspections were visual in nature and did not entail selective demolition to investigate structures
behind walls or hard ceilings. Visual inspections were performed by SP+A’s staff of designers and
M/E/P/FP engineers. Michael Horton Associates (MHA) provided a structural assessment. SP+A did not
engage the services of elevator, food service or acoustics consultants or civil or environmental engineers
for this phase 1 study.

Field visits were conducted by SP+A and MHA from July through September of 2025. The assessment was
conducted through several detailed on-site investigations and included discussions with the ELHS facilities
staff, administration and select department heads as well as access to original construction documents,
reports, studies, and records supplied by ELPS.

This report addresses the existing building conditions and includes visual observations, code compliance
and recommendations. The reports refer to locations within the main Wings of the building and
corresponding photographs.

The code review included in this report references the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code as required
for existing buildings. As the majority of the building was constructed under older, less stringent codes,
the review identifies scope of work mandated for existing buildings and identifies thresholds that trigger
greater degrees of conformity with codes for new construction. An emphasis has been placed on review
of the facilities to meet current Americans with Disabilities Act which is met with the state requirements
in the ICC A117.1 Accessible and Usable Building & Facilities.

Scope Exclusions:
This Phase 1 assessment includes the East Lyme High School building located at 30 Chesterfield Road and
does not include the town’s attached indoor pool facility, surrounding fields or parking.

Please note that the scope of this assessment did not include a Hazardous Building Materials Survey.

ASSESSMENT OF BUILDING
Building History

East Lyme High School building square feet summary:

    ▪    1967 Construction                  102,293 sf
    ▪    1973 Construction                  75,400 sf
    ▪    1999 Construction                  59,794 sf
        Total                              237,487 sf
The current East Lyme High School building is divided into the central A and B two-story Academic
Wings, the east D Music Wing, and the west Athletic Wing (see diagram below). The original 102,293 sf
1967 High School building consisted of the D Wing, the south section of the A Wing, and the North side
of the Athletic Wing. In 1973 the addition of 75,400 sf was added to the original school consisting of a
two-story classroom addition to the north side of the A Wing, and a south gymnasium addition to the
Athletic Wing. In 1999 a 59,794 sf two-story B Wing was added to the south of the original A Wing.
During the 1999 B Wing addition, the A, D, and the Athletic Wings were renovated.

The A Wing lower floor consists of the main office administration, entrance commons/cafeteria and
kitchen, nurse’s suite, toilet rooms, custodial spaces, special education classrooms, art rooms,
technology classrooms and shops.

The A Wing upper floor includes the Media Center, special education classrooms/offices, counseling,
attendance/behavior, social workers, IT, custodial, storage, toilet rooms and academic
classrooms/offices for English, foreign language and social studies.

The B Wing lower floor contains math and science classrooms, supporting offices, technology
classrooms, student toilet rooms, a suspension room and building infrastructure spaces.

    The staff of Michael Horton Associates, Inc. (MHAI) visited East Lyme High School at 30 Chesterfield

    The building was unoccupied at the time of inspection. Our inspection was comprised of visual

Observations and Recommendations:
The original 1967 A-Wing and D-Wing building and subsequent north A-Wing is a two-story structure. The
first-floor structure is a cast in place concrete slab on grade. The second floor and roof structures are a

concrete columns, beams and spandrels. Concrete masonry walls are present throughout the interior and
exterior, with a brick veneer at the exterior.

The Auditorium and Gymnasium roof structures consist of metal roof deck supported by structural steel

 The 1999 B-Wing addition is constructed of a structural steel frame. The supported floor system is a cast

a metal roof deck supported by structural steel framing. Concrete masonry walls are present throughout
