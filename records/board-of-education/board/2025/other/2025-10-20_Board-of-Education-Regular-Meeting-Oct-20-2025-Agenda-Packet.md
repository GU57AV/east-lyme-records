Board of Education Pathways Update Summary October 20, 2025

The Pathways Program is not a single entity; the programming involves a variety of
components.

The goals of the Pathways programs at ELHS are to advance student learning with
community-based experiences, increase capacity in Career and Technical Education
programming, and support students in their post-secondary learning and career choices.

New initiatives that have started for the 2025-26 school year include:

   •   The new Waterford High School Automotive class partnership. Under this new
       partnership Waterford continues to send students to the ELHS EMT Certification
       class (tuition arrangements). However, tuition is offset by the number of students
       (six anticipated) who attend the Waterford High School Automotive class (every
       other day, first block, one cohort per semester). This is a win-win scenario for both
       high schools. Lyme/Old Lyme is again sending students to the ELHS EMT
       certification class. These collaborations provide additional opportunities and
       support for all students involved.
   •   The EMT certification program continues to develop high-impact partnerships such
       as Hartford Healthcare (patient contact hours at Backus Hospital, donation of a top
       end hospital bed), UCONN (donation of student supplies for all students such as
       stethoscopes, blood pressure cuffs, etc., four UCONN ECE credits, and the UCONN
       Pharmacy collaboration), Army National Guard (no cost combat medic/mass
       casualty training), and this year Yale New Haven Health Sky Health (helicopter
       medics). Anticipated that the Global Language collaboration (assessing non-English
       speakers) will grow as a statewide model program, and it is hoped will receive high-
       level recognition.
   •   In November David Fasulo and Mary Turano will be presenting at the first ever
       Statewide CT Public Safety Pathways Summit hosted at the CT Fire Academy. This is
       the result of the EMT program consulting to other districts, such as West Harford,
       who are considering similar EMT preparation programs. It is hoped ELHS can offer a
       Firefighter 1 training course after school at East Lyme High School.
   •   After the successful collaborations with the Army National Guard Cyber Security
       Unit, ELHS is piloting a Cyber Security class this (2025) semester. Seventeen
       students enrolled in this new course. Collaborations with the US Coast Guard
       Academy, University of New Haven, and other organizations are anticipated.
   •   The Adventure PE class started collaborating this (2025) semester with the Army
       National Guard Stone’s Ranch facility for team building experiences. The National
       Guard provided transportation and trained facilitators at no cost.

    Gant funding. Note: David Fasulo writes the Perkins Grant for East Lyme.

    (www.pattagansettartcenter.org) and is offering, at no cost, a four week program led

    school program is geared for young creative artists interested in learning more about
    pursuing a career in the arts and other creative fields.
