2. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – OTHER
A. Discussion/Workshop: Long-term Strategic Planning
The Board had discussions about their goals, vision, and approach to maintaining the existing quality education while finding ways to improve. Topics included:  balance within class sizes, preparing students for success with equitable experiences, and cost effectiveness.
Discussions occurred about the current grade layout and structure of the district school buildings and potentially exploring grade and building shifts that model other area successful districts with similar demographics.
The Board also explored the most effective and supportive way to review potential budget reductions that does not diminish the culture and climate efforts, and progress made with staff this school year.
The Board agreed to schedule an additional Committee of the Whole Meeting to allow further discussions on the 2025-26 school year budget.
