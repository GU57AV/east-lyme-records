4. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – INSTRUCTION
A. East Lyme Middle School Presentation (BOE Goal 3)
Principal Kelly spoke of the variety of clubs at ELMS and shared that student clubs are opportunities that reinforce values of teamwork, enhancing social skills and leadership, problem solving and exploration while addressing a variety of interests and talents. The administration tries to keep all clubs open to allow all students interested in participating. Most clubs are led by ELMS teachers and look for opportunities to link activities directly to the classroom. Mrs. Kelly also shared that the late bus is provided three times a week to allow access to all students.
Members of the Sea Perch Club introduced themselves to the Board. Mrs. Linda Nastri is the club advisor and spoke of the positive experience launching this club has been. The 7
th
& 8
th
graders work as a team on machining, soldering, welding, pipefitting, and electrical to build robotic devices. All of the work is tied to Common Core and NGSS standards and was sponsored by Electric Boat outreach.
The Board asked the students questions to better understand the process and collaborative approach. The device will be tested in the pool this spring.
Mrs. Kelly thanked the Board for the support of expanding athletics at the middle school this year and shared the work of Assistant Athletic Director, Kathleen Kuvalanka and how athletics has supported the climate and culture of ELMS.
