              Assistant Principal David Fasulo led the recognition of

4.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION

         A.    High School Principal Report
               Principal Henry Kydd, Assistant Principal Julie Shvetz, and
               Assistant Principal Lisa Belisle gave an overview of the high
               school's main focus points for this school year: implement
               high impact teaching, foster student belonging &
               experience, create school culture & climate, and shared new
               action steps taken based on last school year's survey data.
               Students Natalie Berg & Samantha Rhodes, part of the Peer
               Mentoring program, spoke about the program benefits, how
               much they enjoyed participating, and how the program has
               positively impacted their school experience.

         B.    Pathways Program Update
               Assistant Principal Dave Fasulo presented on the growth
               and expansion of the Pathways program, and his work to
               foster diverse community partnerships to create
               experiential learning to Pathways students with minimal or
               no cost to the district.
               Board members engaged in discussions and praised Mr.
               Fasulo's approach and dedication to developing the
               program, impressed how it has evolved over the years.
