4. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - OTHER
A. Discussion/Information: East Lyme Education Foundation Presentation (BOE Goal 2)
Ryan Shrader, President of the East Lyme Education Foundation, introduced himself and shared that he is a former East Lyme Board of Education member. Mr. Shrader provided an update on the ELEF and the purpose and mission of the foundation. In the
presentation
, Mr. Shrader outlined the grant funding cycle and their donor outreach approach.
The Foundation’s goal is to fund 10 mini grants up to $1,500 each this upcoming fall. Their focus is what grants will have the most direct impact on students. The applications will be online; complete information and the link will be directly communicated to district staff.
5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
A. Discussion/Information: SuperNovas/Novas Playschool Program for the 2025-26 School Year (BOE Goal 3)
Annaliese Spaziano & Kimberly Davis led the discussion and
presented two options
to maintain the SuperNova/Nova program in the upcoming school year. Board discussion ensued about alternative area programs, enrollment options and requirements to maintain an economically stable program, the challenges and variability of predicting enrollment, and possible options if the enrollment numbers were larger than anticipated.
The Board agreed to make a decision on the program numbers for the 2025-26 school year by May. The Board also plans to have a Committee of the Whole discussion this fall to begin planning for the 2026-27 school year.
6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. Discussion/Information: 2025-26 Budget Deliberations (BOE Goal 2)
Superintendent Newton and Board members discussed the factors that were driving the proposed budget increase of 7.36%. Board members had comprehensive discussions on ways to reduce the proposed percentage with minimal impact on students. Superintendent Newton shared the strategy of keeping the preliminary health insurance numbers in line with the town, so that any adjustments are made together. Board members acknowledged that area districts are proposing similar increases. The Board agreed on budget cuts to reduce the proposed budget increase to 6.72%. The Board will host a public hearing on February 24, 2025, to share the proposed Budget for 2025-26.
Mr. Bauman asked Board members to attend a Committee of the Whole Meeting after the hearing and special meeting on February 24 to have further discussions on possible reductions.
