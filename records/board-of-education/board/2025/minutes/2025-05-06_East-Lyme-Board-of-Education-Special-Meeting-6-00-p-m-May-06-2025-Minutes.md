C. Public Comment
Pat Hesney,
President of the Secretarial Union, thanked the board members that were in support of staff positions and spoke at the BOF Public Hearing, but is concerned about the reduction of the elementary buildings 12-month secretary’s positions, and the importance of their role in school buildings over the summer. Mrs. Hesney also expressed her concerns regarding the reduction of the library assistant and believes it should be reconsidered.
Gen Pasutto,
250 Upper Pattagansett, Vice President of the Secretarial Union requested the board reappropriate the funding of the library assistant and the 12 month building secretaries. Mrs. Pasutto spoke of the impact of the library assistant role and the various contributions 12-month building secretaries make over the summer months that often go unnoticed. Mrs. Pasutto stated if this position is removed much of the preparatory for the next year will go unmanaged over the summer.
Geetha Sivanandam,
4 Mallard Circle, Elementary Library Assistant, spoke on the importance of the library aide position and shared the significant work alone she completed this year to support the librarian and maintain the library. Mrs. Sivanandam stated that a library is the foundation for learning, helping students to develop into curious and engaged readers and should be an investment in literacy and early learning success. Mrs. Sivanandam also advocated that after school clubs are maintained at the middle school level, as clubs are an interest connector (not academically based) for students at a critical stage of development.
Rebecca Ingmundson
, 13 Brookfield Drive, parent of a kindergartener and a teacher in another district, acknowledged the difficult nature of reducing the budget, but is concerned that jobs lost through attrition can negatively impact class sizes for several years. Mrs. Ingmundson shared that as a parent, she would be happy to contribute money to field trips and other activities if all students were able to participate and if jobs that support students are maintained. Mrs. Ingmundson stated that human connection is critical in a post-covid world.
2. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. 2025-26 Budget Updates (BOE Goal 3)
Chair Eric Bauman shared an overview of the budget process for the 2025-26 school year and that the BOE initially adopted a budget with a 6.72% increase over this fiscal year. Once the BOE presented the budget to the BOF, the BOF reduced the BOE adopted budget by 1.5 million after deliberation. Following the community response during the Public Hearing on April 28, the Board of Finance voted to reinstate $675,000 of the budget cut back to the Board of Education. The Town Meeting will be held on May 12 & the referendum on May 22 will decide if the budget passes. Mr. Bauman stated that traditionally, in his experience, if the budget referendum does not pass, the Board of Finance will look to further reduce the Board of Education budget.
Superintendent Newton reviewed the revised proposed reductions based on the updated BOF reinstatement. Mr. Newton shared what was considered with these decisions and the rationale behind each staff reduction, including resolving district inequities and staff-initiated reductions, focusing on the best way to redistribute support based on the reductions made. Mr. Newton also stated that all the building activities and supply reductions initially cut were reinstated, and the only reductions being considered are reflected in the
sheet
.
All Board of Education members contributed to an extensive discussion on the proposed budget reductions and asked clarifying questions to understand the impact on staff and students. Several board members shared they would like to solicit student input with the budgeting process moving forward; stating how student participation, ideas and feedback could bring a new perspective on their needs and direct experience. Discussion also included future interest in teacher and staff feedback in budgeting to find a better understanding of their everyday work experience.
The priority of student connection and how staff contribute towards that connection was addressed several times, as well as how to engage the community to vote in the referendum.
The discussions resulted in board members requesting that the secretarial positions be reconsidered in place of the removal of underutilized or duplicative technological software, athletic training equipment, and an unfilled stipend position at the elementary level.
