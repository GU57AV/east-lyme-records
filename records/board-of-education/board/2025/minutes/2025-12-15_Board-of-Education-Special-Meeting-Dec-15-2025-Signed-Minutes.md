               Each Elementary Principal shared the different ways each of

               fostering student belonging and experiences. Second grade
               teachers, Kari Wiseman and Kristine Dishaw spoke of their
               class visits to Crescent Point senior living residence and
               what a positive experience it has been for students, the
               residents, and the greater EL community.

               coaches: Kim Hill and Melissa Ewers and shared their focus
               and work on high impact teaching and supporting teachers
               based on research, multi-year goals in the school
