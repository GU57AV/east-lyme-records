4. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – INSTRUCTION
A. Discussion: Composting Update (BOE Goal 2)
Ms. Narciss Greene, Business Education teacher, presented an update on the composting program at ELHS. The
presentation
include updates on total waste savings so far, student engagement, fundraising and investments, and future plans of a composting site in Waterford.
B. Discussion: High School Principal Presentation (BOE Goal 3)
Principal Kydd provided an update on this past semester at ELHS with an emphasis on high impact teaching and real-world learning (multiple learning walks, EMT program, etc.). A few notable highlights:
Student Emma Frisbee shared her experience with the National History Day competition.
Ms. Alexa Kydd received an instructor award for excellence from UConn for ECE and shared about her Humans of East Lyme curriculum in that class. Students Callaway Scott and Dalynn Jackson shared their 'Humans of East Lyme' projects.
Ms. Beth Provost spoke about the many ways the Best Buddies club promotes inclusion at ELHS
Ms. Emily Lattanzi was celebrated for receiving an award from music educators association, and shared her work on the restoration of Coffee Night Open Mics with student Aubrey Dalton performing on her Omnichord.
C. Discussion: Nova/SuperNova Enrollment Follow-up (BOE Goal 3)
Assistant Superintendent Spaziano and Mrs. Davis provided an update on enrollment for Creative Playschool, Integrated Preschool, and the Nova/Supernovas and programming for the 2025-26 school year.
5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. Discussion: Food Services Program Update (BOE Goal 2)
Chris Urban and Ashleigh Meek
presented
on the Food Services Program.  Topics included: performance on the tri-annual WELL-SAT, 1
st
day of Spring celebrations, and Sports Day.  They shared there is likely a need to increase prices next year based on state requirements and inflation.
B. Discussion: Salem Schools Cooperative Agreement (BOE Goal 2)
Scheduling a spring cooperative meeting has been challenging and will look to Fall to plan a future meeting. Committee work will soon be established on both sides which will include reviews of the existing agreement.
6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – PERSONNEL
A. Discussion/Action: Superintendent’s Recommendation for Non-Renewal of Non-Tenured Certified Staff Due to a FY26 Budget Reduction Resulting in a Reduction in Force for the 2025-26 School Year
Motion to recommend the Board of Education to move the contract of employment for non-tenured certified staff members across departments not be renewed for the following year upon contract expiration at the end of the 2024-2025 school year due to potential fiscal year 2025-2026 budget reductions.
Motion by Leigh Gianakos, second by Olivia Fairchild
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Jaime Barr Shelburn, Jill Carini, Olivia Fairchild, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, and Vivek Purohit
7. ADMINISTRATION REPORTS
A. Superintendent of Schools – Mr. Jeffrey Newton
Superintendent Newton shared legislative updates which included: the kindergarten age waiver will likely be eliminated in future years and the district expects to receive $77,000 back from excess cost for Special Education.  Mr. Newton shared a reminder that the Budget Referendum is Thursday. Secretary contract negotiations resume tomorrow at 4:30 pm.  ELHS Student Expo is at 6 pm tomorrow.
B. Assistant Superintendent of Schools – Mrs. Annaliese Spaziano
Assistant Superintendent Spaziano shared that she's meeting with all the Curriculum Fellows 1:1 to reflect on action plans and project out to next year.  Tomorrow opens the third and final Satchel Pulse survey of the school year.
C. Director of Finance – Mrs. Maryanna Stevens
Mrs. Maryanna Stevens shared that the budget was close for the current fiscal year, so a freeze was implemented, and is hoping to lift that soon. Mrs. Stevens also provided a quick update on why town mill rates are not necessarily comparable.
