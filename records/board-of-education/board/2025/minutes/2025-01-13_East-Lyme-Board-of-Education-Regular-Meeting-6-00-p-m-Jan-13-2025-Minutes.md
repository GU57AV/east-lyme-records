4. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. Superintendent’s Budget Presentation (BOE Goals 2,3, & 4)
Superintendent Newton delivered the 2025-26
Budget Presentation
, which provided an overview of: the ELPS Budget timeline; cost drivers for FY2026; health insurance projections; proposed budget process; total proposed budget increase of 6.99%; 2025-26 fixed expenses; per pupil expenditures; projected future enrollment; shared services & cost mitigation efforts; capital improvement projects; staffing requests; special education information; options for further reduction and budget reduction scenarios.
Eric Bauman asked about the 1.0 Family and Consumer Science Teacher request to determine if there's an internal staffing solution. Mr. Bauman also inquired about the open positions that the schools are currently operating with.
Olivia Fairchild asked about the difference between 0.4 vs 1.0 FTE positions in practice, clarification on 211 calls, and the difference between IEP and 504 identifiers.
Jaime Barr Shelburn asked for clarification on the access road and asked to ensure the next presentation clearly communicates that road is multi-purpose and not used by just the middle school. Ms. Shelburn also recommended including content on all shared services (food, security, and facilities) with Salem Public Schools (slide 15).
Vivek Purohit noted that the aquatic roof renovation isn't included in the list of Capital Improvement Projects (slide 16). Superintendent Newton confirmed the funding to move forward with the project has been found through collaboration with the town in this year’s budget and will not be included as part of next year's capital improvement list.
Board members will review the proposed budget and bring questions and feedback to the BOE Regular meeting scheduled for January 27, 2025.
