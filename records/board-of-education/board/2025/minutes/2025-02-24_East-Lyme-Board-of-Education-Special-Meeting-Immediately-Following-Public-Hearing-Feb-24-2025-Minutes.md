1. OPENING OF MEETING
A. Call to Order
Chair Eric Bauman called the meeting to order at 6:13 p.m.
2. DISCUSSION/ACTION: FINANCE
A. Discussion/Action: Proposed Adoption of ELPS Budget for FY 2025-2026
Chair Eric Bauman asked the Board if there were any further discussion topics they would like clarified or addressed regarding the proposed Budget.
Esteban Garcia asked if there had been any update on the health insurance costs from the state. Maryanna Stevens anticipates receiving final numbers in April. Chair Bauman reviewed the strategy behind planning for insurance costs without receiving final numbers so that the budget is not negatively impacted based on the state’s final numbers.
Motion to adopt the proposed 2025-26 ELPS Budget in the amount of $64,057,400, a 6.72% increase over the current year’s budget, as presented.
Motion by Bill Derry, second by Jill Carini
Final Resolution: Motion approved.
Pro: Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, and Esteban Garcia
