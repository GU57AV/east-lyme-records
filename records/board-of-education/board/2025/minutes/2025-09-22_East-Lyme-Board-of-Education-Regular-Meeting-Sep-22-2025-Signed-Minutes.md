6.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY

         A.    Discussion/Action: Non-lapsing Account
               Chair Bauman led discussions with board members as they
               reviewed the financial summary of the end of FY dollars and
               the rationale behind the amounts and non-lapsing account
               created by the Board. All Board members engaged in
               discussions which included preserving community
               relationships, creating fiscal limitations to the account,
               future financial requirements based on statue requirements
               and capital improvements, and strategies and potential
               limitations on utilizing the account for long term planning.

               Board members agreed to request the BOE Finance,
               Facilities, and Operations Committee discuss this account
               and allocated dollars in further detail at their next meeting
               and provide the Board a recommendation following those
               discussions.
