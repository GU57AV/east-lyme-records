Topics included: professional development, best practices with media and prompting, and utilizing AI software for research, editing, grading rubrics, and how to integrate into existing curriculum projects. 5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. High School Vision Committee Update and Feasibility Study
The board discussed the funding and need for the feasibility study to understand the limits of the footprint and land in order to begin plans with existing ELHS building. Task 1 is understanding the present state of the building, land and needs. Task 2 is a building assessment to identify all problems with equipment and facilities to allow a comprehensive capital improvement plan.  Dr. Lund confirmed the study is a fixed price.
Motion to approve funding for Phase 1 of the East Lyme High School feasibility study, in the amount of $40,650, as presented.
Motion by: Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by: Leigh Gianakos
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, Leigh Gianakos, Esteban Garcia, Vivek Purohit, and Cate Steel
