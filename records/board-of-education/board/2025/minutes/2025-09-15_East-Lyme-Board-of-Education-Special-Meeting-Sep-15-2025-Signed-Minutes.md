7.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY

         A.    Discussion/Action: Non-Lapsing Account
               Superintendent Newton reviewed the fiscal year-end
               balance and Mr. Bauman began discussions on the history of
               the non-lapsing account and the recent statute that legally
               allows the Board to transfer a percentage of unexpended
               funds into a non-lapsing account without required approval
               from the Board of Finance. The Board of Education has been
               unsuccessful in achieving approval from the Board of
               Finance, when their authorization was requested to
               previously establish an account.
               All Board of Education members participated in extensive
               discussions about the strategic approaches to utilizing the
               non-lapsing account, maintaining community relationships
               while protecting the school district, and restrictions of/and
               allowable uses for the non-lapsing account.
               Year to Date Budget Report - 06-30-2025.pdf
