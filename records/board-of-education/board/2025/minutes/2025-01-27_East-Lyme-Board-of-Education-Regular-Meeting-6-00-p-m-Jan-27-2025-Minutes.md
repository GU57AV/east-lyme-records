4. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS  - INSTRUCTION
A. Discussion/Information: SuperNovas/Novas Childcare Program & Creative Playschool Program (BOE Goal 3)
Annaliese Spaziano, Assistant Superintendent & Kimberly Davis, Director of Student Services, provided an overview of the history, costs, and operational implications of the childcare programs (Creative Playschool; Integrated Preschool; Novas/SuperNovas) hosted at East Lyme Public Schools. In their
presentation
they reviewed the similarities and differences within each program.
A comprehensive discussion ensued also included possible programming options and feasibility for the future of the SuperNova/Novas program.
Leigh Gianakos shared her experiences as a parent and the limitations of the existing structure with families that need a full-time program for preschool aged students. She would like the Board to research and consider a full-time program in the future in East Lyme Public Schools.
Vivek Purohit thinks supporting the community is vital and the Novas/SuperNovas program to be able to accommodate more families’ scheduling requirements for their work needs.
Jaime Barr Shelburn shared that she was in support of providing a full day program and wrap around care for preschool aged students, because many families enroll their children in programs in outside districts. She would like a full day option to be considered and is in support of consolidating as needed to do so.
Esteban Garcia inquired about Care & Share and providing tuition assistance for families that are applicable.
Alisa Bradley would like to zoom out the focus and look into needs and research what families’ first choice would be and what they are choosing based on availability.
Eric Bauman shared his concerns about running a program that is underfunded and the implications of that for the future.
Ms. Spaziano spoke that some of the feedback and requests from board members are long term goals based on the logistic needs of the program and existing conditions.
The Board will discuss further during the upcoming meeting to decide how to move forward with the program in the 25-26 school year.
5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. Discussion/Information: 2025-26 Budget Deliberations (BOE Goal 2)
Superintendent Newton reviewed highlights in the budget materials and provided updates on the budgeting process. One significant factor is that the health insurance increase will be between 8-11% based on preliminary numbers shared by the state. Discussion ensued between board members on existing and potential elementary class sizes; the strategy with current staff and enrollment numbers; possible benefits and/or implications of not replacing open positions that remain unfilled; comparing the cost per student for East Lyme to other area schools and the strategy to maintain quality teachers with competitive pay.
Chair Bauman requested that Board members review the materials and consider what they are comfortable with approving for reductions in the budget.
B. Discussion/Action: Aquatic Center Roof (BOE Goal 2)
Superintendent Newton reviewed the latest update and plans for the Aquatic Center Roof project and how additional funds became available for this project. Dr. Lund provided an understanding of the complex elements that contribute to the high cost of the roof replacement based on the needs of the building and the structural integrity of the electrical wiring. Discussion regarding the plans for the Aquatic Center Roof occurred.
Motion to approve the Aquatics Center Roof Replacement Project proposal, at the cost of $2.4 million, as presented.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Bill Derry
Motion approved.
Pro: Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, Vivek Purohit, and Jaime Barr Shelburn
