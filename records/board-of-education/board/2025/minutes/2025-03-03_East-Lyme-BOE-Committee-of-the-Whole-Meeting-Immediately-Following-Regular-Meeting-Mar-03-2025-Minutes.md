2. DISCUSSION ITEMS
A. 2025-26 School Year Budget Discussion
Board members had strategic and theoretical discussions about the implications for the 2025-26 budget and the timeline for approval with the Board of Finance and Selectman. Discussions included topics such as: the proposed budget affecting the town mil rate, the mil rate comparisons to area competitive school districts; being prepared to adjust priorities and utilizing resources; gathering data from the stakeholders/ administrators to understand programming impact; creating informed decisions and fully understanding the consequences of any budget reduction made; researching possible untapped fiscal efficiencies; major factors and budget driver costs beyond BOE control such as special education and health care; and future strategies to consider when composing next year’s board goals. Board members agreed that the adopted budget is the baseline for continuing existing operations at ELPS and that the administration did their due diligence in preparing this budget.
