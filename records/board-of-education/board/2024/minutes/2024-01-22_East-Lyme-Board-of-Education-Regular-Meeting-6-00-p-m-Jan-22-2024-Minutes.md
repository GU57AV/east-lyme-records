5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. Discussion/Information: Athletic Director Update: Kevin Marcoux/2024-2025 Middle School Sports
Kevin Marcoux provided updates on ELHS winter sports, growth for EL Athletics on social media, score board updates, and partnership with BSN for rebranding and unification of the EL mascot and logo.
An extensive discussion occurred amongst all board members and Mr. Marcoux regarding logistics, successes and challenges faced, his work to foster climate and culture, and promoting student and parent behavioral sportsmanship. Mr. Marcoux shared his thoughts on athletic facilitates’ needs and advocated for the turf field replacement.
Mr. Marcoux shared the background and research he has done on sports programs at the middle school and spoke favorably of incorporating soccer, cheer, and volleyball at ELMS and the requirements needed for the teams to be successfully added. A comprehensive discussion occurred with all board members on all factors in adding select middle school sports including marketing and communication, conference scheduling, academic standards, and the need for a stipend assistant athletic director position to ensure safety and regulations are followed.
B. Discussion/Information: Budget 2024-2025 Budget Discussion Follow-up
Superintendent Newton clarified that the proposed budget is at a 6.08% increase, which was misreported in the Day. The amount reported was based upon the health insurance costs reduction, which has not yet been determined, and the reduction of two teacher positions. Superintendent Newton does not recommend any further budget reductions.  No staff member will lose their position with the proposed budget.
Mr. Bauman requested that the board members review the budget proposal and submit all questions to himself or Superintendent Newton to allow preparation for the next meeting on 2/5. The Board agreed to defer the Board retreat to March to allow focus on the budget before the public hearing scheduled for 2/26.
