A. Call to Order
Chair Eric Bauman called the meeting to order at 7:23 p.m.
2. DISCUSSION
A. Discussion/Workshop: School Budget Planning for FY 2025-26
Superintendent Newton reviewed the
Budget Presentation
and began the open discussion on the preliminary budget numbers. Discussion amongst administrators and board members focused on factors and significant cost drivers contributing to this initial projection, such as salaries, health insurance and benefits, variables in tuition for student services, and transportation.
Discussions also included a review of capital renovations and improvement cycle, repayment status on existing projects, retirement notifications, and staffing needs around the district based on student needs and enrollment projections.
Board members shared topics they would like to see reviewed and considered further during the upcoming Budget Workshop on December 16, 2024.
