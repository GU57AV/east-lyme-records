5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. Discussion/Information: Aquatics Center Update (BOE Goal 4)
Andrea Scott, Revenue Programs Manager, introduced herself and shared her background and aquatics experience at East Lyme.
Ms. Scott provided the Board with Aquatics Center Updates on membership enrollments, private and group swim lessons, procedural improvements such as a self check-in kiosk. Ms. Scott shared there is a consistent waitlist within each enrollment session; she is working on bringing in new instructors and is implementing mandatory teacher training with all swim instructors to ensure classes are consistent. Ms. Scott also shared facility updates and improvements to the event space and lockers.
Board members engaged in discussions with how the aquatics center is utilized for current ELPS students and praised Ms. Scott for her efforts and the improvements since she began this role last February.
B. Discussion/Information: Food Services Update (BOE Goal 4)
Christian Urban, Food Services Director and Ashleigh Meek, Assistant Director, shared this
presentation
and provided updates on the food services program including current breakfast and lunch purchases, engagement with families at school Open Houses, the success of the Flanders greenhouse, student engagement activities to healthy eating, and the plans for a Viking Café which affords the culinary students the opportunity to host and serve lunch to their school community. Board members are invited to have lunch at the café event on 11/22.
C. Discussion/Information: High School Vision Committee Update (BOE Goal 2)
In April 2024, the BOE made a motion to appoint Superintendent Newton to formulate a committee to address significant renovations that will be needed to maintain the high school building . After initial discussions about the scope and significance of the project, a feasibility study on the building is necessary before a larger committee is formed. The smaller group will include up to 10 people, it is anticipated the feasibility study would need the entire school year to gather this information. After the study is complete, a larger group of community members will be formed for the Vision Committee. There are additional steps, such as developing a charter, that needs to be considered before renovation plans move forward.
D. Discussion: Town Capital Improvement Committee
Chair Bauman and Superintendent Newton reviewed the town’s request proposal to create a Capital Improvement Committee with the request of two Board of Education members to join. Discission ensued between Board members on the best way to support the town’s request while ensuring that informed decisions are made. Jill Carini volunteered to serve on the committee. Superintendent Newton, Maryanna Stevens, Christian Lund will be the other members representing the BOE to ensure that subject experts are involved in discussions; two of whom will also satisfy the community member request. The Board will be updated on next steps once the proposed representatives are shared with the Town.
E. Discussion/Action: Non-lapsing Account (BOE Goal 2)
The Board previously attempted to create a non-lapsing account to allow unexpended funds up to 2% into an account to be utilized by the board. This type of account previously required Board of Finance approval.
Recent legislative updates removed the former requirement and will allow the Board of Education to set aside excess funding into a non-lapsing account. Discussion amongst board members ensued on the creation of, logistics, and the future use of the account.
Unexpended Education Funds Account
Motion to create a Non-lapsing Account (Unexpended Education Funds Reserve) for the Board of Education use not to exceed 2% of the prior fiscal year total budget appropriation, as presented.

6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – POLICY
A. Discussion: Review of Bylaw 9325.2 – Order of Business (Meeting Minutes)
Chair Bauman initiated the discussion amongst board members of revising the existing
Bylaw 9325.2
that outlines the order of business for Board of Education meetings. The revision would allow meeting minutes to be approved within consent agenda items. Discussion on the revision was favorable to having the Policy Review Committee consider this revision during their next committee meeting. If the Policy Review Committee proposes a revision to the Board, the change in procedure would only occur after the policy approval cycle at Board of Education meetings.
