B. Discussion/Action:
Proposed Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement - Healthy Food Option
Motion to approve Proposed Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement - Healthy Food Option, as presented. Pursuant to C.G.S. Section 10-215f, East Lyme Board of Education certifies that all food items offered for sale to students in the schools under its jurisdiction, and not exempted from the Connecticut Nutrition Standards published by the Connecticut State Department of Education, will comply with the Connecticut Nutrition Standards during the period of July 1, 2024, through June 30, 2025. This certification shall include all food offered for sale to students separately from reimbursable meals at all times and from all sources, including but not limited to school stores, vending machines, school cafeterias, culinary programs, and any fundraising activities on school premises sponsored by the school or non-school organizations and groups.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Cate Steel
Final Resolution: Motion Passed Unanimously
Pro:  Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, Vivek Purohit, Cate Steel
C. Discussion/Action:
Proposed Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement - Food and Beverage Exemption
Motion to approve Proposed Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement - Food and Beverage Exemptions, as presented. The East Lyme Board of Education will allow the sale to students of food items that do not meet the Connecticut Nutrition Standards and beverages not listed in Section 10-221q of the Connecticut General Statutes provided that the following conditions are met: 1) the sale is in connection with an event occurring after the end of the regular school day or on the weekend; 2) the sale is at the location of the event; and 3) the food and beverage items are not sold from a vending machine or school store. An “event” is an occurrence that involves more than just a regularly scheduled practice, meeting, or extracurricular activity. For example, soccer games, school plays, and interscholastic debates are events but soccer practices, play rehearsals, and debate team meetings are not. The “regular school day” is the period from midnight before to 30 minutes after the end of the official school day. “Location” means where the event is being held and must be the same place as the food and beverage sales.
Motion by Cate Steel, second by Leigh Gianakos
Final Resolution: Motion Passed Unanimously
Pro:  Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, Vivek Purohit, Cate Steel
