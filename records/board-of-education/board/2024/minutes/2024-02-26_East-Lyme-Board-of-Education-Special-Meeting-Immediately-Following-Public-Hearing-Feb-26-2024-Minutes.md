East Lyme Board of Education Special Meeting
Monday, February 26, 2024 (Immediately following the Public Hearing)
MINUTES
Members present onsite:
Eric Bauman (Chair), Jaime Barr Shelburn (Secretary), Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, Vivek Purohit, and Cate Steel
Administrators present onsite:
Jeffrey Newton (Superintendent), Annaliese Spaziano (Assistant Superintendent of Curriculum and Instruction), Maryanna Stevens (Director of Finance), Kimberly Davis (Director of Student Services)
1. OPENING OF MEETING
A. Call to Order
Chair Eric Bauman called the meeting to order at 6:26 p.m.
B. Pledge of Allegiance
Chair Eric Bauman led the Pledge of Allegiance.
C. Public Comment
No public comment.
2. DISCUSSION/ACTION – FINANCE
A. DISCUSSION/ACTION: Proposed Adoption of ELPS Budget for FY 2024-2025
Board members reflected on the proposed budget presented at the Board of Education Public Hearing earlier in the evening. Discussion about the budget ensued amongst all Board Members including facility needs outside of the Capital Improvement Projects, long term planning, and prioritizing educational programs in future budgets.
Mrs. Barr Shelburn shared, for the record, that she would like to have a world language program at the elementary level reinstated at East Lyme Public Schools.
Motion to adopt the Proposed 2024-2025 ELPS Budget in the amount of $61,124,675, which is a 5.77% increase over the current year’s budget.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Esteban Garcia
Final Resolution: Motion passed unanimously
Pro: Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, Vivek Purohit, Cate Steel
3. DISCUSSION/ACTION – PERSONNEL
A. DISCUSSION/ACTION: Teacher Sabbatical Request for the 2024-25 School Year
Superintendent Newton reviewed the
Teacher Sabbatical Request
brought forth to the Board of Education. Extensive discussion ensued amongst all Board members around the sabbatical approval process, contract language, and budget implications of approving the request.
Motion to approve the sabbatical request brought forth by Diane Swan for the 2024-25 school year.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Cate Steel
Final Resolution: Motion does not pass
Pro: Jill Carini, Cate Steel
Against: Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Olivia Fairchild, Esteban Garcia
Abstain: Jaime Barr Shelburn, Bill Derry, Leigh Gianakos, Vivek Purohit
4. ADJOURNMENT
Action: A. Adjourn Meeting
Move to Adjourn the meeting at 7:57 p.m.
Motion by Cate Steel, second by Jill Carini
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, Vivek Purohit, Cate Steel
