Board of Selectmen member, Rose Ann Hardy shared the following updates:

Owners have offered the property to the town under another sales offer to ensure open space areas are maintained. She is hoping for public support for this purchase.
5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – INSTRUCTION
A. Discussion/Information: Artificial Intelligence Follow-up (BOE Goal 3)
Assistant Superintendent Spaziano introduced presenters: Instructional Technology coaches, Gil Gallant, Shannon Saglio, & Tiffany Cozzolino; Sarah Zielinski, ELMS Social Studies Teacher; & Carla Woitovich, ELMS Media Specialist.
The team’s
presentation
reviewed how the district is guiding faculty use of AI to provide planning support and facilitating use in the classroom as an aid in explicit instruction. The team reviewed their approach to managing student use of AI and how they are teaching and instilling appropriate use in students.
Discussion ensued amongst Board members and presenters on the pros and cons of student use; being an informed user of the technology; recognizing fake results from AI; and the necessity of historical knowledge, context and the human element to use AI successfully,
B. Discussion/Information: SBA Review of Assessment Data (BOE Goal 3)
Assistant Superintendent Spaziano gave a
presentation
on the summative data from the Spring state assessment, which included a discussion of the student supports provided based on the assessment scores, data trends and commonalities, growth comparisons and historical growth data, and how the district fared comparatively to other districts in our DRG.
East Lyme students perform above state guidelines consistently within and outside of the District Reference Groups. It was discussed that the data represented a majority of the students at ELPS, few students opted out of the testing, and that less than 1% of the students take the alternative assessment.
The highlights of the Youth Voices survey results were shared and factors that impact students’ anxiety throughout the day were discussed. Assistant Superintendent Spaziano reviewed how her team is using that data to inform best practices to support East Lyme students’ learning and success.

B. Discussion/Action: Steam Boiler Purchase at Central Office (BOE Goal 2)
Superintendent Newton shared that two holes were discovered in the boiler at Central Office, and it is inoperable. There is currently no heat at Central Office. There is a 12 to 16 week delay to have a replacement boiler installed. Temporary heat is needed until the project is complete; the proposal is to bring a separate boiler in on a trailer to the building until a full boiler can be replaced. The temporary heat is significantly higher in cost than originally anticipated.
Discussion ensued amongst board members about alternative options and scenarios to resolve the problem and expedite costs. .
Motion to approve the proposed steam boiler purchase and possible rental boiler of the Central Office building not to exceed the amount of $145,000 as presented.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Cate Steel
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Eric Bauman, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Alisa Bradley, Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, Leigh Gianakos, and Cate Steel
Abstain: Jill Carini
