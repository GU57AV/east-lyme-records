6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – INSTRUCTION
A. Discussion/Information: Flanders School Principal Report - Mrs. Danielle Schoman
Principal Schoman shared work and events occurring at Flanders School that aligns with district and BOE goals. The
partnership with Electric Boat has begun planning for the next school year and will expose students to maritime trades, manufacturing, and buoyancy. Principal Schoman also shared the work with high level instructional practices and creating positive learning environments and responsive classrooms for culture and climate with student engagement. This effort has been a positive experience and influence for teachers.
Principal Schoman introduced students from the 4
th
grade Leadership Club:
Ada Dombrowski, Eliza Roosen, Edie Faulkner, Ishaan Manickam, and Jack Farrior. Each student shared the
qualities and strengths needed to be safe, responsible, and respectful school leaders; the clubs’ community projects such as food drives and knitting hats to babies in need; how they support building-wide expectations; and their service as hallway monitors and assistance with welcoming new students to Flanders.
Dr. Purohit praised the students for their interesting presentation and leadership skills.
7. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. Discussion/Information: 2024-2025 Budget Review
Mr. Bauman, BOE Chair, and Superintendent Newton shared the proposed
budget update sheet
for the 2024/25 school year. Discussion ensued with the Board Members and Administration regarding all factors into the budget, such as: the addition of an East Lyme police officer to support the district during the school year; the estimated costs of health insurance; the logistics and costs of moving the management of nursing services to the district; and supporting school pride, culture, and climate through cosmetic upgrades in facilities.
The Board consensus was to move the Superintendent’s proposed budget forward to the Public Hearing on 2/26/24.
B. Discussion/Information: High School Walkthrough Follow-up Discussion
Board members that participated in the High School Walkthrough on 1/31/24 shared it was a positive and valuable experience to view the high school from a different lens. The tour included viewing building facilities and visiting various classrooms. Dr. Purohit and Mrs. Fairchild thanked the Superintendent and the high school for the opportunity.
C. Discussion/Information: 10- Year Capital Plan Review
Dr. Lund reviewed the
10-year capital plan
and discussed the prioritization of projects presented. Discussion ensued between board members and Dr. Lund regarding concerns with building infrastructure and preventative functionality replacements with areas like plumbing and switchboards to ensure and maintain regular operations in the district.
8. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – POLICY
A. Discussion: First Review – Suggested Revision of Policy in the 4000 series
Ms. Bradley and Superintendent Newton shared the revision of Policy 4111.3 - Increasing Educator Diversity Plan that has been revised based on recent state legislation. All comments should be submitted to Superintendent Newton.
