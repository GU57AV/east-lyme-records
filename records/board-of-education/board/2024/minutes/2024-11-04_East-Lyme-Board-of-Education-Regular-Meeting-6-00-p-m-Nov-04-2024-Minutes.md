6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. Discussion: Director of Athletics Report (BOE Goal 2)
Kevin Marcoux, Director of Athletics, reported on the high school and middle school sports this fall, team championships won, and his focus on his second year at East Lyme. Mr. Marcoux provided updates on social media and marketing efforts, facility updates and repurposing equipment and spoke about fostering student athletes at ELPS. Mr. Marcoux also shared his work with building relationships with donors such as the Beagary Charitable Trust.
Cate Steel thanked Mr. Marcoux for his dedication and service to East Lyme and asked for clarification on athlete recognition and the impact of sports on the academics of East Lyme students. Mr. Marcoux reiterated the focus of academic priorities for students and how they are “student athletes”. He shared there is a small number of students that are ineligible based on their academic performance, which is a high standard.
Bill Derry thanked Mr. Marcoux for his efforts focusing on student character, sportsmanship, and being good citizens.
Alisa Bradley appreciated Mr. Marcoux’s holistic approach, and the community built within sports. She shared the growth of middle school sports has been a welcome, impressive addition.
Chair Bauman appreciates Mr. Marcoux’s dedication to social media updates and the work towards cultivating donations for the program.
B. Discussion: Middle School – Boys & Girls Track and Field (BOE Goal 2)
Mr. Marcoux and Superintendent Newton initiated discussions on the implementation of beginning a track team at the middle school level for this upcoming spring. Initial costs are estimated at ten thousand dollars and would mostly consist of coach stipends. Superintendent Newton will finalize costs with the Business Office.
