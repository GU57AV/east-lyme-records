5.DISCUSSION/ACTION- INSTRUCTION
A. DISCUSSION/ACTION: International Experience Program
Superintendent Newton shared the student international exchange program, International Experience, would like to become an approved program to host exchange students at East Lyme High School. International Experience has been in business since 2002 and has had 2,400 host families and placed approximately 540 students since then.
Discussion ensued amongst the Board members regarding logistics with school involvement and student placement.
Eric Bauman wants to check in mid-year on any participating students for feedback about the program.
Motion to approve including International Experience Program at East Lyme Public Schools
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Cate Steel
Final Resolution: Motion Carried

6.  DISCUSSION/ACTION – FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. DISCUSSION: High School/Facility Update with Walkthrough
Dr. Lund provided brief updates on facility updates and construction on the school buildings over the summer months and early fall.
The South Gym floor replacement at the high school will take place in September, which will allow a week for demolition and replacement.
Diane Nazarko, project facilities manager, hosted a walkthrough of the high school building to allow Board members to view the work in progress occurring which began at 6:27 pm. The Board returned from walkthrough the at 6:54 pm.
Further discussion ensued regarding additional facility projects and the approach to prioritizing projects to maximize funding and the need to ensure that all facilities and infrastructure are well maintained.
