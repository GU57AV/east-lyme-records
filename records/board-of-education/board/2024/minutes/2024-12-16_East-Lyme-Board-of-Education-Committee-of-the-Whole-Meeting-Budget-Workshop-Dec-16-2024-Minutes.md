1. OPENING OF MEETING
A. Call to Order
Meeting started at 6:08 p.m. Chair Eric Bauman called the meeting to order at 6:16 p.m. when a qurorum was present.
2. DISCUSSION
A. Discussion/Workshop: School Budget Planning for FY 2025-26
Superintendent Newton reviewed the
Budget Presentation
and began the open discussion on the proposed budget numbers. Discussion amongst administrators and board members focused on factors and significant cost drivers contributing to this initial projection, such as salaries, health insurance and benefits, variables in tuition for student services, and transportation.
Superintendent Newton reviewed the cuts and eliminations made from the initial budget workshop, such as the elimination request of a full-time floating nurse position. Kim Davis provided additional information on the categorization of student services and the dynamic nature of her department. Discussion amongst the group included: understanding the number of students that are in the umbrella of student services, the different needs between emotional and intellectual disabilities, state qualifiers, and the effects of out-of-district placements.
Discussions also included certified staff retirement plans, enrollment and curriculum needs, and ensuring that cuts do not affect instructional quality. Board members would like to consider enrollment and projection numbers, supplemental positions, and the efficiency of existing bus routes as potential cost savings.
The Superintendent Budget Presentation is scheduled for January 13, 2025.
