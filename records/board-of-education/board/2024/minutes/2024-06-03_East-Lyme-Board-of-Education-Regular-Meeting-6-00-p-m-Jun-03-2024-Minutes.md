2. SPECIAL RECOGNITION
A. CABE Student Leadership Awards – ELMS
Claudine Kelly introduced the two CABE Middle School Scholars: Maeve Graesch and Ankita Basu.
Students are nominated by the staff and two students have been selected by the Life Arts teachers to be considered for this award. This years’ recipients are positive role models, lead their peers by example, and demonstrate leadership qualities such as: thoughtfulness, resiliency, kindness, and diplomacy.  They also consistently produce quality work and participate in several extracurricular activities.
B. Viking Valor Award – Reading Pilot Committee
Assistant Superintendent Annaliese Spaziano presented Viking Valor awards to 40 elementary level teachers and curriculum leadership team in recognition of their efforts in the reading piloting program, some of which participated in three rounds of pilots to select the final curriculum. These staff members demonstrated commitment, grit, and determination through this challenging process.
