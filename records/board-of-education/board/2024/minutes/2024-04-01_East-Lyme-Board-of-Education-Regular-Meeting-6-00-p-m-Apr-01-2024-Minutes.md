3. SPECIAL RECOGNITION
A. ELHS – All State Music Festival
Mrs. Kelly spoke about the All-State Music Festival and the audition requirements for students to be selected. Eight ELHS students were selected to perform at the festival. Mrs. Kelly introduced Emma Clausen (Piccolo) and Will Dumond (Tenor Saxophone) that were in attendance.
Dr. Purohit inquired how often the students practice and if they plan to continue to study music in college. He also commended them for their achievements and acknowledged that this honor is extremely challenging.
B. Niantic Center School – Viking Valor Award
Principal Provost presented the Viking Valor Award, to Gil Gallant, Districtwide Elementary Technology Instructor. Mr. Provost spoke of Mr. Gallant’s dedication to enhancing technology integration in schools and utilizing technology effectively with a creative, hands-on approach.
Mr. Gallant introduced the student Tech Squad, an initiative he began in this role, which allows district students in second through fourth grade to build leadership and their technological skills while helping their school community. Mr. Gallant shared a short
video
highlighting the Tech Squad. Following the video, students in attendance assisted Board members with routine device issues such as clearing a browser cache.

5. DISCUSSION/ACTION – INSTRUCTION
A. DISCUSSION/INFORMATION: Niantic Center School Principal Report – Jeff Provost
Principal Provost introduced Jill Thomson, first grade teacher, Kim Hill, literacy consultant, Sue Muir, math coach, and Lynn Sullivan, third grade teacher. The group shared how they implement social emotional learning, explicit instruction, and high impact strategies and foster culture, climate, and diversity, equity, and inclusion at Niantic Center School and spoke how their work incorporates the Board’s goals.
Bill Derry commented on the challenge of fostering climate and culture and thanked Mr. Provost for his leadership.

Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Esteban Garcia
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, Bill Derry, Olivia Fairchild, Esteban Garcia, Vivek Purohit, and Cate Steel
