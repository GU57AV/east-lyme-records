6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. Discussion: LBH & Aquatics Center – Roof Project Updates
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the planning and timeline for the roof replacements at the Aquatics Center & Lillie B. Haynes School. The Department of Administrative Services is behind in the approval process for reimbursements. The facilities and business departments are working to expedite this process with the goal of the roof work being performed this summer. Contractor bidding would need to be completed by February or March.
Working with the Town to have all the necessary paperwork and procedures completed to begin the project in a timely manner. Superintendent Newton will ensure the board is updated as the projects progress.
