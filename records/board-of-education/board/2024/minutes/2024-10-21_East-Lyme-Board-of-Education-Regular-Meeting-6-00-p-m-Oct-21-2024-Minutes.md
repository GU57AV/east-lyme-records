7. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – INSTRUCTION
A. Discussion/Information: Director of Student Services Update (BOE Goal 3)
Mrs. Kimberly Davis, Director of Student Services, shared her team has been focused on:
Supporting Multilanguage learners through Professional Development facilitated by LEARN. Attendees include ML teachers, administrators, counselors, and literacy coaches.
Working with First Student to provide families consistency and routine with bus transportation
Providing functional learning opportunities for students with learning disabilities that also foster student engagement and the development of fine motor and executive function skills.
Nursing Services are now managed within the district. Nursing Supervisor, Tiffany Cole, shared with Waterford Public Schools, is transitioning well into the role; nursing staff are happy to be a part of the ELPS team
Creative Playschool is beginning the process to upgrade their playground at LBH. Project funding would be through revenue. UltiPlay is a state approved vendor. Mrs. Davis is hoping to have the Board approve the project at the next meeting, to allow installation to occur in the spring.
Leigh Gianakos asked about the implementation of the new ELA program, Wit & Wisdom, and program differentiation concerns for Special Education teachers and students. Mrs. Davis shared that there are no state guidelines on caseload and that it is not always equivalent to workload. Mrs. Gianakos requested the student caseload numbers in each school.
Bill Derry asked for further confirmation that the district is equipped to support ML Learners and families in district. Mrs. Davis shared that in addition to the ML PD provided, the district subscribes to Language Line for immediate translation access, which is available for all staff members. The department is continually working to ensure information on the website can be translated with ease for multilanguage families as needed.
B. Discussion/Information: High School Principal Report (BOE Goal 1& 3)
Ms. Deb Roselli-Kelly provided the following updates on East Lyme High School:
Spirit Week began today and the ELHS community enthusiastically participates
AP, SAT & PSAT testing were administered successfully last week without technical issues
ELHS has been celebrating Hispanic Heritage month, a member of the Spanish Honor Society would recite the Pledge of Allegiance in English & Spanish each morning and the SHS created a banner celebrating Hispanic Pioneers of Change which was displayed in the Commons.
Tri-M Music Honor Society recently inducted new members
Behavioral referrals have reduced since last year and keeping student cell phone use out of the classroom during instructional time has been successful
Utilizing feedback from Satchel Pulse surveys to improve school environment such as bathroom accessibility and the possibility of implementing a digital hall pass program
School leaders executed the first ELHS Learning Walk, supporting teaching strategies learned in recent PD and providing affirmative feedback.
ECE classes are being added to support student learning opportunities such as English for College writing, AP Seminar & Research
Pathways Program grows with the addition of culinary and woodworking classes
ELHS was awarded EdRising Mini grant to encourage student enthusiasm to pursue degrees in education. The grant has allowed the formation of a new club at school and solidifying educator career pathways beginning in middle school through high school
The fall drama production of Little Women will be performed at ELHS 11/7-11/10
The school is hosting a Veteran’s Day Assembly and lunch on 11/11 – veteran speaker Kevin McMahon will share his story and experience with ELHS students
The Food Scrap Collection Fundraiser exceeded the initial goal of $7,500 – currently raised $8,219
A brief discussion amongst board members and Ms. Roselli-Kelly ensued on class option popularity, scheduling, academic levels, and a new model with co-teaching college prep Math & English classes.
