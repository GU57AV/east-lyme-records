C. Discussion/Information: Board of Education Committee Assignments CY 2025
Chairman Bauman initiated discussions with the committee assignments for calendar year 2025. Discussions amongst board members ensued about the necessity of maintaining the Finance Facilities & Operations Committee and the Facilities Asset Management committees and if it is worth combining. Discussions included policy implications and concerns of maintaining the ad hoc committees if groups are not meeting consistently.
Considerations include dissolving Planetarium ad-hoc committee and possibly combining FFO with FAM committees. The Board will hold the first scheduled FFO meeting to allow thorough discussion.
