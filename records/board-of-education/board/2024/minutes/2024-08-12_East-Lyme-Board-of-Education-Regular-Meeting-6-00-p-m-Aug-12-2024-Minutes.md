7. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A. Discussion: High School Facilities Update
Chair Bauman provided update from the Facilities Asset committee discussions from their last meeting and the prioritization of renovation projects at the high school based on funding available. Dr. Lund provided updates on the status of
facilities projects
at the high school and plans for upcoming projects.
B. Discussion/Action: Student Pricing for Food Services
The Food Services department proposed an increase to the
school lunch prices
based on the federal weighted average requirement to increase the difference between the district and federal average. Discussion occurred amongst the board members on the proposed school lunch price increase.  Superintendent Newton shared how he would communicate the price change to families before the beginning of the school year.
Ms. Barr Shelburn shared her concerns about the proposed increase amount and would like to keep it as minimal as possible to support all ELPS families. Discussion occurred regarding food services’ operational needs, the effects of inflation, and the strategy on handling future increases amongst all board members.
Motion to approve the revised student lunch pricing to ten cents per meal price at each school level for the 2024/25 school year.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Cate Steel
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Esteban Garcia, Leigh Gianakos, Vivek Purohit, and Cate Steel
8. ADMINISTRATION REPORTS
A. Superintendent of Schools - Mr. Jeffrey Newton
Introduced his superintendent intern, Peter Camp from Norwich Public Schools.
Summer Staff lunch held last week and was successful and fun despite the weather and location change. We plan to continue the event in future.
Flanders Kindergarten registration is increasing and just above the recommended guidelines established by the Board. Superintendent Newton’s recommendation is to focus on para support within the classroom and not hire a teacher until the enrollment dictates the specific need. He will communicate further as the school year begins.
Legislative update – the BOE can create their own non-lapsing account  without the Board of Finance approval. The BOE can begin to contribute 2% of yearly budget to set aside once the account is approved by the Board.
Convocation is next week, the Board is welcome to attend
Planning for the beginning of the school year continues.
B. Assistant Superintendent of Schools - Mrs. Annaliese Spaziano
New staff orientation next Tuesday and Wednesday – 12 certified staff have been hired amongst all buildings. Teachers are assigned a building buddy to help orient them to the building and is their established go-to person for the year as they transition to ELPS.
Ms. Spaziano has been working on a cyber security grant to offset new server upgrades. Past software purchases have been reimbursed based on risk assessments given at the district level.
C. Finance, Marketing and Growth Management - Mrs. Maryanna Stevens
Finishing up the year end reporting for the 2023-24 budget. There will be approximately $37,000 returned to the Town.
Food services and Aquatics will be added to the MUNIS software.
Pool financials currently show a $19,000 profit and food services accounts are still being cleaned up.
FY25/26 budget is not available on MUNIS yet and she is unsure if she will be able to use it for the budget as the town is not closed out. Will keep the BOE posted.
