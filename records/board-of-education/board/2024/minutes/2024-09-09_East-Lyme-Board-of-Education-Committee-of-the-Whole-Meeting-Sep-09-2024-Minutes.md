A. Discussion/Information: Presentation and Dialogue on Artificial Intelligence
Matt Mervis, Director, Skills21 & AI Strategy at EdAdvance, presented and facilitated discussions on Artificial Intelligence. Discussion topics included:
Using ChatGPT and its’ alternatives – apps that use tone similar to real voice models
Biases, errors, or misinformation generated through AI searches
Fostering and creating values-based BOE policy; ensuring academic integrity and ethical use
Gathering information for the purpose of connection and engagement
Using AI as a Support Tool for teachers and students
AI allowing for greater accessibility and inclusivity in learning
