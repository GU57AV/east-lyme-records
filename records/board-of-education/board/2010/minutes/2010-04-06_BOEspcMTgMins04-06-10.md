                                       EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION
                                            East Lyme, Connecticut

                                    Board of Education Special Meeting Minutes
                                              Tuesday, April 6, 2010
                                                     6:00 p.m.
                                         Superintendent’s Office, Central Office
                                             Board of Education Building


          Board Members Present:      Tim Hagen (Chair), Beth Groeber (Secretary), Mary Broderick,
                                      Melissa Ford, Al Littlefield, Kevin Seery, Rich Steel, Laura Greenstein

          Board Members Absent:       Jamie Ellis, Marlene Nickerson

          Administrators Present:     Dr. James Lombardo (Superintendent)



          I. Call to Order – 6:05 p.m.

        II.   Public Comment - None

        III. Executive Session

MOTION:       Al Littlefield moved to enter into executive session to interview Assistant Superintendent for
              Special Education and Pupil Personnel candidates and to include the Superintendent of Schools;
              Rich Steel seconded.

VOTE:   Unanimous                                                                      MOTION CARRIED

        The Board exited executive session at 8:45 p.m. and reconvened the special meeting.

        IV. Discussion/Action
           1. Discussion/action re: Proposed Appointment of Assistant Superintendent for Special
               Education and Pupil Personnel

MOTION:       Al Littlefield moved to authorize the Dr. Lombardo to enter into negotiations with the selected
              candidate for the appointment of Assistant Superintendent for Special Education and Pupil
              Personnel for the East Lyme Public Schools with an effective start date of July 1, 2010; Melissa
              Ford seconded.

VOTE:         Unanimous                                                                MOTION CARRIED

              2. Discussion/action re: Proposed Action on Temporary Administrative Assignments
                 East Lyme High School Interim Principal Dr. John Sullivan’s last work day is April 30, 2010.

MOTION:       Kevin Seery moved to appoint Jeffrey Provost as Interim 1.0 Principal of East Lyme High School
              for the period May 1, 2010 through June 30, 2010, as presented; Al Littlefield seconded.

VOTE:         Unanimous                                                                 MOTION CARRIED


MOTION:       Mary Broderick moved to appoint Jason Bitgood as Interim 1.0 Assistant Principal of East Lyme
              High School for the period May 1, 2010 through June 18, 2010, as presented; Al Littlefield
              seconded.

VOTE:         Unanimous                                                                 MOTION CARRIED

        V.    Public Comment - None

        VI.   Adjournment

MOTION:       Laura Greenstein moved to adjourn the special meeting at 8:51p.m.; Al Littlefield seconded.
