           4. Discussion re: Proposed Superintendent’s Recommendation for Phase II Race to the Top (RTTT)
              Application
              Dr. Lombardo presented information and the timeline for Phase II of Race to the Top. Dr. Lombardo
              recommended that both the Board of Education and the East Lyme Teachers’ Association do not proceed
              further with the RTTT application and suggested they do not sign the Memorandum of Understanding
              agreement that is due May 19, 2010. The consensus of the Superintendent and the Board was that the RTTT
              direction is not appropriate for East Lyme Public Schools and the monetary resources that the District would
              be need to allocate for the required attendance at workshops and meetings, along with the assignment of an
              RTTT point person, would far surpass the $48, 358.00 (based on Title I) that the District would receive per
              year for four years. No action was required.

           5. Discussion re: Superintendent’s Recommendation for Physical Education Program Grant
              Dr. Lombardo informed the Board of his endorsement of the District PE Department’s efforts in applying for
              a federal grant from the Carol M. White Physical Education Program sponsored by the Department of
              Education and the Office of Safe and Drug Free Schools. Lisa Kriger (ELMS PE Teacher) and Amy Fabry
              (ELHS PE Teacher) were available for questions and noted that the awards will range from $100,000.00 to
              $500,000.000. As part of the application process, height and weight of students, without names, will need to
              be submitted. Parents will have the option to have their child not participate in the data collection. No action
              was required.
