VII. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
     A. Instruction
        1. Continuation of Discussion Regarding ELHS Culinary Arts Program and Facilities Changes
           Board members were given the opportunity to share concerns and to ask further questions of Dr. Karen
           Costello, Freda Gianakos and Pat Newton. Dr. Sullivan reassured the Board that discussion with special
           education staff has taken place and a suitable relocation of the classroom will be made. The importance of
           providing opportunities for our students to find a personal vision of success was recognized.

             As a result of Board discussion, the following information was requested:
             1. a philosophy statement
             2. an estimate of ongoing lab maintenance costs
             3. professional development needs
             4. how the program will connect with other disciplines
             5. program opportunities provided to special education students ages 15-21
             6. are fair opportunities being afforded to all vocational educational programs in the district
             7. actual costs associated with the expansion
             8. what is needed to replace the current facilities without the proposed expansion

             Following receipt of this information, the AAA and FFO committees are expected to move toward a vote at
             their next meeting on March 15, 2010.
