   VII. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
        A. Finance/Facilities/Community
           1. Discussion re: Presentation of Superintendent’s Proposed Budget for 2010-2011
              Dr. Lombardo presented his proposed budget for 2010-2011 which represents a 3.5% increase over the 2009-
              2010 budget which includes but does not designate an additional $250,000 in reductions. The proposed
              budget supports all instructional and planning initiatives. Major increases are in property services, health
              benefits, tuition and instructional improvement, mostly in special education. Teachers and administrators
              have a zero-percent increase in salaries and instructional supplies have also been been leveled. Staff changes
              include the addition of one K-12 Special Education Coordinator which will be funded by stimulus funds and
              paraprofessional reductions. The proposed budget is available on the district website at
              www.eastlymeschools.org. A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, January 19, 2010 at 7:30 p.m. at East
              Lyme High School to receive public comment relative to the 2010-2011 proposed school district budget.

          C. Personnel
              1. Discussion/action re: Superintendent’s Recommendation for Administrative Support for Special Education
                 Based on a number of recent changes, Dr. Lombardo has rethought his original proposal for leadership in the
                 area of special education. In effect, when fully implemented after July 1, the district will have an Assistant
                 Superintendent for Special Education and Pupil/Personnel Services, supported by, at a minimum, one K-12
                 Special Education Coordinator. While the previous proposal had this position as a Teacher Leader (governed
                 by the ELTA collective bargaining agreement). The new coordinator will be an administrator and will be
                 governed by the ELAA collective bargaining agreement, which already identifies this 10-month position in
                 their bargaining unit. Dr. Lombardo views the change as critical to securing the appropriate skills necessary
                 to support the needs of the district, including reducing special education costs and providing children with
                 more effective service. He explained that the plan is an important investment that will be more than covered
                 by cost savings in the coming years. Additionally, the interim Director of Special Education Dr. Connie
                 Berglund’s services will end on January 31, 2010 and Dr. Lombardo requested that the services of
                 Administrative Consultant to the Superintendent Mr. Wayne Sweeney be extended until a replacement is
                 found.

MOTION:        Mary Broderick moved to approve extending the contractual agreement with Mr. Wayne Sweeney,
               Administrative Consultant to the Superintendent of Schools, for up to the remainder of the 2009-2010 school
               year, i.e. June 30th, with the same terms as approved by the Board of Education for the first 90 days of the
               agreement; Kevin Seery seconded.

VOTE:          UNANIMOUS                                                                            MOTION CARRIED
