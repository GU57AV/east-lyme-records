     I.   CALL TO ORDER – 7:30 p.m.
    II.   PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE – Led by the Board of Education
   III.   PUBLIC COMMENT - None

   IV.    DISCUSSION/ACTION
          1. Discussion re: ELPS Curriculum Development, Design and Implementation Presentation
             Dr. Karen Costello, Administrator for Program Improvement, provided a PowerPoint presentation on the ELPS
             current curriculum development, design, implementation and renewal process. Also included was information
             regarding CT education laws, East Lyme Board of Education Policy 6140, and Curriculum Council processes.
             The Board thanked Dr. Costello for the overview and requested more specific examples to further exemplify
             how the process works.

          2. Discussion/action re: Superintendent’s Recommendation for Use of the Education JOBS Federal Funds for
             ELPS
             Dr. Lombardo’s recommendation for use of the Education JOBS (Ed Jobs) Federal Funds for the 2010-2011
             school year was presented. The 2-year grant requires funding be used to save or create education jobs to ensure
             that students receive vital educational and related services. The remaining funding must be used by September
             30, 2012.

MOTION:       Al Littlefield moved to approve the expenditure of $126,000 of the $415,000 Education JOBS Funds by the
              Superintendent for the 2010-2011 school year (0.5 FTE Assistant Principal for Alternative Education Program,
              0.2 FTE music teacher at MS, 1.0 FTE Administrative Assistant I at FL/CO and 0.5 FTE Kindergarten teacher at
              NC), as presented; Laura Greenstein seconded.

VOTE:         UNANIMOUS                                                                              MOTION CARRIED

    V.    PUBLIC COMMENT – None
