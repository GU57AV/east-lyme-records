 I. Call to Order – 7:25 p.m.

II.   Discussion/Action
      1. Discussion re: Proposed 2009/2010 ELPS Calendar Change
          Following the recent server crash which affected the East Lyme High School and some areas
          of Central Office, there remains a need to allow teachers to spend some time recovering lost
          files. Dr. Lombardo requests that a change to the calendar year be made to end classes 1.5
          days earlier than originally scheduled for the high school. With this change, June 16th would
          be the last day of school, and would be a full day. Teachers would use the 1.5 days to work
          on retrieving, restoring, and potentially reconstructing their lost files.


      2.   Discussion/action re: Reconsideration of Race to the Top (RTTT) Application
           An emailed letter, addressed to Tim Hagen, was presented to the Board from Karen Rak, 27
           Black Point Rd, Niantic, CT. In the letter, she expressed her continued concern over the
           participation of ELPS in the RTTT and likewise disappointment over the discussion being
           held outside of the regular board location (where more full discussion and community
           participation could ensue).

           Dr. Lombardo presented his rationale for signing in to participate in RTTT as follows:

           1. The CT Department of Education has revised its allocation for East Lyme to $188,000,
           not the original $48,000 figure.
           2. New state legislation law will require CT school districts to develop and implement many
           of the same provisions that are part of the RTTT grant application. Thus, most of the
           provisions will be enacted, whether or not we are participating in the grant itself.
           3. SE CT regional superintendents will work together as a consortium with LEARN to
           develop a plan that meets our needs. ELPS will have 90 days if the state gets the grant to
           develop our plan. During that 90-day period, East Lyme can withdraw from the grant and the
                                                                                                     4579

              consortium with no penalty, except for the fact that we still would be required to implement
              the provisions of new CT legislation.
              4. East Lyme should have a seat at the table to have some influence for how the grant and
              plan unfold.

              Of note, the deadline to sign in for the program is Wed, May 19th.

MOTION:   Mary Broderick motioned that while the ELBOE has serious reservations regarding the RTTT
