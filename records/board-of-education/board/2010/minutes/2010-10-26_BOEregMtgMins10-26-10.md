B. Instruction

1. Discussion/presentation re: ELPS CMT, CAPT, SAT, ACT, AP Presentation

Dr. Lombardo presented ELPS standardized test report for 2010. The CT Mastery Test (CMT) is
administered in grades 3-8 in reading, math, writing and science in grades 5 and 8. EL students continue to
do well; however, last year’s grade 3 scores have raised concerns especially in reading. Teachers and literacy
specialists continue to meet regularly to monitor progression and assess instruction. Dr. Lombardo is meeting
4619

with principals to ensure instructional alignment and to develop improvement strategies. The CT Academic
Performance Test (CAPT) is administered in grade 10 and tests students in the areas of writing, reading,
math, and science. Percentages of at or above proficiency remain consistently high. Some concern was noted
in the percentage drop of students at or above goals in math, science and reading; however, the state
percentages dropped as well. Forty percent of test takers were recognized as CAPT Scholars achieving goal
(4) or advanced level (5) on all of the CAPT subtests. Scholastic Aptitute Tests (SAT) are taken in grades 11
and 12 and are used for college admissions to determine college readiness. The trend continues with boys
scoring slightly higher in Math and girls higher in reading and writing. SAT II subject tests showed East
Lyme’s best scores ever in U.S. History, Math Level IIC, Biology-M, and Chemistry. Three students were
recognized as National Merit Finalists. American College Testing (ACT) is taken in grades 11 and 12 and is
also used by college admissions offices and tests English, math, reading and science. Our students continue
to perform better than the state and national levels. Dr. Lombardo reported 230 students took 468 AP tests
with a high number of students receiving the highest possible score in the areas of Calculus AB, Calculus BC
and Psychology. Small classes with low performance for several years are being analyzed.
