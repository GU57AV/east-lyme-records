   II.   Discussion re: Board of Education Self-Evaluation

With facilitation help from Robert Rader, CABE Executive Director, the Board conducted a self-
evaluation and identified several ideas and thought for future follow-up. In addition, several ideas were
capture as potential future Board goals.

                                     Ideas and Thoughts for Action

    •    The Board can continue to improve how it uses data and its process for following-up on specific
         recommendations.
    •    The Board and Superintendent should meet twice a year to discuss goals and progress against
         current goals.
    •    The Board should have specific goals for the Board. These may support District goals but should
         be more focus on the Board’s responsibilities and performance. For example, a Board goal could
         be to for the Board to learn more on how to evaluate student achievement based upon data. The
         Board should have 3 to 4 specific goals per year.
    •    Board goals should be set during each summer and rolled out with the district’s goals at the start
         of a new school year.
    •    Board group readings and other learning processes should be conducted in which discussion
         occurs within the Board and within the community.
    •    The Board should review its Code of Ethics every year.
    •    New Board member orientation should be followed.
    •    The Board should conduct a yearly assessment on Policy Manual.
    •    With the District embarking on a process for setting vision and strategic direction, a Board goal
         should be to prepare the Board for their role in adopting a strategic plan for the district.
