                             EAST LYME BOARD OF EDUCATION

                          Board of Education Special Meeting Minutes
                                 Thursday, February 11, 2010
                             Assistant Superintendent’s Office, Central Office

Board Members Present:           Tim Hagen (Chair), Mary Broderick, Melissa Ford, Al Littlefield,
                                 Marlene Nickerson, Kevin Seery (Acting Secretary), Rich Steel,

  II.    Public Comment – None

MOTION:         Marlene Nickerson moved to enter into executive session for the purpose of conducting

                the Superintendent of Schools; Melissa Ford seconded.

VOTE:           Unanimous                                                        MOTION CARRIED

The Board exited executive session at 8:07 p.m.

   IV. Discussion/action re: Proposed Appointment of High School Principal

MOTION:         Kevin Seery moved to authorize the Superintendent of Schools to negotiate a contract
                with Michael Susi for the position of East Lyme High School Principal; Al Littlefield
                seconded.

VOTE:           Unanimous                                                        MOTION CARRIED

   V.    Public Comment – None

  VI.    Adjournment - 8:10 p.m.
