  VII.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
         A. Finance/Facilities/Community
            1. Discussion/presentation re: ELHS Athletic Complex Task Force Presentation Regarding Field Improvements
               William Seymour, P.E. and Director, Civil Engineering, Gale Associates, and Athletic Complex Renovation
               Committee Chair Carl Reichard gave a comprehensive presentation on the East Lyme High School Athletic
               Complex Improvement Project. Project goals include the assessment of current programs and existing
               facilities, the development of a needs assessment, schematics, phasing plans and cost estimates. The mission
               statements of the East Lyme High School, the Board of Education and the Town of East Lyme are all
                                                                                                                    4604

                 committed to the role of sports in the education system. The existing facility has served the district and our
                 community very well; however, it has outlived reasonable life expectancy. Assessment results and proposed
                 improvements of the track, bleacher, press box, field, lighting and other related sites were reviewed.
                 Although the track (23 years old) and the bleacher structure (32 years old) were well constructed, both have
                 safety and compliance issues. The field lacks proper drainage and crowning and is unusable during the
                 summer. Options for natural turf and synthetic turf were presented which included comparative maintenance
                 costs and cost estimates, player safety information, and environmental considerations. The ELHS Athletic
                 Complex Improvement Committee recommended the reconstruction of the ELHS Athletic Complex to
                 include 1) a new all weather track; 2) new bleachers; 3) new lights; a new artificial multi-use field with
                 increased storage. The presentation is available on the district website at eastlymeschools.org and
                 questions/comments may be directed to Dr. James Lombardo, Superintendent of Schools at
                 james.lombardo@eastlymeschools.org. A public hearing will be held on Thursday, October 7, 2010, 7:00
                 p.m. in the East Lyme High School auditorium. Carl Reichard was thanked for his time and effort in this
                 project.

There was no objection to moving up the following two agenda items: VII.B.1. Discussion/action re: Proposed ELHS Out-of-

                 Dr. Lombardo referred to the “Educating the Whole Child 2010-2011” goals chart and noted the addition
