  I. CALL TO ORDER– 6:30 p.m.

 II.    DISCUSSION
        1. Discussion re: Board of Education with ELPS Administrators Regarding ELPS Long-Range Plan
           Wayne and Annie Sweeney of Tomorrow’s Schools Today lead BOE members and administrators
           through several exercises intended to help draft the new ELPS mission statement and core beliefs.
           In breakout sessions teams discussed and added to work previously started by the steering
           committee. Additionally, breakout teams added to the theme of Educating the Whole Child to help
           further refine the strategies for improving our programs and district.

 III.    PUBLIC COMMENT
        1. Karen Rak, 27 Black Point Road, commented that it was a pleasure to see the passion of all the
           individuals and the work in progress. She would like to see it extended to other groups of
           individuals within the district.
