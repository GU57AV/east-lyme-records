 I. CALL TO ORDER AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE – 7:05 p.m. led by Tim Hagen and

II.   DISCUSSION/ACTION
      1. Discussion re: HS Accreditation Process Information
          Principal Mike Susi provided a comprehensive outline of the NEASC 10-year accreditation
          process for East Lyme High School. A team visit is scheduled for the fall (November 7-10,
          2010). More detailed information will follow.

      2.    Discussion/action re: Proposed ELPS District Goals for 2010-2011
           Dr. Lombardo provided a PowerPoint presentation of the proposed 2010-2011 district goals.
           The following proposed goals were established as a result of several collective meetings with
           staff, community members and Board of Education members:
            1) Ensure that each child is supported by caring adults;
            2) Ensure that an engaging and challenging program is provided for all students;
            3) Ensure that all children learn in a safe and healthy school environment and acquire the
                knowledge to live a safe and healthy lifestyle;
            4) Ensure that students are supported by the best leadership and work practices.
                                                                                                   4614

            Additional details for how these goals would be fulfilled were shared. Emphasis will be
            placed on engaging and developing appropriate partnerships with the greater East Lyme
            community; considering the time and resource impact of any new initiative; and exploring
            ways to use appropriate and emerging technologies for effective and efficient
            implementation.

MOTION:         Marlene Nickerson moved to adopt the proposed 2010-2011 ELPS District Goals as
                presented; Laura Greenstein seconded.


VOTE:          UNANIMOUS                                                  MOTION CARRIED

        3. Discussion re: Review and Discussion of Draft Board of Education Goals for 2010-2011
           Chair Tim Hagen reviewed the draft 2010-2011 Goals for the East Lyme Board of Education
           as follows:
           1) Evaluate and refine new BOE committee structure and process;
           2) Develop a process for superintendent evaluation;
           3) Review the Bylaws of the Board – Code of Ethics (9271-B);
           4) Review, define and implement the Board’s role and responsibilities in curriculum
               development for the district;
           5) Engage with the process for setting the new vision and strategic direction for the district
               and identify a Board goal to prepare the Board for their role in adopting and
               communicating the new strategic plan for the district.

            Goal #4 regarding the Board’s role and responsibilities in curriculum development for the
            district was referred to the AAA Committee with Dr. Lombardo. They will brainstorm and
            craft a proposal to bring forward to the Board.

 III.    PUBLIC COMMENT – None

         Superintendent’s Comments
         • ELHS English teacher and department head Pamela Nowak will receive the 2010 Ronald
           McDonald House Charities Local Hero Award on October 26, 2010 at the Governor’s
           residence. Ten teachers from CT and Western MA will be recognized for their hard work,
           dedication and commitment to students.
         • ELHS was rated with a score of 10 out of 10 by Education Nation. The HS staff was
           applauded for its effort.
