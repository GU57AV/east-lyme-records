 III. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
      A. Discussion re: 2010/2011 Superintendent’s Proposed Budget
         Dr. James Lombardo detailed portions of the 2010/2011 budget in two PowerPoint
         presentations entitled ELPS Budget Analysis – 1) Salaries and Benefits and 2) Purchased
         Services, Property Services, Transportation, Insurance, Communication, Dues and Fees. The
         proposed $40.5 million school budget reflects a 3.5% increase over the current year. Dr.
         Lombardo and administrators have spent significant time identifying areas where savings can
         be realized. Salaries represent 62.88% of the overall budget with no increases in salaries for
         administrators or teachers. Approximately 15.66% of the overall budget is dedicated to
         contractual or governmental obligations to provide benefit coverage to all employees. A 12%
         increase is predicted in health/life insurance premiums. The Board requested Dr. Lombardo
         prepare, for consideration, a budget reflecting 2% increase over the current calendar year.

        B. Discussion/action re: Proposed Change to 2009-2010 ELPS Staffing Plan – 1.0 Special
           Education Coordinator
           On January 11, 2010, Dr. Lombardo presented a plan to provide a more effective and cost
           efficient program for our special education students. The plan was developed in response to
           the Futures Task Force recommendation. Funding for the 1.0 Special Education Coordinator
           will be provided through 09/10 stimulus funds and through paraprofessional reductions in
           10/11.

MOTION:         Kevin Seery moved for a change to the ELPS 2009/2010 ELPS Staffing Plan to include
                the administrative position of 10-month 1.0 Special Education Coordinator, salary and
                benefits per the ELAA contract as presented; Mary Broderick seconded.

VOTE:           Unanimous                                                          MOTION CARRIED

         C. Discussion re: Update on HS Athletic Turf Field Discussion
            Kevin Seery stated the projected cost to install a synthetic turf field and to refurbish the track
            and the bleachers would be $1.5 million. Three firms have contributed bids for conducting a
            study/architectural design, ranging in cost from $16,000 - $18,000. Any action taken to
            initiate construction would still require full approval process. Kevin reiterated the need and
            benefits of the artificial turf field. Community support and involvement will be sought. A
            recommendation will be brought to the full Board at a future meeting.
