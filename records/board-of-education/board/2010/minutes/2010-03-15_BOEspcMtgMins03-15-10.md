                           Board of Education Special Meeting Minutes
                                    Monday, March 15, 2010
                                            6:30 p.m.

                             Judy DeLeeuw (MS Principal)


 I. Call to Order – 6:40 p.m. by Tim Hagen

II.   Public Comment
      1. Karen Rak, 27 Black Point Road, encouraged the district to fully withdraw the application for
         participation in Race to the Top (RTT) and expressed concerns how mandates will be funded.
      2. Bob Gadbois, 358 Boston Post Road, expressed concerns regarding the budget increase in
         light of declining enrollment and encouraged the Board to review Capital Improvement Plan
         “wants vs. needs.”

III. Discussion/Action
     1. Discussion re: 21st Century Skills Presentation – Dr. James Lombardo
         The presentation focused on how driving forces of change affect education and the
         framework of 21st century student outcomes to help them master the multi-dimensional
         abilities required of them in the 21st century. Dr. Lombardo stressed the importance of
         critical thinking and problem solving, communication, collaboration, and creativity and
         innovation. He emphasized the importance of building leadership skills in children and
         encouraged the audience to visit the following websites: KnowledgeWorks –
         (www.kwfdn.org); The Partnership for 21st Century Skills (www.p21.org); and 21st Century
         Schools (www.21stcenturyschools.com). The Board looks forward to further stimulation and
         engagement regarding this topic.

      2. Discussion/action re: Proposed Endorsement for Modification of HS Culinary Arts Program
         Responses to questions posed by the Board at the last meeting were distributed. Dr. Karen
         Costello confirmed that the proposed changes are consistent with the philosophy of the 5-12
         curriculum. Both the Academic, Athletic and Activities (AAA) and Finance, Facilities and
         Operations (FFO) Committees had met previously and reviewed the plans for curriculum
         enhancement and facility upgrade, respectively. The Board thanked the culinary arts teachers
         for their work.
MOTION:        Mary Broderick moved to endorse the modifications and enhancement of the ELHS
               Culinary Arts Program, as presented, with the understanding that continued review and
               discussion of the program details would take place; Marlene Nickerson seconded.

VOTE PRO:      Tim Hagen, Mary Broderick, Melissa Ford, Jaime Ellis,
               Marlene Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Rich Steel, Laura Greenstein

VOTE CON:      Beth Groeber                                                  MOTION CARRIED 8:1

        3. Proposed Endorsement of Inclusion of Funds to Town’s Capital Plan for HS Culinary Arts
