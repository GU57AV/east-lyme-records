2. RECOGNITIONS/AWARDS
Subject
A. Recognition: The CT State Department of Education's Recognition of ELHS for it's Exemplary Veterans Education Program
Meeting
Oct 15, 2018 - East Lyme Board of Education Regular Meeting
Category
2. RECOGNITIONS/AWARDS
Type
Recognition
The East Lyme Board of Education will recognize the following ELHS accomplishment:
East Lyme High School is being officially recognized by the Connecticut Department of Education as an exemplary veterans education program as part of the “Remembering Our Heroes: Teaching About Veterans” recognition program. This
distinction is given to schools that offer programs providing students with opportunities to learn about veterans' contributions and collaborate with local veterans’ organizations through classes, extracurricular activities, presentations and symposiums.
The school will be recognized for this accomplishment at a ceremony on Thursday, October 25, 2018 at 4:00 pm at the Department of Veterans Affairs in Rocky Hill.  The program was written by ELHS teachers Henry Kydd and Lori Singer with proof reading by Alexa Kydd.

6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Subject
A. Presentation: Proposed Kevin's Kourt to be Located at the Miracle League Field at Flanders School
Meeting
Oct 15, 2018 - East Lyme Board of Education Regular Meeting
Category
6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Type
Discussion, Information, Presentation
East Lyme Parks and Recreation Director Dave Putnam will present a proposed plan for Kevin's Kourts at Flanders School in association with the Miracle League Field.
Kevin's Kourts are inclusive basketball courts that feature ten stations created by Bankshot.  Representatives have determined that Flanders School would be a suitable site and have pledged monies to pay for the construction of the courts.  The Miracle League of SECT Board of Directors has approved the concept and would like to move forward in adding the courts to the proposed Miracle League Field at Flanders School.  The courts would be accessible for school recess and PE classes as well.
Courts have been constructed in West Hartford, Waterbury, Hartford and South Windsor.
