2. DISCUSSION
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion/Workshop Re: School Budget Planning for 2019-2020
Superintendent Newton stated the purpose of the budget workshop is to talk openly with the Board regarding the 2019-2020 Budget, to gauge the Board's priorities for the district, and to discuss/brainstorm collectively additional work that needs to occur to help offset costs.
Superintendent Newton took the Board through the ELPS Fiscal Year 2020 Proposed Budget Summary and noted that even with a combined total savings of $647,706 the draft budget to date reflects a 4.21% increase over the current year. He expressed frustration regarding some large cost items outside of his control (ie. health insurance, special education tuition, contractual obligations) and noted the administrative staff has spent countless hours reviewing the budget and were hoping to be at a much lower number.
Full discussion ensued and concerns and cost savings ideas shared.
Suggestions for exploration of ideas included:
1. Partnering with other school districts for transportation - currently do some ride sharing
2. Can we go to a third Kiva at the MS?
3. Stipends
4. Student Activity Fees
5. Virtual classes at the high school level
6. Review copier contract and usage
Chair Tim Hagen stated the administrative team has done a great job getting us to this point, but the Board needs to see options for bringing the budget down to under a 3% increase over the current year.
Board of Finance Members Lisa Picarazzi and Anne Santoro concurred with the Board consensus.
Superintendent Newton agreed that more work needs to be done and thanked everyone for their input. The administrative team will meet next week to discuss further reductions, and the proposed budget will be brought forward to the full Board on January 7, 2019.
