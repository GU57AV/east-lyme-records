6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information, Presentation:
A. Presentation: Proposed Kevin's Kourt to be Located at the Miracle League Field at Flanders School
East Lyme Parks and Recreation Director Dave Putnam updated the Board on the status of the Miracle League Field and presented a proposed plan for Kevin's Kourts at Flanders School in association with the Miracle League Field.
-To date, we are over the $200,000 fundraising mark for the Miracle League Field Project at Flanders School. He thanked Paige Kelly for starting a Miracle League Field Club at HS with over 50 student members.
-Kevin's Kourts are inclusive basketball courts that feature ten stations created by Bankshot. Representatives have determined that Flanders School would be a suitable site and have pledged $50,000 to pay for the construction of the courts. The Miracle League of SECT Board of Directors has approved the concept and would like to move forward in adding the courts to the proposed Miracle League Field at Flanders School. The courts would be accessible for school recess and PE classes as well. East Lyme will be the sixth court in CT.
-
Miracle League Field Fundraising Event at Mohegan Sun 11/16/18, 6:00-10:00 p.m.
The Board thanked Dave Putnam for his work and noted Kevin's Kourts would complement the Miracle League Field. The Board will further discuss this at FFO on 10/29/18 and bring to the BOE for approval on 10/29/18.
Discussion:
B. Discussion: Future Use of the LEARN Wing at Lillie B. Haynes School
Superintendent Newton asked the Board to ponder ideas for possible future uses of the LEARN wing at Lillie B. Haynes School. LEARN will vacate space this winter. Wing has 11 classrooms, 3 of which preschool program will move back into.
-Expand Preschool Program
-Relocate Coastal Connections Program to HA or Central Office
-Relocate Central Office
-Clarify the vision for each of the programs (Coastal Connections, Preschool, Central Office)
Chair Tim Hagen and Superintendent Jeff Newton will discuss Board input and return to the Board with a proposal on 10/29/18.
