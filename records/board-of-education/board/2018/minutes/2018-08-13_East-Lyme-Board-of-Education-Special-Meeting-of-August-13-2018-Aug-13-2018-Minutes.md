:
Elementary Alteration Project Update
O&G Construction Manager Joe Vetro updated the Board on the elementary alteration projects. Lillie B. Haynes (HA) paving is scheduled for the week of 8/20/18. The HA bus loop will be relocated to the access road for the start of the school. The HA rainwater drain back up problem has been temporarily fixed pending EPA review. NC fireproofing to bring to code will exceed the $30,000 limit that the Town Building Committee is authorized to approve and will need BOE approval when quotes are received. BOE members toured elementary buildings this afternoon. Superintendent Newton is working with Joe Vetro for timeframe when teachers can return, but the target date is 8/23/18. A meeting is scheduled with the building official on August 14 and the fire marshal will tour all elementary buildings on 8/21/18. Superintendent Newton noted that CO is working on final retrofitting for the Flanders classrooms at CO.
Action, Discussion:
D. Discussion/Action:
Summer Work Update, ELPS Technology Structure and Proposed One-Year Agreement with Custom Computers, Inc. for the 2018-2019 School Year
Suzanne McLaughlin, Vice President and General Manager of Custom Computers, Inc. presented a PowerPoint presentation entitled "ELPS Technology Remediation and Roadmap Status Update and Next Steps" dated August 13, 2018. (The presentation is attached to the 8/13/18 BOE meeting agenda in BoardDocs). She said Custom’s is on track for testing next week and the district will be up and running when teachers and students return. In addition, engineers will be onsite for tech support. The goal is to provide the District with a robust, comprehensive support model that brings timeliness, responsiveness, professionalism and experience to the end users. Included in the presentation was Custom's proposed technology support structure and additional services they would provide in the upcoming school year. Superintendent Newton stated Customs has done an extremely good job for us this summer and supports moving forward with the contract proposal for the 2018-2019 school year.
Move to authorize the Superintendent to enter into a one-year agreement with Custom Computers, Inc. in the amount of $357,000 for the 2018-2019 school year.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Candice Carlson.
