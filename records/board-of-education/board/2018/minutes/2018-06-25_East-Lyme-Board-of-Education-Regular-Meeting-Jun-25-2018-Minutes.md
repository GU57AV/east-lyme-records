Action, Discussion, Presentation: C. Discussion/Action re: Proposed Summer 2018 Contract with Custom Computer Specialists, Inc.
Suzanne McLaughlin, Vice President and CEO. noted that for the last six months the Custom Engineering Team has been working with the District's IT staff to review, remediate and troubleshoot technical issiues that the District has been experiencing. Based on the information collected, a list of recommendations was presented in a PowerPoint presentation entitled
East Lyme Public Schools, Technology Remediation and Road Map
. (The presentation is attached to the meeting agenda). She reported the current network configuration was not designed to support the rapid growth of technology in the District and the network needs to be rebuilt to support the needs of the district. Custom's goal for the end of summer is to redefine and redesign the network, therefore increasing performance and traffic. A team of engineers will be present when teachers and students return in August. Superintendent Newton stated the district cannot move forward with the long range plan until these issues have been addressed. The cost associated with this summer project is $89.095.41. Chair Tim Hagen asked that a update be provided to the Board in July.
Move to authorize the Superintendent to enter into a contract with Custom Computer Specialists, Inc. for summer 2018.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Eric Bauman.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Candice Carlson, Eric Bauman, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Jill Carini, John Kleinhans, Leigh Gianakos, Timothy Hagen, William Derry,
Abstain: Barbara Senges
Action, Discussion, Information: D. Discussion/Action re: Proposed Extension Regarding Use of Space at ELHS by the Planetarium Advisory Committee Through June 30, 2019
On June 6, 2018 Supt Newton reported the BOE ad hoc committee met with the Planetarium Advisory Committee at which time Diane Swan was asked to provide the full Board with a documented timeline and update on its non-profit/tax-exempt status for consideration to hold the space open at ELHS for potential use by the committee.
Chair Diane Swan stated non-profit corporation status (not tax exempt) has been attained and the committee has met with public trust, members of the rotary. Tax-exempt status has not been granted to date.
Spitz representative Joyce Towne presented information regarding a digital planetarium.
Andy Pappas stated the committee has moved from a business model to now asking for Board of Education's support in order for the project to be successful:
1) Written commitment from the BOE agreeing to hold the planetarium space at ELHS through 2019
2) Written endorsement from the BOE indicating support of the educational curriculum
3) Written endorsement from the Administration indicating support to the educational curriculum
He stated that originally ELPS would be charged $23,000 for students to use the facility, however, if the school board endorses this in the district curriculum, the facility could be used by ELPS students at no cost.
Diane Swan stated the timeline is to gain tax exempt status by 1/1/19, enter into a contract with a vendor by May 2019, and implementation in August 2019.
Board concerns were voiced regarding the aggressive timetable, what the ramifications would be to the Board if the project fails, and the committee's new request of #2 and #3 above (curriculum).
Move to continue to hold the planetarium space at ELHS for Stars to STEM Inc. for potential use until June 30, 2019
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Candice Carlson.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Barbara Senges, Candice Carlson, Eric Bauman, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Jill Carini, John Kleinhans, Leigh Gianakos, Timothy Hagen, William Derry
