DISCUSSION ITEMS
Discussion: A.
Consider Community Forum Input of April 12, 2018 and Continue Discussion Regarding Elementary Redistricting Options
-Superintendent Newton and Chair Tim Hagen started the discussion by reviewing the Community Forum input/comments received at the April 12 Community Forum.
-Mike Zuba, Milone & Macbroom, discussed the forum-received comments individually and provided the rationale as to why the proposed redistricting boundaries were as drawn.
-Discussion continued with the preferred option being Option 4A.
-Discussion ensued on recommended "tweaks" to the preferred Option 4A considering student travel time, neighborhood ontinuity, and maintaining building capacity.
-Theresa Landry, First Student, provided a compilation of the detailed bus run times and discussed student travel times within the redistricted Option 4A.  Travel times were not considerably increased and for a number of the runs the travel times were reduced.  First Student still needs to make minor adjustments of bus stop locations and bus run sequences to increase efficiencies.
-
At the end of the discussion, the Board consensus was that the following "tweaks" were to be made to the Option 4A redistricting maps (and to be referred to as Option 4AR)
:
1) Include in the Lillie B. Haynes School boundary the properties along the north side of Boston Post Road from Scott Road to the town line including Joshua Valley Road, Stones Ranch Road and Esther Pond.
2) Include in the Lillie B. Haynes School boundary a small area of Pennsylvania Avenue near Sleepy Hollow Road.  It was noted that this change doesn't impact any current ELPS children.
-
The Board-planned Elementary Redistricting Timeline is as follows
:
1) May 21, 2018, 6:00 p.m. - Elementary Redistricting Community Forum, Mary Broderick Auditorium, East Lyme High School
2) May 21, 2018, 7:00 p.m. - East Lyme Board of Education Regular Meeting for potential action on Elementary Redistricting
Discussion: B.
Brainstorm Ideas for Transitional Year
-Lillie B. Haynes Principal Melissa DeLoreto and Niantic Center School Principal Jeff Provost brought forward a list of student and family activities that would be held at HA and NCS to ease the transition for those students being moved to these schools as a part of the redistricting process.  Both principals, along with Flanders School Principal Linda Anania, spoke of activities that would involve all elementary schools.
-The Board felt that these transition-related activities should be brought forward to the next Redistricting Community Forum to share with the public as to what is being planned for students to ease "student/parent transitioning."
