Information: D. Public Comment
Shengquan Duan, 8 Hickory Court – East Lyme, Southeastern CT Chinese School, asked the Board to reconsider the rental fee for Sunday use of the high school auditorium and classrooms. He stated the Southeastern CT Chinese School is a non-profit organization and may consider another location due to recent notification of the increase in rental fees from $15,000 to $53,000 per year. He noted that over 300 children attend this school, 60% from town, and has a long standing history with EL.
Brian Staskowski, 3 Hickory Court - East Lyme, spoke in support of the Southeastern CT Chinese School. He said he has two children that attend the school and that is one of the reasons he moved to this town. He stated that no programs offer these type of opportunities for students and adults, and the school is an important contributor to the community.
James Archambault, 30 Cardinal Road - East Lyme, echoed the sentiments above and stated his two stepdaughters attended the Southeastern CT Chinese School and he is currently enrolled in the program as a student. He fully supports the school and spoke of the benefits and impacts of the program.
Nathan Sobol - 35 Scott Road - East Lyme, stated he supports the Southeastern CT Chinese School.  He encouraged the Board to support the program and to revisit the fee increase.

Discussion, Action: B. Proposed 2018-2019 Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement - Healthy Food Option
Motion:
Moved that pursuant to C.G.S. Section 10-215f, the board of education or governing authority certifies that all food items offered for sale to students in the schools under its jurisdiction, and not exempted from the Connecticut Nutrition Standards published by the Connecticut State Department of Education, will comply
with the Connecticut Nutrition Standards during the period of July 1, 2018 through June 30, 2019. This certification shall include all food offered for sale to students separately from reimbursable meals at all times and from all sources, including but
not limited to, school stores, vending machines, school cafeterias, and any fundraising activities on school premises sponsored by the school or by non-school
organizations and groups. Motion by Jamie Barr Shelburn, seconded by John Kleinhans.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro:
Albert Littlefield, Barbara Senges, Eric Bauman, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Jill Carini, John Kleinhans, Leigh Gianakos, Timothy Hagen, William Derry
Discussion, Action: C. Proposed 2018-2019 Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement - Exemption for Food Items
Motion:
Moved that the board of education or governing authority will allow the sale to students of food items that do not meet the Connecticut Nutrition Standards provided that the following conditions are met: 1) the sale is in connection with an event occurring after the end of the regular school day or on the weekend; 2) the sale is at the location of the event; and 3) the food items are not sold from a vending machine or school store. An “event” is an occurrence that involves more than just a regularly scheduled practice, meeting, or extracurricular activity. For example, soccer games, school plays, and interscholastic debates are events but soccer practices, play rehearsals, and debate team meetings are not. The “regular school day” is the period from midnight before to 30 minutes after the end of the official school day. “Location” means where the event is being held. Motion by Barbara Senges, second by Jill Carini.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro:
Albert Littlefield, Barbara Senges, Eric Bauman, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Jill Carini, John Kleinhans, Leigh Gianakos, Timothy Hagen, William Derry
Discussion, action: D. Proposed 2018-2019 Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement - Beverage Exemptions
Motion:
Moved that the board of education will allow the sale to students of beverages not listed in Section 10-221q of the Connecticut General Statutes provided that the following conditions are met: 1) the sale is in connection with an event occurring after the end of the regular school day or on the weekend; 2) the sale is at the location of the event; and 3) the beverages are not sold from a vending machine or school store. An “event” is an occurrence that involves more than just a regularly scheduled practice, meeting or extracurricular activity. The “school day” is the period from midnight before to 30 minutes after the end of the official school day. “Location” means where the event is being held, and must be the same place as the food sales. Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Leigh Gianakos.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro:
Albert Littlefield, Barbara Senges, Eric Bauman, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Jill Carini, John Kleinhans, Leigh Gianakos, Timothy Hagen, William Derry
