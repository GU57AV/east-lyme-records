Action, Discussion: A.
Latimer Brook Stormwater Infiltration Project
Judy Rondeau, CPESC Assistant Director, was present to answer questions.  EL Town Engineer Victor Benni provided the estimate and confirmed this offer will provide satisfactory financial and other contractual support for this stormwater infiltration project installation.
Move to authorize the Superintendent to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding committing to relevant project task responsibilities of the EL BOE, including full funding for conducting pavement work and obtaining any necessary permits/approvals for full infiltration project installation in Summer 2019 at a cost of $18,000.00. That signed MOU is to be submitted to DEEP prior to the execution of a grant contract with Eastern CT Conservation District (ECCD).
Motion by Eric Bauman, second by Candice Carlson.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
