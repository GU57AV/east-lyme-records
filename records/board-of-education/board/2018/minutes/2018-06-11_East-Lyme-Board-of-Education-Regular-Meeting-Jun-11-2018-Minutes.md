FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion: A.
Discussion: Medical Insurance Update by Brown & Brown
Chuck Petruccione and Jay Fiorello, Brown and Brown Insurance representatives, provided the Board with an outline of the district's self-insured plan, variable cost of benefits, recent performance, contributions and explained the increase in medical insurance costs for the upcoming year. A full RFP was done and it was determined that a 28% increase remains the District's best option. They explained that with a self-insured plan, every year they work on an estimated rate and forecast what the rate will be the following year with the goal of outperforming the cost of a fully-insured plan. (The PowerPoint presentation is attached to meeting agenda.) Superintendent Newton noted that quarterly meetings will be scheduled with Brown and Brown for the upcoming year.

Action, Discussion: B.
Proposed Substitute Service Contract
Superintendent Newton recommended the Board move to Kelly Services for substitute service for one-year.
Kelly Services will provide administrative, vetting, training and evaluation for substitutes at a break even cost to the district.
Superintendent Newton stated that by hiring this contracted service, the risk factor and substitute pool availability and response will be improved. Kelly Services representatives Megan Bernhard and Sarah Rofrano reviewed the current District screening and hiring process and stated they will assist the District with the transition.
Motion to Authorize the Superintendent to enter into an agreement with proposed substitute service one-year contract with Kelly Services beginning July 1, 2018.
Motion by Timothy Hagen, seconded by Jaime Barr Shelburn.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Albert Littlefield, Barbara Senges, Candice Carlson, Eric Bauman, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Jill Carini, Leigh Gianakos, Timothy Hagen, William Derry
