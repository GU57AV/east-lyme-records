Review of District Improvement Plan and Proposed Goals for 2018-2019
Superintendent Newton and his administrative cabinet began the meeting with an initial overview of the work associated with Year 1 of the ELPS Long-Range Plan (2017-2018) and last year's work regarding the District and School Improvement Plans. Discussion followed regarding Year 2 (2018-2019) of the plans. (Documents are attached to meeting agenda) They noted the district is on schedule with the 5-year District Long Range Plan.
Board members praised the administrative team and the staff for their work and recognized the challenges they will face in the 2018-2019 school year, especially at the elementary level due to the alteration projects and upcoming redistricting.
A short break took place at 7:10 p.m. and the meeting resumed at 7:20 p.m.
