6.
DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information:
A. Discussion: Future Location of the ELPS Coastal Connections Program
Superintendent Newton stated he is mapping out a proposed plan for relocating the Coastal Connections Program to the second floor at Central Office. He will bring proposed plan to the next meeting. The plan will save the district approximately $30k per year in rental fees and utilities. The Fire Marshal will inspect the space to ensure it is a safe place for the program. The Coastal Connections lease expires at the end of this school year.
Action, Discussion, Information:
B. Discussion/Action: Update Regarding Elementary Alteration Projects
Chair Tim Hagen reported that following the Town Building Committee meeting last week the group visited a newly reconstructed classroom at NC and was amazed at the work that has been done, there as well as at FL and HA. A full Board tour will be scheduled after the Thanksgiving holiday. Superintendent Newton asked the Board to take action to approve the Town Building Committee's request for the Board of Education to approve $31,609.50 to equip Lillie B. Haynes School with appropriate technology wireless access points. Improvements will also be made at FL and NC, however the costs do not require Board approval. Interm Facilities Director Dave Semnoski updated the Board on the action being taken to resolve the concrete moisture issue at HA.
Moved to approve the East Lyme Town Building Committee's request to equip Lillie B. Haynes with appropriate technology wireless access points in the amount of $31,609.50
Motion by Barbara Senges, second by Candice Carlson.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, Barbara Senges, Ryan Shrader
