B. Discussion: EL Aquatics Program Update
Superintendent Newton stated he has had good discussion with the Pool Commission regarding the disbandment of the Commission and replacing it with the the Board Ad hoc committee.  Commission members agreed.  Superintendent Newton will closely monitor the program.  Board members requested the Aquatics Director Rob Bouchey be responsible to report out to the BOE at least once per year, to offer an open house for the community, and to survey membership for feedback. Superintendent Newton will reach out to Pool Commission members for input as well.  Further discussion will take place at AAA and FFO Committee meetings early next year.  Superintendent Newton stated things are currently going well.
