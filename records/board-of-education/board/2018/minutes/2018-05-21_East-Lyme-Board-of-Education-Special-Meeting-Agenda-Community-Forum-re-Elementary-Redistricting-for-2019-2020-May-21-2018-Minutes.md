DISCUSSION ITEMS
Discussion:
A. and B. Presentation by Milone and MacBroom re:  Elementary Redistricting Option for the 2019/2020 School Year
-Chair Tim Hagen turned over the meeting to Mike Zuba of Milone and MacBroom who provided a PowerPoint presentation entitled East Lyme Public Schools - School Redistricting Plan Scenarios dated May 21, 2018
-Superintendent Newton provided the a PowerPoint presentation entitled East Lyme Board of Education Community Forum re: Elementary Redistricting dated May 21, 2018 (bus transportation and transition information).
-Both presentations are available on the district website under Quick Links - Elementary Redistricting
Discussion:  C. Questions/Answers and Community Input re:  Elementary Redistricting for the 2019/2020 School Year
1. Dean Dunn, 90 Arbor Crossing, questioned how enrollment projections were determined and why certain areas/students are transitioning from Flanders to Lillie B. Haynes.
Response - Mike Zuba stated that in East Lyme Kindergarten enrollment started picking up a couple of years ago, there is an upkick in the housing market, and Southeastern CT economy has been trending upward since 2016 and continues to climb.
2. Brian Staskowski, 3 Hickory Court, questioned the growth charts and data used for projecting enrollment and cited a 2016 report from the Norwich Bulletin.
Response - Mike Zuba stated the Birth - Kindergarten population is much higher now and a percentage of students that may not attend East Lyme Schools has been factored in, and we are trying to keep neighborhoods together.  He said he would not comment on demographic information provided by other sources.
3. Allison Bosse, 84 Grassy Hill Rd, asked what the policy is for Interdistrict Transfers.
Response - Superintendent Newton stated interdistrict transfers are no longer an option, however siblings of those already in place will be "grandfathered" in to keep families together.  Interdistrict transfers will be phased out completely once siblings have moved on to the middle school.
