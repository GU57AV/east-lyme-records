2. DISCUSSION
Discussion, Information:
A. Discussion/Workshop Re: School Budget Planning for 2019-2020
Superintendent Newton began by prefacing that tonight's budget presentation is in very draft form as salary and insurance information is unavailable at this time. He thanked the BOE and the BOF for allowing the district to bring on additional social workers this year. The principals followed up by reflecting on the drastic positive change the social worker increase has provided for children and families and the transformation from reactive to proactive services in the district. Director of Student Services Kim Davis spoke of the knowledge the social workers has brought to the district and commended them for the in-service training they provided to staff on the October 5 Professional Development Day.
Superintendent Newton provided a breakdown of 2019-2020 elementary staffing adjustments to be made due to redistricting. A savings of approximately $70,850 in this area is expected. Additional support for the Integrated Preschool Program and specialty programs is driven by increased student numbers and student need and will be separated from redistricting adjustments.
Superintendent Newton presented and spoke on the rationale of the 2019-2020 Budget Priorities (Cost) and Budget Reductions (Savings). Following a discussion period, suggestions were offered by both Board of Education and Board of Finance members of information to include in the next budget workshop.
Suggestions included:
-benchmarking and identifying needs while also identifying problem areas
-budget priority list (considering state mandates)
-utilization of present staff and justification for additional staff
-math DRG results to support need for +4.0 FTE Math Consultants
BOE Budget Workshop #2 will take place on Wednesday, December 12, 2018, 5:30 p.m. in the Frances W. Ewers Library, at ELHS.
The above Budget Presentation is attached to the EL BOE Agenda for November 1, 2018 on BoardDocs.
