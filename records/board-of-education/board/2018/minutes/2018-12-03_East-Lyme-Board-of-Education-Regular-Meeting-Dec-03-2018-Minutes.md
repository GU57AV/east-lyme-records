B. Discussion: Proposed Future Location of the ELPS Coastal Connections Program
Superintendent Newton thanked ELHS student Sam Gresh for drafting the diagram for the proposed ELPS Central Office layout. The proposal relocates the Coastal Connections program from the Flanders Road rental to the second floor of the Central Office with most of the Central Office staff moving to the third floor. The present rental lease expires on June 30, 2019 and the move to Central Office would reflect a rental savings of approximately $30,000. The Central Office would allow space for the Coastal Connections program to grow and the possibility of accepting out of district students on a tuition basis. First and second floor conference handicap accessibility will be available. Coastal Connections Assistant Principal Dave Fasulo reviewed the floor plan with the Board and stated the move to Central Office would benefit students. Costs associated with this proposal will be presented at a future meeting. The proposed move would occur in summer of 2019.

D. Discussion/Action: Proposed School District Calendar for 2019-2020
Superintendent Newton presented the proposed ELPS School District Calendar for 2019-2020 and stated he would like to see a 2-year calendar. Elementary projects are ahead of schedule, so school may begin before Labor Day 2019. He will bring a two-year calendar to the BOE at an upcoming meeting. Questions and comments may be emailed to the Superintendent. Discussion ensued regarding the half-days on December 20, December 23 holiday, and why we have two half-days for conferences and not one full day? Superintendent Newton will research and report back to the Board. No action was taken.
Discussion, Information:
E. Discussion: Update Regarding Elementary Alteration Projects
Tim Hagen gave an overview of the Niantic Center School tour held this afternoon for Board of Education members. Additional building tours will be scheduled and updated construction pictures will be posted to the district website. The importance of keeping teachers and parents informed of timelines was discussed. Elementary schools will all have transitioned into Phase 2 of the building projects by the holiday break. NCS transitioned the week before Thanksgiving, FL is moving 19 classrooms this week, and HA will be transitioning the week before the December break.
Superintendent Newton reviewed the Summary of Construction Contingencies prepared by Finance Director Maryanna Stevens.
O&G Contingency $1,109,724.00
Owner Contingency $355,351.00
Balance Contingency $1,465,075.00
Regarding HA concrete moisture issue - Interim Facilities Director Dave Semnoski stated no further issue with recent rains. O&G Construction Manager is preparing a proposal for exterior work and possible remediation.
Discussion, Information:
