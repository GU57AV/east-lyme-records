Discussion: C. Discussion:
Planetarium Advisory Committee Request as it Pertains to ELPS Science Curriculum
Superintendent Newton updated the Board on the June 13, 2018 meeting he and Assistant Superintendent Amy Drowne had with Planetarium Advisory Committee Chair Diane Swan regarding the committee's June 16, 2018 additional request to the Board of Education for written commitment from the ELPS Administration and the Board of Education indicating support of educational curriculum (planetarium). At that meeting, Superintendent Newton told Diane Swan he had concerns that we would be putting the cart before horse and should first see if her committee can successfully bring forth a self-sustaining planetarium. If that occurs, then in three years' time when the science curriculum is on the cycle for review and possible revision, we can then look at possibly adding planetarium curriculum as appropriate. The Board concurred with Superintendent Newton's recommendation. No action was taken.
During the planetarium discussion, the Board took a brief break from 6:42 p.m. to 6:44 p.m. and again from 6:46 p.m. to 6:47 p.m.

Notice: A. Announcement:
Notification of Board of Education Vacancy
Chair Tim Hagen announced the Board of Education vacancy as a result of Al Littlefield's (R) resignation effective July 16, 2018. Al has been a member of the Board since February 23, 2004. The Board, Superintendent Newton and First Selectman Mark Nickerson thanked Al for his dedicated service to the District, Board of Education and the Town of East Lyme. Chair Tim Hagen highlighted some of the outstanding work Al has done over the past 14 1/2 years to include the Non-union/Non-administrative Personnel Policy Committee (Paraprofessionals in particular), extended school day, and school calendar to name a few. Parties interested in filling the vacancy should contact Town Hall.
Notice: B.
Assignment to Fill Vacancy on Personnel Policy Committee for Teachers
Chair Tim Hagen assigned volunteers Jill Carini and Eric Bauman to serve on the Personnel Policy Committee (Teachers) to fill the vacancy left by Al Littlefield.
