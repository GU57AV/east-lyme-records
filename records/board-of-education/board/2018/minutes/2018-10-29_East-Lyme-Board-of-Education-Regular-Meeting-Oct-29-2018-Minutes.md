Action, Discussion:
A. Discussion/Action: Proposed Kevin's Kourt to be Located at the Miracle League Field at Flanders School
Superintendent Newton gave an update of the FFO Committee discussion regarding the proposed Kevin's Kourt at the Miracle League Field at Flanders School. The committee was in agreement to move forward with the proposed plan.
Move to approve Kevin's Kourts project to be located at the Miracle Field at Flanders School
Motion by Barbara Senges, second by Eric Bauman.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Eric Bauman, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, Barbara Senges, Ryan Shrader
Abstain: Jaime Barr Shelburn
Discussion, Information:
B. ELPS Food Service Program Update
ELPS Food Service Director Chris Urban gave an overview of accomplishments over the past year and where the program is headed this year. The PowerPoint included slides on the Farm to School Program, Theme Days, Catering Services, Community Outreach Activities, Specialty Dining, Intern Programs, and the Income/Expense Report. The 2017-2018 program ended with a net income of $37,979.24. Funds will be further built and used to improve the program. The Board thanked Chris for the outstanding operation of the food service program. A three year state review will be held next month and results will be shared with the Board in January.
Discussion, Information:
C. Discussion: Update Regarding Elementary Redistricting for 2018-2019
No report at this time.
Discussion, Information:
D. Discussion: Update Regarding Elementary Alteration Projects
Superintendent Newton said things are going well. We are working on planning for the upcoming phase 2 move. Second parking lot at Lillie B. Haynes opened today.
Facilities Director Dave Semnoski spoke of a water intrusion issue at Lillie B. Haynes and measures being taken to locate the root of the problem to include monitoring the moisture in the concrete. Board members stated the importance of understanding the problem and ensuring a solution. A future presentation on this issue will be made to the full Board.
