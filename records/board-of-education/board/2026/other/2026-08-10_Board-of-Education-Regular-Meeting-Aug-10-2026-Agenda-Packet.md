Good Afternoon Christian,

We are pleased to share that the mini-grant applications for the East Lyme High School, East
Lyme Middle School, Flanders School, Lillie B Haynes School and Niantic Center School have
all been reviewed and approved for $3,000 each. This email contains helpful information on next
steps. The mini-grant award is at the “Plan” level of funding for all schools. These funds are to
help cover costs associated with writing, updating or expanding an Energy Efficient Indoor Air
Quality Management Plan.

Next Steps:
  • Review, sign and return to us the attached Memorandums of Understanding (MOU) for
      each school (preferred format to return the MOU is as a .pdf).
  • Provide a completed W-9 form – because you have been awarded funding for multiple
      sites, if you provide one W-9 we will assume it is for all sites’ payments unless you tell us
      otherwise. If that is not the case, please make sure it’s clear to us which W-9 goes with
      which school.

Attached Files:
   • 5 individual MOUs, one for each school building/location.
   • Blank W-9, for reference if helpful.
   • IAQ Management Plan Writing/Development Guide and Toolkit with optional template
      language, for reference and use in however it is helpful for you.

Process ahead and when funds are remitted:
   1. Sign and return each MOU.
   2. We will countersign, share copies, then at that time, remit an initial $500 for each MOU
   3. You have 6 months to complete the stated activities and share back a written/drafted
      Energy Efficient Indoor Air Quality Management Plan. You can upload the Plan
      into CASCPortal.org or email the Plan to us.
   4. Once Plan documentation is received, the remaining $2,500 for that site is remitted. The
      Lung Association will review all Plans and may make suggestions to improve or expand
      comprehensiveness.

Helpful Logistical Note:
All funds will be remitted via check sent to the address listed on the provided W-9. Thus, if the
address on the W-9 is a district location or some location outside of your building, we recommend
notifying your finance team that you are expecting money from the American Lung Association.

Portal Notifications – FYI only:
Lastly, you will be receiving a number of automated messages from CASCPortal or
Support@CASCPortal for each mini-grant as we change the status from “Review” to “Approved”
– You may also receive some indicating an application was submitted – this is just me uploaded
the PDF/paper applications for the other 4 sites into the portal, so you’ll likely get a small flood of
notifications that do not require any action.
