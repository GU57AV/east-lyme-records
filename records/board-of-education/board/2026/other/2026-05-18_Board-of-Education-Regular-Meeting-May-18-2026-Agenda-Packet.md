       A.      Budget Planning for FY2026-27
               Board members discussed and considered possible options for the
               Board of Finance budget reductions. Superintendent Newton &
               Maryanna Stevens shared the state has granted the district $243,000
               additional funding that must be used in the 2026-27 budget. Members
               clarified the confines and logistics of the allotment and discussed the
               best strategy to use this additional funding.
               All board members reviewed the possible reduction items and
               expressed their support for maintaining funding in teacher and student
               services and using the state funding for one-time expenses.
               Board members requested administrators provide a ranked list of the
               proposed reductions based on student/teacher impact. The board will
               continue budget discussions at the May 18th meeting.
