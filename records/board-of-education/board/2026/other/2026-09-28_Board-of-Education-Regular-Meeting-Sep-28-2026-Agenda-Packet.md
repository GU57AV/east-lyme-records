East Lyme Public Schools Board of Distinction Application – Level Two

1. Board Leadership/Student Achievement
   1A. The Board of Education works to improve student achievement.

   -   High Level Instructional Practices, Assessment, Growth/Development for Students was one of the four
       East Lyme Board of Education goals for the 25-26 school year and continues to be for the 2026-27
       school year. The Board continually uses state data and district reporting to inform and support the
       growth of all district students. Assistant Superintendent reports on curriculum and looking at long term
       growth.

   -   SBA Review of Assessment Data Presentation: 2025 Assessment Data & Analysis
       BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 10.6.25

   -   East Lyme Board of Education 2026-27 Goals

   1B. The Board relates the mission statement and goals to agenda items.

   -   The ELBOE’s mission statement is a part of the Bylaw 9325.2. The mission statement is also included in
       every meeting agenda.

   -   The Board agendas link specific BOE Goals to applicable agenda items.
       BOE Regular Meeting Agenda 9.22.25
       BOE Regular Meeting Agenda 11.17.25
       BOE Regular Meeting Agenda 2.9.26
       BOE Regular Meeting Agenda 6.1.26

   1C. The Board supports the appropriate use of technology in educational programming.

   -   The Board supports equitable access to technology tools for all students and integration of technology
       in district educational programming inside and outside of the classroom. Professional development
       and engagement of understanding Artificial Intelligence was a continued focus over the last school
       year. The Board incorporated AI discussions throughout their meetings and has been woven into one of
       the 2026/27 BOE Goals. The District Technology Committee worked to finalize district AI guidelines for
       both teachers and students.

       ELPS AI Guidelines Final
       AI Strategic Plan Discussion at BOE Regular Meeting 9.22.25
       AI Presentation Elementary Level 11.3.25
       BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 2.23.26
       AI Update Presentation at BOE Regular Meeting 6.1.26

   1D. The Board uses data to make informed decisions regarding student achievement.

   -   ELPS District Assessment & Analysis Presentation 10.6.25

   -   District leadership presents at the Board meetings throughout the year to help understand district data
       and discuss action taken to guide and improve student achievement and learning. The Board invited all

       Elementary Schools - BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 12.15.25
       Middle School - BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 10.6.25
       High School - BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 10.20.25 & BOE Regular Meeting Minutes 6.1.26
   1E. The Board addresses issues of diversity, equity, and inclusion.

   -   The Board continues its’ partnership with LEARN to formulate professional learning cohorts that focus
       on diversity, equity and inclusion. The cohorts participate in a two-year long training that meets four
       times throughout the school year. The sessions focus on productive courageous conversations,
       reframing bias and building common understanding, social identity, culture and microaggressions,
       culturally relevant pedagogy, and facilitating equity conversations in the classroom. This year a fourth
       cohort will begin the training, with the original cohort training the trainer.

   -   The Culture & Climate Committee and the Student Belonging & Experience Committee met throughout
       2025 school year. The committees are joining forces and combining the committees to ensure student
       belonging and experience is integrated into the culture and climate work at ELPS.

4. Community Relations
   4B. The Board has successfully worked with other community leaders

   -   In addition to regular meetings and working with the First Selectman, Town Finance Director, and the
       Emergency Management department, the Board also collaborates with the town’s Wellness,
       Prevention, and Mentoring Coordinator that oversees East Lyme Youth Services.

   -   The BOE continues their partnership with Community Health Center to host a student-based health
       clinic at East Lyme High School for the 2026-2027 school year.

   -   The district continued its partnership with the East Lyme Education Foundation. The foundation works
       to promote and enhance the district’s public schools and funded 8 mini grants to teachers to support
       programs and initiatives beyond the school budget in spring of 2026.

   4D. The Board works with other Boards of Education

   -   East Lyme Public Schools continues the shared services agreement with Waterford Public Schools for
       the Nurse Services Manager. This role serves as a health liaison to both districts equally, overseeing
       nursing operations within both districts’ school buildings.

   -   Continued shared service agreements with Waterford Public Schools and Lyme-Old Lyme Schools for
       the 2026-27 school year. The agreement allows their students to enroll and earn credit in Emergency
       Medical Technician (EMT) Courses at East Lyme High School. Through this agreement ELHS students
       are also aﬀorded the opportunity to take Automotive Technician classes at Waterford High School.

   -   The Board continues the Cooperative Agreement with Salem Public Schools and the shared service
       partnerships for the Directors of Safety & Security, Facilities, & Food Service departments with Salem
       Public Schools. Strengthening the relationship with Salem is one of the 2026-27 BOE Goals.


5. Related Organizational Leadership
   -5A. Representatives of the Board presented a workshop related to a district initiative at the annual
   CABE/CAPSS Convention or NSBA Convention

   -   Jaime Barr Shelburn attended the NSBA Conference and serves as the VP for Professional Development
       on CABE’s Executive Committee.

   5C. The Board works closely with its local legislative delegation to improve the schools.

   -   The Board/Superintendent meets and works with State Representative Nick Menapace & State Senator
       Martha Marx regarding legislative bills and changes impacting education and they are regularly invited
       to school sponsored events. Since elected, State Representative Menapace attended BOE meetings in
       2026 to provide legislative updates and recognize outstanding students.
