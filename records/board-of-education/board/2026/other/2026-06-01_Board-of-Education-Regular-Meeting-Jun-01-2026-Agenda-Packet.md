5.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
     A.     Discussion: Food Services Program Report
            Food Services Director, Chris Urban & Assistant Food Director, Ashleigh Meek,
            provided a comprehensive update on the food services program operations and
            wellness initiatives. Highlights included:

            - Optimizing farm-to-school initiatives and securing local produce through the
            Lighthouse Co-op which includes multiple school districts.
            - Plans to host student mental health fairs to expand information on available
            community services.
            - The state has approved free breakfast for all students for the 2026-27 school year.

            Board members engaged in discussions and praised the directors on their focus
            and diligence towards the program, noting its’ transition from operating on a
            historical deficit to a financially stable and culturally impactful program.
            FoodServicesMay26BOE.pdf
     B.     Discussion: 2026-27 Budget Discussions
            Board members discussed options and strategies to meet the $514,000 reduction
            to the 2026-27 budget. Discussions included reduction options that are the least
            impactful to student learning and how to ensure the effective utilization of the
            budget with additional consideration of the district’s long-term goals. Through
            discussions it was acknowledged that the board is unable to firmly plan until the
            budget is passed.
            Chair Bauman urged the public to vote in the upcoming referendum on May 21st,
            noting that a failure to pass would require additional significant reductions to
            the school district’s budget.
