                • Primary charge
                   • Research options to renovate or replace the high school building and campus due to its
                     aging infrastructure. The committee membership shall consist of parents, teachers,
                     administrators, community members, board members and students (as appropriate)
                • Main Responsibilities
                   • Elect a community chairperson to lead committee
                   • Tour recently built or renovated high schools throughout the region in order to understand
                     recent trends and to establish a baseline of pros and cons or architectural designs, building
                     layouts, facility offerings and associated construction costs
                   • Work with an outside firm (with BOE approval) to develop a feasibility study that will assist in
                     gathering relevant and informative information about the current high school facility and its
                     operations.
                   • Hold regularly scheduled meetings, while also providing updates on the ad-hoc committee’s
                     programs at regularly scheduled Board of Education meetings
                   • Maintain and adjust/modify a continual timeline of forward progression towards a potential
                     future progression towards a potential future renovation or new constructed building.

                Modernization Needs
                ● Recent site visits confirm our facility significantly trails modern high school
                  standards in both infrastructure, technology and spatial utilization. Some
                  general modernization themes that came out of our site visits include:
                    ○   Centralized Student Support: There is a strong preference for a dedicated "support hub"
                        (including, security, administration, nursing, counseling and mental health services) located near
                        an entrance. This improves privacy, safety, and efficiency for both students and outside visitors.
                    ○   Modernized Science & Tech Labs: Science classrooms require a total overhaul to remove
                        obsolete equipment and better flow and storage. Similarly, CAD, woodworking, and tech spaces
                        need updates to support modern CTE (Career and Technical Education) pathways.
                    ○   Strategic Layout & Flow: New designs should prioritize functional proximity. Example: placing
                        the wood shop and art rooms near the auditorium to easily build and move sets for
                        performances.
                    ○   Infrastructure & Public Access: Buildings must have building-wide high-capacity connectivity
                        and ample charging outlets. Additionally, public-facing areas like the gym and auditorium should
