Educational Specification for Niantic Center School
Project: Roof Replacement, Niantic Center School,
7 West Main Street, Niantic, Connecticut 06357




1. RATIONALE:

   The long range plan for the district shows the Niantic Center School (student population 180) calling
   for a replacement of 25,999 s.f. of low sloped roofs at the school. The last roof replacement project
   was performed in 1999 and the roofs are now 27 years old, exceeding their life expectancy. The
   district plans to bid the project in the winter of 2026 and have the roof replaced over the summer
   2026. Extensive interior and deck damage could be caused if the roofs are left un-replaced.



2. LONG – RANGE PLAN:

    The districts school facilities provision calls for a safe and appropriate learning environment for the
    students and staff. In order to comply with this aspect of the plan, it is required to remove & replace
    all of the low sloped roof sections at Niantic Center School.



3. THE PROJECT:
    This project will be bid in one phase and one membrane option as follows:

    Low Sloped Roofs (with existing 2 ply Modified membranes):
          Remove existing 2 ply modified membranes
          Remove existing cover boards
          Remove existing rigid insulation
          Remove & replace all existing perimeter metal flashings
          Remove & replace all drains, clamping rings & metal strainers
