5.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
     A.     Discussion: School Reorganization
            Leigh Gianakos led the discussion and reviewed main points of each scenario
            developed by the committee to address the imbalance of student enrollment and
            ensure building spaces are being utilized effectively and efficiently. Board members
            engaged in discussion considering:
            - maintaining class size equity and the difference between building capacity vs.
            understanding the impact of student classroom size
            - ensuring fiscal responsibility
            - anticipating demographic shifts & changes
            - realistic financial impact and feasibility within existing building structure
            - ensuring as many staff remain employed as possible
            - transitions for students and families
            - location of central administration offices

            The Board agreed to Further investigate scenarios baseline – A/B, C, & F,
            deprioritizing Options D and E due to severe building utilization and capacity
            constraints and will further discuss at a future board meeting.

            School Reorganization Scenarios.pdf
     B.     Discussion: Non-Lapsing Account
            Board members thoroughly discussed the balance of the non-lapsing account and proposed
            replenishing the non-lapsing account to $250,000 (adding $205,588) and earmarking the
            remaining amount, approximately $162,000 for potential school reorganization costs or
            capital projects. The Board will hold a formal vote at the November 2, 2026 meeting.
