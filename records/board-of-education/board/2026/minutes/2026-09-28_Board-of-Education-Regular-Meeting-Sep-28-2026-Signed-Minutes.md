     A.     Presentation: Youth Survey Results with Bonnie Smith
            Bonnie Smith from B. Weyland Smith Consulting provided a report on the anonymous
            youth survey for grades 7-12 conducted this past spring. Discussion included results
            and trends in topics such as social media, substance use, technology, and gambling.
            Significant discussion ensued on the social emotional health of students, navigating
            prevention, and the best approach to providing student support.

            Leigh Gianakos led discussion on the enrollment imbalance within the elementary
            schools and the wide range of options to address and resolve the imbalance.
            Discussion between all board members ensued regarding supporting student
            experience and maintaining operational stability and fiscal sustainability.
            The committee will gather additional information based on discussions and will
            continue discussions over the fall in future meetings and will inquire about MP
            Planning’s availability to attend a board meeting for further discussion on
            enrollment trends.
            The Board will formulate a larger committee with additional stakeholders to review
            and consider district options. further feedback from the East Lyme community and.
            All materials and presentations regarding school rebalancing are available on the
            BOE website. A separate email address is being set up specifically to gather public
