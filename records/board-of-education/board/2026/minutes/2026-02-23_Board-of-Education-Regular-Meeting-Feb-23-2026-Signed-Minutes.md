         D.    Proposed Healthy Food Certification (HFC) Statement - Food
               & Beverage Exemption
               The East Lyme Board of Education will allow the sale to students of
               food items that do not meet the Connecticut Nutrition Standards and
               beverages not listed in Section 10-221q of the Connecticut General
               Statutes provided that the following conditions are met:
               1) the sale is in connection with an event occurring after the end of the
               regular school day or on the weekend;
               2) the sale is at the location of the event; and
               3) the food and beverage items are not sold from a vending machine or
               school store.
               An “event” is an occurrence that involves more than just a regularly
               scheduled practice, meeting, or extracurricular activity. For example,
               soccer games, school plays, and interscholastic debates are events but
               soccer practices, play rehearsals, and debate team meetings are not.
               The “regular school day” is the period from midnight before to 30
               minutes after the end of the official school day. “Location” means
               where the event is being held and must be the same place as the food
               and beverage sales.

A.   Discussion: Budget Discussion, Continued
     Chair Bauman reviewed the budget process up to this
     meeting, which is the final opportunity for discussion before
     the public hearing on March 2, 2026. Mr. Bauman would like
     these discussions to give an indication to the public on
     board considerations and proposed budget reductions
     before the public hearing.
     Superintendent Newton reviewed possible reductions to the
     proposed budget of 6.79%. Board members focused
     discussions on areas to reduce the budget increase,
     including:
        • Cutting positions through retirements, reducing an
          administrative role from a 12-month to a 10-month
          contract, and leaving current vacancies unfilled for
          one year. Superintendent Newton noted the district
          does not have IT Director or HR Director positions,
          which are typically found in districts of the same size.
        • Deferring planned maintenance projects or
          replacement cycles. Board members shared that
          although there are significant facility costs in the
          budget, maintaining schools supports district culture
          and deferring projects typically leads to excess costs
          or worse conditions.
        • Transportation reductions if logical with contract
          requirements of driver charge or route minimums

     Board members agreed to the proposed budget reductions
     presented, lowering the increase to 6.23%.
     They also engaged in discussions on the town’s increased
     debt service, understanding the town budget and how it
     affects the BOE budget. They agreed to continue discussions
     on the town budget at the next meeting.
     Mr. Bauman requested the board and administrators to
     proactively think about possible savings in anticipation of
     possible reductions from the BOF.
