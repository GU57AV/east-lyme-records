4.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
     A.     Discussion: AI Presentation
            Assistant Superintendent Spaziano, Social Studies teacher Kim Izzo, and a student
            panel comprised of AP Computer Science students presented a report on utilizing AI
            within the classroom and engaged in discussions with board members on their
            experience. Highlights included:
            A demonstration of NotebookLM as an instructional tool. Notebook LM allows
            teachers to curate specific sources for students to generate flashcards, study guides,
            and facilitated in the classroom to be a useful tool to students
            The student panel provided feedback on the AI literacy course pilot, emphasizing
            proper prompting and the importance of students learning ethical usage.
            Implementing an AI Literacy course for students and teachers is in place for the next
            school year. A Family University event on educating these initiatives with parents will
            be planned in the fall.
