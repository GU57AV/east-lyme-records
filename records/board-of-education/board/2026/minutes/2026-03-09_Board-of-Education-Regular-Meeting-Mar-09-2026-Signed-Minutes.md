5.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
A.   Discussion: High School Vision Committee Presentation
     Marc Salerno, Chair of the High School Vision Committee, presented an update
     on Phase One of the feasibility study conducted by Silver Petrucelli Architects.
     The presentation provided an overview of the history and existing condition of
     the building, the potential timeline if the BOE moved forward with the study,
     and facility implications with scheduled capital projects. The second phase will
     analyze and provide recommendation if the high school building should be
     rebuilt, renovated, or built new.
     Board members engaged in discussions on the study findings, impact on the
     town, and how to approach the issue strategically. The board will vote on the
     approval of Phase 2 during the April 6th meeting.

C.   Discussion/Action: Non-lapsing Account
     Catherine Steel shared the discussions of the FFO Committee on the non-lapsing
     account; the committee is proposing .75% would be the maximum contribution per
     year, with a cumulative amount of 1.25%. Board members engaged in discussions
     on possible uses and limits of the funding, and board policies set for account.

     MOTION:

     D.     Discussion: District Repair Improvement Project - DRIP
            Superintendent Newton shared information on the new state grant program
            that is focused on funding school facility projects or improvements. The grant
            does not require the district to seek approval from the town to utilize that
            funding but must meet state requirements. The grant also allows the funding
            to be rolled over to be saved for a larger or costly project. This year East Lyme
            has been granted 124,000. Dr. Lund led discussions on facility priorities that
            have been deferred and possible uses for the grant, which will continue once
            the funding has been distributed.
