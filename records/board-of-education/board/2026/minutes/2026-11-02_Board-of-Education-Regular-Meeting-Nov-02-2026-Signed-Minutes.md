5.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
     A.     Discussion: Administrator Presentation - Middle School
            Claudine Kelly introduced students Ishaan Manickam and Azalea Barber, members
            of the ELMS Service Club. Ishaan and Azalea shared the club’s focus and highlighted
            student-led initiatives, such as projects supporting Care & Share and Ronald
            McDonald House. The students are currently working to organize the Veterans Day
            Assembly and a schoolwide fundraiser for "K9s for Warriors" (providing service dogs
            to veterans).

            Principal Kelly presented on the middle school’s focus, supporting executive function,
            academic growth, and fostering homework accountability and how that aligns with
            the high school vision of the graduate. Focus also includes instilling ELMS Core
            Values (Excellence, Leadership, Mindfulness and Service) and supporting student
            belonging, ensuring every student can identify a trusted adult at school,
            encouraging restorative discipline practices, and positive behavioral supports.
            Eric Bauman asked about club participation rates and resource constraints.
            Principal Kelly noted high demand across all clubs; the staff use of the lottery system
            when clubs reach capacity, and student-driven club additions, such as dance club.
