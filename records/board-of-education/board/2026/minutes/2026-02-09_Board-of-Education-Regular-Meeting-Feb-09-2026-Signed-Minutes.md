4.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY

         A.     Discussion: 2026-27 Detailed Budget Discussions, Continued
                Chair Eric Bauman began budget discussions with a brief overview
                of the budget process thus far. Superintendent Newton's proposed
                budget is an increase of 6.79%, driven primarily by contractual
                obligations, salaries, and rising health insurance costs. Mr. Newton
                provided additional information previously requested by board
                members including special education student data, staff lists by
                building, and retiree updates.
                All board members participated in extensive discussions on options
                to reduce the proposed budget. Topics included in discussions were:
                   •   deferring non-essential repairs, renovations, and furniture
                       replacements. Prioritizing projects in order of need to help
                       determine what is necessary.
                   •   creating a buffer in preparation for unbudgeted student
                       services expenses; through discussion it became evident
                       there is not a consistent trend in student services costs
                   •   transportation and the need to clarify community
                       misunderstandings regarding bus usage; the board will
                       continue to review transportation for potential savings
                   •   athletics, gifts-in kind, and revenue accounts
                   •   enrollment and class size, open positions, and which
                       vacancies must be filled to maintain operations. Board
                       members shared concerns on the proposal of cutting vacant
                       paraeducator positions and non-teaching staff. It was noted
                       that 16.5 general teaching positions have been reduced over
                       the last four years.
                   •   concerns of budget cuts without consideration of
                       understanding of educational operations. Members
                       requested administrator feedback on possible budget cuts
                       that result in minimal impact toward schools operations,
                       performance, and climate and culture. Members also
                       requested data on the effects and potential implications of
                       all proposed cuts.
                   •   public perception of the board and proactive strategies to
                       communicate the value and commitment of ELPS staff while
                       highlighting the district's high rankings and student success.


                An initial 10% budget cut was requested to each school building and
                director, through member feedback the request was lowered to 5%
                to ensure school operation stability. Administrators will also
                provide a prioritized list of budget needs and projects at the next
                meeting for further discussions.
                The Board of Education Public Hearing is scheduled for March 2nd.

                Budget Materials 2.9.26.pdf

         B.     Discussion: Strategic School Balancing
                Board members reviewed the discussions from the last meeting, the
                proposal from MP Planning is being considered at approximately
                $35,000 through a CES discount.
                Mr. Newton reported that 10 out of the 11 Connecticut districts
                surveyed used MP Planning. Their firm uses a demographer and is
                local with prior knowledge of community and town.
                Through discussions, board members raised concerns about the
                price, potential duplicative spending if the school reorganization
                committee proposes additional organizational changes, tracking
                enrollment data year over year, and planning for enrollment
                changes and challenges.
                Board members agreed to have the proposal revised to incorporate
                scenario planning with model changes and request a repeat
                customer discount since a majority of context and history is familiar
                with this firm.
                Mr. Bauman noted the study could be funded by the non-lapsing
                account, to be discussed at a future meeting.
