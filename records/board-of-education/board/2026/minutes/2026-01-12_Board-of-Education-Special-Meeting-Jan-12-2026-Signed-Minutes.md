C.   Discussion: Superintendent 2026/27 Budget Presentation
     Superintendent Newton delivered the budget presentation ,
     which provided an overview of the ELPS budget timeline and
     preliminary budget projections for FY27. Superintendent
     Newton shared the significant cost drivers for the FY27
     proposed budget, including salaries confined by existing
     contracts, increased health insurance cost projections, and
     increased transportation costs.
     Superintendent Newton reviewed: Per Pupil Expenditures
     and Special Education prevalence rates in comparison to the
     state average, cost savings with shared services with
     neighboring towns, utilizing community partnerships, and
     staffing plans with retirees.


     Leigh Gianakos requested a graph chart of the per pupil
     expenditure and how the funds are divvied. Superintendent
     Newton shared that information will continue in future
     budget discussions.

D.   Discussion: Town of East Lyme Budget Overview - First
     Selectman Cunningham
     First Selectman Cunningham thanked the Board of
     Education for their work and focus on the budget.
     Selectman Cunningham shared that in FY24-25 the town's
     budget increase was 5.95% and in FY25 it was a 6.3%
     increase, which is not a sustainable trend on the
     community. He encouraged collaboration and partnership
     between the Board of Education, the Board of Finance, and
     the Board of Selectman to find ways to reduce the trend in
     the annual increase.
     Selectman Cunningham acknowledges that there are fixed
     costs within the proposed budgets, but believes the board
     should utilize strategic planning concepts and the abundant
     resources within the town to reduce the proposed budget
     wherever possible to minimize community impact.
     As discussions continue, the Board of Finance will provide
     further details on the town's projections he is looking
     forward to collaborating with the Board of Education in this
     process.


     Eric Bauman would like the Board to meet an additional
     time before the public hearing, as the Salem Cooperative
     meeting is now scheduled for 1/26, which will not allow for a
     regular meeting. Carissa will poll the board for the best
