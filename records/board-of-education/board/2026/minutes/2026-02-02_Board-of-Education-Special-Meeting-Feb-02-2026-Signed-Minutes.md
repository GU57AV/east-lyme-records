4.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION

         A.    Discussion: Strategic School Balancing
               Superintendent Newton initiated discussions on the current
               imbalance of enrollment in the elementary schools with NCS
               having the lowest enrollment, the variance of
               approximately 100 students per building. Based on the
               imbalance and the hesitation to resolve internally, he would
               like the board to consider hiring M.P. Planning Group
               through C.E.S. so that solutions proposed for the imbalance
               are neutral. The consultant is the same as the previous
               study completed 7 years ago and is through a state
               approved vendor.


               Esteban Garcia asked about the potential costs for the
               study. Superintendent Newton shared it would be

                                                                               Page 5 of 11
               approximately 37 thousand dollars.


               Bill Derry asked about fluctuating enrollment and if it can
               be reviewed annually, so that enrollment is not significantly
               out of balance after several years. Superintendent Newton
               wants to be cognizant of the possibility of having families
               shift elementary schools based on redistricting in the midst
               of their students' experience.


               Leigh Gianakos shared she believes that the Board could
               complete an initial review, but that factors that dictate
               zones and boundaries are complex enough to require
               external consulting. Ms. Gianakos would like to consider
               using a consultant instead of a firm to complete this project
               to be as fiscally conservative as possible.


               Alisa Bradley inquired about consultants using their own AI
               tools and would like to understand the previous
               consultant's methodology and approach.


               Superintendent Newton shared this firm is utilized by a
               significant number of schools with positive reviews and will
               gather general district costs for similar studies.

5.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY

         A.    Discussion: 2026-27 Detailed Budget Discussion
               Superintendent Newton shared East Lyme's accountability
               data and how East Lyme's per pupil rate fares within area
               districts and districts within DRG C. Discussion ensued on
               East Lyme's score is extremely competitive with the
               accountability with districts that have a higher per pupil
               rate. Discussions also included current enrollment trends
               and the teacher to student ratio year over year.

                                                                               Page 6 of 11
     Mr. Newton clarified that capital projects for the school
     buildings are outside of the BOE budget based on their
     scope.


     Director of Student Services, Kimberly Davis shared
     transportation logistics from the previous school year and
     addressed questions on existing bus routes and the confines
     with contracts and daily rates to reducing the budget
     through transportation costs. Ms. Davis also shared they've
     explored suggested route adjustments that appear feasible
     on paper, but aren't in practice.


     Assistant Superintendent Annaliese Spaziano shared data
     on curriculum requirements, textbook revision cycle, and
     potential reductions to existing software. Ms. Spaziano
     shared that the concept that digital licensing is less
     expensive than purchasing textbooks is not necessarily
     accurate and that the district reviews programs and
     materials every 5 years to confirm necessary updates.
     Materials purchased are based on course needs and teacher
     recommendations.


     The Board agreed to review the budget materials provided
     and will continue discussions at the next board meeting.

6.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - OTHER

         A.    Discussion: Proposed Security Strategy or Deployment of
               Security Personnel
               Superintendent Newton provided background on the role of
               the Student Resource Office and since Don Hull's
               appointment to Director of Security, that role has remained
               unfilled at the high school. Mr. Newton is hoping in
               collaboration with the police department that they can find
               a replacement and possibly increase the number of officers
               in that role over the next few years.
               Mr. Newton shared that dedicated security staff remain in
               each of the school buildings, but the board is considering
               incorporating Armed Security Officers to ELPS as part of the
               security team.
               The Board is seeking community feedback and will
               communicate to families on the considerations and how to
               provide feedback on the topic. Frequently asked questions
