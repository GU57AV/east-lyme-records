4.   DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
     A.     Assessment Data Presentation
            Assistant Superintendent Annaliese Spaziano gave a presentation on the state
            student assessment data from the last school year. Main topics of discussion were:
            - East Lyme’s DRG shift to from D to C and the demographic changes over the last 10
            years such as increases in high need students, special education, racial diversity, and
            multi-language learners
            - Student growth rates in Math & ELA and progress in closing the achievement gap
            - Growth trends and comparisons to district performance in DRG D and DRG C
            - Looking beyond the assessment results to further support “the whole child”
            -Satchel pulse survey results highlighting students’ responses to feeling safe at
            school, possessing a strong sense of belonging, and the importance of peer-to-peer
            relationships
            - partnering with districts in group C to leverage ideas and best practices
            - leveraging AI data tools to evaluate comparative curriculum and instructional
            variables.
            - understanding how the Salem results fit in with the ELPS results.
