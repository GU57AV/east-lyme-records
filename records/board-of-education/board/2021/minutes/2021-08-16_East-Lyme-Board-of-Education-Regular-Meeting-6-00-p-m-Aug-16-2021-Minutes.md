7. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: COVID-19 Mitigation Strategies for the 21/22 SY
Superintendent Newton shared the interim fall guidance from the Department of Public Health with the board.
Mitigation Strategies:
Masks will be required in all school buildings until September 30, 2021. We will await guidance from the governor and if he will extend the executive order.
Students will be given mask breaks and do not need to wear outside.
Maintain 3ft social distancing guidelines.
There will be no option for remote learning for snow days.
Quarantining will be for 10 days. The high school and middle school students may ZOOM into their classrooms.
The cafeteria will still use plexiglass during lunch waves.
Discussion ensued with the board members and Superintendent Newton.
