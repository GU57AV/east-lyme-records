Action, Discussion, Information: A. Discussion/Action: re: Facility Naming - Aquatics Center
Superintendent Newton, Mrs. Shelburn and Kevin Seery recognized Timothy Hagen for his 18 years of dedicated service to East Lyme Public Schools and would like to dedicate the East Lyme Pool in the East Lyme Aquatic & Fitness Center as the Tim Hagen Pool on this day. We plan to have a ceremony for the naming of the pool and hang the signs in January.
Discussion ensued with board members.
Whereas Tim Hagen has dedicated the past 18 years to improving the quality of education for a generation of students in East Lyme; and whereas Tim Hagen has been a tireless advocate for promoting the education of our students; for promoting educating the whole child; to ensure that they become productive and contributing members of the community; be it resolved that the East Lyme Board of Education, in recognition of Tim Hagen’s outstanding achievements while serving as a Member of the East Lyme Board of Education from 2003 to 2021, the East Lyme Aquatic & Fitness Center from 2000, hereby names the facility currently known as the East Lyme Pool in the East Lyme Aquatic & Fitness Center as the Tim Hagen Pool on this 22
nd
