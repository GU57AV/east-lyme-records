Discussion:
A. Discussion: Update on COVID-19 District Expenses, Cost Projections, and Grant Funding
Superintendent Newton shared that there have not been many changes since last reported. We will provide an update on the January 25, 2021 meeting.
Discussion:
B: ELPS Budget 2021/2022
Superintendent Newton shared the 2021-2022 budget priorities and three key points for discussion between the BOE and the BOF.
Three Key Points for Discussion
How can we as a BOE/Administration better understand the impact of town growth, the Grand List, real estate sales, taxes, and the market? How does it help us?
The pandemic has identified high-needs cohorts of students who will have long-term impacts that we need to address starting now and in the coming years. We don’t know what the change in learning models has done to children, along with the transient changes that students have engaged in this year. School-family partnerships have been tested as well, which is of great concern.
Discuss and review the 2021-2022 Fiscal DRAFT budget sheet with FFO revisions.
Discussion ensued with the Board of Education members, Board of Finance members, and Administration. Mr. Newton thanked the Board of Finance members for joining us for the meeting.
