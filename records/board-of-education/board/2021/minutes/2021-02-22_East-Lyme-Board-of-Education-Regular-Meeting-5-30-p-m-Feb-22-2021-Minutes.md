Discussion: B
:
ELPS 20-21 COVID-19 Update/Phased in Return of Middle School Students
Superintendent Newton presented the phased in return of the middle school students to begin March 15, 2021.
Action Steps:
Reopen four days a week (eliminate Hybrid model but temporarily maintain the Wednesday remote workday).
Utilize a 'Phased In' approach to allow for ongoing monitoring of COVID numbers across the community and allow for school adjustments.
Align with a potential timeframe for teacher/staff vaccinations.
Survey parents to gain feedback to track numbers and understand exactly what class sizes will look like and where support may be needed.
Order additional PPE requested by building Principals.
Discussion ensued with administration and Board of Education members.
Discussion:
C. Credit Recovery for High School and Summer Academic Planning for K-12
Mrs. Roselli Kelly provided an update on credit recovery. She has been working with her team (Assistant prinicpals, counselors, and social workers) to track students that there are concerns with. They have created a tracking sheet for every student with concerns. They will continue to try and engage students who might be having difficulties and having attendance issues.
Mrs. Drowne is currently working with CIL's on the upcoming summer academic planning. She plans to bring to AAA on March 8, 2021 to see what this program may look like and then bring to FFO for the fiscal side. She plans to bring back the board on March 22, 2021 for review.
