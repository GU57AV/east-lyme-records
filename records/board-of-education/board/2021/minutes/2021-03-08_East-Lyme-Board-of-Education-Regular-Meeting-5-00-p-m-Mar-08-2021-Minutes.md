Superintendent Newton provided an update on the phased in return of students.
Update:
-We will have a few representatives from the Virtual Viking group join the Board of Education meeting on Monday, March 22nd.
-We have shared the elementary, middle school, high school and VV survey’s on the website.
-Vaccine distribution for staff seems to be going well. We surveyed on Friday and have not heard anyone having difficulty in receiving a vaccine if they would like.

-March 15, 2021: Grade 2, Grade 5 and 6 will return.
-March 22, 2021: All high school students will return.
-We will be modifying the Wednesday schedule for students to allow the students to adjust and help provide SEL components.
-COVID-19 cost update provided.
