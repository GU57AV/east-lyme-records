Discussion:
B:
ELPS 20-21 COVID-19 Update/Phased in Return of Students
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the return of students to full in-person learning. Today was the first day back for all high school students. The principals have done an amazing job with this smooth transition.
ELHS update:
Mrs. Kelly shared that the opening of high school went very smooth. The lunch is our biggest concern but with the nice weather approaching, it will help as an additional space outside. There was great energy with staff and students. Of course, some students had a bit of anxiety as it was like the first day. Overall, I feel it went great!
ELMS update:
Mr. Bitgood shared that grades 5 and 6 are already in full and we plan to bring grades 7 and 8 back in full on Monday. It has been such a pleasant experience to have everyone back in the buildings. Our teachers have done an amazing job with team building for our students upon their return. Parent pick- up and drop-off is our only issue we are working on now. We do not have many students riding the bus. We are in the midst of planning a class night for the end of the year.
Elementary:
All elementary principals shared that it has been a very smooth transition bringing back grades K, 1 and 2. NCS is having difficulty with pick-up and drop-offs but working through the logistics.
Sports Update:
Spring sports are ready to begin. We will allow some parents to attend some of the outside sporting events. We are awaiting a bit more information regarding this. We plan to have the late bus begin running after April vacation.
Graduation:
We are still awaiting more guidance from the state but are planning to have graduation outside on the field.
Prom:
Mrs. Kelly is working with students to help make prom happen. The seniors have rented a tent that can be outside in the field or at the Pequot location that was previously booked.

PowerPoint
on what the Summer Instructional Pathways would look like. It is a continuation of the school year (6-week cycle). It will be funded through the COVID relief dollars that we will be receiving. This program will span over the next two summers to have maximum benefit.
