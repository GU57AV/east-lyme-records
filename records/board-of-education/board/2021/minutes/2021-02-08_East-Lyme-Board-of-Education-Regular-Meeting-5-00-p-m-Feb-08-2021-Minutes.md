A:
ELPS 20-21 COVID-19 Update/Phased in Return of Elementary Students
Superintendent Newton presented the phased in return of elementary students to begin March 1, 2021.
Action Steps:
Reopen four days a week (eliminate Hybrid model but temporarily maintain the Wednesday remote workday).
Utilize a 'Phased In' approach to allow for ongoing monitoring of COVID numbers across the community and allow for school adjustments.
Align with a potential timeframe for teacher/staff vaccinations.
Survey parents to gain feedback to track numbers and understand exactly what class sizes will look like and where support may be needed.
Order additional PPE requested by building Principals.

. Planetarium Facility Usage
Superintendent Newton shared information from the recent Ad-Hoc Committee Meeting. It has been a challenging year for them due to pandemic. It is recommended to keep the existing planetarium area available at East Lyme High School for an additional year while STARS to STEM, Inc. moves forward to formalize a plan to establish startup equipment costs needed for the proposed planetarium initiative. The Superintendent will continue to be updated monthly on progress and will return to the Board if anything further is needed.
