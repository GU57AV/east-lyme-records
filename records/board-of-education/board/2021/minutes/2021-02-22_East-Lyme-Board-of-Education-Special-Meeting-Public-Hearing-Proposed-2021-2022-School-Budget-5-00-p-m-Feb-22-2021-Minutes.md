2. DISCUSSION ITEM
A.
Discussion: Public Written Correspondence Relative to Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2021-2022
Superintendent Newton provided and update on the proposed East Lyme Public Schools Budget for 2021-2022. The $52,803,380
budget represents a 3.79% increase over the current year.
The budget proposal for FY 2021/2022
included a high school social worker, certified occupational therapy assistant (COTA), one middle school math coach, two elementary math coaches, six paraprofessionals, two additional grade K teachers and two additional grade 2 teachers (based on enrollment). For a more detailed look at the proposed budget and associated documents, go to the East Lyme Public Schools website at
2021-2022 Budget Presentation

Relative to Superintendent's Proposed Budget for FY 2021-2022:
