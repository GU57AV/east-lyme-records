Discussion, Information: D. Discussion: Update on DEI School Based Committees
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the elementary, middle school and high school based Diversity, Equity and Inclusion committees.
Elementary:
Further support of our ELL students and families.  Looking at how we can make our communications more accessible (what does it look like when students register, how are legal documents translated, what native languages do we have in our schools, etc.) and providing opportunities to connect families with the school community.
Additional PD opportunities for teachers and staff
Bring forth a sense of belonging and acceptance for every student who walks through our doors.  The committee is looking at developing themes for each month as a starting point with book recommendations and possible activities to promote diversity, equity, and inclusion.  We will look at the books that we are reading and that are in our libraries to ensure that the authors and perspectives are authentic
Middle School:
6 students have volunteered to become part of the ELMS DEI committee, all 7 & 8 grade student
Developed and distributed a calendar to the ELMS staff regarding monthly meeting times, although all meetings are open to all staff
Agendas and minutes for all meetings are posted in teams
Two bulletin boards in ELMS.  The first board is dedicated to highlighting the committee members, describing the role of DEI, and communicate our focus for the year. The second board will identify diverse groups and celebrate their diversity
Committee members reviewed Important Focus Points from the DEI Audit Report and highlighted three areas to address this year:
-To bring forth a sense of belonging and acceptance for every student who walks through our doors
-To be clear in our commitment to issues of equity and building a better sense of belonging for all students
-To identify additional hands on experiences for students and adults in the building
High School:
14 members of the faculty
4 student representatives (rotating basis from Cultural Awareness and GSA clubs)
Committee has chosen two areas from the Equity Audit to focus on and explore this school year
Using restorative practices, acknowledge past missteps and harm cause to members of the community
Establish school and community norms on respect and inclusion
Committee is involved in exploring a collaboration with PARJE (Public Art for Racial Justice Education) to create Sister Murals in a partnership with Norwich Public Schools
Committee members and administration are working with student volunteers on school-wide programming (Advisory) aimed at continued reinforcement of school and community norms on respect and inclusion
