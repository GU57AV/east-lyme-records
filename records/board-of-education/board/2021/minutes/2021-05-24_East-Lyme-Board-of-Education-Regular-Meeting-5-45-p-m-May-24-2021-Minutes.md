Reports: C
. ELMS Student Representatives
Chris Cambi and Jessica Knapp provided an update:
Food Drive
– Over the past few week ELMS has participated in a food drive to support Care & Share of East Lyme. ELMS students did and awesome job and shattered to old record of 2600 items by collecting more than 4,100 nonperishable food items this year. We used the data from each kiva to create bar graphs and then the information was posted on the Morning News program. Everyone did a great job!
SBAC Update
- From May 10
th
to May 17
th
, we took our Smarter Balanced and Next Generation Science Standard state assessments at ELMS. In the past, we would take these tests over a two-week window, with some in the morning and some in the afternoon. We would also take the assessments in places like the media center, computer lab or on another kiva. However, because we were all provided a laptop by the school this year, we used a new schedule where we tested in the mornings for only a week, with a break on Wednesday. We were also able to test in our own kivas. This schedule was less disruptive for everyone at the middle school, and there has been a lot of positive feedback from teachers and other students. Our classmates working remotely were also given the opportunity to take these assessments at home or in-person. Students who have missed a testing session or need to finish a test will continue to have opportunities to make-up or finish.
Science Club
– Recently four students from ELMS Science Bowl Club, Eric Yang, Jun Huh, Albert Zhu, and Charles Yang, were named the Connecticut State Winning Team in the “You Be the Chemist” Challenge. In addition to the question’s competition, teams were tasked with producing a 5-min video about sustainability. Some 36,000 fifth-
-
through eighth-grade students from 452 schools participated in the Challenge in 40 states, Puerto Rico, and Washington, DC .
Running Club
– The running club at ELMS started about 1 month ago and meets every Tuesday and Thursday. We currently have over 60 members and although we are not officially competing against other schools, the members are training hard. They are running some time trials and getting faster each week. This club is a great preparation for the fall cross country season at ELMS.
Butterfly Garden
– Almost 20 years ago a Certified Organic “Butterfly Garden” was constructed at ELMS in front of the Tech Ed wing. This was created as a memorial for a staff member’s child who passed away. Over the years the maintenance of the garden had declined but we are happy to announce that through the leadership of our social worker, Mario Cruz and our Whelks social studies teacher, Colin McIvor the garden is being restored. Elms students have enthusiastically taken on the project and partnered with various local nurseries in this restoration project. Through generous donations our “Garden Team” has gather almost 30 varieties of plants that help attract butterflies. The ELMS art department is also involved and will help create mosaic tiles to support the theme of renewal and revitalization. The goal will be to continue with the organic certification to help protect the insects, birds and the pollination process.
Class Night
– Class night is scheduled for Monday, June 14
th
from 6-7 with a rain date of Tuesday, June 15. This event will occur at EL high school which will be new for all of us, hopefully it will be a beautiful evening for everyone to share.
Drive-In Music Performance –
Our fantastic music department is rehearsing for our first ever Drive-in Concert which will be held on Thursday, June 3
rd
at 6:00. This event will be for grade 8 music students and their families. Families will enjoy the concert from the comfort of their cars, and we hope to broadcast the music through an FM radio signal directly to cars.
Women in Science –
This Spring many of our students participated in the University of Connecticut’s School of Engineering Diversity and Outreach in a conference called Multiply Your Options. This opportunity was in partnership with the Society of Women Engineers and the goal of the event was to
introduce young women to careers in science, mathematics, engineering and technology. Elms has participated in this event multiple times and it has always been very successful.
