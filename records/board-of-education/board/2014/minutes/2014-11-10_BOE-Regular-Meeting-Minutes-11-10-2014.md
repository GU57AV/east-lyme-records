DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
A. Finance/Facilities/Community

1. DISCUSSION re: Follow-up Discussion on NESDEC Demography and Enrollment Projection
Final Results for East Lyme, CT
Dr. Don Kennedy provided a November 5 update to the NESDEC Demography and Enrollment
Projection Final Report for East Lyme that addressed Board questions raised on October 27. The
current K-4 student enrollment is 843 and continues to decline. NESDEC projects K-4 enrollment to
bottom out in 2024-2025 at 745 and remain flat before it begins to show a slight increase. The state
reimbursement rate for a building project is based on the highest student enrollment number
projected the next seven years. Discussion ensued regarding concerns with long range needs,
difficulty in projecting economic conditions, and the importance of keeping communications open
with the community. The East Lyme Elementary Design Steering Committee is expected to make
its recommendation to the Board of Education at a special meeting on Monday, November 17.

B. Instruction
1. DISCUSSION re: ELMS Student Assessment Results
