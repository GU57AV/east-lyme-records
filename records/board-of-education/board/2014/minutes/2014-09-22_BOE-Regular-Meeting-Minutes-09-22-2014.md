B. Instruction

1. DISCUSSION re: Presentation Regarding ELPS Chinese/American Cultural Summer Camp

- ELPS English Language and American Cultural Camp Coordinator Jen Brush and ELHS senior
Robert Alden provided an overview presentation of the program and a video/music montage of
the fourth summer program at East Lyme High School.

- This past summer a total of 45 Chinese students and 4 Chinese teachers from our sister high
school in Guangzhou, PRC, attended the camp. This was an increase of 15 students from over
last year’s camp.

- The camp provides English and cultural learning opportunities for students from China through a
12-day summer English program.

- Beginning next year, approximately 10-to-15 Chinese students will spend their senior year at
East Lyme High School and will graduate with the Class of 2016.

- Programs are underwritten by SPIRAL International at no cost to the district.

Board of Education Goals for 2014-2015

1. Superintendent

* Hire a new (highly qualified) Superintendent for the ELPS District. Utilize processes that
ensure the Board has, from teachers, administrators, parents, students, and community
leaders, input and perspectives on desired attributes for the Superintendent and on what
the challenges will be in coming future.

« Provide support, advocacy and open communication for the new superintendent.

¢ Work with new Superintendent to insure a smooth transition including Central Office
organization for 2015-2016.

2. Cooperative Agreement
* Continue work to address the upcoming expiration and (likely) renewal of the
Cooperative Agreement between Salem and East Lyme, including drawing in community
involvement and participation.
« Meet with Salem BOE to discuss their plan to possibly extend their agreement with East
Lyme to include grades 7 and 8. (What appears to being go on in Salem could have long
term implications for our elementary project as well.)

3. Support the ELPS Long Range Plan

« Support and review the implementation of the new teacher and administration evaluation
systems.

* Develop a facilities plan to address elementary school needs.

« Ensure and support that we and our school district foster innovation and creativity.

« Educate the Board, parents, and community on the district's implementation of Common
Core Standards through the use of innovative and creative teaching strategies and
learning activities.

* Support and review the development of a plan to implement an elementary World
Language Program to include an extension of the elementary school day.
