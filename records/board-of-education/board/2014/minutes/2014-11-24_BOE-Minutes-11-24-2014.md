2. DISCUSSION re: Program Review — ELPS 18-21 Year Old Transition Program
In response to the Board’s request, Interim Assistant Superintendent for Special Education and Pupil
Services Donna Gittleman provided a program review of the collaborative program between LEARN
Student Support Services and East Lyme Public Schools. The program is designed for 18-21 year
old young adults that have completed high school requirements for graduation, but need additional
programming to prepare for transition into adult life. Components of the program include community
based life skill instruction, work settings, and social skill instruction. Of the 14 students currently
enrolled in program, 12 receive community transition support.
