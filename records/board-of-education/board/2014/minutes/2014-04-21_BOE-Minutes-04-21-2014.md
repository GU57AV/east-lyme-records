IV. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
A. Discussion/action re:_Proposed ELHS Graduation Date for the Class of 2014

Dr. Lombardo explained that the graduation party committee has found another venue for their
party on Wednesday, June 18. He and Principal Susi recommended the Board waive the June 19
school day for seniors only, graduation will be held on June 18, 2014 at 5:30 p.m. and the school
calendar for all other students will remain unchanged with the last day of school as Thursday,
June 19, 2014 (1/2 day). The Board thanked the parent committee for its work.

MOTION: Jill Carini moved to waive the June 19 school day for seniors only, with the graduation
ceremony to be held on June 18, 2014 at 5:30 p.m., leaving the school calendar for all other
ELPS students unchanged with the last day of school as Thursday, June 19, 2014 (1/2 day);
Candice Carlson seconded.

VOTE: UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED

PUBLIC COMMENT

Sarah Benoit, 107 Hillcrest Road, Niantic - Asked how we would move schools to other schools
while keeping in mind small class sizes and stressed the importance of a well thought out process
with parent input.

Maia Vargas, 18 Pontiac Drive, Niantic - Commended Dr. Lombardo and the administration for
creative ways other than closing school.

Tara Dowling, 19 Pleasant Drive Ext., Niantic - Cautioned the Board of Finance about loose and
light comments about closing Niantic Center School and the anxiety to students and the
community. She encouraged options to be looked at and the Board’s creativity to get through the
process.

John Arnold, 99 Sleepy Hollow Road, Niantic - Expressed concerns regarding the negative effect
talking about the possible closure of Niantic Center School has on children and asked the Board
for more detail on the estimated cost savings of doing so.

Karen Delesdernier, 254 Giants Neck Road, Niantic - Spoke of the importance of the “whole
student” and encouraged the Board to rethink the elimination of freshman sports option.

Gil Gallant, East Lyme Teachers Association (ELTA) President - Commended the Board on the
cost savings over the past years and preserving teaching positions and small class sizes. He
described the District as a “Class A District with Class A results” and encouraged the Board to
meet the state law.

Mary Walsh, 2 Spencer Avenue, Niantic - Asked for more specifics regarding low class sizes at
the high school and eliminating positions as a part of potential budget reductions.

Ms. Croteau, 184 West Main Street - Spoke of the stressful affect of careless comments by the
BOF regarding the closing of Niantic Center School and suggested the district explore
redistricting options to bring more students to Niantic Center School.

Jennifer Murray, 7 Tabernacle Avenue, Niantic - Expressed concerns regarding the possible
closure of Niantic Center School to incoming Kindergarten students expecting to attend Niantic
Center School in the fall.

. Sonja Mazzulli, 183 North Bridebrook Road - Stressed the importance of small class sizes and

keeping safety issues in mind.

- Lou deLaforcade, 22 Rose Lane - Spoke of concerns regarding cuts impacting students and asked

if administrative positions were examined.

- Bill Derry, 212 Penn Avenue - Commended Board for keeping class sizes down and stated

community schools are why kids are so successful. He advised the Board not to cut anything
more from the budget.

- Becky Lillie, 14 South Trail, Niantic - Praised Niantic Center School and its passionate parents

and encouraged the Board to focus on preserving Niantic Center School. She also spoke on the
importance of keeping freshman sports.

- Bill Dowling, 3 Cottage Lane, Niantic - Praised Niantic Center School as an unbelievable

institution, He expressed concerns regarding unfunded state mandates and asked the Board not to
make cuts at the expense of children.

Randee Siegal, 65 Old Black Point Road, Niantic - Asked why our exemplary district is not
exempted from government unfunded mandates and spoke of the amazing teachers, parents and
the community in general.

* Chair Tim Hagen announced that more budget discussion will be held at the next Board
of Education meeting on Monday, April 28. The Board of Finance Public Hearing
immediately followed at 7:00 p.m. in the East Lyme High School Mary Broderick
Auditorium.
