DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
A. Finance/Facilities/Community
1. Discussion re: Elementary School Design Steering Committee Update

= Tim Hagen (Chair) updated the Board on the Elementary School Design Steering Committee’s
work,

= Three dates have been set aside for Community Forums: Sept. 23 at Flanders School, Sept. 24 at
Niantic Center School, and Sept. 25 at Lillie B. Haynes School. The materials for the Community
Forums will be previewed for the entire Board in August to solicit common inputs for the Forums.

= The next Steering Design Committee Meeting is to be held at Central Office on August 12.

= NESDEC will be presenting the Demographic Study to the Board and the Elementary School
Steering Design Committee on September 8 with the Final Demographic Study to be released in
October.

2. Discussion/action re: Proposed Ten-Month Contract Renewal with Child & Family Agency of SE
CT, Inc. Effective August 27, 2014 through June 30, 2015

Assistant Superintendent Brian Reas reviewed the proposed ten-month contract renewal with the
Board. For the last three years, the district has secured services with the Child & Family Agency of
Southeastern CT, Inc. to provide support for our students and families dealing with issues that may
not be school based, but have an impact at school. This contract renewal will ensure the same
services are provided to the district for the 2014/2015 school year. The contract reflects no
monetary increase from the previous contract.

ION: = Jamie Barr-Shelburn moved to authorize the Superintendent to sign a ten-month contract with
the Child & Family Agency of Southeastern CT, Inc. for the 2014/2015 school year in the total
amount of $82,400.00 as presented; Pam Rowe seconded.

VOTE: UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED
