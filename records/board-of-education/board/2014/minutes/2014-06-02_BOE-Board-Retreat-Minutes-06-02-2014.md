Il. Board of Education Self Evaluation and Review of Goals for 2013-2014

A. East Lyme Public Schools / Long Range Plan

= The meeting began with a review of the Long Range Plan. The discussion included what had been

accomplished during the year and the status of those goals/strategies that were not yet completed
and a priority of each.

Following the discussion, the several ideas for future consideration were captured as follows:

= The District's Long Range Plan should be more frequently reported at Board meetings as opposed
to being reported when a goal/objective is met and then summarized at the end of the year.

= Goal #3/Objective #1: East Lyme Public Schools’ facilities and grounds will be safe, clean, and
up to date. The Board and the Administration should revisit the addition of Bus Monitors and
conduct a policy review to ensure student safety is maintained.

= There was discussion on Goal #1/Objective #3: East Lyme Public Schools will use multiple
forms of assessments as learning and teaching tools.

The progress toward meeting this goal/objective should be reported to the full Board—What
types of assessments are being used, what the assessments are telling the classroom teachers
about the effectiveness of the instruction, and what needs to be done to counter any education
shortfalls.

The Board follow-up throughout the year after the results of State-wide testing are presented.
How any shortfalls are being addressed and what progress is being made to improve student
scores.

= Goal #1/Objective #2: East Lyme Public Schools will ensure that instructional strategies meet
the needs of each student and encourage exploration and autonomous learning and on Goal
#3/Objective #2: East Lyme Public Schools will develop programs which ensure each student's
success in grade, school and graduation transitions.

The concept of Professional Learning Communities (PLC) philosophy/concept is excellent. We
should make sure that this practice is being used across all three Elementary Schools.
