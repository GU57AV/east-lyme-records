Ill. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS

A. Presentation and Discussion of the East Lyme Elementary Schools’ Design Steering
Committee Recommendation to the East Lyme Board of Education
The East Lyme Elementary Design Steering Committee Chair Tim Hagen, Dr.
Lombardo and Architect Al Jacunski presented the committee's report and
recommendations to the Board. A 2011 Feasibility Study by Kaestle Boos,
Associates, Inc. indentified significant infrastructure and maintenance requirements.
Cost to repair all three elementary buildings, with no improvement to the layout or
appearance and limited to no financial assistance from the state was estimated at $22
million. The Design Committee concurred with the Board of Education’s July 15,
2013 consensus that this is the least desirable approach.

Three Options Considered (although other options were discussed):

1. Renovate “as new’ three existing schools

2. Discontinue use of all three buildings and construct a new elementary school
campus “on an existing

3. Discontinue use of one building and renovate as new and/or build as new two
remaining facilities

Factors considered:

Educational Quality and Experience

Site Location

Site Size

Building/Renovation Costs

Operating Cost Savings

Disposition of Vacated School Site

Other Long Term considerations

Community Culture and Tradition

Committee’s Recommendation: Two Elementary Schools: Niantic Center and Flanders
- Each with ~ 400 K-4 student capacity with classroom space for current pre-school

enrollment
