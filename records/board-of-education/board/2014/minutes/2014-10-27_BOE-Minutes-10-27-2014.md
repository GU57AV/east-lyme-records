DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
A. Finance/Facilities/Community

1. DISCUSSION re: Presentation of NESDEC Demography and Enrollment Projection Final
Results for East Lyme, CT
Preliminary results of the NESDEC Demography and Enrollment Projection were presented in late
September. Dr. Lombardo stated that the revised data in the final report includes updated
information on both projected student enro!lment numbers and residential building projects in town.
The final report continues to predict a decline in student population to 2024, The maximum state
reimbursement for our elementary renovation/construction project will be based on an elementary
student population of 834, assuming that the project goes to referendum and paperwork is
submitted to the state no later than June 30, 2015. Each year thereafter, the population continues
to decline and the state reimbursement potential falls. The East Lyme Elementary Schools Design
Committee continues to meet and will bring a recommendation to the Board. The recommendation
will include expansion options.
The following questions pertaining to the NESDEC Demography and Enrollment Projection Final
Results will be addressed by Dr. Don Kennedy, NESDEC, at the next Board of Education meeting
on November 10:
