1. DISCUSSION re: Update on Community Forums Regarding Elementary School Facilities

Tim Hagen reported that approximately 75 parents/guardians and community members attended
the recent community forums regarding elementary school facilities. The Elementary Schools
Design Steering Committee reviewed the survey results on October 2 and will reconvene on
October 21 to establish a timetable as to when the Board will hear the committee’s
recommendation. Survey results were shared with Board members and are available to the public
on the District's website.

2. DISCUSSION/ACTION re: Proposed Out of Country Field Trip to France
ELHS Principal Mike Susi and Dr. Lombardo recommended the approval of an ELHS field trip to
France on Wednesday, April 8 through Sunday, April 19, 2015. If approved, ELHS French teacher
Nathalie Casey and another adult will chaperone approximately 10-15 high school students to

3. DISCUSSION re: ELPS Student Assessment Results
ELHS Principal Mike Susi presented the grade 10 results of the March 2014 CAPT Science
assessment. While the State average at or above goal is 46.8%, East Lyme students performed at
70.7% (93.4% at or above proficiency) and first in the school’s District Reference Group (DRG).

4. DISCUSSION re: Proposed ELPS Educator Evaluation and Support Plan for 2014-2015
Interim Assistant Superintendent Donna Gittleman informed the Board that the District was notified

by the CT State Department of Education (CSDE) that the ELPS Educator Evaluation and Support
Plan for 2014-2015 has been approved. Implementation can proceed once the Board adopts the
CSDE approved plan.

MOTION: Barbara Senges moved adoption of the ELPS Educator Evaluation and Support Plan for 2014-2015
dated 9/4/14 as presented; Pam Rowe seconded.

VOTE: UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED
