Il. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE - Led by Chair Tim Hagen and Joe Arcarese
Ill. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS
A. Discussion/action re: Election of Board Chair
Dr. Lombardo called for nominations for Board Chair.

MOTION: Al Littlefield nominated Tim Hagen for the position of Board Chair. There were no other
nominations and nominations were closed.

VOTE: UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED
Tim Hagen was congratulated and assumed the position of Board Chair.

B. Discussion/action re: Election of Board Secretary
Chair Tim Hagen called for nominations for Board Secretary.

MOTION: Marlene Nickerson nominated Al Littlefield for the position of Board Secretary; Pam Rowe
seconded the nomination. There were no other nominations and nominations were closed.

VOTE: UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED
Al Littlefield was congratulated and assumed the position of Board Secretary.

C. Discussion re: Board of Education Committees and Committee Memberships

The ASAP Committee and the Tennis Committee will be removed from the CY 2015
list. Board members were encouraged to continue membership on the same
committees. Anyone wishing to move should contact Tim Hagen.

D. Discussion/action re:_ Proposed East Lyme Board of Education Meeting Date
Schedule for Calendar Year 2015

MOTION: Jaime Barr Shelburn moved to approve the proposed East Lyme Board of Education
Meeting Date Schedule for Calendar Year 2015 as presented; Candice Carlson seconded.

VOTE: UNANIMOUS MOTION CARRIED
