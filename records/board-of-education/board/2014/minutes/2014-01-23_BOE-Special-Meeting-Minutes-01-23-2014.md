B. Discussion re:_ 2014-2015 Budget Development
1. Superintendent’s Presentation of the Proposed ELPS Budget for 2014-2015
Assistant Superintendent Brian Reas presented the proposed ELPS Budget for 2014-2015.
= A total budget of $43,989,614; representing a 4.9% increase over the current-year budget as follows:

0.88% - Federal Mandates (Affordable Care Act - $372,000)

1.08 % - State Mandates (Administrator & Teacher Evaluation Plans - $296,587) and new initiatives
2.92% - General Budget

4.88% Total Budget increase for 2014-2015

= The average budget increase between FY 11 and FY 15 is 2.4%.

= The major increases in the budget are reflected in salaries, benefits and insurance, natural gas,
technology lease, support for evaluation, immediate projects, and maintenance.

= Staff changes include the following:

- 1.0 FTE Assistant Principal (Administrator’s Bargaining Unit — Split between Flanders and
Haynes Elementary Schools)

- 2.0 FTE Dean of Students (Teacher’s Bargaining Unit — High School and Middle School)

- 1.0 FTE Elementary Spanish/French Teacher (Reinstatement of Global Language at
Elementary Level)

- 2.0 FTE Instructional Aides (Compensating for those moved to implement Full Day
Kindergarten)

- 1.0 FTE Computer Consultant (High School)

- 3.0 FTE Bus Monitors (Student Safety-- Elementary and Middle School bus runs)
Enrollment reflects a projected overall district decrease of 25 students.

Dr. Lombardo provided an overview of the new state mandated supervision evaluation process.

— The Board heard directly from the ELPS administrators regarding the administrative impacts
and challenges of the evaluation process and the rationale for additional support to allow
them to effectively support the implementation of the evaluation plan while maintaining
other leadership duties.

The Board will continue budget discussion and deliberations until a final budget proposal is
adopted.

The next meeting is scheduled for Monday, January 27, 2014, 7:00 p.m. at ELHS.
A public hearing is scheduled for February 3, 2014, 7:00 p.m. at ELHS.
