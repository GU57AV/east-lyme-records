Personnel

1, Discussion re: Initial Planning for Superintendent Search

Superintendent James Lombardo has announced his intention to retire effective the close of the 2014-2015 year.
Following a Board workshop conducted by Dr. Rich Villanova, Tim Hagen and AI Littlefield met with Dr.
Lombardo to discuss his experience with the last search and to develop a draft plan for the Board’s
consideration.
E.

4982

Tim Hagen appointed Al Littlefield and Barbara Senges to serve with him on the Superintendent Search Firm
Selection Ad Hoc Committee. The committee charge is to review a pool of search firms to screen, interview,
select, and engage a firm to recommend a firm to assist the Board in the new superintendent search.

The second committee, the Superintendent Search Ad Hoc Committee, will be comprised of 3 Board members, 2
administrators, 3 teachers, 1 town government representative, and 2 community at-large representatives. The
committee’s charge is to work with the search firm to conduct research and community forms and select,
consult, and screen candidates to bring a number of qualified, prospective candidates to the full Board for a
series of interviews.

A proposed search timeline was presented and a second workshop will be planned.
