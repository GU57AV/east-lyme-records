B. Discussion/Action: 2020/2021 Budget
Superintendent Newton and members of the FFO committee gave update on the rationale for budget cuts. They are very cognizant to the current situation and will make reductions while still supporting the district.
-PowerPoint on 2020/2021 budget reductions was presented
-Original BOE adopted budget was $51,699,974
-Total proposed changes in the amount of $826,903
-BOE revised budget is $50,873,071
-Percent increase over 19-20 budget is 3.30%
-Technology is critical component to enhance student learning
-Discussion ensued between administration and board members
Motion for the Board of Education
to reduce the 2020-21 budget request by $826,903 and motioned to move forward with the revised budget of $50,873,071 for the 2020-2021 school year.
Motion by Eric Bauman, second by Jill Carini.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, John Kleinhans, Barry Sheckley, Catherine Steel
