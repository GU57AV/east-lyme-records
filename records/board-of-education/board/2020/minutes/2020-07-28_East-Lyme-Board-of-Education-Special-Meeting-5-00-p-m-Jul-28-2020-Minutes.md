3. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - OTHER
Action, Discussion, Information:
A. Discussion/Action: Proposed Appointment of Interim Principal for East Lyme High School
-Superintendent Newton recommended Deb Roselli Kelly as the new Interim East Lyme High School Principal. It was a long search process, but we have decided to move forward with an Interim Principal. Mrs. Roselli Kelly will provide focus and stability for our staff and students. Mr. Newton also recommends that David Fasulo (ELHS Coastal Connections Program Assistant Principal) to assume Mrs. Roselli Kelly's duties at the high school alongside Henry Kydd.
-Deb Roselli Kelly is excited to assume the Interim Principal position and thanked the board for the opportunity.
Motion to approve the Proposed Appointment of Interim Principal Deb Roselli Kelly for East Lyme High School beginning July 29, 2020.
Motion by Barry Sheckley, second by Eric Bauman.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, Leigh Gianakos, Barry Sheckley, Catherine Steel
Not Present at Vote: William Derry, John Kleinhans
Action, Discussion, Information:
B. Discussion/Action: 2020/2021 District Calendar
Superintendent Newton gave a brief summary of the modified changes to the 2020/2021 SY calendar.
-Teachers will begin on August 24, 2020 and we will start bak with 5 Professional Development days. We have discussed with the union to keep start date the same.
-Students will begin on August 31, 2020 and end on June 17, 2020 for total of 180 days. (The state has allowed all district's to decrease to 177 days)
Motion to approve the modified 2020/2021 SY District Calendar as presented.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Candice Carlson.
