-Tracey Burns, Math Coach for FL, LBH, and NCS. She is here to support the 2020-2021 budget with a definite focus on the need for 2 additional math coaches. We have been working hard at all 3 elementary schools but have a long way to go. The need for two more math coaches to expand our impact and accelerate student learning. We want to have an exemplary mathematics program to challenge and persevere.
-Catherine Ellal, 9 Marion Drive, third grade teacher at LBH. She feels that the necessity for math coaches is critical. It will directly improve student learning and improve the morale in the elementary schools. Tracey is outstanding but cannot do it all. We need to provide more support and training as we move forward.
-Jayme Keefe, 6 Mohawk Drive, parent who believes we need to improve the literacy program in the schools. We need to have a strong Structured Literacy program provide appropriate intervention for our students.  She believes we should focus on the Tier 1 Phonics approach. We need to provide our teachers with more trainings/certifications to improve our students learning.
-Melissa Harrington, 55 Roxbury Road, parent who believes we need to focus on a Structured Literacy program. She is in full support of allocating funds to support improving our literacy programs. Our students and teachers deserve to have literacy success. It is critical and necessary in the early years for decoding. It will help them in the students in the future to succeed.
-Jill Johnson, 234 Grassy Hill Road, parent who would like to address the board regarding the current kindergarten class and adding additional teachers due to large class sizes.  Also, she wanted to raise awareness of STEM opportunities from Pfizer (statistics, medical writing, chemistry, etc).
-Leah LeCara, 17 South Washington Drive, parent who believes that it is an urgent matter to address the reading program in the schools. She believes a Structured Literacy program will benefit her children and rest of students in the district. She is a former elementary school teacher who taught structured literacy. As parent of a student in the 1 in 4 students who need support, we need to provide support for our teachers and students. I believe phonics and decoding skills are key. I also believe Wilson and Orton Gillingham are programs we should be utilizing in each school.
-Bonnie Dombrowski, 103 Walnut Hill Rd, parent who shared an article regarding Structured Literacy at last meeting and ask that you to read article and/or podcast. 1 in 4 not proficient in literacy. Would like to address the reading instruction among the district. We need to allocate funds for ELA. We need to provide new training. We have spoken with Margie Gillis from Literacy How who volunteered to speak about the Literacy How program. We need firm commitment and timeline from the district on how we are going to make these changes to our program. Links below:
Podcast link:
https://www.apmreports.org/story/2019/08/22/whats-wrong-how-schools-teach-reading
PDF link:
https://www.apmreports.org/files/at-a-loss-for-words-printable.pdf
-Erin Nock, teacher who works Reading Recovery across the state. This program is research-based and shown to be effective. It is a 20-week intervention plan.  This program has been working and effective for over 20 yrs and 75-80 % effective to make regular education gains. We provide ongoing training every month with data and research. It has made powerful impact on so many children.

5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Superintendent's Proposed ELPS Budget for 2020-2021
Superintendent Newton discussed the proposed East Lyme Public Schools budget for 2020-2021 school year.
-Proposed operating budget is $51,347,546 (4.26 %) and proposed technology budget is $926,546 (1.89% through acquisition plan if approved by Board of Finance)
-Proposed Staffing Initiatives are not included in the current proposed operating budget (salaries only):
1-Elementary Teacher (NCS)
1-Social Worker (HS)
1-Board Certified Behavior Analyst (DW)
2-Math Coaches (Elementary)
1.5 Global Language Teacher (Elementary)
1-Instructional Technology Manager (DW)
2-Kindergarten Teachers on hold based on lower projected birth rate trends (FL and LBH)​​​​
Discussion ensued with the board members:
-Board agrees to keep class sizes within guidelines (Possible increase of 2-K teachers and 2- 1st grade teachers) for next year.
-Board agrees we need to push for 2 Math Coaches as we have deferred in past years.
-Board agrees that IT Tech manger is needed for teachers to implement devices we will be putting in place in the upcoming year for full effectiveness.
-Some board members agree that with the large substance abuse and mental health population, we should move forward with Social Worker.
-Board would like to discuss Literacy Program at the elementary levels further.
-Board discussed possibility of deferring Global Language Program until fully prepared to implement program effectively.
-Board in support of Technology budget to update our infrastructure for a higher level of functioning. It will help to learn, adapt, and build a skill set for our students.
-Board member, Barry Sheckley presented handout that was in support of the need to update technology.
Cost saving ideas:
-Spiral Program
-Pre-K Program (tuition based)
-Coastal Connections
-LBH Wing
-Waiving health insurance for staff
Superintendent Newton thanked the board for their support and will come back with new proposal.
Chair Timothy Hagen requested that we cancel BOE Public Hearing and Special Meeting. We will reconvene next Monday, January 27, 2020 to discuss new proposal.
