Procedural: C. Public Comment
-Jason Bitgood, ELMS Principal, gave update on the Mathcounts Chapter competition yesterday at USCGA. A total of 23 schools and 200 plus students from eastern Connecticut participated in the contest. We sent 11 students (4 in the team, 6 individual and 1 alternate) to the competition. Our team won the first place three years in a row. In the individual written test, our students placed 1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9yh, 10th, 13th, 18th, and 22nd. The only alternate from our school placed 2nd in the alternate group. In the countdown run in the afternoon, we had 5 students in the top 8 and ended up with 1st, 2nd and 4th place. Overall, we have 9 students qualified for the state level, which will be held at University of Hartford on March 7th.
-Lorraine Mattison, 57 Hillcrest Drive, retired teacher of ELPS and wanted to stress the importance of reading recovery instruction. She believes this research based program benefits many students and important to keep implementing.
-Robin Soule, 14 McElaney Drive, she is a Kindergarten teacher at Flanders Elementary and wanted to thank the board and administration for looking to hire additional K and 1 teachers for next year. It is critical for these students and the large class sizes are challenging for staff and students. More behaviors may increase.
-Ellen Weewill, parent who is in favor of reading recovery. She believes that without that program, her children would not have been successful. It helps for early intervention.
-Bonnie Dombrowski, 103 Walnut Hill Road, parent who believes that having reading interventionists is important and we need to make sure that the program is developing. She is in full support of adding additional K and 1 teachers to help with class sizes. She believes that class sizes alone effect learning outcomes. She asks the board to look into class size guidelines for Special Education students and the caseloads that our teachers are acquiring.
-Julie Horner, 42 Manwaring Road, kindergarten teacher who is advocating for the children and expressed how reading recovery benefits so many children. We need to keep this program. She also thanked the board and administration for all their hard work during this process.

Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Superintendent's Proposed ELPS Budget for 2020-2021
Superintendent Newton discussed the proposed East Lyme Public Schools budget for 2020-2021 school year.
-Proposed budget is
$51,988,141 million
(5.57 % increase)
-Savings of $95,000 from prior years lease payments
-Proposed Staffing Initiatives:
2-Kindergarten Teachers (FL and LBH)​​​​
2-First Grade Teachers (FL and LBH)
1-Elementary Teacher (NCS)
1-Social Worker (HS)
1-Board Certified Behavior Analyst (DW)
2-Math Coaches (Elementary)
1.5 Global Language Teacher (Elementary)
1-Instructional Technology Manager (DW)
Discussion ensued with the board members:
-Board agrees to keep class sizes within guidelines
-Board agrees we need to push for 2 Math Coaches as we have deferred in past years.
-Board agrees that IT Tech manger is needed for teachers to implement devices we will be putting in place in the upcoming year for full effectiveness.
-Some board members agree that with the large substance abuse and mental health population, we should move forward with Social Worker or Drug/Alcohol Counselor.
-Board discussed Global Language Program in depth with how to implement program effectively.
-Board discussed how to present the needs and provide a solution with supporting data
Superintendent Newton thanked the board for their support.
The Public Hearing is Monday, February 10, 2020 at 6:00 p.m. and BOE Special Meeting following to continue budget deliberations.

6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
Action, Discussion, Information: A. Discussion/Action: Proposed ELPS Calendar for 2020-2021
Superintendent Newton proposed 2 scenarios for the East Lyme Public Schools Calendar for 2020-2021
182 Day Draft Highlights:
Teachers have 189 days and students have 182 days.
4.5 PD days offered (September, October, February, March).
Removed the May PD day. All PD is completed by April vacation.
All Schools will have Parent Conferences on same days (1.5 days-Fall {2}-.5 Days-Spring)
Included built-in snow/cancellation days (Feb 17, March 15, and May 28)
Last day June 17
181 Day Draft Highlights:
Teachers have 189 days and students have 181 days.
5.5 PD days offered (September, October, November, January, February, March).
All Schools will have Parent Conferences on same days (1.5 days-Fall {2}-.5 Days-Spring)
Additional PD day added January 15.
Included built-in snow/cancellation days (Feb 17, March 15 and May 28)
Last day June 17
Discussion ensued between board members:
-Board agreed that extra PD would be a benefit to our staff
-Board agreed with adding the snow/cancellation days
Board agreed to move forward with the East Lyme Public Schools Calendar for 2020-2021 SY of 181 days.
Moved to approve the ELPS Calendar for 2020-2021 School Year of 181 days.
Motion by William Derry, second by Candice Carlson.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Eric Bauman, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, John Kleinhans, and Catherine Steel
Con: Jaime Barr Shelburn and Barry Sheckley
