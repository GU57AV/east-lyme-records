6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTIONAL
Discussion:
A. Discussion: ELPS 2020/2021 Continued School Progress
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the school progress. We have received a lot of parent feedback on the learning opportunities at East Lyme Public Schools. It is varied from all in-person, hybrid, or moving to remote learning. We have increased positive COVID-19 transmissions in the community. We currently have sent out an Elementary Parent survey and awaiting results. Each Principal provided an update on how students and staff are doing with the current models and the logistics to put in place in-person learning. We will continue in the hybrid learning model to provide a sense of normalcy for all staff and students. The middle school will begin to ZOOM into classes on the alternate days of their cohort beginning in early December. More information to follow from Principal Bitgood. We will reassess the learning model in early January.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
Discussion: B
. Inclement Weather Days
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the guidance from the State Department of Education regarding the flexibility to turn inclement weather/snow days into remote learning days (avoiding a closure). At this time, we are not planning on utilizing this option unless we have multiple inclement weather days throughout the winter. We have three inclement weather days built into our calendar, which we will utilize first. After using the three days, we will then reassess the option of turning to remote days if an inclement weather closure is warranted. Opinions will vary on this topic. Inclement weather days (snow days) bring a sense of normalcy and standard practice that has been in place for years. One less disruption to what students and staff are used to and supports the social and emotional wellbeing of all.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
