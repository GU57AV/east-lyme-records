Chair Timothy Hagen called the meeting to order at 5:03 p.m.
2. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - OTHER
A. Discussion: Equity and Inclusion
-Chair, Timothy Hagen and Superintendent Newton provided an agenda and action plan resources to pre-read to all board members before the meeting.
-Superintendent Newton gave a brief update on the COVID-19 positive cases, closures, and shared recent information that all schools will be taking over the contact tracing. Ledge Light Health District will still confer with all schools, but we will take place in training tonight on what the next steps are. Discussion ensued with board members.
All board members shared their thoughts on what they would like our district to accomplish with diversity, equity, and inclusion. Discussion ensued with board members.
Actions:-
-Continued conversations and increase awareness with the board, district, and community.
-Hiring and increased diversity in teaching staff.
-Initiatives to promote diversity, equity, and inclusion during the week of January 18, 2021, to commemorate Martin Luther King, Jr. Staff to integrate into their lesson plans.
-Finish the Board of Education proposal statement on diversity, equity, and inclusion.
-Start the framework for the Strategic Plan for diversity, equity, and inclusion. (5-year plan)
-Creates an Ad-Hoc Committee to include Tim, Barry & Leigh.
