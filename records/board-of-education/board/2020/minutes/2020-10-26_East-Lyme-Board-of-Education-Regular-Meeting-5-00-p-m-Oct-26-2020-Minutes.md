5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTIONAL
Discussion:
A. Discussion: ELPS 2020/2021 Continued School Progress
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the school progress. We have received a lot of parent feedback on the learning opportunities at East Lyme Public Schools. It is varied from all in-person, hybrid, or moving to remote learning. We have increased positive COVID-19 transmissions in the community and have been moved down to 'orange alert.' Each Principal provided an update on how students and staff are doing with the current models in place.
Discussions included:
-Hybrid Learning Model and ways to improve more teacher contact
-In-Person Learning Model
-How would the In-Person Learning Model look?
-Important to find the balance for all parents, students, and staff
-Communication to all parents, students, and staff
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.

B. Discussion: 2021-2022 SY Budget and Budget Workshops
Superintendent Newton and Board Chair Timothy Hagen have decided to discuss the budget during one of our board meetings in November, along with a workshop in December. Further discussions to be had on what we need for the new budget. Discussion ensued with the board members.
