B. Public Comment
Timothy Hagen read public comment letters that were submitted to Superintendent Newton.
Jacqueline Mazzella, 11 Spring St, Niantic wrote a letter with concerns of bringing back our students due to increasing rise in cases. She is asking the board and administration to keep the learning in the hybrid model.
Jessica Garcia, 10 Winston Rd, East Lyme wrote an email asking the board to encourage social distancing and safe mask wearing and to keep our students protected. She believes with the increasing numbers; it is best we remain in hybrid learning. She also asked that the district think about adopting a program to celebrate Columbus Day or as other districts have made the choice of Indigenous Peoples’ Day.
Nancy Rigdon, Teacher and resident of Niantic wrote an email sharing her concerns if we opened for full in-person learning as a music teacher. She believes that we should look at the increasing numbers of COVID-19 and remain in hybrid model.
Numerous staff wrote a letter with concerns of the health and safety implications if we return to full in-person learning. They stated specific concerns for the board to consider staying in hybrid model.
Kristen Anderson wrote an email with her apprehensions with a full reopening with the increasing numbers of COVID-19 and hoping that our district will remain in hybrid learning.

6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – OTHER
Discussion:
A. Discussion: Concerning Revisions to the Following Board Policies and a Proposed Position Statement re: Equitable Public Education
Superintendent Newton and Board Chair opened the discussion concerning revisions to the following board policies and a proposed position statement re: equitable public education. The board met previously in Executive Session with board attorney regarding the following board policies.
Minority Recruitment
Non-discrimination
Equal Educational Opportunity
Bullying Prevention and Intervention
Equipment, Books and Materials
Dress Code
Policy Concerning Special Interest Groups
Discussion ensued with board members and will bring all policies that were suggested for adjustments to a Policy Meeting to review.
Discussion:
B. Discussion: Follow-Up BOE Equity Workshop
Board chair Timothy Hagen wanted to have follow-up discussion on the Board of Education – Equity Workshop we had with Joelle Murchison on September 16th staff during current situation. I felt that having all board members involved in the equity workshop created a high energy amongst the board.  Superintendent Newton and Board Chair, Timothy Hagen had follow-up discussions with Joelle on what the next steps should be. We are planning another workshop in early November. The board would like to discuss and possibly implement ideas around:
Equity in education
Policy changes regarding equity and inclusion
Discussion of a D & I Task Force
What do we want addressed as a board?

Superintendent Newton provided an update on the current COVID-19 expenses, projections, and grant funding that is available. The district has not had any changes since previous meeting and the projections are still the same.
Discussion/Action:
B. Lillie B. Haynes Boiler Replacement
Superintendent Newton and Director of Facilities, Christian Lund provided an update on the Lillie B. Haynes boiler that needs to be replaced. There are several sections of the 30-year old boiler that has cracked, and it is more prudent to replace than patch. The Town Building Committee would like the board to approve the Lillie B. Haynes boiler replacement contingent upon their approval when they plan to meet on September 30th .
Motion to approve the change order for the Lillie B. Haynes boiler replacement in the amount of $88,700.00- contingent upon the Town Building Committee approval on Wednesday, September 30, 2020.

8. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTIONAL
Discussion:
A. Discussion: ELPS 2020/2021 Continued School Progress
Superintendent Newton provided update on the school progress since reopening. The teachers and staff are having such a challenge with the current Hybrid Learning Model. We are currently reviewing the Wednesday schedule to see if can make any changes.  We originally had planned to move to a full reopening for K-8 but with recommendations from Dr. Sikand (Medical Advisor) and LLHD, we will continue in the Hybrid Learning Model due to increase in COVID-19 numbers. We plan to reassess at the end of October.
Discussion ensued with board members and Superintendent Newton.
