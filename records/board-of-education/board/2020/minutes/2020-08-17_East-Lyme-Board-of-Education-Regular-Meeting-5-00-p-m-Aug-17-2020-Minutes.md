. Action/Discussion: Possible Action Concerning the Adoption of the Proposed Temporary Policies and Regulations Related to the COVID-19 Health Crisis as a Result of Legislative and Regulatory Changes
Attorney Jessica Ritter from Shipman & Goodwin provided a brief summary previously on the proposed temporary policies and regulations that are related to the COVID-19 health crisis as a result of legislative and regulatory changes.
The Board may invoke paragraph 4 of Bylaw 9311-B to circumvent the need to have two readings of the COVID-19 temporary policies given that the changes are necessary due to legislative and regulatory changes.
Motion to approve that the Board adopt the proposed temporary policies and regulations related to the COVID 19 Pandemic, as prepared by Board counsel, Shipman & Goodwin.  Given the unprecedented nature of the COVID-19 health crisis and the critical need for these temporary policies to ensure the health and safety of our staff and students as we plan for school to reopen, the Board further moves to invoke Board policy 9311, which allows the Board of Education to adopt policies after the initial presentation to comply with statutory and/or regulatory changes.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn , second by Catherine Steel .
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Candice Carlson, Leigh Gianakos, Barry Sheckley, Catherine Steel
Abstain: Jill Carini

. Discussion: ELPS Roadmap to Reopening of Schools for Fall 2020
Superintendent Newton provided a brief update on the communication that has been sent out to families along with YouTube videos from Administrators and Virtual Town Hall meetings for all schools. Central Office Administrators have been working with Attorney Ritter on the components of staff requesting leave or working remotely.
Assistant Superintendent Drowne provided a brief update on the learning models. We have about 191 students in Virtual Viking. We will be incorporating our staff along with using Edgenuity for the Virtual Viking model. Discussion ensued regarding choosing best platform for the Virtual Viking model. The Hybrid Learning model will be two days on-site per cohort. The average class sizes will be between 5-10 students. On Wednesda
y,
all students will work remotely in the morning.
Director of Student Services, Kimberly Davis provided an update that we will be offering student's with IEP's to join for four days a week. We will prioritize cohorts with disabilities. We are working hard with staff and parents to make sure we will be implementing the IEP's for our students. We might still be doing some of the related services virtually.
