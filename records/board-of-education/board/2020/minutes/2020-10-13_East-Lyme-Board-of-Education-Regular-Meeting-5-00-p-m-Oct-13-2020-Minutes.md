5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTIONAL
Discussion:
A. Discussion: ELPS 2020/2021 Continued School Progress
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the school progress and the increase in COVID-19 cases and contact tracing. We have received a lot of parent feedback on the learning opportunities at ELPS. It is varied from all in-person, hybrid, or moving to remote learning. Mrs. Drowne and Mrs. Roselli Kelly had a CIL meeting to discuss the current hybrid learning model and move to a remote learning model. We want to ensure we provide consistency for all families and keep the students engaged in five days of education.
Discussions included:
-Zoom or not to Zoom?
-Should we allow 4-days of Zooming?
-What would be the criteria for the Zoom days?
-What to do with the increase in Virtual Viking as a steady increase daily?
-How do we find the balance for all students with the structure of remote learning?
We will focus on high school first and moving down to the other schools.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
