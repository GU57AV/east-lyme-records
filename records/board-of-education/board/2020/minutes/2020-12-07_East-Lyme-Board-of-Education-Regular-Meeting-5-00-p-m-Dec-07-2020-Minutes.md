5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTIONAL
Discussion:
A. Discussion: Coastal Connections Update
Superintendent Newton introduced Abby Demars the Assistant Principal for Coastal Connections.
Mrs. Demars provided an update on the Coastal Connections Program.
The space provided for the Coastal Connections Program in Central Office is a great location.
The students at Coastal have not waivered one bit in their desire to be in school and not learning remotely.
Student engagement during remote learning has recently been at an all-time high with 100% participation at times.
17 students currently enrolled at Coastal
5 students have been referred to Coastal and have expressed an interest in shadowing
There are 3 academic days (Mondays, Thursdays, and Fridays).
Wednesdays are virtual days like the rest of the district.
Tuesdays are for skill building, support, and internship work or the like.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
Discussion:
B. COVID-19 Mid-Term Exam Plan
Superintendent Newton introduced Mrs. Kelly, Mr. Kydd, and Mr. Fasulo to provide up on the mid-term plan for this current year. They met with CIL’s and sent out survey to faculty to get insight on what would be best for students regarding mid-terms.
Mid-Term Recommendations:
Full Year Courses
Quarter 2 will end on January 25th
To maximize instructional time, scheduled midterm examinations will be canceled
Any semester 1 summative assessments (given during regular class time) will count towards Quarter 2 grades
PLCs should continue to plan and administer common assessments as they would in a typical year to ensure common experiences across courses for students
COVID-19 cases have not been transmitted through the school. We need to continue to practice social distancing, mask-wearing, and continue with mitigation strategies.
There have been many cohort changes and is updated every two days.
Mr. Bitgood provided an update on the middle school students ZOOMing each day.
