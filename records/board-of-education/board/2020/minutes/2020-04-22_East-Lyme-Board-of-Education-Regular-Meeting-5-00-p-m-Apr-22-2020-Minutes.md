4. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
A. Discussion/Action: Grading/Assessment
Superintendent Newton shared that we have been working collaboratively with our administrative team on grading and how we can help our students continue to participate and engage in learning activities.
Assistant Superintendent Amy Drowne provided information on what the grading process would look like:
-Based on the recommendation of the Connecticut Department of Education and Higher Educational Institutions, for the Spring Grading Marking Period of 2020, East Lyme Public Schools will adopt a policy of universal Pass/Fail with a provision for teachers to provide narrative commendations, just as they can now. Our policy maintains East Lyme’s high standard of 25 credits for every high school student while providing a clear path to graduation for all students and reduces barriers that may result from extended school closure.
-Goal of Continued Educational Opportunities
-Created guidelines for essential learning
Discussion ensued with board of education members and administration.
Board member asked for an update in few weeks.
Move to approve the adjustment in grading during period of remote learning for students.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Candice Carlson.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, John Kleinhans, Barry Sheckley, Catherine Steel
B. Discussion: Guardian Rights for Learning Platforms
Superintendent Newton shared that administration team has met and discussed guardian rights for learning platforms.
-High School students with IEP/504's or need continued support would be allowed guardian rights.
-We will not open up to all parents of high shool students because we expect our students to be independent learners.
-Middle School families received an email recently asking if they would like guardian rights and had option to opt in.
Discussion ensued between the administration and the board. The BOE is in support of the decision regarding guardian rights.
