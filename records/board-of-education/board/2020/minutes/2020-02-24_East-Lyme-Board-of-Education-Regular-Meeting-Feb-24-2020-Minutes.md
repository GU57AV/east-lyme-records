6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Action, Discussion: A.
A. Discussion/Action: Proposed Adoption of ELPS Budget for FY 2020-2021
Superintendent Newton presented the proposed ELPS Budget for 2020-2021 (PowerPoint presentation is attached to the agenda). The $51,699,974 budget represents a 4.98% increase over the current year.
Superintendent Newton reviewed the budget process and the work that was done between November and January to bring the proposed budget down from 5.57% to 4.98%. Budget savings include reduction in natural gas, reduction in early retirement payment 3 teacher retirements and health insurance increase that has been reduced from 9% to 4.5%. Additional cost avoidance measures were reviewed.
The budget proposal for FY 2020/2021 included two kindergarten teachers at Flanders School and Lillie B. Haynes School ($121,860), two first grade teachers at Flanders School and Lillie B. Haynes School ($121,860), one elementary teacher at Niantic Center School (460,930), two elementary school math coaches, ($121,860), a social worker ($60,930), 1.5 global language teacher ($91,396), a Board Certified Behavior Analyst ($70,000), and district-wide manager for technology services ($70,000).
The district is working with the town to approve our technology budget through the town acquisition plan. It will be paid through an annual payment of $200,000 over the next five years.
Discussion ensued between the Board of Education members.
Move to approve the  Agenda A) Proposed Adoption of ELPS Budget for FY 2020-2021 in the amount of $51,699,974 which represents a 4.98% increase over the current year.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Candice Carlson.
