2. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - OTHER
A. Discussion: Update on the ELPS Roadmap to Reopening our Schools for Fall 2020
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the Reopening our Schools for Fall 2020 to the Board of Education.
ELPS Update on Reopening of our Schools for Fall 2020:
Staffing:
-We have filled most temporary vacancies due to the Family First Act. The act was brought forth by the governor and allows leave under certain conditions.
-Still need to fill 2 Regular Education Teachers and 2 Proctor positions (IA/TA).
PPE and Safety:
-We have distributed all PPE (masks, hand sanitize, and plexi-glass shields) that we have ordered to the individual schools.
-Plexi-glass shields have arrived and have more shipments to arrive shortly.
-We have fulfilled any special accommodations that were requested by staff.
-All cleaning protocols have been implemented in each school.
Health & Safety:
-We have health aides at ELHS and ELMS
-We have received confirmation from the VNA that we will have 3 additional health aides to help in the elementary schools.
Special Education:
-Students who have hearing impairments and are non-verbal will have clear masks available to them along with staff.
-We have provided extra PPE supplies in all of the Special Education classrooms.
-We have prioritized all Special Education students and English Language Learners (ELL) to be able to be placed in Virtual Viking IV. This will allow them to have a 4-day in school schedule.
-We are excited that staff are back and ready to begin the school year.
Staff Professional Development:
-All paraprofessionals and certified staff are back to school as of Monday, August 24th.
-We have held professional development via Zoom and in-person for all paraprofessionals and certified teachers.
-Focus has been on Instructional Learning, Intentional Planning, Social Emotional Learning, and Community Building.
Virtual Viking:
-Facilitated 3 Virtual Town Hall meetings on Virtual Viking for each level (Elementary, Middle and High School).
-We have approximately 200 students in Virtual Viking.
-Originally partnered with Edgenuity because we didn't have exact numbers of staff working remotely and how many families we would have in this model.
-We are excited to have Linda Anania to oversee the Virtual Viking model for our staff/students on a part-time basis.
Technology:
-We will not be receiving all devices until approximately the 3rd week of September.
-With our survey to parents, we will be deploying 837 devices this week.
-We will be providing 86 families with bandwidth in their home.
-Cohort A devices will be sent home on first day of school.
-Cohort B devices will be deployed this week.
-Approximately 3 weeks ago, the Governor announced a learning initiative that we applied for and we will be receiving 356 laptops.
Transportation:
-The bus routes are not changing from previous year.
-All bus drivers have driven bus routes.
-We anticipate that many parents will be driving their students, but families need to allow 10-15 minutes before or after their approximate times due to many students not being on bus.
Discussion ensued with the Board of Education and Administrators.
