4. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
A. Discussion: Update on Remote Continuing Educational Opportunities for All Students
Superintendent, Assistant Superintendent, and Director of Student Services gave update on the Continuing Educational Opportunities for students.
-Parent Survey analysis will be posted on ELPS Website.
-Next cycle of CEO's will be started after April vacation to include Seesaw for Pk-4 and Google Classroom for Grades 5-12.
-Integrate new learning in CEO's in next cycle.
-Focus on skill development, pre-recorded videos, and engagement tasks.
-Will have opportunity to use FlipGrid, Wakelet, and Nearpod which are all approved through data privacy.
-Professional Development will be provided to Teacher(s) and Related Service Providers.
Discussion ensued with Board of Education members and administration. They are in fully support of the next cycle of Continuing Educational Opportunities.
B. Discussion: Grading/Assessment
Superintendent Newton gave update on grading/assessment. Unfortunately there is not much to report out currently. We are waiting to hear guidance from the state regarding guidelines that they will set. Will keep you aprised to any new information.
