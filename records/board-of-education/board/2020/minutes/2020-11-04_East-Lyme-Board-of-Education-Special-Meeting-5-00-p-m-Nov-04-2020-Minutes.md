Chair Timothy Hagen called the meeting to order at 5:02 p.m.
2. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - OTHER
A.  Discussion: ELPS 2020-2021 Continued School Progress
Superintendent Newton introduced Stephen Mansfield (LLHD Health Director) and Dr. Vijay Sikand (Medical Advisor) to provide more information on COVID-19 transmissions and their perspectives as it related to the reopening of schools In-Person Model or to remain in the Hybrid Learning Model.
Recommendation by Health Director and Medical Advisor:
Continue in the Hybrid Learning Model and reassess after January 1, 2021.
Supporting Evidence to Support this Recommendation:
-COVID-19 transmissions have increased among the community.
-Increasing the number of students in the schools would prevent us from social distancing and keeping students and staff safe and healthy.
-We need to see progression to a plateau before making any further changes and this information comes from DPH.
-We are reviewing and monitoring the two-week models of COVID-19 transmissions in our town with the governor and DPH each Thursday.
-Metrics are in place from the state DPH and policy decisions can change at any time due to increased transmissions over the state.
Discussion ensued between the board, health director, and medical advisor.
The goal is to bring students back in-person when transmissions have decreased.
