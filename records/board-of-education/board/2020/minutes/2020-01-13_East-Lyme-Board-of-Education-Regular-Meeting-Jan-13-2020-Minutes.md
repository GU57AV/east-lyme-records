6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion: A. Discussion: Presentation of Superintendent's Proposed ELPS Budget for 2020-2021
Superintendent Newton presented the proposed East Lyme Public Schools Operation Budget for 2020/2021 of $51,347,548 representing a 4.26% increase. We have a Technology Budget of $926,546 representing a 1.89% increase which we have requested to be part of the Town Acquisition Plan.
Staffing Request
:
-2.0 Kindergarten Teachers (ensure appropriate class sizes)
-1.0 Elementary Teacher (NCS)
-1.0 Social Worker (ELHS)
-1.0 Board Certified Behavior Analyst (District-Wide)
-1.5 Global Language Teachers (Elementary)
-1.0 Instructional Technology Manager (District-Wide)
-2.0 Math Coaches (Elementary)
Budget Savings:
-Reduction in Natural Gas
-Reduction in Early Retirement Payment
-3 Teacher Retirements (2.5 FTE)
Total Savings Included in Budget-$559,806
Further budget discussion will take place at the next BOE Regular Meeting on Tuesday, January 20, 2020 at 6:00 p.m., B101, ELHS
