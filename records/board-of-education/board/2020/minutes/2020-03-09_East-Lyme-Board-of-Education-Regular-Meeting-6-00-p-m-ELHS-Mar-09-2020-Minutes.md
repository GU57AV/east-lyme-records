5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTION
Discussion, Information:
A. Connecticut Parent Advocacy Council (CPAC) Presentation
Kimberly Davis and Jen Lusier the leader of the council gave brief overview of the program to include:
-The Special Education Advisory Council shall advise the Board of Education on matters that pertain to the education and safety of students with disabilities
- The council will provide guidance rather than governance
-The council will make recommendations but not policies
- Provide parents, district and community stakeholders a formal voice to create positive outcomes for students with disabilities
- Develop and foster trust, collaboration and true partnership between parents, district staff and community members
The membership must consist of at least 50% of parents of students with disabilities:
- The membership should be representative of the diversity within the district.
- Other members consist of district staff, including the Director of Special Education, and for some councils, community members.
- Each SEAC will determine specifics of their membership when they create their bylaws.
Will bring back to next meeting to be approved by board of education.

9: DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS-OTHER
Discussion:
A. Discussion: Coronavirus Preparation/Preparedness
Superintendent Newton gave update on coronavirus preparation/preparedness after conference call this evening.
-If there is a need for school closures, the State Department of Education recommends adding any missed school days onto the end of the academic year and use April Vacation to make them up. A waiver could be requested if we cannot meet the required 180 school days by June 30, 2020. Currently, there are no plans to close our schools. We are monitoring this virus outbreak by the hour and day, and planning for the possibility of a future closing if things get worse across the state or in our region.
-It is recommended that districts cancel or post-pone large supplemental events (concerts, family engagement events, etc. consisting of over 100 people through April 30, 2020. This would include events such as the upcoming HS Spring Fling Dance scheduled for this Friday evening. That is not an open community event, but a student event which will proceed as scheduled at this time.
-All trips that were planned to occur out of the country and out of state have been canceled due to travel concerns and students congregating or visiting highly public areas or locations.
-All in-state and out-of-state field trips across the district will be monitored as we gather more information.
