Ben Ostrowski, 63 Sleepy Hollow Rd, came to speak about the BLM group that serves the East Lyme community. We have over 400 members. We would like to have East Lyme be a more inclusive town where there is no racism. Implement perspectives of color for a positive change.
Nickie Padilla, 19 Stonywood Drive, came to speak about the BLM group in the East Lyme community. I grew up here and attended from grades K-8 and now I am raising 2 of my own children here. I am a former teacher and adjunct professor at Mitchell College. We need to change the social and political climate in East Lyme that is already happening nationally. I would like to see East Lyme provide training and professional development for all staff and BOE members. We can help provide speeches, testimonials, local trainings that are free. We are here to help and make a difference.
Serena Valentin, 94 Society Rd, came to speak about the BLM group that serves the East Lyme community. I graduated in 2016 and remember a vivid racist incident that occurred during my senior year. There were no policies on how to handle racism at ELHS. I want to make a change and have revisions in the policies regarding racism and add into the school handbooks. This needs to be addressed by all administration and hoping that by coming here, we can make a difference.
Analiese Lapides, 39 Romagna Rd, came to speak about the BLM group of East Lyme. She would like to see more diversity when hiring teachers and staff members to positively influence our students. We have provided a recruitment plan that would bring a greater cultural competency in East Lyme Public Schools. We just ask that you please listen to us and help us make positive changes in the East Lyme community.
Scott Mahon, 11 Birchwood Lane, ELTA president, came here on behalf of the ELTA to have general aggreance that August 24th remains the start day for all teachers. We are open to having more professional development but hoping we can explore other options for more PD days

8. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTIONAL
Discussion, Information:
A. Discussion: ESY Update
Superintendent Newton thanked Kimberly Davis, Cheryl Felix, and Jennifer VanDusen along with all the ESY staff for the outstanding job they are doing with the onsite program.
Kimberly Davis (Director of Student Services) gave update:
-Over 100 students onsite
-We have 15 students working virtually
-We have 65 staff members working onsite and virtually
-Related service providers are providing services virtually
-We currently have 28 students riding the buses (8 buses/vans) with bus monitors
-All students and staff are complying with wearing masks and following social distancing guidelines
-We are using all these new guidelines as a life skill for our students on how to comply.
Discussion, Information:
B. Discussion: Reopening of Schools
Superintendent Newton shared information on the plan for reopening of schools that is due to the state by July 24, 2020. We have been working non-stop with our five committees to create the East Lyme Public Schools Reopening Plan. We have shared the CT LEA Reopening Template with all staff to review.
Committee Updates:
RO-Communication
Superintendent Newton shared that the Communications Committee has been working hard during the last few meetings.
-We are working on the best methods of communication to parents and staff.
-Committee feels that the website is the most conducive way to streamline this information.
RO-Instructional Pathways
Assistant Superintendent Drowne shared that the Instructional Pathways Committee is on their 7th meeting today.
-They have 69 stakeholders
-We have 3 channels that we are working on (Planning active learning, and active learning with a blended learning approach)
-We are also working on the three components of elementary, middle school, and high school.
-We will have three models developed for the reopening of schools.
RO-Operations
Director of Facilities, Christian Lund shared that we are just starting our meetings. We have about 25 members on our committee.
-Focus on cleaning, ordering enough PPE, Food Services, and Before/After Care, Pre-K, building usage.
-Will we need additional staff?
RO-SEL
Superintendent Newton and Kimberly Davis shared that they have had a few meetings regarding the social and emotional learning.
-Using resources to support SEL especially CASEL.
-We will need to help students along with staff coming back into the buildings.
-We are preparing a toolbox and professional development for support.
RO-Logistics
Superintendent Newton shared an update on Logistics committee. Jeff Provost and Chris Majchrzak have been working hard on this committee. There is a lot of crossover with operations and logistics.
-Main focus has been on scheduling which is a giant task.
-Transportation
Discussion ensued between the board members and administrators on the reopening process.
Discussion, Information:
C. Discussion: BOE Retreat
Superintendent Newton and Chair Timothy Hagen shared a document with numerous ideas for discussion on July 28, 2020 for our BOE retreat. We will resend to all board members and focus on narrowing down the list by priority to 2-3 items.
Discussion ensued between the board members.
