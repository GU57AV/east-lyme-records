8. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS – OTHER
Discussion/Action:
A. Discussion: Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Action Planning
Board chair Timothy Hagen and Superintendent Newton wanted to follow-up with the board after the Committee of the Whole meeting regarding diversity, equity, and inclusion action planning.
Short-term actions are as follows:
-Finalize a commitment statement using Appendix A as substrate
-Prepare and adopt a problem statement that will serve to guide the development of a strategic plan
-Explore ways to weave activities related to issues of diversity, equity, and inclusion into all school activities.
-Initiate building programs across the district that will bring guest speakers of diverse background to share their stories and professional development.
-Plan and implement increased engagement within the district during MLK week to recognize diversity and the importance of equity and inclusion.
-Explore options including the CREC’s Teacher Residency Program, ARC and HBCU and implement actions to add diversity in the district’s teaching and administrative staff
-Begin a tab on the District’s website for Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion.
-Continue to advance the Board’s understanding of issues related to diversity, equity, and inclusion as they exist within our schools.
An Ad-Hoc Committee of the Board consisting of Barry Sheckley, Leigh Gianakos and Timothy Hagen will be convened to build a long-term strategic plan proposal for the BOE on diversity, equity and inclusion.
