5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information:
A. Discussion: Be SMART Program
Superintendent Newton introduced Rachel Everley who wanted to speak about the Be SMART Program.
Rachel Everley gave brief description of the Be Smart Program.
-It is an educational program that helps parents/caregivers practice having safe and secure firearm storage in your home.
-She is asking the Board of Education and Administration to post a link to the website for Be SMART and post the one-page flyer attached the meeting agenda.
Discussion ensued between board members and Rachel Everley. The Board of Education will take all the information and bring to a Policy Committee Meeting to discuss further.
Action, Discussion, Information:
B. Discussion/Action: 2020 HS Graduation
Superintendent Newton worked with Steve Mansfield (LLH District) and Dr. Sikand (Medical Advisor) to ensure that the High School graduation plan that Principal Susi, students, and staff wanted to move forward with.
Principal Susi provided an update the Board of Education on the final plan for the 2020 High School Graduation. (Please see the plan attached the meeting agenda)
-Senior Cap/Gown, yearbook, and mask pick-up week before graduation at designated dates/times
-Graduation Ceremony will be spread over three days with designated times. Each student/family will be escorted to designated area where student will receive diploma and picture taken
-On Friday, June 19, 2020 we will release the graduation ceremony video for public viewing to celebrate with families. All students will be able to download as a keepsake
Discussion ensued between Board of Education members and Principal Susi.
Move to approve the Proposed and Revised 2020 HS Graduation as presented.
Motion by Eric Bauman, second by Jaime Barr Shelburn.
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Pro: Timothy Hagen, Candice Carlson, Jaime Barr Shelburn, Eric Bauman, Jill Carini, William Derry,  Barry Sheckley, Catherine Steel
Discussion, Information:
C. Discussion: Summer Camp Programs
Superintendent Newton gave update on Summer Camp Programs. Mr. Newton has been working with Steve Mansfield (LLH District) and Dave Putnam (EL Parks and Recreation Department) to decide the best choice with so many requirements. We feel that with the many restrictions that have been brought forward, it would be in the best interest to cancel summer camp programs. We are working with guidance from the state and other regional superintendents for ESY and will be forthcoming when more information is available.
Discussion ensued with Board of Education members and are in full support of canceling.
Discussion, Information:
D. Discussion: 2020-2021 Budget Follow-up
Superintendent Newton is happy to report that the Board of Finance completing budget deliberations. The Board of Finance will have a meeting this week to determine the mill rate. There is CNRE money left to  in the amount of $14,456.85. The Board of Finance will discuss putting this money toward the turf field and the Board of Education is in aggreance.
Discussion, Information:
E. Discussion: Minimum Wage Increases
Superintendent Newton reported out that the minimum wage is going up. We will have to start thinking about giving increases to bump up to this rate. Another area we may need to look into is with substitutes. There will be more information to follow. (See attached sheet with new increases attached to meeting agenda)
