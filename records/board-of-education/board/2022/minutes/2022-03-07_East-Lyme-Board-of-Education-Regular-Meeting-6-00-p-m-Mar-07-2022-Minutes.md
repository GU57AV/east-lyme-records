5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Superintendent Newton and Mr. Urban provided an update on the Food Service financials. Mr. Urban shared that we have prepared over 30,000 meals a month and have extra revenue due to state reimbursements. Our staff have been amazing! Their commitment and hard work is admirable to prepare and put out food for our students at double the capacity with the same number of staff. This also has been a huge savings in the Food Service department. Due to the excess cash on hand, we would like to recommend the purchase of a food service vehicle, tilt kettle and Flanders School tables.
Discussion ensued with the board and the administration.
