5. SPECIAL REPORTS
Reports: A. Selectmen's Representative
Anne Santoro reported that the Board of Selectman created a Subcommittee to establish a plan to spend the remainder of the initial $5.4 million ARP funding the town of East Lyme received in May 2021. From May 2021 to January 2022, the town propagated about $1.6 million of that allocation to town projects and local town profits. On March 16, 2022, the Subcommittee presented a report of their plan and received approval from the Board of Selectmen. The plan will be presented in front of the Board of Finance for their consideration on Wednesday, April 13, 2022. She recommended that the Board of Education members review the report available on the Board of Selectmen’s website.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
