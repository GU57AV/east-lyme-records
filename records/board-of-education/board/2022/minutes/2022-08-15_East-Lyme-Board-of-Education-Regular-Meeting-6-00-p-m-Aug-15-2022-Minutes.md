5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Food Services/Student Lunch Update for 2022/2023 School Year
Christian Urban, Director of Food Services provided an update for the upcoming 2022/2023 school year.
The USDA stopped all waivers at the end of the 2021/2022 school year. This would make all lunch prices to go back to their original price.
CT Smart Funds has offered a transition period which will bridge the costs until approximately November 2022. Each district was given an allotted amount.
