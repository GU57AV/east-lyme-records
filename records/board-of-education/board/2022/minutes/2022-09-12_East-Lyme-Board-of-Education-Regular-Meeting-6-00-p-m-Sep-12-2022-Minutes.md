6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - INSTRUCTIONAL
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: ELPS Pathways Update
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the East Lyme Public Schools Pathways Program. Assistant Principal David Fasulo has been working hard to create connections with the community for opportunities for students who are not college bound. We plan to provide updates to the Board monthly on the program.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
Discussion, Information: B. Discussion: 2022/2023 Opening of School
Superintendent Newton provided an update on the opening of schools for the 2022/2023 school year. It has been a great start to the new school year. Staff and students are excited to be back to a sense of normalcy. We will continue to work on recruiting staffing for the schools, especially in the roles of the paraprofessionals. The covid requirements are in place under recommendations from the Department of Public Health. We will continue to monitor the numbers and recommendations.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
