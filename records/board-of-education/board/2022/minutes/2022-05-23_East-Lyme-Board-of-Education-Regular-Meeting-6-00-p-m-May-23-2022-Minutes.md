Discussion, Information: C. Discussion: 22/23 Budget Adjustments/Reductions
Superintendent Newton and Mrs. Stevens provided the 2022/2023 budget adjustments and reductions as presented. The Board of Finance has made a budget reduction of $500,000 along with an additional increase in health insurance premium of $341,000 with a total of $841,000. The administration has worked hard to offset the cost with reductions, and we have proposed a total reduction of $642,515. We still need to find other cost reductions for a total of $198,485.
Superintendent Newton also shared with the increase enrollment at LBH, we need to add an additional Kindergarten teacher. We also have a large class size at Flanders School in grade 3 moving to grade 4 that would be over 20. We would recommend adding an additional teacher for grade 4. This would be an additional $120,000.
Discussion ensued with board members with suggestions on how to cover the adjustments and reductions. They recommend going back to BOS and BOF for discussions.
The board will move forward with Superintendent Newton adding two additional elementary teachers.
