4. SPECIAL REPORTS
Reports: A. Selectmen's Representative
Selectmen's Representative Update: (Kevin Seery)
Provided COVID-19 update
At the town level, they have received KN95 masks and will be distributed M-F and from 8:00 – 4:00 p.m. at town hall and on January 13
th
will be distributed from the library from 4:00 – 6:00 p.m. They have a good supply of masks remaining but no test kits. They distributed over 1100 kits.
Some community members have voiced concerns about traffic regarding the gas station by exit 75. This is state DOT driven and will voice concerns to the DOT.
We have formed an ARP Sub-Committee that consists of 2 selectmen representatives, 2 BOF members, 1 Non-Profit member, 1 Business community member, and 2 civilians.
We have hired 2 new police officers recently.
Reports: B. ELHS Student Representatives
ELHS Student Representative Update: (Sachi Vora and Brendan Fant)
Winter sports are underway with guidelines for spectators. For all home games, each player is allowed 4 tickets and away games there will be no spectators allowed.
Mid-Terms begin next week, and many students have concerns with the uptick of COVID cases and students being absent and/or coming to school for exams with symptoms. Also, the students have concerns on how they will make up their exams.
Winter Ball has been canceled due to uptick in COVID cases.
The Planetarium projector was delivered to the high school before winter break.
Clubs are running smoothly with flexibility and revisions due to COVID.
The Winter Extravaganza was a great way to get many students involved before heading into winter break. It was a great success.
The first winter concert was a great success for choir and orchestra.
