5. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Board Attendance at District Activities
Chair, Eric Bauman and Superintendent Newton shared thoughts on how we can improve the board attendance at upcoming district activities.
Discussion ensued with board members on the use of calendar, google docs and sign-ups. More discussion to be done but all on board to increase attendance at district school activities.
Action, Discussion, Information: B. Discussion/Action: East Lyme Middle School Sports
Chair, Eric Bauman and Superintendent Newton wanted to gain feedback from the board on the reinstatement of East Lyme Middle Schools Sports (Soccer and/or Basketball). At the last board meeting on June 6, 2022, many students and parents provided information on how important and valuable it is to have sports happening in middle school. It would cost approximately $25,000 to continue the boys/girls' basketball and soccer programs.
Discussion ensued with board members on all being in favor of reinstating the sports teams but with the current $250,000 budget deficit we need to address it may be difficult. The board agreed to reinstate basketball for boys/girls as there were more students in attendance.
Moved to Approve and Reinstate East Lyme Middle School Boys and Girls Basketball for the 2022-2023 SY as presented.
Motion by Eric Bauman, second by Jill Carini.
Pro:   Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, Laura Greenstein, Jaime Barr Shelburn, and Catherine Steel
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Action, Discussion, Information: C. Discussion/Action: Proposed Appointment of Board of Education Member
Dr. Barry Sheckley filed his resignation as a member of the East Lyme Board of Education with Karen Galbo on June 3, 2022. Eric Bauman shared that the board needs to appoint a new board member. The candidate that has been moved forward to the board from the Democratic Town Committee is Esteban Garcia.
Esteban Garcia shared his knowledge and qualifications that he believes will be a great addition to the East Lyme Board of Education.  He is currently a parent, a committee member on the Districtwide DEI committee and believes his financial background will help in the budget process.
Discussion ensued with the board members and Mr. Garcia. No questions were asked of Mr. Garcia
Move to appoint Esteban Garcia, who presented himself as an applicant for the Board vacancy created by Barry Sheckley with a term expiration of 12/4/23 and no questions were posed by Board members.
Motion by Catherine Steel, second by Jill Carini.
Pro:   Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, Laura Greenstein, Jaime Barr Shelburn, and Catherine Steel
Final Resolution: Motion Carried
Action, Discussion, Information: D. Discussion/Action: Proposed Substitute Rates for 2022/2023 School Year
Chair, Eric Bauman and Superintendent Newton shared the proposed substitute rates for the 2022/2023 school year. The rates were discussed in the FFO meeting prior to the Regular Board Meeting. It was determined that the rates were appropriate as we need to stay marketable to have substitutes want to come work at East Lyme Public Schools. We are currently contracted with Kelly Educational Services who is working with the administration to make it a favorable onboarding process. We hope we have a successful year of being able to fill substitute positions.
Discussion ensued with board members.
Move to Approve the Proposed Substitute Rates for the 2022-2023 School Year as presented.
Motion by Jaime Barr Shelburn, second by Candice Carlson.
Pro:   Eric Bauman, Alisa Bradley, Jill Carini, Candice Carlson, William Derry, Leigh Gianakos, Laura Greenstein, Jaime Barr Shelburn, and Catherine Steel
