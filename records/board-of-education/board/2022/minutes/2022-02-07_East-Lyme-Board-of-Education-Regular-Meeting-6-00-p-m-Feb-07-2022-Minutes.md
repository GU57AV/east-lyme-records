6. DISCUSSION/ACTION ITEMS - FINANCE/FACILITIES/COMMUNITY
Discussion, Information: A. Discussion: Pool Roof Update
Superintendent Newton and Mr. Lund (Director of Facilities) provided an update on the pool roof.
A complete replacement would cost upwards of $200, 000 for material only, with labor it would most likely look to be over $500,000.  More discussion will take place at what is hopefully an FFO committee meeting and the maybe new ad-hoc Building committee.
Discussion, Information: B. COVID-19 Update
Superintendent Newton provided a COVID-19 update. Today, February 7
th
, Governor Lamont announced that he will be ending the executive order for a mask mandate in schools by February 28
th
. We will await further guidance from DPH and LLHD and bring it back to the Board of Education for approval at possibly a special meeting.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
Discussion, Information: C. Discussion: ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget 2022/2023
Superintendent Newton and administrative team discussed the ELPS Superintendent's Proposed Budget for 2022/2023 school year.  The Public Hearing will be held on February 28
th
followed by a Special Meeting for the BOE to approve the Superintendent’s budget for 22/23 SY. We are continuing to look at student numbers at LBH and FL and hoping to hold back a teacher at Flanders School to keep students class size within guidelines.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
Discussion, Information: D. Discussion: Board of Education Ad- Hoc Building Committee
Superintendent Newton and Eric Bauman shared information regarding forming a Board of Education Ad-Hoc Building Committee.  In forming this committee, it will ensure a small group of committee members work on CIP items. Superintendent Newton will work with Bill Derry and Chris Lund to write a charter and have 2-3 committee members.
Discussion ensued with board members and administration.
