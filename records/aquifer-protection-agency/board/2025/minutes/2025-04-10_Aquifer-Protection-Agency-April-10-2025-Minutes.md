         As the Board is aware, the Connecticut DEEP is responsible for overall Aquifer Prograrn
administration, establishing state land use regulations and standards, approving Aquifer Protection Area
maps and local regulations, and developing guidance materials. Municipalities in the progrirm are
responsible for appointing an Aquifer Protection Agency, inventorying land use within the Aquifer
Protection Area, designating the Aquifer Protection Area boundaries, and adopting and implementing
local land use activities within the Aquifer Protection Area by registering existing regulated activities,
issuing permits for new regulated activities, overseeing the facilities and educating citizens on
groundwater protection
