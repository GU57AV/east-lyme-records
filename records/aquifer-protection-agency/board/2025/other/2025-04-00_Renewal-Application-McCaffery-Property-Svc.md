McCaffery Property Services, LLC
Best Management Practices
Prepared For:

Town of East Lyme Aquifer Protection Agency

Materials Management Plan:

McCaffery Property Services shall not have an excess amount of hazardous fluids at
any point in order to abide by aquifer protection regulations. No more than 2.5 gallons of
motor oil. Anti-freeze or gasoline will be on the premises at any given time. Oil and anti-
freeze shall be recycled safely and responsibly at the Town of East Lyme Transfer
Station as soon as materials have been drained from trucks, mowers, misc. equipment,
etc. Oil, anti-freeze and gasoline are for fleet equipment use only, not for retail or
wholesale.

Repair/maintenance shall be performed onsite inside of building to prevent hazardous
fluids from becoming wastewater/runoff in the event of rain. Any spillage of materials
shall be cleaned at time of operation by using appropriate absorption products.

Equipment — 9 1-ton trucks (parked in the back of the building), 6 gas zero turn mowers
(stored inside the building), approximately 30 small engine machines (blowers, string
trimmers, chain saws, etc.) all stored inside the building. 3 Compact tractors, 1 UTV
style skid loader, 1 mid-sized wheel loader and 2 landscape trailers, all stored behind
the building

Storage of de-icing materials are stored at location only during winter months. No
more than 200 50 lb. bags of rock salt stored inside the building. Road salt picked up at
separate location and stored inside salt spreader on truck and used when necessary.

Any questions or concerns can be made to Owner Adam McCaffery during normal
business hours.
