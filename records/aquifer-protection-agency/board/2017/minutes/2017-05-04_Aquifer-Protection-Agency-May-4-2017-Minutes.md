Mr. Walker called the meeting to order at 7:06 p.m. and noted no alternates were present to sit.

ne New Business
A. Approval of Registrations & Issuance of Certificates

Mr. Mulholland highlighted the information contained in the member packet and Mr. McPherson read
Mr. Mulholland’s memorandum dated May 4", 2017 into the record.

Mr. Mulholland said that Land Use prepared the status report in the member packet; 25 businesses in
Town conduct activities that may require them to register with the Aquifer Protection Agency. He
detailed the progress on this determination and said that two businesses on tonight’s agenda are
required to register- Niantic Tool at 32 Industrial Park Road, and Stop & Shop Gas Station at 252
Flanders Road. Monro Muffler of 252 Flanders Road is asking that the current registration done by Tires
Plus be transferred to them, as the new owner.

1. Niantic Tool, Inc.
Mr. Mulholland said Niantic Tool is required to register because they produce or fabricate metal
products. He pointed out the narrative of Best Management Practices submitted by Niantic Tool in the
member packet.

MOTION (1)

Mr. McPherson moved to accept the registration of Niantic Tool, Inc, of 32 Industrial
Park Road, and issue a Certificate of Registration for production or fabrication of
metal products.

Mr. Dwyer seconded the motion.

Motion passed 5-0-0.

Mr. Walker signed the certificate.

2. Stop & Shop Gas Station
Mr. Mulholland explained that the pumping of gasoline requires registering with the agency;
they supplied a safety narrative which is located in the member packet. He clarified that all of
these materials are submitted to DEEP as well.

MOTION (2)

Mr. Donovan moved to accept the registration of Stop & shop Supermarket Company
LLC, Stop & Shop Gas Station and issue a Certificate of Registration for Underground
storage or transmission of oil or petroleum and oil or petroleum dispensing for the
purpose of retail, wholesale or fleet use.

Mr. McPherson seconded the motion.

Motion passed 5-0-0.

Mr. Walker signed the certificate.

3. Monro Muffler
Mr. Mulholland pointed out the Emergency Spill Plan and Procedures included in the member
packet.

MOTION (3)

Mr. Donovan moved to accept the transfer of Registration R17-004 from Tires Plus to
Munro Muffler and issue a new Certificate of Registration for oil or petroleum
dispensing for the purpose of retail, wholesale or fleet use and the
Repair/Maintenance of vehicles or internal combustion engines of vehicles.

Mr. Manning seconded the motion.

Motion passed 5-0-0.

Mr. Walker signed the certificate.

MOTION (4)

Mr. Donovan moved to approve the Regular Meeting Minutes of March 16", 2017 as
presented.

Mr. McPherson seconded the motion.

Motion passed 6-0-0. (Mr. Peck arrived at 7: 20 p.m.)
