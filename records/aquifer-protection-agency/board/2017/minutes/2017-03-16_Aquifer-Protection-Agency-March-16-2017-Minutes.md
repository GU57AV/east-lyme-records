A. APPROVAL OF REGISTRATIONS & ISSUANCE OF CERTIFICATES

Mr. McPherson read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.
Mr. Mulholland provided materials to the Agency with a status list and updates.

Mr. Mulholland stated this is mandated by the State. If it is determined that a business has to register,
we have to issue them a certificate. His office evaluates existing business and new businesses as they
come into town. Jen Lindo has been outstanding getting things done that need to be accomplished, and
communicating with DEEP.

Chairman Walker stated our primary duty is to review and register businesses as appropriate.
Mr. Mulholland stated the fees have been waived and this Agency is operating on the Zoning
Commission’s budget, even though they are a separate Board. DEEP provided us with a list of

businesses, we have added a couple businesses and DEEP has added to their list also. There are forms
