1. CALL TO ORDER
Chairman Walker called the meeting to order at 7:01 p.m.

2. PROGRAM UPDATE — REGISTRATIONS
Mr. Mulholland handed out a spreadsheet listing all of the properties who have been contacted. His
office has contacted everyone on the list and will get back to all of the owners to let them know if they
have to register or not. His office has been in contact with the State.
Mr. McPherson asked if that was every business in the aquifer.
Mr. Mulholland stated that is everything in Level A Mapping.

Mr. Manning asked about the Navy.

Mr. Mulholland stated they are typically exempt. He may have to ask DEEP about that. He believes it’s
important the Aquifer Protection Agency has a budget in the next budget cycle.

Mr. Peck asked if this is all of the businesses in the Aquifer Protection Zone.

Mr. Mulholland stated that list was sent to them by the State and we added one or two. Gas stations
are considered pre-existing nonconforming uses.
Chairman Walker stated we share this responsibility with DEEP. DEEP administers the program. This
agency has been given specific tasks.

Mr. Mulholland stated it is on the town website, and there is a link to the State website.

Chairman Walker stated we are one of 80 towns in the State that has an aquifer that services 1000 or
more customers.

Mr. Peck stated he thinks there are a lot more businesses in the zone, as least twice as many as this list
shows.
