Mr. Mulholland handed out a list of businesses that were contacted.

3. PROGRAM UPDATE
Letters were sent to 19 businesses, one is now defunct. They are required to register with the Town and
this is mandated by the State. There are different types of businesses that would fall into this
requirement such as auto repair, gas station, car wash, nursery, and others. Once they register with the
Town the information is sent to the State. There is a separate mapping system for the Aquifer and the
Aquifer Regulations. The Agency will meet every other month. The Aquifer Protection Agency decided
not to charge businesses for registering. They can hold a Special Meeting if they want to. He will send a
second letter to the businesses that we haven’t heard back from.
