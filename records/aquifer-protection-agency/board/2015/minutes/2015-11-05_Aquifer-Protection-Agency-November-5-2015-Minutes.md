1. CALL MEETING TO ORDER
Chairman Walker called the meeting to order at 8:22 p.m.
Chairman Walker sat Mr. Liska and Mr. Lukas as Regular Members of the meeting.

2. GENERAL DISCUSSION

Chairman Walker stated the Zoning Commission has been designated as the Town’s Aquifer Protection
Agency. We have been overdue for a meeting.

3. PROGRAM UPDATE
Mr. Mulholland stated each member has been provided with the Aquifer Protection Regulations. We
are lacking on businesses registering within the Aquifer Protection Zone. The Zoning Commission chose

to not charge a fee. There is a separate page on the Town Website for the Aquifer Protection Agency.
They should anticipate meeting every two months.

Chairman Walker asked if DEEP monitors compliance.

Mr. Mulholland stated yes, that they have been in touch with his office. All of the forms are on the
website.
Mr. Donovan asked if something has to be submitted for construction by I-95.
Mr. Mulholland stated that is outside our regulatory authority.
Mr. Donovan asked if Public Comment can be on the Agenda.

Mr. Mulholland stated not unless it is a public hearing or required by the Regulations. They can meet
again in January, or as needed. This is another unfunded mandate.

Chairman Walker asked when the Zoning Commission became the Aquifer Protection Agency.
Mr. Mulholland stated about 3 or 4 years ago.
4. ADJOURNMENT
Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to adjourn the meeting at 8:35 p.m.
Seconded by Mr. Liska.
Motion Passed 6-0.
Respectfully Submitted,

(Qeeulu Galbo
