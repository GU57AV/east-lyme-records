TOWN OF EAST LYME “FEAST LYME TOWN CLERK
NOTICE OF SPECIAL TOWN MEETING

Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting of the Town of East Lyme to be held on August
3, 2016 at 7:00 p.m. (E.D.T.) at the East Lyme Town Hall Upper Meeting Room for the following
purposes:

1. TO APPROVE A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF SELECTMEN OF THE TOWN OF EAST
LYME, CONNECTICUT AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION AND DELIVERY OF AN EQUIPMENT
LEASE/PURCHASE AGREEMENT AND SCHEDULES THERETO FOR THE ACQUISITION,
PURCHASE IN THE AMOUNT OF $969,436.00 PLUS FINANCING COSTS; FINANCING
AND LEASING OF CERTAIN STREET LIGHTING EQUIPMENT; AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION
AND DELIVERY OF OTHER DOCUMENTS REQUIRED IN CONNECTION THEREWITH; AND
AUTHORIZING ALL OTHER ACTIONS NECESSARY TO THE CONSUMMATION OF THE
TRANSACTIONS CONTEMPLATED BY THIS RESOLUTION

2. To Approve a Special Appropriation in the amount of $60,000 in CNRE Fund 32 for
McCook’s Amphitheater to an account to be established titled, “McCook’s Band Shell”
for donations received from East Lyme Lion’s Club, Rotary Club and Public Trust.

3. To receive communications from the Board of Selectmen and Board of Finance with
respect to a resolution entitled, “Resolution Making an Appropriation in the Amount of
$480,000 for the Acquisition and Purchase of a full size Class A Fire Engine Pumper
Apparatus and Authorizing the issuance of Bonds of the Town to Meet Said
Appropriation and Pending the Issuance Thereof the Making of Temporary Borrowings
for Such Purpose”.

4. To consider and take action on an appropriation in the amount of $480,000 for the
Acquisition of a full size Class A Fire Engine Pumper Apparatus and authorizing the
Issuance of $480,000 bonds of the Town to Meet Said Appropriation and pending the
Issuance Thereof the Making of Temporary Borrowings for Such Purpose.

Dated at East Lyme, CT this sixth day of July, 2016.

EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Holly Cheeseman, Marc Salerno, Rose Ann Hardy,

Dan Cunningham
