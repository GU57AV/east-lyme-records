SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MINUTES East Lyme Town Clerk

DECEMBER 21, 2016
EAST LYME TOWN HALL

Town Meeting Moderator Gene Cushman called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.
Darlene Stevens led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.
Karen Galbo, Assistant Town Clerk read the Call of the Meeting

Mr. Cushman reviewed the rules of the Special Town Meeting per Connecticut General Statute Section
7-6 and Section 9-360.

Mr. Cushman submitted a letter from Paul Dunphy for the record (Attached).

1. Special Appropriation $13,350 for the 2016/2017 FY — Connecticut Department of
Transportation Comprehensive DUI Grant

Motion (1) Kevin Seery moved to approve Item 1.
Seconded by Holly Cheeseman.

Mr. Nickerson stated this is a Grant we receive every year. It puts extra police on our street and is an
important part of the operation. He urged everyone’s support.

Item 1 Passed.

2. Special Appropriation $398,444 for the Planning and Design for Modifications and Upgrades to
the East Lyme Well 1A and Well 6 Water Treatment Plant Project and Authorizing the Issuance
of $398,444 Bonds of the Town to meet said Appropriation and pending the issuance thereof
the Making of Temporary Borrowings for such purpose

Motion (2) Kevin Seery moved to approve Item 2.
Seconded by Holly Cheeseman.

Mr. Nickerson stated that Joe Bragaw and Brad Kargl were here tonight to answer any questions. He
stated that the Town issues bonds, and the Water and Sewer Department cannot. Water and Sewer
asks the Town to issue the bonds and the Town will issue them if it is passed. He also stated this is
something the ratepayers will pay.

Brad Kargl, Utilities Engineer, East Lyme Water & Sewer, stated he is a not a resident and he has no
ability to vote at this meeting. He stated Well 1A is one of seven Wells we have. 5 of our 7 are already
treated for iron and manganese removal. 1A and 2A are the last wells that need to be set up for
treatment. They would have started this 10 years ago, but quantity of water was more of an issue at the
time. This was always a project that needed to be done. The proposal is to pipe 1A over to the Well 6
treatment plant and expand that building to handle the additional treatment. This is necessary to
improve the water quality.

Ron Rando, 194 Boston Post Road, East Lyme stated we are giving more debt to our kids and to their
kids. What happened to the New London deal? We have bigger problems than this. There are water
meters that are 30 years old. We are probably losing money right and left with those. That is more
important and he doesn’t think this needs to be done right away.

Paul Mock, 41 Whiting Farms Lane, Niantic asked if both 1A and 2A don’t have treatment now why
aren’t they doing both for this project. He also asked if the $398,444 included construction.

Mr. Kargl stated the initial study did include 1A and 2A, but they can’t afford to do both at this time.
Well 1A serves 1/3 of our water system. Well 2A serves only a fraction of that. They can work this into
their debt structure, if they added Well 2A in they couldn’t do it. The $398,444 is for design and
planning of the facilities to get to a point for bids. Construction would be beyond that.

Mr. Cushman asked about Well 6, which is referenced on the Call of the Meeting.

Mr. Kargl stated Well 6 is part of the solution. Rather than an entirely new treatment plant for Well 1A,
they can just upgrade the Well 6 treatment plan to handle additional flow.

Mr. Rando stated if we wait to do Well 2A it would double the cost of what we would pay now to do it.
Mr. Nickerson stated there are dozens of projects that need to be done. This is a top priority.

Mr. Rando asked if they have an estimate to finish Well 1A.

Diane Hyland-Aubin, 114 North Bride Brook Road, Niantic stated they have had to replace their shower
heads and water flow is an issue. The Water Department has been very responsive when she has called
them. She wholeheartedly supports this expenditure.

Item 2 Passed.

Motion (3) Holly Cheeseman moved to adjourn the Special Town Meeting at 7:20 p.m.
Seconded by Esther Williams.

Motion Passed.
