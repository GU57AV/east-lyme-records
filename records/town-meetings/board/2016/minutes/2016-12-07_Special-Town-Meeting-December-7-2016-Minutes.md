TOWN OF EAST LYME
SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MINUTES
DECEMBER 7, 2016
EAST LYME TOWN HALL
Town Meeting Moderator Gene Cushman called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.
Mr. Cushman led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Karen Galbo, Assistant Town Clerk read the Call of the Meeting

Mr. Cushman reviewed the rules of the Special Town Meeting per Connecticut General Statute Section
7-6 and Section 9-360.

1. TO APPROVE AN APPROPRIATION IN THE AMOUNT OF $18,000 CT GREEN BANK REWARDS
FOR STREETLIGHT FIXTURE RETRO-FIT WITH LED LIGHTS.

Motion (1) Kevin Seery moved to approve Item 1.

Seconded by Dan Cunningham.

Mr. Nickerson stated this is a gift to the town from CT Green Bank, they are giving this to the Town for a
variety of different program. We thought it would be wise to apply this to the new street light program
and buy down the principal for greater savings.

Item 1 Passed.

Motion (2) Kevin Seery moved to adjourn the Special Town Meeting at 7:05 p.m.

Seconded by Holly Cheeseman.

Motion Passed.
