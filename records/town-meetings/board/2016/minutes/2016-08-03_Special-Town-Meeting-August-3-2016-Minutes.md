TOWN OF EAST LYME
SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 3, 2016
Moderator, Gene Cushman, called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.
Mr. Cushman led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Assistant Town Clerk, Karen Galbo, read the call of the meeting, stating it had been posted on
the signpost, published in The Day newspaper, and recorded in the Town Clerk’s office.

Moderator stated the rules of the Special Town Meeting, saying that only registered voters or
taxpayers with a minimum assessment of $1,000 on the last completed Grand List were eligible

to vote and that there are penalties for anyone doing otherwise.

41. TO APPROVE A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF SELECTMEN OF THE TOWN
OF EAST LYME, CONNECTICUT AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION AND DELIVERY
OF AN EQUIPMENT LEASE/PURCHASE AGREEMENT AND SCHEDULES
THERETO FOR THE ACQUISITION, PURCHASE IN THE AMOUNT OF $969,436.00
PLUS FINANCING COSTS; FINANCING AND LEASING OF CERTAIN STREET
LIGHTING EQUIPMENT; AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION AND DELIVERY OF
OTHER DOCUMENTS REQUIRED IN CONNECTION THEREWITH; AND
AUTHORIZING ALL OTHER ACTIONS NECESSARY TO THE CONSUMMATION OF
THE TRANSACTIONS CONTEMPLATED BY THIS RESOLUTION.

Motion (1) | Kevin Seery Moved To Approve the Resolution.

Seconded by Marc Salerno.

Mr. Nickerson stated all three of the items tonight have been vetted through the Board of
Selectmen and Board of Finance. The Department Heads are here to answer any questions.

Joe Bragaw stated they are not asking for money, this is to pay as we go. After 7 years we will
save $160,000 to $170,000. New London has done this, and Montville is in the process of

doing it.
Item 1 passed unanimously.

Mr. Nickerson introduced Bill Rix, of the Flanders Fire Department.

William Rix, 140 Roxbury Road, Niantic stated the truck they are replacing is from 1993. The
company that made it is no longer in business. It is currently out of service. There are $20,000
of repairs needed on it, which is more than the worth of the apparatus. It is 23 years old. The
NFPA recommends replacement every 20 years. The new model they are discussing tonight is
a 2016 model, which carries 1250 gallons of water.

Mr. Rando asked if they had looked into replacing the chassis of the present one.

Mr. Rix stated they had not.
Mr. Rando asked if the present one had a cracked frame.

Mr. Rix stated he did not say that.

Mr. Rando asked about a dual axle.

Mr. Rix stated that would not fit in their fire house.

Mr. Rando stated we keep bonding more and more. He is strictly against this purchase. They
can’t afford it and they don't need it. It is time to start thinking about the taxpayers. The town is
stagnant. We need buildings in town to help the taxpayers.

Item 4 Passed (1-Nay)
