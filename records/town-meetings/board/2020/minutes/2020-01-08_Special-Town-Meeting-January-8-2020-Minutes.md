TOWN MEETING _ 1.8-20 - TALKING POINTS
My name is Joe Bragaw. I am an East Lyme resident, anEL Water and Sewer customer, and also the
EL Public Works Director. I have also brought with me tonight Ben North, the EL W&S Asst Utility
Engineer to help me answer any questions that might come up

   O   I would like to break this subject down into three topics;
          1. I will first explain what this project is and why is it needed
          2. Then I will explain why we are coming back to the Town Meeting since this was already
             approved at a Town Meeting in January of 2019
          3. Lastly, I will quickly discuss what impact this additional appropriation would have on
             East Lyme water ratepayers


Topic #1 - Discussion of what the proiect is & why it is needed
  1. This project has been discussed by the Water Dept and subsequently at the Water & Sewer
      Commission meetings all the way back to 2015.
  2. The Department utilizes seven wells throughout"Town to supply our water system, of which 5
      have filtration for iron and manganese. Two of these wells, Well 1A, which is just north of the
      little league fields by the ELMS and Well2A on North Bridebrook Rd do not have filtration.
  3. Iron and manganese are naturally occurring in the groundwater and are primarily an aesthetic
      concern because they can cause a staining of fixtures and laundered goods and have led to
       numerous customer complaints.
   4. Over the past year, the CT DPH has new requirements for removal of manganese in drinking
      water, so it further amplifies the need for moving forward for this project.
   5. Because of this problem, we are trying to correct this issue and improve the water quality in the
      East Lyme drinking water system. The Water Department hired an engineering consultant to
      study this issue and provide cost estimates for filtering Well 1A and 24. Based on this study,
      the Water & Sewer Commission determined that it was too costly at this time to filter both wells,
      so they decided to prioritize moving forward with the Well lA project because of the following
       reasons;
          a. Well 1A is one of the biggest producing wells in Town
          b. Well 2A can be managed by lowering the yield and because we have another large well
             in close proximity to it.
          c. Well 1A is located in the center of town and therefore impacts a greater percentage of the
             water customers and businesses
  6. This project has nothing to do with Costco and Gateway but is more to do with the arca that
     Well 1A seryes.
  7. The project involves pumping water from Well 1A to Well 6 (near the youth football field). A
     big part of the project is expanding the Well 6 filtration building to include an additional large
     filter to accommodate the larger flow of water, and to upgrade the equipment and controls in
     both the Well #1A and 6 buildings.
  8. By running the transmission line parallel to the middle school access rd., we would also be
     solving a problem for the Town and the school as there is no drainage on the Middle School
     lower road and there is a berm that runs along the road, so the water puddles up and is breaking
     apart the edge of the existing pavement. This project will remove the existing fence and knock
     down the berm so this area will now be able to drain off the road. Then we would reinstall the
     fence after we are done.
  9. In summing up the project, we really need to move forward with this project
Topic #2 - why are we back

   1. The project would be paid for out of the EL Water Department Operating budget which is
     separate frorn the Town budget and would then be funded by the water rates as establishecl by
     the EL Water & Sewer Commission every October.
  2. To pay for this project, the Water Dept is looking to take a 2o/o loan from the State of CT
     "Drinking Water State Revolving Fund - DWSRF". The CT DPH is supportive of this project
     and has approved the additional appropriation request.
  3. We have already received approvals from the Water & Sewer Comm, the BOS and BOF and
     the reason we must go to a Town Meeting is we are looking to have the Town of East Lyme
     back up the loan with the "full faith and obligations of the Town" and it is the appropriations
     process as identified in the Town charter.
  4. Back in 2018, our consultant gave us the budget figure of $4,640,000 that they were very
     comfortable at that time would cover all engineering, construction, inspection and
     contingencies. Because of the number that they provided us, we went forward with the
     appropriation process starting last November so that we could get shovels in the ground in the
     fall of 2019.
  5. After approval at the Town Meeting on ll2l19, we went out to bid in June of 2019 and only got
     one bid that exceeded our project budget by over a million dollars. After that, we revised the
     bid documents and went back out to bid in October of 2079 and received 3 competitive bids that
     were over $260k less than the first bid but still over our original budget. With the new low bid,
     we went back thru the process of approving the additional funds which is why we are here
     tonight.
