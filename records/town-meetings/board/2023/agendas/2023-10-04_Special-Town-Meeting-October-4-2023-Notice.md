                            TOWN OF EAST LYME
                      NOTICE OF SPECIAL TOWN MEETING
                          OCTOBER 4,2023, AT 7 PM

Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting of the Town of East Lyme to be
held on October 4,2023, at 7:00 p,m. in the East Lyme Town Hall Upper Meeting
Room for the following purposes:

L. To consider and act upon a Motion to approve a Special Appropriation in the
  amount of $41,107.50 from CNRE Account 32-30-400-700-999 (PWD- Reserve)
  for the purchase of a Live Edge Metal Pless plow system.

2. To consider and act upon a Motion to approve a Special Appropriation in the
   amount of $34,240 for the purchase of equipment and construction services for
  the Emergency Operation Center. The source of funding will be the Nuclear
   Safety Emergency Fund (NSEF) grant allocation #2024-07.

3. To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting.
