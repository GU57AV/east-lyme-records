                               TOWN OF EAST LYME
                         NOTICE OF SPECIAL TOWN MEETING
                              May 3, 2023 @ 7:00 P.M.

        Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting of the Town of East Lyme to be
 held on Wednesday, May 3,2023, at 7:00 P.M. (ET) atthe East LymeTown Hall Upper
 Meeting Room for the following purposes:

1. To approve a Special Appropriation from American Recovery Plan Act funds in an
amount not to exceed $98,160 for the purchase of the following item
listed in the FY 2023/24 Capital Plan:
                   Flanders' Fire Department - Five (5) SCBA Cylind
                   Niantic Fire Department - Six (6) SCBA Cylinders
                   Niantic Fire Department - Upfitting of Engine 3 -
                   Fire Marshal - One (1) set of Turnout Gear - $5,
                   Fire Marshal - Two (2) Gas Meters - 92,000
                   Public Works - Sidewalk Repairs - 925,000
                   Public Works - Various Roof Repairs - 910,000
                   Public Works - HVAC Pump Replacements - $20,000
                                      Total - $98,160
 2. To approve the Special Appropriation of the funds in the Emergency Management
 Performance Grant (EMPG) in the amount of $10,154.10. Of the requested funds
 $9,231.00 will be utilized to cover a portion of the Emergency Management salaries
 and $923,10 is funding for personal protective equipment purchased between
 L0 / / 01/2022 and 09 / 30 / 2023.


 3. To appropriate and transfer $17,558,00 from CNRE Fund revenue account 32-04-
 400-405 (CT DOT Grants - PD) to account 32-25-400-700-402 (CT DOT Grant
 Equipment - PD) for the purchase of electronic citation equipment for East Lyme
 Police Department Vehicles.

 4. To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting


 Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, this 19th day of April 2023.

                           EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
