                                 TOWN OF EAST LYME
                           SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MINUTES
                                  SEPTEMBER 6, 2023

Moderator, Eugene Cushman, called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 PM
at the East Lyme Town Hall.

Mr. Cushman led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Karen Miller Galbo, East Lyme Town Clerk, read the Call of the meeting, stating it
had been posted on the signpost, published in The Day Newspaper and recorded in
the East Lyme Town Clerk's office.

Moderator went over the ground rules of the Town Meeting.

     1, To consider and act upon a Motion to approve a Special
Appropriation of $5Or222 for the installation and operation of two Tier 3
Electric Vehicle charging stations from American Rescue Funds to an account
titled "EV Charging Station" Account 85-7O-OO9-7OO-9O1,
Motion (1) Anne Santoro moved to approve Item 1.
Seconded by Bill Weber.

First Selectman, Kevin Seery stated these are rapid chargers. They will be at Town
Hall. They will be available to the public for a fee.
Barbara Booth, 34 Riverview Road discussed safety concerns she has on Riverview
Road, and if this money could be used for other items

Mr. Seery stated this is being paid for through ARP funds, and has to be used for
specific items. We have Town Road funds, and if she has a specific problem she
should contact Public Works.

Motion (1) Passed.
