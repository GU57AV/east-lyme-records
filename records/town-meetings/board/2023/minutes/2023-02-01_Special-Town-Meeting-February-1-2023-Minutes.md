                                           TOWN OF EAST LYME
                                          SPECIALTOWN MEETING
                                             FEBRUARY 1.,2023
                                             MEETING MINUTES

Moderator, Eugene Cushman, called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 PM at the East Lyme
Town Hall.

Mr. Cushman led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance

Karen Miller Galbo, East Lyme Town Clerk, read the callof the meeting, stating it had been posted on
the signpost, published in The Dav Newspaper and recorded in the East Lyme Town Clerk's office.

Moderator went over the ground rules of the Town Meeting.

1.      To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $19,L76.20, to cover the cost
of purchasing the below listed equipment for the Emergency Operations Center.

Max Standard Dispatch Laptop - complete w/ install, setup, software and card printer. This EOC
Communications System and Card printer are essentialfor Operations during a man-made or natural
disaster event.

Motion (1)        Anne Santoro moved to approve ltem 1.

Seconded by Ann Cicchiello.

First Selectman, Kevin Seery stated this is the remainder of a Nuclear Safety Grant received last year. lt has
to go through Town Meeting because of the amount.

Motion (1) Passed.

Mr. Cushman wanted to make everyone aware that a former First Selectman, Dennis Murphy has passed
away. He was a mentor to Mr. Cushman and was a first class First Selectman.

2.     To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting.


Motion (2)        Anne Santoro moved to adjourn the meeting at 7:05 p.m.

Seconded by Dan Cunningham.
