                                          TOWN OF EAST LYME
                                        SPECIAL TOWN MEETING
                                              April5th, 2023



Moderator, Eugene Cushman, called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 p.m., at the East Lyme
Town Hall.

Mr. Cushman led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Brooke Stevens, East Lyme Assistant Town Clerk, read the Callof the meeting, stating it had been posted
on the signpost, published in The Day Newspaper and recorded in the East Lyme Town Clerk's office.

Moderator stated the ground rules of the Town Meeting.



7. To approve a Special Appropriation in the amount of $15,000 to account #32-30-400-700-
779, transferred from account #01-50-522-620-600. Of this $75,000, $4,972.50 will be paid
to Environmental Systems Corporation (ESC) upon acceptance of a contract between the Town
of East Lyme, executed by the First Selectman Kevin Seery and ESC. The remainder of the
funds, $10,027.50, will be used for site construction items not covered in ESC's proposal, mostly
concrete and paving work for the parking areas.


Motion (1)
Anne Santoro moved to approve ltem 1,
Seconded by Ann Cicchiello

Kevin Seery, First Selectmen, stated the following:
The installation will be for two EV charging stations, for electrical vehicles, each will have two ports.
One will be on Methodist Street, and one will be at the municipal parking lot.
The cost is around 596,000; Normally the town would have had to put up all of this and then get
reimbursed, but Environmental Systems Corporation agreed that they would put up the front money,
and we will pay them back when we receive the rebate from Eversource.
People will be allowed to come in and charge their vehicles, there will be a fee set by the town to
maintain it, and town is not looking to make a profit.
It will take about six hours to have a full charge, but the assumption is most people are coming into the
town for a couple hours.

Item 1 Passed.
