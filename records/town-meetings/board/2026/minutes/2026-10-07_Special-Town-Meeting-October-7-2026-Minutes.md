Moderator, Marc Salerno, called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 PM atthe

      1.     To consider and act upon a Motion to approve an appropriation of $46,000
             in Local Cannabis Tax Revenue to pay for streetscape improvements around
             Town.

Motion (1) Ann Cicchiello moved to approve ltem 1.
Seconded by Dan Cunningham.

Dan Cunningham, First Selectman stated he supports replacing the trees on Main Street.
The trees were planted 15 years ago, some have broken limbs and some have died.

Joe Bragaw, Director of Public Works stated this is for improvements for the streetscape,
this is not $46,000 for trees, it is for 11 treewells, to improve signage, enhance the
streetscape at Colton Road, and improve a section of sidewalk in Flanders. The treewells
are bumpy and are a hazard. The money from Cannabis can only be used for certain
things and one of them is streetscape,

Motion (1) Passed.
