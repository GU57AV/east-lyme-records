                               TOWN OF EAST LYME
                         NOTICE OF SPECIAL TOWN MEETING



             Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting of the Town of East Lyme
to be held on Wednesday, December 2,2026 at 7:00 P.M. (EST) at the East Lyme Town
Hall Upper Meeting Room, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, for the following purposes:

      1.    To consider and act upon a motion to approve an appropriation of $10,000
from cannabis revenue funds to support the development and operation of the Youth
Therapeutic Garden.

      2.      To consider and act upon a motion to approve a three-year allocation of
950,000 per year in opioid settlement funds to offset the cost of the Wellness and
Prevention Coordinator's salary, provide professional development and prevention-
related training for staff, and support prevention programming as part of an 8-year plan
to expend opioid settlement funds. Following this three-year allocation, appropriations
will be made on a yearly basis.

      3.     To consider and act upon a motion to approve an appropriation of $6,4L7
from Local Prevention Council (LPC) Grant funding for prevention and education
initiatives.

      4.      To consider and act upon a motion to approve an appropriation of $37,854
from Youth Service Bureau funds to suppoft various programs and services for local youth
and families.

      5.     To transact any other proper business to come before the Town Meeting.


              Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, this 18th day of November 2026.
