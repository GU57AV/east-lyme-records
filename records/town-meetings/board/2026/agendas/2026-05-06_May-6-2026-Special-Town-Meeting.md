       Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting of the Town of East Lyme to
be held on Wednesday, May 6, 2026 at 7:00 P.M. (EST) at the East Lyme Town Hall
Upper Meeting Room, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, for the following purposes:


       1.   To consider and act upon a motion to approve an appropriation of
$14,000 of opioid settlement funds for the purpose of establishing the East Lyme
Bicycle Unit.


       2.   To consider and act upon a motion to approve a special appropriation
from the FY2026 NSEF Grant, which was awarded on July 1, 2025, in the amount of
$79,009.68 to cover the cost of purchasing equipment and services for the
Depaftment of Emergency Management.


       3.       To transact any other proper business to come before the Town
Meeting.
