TOWN OF EAST LYME
SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MINUTES
MARCH 18, 2015
Moderator, Gene Cushman, called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.
Mr. Cushman led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Assistant Town Clerk, Karen Galbo, read the call of the meeting, stating it had been posted on
the signpost, published in The Day newspaper, and recorded in the Town Clerk's office.

Moderator stated the rules of the Special Town Meeting, saying that only registered voters or
taxpayers with a minimum assessment of $1,000 on the last completed Grand List were eligible
to vote and that there are penalties for anyone doing otherwise.

1. Special Appropriation $15,213 — Parks and Recreation — Software purchase to
appropriate as approved by the Board of Finance a Special Appropriation of
$15,213 — Parks and Recreation — Purchase of Software and Training from Special
Revenue Fund 18 available fund balance to purchase WEB TRAC software and
related training and maintenance fees. An account titled, “WEB TRAC” will be
established for this purpose.

Motion (1) | Holly Cheeseman moved to approve Item 1.

Seconded by Darlene Stevens.

Mr. Nickerson introduced Dave Putnam, the Parks and Recreation Director.

Mr. Putnam stated this software will enable citizens to apply for Parks and Recreation and
Senior Center activities online. There will be a Special Revenue Account to fund WEB TRAC.
This will be for the convenience of the residents.

Item Number One Passed.

2. Special Appropriation $12,500 Part Time Assist. Building Officials - To
appropriate as approved by the Board of Finance a Special Appropriation in the
amount of $12,500 to budget account 5-01-01-104-100-212 (Building Official) and
revenue account 5-01-05-500-501 (Building Permits) due to increased activity in
permitting and inspection activity.

Motion (2) Rob Wilson moved to approve Item 2.
Seconded by Holly Cheeseman.
Mr. Nickerson introduced Joe Smith, Chief Building Official.

Mr. Smith stated the money will be used to fund a part time building official for the remainder of
this year, in order to continue to use those individuals.
Item Number Two Passed.

Motion (3) | Rob Wilson moved to adjourn the Special Town Meeting at 7:12 p.m.
Seconded by Mark Nickerson.

Motion Passed.

Respectfully Submitted,
