TOWN OF EAST LYME East Lyme Town Clerk
SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 5, 2015

Moderator, Pierce Connair, called the meeting to order at 7:00 PM
at the East Lyme Town Hall and then led the assembly in reciting
the Pledge of Allegiance. Town Clerk, Lesley Blais read the
Notice of the meeting and stated that said Notice was signed by
the Board of Selectmen, had been posted on the Town signpost and
that a copy thereof had been published in The Day, a newspaper
published in the City of New London and of general circulation in
the Town of East Lyme and filed in the Town Clerk’s office.

The Moderator stated that in accordance with Section 7-6 of the
General Statutes of Connecticut, as amended, only an elector of
the Town or a citizen of the United States of the age of eighteen
years or more whose names appear on the Grand List of October 1,
2014 and are liable to the Town for taxes assessed against him
based on an assessment of not less than $1,000 are eligible to
vote.

Item #1 of the Call: To approve the Allocation in the amount of
$350,000 from Open Space Fund 59 for the purpose of purchasing a
conservation easement on property to be owned by the New England
Forestry Foundation at the closing of the purchase and sale of
the property; consisting of approximately 166 acres with an
entrance at the intersection of Goldfinch Terrace and Cedarbrook
Road in East Lyme. The conservation easement shall be in a form
satisfactory to the Board of Selectmen and the Town Attorney.
Also resolved, that said amount shall not be paid unless and
until other funds from the following sources are made available
to the New England Forestry Foundation at the closing of the
purchase and sale of the property; $500,000 from the Connecticut
Department of Energy and Environmental Protection; $375,000 from
private or public funding received by the New England Forestry
Foundation.

Motion was made by Steve Kelly and seconded by Bill Henderson to
move the motion.

William Young asked what is the price to be paid to the present
owner of the property. Art Carlson responded stating it would be
$8,000 per acre.

Mike Schultz questioned what are the taxes currently being paid
on this property and Steve Harney a member of KSK Associates, who
is the current owner of the property, responded that the taxes
are approximately $380 per year.

After several more comments both for and against the Call,
Moderator called for a vote. Item #1 of the call was adopted.

Item #2 of the Call: To approve a special appropriation in the
amount of $20,600 in CNRE Fund 32 to an account to be established
titled “Fiber Optic Network” for a grant awarded by Connecticut
Office of Policy and Management (OPM) to install and lease for 20
years a fiber optic connection from the Town Hall to the Niantic
Fire Station where it will tie into the Connecticut Education
Network (CEN).

Motion was made by Rob Wilson and seconded by Kevin Seery to move
the motion.

Bill Scheer, Deputy Director of Public Works, stated that the
State of Connecticut wants to get all the towns in the state ona
high speed optic network and they have offered grants to
accomplish this. Our current internet connection at the Town Hall
is extremely slow. We have a lot of computer usage and this is
currently hindering our abilities to do our jobs. The State has
offered us a grant to allow us to tie into the state’s fiber
optic network so that we can have very fast speeds.

Bill Henderson has some serious concerns. He stated that the
fiber optics network is in everyone’s best interest and that
everyone should be connected, especially our emergency services.
Technology is moving very fast and what is the best today is
outdated tomorrow. Looking at the 20 year lease, Mr. Henderson
feels this will be outdated in 5 years. He stated we should be
talking about bringing fiber network to the businesses in town.
We should be looking at a wider scope. He feels a bidding
procedure would give us the best price and having private
enterprise do it will serve us all better.

Bill Scheer stated that this is the fiber network that the
schools started with years ago. The schools are on it, the
library and the police department will be on it. We need this
now. This will bring our speeds up 300 fold.

After further discussion, Moderator called for a vote. Item #2 of
the call was adopted.

Item #3 of the Call: To approve a special appropriation
allocating $40,000 to upgrade the Town Hall Servers and purchase
and install a new firewall and redundant back-up units.

Motion was made by Holly Cheeseman and seconded by Kevin Seery to
move the motion.

Bill Scheer discussed some of the problems the Town has been
having with their computers. He stated Star Computers has been
brought on as consultants. After reviewing these problems, it
appeared that we need to replace our servers and get a better
backup system. We will have an off-site backup at dispatch so
that if anything happens here at Town Hall, we can recover all
our information.

Moderator called for a vote. Item #3 of the call was adopted.

Item #4 of the Call: To approve a special appropriation in the
amount of $114,460 to complete projects consistent with the
Capital Improvement Plan.

Motion was made by Darlene Stevens and seconded by Rob Wilson to
move the motion.

Mark Nickerson discussed the various projects that this money
will be used for including generators for the Niantic Fire
Department, Station #2 as well as the Grand Street property. He
also discussed the East Lyme Community Center natural gas
conversion energy efficiency and energy efficient HVAC controls
at the Town Hall.

Moderator called for a vote. Item #4 of the call was adopted.

Item #5 of the Call: To approve a special appropriation in the
amount of $178,668 in the 2015/16 budget to the following budget
accounts $178,668 to 01-30-317-500-224 (Town Aid Roads) and
corresponding revenue account 01-03-300-301 due to the adoption
of the State of CT budget. Total amount is $156,226 Town Aid
Roads and $22,442 MRSA for Municipal Projects to be used for
roads.

Motion was made by Holly Cheeseman and seconded by Steve Kelly to
move the motion.

Mark Nickerson explained the budget process is a difficult one.
We set our budget and the State, a month later, sets its budget
so that we are always in a guessing game. We budgeted
conservatively on purpose for the fiscal year 2015/16,for $62,169
but we were approved for $318,000 so this is making up the
difference. We now have extra money to fix our 120 miles of Town
road.

Moderator called for a vote. Item #5 of the call was adopted.

Motion was made by Rob Wilson and seconded by Holly Cheesemen to
adjourn the meeting. Meeting was adjourned at 8:10 PM.
Respectfully submitted,

Lesley A Blais
Town Clerk
