TOWN OF EAST LYME

NOTICE OF ANNUAL TOWN MEETING

Notice is hereby given of the Annual Town Meeting to be held on Monday, May 11, 2015 at the
East Lyme High School Auditorium at 7:00 p.m. to act on the following:

1. To review and discuss the budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2015, as recommended
by the Board of Finance.

2. In accordance with Chapter 7.3 of the Town Charter, the Moderator shall adjourn the
Annual Budget Meeting to a referendum on the voting machines. The budget resolution will
be submitted to the persons qualified to vote in a Town Meeting by referendum by machine
vote, which referendum shall take place on May 21, 2015 at the East Lyme Community
Center from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

3. To adopt a Five Year Capital Plan

4. To conduct such other business as may properly come before the meeting.
