TOWN OF EAST LYME
NOTICE OF SPECIAL TOWN MEETING

Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting to be held on Wednesday
January 6, 2015 at the East Lyme Town Hall Upper Meeting Room at 7:00 p.m. to
consider and take action on the following:

1. To appropriate as approved by the Board of Finance a Special Appropriation of
$650,000 for Settlement of Litigation and full and final releases by New England
National and all of its affiliates of all matters; $325,000 would come from Open
Space Fund 59, $50,000 from General Fund Contingency, $146,000 from General
Fund Undesignated Fund Balance and $129,000 from Legal Settlement Funds.
Dated at East Lyme, CT this 17" day of December, 2014
