Mr. Nickerson stated the items tonight will not require new town money. They are either Grant money
or money that has already been bonded. This item is to accept the $500,000 to build bathrooms,
launch, docks and whatever is left over would be put toward the park on Main Street. The State gives

these STEAP Grants out to improve downtowns, he urged the citizens to approve this item, and it could
be the last STEAP Grant for a long time.

Ron Rando, 194 Boston Post Road thinks this is ridiculous; we can put porta potties down there and save
some money. They will be trashed in the summertime. He thinks it’s ridiculous to run water and sewer
down there. He recommended voting against this.

David Godbout, 15 Cardinal Road had issues with the Notice of the meeting. Agenda item 5 is not a

proper agenda item. The agenda also doesn’t have an address and they shouldn’t assume everyone
knows where the town hall is.

Item 1 Passed.

2. To approve the re-allocation of $70,227.67 STEAP Grant from the Streetscape Phase III for the
construction of a public park at 224 Main Street (former Mobil Station Property), Niantic.

Motion (2) Kevin Seery moved to approve Item 2.
Seconded by Esther Williams.

Mr. Nickerson stated this is leftover money from another STEAP Grant. They called the State and they
will allow us to apply it to the Main Street park. This is money we already have.

Mr. Rando asked what this money was originally allocated for.

Bill Scheer, Deputy Director of Public Works stated it was from the streetscape on Pennsylvania Avenue
and Hope Street.

Mr. Godbout stated he doesn’t think the meeting notice complies with State rules for meeting notices.
Item 2 Passed.

The Making of Temporary Borrowings For Such Purpose.

Town Clerk, Karen Galbo confirmed receipt of the communication from the Board of Selectmen and the

Pending The Issuance Thereof The Making of Temporary Borrowings For Such Purpose. Copies
of the proposed bond resolution are on file and open to public inspection at the office of the
Town Clerk.

Motion (3) Kevin Seery moved to approve Item. 4.
Seconded by John Birmingham.

Mr. Nickerson stated we bought a 1/3 of an acre. It was a dilapidated building that they thought may
have had pollution. It was voted on at Referendum in 2010 to purchase it for around $700,000. They
ended up buying the property after several years of negotiations for less than was approved at
Referendum. It was approved by 73% of the voters. The Town received the Brownfield Grant to clean it
up. A task force met for about 6 months, a landscape architect donated his time and they came up with
the best use. This is leftover money from sale of bonds.

Mr. Rando asked about the cleanup of the property.
Mr. Scheer stated there were two small spots with hydrocarbons; they estimate the cleanup will cost

around $4500.00. They are required to monitor it for 2 years. There was a 666 gallon tank that had not
leaked. It was very much cleaner than we thought it would be.
Item 4 Passed.
