TOWN OF EAST LYME
NOTICE OF ANNUAL TOWN MEETING

Notice is hereby given of the Annual Town Meeting to be held on Monday, May 14,
2018 at the East Lyme High School Auditorium at 7:00 p.m. to act on the following:

1. To review and discuss the budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2018 as
recommended by the Board of Finance.

a. In accordance with Chapter 7.3 of the Town Charter, the moderator shall
adjourn the annual budget meeting to a referendum on the voting machines.
The budget resolution will be submitted to the persons qualified to vote in a
Town Meeting by Referendum by machine vote, which referendum shall take
place on May 24, 2018 at the East Lyme Community Center from 8:00 a.m. to
8:00 p.m.

2. To adopt a Five Year Capital Plan.
3. To conduct such other business as may properly come before the meeting.

Dated at East Lyme, CT this 18™ day of April, 2018.
