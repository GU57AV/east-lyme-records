TOWN OF EAST LYME
NOTICE OF SPECIAL TOWN MEETING

Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting of the Town of East Lyme to be held on
Wednesday, July 11, 2018 at 7:30 P.M. (E.D.T.) at the East Lyme Town Hall Upper Meeting Room
for the following purposes:

1. To approve a special appropriation in the amount of $30,195.15 to revenue account
01-04-400-401 (NSEP) Grant and budget account 01-70-725-224-006 (NSEP Equipment), for the

purchase of portable radios, mobile Police scanners and miscellaneous equipment for the EOC to be
used in the field during disasters.

2 To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting.

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, this 25% day of June 2018.

EAST LYME FIRST SELECTMEN

FILED IN EAST LYME
CONNECTICUT |
