SPECIAL TOWN MEETING EastLyme Town Clerk

JANUARY 2, 2019

Town Meeting Moderator Gene Cushman called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.
Mr. Cushman led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Karen Galbo, Town Clerk, read the Call of the Meeting and stated that said Notice had been recorded in
the Town Clerk's Office, advertised in The Day Newspaper, and posted on the signpost.

Mr. Cushman stated that in accordance with Section 7-6 of the General Statutes of Connecticut, as
amended, only an elector of the Town or a citizen 18 years older or more, who was liable to the Town for
taxes assessed against them based on an assessment of not less than $1000 on the last completed
Grand List who would have been so liable if not entitled to an exemption may vote

Mr. Cushman appointed the Registrar of Voters and her Deputy as tellers and checkers, if the need be.

1. To receive communications from the Board of Selectmen and the Board of Finance with respect to a
resolution entitled “Resolution Appropriating $4,640,000 For The Construction Of Modifications And
Upgrades To The East Lyme Well 1A And Well 6 Water Treatment Plant Project And Authorizing The
Issuance Of $4,640,000 Bonds Of The Town To Meet Said Appropriation And Pending The Issuance Thereof
The Making Of Temporary Borrowings For Such Purpose”.

The communication was received (attached).

2) To consider and act upon a resolution entitled “Resolution Appropriating $4,640,000 For The
Construction Of Modifications And Upgrades To The East Lyme Well 1A And Well 6 Water Treatment Plant
Project And Authorizing The Issuance Of $4,640,000 Bonds Of The Town To Meet Said Appropriation And
Pending The Issuance Thereof The Making Of Temporary Borrowings For Such Purpose”.

Motion (1) Joe Mingo moved to approve Item 2.
Seconded by Marc Salerno.

Brad Kargl, East Lyme Water Utility Engineer stated that the last ten years they have been focused on water
quantity, to add to our supply needed for summer demands. This resolution is to improve water quality.
There are Iron and Manganese in Well 1A, the Well 6 upgrade will treat the Well 1A water. Well 1A will be
piped to Well 6. Well 1A is one of our two largest Wells. Aesthetically the Iron and Manganese have led to
numerous complaints about appearance, taste, and odor. Operationally there has been additional time spent
flushing the water mains to manage the discoloration. The design for this resolution has been completed, the
Connecticut Department of Health supports this project, there is a 14 month timeframe on the project and
they plan to start it in June of 2019.

Joe Bragaw, Director of Public Works stated they have identified the need for this project, it will not be paid
for by taxpayers, but will be paid by the rate payers. They have a lot of debt coming offline, and our rates are
reasonable.

Mike Schulz, Lovers Lane asked if Well 1A is offline will the interconnection from New London be able to
supply water to the Town. Also he asked if Well 1A was ever contaminated.

Mr. Karg! explained that we have to send water to New London per our interconnection agreement with them.
We still need to be fully capable to be able to send it to them in order to receive it from them. Well 1A does
have elevated sodium levels, there is not a lot we can do about sodium.
Joe Mingo, Boston Post Road explained to Mr. Schulz that he may be referring to Well 1, which was
contaminated about 20 years ago with hydrocarbons. Well 1A replaced Well 1.

Item 2 Passed.
3. To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting.
There was no other business
Motion (2) Kevin Seery moved to adjourn the Special Town Meeting at 7:15 p.m.
Seconded by Dan Cunningham.
Motion Passed.
