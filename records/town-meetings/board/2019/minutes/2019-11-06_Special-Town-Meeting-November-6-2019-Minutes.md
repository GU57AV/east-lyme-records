                                       TOWN OF EAST LYME
                                 SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MINUTES
                                        NOVEMBER 6,2019

Gene Cushman, Town Meeting Moderator called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and led the assembly
in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Karen Miller Galbo, East Lyme Town Clerk read the Call of the Special Town Meeting and stated that
said notice, had been signed by the Board of Selectmen, published in The Dayl{ewcpapel and recorded
in the Town Clerk's Office.

Mr. Cushman discussed the rules of the Town Meeting in accordance with Section 7-6 of the Connecticut
General Statutes

l.     To consider and act upon a resolution to appropriate $69,000 in CNRE Fund 32 for the purchase of a
new 12 passenger and 2 wheelchair bus to be utilized at the Senior Center - Delivery expected between July
- October 2020 - to the account titled, "COA DOT 53 I 0 Bus" as follows:
$55,200 from the Connecticut Department of Transportation 5310 Grant and to transfer the town's share in
the amount of $13,800 from Fund 32 account number 32-60-120-100-002 (Proceeds from the Sale of
Vehicles).

Motion (1)         Kevin Seery moved to approve Item 1.

Seconded by Marc Salerno.

Cathy Wilson, Senior Center Director stated she wrote the Grant Application for this. It is a 5310 Grant
which is available to municipalities and non-profits. They are typically delivered in the Summer or early Fall.
If the price for the bus ends up being higher than what has been requested she will use the Parahus Account
for additional funds.

Item l was approved.

        2.         To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting.

Motion (2)         Kevin Seery moved to adjourn the Special Town Meeting at 7:05 p.m.

Seconded by Mark Nickerson.

Motion Passed.
