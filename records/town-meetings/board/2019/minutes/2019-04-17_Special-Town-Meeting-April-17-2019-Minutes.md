Gene Cushman, Town Meeting Moderator led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

              1) $60,477 .86 from the remaining balance of the 2016117 streetlight Acquisition Program
              2) $8,022.14 from CNRE Account 32-60-120-100-002 - Proceeds from the Sale of Vehicles.
Motion (1)       Kevin Seery moved to approve Item 2.

Seconded by Ron Rando.

Joe Bragaw, Director of Public Works, 19 Attawan Avenue stated this is left over money from the streetlight
program that is still available. The Skid-Steer will be used every season and will be a great acquisition for the
Town.

Mr. Rando asked why Item 1 wasn't going forward.

Mr. Nickerson stated that is funding we thought we had and we do not. That item will be in the Capital Plan
next year.

Mr. Rando asked what the John Deere will be used for.

Mr. Bragaw stated it will greatly increase their effrciency; it can be used for snow removal for sidewalks, and
can be used year round.

Mr. Rando asked why we have that money left.

Mr. Bragaw stated we didn't use all the money for the streetlight project.

Mr. Rando asked why we don't put it back in the budget.

Anna Johnson, Director Finance stated because of the financing we did we cannot return it to the budget, it is
available for Capital items.
