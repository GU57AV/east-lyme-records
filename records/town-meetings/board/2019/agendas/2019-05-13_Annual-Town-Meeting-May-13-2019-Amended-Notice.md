                                  TOWN OF EAST LYME

                         NOTICE OF ANNUAL TOWN MEETING
                                     Arredcd

       Notice is hereby given of the Annual Town Meeting to be held on Monday, May 13,
2019 at the East Lyme High School Auditorium at 7:30 p.m. to act on the following:

       1. To review and discuss the budget for the fiscal year beginning July l, 2019 as
          recommended by the Board of Finance.

          a.   In accordance with Chapter 7.3 of the Town Charter, the moderator shall adjourn
               the annual budget meeting to a referendum on the voting machines. The budget
               resolution will be submitted to the persons qualified to vote in a Town Meeting
               by Referendum by machine vote, which referendum shall take place on May 23,
               2019 af the East Lyme Community Center from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

      2. To adopt a Five Year Capital Plan.
      3. To conduct such other business as may properly come before the meeting.
Dated at East Lyme, CT this 77th day of April, 2019.
