NOVEMBER 19, 2014 East Lyme Town Clerk

MEETING MINUTES
Town Meeting Moderator Gene Cushman called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.
Mr. Cushman led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Mr. Cushman stated that Item 1 was not acted upon by the Board of Finance, therefore that Item would
not be heard tonight.

Karen Galbo, Assistant Town Clerk read the Call of the Meeting

Mr. Cushman reviewed the rules of the Special Town Meeting per Connecticut General Statute Section
7-6 and Section 9-360,

Item 2:
To approve that the portion of Mostowy Road shown as "Existing Mostowy Road to be
Abandoned by the Town of East Lyme" on a map or plan entitled "Lot Line Modification After
Road Abandonment by the Town of East Lyme Resubdivision Darrow's Ridge Lot 25, 70 Mostowy
Road East Lyme, Connecticut, Owner/Applicant: New England National, LLC Date: January 8,
2010, last revised 4/23/10 Scale 1"=40', Sheet 4 of 4, James Bernardo Land Surveying, LLC" be
discontinued. This action is in the interest of the public safety for the ingress and egress from
Chesterfield Road.

Motion (1) Kevin Seery moved to approve Item 2.

Seconded by William Dwyer.

Mr. Formica stated this has been a number of years coming. This will allow a 90 degree exit and

entrance at the intersection of Mostowy Road and Route 161. The Statutes require the Special Town

Meeting to discontinue roads.

Item 2 passed unanimously.

Motion (2) Rob Wilson moved to adjourn the meeting at 7:10 p.m.

Seconded by Kevin Seery.

Motion passed unanimously.
