TOWN OF EAST LYME
APRIL 19, 2017
SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MINUTES

Town Meeting Moderator Gene Cushman called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.
Mr. Cushman led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.
Karen Galbo, Assistant Town Clerk, read the Call of the Meeting

Mr. Cushman reviewed the rules of the Special Town Meeting per Connecticut General Statutes Section
7-6 and Section 9-360.
