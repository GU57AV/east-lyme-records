Mr. Cushman appointed the Registrar of Voters as tellers and checkers, if the need be.

1. To approve a special appropriation in the amount of $388,080.60 for the
purpose of acquiring by way of lease or purchase various vehicles and equipment more
particularly described in the Town’s FY 2017/2018 Equipment Acquisition Plan, as evidenced
by one or more agreements for the acquisition of said vehicles by lease or purchase or a
combination thereof, for a total price of $369,400 plus interest at the rate of 1.98% per annum,
payable in annual installments of $77,616.12 ($6,468.01/month) over a period of five (5) years.

Motion (1) Kevin Seery moved to approve Item 1.
Seconded by Marc Salerno.

Mr. Nickerson, First Selectman, stated this is to replace certain items. The Town has a very
organized way of maintaining and replacing our vehicles. This is for a middle sized dump
truck; flail mower, pickup truck, and two SCOTT packs for the Fire Departments. This will also
be for a portable restroom for Cini Park; this will be an industrial restroom for our
specifications. We had received a STEAP Grant to build restrooms but we cannot build them
now due to the new FEMA flood regulations. Restrooms were part of the plan down at Cini
Park all along.

John Drabik, 18 Drabik Road stated this is a lot of money and he would like to see it reduced,
times have changed. He asked about a pay loader at the transfer station that rumor has it cost
$75,000 to repair. He wants to know where that money came from.

Item 1 Passed.
