SPECIAL TOWN MEETING
JUNE 21, 2017
MEETING MINUTES

The meeting was called to order at 7:20 p.m. by Town Meeting Moderator, Gene Cushman.
Mr. Cushman read the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Karen Galbo, Assistant Town Clerk read the Call of the Special Town Meeting and stated that said
Call, signed by the Board of Selectmen, had been posted on the Town signpost on June 8, 2017, and
that a copy thereof had been published in The Day newspaper in its issue of June 14, 2017.

The Moderator stated that in accordance with Section 7-6 of the Connecticut General Statutes, as
amended, only an elector of the Town, or a citizens of the United States of the age of eighteen years
or more who jointly or severally, was liable to the Town for taxes assessed against them based on an
assessment of not less than $1,000 on the last completed Grand List of the Town or who would have
been so liable if not entitled to an exemption under subdivision (17), (19), (22), (23), (25), or (26) of
Section 12-81 of the General Statutes would be entitled to vote at the Special Town Meeting.

The Moderator appointed the Registrars of Voters as checkers and tellers.

1. To receive communications from the Board of Selectmen and the Board of Finance
with respect to a resolution entitled “Resolution Making An Appropriation In The Aggregate
Amount Of $150,000 For The Planning, Design, Construction And Improvement Of A
Basketball Court At Peretz Park And Boathouse Rowing Docks And Authorizing The
Issuance Of $150,000 Bonds Of The Town To Meet Said Appropriation And Pending The
Issuance Thereof The Making Of Temporary Borrowings For Such Purpose”.

The Communication from the Board of Selectmen and the Board of Finance were both received.

yay To consider and act upon a resolution entitled “Resolution Making An
Appropriation In The Aggregate Amount Of $150,000 For The Planning, Design,
Construction And Improvement Of A Basketball Court At Peretz Park And Boathouse
Rowing Docks And Authorizing The Issuance Of $150,000 Bonds Of The Town To Meet Said
Appropriation And Pending The Issuance Thereof The Making Of Temporary Borrowings
For Such Purpose”.

Motion (1) Kevin Seery Moved To Approve Item 2.

Seconded by Marc Salerno.

Mark Nickerson, First Selectman welcomed everyone to the meeting. $130,000 of this is to replace
the basketball courts at Peretz Park; they will also be replacing the hoops. $20,000 of this is for the

docks on Pattagansett Lake, which is used by the crew team and citizens of this town. The docks
are very old and can be a liability to anyone using them and they are beyond repair.
Ron Rando, 194 Boston Post Road, East Lyme thanked everyone for being here at the meeting. We
still don’t know what money we are getting from the State. The Town should take a hard look at
what we are spending. How many towns use that basketball court?

Dave Putnam, Parks and Recreation Director stated it is an open basketball court; there is no way of
knowing how many non-residents use it.

Mr. Rando stated he can’t see spending money that is not needed, just patch it.

Kristina Fenton, 130 Boston Post Road, East Lyme stated she graduated on Friday; she had the
honor and privilege of being on the crew team for the high school and was a captain for 2 of her 4
years. Slowly that dock has become less and less safe. When the rowers have been carrying heavy
boats the dock would sink into the water. Some parents have made some temporary fixes, but the
public could be in danger.

Item 2 Passed.

3. To receive communications from the Board of Selectmen and the Board of
Finance with respect to a resolution entitled “Resolution Making An Appropriation In The
Amount Of $990,000 For The Rehabilitation Of Various Town Roads, Sidewalks And A
Parking Lot And The Replacement Of A Crossing Light And Authorizing The Issuance Of
$990,000 Bonds Of The Town To Meet Said Appropriations And Pending The Issuance

The Communication from the Board of Selectmen and the Board of Finance were both received.

4, To consider and act upon a resolution entitled “Resolution Making An
Appropriation In The Amount Of $990,000 For The Rehabilitation Of Various Town Roads,
Sidewalks And A Parking Lot And The Replacement Of A Crossing Light And Authorizing
The Issuance Of $990,000 Bonds Of The Town To Meet Said Appropriations And Pending
The Issuance Thereof The Making Of Temporary Borrowings For Such Purpose”.

Motion (2) Kevin Seery Moved To Approve Item 4.

Seconded by Richard Morris.

Mr. Nickerson stated we are always planning for the next generation. We plan to spend $900,000
per year on roads, we have to keep up on repair, and we are also doing a lit crosswalk at the high
school. One student was recently hit in that crosswalk. Cars don’t see the kids crossing there. They
are also going to do a sidewalk between the Middle school and Lillie B. Haynes. This is part of our
Capital Plan.

Mr. Rando asked what our bonding debt is.

Mr. Nickerson stated $57,000,000 in principal and interest.
Mr. Rando stated our interest is climbing, we need to cut down on spending, if we can’t afford it
don’t buy it.

Ethan Stewart stated that seems like a lot of money, he understands that we have to spend money on
sidewalks, but should we be spending that now?

Mr. Nickerson stated Ethan had won a C-Span award last year. We have some remarkable kids in
our school system. If we don’t do the work now we will have to do a lot of work later. Joe Bragaw

our Director of Public Works has every road mapped out as far as what is needed for each road. If
we don’t spend the money this year then that is roads that are not being fixed.

Item 4 Passed.

7. To approve a special appropriation in the amount of up to $81,726.35 from the
remaining balance of the 2016/17 Vehicle Acquisition Program to purchase two Police
vehicles with related equipment.

Motion (4) Steve Kelley Moved to Approve Item 7.
Seconded by Dan Price.
Mr. Nickerson stated this is not in next year’s budget, this is unspent vehicle money. They typically

buy 2 vehicles and remove 2 vehicles from the fleet every year. One of the vehicles they are
replacing has 217,000 miles; the other one has 137,000 miles.
Sgt. Macek of the East Lyme Police Department stated they went a year without purchasing any
vehicles. There are 9 vehicles in the fleet with over 90,000 miles on them.

Steven Kelley, 8 Joval Street, asked if we charge contractors for just the officer’s salary or do we
also charge for wear and tear on the cars while idling at work sites.

Anna Johnson, Finance Director stated they charge $5.00 per hour for the vehicles while officers are
working at job sites and that money goes to the General Fund.

Sgt. Macek stated the new chief is re-evaluating that number as it is extremely low.
Mr. Rando asked why they don’t shut cars off when working at a job site.

Sgt. Macek stated if cars are shut down the battery will be dead within a half hour because of all of
the equipment in them.

Dan Price, 205 Upper Pattagansett Road, stated that issue will be addressed shortly by the Police
Commission. He likes to save money, but getting these vehicles is a good idea.

Item 7 Passed.
