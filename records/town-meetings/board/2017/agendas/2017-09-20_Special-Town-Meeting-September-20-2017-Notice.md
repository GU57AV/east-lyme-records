TOWN OF EAST LYME
NOTICE OF SPECIAL TOWN MEETING

Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting of the Town of East Lyme to be held on
September 20, 2017 at 7:00 P.M. (E.T.) at the East Lyme Town Hall for the following purposes:

1. To receive communications from the Board of Selectmen and the Board of Finance with
respect to a resolution entitled “Resolution Making An Appropriation In The Amount Of $139,522 For
Brownfield Remediation And Construction Of Park Improvements And Authorizing The Issuance Of
$139,522 Bonds Of The Town To Meet Said Appropriation And Pending The Issuance Thereof The Making
Of Temporary Borrowings For Such Purpose”.

2. To consider and act upon a resolution entitled “Resolution Making An Appropriation In The
Amount Of $139,522 For Brownfield Remediation And Construction Of Park Improvements And
Authorizing The Issuance Of $139,522 Bonds Of The Town To Meet Said Appropriation And Pending The
Issuance Thereof The Making Of Temporary Borrowings For Such Purpose”.

3. To approve a special appropriation in the amount of $31,855 from CNRE Fund 32 to
account 32-99-400-700-701 (BoE Vehicles).

4, Establish a CNRE account for the purpose of acquiring Police vehicles.

5. To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting.

Copies of the proposed bond resolution is on file and open to public inspection at the office of the
Town Clerk.

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, this 6" day of September, 2017.

AST LYME BOARD OF S: TMEN
