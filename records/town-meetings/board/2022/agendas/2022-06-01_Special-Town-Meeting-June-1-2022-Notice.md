                                TOWN OF EAST LYME
                          NOTICE OF SPECIAL TOWN MEETING

       Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting of the Town of East Lyme to be
held on June 1,2022 at 7:00 P.M. (E.T,) atthe East LymeTown Hall Upper Meeting Room
for the following purposes:
      1,    To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $25,000 to
CNRE fund 32 and establish an account titled, "JAG Grant Projects," and authorize said
purchase from Star Computers.

      2.      To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $70,000
from CNRE Fund 32 account number 32-70-300-500-999 (Town-wide Projects) and
establish an account titled, "NBB Concrete Repairs" more specifically for the structural and
cosmetic repair of the concrete cracks on the Niantic Bay Boardwalk.

       3. To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $15,200
for the evaluation of the permeability of the exterior of the new public safety building,
provide solution options, a cost estimate, and the production of construction plans and
specifications to an account to be established in the project Fund 57 titled, "PS Building
Masonry Repair" source of funding to be from Bond and Note Sale Premium Funds.

      4. To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $23,000
from CNRE Account 32-30-400-700-999 PWD - Reserve to replace the existing engine
in one of the Town's garbage truck (76EL).
      5.     To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $30,489
in CNRE Fund 32 to account32-30-400-700-999 titled, PWD Reserve forfuture allocation
for Publics Works Department equipment,
      6. To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $15,000
in CNRE Fund 32 to an account to be established titled, "Affordable Housing Planning
Grant" for the grant awarded by the State Department of Housing for professional
consulting and technical services to update the Plan of Affordable Housing.
      7.      To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $70,284
in CNRE Fund 32 for the purchase of a new 16 passenger and 2 wheelchair bus to be
utilized at the Senior Center - Delivery expected late 2022 / early 2023 - to account
number 32-40-400-700-702 "COA DOT 5310 Bus".
      B,    To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $L2,42I
in CNRE Fund 32 to an account to be established titled "Surcharge - SB L037", for the
purpose of setting aside funds collected for the 5-cent surcharge on 50ml "nip" liquor
bottles in accordance with SB L037 AN ACT CONCERNING SOLID WASTE
MANAGEMENT,

      9.     To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting.
