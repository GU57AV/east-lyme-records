                                       TOWN OF EAST LYME
                                      SPECIAL TOWN MEETING
                                           DECEMBERl,202l
                                          MEETING MINUTES

Moderator, Eugene Cushman, called the Special Town Meeting to order at7:00 PM at the East Lyme
Town Hall.

Town Attorney, Ed O'Connell led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Karen Miller Galbo, East Lyme Town Clerk, read the call of the meeting, stating it had been posted on the
signpost, published in The Day and recorded in the East Lyme Town Clerk's office.

Moderator stated the ground rules of the Town Meeting.

L       To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $1,010,000 for the following
items with the source of funds being America Rescue Funds:

$920,000        Water & Sewer - Well 5 Reconstruction
$30,000         Niantic Main Street
$60,000         Town Clerk Digitizing of Land Records

Motion (1)      Kevin Seery moved to approve Item 1.

Seconded by Holly Cheeseman.

Mark Nickerson thanked Mr. Cushman for his service to the Town as Town Meeting Moderator. He said
there is a representative from Water and Sewer to answer questions. For Niantic Main Street they are
asking for money to do another Yale Charette study. The Town Clerk has been chipping away at the land
records online, but it is in the public's and town employee's best interests to be able to access these from
home and to cut down on foot traffic in Town Hall.

Joe Bragaw, Director of Public Works stated Well 5 is the only Well in town north of Boston Post Road,
it is on Upper Pattagansett Road, it has the lowest level of sodium, but is only at lSoh of capacity. This is
a huge investment for the Town and he would appreciate the Town's support.

Holly Cheeseman, 16 Mitchell Drive, stated that she supports all three of the items. The Well affects our
water supply, the Charette study will include Flanders also, and we don't know what the future will bring
so being able to access land records from home will be important. She urges everyone to support these
items.

Motion (1) Passed.

2.       To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting.

Motion (2)       Kevin Seery moved to adjourn the meeting at 7:10 p.m.

Seconded by Mark Nickerson.

Motion (2) Passed.

              Su



Karen Miller Galbo
East Lyme Town Clerk
