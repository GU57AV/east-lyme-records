                                       TOWN OF EAST LYME
                                      SPECIAL TOWN MEETING
                                          ocToBER 6,2021
                                        MEETING MINUTES

Moderator, Eugene Cushman, called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 PM at the East Lyme
Town Hall.

Mr. Cushman led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Karen Miller Galbo, East Lyme Town Clerk, read the call of the meeting, stating it had been posted on the
signpost, published in The Day and recorded in the East Lyme Town Clerk's office.

Moderator stated the ground rules of the Town Meeting

1.      To consider and act upon a special appropriation in the amount of $126,053 for the following items
with the source of funds being America Rescue Funds:

                 $ 54,638         Ledge Light Health District
                 $ 25,000         East Lyme Giving Garden
                 $ 8,965          SECT Council of Governments
                 $ 12,500         Save the River/Save the Hills (pumpout boat)
                 $ 13,950         American Legion
                 $ 9,000          v.F.w.
                 $ 2,000          NL Homeless Hospitality Center

Motion (1) Kevin Seery Moved To Approve Item 1.

Seconded By Paul Dagle.

Mark Nickerson stated the Town is receiving approximately 5.4 Million Dollars in American Relief Funds, we
have already received half. We see these as immediate needs and want to address them immediately. Ledge
Light Health District and Southeastern Connecticut Council of Governments were the Town's resources
throughout COVID. The East Lyme Giving Garden has bloomed, with volunteers and their donated land, they
are growing food that is given to people in need. Save The River/Save The Hills needs to put money toward a
new pumpout board, with money also coming from the Town of Waterford and DEEP. The American Legion
and the VFW are helping our Veterans in Town, they are not asking for handouts, they want to deliver services
to our Veterans. The New London Homeless Hospitality Center provides services to people from East Lyme,
we already contribute to them, and this is just a little more. He feels these are intelligent well thoughout
decisions, and he asks for everyone's support.

Deb Mosier-Dunn, the Vice President of Save the River/Save the Hills read a letter into the record (attached as
Exhibit A).

Motion (1) Passed.

   Save The River-Save The Hills has requested $12,500 from the Town of East Lyme (as
well as $12,500 from the Town of Waterford), for a shared percentage with DEEP for a new
boat for our 2O-year-old Pumpout Program. We are desperately in need of a new boat. This
past season it was necessary to use our 2O-year-old retrofitted back-up boat as an
emergency vessel due to the loss of the main pumpout boat, which was deemed inoperable
("unseaworthy") by our insurance company, due to a partial sinking while at dock in the Fall
of 202Q.
   DEEP has allowed us to use the old vessel only on a temporary basis. We have
received estimates for a new Pumpout boat of around $100,000. The boat will have to have
specifications to allow it to be not only in the calmer river, but also in the Niantic Bay which
is part of the territory our boat services. The $12,500 will be used as a required match to
the DEEP 75% funding for the new boat. The boat manufacturers are asking for 50%
upfront and 50% when delivered. DEEP will only grant us the money after the boat is
delivered. The two town's donation will allow us to order the boat with matching STR-STH
donations and membership funds.
   For your consideration: The STR-STH Pumpout program staff have collected over
25,000 gallons of sewage from boats on the Niantic River and Bay during the two COVID
Pandemic seasons (May to November 2020-21). Our service continues to be FREE for any
boaters in the Niantic River or Bay.
   Our Communities are blessed with the wonderful Niantic River Estuary for fishing,
swimming, skiing and all related boating experiences. STR-STH's Pumpout Program has
been instrumental in helping to keep the Niantic River healthy for the towns of East Lyme
and Waterford. lndeed, Fred Grimsey's persistence in creating this program 20 years ago
allowed DEEP to close the entire coast of CT to dumping as the Niantic embayment was
the last to have a Pumpout Program. Please help us in keeping this critical program alive.
   Thank you to the town for continuing to be strong advocates for the health and safety of
the Niantic River!


Sincerely,
STR-STH Board
