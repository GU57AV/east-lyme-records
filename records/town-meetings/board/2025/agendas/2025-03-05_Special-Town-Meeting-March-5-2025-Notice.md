                                    TOWN OF EAST LYME
                              NOTICE OF SPECIAL TOWN MEETING
                                   March 5,2025 @ 7:00 P.M.


        Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting of the Town of East Lyme to be held on
Wednesday, March 5,2025, at 7:00 P.M. (ET) at the East Lyme Town Hall Upper Meeting Room
for the following purposes:

       1.    To consider and act upon a motion to approve a special appropriation of
$38,230.63 for the Public Works Department from NIP funds for cleaning Town catch basins.
       2,     To consider and act upon a motion to approve a special appropriation of
$14,500  for the  Parks and Recreation Department from NIP Funds to purchase 15 trash
containers and liners for downtown Niantic.

       3,        To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting,

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, this 19th day of February,2025
