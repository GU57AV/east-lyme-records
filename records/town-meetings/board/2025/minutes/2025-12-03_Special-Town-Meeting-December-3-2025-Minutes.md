                                    TOWN OF EAST LYME
                              SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MINUTES
                                     DECEMBER 3, 2025

Moderator, Marc Salerno, called the Special Town Meeting to order at7:01 PM at the East Lyme
Town Hall.

Mr. Salerno led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Karen Miller Galbo, East Lyme Town Clerk, read the Call of the meeting, stating it had been
posted on the signpost, published in The Dav Newspaper, recorded in the East Lyme Town
Clerk's office, and posted on the Town Website.

Mr. Salerno reviewed the ground rules of the meeting.

1.       To approve a special appropriation in the amount of $63,300 representing a Special
Education Expansion and Development (SEED) Grant from the State of Connecticut to be used by
the Board of Education in accordance with Public Act (PA) 25-67 S 7.

Motion   (1) Jaime Barr Shelburn moved to approve ltem 1.
Seconded by Ann Cicchiello.

Jeffrey NeMon, Superintendent of East Lyme Schools stated this is a piece of $30 Million dollars to
municipalities to support special education funding across Connecticut. They are hopeful this will
continue each year, this will be used for therapeutic services and support.

Item 1 Passed.

2.       To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting.

Motion   (2) Jaime Barr Shelburn moved to adjourn the SpecialTown Meeting at 7:06 p.m.
Seconded by Ann Gicchiello.

Motion (2) Passed.

Respectfully Submitted,



Karen Miller Galbo
East Lyme Town Clerk
