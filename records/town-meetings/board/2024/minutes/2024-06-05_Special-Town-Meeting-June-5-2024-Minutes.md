                               TOWN OF EAST LYME
                         SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MINUTES
                                  June 5, 2024

Moderator, Marc Salerno, called the Special Town Meeting to order at 7:00 PM at the East
Lyme Town Hall.

Mr. Salerno led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance,

Karen Miller Galbo, East Lyme Town Clerk, read the Call of the meeting, stating it had been
posted on the signpost, published in The Day Newspaper, recorded in the East Lyme Town
Clerk's office, and posted on the Town Website.

Mr. Salerno reviewed the ground rules of the meeting,

1. To approve a special appropriation in the amount of $157,000 to the FY23-34 Highway
Road Reconstruction account 01-30-317-2OO-224 to offset utility pavement restoration costs;

Motion (1) Ann Cicchiello moved to approve Item 1.
Seconded by Rose Ann Hardy.
First Selectman, Dan Cunningham stated that Eversource did a 9000-foot trench for a gas
main on Roxbury and Society Road. Part of their obligation was to re-pave the roads. Since
we were planning on doing repairs it was agreed that it would be better for them to just give
us the money to perform the re-paving ourselves.

Motion (1) Passed.
2. To authorize the First Selectman, in the name and on behalf of the Town, to execute and
deliver a grant agreement with the Community Foundation of Eastern Connecticut for
$15,000, and to do all things necessary to effectuate said grant agreement;
Motion (2) Jason Deeble moved to approve Item 2.
Seconded by Ann Cicchiello.
Mr. Cunningham stated this is for Brookside Farm to be a Homegrown National Park and will
allow him to sign the paperwork.

Motion (2) Passed,
3. To approve a special transfer in the amount of $20,000.00 to account 01-25-218-200-220
Vehicle Maintenance from Contingency account 01-01-120-200-500 as identified below:
