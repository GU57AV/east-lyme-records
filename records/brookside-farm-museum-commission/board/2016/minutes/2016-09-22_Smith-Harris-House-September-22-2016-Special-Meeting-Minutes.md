A. Bat Problem
After a discussion of the problem by all present it was concluded to
find the cost to do the following:
Purchase bat houses to be put outside the barn. Clean the barn. Get a cost for bat removal

and measures to keep the bats out.
For the present Friends member Bob Patterson will put a sonic noise maker and use the

LED fixture to see if that will rid the barn of bats.
B. Cancellation of events

It was voted on by the commission to cancel the train display for October 1°.
