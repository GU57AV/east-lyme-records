was informed the Connecticut Trust for Historic Preservation will visit the house and give
information on how to seek grants. They recognize in kind services for matching funds. They

handicapped access. They discussed the grounds and plants and appropriate decorations. They
recommended the following two books: Visual Dictionary and Caring for Historical Houses. They

has much information on the people that lived in the house. They suggested a seasonal inspection
of mechanical equipment. The next STepCT meeting will be held on August 29. The topic will be

about pests.
