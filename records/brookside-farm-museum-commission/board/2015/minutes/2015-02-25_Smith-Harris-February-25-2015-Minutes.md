Summer Tours

+ I would like to recommend to the Commission that tours be slated for specific times: Fridays,
Saturdays, and Sundays, June--August, open from 12-4 with tours scheduled at 12:30, 1:30,
2:30, and 3:30. (last tour at 3:30).

+ The reasons are two-fold: I would like paid staff to be assigned/select a project to work
on over the summer related to the House, and time between tours would allow for that
to occur.

+ The current technology today allows people to better plan their time, which is of value,
and to be able to let them be clearly aware of when they can access the House will
make for a more appreciative and attentive audience.

+ I would like to ask for a discussion and vote to pilot this format for the 2015 summer,
with the knowledge that if it doesn’t prove beneficial we can reinstate the previous
plan.

+ I will change my schedule to Tuesdays and Fridays (instead of Thursdays) throughout the
summer to assist with tours.

+ I would like to set a date in April to offer a training session to Commission and Friends Board
members regarding the new tour.

Mission Statement Drafts

* Smith-Harris House Commission:

The purpose of the Smith-Harris House Commission is to maintain the Smith-Harris House. The
House serves as an historic house museum that engages the community in the history of
domestic and agricultural life through education and preservation of the Avery, Smith, and
Harris family stories.

+ Friends of Smith-Harris House:

The Friends of Smith-Harris House provides volunteer staff and financial support to further the
preservation of the Smith-Harris House, the historic site, and family stories.
