B. CT StEPS Program — Joanie DiMartino gave a brief summary of what the CT StEPS
Program is and what our involvement would require.

The program is a two year process where we would have the opportunity to develop a
strong foundation for our program with the assistance of the CT Humanities Council, the CT
League of History Organizations and the American Association for State and Local History.
Through this program, we would be guided through the processes of creating a mission
statement, management and interpretation of the site and grant writing for the Smith Harris
House. In order to be successful, the Friends and the Commission will need to work together.
Approval from both organizations is required and Chairman Lakowsky and Donna Massung will
have to sign a letter of resolution to confirm the commitment of both the Friends and the
Commission for the Smith Harris House. There are 7 Sessions that are offered for education,
development and grantwriting. Each session much be attended by one lead person (Joanie
DiMartino) and one other person either from the Friends or from the Commission. Each session
is comprised of six different meetings and the second person in attendance should be able to
attend all workshops within one session. All workshops are held at the CT Historical Society
office.

The total cost for participation in this program is $940.00. This is broken down into membership
fees for two organizations: $50 for the CT. League of History Organizations and $290.00 for
the American Association of State and Local History. There is also a $600 fee to the CT
StEPSProgram. The $600 can be paid in $100 increments. The first increment of $100 is due

by January 12, 2015.

Maggie Prokop offered to write a check for $100 and be reimbursed by the town to cover the
initial fee to the CT StEPS Program.
