AUGUST 2015 COLLECTION COMMITTEE REPORT

We held our first Collection Committee meeting on August 14. In attendance were Kathleen Patterson, Donna
Massung, Maggie Prokop, Joanie DiMartino and Barbara Odgers. The next meeting will be September 19 at 9:00.

Recent donations of period clothing were examined and we decided the items needed further research. We
also looked at a small piece of needlework donated by a Girl Scout leader. | will be contacting the donor for more
information.

Two sets of books were discussed and the committee recommended:
Keep for Special Use — Books by Eugene Field (1850-1895) 10 Volumes published 1889-1901
De-accession — Time Life Civil War Books 17 Volumes (4 Volumes are duplicates) publication dates 1990s
None of these books have any significant resale value but are good reading material,
How to dispose of them? Friends Sale, Library Sale, Transfer Station?

There will probably be more Items from the attic to consider. Most of the attic items will most likely fall Into the
Special Use category. These would include documents, records, craft supplies, dinner wares etc.

Asection of the attic directly opposite the stairs has been cleared for a Friends file cabinet donated by Town Hall
courtesy of Darlene Stevens.

The next area that will be cleared and set up is directly to the left of the file cabinet. This is where the dining
Room chairs will be lined up. The front row of chairs will then be used to temporarily lay out the Holiday decorations for
easy access when the house decorating begins.

Other discussion at the Collection Committee meeting had to do with how to mark individual items with
accession numbers for unique identification. Kathleen Patterson will be bringing this issue to STEPS when they discuss
Inventory Policies & Procedures.

With Joanie’s help | have collected inventory records consisting of 90 printed pages, 73 file cards each listing up
to 10 articles, 59 photos in an inventory album and numerous references of donated items. | plan to enter all of the
pertinent data into an Excel spreadsheet where | can match up and weed out duplicates. Hopefully this will be the base
of a comprehensive inventory.

In August | spent 13.5 hours in the attic. The last half hour on August 18 | removed the dried Hydrangea. The
heat was excessive that day so | have decided to limit my time in the attic until it cools off a bit.
