Curator’s Report--January 2015
Submitted to the Smith-Harris Commission

A New Year, and we begin to work toward a new and improved Smith-Harris House, thanks to STEPS-CT! In
December we learned that we were selected to participate in the 2-year intensive museum program, STEPS-CT,
created by the American Association of State & Local History (AASLH), and administered by CT-Humanities.

reinvigorating the Smith-Harris House will be a boon to the Niantic/East Lyme community.

STEPS-CT

« Donna Massung, President of the Friends Board, agreed to participate in the first STEPS-CT segment with me on
Mission, Vision, and Governance. We attended full two-day workshop sessions in Hartford last week. It was
intensive, informative, and eye-opening.

Our first task is to develop a new mission statement & vision statement for both organizations, so we have a
meeting scheduled for Thursday, 1/29 at 3:30 pm at the House. Maggi Prokop & Kathleen Sasso will represent
the Commission, Donna Massung & Kathleen Patterson will represent the Commission, and I will attend as well.
Other members of both the Commission and Friends Board are welcome to attend and participate. Our goal is to
have written drafts to present to each organization at the February or March meetings.

We will also need to publicize our participation in the program. I’ve received information on the language of the
text for both press releases and notifying our legislators.

We will need to schedule a visit with our peer advisor, Sarah Griswold, within the next two months. Considering
a possibility of a Sunday brunch, where members of both organizations can meet with her.

Programs
on-going meetings with Tara Borden for the Civil War book discussion group. We have confirmed two speakers,

and are looking at two more. Possibly applying for a QuickGrant from CT-Humanities to cover cost of program.

I will be meeting with Tara again tomorrow to try and finalize particulars. Dates currently are: Wednesdays, April

8th, May 13th, June 10th, and July 8th.

Saturday, June 13th, is the next Museum Open-House Day.

Donna & I also discussed the possibility of getting some people together to plan further programs for 2015. What

would be the best way to proceed?
