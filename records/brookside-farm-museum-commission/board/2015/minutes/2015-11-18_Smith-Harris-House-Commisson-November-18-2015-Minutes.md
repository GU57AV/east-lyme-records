NOVEMBER 2015 COLLECTIONS COMMITTEE REPORT

There was no meeting held in November.

Saturday Nov 14 — Recent Donation Inquiries — Olive Weiss, from Salem, is offering a Quilting Frame, about 25 years
old, and some historic clothing. She is willing to put the frame on her car and drive it to the museum. Perhaps it would
be more prudent for us to go to her so if it is not anything we could use she would not have driven it down here and
back home again. Instead | can drive there in my truck and pick it up if it is something we can use (and store) in the
barn. | would need someone or two that are knowledgeable about Quilting Frames to make the decision. Olive has
previously donated a crazy quilt and a vintage red velvet blouse worn as part of a wedding outfit.

Sunday Nov 15 — Attic - The floor space covered by carpet directly opposite the head of the stairs has been cleared and
swept. A single row of chairs are lined up and now hold small boxes of Holiday decorations. Larger tubs have been
placed in front of the chairs. Small grape vine wreathes and a basket are on taller chairs behind and near the file
cabinet, boxes of ribbons are by the chimney.

When Holiday decorations are taken down all items may be returned to the chairs for outside labeling and secure
storage.

The attic needs another extension cord to use with the shop vac. The present extension cord connector does not have
the third hole to accommodate the ground(?) post that the shop vac has.

The blown insulation along the end of the attic floor boards is very loose and dirty, especially across the front of the
house. Blanket insulation (rolled) is much neater and can be just as effective. It also may not be as appealing to critters
as the loose insulation. Rings End has what we need and perhaps True Value, | haven’t had time to check or get prices
but this shouldn’t be a big expense. No hurry, | can put it in myself but first | need to evict the mice and bugs.

Storage for the Friends excess sales items is needed. These would be non-food items; cook books, mugs, post cards
etc. The Friends should have easy access to their inventory. At this time the attic is not the place, it is a little remote
and an extra set of stairs for someone who needs an item immediately. Would the Commission or Director consider
using one of the two secure closets on the second floor for storage; the small closet in the Children’s room or the
shelved closet built against the chimney in the Textile room.

For lack of another place to put this | have purchased the small burner and a set of four drip pans for the electric stove.
They will be installed before the December 5 Wreath sale.
