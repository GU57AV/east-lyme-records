+ Discussion/Action — Vision Statement
Mr. Lakowsky noted that everyone has seen and read this.

Ms, Sasso read the following Vision Statement:

The Smith-Harris House Commission and the Friends of Smith-Harris House work in tandem with each
other to position the Avery, Smith, and Harris families in the historical framework and cultural
knowledge of East Lyme. This includes the development of continued research into the families’ history,
and demonstrating relevance to the lives of East Lyme residents today through educational programs
provided for the community. Both organizational branches actively strive to collaborate with the East
Lyme Historical Society/Thomas Lee House and the Samuel Smith House as integrated partners to
deepen the sense of connection to East Lyme history as it creates a sense of belonging and
understanding of modern life by encouraging residents and visitors to participate in the study of the
past.
