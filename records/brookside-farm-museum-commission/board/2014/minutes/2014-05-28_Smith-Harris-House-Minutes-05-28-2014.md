Curator’s Report--May
submitted to the Smith-Harris Commission

“This is the best place to be--ever!” My son, Dante DiMartino-Hart, on his time participating
with the high school students at the archaeological dig at the midden over the past few weeks.
As the Smith-Harris House plays host to the 8th-grade classes from the East Lyme Middle
School, families and individuals at the CT Open-House Day, and begin our tour season next
month, I hope many more people come to agree with this sentiment, that the Smith-Harris House
is indeed a great place to discover history!

East Lyme Middle School Program

+ After meeting with Richelle Johnson in the beginning of May, I’ve worked over the past month
to develop a two-day program for 180 students; 90 on each day, Tuesday, June 3rd, and
Wednesday, June 4th, from 10:30 am to 2:30 pm. The program includes the following topics:

« “Civil War Music” performance with Rick Spencer
Spencer is an experienced performer, in both national and international
venues, solo, and with the sea music group, ‘Forebitter.”
"Garden to Hearth: Cooking Demo" with Jennifer Emerson
Emerson is a historian and author; she developed her culinary skills at the
Buckingham-Hall House at Mystic Seaport.
« "Murals & Home Interiors" with Carol Marelli
Marelli isa former Commission Chair of the Smith-Harris House; she isa n
artist who designed and painted the murals in the Smith-Harris House
front hall in the Rufus Porter style.
+ "Abolitionist Struggles in CT" with Hank Savin
Savin is a professional storyteller and an interpreter at Mystic Seaport,
where he has had occasion to share the story of the "Amistad" with
visitors.
¢ "Home & Front: Civil War Medicine" with Leslie Evans
Evans is the director of the Avery-Copp Museum and will portray a
distant cousin of the Thomas Averys visiting the family. She is a noted
museum professional and an expert in the use of herbs for culinary and
medicinal purposes.
+ "Still Around: Invented Then, Used Now" with Gary Lakowsky
Lakowsky is the current Commission Chair of the Smith-Harris House; his
work as an electrician and interest in history has allowed him to study the
roots of many everyday objects we use daily that were invented while the
Smith-Harris House was being built.

+ Wil Reed, the anthropology teacher at East Lyme High School led 18 students and a few
