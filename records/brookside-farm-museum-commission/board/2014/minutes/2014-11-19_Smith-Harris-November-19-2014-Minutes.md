1. Call to Order
Chairman Lakowsky called the November 19, 2014 Meeting of the Smith-Harris House Commission to

order at 7:04 PM.

2. Approval of Minutes
Mr. Lakowsky called for any additions, deletions or changes to the October 22, 2014 Regular Meeting

Minutes.

**MOTION (1)

Ms. Prokop moved to approve the October 22, 2014 Regular Meeting Minutes of the
Commission as presented.

Ms. Sasso seconded the motion.

Vote: 4-0-0. Motion passed.

3. Reports

« Public Delegations:

Donna Massung, President of the Friends reported that he House is decorated and that everything for
the wreath sale seems to be in order.

+ Ex-Officio Report
Mr. Wilson was not present and there was no report.

+ Curator’s Report

Ms. DiMartino reviewed her report (attached to Minutes) noting that the Steps-CT paperwork was
completed and submitted on time. They will find out in December if they were accepted. She noted that
the Fiendish Folklore part of the Trick or Trunk Halloween event was very successful. She also reported
that they have been offered another baby cradle, one that is intact and of the period of the House by
Karen Peacock and they will need to make a decision on whether to accept it or not.

« Agenda Additions
Mr. Lakowsky said that he did not have any items to add to the agenda.

= Chairman's Report

Mr. Lakowsky reported that the Lions had made a donation to the Friends for the wire shelving for the
closet. He suggested to Ms. Massung that if they purchase it that it should be wire shelving with a
plastic coating for air flow. He said that there is a dig going on at the east side of the House and
suggested placing yellow caution tape around the area prior to the Holiday events at the House.

Mr. MacDonald said that he would help him with it.

Mr. Lakowsky lastly reported that the crew had come and cleared the grounds very nicely and had
made a nice compost pile of the leaves for them.

He provided Ms. Prokop with the names of the Warden and inmates from York CI who had come to
help and do the painting and asked that she send them a thank you along with one for Mr. Holyfield,
Mr. Ball; Ron Bence and Dave Putnam for their help with all of the work at the house and grounds. He
also asked that a thank you be sent for the donation of the scanner.

= Financial Report

= Status Update
Mr. MacDonald reported that including encumbrances that they have $10,866.06. The unencumbered
amount is $7,338.46. The budget seems to be on target.

* Presentation of Bills
Mr. Lakowsky asked if there were any bills.
Ms. Prokop presented invoices from Cash True Value to Mr. MacDonald for a plug for the House and a
Staples bill for $28.70 that she had spent on items for the House.

**MOTION (2)

Mr. MacDonald moved to reimburse Maggi Prokop $28.70 for items that she had purchased
for the House at Staples.

Ms. Sasso seconded the motion.

Vote: 4-0-0. Motion passed.

= Barn Rentals
Ms. Prokop reported that she has booked three (3) barn rentals for next year already. None are during
the winter months.

Ms, Prokop also reported that for the Wreath Sale and Open House that the Montville Choir (15
members) would be coming down on Sunday December 7, and caroling on the grounds from 2 - 3
PM. In retum for this they would like service hours.

The members suggested having hot chocolate along with the cider in the barn and cookies.

Ms. Prokop said that she had sent all of the thank you letters and had also made cookies for Mr.
Putnam and his crew and Mr. Bence.

+ Collections Committee
Mr. Lakowsky asked for a motion on whether or not to accept the cradle from Karen Peacock.

**MOTION (3)

Mr. MacDonald moved to accept the cradle from Karen Peacock.
Ms. Sasso seconded the motion.

Vote: 4-0-0. Motion passed.

Mr. Lakowsky asked Ms. Prokop to send a thank you from the Commissioners for the cradle.

+ Correspondence
Mr. Lakowsky said that he had received a letter dated 6/11/14 from the Anthropology Class at the
school thanking them for the guided tour of the House and the information they provided.
