Curator’s Report
submitted to the Smith-Harris Commission

It’s been a very success holiday season! Although Mother Nature made her presence felt, which
caused a rescheduling of a program, the House was beautifully decorated, the Open-House and
Wreath Sale were entertaining events and great fundraisers, and everyone is looking forward to
continued growth and opportunities to share the Smith-Harris House and its history with visitors.

Thank You!

I want to extend a special thank you to both the Friends Board and the Commission for all the
time and energy contributed to decorating the House. Each room looks lovely, and, while some
decorations gave us moments of frustration as we approached how to best “deck our halls,” with
them, those moments were met with laughter, great suggestions, and positive support. It’s a joy
to work with such committed people. I will be organizing a colleague-to-colleague tour for both
groups--hopefully at the Shaw Mansion in New London--as a fun “end of year celebration”
opportunity for all of us.

Members of the Friends Board and Commission have agreed to meet on Thursday, January 30th
