Curator’s Report
submitted to the Smith-Harris House Commission

April showers have arrived, and along with them are green grasses, flowers blooming, barn
rentals, and another semester of young archaeologists to the site! Summer will arrive once again,
sooner than we can imagine, and work is occurring to prepare the house for upcoming programs

and tours.

Open-House Day

Sponsored by the CT Dept. on Tourism, CT Open-House Day occurs this year on
Saturday, June 14", from 10 am to 3 pm. The Smith-Harris House is participating with
the Thomas Lee House and the Samuel Smith House to offer visitors an opportunity to
discover more about what life in East Lyme/Niantic was like several centuries ago.

I’ve been slated with overseeing the publicity for this event; I’ve begun contacting
various our promotional venues.

I’ve organized our offerings for the day, which will include Jennifer Emerson returning to
offer open-hearth cooking demonstrations in the dining room (rug will be removed for
this demonstration), games on the lawn (I’ve asked Arielle DeLaura if she would be
interested in overseeing this aspect), tours of the House (presented by Maggi Prokop and
other Commission members), and refreshments. I am trying to locate a few reenactors to
participate as well; so far no luck, but I plan to keep trying. I’ve faxed our agreement
form to the Dept. of Tourism, so we should be all set there.

The bus has been approved; we’re hoping to offer a bus tour around the route to all three
museums; a bus driver is being located by Cathy Wilson of the Senior Center.

I think this might be a good opportunity to go down our list\of perspective volunteers
from membership renewals and find out if anyone wants to participate and learn more
about the site.

Archaeology Class

Willard Reed, the high school anthropology teacher who now oversees the archaeology
program has begun the spring semester. He put grass seed down by the outhouse where
the previous dig was located. This semester’s dig will be at the midden.

15 students from his class of 18 visited the museum on Thursday, April 10 at 7:30 am and
were treated to an in-depth tour of the house and families who lived here. Special thanks
to Maggi Prokop for generously donating her time and expertise with the group!

I plan to participate for a couple days during the dig, to learn a little more about the site.
