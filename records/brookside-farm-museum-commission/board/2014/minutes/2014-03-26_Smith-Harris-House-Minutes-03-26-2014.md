Above my desk is a gorgeous framed color photograph of the House, with a field of daffodils
blooming across the lawn. As I write this report, the weather forecast tells of one more snowfall,

office. The newness of the spring season is in the air, and with it comes positive change for the
House, as we work toward meeting the goals we set over the preceding months.
