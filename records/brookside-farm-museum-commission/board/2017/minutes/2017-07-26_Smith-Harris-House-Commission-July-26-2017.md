Illuminating the Process of Learning in History Museums Through the Perspective of Adult
Visitors

Dissertation Research Study
Lisa Marcinkowski June

Research Objective:

To capture evidence of learning in history museums in the words of adult visitors. This study
focuses on the beginning of learning rather than long term learning. | presume that adult
visitors are most likely to add to the store of knowledge they already possess (constructivism).

Research Questions:

RQ1. What are adults learning in experiential history museums?

RQ2. What learning processes do adults engage in during visits to experiential history
museums?

Research Sites: History museums that offer guided tours

Current list of participating museums

e Avery Copp House Museum, Groton, CT

e Connecticut’s Old State House, Hartford, CT
e Lockwood-Mathews Mansion, Norwalk, CT
e Mark Twain House & Museum, Hartford, CT
e Stanley-Whitman House, Farmington, CT

Participants: Adults over 18 with no accompanying children

e Participation is voluntary
e Those who choose to participate will review and sign a consent form (5 minutes)

Research Protocol:

e Pre-tour Survey (5 minutes)
e Observation of Research Participant During Tour (Length of Tour)
e Post-Tour Interview (20-25 minutes)

o Interview will be recorded (nothing else will be recorded)

Researcher requirements:

e A brief sentence that covers the topic of the tour for the pre-tour survey
e The opportunity to invite museum visitors to participate in my research
e A quiet place in which to conduct the post-tour interview
