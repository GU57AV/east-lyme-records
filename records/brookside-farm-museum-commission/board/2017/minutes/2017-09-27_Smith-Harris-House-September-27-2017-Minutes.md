Strategic Plan
+ Please note date/time of our third Board Retreat:
+ Saturday, October 14, 11:30 am to 3:30 pm (11:30 to 12:30 with Town Officials)

Please make every effort to attend; our grant match is based on Trustee time spent on the project.

+ As per our consultant’s request, a moratorium has been placed on new Board members for the
Friends and new Commission members until we finish the strategic plan process; this is
temporary, until mid-November, and it doesn’t prevent interested people from volunteering
their time with us, just not formally joining the Trustees until after the final process. [Note:
when I refer to both “Friends” and “Commission” together, I will use the term “Trustees,” for
ease of use.]

the property/landscape, as our new interpretation and scope have not yet been established.

Collections

Several donors have signed Deed of Gifts; working on establishing times for others to stop by

the House.

We are still in the long-term process of locating donors for items with no provenance in the

collection.

The Town insurance connections are reviewing our forms.

We were approached with the idea of having items donated to the Historic Properties

Commission, and stored at the Smith-Harris House. After speaking to the Friends, members of

the Commission, and our Strategic Plan consultant (who is also a STEPS peer mentor), we are

all in agreement that this is not in the best interests of our site to store artifacts we do not
legally own. I’ve sent an email to Barbara Johnson Low, Chair of the Historic Properties

Commission, explaining that we would not be able to accommodate this request, and why we

have concerns. I did also make it clear that we would be happy to accept donations and share

with the other historic sites in town, following best museum practices for collections.
