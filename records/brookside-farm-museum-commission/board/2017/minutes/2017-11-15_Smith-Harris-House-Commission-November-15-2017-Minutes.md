Director’s Report—November, 2017
Smith-Harris House Commission & Friends of Smith-Harris House, Inc.

Strategic Plan

* Thank you everyone on the Commission & Friends Board of Directors for your time,

perspective, and dedication during this amazing process! I hope you found the retreats as

inspiring and exciting as I did—we have begun a great new journey for the museum, and we
are become a rising tide, for our own site and through what we can offer the heritage and
preservation community of East Lyme, as well! I’m looking forward to reading the final
document!

Thank you also to the members of the Samuel Smith House, the East Lyme Historical Society,

Town Historian Liz Kuchta, members of the Historic Properties Commission, and First

Selectman, Mark Nickerson, for taking time out of their busy schedules to meet with us

throughout this process and share their ideas, suggestions, perspectives, and concerns—our

collaborative work and sharing of resources and networking will be stronger for it, and I look
forward to developing innovative ways we can work together to improve visitor experiences as
we continue this necessary and important work!

Website/Technology

+ No current updates; Kathleen is working on our social media imprint through Facebook.

+ Website work will begin shortly, and will be a primary focus over the winter months.

Programs

« The “Novel History” collaborative program with the East Lyme Public Library is all set; please
check your program planning meeting notes for dates. The theme for 2018 is “WWI in
Literature.” Books have been selected, and we are in the process of seeking guest speakers,
and, with the CTHumanities funding restored, may consider applying for a QuickGrant.

+ Preparations for the Holiday programs (wreath sale & open-house) events will be similar to
last year; the “Belsnickel” will appear during the Holiday Open-House this year.

+ Jeff Belanger has contacted me about another “Belsnickel” feature on PBS, this time for a
program entitled, “Creepy Christmas,” that they are producing.

+ “Spirit Voices” 2017 was another great success! We had 83 people in attendance, raised funds
for the Friends of Smith-Harris House, and donated two large tubs full of food to the East
Lyme Care & Share, Inc. Potential tweaking of the program for next time is included in the
program planning meeting notes. Special thanks to the Florence Griswold Museum for the
loan of the 19th-century hearse and Advance Power Equipment for donating their service.

+ Further programming details are the in planning notes submitted to Commission & Friends
Board previously; 2018 is still a transitional year, as we embrace the changes from the
Strategic Plan and continue with STEPS work.

CLHO Fundraiser Auction Update

* The CLHO (Connecticut League of History Organizations),auction is going strong! We have a
first bid, so there will be a barn rental from this donation.

« Please peruse the CLHO on-line auction; they have many services and unique experiences that
can be bid on for personal or professional development use.

STEPS Sub-Committee
« We are developing a series of committee descriptions, including oversight and commitment.
The plan is for new Commission & Friends Board and volunteers to be able to easily discover
ways to participate, and also for a greater understanding of the key responsibilities of each
committee. A flow chart have been suggested to demonstrate how the committees can work
together and support each other to ease management of the museum.

* We hope to have our new board manuals for Commission & Friends Board completed by the
end of January.

+ New policies will be coming soon, too: site usage contracts, food policy, Trustee job
descriptions, etc.

Collections

+ The Collections Committee has several items for recommendation.

+ At present the Committee has placed a moratorium on collections (with the exception of any
artifact or document directly related to the Avery, Smith, or Harris families) until we
accomplish more with the inventory/preservation, and also until we have a clearer scope of
what we want to collect in relation to the new upcoming strategic plan.

+ We are looking at applying for at CAP (collections assessment program) grant to assist us with
re-envisioning storage for our collection.

Landscape
