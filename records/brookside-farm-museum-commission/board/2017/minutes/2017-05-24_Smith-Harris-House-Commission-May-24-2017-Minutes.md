I. CALL TO ORDER. Chairman Seifel called the Smith-Harris House Commission May 24,
2017 Meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.

I. APPROVAL OF MINUTES. Mr. Seifel asked for additions, deletions or corrections to the
April 26, 2017 Smith-Harris House Commission Meeting Minutes.

2017 Smith-Harris House Minutes, as presented.

Ill. REPORTS.

A. Public Delegations. Ms. Massung reported the Friends/Commission Planning
Meeting will be held on June 2 at 4 p.m. in the barn.

The Friends' Annual Meeting will be held on June 10 at 9 a.m. in the barn.
Ms. Patterson gave the attached report from Mr. Patterson.

Ms. Massung received an email from Eli Doggart. After he receives approval, he will get
back to us.

Ms. Patterson and Ms. Massung have identified and listed 129 items in the house
collections.

Mr. Bumgarner works for the East Lyme Sewer Department. Ms. Rossi is a dispatcher
and Sergeant Hallbauer has worked for the Town for nineteen years. They would like to have a
Halloween Festival on the Smith-Harris House property. Non perishable food items will go to
Care and Share and pet supplies will go to Waterford/East Lyme Animal Control. Any cash
donations will be donated to the Friends of the Smith-Harris House. Ms. Rossi stated we have
had this event for five years at other locations. It is a family event. Last year there were 300 in
attendance. We control the size of the group. Mr. Cunningham asked how large is the group?
Ms. Rossi informed him the largest group we have had is ten.

Sergeant Hallbauer would like the Police Cadets to become involved. He reported they will use
LED lights. We will have guides. Portapotties will be needed.

This event will be held on October 27 and 28 from 6 p.m. to 9 p.m.

Ms. DiMartino asked will you go out to the woods? Sergeant Hallbauer replied it will take place
at the perimeter of the barn and the house grounds.

Ms. Rossi added we will bring in our own generator.

B. Ex-officio Report. Mr. Cunningham reported the Board of Selectmen received a
suggestion to put banners on the Main Street lights. This matter was tabled for more thought. It
was felt it might be dangerous to have another distraction on Main Street.

The Board of Selectmen approved a lease agreement with the Lions to allow them to hold the
Lobsterfest.

The Band Shell on the upper parking area has been completed. All of the sound will be projected
over the water and will not disturb the neighbors. Music and/or arts will be held on Friday nights
throughout the summer.

C. Director's Report. See attached report.

Ms. DiMartino suggested using sign-up.com for dates Commission or Friends members are
available to give tours. Volunteers will receive a reminder through this website.

weeding.

D. Upcoming Events.
1. Bike and BBQ 2017. The Bike and BBQ 2017 is scheduled for October 1.
They anticipate 400 visitors to the house on that day. They are looking for major sponsors and
are getting help from an outside fundraiser. The Lions will prepare the food. Each historical
house will have a booth or table. There will be a stop at the Lee House and Samuel Smith

2. Middle School Program. (See Director's Report).
