+ We are also putting a moratorium on collections/donations, so that we can determine what our

Legend Trips
* The calendar is out! Jeff is giving a talk in Wallingford, CT tomorrow, 10/19. If someone
could pick up the calendars then, we will save shipping money and have them to sell by
Saturday for our program.
+ We are planning on a 2018 date for another ghost hunt.
Website/Technology
« We have shut-down our old website, and Kathleen Sasso and I have started the process for a
new one. This will be my main focus over the next few weeks after Spirit Voices had ended.
Tours/Research
+ Lisa Marcinkowski-June, a Ph.D. candidate at UConn, was thrilled with the response she
received at the Bike & BBQ event regarding interviews on our tours.
+ All went well for Wil Reed’s class tour; special thanks to Maggi Prokop for joining me at 7:30
am to share our House with his students.
Programs
We participated in the Groton Public Library Local History Fair on Saturday, September 30th,
from noon to 5 pm. It was a lovely afternoon, and we received responses to our survey and
may have a few new volunteers as well. We had flyers, program/membership handouts, a short
survey, and photos.
Explore History! was a lot of fun, even though the weather refused to cooperate; we quickly
switched to activities that could occur indoors due to the rain. Audience was small, but we
received press coverage from The Day that afternoon. We once again had more adults in
attendance than families/children, so we may want to revisit our approach to this program for
next year.
Spirit Voices information has gone out to all Trustees. We are decorating tomorrow, 3 pm,
please join us if you are available, so that we can get it done quickly.
T’ve spoken with Verline Eldridge (Medium), and she and another Medium, Lynne Gaffin,are
looking forward to participating once again in Spirit Voices. The cost will be $10 for a 10-
minute reading. We will be able to accommodate 36 people for readings, total. Calls are
already coming in for reservations! I have created a reservation sheet, Also, Pine Grove has
publicized Spirit Voices through their channels.
For Slightly-Creepy tales we have Gary Lakowsky, Valerie Kibler, and Michael Hart. Dante

DiMartino-Hart will once again distribute pencils. Tales to be distributed very soon; let me

know if any storytellers have a preferred story to share.

I confirmed with the volunteer from Care & Share to feature a winter coat drive at the Slightly-

Creepy Tales / Trick or Trunk program. C&S will be providing the bin, and I suggested the

location of in front of the porch, where it is out of walking paths, but still visible in the light.

Plans are moving forward with the “Novel History” collaborative program with the library.

Wine Tasting flyer has been revised; publicity has gone out as usual, and includes an ad in the

Post Road Review. Donna will report further on details of this fundraiser.

We will plan for the Holiday programs (wreath sale & open-house) next week; events will be

similar to last year.

I will be scheduling a joint meeting to plan our 2018 program schedule; bring your ideas,

keeping in mind all we’ve learned from STEPS and our consultant.
