On Wednesday March 8, 2017 | met with Richard Cullen , a licensed arborist, of
Tomorrow’s Trees, to discus trimming work on the tree encroaching the Christmas Tree
near the corn crib. The Christmas Tree was planted around the time of the formation of
the Smith-Harris Friends and Commission, | believe. The large tree next to it has two
large branches growing through the limbs of the Christmas Tree and is causing
damage. We would like those limbs removed.

While inspecting the tree, Mr. Cullen noticed cracks and damage to the tree. In his
estimation in a few years that tree will be compromised. He also pointed out that limbs
were hanging over the corn crib and affecting the roof area. Those need to be removed
also. The apple tree in the center of the parking lot is not a fruit producer, but he feels
with extreme trimming he could make it look much better. Hence no removal would be

necessary.

Walking around the property Mr. Cullen noticed several trees needing extreme trimming,
due to dead limbs and low hanging branches. His recommendation would be to address
those limbs, eliminating a hazard to visitors to the grounds, thereby making the property
safer to our visitors. Eliminating several branches close to the house would discourage

squirrels etc. seeking refuge into the attic.

While addressing the corn crib area, Mr. Cullen said he would remove the two offending
limbs at NO CHARGE to us. We could have the limbs cut to manageable size for us to
use at a later date. Also, he stated, while on site he would trim, remove ( if we did not
want the wood), and chip the damaged and dangerous trees, limbs, etc., around the
grounds, house, orchard area etc., also at NO CHARGE to us. A proposal with a dollar

figure would be drawn up.

The proposal would include a dollar amount up to $2000 for services to the site. A
recognition letter on Smith-Harris letterhead stating we are non-profit, etc., and/or a
certificate of services provided would be included.

I stated to him we would discus his informal proposal at our next combined Friends and
Commission meeting. If his services and proposal are approved, he would schedule the
work when the site is not being used.

We would recognize his donation of services with a letter, certificate, or however the
Friends/Commission sees fit.

In conclusion, Mr. Cullen feels we have serious limb hazards on the property, and its in
out best interest to remove these hazards.

Robert Patterson
