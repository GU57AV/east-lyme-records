III. REPORTS

           a. Agenda Additions. There were no additions to the agenda.
           b. Public Delegations. Ms. Massung reported spring cleaning has been done. The rugs in the
               dining room, the two runners and the children's room have been cleaned.

           c. Ex-Officio. Mr. Cunningham informed the Commission that a proposal for the Darrow Pond
              area has been submitted by the subcommittee of Parks and Recreation Commission. A
              proposal will be made to the Board of Selectmen on June 13. The subcommittee has been
              discussing picnic areas, outdoor classrooms, a birding and wildlife area, trails and disc golf.

              He stated land in Bridebrook Park has been set aside to build pickleball courts. There is much
              demand for pickleball courts.

             The Charter Revision Commission has discussed increasing the term of office for the First
             Selectman from two to four years. Discussion has been held on eliminating an automatic
             referendum because of its cost.
