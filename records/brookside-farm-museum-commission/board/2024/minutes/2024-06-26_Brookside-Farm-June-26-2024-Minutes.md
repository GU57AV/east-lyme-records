Grants and Assessments
I completed the grant application for a historic structures report from SHPO. The consultant
who would work on the historic structures report if successful knows the people at SHPO very
well and did a great job putting our application in the face of the right people.

This is a grant for $10,000 with a $10,000 match required. Friends approved a 95,000 match
and I committed $5,000 of our capital budget by the town to complete the match (the town didn't
get back to me when I asked permission to do this, so ljust did it and cannot accept the grant
later if necessary).

Grounds Security
Nothing surprising was caught on the security cameras. There were people here on 6122 at
10:15 am looking around the barn. I don't know the purpose of their visit.

Programs
We held a very successfulfir book discussion group on June 6.
We had a mildly successful presentation of the UConn presentations on June 5. The showing
didn't match the number of people who registered and was pretty poorly attended. Mariana,
one of the professors, and I will be working on a big grant to the USDA later in the summer (due
September 1). The grant is for $100,000 per year and will be used to implement some of the
initiatives developed by the students that the Town approves of and can be a multi-year grant.
Mariana would like to continue the school's close relationship with us, which is very exciting.

I have secured Tom Schuch to start the research on the Christian Freeman newspaper as it
may relate to the history in East Lyme. The work will start this summer and I will use the
remaining few dollars of this year's budget to pay him a stipend. I will also apply for a "quick
grant" from CT Humanities and Community Foundation of Eastern Connecticut by July 1.

Misc.
Frontier installed Wi-Fi. The Wi-Fi should extend throughout the house and into the barn.

A boy scout followed up with me about his Eagle Scout project. We met a couple of times and
went over his proposal He plans to develop an outdoor classroom at the front of the trail. He
plans to build rustic benches using the downed trees on the property. The benches will be
portable so if we develop a different strategy for the grounds in the future or choose to put the
benches somewhere else, we can.
