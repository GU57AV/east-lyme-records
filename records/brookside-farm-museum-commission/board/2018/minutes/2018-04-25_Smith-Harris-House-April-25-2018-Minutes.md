B. Ex officio Report. Mr. Cunningham reported after concern of liability for the Town,
the Board of Selectmen have revised the Town's policy on the use of alcohol at the
Smith-Harris House. The Board of Selectmen have approved three events with alcohol at
the Smith-Harris House. Ms. DiMartino felt the best use of the three events is for the
Friends to have fundraisers.

Ms. Prokop asked that renters also be allowed to have alcohol, since several have
cancelled because they were not allowed to have alcohol. Mr. Cunningham stated the
Selectmen are willing to try this new policy. He has reviewed the agreement and
suggests some changes and recommends that the Town Attorney also review it. He felt
the Commission needs to keep track of issues that may arise.

Ms. Prokop reported most rentals are held on Saturday nights. Ms. DiMartino suggested
using one of the events for a fundraiser. First Selectman Mark Nickerson informed Ms.
DiMartino he would like to see more fundraising by the Friends. Mr. Lakowsky did not
feel the Friends would be able to have three fundraising events during the year. He felt
the Friends could have a beer/wine tasting event. Two events could be allowed for barn
renters on a first come first serve basis. He felt we need to be prudent on how we allow
alcohol. Ms. DiMartino reported we will request a liability form and a certificate from
the bartender.

Mr. Patterson asked how did they determine three events with alcohol would be allowed?
Mr. Cunningham felt if it goes well there is a potential to go back to the Board of
Selectmen to request more.

Ms. DiMartino stated Mr. Nickerson suggested having a large event. Mr. Lakowsky felt
with the volunteer help we have, it is not possible to have more than one event per year.
Ms. Prokop suggested having a meet and greet the politicians fundraiser. Ms. DiMartino
suggested announcing the new name to pay for a new logo. Mr. Nickerson suggested
requesting a donation of $50 per person.

Mr. Cunningham reported we are finally seeing some activity on the Main Street project.
