VI. NEW BUSINESS

b. Discussion and Possible Action on the Commission's Meeting Schedule
The Commission reviewed its existing schedule of meetings on the fourth Wednesday
of each month. Members also discussed meeting format and expressed a strong
preference for in-person meetings because of recurring Zoom audio problems. Possible
locations included the Senior Center and Brookside Farm Museum. No separate action
was taken under this item; the meeting frequency was addressed under item c.

c. Discussion and Possible Action on Holding Commission Meetings Every Other Month
The Commission discussed meeting every other month on the fourth Wednesday,
beginning in September 2026, with special meetings called when needed with proper
notice.
Motion: Ashley moved that the Commission meet every other month. Danielle seconded
the motion. The motion passed unanimously, with no opposition or abstentions.
Ashley noted that the change might require an amendment to the Commission's bylaws.
Jason advised that the meeting schedule could be adopted by vote while the separate
bylaw-amendment process is reviewed. Chair Meier agreed to file the annual schedule
of regular meetings with the Town Clerk by January 31 and to reserve meeting space.
Zoom may remain an option when winter weather makes travel unsafe.

d. Discussion and Possible Action Authorizing the Curator to Spend Funds from
Approved Expendable Budget Lines
The Commission deferred discussion because the Curator was not present to explain
the proposal. Members agreed that the matter should receive careful review. A special
meeting may be scheduled before the next regular meeting if timely action is required.
No action was taken.
