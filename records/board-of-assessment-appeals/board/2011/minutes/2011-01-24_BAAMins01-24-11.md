TOWN OF EAST LYME
BOARD OF ASSESSMENT APPEALS
JANUARY 24, 2011
EAST LYME TOWN HALL
MEETING MINUTES
Members Present:

William Weber, Chairman
Joan Schwartz, Secretary

Paul Dagle
Susan Graham
Holly Cheeseman
Chairman Weber called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.
1. ELECTION OF BOARD OFFICERS
Motion (1) Ms. Schwartz moved to nominate Mr. Weber as Chairman.
Seconded by Mr. Dagle.

Mr. Weber stated that he would be happy to be Chairman of the Board again but he did
want to advise them that he would not be running for re-election.

Motion Passed Unanimously.
Motion (2) | Mr. Weber moved to nominate Joan Schwartz as Secretary.
Seconded by Ms. Graham.
Motion Passed Unanimously.
