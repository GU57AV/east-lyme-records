1. MARTIN & LINDA STEIN- 285 OLD BLACK POINT ROAD-ACCOUNT NUMBER 001851

Mr. Foley swore Mr. & Mrs. Stein in.

The Homeowners bought the house in 2013 for 1.4 million dollars. There have been 14 house sales since
the last reval, and all 14 sold significantly under their valued amount. Their house is the only one that
received a 5% increase; they don’t have beachfront or water accesses like the other homes do. The
Steins asked the Board to consider the multiplier being used for land value.

2. NANCY YARIO- 7 ORCHARD STREET- ACCOUNT NUMBER- 000570

Mr. Foley swore Ms. Yario in.

Ms. Yario explained that her budget is small and the renovation on the property is slow going. The house
was assessed at $144,000 and she feels that is too much since it is in really horrible condition. The house
was advertised as a 3 bedroom but it’s really a two bedroom. The third “bedroom” is L shaped- probably
once an enclosed porch and cannot be used as a bedroom.

3. JOHN CELLINO- 60 EAST SHORE DRIVE- ACCOUNT NUMBER-006354

Mr. Foley swore Mr. Cellino in.

Mr. Cellino said him neighbor has a larger home and 90% larger lot yet his assessment is only 5% more
than his. He said the math does not make sense to him and he would like the Board to take a look at it.

4, DUAN SHEQUAN & YONG HONG CHEN- ACCOUNT NUMBER- 008920
Mr. Foley swore Mr. Shequan in.

The homeowner explained that the value of his house is below the purchase price yet his taxes will
probably increase. The Assessor told him the land code changed, and Vision told him his property is
overvalued. The numbers don’t seem to be correct so he would like the Board to review this matter.

Mr. Foley swore Mr. Donovan in.
