STEPHEN MASSAD — 7 WHITING FARMS LANE —- ACCOUNT NUMBER 009451
JOSEPH ARCARESE — 5 WHITING FARMS LANE — ACCOUNT NUMBER 009450

Mr. Foley swore Mr. Massad and Mr. Arcarese in.

Mr. Massad stated the properties are appraised too high. They were built differently than the other
units in the complex. They were built by a different developer. Properties are not selling there. There is
a dispute with the homeowners association. They have no street lights, there are no services. There are
25 units total. These two units are bigger units, but that doesn’t mean they are worth more. These do
not have the open concept of the other units.

Mr. Arcarese stated that appraised value is based on comps, and if so, then none of the units are near
the value being given by the Town.

There was a discussion with Steve Ferreira of Vision regarding the revaluation and Mr. Ferreira
clarified certain items on the Street Cards for the Board.

CAROL NEWMAN ~ 31 WELLS STREET — ACCOUNT NUMBER 002160

Mr. Foley swore in Carol Newman.

$525,000. She has been trying to sell the property and it was last listed at $470,000. They are near the
National Guard property and they are not part of the Pine Grove Association. The property is not selling.
