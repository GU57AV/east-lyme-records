3. REVIEW OF BAA POLICIES & PROCEDURES

Mr. Foley stated everything in their meetings is open to the public including deliberations. If someone
is disruptive they can be asked to leave. Anyone is welcome to videotape and take pictures.

Donna Bekech stated the onus is on the Appellant. There should be some sort of appraisal with them
when they appeal. Her office will tell Appellants to bring documentation with them. It is up to the
Appellant to make a strong enough case. Also, it is important to remember the assessment is as of
the last revaluation date.

Mr. Napiany asked if the people in the audience can engage in a conversation with members of the
Board.

Mr. Foley stated no.
