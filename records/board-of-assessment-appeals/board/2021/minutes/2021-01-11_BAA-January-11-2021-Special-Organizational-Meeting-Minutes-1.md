 lll.   New Business
Mr. Bekech said 2Q22 is a revaluation year, could be quite busy, and suggested that if anyone's
term is up and they don't wish to continue it would be good to know now so that a new person
could be trained prior to the term ending, and be ready to go. Mr. Attanasio said his term is up
and as of now, he is undecided.

The Board had a general discussion regarding site visits and agreed it will be best to drive by
the appeal site at a minimum. Mr. Attanasio said when he served on this Board many years ago
you were compensated especially during a reval year, and Ms. Vitagliano said she has put
money in the budget for this very purpose.

Mr. Hughes asked that Ms. Vitagliano let them know as soon as she determines whether the
