                                 Town of East Lyme
                              Board of Assessment Appeal
                                     Legal Notice


The Town of East Lyme Board of Assessment Appeals will hold informal hearings in
March 2021 for appeals to the assessments for the October I,2020 Grand List and
Strpplemental Motor Vehicle for the October 1,2079 Grand List. As per Connecticut
General Statutes, appeals must be submitted in writing and received by Februry 22,
2021. The Board of Assessment Appeals, without the timely submission of the
completed petition form, cannot hear the appeal. The Board will hold meetings by
appointment only.

Appeal petition forms are available online on the Board of Assessments home page and
in the Assessor's Office, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic. Please note the Town Hall
is currently closed to the public. If you are unable to download the form, please call
during normal business hours Monday- Friday, 8:00 am to 4:00 pm.

It should be noted that the. Real Estate assessments are based on the market conditions as
of the last revaluation date of October I,2016. For further information please contact the
Assessor's Office at 860-739-693L

East Lyme Board of Assessment Appeals
