Mr. Foley called the Board of Assessment Appeals Meeting to order at 4:23 p.m. noting the
meeting does not meet the quorum requirements, and will be for informational purposes only.

Mr. Foley said he is happy to continue as Chair if no one else from the Membership is
interested.

Mr. Foley recommended following the same appeal dates the Board has followed in years past:
e March Sth, 6th, and 7th, 6:00 p.m.- 9:00 p.m. each evening
e Saturday March 10th from 9:00 a.m. - 12:00 p.m.
e The 12th and 13th can be utilized the following week if needed, and if not, cancelled.

Mr. Foley briefly reviewed policies and procedures with Mr. Mather, and encouraged that he visit
the Assessor's office and review how to read a field card.

Mr. Foley added that from time to time a Member will have to recuse himself from the
proceedings. If Mr. Foley needs to recuse himself they will go by seniority in terms of who will
take over the meeting; the seniority is as follows- Sue, Patrick, Will followed by Tony.

Mr. Foley explained that we never know how many people will appeal and said the new tax laws
may impact this. Each applicant has a 15 minute window to state their case and the
Membership deliberates afterwards and on occasion, may need to visit sites.

Mr. Foley explained they have the option of not ruling on commercial sites over a million dollars-
they would instead go to court. He added that an income approach is not relevant for their
purposes, they are concerned with the value of the property.
Ms. Vitigliano said she will prepare the legal notice for the schedule of meetings and shared the
updated handbook from the CT Association of Assessment Officers.

The informational meeting adjourned at 4:35 p.m.
