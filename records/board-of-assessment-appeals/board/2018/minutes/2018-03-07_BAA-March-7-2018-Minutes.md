Mr. Foley called the Board of Assessment Appeals Meeting to order at 6 p.m.

J. Appeals-

15) Hickory Home Restoration- 59 Upper Pattagansett Road- Account #44291

Mr. Foley swore Gene Parks in. Mr. Parks explained he is a new business in Town, unfamiliar
with this paperwork, and neglected to fill out the declaration form. He gave the Board an
itemized copy of his equipment for their review. He said the only office he has is his netbook
computer which travels with him job to job. Mr. Foley clarified they can’t do anything about the
penalty for not filling out the form, but they can change the assessment of what he actually has.
The Town's figure including penalty is $2,962 and he estimates the actual value is $621.40.

Il. Deliberations:

15) Hickory Home Restoration- 59 Upper Pattagansett Road- Account #44291
Mr. Hughes moved to decrease the total assessment to $621.40 and retain the fine.
Mr. Attanasio seconded the motion. Motion carried, 5-0-0.

First Selectman Mark Nickerson briefly appeared and addressed the Board regarding policy and
procedure.

Ill, Deliberations-
14) Kathleen Amanti- 9 Summit Avenue- Account #2613
Mr. Hughes said he drove by the property. Mr. Foley reviewed the comparables and said there is
a discrepancy in the C factors between 7,9, and 11 Summit Avenue, all of who are the same
distance from the water.

Mr. Hughes moved to change the C factor of 9 Summit Avenue from 3.25 to 2.75.

Ms. Graham seconded the motion. Motion carried, 5-0-0.

7) Bhagia Laxmi LLC- 265 Flanders Road- Account #40514

Mr. Hughes moved to reopen this appeal and revisit this matter upon review of the statute.
Mr. Mather seconded the motion. Motion carried, 3-0-2.

Ms. Graham and Mr. Foley voted against the motion.

The Board discussed the statute. Mr. Hughes said he is concerned about setting a precedent.
Mr. Hughes moved to reverse the previous decision made on March 6, 2018, and instead, take

no action.
Mr. Attanasio seconded the motion. Motion carried, 5-0-0.
