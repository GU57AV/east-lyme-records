                                      LEGAL NOTICE

                           BOARD OF ASSESSMENT APPEALS
                               TOWN OF EAST LYME

All owners of motor vehicles registered in the Town of East Lyme are hereby notified that the
Board of Assessment Appeals will meet at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue,
Niantic, Connecticut on the following date:

              Saturday, September 16, 2023        9:00 AM – 12:00 PM
                                      In the parking lot


The sole purpose of the appeals are to hear any persons claiming to be aggrieved by the doings of
the Assessor of the Town of East Lyme relating to the assessment of motor vehicles on the
October 1, 2023 Grand List.

                                 Board of Assessment Appeals
                                  Patrick Hughes, Chairman
