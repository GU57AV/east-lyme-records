TOWN OF EAST LYME
BOARD OF ASSESSMENT APPEALS
MARCH 9, 2015

MEETING MINUTES

Members Present: FILED IN EAST LYME

Michael Foley, Chairman CONNECTICUT, Ass
Ralph Napiany
Joan Schwartz SAST LYME TOWN CLERK
Sue Graham

Mary Ann Salvatore

Mr. Foley called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

The members set the September meeting date for September 12, 2015 from 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.

The March 11, 2015 meeting will be cancelled as there are no appointments scheduled.

Matvey Sokolovskaya — 26 White Birch Circle — List #339

Mr. Foley swore in the appellant.

Mr. Sokolovskaya stated the work has not been finished. He is doing much of the work himself. The
materials cost $2,800.00 to purchase and $500.00 to install. The system requires drainage. When that
is hooked up the building inspector will come out. He feels the value should not have changed yet as
the system is not complete.

Robert Conrad — Shoreline Insurance — Personal Property List #42185

This appellant was not in attendance.

William Willetts —- Law Office — Personal Property List # 44031

Mr. Foley swore in the applicant.

Mr. Willetts stated he did not receive his personal property notice. He moved his office to East Lyme
from New London. It is now in his home, and he does have some of the furniture from his office in his
home, but it is no longer used for the office. He believes the value to be $700.00.

Children’s Dental Associates NLCPC ~ Personal Property List # 41856

This appellant was not in attendance.
Paul L. Raimondi ~ Motor Vehicle List # 901934

Mr. Foley swore in the appellant.

Lorraine Raimondi was representing the owner. She stated the vehicle is a 2009 Toyota Camry with
236,000 miles on it. It was used as a taxi in Boston. She believes the value to be $4300.00.

Frank & Karen Natale — 23 North Edgewood Road ~ List #3354

Mr. Foley swore in the appellant.
Mr. Natale stated he obtained an appraisal showing the property to be worth $475,000 in 2011. He has

a .13 acre lot. He believes the assessed value to be $322,000.00. He believes land value is skewed. He
also stated the home is 2 bedrooms and not 4 bedrooms.

Sandbar Properties — 25-27 Ocean Avenue ~ List #3483

Mr. Foley swore in the appellants.

Paul Powers and Robert Barbaro represented the appellant.

Joan Schwartz recused herself from this Item.

Mr. Powers stated the property was condemned by the town on August 15, 2014. They have paid all of
the back taxes, relocations fees, and police fees. They are asking for relief on the building. It is
uninhabitable. They will be demolishing the building. They believe they should only be taxed on the

land. The demolition permit is not in place yet. There is no water or electricity. They feel the total
value should be $510,000.00.
