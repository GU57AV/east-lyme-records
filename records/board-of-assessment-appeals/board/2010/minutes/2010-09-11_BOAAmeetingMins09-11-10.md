EAST LYME BOARD OF ASSESSMENT APPEALS
SEPTEMBER 11, 2010 MEETING MINUTES

The East Lyme Board of Assessment appeals meeting of September 11, 2010 was called to
order in the East Lyme Town Hall at 10:02AM. In attendance were Chairman William Weber,
Joan Schwartz, Susan Graham, Holly Cheeseman, and Paul Dagle. The September meeting is
held to hear appeals on the October 1, 2009 Motor Vehicle GL and the 2008 Supplemental
Motor Vehicle List. The following is the summary of appeals heard by the Board on this day.

#1 David Godbout, 15 Cardinal Road, appeared before the Board representing Zeng Ming to
appeal the assessment of a 2001 Honda Insight on the 10/1/2009 Grand List. Mr. Godbout
noted that the vehicle was in average condition and provided a number of exhibits discussing
the Board of Assessment Appeals standard of review and approach to determining vehicle
value. Documents were reviewed by board members and placed on file for further review
during deliberations. He further presented that his vehicle was not listed in NADA book
because his vehicle is in average condition. He made note of several points of damage as
presented in exhibit C photos, defective electric window, and 130,000 miles. The vehicle was
not physically present for review by board.

#2 Guodong Zhang, 22 Cardinal Road, appeared before the Board to appeal the assessment of
a 1998 Toyota Camry on the 10/1/2009 Grand List. The applicant noted that the vehicle had
117,000 miles and a significant amount of damage from previous accidents. The vehicle was
present and the board members visually inspected the car to observe the damage.

#3 Thomas Sullivan, 12 Bush Hill Drive, appeared before the Board to appeal the assessment
on 2006 Ford Freestyle on the 10/1/2009 GL listed under his wife Caroline’s name. Mr.
Sullivan presented data showing the car price decrease over the past several years was non-
linear and increased on the latest tax bill. He stated he paid $150 of his past tax bill and felt a
lower bill was justified. Mr. Sullivan stated the vehicle has 65,000 miles and damage in the left
side from hitting a pole. The vehicle was not physically present for review by board.

#4 Jisung Li, 74 Charter Oak Drive, appeared before the Board to appeal the assessment of a
1999 Honda Accord on the 10/1/2009 Grand List. The applicant noted that the vehicle had
177,000 miles and significant degradation in the engine condition causing excessive oil burn.
The vehicle was present and the board members observed the car including the starting of the
engine. Applicant provided Kelly Bluebook information showing value of “Fair” condition for
his car is $2050.

#5 Thomas Herlihy 55 South Edge Wood Road, appeared before the Board to appeal the
assessment of a 1976 Cadillac Seville on the 10/1/2009 Grand List. Mr. Herlihy noted that the
vehicle was in need of paint. He requested that the $500 assessment be applied as per PA 08-
150. He filed the affidavit.

Holly Cheeseman left the meeting at 11:45 after all applicants presented.
Motion to adjourn was made by Joan Schwartz seconded by Paul Dagle. Motion carried 4:0

Chairperson Weber thanked the members for attending this meeting. The meeting was adjourned at
12:02 PM.

Respectfully submitted,

William Weber

Chairman Board of Assessment Appeals.
9/11/2010
EAST LYME BOARD OF ASSESSMENT APPEALS
SEPTEMBER 13, 2010 MEETING MINUTES

The East Lyme Board of Assessment Appeals meeting of September 13, 2010 was called to
order in the East Lyme Town Hall at 6:00PM. In attendance were Chairman William Weber,
Joan Schwartz, Susan Graham, and Holly Cheeseman. Paul Dagle arrived at 6:02. The
September meeting is held to hear appeals on the October 1, 2009 Motor Vehicle GL and the
2008 Supplemental Motor Vehicle List. Applications #1 - #5 were seen by the Board of
Assessment Appeals at the September 1 3; 2010 meeting as noted in the meeting minutes for
that date. The following is the summary of appeals heard by the Board on September 13, 2010.

#6 Andrew Radynski, 15 Upper Walnut Hill Road, appeared before the Board to appeal the
assessment of a 1990 Mercedes Benz 300D on the 10/1/2009 Grand List. Mr. Radynski noted
that the vehicle was not in normal use. He requested that the $500 assessment be applied as
per PA 08-150. He filed the appropriately signed affidavit. The vehicle was not physically
reviewed by the board.

#7 Kay D. Wilson, 4 Blackberry Lane, appeared before the Board to appeal the assessment on
1996 Mercury Grand Marquis on the 10/1/2009 GL list. Mr. Wilson presented the board with
a copy of the Certificate of Title stamped “Salvage” and stated the car was in an accident
causing the car to lose its value. He also stated the car had been repaired, but could not be
resold. The vehicle was physically reviewed by board.

#8 William Lesick, 482 Boston Post Road, appeared before the Board to appeal the assessment
of a 1997 Ford F250 Pickup on the 10/1/2009 Grand List. The applicant noted that the vehicle
had 286,000 miles and interior and body damage including dents, rust, and a broken tail gate.
He stated this was a work truck. The vehicle was present and the board members observed the
truck.

#9 Ray Greenwood, 31 Grassy Hill Road, appeared before the Board to appeal the assessment
of a 1974 Toyota Land Rover on the 10/1/2009 Grand List. Mr. Greenwood noted that the
vehicle was primarily a farm work vehicle and infrequently used. He noted the window seals
were bad; it had heavy rust, the 4 wheel drive only worked sometimes. He stated the vehicle
had 520,000 miles and had many repairs with non-Toyota parts including a Chevy engine and
various Chevy and Cadillac parts. The vehicle was not present for the board members to
observe, but the applicant said he could go get it. The board did not feel it was necessary to
visually review the car so declined the offer.

The meeting minutes from the September 11, 2010 meeting were reviewed and discussed. A
motion was made to accept by Sue Graham, second by Joan Schwartz. Motion passed
unanimously.
