                         EAST LYME SHORT-TERM RENTAL COMMITTEE

                QUESTIONS TO ANSWER TO REACH FINAL RECOMMENDATIONS

                        FOR REGULAR MEETING OF NOVEMBER 18, 2024


Committee Members—As has been discussed at a number of our meetings, it is critical in order
to complete our charge that (1) we answer the threshold question of whether short-term
rentals are desirable in East Lyme, and that (2) our policy recommendations are based on the
evidence we have gathered to date. To this end, Co-Chairs Santoro and Cicchiello would like you
to answer the following questions. Please bring your responses to our meeting of 11/18/24 for
distribution and discussion.

Resources to review are the minutes of our two Public Hearings (9/25/23 and 1/29/24), public
delegations at our meetings, as well as our Interim Report of April 2024.

   (1) What is the most compelling evidence this Committee has gathered, apart from your
       personal experiences, to support allowing short-term rentals in East Lyme?

   (2) What is the most compelling evidence this Committee has gathered, apart from your
       personal experiences, to ban short-term rentals and to allow only longer-term rentals
       (greater than 30 days)?

Thank you.

--Anne S. and Gary C.
