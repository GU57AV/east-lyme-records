5. Committee's Final Editing and Approval of our Final Report and Recommendations to the
Board of Selectmen, including review of Gary Cicchiello's suggested edits of Section 5 of
the Report

  Note To facilitate conversation Ms. Santoro provided the Committee with a revised draft report and noted
 some of the following:
 The latest edits are based on the February 25th meeting.
 The color-coding, orange= agreed edits from February 25th meeting, green= new edits based on Gary
 Cicchiello's rewritten Section 5, red= emphasis points carried over from previous draft.
 The final report is meant to be an extension/continuation of the previous status report, and not a replacement.
 She gleaned from Mr. Cicchiello's comments that they need to be clear regarding purpose.
 She thanks him for his work and contributions but disagrees with nearly all of it.
 The pros and cons are discussed in the status report.
 She doesn't like the proposed footnotes, citing readability issues; the earlier reports did not use footnotes.
 She's concerned about including his fictional narratives.
 She's not sure whether scenarios like skinny dipping, noise, or other colorful anecdotes add to the factual
 basis or confuse the report.
 She believes Section 4 (methodology) is critical and should not be removed.
 In terms of zoning regulations and property rights, the Board of Selectmen and Zoning will have a full legal
 review.
 She thinks they put forth a number of things along with rationale.
       Draft_report.pdf

  Note Mr. Cicchiello replied that the current draft does not present options clearly nor does it include sufficient
 rationale. Ms. Santoro said she believes the document captures the key issues and portrays a balanced
 summation.

  Note The Committee discussed how the purpose of the document is to be a supplement to the status report
 and some of the following key points were highlighted by some of the Committee Members:
 The previous status report and academic research, along with Mr. Farrugia's survey support the current
 recommendations.
 Legislative proposals and changes will influence the final recommendations.
 The final document needs to accommodate evolving public policy.
 The intent is not to rewrite the entire previous report, but to create an addendum.
 Mr. Cicchiello rewrote more than just Section 5 of the report.
 Concerns were raised about the extent of changes beyond the agreed-upon scope.

  Note Mr. Cicchiello said he wants to include options, issues, and pros and cons of different
 approaches. He thinks the current document is unclear and won't sign it. It should be a standalone
 recommendation and the document itself doesn't discuss anything from the public hearings.

  Note Ms. Thurlow noted that she reread it, and it seems clear to her. Although she liked his fictionalized
 scenarios, there could be a hundred different scenarios and that's something that could come up in
 conversation during their presentation to the Board of Selectmen. She's concerned that this is reinventing the
 wheel” at the last minute.

  Note Mr. Farrugia appreciates how Mr. Cicchiello distilled the discussions into a balance of rights, and he
 would like to see that theme in the front part of the report. He doesn't think the addendum report needs a total
 overhaul or lacks clarity.

as well as the rights of the residents, which the Committee discussed.
