 Mr. McIntire viewed this evening's comments as a microcosm of the previous Public Hearings.

 limited opposition to short-term rentals observed overall, with some comments about parking and noise.
 He thinks most short-term rental visitors do have ties to the community.
 Mr. Cellino said the comments didn't change his position and noted he's a firm believer in a 7-day minimum
 stay.
 He believes daily rentals (e.g., Airbnb) are inappropriate in residential zones.
 He considers short-term rentals as businesses that should be treated as such.
 He supports creating an ordinance to address these issues.
 Mr. Farrugia noted 5 people spoke from his neighborhood and the comments illustrated the importance of
 respecting everyone's rights, the residents and the property owners.
 He supports an ordinance that requires a permitting process; anyone aggrieved can petition the town and this
 gives the town enforcement measures to act.
 Mr. Cicchiello thinks there was some new commentary this evening, especially as it relates to STRs
 decreasing diversity and their impact on communities.
 The role of government is to make lives better.
 He tends to agree more with the residents who said that short-term rental problem houses decrease the
 quality of life of residents.
 Ms. Thurlow said what she heard tonight reinforced the opinion that she formed over the last few weeks.
 There seems to be a need for them, but she doesn't think they should be in every neighborhood.
 They should be in commercial and multi-use areas, not in residential areas.
 Families should be able to live without worrying about a motel next door.
 She thinks the three beach communities with their own zoning can make up their own regulations.
 She thinks any decision affects the whole community and that there should be a referendum, that way the
 entire town can choose what they want.
 Ms. Santoro said they did hear a bit more in terms of the quality-of-life issue concerning residential
 communities.
 She was struck by Mr. Garofalo’s comments noting he made an interesting point that they actually made very
 briefly in this new report, about the town pursuing traditional lodging options.
 She's not sure anything she heard tonight alters her thinking about the approach that the majority took as
 Committee.

  Note The Committee discussed the editing needs:
 Removing “draft” language.
 Update Section 1 to reflect final status.

 Mr. Formica recommended moving statement of purpose to the beginning or end.
 Mr. Cicchiello said the report structure should be changed to present options more clearly for the Board of
 Selectmen.

  Note Mr. Formica detailed some current bills under consideration:
