3. Delegations
There were no delegations

4. Approval of Minutes
These will be reviewed at the next meeting.

5, Presentation - Public Works
Mr. Joe Bragaw, Director of Public Works, presented the request from the Public Works, attached hereto as
Exhibit #1.

6. Evaluation Forms
- It was decided that the department heads do not need to fill out an application; they should submit a letter
of interest with their package outlining the purpose of their request.
-The final application form for small business and not for profit organizations was agreed upon and is
attached hereto as Exhibit #2.

7. Publicizing Program Information
-Information and fillable form on the town website.
-Slide on Channel22.
-Electronic board in front of Town Hall.
-Hard copies at'lown Hall and Community Center/Library

8. Next Steps
-The original deadline was February 21", which is a holiday;the updated deadline will be February 22nd,
-The decision was made to receive and hold all applications to be opened and reviewed together at one time.

Subcommittee at 6:46 p.m,
