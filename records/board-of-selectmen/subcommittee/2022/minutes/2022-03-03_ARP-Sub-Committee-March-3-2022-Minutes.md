6, Chair's Update / Contingency Discussion
Ms. Santoro explained that in discussions with the First Selectman and Finance
Director, they suggest a $200,000 contingency to coverthe following: $100,000 to
put towards further digitizing of town records, such as planning and zoning; $75,000
for emergency management, pandemic related past and future expenses; and
$25,000 toward legal and administrative fees incurred throughout and because of the
pandemic,

The subcommittee felt that the $100k request for digitizing is really a department
head request and that the respective department missed the deadline to submit a
request under the ARP funding. Everyone agreed that it is an appropriate project,
especially in light of the pandemic, but they missed the deadline so the project
should be separate from the $100k contingency amount. Ms. Santoro asked the
group to put this item aside for a moment to think about it and come back to it.
