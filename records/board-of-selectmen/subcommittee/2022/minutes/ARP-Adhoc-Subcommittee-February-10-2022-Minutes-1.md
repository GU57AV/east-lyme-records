I would like the following two projects for consideration for the use of ARP funds.


State Radio System
The East Lyme Police Department would like to propose a switch to the State Radio Communications system for its
Officers. Currently, the Town of East Lyme operates its own radio system which allows communication for all Town
agencies in the Town. However, this communication does not extend to Police agencies outside of our borders. This lack
of ability to operate with interoperability has a significant effect on operations and our ability to safely and efficiently
work with outside agencies, as well is not in line with the industry standards to create interoperability for first
responders,
The State of Connecticut received funding this year to put the infrastructure for the State radio system backbone into
our dispatch center. With this key piece in the planning stage, and with funding outside the Town of East Lyme, this will
allow the dispatch center to hear/communicate with all users on the State system. However field Police units would not
be able to communicate on the system on an individual basis. We have already established "talk groups" on the system
for which would allow us to communicate directly with i.1 Departments and multiple State agencies.

This project isasking to fund the purchase of the mobile and portable radios required to work on the State system
Mobile radios are installed in each Police vehicle and each Officers is required by policy to carry a portable radio.

This project qualifies for funding under ARP based upon its interoperability function. This is extremely helpful in
situations of all magnitudes, from smallto large scale, and those regional in nature. This will also include operations
related to the safety and security of the community, an area of which would include the operation of a point of
dispensing event, vaccination clinics, and response to pandemic related emergencies.

Attached you will find two quotations from our established radio communications vendor. The State radio system is a
P25 based system, which has a limited number of radio makes/models allowed on the system. These quotes are for two
of the allowed rrrakes/rttodels.

*The purpose of listing the Police Department conversion at the top priority level of parks and recreation's list
of projects is due to the burden placed on the Hole in the Wall restroom facilities by being the only public
restroom along Main Street. The Hole in the Wall building is now roughly 12 years old and does not have the
number of facilities required to keep up with the usage demand on patrons using the beach, boardwalk, and
main street businesses. Our staff has replaced many of the toilet hardware, many times mid-summer, to keep
them operational and does a tremendous job upkeeping the facilities to the highest standard. Each restroom
has just two toilets (men's room 1 stall/l urinal), and often a line is formed, even during low traffic times. Hole
in the Wall does not have space to expand the existing facility. ldeally, the public restroom on Main Street
would be operational year-round {Hole in the Wall is not)to accommodate winter events {Holiday Stroll and
Light Parade) as well as the holiday shopping rush and winter boardwalk usage. Currently we rent portable
toilet units at Cini Park, Hole in the wall, and McCook's playscape to make up for lack of winter friendly
facilities.

Darrow Pond: Master Plan to best design a recreation area for people of all ages and ability levels. The plan
would look to provide a safe place to recreate, utilize the pond for water activities, and observe nature.

Roxbury Field Park:
Walking Path similar to walking trail at Peretz Park {possibly lit for evening and morning users}, Dog Park
{Small/Large dog areas}, Pickleball Courts (3 or 4 courts with fencing}, Gaga Pit and parking area.
Future vision: Obtaining access to the existing trail on prison property for a bike/walking path. This path would
connect to Society Road near highway overpass and provide a safe cycle route from Niantic to East Lyme.
Possibly link with a trail on other side of The Cove at Gateway and lead all the way to Community Garden area
of Flanders.

Playscape I mprovements:
The playscape areas at both McCook Point Park and Peretz Park at Bridebrook need upgrades and updating.
These areas on our parks are used heavily and are a main attraction for patrons at both locations. All updates
should be done to be accessible to all patrons,

Water fountain conversions:
We would like to convert existing water fountains to bottle filler and pet bowl stations to better serve our park
visitors.

Restroom Enhancements:
This would be in lieu of the police department conversion for the case of Hole in the Wall, a way to add toilet
facilities,
Upper McCooks and Peretz Park restrooms are both in serious need of updating to facilities that better serve
our patrons.
