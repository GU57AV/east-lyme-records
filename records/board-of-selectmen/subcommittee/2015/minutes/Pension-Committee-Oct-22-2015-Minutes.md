EAST LYME PENSION COMMITTEE
MEETING OF October 22, 2015
MINUTES

PRESENT: M. Nickerson, A. Johnson, D. Connolly, R. Fecteau, J. LaFond by phone
ABSENT: N. Paciotti, P. McDonough, B. Hogan, M. Whalen

ALSO PRESENT: J. McAraw, R. Macomb; Representatives Northstar Wealth
Partners

Mr. Nickerson called the meeting to order at 3:00 pm.

Motion by J LaFond, second by R Fecteau to approve July 16, 2015 minutes. Passed.
(Abstain by: D Connolly)

Ms. Romaine Macomb of Northstar Wealth Partners (NWP) noted that due to changes in
Federal Regulations, Financial Advisors can no longer produce manual reports. NWP
will continue to produce internally to monitor our portfolio. They will transition the
preparation of the portfolio monthly balance report to Town of East Lyme staff so the
committee can continue to receive the information.

Mr. J. McAraw of NWP noted the markets have been in correction mode since July and
at the end of September the turnaround is approximately 4%. In December 2014 our
portfolio balance was $16.3M, at the end of September 2015 it was $16.5M and two days
ago $17.1M. The results have been better than expected with a few exceptions. There
are two items affecting earnings; (1) US Companies being global and (2) the energy
market. The market is experiencing the first correction since 2011. In 2015 China and
global growth is slowing. Dividend paying companies prior to 1980’s were producing
one half of the stock market return. Companies stopped increasing dividends, thus
having more cash. Shareholders want some cash returned to them, therefore, now
dividends-are increasing. There is also a lot of focus on the Federal Reserve, the problem
being lack of confidence.

Plan Financial Advisors made a recommendation to make portfolio changes as follows:
Motion by D Connolly second by J LaFond — Motion Passed.

From: To: Amount:
Ivy Asset Strategy Inv Opt (ZY) Nuveen Santa Barbara Div Growth (ZOR) $ 612,038
Temple Global Bond Inv A (Y2) MFS Research Bond —R3Total Return Bond $1,265,785

Motion by J LaFond, second by D. Connolly for approval of invoice $500. Passed.
Renee Fecteau reported no new pensions at this time.

Meeting adjourned at 3:30 PM

Respectfully submitted,
For the Committee:
