Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 3:08 pm.

Motion passed.

Mr. J McAraw of Northstar Wealth Partners began discussing the spreadsheet with
monthly balances since inception of working with the Town on its investment portfolio.
He noted that with the beginning of their receiving more information from Mass Mutual,
they will add contributions and distributions to this monthly report.

Mr. McAraw continued noting 2013 ended strong. The Federal Reserve continued to
increase the supply of money to encourage the corporate sector to start spending. He also
reported there are more global investments and less US investments. When questioned
regarding making any changes in our portfolio Mr. McAraw noted that after significant
under performance of bonds there is now an improvement in the bond market so it makes
sense to hold off making changes to the existing portfolio. We will look at this again at
our July meeting.

4

Ms. Romaine Macomb discussed that the .10% contract fee will be eliminated by Mass
Mutual effective May 1, 2014. On-line web enhancements are expected in August or
September 2014. She will work on obtaining quotes for other investment options. Ms.
Macomb also agreed to check with other towns to see how they handle their respective
defined benefit pension plans.

Motion by D Connolly second by N Paciotti to pay the $500 benefit calculation invoice
#90000272 from plan assets. Motion passed. Ms. Johnson was to do some investigation
on invoice #90000648 in the amount of $4,750.

*Renee Fecteau reported two new pensions one for a former vested town employee
commencing June 1, 2014 and a BoE employee. In addition, a Library employee will be
leaving due to no longer being able to work in September 2014. There will be a six
month waiting period and the individual will qualify for a disability pension.
