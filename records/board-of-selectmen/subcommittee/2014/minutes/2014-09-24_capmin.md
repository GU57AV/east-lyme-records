CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PLAN SUBCOMMITTEE
MINUTES OF SEPTEMBER 24, 2014
EAST LYME TOWN HALL - UPSTAIRS MEETING ROOM
2:00 P.M.
MINUTES

PRESENT: P Formica, T Hagen, Bill Scheer, R Bence, A Johnson, Sgt Blanchette, J San
Juan, D Putnam, C Holyfield, J Bragaw, Dr. Lombardo, D Meltabarger

ABSENT: _ S Harney

Meeting was called to order at 2:00 p.m.

A status review of the 2014/15 plan took place

J Bragaw noted he meets monthly with the Police Dept staff and part of that
process is to review vehicle needs.

The State Police Radio system is targeted to be off line at the end of 2015. This
will be a consideration for State Police or Independent Dept.

There are ongoing HVAC needs at the ELCC.

The goal of Building Maintenance for 2015/16 is to improve efficiency.

Dr Lombardo discussed the Elementary School Projects. The best scenario would
be to begin construction in summer 2016 (2016/17 FY). The best case would be
to have an authorization in place by June 30, 2015.

P & R is looking to begin the process of developing the Roxbury Road Property.
Dr Lombardo introduced the need for new accounting software — we should
combine efforts for the town and BoE.

The BoE needs student data software (Power School). The BoE is overwhelmed
by data. Est $200-$250k.

Discussed the need for 14 passenger vans for the Special Needs Program.
Recommended including Architect funds in the 2015/16 CIP for the Elementary
Schools.

J Bragaw discussed the fuel tanks for 2015/16. They will work on fine tuning the
numbers and look for the solution that is in the “best interest” of the town.
Restrooms at Cini Park.

Adjournment at 3:00 pm

Respectfully submitted,

Anna M. Johnson FILED IN EAST LYME

Director of Finance
