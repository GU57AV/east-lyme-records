                          EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
            SUBCOMMITTEE TO REVIEW THE FIRE DEPARTMENTS ORGANIZATIONS
                            MEETING OF DECEMBER 7,2020
                                     MINUTES


This meeting was held via Zoom online meeting services and was available for the public to view, listen and
participate via videoconference and dial-in options,

PRESENT: Kevin Seery, Marc Salerno and Dan Cunningham

The meeting was called to order at 5:00 p.m.

2.      Election of Chair
MOTTON (1)
Mr. Salerno MOVED to elect Kevin Seery as the Chair of this Subcommittee.
Seconded by Mr. Cunningham. Motion passed 3-0.

3,      Review of the2017 Fire Study
All of the members were provided with a copy of the 2017 Study for discussion at the next meeting.

4.      Fire Department Organization and Scope of Review
It was agreed upon that this Subcommittee would invite the Flanders and Niantic Fire Chiefs to participate in
a discussion on this topic at the next meeting.
