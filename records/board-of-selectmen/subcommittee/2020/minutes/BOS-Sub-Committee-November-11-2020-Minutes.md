                EAST L\TVIE BOARD OF SELECTMEN
      SUBCOMMITTEE TO REVIEW CINI PARK FISHING DOCK LEASES
                        NOVEMBER 11, 2O2O

                                MEETING MINUTES


Meeting was brought to order at 7:02 p.m. on November II, 2020 vra Zoom

In attendance were Marc Salerno, Paul Dagle and Dan Cunningham

A discussion ensued about selection criteria for dock leases. A set of criteria was
established and put into draft form.

The committee decided to recommend two options to the Board of Selectman:
   1. Extend leases until forth dock becomes available. When forth dock becomes
      available, put out a call for applications.
   2. Extend current lessees for another year and put a call out for forth dock when it
      becomes available.

The committee will also finalize the draft set of criteria and present it to the Board of
Selection at the next meeting for adoption.

Meeting was adjourned at 7:50PM.

Submitted by Marc Salerno
