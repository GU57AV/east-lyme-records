                        EAST LYME PENSION COMMITTEE
                            MEETING of February 6,2020
                                  MINUTES



PRESENT: M. Nickerson, A. Johnson, D. Connolly, J. Wohler and B. Lucovich

BY PHONE: J. LaFond, B. Hogan and P. Goulekas

ABSENT: M. Stevens

ALSO PRESENT: Mr. J. McAraw and Ms. Romaine McComb Representatives
Northstar Wealth Partners

First Selectman Nickerson called the meeting to order at 3:05 pm.

MOTION by: B. Hogan seconded by J. LaFond to approve minutes of October 17,2019
meeting. Passed.

R. McComb of Northstar Wealth Partners noted the December 31, 2019 portfolio balance
was: $25,666,733.20. During the year $551,000 in contributions were made and
$903,000 were paid out in distributions. The twelve- month return was 19.2%o

J. McAraw of Northstar Wealth Partners discussed targeting the plan cash levels. In
looking at market risk, where are we in the business cycle? The peak cycle ran from
January of 2016 through January of 2018. We next experienced a choppy cycle
beginning in January 2018 through August of 2019. There was a good run through the
end of 2019 and we exited on the upside.

We are looking at having the plan cash goal be 20% and at the recommendation of our
Investment Managers the following motion noted below was made. In addition, at our
next meeting they will have additional recommendations for the committee.

Motion by: D. Connolly, second by J. Wohler to move $500,000 from Blackrock Global
and $250,000 from MM Select Mid-cap to Invesco Oppenheimer Government Money
Market. Passed.

Discussion was held on the Five-Year Annual Required Contribution Projection for the
Pension Plan. We will explore re-amortizingthe remaining unfunded liability over up to
a ten year period to reduce the budget impact.


MOTION by: J. LaFond, second by B. Hogan to re-amortize the remaining unfunded
liability over up to a ten-year period. Passed.              FILED
