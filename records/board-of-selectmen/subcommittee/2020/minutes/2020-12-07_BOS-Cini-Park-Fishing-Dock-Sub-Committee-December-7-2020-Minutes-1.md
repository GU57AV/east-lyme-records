                           EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                 SUBCOMMITTEE TO REVIEW CINI PARK FISHING DOCK LEASES
                             MEETING OF DECEMBER 7,2020
                                      MINUTES


This meeting was held viaZoom online meeting services and was available fbr the public to view, listen and
participate via videoconference and dial-in options.

PRESENT: Marc Salerno, Paul Dagle and Dan Cunningham

The meeting was called to order at 5:25 p,m

It was noted that at approximately 5:35 p.m., this meeting was interupted by hackers and forced to shut
down. The meeting resumed at approximately 5:45 p,m. with different, more secure Zoom meeting
credentials due to the security issues with the original credentials.

All four applications were discussed and compared to newly adopted criteria approved by the Board of
Selectman. The committee was unanimous in recommending continuing the lease for Michael Stepski, who is
an existing lessee, There was discussion about renewing Michael Beebe's lease or offering the dock to
applicant JoelLizza. The subcommittee could not come up with a consensus for the third dock, so it was
recommended to present both options to the Board of Selectman at their meeting on December 16th.

Dan Cunningham moved to adjourn at 6:15 with Paul Dagle seconding.


MOTTON (1)
Mr. Cunningham MOVED to adjourn the December 7,2020, meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen
Subcommittee to Review CiniPark Fishing Dock Leases at 6:15 p,m,
Seconded by Mr. Dagle. Motion passed 3-0.

Respectfully Submitted By:


Marc Salerno
Board of Selectmen
