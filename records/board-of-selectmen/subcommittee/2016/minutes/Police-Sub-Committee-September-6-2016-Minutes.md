Minutes:

1.

In Attendance were 1° Selectman Mark Nickerson, members of the committee including :
Thomas Gardner, Kathleen Miller, Joseph Perkins, Mark Powers and Stephen Rebelowski. Also
present were Sgt. Joseph San Juan and Michael Macek of the East Lyme Police Department.
The meeting was called to order by 1st Selectman Mark Nickerson who tasked the committee
with a twofold mission: First, to undertake a review of facts and circumstances presented and
then, to issue a recommendation based on their information and knowledge of whether the
Town of East Lyme should or should not establish an independent police department at this
time.

It was decided that the regular police subcommittee meeting would be scheduled on
Wednesdays from 5-6 p.m from September 14, 2016, until November 16, 2016.

During this meeting, the Chairperson appointed was Thomas Gardner, and the recording
secretary Kathleen Miller.

Two work sessions were scheduled for Friday, September 9, 2016 at the East Lyme Police
Department and Wednesday, September 14, 2016, at the Ledyard Town Hall.

The format of the meeting will be open discussion.

The meeting was adjourned approximately 7:30 p.m.

Kathleen A. Miller
