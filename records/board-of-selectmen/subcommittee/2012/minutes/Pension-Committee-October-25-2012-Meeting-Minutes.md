EAST LYME PENSION COMMITTEE ;
MEETING OF OCTOBER 25, 2012

ATC

MINUTES EAST LYME TOWN CLERK

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Anna Johnson, Renee Fecteau, Nazz Paciotti, Paul
McDonough, Raymond Hart, Don Meltabarger and Jim LaFond by telephone

ALSO PRESENT: J. McAraw and R. Macomb of Essex Financial, and L: Wang of
USI Consulting Group

Paul Formica called the meeting to order 2:39 PM

Mr. Formica entered a motion to add under item #9 move into executive session to
discuss pension plan amendments regarding collective bargaining groups. Motion
passed,

Motion by R Hart second by N Paciotti to approve minutes from the September 14, 2012
meeting. Motion passed.

Mr. J. McAraw reviewed the investment rates of return year to date at 10.3%, trailing
one year at 16.2% and the annualized rate of return since the completion of transferring
the plan assets at 8.28%. Mr. McAraw introduced reviewing the current asset allocation
and making changes within the next quarter. At this time he is considering reducing the
Foreign Exposure and increasing the Small and Mid-Capital U.S. exposure.

Ms. R. Macomb brought a hand-out to distribute concerning the Mass Mutual merge with
The Hartford. They are purchasing the entire book of business. Mass Mutual has a
strong DB product. They are making offers to many Hartford employees which include
Ms. Humphries and Ms. Priola who currently service our account. We will have no
change in agreement.

Mr. L Wang of USI presented a draft copy of the January 1, 2012 valuation using the
five year smoothing and entry age normal calculation methods. Motion by N Paciotti,
second by R Hart to accept the January 1, 2012 valuation as presented. Motion passed.

Mr. L Wang also presented calculation information on the impact of financial reporting
when implementing GASB Statements 67 & 68.

Discussion regarding charging employees for additional pension calculations for non-
retirement issues. A policy statement would be drafted and presented to the committee at
a later meeting for consideration and recommendation to the Board of Selectmen for
approval.

Ms. R Fecteau, HR Manager reported Quadro calculation request and retirement of Mr.
James Walsh from the BOE.
