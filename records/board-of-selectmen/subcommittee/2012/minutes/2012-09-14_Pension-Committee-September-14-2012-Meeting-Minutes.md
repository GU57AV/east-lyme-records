ALSO PRESENT: _ L. Wang and K. McDonough of USI Consulting Group
Paul Formica called the meeting to order 9:30 AM

Minutes from the August 27, 2012 meeting were approved with amendments.

Mr. Lloyd Wang of USI presented additional funding methods for the January 1, 2012
plan valuation. With the implementation of GASB Statements 67 & 68 in the future, the
committee a motion was made by Nazz Paciotti seconded by Ray Hart to have an
analysis with the following assumptions prepared:

Cost Method — Entry Age Normal Cost Method. The funding contributions consist of the
normal cost and amortization charges. The amortization charges are closed periods based
on the following schedule:

(i) Initial unfunded accrued liability - 20 years
(ii) Subsequent plan amendments — 20 years
(iii) Subsequent method changes — 10 years
(iv) Subsequent assumption changes ~ 10 years

(v) Subsequent actuarial gains and losses — 5 years
Asset Method — Five-year Asset Averaging Method.
Attorney Karen McDonough from USI will present the plan re-statement document at the
meeting. It was agreed that staff would have meetings with Attorney McDonough for
review and final presentation to the committee.
Motion to adjourn.
Meeting adjourned at 11:00 am
Respectfully submitted,

For the committee:
Anna M. Johnson
