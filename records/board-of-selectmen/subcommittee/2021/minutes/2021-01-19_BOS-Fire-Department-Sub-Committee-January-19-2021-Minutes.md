            SUBCOMMITTEE TO REVIEW THE FIRE DEPARTMENTS ORGANIZATIONS

                                     MINUTES


This meeting was held viaZoom online meeting services and was available for the public to view, listen and
participate via videoconference and dial-in options.

PRESENT: Kevin Seery, Marc Salerno and Dan Cunningham

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m.

2. Approve Minutes - December 7,2020
Mr. Seery stated that those minutes will be presented at a future meeting for approval,

3.      Discussion and Review ofthe 2017 Fire Study
A discussion and review of the 2017 fire study was conducted. It was coucluded that only areas of the study
that addressed fire department organization, staffing levels, and chain of command would be reviewed,

4.       Discussion with Fire Chiefs
There was discussion on the organization, by-laws, and operations of the Flanders and Niantic Fire
Departments. Flanders FD Chief Chris Taylor and Niantic FD Chief Jim Barone were invited to partici8pate
in the discussion. There was discussion about current volunteers and challenges being faced by the respective
departments. It was agreed that members of the subcommittee would arange to meet individually with he
Chiefs at their respective fire departments for in-depth discussion on fire department operations.

MOTTON (1)
Mr. Salerno MOVED to adjourn the January 19, 2021 , meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen
Subcommittee to Review the Fire Departments Organizations at 7:38 p,m.
Seconded by Mr. Cunningham. Motion passed 3-0.
