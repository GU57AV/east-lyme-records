                          EAST LYME PENSION COMMITTEE
                          SPECIAL MEETING of October 14,2021
                                    MINUTES


PRESENT: M. Nickerson, A. Johnson, J. Wohler, P. Goulekas, J. LaFond, D. Hall,
B. Hogan

ABSENT: M. Stevens, D. Connolly, T. Santos




First Selectman Nickerson called the meeting to order at 1:05 pm.

The Committee went out to bid for a Pension Asset Manager. Twelve proposals were
received. Committee members present had reviewed the proposals received. The
members discussed which proposals that could be eliminated. Next discussed rankings to
determine which firms to interview.

It was determined to interview four (4) firms. The interviews will take place on October
25, 2021. The format will be to allow thirly minutes for the firm to conduct a
presentation and allow fifteen minutes for the committee to ask questions. A few
questions will be sent to the firms to include the information in their presentations.

The meeting adjourned x255 pm
