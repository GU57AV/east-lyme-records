Due to traffic issues on I95, committee deferred discussion on Investment Portfolio

MOTION by: D. Connolly seconded by J. LaFond to approve minutes of April lI,2019
meeting. Passed.

Discussion took place regarding the new Actuarial Tables adopted in August 2018 by the
Society of Actuaries. The impact of the changes to the funding of the plan was discussed.
It is estimated the prior year's change in liability to be $878,345. Using a ten year
amortization it would increase the town's annual contribution by $116,875. The increase
in normal cost is estimated to be $23,451.

MOTION by: J. LaFond seconded by J. Wohler if it is mandatory to implement the
change if we have flexibility in implementation committee would like to discuss further.
Passed.

Ms. Johnson reported there was one Board of Education Secretary Retirement and that
we are expecting a Public Works employee to submit notice to retire by the end of this
month.

MOTION by: D. Connolly, second by: J. LaFond to pay USI invoices in the amount of
$500. Passed.

Leading into discussion on Investment Portfolio, J. LaFond questioned high risk,
declining rates on investments and building cash to a comfortable level.

J. McAraw of Northstar Wealth Partners noted the companies now making new highs in
the marketplace are McDonald's, Coke, P&G now waiting for the big tech companies to
make new highs. In our current portfolio the Invesco Money Market account currently
has $2.1M. This is an interest bearing account and currently is 8.9% of the portfolio
recommending we bring this to 15% in the next 12 to 18 months. The current annual
outlay for Pension Payments is approximately $450,000.

Ms. Macomb discussed making improvements to the plan reducing the fees from .35% to
.20%. The estimated savings based on 6130119 contract market value would be $49,228.
We currently have 16 funds in the portfolio and would need to move funds to ones that
are within the new fee structure. A mapping was done from current funds to new funds
with similar composition, history and to meet our investment goals. (see attached)

MOTION by: J. LaFond, second by to make changes to the portfolio funds as presented
by Northstar Wealth Partners. Passed.

MOTION by: D. Connolly, second by: P. Goulekas to transfer $465,000 to Oppenheimer
Government Money Market from Putnam High Yield. Passed

MOTION by J. LaFond, second by D, Connolly: To adjourn meeting at 4:05 pm
