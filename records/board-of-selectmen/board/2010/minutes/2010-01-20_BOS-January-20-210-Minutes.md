3b. Agricultural Commission
Attorney O’Connell reviewed the draft ordinance and pointed out the sections that were
redundant with the Charter and or unnecessary.

The Board members reviewed the sections and discussed whether the ordinance should
be drafted as a 3 member with 1 alternate or 5 member with one alternate commission;
whether appointments should be made 60 to 90 days after adoption; and how many times
per year the commission should meet. The consensus was for Town Attorney to make the
corrections and deletions and to come back to the February 3rd meeting with a draft
ordinance reflecting the Board’s comments.

5a. Ex-Officio
Mr. Hogan had no meetings to attend however; he noted that he had drafted a proposed

to East Lyme’s newest Eagle Scout Kevin Santa Maria. He also attended the Water and
Sewer meeting.

5b. First Selectman’s Report
Mr. Formica thanked Mr. Nickerson for filling in for him and offered his thanks to
everyone for their prayers and wishes during this difficult time.
He attended the annual law enforcement luncheon with Sgt. Blanchette and has begun
departmental budget reviews with the Finance Director. He said the budget will be
presented to the Board on February 3rd. He attended a Legislative Committee meeting and
heard the different initiatives they intend to pursue. He added he will be speaking
tomorrow at the DEP since they are looking to further restrict stream flow initiatives. He
stressed that this was not the time for this and it was misguided since it forces
municipalities to divert much needed funding to defend against these new initiatives. He
will be speaking against these regulations. He had a meeting regarding the
interconnection of water. He said that the draft audit was complete and will be sent to the
State by the end of the month. He noted that a review of established fiscal policies was
performed at the COG (Council of Governments) meeting and he also attended the COST
(Council of Small Towns) Annual meeting. He said that on the state level, they were
looking at mid year cuts and are facing a $4-5 million dollar deficit.
