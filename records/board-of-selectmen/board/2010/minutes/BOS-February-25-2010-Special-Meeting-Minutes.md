East Lyme Board of Selectmen Special Meeting of 2/25/10

                       EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                     SPECIAL MEETING OF FEBRUARY 25, 2010
                                  MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, John Birmingham.

EXCUSED: John Hogan, Rose Ann Hardy.

1. CALL TO ORDER
Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 5:15 p.m. and led the Pledge. He said that
since Mr. Wilson had not yet arrived, the Board would merely listen to the Director of
Public Works present his budget and no votes would be taken. He added that Mr. Hogan
and Ms. Hardy were both out of state but that Ms. Hardy had sent in an email noting she
had reviewed the budgets and had no questions (identified as Attachment A).

PUBLIC WORKS:
Director Mike Giannattasio noted that the storm overtime line had increased slightly and
there was a $1000 increase in the traffic signals and street signs line. He added that the
Tree Warden line showed a $4000 increase and this was to take down trees in the town’s
right of ways that created safety hazards. He noted that he was requesting an increase in
the vehicle maintenance line and a slight increase for supplies and miscellaneous.

Mr. Wilson arrived at 5: 22 p.m.

Mr. Giannattasio noted that $107,000 had been spent already this year for storm materials
and supplies. He said he would like to keep the streetlight budget flat although CL&P
was talking of an increase. He added that we might need to revisit this line at a later date.

Mr. Formica commented that we could be through the budget process when we receive
the LOCIP funding from the state and added it has not been bonded yet. He said we could
experience trouble since the state’s budget has a $4 million dollar deficit for this year and
a 5 million dollar deficit for next year. He proposed increasing line 315 Storm Overtime
to $46,400 and storm materials line (440) to $100,000.

Mr. Nickerson inquired when the new vehicles would come in.

Mr. Giannattasio responded “very soon and some up until 6 months from now”.
He stressed that if everyone jumps on with single stream recycling, the town will realize
even more savings.

TOWN ENGINEER:
Town Engineer Bill Scheer commented that his budget reflected no increases other than a
COLA and longevity pay.
IT:
Bill Scheer said that line 215 maintenance of equipment has increased. He added that the
telephone system in the Town Hall is approximately 15 years old and in order to keep it
running, we need a central processor upgrade costing about $5000. He also said that the
Police Mobile Computer Support ($4200) had been transferred to the Police budget to
allow the officers the ability to track the funding.

Mr. Nickerson commented that when calling in to Town Hall, he gets a busy signal quite
often.

Mr. Scheer noted that this upgrade would increase the amount of lines to the Town Hall.

BUILDING MAINTENANCE:
Mr. Formica congratulated Mr. Giannattasio on receiving his Building Operations
Certification Level 2.

Mr. Birmingham inquired about the status of the Colton Road building?

Mr. Giannattasio said that the property had been closed and the single stream recycling
containers would be stored and assembled there. He said they were in the process of
deciding how to design the inside and he hoped to be in by January of next year.

Mr. Nickerson noted his main concern was the custodial line which showed an increase
of $27,000.

Mr. Giannattasio noted that a mistake had been made on last year’s budget and a step and
a 3.5% COLA had been omitted and this was now corrected. He added that no additional
personnel had been added.

Mr. Formica commented that he was talking to a new electrical supplier who actually
gives rebates.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Wilson moved to adjourn the Special Meeting of the Board of Selectmen at 6:00 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Nickerson. Motion passed 4-0.



Respectfully submitted,



Darlene C. Stevens, Recording Secretary
