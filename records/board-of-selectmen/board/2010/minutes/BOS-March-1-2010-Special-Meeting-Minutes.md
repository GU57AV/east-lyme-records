                       EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                       SPECIAL MEETING OF MARCH 1, 2010
                                  MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Rob Wilson, Rose Ann Hardy.

EXCUSED: John Hogan, John Birmingham.

Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 5:20 p.m. and led the Pledge.

PUBLIC SAFETY / FIRE MARSHAL:
Fire Marshal Dick Morris reviewed his budget noting the large increase request for a
change in the staffing levels of 911. He said that the dispatch center has become
overworked and it needs two more fulltime dispatchers to handle the increased volume.
He said that as it is now with one person on the desk, it’s a big problem and a safety
issue. He also noted that this would be the first year of a five year payment for
radio maintenance.

Mr. Formica noted that he supported bringing the increase request to the Board of
Selectmen and agreed the 911 Center is getting busier and asked the Board, where do we
want to go from here.

Mr. Wilson agreed with the need saying that the Board has to make a decision on
direction. He said that the Police Department also needs to get on board with this and
share some of the responsibility. He suggested a sergeant controlling the dispatch of
police officers.

Mr. Formica said that this operation is ripe for regionalization however; getting the
mechanics in place to do this is difficult.

Ms. Hardy inquired about the possibility of the receptionist at the Police Department
answering a dispatch line during the day. She said that this was the third time that Mr.
Morris had brought this request to the Board.

Mr. Morris commented that this would not work. He agreed with the possibility of
moving a police officer to dispatch.

Mr. Formica noted that the mileage rate went from .55 to .50 this year.

Mr. Wilson noted that the supplies line doubled.

Niantic Fire Chief Pringle said that if we want the dispatch center to operate the way that
it has in the past, we need to hire additional dispatchers. He added that it was physically
impossible with just one person sitting there.

BOARD OF EDUCATION:
Superintendent of Schools Dr. James Lombardo and Chair Tim Hagen were present and
reviewed his power point presentation (identified as attachment A.)

Dr. Lombardo said that they had made a conscious reduction in the budget share of
special education and was presenting a budget with a 1.97% increase over last year or
$773,200. He said that the increases were mainly in property services such as
maintenance needs, health insurance benefits, and instructional improvements for special
education. He stressed that this budget does not have a lot of flexibility but, sometimes
we have to sit back and take a deep breath and say – this is what the times require.

Ms. Hardy noted that the student supplies line was cut last year and this year there was an
early budget freeze.

Dr. Lombardo responded that he did freeze the supplies line since this was one budget
line he could freeze. He said he is concerned, but in tough economic times they were
trying to keep as close to a 0 budget as possible.

Ms. Hardy inquired if the tuition rate for out of district students had been set yet?

Dr. Lombardo said that the rate was increased 5% this year, but was not set for next year
yet. He said that the Salem rate is set by a formula.

Mr. Nickerson said that he found this budget inspiring and that the 1.97% increase was a
realistic figure.

Dr. Lombardo thanked Mr. Nickerson but added that this is the leanest budget he has had
in his 24 years as a Superintendent. He said when times are tough; you have to do what is
necessary.

Mr. Formica inquired if the Board was relying on stimulus funding to support the new
special education coordinator?

Dr. Lombardo said that the Special Ed Coordinator is critical toward the long term
program improvements and cost effective programming. He added that there will be no
reduction of services for the kids; we will just be doing more in town. He said that since
the position would not be hired until later this year it would be fully funded the remainder
of the year and next with the stimulus funding.

Mr. Formica thanked Dr. Lombardo for doing great things in a very short time.

Ms. Hardy said that this appears to be a minimalist budget with no funding for
innovations.
