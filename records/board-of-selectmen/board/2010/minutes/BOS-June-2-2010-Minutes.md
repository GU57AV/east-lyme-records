3a. Golf Cart Regulation
Mr. Formica said that he had inquired of Town Attorney if the town’s liability would
increase or decrease if the Board passed this regulation. He said that Attorney O’Connell
had responded with the letter he then passed out to the Board (Attachment C).

Attorney Collins read the letter into the record.

Mr. Hogan noted the suggestions received from Arthur Saunders. He recommended
adding to section 2.2, “at least one foot above any installed roof”; on section 7, he
recommended adding the following after the word “infraction” in the last sentence, “and
will be subject to the same fine structure for motor vehicles as defined in the CT State
Statutes”.

Mr. Formica said that he has now gone full circle on this matter. He said he originally
brought this to the Board thinking it was a good idea. He said now he is not sure if it
makes sense to go with this regulation because of liability reasons. He added that without
all of the discussions and input, he would not have come to this conclusion and thanked
Mr. Hogan for doing a great job in drafting the regulation.

Ms. Hardy said that the Board had just received this letter this evening and they would
need more time to consider the liability factor. She added the Board does not want to
willingly put the Town into a position of liability.

Mr. Nickerson said that he has always been concerned with the liability and extremely
concerned about the hills in Giants Neck.

Mr. Wilson said that the alternative would be to not pass anything and people will
continue as they are now.. He said he is still in favor of the regulation and that Attorney

O’Connell was looking out for the worst case scenario. He said that he would find it hard
to disregard the whole beach area based on a hill.

Ms. Hardy said there are two very steep hills in Giants Neck and inquired if there was
any other way to get to the beach without using them. She said we have spent a lot of
time on this and it is very rare to not follow the Town Attorney’s advice. She said this
regulation would benefit a minority of people. She asked do we take the minority and put
the majority at risk.

Mr. Hogan stressed that if the Board does not pass this regulation, enforcement of golf
carts all over town would need to take place. He likened the Town Attorney’s letter to
“chicken little asking what if the sky falls”. He said he was surprised by the Attorney’s
statement and added we could be sued with or without the regulation and at least with a
regulation, golf carts would be kept in certain areas. He said that all of Sgt. Blanchette’s
points are reflected in the regulation.

Mr. Formica said that golf carts are prohibited currently and based on the opinion of the
Town Attorney; he is not comfortable in moving forward.

Mr. Nickerson said he did not see how having a regulation would absolve the Town from
liability. If there was no regulation and there was a problem, the town gave no one
permission to operate golf carts in any area. He said that Giants Neck is not a typical
beach community – referring to the way it is laid out. He said if Giants Neck were
removed, he could vote yes.

Mr. Birmingham agreed with not using carts on the two roads in Giants Neck.

MOTION (9)
Mr. Wilson moved the following:
Resolved, the Board of Selectmen, acting as the town’s Traffic Authority, adopts the
regulation entitled “Regulation Concerning the Use of Golf Carts as amended”, to be
effective on June 15, 2010.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan.

Aye: Mr. Hogan, Mr. Wilson.
Nay: Mr. Formica, Mr. Nickerson, Mr. Birmingham, Ms. Hardy. Motion fails 2-4.
