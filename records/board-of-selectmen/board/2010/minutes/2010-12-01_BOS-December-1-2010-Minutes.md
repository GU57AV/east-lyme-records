                       EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                         MEETING OF DECEMBER 1, 2010
                                  MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Jack Hogan, Rose Ann Hardy.

1a. Pledge Allegiance to the Flag
Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 7:32 p.m. and led the Pledge.

Mr. Formica introduced Assistant Principal of the High School Jeffrey Provost and
presented him with a proclamation about his being chosen Assistant Principal of the
Year.

1b. Additional Agenda & Consent Calendar Items
There was none.

1c. Delegations
Holly Cheeseman of 16 Mitchell Drive said the holiday decorations on Main Street are
“glorious”.

1d. Approval of Minutes, Meeting of November 17, 2010
Mr. Formica noted a change to be made on Page 6., second item should say Board of
Education.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Nickerson moved to approve the minutes of the meeting of November 17, 2010 as
amended.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan.

Aye: Mr. Formica, Mr. Nickerson, Mr. Hogan.
Nay: None.
Abstain: Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 3-0-1.

1e. Consent Calendar
MOTION (2)
Mr. Nickerson moved to approve the Consent Calendar for the meeting of December 1,
2010 in the amount of $7.25.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan. Motion passed 4-0.

2a. Resolution – STEAP Grant
Mr. Formica noted that one of the requirements of this grant was to have this resolution
made and certified by the Town Clerk.

Mr. Wilson arrived at 7:40 p.m.

MOTION (3)
Mr. Nickerson moved the following resolution:
        WHEREAS, pursuant to The Small Town Economic Assistance Program
(STEAP) CGTS 4-66g, the Connecticut Department of Economic and Community
Development is authorized to extend financial assistance for economic development
projects; and
WHEREAS, it is desirable and in the public interest that the Town of East Lyme
make an application to the State for $ $200,000 in order to undertake the Main Street
Enhancement Project Phase III and to execute an Assistance Agreement
.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE East Lyme Board of Selectmen

1.      That it is cognizant of the conditions and prerequisites for the state financial
assistance imposed by
The Small Town Economic Assistance Program (STEAP) under CGTS 4-66g

2.      That the filing of an application for State financial assistance by
The Town of East Lyme in an amount not to exceed $200,000 is hereby approved and
that Paul M. Formica – First Selectman of the Town of East Lyme is directed to execute
and file such application with the Connecticut Department of Economic and Community
Development, to provide such additional information, to execute such other documents as
may be required, to execute an Assistance Agreement with the State of Connecticut for
State financial assistance if such an agreement is offered, to execute any amendments,
decisions, and revisions thereto, and to act as the authorized representative of Town Of
East Lyme.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 5-0.

2b. Appropriation $7740 – CNRE CAD Software – Replacement Survey Equipment
Town Engineer Bill Scheer was present and noted that the Town surveying equipment is
over 20 years old and if this new equipment is purchased, would cut the field time by
50%.

Ms. Hardy inquired if this new equipment would interface with our computers.

Mr. Scheer said that the current software was purchased two years ago and was
compatible with the equipment he was requesting to purchase.

MOTION (4)
Mr. Hogan moved to appropriate $7,740 from the CAD software and survey equipment
CNRE for the purchase of new replacement survey equipment and send to the Board of
Finance for approval.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 5-0.

2c. Insurance Waiver Request – EL Republican Town Committee – ELLCC
MOTION (5)
Mr. Nickerson moved to approve the request of the East Lyme Republican Town
Committee for an insurance waiver for their use of meeting rooms at the East Lyme
Library Community Complex through January, 2012.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 5-0.

2d. Appointments – Part time Constables
MOTION (6)
Mr. Wilson moved to appoint Robert Cutillo of 7 Grand Street Niantic, CT and Paul
Holmes of 9R Center Road, Waterford, CT to serve as part time constables through June
30, 2011.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan. Motion passed 5-0.

Mr. Formica noted the letter to the Director of Ledge Light in the Selectmen’s folders and
said that there are five other towns exploring an option for another health district. He said
that per the contract, a letter putting Ledge Light on notice that we might not renew our
contract for the upcoming year was necessary.

5a. Ex-Officio
Ms. Hardy had no report but added that the Zoning meeting was cancelled for tomorrow.

Mr. Nickerson attended the Town Building Committee meeting where there was
discussion about the work being done at the new Colton Road building. He agreed with
Ms. Cheeseman’s comments made earlier about the holiday decorations adding, Main
Street makes you proud to be an East Lymer. He said the decorations were a fabulous
improvement.

Mr. Hogan attended the Board of Education meeting and said he was very impressed with
the kids presentations about November 11th and the video for the accreditation
committee.

Mr. Wilson said that the Parks and Recreation meeting was tomorrow and said that the
Holiday Stroll was great.

Mr. Nickerson congratulated the girls volleyball team on becoming state champions.

Mr. Formica added his congratulations noting that Molly Giannattasio was named All
State.

5b. First Selectman’s Report
Mr. Formica said that the Incentive Housing plan had been approved by Planning, Zoning
and the Selectmen and had moved on to the CT Office of Policy and Management.

He said that our long time utility engineer Brad Kargl had a very dangerous house fire
and lost everything and said that he will be starting something to coordinate assistance.

He attended the SECTER Annual Meeting.

He attended a Probate District meeting and added that the cost for the move of the
Registrars and the renovations to the building would be borne by all of the towns.

He attended a press conference with the CT State Police announcing the recovery of
approximately one million dollars worth of stolen items due to the hard work and diligence
of Detective Marr and Officer Cuomo.

He attended a Zoning Commission meeting where after discussion, the Board endorsed
the Incentive Housing Zone.

He attended a meeting with the Department of Health and the DECD regarding the water
interconnection between East Lyme and New London.

He attended a 50th Wedding Anniversary celebration for Phyllis and Bob Stevenson.

He attended a meeting of the Council of Governments Legislative Committee where they
received a forecast for next year’s state budget.

He attended the East Lyme High School pre game football dinner.

He took part in the Niantic Stroll and praised the holiday decorations.

He attended a retirement ceremony for Steve Hartzell having served 34 years with the
Navy.

He attended a meeting of the Niantic River Watershed. He said anyone interested in
serving from East Lyme should contact his office.

He attended a retirement celebration for New London City Manager Marty Berliner.

He attended a stream flow coalition meeting in Hartford.

He noted he was exploring regional efforts for the following:
       Health district; policing; expanded Animal Control and the regional water plan.

He said he received a postcard from one of Ms. Hardy’s students and complimented her
on the work she does with her students.

Mr. Birmingham arrived at 8:01 p.m.

Joe Mingo of 397 Boston Post Road said he had seen a public TV program about liquid
assets (water) where they noted that the average American uses 176 gallons of water a
day and the average African uses 5 gallons of water a day. He said developments are
increasing and no one is increasing our water sources. He said we have a serious shortage

7. Selectmen’s Response
Mr. Formica said that the draft environmental report was in however, negotiations with
the owner were still on-going. He said when they are concluded, the report would become
public.

in under budget.

Mr. Formica responded that it was operational and no additional money was necessary.

MOTION (7)
Mr. Nickerson moved to adjourn the December 1, 2010 meeting of the East Lyme Board
