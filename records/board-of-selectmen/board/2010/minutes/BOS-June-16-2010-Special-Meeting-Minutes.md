                       EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                       SPECIAL MEETING OF JUNE 16, 2010
                                  MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, John Birmingham, Rose Ann Hardy, John
           Hogan.

EXCUSED: Mr. Wilson.

Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 7:03 p.m.

Executive Session.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan. Motion passed 5-0.

The meeting was adjourned at 7:35 p.m.
