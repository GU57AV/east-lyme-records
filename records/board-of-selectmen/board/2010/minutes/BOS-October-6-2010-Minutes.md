2a. Presentation – East Lyme Affordable Housing Plan – Jane Dauphinais
Mr. Formica introduced Jane Dauphinais and noted that she was the architect of the
report which had been funded through a grant from Home CT. (identified as Attachment
B). She noted that the guiding principals for this study were to:
        Do No Harm; Focus on the needs of East Lyme and its residents and employees;
Focus on redevelopment rather than new development and development must solve more
than one problem at a time.
She added that this plan was developed as part of the 2009 revision of the East Lyme
Plan of Conservation and Development (POCD). She said that a community survey was
taken in 2007 and the results indicated that there was a need for more affordable housing
in town and residents wanted this housing where it would strengthen village centers.
Residents showed a strong desire to accommodate affordable housing to reach the 10%
threshold and they desired an exemption from the Affordable Housing appeals Act (GSA
8-30g). She said that in 2007, the CT General Assembly passed the Housing for
Economic Growth Program (Public Act 07-4) to incentivize towns to plan proactively for
affordable housing. This program offers towns a way to have local control, exemption
from 8-30g, with cash incentive payments and is voluntary in nature. As overlay zones
for affordable housing, a developer is provided with an additional option for the property,
but it does not replace the underlying zone. She added that incentive payments are
offered and that the first payment would be received when the zone is created and the
second payment to the town would be when the town issues permits. In East Lyme, the
four areas were identified for Incentive Housing Zones and two were then eliminated.
The two that are identified are Midway Plaza and there zones in Niantic which in total
would add only 162 housing units. She commented that the Planning and Zoning
Commissions wanted to be proactive on this issue and were tired of being reactive.

Mr. Formica said that he has had contact with the property owner from Midway Plaza
and he was interested in utilizing the tools from this report. He continued that COG’s
Executive Director Jim Butler wrote revised regulations for our Zoning Board to consider
and this would take place at their meeting of October 21st.

Ms. Dauphinais suggested that Mr. Formica convey the conversations with Midway Plaza
to OPM.

Mr. Nickerson said that he was on Zoning when several 8-30g applications came forward
for Oswegatchie Hills and that this study was great.

Ms. Hardy said that this Board had not received the results of the 2007 study.

Ms. Dauphinais responded that that survey had been done separately by Ms. Parulis.

Ms. Hardy asked if affordable housing and workforce housing were the same.

Ms. Dauphinais said that workforce housing is not a legal term.
