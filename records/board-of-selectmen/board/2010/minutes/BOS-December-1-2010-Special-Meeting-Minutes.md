                           EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                                DECEMBER 1, 2010
                               SPECIAL MEETING
                                   MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, John Birmingham, Jack Hogan, Rose Ann
           Hardy, Rob Wilson.

Mr. Formica called the Special Meeting to order at 8:13 p.m.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Nickerson moved to enter Executive Session for the purpose of discussing the
potential purchase of real estate and personnel and to invite Attorney Andrew Cellemme
to attend as appropriate.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 6-0.

MOTION (2)
Mr. Nickerson moved to return to regular session at 9:15 p.m. with no action taken during
Executive Session.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 6-0.

Mr. Formica adjourned the Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at 9:16
p.m.
