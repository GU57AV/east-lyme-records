4a. Mr. Nickerson called the public hearing to order at 7:02 p.m. and led the Pledge.
1b. Ordinance Prescribing Fees for Building and Demolition Permits

Mr. Nickerson explained that a major building project such as a school building might require the
addition of a part-time building inspector; the amendment would allow this cost to come out of the
project budget rather than the operating budget.

Currently all permit fees for work done by or for the Town are waived. The amendment would provide
that permit fees be waived for the first $2,000,000 of construction for any Town project.

Mr. Nickerson asked if anyone from the public wished to speak.

John Drabik of 34 Drabik Road asked what amount of money is reimbursable and Ms. Cheeseman
explained that it depends on what the project is.

Mr. Nickerson said it is easier to plan the project if the money is coming out of the project budget.

Mr. Drabik said that he doesn’t know enough about this topic to comment on it and added that he
doesn’t think the proposed change has been investigated enough.

Mr. O’Connell commented that this amendment has been proposed by the Building Official and
accurately reflects these very large projects and the fees they will be charged.

Joe Mingo of 397 Boston Post Road said this is another example of voodoo economics and should never
happen.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Seery moved to close the Public Hearing.
Ms. Cheeseman seconded the motion. Motion passed 6-0.
