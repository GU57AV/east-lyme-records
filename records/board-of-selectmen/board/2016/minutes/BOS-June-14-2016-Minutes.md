Re: 3D Engine Refurbishment
Chief James Levandoski:

In the above mentioned matter it is our recommendation that your department does not
refurb the existing pumper in your fleet. Our reasons are follows:

1. Regardless of what is done to the vehicle; we have found the value of apparatus will not

2. Buyers of used apparatus purchase the vehicles based on the year, mileage, make, model
manufacturer, tank size, condition and component specifications. With date of manufacture
being the key item when purchasing a used apparatus. When you refurb a existing
apparatus you cannot change the date of the vehicle on the title; so there is no increase in
valve to the buyer.

3. 3D Fire Apparatus is currently out of business. Parts required for the rebuild could be
unavailable.

4. Technical support is also unavailable for this unit.

5. A danger with these projects is that you may find something that cannot be repaired or the
cost of repairing the item is more than the total budget for entire the project. There are many
items that cannot be explored or detected until the unit s disassembled.
