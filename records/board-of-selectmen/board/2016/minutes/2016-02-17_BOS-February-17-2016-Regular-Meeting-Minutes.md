Board of Selectmen
February 17, 2016
MINUTES

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Holly Cheeseman, Marc Salerno, Rose Ann Hardy, Dan
Cunningham.

the prior meeting.

2d. Budget Reviews

BUILDING DEPARTMENT #104

Chief Building Official Joe Smith was present to review his budget. He noted that the only change he was
proposing was increasing line #212 Assistant Building Official to a benefitted 30 hour position. He added
that presently, 3 different individuals perform this service.

Finance Director Johnson noted that the benefit package for this position was approximately $18,000.

COMMISSION ON AGING #418

Cathy Wilson was present to review her budget. She reminded the Board of her hire of a Program
Manager who have been very busy creating new programs at no or little cost to the Town. She noted
that her transportation requests were increasing and that she is looking for 4 additional hours of driving.
She added that she is also looking for transportation software.

Mr. Nickerson requested that people attend the lunches at the Senior Center since their attendance will
help out the meals on wheels program. He added that we are very lucky to have 50 — 60 volunteers.

PUBLIC WORKS HIGHWAY AND SANITATION 3317

Director Bragaw noted that he was eliminating line 412 PT Seasonal to try and cut back on the budget.
He noted the increase in line #440 Storm Materials and Supplies adding he was hoping to fill up the new
salt shed for the winter. He noted the increase in line #210 Streetlights and added every time there is a
new development, etc. with street lights they are paid for from this line. He added he has no control
over this line since other commissions deem the lights required.

Director Bragaw passed out his Town of East Lyme Capital Improvement Plan FY 16-17 Pavement Plan
(attached and identified as Attachment B) and said that his priority was to pave the Bush Hill
neighborhood which had 136 homes and Cavasin Drive and Latimer Drive with 53 homes. He noted that
this would be expensive ($720,000) but if the Board cut this amount, he would take out the Bush Hill
neighborhood. He added that our Town Aid Road money next year needs to be spent on fixing the
Walnut Hill Road retaining wall and the Atlantic Street drainage.

Ms. Johnson passed out the Projected Revenues (identified as Attachment C).

3a. Approval — Commercial Fishing Leases — Cini Park

Ms. Hardy noted that she has concerns that there is adequate parking for our commercial fishing
businesses and asked how many additional spaces we would gain by removing some of the long term
storage area. She also stated that she was uncomfortable having a lease for three years and suggested
having a re-opener in one year.

Mr. Nickerson stated that the fishermen prefer a 3 year lease term and if there are problems that crop
up, we will work through them. He said that we all support the commercial fishing businesses. He added
that we should glean approximately six.

Mr. Cunningham noted that he agreed with Ms. Hardy’s statements.

MOTION (8)
Mr. Seery moved to authorize the First Selectman to sign the Commercial Fishing Leases from January 1,
2016 through December 31, 2018.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 6-0.

REGARDING ADOPTION OF A BLIGHT ORDINANCE

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 7:00 p.m. on March 2, 2016 at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania
Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to consider the adoption of a Blight Ordinance. Said Ordinance
would prohibit any owner, occupant, agent, tenant and/or person in control of real property
located in the Town of East Lyme from allowing, creating, maintaining or causing to be created
or maintained blight on the premises.

Copies of the full text of the proposed Ordinance are on file for public inspection at the
office of the East Lyme Town Clerk, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut and at
http://www. eltownhall.com/ to which reference may be had. Interested persons are invited to
attend and be heard.

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, on this 17" day of February, 2016.
Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman. Motion passed 6-0.

Ms. Cheeseman thanked Mr. Salerno and the Zoning Official for all the effort they expended on this
potential ordinance.

5a. Ex-Officio

Ms. Hardy attended the Planning Commission meeting where they were discussing a referral to Zoning
regarding a text amendment for White Gate Farm. The amendment would redefine the meaning of Bed
and Breakfast, allow 8 weddings per year and farm dinners. The text amendment if approved, would
apply to all RU-40-80 parcels in town.

Mr. Salerno said that the Inland Wetlands meeting was cancelled. Harbor Management was reviewing an
application for a dingy dock at the end of Grand Street which was approved.

Ms. Cheeseman had no report.
Mr. Seery had no report.

Mr. Cunningham attended the Historic Properties Commission where they discussed how the commission
fits in with the other town commissions such as Smith Harris, etc. He said they have started creating a
flow chart.

5b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson noted that he had made an offer on 224 Main Street per the citizens request at a
referendum which passed by more than a 2-1 vote. He added that he had made a low ball offer and it was
accepted. He stressed that this parcel is the keystone of Main Street. He said that initially the thought is to
provide some parking and a green park area. He noted that we have applied for a STEAP grant for the
demolition of the building and ground remediation. He said that the proposed cell tower for Boston Post
Road has been granted an extension of time until April 15" by the Siting Council. He added that he just
