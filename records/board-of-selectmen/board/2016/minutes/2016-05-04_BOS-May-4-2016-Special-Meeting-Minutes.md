EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
SPECIAL MEETING OF MAY 4, 2016
MINUTES
PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Rose Ann Hardy, Dan Cunningham.

EXCUSED: Ms. Cheeseman.

ALSO PRESENT: Finance Director Anna Johnson, Bd. Of Education Chair Tim Hagen, Superintendent of
Schools Jeff Newton, School Facilities Services Supervisor Ted Hunt.

The Selectmen met Principal DeLoreto at Niantic Center School at 4:00 p.m. She led them through the
building (Attachment A) noting older bathrooms, and cracked and drafty windows. It was noted that
with the present plan, Niantic Center School would be closed after renovations had been made to the
other two schools.

Mr. Salerno joined the tour at approximately 4:15 p.m.

Mr. Nickerson noted that the purpose of this tour was to provide first- hand knowledge of the schools so
they would have a better understanding of plans that would come before them in the near future.

Head Custodian James Goode along with Superintendent Jeff Newton led the tour at Lillie B. Haynes
School (Attachment B.) Boiler and plumbing issues were brought up during the tour.

Superintendant Newton, Tim Hagen and Ted Hunt reviewed Flanders School with the Selectmen noting
ventilation problems in the older section.

Mr. Nickerson noted that maintenance had been delayed on all three buildings.

The tour ended at 6:10 p.m.
