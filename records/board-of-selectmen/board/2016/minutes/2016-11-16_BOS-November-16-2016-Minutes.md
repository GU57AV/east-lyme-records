2c. Approve up to $1396 from Contingency to purchase 4 filing cabinets for Building Dept.
Mr. Nickerson noted that he Building Department of the East Lyme Land Use Offices requests
contingency funds to purchase four new HON Filing cabinets, at the cost of $349.00 each, for a
total of $1,396.00. Currently, the “street” files which contain all of the documentation on
structures located on parcels of land have outgrown their storage cabinets. There are several
boxes of files on the floor in the office, and a large number of files on top of filing cabinets that
do not fit in their respective drawers. The State requires that we retain these records for the life
of the structure. The State building code changed in February of 2014, resulting in additional
documentation being required for certain types of permits, thus enlarging files. The State
building code changed again in October of 2016, resulting in even more documentation being
required for building permits, and enlarging our files even further. He added that this is an
unplanned expense that has come up and falls within the cost of doing business.

MOTION (6)
Mr. Seery moved to approve the use of up to $1,396 from account number 01-01-120-200-500
(Contingency) for the Building Department to purchase four (4) HON Filing cabinets and
forward to the Board of Finance for approval. This purchase is necessary due to the increased
documentation required for building permits as a result of code changes.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 5-0.

5a. Ex-Officio

Mr. Salerno noted that he attended the Harbor Management meeting and they were still working on
updating the Harbor Management Plan. He added that there is a $5 million dollar grant opportunity
available in January that they would like to apply for.

Mr. Salerno added that he attended the Board of Education meeting where redistricting was discussed
and that now the updated enrollment figures were up to 960 from 700-800 in grades K-8.

they would be discussing what beach passes would cost for the coming year. He added that he had

everyone.

Mr. Cunningham had no report.

5b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson congratulated everyone on the ballot at the recent election. He said that before the
election, the State said that they were $133 million dollars in the red. Since the election he noted that
they have come out and said that we are $1.3 billion dollars in the red — off by $1.2 billion dollars.

He spent the last two days at the Connecticut Conference of Municipalities seminar reviewing the latest
FO! rulings. He noted the passing of Veterans Day and thanked all who gave so much. This evening he
went to Castello’s for dinner where they had “Tip a Cop”. He said that there were several police officers
serving food and all of their tips would be donated to Care and Share. He said that the Holiday Stroll will
be on November 26". He noted The Day article today offering support for the independent police
department. He wished everyone a great Thanksgiving adding that we all have so much to be thankful
for.
