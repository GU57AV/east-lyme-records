2a.Authorization and Appropriation for Turn-Key Street Light Project
Director of Public Works Joe Bragaw was present and reviewed the Turn Key Street Light Project. He
noted that he came before the Board in October of 2015 to discuss the possibility of the Town
purchasing our streetlights from Eversource and converting the lights to LED that will allow us
to save a lot of money down the road. He said that he went before the Board of Finance in
their November 2015 meeting to pitch the same concept and they were very interested. The
next step was to do a comprehensive audit to verify the exact number of streetlights we have in
our system. With the Board of Selectman’s approval, we entered into contract with Tanko
Lighting to perform an audit of our streetlights in December 2015. Tanko finished the audit and
have now given us more exact numbers of what it will cost to do the retrofit work. He said that
at this point, he is requesting that we move forward, purchase the streetlights from Eversource
and enter into contract with Tanko Lighting to retrofit the system with new LED lights. The
total cost of this work should not exceed $969,436 plus interest. Of this amount, $421,536
would be needed to pay Eversource for the streetlight system. Our Finance Director, Anna
Johnson is putting out an RFP for financing the purchase of the lights and retrofit over a 7 year
period. The proposal would be to pay for this work out of the existing budget line item as if we
never did this work.

He said that we are not looking to borrow any money, we will pay as we go. He noted that the
project would take 7 years and in the 8" year we will be cash positive. He said that there is a
timeline on this project and Eversource has to be notified by September. He added that Tanko
had been vetted by CCM and everyone who had dealt with them have been happy. He said that
Tanko does not do the work, they manage the work. He added that a local CT contractor will be
hired to do the work.

Mr. Nickerson noted that this was a great, green project that saves money and energy. He
commended Mr. Bragaw for his efforts.

2b2. Special Appropriation $60,000 — CNRE 32 — McCook’s Band Shell

Director of Parks and Recreation Dave Putnam noted that he had coordinated a subcommittee
comprised of members of the Commission, residents and members of the Lions, Rotary and East Lyme
Public Trust to study the potential of a band shell for McCook’s Park. He said that we will not be utilizing
taxpayer money since the Lions, Rotary and East Lyme Public Trust have each donated $20,000 to this
project. He added that the Town had approved $15,000 in the capital budget for this item. He said that
the Parks and Recreation Commission unanimously approved the purchase of a kit for the band shell and
also approved the placement at McCook’s. He said a local contractor would be selected by bid and hired
to assemble the kit. He added that this was rated for 120 mph winds in the event of a hurricane. He
added that the Planning Commission had voted that this project was consistent with the Plan of
Development.

Ms. Cheeseman added that there were several residents of Crescent Beach at the Planning meeting all
in favor of the Bandshell.

Mr. Seery noted that the McCook’s deed had been reviewed and there is no conflict if this project were
completed.

5b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson noted a very busy 4" of July weekend with little incidence. He said that the parks and
beaches were extremely busy, but things worked out well. He added that wonderful things are
happening in the Town and noted the Farmers Market that takes place every Thursday afternoon at
Methodist Street. He commented on the Sub Base celebrating their 100" birthday and remarked on
what a great job the Lions did at Lobsterfest. He said the recent bonding sale was very successful and
the Town also did some re-financing and saved approximately $300,000. He noted that upcoming ribbon
cutting for Marvel Homes which was relocating in Flanders from New London. He said Bayside will
celebrate their project’s completion on July 16", which is also Celebrate East Lyme day. He added that
the Annual Farm Day is July 17". He noted that the East Lyme Regional Theater will be leasing space
from the Masonic Temple and will celebrate on July 15"". He commented that the Board of Education
will be voting at their next meeting to pull back on their school plan. He noted that they would be
regrouping and started the process again in the future. He added that we are in the process of
demolishing two buildings at the old Town Garage site on Romagna Road and the property will then be
regarded and replanted. He announced that we had received a $200,000 grant from DECD for the
former Mobil Station property that the Town now owns. He said that this money would be utilized to
demolish the building and to remediate any problems found beneath the building. He added that the
Water and Sewer Commission will be holding public meetings about future sewering projects and
assessments.

He noted that prior First Selectman Dick Lougee’s son had contacted him and expressed his thanks for
all those who had worked to make East Lyme even better.

Ms. Hardy inquired about the Board of Education and asked if they were going to file any paperwork.

Mr. Nickerson replied that they will not be filing paperwork with the state. He said all things will be
postponed until they decide to come forward with another plan.
