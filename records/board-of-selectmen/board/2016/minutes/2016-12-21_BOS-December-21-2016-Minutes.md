2b. Review/ Discussion of Hazard Mitigation Plan Progress Report

Mr. Nickerson acknowledged the hard work put into this project by Victor Benni and his Team.
The Town of East Lyme is a participant in the FEMA Community Rating System program,
which entitles its residents to discounts on their flood insurance policies. The Town of East
Lyme Engineering Department worked with our FEMA CRS Specialist to increase the Town’s
rating to a Class 8 CRS community, which entitles residents with flood insurance to get a 10%
discount on their premiums. The Town was previously rated as a Class 9 Community, which
afforded residents a 5% discount on their flood insurance premiums. Thank you to Victor Benni
and the Engineering Department for their efforts in obtaining this higher rating.

2c. Ex-Officio Appointments for 2017

Mr. Nickerson opened discussion on the Ex-Officio Appointments for 2017. The Board
discussed their current assignments and suggested changes. Marc Salerno will give up Inland
Wetlands and take on the Planning Commission. Mr. Nickerson noted that Ms. Cheeseman will
be leaving the Board of Selectman in January to take over her elected position as State
Representative, representing East Lyme and Salem. The incoming 6" Selectman will take over
the Inland Wetlands and Library ex-officio assignments. Mr. Nickerson also noted that he will
take over the January and July Board of Education meetings, and the incoming 6" Selectman
will be appointed to the February and August meetings. See Exhibit 1 attached hereto.

2d. Formation / Charge of Finance Purchasing Policy Subcommittee

Mr. Nickerson reported that a subcommittee will be formed for the creation and review of a
Finance Purchasing Policy. This Subcommittee will consist of a Finance Department
representative, Anna Johnson; two Selectmen, Kevin Seery and Dan Cunningham; and two
Board of Finance Members, William Weber and Peter DeRosa. This subcommittee will publish
appropriate public meeting announcements, and submit agenda and minutes for the public’s
review.

6a. Ex-Officio

Mr. Salerno reported on Harbor Management and noted that scallop season is open but there
does not seem to be a high yield of scallops so far this season. In any event, the public is
buying their licenses and out on the water enjoying the season.

she will be appointing a representative from TVCCA to attend a future Board of

Mr. Seery reported that the Parks & Recreation Subcommittee met last week and set the fees for
the 2017 summer season; noting that there will only be an increase in fees for out-of-town
weekend passes.

Ms. Cheeseman had no report but wanted to remind everyone of the upcoming Countdown to
Noon on December 31“ at the East Lyme Community Center from 10:00am to 2:00pm.

6b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson addressed Ms. Holly Cheeseman and thanked her for serving on the Board of
Selectmen. He wished Ms. Cheeseman good luck in Hartford!

He noted that the Executive Assistant to the First Selectman, Darlene Stevens, will be retiring
after 27 years of service and serving 7 First Selectmen. He commended Darlene for her
service to the Town, and invited anyone from within the Town or from the public to please
feel free to attend Darlene’s reception on Friday, December 30" at Town Hall.

He noted that NBC30 did a very nice report on Tuesday’s 6 o’clock news program that outlined
how East Lyme may be impacted by EB’s recent announcement of adding 14,000
potential jobs in the near future. Our community is preparing for a surge in families
moving into East Lyme, and ultimately a surge of school age children entering our school
system. Overall our community is preparing for this influx of new residents, and we are
ready to welcome them as they join us.

Mr. Nickerson reported that the newly formed Police Commission Board will be meeting on
Thursday, December 22" at 7pm at Town Hall.

He reported that he has been at attendance at the recent meetings of the CCM and COG, both of
which are currently working on issues that will affect upcoming budgets.

He reported that Care & Share handed out thousands of gifts last Saturday to families and
children in need; and noted that they accept food every Saturday from 11am to 1pm at
their Roxbury Road location.
He reported that the recently held Jingle Bell Run was a success despite the cold weather. They
had upwards of 780 participants and many spectators downtown to enjoy the event. The
annual Light Parade was held that same evening and despite the very cold weather, the
event was a success. The participating floats were excellent, the holiday spirit was
prevalent, and Main Street and all the businesses downtown were packed.

Mr. Nickerson reported that the investigative portion of the ongoing Schools project is nearing
completion. The current plan is to rejuvenate the three (3) existing elementary schools,
upgrading items that are necessary. A final plan for review will be presented to the public
in the near future. He reports that the re-districting program will begin soon.
