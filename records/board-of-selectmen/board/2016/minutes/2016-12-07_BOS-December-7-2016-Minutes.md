2a. JLUS Presentation and Acceptance of Study

Mr. Nickerson noted that The Department of Defense Community Planning Assistant Fund
awarded the Town of East Lyme $161,770.00 to conduct a Joint Land Use Study (JLUS) with the
Town’s of Old Lyme and Lyme, for Stones Ranch Military Reservation and Camp Niantic. A JLUS
is a cooperative land use planning effort between affected local government and the military
installation. The recommendations provide a rationale, justification, and a policy framework to
support adoption and implementation of compatible development measures designed to
prevent urban encroachment while safeguarding the military mission and protecting the public
health, safety, and welfare. The grant award requires the acceptance/approval of the Final
JLUS Report by the governing body of the Town of East Lyme for completion of the grant and
final reimbursement approval prior to December 31, 2016. The Final JLUS report and
associated background materials have been reviewed by the Technical Committee for
comment, and have been approved by the Policy Committee for acceptance and forwarding to
the governing body of the Town of East Lyme, the Board of Selectmen.

Rick Rust of Matrix Design Group prepared the Joint Land Use Report along with staff support
from Town Planner Gary Goeschel. He said that this report involves Camp Niantic and Stone’s
Ranch and therefore the towns of East Lyme, Lyme and Old Lyme were all part of a committee
along with the State of Connecticut and the Military. He stated that it was important to note
that the JLUS (Joint Land Use Study) is not an adopted plan, but rather a recommended set of
strategies which should be implemented by the JLUS participants in order to address current
and potential future compatibility issues. He noted that the Board was not being asked to
implement anything, only to acknowledge that the study was complete.

Ms. Cheeseman inquired if anything surprising came up compared to other studies.
Mr. Rust replied that the same basic issues came up such as lighting issues, noise and dust.
Mr. Salerno inquired what the military wanted on the waterfront at Camp Niantic.

Mr. Rust stated that they wanted to utilize some fencing for certain areas and better signage.
He added that as yet, the waterfront area was not an exclusionary zone.

Ms. Hardy inquired about the vertical obstruction.

Mr. Rust responded that the military utilized helicopters and they take off and land at both
Stones Ranch and Camp Niantic and they wanted to make sure no tower, etc., was erected to
interfere with this practice.

2f. Special Appropriation $13,350 for the 2016/2017 FY — Connecticut Dept. of Transportation
Comprehensive DUI Grant

Sgt. Macek said that every year the Town of East Lyme Police Department participates in the
Comprehensive DUI Enforcement program. This program is imperative in helping maintaining
safe roads for our residents to travel on. This grant covers saturation patrols which are not
included in the minimum manning staffing levels. This grant also provides the department the
ability to conduct several sobriety check points throughout the campaign period. As in the past,
there is no guarantee on a specific dollar amount awarded to each municipality. The town has
been awarded $73,350.00 in Federal Funds which accounts for 75% of the total funding for the
16/17 FY. The towns portion of the grant is $24,450.00 total funding value $97,800.00. With the
Click-It or Ticket campaign and the DUI grant, reimbursement deadline dates are not every
calendar quarter. This appropriation will help maintain a positive balance in the Grant-OT
account.

for approval.
