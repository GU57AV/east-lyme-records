TOWN OF EAST LYME
BOARD OF SELECTMEN
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
REGARDING ADOPTION OF A BLIGHT ORDINANCE

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme
Board of Selectmen at 7:00 p.m. on March 2, 2016 at the East Lyme Town Hall,
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to consider the adoption of a
Blight Ordinance. Said Ordinance would prohibit any owner, occupant, agent,
tenant and/or person in control of real property located in the Town of East Lyme
from allowing, creating, maintaining or causing to be created or maintained blight
on the premises.

Copies of the full text of the proposed Ordinance are on file for public
inspection at the office of the East Lyme Town Clerk, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue,

Niantic, Connecticut and at http://www.eltownhall.com/ to which reference may
be had. Interested persons are invited to attend and be heard.

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, on this 17" day of February, 2016.
