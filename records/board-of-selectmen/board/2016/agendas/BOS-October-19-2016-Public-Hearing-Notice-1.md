TOWN OF EAST LYME
BOARD OF SELECTMEN
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
REGARDING AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING
A BOARD OF POLICE COMMISSIONERS

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme
Board of Selectmen at_7 p.m. on_QOe7v /9 zw __, 2016 at the East Lyme
Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to consider the
adoption of an Ordinance Establishing a Police Commission. Proposed
Ordinance provides that the Board of Police Commissioners would have all the
powers and duties given by the Connecticut General Statutes for the general
management and supervision of an organized Police Department of the Town of
East Lyme, and also provides for the transition from a constabulary to an
organized municipal Police Department.

Copies of the full text of the proposed Ordinance are on file for public
inspection at the office of the East Lyme Town Clerk, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue,
Niantic, Connecticut and at http://www.eltownhall.com/ to which reference may
be had. Interested persons are invited to attend and be heard.

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, on this S day of TORE, 2016.
