2b. Tax Abatement Ordinance

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson reported that per the Tax Collector, if a volunteer has back taxes they will
not be eligible for this abatement; each volunteer must be in good standing in order to participate in this
program. Attorney O’Connell stated that this is an administrative procedure performed by the Tax
Collector per Title 12 of the Connecticut General Statutes, so it does not need to be spelled out in the
ordinance. Mrs. Hardy noted that there could be an estimated 30 volunteers taking advantage of this
abatement, and asked for a timeline for budgeting. Mr. Nickerson stated that the program will start on
September 1, 2018 for the first year, with volunteers being able to take advantage of the abatement after
August 31, 2019 when their eligibility can be determined. The Tax Collector will then have plenty of time
to make the adjustments to his budget based on the number of eligible volunteers and their tax liability is
determined. Mr. Nickerson thanked all of the volunteers in our town and stated that they provide a valuable
service to our residents. Mrs. Hardy complimented the Chiefs on their recent efforts in advertising to
recruit volunteers; she stated that she thinks they are doing a great job.

MOTION (5)

Mr. Seery MOVED to adopt the ordinance entitled “Ordinance Establishing A Tax Abatement Program for
Certain Emergency Services Volunteers”, as submitted.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 6-0.

2di. Parking Ordinance

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that he is supportive of this ordinance, but thinks that the fine is too
low and should be $50.00. Mr. Dagle asked if that is too much from the $5.00 it is currently. It was
reported that the State fine for parking violations is $92.00, so $50 is still lower than the average but high
enough so as to encourage parking in the beach lots instead of the street. Although other Town’s ordinances
are more detailed, Attorney O’Connell stated that reference to the CGS is adequate unless the town will
deviate from the statute. It was discussed that towing fees should be included in the ordinance, and
Attorney O’Connell stated that he will look into it. A hearing officer will have to be appointed by the First
Selectman, and it was suggested that we consider appointing two people so there will always be someone
available. It was decided to continue this matter to a future meeting to allow the town attorney time to look
into some things and make some adjustments.
