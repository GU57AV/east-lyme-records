3a. Review and possible action - MOU for Regional Animal Control Facility

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson reported that this memorandum of understanding between the four towns is
simply an agreement to open discussions and begin looking for funding. All in attendance were in
agreement that this is necessary for many reasons.

MOTION (4)

WHEREAS, Connecticut General Statute §7-148cc authorizes two or more municipalities to jointly perform
any function that each municipality may perform separately under any provisions of the general statutes or
any special act, charter, or home rule ordinance upon entering into an interlocal agreement to such effect
with other municipalities;

WHEREAS, C.G.S §7-148cc requires that any such agreement be approved by each participating
municipality in the same manner as ordinances are approved or, if no ordinances are approved, in the same
manner as the budget is approved;

WHEREAS, the Towns of Waterford, East Lyme and Montville (“Towns”) require a new Animal Control
Facility, and the City of New London (“City”) is willing to consider a regional expansion of its existing
facility;

WHEREAS, the Chief Executives of all three Towns and the City have discussed regionalizing these
services;

WHEREAS, an expanded regional animal facility that will serve the four communities has been proposed to
be built on City of New London property at Bates Woods Park, located at 80 Chester Street;

WHEREAS, the municipalities recognize that regionalizing services may result in mutual cost savings,
along with mutual assistance at such a facility;

WHEREAS, the municipalities seek to enhance existing regional animal control services and facilities by
all City and Town personnel;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants herein contained, the parties
agree as follows:

PURPOSE OF THIS AGREEMENT

That the Towns and City shall begin the process of obtaining funding and seeking other requisite approvals
from the respective Towns and City, and to enter into an interlocal agreement for a regional animal shelter,
which agreement shall address capital expense and expanded construction of the facility, on-going expenses
of the facility, personnel, and other such provisions at the location listed above. No financial commitment
is required of any Town or City until the interlocal agreement is approved by the requisite authorities in that
Town or City.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 6-0.

3c. Discussion and possible action — Addition of Dispatcher

DISCUSSION: Sgt. Macek reported that they currently have 4.5 full time employees, but that it is
necessary to make that part time dispatcher a fifth full time position. This will bring our dispatch
