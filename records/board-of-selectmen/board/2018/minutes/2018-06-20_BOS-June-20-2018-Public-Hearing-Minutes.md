EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
PUBLIC HEARING OF JUNE 20, 2018
MINUTES

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Marc Salerno, Rose Ann Hardy, Daniel Cunningham
and Paul Dagle.

Mr. Nickerson called the Public Hearing to consider the adoption of an Ordinance entitled
“Ordinance Establishing A Tax Abatement Program for Certain Emergency Services
Volunteers” and an Ordinance entitled “Ordinance Amending Ordinance Concerning Smith-
Harris House Commission” to order at 7:31 p.m., and he reported that the Pledge of Allegiance
was done at the Town Meeting.

Mr. Nickerson stated that he caused to have published the Notice of Public Hearing in The Day
on June 9, 2018.

Mr. Nickerson opened the public hearing to consider the Ordinance Establishing A Tax
Abatement Program for Certain Emergency Services Volunteers. Present to speak on this matter
was Chief Stephen Wargo, Niantic Fire Department, and Chief Bill Rix, Flanders Fire
Department. They explained that this is a way to help them recruit new volunteers in, as well as
to provide incentive to keep our existing volunteers. If a person is on the payroll, they will not
qualify for this program. In order to eligible for this abatement, a person much qualify; and the
qualification process goes through a point system, then the Board of Directors, then finally the
Chiefs, so it is very thorough. Mrs. Hardy inquired as to the approximate number of people that
may take advantage of this abatement, and both Chiefs stated about a dozen, so they anticipate
around 24 people total.

With no one else to speak on this matter, Mr. Nickerson closed the public hearing regarding the
Ordinance entitled “Ordinance Establishing A Tax Abatement Program for Certain Emergency
Services Volunteers” at 7:33 p.m.

MOTION (1)

Motion by Mr. Seery, Second by Mr. Salerno, to close the public hearing regarding the
Ordinance entitled “Ordinance Establishing A Tax Abatement Program for Certain Emergency
Services Volunteers”. Motion passed 6-0.

At 7:34 p.m., Mr. Nickerson opened up the public hearing regarding the Ordinance Amending
Ordinance Concerning Smith-Harris House Commission.

Speaking on behalf of this subject was Gary Lakowski, 12 Methodist Street and member of the
Smith-Harris House Commission. He stated that the new name will give the public a better idea
of what the house does as a museum, and it also incorporates farming, which was a big part of
our community. He reported that there is a great display at the Smith-Harris House, provided by
Historic Properties and that the public should stop in and check it out.
