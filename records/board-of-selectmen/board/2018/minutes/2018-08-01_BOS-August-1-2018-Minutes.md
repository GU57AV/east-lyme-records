EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
REGULAR MEETING OF AUGUST 1, 2018
MINUTES

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Rose Ann Hardy, Mare Salerno, Dan Cunningham and Paul
Dagle.

Also present was Finance Director Anna Johnson, Police Chief Mike Finkelstein, and Inland Wetlands
Officer Gary Goeschel.

Mr. Nickerson called the meeting to order at 7:24 p.m. and stated that the Pledge of Allegiance had been
done at the beginning of the public hearing.

le. Delegations

Ms. Holly Cheeseman, 16 Mitchell Drive, Niantic, and State Representative for East Lyme and
Salem. She reported that there is not a lot going on right now up in Hartford, and that she is expecting that
the Governor may call them into special session to review sports betting. She will report back on the
progress of the study to put in electronic tolls on the highways. She reported that the zero carbon RFP has
been revised to permit Dominion to bid, and that this is very good news for the region and for the state.
Thank you to Paul Formica and all those that fought to get Dominion eligible.

ld. Approval of Minutes

MOTION (1)

Mr. Seery MOVED to approve the Board of Selectmen Regular Meeting Minutes of July 11, 2018, as
submitted.

Seconded by Mrs. Hardy. In Favor; Mrs. Hardy, Mr. Seery, Mr. Nickerson, Mr. Cunningham and Mr.
Dagle. Abstained; Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 5-0-1.

le. Consent Calendar

MOTION (2)

Mr. Seery MOVED to approve the Consent Calendar for the meeting of August 1, 2018, in the amount of
$4,259.55.

Seconded by Mr. Dagle. Motion passed 6-0.

2a. Review and possible action — proposed ordinances

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated creating a municipal ordinance to cover handicap parking was
considered, and it was decided that utilizing the state statute would be the best option for our Town.
Overnight parking in the event of a snow emergency is addressed in Section 5 entitled Snow Emergencies; a
person will be allowed to park overnight but must move the vehicle within a certain amount of time or risk
being towed and ticketed. He thanked everyone who spoke at the public hearing on this matter. Mr. Seery
stated that the ordinance should be amended as discussed by adding the comma after the word
“intersection” in Section 1g. Mr. Cunningham noted that painting the curbs might also be a good idea.
Chief Finkelstein thanked the Board of Selectmen for moving forward with this ordinance as this will give
our officers another option if necessary.

3a. Approve Transfer - Building Maintenance Custodians

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that the town only has three custodians and one back up for all town
buildings, and one of them is going out on medical leave shortly and then will be retiring in January of
2019. In the meantime, the plan is to move the backup person into that position to cover the medical leave
while we find a permanent replacement to start in January. The backup custodian will start full time on

3b. Approve Special Appropriation - NSEP Grant

DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein stated that these funds are part of a yearly nuclear safety grant that the
EOC received, and will be used to purchase a laptop for the field and other essential supplies. He
confirmed that everything they intend to purchase is approved under the grant and will be moved back to
the appropriate account once the grant money is received. Mr. Dagle inquired if the grant has already been
approved by the State, and the Chief indicated that it has been approved they are just waiting to receive the
money but need the equipment now.

725-224-006 (NSEP Equipment) and revenue account 01-04-400-401 (NSEP Grants) to cover the cost of
purchasing computer equipment and office furniture for the EOC, and forward to the Board of Finance for
action.

3c. Authorize First Selectman to sign the CRCASS Agreement

DISCUSSION: Mr. Goeschel explained that this agreement has already previously been approved by both
the Board of Selectmen and the Board of Finance in November and December of 2017. The town attorney
has been reviewing the agreement, as directed by the Town, and has made updates and has given final go
head to enter into this agreement and move forward with the project. Mr. Goeschel stated that the project
has to be completed by the end of the year, so they will be moving forward quickly. Mr. Nickerson stated
that we need to be mindful of keeping the public informed of what is going on especially since the process
will be expedited; he suggested extra notices in the paper, on the signpost, etc.

4a. Harbor Management Plan / Ordinance

Mr. Nickerson stated that the Board received the draft plan, draft plan with markups, and the proposed

4b. Letter of Appreciation from Superintendent of Schools

Mr. Nickerson stated that he is very pleased and proud with the level of communication between our
department heads. When we all work together for the greater good of our Town and residents, everyone
wins. Having the Superintendent of Schools send a letter of appreciation to the Chief of Police
acknowledging the positive and dedicated work of the schools resource officer and the overall support and
collaboration between the schools and the police department is a testament to our department heads working
together for the better good of the residents.

5; Information and Reports
There were none.

6a. Mr. Dagle reported that Inland Wetlands closed the public hearing for the Green Valley project, and
that they are still deliberating. For Harbor Management, there is talk of a new application being put in for
area 5 between the marinas and route 156 and he will report back when and if there is something to report.

Mr. Cunningham reported that the Shellfish Commission had a joint meeting with the Waterford
Harbor Management Commission to discuss shellfish activities in the river. Discussion included topics
such as how water quality would be improved with a shellfish farm in place, and how the hardware
associated with such a farm might impede recreation on the river. | Brookside Farm Museum met and
concentrated on the search for a new curator to replace Joanie DiMartino, who has recently resigned. Plans
and preparations are underway for this year’s Bike & BBQ event, scheduled to take place on September
29": the event is getting bigger each year and is shaping up to be another fun event.

Mr. Salerno reported that Planning had two special meetings recently; the Walnut Hill 3-lot
subdivision matter has been closed, and the Twin Lakes matter remains open and has been continued. They
have received a couple of zoning referrals that they are working on. They have filled the vacant full
member position on the commission; now they have one alternate position that remains vacant and needs to
be filled. Regarding the Building Committee, the schools project is moving along nicely with some small
change orders being submitted.

Mrs. Hardy reported that Costco is the main issue being discussed at the next Zoning meeting; she
noted that this application and project has already been approved and further discussion will be for
proposed changes to the original plan.

Dominion, were fantastic; although not as easy to see now that the trees at McCook’s have grown. There

Shell is also running their show for the season through August 5", Neil Simon’s Last of the Red Hot
Lovers; Mr. Nickerson stated thal he has a walk on part in this show also. Summer music is in full swing at
