3a. Approve the Unaffiliated Employees Salary/Benefits Package

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson recused himself from this discussion as it directly affects him. Mr. Seery
stated that the unaffiliated group is the small group of employees that are not covered by a union contract
and benefits. Mr. Seery reported that he and Mrs. Hardy met with the unaffiliated group’s representative
and discussed the following: each employee will receive a 2% salary increase. He noted that previously an
employee might be eligible for both a GWI and a STEP increase, but this year each of these employees will
take one or the other to give everyone an equal 2% increase across the board. Also, currently the Town
pays 80% to the employees HSA; this will adjust to a 75% / 20% contribution. There will also be a 1%
increase in premiums to the employees in question.

MOTION (7)

Mr. Seery MOVED to approve the Unaffiliated Employees Salary/Benefits Package, as presented.
Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 4-0.

3d. Discuss Smith Harris House and functions with alcoholic beverages

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that this discussion has come before the Board on a couple
of previous occasions, and it is once again time to consider the viability of allowing alcohol at
certain events. In today’s tight budget times, this may be a very good source of revenue for this
historic property and should be looked at again. He stated that the Town and the House itself have
received many requests to rent the barn but most have declined due to the no alcohol rule. Mrs.
Hardy has been and remains in opposition of allowing alcohol at any town event. Ms. Maggi
Prokop, 52 Lovers Lane and commission member of the Smith Harris House, addressed the Board
and stated that she understands Mrs. Hardy’s concerns, but assured the Board that there is a way to
properly hold these kinds of events and secure the proper insurance coverage to protect the Town.
Also in attendance to support this was commission member Bob Seifel. The general consensus of
the Board of Selectmen was that it is worth looking into further, and it was decided that more
research would be done on how other venues such as Rocky Neck State Park deal with such
events, and also that we would contact CIRMA to inquire about insurance needs. A more detailed
packet of information will be compiled and ready to discuss at the next Board of Selectman
meeting. Mr. Nickerson thanked the Smith Harris House members for their continued support and
keep up the good work.
