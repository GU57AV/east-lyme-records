3a. Animal Control Officer Interlocal Agreement

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that this agreement will bear no financial burden to East Lyme
taxpayers in respect to any construction costs; Waterford is holding the $212,800.00 that has been collected

3b. Non-Emergency Interagency Agreement

DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reported that this type of cooperative policing will apply only to the
specific officers that have been appointed to the joint narcotics task force, and the specific officers that
make up this narcotics task force will be cross sworn in through all the three towns. Town attorneys from
all three towns have reviewed this agreement thoroughly. The focus of the task force is specific to narcotics
and will not allow these officers full enforcement of all laws.

3d. Agreement between the Town and the East Lyme Ambulance Association to add two overnight fire
fighters.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that there is a critical need for having a full time, paid fire fighter
during the overnight shifts, and that they have been discussing this possibility for quite some time. Flanders
Fire Chief Bill Rix stated that the East Lyme Ambulance Association has agreed to pay the salary for these
fire fighters, and the Town will pay for the benefits, for a three year period starting July 1, 2019. After 3
years, for year four and five, the Town will pay the salary and benefits of one of the fire fighters and the
Ambulance Association will pay for the second fire fighter. The Ambulance Association has agreed to pay
for both salary and benefits for these two fire fighters from January 1, 2019 through June 30, 2019, pending
the signed contract.

3e. Senior Citizen tax relief

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that some taxpayers have asked him to look into a tax relief program
for senior citizens in town. Mrs. Hardy reported that this subject has been discussed numerous times
throughout the years but that no action has been taken. It was agreed that this matter would be researched
and discussed again at a future meeting.

3f. Appointment to Commission

DISCUSSION: Mrs. Hardy stated that she met with Ms. Ostfeld to discuss this appointment, and that she
full endorses Ms. Ostfeld’s appointment to the Inland Wetlands Agency. Ms. Ostfeld is a 2006 graduate of
East Lyme High School, and a Visiting Assistant Professor of Environmental Studies at Wesleyan
University. Mrs. Hardy is pleased to see former students, especially recent graduates, getting involved and
giving their time to serve the community.

4b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson reported that he has been appointed the Vice President of the CT Council of
Governments (COG). He has recently sat in on a State DOT meeting to discuss several projects. One of
the projects is the project to re-build the bridge over an unnamed brook at Lovers Lane and Heritage. This
is going to be a major project that will impact traffic and travel for an extended period of time, but the
bridge needs repair and has to be done sooner than later. The bridge on Route 156 around the East Lyme /
Old Lyme line over the 4 Mile River will be re-built. It will be raised five feet and be arched top resolve
the flooding issues on that river and in that area. There is a concern about the amount of time that the
roadway is proposed to be closed, so the DOT is going to look at the plan and the options again and will be
back with suggestions. The Niantic River Bridge is scheduled to be repaired and painted in 2019. We are
negotiating with the DOT on how much of the Cini Park parking lot that they will be closing off during
construction, but it is clear that the bridge will be closed for a period of this construction. The Town has
been gifted three Friendship Benches that were built and decorated by our local Girl Scout Troop; be on the
lookout for these benches downtown in the spring. These benches will be placed in our community to
inspire kindness and friendship. The open house at the police department on Main Street was well attended.
There is going to be an open discussion for current board and commission members on January 10", and
then a second public forum at ELHS on January 15". The annual Breakfast with Santa and EL Rowing
Association cookie sale was last weekend and once again a fun and successful event. The upcoming
legislative session is about to begin, and he has been attending more CCM meetings in preparation. Please
note the court ruling on the Water and Sewer capacity hearing for Landmark. The Water and Sewer
Commission will be looking closely at our water capacity and what we have available. The 30" annual
light parade was outstanding and a huge success with thousands of people in attendance. Happy New Year
everyone!
