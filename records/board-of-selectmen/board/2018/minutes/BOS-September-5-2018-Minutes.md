3c. Purchasing Plan

Mr. Salerno stated that he has been working on this plan with Dan Cunningham, Anne Santoro, Bill Weber
and Peter DeRosa. Ms. Lisa Picarazzi, Vice Chairman of the Board of Finance, addressed the Board and
recommended that they consider adding a paragraph regarding a Consideration of local bidder option, and
suggested that we might want to review and take wording from Wethersfield’s plan. Ms. Santoro stated that
she does think it is a good idea but feels that we should really consider and take the time to get it right
before adding it into the plan. She reminded everyone that this is a good starting point and since it is a plan
and not an ordinance, we can modify it if needed. Regarding RFPs, the current threshold is $50,000 and he
feels reducing it to $25,000 may affect workflow and cause for an additional engineer to be on staff to deal
with the RFPs; Mr. Seery also expressed concern with the threshold change, but stated that we will be able
to make changes to the plan and policy if needed. It was agreed that the first paragraph of Section 2 would
be removed, as well as the first sentence of Section 1, and Mr. Nickerson stated that the plan would be
updated and that the Board will review it at the next meeting.

6a. Mr. Salerno reported that he attended the Town Building Committee meeting and main focus was
the schools project which is going well. Planning approved a 2 lot subdivision on Walnut Hill, and
continued the public hearing for Spring Rock Road awaiting Inland Wetlands decision. As a reminder to
the public, service dogs are always allowed on the Boardwalk. He also wanted to thank the Police
Department for doing a great job at getting information out to our residents regarding the recent kidnapping
scam,

Mr. Seery reported that Parks and Recreation reviewed summer information including revenues,
parking stats, etc.

Mr. Cunningham reported that the Historic Properties Commission is working diligently to catalog
and organize all of their historic items into an online database. The Bike Hike & BBQ is going to be held
on Saturday, September 29".

Mr. Dagle reported that the Inland Wetlands Agency is working on a 23 lot subdivision on Twin
Valley Lakes Road, as well as the second phase, 120 residential units. The Harbor Management
Commission submitted three names to the Governor for him to appoint one of them as Harbor Master; the
commission has endorsed Mr. Ron Johnson for this position. The Board of Education applied for and has
received a $20,000 grant to be used over a two year period, $10,000 per year, for programs to education our
students on suicide prevention.

6b. Mr. Nickerson reported that all three schools that are under construction are open and the students
are doing great. Construction is moving right along under safe conditions for our children. He attended the
Senior Center’s annual pig roast and had a great time. Miracle League held a waffle ball tournament to
raise funds and that went very well and a lot of fun. They will be holding their Gala fundraiser at Mohegan
Sun in November. They have raised just under $200,000 to date. He reported that the plans for Costco are
well underway and they are working hard within our departments to get all of the permits and approvals that
