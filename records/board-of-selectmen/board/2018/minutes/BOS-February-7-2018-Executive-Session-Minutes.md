EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
SPECIAL MEETING OF FEBRUARY 7, 2018
MINUTES

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Mare Salerno, Rose Ann Hardy, Dan Cunningham and

Also present was Finance Director Anna Johnson
Mr. Nickerson called the special meeting to order at 7:05 p.m.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Seery moved to enter into executive session for the purpose of discussing personnel matters.

Finance Director Anna Johnson to join them. They returned at 7:48 p.m. and Mr. Seery stated that
no formal votes were taken.

MOTION (2)

Mr. Seery moved to adjourn the February 7, 2018, special meeting of the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 7:48 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 6-0.
