EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
SPECIAL MEETING OF FEBRUARY 7, 2018
MINUTES

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Mare Salerno, Rose Ann Hardy, Dan Cunningham and
