EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
SPECIAL MEETING OF JUNE 20, 2018
MINUTES

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Marc Salerno, Rose Ann Hardy, Dan Cunningham
and Paul Dagle.

Mr. Nickerson called the meeting to order at 6:46 p.m., and he stated that the Pledge of
Allegiance will be performed at the start of the town meeting.

Executive Session

MOTION (1)

Motion by Mr. Seery, Second by Mr. Dagle to enter into Executive Session for the purpose of
discussing labor contracts. Motion passed 6-0.

They entered at 6:47 p.m. They returned at 6:58 p.m. and Mr. Seery reported that no formal votes
were taken.

MOTION (2)

Mr. Seery moved to adjourn the June 20, 2018 special meeting of the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 6:58 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Dagle. Motion passed 6-0.

Respectfully submitted,
