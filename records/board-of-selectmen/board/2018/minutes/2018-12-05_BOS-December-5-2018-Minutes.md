2a. Fishing Dock Leases

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson reported that seven applications were received for the available fishing
docks at Cini Park. It was noted that the current leases expire on December 31, 2018. He explained that
there are currently four docks, one of which is locked in for a number of years and will be renewed
automatically; and another that will not be re-leased at the moment due to upcoming work scheduled to be
performed by the DOT on the bridge. This particular dock’s lease will be addressed upon completion of the
DOT’s project. Mr. Nickerson recommended that he and two more Board members meet to review the
applications; Mr. Dagle and Mr. Cunningham volunteered.

3a. Presentation from Land Use regarding the Climate Adaptation & Sustainability Study
DISCUSSION: Mr. Goeschel briefly explained what the Climate Adaptation & Sustainability Study is, and
then he introduced Mr. Nels Nelson, Stantec, and Mr. Alex Felson, Ecopolitan Design. They reviewed the
Outcome Report of the Coastal Resilience, Climate Adaptation, and Sustainability Study and presented a
slide show to the Board of Selectmen. These documents can be reviewed by visiting the Planning Office at
East Lyme Town Hall. Mr. Mulholland stated that the Zoning Commission recommends this study and that
they have started advising clients of the changes recommended in this study and that they are working with
the DEEP to work toward these standards. He reported that Victor Benni has already been working on an
outreach program with residents in which they receive a discount on their homeowner’s insurance for
participating. Another way to begin getting the word out to residents is to have some information posted on
our webpage. Regarding future funding; although these policies are not currently the industry standard,
they will be over time and the best course of action would be for the Town to be proactive with
homeowners and policies so that when the time comes everything will be in place and we will be ready to
apply for any funding that might become available. Mr. Cunningham inquired about what to do about the
pumping stations that will be in the adjusted flood zones, and it was reported that in Boston they are
experimenting with building reinforced storm walls around the structures and raising the power up above
the flood line, so there are options that will need to be explored. Regarding funding, it was reported that
there should be funding available for municipalities; however, funding for individual homeowners may not
be available. There are have been opportunities for homeowners that band together as a group to possibly

4a. Mr. Cunningham reported that he attended the Board of Education meeting and they discussed
possibly moving the Coastal Connections program to Central Office to save some money and for efficiency.
The elementary schools project is going well some of the completed areas already have kids in the new
classrooms.

4b. Mr. Nickerson reported that a public forum is scheduled for Monday, December 10" at 7pm at the
East Lyme High School auditorium to present the proposed public safety complex project to the public.
The task force has been working very hard to compile information and answers to many questions that the
public has brought forth. If anyone has any questions regarding this proposal, please contact the First
Selectman directly, or submit your questions to questions@eltownhall.com. All questions will be answered
on the webpage and at upcoming public forums. A public tour of the current police department is being
arranged and will be announced at the forum on the 10". Important to note is that this current building has
been properly maintained throughout the years but there are too many issues to make that property work as
a long term solution. Mrs. Hardy inquired as to why the matter is coming up on the December 6" Zoning
Commission agenda, prior to the public forum. Mr. Nickerson stated that it is important to get approval in
advance from such commissions; if approval cannot be granted, then the Town would not pursue the
property any further.

Mr. Nickerson stated that the annual Holiday Stroll was a successful event and well attended; great
job by the Niantic Main Street Group in organizing another great event for our community and business
owners. The annual Jingle Bell 5k will be held this Saturday with 8:30 registration and a 10am race start.
This annual event is for a great cause, the Brian T. Dagle Foundation, and will be well attended again this
year with 600 registrations already received, Saturday night is the annual Light Parade; this is the 30" year
of this event and will begin this year with fireworks donated by Dominion at 6:00pm. On Sunday, the East
Lyme Police Department will host its annual Stuff-A-Cruiser event from 1pm to 4pm; please bring your
new, unwrapped toys to the Police Department on Main Street to bring some joy to those in need this
holiday season. On Sunday night there will be a banquet to induct the fourth group of people into the East
Lyme Sports Hall of Fame; please see the Parks & Recreation Department for tickets to this event.
