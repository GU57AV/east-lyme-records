TOWN OF EAST LYME
BOARD OF SELECTMEN
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
REGARDING ADOPTION OF A PARKING ORDINANCE
AND
AN ORDINANCE TO REPEAL THE ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING FINES FOR ILLEGAL
PARKING

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 7:00 p.m. on August 1, 2018 at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania
Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to consider the adoption of the following Ordinances:

PARKING ORDINANCE

Section 1. General Parking Restrictions.
No vehicle shall be parking in the following manner on a highway or a municipal parking
lot in the Town of East Lyme:

1. Upon a Highway:

a. Within ten feet (10’) of any fire hydrant.

b. Parallel and adjacent to a vehicle already parked in a particular area.

c. Upon any public sidewalk except when in the process of crossing said
sidewalk or when necessary to perform sidewalk construction, maintenance or snow
removal or when other emergency circumstances require.

d. In such manner as to obstruct or interfere with the ingress or egress from
a private driveway or alleyway, except with the permission of the owner of such
private driveway or alleyway.

e. Upon the traveled portion of any highway except upon the right-hand side
of such highway in the direction in which such vehicle is headed.

f. With the right hand wheels further than twelve inches (12”) from the curb
or edge of the highway.

g. Within twenty-five (25’) of any intersection marked crosswalk or stop sign.

h. Ata bus stop, taxi-stand, loading and unloading zones except vehicles
performing the intended purpose in those designated areas.

i. In such manner as to obstruct the flow of traffic.

j. In violation of any sign posted by the Traffic Authority of the Town of East
Lyme or the State of Connecticut which prohibits, limits or regulates the parking of
vehicles within the Town of East Lyme.

2. Ina Municipal Lot:
a. In violation of any restrictions posted by order of the Board of Selectmen

b. In such manner as to obstruct or interfere with the ingress or egress from

3. For purposes of this ordinance, the term “Highway” includes any state or public
highway, road, avenue, street, alley, driveway, parkway or thoroughfare under
the jurisdiction of the Town of East Lyme. “Municipal Lot” shall mean any area
under the jurisdiction of the Town of East Lyme that is dedicated to and posted
for the parking of motor vehicles.

Section 2. Enforcement & Penalties.

A. Enforcement — Any officer, or other designated representative of the East Lyme
Police Department, may attach to any vehicle found in violation of any of the
regulations set forth in the foregoing section, a notice to the owner or operator
thereof that such vehicle has been parked unlawfully, identifying which prohibited
act(s) have been violated and the penalties for such violation(s), and that the owner
or operator shall pay the penalty for such violation, by appearing in person at East
Lyme Police Headquarters or by mailing such notice, with the amount of the penalty,
to the East Lyme Police Department.

B. Penalties — The penalty for any violation shall be fifty dollars ($50.00). If the
designated fine is not paid within 14 calendar days, said fine shall double in amount.

Section 3. Collection & Disbursement of Funds Received as Penalties.

The East Lyme Police Department (“Department”) is authorized to accept payment of
the penalties from any person found to be in violation of this ordinance. The Department
shall deposit such payments into an account designated by the Board of Selectmen.
The Department shall keep a record of all such penalties paid to it for such violations
and the registration information of all such vehicles found to be in violation and present
the same to the Board of Police Commissioners as requested by the Commission. Such
report shall be kept for record in the Town Hall in East Lyme.

Section 4. Appeals Process.

The town hereby adopts the provisions of section 7-152b of the General Statutes, as it
may be amended from time to time, which section establishes a hearing procedure for
parking violations as more particularly set forth in said statute.

Section 5. Snow Emergencies.

Any vehicle parked on a highway within the Town during, or within twelve hours after, a
snowfall of three inches or more shall be subject to being removed and towed. Such
removal shall be at the risk and sole expense of the owner of the vehicle.

Section 6. Towing.

In addition to penalties described herein, an East Lyme police officer may order any
vehicle which is parked so as to impede traffic or to obstruct snow removal operations to
be towed from the location in which it is parked to a suitable location at the owner's or
operator's expense. The procedure for such removal is set forth in Connecticut General
Statutes Sections 14-145 and 14-150.

The foregoing towing charges are in addition to, and not in lieu of, any penalties for
parking violations imposed on the owner of the impounded vehicle pursuant to this
ordinance.

Section 7. Effective Date.

This Ordinance shall become effective ten (10) days after publication of notice of its
adoption.

ORDINANCE TO REPEAL THE ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING FINES FOR ILLEGAL
PARKING

The Ordinance entitled “Ordinance Establishing Fines For Illegal Parking” is hereby repealed
and is of no further force and effect.

This Ordinance shall become effective ten (10) days after publication of notice of its adoption.

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, on this 11" day of July, 2018.
