2b. Authorization for First Selectman to Sign Historical Preservation Technical Assistance

Ms. Luane Lange noted that East Lyme is a CLG and therefore we have access to federal grants

performed a Phase I Archeological study on the Samuel Smith property. Now with these two
grants, they are suggesting that a Phase II study be performed and one grant will match the other
so no town money will be needed.

5b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson introduced and welcomed Selectman Elect Dan Cunningham.

Mr. Nickerson commented that Eversource Energy had announced that our prices will drop and be 24%
less than last year — which is very good news. He attended the school meeting this evening which
presented the school plan to the public. He suggested everyone either go to a meeting, contact the
school for a hard copy of the presentation of go online to view the presentation. He added that there
will be more public sessions held. He congratulated the Youth football team for a great year and
commended all those who came out in support. He noted he would be attending the football banquet
on Sunday. He mentioned the “Roar for Maddie” signs which are in support of young Maddie Guarraia
on display all over town and stressed how proud he was to be a part of the Town of East Lyme. He
attended a Public Trust meeting and added that they will be contributing money to the band stand at
McCook’s. He said that the Boardwalk is 90% finished and we will be done paving cement in a few
weeks. He pointed out the original painting donated by Society for Savings and permanently hung in the
upper meeting room and thanked the bank for their donation. He commented that he had an interview
the day after the election with a second grader from Niantic Center School. He noted that her Dad was
in the Navy and said how not only the servicemen and vets make sacrifices for our country — so do all
the family members. He offered his peace and prayers to our brothers and sisters in Paris.

Ms. Cheeseman wished everyone a safe and blessed Thanksgiving . She noted that on November 28",
there will be our annual Holiday Stroll and the lighting of the tree and encouraged all to attend.
