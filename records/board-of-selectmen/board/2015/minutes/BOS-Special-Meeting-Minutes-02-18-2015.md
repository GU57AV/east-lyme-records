1, CALLTO ORDER
Mr. Nickerson called the Special Meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

2. DISCUSSIONS OF THE FOLLOWING BUDGETS:

- NIANTIC FIRE DEPARTMENT

John McDonald, Chief of the Niantic Fire Department presented the budget. He stated the Fuel Budget
Line covers both buildings. The First Selectman’s will be sending a request to the Ambulance Association
asking them to purchase diesel fuel for the Fire Departments. That number may have to be re-visited.

Mr. Nickerson stated he sent an email today, and a formal letter will follow.

Mr. McDonald stated all of their fuel is diesel except for the brush truck and the Chief’s car. There isa
decrease in radio maintenance because last year they needed new batteries.

Ms. Hardy asked about the OSHA required testing that was decreased.

Mr. Nickerson stated he would like both departments to do their testing on the same day to lower the
cost of the testing. He asked Mr. McDonald to work with Mr. Levandoski on getting a quote for the
required testing to see if the $13,000 per department would cover it. He hopes to find savings on this
and have them treated as one department with their testing.

1
Mr. McDonald stated there should be some savings if they do them all together. Mostly all of their
testing is done annually.

Ms. Hardy asked if there have been any OSHA deficiencies that needing correction in the past.
Mr. McDonald stated they haven’t had an OSHA inspection.
Mr. Nickerson asked if when the testing was done last year if there were any deficiencies.
Mr. McDonald stated no, everything passed.
Ms. Cheeseman asked if the cellular phones are bundled with the rest of the Town.
Mr. McDonald stated they were.
- FLANDERS FIRE DEPARTMENT
James Levandoski, Chief of the Flanders Fire Department presented the budget.

Mr. Seery asked about the increase in small equipment, is that an increase because of the purchase of
new radios.

Mr. Levandoski stated it is for a radio that would be on the same frequency as Waterford for calls for Aid
to Waterford. Currently they cannot use the one they have for that purpose. When they go toa
Waterford call it doesn’t work. This new radio would work.

Mr. McDonald stated their radio is sometimes successful if they have to go to Waterford, but they do
not go to Waterford as much as Flanders does.

Mr. Levandoski stated they would like to catch up with the times.

Mr. Nickerson asked why the fuel for Flanders was the same as the fuel for Niantic, who has two
buildings.

Mr. Levandoski stated the $3000.00 has been there the last three or four years. He also stated that he
felt there could be some savings if all of the testing was done the same day as Niantic.

- EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT
Richard Morris, Emergency Management Director presented the budget.

Ms. Hardy asked about the full time dispatcher position that the Board of Selectmen had authorized last
year.

Mr. Morris stated they are filling the full time position with part time workers.

Ms. Hardy asked if that is efficient.
Mr. Morris stated it is adequate and they are well trained. He asked for another position to fill vacation
and leave time.

Mr. Nickerson stated it makes business sense to fill the full time position with part time employees.
- POLICE
Sergeant Wilfred Blanchette of the East Lyme Police Department presented the budget.

Mr. Nickerson stated in the Governor’s budget today there was talk about the Resident Trooper
Program becoming full funded by the Towns.

Anna Johnson stated they revised the salary amount to $1,496,425.00 because the roster has one less
employee on it than originally calculated.

Mr. Blanchette stated the radios are 15 or 16 years old. Two of them they can’t even get parts for. Two
new vehicles have the new State radios in them. There will be a drop dead date to change all of the
radios over. All new radios are compatible with the State system.

3. ADJOURNMENT
Motion (1) Mr. Seery moved to adjourn the Special Meeting at 6:55 p.m.
Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman.
Motion Passed 5-0.
