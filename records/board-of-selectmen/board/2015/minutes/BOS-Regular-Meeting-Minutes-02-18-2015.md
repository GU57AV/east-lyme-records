C. REVIEW VARIOUS DEPARTMENT BUDGETS
The Board discussed which departments they want to review at their next budget review meeting. They
will review Public Works, Parks and Recreation/Youth Services, Building, General Government, Harbor

Management, and Planning at their meeting on February 25°.

Ms. Hardy stated there was an issue at the animal shelter and the pipes froze. We have to decide what
we are going to do with the shelter, it is sub-standard.

Mr. Nickerson agreed. There is ongoing discussion regarding the shelter. The process is slow, but he
agrees that there is an immediate need to do something with our present shelter.

Ms. Cheeseman stated the fundraisers aren’t happy with the location potentially being in Montville.

Mr. Nickerson stated there is an obvious need for a better facility. The money has been raised without a
specific plan. This is important and what happened with the pipes may push this to a higher priority.

Ms. Hardy asked that Mr. Nickerson write a letter to the Governor regarding the Resident State Trooper
issue. This could be disastrous for local budgets to have to fund it 100%.

Mr. Seery stated there was a study that said East Lyme was ready for its own department, but the cost is
always the concern.

Mr. Nickerson stated he will write the letter, he would like this discussed more at the next meeting and
he would like to convene a meeting of the sub-committee for this and for the Police Manual to be
reviewed. We cannot go backwards in service.

Ms. Hardy stated the restructuring in the Senior Center turns out to be a wash this year.

Mr. Nickerson stated that is an ever expanding department.

The Board discussed the health insurance budget, workers compensation and unemployment.

Mr. Nickerson stated they are getting bids on workers compensation coverage.

Mr. Nickerson stated the March 4" meeting will end with a budget.

A. EX-OFFICIO
Ms. Cheeseman stated she attended a Zoning Commission meeting. There was a public hearing on

B. FIRST SELECTMAN’S REPORT

Mr. Nickerson stated there will be a fundraiser at the High School on February 20" for Madeleine
Guarraia. The community is coming together with a lot of support. He attended a meeting for groups
that are trying to put a band shell at McCooks. He attended a SCCOG meeting this morning, and a SEAT
Board of Directors meeting. He was in court in Bridgeport on Friday and the lawsuit is officially closed.
It was a long chapter. He met at Lawrence and Memorial regarding their paramedics group. He met
with CIRMA. He also met with the Towns that share the Probate Court. All of the towns are satisfied
and are looking to renew as they are coming to the end of the Agreement. There was a water main
break today at the Gateway site in their pump house. It spilled a lot of water and washed out their
service road. The Towns Wetlands Officer was on the scene, and Mr. Mulholland and Mr. Morris. There
was an armed robbery on Saturday at Citizens Bank and there was an arrest yesterday. He is very proud
of all of the people who work with us and for us. He saw firefighters performing cold water training in
frigid temperatures. He is very grateful for them.
