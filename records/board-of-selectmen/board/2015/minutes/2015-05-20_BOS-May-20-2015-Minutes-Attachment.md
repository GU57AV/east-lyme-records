New England |
| Forestry Foundation

To: Board of Selectmen, East Lyme, Connecticut
Date: May 18, 2015
Subject: Niantic River Headwaters Land Conservation Project Proposal

The New England Forestry Foundation (NEFF) proposes to purchase the approximately 166-acre parcel
of land located off Goldfinch Terrace and owned by KSK Associates LLC. The purchase would involve
utilizing $500,000 granted by the State of Connecticut, which requires matching, $350,000 from the
Town of East Lyme, and additional funds that NEFF would be responsible for raising.

The land would be protected permanently by a conservation easement held jointly by
Connecticut DEEP and the town. The conservation easement would include a permanent trail easement
and public access to the property. NEFF would own the property and would manage it for water supply
protection, wildlife habitat, public recreation, and sustainably grown, "green certified" forest products.
The Town of East Lyme would have no management responsibilities for the Niantic River Headwaters
parcel after acquisition. It is critical for the Board of Selectmen to vote in favor of advancing this
proposal to the Finance Committee at its May 20th meeting in order to avoid the reallocation or
termination of the state grant.

Purchase price: $1,225,000
Revenue: $500,000 State DEEP grant
350,000 Town of East Lyme
375,000 Bargain sale and fundraising by NEFF
$1,225,000

Options for Town funding:
* Special appropriation from Open Space Fund 59
* Bonding at approximately 2.5%: 20-year amortization [median home expense = $1.73/year]
