Town, with exception of those of a capital nature over $50,000 where bids are required; and further that

presented at each regular meeting of the Board for review.

Selectman may delegate the obligation to sign the orders of the Board of Selectmen to Holly Cheeseman
for payment of the expenses of the Town.

Mr. Nickerson noted that on behalf of the entire Board of Selectmen, he wanted to thank all those who

2g. Ex-Officio Assignments

Mr. Nickerson noted that this item would be tabled until the next meeting.5a. Ex-Officio

Mr. Seery noted that he chaired the Water and Sewer Commission in Mr. Nickerson’s absence. He added
that a fire hydrant was installed on Cardinal Road and commended Chief Levandoski on his explanation
on the need and how a hydrant would assist the neighborhood. He also attended the Parks and
Recreation Commission meeting and noted that they were reviewing rates for the upcoming season.

Ms. Hardy stated that the site manager position for the meal site was again vacant. She suggested that
she and Mr. Nickerson meet with TVCCA and Ms. Wilson to find out what is going on and how to fix the
problem. She added that for many seniors, this is their only outing.

Mr. Salerno attended the Inland Wetlands meeting and they reviewed their 0 increase budget. He also
attended Harbor Management's meeting where Docko presented the plan for docks at Cini Park. He
said that this item was continued until January. He noted that this has been an excellent year for
scallops in the Niantic River. He said that Steve Dinsmore was again elected Chair and Mr. Hitchery
Secretary and Don Landers Treasurer.

Ms. Cheeseman noted that the First Selectman had attended the Zoning meeting on her behalf.

5b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson commented on the great inaugural ceremony last week and congratulated everyone. He
stated that our Resident State Trooper Bill Blanchette was moving on to another position with the State
and that he will be sorely missed. He attended a CCM Legislative meeting where initiatives for towns
were discussed. He added that there was a meeting hosted by the CT Siting Council for the proposed
cell tower off of Boston Post Road on Plum Lane. He said that he had attended the Change of Command
at the sub base. He said that he attended the Jingle Bell Run Suicide Prevention Run in memory of Brian
Dagle. He said that approximately 400 people had been signed up and actually 750 people ran the 5K
race. He noted how great the recent Light Parade was. He attended a health care seminar where they
reviewed the state of health care in America. He also attended the Save the River fund raiser with State
Representative Ed Jutila and Senator Formica. He attended the Zoning meeting where they approved
the temporary road for Costco and he has been reviewing final designs.

He noted that he opened more of the Boardwalk to the public and he has heard wonderful comments.
He said they are still finishing up the railings and there will be a grand opening in the spring.

He offered his wish that everyone have a Merry Christmas and if you celebrate another holiday, he
wishes you a Happy Holiday and Happy New Year.

Ms. Hardy suggested that Mr. Nickerson send a letter to Sgt Blanchette on behalf of the Board of
Selectmen.

East Lyme Board of Selectmen 12/16/15 minutes

Mr. Seery noted that there is no better friend of supervisor than Bill Blanchette and he will be sorely
missed. He added he will remain a very close friend.

Ms. Cheeseman noted that there will be a joint event with the Parks and Recreation Department and
the Children’s Museum starting at noon on New Year’s Eve. There will be a balloon drop, a family photo
booth and Rolling Tomato will be supplying the food. There will also be face painting and all the events
will be at the Community Center.

Mr. Cunningham thanked the Board for their warm welcome and said he looked forward to working
with them in the future.
