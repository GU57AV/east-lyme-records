EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
SPECIAL MEETING OF FEBRUARY 4, 2015
MINUTES
PRESENT: Mark C. Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Rose Ann Hardy, Holly Cheeseman, Marc Salerno.

CALL TO ORDER
Mr. Nickerson called the special meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Seery moved to enter into Executive Session for the purpose of discussing:
-A. Pending Litigation — Candels vs. Town of East Lyme
-B. Potential Purchase of Real Estate 224 Main Street
and to invite Finance Director Anna Johnson and Attorney Tallberg to attend as appropriate.

Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman. Motion passed 5-0.

Mr. Wilson entered the meeting at 7:12 p.m.
The Board returned to regular session at 7:27 p.m. with no action taken during Executive Session.

Mr. Nickerson adjourned the February 4, 2015 Special Meeting of the Board of Selectmen at 7:28 p.m.

FILED IN EAST LYME
