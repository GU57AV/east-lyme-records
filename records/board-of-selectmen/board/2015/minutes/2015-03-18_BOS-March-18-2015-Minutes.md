2a. Special Appropriation $1811 - Grant from DEEP Watershed Mgt. Program — Hole in the Wall
Mr. Nickerson said that it is anticipated that the $1,811 Grant will be awarded to the Town of
East Lyme to purchase plantings and provide upgrades to the detention basins associated with
the Outdoor Stormwater Classroom at the Hole-in-the-Wall parking lot. Long Island Sound (LIS)
is considered CT’s largest and most important natural resource; providing breeding & nesting
areas for a variety of plants & animals. Untreated stormwater runoff creates water quality
problems for LIS. When it rains, pollutants are carried to LIS after stormwater passes over
impervious surfaces such as rooftops & pavement; carrying accumulated greases, oil, salts,
fertilizers & pesticides that can damage & kill plants & wildlife. The proposed upgrades &
plantings at the detention basins will improve the aesthetics of the basins, while contributing to
improved water quality prior to stormwater being discharged to Long Island Sound. The
remaining project cost of $ 3,453.75 is based on an estimated “matching” contribution from
Town staff. The matching contribution is based on labor time from Town employees to prepare
the detention basins for the plantings and install the plantings; there will be NO in-kind funds
that the Town of East Lyme allocates for this program.

MOTION (3)

Ms. Cheeseman moved to approve a special appropriation in the amount of $1,811 grant from
the CT DEEP Watershed / NPS Management Program — Section 319, to an account to be
established titled, (DEEP NPS 319 - Stormwater Classroom) to provide support for the purchase
plantings and upgrades to the detention basins associated with the Outdoor Stormwater
Classroom at the Hole-in-the-Wall parking lot and forward to the Board of Finance for approval.

Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 4-0.

5b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson noted that he had attended meetings for the Connecticut Council of
Municipalities and Council of Governments. He said that legislative bills that were in front of
the legislature were discussed along with the status of the State budget. He met with 24 French
students for an exchange program pot luck dinner and declared them unofficial East Lyme
citizens. He delivered the Selectmen’s budget to the Board of Finance and they have started
their review. He toured LB Haynes School and noted that the Board of Education’s plan is to
close this school and give it back to the Town with a suggestion that it be used as a new town
hall site. He said that he was amazed at the size of the building and what great shape it was in.
He added that more discussion would be needed on this topic and promised “more to come.”
