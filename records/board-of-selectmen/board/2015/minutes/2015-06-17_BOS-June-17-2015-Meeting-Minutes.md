2b. Special Appropriation $20,600 — Grant CT OPM install fiber optic

Mr. Nickerson noted that a $20,600 Grant from OPM was awarded to the Town of East Lyme to
tie into the State of Connecticut high speed CEN network to promote high speed internet
connections and to facilitate inter connectivity between other Town and State agencies. This
will allow the Town Hall to have internet access with speeds up to 1 gigabyte per second. The
current Town hall internet speeds are around 3 megabytes per second. This slow speed limits
that ability of the Town hall to perform our everyday work in an efficient manner. This high
speed data connection will also allow the Town the opportunity to utilize hosted exchange
services, remote data backup, and many other services that are only possible though a high
speed data connection. This grant includes funding for the installation a full 20 year lease. The
only reoccurring cost is $50 per month maintenance fee and the nutmeg network fees, which
are comparable to the internet fees we are currently paying.

Application to the First Selectman of East Lyme for 5 Permits under the Mass Gathering
Ordinance to allow White Gate Farm to host the 2015 “Dinners at the Farm”

Now in its ninth year, “Dinners at the Farm” has been operated by Chef Jonathan Rapp,
owner of River Tavern in Chester. These dinners showcase local produce and meat
grown on local farms including Scotts, Four Mile River Farm, Sankows Beaverbrook Farm,
Smiths Acres, and White Gate Farm, and fish harvested from local waters. The dinners
are very popular and have been instrumental in boosting public awareness of healthy
local eating as well as the importance of preserving land and supporting the farms that
grow food. Beneficiaries of the dinners include CT Farmland Trust and Working Lands
Alliance as well as groups that provide healthy local food to underserved populations.
White Gate Farm donates the land for these dinners. Up to 220 people attend each dinner
under a tent in the field.

White Gate Farm is a certified organic farm in East Lyme and offers to the community

produce, eggs, chickens and turkeys, sold through their farm store, at the Niantic
Farmers Market and to area restaurants and markets. The farm has hosted Dinners at

the Farm for eight years.

Exhibit definitions are as follows:

Incurred losses - for open claims, it’s the sum of each claim’s losses paid to date plus current
loss reserve. In the case of a closed claims, losses paid through the date of closure.

Developed losses (WC only) - for each year’s total incurred losses, we apply a loss development
factor that reflects our actuary’s (Deloitte) opinion on how that year’s total claims will develop
to its ultimate loss value.

Expenses - these are CIRMA’s expenses as allocated to each individual member that includes
reinsurance, unallocated loss adjustment expense, risk control expense, other overhead
expense and any other insurance charges.

Premiums - for Workers’ Compensation, the sum of the CIRMA premium plus the collection of the
state’s Second Injury Fund (2.75%) and for Liability-Auto- Property, the CIRMA premium (no
premium assessments collected for LAP).
