Mr. Cunningham recognized Ed Waido as a longtime employee and supporter of our
community; he read a Town-issued proclamation into record, attached hereto as Exhibit #1.

Firefighters Association Hall of Fame. we are all very proud of you, Ed waido!

community.

2b.     Liberty Green Deed Restrictions
DISCUSSION: Mr, Cunningham reported that they had opportunity to review the Deed for
Liberty Green and discovered that there is a sentence in there that states "this document will
expire after six months". As it is universally agreed that the deed for Liberty Green was
meant to be permanent with no expiration date. Mr. Cunningham will work with the Town
Attorney to start the necessary process to make this change in order to preserve the intent
the family intended by placing the original deed.
