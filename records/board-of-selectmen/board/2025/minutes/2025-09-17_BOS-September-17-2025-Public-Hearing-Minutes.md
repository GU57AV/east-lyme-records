                              PUBLIC HEARING OF SEPTEMBER 17, 2025
                                     .      MINUTES


PRESENT: Board of Selectmen Members Dan Cunningham, Ann Cicchiello, Rose Ann Hardy, Candice
Carlson, Jason Deeble and Don MacKenzie

Mr. Cunningham opened the Public Hearing at 7:00 p.m. and read the Public Hearing Notice into
record.

Mr, Cunningham explained that when the initial building fees were passed, some of the information
got switched before being filed; this amendment will correct the fees as approved. As there were
no members of the public present to speak on this matter, Mr. Cunningham called for a motion to
adjourn.


MOTTON (1)
Motion by Mr. Deeble to close the Public Hearing and adjourn at 7:02 p.m
Seconded by Ms. Carlson, Motion passed 6-0.
