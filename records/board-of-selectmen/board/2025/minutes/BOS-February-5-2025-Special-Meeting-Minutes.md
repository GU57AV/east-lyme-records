From the Director
                          During Fiscal Year zoz4 (July zoz3-June zoz4) we were busy planning a
                          robust future for the East Lyme Public Library. We completed a Community
                          Center feasibility study in late 2023, providing three possible options for
                          renovating and/or expanding the library.During the spring a committee of
                          staff and board members undertook an extensive strategic planning process,
                          resulting in a document to guide us for the next five years. Public input for
                          the strategic plan was enthusiastic, with just under 4oo surveys completed.
                          Patrons told us that they really like our current services, but would like the
                          library to do more in terms of providing meeting spaces, services for young
                          adults, intergenerational programming, and access to more cutting*edge
                          technology. The final results for the feasibility study can be found at
                          ea st I ym e p u b I i cl i b ra ry. o rglf ea s i b i I i ty - stu dy.
                            The strategic plan can be found at: eastlymepubliclibrary.org/strategic-plan.
                            The surge in use of the children's library that we experienced in the summer
of zoz3 continued this past summer as we offered significantly more children's programs in response
to last year's surge.
Our new conference room continues to be popular hosting a wide range of activities, from lcelandic
language lessons to printing posters. We've begun using a new database tool to analyze the town's
collection use so we can be more efficient with book purchases.
The East Lyme Archives received a large donation of local diaries, pictures and other items from the
early zoth century, as well as a land document from t686.
This fall we staged a large ComicCon-like program for all ages. This event helped us reach young
people and provided the type of intergenerational connection our strategic plan describes. Our new

The East Lyme History Collection and Archive (ELHCA) is a collaboration between the East Lyme Public Library and
the East Lyme Historical Society. ELHCA collects and preserves materials that document the history of East Lyme,
Connecticut. ELHCA provides public access to the local history archives, through programs, events, exhibits, and
research requests.
The emphasis of the collection is historical, providing information about the Town of East Lyme and includes books,
periodicals, diaries, yearbooks, maps, scrapbooks, ledgers, manuscripts, photographs, correspondence, papers,
records of local organizations and government, and more. Subject areas include, but are not limited to: businesses,
prominent individuals, events, local families, schools, churches, and shared history with other local towns.
The past year has been very busy for the ELHCA. We received numerous donations, including local family papers,
documents, and photos. We have received digital donations of family photos and materials relating to past library
Programs. Throughout the year a wide range of research requests have come in, ranging from questions about
locations around town, house histories, and people. We received a grant from the Connecticut League of Historic
Organizations to create digital finding aids for five of our collections. When completed the finding aids will be
searchable online at Connecticut Collections.
Last Octoberwe debuted our local history-related programs with two cemetery tours to the Old Stone Church Burial
Grounds. This October we will hold two cemetery walking tours along with our first meeting of the Local History
Book Club, for which we will read local history books and engage with materials relating to the book. The ELHCA
has created rotating exhibits of local history materials from our collection alongside loaned materials in the glass
display case at the front of the library. The most popular ones have been East Lyme's Frozen History, Gardening,
and Back to School. Stay tuned for the upcoming programs and displays!
lf you have items you would like to donate, or have us make a digital copy of, please contact us at
el localhistory@ely.lioninc.org.
