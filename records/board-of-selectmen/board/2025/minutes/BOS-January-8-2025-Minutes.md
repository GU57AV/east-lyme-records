3a.     Niantic Main Street Regarding Dominion Building on Main Street
DISCUSSION: Dan Walsh, President of Niantic Main Street, along with other members,
reported that they have been working with Dominion to explore turning the former police
department building into a cultural center. They have confirmed eligibility of receipt of a
$200,000 Brownfield Grant to do an assessment of the property; they will need the Board's
backing before going any further. Mr. Cunningham and the Board are fully supportive of
Niantic Main Street moving forward with the Brownfield Grant application. Mr. Cunningham
asked for clarity on whether Dominion will authorize a Phase II study, which is where the
process has stopped previously. Mr. Cunningham stated that he will reach out to Dominion
to open up this discussion; but in the meantime, Niantic has permission to move forward
with the grant application.
