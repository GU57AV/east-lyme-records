of Allegiance. He stated that there are no public comments or delegations at

   . Budget presentation is attached hereto as Exhibit #1.
   . They currently have a waitlist, booking into September, and frequently have

   . Providing a service to meet the needs of the community will require two full-
     time, benefited positions; a social worker and a case manager.
   . There are limited funds available through the Opioid Settlement, but that is
     only a temporary solution.
   . They currently share a shed on the property with the Parks and Recreation
     department, but a shed of their own would allow them to move things around
     and create more office space.
   . Services are available to Salem residents as well; Ms. Firmin is scheduled to
      meet with the First Selectman of Salem to discuss cost sharing-for said
