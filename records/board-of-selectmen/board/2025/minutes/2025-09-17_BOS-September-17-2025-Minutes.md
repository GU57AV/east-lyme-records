lc.    Delegations

Mr. Eric Bauman, Chairman of the Board of Education, appealed to the Board of Selectman to get
the Police Department to do the following things: 1) appoint a uniformed officer as soon as possible,
tomorrow if possible, as the interim SRO; and 2) bring on board a second SRO. He stated that the
former SRO, Don Hull, left that position to take the role of Director of Security for the schools.

Mr. Don Hull, Director of Security for the East Lyme School System, stated that he enjoyed his role
as SRO, but could not pass up the opportunity to oversee security for the schools. Being an SRO, it
is difficult to build trusting relationships with the students, parents and staff, and the longer the
position remains open the more difficult it will be for someone to settle in and build relationships.
He admitted that a second SRO is definitely needed; it is full time job and difficult for only one
person to manage and make effective changes. He noted that our surrounding towns have multiple
SROs, including Waterford who have three. He supports replacing the SRO position ASAP and then
adding a second SRO position.

Mr. Jeff Newton, Superintendent of East Lyme Schools, stated that he has already made contact
with Chief Glenn, and communicated their desire for immediate replacement of the open position,
and then to appoint another officer as a second SRO. He looks forward to further building the
partnership between the schools and the police department.

Ms. Jaime Barr Shelburn, 31 McElaney Drive, Niantic, and Board of Education member, stated that
she is in support of everything that both Mr. Hull and Mr. Newton said, and she supports the
immediate replacement of the former SRO and the addition of a new one, She noted that the SRO
also runs the Challenge Program and builds irreplaceable relationships with students and parents,

Mr. Paul Spakowski, 37 Mill Road, East Lyme, stated that he is extremely disappointed that the
tipping fees went back to being one rate for all for leaf disposal; noting that the Board of Selectman
had previously agreed to offer a lower rate to the local contractors.

2a. East Pattagansett Sidewalk Project Update
Town Engineer Alex Klose presented the final update to the plans for the sidewalk project on East
Pattagansett, which will now go to the District 2 DOT office for review of the proposed signal work at
the intersection with Route 161, He reported that he has addressed very few comments and
concerns from the affected property owners, and that overall, very few properties need an
easement. Once the Town receives approval from the DOT, the project will then go out to bid, He
stated that he has received positive feedback from the public overall, and that completion of this
project will satisfy the POCD as presented by the Planning Commission in 2020.
