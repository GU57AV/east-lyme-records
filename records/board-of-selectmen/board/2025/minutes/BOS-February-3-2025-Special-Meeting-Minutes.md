2,       Review Budgets

109 IT - Dan Cleary and Jason Finnigan were present.
   . They are actively working on obtaining a $100,000 federal grant to upgrade
      and connect our IT infrastructure throughout all town buildings. After the first
      round of $100,000, it is anticipated that there will be an additional $300,000 of
      funding in the second round. This is a fully funded grant.
   . Ongoing project - replace outdated machines that have Windows 10 as their
      operating system, before their end of life in November 2025.
   . Ongoing project - sunset all small office printers and replace them with
      equipment that is covered by the existing service plan. This plan covers the
      large copiers and some of the office printers.
   . Completed project - replaced old phones with one new system for all
      buildings.
  . Ongoing project - realizing the full potential of the Town's GIS (Geographic
      Information System ) application.
  . Equipment Maintenance - annual maintenance for the body cam system will
      revert to full price after enjoying a reduced rate for the first couple of years in
      operation. The new S-year contract is at full price; there are other vendors,
      but they appear to be not reliable as departments are leaving their current
      vendor when the contract is up to move to the Axon system.
  . Working with the Board of Education to see if we can find some ways to
      combine and save,
  . Continue to try to find savings by consolidating services.
2L4 Emergency Management - Julie Wilson, Acting Emergency Management Director
   . Ms. Wilson summarized everything that has been done or is an upcoming
     event for the Emergency Management department, The complete budget
     presentation is attached hereto as Exhibit #1.

Mike Macek, Acting Police Chief, was present to speak on the following budgets:

2t5 - Dispatch
     .   A majority of the increases are due to contractual salary and benefits
         increases.
       Changed the process to now allow the full-time dispatchers right of first
       refusal, then it is open to part-time dispatchers.
   a   Adding one (1) dispatcher would allow forfull-time coverage and reduce the
       number of overtime hours that we see now.
   a   Attrition is high because surrounding towns are paying better with better
       benefits.

2L6 - Police Department
   a  Will increase department by two officers this year,
   a  Currently operate with a three-man minimum per shift; one sergeant and two
      officers; if there is a prisoner in house, that would reduce the shift to two
      officers on patrol as one needs to be with the prisoner,
   a  Goal in the summer is to have four officers on each shift, but that has not
      happened due to absences and retirements.
      Holiday had not previously been accounted for in advance and is now being
      accounted for in the police payroll line, as well as STEP increases.
   a  Parks and Recreation has always paid the PD $5,000 for Celebrate East Lyme
      Day but has not the past couple of years. This needs to be put back in the
      budget.
   a  The Fuel line is down only because the marine patrol boat was out of service
      for most of the summer last year.
      Saving money on prisoner meals by working with Stop and Shop and looking
      for possible ways to share costs regionally.
   a  Would like to add an additional School Resource Officer; maybe the Board of
      Education would cover the cost of a second SRO,

225 - Fire Service - Bill Bundy and Erik Quinn were present.
   o Full-time firefighters work a 24/48 schedule with a Saturday Kelly Day.
   . Two firefighters are always on duty 24/7 at Station 1 NHQ and
      Station 3 Flanders,
   . Currently have 11 full-time firefighters; we should have 18.
   . The plan is to have 18 full-time firefighters on staff by 2028.
   . The Fire Service Annual Report and Budget Presentation are attached hereto
      as Exhibit #2.
