                               EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                              PUBLIC HEARING OF APRIL T6,2025



PRESENT: Board of Selectmen Members Dan Cunningham, Rose Ann Hardy, Ann
Cicchiello, Candice Carlson, Jason Deeble and Don MacKenzie

ALSO IN ATTENDANCE: Public Works Director Joe Bragaw

Mr. Cunningham opened the Public Hearing at 7:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of
Allegiance. Ms. Cicchiello read the Public Hearing Notice into record.

Mr. Bragaw gave a summary of the amendments and the repeal of ordinances
before the Board this evening. Mr. Cunningham asked if anyone from the public
would like to speak on this matter. As no one from the public was present to speak
on this matter, Mr. Cunningham called for a motion to adjourn the public hearing.


MOTTON ( 1)
Motion by Ms. Cicchiello to close the Public Hearing of April L6,2025, at 7:05 p.m
Seconded by Ms. Carlson. Motion passed 6-0.
