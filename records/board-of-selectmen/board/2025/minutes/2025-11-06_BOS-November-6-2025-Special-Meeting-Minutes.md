                               EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                                  SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
                                      NOVEMBER 6,2025


PRESENT: Board of Selectmen Members Dan Cunningham, Rose Ann Hardy, Jason Deeble and
Don MacKenzie
ALSO IN ATTENDANCE: Finance Director Kevin Gervais
EXCUSED: Ann Cicchiello and Candice Carlson

Mr. Cunningham called the special meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen to order at
3:04 p.m. This meeting was held via Zoom.

MOrroN (1)
DISCUSSION: Ms, Hardy noted that the Town has used Vision in the past and has had a good
experience, This award is in accordance with the Town's Purchasing Policy and three (3) bids
were received and the job awarded to Vision Government Solutions, Inc,
Ms. Hardy MOVED the following: RESOLVED, to authorize the First Selectman to execute in the
name of and on behalf of the Town, a contract with Vision Government Solutions, Inc. for
appraisal and revaluation services for all taxable and tax-exempt real property located in the
Town of East Lyme,
Seconded by Mr. MacKenzie. Motion passed 4-0,

MOTTON (2)
Mr. MacKenzie MOVED to adjourn the November 6,2025, special meeting of the Board of
Selectmen at 3:06 p.m,
Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 4-0.
