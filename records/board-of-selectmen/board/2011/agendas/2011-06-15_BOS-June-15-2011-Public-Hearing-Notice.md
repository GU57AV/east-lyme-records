The East Lyme Board of Selectmen will conduct a public hea
June 15, 2011 at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Nianite
Connecticut, commencing at 7:00 p.m. regarding the following proposed amendments
to ordinances:

1. Amendment to the “Scenic Roads Ordinance” to provide for notice by way of a
certificate of mailing to owners of property having frontage on a highway proposed for
designation as a scenic road.

2. Amendment to the “Ordinance Establishing Schedule of Fees” to provide for
an application fee payable to the Zoning Board of Appeals in the amount of $350.00.

3. Amendment to the “Ordinance Concerning Flood Damage Prevention” to
conform to language required by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

4. Amendment to the “Ordinance Prescribing Fees for Building and Demolition
Permits” to add the following provision:

The Chief Building Official, at his discretion, may direct an applicant to hire

the International Code Council or any other consulting firm to perform plan

review for any project. The cost of said review service shall be paid by the

applicant directly to the firm performing the plan review. The cost of this

service shall be deducted from the total fee as per items one and two of

this ordinance.

5. Amendment to the “Peddling Ordinance” to increase license fees to the
following amounts:

a. for each foot peddler, $10.00 per day;

b. for each organization of or company employing peddlers, at the
option of such organization or company in lieu of the license fee
established by Section 4a, $40.00 per week;

c. for each peddler, using a team or vehicle, $45.00 per year.

At this hearing, interested persons shall have the opportunity to be heard and
written comments will be received. Copies of the full text of the foregoing ordinances
proposed to be amended are available for public inspection at the office of the East
