2.a. Parks & Recreation Special Revenue Fund Report
Mr. Formica said that Mr. Putnam was present to give this update.

Dave Putnam, Parks & Recreation Director passed out the report through 1/31/2011. He noted that
while they currently show a deficit of $36,083.02 that it is attributed to two major programs that occur

2.b. Discussion/Action – Transfer of Funds – Purchase of Police Cruisers
Mr. Formica explained that they have the vehicle acquisition program but that some of them did not
come in at that time so they are proposing to take some of the money to purchase two police cruisers
this year and eliminate the need for the capital expense next year. This will work well as the Crown
Victoria is in its last year of production and the new vehicle model that will replace it will need to have
the bugs worked out during its first year in use. This will also allow for some money for the storm
overtime account.

2.e. Introduction / Overview – FY 2011/2012 Budget
Mr. Formica gave a brief overview of the FY2011-2012 Budget noting that he is obligated to present a
budget that makes sense. He said that he has presented the Board of Ed budget as it is. Human
Resources will be reduced to finance an Office Manager at the Field Services Building. The Engineering
department has requested a Civil Engineer II whose cost would be shared with the Water & Sewer
department. Some housekeeping has been done with respect to fees collection. The new Probate
Office is up and running. They have consolidated some items such as the Planning Commission with
the Planning Department. There is an additional $300,000 in revenue from the growth of the grand list.

It was anticipated that the Health Premiums would increase by 12% however they are hearing that it
would be in the 8% - 10% range. They are looking at different methods of funding this. There is
$145,000 in contingency to alleviate having to dip into fund balance.

With regard to the Health District they are looking at creating another through the Southeastern Council
of Governments. Ledge Light proposed to lower their fee from $7.60 per person to $7.41 and have
since noted that if we could stay with them that they could go to $6.85 per person which is $13,000 less.
They will be able to discuss this further at their next meeting.

They have consolidated the buying of Building Maintenance items. There is a $209,000 decrease in
debt service and with the refunding that would further reduce the amount of interest that would be paid.
The State has a $3.5B deficit and he said that he believes that when Mr. Malloy presents his budget
that it will be close to what they can expect to get as he is working with his political party.

The budget presented reflects the maintenance of basic services.
He congratulated the department managers for their hard work and conservation.
