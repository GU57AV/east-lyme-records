                       EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                       SPECIAL MEETING OF MARCH 2, 2011
                                  MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, John Birmingham, Rose Ann Hardy.

EXCUSED: Rob Wilson, Jack Hogan.

Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Nickerson moved to enter into Executive Session for the purpose of discussing
Potential Purchase of Real Estate and Personnel Matters at 6:31 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Birmingham. Motion passed 4-0.

MOTION (2)
Mr. Nickerson moved to return to regular session at 7:07 p.m. with no action taken in
Executive Session.

Seconded by Mr. Birmingham. Motion passed 4-0.

MOTION (3)
Mr. Nickerson moved to adjourn the March 2, 2011 Special Meeting of the East Lyme
Board of Selectmen at 7:08 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Birmingham. Motion passed 4-0.
