                       EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                          MEETING OF APRIL 20, 2011
                                 MINUJTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Rob Wilson, John Birmingham, Jack Hogan.

EXCUSED: Rose Ann Hardy.

Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 7:32 p.m. and led the Pledge.

1b. Additional Agenda & Consent Calendar Items
There were none.

1c. Delegations
There were none.

1d. Approval of Minutes, Meeting of April 6, 2011
MOTION (1)
Mr. Nickerson moved to approve the minutes of the meeting of April 6, 2011 as
submitted.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan. Motion passed 5-0.

1e. Consent Calendar
MOTION (2)
Mr. Nickerson moved to approve the Consent Calendar for the meeting of April 20, 2011
in the amount of $74.21.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 5-0.

2a. Sign annual Town Meeting Call and Set Referendum Hours
Mr. Wilson suggested keeping the same hours as last year’s budget referendum of 8:00
a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

MOTION (3)
Mr. Nickerson moved the following call:

                               TOWN OF EAST LYME

                      NOTICE OF ANNUAL TOWN MEETING

       Notice is hereby given of the Annual Town Meeting to be held on Monday, May
9, 2011 at the East Lyme High School Auditorium at 7:00 p.m. to act on the following:

       1. To review and discuss the budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2011 as
          recommended by the Board of Finance.

       2. In accordance with Chapter 7.3 of the Town Charter, the moderator shall
          adjourn the annual budget meeting to a referendum on the voting machines.

       3. To adopt a Five Year Capital Plan.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan. Motion passed 5-0.

2b. Budget Re-Allocation – Assessor
Mr. Formica noted that in order to complete the 2011 mandated revaluation, an RFP for
five unique properties was sent out. $50,000 had been budgeted as an estimated cost for
these appraisals however; four bids were received, the lowest being $57,000. He said that
there are currently funds available in the current budget account for tax appraisals,
pretrial hearings and court preparation and the $7000 could be taken from this budget
line.

MOTION (5)
Mr. Nickerson moved to approve the re-allocation of $7000 from PO # 10-01461 to be
used for the remaining balance of the appraisals of unique properties and forward to the
Board of Finance for their approval.

Seconded by Mr. Birmingham. Motion passed 5-0.

2c. Discussion – Police Rules and Regulations
Mr. Formica noted that this item would be tabled until next meeting and per the request
of Ms. Hardy, an Executive Session would be held.

2d. Appointments (2) Niantic River Watershed
MOTION (6)
Mr. Nickerson moved to appoint Marvin Schutt of 29 N. Edgewood Road, Niantic, CT to
serve on the Niantic Watershed Board of Directors through January 2, 2013 and John
Jasper of 8 Old Oak Lane, Niantic, CT to serve on the Niantic Watershed Board of
Directors through January 6, 2014.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 5-0.

3a. Discussion – Fee Schedule – Building, Planning and Zoning Ordinance – ZBA

Mr. Formica said that he had met with Building Official Joe Smith and he was in the
process of reviewing his fees, but was not ready to present them to the Board. He
suggested that this item be tabled until next meeting.

5a. Ex-Officio
Mr. Hogan had no report.

Mr. Birmingham attended the Harbor Management meeting where they discussed a
Memorandum of Understanding about shellfishing in the river and bay. He said that there
is a meeting about commercial clamming in the river on Thursday at 7:30 p.m. in
Waterford.

Mr. Nickerson noted that the Youth Services meeting had been cancelled. He said that the
attended the Economic Development Commission meeting and that Zoning Chair Mark
Salerno was present and there was a good discussion about sign regulations and the
village district. He said that no action had been taken at that meeting.

Mr. Wilson said that the Parks and Recreation Commission had all the ball fields
operational. He said that they were in the middle of doing a lot of work trying to clean up
the graffiti on the boardwalk benches and signs.

5b. First Selectman’s Report
Mr. Formica said that May 7th was shredding day at the Commission on Aging.

He said that the budget would be sent to public hearing by the Board of Finance on April
25th and that copies of the budget were available at Town Hall or online.

He had a meeting with the Director of SECHA (Southeastern CT Housing Authority)
along with the Town Planner and Zoning Official regarding the affordable housing we
have in town.

He hosted the monthly department head meeting where he thanked them for their hard
work on the budget and to continue to hold the line on spending.

He said that the Water and Sewer Commission approved service to the Village
development.

He said that the water and sewer budget had changed due to the closure of Gates Prison
and they were just tightening up the figures.

He said he attended a number of meetings with the Board of Finance and the budget
being forwarded to public hearing reflected a reduction in spending from this year. He
again reiterated that the public hearing would be on April 25th. He added that the Annual
Town Meeting would be held at the High School on May 9th and the budget referendum
would be on May 19th at the East Lyme Community Complex from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00
p.m.

Mr. Formica met with the Comptroller regarding the Resident Trooper issue of the fringe
benefits percentage the towns are charged on top of the trooper’s salary.

He attended the High School Environmental Club’s benefit dinner, proceeds going to
Haiti and Japan.

He offered a customer service seminar to the Library staff.

He read a story to a Flanders School kindergarten class.

He said that the Town held their annual health fair and our Human Resource Manager did
a great job.

He attended meetings at the Council of Governments and an Executive Committee
meeting regarding SEAT (Southeastern Area Transit).

He spoke with Parks and Recreation Director Dave Putnam about the trouble they were
having removing the graffiti and added that they may have to paint the benches. He said
that the East Lyme Public Trust was offering a $500 reward.

He added that he met with the Opsail Committee and said that the tall ships and
destroyers were coming to New London in July of 2012 and they had asked us to host
them over the Friday night. He said they were reviewing the change of date of Celebrate
East Lyme to coincide with their visit.

Mr. Nickerson said that he had received notification that the Cooking at McCook’s music
events would again take place starting in mid July for several weeks.

Mr. Formica said that Parks and Recreation will continue their Saturday night music at
the Town Hall pavilion and Pro Tec Auto has their bands on Friday nights noting that
East Lyme will be having a very musical summer.

MOTION (7)
Mr. Wilson moved to adjourn the April 20th meeting of the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 7:58 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan. Motion passed 5-0.



Respectfully submitted,



Darlene C. Stevens, Recording Secretary
