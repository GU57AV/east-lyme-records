EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
Wednesday, JANUARY 19, 2011
IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING THE REGULAR BOARD OF SELECTMEN MEETING

Members In Attendance: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, John Birmingham, Rose Ann Hardy,

Jack Hogan
Invited Attendance: No One
Excused: Rob Wilson

1. Call to Order
First Selectman Paul Formica called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen to order
at 9:09 PM immediately following the previously scheduled Regular Meeting of the Board of Selectmen.

2. Enter Executive Session to Discuss Personnel / Real Estate

**MOTION (1)

Mr. Nickerson moved to enter Executive Session at 9:10 PM for the purposes of discussing personnel
and real estate.

Mr. Hogan seconded the motion.

Vote: 5-0-0. Motion passed.

3. Return to Regular Session

**MOTION (2)

Mr. Nickerson moved to return to Regular Session at 9:50 PM with no action taken in Executive
Session.

Mr. Hogan seconded the motion.

Vote: 5-0-0. Motion passed.

4. Adjournment

*MOTION (3)

Mr. Nickerson moved to adjourn this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at 9:51 PM.
Mr. Hogan seconded the motion.

Vote: 5-0-0. Motion passed.
