                        EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                        SPECIAL MEETING OF MAY 18, 2011
                                   MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Jack Hogan, John Birmingham, Rob Wilson
           Rose Ann Hardy.

   1. Mr. Formica called the Special Meeting to order at 6:30 p.m.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Hogan moved to enter into Executive Session for the purpose of discussing Attorney
/ Client Privileged Communications and to invite Steve Rebelowski and Attorney Grello
to attend and;

Darci Schofield and Kerri Culhane to discuss the potential purchase of real estate.

Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 6-0.

MOTION (2)
Ms. Hardy moved to return to regular session with no action taken during Executive
Session.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan. Motion passed 6-0.

Mr. Formica closed the Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at 7:30
p.m.
