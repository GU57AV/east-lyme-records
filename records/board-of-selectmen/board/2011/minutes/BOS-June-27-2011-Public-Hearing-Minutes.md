                         EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                          PUBLIC HEARING OF JUNE 27, 2011
                                    MINUTES

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, John Birmingham, Jack Hogan, Rob Wilson.

EXCUSED: Paul Formica, Rose Ann Hardy.

Mr. Nickerson called the Public Hearing to order at 5:08 p.m. and led the Pledge.

Mr. Nickerson read the Call for Public Hearing into the record (identified as Attachment
A).

Municipal Utility Engineer Brad Kargl reported that at this point in time, streams were
flowing quite well. He said that we are not presently in a deficit situation and there is no
need to put these restrictions into effect due to the rainfall that we have received. He
asked the Board to consider allowing the First Selectman and he to proceed and when we
have reached a point where they feel it is necessary to institute the restrictions, they can.
He asked for flexibility in the language to accomplish this.

Karen Rak of 27 Black Point Road stated that she was glad to hear Mr. Kargl’s
statements and at this point, it was premature to institute the restrictions due to the rain
we have received. She added that attending a scheduled 5:00 p.m. public hearing is
difficult and some people might be excluded from coming.

Hearing no further comment, Mr. Nickerson suggested closing the public hearing.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Wilson moved to close the Public Hearing at 5:14 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan. Motion passed 4-0.
