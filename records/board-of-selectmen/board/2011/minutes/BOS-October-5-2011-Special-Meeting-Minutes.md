                      EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                     SPECIAL MEETING OF OCTOBER 5, 2011
                                 MIINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, John Birmingham, Rob Wilson.

Mr. Formica called the Special Meeting to order at 7:01 p.m.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Nickerson moved to enter into Executive Session for the purpose of discussing
Pending Litigation and to invite Attorney O’Connell to attend as appropriate.

Seconded by Mr. Birmingham. Motion passed 4-0.

Ms. Hardy entered the meeting at 7:21 p.m.

MOTION (2)
Mr. Nickerson moved to return to regular session at 7:34 p.m. with no action taken during
Executive Session.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 5-0.

Mr. Formica adjourned the Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at 7:35
p.m.
