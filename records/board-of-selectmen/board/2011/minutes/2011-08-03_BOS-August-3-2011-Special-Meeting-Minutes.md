                              EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                                SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
                               Wednesday, AUGUST 3rd, 2011

Members In Attendance:        Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, John Birmingham, Rose Ann Hardy,
                              Robert Wilson

Invited Attendance:           Kristen Wainright, Attorney with Tobin, Carberry of New London

Excused:                      Jack Hogan


1. Call to Order
First Selectman Paul Formica called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen to order
at 6:30 PM.


2. Enter Executive Session for the purpose of discussing Potential Purchase of
   Real Estate

**MOTION (1)
Mr. Nickerson moved to enter Executive Session at 6:34 PM for the purpose of discussing the Potential
Purchase of Real Estate inviting Attorney Kristen Wainright of Tobin Carberry to join them.
Mr. Wilson seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


3. Return to Regular Session
**MOTION (2)
Mr. Wilson moved to return to Regular Session at 6:58 PM with no action taken in Executive Session.
Mr. Birmingham seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


4. Adjournment
**MOTION (3)
Mr. Nickerson moved to adjourn this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at 7:00 PM.
Mr. Wilson seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


Respectfully submitted,



Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary, Pro-tem
