2a. Settlement of Pending Litigation with National Union Insurance Co.
Attorney Mark Block and Atty. Amanda Sisley were present to review this item.

Attorney Block said that this was a proposed settlement concerning litigation against
AIG, now known as National Union Insurance to show that the town was covered by
their insurance for actions taken against the town by New England National. He noted
that this proposal was the outcome of mediation regarding only the insurance company
claim against AIG. The mediated proposal was for AIG to pay the town $275,000 and the
town in return would release AIG from future known and unknown claims from AIG.
East Lyme Board of Selectmen Meeting of June 1, 2011


Attorney Block noted that the settlement was fair and reasonable and in the best interest
of the Town.

MOTION (3)
Mr. Nickerson made the following motion:


       Move that the First Selectman, Paul Formica be authorized to execute a
       Settlement Agreement settling the declaratory judgment action between the Town
       of East Lyme and National Union Insurance Company (formerly A.I.G), upon the
       following terms.

       1. National Union Insurance will pay the sum of $275,000 to the Town.
       2. The Town of East Lyme releases National Union from future liability under
       the policies issued by National Union and further waives its right to seek
       insurance coverage under any of the National Union policies for any known or
       unknown claim by New England National, LLCl and its affiliates.
       3. The Town of East Lyme indemnifies National Union against any claims that
       New England National, LLC or its affiliates may make against National Union
       arising out of the policies issued by National Union. In the event such claim is
       made by New England National, LLC or any of its affiliates against National
       Union, the Town is required to defend the claim and pay any judgment should
       New England National, LLC prevail in such claim.

       The Settlement Agreement is subject to review and approval of the Town’s
counsel prior to execution by the First Selectman.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan. Motion passed 5-0.

5a. Ex-Officio
Mr. Hogan said he attended the Waterford / East Lyme Shellfish meeting where they had
granted permission to test for oyster planting and they were moving forward with a
clamming project. He also attended the Veteran’s Vigil at the Town Green to honor our
veterans and walked in the Memorial Day Parade. He congratulated the girls and boys
lacrosse teams, the baseball team and the girls track team for all winning in their
divisions.

Mr. Wilson noted that the Parks and Recreation meeting was canceled due to lack of a

great.

Mr. Nickerson attended the Alcohol and Substance Abuse Prevention meeting and noted
the guest speakers were very informative. He was also a guest speaker for the Sub School
Graduation and met some of the Legion of Merits recipients.

5b. First Selectman’s Report
Mr. Formica noted that he attended a presentation of the junior class mock crash. He said
that the budget had been passed at referendum, but that we are still waiting on the State
budget and he hoped that the unions vote to accept the concessions. He said that the
Board of Finance met and set the mill rate which will show a slight reduction from last
year. He attended the Dare Graduation and commended Officer Jean Cavanaugh for all
the fine work that she does. He said that the also attended the Alcohol and Substance
Abuse Prevention meeting. He toured the Amtrak bridge area and attended an
informational status meeting where they were told that the project is on target and half
way done. He said the finishing date is set for May of 2013 and Amtrak was doing a great
job. He held a 20 / 20 Facilities meeting and added that they had been waiting for a
feasibility study done regarding the schools. He added that there would be another
meeting next month and then public discussion. He said that the Council of Governments
met and he held a meeting with all the historical committees in town (Historic Society,
Historic Properties and Smith Harris House Commission) to discuss consolidating their
efforts into one entity. He said that the Police negotiations were continuing along with the
Fire Fighters wage re-opener. He attended the Lillie B. Haynes Memorial Day
presentation and the Annual Black Point Beach meeting over the weekend. He noted that
he marched in the parade with Congressman Courtney, Senator Stillman, State Rep.
Jutila. He said that today he attended the Rotary meeting where they honored the student
of the month and met with Attorney General George Jepsen today.
