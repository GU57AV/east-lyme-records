   NOTICE lS HEREBY GIVEN, of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme Board of

   following ordinance entitled :

                ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A
        CANNABIS ESTABLISHMENT WITHIN THE TOWN LIMITS AND PROHIBITING
        SMOKING/VAPING OF CANNABIS ON ALL TOWN OWNED OR CONTROLLED
                     PROPERTY FOR A PERIOD OF SIX MONTHS

   WHEREAS, the Connecticut GeneralAssembly has enacted a law concerning the
   responsible and equitable regulation of adult-use cannabis (hereinafter "S,8. 1201");
   and

   WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 148 of S.B. 1201, a municipality may, by ordinance,
   prohibit the establishment of a Cannabis Establishment;
   and

   WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 84 of S.B. 1 201 , a municipality may prohibit the use or
   consumption of cannabis including smoking and vaping of cannabis on town-owned or
   controlled property; and

   WHEREAS, the Town of East Lyme desires to prohibit, for a period of six (6) months,
   (1) the establishment of a Cannabis Establishment within the limits of the Town and (2)
   the use or consumption of cannabis, including smoking and vaping of cannabis on all
   town owned or controlled property as follows:

                  A. A "Cannabis Establishment" shall mean a producer, dispensary facility,
                     cultivator, micro-cultivator, retailer, hybrid retailer, food and beverage
                     manufacturer, product manufacturer, product packager, delivery service or
                     transporter, as all those terms are defined in S,B. 1201.

                  B. No Cannabis Establishment shall be established or used within the limits of
                     the Town of East Lyme for a period of six (6) months from the date this
                     Ordinance is effective,

                  C. Smoking/vaping of cannabis is prohibited on town owned or controlled
                      property including, but not limited to, sidewalks, parks, beaches, outdoor
                      sections of restaurants, and municipal land and buildings, The fine for a
