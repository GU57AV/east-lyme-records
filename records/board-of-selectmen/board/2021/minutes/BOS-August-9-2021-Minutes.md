                                EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                               SPECIAL MEETTNG OF AUGUST 9,2021
                                           MINUTES


PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Rose Ann Hardy, Paul Dagle and Dan Cunningham
EXCUSED: Marc Salerno

Mr. Nickerson called the meeting to order at2:05 p.m, and led the Pledge of Allegiance

2, Special Appropriation of 5280,557 from the American Rescue Plan Funds
MOTTON (1)
Mr. Dagle MOVED to approve a special appropriation in the amount of $280,557 for the following items with
the source of funds being America Rescue Funds:

$5,129 - WIFI improvements at EL Town Hall
$14,428 - Virtual Meeting Package at EL Town Hall
$132,000 - Microwave Dishes for Emergency Public Safety Management
$44,000 - Security Cameras at various town locations for Public Safety
$75,000 - Clinical Therapist/Counselor for EL Youth Services
$ 10,000 - Shoreline Kitchen and Food Pantry - Regional Refrigerated Truck


And forward to the Board of Finance for approval, Note: Town Meeting is required.
Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 5-0.

Ms. Hardy stated that the foregoing special appropriation was voted on and recommended by the Board of
Selectmen at its meeting on July 7,2021 and sent to the Board of Finance for approval. Because the Board
of Finance choose not to act on the request within 30 days as provided in the Town Charter Section 6.3.4.,
the Board of Selectman have placed it back on its agenda for this special meeting

MOTTON (2)
Mr. Dagle MOVED to adjourn the August 9,2021, special meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at
2:07 p.m.
Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 5-0.



Respectfully Submitted By:
