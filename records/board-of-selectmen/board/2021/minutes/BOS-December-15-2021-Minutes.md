lc. Delegations
        Mr. Mark Christensen, 66 Grassy Hill Road, and member of the Agribusiness Subcommittee
addressed the Board and stated that they seek to promote and preserve agriculture in East Lyme and educate
the public and town officials of the need and value of agriculture in our town, Open space and agriculture
help to protect the watershed which feeds the aquifer from which we get our drinking water, as well as
helps to stabilize the tax base as there is less strain on infrastructure from those properties versus a sub
divided property. Town must do all that is possible to help keep these agriculture businesses flourishing.
Some activities that the Agribusiness subcommittee has been involved in are the approval of new
regulations for keeping poultry and allowing bee keeping. They have been a popular presence at Celebrate
East Lyme each year and have held many Open Farm Days where they highlight the different farms in
town. We have held numerous events at the Library and have created the much-needed East Lyme Giving
Garden, which is now a standalone 501(c) organization. He stated that this subcommittee and its work by
volunteers is a great example of sustainable agriculture, community members working together, and
protecting valuable acreage over our aquifer.
         Mr. Rich Steel, 23 Rose Lane, spoke regarding the zoning approval of the proposed Viking
Firearms facility in the former Bob's Warehouse building. He understands that it met all the criteria of the
zoningregulations so there was no reason to deny it, but he does not agree with such a facility being
allowed in town. He has many concerns that may be addressed by employing an officer at the entrance to
check for underage patrons, as well as if there are any live firearms or ammunition in a person's vehicle
which can then be removed and placed into a lock box while the person is in the facility. He is concerned
that people will want to show off their weapons in the parking lot and may even possibility want to sell
weapons from their vehicle. He would like the town to consider a live feed of the establishment to be
broadcast at our dispatch center. Secondly, he spoke on the matter ofthe proposed gas station and truck
stop off exit 7 5, and he does not support an establishment like this in that area. He feels it is a safety risk, as
well as an environmental risk,

3d. Hathaway Property
DISCUSSION: Mr. Seery reported that the East Lyme Land Trust, Inc, has been approved for a grant of up
to $400,000 for the acquisition of the Pattagansett River Watershed Preserve II, which is attached hereto as
Exhibit l.   Mr. Ron Luich, l3 Enid Lane and President of the East Lyme Land Trust, addressed the Board
and explained that they have spoken with all of the town agencies that would benefit from the purchase of
this land, including the First Selectman, Planning, Inland Wetlands, Parks and Recreation and Natural
Resources, and everyone he has spoken to so far supports the purchase of this property. He noted that there
is also 3,000 feet of waterfront included on this properfy. Mr. Luich stated that they have secured a
temporary loan in order to secure the land, and that they are in the process ofraising funds to pay back that
loan and fully acquire the property, Mr. Steve Harney, representative of Hathaway Farms LLC stated that
he would like to see the Town acquire this land and although they have gotten an appraisal and done their
own title search, the Town will need to do this, and it can be a lengthy process. Mr. Art Carlson addressed
the Board and stated that this piece of property is sitting on top of the aquifer that feeds this entire area and
that also feeds into Well 5, which is the only well in town that contains no iron or manganese and is
currently running at a level much below its capability, The property was cleared early in the 1900s so was
essentialundisturbed by the hurricane of 1938, so the land is thick with healthy trees; there is also a large
parcel of farmland on the properfy. Mr. Seery stated that he has spoken with the town attorneys, and they
recommend moving forward with the properfy owner on discussions to possibly purchase this land.

MOrroN (7)
