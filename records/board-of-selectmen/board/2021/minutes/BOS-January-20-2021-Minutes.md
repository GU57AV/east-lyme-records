4a.      Ex-Officio Reports
         Mr. Salerno reported that the Planning Commission submitted the POCD and it was approved by the
State OPM; the commission will now start meeting with other boards and commissions that may be affected
by the newly adopted POCD. He reported that they approved a four-lot subdivision on Upper Kensington in
the north end of town.
         Mr. Dagle reported that the Harbor Management and Shellfish Commission will be exploring some
potential opportunities for habitat restoration in the Pattagansett Estuary. Also, they created a subcommittee
to review the shellfish management plan, which is about ten years old and needs updating, and they will
request that the Town Attorney review any changes before it goes to the Board of Selectmen for final
approval. Mr. Dagle stated that this commission is awaiting word on the status of the discussions between
East Lyme and Waterford regarding where the town line boundary is in the river. He reported that the Town
Building Committee held a special meeting to approve some contract changes, most notably that they
discovered that the sheetrock needs to be replaced with fire rated sheetrock, which amounts to an approximate
$48,500 change order including some of the ceiling tiles in these areas. He reported that the town received a
$4,000 credit from the contractor because the Clerk of the Works has been doing all of the photographic
documentation on the progress of this project. The project is moving along well and is ahead of schedule.
         Mr, Cunningham reported that WELSCO is curently exploring alternative ways to make the shellfish
permits more accessible through online measures.
         Mr. Seery reported that he and Mr. Cunningham and Mr. Salerno met as the Fire Department
Organization Subcommittee and reviewed the fire study and identified applicable recommendations to focus
on. They also met with the two fire chiefs and their deputies to identif, any concerns they may have within
their departments. They are planning the next meeting to visit each of the firehouses. The Parks and
Recreation Commission met and slightly increased the fees for tent and pavilion rental at McCook's. They
also discussed their desire to take over the Darows Ridge park; they have discussed making this a
recreational area for activities such as disc golf. Mr. Seery reported that they also discussed honoring Charlie
Fenick by naming one of the athletic fields after him and will discuss this further.
