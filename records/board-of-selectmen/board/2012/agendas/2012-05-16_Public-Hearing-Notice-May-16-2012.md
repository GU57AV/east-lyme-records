                               TOWN OF EAST LYME
                              BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                            NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

       NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that, pursuant to Section 7-163e of the Connecticut

General Statutes, the East Lyme Board of Selectmen will hold a public hearing on

Wednesday, May 16, 2012 at 7:00 p.m. at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania

Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut, to consider the following:

       1. Proposed lease of a portion of the building known as the “Old Town Garage”

generally known as 12 Roxbury Road in Niantic, Connecticut to Care and Share, Inc.

       2. Proposed lease of a portion of the building known as the “Upper Garage Area” of

the Parks and Recreation Maintenance Facility generally known as 8 Roxbury Road in

Niantic, Connecticut to the Children’s Museum of Southeastern Connecticut, Inc.

       Copies of the proposed leases are on file at the office of the East Lyme Town Clerk,

to which reference may be had.

       Interested persons are invited to attend and be heard.

        EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMAN


BY:            Paul Formica, First Selectman
