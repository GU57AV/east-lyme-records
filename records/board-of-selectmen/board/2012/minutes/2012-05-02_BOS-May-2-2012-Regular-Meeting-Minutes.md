                       EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                           MEETING OF MAY 2, 2012
                                  MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Rob Wilson, Rose Ann Hardy.

EXCUSED: Holly Cheeseman.

Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m. and led the Pledge.

1b. Additional Agenda & Consent Calendar Items
There were none.

1c. Delegations
Joe Mingo of 397 Boston Post Road named several items that were troubling:
       1. Flanders Post Office – very unsafe – should be shut down.
       2. Right turn on Boston Post Road down Flanders Road should be a dedicated
          right hand turning lane.
       3. Right hand turn lane from Flanders Road to Industrial Park Road should be a
          dedicated right turning lane.
       4. School buses have no business sitting on wetlands and should be moved
          immediately.
       5. There are numerous illegally operating businesses in town.

5a. Ex-Officio
Mr. Wilson said that the Smith Harris Commission is having several barn events and their
June meeting was their annual meeting with the Friends of Smith Harris.

Mr. Seery had no report.

Ms. Hardy attended the Zoning Commission meeting where they approved a new
building from Main Street – the first floor will be commercial and the upper floor will
house apartments. She said they also reviewed the leases for town property and referred
them to Planning and they will be holding a public hearing to prohibit outdoor service
bars. She added that they have received an explosion of outdoor dining requests. She also
attended the Planning Commission meeting and received a report by a committee
member about the Samuel Smith property and Darrow Pond. She added that the
discussion about Darrow Pond centered on the difference between active and passive
recreation.

Mr. Nickerson attended the Flanders 5K race and noted what a great job Dave Putnam
and his staff did.

5b. First Selectman’s Report
Mr. Formica attended the planting of 150 trees by the Leos at Darrow Pond.

He noted that the budget is to be presented on 5/14 to the town and then the meeting will
be adjourned for voting at a referendum on May 24th. He said that the budget reflects a
1.4% increase, which will necessitate an approximate .5 mill increase.

He met with Deputy Commissioner of Public Safety Stebbins regarding regional policing.

He noted that Water and Sewer had a meeting and that the water interconnection project
was 30% complete.

He said that Attorney O’Connell is meeting with the Huber’s on May 11th and then he
would have more information about the Samuel Smith property at the next meeting.
