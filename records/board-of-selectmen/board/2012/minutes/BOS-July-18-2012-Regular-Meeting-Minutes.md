2e. Bonding Resolution - Logger Hill Road Sewer Main
Rehabilitation

Mr. Formica noted that portions of the pipe along logger
Hill are starting to deteriorate. He said the total cost
for engineering and replacement of the sewer pipe is
$1,700,000 and per the agreement we have with Waterford we
have to pay a percentage of that cost. He said the pipe had
been expected to last up to 50 years however it has only
lasted about 20 years. He said he and Attorney O'Connell
met with Waterford and their attorney to negotiate a
reduction of the percentage to be owed by East Lyme. He
said there's a question whether this is a capital ora
maintenance expense. He said they negotiated a 62% payment
which would be $1.2 million. He said they have
approximately that much money in the sewer fund but they do
not want to totally draw that account down. He said they've
been paying depreciation funds to Waterford totaling
$345,000 and they were going to use that money toward the
$1.2 total which would leave $855,000 to fund. He added
that they would be taking $175,000 from the sewer
assessment fund which would leave a balance of $680,000 and
that this amount would be paid by the ratepayers. He said
that there was no provision for Point O Woods to pay for
any the capital items since they are on our owed state
capacity. He stressed that we need we need to sewer
Saunders Point before we bring another towns sewage through
our town. He added that the Water and Sewer Commission is
contractually obligated to perform these repairs.

AQUIFER:

The Samuel Smith House sits atop an aquifer from which the town draws water.
Rainwater percolates down through the porous soil into the aquifer at a slow, fixed rate
and is filtered in the process. The volume of water entering the aquifer is directly
proportional to the porous acreage above it. Porous acreage is reduced by paving or
building; most of the runoff from impervious surfaces ends up in storm drains and
streams that feed into the bay.
