                       EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                           MEETING OF MAY 16, 2012
                                  MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul M. Formica, Mark Nickerson, Holly Cheeseman, Rob Wilson.

EXCUSED: Rose Ann Hardy, Kevin Seery.

Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 7:32 p.m.

1b. Additional Agenda & Consent Calendar Items
There was none.

1c. Delegations
There was none.

1d. Approval of Minutes, Meeting of May 2, 2012
MOTION (1)
Mr. Nickerson moved to approve the minutes for the meeting of May 2, 2012 as
submitted.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson

Aye: Mr. Formica, Mr. Nickerson, Mr. Wilson.
Nay: None.
Abstain: Ms. Cheeseman. Motion passed 3-0-1

1e. Consent Calendar
MOTION (2)
Mr. Nickerson moved to approve the Consent Calendar for the meeting of May 16, 2012
in the amount of $536.08.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 4-0.

2a. Action – Public Hearing re: Leases for Town Property to Care and Share and
Children’s Museum
MOTION (3)
Ms. Cheeseman moved to authorize the First Selectman to enter into a lease with Care
and Share as presented and modified as necessary.

Seconded by Mr. Nickerson.

Mr. Wilson noted that the Planning Commission had performed an 8-24 review on this
request on May 2, 2012 and found it consistent with the Plan of Development.

Motion passed 4-0.

MOTION (4)
Ms. Cheeseman moved to authorize the First Selectman to enter into a lease with the
Children’s Museum as presented and modified as necessary.

Seconded by Mr. Nickerson.

Mr. Wilson noted that the Planning Commission had performed an 8-24 review on April
4, 2012 and found it consistent with the Plan of Development.

Motion passed 4-0.

2b. Appointment – COA – John Whritner
MOTION (5)
Mr. Wilson moved to appoint John Whritner of 4 Blueberry Lane, Niantic, CT to serve
on the Commission on Aging through January 2, 2014.

Seconded by Mr. Nickerson. Motion passed 4-0.

3a. Continued Discussion – Samuel Smith House
Mr. Formica noted that Ms. Hardy was travelling with her students and Mr. Seery was
out of state. He added that we are still awaiting appraisals and have started negotiating
with the owners. He added that this item will be continued on the next Selectmen’s
agenda.

5a. Ex-Officio
Mr. Wilson had no report.

Ms. Cheeseman had no report.

Mr. Nickerson went to the Economic Development Commission meeting. Chair Steve
Santino stepped down and was presented with a plaque by the membership. He added
that he was a great leader and wished him well. He said that Ms. Bengston attended her
first meeting and that they will be rotating the office of Chair for the next few meetings.
He said that they discussed the Visitors Center and the Plan of Development economic
development chapter.

5b. First Selectman’s Report
Mr. Formica said that the regional water connection continues to move forward. He said
that he served as a “mystery reader” at LB. Haynes School.

Plans for Opsail continue to move forward for 7/7 and 7/8 and that the opening ceremony
will be at McCook’s on 7/6 with approximately 10-15 ships in the harbor. He said that we
hope to have a street festival that day, but there will be no street closure.

Celebrate East Lyme will be on July 21st.

6. Public Discussion
Mark Christensen of Grassy Hill Road and Co-Chair of Conservation of Natural
Resources Commission requested that the First Selectman write a letter to CT
Department of Agriculture noting that the Town of East Lyme satisfies the pre-
qualifications for the Community Farms Preservation Grant. He added that the letter had
to be received by May 31st.

Mr. Wilson noted that Thursday evening there will be an Art and Music Expo from 6:00
p.m. – 8:00 p.m. at the High School.

Mr. Nickerson moved to adjourn the May 16, 2012 meeting of the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 7:51 p.m.

Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman. Motion passed 4-0.
