FILED IN EAST LYM
Gym 209 ATE:
EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN :
SPECIAL MEETING OF JULY 24, 2012 ea bey bias
MINUTES EAST LYME TOWN CLERK

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Rose Ann Hardy, Holly Cheeseman, Kevin Seery, Rob
Wilson.

EXCUSED: Paul Formica.
Mr. Nickerson called the special meeting to order at 5:55 p.m.

MOTION (1)

Ms. Hardy moved to enter into Executive Session at 5:56 p.m. for the purpose of
discussing pending litigation - Homes and to invite Attorney McQuade to attend as
appropriate.

Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman. Motion passed 5-0.

MOTION (2)
Ms. Cheeseman moved to return to regular session at 6:13 p.m. with no action taken
during Executive Session.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 5-0.

MOTION (3)
Mr. Seery moved the following:

Does the Town consent to a settlement reached by the Town’s insurer, the State of
CT and Paul Holmes to resolve Mr. Holmes’ lawsuit against the Town, Paul Formica and
retired State Trooper Richard Crooks?

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 5-0.
MOTION (4)
Ms. Cheeseman moved the following:
Shall Paul Formica be authorized to execute papers, on behalf of the Town,
necessary to effectuate such settlement?
Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 5-0.
MOTION (5)
Ms. Cheeseman moved to adjourn the Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of

Selectmen at 6:15 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 5-0.
