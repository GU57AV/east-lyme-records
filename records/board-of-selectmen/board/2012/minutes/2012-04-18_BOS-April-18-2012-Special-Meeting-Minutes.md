                              EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                                SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
                                Wednesday, APRIL18th, 2012

Members In Attendance:        Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Holly Cheeseman, Rose Ann Hardy,
                              Rob Wilson

Invited Attendance:           Attorney Joe McQuade, Attorney Edward O’Connell, Anna Johnson,
                              Finance Director, Gary Goeschel, Planning Director (for Samuel
                              Smith Property discussion only)

Excused:                      Kevin Seery


1. Call to Order
First Selectman Paul Formica called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen to order
at 6:35 PM.


2. Enter Executive Session for the purpose of Discussing: Pending Litigation –
    Holmes vs. Town of East Lyme and - Potential Real Estate Purchase – Samuel
    Smith Property
**MOTION (1)
Mr. Nickerson moved to enter Executive Session at 6:36 PM for the purpose of discussing Pending
Litigation – Holmes vs. Town of East Lyme and the Potential Real Estate Purchase – Samuel Smith
Property inviting Attorney Joe McQuade, Attorney Edward O’Connell and Anna Johnson Finance
Director to join them for the Holmes discussion – and with Attorney McQuade leaving and Gary
Goeschel, Planning Director asked to join them for the Samuel Smith Property discussion only.
Mr. Wilson seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


3. Return to Regular Session
**MOTION (2)
Mr. Wilson moved to return to Regular Session at 7:45 PM with no action taken in Executive Session.
Mr. Nickerson seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


4. Adjournment
**MOTION (3)
Mr. Nickerson moved to adjourn this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at 7:47 PM.
Mr. Wilson seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


Respectfully submitted,



Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary, Pro-tem
