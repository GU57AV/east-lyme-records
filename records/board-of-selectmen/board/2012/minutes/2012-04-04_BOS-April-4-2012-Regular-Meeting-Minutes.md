Ms. Hardy moved the following:

                                   PROCLAMATION

The East Lyme Selectmen recognize Agriculture as defined by Connecticut General
Statutes 1-1(q) benefits the Town in many ways.

Agriculture has economic benefits including jobs, agri-business and efficient tax revenue
from farmland.

Additionally, certain Agriculture related business can bring tourists to spend money in
Town.

Finally, farmland offers scenic vistas for all and can help protect water quality and reduce
water runoff.

Therefore, be it known that the East Lyme Board of Selectmen supports Agriculture in
East Lyme.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 6-0.

3a. Samuel Smith House
Ms. Hardy said that they would be using this fact sheet along with some pictures to
inform the public. She reminded the Board that there are two grants available, one for up
to $200,000 for the purchase of the house and the second grant from DEEP is for open
space with a 50% match ( with the potential of 65%)and a 50% match by the town. She
said that the first grant must be submitted by October. She said that both grants will need
2 appraisals, the first appraisal to be finished – covering both parcels and another
appraisal valuing the both parcels. She said that the grant writer will cost $4700.

Mr. Formica said that he has been playing phone tag up until this week with the owners.
He said that he will contact them and get a set price. He said that this Board made an
appropriation of $400 for a partial appraisal and two full appraisals will cost $1500. He
added he will talk to the Planner and get the appraisals done, noting that they cannot be
dated until after April 28th.

Ms. Hardy noted her concerns with the timeline. The information needs to get out to the
people, Selectmen and Board of Finance have to vote, a public hearing needs to be held
and then a town vote.

Mr. Seery said that he is supportive of the project however to get this item on the ballot
we will need a price and information out and action by both Boards. He said the rush to
