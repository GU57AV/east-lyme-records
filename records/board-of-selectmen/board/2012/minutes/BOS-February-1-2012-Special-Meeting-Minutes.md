                              EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                                SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
                              Wednesday, FEBRUARY 1st, 2012

Members In Attendance:        Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Holly Cheeseman, Rose Ann Hardy,
                              Kevin Seery. Rob Wilson

Invited Attendance:           Attorney Nicholas Grello

Excused:                      No One


1. Call to Order
First Selectman Paul Formica called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen to order
at 6:40 PM.


2. Enter Executive Session for the purpose of Discussing: Police Union Contract
**MOTION (1)
Mr. Seery moved to enter Executive Session at 6:41 PM for the purpose of discussing the Police Union
Contract inviting Attorney Nicholas Grello to join them.
Mr. Nickerson seconded the motion.
Vote: 6 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


3. Return to Regular Session
**MOTION (2)
Mr. Seery moved to return to Regular Session at 7:10 PM with no action taken in Executive Session.
Mr. Nickerson seconded the motion.
Vote: 6 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


4. Adjournment
**MOTION (3)
Mr. Nickerson moved to adjourn this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at 7:11 PM.
Mr. Seery seconded the motion.
Vote: 6 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


Respectfully submitted,



Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary, Pro-tem
