2. NEW BUSINESS
a. Discussion — High School Athletic Complex

Bill Scheer the Town Engineer is present, and is on the Committee overseeing the construction
at the High School

Mr. Formica stated the entrance between the Fire House and the Laundromat is intended for a
stop light. They are going to complete the build out first then do the traffic counts and studies
needed to obtain the light. This will also enable the Fire Department to control the light in an
emergency. Eventually it will serve as an entrance to the Flanders School and the High School.
Eventually they will close the entrance that is presently at the Flanders School.

Mr. Scheer stated the project is moving quickly. November 17° is the anticipated completion
date.

Ms. Hardy stated there were questions raised by the public at delegations at the last meeting.
She would like help from Mr. Scheer to understand the process and once the contract is given
out does it state that the contractor has disposal rights?

Mr. Scheer stated that is correct. The contract outlines the specifications of the job. It does
reflect disposal of items. The Town wanted the top soil taken to the transfer station. Any other
materials became the property of the contractor.

Ms. Hardy asked if when the contract is signed the value of the products that are being
disposed of is determined by the Town.

Mr. Scheer stated the contractor has a value and subtracts accordingly as to items they can
make money on by disposing, they would subtract that value out of their bid. He does not
know how they value them. There would be a base bid and the Town can negotiate the value
of the bid down once they do determine the value of certain items. For example the bleachers
which were made of wood and steel had a certain value to the contractor who would be
disposing of them.

Ms. Hardy asked if a Contractor uses a sub-contractor would the Town see that contract.

Mr. Scheer stated the Town would not see those contracts. He then stated the Contractor gave
the light poles to the Waterford Youth Football League, he does not know what happened to
the Press Box or the scoreboard.

Ms. Hardy stated we should make materials available to town residents.

Mr. Seery stated the visitor bleachers were re-assembled at the East Lyme Youth Football field.

Ms. Hardy stated she sees a need for us to look at our policies and see if we are making
materials reasonably available to our own taxpayers.

b. Disposal of Properties

Mr. Formica stated there is a process as to the disposal of property within the Town. Inventory
of property is up to the respective departments. One benefit of opening the Colton Road

facility is that they can now further control the inventory. Everything has been organized and is
better controlled. He hopes to have a full time manager in that building to manage personnel
and equipment. At the Town Hall everything that is bought goes through his office.

Ms. Hardy asked about filing cabinets and chairs.

Mr. Formica stated if she was to ask him to produce a book of every chair he cannot do that,
but there is a purchase order for everything. He thinks they are in pretty good shape.

Ms. Hardy asked if there was some way to get on the website materials to be disposed of and
make them available to residents.

Mr. Formica stated when they got new copiers they had bidding for copiers and people were
aggrieved that we didn’t advertise, but advertising would have cost more than was made on the

machines.

Ms. Hardy stated if people knew of the availability of items it wouldn’t appear that someone
got something because of insider information. She will propose something for the Board to
consider.
