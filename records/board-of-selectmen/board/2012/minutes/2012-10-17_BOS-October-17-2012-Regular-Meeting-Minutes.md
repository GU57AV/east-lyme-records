2c. Discussion - CCFM Discount Prescription Program

Ms. Gina Calabro an East Lyme resident and the Director of Member Services for CCM
was present to review this program. She said that several towns in the state are turning to
this new discount card program which is designed to help uninsured and under insured
residents to receive discounts on their prescription drugs. She noted that savings average
45% on prescriptions that are not covered by insurance. She said that CCM partnered
with ProAct Inc, a discount card provider that negotiates discounts with participating
pharmacies. She noted that CVS, Rite Aid and Stop & Shop are among the pharmacies
that participate along with Walmart and Target. She said that the discount cards are
mailed to all residents in participating towns, there are no forms to fill out and no
restrictions based on age, income or existing health coverage. She added that this
discount program will also help with vision screenings and lens, hearing aids and Lasik
eye surgery. She noted that the Town must sign an enrollment agreement in order to
participate and then the process would take 5-6 weeks. She said that the response has
been tremendous from the towns already enrolled. She noted that the mailing costs to
participants are picked up by ProAct and this program is funded through the pharmacies.
She added that CCM receives a marketing stipend from ProAct, but this money goes back
into keeping the dues low for towns to be members of CCM.

Ms. Cheeseman asked how long this program has been in existence.

Ms. Calabro noted it launched in July of 2012 however, New Jersey has been running this
program with great success since 2006.

Mr. Wilson commented that it sounded too good to be true.

STATE OF CONNECTICUT

OFFICE OF POLICY AND MANAGEMENT

October 15, 2012

FIVE MUNICIPALITIES REWARDED
FOR PROGRESS IN AFFORDABLE HOUSING

(HARTFORD, CT) - The state Office of Policy and Management announced that five
municipalities will each receive $20,000 grants in recognition of their progress in
adopting “Incentive Housing Zones.”

The “Housing for Economic Growth Program” rewards municipalities that voluntarily
established the zones, which include mixed-income housing.

“Connecticut residents need housing options they can afford,” said OPM Secretary
Ben Barnes. “This program does that while also encouraging ‘smart’ development.
As Connecticut works to spur economic growth, a critical component of that effort is
supporting a diverse mix of housing options for people of all incomes.”

Barnes pointed out that grants can be used by the receiving municipalities for any
purpose.

The five municipalities receiving the grants are: Old Saybrook, Torrington, New
London, East Lyme, and Sharon.
