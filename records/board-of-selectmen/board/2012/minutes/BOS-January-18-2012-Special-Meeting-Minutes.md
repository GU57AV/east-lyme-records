                              EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                                SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
                              Wednesday, JANUARY 18th, 2012

Members In Attendance:        Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Holly Cheeseman, Rose Ann Hardy,
                              Kevin Seery

Invited Attendance:           Attorney Mark Block, Amanda Sisley

Excused:                      Robert Wilson


1. Call to Order
First Selectman Paul Formica called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen to order
at 7:05 PM.


2. Enter Executive Session for the purpose of discussing Pending Litigation –
   New England National

**MOTION (1)
Mr. Nickerson moved to enter Executive Session at 7:06 PM for the purpose of discussing Pending
Litigation – New England National inviting Attorney Mark Block and Amanda Sisley to join them.
Ms. Cheeseman seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


3. Return to Regular Session
**MOTION (2)
Mr. Nickerson moved to return to Regular Session at 7:38 PM with no action taken in Executive
Session.
Ms. Cheeseman seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


4. Adjournment
**MOTION (3)
Mr. Nickerson moved to adjourn this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at 7:39 PM.
Ms. Cheeseman seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


Respectfully submitted,



Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary, Pro-tem
