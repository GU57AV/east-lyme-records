2a. Special Appropriation $8,400 — NFD Hose

Mr. Formica noted that during the 2013/14 proposed budget process, the Niantic Fire
Department requested $8,400 for the purchase of replacement hose which was decreased by the

East Lyme Board of Selectmen 11/7/13 minutes

Board of Finance. The National Fire Protection Association hose standard requires that all hose
manufactured prior to 1987 be taken out of service. Currently, we have 89 lengths of 3 inch hose
manufactured prior to 1987. The 20 lengths of 1-1/2 inch hose also were manufactured prior to
1987. He said that the following was what Niantic Fire Department hoped to do:

Niantic Fire Department Plan: Replace the 20 lengths of 1-1/2 inch hose with color coded 1-
3/4 inch hose. Currently there is 1-1/2 inch hose mixed in with the 1-3/4 inch hose. Color coded
hose will prevent the incorrect line being secured while operating at a fire. Removing the older
1-1/2 inch hose will prevent the different hoses from being mixed together. We will also begin
replacing the older 3 inch hose. The requested funds will allow the department to finish
converting all of the apparatus to color coded hose while replacing the older hose. Our goal is to
purchase 1600 feet of 1-3/4 inch hose and 600 feet of 3 inch hose. All of the new hose will be
used to replace older hose.

MOTION (4)

Mr. Nickerson moved to approve the use of $8,400 from account 4-01-01-120-200-500
(Contingency) for the Niantic Fire Department to purchase 1600 feet of 1-3/4 inch and 600 feet
of 3 inch hose to meet National Fire Protection Association standards being in the best interest of
the Town of East Lyme and forward to the Board of Finance for approval.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson.

Ms. Hardy said that she was upset to see this item on the agenda. She said that during the budget

2d. Special Appropriation - CNRE — Painting — Re-allocated Town Vehicles

Mr. Formica noted that in the fall of 2012 we purchased a new autoloader to replace a 2004
model. At that time we were unable to find suitable disposal of the old truck. Our Highway
Superintendant, Chuck Holyfield worked with the dealer and local trash companies to no avail.
He did not take it to auction for fear of not obtaining a fair price. More recently after purchasing
another autoloader in our 2014 acquisition program, the City of New London approached Mr.
Holyfield regarding worked with the dealer and did some independent research and determined
$35,000 for each unit was fair to the City of New London and the Town of East Lyme. We
received the $70,000 from the City of New London and deposited it into the Capital and Non-
Recurring Fund to reinvest in future vehicle acquisitions.

MOTION (7)
