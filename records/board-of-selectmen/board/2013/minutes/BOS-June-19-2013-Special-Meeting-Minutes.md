EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN CONNECTICUT
SPECIAL MEETING OF JUNE 19, 2013 020 VAT_F: SO. Woe

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Holly Cheeseman, Rose Ann Hardy.

1, CALL TO ORDER:
Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m.

MOTION (1)

Mr. Nickerson moved to enter into Executive Session at 6:31 p.m. for the purpose of
discussing Pending Litigation and the Temporary License Agreement with the National
Railroad Passenger Corporation and to invite Attorney Tobin to attend as appropriate.

Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman. Motion passed 4-0.

Mr. Wilson entered the meeting at 6:40 p.m.

Mr. Seery entered the meeting at 6:52 p.m.

MOTION (2)

Mr. Nickerson moved to return to regular session at 6:59 p.m. with no action taken in
Executive Session.

Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman. Motion passed 6-0.

Mr. Formica noted that since they were not finished with discussions, the Board would
return to Executive Session to finish discussing the IAFF Collective Bargaining Unit
Agreement at the end of the regular Board of Selectmen meeting.

Mr. Formica adjourned the Special Meeting at 7:00 p.m.

The Board (minus Mr. Seery) re-convened at 8:26 p.m. and invited Finance Director
Anna Johnson to attend as appropriate.

Mr. Formica and Ms. Johnson left the meeting at 8:40 p.m.

The Board returned to regular session at 8:48 p.m.
