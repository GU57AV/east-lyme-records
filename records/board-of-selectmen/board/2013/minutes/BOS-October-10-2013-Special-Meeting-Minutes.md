FILED IN EAST LYME

EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
SPECIAL MEETING OF October 11, 2013
MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Holly Cheeseman, Rob Wilson.

Mr. Formica called the Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen to order at
3:30 p.m.

MOTION (1)

Mr. Nickerson moved to enter into Executive Session for the purpose of discussing
Pending Litigation and Real Estate Acquisitions and to invite Attorney Tobin to attend as

appropriate.
Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 4-0.

MOTION (2)
Mr. Nickerson moved to return to regular session at 4:02 p.m. with no action taken during

Executive Session.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 4-0.

Mr. Formica adjourned the Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at 4:05
p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson. Motion passed 4-0.
