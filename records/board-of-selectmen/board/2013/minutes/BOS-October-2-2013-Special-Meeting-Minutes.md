SPECIAL MEETING OF OCTOBER 2, 2013
MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Kevin Seery, Holly Cheeseman, Rose Ann Hardy.

Mr. Formica called the special meeting to order at 6:30 p.m.

MOTION (1)

Mr. Seery moved to enter into Executive Session at 6:32 p.m. for the purpose of
discussing pending litigation and real estate acquisitions and to invite Attorney Block,
Attorney Tobin and Finance Director Anna Johnson to attend as appropriate.
Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman. Motion passed 4-0.

MOTION (2)

Mr. Seery moved to return to regular session at 7:22 p.m. with no action taken during
Executive Session.

Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman. Motion passed 4-0.

Mr. Formica adjourned the Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen at 7:23
p.m.
