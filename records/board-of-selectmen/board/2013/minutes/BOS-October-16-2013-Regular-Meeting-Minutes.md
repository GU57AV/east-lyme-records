2a. Checking of the Indices

Mr. Formica noted the letter from Town Clerk Lesley Blais stating that she had submitted
letters with incorrect dates for the last two years when requesting the appointment of
Attorney Willetts to check the Indices. He added that an additional motion would be
needed to correct these errors.

MOTION (4)

Mr. Nickerson moved to appoint Attorney William Willetts, Jr. of 216 Broad Street, New
London, CT to check the indices of the land records for the calendar year 2012 and 2013
in accordance with CGS Section 7-14.

Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 5-0.

2b. Appointments — Parks and Recreation

Mr. Formica noted that two people; John Wilson and Patrick Larkin had come forward
hoping to be appointed to this position. Mr. Wilson had been contacted and he had said if
there was someone more passionate he would step back from this appointment. Mr.
Formica noted that both gentlemen would be excellent candidates.

Patrick Larkin of 14 Oakhill Drive noted that he had been serving on the Youth Services
Commission since its inception and believed an appointment to the Parks and Recreation
Commission would offer an additional asset.

Mr. Formica said that since the Board of Selectmen is serving the last 60 days of their
term, Section 4.1.1 of the Town’s Charter prohibits them from appointing anyone beyond
the first Monday of January of the upcoming year.

MOTION (5)
Mr. Nickerson moved to appoint Patrick Larkin of 14 Oakhill Drive, Niantic, CT
to serve on the Parks and Recreation Commission through January 6, 2014.

Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman. Motion passed 5-0.

Harbor Management:

Mr. Formica noted that Marvin Schutt had resigned from the Harbor Management /
Shellfish Commission effective immediately. He noted he would be sending a letter to
Mr. Schutt thanking him for his many years of service. He said that prior Harbor Master
Joe Hitchery was willing to step in.

MOTION (6)

Mr. Nickerson moved to appoint Joseph Hitchery of 32 Bishop Bay Road, Niantic, CT to
serve on the East Lyme Harbor Management / Shellfish Commission through January 6,
2014.

Seconded by Mr. Seery. Motion passed 5-0.

2c. Public Safety Committee Discussion ~ Possible Action

Mr. Formica said that he had been talking with Sgt. San Juan about the potential of
setting up a Public Safety Committee to oversee and investigate regional efficiencies for
the police department, along with the fire and dispatch services. He noted that Mr. Seery
and Mr. Wilson would be excellent members of this Committee due to their police
background.

Ms. Hardy noted that years ago, a Public Safety Committee had concerned itself with non
negotiable items for the police department and discussed their wants and needs. She
suggested that a Board of Finance member be part of this Committee since many of the
suggestions would have a financial impact.

Mr. Wilson and Mr. Seery both agreed that this would be a good idea and agreed to serve.

Ms. Cheeseman said that it is always useful to have other eyes on any topic and
suggested checking with other towns for their best practices.

Mr. Formica said that he would carry this item on the agenda.

serving on. He said that there will be no scallop season this year and that there are 267
mooring permits.

Ms. Hardy noted that the Planning Commission meeting had been cancelled. She asked
for an update of the Solar Park from the First Selectman.

Mr. Wilson said that he had received notification that the ribbon cutting of the athletic
field would be on October 25".

5b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Formica said that the books are closed for the fiscal year and the Town would be
returning $685,000 and the Board of Education would be returning $15,000 to the
General Fund. He said that $750,000 had been used as a reserve from the undesignated
fund balance since at that time; we were unsure what the State would do. He added that
PILOT funding had increased by $300,000.

Mr. Formica noted that the High School athletic field ribbon cutting would be at 6:00
p.m. on October 25",

He said that the regional water interconnection was moving forward. He noted that they
would soon start directional drilling with no exposed pipes.

He said that the Amtrak beach was open and the Boardwalk design was being worked on.
The Light Parade will be on December 7" at 7:00 p.m.

He noted that he toured the site of the Solar Park and some of the housing area on Walnut
Hill Road and listened to the residents’ concerns. He hosted an informational meeting
with the residents and the developer and most in attendance left feeling better. He said
that the trees have been cleared and he talked to Mr. Lombardi abut the truck traffic. He
said that the next thing that will be done is the replacement of poles along Walnut Hill
Road along with some additional tree cutting. On Wednesday, October 23" at 7:00 p.m.
CL&P will have an informational meeting at Town Hall with the residents of Walnut Hill
and Grassy Hill Roads.

Mr. Formica said that the Town had hired a new Director of Public Works, Joe Bragaw,
who will start on November 4". Joe has been Public Works Director in Stonington and is
happy to be able to work in the same town where he lives and his kids go to school. He
extended his thanks to Bill Scheer who has been serving as Director of Public Works and
taking on any number of extra tasks, all for no extra money. He said that once Mr.
Bragaw is on the job, he will be reorganizing the departments and probably eliminating
the field service position, since he will be taking on some of those tasks along with the
new Foreman Matt Devore.

Ms. Hardy inquired what the anticipated completion date for the solar park was.

Mr. Formica responded sometime this spring. He said that bids are not out yet for the
Boardwalk since we first need the permits from AMTRAK.
