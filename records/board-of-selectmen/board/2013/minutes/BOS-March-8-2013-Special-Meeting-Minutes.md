1. Call to Order

First Selectman Paul Formica called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen to order
at 3:15 PM and led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

He noted that Mr. Wilson and Mr. Seery would be joining them after their work day ends.

2. Sign Call — Amend Town Meeting — Equipment — Port Security Proposal

Mr. Formica said that they needed to add this $29,000 for the ATV's and Jet Skis for Cini Park that they
had approved at their February 20, 2013 meeting of the Board. They would make this Item 3 on the call
and the current Item 3 would become Item 4.

**MOTION (1)

Mr. Nickerson moved to Amend the March 20, 2013 Notice of Special Town Meeting to add Item 3. to
read: To appropriate the amount of $29,000 to budget account 01-70-725-224-006 (NSEP —
Equipment) and corresponding revenue account 01-04-400-401 NSEP) Grants to purchase the
equipment noted in the Port Security Proposal Town of East Lyme to include three (3) 2012 Polaris
500CC ATV's with storage boxes and safety gear and one (1) 2012 Yamaha VX1100A-MA Jet Ski
including Coast Guard Safety Package and associated trailer. (Copy of Amended Meeting Call
Attached)

Ms. Cheeseman seconded the motion.

Vote: 4-0-0. Motion passed.

3. Approval of 2013/2014 Budget
Mr. Formica said that they had added some cuts from their previous meeting on Wednesday evening

and the total in cuts is now $662,268. The new totals are: General Govt. - $15,634,383; Debt Service -
$4,781,959; Capital - $1,170,399 and the Board of Ed with a pass through at $42,591,731 as they
cannot alter that budget. He provided everyone with a summary of the adjustments and the information
he had just given. (Copies attached) He noted that the revenue sheets that he had provided them with
at their previous meeting are the most current. He asked if there was any discussion.

Ms. Hardy asked which departments the cuts listed on lines 20 and 23 represented.

Mr. Formica said that line 20 was the COLA increase for the Director of Public Safety/Emergency
Management that they had approved on February 6, 2013 as well as a reduction in the transportation
allowance and line 23 was for Parks & Recreation — the COLA increase for the Director and decreases
in PT Seasonal Labor and Misc. Supplies line items. He reviewed the remainder of the list of Board of
Selectmen Budget Adjustments.
He said that there are some revenues that they can control and they have taken into effect the
Governor's proposal on the Pequot Fund’s loss of $291,000 that is being added to LoCIP. He noted that
this is also the first time that LoCIP is including the Board of Ed buildings in the use of the dollars. The
Board of Ed is proposing approximately $200,000 for buildings which would represent approximately
half of the LoCIP monies. There is also $54,000 in a Hold Harmless Grant put in by the Governor to get
the bottom line to zero. The Governor's budget also shows a $749,952 reduction in PILOT with an
increase of $772,000 to ECS. The $749,000 is supposed to have ‘no strings attached’ which would
mean that they could move it from ECS to PILOT and also keep the extra $30,000 in ECS. Right now
there are ‘no strings’ so they are assuming that this is what they can do to keep the funding the same
as last year.

This year they used $350,000 from fund balance in revenue and are proposing using $300,000 in the
new fiscal year. The fund balance as of June 30, 2012 was 7.8% which is a good fund balance.

The only other wild card is the Governor's proposal on the cars valued at $20,000 or less. This would
represent a $2.4M loss in revenues. If this loss was shifted to the homeowners — it would represent
approximately $220 more in taxes to each home.

Mr. Formica called for discussion on the budget.

Ms. Hardy asked Mr. Formica if he had testified on this.

Mr. Formica said that he did and that he was #12 on the list of well over 100 Town leaders who signed
up to speak. The legislature is looking at it as a shifting to property tax that the homeowners would pay
to make up the difference.

Ms. Hardy asked if they should send a follow-up letter.
Mr. Formica said that he has spoken with Representative Jutilla and others and was told that this would

not be done in the form that they now see.

Ms. Cheeseman noted that at a recent event that she was seated next to Mayor Finizio of New London
who said that his city would lose $1.8M in tax dollars and is not in favor of this plan. She said that it is
rare when both parties agree so strongly on this issue.

Mr. Formica suggested to Ms. Hardy that with her tenure on the Board that she might wish to draft a
letter saying that they are hoping that this does not go into effect.

Ms. Cheeseman asked with the change in ECS of the Town’s money, how it would work and if they
would have to ask to use it.

Mr. Formica said that it is the Board of Finance who typically allocates the dollars as they do not vote on
the budgets separately.

Ms. Cheeseman said that she was very pleased to see the reductions that were made as she felt that
they had to make some concessions. She said that she would agree that a letter from Ms. Hardy to
State Government would carry weight. She also urged the public to contact their State Government
regarding how they feel about moving the car tax revenue loss to the homeowners to make up. She
noted that she agreed with a lot of the cuts/reductions to Town projects and had considered not opening
Amtrak Beach to swimming as it would save with not having to provide lifeguards. She suggested that
this would be something that they could go back to should they need further cuts. She lastly said that
she would recommend to the Board of Finance that they urge the Board of Ed to put off the
implementation of an all-day kindergarten at this time.

(Note: 4 PM — Mr. Wilson joined the meeting)
Mr. Formica recapped where they were for Mr. Wilson.

Ms. Hardy noted that they have $145,000 in the contingency budget and asked how much they have in
fund balance.
Mr. Formica said that the 7.8% is approximately $4.2M.

Ms. Hardy asked what the percentage rating is to keep the bond rating.

and they have expressed interest in having Amtrak Beach open throughout the summer season.

that they want to get a jump on.

come together on the issues facing the taxpayers.

3.15%.

Cheeseman’s suggestion of eliminating all-day kindergarten was taken out — that $426,000 would
reduce the whole budget. He added that they also would not need the $100,000+ for teacher

Ms. Hardy suggested that they just find one item and take it out as some budgets do not have much to
