1c. Delegations

. Mr. Chris Seery, 25 Quailcrest Road, of Ledge Light Health District and the Medical
Reserve Corps, and part of the Disaster Epidemiology Disaster Strike Team (Disaster
Epi-Strike Team) advised that they will be doing an emergency preparedness door-to-
door survey on Saturday and Sunday, Sept 7 and 8, 2013, with residents in East Lyme,
Waterford, New London, Groton, Ledyard, as well as Old Lyme. Whoever answers the
survey, which will take approximately five minutes, will get a $5 gift card to Dunkin
Donuts. The team will wear bright colored shirts, and their cars will have magnet
stickers, to identify themselves when taking the surveys.

. Ms. Sally Wade, 392 Boston Post Road, spoke on the subject of Bamboo, and voiced
her objection regarding the fact that Mr. Mingo has stated, at the past Board meeting, that
the law to control it is unenforceable. Ms. Wade said that is not true, it is enforceable,
per Mr. Logan Senack, of the DEEP; and towns are given the option to assign someone to
enforce the Bamboo law. She advised that there is bamboo along her property line,
Yellow Groove bamboo, which was identified by a professional of the state. Therefore,
she believes that East Lyme should pass an ordinance. Ms. Wade also provided photos of
the yellow groove bamboo, and Selectman Formica instructed for it to be entered with the
title: Delegation ~ Wade Exhibit. Ms. Wade also feels that East Lyme should pass an
ordinance based on the facts, i.e., the need of a 40-foot set back.

. Mr. Robert Carlson of 68 Spring Glen Road stated that for this coming Winter, he
requests that tire chains be on vehicles and that contractors should be on stand-by for the
next big snow storm. He was concerned about the potential pile-up of snow and vehicles
getting stuck, if there is no snow plowing in the developments. He stated that he has
extensive experience working with contractors and knows how it should be handled. He
feels that since there is garbage pick-up and recycling pick-up, East Lyme should provide
all services — snow plowing developments included. He believes that the town should
take a stand of: “All or Nothing.”

. Ms. Terri Groff from the Institute for Invasive Bamboo Research brought to the
meeting (and left at each Selectman’s seat) a list of 22 properties in Niantic/East Lyme
that have the invasive Bamboo. She also included a printout of Connecticut laws
showing the wording of the CT Nuisance Law. Ms. Groff believes there should be an
ordinance of a 40-foot set back. She went on to say that she attended the DEEP and
Invasive Plant Forum in Milford, on how to identify the plant, Public Act 13-82, and Mr.
Mingo was there and took the packets that were handed out. She again reiterated that the
town should establish an ordinance for 40-foot set back, minimum. She would also like a
provision or stipulation established that rhizomes must be incinerated, NOT dumped.
Example: a dug up 60’x32’ above ground plant yields a weight of 14,000 Ibs. of sifted
rhizomes.

2b. Budget Transfers

Ms. Hardy questioned Anna Johnson, Finance Director, on last year’s budget and Ms.
Johnson stated that the deadline is 60 days until the end of the fiscal year (which is June
30") to pay any bills and process requests. Ms. Hardy also questioned Energy
Management — cannot transfer out of salary accounts into general usage accounts and Ms.
Johnson advised that we do transfer from salary accounts.

Ms. Hardy also was concerned with the large number of transfers to the Niantic Fire
Department, over $3,000 to go to the part-time firefighters. Ms. Johnson explained that
this was related to beginning of the year storms, so shortages will be at the end of the
year. She said that each department has different needs.

Mr. Wilson then pointed out the differences between full and part-time between the two
fire stations, and their different salary needs. The funds went to storm issues for
weekends in Flanders — Flanders is one way, Niantic is the other way.

Ms. Hardy also had a Park & Rec comment that there is a large increase in utilities, and
was this due to switched maintenance from part-time to full time, asking if the $3,000
seasonal labor was because of the beach. Ms. Johnson stated that this year was hit hard
by rain, so mowing was done on Saturdays by Park & Rec.

Mr. Formica then asked Ms. Johnson to give an estimate of how much the town will
return to the general public and Ms. Johnson stated that $700,000-800,000 is unspent.

Mr. Nickerson then moved to ratify departmental transfers in the amount of $26,305.69
processed for fiscal year end June 30, 2013 and to be forwarded to the Board of Finance
for approval.
