EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
SPECIAL MEETING OF NOVEMBER 20, 2013
MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Mark Nickerson, Holly Cheeseman, Kevin Seery.

Executive Session.

Seconded by Mr. Seery. Motion passed 5-0.
