EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
PUBLIC HEARING
MAY 15, 2013

MINUTES
PRESENT: Paul Formica, Holly Cheeseman, Kevin Seery, Rose Ann Hardy.

Mr. Formica called the public hearing regarding Water Conservation Measures to order at
7:02. p.m.

Mr. Formica led the Pledge of Allegiance. He noted that the public hearing call was
published in The Day on May 11, 2015. He said that he would like to make the
restrictions dependent upon stream flow. He added that we enact these conservation
measures on a yearly basis for several years and because of the regional interconnection
program which should be finished by May of next year, this should be our last year to do
this. He added that the regional interconnect program will send water to New London
and East Lyme will draw back water in the summe,r when we need it.

Since there was no public comment, Mr. Formica closed the public hearing at 7:06 p.m.
