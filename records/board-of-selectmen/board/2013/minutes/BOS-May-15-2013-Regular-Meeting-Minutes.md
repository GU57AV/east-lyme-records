Karen Rak of 27 Black Point Road wanted to speak to the Board about process. She said
that it is essential that when a decision is made, it must be done fairly and openly and this
promotes confidence in the process. She stressed that process must be open, impartial,
unbiased, justifiable and inclusive. She said that when process is lacking as in the health
insurance change, Coastal Connections, the MOU between Montville and East Lyme, and
the facilities committee there is a lack of transparency and openness. She encouraged the
Board of Education and this Board to practice openness.

2a. Action — Public Hearing re: Mandatory Water Restrictions

MOTION (3)

MR. Seery moved the following:

Water restrictions will be in effect from 9:00 a.m. on Friday through noon on Monday of
each week subject to stream flow conditions.

The following restrictions will apply to all customers of the East Lyme Water System:

1) Prohibiting the sprinkling, watering or irrigation of lawns.

East Lyme Board of Selectmen 5/15/13 minutes

2) Prohibiting washing of motor vehicles and the outdoor surfaces of all buildings
and structures, sidewalks and driveways.

3) Prohibiting the filling or cleaning of swimming and wading pools.

4) Prohibiting the service of water to customers of public restaurants except at the
specific request of customers. ‘

Persons found violating any provision of the restrictions imposed by the Board of
Selectmen shall be fined not more than ninety-nine dollars ($99.00). In the case of a
continuing violation, each day’s continuance thereof shall be deemed to be a separate and
distinct offense, These restrictions may be imposed by the First Selectman upon
recommendation of the Municipal Utility Engineer and upon due publication of a notice
in a newspaper having a general circulation in the Town.

Seconded by Ms. Cheeseman.

Ms. Hardy said that the addition of the words “subject to stream flow conditions” is a
change from implementing the restrictions on June 28" and asked how this would be
monitored and the public informed.

Mr. Formica said that the Utility Engineer Brad Kargl monitors the wells every day and if
the restrictions are to be implemented when the stream flow conditions warrant, this
information will be on Channel 22, on the website, on our Facebook page and we will
publish notice in the paper. He said that this is the normal practice that we have followed
in the past.

Ms. Hardy stated that it would be confusing to have the restrictions on some weekends
and not on others and she said that we should be consistent.

Mr. Seery said that he understood Ms. Hardy’s comments and said that some of the
general public does not have access to Channel 22.

Ms. Hardy inquired about the sign that was posted out front saying that we are under
water conservation restrictions.

Ms. Cheeseman said that if there is not substantial difference and this is what we have
done in the past, she has no problem adding the words.

Motion passed 4-0.
