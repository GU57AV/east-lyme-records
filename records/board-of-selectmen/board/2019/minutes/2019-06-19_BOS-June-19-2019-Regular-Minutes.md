2e.      EL Ambulance / Fire Fighters Agreement
MOrroN (9)
DISCUSSION: Niantic Fire Chief John Dwire and East Lyme Ambulance Fund President Dave Baer were
in attendance. Mr. Nickerson reported that this is a 5-year renewable and negotiable agreement. This
agreement will allow for an additional full time fire fighter to be hired to work the overnight shift from
1Opm until 7am. The Ambulance Fund will pay for the salary and overtime, and the Town will pay for this
employee's benefits. Mrs. Hardy thanked the East Lyme Ambulance Association and our Fire Chiefs for
working with the Town to come to this agreement, and for the invaluable service that they provide to our
community. Mr. Nickerson also thanked our first responders for their hard work, and announced that he is
very pleased to call the vote on this motion.
Mr. Seery MOVED to authorize the First Selectman to enter into a Memorandum of Agreement between
the Town of East Lyme and the East Lyme Ambulance Fund, Inc. effective June 19,2019.
Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 5-0.
