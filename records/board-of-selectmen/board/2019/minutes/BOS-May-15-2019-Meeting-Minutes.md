DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that as we have seen in the news, there is discussion happening all
over regarding the Federal statute that states you cannot volunteer in the town in which you are a paid
employee. In our town we have many people who do just that so this matter needs to be addressed further
We have four entities in town; the Town itself, the Flanders Fire Department Volunteers Inc., the Niantic
Fire Department Volunteers Inc. and the East Lyme Ambulance Association. The Town will be working
closely with town attorney and Fire Chiefs for clarification, and we are waiting for an opinion from the
State Labor Board. He reported that everyone will continue on with business as usual while this is being
worked out,
