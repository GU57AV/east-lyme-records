                        EAST L\.A4E BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                      PUBLIC HEARING OF NOVEMBER 6,2019
                                     MINUTES

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Rose Ann Hardy, Marc Salerno, Daniel
Cunningham and Paul Dagle

Mr. Nickerson opened the Public Hearing to consider the adoption of an ordinance
entitled "Ordinance Modifying The Ordinance Concerning The Board of Police
Commissioners" at 7:I5 p.m. He stated that he caused the Notice of Public Hearing
to be posted on the signpost, and that it was published in The Day on October 25,
2019. Mr. Nickerson asked if anyone from the public would like to speak on this
matter.

Mr. Mike Shulz, Lovers Lane, East Lyme, addressed the Board and congratulated the
Board Members for their re-appointments to the Board of Selectmen and thanked them
for their service. Mr. Shulz stated that he has some reservations with this modification
of ordinance. He does not disagree with the First Selectman being removed as the
Police Chief, but he is not comfortable with the Police Commission not having an
elected official. He feels that the Police Department offers the most significant services
to the community, and he would like an elected official to oversee that department. He
stated that he feels like the Police Commission has total authority over the Police
Department, and that the Chief has no authority, and he inquired if the Chief is a
voting member of the Police Commission. He stated that by Charter, the First
Selectman hires and fires all Town employees, so he does not understand why the
Police Commission would have the authority to hire or fire Police Department
personnel.
