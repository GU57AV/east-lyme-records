                              EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                              SPECIAL MEETING OF JULY 24,2019
                                         MINUTES

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Paul Dagle, Marc Salerno and Dan Cunningham

EXCUSED: Rose Ann Hardy

Mr. Nickerson called the meeting to order at 5:31 p.m, and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

3. Paul Dagle, Chair of the East Lyme Public Safety Vision Committee, presented the recommendation of
the Committee for the selection of an architect for the project. He stated four firms were considered based
on the recommendation of the Architect Review Sub-Committee. That sub-committee interviewed the
architecturalfirms, thoroughly checked references, and reviewed projects performed.

Taking into consideration the qualifications, bid price, and project schedule estimate, the Public Safety
Vision Committee unanimously recommended that Silver/Petrucelli and Associates be retained as the
architectural firm to perform necessary upgrades to the public safety building. (Exhibit A).

4. Kevin Seery made the following motion:

Move that the Board of Selectmen hereby accept the Public Safety Vision Committee's recommendation to
hire Silver/Petruceli and Associates as the architectural firm to perform necessary upgrades to the Public
Safety building; and the First Selectman of the Town of East Lyme be and hereby is authorized to execute
on behalf of the Town of East Lyme an agreement with Silver/Petruceli and Associates and is authorized to
execute any and all documents necessary to consummate the transaction.

Seconded by Marc Salerno, ln favor of the motion: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Marc Salerno, Dan
Cunningham, and Paul Dagle, Against the motion: None

5. Motion to adjourn by Kevin Seery at 5:48 p.m. Seconded by Marc Salerno. Unanimous vote in favor of
adjournment.




Respectfully submitted,




Kevin Seery
Acting Recording Secretary
