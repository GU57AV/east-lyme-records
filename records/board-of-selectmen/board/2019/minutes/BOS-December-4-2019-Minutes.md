2a.       Special Appropriation - Wells lA and 6
MOTTON (4)
DISCUSSION: Mr. Joe Bragaw, Director of Public Works, addressed the board and explained that this
project is necessary, and that this additional money will be needed to complete said project. He explained
that it is a matter of water quality in Well lA which has been testing high in iron and manganese. Well lA
does not have a filtering system in place to handle removal of these minerals, so the project is to tie Well lA
to Well 6 to solve that problem, Initially the Engineering firm estimated the project cost to be $4,640,000,
which was approved at a special town meeting on January 2,2019. They went out to bid in May of 2079,
and only one bidder participated and came in at -$lM over hudget. They went hack to the drawing board
and put this project out to bid again in November 2019, at which three bidders participated and the lowest
bid came in at 1i950,000 over the approved amount. Total cost for this project is now $5,590,000, Mr.
Bragaw reported that they put some money aside last fiscal year and again this year in order to lessen the
burden on the ratepayers; this money will allow them to equally spread out the repayment on the bonds over
the years so as not to significantly impact the ratepayers. Mrs. Hardy inquired if any of the interested
bidders were from Connecticut, and Mr. Bragaw stated that all three bidders were from out of state. He
noted that the first bidder, which came in over $lM over budget, was a local company but that they have
since closed their business. Mrs. Hardy inquired if there will be a stipulation included in the contract that
there be a time limit adhered to or a penalty can be imposed, and Mr. Bragaw advised that there will be such
