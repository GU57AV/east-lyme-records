         Mr. Ron Rando, 194 Boston Post Road, stated that people are driving way too fast on Boston Post
Road and he would like to see more police presence in the evenings. He requested that the executive
session be held at the end of the meeting so as not to hold up any public present. Regarding the Board of
Finance Public Hearing on Monday, April 29'r'; Mr. Rando stated that according to Section 6.1,4 of the
Town Charter only qualified voters should be allowed to speak at a public hearing. He feels that allowing
the children to speak at this recent public hearing should void the meeting and require another to be held to
allow all qualified voters to speak on the budget. He also would like to see time limits enforced.
         Mr. John Drabik, l8 Drabik Road, also cited Section 6.1.4 of the Town Charter and stated that the
public hearing did not allow actual qualified voters to speak. He also disagrees with the Superintendent's
decision to send a letter out to parents requesting that they come to the meeting and speak, and he requested
that the Board of Selectmen look into this and also at how the public hearing was run and enforce the
charter, Mr. Drabik also spoke against the Costco project and stated that the town should not be spending
money it doesn't have at this time. He stated that he had witnessed a Board of Education truck plowing a
private driveway, and Mr. Nickerson stated that if someone witnesses something like that, they need to
report it immediately and with details.
