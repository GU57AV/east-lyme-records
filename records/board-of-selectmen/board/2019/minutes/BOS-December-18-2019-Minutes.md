lc.     Delegations
         Michelle Williams, member of the Planning Commission and Chair of the POCD, addressed the
Board of Selectmen and stated that the POCD is working on required updates that are due by the end of 2020
in order for the Town to stay compliant and eligible for possible programs and grants. The last updates were
performed in 2009. She reported that they have targeted a number ofkey stakeholder groups and have reached
out to them directly in order to inform the public of the online survey available for feedback through the end
of December. A preliminary timeline was communicated: at the end of January will be an open forum to
discuss the results of the survey with results being communicated by the end of spring; they anticipate
presentation to the Board of Selectmen by the end of August; and final results and the required updates turned
into the State by December 1,2020.
