 1c, Fire Fighter Recognition
Mr. Nickerson stated that it is an honor and a privilege to have all of the Fire Fighters in attendance tonight
to take part in this new tradition for the Town. Tonight will be the first official public badge pinning of new
fire fighters, as well as a recognition ceremony for all of the full time fire fighters. He then introduced the
two Fire Chiefs, Bill Rix for Flanders Fire Department and John Dwire for the Niantic Fire Department.
Chief Rix introduced the Town's lOth full time Fire Fighter, John Forier, and invited his family up to
officially pin his badge on his uniform. Chief Dwire introduced the Town's 11th full time Fire Fighter,
Christopher "CJ" Leindecker, and his family was invited up to officially pin his badge on his uniform. Mr,
Nickerson and the other Board Members congratulated these men, and Mr. Nickerson expressed his
gratitude to all of our fire fighters and the families that support them; our community is grateful for their
service and protection. Mr. Nickerson then presented proclamations to the following fire fighters to honor
their service and dedication to the Town: William Allen III, 27 years of full time career service; Dave
Connolly,3T years; Bill Haylon,2years; Tom Redden,2 years; William G. Rix,37 years; Ed Waido, 19
years; and Miles Wofthington, 15 years. Mr. Nickerson closed the ceremony by thanking everyone and
their families for their support and dedication, and noted that he appreciates the strong working relationship
between the two fire houses, and looks forward to continuing and improving this tradition.

Mr. Nickerson called for a short recess at 8:14 p.m. Everyone returned and Mr, Nickerson called the
regular meeting back to order at 8:17 p.m.

3b.       First Selectman's Report
          Mr. Nickerson reported that our summer band series is well underway at McCook's with music on

two plays run last week, one at the high school and one at the band shell. He attended a ceremony in honor

in the East Lyme Public Trust throughout the years, Mr. Nickerson attended a ribbon cutting at the Aces
High Campground; and although this is not a new business in town, the woman who has been managing the
campground has recently taken ownership. He thanked the members of the Harbor Management
Commission et al for all of their hard work in getting the Harbor Management Plan and ordinance updated,
Mr. Nickerson thanked all of the fire fighters and their families for coming out tonight, and that it was an
honor to have them here to recognize them for their hard work and dedication. He stated that he is
scheduled to attend the 300tr'anniversary celebration of the Niantic Community Church on Saturday the 10tr'
at McCook's; and noted that they will also be having a special service on Sunday morning also. He had a
proclamation prepared for the occasion and read from it giving a brief history of the church and how it came
