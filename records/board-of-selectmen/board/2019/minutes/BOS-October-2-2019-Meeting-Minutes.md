2c.      2020 Census Complete Count Committee
DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that at the direction of Lt. Governor Susan Bysiewicz, we are directed
to establish this committee and appoint people as necessary in preparation for the 2020 Census. Most
important is for people to understand that the census is only to gather a headcount of people in our
community, and that no formal reporting other than number of people will go to the government. The
general rule to follow is where you put your head on your pillow on April 1,2020 is where you will be
counted. On April l't the process will start as an online survey, then it will progress to a mailing, and the
last step will be a door-to-door survey. Mrs. Hardy inquired as to if the prison population is counted as East
Lyme residents, and Mr. Nickerson stated that yes, they will be counted as part of the East Lyn,e
community. More information on this Complete Count Committee and the Census itself can be found at
www.census.gov. Mr. Nickerson noted that the Census is pivotal in the government determining the amount
of financial assistance our community will receive based on our population, so it is crucial that everyone
participate.

2d.      Ordinance - Board of Police Commissioners
DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that this is just a discussion to consider removing the First Selectrnan
from the Board of Police Commissioners. Currently, the First Selectman is a voting member of the seven-
member board, and Mr. Nickerson, with the approval of the Board of Police Commissioners, is
recommending that the First Selectman be removed and becomes an ex-officio member, as with other
boards and commissions. This would leave an additional spot for a community member to sit on the Board.
He stated that the ordinance was written this way because at the time the Town still operated under the State
Resident Trooper program. Now that the Police Department is independent and operating under a Chief of
Police for the past two years, he feels as though it is time to make the change. He noted that the State
Statute allows for a three, five- or seven-member board, but he recommends staying at a seven-member
board. Mr. Seery stated that he agrees with this change, and also recommends staying at seven members.
Mr. Salerno is also in agreement with this change and stated that there was a lot of interest from the public
in membership so there should be no problem filling the open spot. Mrs. Hardy requested that the matter be
tabled until the next meeting in order to give her and the other Board members time to review it more. Mr.
Nickerson stated that he would direct the Town Attorney to bring a draft of the modified ordinance to the
next meeting.
