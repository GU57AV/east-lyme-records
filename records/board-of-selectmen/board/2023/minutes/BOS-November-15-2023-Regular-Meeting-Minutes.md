3a. Youth Family and Human Services Ordinance
DISCUSSION: It was discussed and agreed to amend Section 2. Purpose, as follows; " . . . .
meet the needs of the youth in the Town of East Lyme, and Salem students enrolled in East
Lyme High School, as well as families and individuals in the Town of East Lyme from youth to
Senior Citizens. This amendment makes the language more specific to Salem youth that are
enrolled in East Lyme High School, There was also concern raised about the impact on the
budget in future years; the consensus was that the benefits to our community of such a
commission will outweigh the future cost. Ms. Firmin reported that Salem signs over their
share of the grant to East Lyme; this yearthat grant amount was $6,417.00, Mr. Seery stated
that the inception of this ordinance and commission will allow the Town to apply for many
additional grants.

MOTTON (3)
Ms. Santoro MOVED the following: RESOLVED to repeal Ordinance 5 31.10 entitled "Youth
Services Commission" and enact the Ordinance entitled "Ordinance Establishing a Youth, Family
and Human Services Commission" presented at the public hearing before the Board of
Selectmen on November 15, 2023. The effective date of the Ordinance shall be ten (10) days
after publication of the notice of its adoption.
