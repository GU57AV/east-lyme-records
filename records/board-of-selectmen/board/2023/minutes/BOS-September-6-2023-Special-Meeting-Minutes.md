                               MINUTES OF THE
                        EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
               SPECIAL MEETING OF SEPTEMBER 6, 2023, AT 6:45 PM
                  EAST LYME TOWN HALL UPPER MEETING ROOM

In Attendance: Kevin Seery, Anne Santoro, Rose Ann Hardy, William Weber


1         Call Meeting to Order and Pledge of Allegiance - The meeting was
          called to order by the First Selectman at 6:45 PM. Members of the U-
          12 Little League Boys' BaseballSfate Championship Team lead the
          Pledge of Allegiance and recited the Little League Pledge.

2         Recognition of the East Lyme U-12 Little League Boys' Baseball State
          Championship Team - Head Coach Andrew Cellemme introduced the
          players and coaches of the State Championship team. He recognized
          the dedication, hard work, and spirit of the players and coaches.
          Also, he thanked the parents and community for their support.

3         Adjourn - A motion to adjourn was made by Anne Santoro at 6:51
          PM, seconded by Rose Ann Hardy - unanimous. Meeting adjourned.




Submitted by




Kevin Seery
First Selectman
