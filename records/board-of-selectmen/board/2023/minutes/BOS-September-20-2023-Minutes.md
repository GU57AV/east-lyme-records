The goals and mission for the proposed human services department are to foster the positive

Services and Youth Services. The department will have the senior center director and the youth
and family services director as co-human services department heads.

RE: Letter of support for creation of new department providing expanded Human Services
Dear Board of Selectmen,

I write in strong support of the creation of a new department to expand human services provided to our
citizens. East Lyme prides itself on being a communitythat serves its residents and provides a wonderful
quality of life, That was an important factor for me in choosing to continue my career here, and it is an
important factor for many others as they decide where they will live, work, and play.

East Lyme has much to be proud of, but we do not have a dedicated department providing citizens with
a full array of human services such as direct assistance, case management, advocacy, crisis intervention,
short-term counseling, screening for depression, suicide, sexual/physical abuse, ADD/ADHD and
information and referral services to individuals and families who need temporary assistance with
housing, food and other basic necessities.

The recent pandemic highlighted gaps in our services and l, and others, believe the time is right to take
steps towards a more robust community response to meet the need. The effort has already begun. We
utilized Covid funding to hire a part-time Prevention, Wellness and Mentoring Coordinator, and then
took an additional step in the last budget cycle to make the position full-time. The Town has also added
a part-time social worker to begin providing necessary services.

The next step is to consolidate those resources and coordinate service delivery. lt is clear that the
current resources will not be adequate to meet all of the needs immediately, but the creation of a
department will provide the framework for new services to be added in a meaningful way in the months
and years to come.

East Lyme Parks and Recreation and Youth Services have proudly served the community, and we look
forward to continuing that service in newer and more impactful ways that result in an improved quality
of life for our residents.
