To East Lyme residents,

1n2022,'the Board of Selectmen establishedthe2022 Charter Revision Commission, which has
completed the work with which it was charged.

This explanatory text provides information on the proposed Charter revisions under
consideration in a series of eleven ( I 1 ) questions to be voted on at a public referendum to be held
in conjunction with the municipal election on November 7. The ballot questions reflect the
recommendations of the 2022 Cha*er Revision Commission which were approved by the Board
of Selectmen.
Full copies of the.Charter as revised with red-lined proposed changes are available at the Town
Clerk's office and on the Town's website at: https://eltoYmhall.cgm.

The proposed Charter revisions are not final unless they are approved by the East Lyme voters on
November 7. Each ballot question will be counted and implemented separately.

Below are the ballot questions and an explanation of the proposed changes. Each question will
have a yes/no vote on the ballot.




               Shall the town charter be amended to increase the First Selectman's term of office from
two to four years?

The intent is to reduce campaigning time and increase tirne dedicated to the duties of the
First Sclectman.

of office from two to four years?

  The intent of this amendment is to reflect that certain professional credentials are required

  terms of office (December 1r2025 unless a vacancy occurs sooner).

 4.      Shall the town charter be amended so that an appointee to a vacancy in an elective office
 serves the remainder of the.term that was filled?

 The intent of this amendment is to fill vacancies in a manner consistent with Secretary of
 the Statets guidance and the majority of other towns.

 5.     Shall the town charter be amended to increase the price of real estate that may be
 purchased or sold upon the Board of Selectmen's authority and without town meeting approval,
 from $10,000 to $100,000?

 The intent of the amendment is to reflect the present day value of the threshold set in 2009,
 when last revised. Approval by the Board of Finance is still required in all instan'ces.

 6.      Shall the town charter be amended to provide for automatic approval of requests for
 special appropriations or transfers of funds if the Board of Finance fails to act on such requests
 within 30     days?                                                               '
 The intent of the amendment is to increase efficiency and the ability to act on urgent Town
 funding by eliminating delays if the Board of Finance fails to act within the required
 period.

 7.     Shall the town charter be amended to not require Board of Finance or Town Meeting
approval ofprojects funded entirely by state, federal, orprivate grants that do notrequire an
appropriation of town funds, unless acceptance of such grant would have an adverse impact on
future operating budgets?

The intent of the amendment is to streamline the ability of the town to spend grant funds
when appropriation of Town funds is not required.

8.     Shall the town charter be amended to increase the Board of Selectmen's authority to
make special appropriations, with Board of Finance approval, from $10,000 to an aggregate of
$100,000 per town department in each fiscal year?

The intent of the.amendment is to reflect the present day value of the threshold that was set
in 1965. Approval by the Board of Finance is still requined in all instances.
