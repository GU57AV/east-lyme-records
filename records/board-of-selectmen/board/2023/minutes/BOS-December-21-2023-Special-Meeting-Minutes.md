                             EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                          SPECIAL MEETING OF DECEMBER 2I, 2023
                                        MINUTES


PRESENT: Dan Cunningham, Ann Cicchiello, Rose Ann Hardy, Jason Deeble and Don
MacKenzie
EXCUSED: Candice Carlson

Mr. Cunningham called the meeting to order at 2:00 p.m.

MOrroN (1)
Mr. Deeble MOVED the following:

                                   TOWN OF EAST LYME
                             NOTICE OF SPECIAL TOWN MEETING
                                  January 3,2024 @ 7:00 P.M.

        Notice is hereby given of a Special Town Meeting of the Town of East Lyme to be held on
Wednesday, January 3,2024 at 7:00 P,M. (ET) at the East Lyme Town Hall Upper Meeting Room
for the following purposes:

       1.     To receive communications from the Board of Selectmen and the Board of Finance
with respect to a motion to approve a special appropriation in the amount of $25,000 from
remaining unexpended American Rescue funds to a new ARPA Account 85-70-008-700-802
"McCooks Beach Seawall"

       2. To consider and act upon a motion to approve a special appropriation in the amount
of $25,000 from remaining unexpended American Rescue funds to a new ARPA Account 85-70-
008-700-802 "McCooks Beach Seawall", for a temporary fix to prevent further erosion.

       3.     To transact any other business proper to come before the meeting

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, this 2lst day of December,2023. EAST LYME BOARD OF
SELECTMEN

Seconded by Ms. Cicchiello. Motion passed 5-0.

MOTTON (2)
Ms. Cicchiello MOVED to adjourn the December 2L, 2023, special meeting of the East Lyme
Board of Selectmen at 2:03 p.m.
Seconded by Ms, Hardy. Motion passed 5-0.
