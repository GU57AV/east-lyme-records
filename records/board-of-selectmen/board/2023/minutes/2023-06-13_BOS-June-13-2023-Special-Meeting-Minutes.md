                              EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                             SPECIAL MEETING OF JUNE 13, 2023
                                         MINUTES


PRESENT: Kevin Seery, Dan Cunningham, Bill Weber and Ann Cicchiello
EXCUSED: Rose Ann Hardy and Anne Santoro

Mr. Seery called the meeting to order at 2:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Mr. Seery stated that the Board of Selectmen are in receipt of the Charter Revision
Subcommittee's draft report/proposed charter revision amendments. He reported that this
document was received by all Board members via email, and that a hard copy may be
obtained by visiting the Town Clerk in person or visiting the webpage at www.eltownhall.com

MOTTON (1)
Ms. Cicchiello MOVED the following

                                  TOWN OF EAST LYME
                                 BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                             NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING RE
                      CHARTER REVISION COMMISSION DRAFT REPORT

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the East Lyme Board of Selectmen will hold a public hearing at
6:30 p,m, on Wednesday, June 21,2023 at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue,
Niantic, Connecticut to receive comments concerning the June 6, 2023 Draft Report with
proposed Charter amendments as submitted by the East Lyme Charter Revision Commission to
the Board of Selectmen regarding amendments to the East Lyme Town Charter. Copies of the
Charter Revision Commission's draft report may be obtained atthe office of the East Lyme town
clerk or on the Town's website at www.eltownhall.com. Interested persons are invited to attend
and be heard. Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut on this 13th day of June, 2023. EAST LYME
BOARD OF SELECTMEN

Seconded by Mr. Weber, Motion passed 4-0.

MOTTON (2)
Mr. Cunningham MOVED to adjourn the June t3,2023, special meeting of the East Lyme
Board of Selectmen at 2:03 p.m.
Seconded by Mr. Weber. Motion passed 4-0.
