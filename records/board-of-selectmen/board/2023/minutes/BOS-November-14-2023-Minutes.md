                            EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                         SPECIAL MEETING OF NOVEMBER 14, 2023
                                       MINUTES


PRESENT: Kevin Seery, Dan Cunningham, Bill Weber, Anne Santoro and Ann Cicchiello
EXCUSED: Rose Ann Hardy

Mr, Seery called the meeting to order at4:02 p,m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

MOrroN (1)
Ms, Santoro MOVED to enter into executive session for the purpose of Collective Bargaining -
Police Union Contract.
Seconded by Mr, Cunningham. Motion passed 5-0.

The Board entered into executive session at 4:03 p,m. They returned at 4:18 p.m. and Ms
Santoro stated that no formal votes were taken.

MOTTON (2)
Ms. Santoro MOVED to authorize the First Selectman to enter into the collective bargaining
agreement, as amended, between the Town of East Lyme and Police Union Local #2852,
Council 4, AFSCME, AFL-CIO, for the period July 1, 2023 through June 30,2026.
Seconded by Ms, Cicchiello. Motion passed 5-0.

MOTTON (3)
Ms, Santoro MOVED to adjourn the November 14,2023, special meeting of the East Lyme
Board of Selectmen at 4:19 p.m.
Seconded by Ms. Cicchiello, Motion passed 5-0.


Respectfully Submitted By




Sandra Anderson
Recording Secretary
