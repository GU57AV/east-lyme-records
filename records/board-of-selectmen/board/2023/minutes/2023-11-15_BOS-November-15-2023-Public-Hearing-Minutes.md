                            EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                         PUBLIC HEARING OF NOVEMBERE L5,2023
                                       MINUTES


PRESENT: Board of Selectmen Members Kevin Seery, Rose Ann Hardy, Anne Santoro,
Daniel Cunningham, Bill Weber and Ann Cicchiello,
ALSO PRESENT: Finance Director Kevin Gervais and Town Attorney Tracy Collins

Mr. Seery opened the Public Hearing at 7:00 p.m. to receive comments concerning
a proposed Ordinance entitled Ordinance Establishing a Youth, Family and Human
Services Commission.


Pat Larkin, L4 Oak Hill Road, East Lyme, supports this proposed ordinance and
establishment of such a commission, but he feels that this should be a matter taken
up by the new administration as it involves the Town's future budget,

Candice Carlson, 46 Riverview Road, East Lyme, stated that she is a Board of
Selectman elect, and that she is in support of moving forward with this ordinance
tonight.

There were no other members of the public present to speak on this matter


MOrroN (1)
Motion by Ms. Santoro to close the Public Hearing of November 15, 2023, at 7:03 p,m,
Seconded by Mr. Cunningham, Motion passed 6-0.
