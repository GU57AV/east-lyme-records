2. Budget Discussion

117 Planning
Planning Director Gary Goeschel was in attendance to speak on this budget. He
stated that he had requested $30,000 for a new Environmental Planner/Assistant
Town Planner position, and the First Selectman cut that to $15,000, which will be
sufficient to get someone in part-time with no benefits for a couple of months. He
also requested a budget of $3,000 in the miscellaneous supplies line item, and this
was cut to the original amount of $1,500.

104 Building Department
Building Official Dave Garside was in attendance to speak on this budget. The
proposed budget increases for this department are due to contractual salaries and
benefits. Suggested increase in fees will offset some by adding to the revenue.
Since this is his first budget session, he will have a better understanding of the
budget next year; this year is pretty much status quo,

116 Zoning
Zoning Director Bill Mulholland was in attendance to speak on this budget. The
proposed budget increases for this department are due to contractual salaries and
benefits,

110 Registrars
Registrars Mary Smith and Wendi Sims were in attendance to speak on this budget.
They reviewed the process that they are consistently following throughout the year,
but especially during the quiet time of January through February, to keep up and be
proactive in keeping up with changes in the voter registration list. There is significant
increase in the line item 402 Referendum/Primary as this year is a Presidential
primary, as well as a municipal election year.

109 Information Technology
IT Director Dan Cleary was in attendance to speak on this budget. He expects that
line item 217 Technical Assistance Town will continue to go down as he and Carmen
are doing more in-house without having to call upon an outside contractor. Line item
218 Other IT Services includes town-wide training required for all Town employees,

216 Police
Chief Mike Finkelstein and Lt. Mike Macek were in attendance to speak on this
budget, and his presentation is attached hereto as Exhibit #1.

Mr. Seery called for a five-minute break; after which the Board returned, and the
meeting resumed.

317 Public Works / Highway / Sanitation
Public Works Director Joe Bragaw was in attendance to speak on this budget, which
is proposing -2.99o/o increase and is primarily contractual salaries and benefits. He
noted a significant reduction in line item 210 for street lights, which is the first year
that we have realized the savings from the streetlight replacement project from a few
years ago. Projected revenues are also showing an increase because we have begun
charging fees to contractors to offset the tipping fees,

Capital Improvement
Public Works Director Joe Bragaw was in attendance to speak on this budget. Mr.
Bragaw reviewed this fiscal year's road improvement plan. They have been following
this plan for a number of years and it has proven efficient in keeping our roads and
parking lots in good shape, Mr. Bragaw reviewed the list of upcoming projects in the
Capital Improvement Plan.
