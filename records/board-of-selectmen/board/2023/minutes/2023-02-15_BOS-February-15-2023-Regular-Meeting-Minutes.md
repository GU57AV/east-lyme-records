2b, Cini Fishing Dock Leases
DISCUSSION: Mr. Cunningham and Ms, Santoro are working on these leases and have
discussed the following; two of the leases are currently yearly renewal; should these be a two

3a.    Fire Marshal Permit and Inspection Fees
DISCUSSION: Deputy Fire Marshal Erik Quinn was in attendance and reported that his office

such an ordinance in place. Mr. Seery noted that the Town has not been charging fees for a lot
of these services currently, and that Waterford is also currently reviewing their fees. The draft
ordinance will be reviewed, and this will come up for discussion at an upcoming meeting.
