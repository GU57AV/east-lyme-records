                               EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                              SPECIAL BUDGET MEETING MINUTES
                                       MARCH t,2023


IN ATTENDANCE: Kevin Seery, Anne Santoro, Rose Ann Hardy, Dan Cunningham, Ann
Cicchiello and Bill Weber
ALSO IN ATTENDANCE: Finance Director Kevin Gervais

Mr. Seery called the special budget meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen to order at
5:40 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

2. Capital Improvements
DISCUSSION: The Board reviewed the proposed Capital Improvements and considered a
couple of adjustments to be made later in the meeting.

3, Revenues
DISCUSSION: The Board reviewed the proposed Revenues, and Mr. Gervais noted that he
made an adjust in how some revenues are reported. He explained that in past years the
supplemental motor vehicle taxes went into the current year tax revenue; he suggests moving
that line item into its own revenue account.

4. Budget Discussion/Deliberations
DISCUSSION: The following changes were made to the proposed budget:

MOrroN (1)
Ms. Santoro MOVED to add $15,000 to the Capital Improvements Line Item for Parks and
Recreation to purchase a total of two (2) Zero Turn Motors for a total of $30,000.
Seconded by Mr. Cunningham. Motion passed 6-0.

MOTTON (2)
Ms. Santoro MOVED to remove the Capital Improvements Line ltem for Peretz Park
Restrooms/Garage Expansion project Bonding for $750,000.
Seconded by Ms. Cicchiello. Motion passed 6-0.

5. Adjournment

MOrroN (3)
Mr. Cunningham MOVED to adjourn the March 7, 2023, special meeting of the Board of
Selectmen at 7:04 p.m.
Seconded by Ms. Santoro. Motion passed 6-0.
