                             MINUTES OF THE
                     EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
            SPECIAL MEETING OF SEPTEMBER 27 , 2023, AT B: 14 PM
     EAST LYME MIDDLE SCHOOL CAFETERIA, 31 SOCIETY ROAD, NIANTIC CT

In Attendance: Kevin Seery, Anne Santoro, Ann Cicchiello, Daniel Cunnigham, Rose
Ann Hardy


1         Call Meeting to Order - The meeting was called to order by the First
          Selectman at B:14 PM.

2         Discussion - East Lyme Land Trust Specified Properties Known as
          Hathaway Farms, Oswegatchie Hills, and Brainerd Road - The First
          Selectman discussed the next steps in moving the topic to referendum
          by ballot. The Board of Selectmen would vote on requesting the Board of
          Finance to approve the purchase of the properties at its 10/04 regular
          meeting. The Board of Finance will take action on 10/05. The Board of
         Selectmen will meet immediately following the Board of Finance meeting
         and move the purchase question to a Special Town meeting that will
         adjourn to a referendum by ballot on L0/17 at the Community Center
         from B AM to B PM.

3         Adjourn - A motion to adjourn was made by Anne Santoro at B:16
          PM, seconded by Dan Cunningham - unanimous. Meeting adjourned.
