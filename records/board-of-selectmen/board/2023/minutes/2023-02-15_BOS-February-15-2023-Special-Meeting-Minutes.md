2, Budget Discussion

420 library
Library Director Lisa Timothy reported that their proposed budget includes a 4.87o/o
increase; this is primarily due to contractual annual salary and benefit increases.
She noted that she was able to eliminate one full-time position as someone left and
she did not replace them and the workload was absorbed into existing positions. The
Library is partnering with Care and Share to provide internet services to families in
our community through their registration process, Ms. Timothy reported that the use
of the former dispatch building on Boston Post Road is not going to work for a historic
archive / museum; looking into moving the periodicals out of the adult area and
creating a space back there. They are also considering the open space in the lobby
to the right of the library door for a historical area; there is feasibility study currently
underway and there will be more information generated from this study and should
be wrapped up by the fall.

418 Commission on Aging
Senior Center Director Cathy Wilson was in attendance to speak on this budget. Ms,
Wilson stated that a majority of the increases in this budget are due to contractual
salary increases, health insurance premium increases, as well as general inflation.
One of the Senior Center drivers will be retiring and they are looking into filling that
position with two part-time drivers instead of one full-time driver. She reported that
a letter went out to all participants that they will now have a ride cap of 24 round trip
rides per year. Ms, Wilson reported that the decrease in the program subsidy line is
due to the paid instructors being funded out of the special revenue fund; she will
provide a more detailed breakdown of this special revenue line item. She reported
that during the height of the pandemic from July 202L through June 2022, they
assisted 1,691 residents; last year they assisted 1,010; that's an average of about 70
people per day, The new van that was supposed to be received in the summer of
2022 has not yet been delivered; in the meantime they have repaired the existing
van enough that they will continue to use it as a backup once the even after the new
van is received.

42L / 422 Parks and Recreation / Youth Services
Parks and Recreation Director Jerry Lokken was in attendance and presented the
budget with a proposed 8,47o/o increase from last year; this consists primarily of
contractual salary and benefits increase, as well as making one part-time position a
full-time position, and contracting out the cleaning of the parks restrooms. His
primary goal over the next fiscal year is to build back up the special revenue fund,
which was drained during the pandemic, It is funds from this account that will pay
for the replacement of the floating dock at McCook's Park, which had to be removed
from the water as it is past its useful and safe lifespan. The key season of the Parks
and Recreation department is June through September, and he estimates that it
costs approximately $1,100 per location per month during their busy season to keep
these parks in good condition. The new lighting that is proposed will safe money
over time as the town will be able to control better how long the lights stay on at
each individual location. Two items in the capital projects budget are the
replacement of two zero turn mowers, and a new automatic line painter.
