2b.     EV Charging Stations
DISCUSSION: Mr. Seery stated that the Town Engineer is still working with the vendor, and
that he will be at the next meeting with the information. Ms. Hardy asked if the Town would be
receiving all of the money that is collected, and Mr, Seery stated that yes, the Town would set

2c.      Public Safety Building Sealant
DISCUSSION: Mr. Seery stated that he recently met with Public Works DirectorJoe Bragaw,
Town Engineer Bill Scheer, Police Chief Mike Finkelstein, and Bill Silver from Silver Petrucelli &
Associates on January 9th. The Water Infiltration Study that was discussed is attached hereto as
Exhibit #1. It has been discovered that the building will require a sealant be applied
approximately every 10 years or so, due to the porous material that was used; without the
sealant the moisture will seep through, as is happening now. No interior damage due to water
infiltration has been noted to date. Representatives will be attending an upcoming Board of
Selectmen meeting to offer more information and answer questions.

3a.     SustainableCT
DISCUSSION: Ms. Penny Howell-Heller is representative for the Sustainable CT group, and
reported that the Town of East Lyme achieved the Bronze Certification for 2022, She reported
that of the 169 towns in Connecticut, 130 have registered for this program, and only 59 of those
towns has achieved Certification; 33 Bronze and 26 Silver, Gold Certification is a new level of
achievement, so no Town's have yet to achieve that level of certification. She reviewed each
action item achieved by the Town to reach the Bronze Certification; the full list of action items,
as well as the list of actions taken are attached hereto as Exhibit #2.

3b.    I-95 Diversionary Plan
DISCUSSION: Mr. Seery stated that the State came in to meet with him, and also with
Waterford's First Selectman, to discuss their plan of diversion in the event of a major highway
shutdown within East Lyme and Waterford. This was a very interesting and informative meeting
and outlines that plan to take traffic off of 95 down Route B5 to Route 163 in Montville.
