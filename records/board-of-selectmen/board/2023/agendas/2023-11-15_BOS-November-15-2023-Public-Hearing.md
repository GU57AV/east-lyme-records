NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, of a Public Hearing to be held by the East Lyme
Board of Selectmen on Wednesday, November 15,2023, atthe East LymeTown
Hall at 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut beginning at 7:OO p.m. to
receive comments regarding the following ordinance entitled:

      ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING YOUTH, FAMILY AND HUMAN SERVICES
               COMMISSION FOR THE TOWN OF EAST LYME

      Said Ordinance proposes the repeal of Ordinance Section 31.10 entitled
"YOUTH SERVICES COMMISSION" and establishes a new Commission entitled
"YOUTH, FAMILY AND HUMAN SERVICES COMMISSION" to act as an advisory
board to the Board of Selectman and shall be responsible for providing
outreach, community education, and services to meet the needs of youth in the
Towns of East Lyme/Salem and families and individuals in the Town of East
Lyme from youth to senior citizens.

     Copies of the proposed Ordinance is available for review in the East Lyme
Town Clerk's Office and on the Town of East Lyme website www.eltownhall.com,

      Interested persons are invited to attend and be heard.
      Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, on this 1st day of November 2023

                      EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
