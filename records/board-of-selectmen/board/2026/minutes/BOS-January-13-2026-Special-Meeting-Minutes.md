Mr, Cunningham called the special meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen to order at
4:34 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Mr. Cunningham read into record a letter received by Anne Thurlow on behalf of the Zoning
Commission, attached hereto as Exhibit #1.

Town Attorney Tracy Collins explained that she has been a part of two previous Siting
Council public hearings for the Town of East Lyme:

    1) 2010 - Brainerd Road - lots of public outcry against so the town did intervene -
      Siting Council denied the Town's request to intervene deciding that there was a need
      for a tower in that location.
   2) 2015 - Orchards Neighborhood - originally slated for Ancient Highway but public was
      against and there were found to be Indian burial grounds, so the project was moved
      to the Orchards neighborhood where there is a tower now.

Attorney Collins explained that the approximate cost to the Town to intervene could be
upwards of $35,000; but she noted that the Town missed previous deadlines for filing for
intervener status, but that the First Selectman could go to the hearing on January 22nd with
a compilation of questions and concerns gathered from the last couple of meetings.

Attorney Collins stated that Mr. John Drabik has filed as intervener, request form attached
hereto as Exhibit #2, asking that the tower be placed on the site at Ancient Highway. Since
the previous application in 2015, Mr. Drabik was approved to put a radio tower on the
Ancient Highway site and is requesting that this new proposed tower be added to the Ancient
Highway tower,

The Board of Selectman decided that the course of action to take will be to direct the First
Selectman to compile questions and concerns for the Siting Council and attend the hearing
on January 29th. The Siting Council must be notified by January 22nd of the Town's
intentions. The public is encouraged to send in comments to be included in the First
Selectman's presentation; please send said comments to -E_p_mm"e.nt_S"@"ellsy{"n"helLgsm.. All of
the information regarding this application can be found on the Siting Council website at
Docket No 5-45. Mr. Cunningham stated that the Town has received only one comment
regarding this project and it was in favor, attached hereto as Exhibit #3.

Zoning Official Bill Mulholland had the following points:
   . Need more information on the fall zone.
   r The other sites and testimony they provided lack supporting evidence that they made
      a good effort to find a different site.
   . They were invited to attend a Zoning meeting but declined as it is late in the game
      and they met at length with the Board of Selectmen.

         is owned by the same person, but he does not believe this will be approved by the
         Zoning Commission as there will not be enough parking to support a restaurant of
         that size.

It was clarified that the existing tower is not being decommissioned, and that it will remain
at that site with Eversource equipment on it. Eversource owns the tower, and the leases for
Verizon and AT&T are going to expire and Eversource has made the decision to remove all
non-Eversource equipment from said tower.

First Selectman Cunningham had the following points:
    . There has been no ground swell of opposition for the project from the public,
    . The potential cost to the town to intervene is not prudent.
    . The Zoning Commission had requested in their letter that the First Selectman meet
       with Eversource, but the Town Attorney advised that the Town should not intervene
       at this point in time.

Nancy Kalal, B0 Grassy Hill Road and member of the Zoning Commission had the following
points
    a  She did not think that there was enough advertising for this matter.
    a  She does not believe that equipment at this proposed location will improve the bad
       service in the north end of town.
    a  She stated that the Town has a piece of property next to Mr. Drabik's Ancient
       Highway tower and that could be an option for the tower.
    a  She does not believe the applicant did their due diligence in the search process.
    a  She believes the Town should intervene despite the cost.

It was decided that Bill Mulholland would work with the Zoning Commission to get comments
to the Town Attorney and the First Selectman by Friday the 16th. The Town Attorney must
compile the comments and get them to the Siting Council by January 22nd. The First
Selectman will work with the Town Attorney to finalize the comments and prepare for his
presentation to the Siting Council on January 29th.
