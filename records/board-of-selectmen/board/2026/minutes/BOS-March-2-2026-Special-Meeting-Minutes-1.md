225 East Lyme Fire Service
Chief Bundy spoke on this budget; his presentation is attached hereto as Exhibit #1.
   . The goal is to have 18 full-time firefighters on staff.
   . They are requesting six additional firefighters be added in this budget in order
      to successfully man each of the three fire stations with two fire fighters 24/7.
   . As with the Police budget, it is suggested that three firefighters start on July 1,
       2026, and the additional three start on January L,2027.
   . Should only three firefighters be hired, the overtime budget will be high again;
      having a full staff of firefighters will bring the overtime line down.
   r There was discussion on boosting the volunteer program; currently there is not
      a lot of interest. There is an incredible amount of time and resources that go
      into training volunteers, for both the town and the individual.
   o The Appendix 13 Recommendations from the 2017 Fire Study were discussed
      and are attached hereto as Exhibit #2.

      cannot be forced to take a shift.
   o Chief Bundy reported that he has a number of applications for volunteers, but
      that only one has all of the necessary certifications,
   . It was asked if the town could only staff two of the three firehouses; the
      answer is no because through the lease we have with the State for that
      propefty, the firehouse must be staffed.
   o Under the current contract which would need to be re-negotiated to change
      this, every Saturday is a Kelly Day. A Kelly Day is a designated day off, in this
      case Saturday, that firefighters receive as part of their work schedule to serve
      as a crucial component in managing work-life balance.
