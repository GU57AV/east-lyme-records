NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme Board of Selectmen
at 7:00 p.m. on January 2L,2026 atthe East LymeTown Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue,
Niantic, Connecticut to consider an amendment to Ordinance Section 52.2L entitled "Various
Water Charges". The proposed amendment raises the interest rate on overdue fees and
charges imposed by the Water and Sewer Commission from L2o/o to 18o/o. Copies of the full
text of the proposed Ordinance are on file for public inspection at the office of the East Lyme
Town Clerk, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut and at www.eltownhall.com to
which reference may be had. Interested persons are invited to attend and be heard. Dated at
East Lyme, Connecticut, on this 7th day of January, 2026. East Lyme Board of Selectmen.

Ben North, Chief Operating Officer of Water and Sewer, stated that the rate was approved
in 2023 by the Water and Sewer Commission who were, at the time, deemed the authority
to do so. In November2023 the new rate of 18o/o started, and users that are late on their
payments have paid the 18o/o since that time. There were a number of public meetings
held in advance of this increase, but no public attended and spoke. t8o/o is a universal
rate for late payments, so this brings this policy in line with the others in Town. Since this
change took place in2023, tonight's action is to simply to the housekeeping and amend
the ordinance as it was not done previously. Documents referenced are attached hereto as
Exhibit A.

To watch the full meeting and to find a full transcript using the Town of East Lyme YouTube
channel, please go to eltownhall.com/government/videos/.
