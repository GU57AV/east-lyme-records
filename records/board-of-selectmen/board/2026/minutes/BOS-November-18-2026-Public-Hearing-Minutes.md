Mr. Cunningham opened the Public Hearing at 7:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme Board of

of the proposed ordinance as the result of information presented in the public
hearing. Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, on this 4th day of November,2026. East
Lyme Board of Selectmen.

Mr. Cunningham explained that the public hearing tonight is to accept public
comment on a very narrow issue; this is to adopt an amendment to the already
adopted ordinance, which would bring the ordinance in line with the State statute. It
was noted that if no action was taken to amend this language, the matter would end
up following State statute anyway because State statutes take precedence. The First
Selectman lsked that if anyone in attendance was going to speak on the automated
cameras in general, that they should wait until the regular meeting and speak during
public comment. Town Attorney Tracy Collins then intervened and stated that the
ordinance is a piece of the whole camera program, so it would be appropriate for the
public to speak about anything related to said program during this public hearing. It
did not have to specifically relate to the ordinance amendment before the public
tonight.

There was no one in attendance to speak on this proposed amendment.
