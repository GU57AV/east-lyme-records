2a.    Automated Ticketing Ordinance
MOrroN (2)
DISCUSSION: Chief Glenn reiterated that there is no cost to the town for this program; the
administrative fee pays for all costs, including the study. The camera will only be capturing the
license plate and no identifying information as to the driver. The vendor will regularly calibrate
the system, The tickets will not be reported to insurance companies or the DMV. Once reviewed
by East Lyme staff, the tickets will be mailed by the vendor. The entire contract can be adjusted
and tailored to our Town needs by the Board of Selectmen, The first step would be to sign the
agreement with the selected company, Altimint. They are the vendor he will work with to prepare
the application packet and help us navigate the public hearing process to determine where we
want to place the cameras. Once the location is chosen, the camera will remain in that spot; it
cannot be moved. The goal of the program is not to bring in revenue, but to reduce speeding.
Mr. Deeble expressed concern about where the data is going and who gets to see it once in the
hands of the vendor. It was discussed that approval tonight would allow the Chief to begin
working on this, but that the Board of Selectmen have plenty of time and opportunity to adjust
the contract to our specifications.

Ms. Cicchiello MOVED that the Board of Selectmen approve and adopt the proposed ordinance
titled "Ordinance Authorizing The Use of Automated Traffic Enforcement Safety Devices" as

 Proposed Amendment to Ordinance S 52.21, entitled VARIOUS WATER CHARGES.
      (A) The Town Water and Sewer Commission may impose the following charges upon the
 owner or owners of property which is served with water by the Commission; annual
 private hydrant charge; annual private sprinkler charge; meter deposit; frozen meter
 repair or replacement fee; fee for installation and disconnection of meters for seasonal
 residences; meter test fees; fee for inspection of inground water service pipelines being
 installed in subdivisions; charge for labor provided to owner at owners request for
 corrections or repairs that are owner's responsibility; charge for sale of materials held in
 stock by Water Department.

  (B) The meter deposit described in division (A) above shall be due and payable on or
 before the date that the Commission provides water service to the owner or owners of
 property requesting such service. All other charges described in division (A) above shall be
 due and payable when the property owner is billed by the Commission.

      (C) The amount of such charges shall be established and from time to time revised by the
Town Water and Sewer Commission, and shall reasonably reflect the costs incurred by the
Commission in providing such services and materials. The Commission shall establish and
revise such charges in accordance with the provisions of Conn. Gen. Stat. $ 7-239, as
amended from time to time.

   (D) From the time that any fee or charge described in this section becomes due and
payable, it shall bear interest at the rate of 11J:8_o/o per annum until paid, unless paid
within 30 days from the date it becomes due and payable. Any such fee or charge shall, at
the time it becomes due and payable, become a lien upon the property, Any such lien shall
be liable to be foreclosed in the same manner provided by law, for the foreclosure of tax
liens.

      (El Any such lien shall not continue to exist for a period longer than one year after any
such fee or charge becomes due and payable, unless within that period a certificate signed
by the First Selectman or his or her designee describing the premises upon which the lien
exists, the reason on account of which it is claimed, and the amount claimed by the town as
a lien shall be filed in the town land records. Any such lien shall cease to exist whenever a
certificate discharging the same signed by the First Selectman or his or her designee shall
be filed in the town land records. Such liens shall be discharged by the First Selectman or
his or her designee upon payment of such unpaid charges, interest fas set forth in division
(D) above), and a lien fee which shall be an amount which is equal to the sum of a $5
administrative fee and the amount charged by the Town Clerk for recording and releasing
such lien.
