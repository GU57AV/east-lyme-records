   7-3. Discussion - Freedom of Information Act

      Note Mr. Cunningham summarized several key points regarding FOI requirements and the responsibilities
     of town officials. He noted that civil penalties of up to $5,000 may be imposed personally on officials or
     attorneys for egregious violations of FOI law, underscoring the importance of compliance. He emphasized
     that FOI requests must receive prompt acknowledgment—typically within four business days and must be
     followed by a good‑faith search for any existing records, though the Town is not required to create new
     documents in response. He also explained that the Town uses a central FOI email system to ensure
     consistent tracking and management of requests, and that some submissions may require redaction of
     confidential information before release. The Board agreed to invite Russell Blair from the FOIC to provide
     updated training for staff and officials.
