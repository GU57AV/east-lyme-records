2b.     Cini Dock Leases
DISCUSSION: Parks and Recreation DirectorJerry Lokken explained that, during budget.
discussions, he, the First Selectman and the Finance Director agreed it would be beneficial to
transfer oversight of the leases from the Board of Selectmen and the Town Attorney to the
Parks and Recreation Department. This department will now take an active role in managing
the leases and the docks themselves, and the money collected from the leases will 9o into the
parks and Recreation special revenue fund. This makes sense because it is the Parks and
Recreation Department that works closely with the captains during the year on all things,
including parking and the bathrooms. This will not require additional staffing as the staff
already does sufport these boats and the docks. This will also save the town money because
we will be eliminating the need for the town attorney.
