3c.    Parks and Recreation Youth Baseball Field Reservations
DISCUSSION: Mr. Cunningham opened the discussion regarding the youth baseball field
reservations, stating that there has been some angst over the situation and he wanted to have
an open discussion about it with all of the stakeholders. The First Selectman introduced Attorney
Robert Day, who is both an attorney and has a college degree in accounting, and stated that he
had asked Attorney Day to perform a forensic audit on the field usage situation.

Attorney Day explained that he was tasked with looking at field usage during the years 2023,
2024 and 2025 and also to concentrate on certain organizations. He gave a summary of his final
spreadsheet/report, which is attached hereto as Exhibit #1.

Parks and Recreation Director Jerry Lokken addressed the board and presented his information
from a Parks and Recreation standpoint, prestation attached hereto as Exhibit #2. He stated that
he compared Attorney Day's report with one he pulled from the Parks and Recreation reservation
system, which is different, and the results were fairly similar with a couple of discrepancies. For
example, Attorney Day's report says in 2024Babe Ruth used the field for a total of 453 hours;
Park and Recreation total is 449 hours, The one discrepancy is in the East Lyme Little League line
and it is so far off that he would need to compare where the data was collected from for Attorney
Day's report. Little League is the biggest user of the fields so he does not think that Attorney
Day's data was inclusive of all factors. Mr. Lokken stated that the current policy allows for the
Director to use his discretion when modifying the fees, either up or down; they are finalizing a
new process that makes this piece of it more transparent and reportable,

Eric Parker, CT Shell Shockers, and Corey Farrior, 33 Corey Lane, also representing the CT Shell
Shockers, addressed the board and reported that they have approximately 150 kids in the
program, with approximately B0o/o of them being East Lyme residents,

Steven Licitra, 62 Spring Glen Road, President of East Lyme Babe Ruth, has been in this position
for about eight years. They have three charter teams (sometimes four) which play in three
seasons and have around 36-40 kids registered for this spring season, They are a 501(3)c so
they are not charged to use the fields through Parks and Recreation. The Babe Ruth teams often
play in tournaments and travel games, but that is at the expense of the student athlete, He
stated that when the Shell Shockers play under the 501(3)c umbrella of the Babe Ruth
organization, they are then considered not for profit.

The First Selectman asked for someone to explain why this matter has escalated to this point and
why there seems to be animosity between the Little League and Babe Ruth organizations.

Jerry Lokken explained that there has always been a set schedule as to when all the different
organizations use the fields. This year, the Little League organization made some changes to their
schedule that directly impacted the field usage of the Babe Ruth / Shell Shockers organizations.
Historically the Little League has used the fields Monday-Friday, leaving the fields available on the
weekends for the travel teams. This year, the Little League has added events on the weekends
for the first time, which has directly impacted the organizations that use the fields on the
weekends and has made scheduling difficult,

Dr. Benjamin Armstrong, L2 Naomi Lane, stated that he is a member of the Parks and Recreation
Commission and on the Board of the Little League, as well as a member of the Shell Shockers
organization. He expressed that although CT Shell Shockers is a for profit organization, there is
very little profit that it made; the goal is to provide enhanced recreation to the kids,

Ms. Carlson stated that she wants to have further discussion to understand Mr. Licitra's
comments on how the for profit organization falls under the nonprofit umbrella.

Rob Tukey, introduced himself as the Chairman of the Parks and Recreation Commission, and
explained that the parents are driving the popularity of the kids being on the travel teams
because they offer advanced training and more opportunities. This turn of events has caused the
Babe Ruth organization to be effected negatively. He explained the course of how the travel
teams came in and a lot of kids started playing in that program and in order to keep the Babe
Ruth organization running they had to change the way they were doing things.

presented tonight and put this on an upcoming Board of Selectman agenda for further discussion
