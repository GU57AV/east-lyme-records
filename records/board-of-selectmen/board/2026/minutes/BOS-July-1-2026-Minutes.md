3a.    Noise Ordinance Variance Request - Parks and Recreation
DISCUSSION: Mr. Lokken explained that they have been receiving complaints from a resident
when they perform needed landscaping in the Main Street and Pennsylvania Avenue area, He
noted that to effectively clean and landscape in those areas, they need to do it early before
people start heading downtown; they have been starting at 5:30 a.m, The Town has a noise
ordinance which restricts loud noise before 7:00 a,m, in the morning, but the Charter also allows
for a variance. To that, he is requesting that the Board of Selectmen grant him a variance to
the ordinance to allow his crew to perform those necessary activities from 5:30 a.m. until 7:00
a.m. every other week from April through October. He noted that his crew has been operating in
this fashion for the past five years with no complaints up to now; all the current complaints have
come from one person.
MOrroN (s)
Mr. Deeble MOVED that a variance to the Town's Noise Ordinance is hereby granted to the Parks
and Recreation Department for trimming and blowing activities along Main Street and
Pennsylvania Avenue from approximately 5:30 a.m. until 7:00 a,m., approximately bi-weekly
from April through October each year.
Seconded by Ms. Carlson. Motion passed 5-0.
