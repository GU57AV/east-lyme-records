        On March 6,2026 Cindy Collins, a member of the Board of Selectmen resigned
 from the Board "effective immediately". This memorandum outlines the process the
 Board of Selectmen must follow to fill the vacancy.

     :   Vacancies in Elective Offices are covered in Section2.S of the Town Charter (a
 copy is attached). Section 2.5.1 provides "the Board of Selectmen shall fill by
 appointment a vacancy in an elective office within thirty days from the time that office
 becomes vacant, except as othenrvise provided by law." Section 2.5.3 provides that
 "vacancies on the Board of Selectmen shall be filled in the manner prescribed by the
 general statutes." Section 2.5.4 provides "an appointee to a vacancy in an elective
 office shall serve the remainder of the term that was filled."

     'i-r Connecticut General Statutes 9-222 (a copy is attached) governs the process for
 filling.vacancies in the office of selectmen and provides that when a vacancy occurs it
 "shall be filled within thirty days after the day of its occurrence by the remaining
 mEmbers of the Board of Selectmen."

        Connecticut General Statutes 9-222 provides that any person appointed to fill a
selectman vacancy "shall serve for the portion of the term remaining unexpired or until a
special election called as...provided upon petition." A "petition" is when a special
election may be triggered if electors equal to five percent of the names on the last-
completed registry list, but not fewer than fifty electors, file a petition "no later than
fifteen days after the appointment by the remaining selectmen." Upon filing of such
"petition", the town clerk must "forthwith" call a special election to be held in accordance
withispecified procedures set forth in the statute.

       ln conclusion, the remaining members of the Board of Selectmen have until April
6,2026 to appoint a qualified person to serve for the unexpired portion of Cindy Collins'
term or until a special election is called upon "petition" as provided above. The
appointment must comply with the minority representation statute. 1

RE: East Lyme Public Trust Path Lighting Project
This is a request to be put on the agenda for an upcoming Board of Selectman meeting to present
information on the path lighting project. No BOS approvals are needed, however, those involved desire
to keep the Board notified of progress.

The East Lyme Public Trust is taking the lead in fundraising for safety lights along the path from McCook
Point Park to Hole in the Wall. The proposed solar-powered light bollards can be controlled to come on
after events or based on sunrise and sunset times. The lights would be an aesthetically pleasing addition
that helps park visitors safely travel between the two highly used and valued beach properties,
especially in the summer for events like the beach concerts and Celebrate East Lyme Fireworks. The
Zoning Official has given administrative approval for the lights.

The Public Trust has a long history of fundraising for dozens of projects and events that benefit the
community - the Niantic Bay Boardwalk, the McCook Bandshell, the Liberty Green Flagpole, beach
concerts, Buoys By the Bay, bench and tree memorials, and much more. When the ELPT gets involved,
the community knows that it is done well and that it adds to the livability of the town.

The project cost is 527,470 and plans call for the entire amount to be obtained through fundraising.

who will also be attending.
