4a.    Ex-Officio Reports

Jason Deeble reported:
   o The Commission on the Conservation of Natural Resources heard a presentation on the
       dangers of using SGARs (Second-Generation Anticoagulant Rodenticides) These are
       powerful rodent poisons designed to kill rats and mice after a single feeding. Once the
       critters consume the poison, they walk around potentially spreading the poison to other
       critters. He noted that in the future this group will be asking for a townwide ban on
       such poisons on all town property; Old Lyme and Waterford have expressed interest in
       participating in a regional effort to ban use of these products. Reports show that in
       2025, -2,000 children were reportedly poisoned. There will be a public presentation on
       this topic on November 5th in Old Saybrook entitled "Think Outside the Bait Box".

Re: East Lyme Youth and Family Services                                                             October 19,2026



Dan,



I am following up on a conversation we had approximately a month ago in your office. Just to recap, I had
concems following a conversation with Don Hull and subsequent emails from Sarah Firmin. The subject of
these communications involved services offered by East Lyme through your Youth and Family Service
Department. It was stated a number of times that these services are available to Salem residents. This is not
the first time that this has been offered. A few years ago, Salem School was contacted by Ms. Firmin to post
a flyer on the school's website offering social services to Salem residents. At that time, I was on the Board
of Education. I was also an East Lyme Town Hall employee and was aware there was an ordinance passed
when Kevin Seery was First Selectman establishing the YOUTH, FAMILY AND HUMAN SERVICES
COMMISSION. It is very generous of East Lyme to extend those services to Salem students that attend
East Lyme High School and is very much appreciated.

My concem was that I knew our town did not pay or contribute to the services being offered. Like any town
we would love to get services for our residents that someone else pays for, but it is not fair to the residents
of East Lyme who have the tax burden of funding this department. Full disclosure, I own property in East
Lyme.

In the future, and taking into consideration our budget constraints, I can envision where the Town of Salem
would partner with East Lyme for social services, but we do not have an agreement at this time.

I hope this clarifies where Salem stands on this issue and appreciate any clarity your or your Board of
Selectmen can provide.

All the best to you in the upcoming budget season!


Respectfully,

Sue Spang
First Selectwoman
