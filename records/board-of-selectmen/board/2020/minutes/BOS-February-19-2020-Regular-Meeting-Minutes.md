3a.     Ex-Officio Reports
        Mr, Seery reported that atthe Parks & Recreation Commission meeting they discussed the necessity
to increase the parking rates to reflect the new Connecticut State sales tax being imposed on public parking
fees. The new 6.350/o tax imposed on the existing parking fees will be passed on to the residents. This new
tax does not apply to walk in visitors, just parking.
         Mr. Dagle reported that the Public Safety Building Vision Committee voted to go out to bid and
expect to do so by the end of next week with a return date sometime by the end of March. There will be a
bidder's conference scheduled for some time around the second week in March, Once all of the bids have
been received, a subcommittee will be assigned to hold the public meeting to open the bids and review
them. That subcommittee will then bring thcir fccdback and recommendations to the full vision oommittee,
who will in turn bring their findings to the Board of Selectrnan. Mrs. Hardy asked for the status of the
second floor. Mr, Dagle stated that the second floor will remain unoccupied, but that the IT equipment

         Mrs. Hardy stated that she would like to see WJ Tree Service remain the contractor of choice for
