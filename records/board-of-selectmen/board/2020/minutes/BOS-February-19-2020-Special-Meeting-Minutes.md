#109 - tT
   Mr, Bragaw addressed the Board and he stated that the Town is falling behind in lT gave an overview of the
progression of the lT staff over the years, beginning with Bill Scheer early on and then including Carmen Ames, who
stepped up in around 2004 to become full-time lT support.
   Mr. Scheer explained that when he began overseeing the lT systems around 2002, there were a handful of computers
in various offices, a few printers and the police department only had four computers. Today there are hundreds of pieces
of equipment in various departments, and the police department alone have more than 20 computers in the building; not
to mention all of the computer equipment in the vehicles including different software applications that need to be
maintained. Also adding to the complexity is networking all of that with emergency services; dispatch, fire marshal, and
the fire and ambulance services. Early on, Carmen Ames stepped up and took on mid-level lT duties, but the needs have
exceeded our current staff's expertise, Also adding to the shortfall in staff expertise is the fact that the key employees
that were involved in all of this infrastructure and supported the systems have all retired and taken with them their
expertise and knowledge. A few years back the Town brought in Star Computers on contract to help with the lT needs,
especially with the public safety infrastructure which has become increasingly complex and specialized. The town can no
longer support the growing lT needs with the existing model,
   Mr. Bragaw stated that the need for improvement has been recognized, and a group has formed as an lTsteering
committee to begin working on a town-wide solution to this issue. The committee currently consists of the Director of
Public Works, The Deputy Director of Public Works, the current lT Supervisor, the First Selectman, the Director of Finance,
the Chief of Police, the Fire Chiefs from both Flanders and Niantic Fire Departments, and the President of the Ambulance
Association, They have only met once so far, and he stated that they are first concentrating on assessing the current
infrastructure and working together to build a strong town-wide infrastructure. Star Computers was invited to attend
that meeting, but they are not a member of the Committee, lt was also decided that in order to truly make this a strong
town-wide infrastructure, the Board of Education will be asked to join the steering committee and work together on a
shared lT Department. A quick comparison was done of a couple of comparable communities and East Lyme is behind in
our lT efforts.
  All ofthe Emergency Responders in attendance concurred that that town needs to focus on infrastructure, that all of
these departments should work together, and an lT Director position needs to be created to oversee and manage the
entire system. lt was agreed that each department's individual budget line item for lT services will be pooled into a
shared line item for the entire town; and to hopefully include the Board of Education.
