                             EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                      SPECIAL EMERGENCY MEETING OF MARCH 16,2020
                                                 MINUTES



PRESENT: MarkNickerson, Kevin Seery, Rose Ann Hardy, Marc Salerno, Dan Cunningham and Paul Dagle

Mr. Nickerson called the meeting to order at7:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Mr. Nickerson explained that this special emergency meeting has been called to discuss COVID-19
(Coronavirus) and its impact on Town services, departments and municipal meetings. He explained that he
signed a Declaration of State of Emergency for the town of East Lyme earlier in the day, and Mr. Seery
read the declaration into record. Said declaration is attached hereto as Exhibit 1. Mr. Nickerson stated that
this declaration gives him the authority to do things such as close town buildings and cancel municipal
buildings, but that it also makes the Town eligible for financial assistance in the wake of this emergency.
Mr. Nickerson then read into record a letter outlining East Lyme's current response to this emergency,
attached hereto as Exhibit 2.



MOTTON (1)
Mr. Seery MOVED to support the Declaration of State of Emergency for the Town of East Lyme declared
by the First Selectman, Mark C. Nickerson on March 16,2020 at 1:00 p.m.
Seconded by Mr. Cunningham. Motion passed 6-0.



MOTTON (2)
Mr. Seery MOVED to adjourn the March 16,2020, special emergency meeting of the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at7:44 p.m.
Seconded by Mr. Cunningham. Motion passed 6-0.



Respectfully submitted,

                             DECLARATION OF STATE OF EMERGENCY
                                 FOR THE TOWN OF EAST LYME

     WHEREAS, on March 10,2020, the Governor of the State of Connecticut declared a
     public health emergency and civil preparedness emergency for the State of
     Connecticut, pursuant to Connecticut General Statutes 19a-131 and 28-9, in response
     to the global pandemic of COVID-19 disease associated with a novel coronavirus that is
     currently affecting multiple countries and states; and

     WHEREAS, on March 13,2A20, the President of the United States declared a national
     emergency to combat the coronavirus that is currently infecting the population of the
     United States; and

     WHEREAS, the effect of the transmission of COVID-19 has resulted in the spread of
     infections in Connecticut and throughout the country, as well as resulting in shoftages of
     personal protective equipment and other supplies that could jeopardize public safety
     and civil preparedness; and

     WHEREAS, in response to the issuance of declarations of a public heath emergency
     and civil preparedness emergency by the Governor of this State, and a national
     emergency by the President of the United States, and the spread of COVID-19 as a
     result of the global pandemic, the Chief Executive Officer of the Town of East Lyme, its
     First Selectman, Mark C, Nickerson, hereby finds that a state of emergency now exists
     in the Town of East Lyme; and

     WHEREAS, due to the significant danger to public health posed by the spread of

     health and safety, the First Selectman must take action for the purpose of ensuring civil
     preparedness and mitigating the effects of this public health emergency situation upon
     the residents of the Town of East Lyme,

lmpactsoftheviruscrisisarebeingfeltallovertown, Wearestrugglingtokeepupwiththerapid
changes and updates.

As of tonight at 8PM, all town buildings will be closed to the general public until further notice, Town
Hall phones will be answered during normal business hours at 860-739-6931. Other departments in the
Community center and other buildings will do the same. I have asked town employees to continue to
report to work normally and do their business via phone, regular mail and email, We are confident that
we will find workarounds for most of the issues that will come up.

The town transfer station will remain open during normal business hours but they have changed the
way they do business. The public will not be allowed in the toll house/weigh station. The only form of
payment accepted will be credit cards. ln addition, town personnelwill not be allowed to assist patrons
unload their items,

Trash and recycle pick-ups will continue under regular schedules until further notified

While the senior center is closed, transportation for our seniors will continue on a limited basis (medical
and grocery only). Those citizens already know the drill...they should contact the Senior Center for
further information and to schedule a ride.

There will be NO PUBLIC MEETINGS for any commission for the duration of the town hall shutdown,
While we are hopeful that we can resume operations by the beginning of next month, we are being told
to plan for a longer period of time.

We are hopefulthat we will not have to fully shutdown all non-essentialtown services. However, the
rapid pace of the events and cancellations surrounding this has caused ourteam to begin to prepare for
different scenarios. Our town's leadership team and Emergency Operations Center has been busy
planning for the possible next steps.

Closings that were previously announced continue and are as follows

        All public school, activities including sports. All activities at the schools.
        Park and Rec - suspended all program activities
        Senior Center - all programs and activities including caf6 meals. Meals on Wheels will continue
        Fire Marshal - prioritizing inspection schedule
        Town Clerk & Registrars - Awaiting word from Sec of State's office re: upcoming primary
        Absentee ballots will be available on AprilTrh unless primary is cancelled (4/2g)
        Water Department - Water meter replacement has been suspended
        Library - closed




Emergency Services:

        Fire and EMT-A number of procedures to enhance the safety of personneland their patients
        have been put in place.
        Police - procedures in place to limit exposure, overall state of awareness.




This is an ever-evolving plan. Closing public buildings to the general public and keeping employees at
work has been referred to as Plan A, I am hopefulthat we do not have to implement further reductions
in services however our team is already working on alternate plans to include only essential personnel
and first responders working and a total reduction of town non-essential services.

I ask everyone to take extra caution during this period of time, Avoiding this virus can mean the
