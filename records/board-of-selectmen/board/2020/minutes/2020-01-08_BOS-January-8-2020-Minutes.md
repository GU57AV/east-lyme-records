        Holly Cheeseman, 15 Mitchell Drive, Niantic, and State Representative forEastLyme and Salem,

announced that the State released a total $9.1 million in state grants, of which East Lyme will receive a

program gave a presentation; for more information on this program please visit https://veteransrallypoint,com.

on sex crimes, to name a few. She thanked everyone for their time and hard work.
