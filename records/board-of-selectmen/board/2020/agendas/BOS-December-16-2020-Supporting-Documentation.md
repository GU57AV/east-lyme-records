2a. CNRE - $19,118 ~ Communications Tower
MOTION (3)
DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that this project was approved by the Board of Selectmen back in
August of 2017, and the tower is now built and ready for us to move our equipment over from the temporary
tower located on Walnut Hill Road. He noted that this agreement was entered into in August of 2017 with
Marcus Communications / Mostowy Towers, and that the Town owns the top twenty feet of the tower to use
for our equipment. The temporary tower on Walnut Hill Road has improved emergency communication in
the north end of town, and the new tower will improve the service in this area, as well as in the Giants Neck
Heights-area. Mr. Nickerson noted that Dick Morris was a key player in this project, as well as Chris Taylor;
both gentlemen have left the Town since then, so he thanked Chief Finkelstein for picking up where they left
off in seeing this project through to fruition.

Chief Finkelstein stated that the lease was signed in 2017 and will be utilized for Town of East Lyme
Police, Fire, EMS and school buses. He reported that the temporary tower has served us well, but this new
tower will provide better service, and as Mr. Nickerson noted it is high enough to provide clear service to
Giants Neck Heights. This tower is equipped with a propane generator in case of power failure. Chief
Finkelstein stated that Marcus Communications owns the tower and the Town of East Lyme owns exclusive
use of the top 20 feet of the tower for emergency services and bus communication. He stated that this new

4a. Ex-Officio Reports

Mr. Cunningham reported that the Historic Properties Commission continues to work on the grant for
the roof at the Sam Smith Farmstead, and they anticipate submitting that application soon. Mr. Cunningham
reported that some East Lyme High School seniors, Nicole Burgon, Maggie D'Amico, Caroline French and
Natalie Libby, created a website called “History In The Making”, which highlights East Lyme’s local history.
This webpage can be found by visiting the Historic Properties section of the East Lyme Town Hall website.

Mr. Salerno reported that the Planning Commissicn approved their FY21/22 budget. He stated that
because of the pandemic and because of the complexity of these meetings, it has been decided that the
Administrative Assistant will attend all land use meetings to run the Zoom controls until the pandemic is over
and we are meeting in person at Town Hall. He reported that they closed the public hearing on the Old Black
Point Road matter, and that they scheduled a special meeting for December 15" to address the matter on
Upper Kensington. Mr. Salerno reported that both Kirk Scott and Michelle Williams were reappointed to
their respective positions on this commission. He reported that the Town Building Committee is finishing up
loose ends on the schools’ renovation project, and that they are working on the Public Safety Building and
Mr. Dagle will provide a brief report.

Mr. Dagle reported the work has begun on the public safety building, and they are anticipating a mid-
June 2021 completion. He reported that this year’s Jingle Bell 5K run by the Brian Dagle Foundation will be
happening this year virtually, and also includes a scavenger hunt this year. For more information please visit
nianticjinglebell5k.itsyourrace.com.

4b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson reported that they are currently working on the tri town water agreement, which has
been in place for ~30 years. East Lyme, Waterford and New London participate in this agreement, and he
noted that New London has a separate agreement with Old Lyme that is in place. This year’s light parade will
take place on December 12" with December 13" as a rain date. This year, because of the pandemic, the floats
will be parked at McCooks and the spectators will drive through to view them, The Cini Park fishing dock
leases are up for renewal at the end of December, and the subcommittee is working on reviewing the
applications for discussion and action at the next regular meeting.

Mr. Nickerson reported that it has been decided to utilize an Admin at all of the land use meetings to
run the Zoom controls. As these meetings and public hearings are heavily attended and the results will
generally carry legal consequences for the Commission as well as for the Town. He reported that the work is
wrapping up at Vets Park, and he noted that the parking lot is being re-worked to add more parking spaces;
they are also working on the puddle issues by installing new curbing and adding a slight slope for runoff to
the sewers.

Mr. Nickerson reported that he has been meeting with fellow leaders from our neighboring
municipalities to coordinate vaccination efforts, including shared campaigns and distribution of the vaccines.
Priority will be given to first responders, healthcare workers, SEAT bus drivers, ete. They are working with
the governor’s office to build the priority roster. He thanked everyone for their well wishes after his recent
hip replacement and reported that he is doing really well and that he is on the mend. He appreciates what a
caring and supportive community we live in and is grateful to have had 22 years involvement in town
government.

EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN Agenda Item
SUBCOMMITTEE TO REVIEW CINI PARK FISHING DOCK LEASES
MEETING OF DECEMBER 7, 2020
MINUTES

This meeting was held via Zoom online meeting services and was available for the public to view, listen and
participate via videoconference and dial-in options.

PRESENT: Mare Salerno, Paul Dagle and Dan Cunningham
The meeting was called to order at 5:25 p.m.

It was noted that at approximately 5:35 p.m., this meeting was interrupted by hackers and forced to shut
down. The meeting resumed at approximately 5:45 p.m. with different, more secure Zoom meeting
credentials due to the security issues with the original credentials.

All four applications were discussed and compared to newly adopted criteria approved by the Board of
Selectman. The committee was unanimous in recommending continuing the lease for Michael Stepski, who is
an existing lessee. There was discussion about renewing Michael Beebe’s lease or offering the dock to
applicant Joel Lizza. The subcommittee could not come up with a consensus for the third dock, so it was
recommended to present both options to the Board of Selectman at their meeting on December 16th.

Dan Cunningham moved to adjourn at 6:15 with Paul Dagle seconding.

MOTION (1)

Mr. Cunningham MOVED to adjourn the December 7, 2020, meeting of the East Lyme Board of Selectmen
Subcommittee to Review Cini Park Fishing Dock Leases at 6:15 p.m.
