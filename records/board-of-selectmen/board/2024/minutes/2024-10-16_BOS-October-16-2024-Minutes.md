3a.     EV Charging Rates
DISCUSSION: Town Engineer Alex Klose was present for this discussion. He reported that
they are getting close to installing the EV chargers in Town; a Level 3 faster charger at Town
Hall, and a Level 2 charger at the Hope Street and Methodist Streets parking lots, Three
things that he needs the Board to consider in setting rates are 1) the base fee, 2) Incentives
(town vehicle, resident, etc.), and 3) imposing an Idle Fee, An idle fee is an incentive for
people to remove their car when charging is complete instead of leaving it there for an
extended time. He recommends rounding up or down to the whole cent, and using language
that fees are on top of the base supply charge. He will bring examples of surrounding towns'
Idle Fees structure. The base fees from surrounding towns are as follows for Level 2
chargers; New London $0.25, Old Saybrook $0.25, Madison $0.20, Guilford $0.35, Branford
$0.16.
