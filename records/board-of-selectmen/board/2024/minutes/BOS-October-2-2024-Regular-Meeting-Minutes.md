Ms. Donna Palumbo, 133 Black Point Road, Niantic, spoke in favor of the dog park project.
She has been involved with trying to build a dog park in town for ten years and is
disappointed that the Town has not offered more support as with the pickleball courts, She is
hopeful that the Board will reconsider supporting this project.

East Lyme community and will be celebrating National Hispanic Heritage Month. She feels

2b.    EV Charging Rates Discussion

Alex Klose, Town Engineer, reported that this is a project that was discussed and approved
at a previous Board of Selectmen meeting under the previous Board, and we are now a
couple of months away from installation. There will be three chargers installed; one at Town
Hall, one at the Methodist Street parking lot, and one at the Hope Street parking lot. Now is
the time to decide what rate the town will charge, and he outlined this information, which is
attached hereto as Exhibit #1. He is hoping that the Board will be able to set the rates at
their October 16th meeting. He noted that the average rate being charged in our region is
around $O.aal he will provide a chart with rates from existing area charging stations.

2c.      Appointments to the Fair Rent Commission
DISCUSSION: Mr, Cunningham stated that of the five people appointed to the Fair Rent
Commission at the last meeting, two of those people are unable to assume those seats so that
leaves two positions to fill. Everyone that has been appointed needs to go o the Town Clerk's
office to be sworn in. Once everyone is sworn in, they will schedule the first meeting, be sure
to file an agenda, then a chairperson will need to be appointed. At that time, the commission
will begin their business. There is an open Alternate seat; please contact a member of the
Board of Selectmen if you are interested in being on this commission.

2026.
Seconded by Ms. Carlson. Motion passed 6-0.

MOTTON (s)

the Fair Rent Commission for the Town of East Lyme with a term to expire on January 3,2028.
Seconded by Mr. Deeble. Motion passed 6-0,

2d. Presentation on Open Space by Art Carlson and Atty. Anthony Novak
A presentation was given by Attorney Anthony Novak, Counsel for the East Lyme Land Trust,
and Art Carlson. The Land Trust would like the Town to purchase the 6.1 acres referred to as
"The Point". Maps and supporting documents provided during the meeting are attached
hereto as Exhibit #2, It was noted that there is a DEEP $400k grant available to purchase
this land; granted to the East Lyme Land Trust and conveyed to the Town. Purchasing this
piece of property will protect Well 5, which is the only well upstream of I-95. The landowner
Mr. Steve Harney has given the Town until the end of the year to decide, then he has another
buyer who is interested.

Department of Economic and Community Development, and to affix the corporate seal, if any.

Town Attorney Ed O'Connell was present and explained that at their initial meeting, the
commission will elect a chairperson. They will need to be trained in the Freedom Of
Information Act, they will need to get up to speed with the ordinance, get familiar with the
statutes, and they will need to create all of the necessary forms, The ordinance is specific in
that the commission must consider thirteen specific points in making their decisions. A
dedicated staff person will need to be appointed right away to get started with the forms and
ither administrative duties.

member from the Water and Sewer Commission. The Board of Selectmen will appoint their
members tonight, and the other two Boards will appoint their member(s) at their next
regularly scheduled meetings.
