DISCUSSION: Mr. MacKenzie handed out a couple of maps, attached hereto as Exhibit #2. He
explained that there are four docks available for lease; The Blackhawk is the biggest revenue
maker, with two other smaller fishing boats docked there as well, the Sea Otter and the Tartan
IL The Tartan II pays for use of the fourth dock which was taken out of use for the bridge
construction. Mr. MacKenzie suggested putting this fourth dock back out to lease to a fourth
fisherman, Great spot for this type of business; has parking, restrooms and water, The
fishermen are asking for five-year leases; the suggestion is that we increase the term to three
years, Increase the rates 5olo each year, which is what their lease is now. They are asking for
no less parking than they have right now as parking is tight as it is, Ms. Hardy stdted that we
should support these fishermen as they bring a lot of business to the area.

It is recommended that the following changes be made to the Sea Otter and Tartan II leases:
   .   3-year leases
   .   They are asking for running water to those docks.
   .   Add a section to prohibit them from bringing gasoline to the dock to fill the boat.
