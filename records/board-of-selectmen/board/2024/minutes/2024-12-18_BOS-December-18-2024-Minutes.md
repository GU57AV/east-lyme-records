IN ATTENDANCE: Ann Cicchiello, Dan Cunningham, Jason Deeble and Don MacKenzie.

introduced himself to the Board and the public and stated that he is excited to take office
and that he wants to help, He stated that he has found some grant opportunities, and he is
not sure who can help him at Town Hall.

4a.      Ex-Officio Reports

Ms. Cicchiello reported:
    . At Parks and Recreation, they discussed the future of a dog park in town and are
going back to the drawing board to work on a location,

Ms. Carlson reported:
    . The Board of Education discussed the possibility of offering a Pre-K program in town
and continued work and discussion on the budget,
    . Parks and Recreation worked on their budget, and discussed the future of a possible
dog park, and should start meeting about that in January or February. They have identified
Darrow Pond as possible home for said park.
    o The Annual Jingle Bell 5K and Light Parade were fantastic.

4b.    First Selectman's Report

Mr. Cunningham reported :
    r Police Commission discussed the current project with IT to get the radio system

Academy.

other local emergency services.

5. Communications
There were none.

6, Public Comments

7.     Selectman'sResponse

Ms. Cicchiello, in response to Nick Menapace's comments during delegations, stated that a
lot of times we don't apply for a grant because we don't have the resources to apply. Both
she and Mr. MacKenzie suggested that Mr. Menapace bring the grant information to the
upcoming Planning and Zoning meetings.
