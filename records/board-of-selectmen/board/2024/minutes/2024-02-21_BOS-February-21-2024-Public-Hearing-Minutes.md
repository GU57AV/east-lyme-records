                           EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                        PUBLIC HEARINGS OF FEBRUARY 21, 2024
                                      MINUTES


PRESENT: Board of Selectmen Members Daniel Cunningham, Ann Cicchiello, Rose Ann
Hardy, Candice Carlson, Don MacKenzie and Jason Deeble,

Mr. Cunningham opened the Public Hearing at7 O4 p.m. to receive comments
concerning revisions to the Ordinance entitled "Peddling". The proposed revision
transfers the application and licensing responsibilities from the First Selectman to
the Chief of Police.

Mr. Cunningham asked if anyone from the public would like to speak on this matter.
There were no members of the public present to speak on this matter.

MOTTON ( 1)
Motion by Ms. Cicchiello to close the Public Hearing of February 2t,2024, at 7:05 p.m,
Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 6-0.

Mr. Cunningham opened the Public Hearing at 7:06 p,m. to receive comments
concerning a revision to the Ordinance entitled "Board of Police Commissioners".
The proposed revision eliminates Section 32.16 (B), which provides that no
member may serve more than two consecutive terms and that a member who has
served two consecutive terms may not be reappointed to the Board until four years
have elapsed from the conclusion of his or her most recent term.

Mr, Cunningham asked if anyone from the public would like to speak on this matter.
There were no members of the public present to speak on this matter.

Motion by Ms. Cicchiello to close the Public Hearing of February 2L,2024, at7:07 p.m
Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 6-0.
