Paul Spakowski, 37 Mill Road, East Lyme, thanked Mr. Bragaw and the Board of Selectman

disappointment that the Board of Selectmen cut their budget this year by 44o/o, stating that
they had already allotted the full amount for the upcoming year, He listed the events that
take place or are sponsored by the Historical Society, including but not limited to the Thomas

2a.    Notification to BOS of Hiring of Fire Chief and Deputy Fire Chief
DISCUSSION: First Selectman Cunningham stated that under the powers granted to him
under Section 3.2.3. of the East Lyme Town Charter, he is officially making notice to the
Board of Selectmen that he hired Bill Bundy as the permanent Fire Service Chief, and Erik
Quinn as the permanent Fire Service Deputy Chief, Chief Bill Bundy addressed the Board
and gave a summary of his qualifications, which includes 42years of firefighting experience,
26 years of volunteer service to his hometown volunteer department, he was an emergency
management director where he oversaw a $2M budget, and 32 years in law enforcement.
He reported that they are currently concentrating on recruitment and retention of both full-
time and volunteer positions, They are currently collaborating with the high school to bring
EMT classes to the students, and they are now considering adding a firefighting course. Erik
Quinn, Deputy Chief, is currently working on his master's degree, and is the current
Volunteer Fire Chief for the Niantic Station.

incident where they saved the life of a child, Thank you for your service to our community,
and specifically on this incident; Wick Haylon, CJ Leindecker, Dave Cabington, Dave Baer

2c.      Dog Park Subcommittee
DISCUSSION: Mr. Jay Ginsberg, representative from the Parks and Recreation Dog Park
Subcommittee, addressed the Board and stated that a lot of good pros and cons were
presented tonight by the public regarding the dog park. Mr. Ginsberg reported that they
sent out a survey which yielded around 500 responses, with B7olo amenable to a dog park.
He stated that they feel confident they could construct and maintain a dog park with minimal
cost to the Town. Although they presented tonight the location of Roxbury Road, after
listening to the residents not in favor of that location, they will continue to search for an
acceptable location. The subcommittee's full report is attached hereto as Exhibit #1. There
was concern raised about upkeep of a dog park, and Mr. Lokken stated that adding one more
"park" is not going to make too much of a difference to the existing responsibilities, Ms.
Carlson asked about the liability to the Town, and Mr. Lokken stated that CIRMA advised that
it is no different that our other parks and beaches in town. Ms. Hardy agreed that it would
be a nice idea to have a dog park in town, but she feels that we have too many other
projects going on at the moment and the Town should not be taking on more. The
subcommittee will go back to the drawing board and return at a future meeting to discuss
fu rthe r.
