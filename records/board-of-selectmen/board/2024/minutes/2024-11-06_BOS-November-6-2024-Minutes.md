7.      Selectman'sResponse
Mr. Cunningham asked Finance Director Kevin Gervais to give a summary of the situation
affecting a few of the police retirees. He stated that this is an ongoing issue that was caught
when our new payroll person started and noticed that something was wrong. They have
since been working with a tax attorney and a pension attorney to properly rectify the
situation. He is also working with an actuary, with approval and at the direction of the
Pension Board. They will go back three years, per IRS instruction. He will continue to work
on this matter until it is resolved, Ms. Carlson requested that an open discussion on this
matter be on an upcoming agenda.
