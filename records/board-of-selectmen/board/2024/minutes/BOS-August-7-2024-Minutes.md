3a,    East Pattagansett Sidewalk Project

Gary Goeschel, Planning Director, and Alex Klose, Town Engineer, were present to speak on
this project. The presentation is attached hereto as Exhibit #3. Letters were sent to all
affected property owners to gather feedback; the Public Works office would like to hear from
you if you have any questions or concerns regarding this project, especially if the sidewalk is
going to cross your property. This project is supported by DEEP, the East Lyme POCD, as
well as the 2017 Regional POCD. The process started a couple of years ago with an
application for a LoCIP grant; this project is lOOo/o funded by this grant, and is expected to
begin in 2026, but it is possible that they could start in 2025. In addition to the new and re-
constructed sidewalks, the entire road will be repaved through a reclamation process, Mr.
Klose stated that they are still working out details such as where to put mailboxes and trash
cans. The public is encouraged to contact either Gary Goeschel or Alex Klose with any
questions, concerns or for more information.
