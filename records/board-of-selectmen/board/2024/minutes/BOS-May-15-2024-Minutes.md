4b. First Selectman's Report

Mr, Cunningham reported :
   o He attended the Police Commission meeting where a group of residents addressed their
      concerns with recent break-ins within their community; the police are investigating,
   . There was discussion regarding the golf cart regulation; it was agreed that the Police
      Commission has control over this regulation,
   . There was discussion regarding existing ordinances, such as noise and parking, which
      exist but cannot be enforced due to lack of equipment and/or resources. For instance,
      the town has a noise ordinance but does not own a decibel reader. The Board of
      Selectman will review these ordinances and amend if needed.

      Guard and our partnership in the community, Thank you all of your service.
   o He attended Friends of Oswegatchie Hills annual meeting,
   r DOT I-95 project management meeting was held this week; lots of work still to be
      done. We will see a lot of construction and travel through that area is going to continue
      to be a challenge.

   o He attended a presentation by Community Foundation of Eastern Connecticut (CFEC)
      with Selectmen and CEOs from other towns; they discussed affordable housing.
