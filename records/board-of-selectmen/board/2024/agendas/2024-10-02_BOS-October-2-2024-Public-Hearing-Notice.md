                            TOWN OF EAST LYME
                           BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                         NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
                    REGARDING AN ORDINANCE ENTITLED
           “ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A SCHOOL BUILDING COMMITTEE”


NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 7:00 p.m. on Wednesday, October 2, 2024, at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108
Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to consider the Ordinance entitled “Ordinance
Establishing a School Building Committee”.

Copies of the full text of the proposed Ordinance are on file for public inspection at the office
of the East Lyme Town Clerk, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut and at
http://www.eltownhall.com/ to which reference may be had. Interested persons are invited
to attend and be heard.

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, on this 18th day of September, 2024.
EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
