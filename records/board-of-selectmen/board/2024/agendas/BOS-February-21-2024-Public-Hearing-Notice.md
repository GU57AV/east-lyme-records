NOTICE lS HEREBY GIVEN of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme
Board of Selectmen at 7:00 p.m. on Wednesday, February 21 , 2024, at the East
Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to consider a
revision to the Ordinance entitled "Board of Police Commissioners". The
proposed revision eliminates Section 32.16 (B), which provides that no member
may serve more than two consecutive terms and that a member who has served
two consecutive terms may not be reappointed to the Board until four years have
elapsed from the conclusion of his or her most recent term.
