                                  Establishment of a Grants Fund
       1. There is established within the East Lyme General Fund a "Grants Fund."
                 The Grants Fund shall continue year-to-year without lapsing unless
                 terminated by the Board of Selectmen.

       2. The purpose of the Grants Fund is to hold grant funds received by the
                 Town of East Lyme in a central location in order to facilitate efficient and
                 accurate tracking and accounting of said funds, and to enable the Finance
                 Director to more readily and efficiently report on the status and use of
                 grant funds to the sources of those funds when required by law or the
                 terms of a particular grant,

       3. The Finance Director, under the supervision and direction of the Board of
                 Finance, shall have custody of the Grants Fund. Further, the Finance
                 Director shall:

                    a. Upon the receipt of grant funds by the Town of East Lyme, deposit
                       or otheruuise place said funds into the Grants Fund, unless said
                       funds are required by law or the terms of a particular grant to be
                       deposited elsewhere.

                    b. Disburse funds from the Grants Fund for purposes authorized by
                        law and the terms of the source grant for the funds disbursed.

                    c. Account for the status and use of said funds within the Grants
                        Fund.

                    d. Prepare reports for grant providers, as required by law or the terms
                       of a source grant, concerning the status and use of grant funds.
                       Whenever such reports are prepared, a copy shall be sent to the
                       Board of Finance for information purposes as part of the Board of
