3b. First Selectman’s Report

e In the interest of being careful and forward thinking; the Water Department tested for
PFAS in our wells. We have seven wells in town, and in two of the wells there were trace
levels of PFAS found. New London and our water customers are being informed. The
levels are well below the “action” limits, but he feels that making everyone aware of the
situation is the best course of action. The levels are above the detectable level but well
below the action level. The town is on top of the situation and will keep the public
informed. The information has been communicated through mailings, our web site, and
The Day was contacted. The safety of the public is the main concern and responsibility.
