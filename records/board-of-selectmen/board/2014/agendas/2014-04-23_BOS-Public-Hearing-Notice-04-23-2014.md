BOARD OF SELECTMEN
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
REGARDING ADOPTION OF A REGULATION
CONCERNING THE USE OF GOLF CARTS

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme
Board of Selectmen at 7:00 p.m. on April 23, 2014 at the East Lyme Town Hall,
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to consider the adoption of a
“Regulation Concerning the Use of Golf Carts”. Said Regulation permits the
operation of golf carts on certain town roads pursuant to Connecticut General
Statutes Section 14-300g.

Copies of the full text of the proposed regulation are on file for public
inspection at the office of the East Lyme Town Clerk, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue,
Niantic, Connecticut and at http://www.eltownhall.com/ to which reference may
be had. Interested persons are invited to attend and be heard.

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, on this 10" day of April, 2014.

EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
