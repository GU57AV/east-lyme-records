3. Approval of 2014/2015 Budget

Mr. Formica went over proposed revenue increases and decreases for this fiscal year.
Some highlights:

PILOT State Owned Property - increase on what we had projected - $290,806

Tax relief for elderly - dependent on assessor's list sent to the state — projected to be a decrease
Gateway Project - projected revenue in taxes and water & sewer

Also projected increases on:

- permits for summer season

-beach recreation - revenues increase - due to increase traffic on new beach
-increase on home sales

- zoning fees

- mooring permits

** dog licenses — is projected to stay flat
