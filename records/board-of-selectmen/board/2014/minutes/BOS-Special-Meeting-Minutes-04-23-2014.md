B. GOLF CARTS — DISCUSSION/POSSIBLE ACTION
Mr. Seery stated he would be comfortable taking the head light requirement out, since they may be

encouraging people to drive at night. He is concerned with not having any brake lights; he does want to
see a restriction on the number of people that are allowed to be on the golf cart.
Ms. Hardy stated when they initially discussed this she thought it was for the benefit of seniors and
disabled people. Now they only need a Connecticut Drivers License. There have been emails circulation
and votes taken by phone. She would be more comfortable with all the beach community members
discussing this and having their input. There are other developments in Town without beaches that may
also like to be involved in this Regulation.

Mr. Wilson stated he appreciates Giants Neck Beach and Pine Grove coming forward and doing their due
diligence. The regulation states there can be up to 4 passengers. 55 and over communities have voiced
no opinion on this either for or against. If they do come forward we can deal with that issue then. He
could see this at Spinnaker, but they haven’t heard from them.

Ms. Cheeseman stated she also appreciates the input from Giants Neck Beach and Pine Grove.
Headlights are not just used at night, they provide additional visibility. The cost of a golf cart will limit
the number that are used. She feels that a car speeding is more dangerous than a golf cart. If this is
abused they can re-visit it in the future.

Mr. Nickerson stated the Regulation says it is for people with a Connecticut Drivers License only. If
parents buy a golf cart he would hope they have the control in their households so those without a
license won’t drive them. As far as headlights we may be encouraging their use at night. If any other
community says in the future that they don’t want to be involved they can be removed from the
Regulation.

Mr. Formica stated he would like to see headlights left in the Regulation but that is up to the group.
Pine Grove and Giants Neck Beach will be removed and they will prepare a revised map. East Lyme
Public Works will prepare the new map. The police will include the map with the registration. He
suggested they also include a list of the road names that are included.

Attorney Collins stated this is a Regulation, not an Ordinance; they have the right to change this in the
future.

Motion (5) Mr. Nickerson moved to exclude the headlight requirement in the Regulation.
Seconded by Mr. Wilson.

Ms. Hardy stated it is a safety feature; they can be used on overcast and cloudy days.

Ms. Cheeseman agreed.

Motion Failed 3-3 (Nay — Ms. Hardy, Ms. Cheeseman, Mr. Formica)

Motion (6) Mr. Nickerson moved the following Resolution:

RESOLVED, the Board of Selectmen, acting as the Town’s Traffic Authority, adopts the regulation
entitled “Regulation Concerning the Use of Golf Carts” as amended to exclude Giants Neck Beach and
Pine Grove (Sections 5.5 and 5.6). In addition, Section 6 shall change from “East Lyme Police

Department” to “East Lyme Public Works Department.” To be effective on May 12, 2014.

Seconded by Mr. Wilson.
Motion Passed 6-0.
