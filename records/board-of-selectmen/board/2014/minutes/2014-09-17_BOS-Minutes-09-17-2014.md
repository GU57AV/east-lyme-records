3A. CARE HERE — CONTINUED DISCUSSION/POSSIBLE ACTION

Mr. Formica stated that Attorney Collins has gone over the contract. It has been available in his office
for review. The contract satisfies the attorneys and CIRMA. He is proposing that the town participate in
this. Care Here would manage the clinic. The town would hire the doctor and nurse. This is for the
benefit of the town employees and for the town. We are thinking outside the box as to health care.
This would be for employees and their families. The town is self-insured. There are three appointments
made per hour. There is no waiting. Generic drugs would be available for free. Blood work would be
done through a national network. Employees can still go to their general physician. They also do
biometric screenings and provide a health coach.

Anna Johnson stated the potential savings projected by Care Here are $226,000 in year one, 1.2 million
dollars by year 3 and 3.3 million dollars by year 5. Those numbers are based on every employee using
the service, with an estimated 2155 visits per year.
Mr. Formica stated this would be the first Care Here Clinic in Connecticut. There are three proposals for
locations. One being an addition to the Community Center, one is a lease of space at the Midway Mall,
and one is for a tease on Hope Street.

Ms. Hardy asked if employees could go during the day.

Mr. Formica stated it would be as they do now, either taking personal or sick time for a doctor’s
appointment.

Ms. Hardy asked about the additional work for the finance department, and the work that will be done
for the build out to the space.

Mr. Formica stated we may be able to do most of the build out work ourselves, except for plumbing and
electrical. He asked Mrs. Johnson about the cost to the finance department.

Anna Johnson stated she doesn’t believe this will be any additional work for her office. Her office would
not be handling any medical records. The work for her office would be similar to what it is now.

Ms. Hardy stated she is reluctant to spend money ona build out on a property that the town doesn’t
own. The library has already requested additional space and they were turned down. There are two
existing clinics in town that are taxpaying.

Mr. Seery stated this is an innovative move to the future. Because we are self-insured we pay the
premiums. It is cheaper for the town if employees go to this instead of the clinic in town. There is also a
prescription savings. There is also no co-pay.

Mr. Formica stated a number of retirees still remain on our health plan. This will be open to them as
well.

Mr. Nickerson stated this is forward thinking and it’s a great idea. We will have to make sure it catches
on. There are needs at the Community Center for space, but we can start this sooner if we lease a
property.

Mr. Wilson stated this is based around insurance, so kids at the age of 26 would not be able to be
involved.

Mr. Formica stated he believes it would mirror the insurance.
Mr. Wilson asked about the co-pay.

Mr. Formica stated there would not be a co-pay. Also if someone has the HSA he believes the co-pay
may be $5.00.

Mr. Wilson asked about room at Lillie B. Haynes or Niantic Center.

Mr. Formica stated there is a perfect spot at Niantic Center, but there are concerns about the clinic
being open while school is in session.
Mr. Wilson stated he would like us to find a place we already own.

Ms. Hardy stated $35,000 seems to be on the low side for the build out.

Mr. Scheer stated for the leased space only walls would have to be added to the interiors and a few
doors would have to be changed. They would also have to add Air Conditioning. Our employees can do
the walls, doors, and floor. A plumber would have to do an ADA bathroom. New circuits would have to

be done by an electrician. He believes $35,000 could do it, and that may be high.

Mr. Formica stated he would like to add it to the Senior Center. He believes if we try it for a couple
years and see where we are then adding onto the Senior Center would make sense then if it’s a success.

Ms. Hardy asked about liability to the town.

Attorney Collins stated the contract has been looked at by the town’s insurance company. The town is
covered.

Ms. Hardy asked about the potential to be sued if there was an accident.

Attorney Collins stated this would be no different than other claims against the town.

Mr. Nickerson asked about medical malpractice.

Attorney Collins stated the doctor is required to have medical malpractice insurance.

Ms. Hardy stated she is not ready to support this. The Town has just switched to Aetna and there have
been numerous problems and the Unions have all filed grievances. She would be willing to review the

contract and give it consideration.

Mr. Formica stated he doesn’t think every Union grieved the medical insurance, in fact he doesn’t know
of any that did.

Mr. Seery stated this doesn’t change any current health care contract.

Mr. Wilson asked about the Niantic Center option.

Mr. Scheer stated the build out for Niantic Center would be about $125,000.
Mr. Nickerson stated it’s probably not a good time to do in a school.

Mr. Formica stated it makes sense to look at other options. He suggested they authorize him to sign the
contract and then we can begin the process.

Mr. Wilson stated he would like it to be on town owned property.

Motion (5) Mr. Nickerson moved to approve authorizing the First Selectman to sign a contract
with Care Here.
Ms. Hardy asked where the funding from this will come.

Mr. Formica stated it will come from the health care fund.

Ms. Hardy stated she will not vote in favor of this and she has not reviewed the contract.
Mr. Formica stated he will have to come back with options on locations.

Mr. Seery stated he is in favor of this but he would like more information on where it will be.

Seconded by Mr. Seery.
