b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Formica stated he will go and view the construction on the Boardwalk regarding Mr. Barry’s
concerns. He did lock in the electric rates from March 30, 2016 through December of 2017 at 8.87
cents. There is an inspector on site at the Boardwalk who is paid for by the architect. Darrows Ridge will
be on the next agenda. They used the bond funds to pave the final coat on Darrows Court. The Town is
finishing the realignment of Mostowy Road. They are doing drainage work now. They also paved the
circles for plowing and garbage pick up.
