EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
PUBLIC HEARING RE: AN AMENDMENT TO THE HISTORIC PROPERTIES
ORDINANCE
FEBRUARY 19, 2014
MINUTES

PRESENT: Paul M. Formica, Mark Nickerson, Holly Cheeseman, Rose Ann Hardy,
Kevin Seery, Rob Wilson.

Mr. Formica called the Public Hearing to order at 7:35 p.m. noting that this hearing’s
notice had been published in The Day.

Town Attorney Tracy Collins stated that this is the final step in having the Samuel Smith
House become an historic property in the Town of East Lyme and once acted upon, the
ordinance will be amended.

Marvin Schutt of 29 N. Edgewood Road identified himself as President of the Friends of
the Samuel Smith House and thanked Luane Lange and the Historic Properties
Commission members for processing the paperwork and obtaining required approvals for
the Samuel Smith House and Property, Inc. to be preserved in perpetuity by a required
Ordinance. (Attachment A).

FILED IN EAST ae

There was not further comment.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Wilson moved to close the public hearing at 7:38 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Seery. Motion passed 6-0.

Respectfully submitted,

Ope Ste

Darlene C. Stevens, Recording Secretary
