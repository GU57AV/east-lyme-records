GOLF CART USE ON TOWN ROADS IN BEACH AREAS

Attorney Collins confirmed the Public Hearing was duly advertised in the New London Day newspaper.

Mr. Formica stated that Connecticut General Statute allows the Town to adopt a Regulation concerning
the use of golf carts on town roads, with the Board of Selectmen acting as the Traffic Authority. Golf
carts are prohibited on any road with a speed limit above 25 miles per hour. Mr. Formica showed the
audience a map of all of the roads affected by the proposed Regulation. There are roads on the map,
shown as yellow that have a sign of 25 miles per hour, but the State shows that speed limit as 30 miles
per hour. The map will have to be modified. Golf carts will not be able to pass on the yellow roads. He
has received a written request from the Board of Governors of Giants Neck Beach requesting that they
not be included in the Regulation because they have no on street parking, and they have no parking at
their beaches.

Subject: Public Hearing to consider adoption of a Regulation Concerning the Use of Golf Carts

Dear Selectpersons,

A few years ago, Selectman John Hogan proposed a similar regulation. Lengthy discussion by
the Board of Selectmen resulted in what could only be considered a “draw.” Mr. Hogan’s
proposal was deemed unworkable and unenforceable due to its amount of detail and excessive
restriction.

Tonight, you will once again enter into discussion where the proposed regulation will be
discussed and commented upon by the Board as well as interested citizens. With some work and
cooperative attitudes by all those involved, the Town can have a very effective regulation which

is fair to all and not overly bureaucratic.

As I am unable to attend the Public Hearing this evening, I offer the following points for
consideration by the Board:

e Safety — since the use of golf carts have been popularized in the Niantic beach
communities, no accidents involving them have been publicized. Additional safety
equipment may be unnecessary.

e Applicability — addressing a rumor, usage by senior citizens only is discriminatory and
should not be considered.

e Usage — if golf carts are not allowed to be operated in hours of darkness, the requirement
for headlights and brake lights is superfluous.

e Registration — as most municipalities feel that an identifying number should be affixed to
everything under its purview, a one-time only registration should be conducted.

e Proliferation — the proposed regulation is limited to beach communities only.
Consideration for applicability should be given to Niantic’s several “55 and over”
developments as they could provide useful neighborhood transportation in those areas as

well.

All other requirements should be invoked as per the State Statues concerning these vehicles.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

REGULATION CONCERNING THE USE OF GOLF CARTS

1. Pursuant to the provisions of Section 14-300g of the Connecticut General
Statutes, as amended, the East Lyme Board of Selectmen, acting herein as the
duly constituted Traffic Authority of the Town of East Lyme, hereby permits the
operation of Golf Carts within the Town in accordance with the terms of this
regulation.

2. Gas or electric powered Golf Carts permitted on East Lyme town roads must
be equipped with a minimum of four (4) wheels, capable of a maximum speed of
no more than twenty five (25) miles per hour, capable of transporting no more
than four (4) persons and comply with the following standards:

2.1. equipped with a locking brake, main power switch, head lamp(s),
tail/orake lamps, operable horn as defined in section 14-80(e) of the Connecticut
General Statutes, rear view mirror and seatbelts/passenger safety restraints for
rear-facing passengers.

2.2. a flag with a minimum height of seventy two (72) inches from the
ground and at least one foot above any installed roof shall be placed on the left
rear of the Golf Cart to assist pedestrians and motor vehicle operators in
observing the location and operation of a Golf Cart.

2.3. a valid registration sticker from the East Lyme Police Department
placed in a visible location on the rear of the Golf Cart.

3. Prior to operation of a Golf Cart the owner shall register it with the East Lyme
Police Department. The East Lyme Police Department shall prepare a
registration form and registration sticker for the registration of Golf Carts. The
registration shall expire on May 31st of each calendar year. The owner of a Golf
Cart shall at all times meet the following standards to obtain and maintain a valid
Golf Cart Registration:

3.1. The owner must submit proof of ownership, such as a certificate of
title or bill of sale, and a certificate of insurance covering the Golf Cart that
meets the State of Connecticut minimum liability insurance requirements for a
motor vehicle or insurance requirements that are established by the Connecticut
Department of Motor Vehicle Regulations concerning Golf Carts upon adoption of
same.

3.2. The Golf Cart must satisfy the standards of section 2 above.

3.3. The payment of a fee in the amount of $35.00 is due for each
registration period. From time to time the Board of Selectmen may amend the
amount of such fee to an amount sufficient to cover all costs associated with the
implementation and enforcement of the requirements of this regulation.
