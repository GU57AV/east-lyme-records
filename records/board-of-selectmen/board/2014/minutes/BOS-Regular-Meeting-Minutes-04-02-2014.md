1c. Delegations

David Lopriore of the Community Acupuncture for Veterans program stated that they give Veterans the
service for free or at a very low cost. They have started the program at the Community Center. He
handed out literature explaining the program (Exhibit A.) They are here tonight to request a fee waiver
for using the room at the Community Center. They have non-profit status pending at this time. He has
liability insurance and has listed the Town of East Lyme on his policy. This is a fantastic program, and
any money that they save will be used to help the Veterans.

David Ogle or 19 Park Court, Niantic spoke on behalf of Robert Shea who could not be present. He
requested they authorize the use of golf carts in Black Point. They do not endorse or oppose any other
beaches also being authorized to do so. Black Point was established in 1931, their charter delineates the
boundaries of their Association. They have provided a letter to the Board of Selectmen from the Chair
of the Board of Governors stating that they unanimously agree and have no opposition to the use of golf
carts. It must be authorized by the local transportation authority. He requests and urges the Board’s
approval of golf carts within the confines of Black Point Beach.

Sally Cini stated they are hoping they will take action tonight. They are only talking about the use of golf
carts on the Black Point streets. The Town often takes liability in other situations. The golf carts would
need to be insured. They are asking for Black Point and the other beaches can argue for themselves. It

is unfair to cut it off for all of them because they have a great concern over one beach. She suggested

advertisements. There would be no added enforcement; they do not check every car that is on the
road. They have motorized bikes on the roads. All beaches are different. Many people in their
neighborhood need these golf carts.

Ray O’Connor, 23 Roxbury Court urged the Board of Selectmen to authorize the Town Building
Committee to go forward with the Boardwalk. If they wait too long they may not be able to pour the
concrete when it gets too cold. He would appreciate the Board acting on this soon.

Joe Mingo, 397 Boston Post Road spoke in support of Kimberly Barber Bradley's appointment as an
Alternate to the Inland Wetlands Agency. She has a very impressive resume. He also urged Mr. Formica
to hire someone to help get the Town website straightened out. He would like to see the Town hire a
vendor to lease electric cars on the Boardwalk so the physically challenged can enjoy the Boardwalk.

Dick Waterman, 11 Lake Avenue Extension spoke of the East Lyme Puppetry Theatre. They are
requesting a waiver of insurance to use the Community Center for their programs. They have three
programs and a lecture scheduled. They started this project last year, and it is really growing. They
have made up bylaws, and they intend to become a 501(3)(c) in the future. They are family oriented,
and they hope to continue this project. It is educational, promotes literacy, and they intend to develop
student workshops. They could just use a little help, which is why they are asking for the waiver.

Barbara Johnston, 35 Seacrest Avenue stated the golf cart issue started in 2010, they can’t cross state
highways with golf carts, she doesn’t think it should just be for Black Point. There is no room on their
roads for golf carts. The speed limit is only 20 miles per hour. According to the State Statute the only
people allowed to use golf carts would be people with Connecticut Drivers Licenses, that is going to be
interesting to enforce. This was never approved by the Black Point residents. She asked them to put it
on the Agenda and that request was denied. There was an accident last year in front of the Black Point
store. The Black Point rules state that the Association Manager shall cause the motor vehicle laws and
regulations to be strictly enforced, but there are already golf carts driving around down there, being
driven by a member of the Board of Governors. The Association Manager told her that it was up to the
East Lyme Police to enforce. She was not informed of a meeting where the Board of Governors of Black
Point met and voted on this issue. She presented materials for the Board’s review (Exhibit B.)

Sally Cini, 28 Seabreeze Avenue stated the Board of Governors did not meet this Spring, they were
polled by telephone and it was not a formal meeting and there was no Agenda. Their roads are as wide
as any in town.

Barbara Johnston stated she was not on that call list, and the Board of Governors can call a Special

panels.

Ms. Hardy stated it is the Board’s job to provide for the entire town, not special groups. These also
would require publishing legal advertisements. She is uncomfortable with doing this for one beach.

Re: Golf Cart Regulation

Dear Mr. Formica:

You have requested our advice regarding the following: if the Board of Selectmen,
acting as the town’s traffic authority, adopts the proposed regulation authorizing the operation
ca) of golf carts on certain roads of the town, would the town be more likely to incur liability than if
it does not adopt the regulation?

Discretionary acts of a town usually do not result in liability, but that is not always the
case. If a golf cart were to be involved in a serious accident involving bodily injury or worse, it
is possible that a claim could be made that the town was negligent in designating a particular

road or that it did not properly examine and consider all the circumstances relating to the use
of golf carts. Wheiher such a claim would be successful ‘is dependent on a variety of factors,
including a determination of the sole proximate cause of the accident, but that would not

prevent an injured party from advancing such a claim and requiring the town to defend it.

In contrast, if the town were to not adopt the proposed golf cart regulation and a
person nonetheless drove an unauthorized golf cart on a town road and was involved in an

accident, the town would not be liable. In those circumstances, the injured person could not
claim that the town was the sole proximate cause of the accident.

Please let us know if you need anything further.
