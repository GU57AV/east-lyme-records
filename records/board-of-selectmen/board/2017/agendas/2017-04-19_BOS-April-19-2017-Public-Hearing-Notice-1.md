TOWN OF EAST LYME
BOARD OF SELECTMEN
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
REGARDING AN AMENDMENT TO THE ORDINANCE ENTITLED
“ORDINANCE CONCERNING AREAS UNDER JURISDICTION OF
PARKS & RECREATION COMMISSION"

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme
Board of Selectmen at 7:15 p.m. on April 19, 2017 at the East Lyme Town Hall,
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to consider a proposed
amendment to the Ordinance entitled “Ordinance Concerning Areas Under
Jurisdiction of Parks & Recreation Commission”. Said amendment would prohibit
any person from bringing a dog (other than a certified service dog) on to the
Niantic Bay Boardwalk and would prohibit any person from bringing a dog (other
than a certified service dog) onto any Town owned beach and McCook Point
Park from Memorial Day to Labor Day.

Copies of the full text of the proposed Ordinance are on file for public
inspection at the office of the East Lyme Town Clerk, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue,
Niantic, Connecticut and at http:/Awww.eltownhall.com/ to which reference may
be had. Interested persons are invited to attend and be heard.

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, on this 5th day of April, 2017.

AMENDMENT TO THE ORDINANCE ENTITLED
“ORDINANCE CONCERNING AREAS UNDER
JURISDICTION OF PARKS & RECREATION COMMISSION”

The Ordinance entitled “Ordinance Concerning Areas Under Jurisdiction of Parks &

Recreation Commission” which was last amended on December 21, 2005 is hereby amended
to add paragraphs 8 and 9 as follows:

1.

10.

This ordinance shall apply to McCook Point Park & Beach, Veterans Memorial Park,
Boardwalk, Cini Park, Samuel Peretz areas and all other areas within the Town of East
Lyme designated as under the control of the Parks and Recreation Commission by
vote of the Board of Selectmen.

No person shall enter any area which is close to the public as plainly designated by
signs.

No person shall kindle, build, maintain or use a fire except either in fireplaces provided
for that purpose, or in portable fireplaces in areas plainly designated for that use.

No person shall possess or have under his control any alcoholic beverages in areas
where it is prohibited as plainly designated by signs.

No person shall park any motor vehicle except in areas plainly designated by sign or
other marking as parking areas.

No person shall discard or abandon any refuse, rubbish or garbage except in
containers provided for that purpose.

No person shall possess or have under his control any glass bottle or crockery in
areas where it is prohibited as plainly designated by sign.

NEW No person shall bring any dog (other than certified service dogs) on to the
Niantic Bay Boardwalk.

NEW No person shall bring any dog (other than certified service dogs) onto any Town
owned beach and McCook Point Park from Memorial Day to Labor Day.

Any person who shall violate any provision of this ordinance shall be fined not more

than fifty dollars ($50.00).
