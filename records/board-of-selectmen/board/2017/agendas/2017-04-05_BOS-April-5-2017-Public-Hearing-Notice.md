TOWN OF EAST LYME
BOARD OF SELECTMEN
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that, pursuant to Section 7-163e of the Connecticut
General Statutes, the East Lyme Board of Selectmen will hold a public hearing on Wednesday,
April 5, 2017 at 7:00 p.m. at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic,
Connecticut, to consider the following:

1, Proposed lease of a portion of Town owned property located at 16 Mostowy

Road, in East Lyme, Connecticut, to Mostowy Towers, LLC, for the purpose of
the construction and operation of a communications tower.

Copies of the proposed lease are on file at the office of the East Lyme Town Clerk, to
which reference may be had.

Interested persons are invited to attend and be heard.
