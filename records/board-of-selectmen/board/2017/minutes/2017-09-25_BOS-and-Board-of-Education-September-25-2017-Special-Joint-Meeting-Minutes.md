II. DISCUSSION ITEM

A. Dis

ssion re: Informational Session on a Proposed Microgrid Project for the Town of East Lyme

Attorney Paul Michaud of Michaud Law Group and Michael Coskun of Doosan provided two
PowerPoint presentations regarding the legal, construction administration services, and
technical services they would provide in conjunction with the CT Microgrid Grant Program
(see attached).

The program helps support local distributed energy generation for critical facilities to ensure
that critical buildings remain powered during electrical grid outages at no cost to the town.
The unit would serve the East Lyme Middle Schoo! (town shelter), Lillie B. Haynes, and the
Community Center.

The microgrid system operates on the basic process of |) Reformer converts natural gas into
hydrogen, 2) Fuel Cell Stack generates DC power, 3) Inverter generates high-quality AC
power, and 4) Internal heat recovery system generates hot water (that can be used to augment
adjacent building heating).

They noted that East Lyme has been identified as a good candidate for Round 4 of the
Connecticut Department of Energy & Environmental Protection (DEEP) program allowing up
to $4 million per municipal microgrid of the approximately $30 million available under the
grant. A rich discussion ensued regarding mecting minimum energy use requirements,
potential savings, safety and security, project timeline, and next steps.
