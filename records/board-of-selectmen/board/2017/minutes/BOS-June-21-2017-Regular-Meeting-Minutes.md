3a. Discussion and possible action on instituting a Smoking Policy at the East Lyme
Community Center

DISCUSSION: Mr. Seery explained that he has met with Cathy Wilson, Director of the Senior
Center, and the major issue seems to be that the smoking receptacle is directly outside of the
doorway so that people coming in and out of the building pass by the designated smoking area.
Also, the smoke is blowing into the building when the door is opened and closed. It was decided
to move the designated smoking area 35 feet away from the door.

3b. Discussion regarding ADA Service Animals in Town Buildings

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that the Custodian has requested that we put a policy in
place to prohibit dogs, except ADA Service dogs, from entering Town buildings. This is being
requested due to a large number of accidents that have occurred over the last couple of weeks
because people are bring their pets in to renew their dog licenses. This has been an issue in the
past, and it is now being requested that a permanent policy be put into place. Mr. Nickerson
stated that the Board would review the situation and discuss a permanent policy at a future
meeting.

6b. _ First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson reported that the annual Lion’s Club Lobster Fest will be held on July 1"
and 2" this year. The event takes place on Town Hall property, and includes the lobster fest, arts
and crafts, as well as the community tag sale held the same weekend. Ms. Hardy noted that the
proceeds from the community tag sale go toward offsetting costs for the students to attend mock
congress. Celebrate East Lyme is on Saturday, July 15"" this year; let’s get ready for a fun and
busy day as always! The next Board of Selectmen meeting will be on July 5", and will be the
only meeting in July. Although the Town Hall is closed on Tuesday, July 4" the meeting will be
held on the 5" and the office will be open on Wednesday, business as usual. Mr. Nickerson
reported that he attended the scheduled COG meeting earlier in the day, and discuss revolved
