2a. Approve an expenditure of up to $850 from the Parahus Account

MOTION (4)

Mr. Seery moved to approve an expenditure of up to $850 from the Parahus Account for the
purpose of purchasing an additional phone and install a phone line in the Conference Room.
DISCUSSION:

Ms. Cathy Wilson, Director of the Senior Center, addressed the Board and explained that
the Parahus Account is a an account that accepts donations from the public given directly to the
Senior Center; for example if someone leaves a donation in their Will or “in lieu of flowers
please donate to .. . “. Mr. Nickerson thanked Ms. Wilson for her explanation, and stated that if
anyone from the public would like to donate to the Senior Center Parahus Account, please call
the Senior Center directly for more information. Ms. Wilson stated that these funds are used for
the benefit of the seniors and not generally for operational matters. It was further explained that
this account was created and named after Mr. Parahus, who was the Chairman and long time
member of the Commission on Aging, and who was also pivotal in raising funds to construct the
building. Ms. Hardy stated that the intent of this fund is to provide for the people who frequent
the facility, and she feels as though this is not an appropriate use of the funds. Ms. Wilson stated
that she understands Ms. Hardy feeling, but that this is something that the center needs in order
to provide adequate services to the seniors, Although she requested up to $850, Ms. Wilson
stated that she has the funds, out of the operations budget, to pay for the phone itself, but will
require an undetermined amount to cover the wiring; it shouldn’t be $850 but she wants to be
prepared in case they find issues when doing the wiring. Mr. Seery stated that he would
reluctantly agree to this expenditure because he realizes it is something that is needed to enhance
the operations of the Senior Center. Ms. Wilson stated that if she does not need to use the funds
at all, she will not do so. Mr. Nickerson agreed that this is a needed expenditure at this time and
will support it, but he asked Mrs. Hardy as Ex-Officio to this Commission to remind them what
the purpose of the Parahus Fund is and how the funds should be used. It was agreed that
guidelines should be created to oversee such funds; currently only the Senior Center, Smith-
Harris House and the Library have such accounts set up.

Mr. Dagle stated that he would like to make a personal donation of $500 to the Parahus
Account to cover the cost of the wiring for this new phone, which should eliminate the need to
use any funds from the Parahus Account.

It was agreed that this motion would be passed tonight, and that the $850 approved would
only be utilized if necessary.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion Passed 5-0-1. Abstained; Ms. Hardy

2b. Discussion and possible action on Parks & Recreation Department’s request to consider a
Town Ordinance to extend fines for walking dogs on the Niantic Bay Boardwalk

MOTION (5)

Mr. Seery moved to adopt the amendment to the Ordinance entitled “Ordinance Concerning
Areas Under Jurisdiction Of Park & Recreation Commission”, as submitted.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Seery thanked Counsel, and Mr. Putnam and the Parks & Recreation
Commission for doing all of the research to come up with the most productive updates to this
ordinance. He reported that at the last P&R Commission meeting, they spoke about getting
updated signs made that would outline the ordinance and rules clearer for the public. Mr. Seery
agrees that instituting a fine for violators is the right course of action. Mr. Salerno also agrees
with adding a fine, and recommends that the fine information be put on the signs as well. Mr.
Dagle stated that it might be prudent to outline the entire ordinance on the new signs so the
public is aware of all the rules subject to fines if violated.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 6-0.

2c. Discussion and possible action on the proposed lease for the communication tower on the
Mostowy Road site

MOTION (6)

Mr. Seery moved to authorize the First Selectman, in the name and on behalf of the Town, to
execute and deliver a document entitled “Site Lease with Option” with Mostowy Towers, LLC,
for certain property located at 16 Mostowy Road for use as a communications tower, and to take
such action as may be necessary or desirable to implement said agreement.
DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson reported that since the last meeting, the lease has been reviewed
and everyone’s input and concerns were considered. He stated that this is a very simple lease,
and it has been found to be sufficient as it is presented. Ms. Hardy asked why the other existing
sites that were identified not chosen as opposed to putting up a new antenna; and it was reiterated
that all of the identified sites were looked at prior to last meeting, but that none of these sites
were sufficient for our needs. She inquired as to where the temporary tower will be constructed
and who is paying for that; it was reported that they are considering a couple of sites, one of
them the solar farm on Walnut Hill Road, and that Marcus Communications aka Mostowy
Towers LLC is installing this temporary tower as a courtesy to the Town of East Lyme to get our
new communications system up and running prior to the permanent tower installation. Mr.
Salerno and Mr. Dagle both appreciated being given the additional couple of weeks to review the
lease and consider all the points brought up at last meeting, and they are both satisfied with the
lease.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 6-0.
