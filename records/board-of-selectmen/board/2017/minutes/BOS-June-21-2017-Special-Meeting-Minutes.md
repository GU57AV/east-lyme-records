EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN FILED IN EAST
SPECIAL MEETING OF JUNE 21, 2017 on

CONNECTICUT.
MINUTES a 2017 2245 anion)

EAST ZYME TOWN CLERK

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Marc Salerno, Rose Ann Hardy and Paul Dagle.
EXCUSED: Dan Cunningham

ALSO PRESENT: Holly Cheeseman; and special guests Floyd Welch and Harry Danos
Mr. Nickerson called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m., and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

3. Watch a student produced special file honoring Mr. Floyd Welch, Pearl Harbor Survivor,
and Mr. Harry Danos, Battle of Iwo Jim Survivor; and honor said guests for their service to the
community.

Mr. Nickerson welcomed and thanked everyone for attending this special presentation to honor
these brave and honorable men. He introduced the two Veterans being honored, and handed the
presentation over to Rose Ann Hardy.

Ms. Hardy introduced her students, Victoria Chong, Jack McDonald and Jack Mountain, and gave
a little background on how the students came to produce this film. She then turned the
presentation over to the students, who took turns reading a letter from Mr. Peck, who was unable
to attend the presentation, and then they introduced and played the film. At the completion of the
film, both Mr. Welch and Mr. Danos shared some stories and experiences with the audience.

Ms. Holly Cheeseman thanked both gentlemen for their service, and then she read and presented
to each gentleman a proclamation of honor from both herself and Mr. Paul Formica, who could
attend this evening.

Mr. Mark Nickerson thanked both gentlemen for their service and for sharing their stories with us.
He then called up Kristina Fenton, a recent graduate from East Lyme High School, stating that as
we honor our Town’s oldest Veteran’s of their respective battles, we should also honor our
Town’s newest sworn-in Service Member. Miss Fenton is scheduled to leave for Marine boot
camp in November 2017. Mr. Nickerson read and presented each gentleman with a Proclamation
of Honor.

MOTION (1)

Mr. Dagle moved to adjourn the June 21, 2017, special meeting of the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 7:12 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 5-0.

Respectfully submitted,
Gerucloc 2. (Cb, chowoe_

Sandra A. Anderson, Recording Secretary
