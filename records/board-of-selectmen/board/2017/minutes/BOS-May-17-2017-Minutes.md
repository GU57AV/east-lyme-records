2b. Authorize the Board of Education to apply to the Commissioner of Education and to accept a
grant for the Three Elementary Schools project including Center School, Lillie B. Haynes, and Flanders
DISCUSSION:

Mr. Jeffrey Newton and Mr. Tim Hagan were present to speak to the Board on this matter. The Board of
Education is proposing an amendment to the Town Building Committee to include the Superintendent of
Schools, two representatives from the Board of Education, and the three principals from the three

getting on that list and getting this project rolling as scheduled.

replacing the onsite sanitary line and adding a lining to the waste lines, replacing one boiler, replacing
corridor lockers with new lockers, reestablishing the second gym, and building out old locker rooms to
usable classroom space.

2c. Discussion on possible Town wide smoking policy

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that this proposed policy was brought to the Board of Selectman by
Cathy Wilson, Director of the Senior Center, and it specific to the Community Center/Library building
and is not being proposed to be a town wide policy, as the agenda states. It is being requested that
smoking be prohibited within 25’ of the doorways. It was discussed that maybe we could put up signs to
that effect to make a rule for the building itself without instituting a formal policy. Mr. Nickerson noted
that there is currently a no smoking rule at our Town parks and beaches. Mr. Seery indicated that he
would meet with Ms. Wilson to discuss the matter further, and will bring an update to the next Board of
Selectmen meeting.

2e. Discussion and possible action on Public Works’ request to put “No Parking” signs in Oak Grove
Beach Association

MOTION (10)

Mr. Seery moved to approve the installation of two (2) No Parking signs on the west side of Shore Road
in Oak Grove Beach Association, as amended by the Public Works Director at the May 17, 2017 Board of
Selectmen meeting, for the reasons identified in the President of Oak Grove Beach Association’s email
dated October 12, 2016, which is included in the supporting documentation as Exhibit 1.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that we have heard the public speak on this matter tonight, and he
invited Mr. Joe Bragaw, Public Works Director, to present to the Board. Mr. Bragaw reported that a
complaint was received in the fall of 2016 from Ms. Patty Travali of 29 Shore Road. At that time he went
out to the site, and then sent out letters to all of the neighbors to get feedback on putting up no parking
signs. This proposal was not accepted by the neighbors, so at that point Mr. Bragaw sent the matter to the
Oak Grove Beach Association Board of Governors for their consideration and recommendation. The
Board of Governors made their recommendations, Exhibit 1 attached hereto. The Board of Selectmen
agree with the resolution, but changed the location of the south sign and moved it north 40’-45’, which
would put back two parking spots and would still allow for good visibility around the corner. All Board
Members were in agreement with this amendment.

Seconded by Mr. Cunningham. Motion passed 4-0.

2h. Review and authorize the First Selectman to enter into the collective bargaining agreement
between the Town of East Lyme and Local 3377 of the International Association of Firefighters for the
period from July 1, 2015 through June 30, 2018

MOTION (13)

Mr. Seery moved to authorize the First Selectman to enter into the Collective Bargaining Agreement
between the Town of East Lyme and Local 3377 of the International Association of Firefighters for the
period from July 1, 2015 through June 30, 2018.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that this contract is actually a one year extension to the existing
contract. There are a couple of changes to the current contract, as follows: provides for a 2.5% salary
increase, which is standard through the arbitration process; there is a probationary step added where there
will be a one year probationary period followed now by a 2-5 year secondary probationary period after
which the fire fighter would then go to full, standard pay. Mr. Nickerson stated that we have 7 full time
fire fighters that this contract applies to, all of which are East Lyme residents. They are hard working,
good employees and the Town has a very good relationship with them and this is a good contract.
Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 4-0.

6a. Ex-Officio

Mr. Salerno reported that at the Library Commission there was no action taken, but that the
meeting was very efficient and a lot of discussion on different items. He reports that the Town Building
Committee will be meeting on Thursday, May 18" for the first time in approximately 3 years to begin

alcohol free graduation parties. The East Lyme Youth Services will be awarding scholarships to three

6b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson reported that he attended the State of East Lyme on May 9, 2017. On May 10",
Town employees attended Health & Safety training; this training is necessary to keep the Town in
compliance with insurance and OSHA requirements. The Rotary Club held their annual gala a couple of
weeks ago and raised a lot of money, which as we know they put right back into our community. On May
10" he attended the COST Town Leaders Day in Hartford, where much of the discussion revolved around
the budget. He reports that we are working with Old Lyme to get them tied into our waste water system,
which goes through Waterford and ends up in New London, On Monday night, the high school hosted a
program entitled “Understanding PTSD with First Responders”, organized by ELHS Senior, Nicole
Betulia. This program was very informative and moving, and Nicole did a great job putting it together.
On May 16" we hosted our annual Hole-In-The-Wall classroom for East Lyme 3" graders, and it was a
lot of fun and very informative. Mr. Nickerson reported that he attended this year’s US Coast Guard
Academy graduation, and it was a great event and congratulations to all of the graduates. The budget
referendum is on Thursday, May 18" from 8am to 8pm at the Community Center; please remember to go
out and vote, and you can check out the parking lot improvements that we have recently made at the
Community Center. On May 26" at 6pm will be the ribbon cutting on the new band shell at McCook’s;
come on down and enjoy the festivities. On May 28" there will be a candlelight vigil at Liberty Green;
this year’s theme is “A Salute to our Armed Forces”. The annual Memorial Day Parade will be
downtown on Monday, May 29" at Ipm.

Mr. Nickerson stated that we are all still waiting patiently for the State to finalize the budget.
There are proposed cuts to municipalities, and we need to be prepared for this and will need to revisit our
budget and make changes and cuts as necessary. The latest proposal reduces our state funding by 9%, 2%
higher than originally forecasted.

Mr. Salerno inquired as to who is responsible for tying Old Lyme into our waste water system;
and Mr. Nickerson responded that Old Lyme is financially responsible for all costs associated with tying
into the current system, including paying to upgrade our current pump station.

8. Selectman’s Response

Mr. Nickerson responded to Mr. Rando’s comments. Yes there are time clocks for Town employees;

affected by Old Lyme tying into the sewer system, and this tie-in was order by the State of Connecticut.
Regarding the issues with the water bill; the Water & Sewer Department is aware of the issues and is
correcting resident’s bills if appropriate, as they did with Mr. Rando’s water bill. The Water & Sewer
