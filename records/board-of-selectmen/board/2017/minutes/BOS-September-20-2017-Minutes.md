3a. Review and approve the Smoking Policy for all East Lyme Town Buildings

3b. Review and approve the Memorandum of Agreement between the Town and the East Lyme Police
Local Union #2852, Council 4, AFSCME, AFL-CIO

DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein stated that this Memorandum of Agreement is a necessary change since the
switchover to an independent police department. Under the current contract, if a Sergeant was not available
for a shift, the open shift would become available to all officers. Since going independent, it has been agreed
that there will be a sergeant on each shift. This MOA changes the language so that there are two separate lists
to work from, one for Sergeants and one for Officers. The Chief noted that having a Sergeant on each shift
was not a necessity when we were under the Resident State Trooper program because the State Police always
had a Sergeant available on each shift. Ms. Hardy pointed out that we have six (6) Sergeants and one (1)
Administrative Sergeant, and she inquired if the Administrative Sergeant and the Chief were eligible to be on
the call list, and the Chief responded yes to the Administrative Sergeant and no to the Chief, as he is not a
member of the Union. The Chief stated that the MOA would go to the Union Executive Board for approval
and then will be ratified before coming back for the First Selectman’s signature to finalize it. Mr. Nickerson
thanked the Chief and noted that he has been doing a great job in our Town since he came on board, and
reminded the BOS that they will continue to approve all contracts for the Union, and that this responsibility
will not shift to the newly formed Police Commission.

MOTION (5)

Mr. Seery moved to approve the Memorandum of Agreement between the Town and the East Lyme Police
Local Union #2852, Council 4, AFSCME, AFL-CIO.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 6-0.

6b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson stated that this is obviously a very tough situation with the budget, and no matter
how it ends up some will be unhappy. Under statute if a decision cannot be made by October 1*, the
Governor’s proposed budget would go into effect.

There are a lot of fun things going on this weekend in town, and the weather is shaping up to be
beautiful. On Saturday and Sunday, for the first time we will welcome jet ski races, hosted by East Coast
Watercross, in the Niantic Bay. Spectators are encouraged to sit on Cini Beach or anywhere along the
Boardwalk to watch this exciting event. On Saturday night at 6:30pm, the Harlem Wizards will be at the
East Lyme High School to raise funds for the East Lyme Youth Coalition. This group brings invaluable
speakers and information to our Town to speak to our students and residents about many important topics.
Berkshire Hathaway is hosting its 8" annual Sunshine Kids SK at McCook Point on Saturday at 10am; for
more information please contact the Berkshire Hathaway office.

On Monday, September 25" at 6:30 p.m. the Board of Selectmen and the Board of Education will
hear a presentation at the East Lyme High School, Room B101, regarding a proposed micro grid project
that will save the Town money by putting numerous Town and BOE buildings together on one grid. The
money that would be used to pay for such a project, should it move forward, would come solely from
grants.
Mr. Dagle announced The Brian T. Dagle Foundation will be holding its 5" annual Community
Forum on Suicide Prevention and Mental Health on September 28" at 7:00 p.m. at the East Lyme High
School.

Mr. Nickerson reported that there had been an issue with the water in town today, and that they
had made the decision to be overly cautious. As part of daily testing of the water system, it was found that
there were traces of E Coli in the raw, untreated water. Subsequent test were done on the treated water and

7. Public Comment

Mr. David Funk, 38 Hope Street, Apartment 1212, addressed the Board with several issues.
Regarding the micro grid presentation, Mr. Funk stated that the town has participated in numerous such
projects in the past, and he has yet to hear feedback on dollars saved by participating in these programs.
Regarding the budget, Mr. Funk stated that it is affecting all Towns in Connecticut. In East Lyme he is
wondering why student enrollment is going down but both our BOE and Town budgets are going up.
Regarding the dog park being located up at the Darrow Pond site, he stated that this property was
purchased as open space and should not be used for the dog park. Regarding the allocation of funds for the
BOE vehicle, which was approved at the town meeting this evening, he asked the BOS to review the
policy regarding the Capital Non-Recurring Fund to make sure that this was an appropriate place to be
using the funds.

8. Selectman’s Response

Mr. Nickerson stated that we continuously report in as to how we are saving by utilizing these
projects; most recently the street lights, which is a very successful program that is yielding a good deal of
savings in electricity costs. Regarding student enrollment, it has actually gone up and is projected to keep
increasing in the coming years; and although the budgets have increased slightly each year, it was noted
that the BOE has been operating at bare minimum and not added any new programs. Regarding the
Darrow Pond land, the Town purchased approximately 300 acres; 200 of which were set aside for
conservation and the other 100 acres to be used for town projects. There is a beautiful trail system that
connects with the Airline Trail. Regarding the BOE vehicle, the funds are coming from the rent the BOE
collects from Project LEARN and not for taxpayer money.
