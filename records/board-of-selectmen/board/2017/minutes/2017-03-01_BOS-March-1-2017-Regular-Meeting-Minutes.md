2a. Authorize First Selectman Mark Nickerson to execute all documents including a contract
with the State of Connecticut for a $500,000 STEAP grant
MOTION (6)
Mr. Seery Resolved, that Mark Nickerson, First Selectman of the Town of East Lyme be and
hereby is authorized to execute on behalf of this municipal corporation a (Grant Agreement /
Personal Services Agreement) with the State of Connecticut for financial assistance to
“Complete the connection to, and the construction of, a sanitary facility at Cini Park in Niantic
(East Lyme)” (STEAP 2016-02).

In addition, that Mark Nickerson, First Selectman of the Town of East Lyme is hereby
authorized to enter into such agreements, contracts and execute all documents necessary to said
grant with the State of Connecticut.

It is further certified that Mark Nickerson, First Selectman of the Town of East Lyme was
elected First Selectman. His term of office began on December 7, 2015 and will continue to
December 4, 2017. As the East Lyme First Selectman, Mark Nickerson serves as the Chief
Executive Officer for the Town of East Lyme and on March 1, 2017, Mark Nickerson, First
Selectman of the Town of East Lyme had both the authority and the office to sign on behalf of
the Town of East Lyme a (Grant Agreement / Personal Services Agreement) for financial
assistant to “Complete the connection to, and the construction of, a sanitary facility at Cini Park
in Niantic (East Lyme)” (STEAP 2016-02).

DISCUSSION: Mr. Bill Scheer addressed the Board of Selectmen and stated that this STEAP
Grant was awarded in 2015, but that it took the State this long to prepare the final paperwork. In
that time, the wave velocity flood zone has changed, which will require some changes on our
end. Mr. Sheer reported that he is working closely with the State to modify our plans to make
them work with the new regulations. Ms. Hardy inquired about the Town’s monetary
contribution to this project, and Mr. Scheer advised that this project is fully funded by the Town.
Ms. Hardy inquired as to what would happen if the costs of the project are now higher due to the
lapse in time as well as the change in zoning, and Mr. Scheer responded that no work will be
done or money spent until there is a solid plan in place. Mr. Cunningham inquired if we changed
our plans to comply with the new zoning regulations, will the State still fund the Grant; Mr.
Sheer responded that yes they will and that is why he is working closely with them to find a
solution that fits with this Grant’s guidelines.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. In Favor; Mr. Nickerson, Mr. Salerno, Mr. Seery, Mr. Cunningham
and Mr. Dagle. Opposed; Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 5-1.

2b. Temporary closing of the unpaved section of West Society Road

MOTION (7)

Mr. Seery moved that WHEREAS, the East Lyme Board of Selectmen, acting as the Town’s
statutory traffic authority, has determined that the Town of East Lyme is the owner of West
Society Road including the portion more particularly described in Schedule A attached hereto;
and WHEREAS, the Board of Selectman has determined that the condition of the above-
described portion of West Society Road is such that the public should not make motorized
vehicular use of that portion of the road, and that the public convenience and necessity does not
require the Town to keep said portion of West Society Road open for motorized vehicular travel;
and WHEREAS, the Board of Selectmen has determined that it is in the best interest of the Town
to temporarily close the above-described portion of West Society Road to motorized vehicular
use;

IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED:

1. That the above described-portion of West Society Road is hereby closed to and shall not
be used for motorized vehicular travel of any kind or nature whatsoever until further notice.

2. Said portion of West Society Road shall be closed in accordance with the standards and
procedures set forth in the current edition of the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices,
and as presented hereto and attached as Exhibit 1.

This action is a temporary closing of the above-described portion of West Society Road and shall
not be construed or regarded as the Town’s abandonment or discontinuance of West Society
Road or its title to the land comprising said road.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Scheer addressed the Board and explained that this is a temporary closing in
order to leave the option open for using this road in the future should it become feasible. He
noted that there is currently a large undeveloped piece of land adjacent to the road; and should
someone make plans to develop this property, they will also take on the responsibility of paving
that road. Mr. Scheer explained the details of what safety measures are being taken, as well as
an outline of where new signs, stop signs and caution signs, will be placed to ensure the public is
well aware of the changes and how to proceed. Mr. Salerno noted that the Inland Wetlands
Commission has reviewed this plan and is in favor this temporary closure. Ms. Hardy inquired
about the budget to make these changes and put in signs, and Mr. Scheer explained that there
will be no additional funds needs for this project, and it can be executed within the existing
budget utilizing our Town employees complete the work. Mr. Scheer expects to begin moving
forward with this project in the next couple of weeks.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 6-0.

2c. Authorize the reallocation of funds for vehicle acquisition

MOTION (8)
Mr. Seery moved to approve a special appropriation in the amount of up to $36,586.48 from the

remaining balance of the 2015/16 Vehicle Acquisition Program to purchase a vehicle for the
Police Chief and forward to the Board of Finance for approval.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson explained that there are leftover funds in this Acquisition
Program due to the Town finding best deals and making trade-ins in order to bring the cost of
acquisition down. This money is now available and needed to provide the new incoming Chief
of Police with a vehicle. Mr. Nickerson reported that the Public Works Director, Joe Bragaw is
working with Sgt. Mike Macek to order an appropriate vehicle with the necessary package,
which is much less than the standard police vehicle. Mr. Seery inquired if they had priced out
the vehicle with the appropriate package, and Sgt. Mike Macek confirmed that they had and the
estimated cost is $33,976.34. They are requesting the full amount of $36,586.48 in case any
additional costs come up.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 5-0.

moving so quickly to raise the funds for this park, and he noted that they are so far doing a great
job.

Regarding dogs on the Boardwalk, Mr. Nickerson stated that dogs are not allowed on the
boardwalk, and this rule will not be changed. It’s not simply the matter of dog waste not being
picked up, it is also a safety issue as the boardwalk is too narrow to allow for dog walking. Mr.
Nickerson agreed that “dogs are people too” and we all love our pets but there are plenty of
places in town to walk your dog, just not on the boardwalk.

Mr. Nickerson reported that the Police Commission is doing a great job and the process is
moving along. The first round of interviews for the Chief of Police position are being held next
week, with a second round of interviews scheduled for the week of March 13%,

The Water & Sewer Department is working to upgrade the water meters throughout
Town, and the goal is to upgrade to a digital system where the meters can be read from the home
base office. The goal is also to move to a quarterly billing system. The Water Department is
working on funding for this project.
