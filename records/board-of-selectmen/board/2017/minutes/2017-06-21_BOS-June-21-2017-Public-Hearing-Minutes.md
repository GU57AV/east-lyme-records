PUBLIC HEARING OF JUNE 21,2017 ~ “Exar LYE TOWN CLEAR

MINUTES

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Marc Salerno, Rose Ann Hardy and Paul Dagle.
EXCUSED: Dan Cunningham.

Mr. Nickerson called the Public Hearing to consider the adoption of the Ordinance Establishing a
School Building Committee for Alternations and Upgrades to the East Lyme Elementary Schools
to order at 8:20 p.m. and stated that the Pledge of Allegiance was done at the start of the Special
Meeting at 6:30 p.m., as well as at the start of the Town Meeting which followed.

Mr. Nickerson stated that he caused to have published the Notice of Public Hearing in The Day
on June 14, 2017.

Mr. Nickerson explained that it is typical for members of the Board of Education to be added to
the standing Town Building Committee during a school project. The two Board of Education
Members that will be appointed are Dr. Timothy Hagan and Mr. William Derry.

There was no one from the public in attendance to speak on this matter.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Seery moved to adjourn the June 21*' Public Hearing at 8:24 p.m.
Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion Passed 5-0

Respectfully submitted by:
