2a. Discussion and possible action on the proposed lease for the communication tower on

Mostowy Road.
DISCUSSION:

Mr. Seery stated that he is confident that all involved did an exhaustive job of coming up
with a solid project and plan; and he is comfortable moving forward with this project and glad
that our first responders will be safer because of this communications tower.

Attorney Zamarka reported that although there was a lot of discussion at the public
hearing about the terms of the lease, he stated that all of the terms have been agreed upon after
lengthy negotiations over approximately a year’s time.

Ms. Hardy stated that she would like to have a little more time to review the terms of the
lease. Mr. Cunningham also stated that he would be comfortable holding off to the next meeting
to review the lease further. Ms. Seery stated that Board members should remember that the
intent of the tower is not to produce income from rentals, but to provide enhanced safety
measures for our first responders. Mr. Salerno stated that he is okay with waiting but does not
want to wait any longer than the next regular meeting so the project can start.
Mr. Nickerson reported that once construction has begun on the new tower, Marcus
Communications has agreed to bring in a temporary tower so that the setup of the
communications system can begin simultaneously and enhanced service provided for our first
responders sooner and ideally in line with the Independent Police Department.

Mr. Nickerson announced that Chief Mahoney of the Waterford Police Department was
present at tonight’s meeting, and reported that as of April 4, 2017, East Lyme and Waterford
finalized the Memorandum Of Understanding to share police services at the Waterford facility.

Mr. Nickerson announced that as of the next rotation, Sgt. Mike Macek will officially be
moved into the position of Administrative Sergeant; Congratulations to Sgt. Macek.

2b. Appropriation and Transfer $40,000 from Contingency for Police Overtime

MOTION (3)

Mr. Seery moved to approve a transfer in the amount of $40,000 from account 01-01-120-200-
500 (Contingency) to account 01-25-216-100-514 (Police Overtime) due to overtime being
greater than original budget and forward to the Board of Finance for Approval.

DISCUSSION: Sgt. Macek reported that the overtime budget requires an appropriation and
transfer due to having 6 officers out at one time in the past months, which put a big hole in the
overtime budget. Five of the six officers are now back to work. Ms. Hardy inquired if this
covers road work overtime, and Sgt. Macek stated that this is regular duty overtime and not road
work overtime.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 5-0.

2c. Appropriation and Transfer $11,775 from Contingency for ACO

MOTION (4)

Mr. Seery moved to approve a transfer in the amount of $11,775 from account 01-01-120-200-
500 (Contingency) to the following accounts: $66.82 to account 01-25-216-300-326 (Police
Training Supplies), $75.00 to account 01-25-216-300-320 (Police Misc Supplies), $1,633.18 to
account 01-25-216-300-313 (Police Uniforms) and $10,000 to account 01-25-226-100-215
(ACO Fees) due to this being our inaugural year having the ACO we share with Waterford be a
Town of East Lyme employee and forward to the Board of Finance for Approval.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson reported that each town is required to employ an ACO. We share
an ACO with Waterford, and now employ a part time ACO to work evenings and weekends to
take some of the burden off of the full time ACO. These are expenses incurred specifically to
get the part time person in place and get through this fiscal year.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 5-0.

2i. Discussion on Parks & Recreation Department’s request to the BOS to consider a Town
Ordinance to permanently prohibit Dogs on the Niantic Bay Boardwalk
MOTION (9)
Mr. Seery moved the following call:
TOWN OF EAST LYME
BOARD OF SELECTMEN
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
REGARDING AN AMENDMENT TO THE ORDINANCE ENTITLED “ORDINANCE
CONCERNING AREAS UNDER JURISDICTION OF
PARKS & RECREATION COMMISSION”

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 7:15 p.m. on April 19, 2017 at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania
Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to consider a proposed amendment to the Ordinance entitled
“Ordinance Concerning Areas Under Jurisdiction of Parks & Recreation Commission”. Said
amendment would prohibit any person from bringing a dog (other than a certified service dog)
on to the Niantic Bay Boardwalk and would prohibit any person from bringing a dog (other than
a certified service dog) onto any Town owned beach and McCook Point Park from Memorial
Day to Labor Day.

Copies of the full text of the proposed Ordinance are on file for public inspection at the
office of the East Lyme Town Clerk, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut and at
http://www.eltownhall.com/ to which reference may be had. Interested persons are invited to
attend and be heard.

Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut, on this 5th day of April, 2017.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that this is not a new ordinance, but an amendment to an
existing ordinance, and will allow the police department to enforce the items being added with an
infraction. Mr. Seery noted that the group of citizens that has come together to work on building
a dog park in town are very supportive of this ordinance.

Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 5-0.
