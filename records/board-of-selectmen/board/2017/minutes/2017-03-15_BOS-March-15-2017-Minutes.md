2b. Authorize the First Selectman to sign an Interim MOU between East Lyme and Waterford
to share services related to the Independent Police Department

MOTION (6)
Mr. Seery RESOLVED, to authorize the First Selectman, in the name and on behalf of the Town,

to execute and deliver on Interim Memorandum of Understanding between the towns of East
Lyme and Waterford the for detention of persons arrested by the East Lyme Police Department,
and to do all things necessary or desirable to implement said Memorandum of Understanding.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that as we move into our Independent Police Department
and away from the Resident State Trooper program, we will need to make arrangements for
prisoner processing, holding, etc., as we currently utilize the State barracks. We have been in
communication with the Waterford Police Department and their First Selectman and have come
up with a mutually beneficial arrangement for sharing space and resources. Mr. Nickerson noted
that this agreement is fair to both towns, and specifically they made sure to be fair to the
taxpayers in Waterford. Chief Brett Mahoney addressed the Board and agreed that he has taken
great care to make sure that this agreement is fair to all parties including Waterford taxpayers.
Chief Mahoney stated that their facility in Waterford is a state of the art facility with plenty of
space and resources to share, including the services of their Community Service Officer. This
person currently works evenings and on Saturday, and picks up prisoner processing after the
arresting officer has completed his or her mandatory processing, which frees up the officers to be
back out on the road in the community as soon as possible. He reported that they have checked
with all governing agencies, such as the State’s Attorney’s Office, and we have received the go
ahead from all. Although processing will physically take place in Waterford, our prisoners will
be processed using the East Lyme Police Department computer system, which will be installed at
Waterford.

Mr. Seery stated that he is pleased with this agreement and the services that will be
available to our police officers, He inquired if the members of the Board of Selectman could
tour the facility if they so choose; and Chief Mahoney said that they could arrange that any time.

Ms. Hardy inquired as to what would happen if the Waterford Police Department ran out
of space and didn’t have the room for one of our prisoners. Mr. Nickerson explained that there is
something called a Mutual Aid Compact that we have with surrounding towns in which we are
free to utilize each others’ facilities should the need arise. Regarding Schedule B, paragraph 9,
Sgt. Macek explained that this clause is standard and applies generally to a prisoner that may
require medical treatment.

Mr. Dagle inquired as to Schedule B, paragraph 8, and asked if the Waterford Police
Department General Orders and Operating Procedures had been fully reviewed by someone in
East Lyme, and Sgt. Macek responded affirmatively.

Ms. Hardy inquired about computer technical support, and Sgt. Macek responded that we
will need to install a computer and necessary hardware at the Waterford Police Department in
order to be able to access our processing system. He stated that the Waterford officers that will
be assisting in processing prisoners will receive training on our system.

Seconded by Ms. Hardy. Motion passed 5-0.
