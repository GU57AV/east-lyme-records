lc. Delegations

Mr. Ron Rando, 194 Boston Post Road, addressed the Board and first thanked Mrs. Hardy for
facilitating the presentation at the last meeting, given by three East Lyme High School students,
honoring a couple of our Town’s oldest Veteran’s. Mr. Rando expressed disapproval in how the
Costco project has been handled, police cars idling, extra money being put into next year’s
budget, and the fact that the State has not yet finalized the budget; and also read an excerpt from
another resident expressing disapproval. Mr. Rando stated that in his opinion some of the town
departments should be privatized; i.e. Parks & Recreation and Water & Sewer.

3a. Presentation and Review of the Fire Study

DISCUSSION: Mr. Mark Nickerson introduced Mr. John Nickerson and Mr. Nick Delia of JLN
Associates, and stated that there is no relationship between himself and Mr. Nickerson of JLN
Associates. JLN Associates won the bid to conduct this fire study about a year ago, and has
worked hard with our Fire Departments, Fire Marshal and Emergency Services to compile the
report being presented tonight. The full presentation as well as the complete Fire Study can be
found on the Town of East Lyme webpage. The meeting itself will be re-played periodically on
Channel 22. Mr. Mark Nickerson stated that he was pleased with the study overall and feels that
there are some good recommendations that should be discussed. Bottom line is we have a great
staff of both career and volunteer fire fighters and EMTs that are doing a great job. The study and
its recommendations will be reviewed and considered for future discussion.

Mr. Nickerson called for a short recess at 9:04 p.m. The Board returned at 9:10 p.m. and Mr.
Nickerson called the meeting back to order.

3b. Appoint two BOE members to the Town Building Committee — Timothy Hagen and
William Derry

MOTION (5)

as voting members for the East Lyme Elementary Schools’ Alteration Projects, each with a term

6a. Ex-Officio

Marc Salerno reported that the Town Building Committee met and out of the six (3) bids
received, they choose three (3) companies to interview. At a meeting on July 20", these three
companies will be interviewed by the Committee.

Rose Ann Hardy recognized two outstanding women that have recently passed away; Ms.
Betty Murphy and Ms. Luane Lange. Both women dedicated many hours of their time and
energy volunteering for various organizations within our Town, and their presence will be missed.
She noted that their absence is going to leave a large gap in our volunteer community. All Board
Members were in agreement with Ms. Hardy’s statement, and expressed their sympathy in the
passing of these women.

Paul Dagle reported that the Inland Wetlands Commission held a special meeting on June
26" at which time the public was able to speak regarding the Green Valley and Spring Rock Road
matters. Not everyone had an opportunity to speak before the close of the meeting, so the public
hearing has been continued until the next regular meeting of the IWC.

6b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson reported that for the 2’ year in a row, out town was not hit with a
mandatory water conservation ruling. The Lions hosted an outstanding event once again this
year, with the lobster fest and arts & crafts event being held this past weekend on Town Hall
grounds. Lots of people came out and enjoyed great food and a variety of arts and crafts. The
Fourth of July holiday brought in a huge amount of tourists to our beaches, and overall our Parks
& Rec employees and our police department did a great job of keeping traffic moving and dealing
with the overflow of parking that the crowds brought with them. Being the first major beach
holiday for our newly formed independent police force, they did a great job and we certainly have
identified some opportunity for improvement as the summer rolls along. Arthur Murray
celebrated 30 years on Main Street with a gala event and sidewalk dancing on Friday night, which
was a lot of fun. Mr. Nickerson attended the ribbon cutting for the newly re-built Crescent Beach
Bluff walk, which extends from Crescent Beach to the Niantic Yacht Club. A lot of community
effort went into this project, which rebuilt the bluff after super storm Sandy wiped it out a few

years ago.
