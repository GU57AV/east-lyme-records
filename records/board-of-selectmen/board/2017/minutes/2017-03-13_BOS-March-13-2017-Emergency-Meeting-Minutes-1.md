TOWN OF EAST LYME
BOARD OF SELECTMEN
EMERGENCY MEETING CALLED DUE TO IMPENDING BLIZZARD
TO CANCEL THE SCHEDULED REFERENDUM
MONDAY, MARCH 13, 2017

MINUTES
PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Kevin Seery, Rose Ann Hardy, Dan Cunningham and Paul Dagle

ALSO PRESENT: Anna Johnson, Finance Director; and Town Attorneys Ed O’Connell and Tracy
Collins

EXCUSED: Marc Salerno
Mr. Nickerson called the emergency meeting to order at 4:00 p.m. and led the pledge of allegiance.

3a. Cancel the scheduled Referendum of Tuesday, March 14, 2017, due to the impending blizzard.
MOTION (1)

Motion by Mr. Seery. Resolved, after receiving reports from multiple weather services and consultation
with the registrars of voters and moderator, due to a severe weather alert in effect for March 14, 2017,
the Board of Selectmen hereby cancels the March 14, 2017 referendum appropriating and authorizing
bonds for renovations and upgrades of the elementary schools in the amount of $37,500,000.

Seconded by Mr. Dagle. Motion Carried 5-0

MOTION (2)

Motion by Mr. Seery. Resolved, to amend the existing Notice for the March 15, 2017 BOS meeting to
include on the agenda; (1) resolution authorizing the First Selectman to call a Special Town Meeting on
March 21, 2017 to consider the resolution entitled “Resolution Making An appropriation In The Amount
Of $37,500,000 For The Planning, Design, Acquisition, Construction, Equipping and Furnishing Of
Alterations And Upgrades of The Lillie B. Haynes, Flanders and Niantic Center Elementary Schools
And Authorizing The Issuance Of $37,500,000 Bonds Of The Town to Meet Said Appropriation And
Pending The Issuance Of $37,500,000 Bonds Of The Town to Meet Said Appropriation and Pending
The Issuance Thereof The Making Of Temporary Borrowings For Such Purpose” (the “Bond
Resolution”) and (2) to submit such Bond Resolution to referendum on March 28, 2017.

Seconded by Mr. Cunningham. Motion Carried 5-0

3b. Publish notice of cancellation of referendum in The Day newspaper.

DISCUSSION:

Mr. Seery confirmed that a notice of cancellation of referendum was appropriately published in The Day
newspaper, evidenced by the attached Exhibit 1.

4. Adjournment

MOTION (3)

Motion by Mr. Seery to adjourn the March 13, 2107, Emergency Meeting of the Board of Selectmen at
4:05 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Cunningham. Motion Carried 5-0. FILED
