Id. Delegations
Holly Cheeseman, State Representative, addressed the Board and gave an update on how things are

progressing in Hartford. She reported that the Legislature has called for a special session on
December 29, 2017, to restore $54 million to the Medicare Savings Program, but will likely set a
new date for the first week in January when everyone has returned from the holiday break.
Currently, the state is operating under an approximate $200 million deficit. Passed today was the
Tax Cuts and Jobs Act. A public hearing was held at Waterford High School this week to discuss
the future of Dominion, and the overall discussion was positive. Ms. Cheeseman stated that the
Children’s Museum annual “Countdown to Noon” event will take place at the East Lyme
Community center from 10am until noon on Sunday, December 31°. This is a great event for the
kids and family. Merry Christmas and Happy New Year!

3c. Authorize First Selectman to Sign Check Registers

MOTION (21)

Mr. Seery RESOLVED, that the Board of Selectmen delegates to the First Selectman and upon the
absence or disability of the First Selectman, to the Deputy First Selectman, the Board’s obligation to sign
the orders of the Board of Selectmen on the Town Treasury for payment of regularly budgeted expenses
of the Town, with exception of those of a capital nature over $50,000 where bids are required; and
further that the signed orders, covering the period subsequent to the previous regular meeting of the
Board, be presented at each regular meeting of the Board for review.

FURTHER RESOLVE that in the absence of the First Selectman and his Deputy, the First
Selectman may delegate the obligation to sign the orders of the Board of Selectmen to the Second
Deputy First Selectman for payment of the expenses of the Town.

Seconded by Mr. Salerno. Motion passed 6-0.

7a. Ex-Officio

Mr. Seery reported that at the Parks & Recreation Commission they honored the passing of
Milan Keser with emotional memories and stories of his many years of service to our community.
He reported that there will be further discussion to set the 2018 fee schedule at the next meeting.

Mr. Salerno stated that the Town Building Committee will be meeting on Thursday and the
budget is on the agenda for review and discussion.

Mr. Dagle reported that the Inland Wetlands Agency did not meet due to lack of a quorum.
He reported that at the Harbor Management / Shellfish Commission meeting, it was decided that the
dingy dock proposal for the Grand Street boat launch will be going back to the drawing board. He
stated that up for consideration is that any revenue generated that exceeds costs should be put into a
separate fund. Mr. Nickerson stated that it would be reviewed by Finance Director Anna Johnson
and the Town Attorney. Mr. Dagle reported that the Harbor Management plan ordinance is under
review and a procedural discussion with the Board of Selection will be on an upcoming meeting.
6b. First Selectman’s Report

Mr. Nickerson stated that the passing of Milan Keser brings a great loss to the community
as Mr. Keser gave his all and then gave some more. He will be sadly missed, and best wishes to his
family during this difficult time.

Mr. Nickerson announced that everyone with little children or anyone who likes to have fun
should go to Countdown to Noon hosted by the Children’s Museum and taking place on Sunday,
December 31" from 10am until Noon. It’s a lot of fun and includes a balloon drop at Noon. On the
calendar, we are halfway through the current budget year already which means the time for
reviewing next year’s budget is coming up. Mr. Nickerson was very pleased to see all of the
taxpayers that came out tonight to vote on the items at the Town Meeting. Merry Christmas, and
Happy Hanukah as it comes to an end, and have a joyous and healthy new year. See everyone al
the next Board of Selectmen meeting on January 3", where there will be a couple of big items on;
including the Cini Park bathrooms and the park on Main Street.
