1c. Delegations
Mr, Seery stated that he understands that a few people are here to speak about concerns with
the coyote population in town, and he advised that comments should be kept to five minutes or
less, He stated that our two Animal Control Officers were in attendance this evening to give a
presentation on this topic, and that it is on the agenda so more information will be provided
shortly. On Thursday, July 28th at 7:00 p,m., there will be a presentation here at Town Hall by a
DEEP representative,

The following people spoke regarding their concern with the coyote population in town and
would llke to see more done:

Anne Thurlow, B0 Smlth Street, Nlantlc; Ann York, 86 Srnlth Street, Niantlc; James McClure, 5
Faulkner Drive, Niantic; Jean Mountzoures, BB Smith Street; Dorothy North, 42 Smith Avenue,
Niantic; Lisa Steinberg, 22 York Avenue.

2. Presentation by the Animal Control Officer - Animal Control Officer Robert Yuchniuk took the
podium and explained, as Mr. Seery did earlier, that coyotes are managed by the State
Department of Energy and Environmental Protection (DEEP) and not the local animal control
officers. He explained that he and the assistant ACO only respond to calls for wildlife if the
wildlife is exhibiting signs of sickness or aggression. They must abide by the State statutes
which require that if you trap a coyote, you must euthanize that animal; they cannot be
relocated because they are classified as a rabies vector species. He stated that a representative
from the State DEEP will present information and answer questions at the Town Hall on
Thursday, July 28th at 7:00 p.m. More information can be found on the State DEEP website at
https://porta l.ct.gov/DEEP/Wild life/Ngisance-Wildlife/Living-with-Covotes.
