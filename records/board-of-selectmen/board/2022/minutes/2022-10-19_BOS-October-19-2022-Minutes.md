3b.     Charter Revision Update
DISCUSSION: Mr. Seery reported that the appointed Charter Revision commission held their

4a, Ex-Officio Reports

Ms. Cicchiello Reported :
   . Harbor Management Shellfish Commission continues their search for a Harbor Master.
   . Commission received information from Moorings Inc. to switch the mooring registration
       process to an electronic process. They will hear a public presentation on November 2nd,
       and they are seeking additional bids from similar companies for this service,

Mr. Cunningham Reported:
   . Parks & Recreation Commission has a reported fund balance of approximately $41,000,
      They will continue to review what makes up this account, as well as scheduling an audit
      for said account.
   . The new tent at McCook's was destroyed in a recent storm; the Town is seeking a full
      refund and the Commission is investigating what type of structure should replace said
      tent keeping in mind the deed restrictions in place.
   . There is still a high demand by the public for pickleball courts.
   . The Commission is developing a revised vision statement and set of goals.
   o Discussion was started regarding branding of the department.

        event will take place on October 27th at 6:00 p.m, at the East Lyme High School.
