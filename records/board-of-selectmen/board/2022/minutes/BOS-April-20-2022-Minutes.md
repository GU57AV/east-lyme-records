2a. Special Appropriation - FEMA Boardwalk Repairs
DISCUSSION: Mr. Scheer explained that stress cracks are expected and simply need
to be taken care of properly. He reported that the Town will be filling the cracks with
a product that will not only create a permanent bond, but it will not discolor due to
direct exposure to UV sunlight. It was reported that the cracks that are being filled
developed within six months of the project completion, with no additional cracks
surfacing since that time. The proposed funds to be used are from FEMA in response
to Hurricane Sandy. This project is anticipated to begin in late summer/early fall.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Scheer explained that while investigating the leaks in the new

Request for Proposal (RFP) - East Lvme Police Building Masonry repair and
sealing design proiect

        The Town of East Lyme Engineering Department is requesting proposals from qualified
Architectural firms to prepare plans and specification for the repair and sealing of the exterior of the
newly occupied East Lyme Police department at277 West Main Street in Niantic. This will be a
prevailing wage job for the contractor. The proposal shall include the three sections described below.
The Town will provide an award and a notice to proceed with design phase of the project to the
selected Architecture firm. The bidding and construction inspections phases will be awarded
separately during the course of the project. The Town reserves the right to not award the subsequent
phases, and to perform these phases with Town Staff.



Background:

        The Town of East Lyme purchased and has renovated2TT West Main Street in Niantic as a
new police station and emergency operations building. The Renovation included a new roof, which
included a new cap and flashing on the top of the block walls, which has alleviated water entering the
masonry units from the top of the block walls. New caulking was applied around the existing
windows and doors. The exterior of the building consists of 1980's split faced block and regular
cinder block which has water infiltration issues from the face of the block. There is also minor
cracking, an area with impact damage, clogged or possibly covered weep holes, areas of block
susceptible to water infiltration ftom snow piles, and other localized minor defects.

Proposal breakdown:

The Town of East Lyme is requesting a proposal from architecture firms broken down onto these
three sections:

    1. Provide a price / proposal to:
           a. Inspect the exterior of the building and identiff all the areas that will need masonry
             repair, determine what the proper repair should be, and provide an opinion of
             probable cost.
          b. Prepare option(s) for the Town to choose the most cost-effective long term liquid
             sealing product, means and method of application, and opinion of probable cost.
          c. Prepare construction bid documents for the repair and sealing of the masonry exterior
             of the building.
   2. Provide a price / proposal to provide construction bidding services including
      recommendations to the Town for contractor selection.
   3. Provide a price / proposalto for construction administration and inspection.
