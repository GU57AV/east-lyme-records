4a. Ex-Officio Reports
        Ms. Santoro reported that she attended the Library Board of Trustees meeting;
they filled one open vacancy and have one yet to fill. They are switching auditors
and will go out to bid soon. Lots of excitement about the East Lyme History Center;
the building on Boston Post Road is going to work out great for that use.

4b. First Selectman's Report
        Mr. Seery reported that Covid is starting to rise, and he is working closely with
Ledge Light Health District. to monitor such. Senator Blumenthal came to town and
wrote a letter to Amtrak to support our local effort to work with Amtrak on replacing
their fencing with a suitable option, such as the link fencing that is currently there.
Their proposed fencing is a thick rail that may inhibit the views of the water. He met
with a group to look into hiring a contracted firm to work with us to seek out, find
and even apply for such grants if we so choose; they work on a contracted, hourly
rate. Next meeting agenda will include an item for sealant work on the public safety
building as well as some repairs to the Boardwalk that will be covered under a FEMA
grant already received. The Miracle League Gala was held last week, and it was a
successful event raising a good deal of money. This year's Boardwalk 5k will be held
on April 23'd.
