 2b. Proposed Emergency Access to Romagna Road via Stoneywood Drive
 DISCUSSION: Alex Klose, Town Engineer, was present to speak on this matter. He stated that
 there is no action being taken on this matter tonight, and that tonight's presentation is to get
 the information out to the Board and the public for information purposes. Mr. Klose stated that
 this area is prone to flooding, and the Town would like to be proactive and create an alternate
 route in and out of this neighborhood to allow for safe egress in the event of future flooding. He
 reported that this matter was approved at the July L2, 2022, meeting of the Planning
 Commission, noting that an approved B-24is a statutory requirement. He stated that they are
 currently preparing the plans and conducting a survey of property, which is a Town of East Lyme
 owned right-of-way, in preparation for applying for any grants that may become available; he
 noted that he would re-visit the Federal Infrastructure Bill to see if any funds are available
 through that source.

3b.     Charter Revision
DISCUSSION: Mr. Seery stated that no action will be taken tonight and that he would like to
communicate some possible topics for said charter revision. Some of the items that have come
up for review are; consider four year term for Board of Selectmen, consider changing the term
of the First Selectman to four years, consider a Town Manager versus a First Selectman,
eliminate the automatic referendum in the yearly budget process, consider changing the town
meeting process and the amount that needs to go to town meeting for approval; currently
purchase of $10,000 or more requires town meeting and that should be increased. Consider
setting up a separate grant account, change the policy on filling vacancies on boards and
commissions, review the policy covering the absence of the First Selectman, and consider
changing the policy on appointed positions. Ms. Santoro suggested that former First Selectmen
be queried on their thoughts for possible changes. Mr. Seery stated that they will consider
appointing from nine to eleven people to this Charter Revision Committee.
