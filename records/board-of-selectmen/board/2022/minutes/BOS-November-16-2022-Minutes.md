3c.    Discussion - Dominion Building 278 Main Street (former Police station)
DISCUSSION: Mr. Seery stated that as per previous Board of Selectmen discussions, the Town
had decided to pass on purchasing the property from Dominion. Mr. Seery reported that he has
been contacted by the Niantic Main Street group asking if we could put off any final decision
whether to purchase the property or not until after the Yale Charette study is complete. All of
the Board of Selectmen members were in favor of waiting until after the results of the Charette
study have been published to have a final discussion with Dominion regarding the possible
purchase. Mr. Seery stated that he has spoken with the representative from Dominion, and they
are amenable to waiting for the Charette study to be completed.
