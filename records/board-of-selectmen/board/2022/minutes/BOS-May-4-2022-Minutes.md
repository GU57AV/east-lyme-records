2f. CNRE Surcharge NIP Bottle Funds Return
DISCUSSION: SB 1037 (AN ACT CONCERNING SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT) was adopted,
and it establishes that Connecticut liquor wholesalers will begin collecting a 5-cent surcharge
on 50ml "nip" liquor bottles, the revenue of which will go to cities and towns to fight the
widespread litter of these liquor bottles. The purpose is to help eliminate a large amount of
trash in parks and along roadways and trailways. This is a surcharge, not a deposit. It was
implemented in October 2021. Starting in April 2022 and every six months after, the funds
for each bottle will be paid by the distributor to the municipality. The funds can be used for
reducing the impact of solid waste, from hiring a coordinator, to buying storm drains that
keep trash out, to street sweepers. We received our first check from this process in the
amount of $72,421,8O (248,436 bottles). We expect the next six months to be greater as it
includes our peak seasonal months. Our Main Street representatives have contacted me with
some suggestions for use of these funds, for example, additional trash cans. Allocation of the
funds will be in accordance with our appropriations process.

MOTTON (10)
Ms. Santoro MOVED to establish corresponding revenue and expenditure accounts in CNRE
Fund 32 for the purpose of setting aside funds collected for the 5-cent surcharge on 50ml
"nip" liquor bottles in accordance with SB 1037 - AN ACT CONCERNING SOLID WASTE
MANAGEMENT and forward to the Board of Finance for approval, Account to be titled,
"Surcharge - SB 1037",
