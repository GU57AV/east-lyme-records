                                EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                              SPECIAL MEETING OF FEBRUARY 2,2022
                                           MINUTES


PRESENT: Kevin Seery, Rose Ann Hardy, Dan Cunningham, Bill Weber, Ann Cicchiello and Anne
Santoro

ALSO PRESENT: Chief Mike Finkelstein, Lt, Mike Macek and Lt. Dana Jezierski

Mr. Seery called the meeting to order at 5:31 p.m

MOTION (1)
Ms. Santoro MOVED to enter into executive session for the purpose of taking a detailed tour of the new
public safety building; this tour must happen in executive session for safety and security reasons, and will
include Chief Finkelstein, Lt. Macek and Lt, Jezierski.
Seconded by Mr. Cunningham. Motion passed 6-0.

The Board entered into executive session at 5:31 p.m. They returned at 6:28 p.m. with no formal votes
taken.

MOTTON (2)
Mr. Cunningham MOVED to return from executive session at 6:28 p,m, and stated that no formal votes had
been taken.
Seconded by Ms, Santoro. Motion passed 6-0.

MOTTON (3)
Ms. Santoro MOVED to adjourn the February 2,2022, special meeting of the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 6:29 p.m.
Seconded by Mr, Cunningham. Motion passed 6-0.

Respectfully Submitted By




Sandra Anderson
Recording Secretary
