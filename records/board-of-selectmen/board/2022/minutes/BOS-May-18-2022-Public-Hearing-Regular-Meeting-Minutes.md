2b. Parks and Recreation - Compost Bathrooms
DISCUSSION: Mr. Putnam handed on information on the proposed compost bathrooms, attached
hereto as Exhibit #1. He explained that they have three coming that are being funded using
ARP funds; one at McCook's by the upper bathrooms, and two at Hole in the Wall next to the
existing facility. Summary of the product is that it is year-round, it is solar powered and will
have a fan and proport to be odor free even in high heat and humidity. The wind load is rated
to 120 mph. The cost is approximately $75,000 per unit. The town receives many inquiries into
adding more public restrooms downtown and at the parks and beaches, and this product may be
a particularly good solution.
