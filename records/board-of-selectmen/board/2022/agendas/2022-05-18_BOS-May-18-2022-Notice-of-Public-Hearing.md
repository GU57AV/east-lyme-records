                            TOWN OF EAST LYME
                            BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                        NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
REGARDING ADOPTION OF AN AMENDMENT TO AN ORDINANCE ENTITLED:
         "Flood Damage Prevention" Town Ordinance Chapter 151


Notice is hereby given, of a public hearing to be held by the East Lyme Board of
Selectmen at 7:00 pm on May 18, 2022, at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108
Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to consider the adoption of an
amendment to an ordinance entitled: "Flood Damage Prevention" Town
Ordinance Chapter 151. Said ordinance provides regulatory controls for flooding
in the Town of East Lyme.
       A copy of the full text of the proposed ordinance is on file for public
inspection at the office of the East Lyme Town Clerk, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue,
Niantic, Connecticut and at http://www.eltownhall.com/ to which reference may be
had. Interested persons are invited to attend and be heard.


Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut on this day May 4,2022.
EAST LYME BOARD OF SELECTMEN
