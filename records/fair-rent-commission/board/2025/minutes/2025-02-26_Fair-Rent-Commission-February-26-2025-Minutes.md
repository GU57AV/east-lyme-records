IN ATTENDANCE: Seated Fair Rent Commission Members Chairman Steve Carpenteri,
Deputy Chairman Dan Horgan, John Vilcheck, Nancy Johnson and Mike Bekech (alt member
to replace recused member Alicia Chandler). Gwen Dowd (alt member) and Alicia Chandler
(recused from Public Hearing) were also in attendance in the audience.

ALSO IN ATTENDANCE: Fair Rent Commission (FRC) Town Staff Member Stacey Konakowitz,
Board of Selectmen Ex-Officio Jason Deeble, Town Attorney Tracy Collins, Tenant Representative

Mr. Carpenteri called the meeting to order at 6:00 p,m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance

Chairman Carpenteri reviewed the ground rules and procedures, and he introduced Attorney
Richard Pascal representing Alpha Capital LLC, and Attorney Chelsea Connery of the CT Fair
Housing Center.

Chairman Carpenteri read the Public Hearing Notice into the record, along with the list of
applicants, and then Sandra Anderson (Notary Public) administered the oath to testify
truthfully to all the witnesses,

For the Tenants
Attorney Chelsea Connery stated that she will have two tenant representatives testify
tonight; these two people were chosen to speak on behalf of all the Windward Village
tenants, Bessie Mountzoures and Charles LaPlante; both tenants were unable to attend
tonight's public hearing due to health issues, so they each had a representative present.
Attorney Connery stated that most of the tenants overall are elders over the age of 62 and
disabled persons. Title 47a of the General Statues of Connecticut protects both classes at
the state level. Attorney Connery presented documents of evidence and entered such as
Plaintiff's Exhibit 1.

The Commission members reviewed each of the individual applications, and asked questions
for clarity on some items, It was decided that the complaints for Scott Olenick and Charles
LaPlante, Jr. be tabled in order for those complainants to provide 1099's in order for the
commission to make a better informed decision. It was agreed that any tenants that have
investment income should bring that information to the Town Hall and deliver it to Stacey
Konakowitz, either in person or via email to skonakowitz@eltownhall,com. The
complainants were given until Thursday, March 6th to provide the requested information.

Commission Member Mike Bekech stated that he currently is a member on the Commission
on Aging and had worked closely with the Town's social worker at the time, Jennifer Yu, who
also has been assisting a complainant. He asked for confirmation that this is not a conflict
of interest; both Attorney Connery and Ms. Kinseley stated that they had no concerns with
Mr. Bekech remaining on the commission for these complaints.

MOTTON (1)
Mr. Horgan MOVED to close the record for the following complainants; Bessie Mountzoures,
John Mountzoures, and Peter Roberts.
Seconded by Mr, Vasko. Motion passed 5-0.

MOTTON (2)

of February I,2025 through January 37,2026; March's rent should include the 9175
arrearage payment for February 2025.
Seconded by Mr. Bekech. Motion passed 5-0.

Motion made by Dan Horgan to increase Mr. Mountzoures'rent to $1,400. MOTION
WITHDRAWN.

MOTToN (3)
Mr. Bekech MOVED to increase Mr. Mountzoures'rent by $300, adjusted rent is g1,450 as of
December 1,2024 through December 3I,2025.
Seconded by Mr. Horgan. Motion passed 5-0.

Peter Roberts vs. Alpha Capital LLC
MOTTON (4)
Mr' Horgan MOVED to maintain the original order from the February 4th meeting of the Fair
Rent Commission as follows: Increase Mr, Roberts'rent by $210 per month, adjusted rent is
$1,260 per month, through December 3I,2025; this is retroactive back to January I,2O2S.
Seconded by Mr. Bekech. Motion passed 5-0.

A discussion was held on whether or not the commission has the authority to order
retroactive action before they were appointed and the commission was in existence.
Attorney Collins stated that this is a matter of law and Alpha Capital does have the right to
bring this up in the case of an appeal. She reviewed the language in the ordinance with the
commission members, and it was determined that it is within their authority, according to
the ordinance, to go retroactive back to the date the complaint was filed.
