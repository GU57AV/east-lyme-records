IN ATTENDANCE: Seated Fair Rent Commission Members Chairman Steve Carpenteri,
Deputy Chairman Dan Horgan, John Vilcheck, Nancy Johnson and Mike Bekech (alt member

attendance in the audience.

ALSO IN ATTENDANCE: Fair Rent Commission (FRC) Town Staff Member Stacey Konakowitz,
Board of Selectmen Ex-Officio Jason Deeble, Town Attorney Tracy Collins, Tenant Representative
Attorney Chelsea Connery of the Connecticut Fair Housing Center, and Alpha Capital
representatives Attorney Richard Pascal.
Mr. Carpenteri called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Attorney Collins explained the evidentiary portion of the public hearing is closed, so the
commission cannot ask any questions, They may, however, ask her for clarification, but
they cannot ask for facts. She reviewed the thirteen points with them, noting that their job
is to determine if the proposed rent increase is harsh and unconscionable; and if so, the
commission needs to determine what a fair increase will be.
