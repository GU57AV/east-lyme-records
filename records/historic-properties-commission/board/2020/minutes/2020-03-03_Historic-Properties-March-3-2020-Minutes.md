             EAST LYME HISTORIC PROPERTIES COMMISSION
                          MEETING MINUTES
                                 3 North Road, Niantic
                                    March 3,2020


Members present: Barb Johnson Low, Mark Christensen, Steve Marks-Hamilton, Dan
Cunningham, Rob McCauley, Maggi Prokop. Dick Waterman and Bem Bullock. Mr.
Waterman was seated.                                     FILED

Pledge of Allegiance
                                               EAST LYME TOWN CLERK
PUBLIC DELEGATIONS: Art Linares, Jan Larson, John Bullock.

AGENDA ADDITIONS: None.

MINUTES: February minutes approved. Motion by Mark, second by Rob, unanimous

CORRESPONDENCE: None

OLD BUSINESS:
. Signage for Historic Houses / Property / Landmarks: John Wilson, owner of
  Signcraft Signs, presented mock-ups of "circa signs" as designed by Burt and Steve
  for consideration. Discussion ensued regarding shape, size, font, mounting holes
  and background. Rob moved to approve an oval sign with the font as presented by
  mock-up #4 on the handout sheet. Mark seconded. Approved, unanimous.

    Addition discussion ensued regarding "circa sign" price and creating a vehicle for the
    historic properties commission to receive funds. Currently there is no vehicle. ln the
    absense of a vehicle, John Wilson has agreed to donate $25 per sign to the three
    historic properties by dividing the amount between the three. As this is
    cumbersome, Barb has offered to discuss creating another option with Anna
    Johnson and Mark Nickerson

a   Demolition Delay Ordinance Process Update: Barb has phoned and emailed
    Bill Mulholland and Carmen Ames on several occasions to establish the
    historic properties commission email address and we await its creation.

a   Historic Properties Documentation Project Update: Barb has asked for
    $2000.00 for documentation of houses built between 1888 and 1920 in the
    proposed 2020 budget.

POCD: Barb passed out copes of the commissions recommendations plus the

video program.
