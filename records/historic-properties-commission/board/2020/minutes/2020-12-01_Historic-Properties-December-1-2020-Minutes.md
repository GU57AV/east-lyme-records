NEW BUSINESS:
Review of definition and organization of HPC -Discussion led by Benn Bullock and
Dick Waterman. Dick and Benn reviewed the original stated mission of the HPC and
pointed out that even though the Brookside Farm Museum now has met the state
requirement to survey and define its boundaries, the museum has not yet been added
to the HPC by the town. They would like to correct this omission and address other
concerns such as solidifying the HPC as the clearinghouse for all historic properties
issues.

New ZOOM Account: Barb reported that HPC has used some of its funds to purchase
a ZOOM account that will allow unlimited meeting durations. 2021 HPC meetings will
meet at 7:00PM via ZOOM until further notice.
