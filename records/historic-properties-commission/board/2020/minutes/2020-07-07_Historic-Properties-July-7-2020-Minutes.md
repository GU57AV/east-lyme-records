OLD BUSINESS:
. Earlier this year, Dick Waterman proposed that the town make appropriate repairs to
  and then repurpose the original1923 Niantic Fire House building as a town historical
  museum or arts center. Barb Low consulted with NFD John Dwire regarding this
  idea. Mr. Dwire reported that the NFD currently makes use of the entire first floor for
  rescue equipment storage, and has recently expanded its use of the second floor
  storage of personal protective equipment due to COVID-19. As to the building's
  current condition, it has a new roof, doors, access ramp and signage. Based on this
  information, Mr. Waterman has withdrawn his proposal for the time being.


NEW BUSINESS: Mark Christensen reported that the Tomasczyk property on Grassy
Hill Road has been sold. People are tearing down the boundary stone walls Th outer
part of the walls belong to the town of East Lyme and the inner part of the walls belong
to the landowners.
