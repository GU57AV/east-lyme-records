REA LY ME TOWN CLERK

EAST LYME HISTORIC PROPERTIES COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES FEBRUARY 16, 2016
EAST LYME TOWN HALL 700 PM

MEMBERS PRESENT: L. Lange, S. Marks-Hamilton, B. Murphy, D. Cunningham ex-
officio, M. Christensen arrived at 7:38 pm.

Pledge to the Flag

S. Marks-Hamilton brought up the subject of 1750 Capt. Noah Lester House which is
being restored by Tom Hall. He was wondering if there was any Grants money available
to individuals restoring private historic properties. Discussion re: Preservation Grant
Funding.

A discussion of all the preservation support groups including the Historic Properties
Commission, its being recognized as one of Ct Historic Property Commissions and a
Certified Local Government (CLG) which opens many doors to State and Federal Funds,
by way of different types of grants. In order to qualify for some of these grants/funds
these properties must owned by the requesting agency or have a lease with the Town if
they are owned by the Town. This applies to The Smith-Harris House, Lee House,
Samuel Smith House, EL Historic Cemeteries because they are 501c3 designated as well
as the Historic Properties Commission by way of the Town. D. Cunningham, BOS ex-
officio to the HPC volunteered to work on a flow chart which would explain the way
these organizations are related to each other and the Town.

Minutes of January 19, 2016 — S, Marks-Hamilton — L. Lange unanimous

Samuel Smith House — L. Lange made sure that all the Selectmen had copies of the
enclosures she had given the First Selectman in January for 2016-2017 Budget. M.
Nickerson First Selectman has emailed Boards and Commissions to expect very small
budget increases this budget process. The Archeological Report/lecture at the Niantic
Community Church was well received. L. Lange will check to make sure all the correct
paperwork has been completed for that Grant. She also reported that Phase II
Application has been submitted.

Bike Ride Meeting — M Christensen reported that there will be a 10 mile ride included in
the October 1, 2016 - Open Spaces — Historic Places and EL Bikes and Barbecue Ride,
the 10 mile ride will include stops are the three Historic Houses. They are requesting
help with all the planning and arrangements that need to be done this Spring. EL Parks
and Recreation is coordinating these efforts the next meeting will be March 1, 2016 at
the EL P & R Office at 600 PM. Attendance is requested.
