Samuel Smith Update -

The Town has received a half of a Grant to match a CT Trust grant, for $9,630 for
Samuel Smith House Phase IJ Archaeological Study as a SHPO/CLG. The contract has
been signed by Mark Nickerson and will go through the State signature process. It is
expected that the other half will be granted soon.

The Friends lease notes have been given to the Town Attorney by Mark Nickerson. A
draft lease will be prepared.

Lee House Update — the School House has been painted, a “bug” inspection of the

properties has been completed. R. McCauley has completed a docent handbook and
given a guides update class this past week.

Niantic River Bridge project is on going. The bridge pieces are at the Highway Garage
on Colton Road.

The Bike and Barbeque - Historic Places — Historic Spaces in progressing — it will be
held October 1, 2016.

Reports: Dick has provided Dan with the Lee House lease information. Burt will pursue
Gary. Barbara has received some information and will continue to purse the historical
signs information.
Discussion on the next phase of developing a "significant historic houses" product from

determined to be the priority. Luane will check with Gary Goeschle about the existing
computer data and potential ways to use it to produce maps.

Smith- Harris Reports that their plant sale was a success and that the Ghost Tour and
Dinner was a success. June 5 the CT Cultural Coalition — Brochure will launch at
program at the Smith- Harris Barn, the program will include Walter Woodward, CT State

18 & 19 there will be a Tea. A Volunteer Thank you will be June 24.
