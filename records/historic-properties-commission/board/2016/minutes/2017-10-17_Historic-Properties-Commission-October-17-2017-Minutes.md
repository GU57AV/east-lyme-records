EAST LYME HISTORIC PROPERTIES COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES OCTOBER 17, 2017
EAST LYME TOWN HALL 700 PM

MEMBERS PRESENT: S. Marks-Hamilton, B. Murphy, R. Waterman, M. Christensen,
D. Cunningham Ex-Officio, B. Hobby, Alt. Seated.

Meeting call to order by B. Johnson Low, Acting Chair — 702 PM

Pledge

Minutes of September 19, 2016 Meeting approved — M. Christensen — S. Marks-
Hamilton. Unanimous. 1 abs.

OLD BUSINESS

SAMUEL SMITH HOUSE - M. Christensen spoke about the Archaeology Grant work
being done this week and in the next few weeks. Digging on the east side of the house
and research into the history of the house, dates, owners; Request for a Grant from the
Rotary to add to the Garden - shed. The annual fund raiser Soup & Cider will be
October 22, at the house. Displays will be local artists, antique tools — wood work, goats,
Five Bean Band. The Bug inspection was successful. There is no new activity as far as
Powder Post Beetles, just a few mice. WJ Tree Service will continue to clear trees along
the walking trail.

THOMAS LEE HOUSE — New gutters and down spouts, trenches are being put on the
rear of the house to direct water away from the house. The Bike and Barbeque produced
about 20 visitors as did the other two houses. The weather was not cooperating but all
around B and B was successful and will in all probability be done again next year.

EAST LYME HOUSE INVENTORY - S. Marks-Hamilton reported the he has been
researching old maps and dates. He suggests that we look at houses in 50 years
increments and development. This project would also include schools and churches to
be put on maps for easy research and archival retrieving. M. Christensen made a motion
to the above, B. Hobby seconded. Unanimous.

Discussion that it might be possible to receive funding the CLG as the Commission did
to do the original House Inventory.
