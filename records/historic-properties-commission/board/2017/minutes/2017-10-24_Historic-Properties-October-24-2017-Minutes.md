EAST LYME HISTORIC PROPERTIES COMMISSION MEETING
EAST LYME TOWN HALL
October 24, 2017

MEMBERS PRESENT: B. Johnson Low, Acting Chair, M. Christensen, D. Cunningham (ex-
officio), Steve Marks-Hamilton, J. Bengtson, R. McCauley. Alternate members present: , R.
Waterman, B. Hobby, Maggi Prokop.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS: None.

CORRESPONDENCE: Many thanks to Burt and Lois Hobby for a second generous donation of
antique tools to the Commission. These tools include: Geared nutcracker, corn cobber with
winnower, utility grinding wheel (foot pedal style), miscellaneous burlap bags and pieces, hand
cranked grape crusher, small wooden cider press ( Westford CT) and a broom making set-up.
These tools can be used in exhibits at the town’s three historic houses.

OLD BUSINESS:

e Vacancies: Welcome HP Commission alternate Maggi Prokop who was re-appointed by the
Board of Selectmen on Wednesday, October 4, and who brings her extensive background in
historical properties to the Commission.

e Archeological Survey Project, Bridge Art, Flow Chart, East Lyme House Inventory and the
Demolition Delay Ordinance were discussed and ideas were shared.

meeting will be Tuesday, December 5 at 7PM. Motion by Steve Marks-Hamilton, second by
Burt Hobby.

could use. The need for additional archival /storage space was also discussed.
