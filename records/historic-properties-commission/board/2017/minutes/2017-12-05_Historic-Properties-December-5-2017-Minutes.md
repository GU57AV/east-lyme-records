OLD BUSINESS:
e Demolition Delay Order — B Low and B Hobby met with Joe Smith to simplify the language
for this ordinance for passage by the Board of Selectmen. Burt and Joe offer this short

proposed statement for a 30 day delay:
“ORDINANCE CONCERNING A WAITING PERIOD BEFORE DEMOLITION

Pursuant to CGS Sec. 29-406, the town of East Lyme hereby adopts a waiting period
before granting a permit to demolish any building or structure or part thereof.

Upon submission to the Building Official of a permit to demolish per said statute, the
applicant for said permit must notify the East Lyme Historic Properties Commission of its
intent to demolish.

The Commission must within 30 days calendar review said application.

The Building Official may not approve said application until approval is provided by the
Commission, or until the passage of 30 calendar days from the date of notification.”

R McCauley moved to amend this statement amended to replace all “must” or “may” with
“shall.” M. Christensen seconded. Amendment and motion carried. Unanimous. Low and
Hobby will now take this verbiage to First Selectman Nickerson.

Florence Griswold Museum. Barb has also contacted Connecticut Humanities in this regard
