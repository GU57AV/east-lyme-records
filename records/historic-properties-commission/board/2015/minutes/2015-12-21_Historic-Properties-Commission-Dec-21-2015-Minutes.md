EAST LYME HISTORIC PROPERTIES COMMISSION
MEETING 700 PM DECEMBER 21, 2015
EAST LYME TOWN HALL

FILED IN EAST |L_YME

MEMBERS PRESENT: S. Marks — Hamilton, L. Lange, B. Murphy, B. J. Low, B.
Hobby - (Alternate seated) M. Christensen, R. Waterman

Smith-Harris — B. Johnson Low reported that she attended the Smith-Harris greens sale
which appeared quite successful.

MINUTES — November 16, 2015 meeting approved as read — M. Christensen — B.
Johnson Low unanimous.

Samuel Smith House - L. Lange reported that the 2" Archeological grant has been
applied for. That the 2016 Budget applications will need to be presented in January.

L. Lange also reported that the 1772 Grant application date has changed and is due in
December, however they also require that the 501-3c applicants either own or lease the
property they are applying for, which will make a difference when applying for a grant
next year.

Niantic River Bridge Park has nothing new to report.
Commissioner Grant Publication will be submitted after the 1 of the year.

S. Marks-Hamilton — presented a proposed sign for the Thomas Lee House School needs
repair, the Commission approved M. Christensen — B. Hobby unanimous.

M. Christensen reported that the Annual Preservation Pot Luck Dinner will be January 8,
2016 at the Niantic Community Church. It was agreed to contact the EL Land Trust, the
Beach Associations, Nature Conservency for an invitation.

M. Christensen reported that the Bike Ride is probably going to be on or about October 1,
2016.

L. Lange reported that the Lambert/Perry Property facing West Lane has been cleared.
Both the Old Black Point Association and Town Planner are aware.

L. Lange reported that a meeting with First Selectman M. Nickerson will be January 7,
2016 at 1:30PM - subject will be Historic Properties Budget for 2016 and Samuel Smith
Budget for 2016 and the Samuel Smith proposed lease.

Meeting adjourned - Barbara Johnson Low — M. Christensen unanimous
