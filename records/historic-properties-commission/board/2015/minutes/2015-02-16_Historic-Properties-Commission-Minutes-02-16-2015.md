MINUTES
WORKING MEETING OF HISTORIC PROPERTIES COMMISSION
7:00 p.m., FEBRUARY 16, 2015
EAST LYME POLICE STATION

Present: Mark Christensen, Steve Marks-Hamilton, Chair, Luane Lange. Alternates
seated: Elizabeth Murphy, Richard Waterman.

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. Luane volunteered to make notes.

The descriptions of the first 41 properties of the By Age, Housing Inventory were
distributed, eight properties to each commission member. Also, distributed and discussed
were the Review Forms to be used for criteria decisions on "historically significant
properties."

After individual reviews were completed, the recommendations of each member were
discussed and decisions made. Three properties of these early houses were determined to
be “not significant" due to the amount of alterations. (Numbers 28, 31, and 41.
Information will be sought on twelve of the accepted properties related to previous
owners names, events, etc., to clarify town memories.

Steve suggested using the "Demolished" list in Olive Chendali's Book, East Lyme: Our
Town and How It Grew as part of the references included in this inventory.

It was agreed that further review of the Inventory could be done in pieces, working on
about thirty houses per meeting.
FILED IN EAST LYME.

The meeting adjourned at 8:45 p.m. CONNECTICUT
