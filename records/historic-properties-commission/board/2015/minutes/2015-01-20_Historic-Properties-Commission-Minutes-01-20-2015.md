FILED IN EAST LYME

MINUTES: JAN. 20, 2015
EAST LYME HISTORIC PROPERTIES COMMISSION

Present: Chair, Luane Lange, Steve Marks-Hamilton, Mark Christensen. Alternate,
Maggi Propos was seated. Visitors: Barbara Johnston; First Selectman, Mark
Nickerson.

Steve Marks-Hamilton agreed to serve as Acting Secretary

Chairman asked to add three items to the Agenda: New Business: Historic Districts and
Commission Secretary. Reports: Preservationists Pot Luck.

Minutes of November 17, 2014. Motion by M. Christensen to approve as presented, M.
Prokop, seconded and passed.

Correspondence: CT Tourism meeting in Mystic on March 3, 2015
CT Office of Tourism, Gov., May 12
State Museums’ Open House Day: June 13

Samuel Smith Update: M. Christensen reported a Rotary Grant funding for a Community
Garden. Top soil has been delivered. An attorney is working with the Board, pro bono,
on the Lease with the Town.

Niantic River Bridge Metal Art Sculpture: Design continues with issues of safety, cost,
location, signs, ete. Alvin Sher continues communication with Joe Brogaw in Public
Works. B. Johnston, Harbor Management had questions about the previously chosen
location and having input from them and Parks and Recreation. M. Nickerson was open
to discussion with all groups. More pieces might be available and Alvin will be
informed.

Samuel Smith property Archaeological Survey: Contract with consultant is being
developed with Town Finance Director. Project will begin in the Spring.

M. Nickerson was welcomed and he contributed to some discussions, including the
potential for a three pronged event with the volunteers who work with the three historical
houses, ending with a selectman's picnic celebration.

Samuel Smith House sign: Discussion on the potential sign materials under consideration
by FSSH&P. Consensus recommendation that it be of wood, not plastic. M. Nickerson
wondered if a scallop shell could be on it someplace, to indicate that it was Town

property.

E.L. House Inventory: The library was unexpectedly closed for painting and the "By
Age" copy of the Inventory was not available. Thus the inventory could not be reviewed.
A focused meeting/workshop of Commission will be held to finalize decisions about
significant properties (per Town Historian request). To be scheduled on regular Feb. 16
meeting date, at a non-town location...perhaps, Police Station. L. Lange will check.

E.L Schools/Historic Districts: Waterford sent its records. M. Christensen will review
these. L.Lange will contact Alan Scott about interest, and Gary Goeschel about potential
maps.

Secretary: Betty Murphy cannot be listed as Secretary since she is an Alternate. She has
signed the Minutes, Acting Secretary, which is appropriate. L. Lange will check with
Burt Hobby, the former Secretary, to resolve how to proceed.

Preservationists Pot Luck: FSSHP publicity has asked for a written article giving
information shared by each group at the event. L. Lange distributed what was sent for
HP. Discussion followed about available grants as HP cited at the pot luck.

Adjourned: M. Christensen moved and seconded: 8:52 p.m.

Acting Secretary,

Steve Marks-Hamilton
