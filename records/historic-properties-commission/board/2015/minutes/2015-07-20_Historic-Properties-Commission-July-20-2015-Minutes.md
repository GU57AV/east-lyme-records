Meeting Minutes
Members present: L. Lange, S. Hamilton-Marks, B. Murphy, B. Johnson Low, seated R.
Waterman Alternate.
Members greeted new member Barbara Johnson Low.
Minutes of June 15, 2015 accepted as read — S. Hamilton Marks, L. Lange Unanimous
L. Lange reported that PAST Archeology Grant at the Samuel Smith House is being
completed, report has turned up some historical inconsistencies. There is a possibility
for a matching grant for a second phase through the CT Trust for Historic Preservation.
L. Lange is pursuing this option.
The Samuel Smith House participated in the Agriculture Day July 19 of the three Farm
Tours — SSmith had over one hundred visitors, Melissa Miko had her goats, a turkey,
“Butch” Foster and another member of his band played all afternoon, and members of the
Smith Committee took visitors on tours of the house.
Niantic River Bridge design work at Cini Park — is reported progressing by Al Sher.
Nothing has been heard of the new CIA Budget, and the Governor’s Budget.

Copies of the Commission Duties and House Inventory are now available in the Clerk's
Office.

The CT Trust for Historic Preservation visited the Griswold House on East Society, and
has listed it in their Newsletter Property exchange.

The draft of Scenic Roads Publication is completed but awaits how the HP Commission
will make it accessible to the public. A similar brochure exists in the Planning Office.

The Commission discussed and rated 70 more houses in the EL House Inventory.

Thomas Lee House update — New windows/screens have been in stalled in the basement,
along with a new bulkhead door — that can be lifted by all docents to allow for more air
circulation in the basement. Many trees have been removed and many of the locust
trunks went to the S Smith House for use in the Community Garden.

An overview of the CLG 2013-2014 Federal Report was given by Luane (See attached.)

Meeting adjourned — B. Low — L. Lange unanimous
Respectfully submitted — Elizabeth H. Murphy, Secretary

Commission member retirement and new appointment:

Norman Peck Jr., retired as a member of the commission. He remains available for
information as a man in his 80's, with extensive knowledge of town history. He was one
of the initiators of the study that resulted in the CLG.

Steve MarksHamilton was appointed in January, 2014. He is a retired high school Art
teacher who has moved to East Lyme. He has a strong interest in the history of this
community, traveling the area doing pen and ink drawings of the historical sites and
buildings. He is also on the East Lyme Historical Society Board of Directors.

Certificates of Appropriateness:

A contained fire at the Thomas Lee House required interior repair and restoration in
the kitchen. The restoration plan was approved by the Commission in April, 2014.

The 1660 east chamber girt system of the Thomas Lee House was replaced during the
Summer/early Fall of 2014. The replacement beam was lumbered and held for three
years prior to its final preparation and installation.

Samuel Smith House and Property:
The 1685 Town of East Lyme property was added to the CLG responsibilities by
Town Ordinance on February 19, 2014.

Funding for an archacological survey was granted by the State, through the CLG, on
November 6, 2013.

William Tinker House demolition:

With no legal recourse, the 1770 William Tinker House was demolished during the
Spring of 2014, as approved by the town, prior to the existence of the East Lyme Historic
Properties Commission/CLG. With the Commission's intervention, and multiple
documentation and legal papers, the developer cooperated to have historical materials
carefully removed and provided to the Friends of Samuel Smith House and Property, Inc,
for use or resale. A reproduction of the house is being presently built on that site.

Other:
The Historic Buildings Inventory, a 2012 funded project through the CLG programs,
continues to be a very useful resource.
