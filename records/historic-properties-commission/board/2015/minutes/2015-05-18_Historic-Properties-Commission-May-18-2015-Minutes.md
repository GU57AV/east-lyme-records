Minutes of March 16, 2015 are amended to note the Elizabeth Murphy was elected Secretary and Luane Lange,

April 20 Minutes amended to note that S. Marks-Hamilton was voted by the members to Chair the meeting. R.
Waterman — S. Marks-Hamilton unanimous B. Hobby abs.

New Business — L. Lange presented the Historic Properties Commission Duties as amended reflecting the
Amended Ordinance, February 2014, Board of Selectmen. The amended Duties were approved and

Commission members signed the vote for recording with the Town Clerk.

Notice has been received re: the cell towers proposed for Ancient Highway and secondary property of 351
Boston Post Road.

Ct Historic New England will hold a workshop, teaching how to apply your property for Historic Registry.
Notice will be sent out through the EL Newsletters and Channel 22.

The Samuel Smith House Archeology Dig will begin at the end of the month. Progress is being made on the
establishment of Community Gardens, sponsored by the EL Rotary.

Al Sher is still working with the Town for the construction of his Metal Art Sculpture of from the parts of the
AMTRAK Railroad Bridge at Cini Park.

The CIA funding and the Governors Budget is still in question, this Legislative session will end in a couple of
weeks, more should be known.

With the permission of the owner, Margo Crump, the available 1750 Samuel Griswold property on East Society
Rd. had been referred to the CT Trust for Historic Preservation to promote it for purchase. A team from the CT
Trust will visit the site next week.

The Commission publications were discussed. Luane will present a revised draft format.

It was decided to postpone East Lyme House Inventory discussion 41-70.

Meeting adjourned S. Marks-Hamilton — B. Hobby

Respectfully submitted, Elizabeth H. Murphy Secretary
