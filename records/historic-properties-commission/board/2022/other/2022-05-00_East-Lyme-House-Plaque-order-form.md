                    East Lyme Historic Properties Commission
                             House Plaque Program




The East Lyme Historical Properties Commission is offering residents an opportunity to
purchase a customized house plaque as part of the Commission’s town-wide effort to recognize
significant historic buildings as well as all houses in East Lyme. Homeowners are encouraged to
be active and conscious custodians of their house’s heritage. Plaques will enhance the
appearance of East Lyme’s many properties and residences. The plaques will enhance the
enjoyment of historic houses by residents and visitors alike.
Specifications:
Dimensions: 12” wide x 9” high. Mounting holes are pre-drilled.
Color: White background with black lettering, black and white logo of the East Lyme Scallop
Shell.
Installation: Plaque should be displayed at street side of home. Installation is the responsibility
of the homeowner, stainless screws provided.
     NOTE: House or building of any age is eligible to purchase and install a plaque.
