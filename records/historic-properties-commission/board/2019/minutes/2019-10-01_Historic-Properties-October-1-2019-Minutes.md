NEW BUSINESS:

Signage for Historic Houses, Properties and Landmarks: HP members are
gathering photos of area towns' historic house markers for our commission to review.
Our goal is to design and produce historic markers for our citizens to place on their
homes and at other locations of importance to the history of East Lyme. For residences
and historic buildings, these markers will identify the name of the original builder and the
date the edifice was built. Similar identifying information will be posted on the landmark
signs.
