OLD BUSINESS:

e House Inventory: No report. B. Johnson noted that an inventory house located at 99

e Connecticut Collections Museum Management Software Update: No update yet.

e Bridge Art: S. Marks Hamilton reported that they are still working with the town to get
assistance.

e Flow Chart — No report
e National Register of Historic Places: No Report.

e East Lyme as Destination Town: B. Hobby proposes that he work with each of the
houses to develop a tri-fold print brochure for this tourist season. He will speak to First
Selectman Nickerson about this. B. Johnson Low recommends the team develop a
consistent masthead and color scheme/format for the three houses and use this in the
brochure.

e Historic Properties / Library Collaboration: B. Johnson Low will meet with Library Board
Member Lois Hobby and Library Director Lisa Timothy regarding collaborating on a new
publication that tells the story of East Lyme’s three historic houses. She suggests one
representative writer from each house work with the library on this project.

e Brookside Farm on the Historic Properties Commission: B. Johnson Low and Gary
Lakowsiky have met with First Selectman Mark Nickerson regarding same. Mr.

Nickerson reports that in order to make Brookside Farm Museum a full member of HPC,
there are additional legal issues beyond completing the border mapping. He and the
town attorney will work to resolve them as they involve how organizational management
for each of the houses is structured. B. Johnson Low referred the team to the
Connecticut State Statute for additional details and moved that the discussion be tabled
until a future meeting. M. Christenson seconded. Carried, unanimous.

NEW BUSINESS

Calendar: B. Johnson Low proposed 2019 meeting calendar. B. Bullock moved to
accept. B. Hobby seconded. Carried, unanimous.

Budget: B. Johnson Low stated that 2019-2020 proposed budget is due December 23.

Preservation Pot Luck Dinner: M. Christensen reported the dinner date is Friday
January 18", 6-8PM at Niantic Community Church.

December 2.

cemetery.

The Samuel Smith House —B. Bullock the winter open house and greens sale on
November 25 was not as well attended than the previous year, possibly due to the date
being on Thanksgiving weekend.

Thomas Lee House: R. McCauley reports that construction for the new barn has begun.
