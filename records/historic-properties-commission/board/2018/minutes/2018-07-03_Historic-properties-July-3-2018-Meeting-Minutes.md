EAST LYME HISTORIC PROPERTIES COMMISSION MEETING
EAST LYME TOWN HALL
July 3, 2018

MEMBERS PRESENT: B. Johnson Low, Chair, M. Christensen, M. Prokop, R. McCauley,
Steve Marks-Hamilton, Alternate members present: R. Waterman, B. Hobby.

Meeting opened at 7:02PM

Pledge of Allegiance * enero
July 420 AMM)
EC.

=AST LYME TOWN CLERK

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS: None
AGENDA ADDITIONS: None.

MINUTES: June 2018 minutes were approved. Motion by M. Christensen, second by R.
Waterman. Passed. Unanimous.

CORRESPONDENCE: None.

OLD BUSINESS:

Vacancy:
o Clerk / Secretary: Motion by S. Marks-Hamilton, second by Mark Christenson to
elect Maggi Prokop secretary / clerk. Passed. Unanimous.
o Alternate: Donna Massung and Benn Bullock attended the meeting and each
expressed interest in becoming an alternate. Each was advised to submit a letter of
intent and bio / resume ASAP.

e Demolition Delay Ordinance: BJ Low reported that the Board of Selectmen at the June 6,
2018 meeting passed the ordinance.

e House Inventory: S. Marks-Hamilton will invite Jan Pearson of the Crescent Beach
Association to attend a future and make a presentation about the history of the
neighborhood. He will make a list of other neighborhood groups who might be interested in
sharing their history with the Commission / townspeople.

e Connecticut Collections Museum Management Software Update: BJ Low reported they are
interviewing for a new director and plan to present a training / workshop later this summer.

e Archeological Survey Project: No report.

e Bridge Art: S. Marks-Hamilton reported that Mark Nickerson has approved an art piece but
there has been no additional activity.

e Flow Chart, - No reports.
e National Register of Historic Places: No report.

e Smith Harris House Name Change: SHH is now named Brookside Farm Museum.

e Celebrate East Lyme Booth: Has been paid for and representatives from the three museum
houses and the East Lyme Puppetry Project (who recently received a grant to develop a
show about America’s First Submarine, the Turtle) will provide information to citizens.

REPORTS

e Bike and BBQ: Dawn Robinson continues to do radio interviews. Posters are ready for store
windows, still seeking sponsors. B. Low has created an ad and an article for August’s Post
Road Review. Many request have been made to Parks and Recreation to ascertain the
names of sponsors and amounts of donations so that the team can appropriately budget
and not solicit those who have already donated. There will be a Bike and BBQ booth at
East Lyme Day on July 21.

e Cemetery Association — No report.

e The Samuel Smith House — M. Christensen reported that the June 9, 2018 open house day
was a success — about 60 visitors. In order to spend down the 2017-2018 budgeted
construction funds, it was voted to replace roof shingles for the house. July 22 will be Farm
Day for the House. September 8 will be a Colonial soldier encampment by the Lebanon
Militia.

e The Smith Harris House — Name change was passed at June 20 Board of Selectmen
meeting. Joanie DiMartino has resigned to take a position with the Prudence Crandall
Homestead. The STEPS program has been completed.

e Thomas Lee House: R. McCauley reported that D. Waterman and S. Marks-Hamilton have
completed a new exhibit about the building of the Turtle, America’s first attack submarine
that was manned by Thomas Lee’s nephew Ezra and tested in the Connecticut River.
Construction of the new barn is slated for this fall. The new exhibit also includes a piece
about Lyme in the Revolution, and a "Meet the Lee Family " display.

Meeting Adjourned at 8:00PM Motion by M. Prokop, second by S. Marks-Hamilton. Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Maggi Prokop
