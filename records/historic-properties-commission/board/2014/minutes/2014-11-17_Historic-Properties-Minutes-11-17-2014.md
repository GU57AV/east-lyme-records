EASTLYME HISTORIC PROPERTIES COMMISSION
NOVEMBER 17, 2014 7PM
EAST LYME TOWN HALL

MEMBERS PRESENT: C. Hallas, R. Waterman, S. Marks-Hamilton, B. Murphy, M.
Christensen, L. Lange, B. Hobby
FILED IN EAST LYME

R.Waterman seated as an alternate Novy4 Oe Lid B Guu
ERST LY MEMTCOWN CLE

Minutes of October 20, 2014 accepted — with 2 abstentions

L. Lange reported the Scenic Roads Brochure is drafted. She will be meeting with G.
Goeshal - Planning to discuss the brochure, Archeology Grant overview, and the Historic
House Designation process.

S. Smith House — M. Christensen reported that there was some discussion about keeping
random public or hunters off the property for insurance/liability purposes. No
Trespassing signs were suggested. Question about No Trespassing on Town property?
The Fire Marshal, Town Building Inspector visited the property and went over the
reasons of public access limitations to the house from the insurance company. The
Insurance Company has agreed to follow the suggestions of the Town Officials. A Sub-
Committee was formed to put together a Lease with the Town and the Friends
relationship. Also a sign for the front of the house is in the planning process.

Niantic River Bridge - Cini Park project is progressing the RR pieces are at the Town
Garage to be cleaned ete.

House Inventory — is in the hands of the Town Historian L. Kuchta for final adjustments.

Archeology Survey — four requests had been sent to firms as directed by the State — with
little response only one showed up and spent several hours going over the property. They
must respond with their proposal by December 1, 2014. Then the plans will go to the
State.

The Huber Family (previous owners) have Chards collected while they owned the
property which they have agree to hand over to the Archeologists.

Historic Property Insurance discussion — several members have been exploring
Companies that participate in historic properties. M Christensen contacted his insurer
who was not interested in the Smith House. L. Lange discovered that Smith Insurance in
Niantic, have a carrier that does Historic Properties.

EL Public Schools — much discussion about the Education Study regarding the condition
of the schools, enrollments, and the future. M. Christensen suggested a Flanders Historic
District as a way of preserving the old portion (Administration Building) of the Flanders
School which was built about 1913.

The Tinker House has been demolished and a footing for basement has been poured.
Meeting adjourned — M. Christensen - B. Hobby unanimous

Respectfully submitted — Elizabeth H. Murphy, Acting Secretary
