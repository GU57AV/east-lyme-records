TOWN OF EAST LYME
HISTORIC PROPERTIES COMMISSION
PUBLIC HEARING MINUTES
Wednesday, JANUARY 8th, 2014

Present: Luane Lange, Chairman; Mark Christenson; Steve Marks-Hamilton; Elizabeth
Murphy, Secretary & Alternate; Maggi Prokop, Alternate

Also Present: Tracy Collins, Town Counsel
Absent: Burt Hobby, Carol Hallas, Richard Waterman, Alternate

Call to Order
Chairman Lange called the January 8, 2014 Public Hearing of the Town of East Lyme Historic Properties

Commission to order at 7:00 PM.

Pledge of Allegiance
Ms. Lange led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Welcome

Ms, Lange introduced the members of the Historic Properties Commission to the audience and
explained that the Public Hearing this evening is to receive comments regarding the designation of the
Samuel Smith House as an historic property and the State requires that this be done as a part of this
procedure. She thanked past member Norm Peck for all of his help with this process.

Notice of Hearing
Ms. Lange asked Ms. Murphy, Secretary to read the Notice of Public Hearing. She noted that this notice

was published in the Day paper on 12/29/2013 and 1/5/14. (Copy of public hearing notice attached).

Ms. Murphy read the following:

Notice is hereby given of a Public Hearing to be held by the East Lyme Historic Properties
Commission at 7 PM on January 8, 2014 at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania
Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to receive comments regarding the designation of the
Samuel Smith House as an historic property. A copy of the report of the Historic Properties
Commission regarding said property Is on file at the office of the East Lyme Town Clerk and
is available for inspection during business hours. Interested persons are invited to attend
and be heard. Dated at East Lyme, Connecticut on this 16th day of December 2013.

Ms. Lange noted the Public Hearing procedure asking that those wishing to speak please state their
name, address, and organization/position within the organization if applicable. She explained that as
part of the procedure that comments were also solicited from various agencies as required by the State
as well as from the State. She then noted that they had received written correspondence from the
State and asked Ms. Murphy to read the letter into the record.

Ms. Murphy read a letter dated November 8, 2013 received from Mary Dunne, Architectural Historian at
the State Department of Economic and Community Development into the record stating that the
Historic Preservation Council had voted unanimously to recommend approval of the East Lyme Historic
Properties Commission’s study report for the proposed Samuel Smith House historic property and that
they fully support and encourage the protection and preservation of this outstanding architectural
resource. (Letter attached)
Ms. Lange then called for anyone from the public wishing to comment.

Marvin Schutt, 29 North Edgewood Road, said that he is the President of the Friends of Samuel Smith
House and Property, Inc. and that they wish to thank Ms. Lange and the members of the Historic
Properties Commission for processing the paper work and obtaining the required approvals for the
Samuel Smith House & Property, Inc. to be preserved in perpetuity by a required Ordinance. He
thanked them for this opportunity to be heard. (Statement submitted and attached)

Norman Peck, 32 West Main Street said that he is the President of the East Lyme Historical Society
which fully supports the Samuel Smith House as a historic property. They are fully in favor of such
designation for such important pieces of history to be forever preserved.

Ms. Lange asked if there were any other comments from the public —
Hearing none —

Ms. Lange thanked everyone for coming and closed this Public Hearing at 7:12 PM.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary, Pro-Tem
