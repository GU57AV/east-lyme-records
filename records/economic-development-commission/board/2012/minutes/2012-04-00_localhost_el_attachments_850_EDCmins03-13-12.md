                              Economic Development Commission
                                      Regular Meeting
                                       March 13, 2012

Present:       Steven Santino
               Brian Donahue
               Ed Shapiro
               Brian Kennedy
               John Jensen

Also Present: Gary Goeschel, Planning Director
              Paul Formica, First Selectman

A. Preliminary Business

1. Call to Order. Chairman Santino called the Economic Development Commission Regular
Meeting of March 13, 2012 to order at 7:02 p.m.

2. Approval of Minutes. Mr. Santino asked for corrections, additions or deletions to the January
10, 2012 Regular Meeting Minutes of the Economic Development Commission.

MOTION:        John Jensen moved to approve the January 10,
               2012 Regular Meeting Minutes of the Economic
               Development Commission, as presented.
               Seconded by Brian Donahue. Mr. Jensen
               abstained from voting. Motion carried. (3-0-1)

3. Delegations. There were no delegations.

4. Public Comments. There were no public comments.

B. Reports

       Commission member Mr. Shapiro joined the meeting.

1. Ex-officio Report. Mr. Formica informed the Commission each of their appointments is up.
The Town Charter requires that we have an Economic Development Commission.

2. Chairman’s Report. Mr. Santino informed the Commission he rescheduled Tony Sheridan
for the April meeting.

      Mr. Santino informed the Commission of an Economic Development Seminar on April 3
and April 10 at the Groton Motor Inn & Suites.

3. Presentation of Bills. The Recording Secretary presented a bill for 2.50 hours of secretarial
work for the Economic Development Commission from January 10, 2012 to March 9, 2012.
MOTION:        Mr. Jensen moved to pay the bill from the
               Recording Secretary for 2.5 hours of secretarial
               services from January 10, 2012 to March 9, 2012.
               Seconded by Mr. Donahue. (5-0) Unanimous.

C. Old Business

1. Agri-Business Committee. Commission members reviewed the February 15, 2012 Agri-
Business Subcommittee Meeting Minutes.

      Mr. Santino received an email from Tom Kalal asking if the Economic Development
Commission would fund $15 each for four people to attend a seminar on “Growing Farms in
Your Community”.

MOTION:        Mr. Shapiro moved to approve the expenditure
               of $60 for four members of the Agri-Business
               Subcommittee to attend a seminar on “Growing
               Farms in Your Community”. Seconded by
               Mr. Donahue. (5-0) Unanimous

D. New Business

1.      Mr. Gary Goeschel, Town Planner, gave a presentation on Economic Gardening. It
focuses on entrepreneur growth. Economic gardening focuses on creating jobs locally. It works
to identify the location of existing customers, by site selection and marketing analysis and it
compares locations.

       Mr. Goeschel stated it will tell what consumer’s habits are. Mr. Goeschel will provide
information on infrastructure and connections.

        Mr. Goeschel informed the Commission he has begun to reach out to a number of
different business organizations in town. He has also met with some local businesses.

       Mr. Jensen stated in the field of commercial real estate the stumbling block he sees is the
permitting process. Some of it costs too much for a small business. Mr. Goeschel stated he will
put together a team with the Town Engineer, Zoning Official and Health Department and direct a
potential business in the right direction.

       Mr. Jensen discussed the special permitting process. He gave an example of a hairdresser
moving its business out of town and a barber who would like to move in to its location. It is
necessary to get a special permit. The process revolves around meetings.

       Mr. Shapiro felt it is better for a prospective business to know everything upfront.
        The Economic Development Commission should set up the environment for a business to
take place with less government control. Mr. Goeschel felt they can obtain reports at the Town
Hall. Government can connect with individuals who understand marketing.

       Mr. Goeschel asked how can we work together to make the permitting process easier?
He stated he is willing to meet with the Commission and look at how we can make the permitting
process easier.

         Mr. Jensen suggested that the First Selectman meet with North Stonington’s First
Selectman to see how its Economic Development Commission handles new businesses coming
to its town.

        Mr. Jensen feels the Economic Development Commission should be involved with
Zoning, the First Selectman and the Fire Marshal. He felt the permitting process should be
streamlined; the Zoning hurdles should be eliminated. This Commission should meet on a
quarterly basis with each of these departments and express its concern for the business
community. Mr. Kennedy stated while working with individuals in Windham he was
disappointed with the permitting process the individuals had to go through while trying to open
up a dance studio.

       Mr. Jensen stated this Commission has helped Main Street, worked on the Charrette
Study and now has a subcommittee of Agri-Business.

E. Open Discussion. There was no further discussion.

MOTION:       Mr. Shapiro moved to adjourn the March 13, 2012
              Regular Meeting at 8:10 p.m. Seconded by
              Mr. Donahue. (5-0) Unanimous.

                                     Respectfully submitted,


                                     Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
