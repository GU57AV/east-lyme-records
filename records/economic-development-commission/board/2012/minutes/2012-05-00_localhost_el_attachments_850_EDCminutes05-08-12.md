                             Economic Development Commission
                                     Regular Meeting
                                       May 8, 2012

Present:      Steven Santino
              Ed Shapiro
              Dick Gada
              John Jensen
              Joan Bengtson

Also Present: Mark Christiansen
              Mark Nickerson

A. Preliminary Business

1. Call to Order. Chairman Santino called the Economic Development Commission Regular
Meeting of May 8, 2012 to order at 7:05 p.m.

2. Approval of Minutes. Mr. Santino asked for corrections, additions or deletions to the April
10, 2012 Regular Meeting Minutes of the Economic Development Commission.

MOTION:       Mr. Jensen moved to approve the April 10,
              2012 Regular Meeting Minutes of the Economic
              Development Commission, as presented.
              Seconded by Mr. Gada. Joan Bengtson
              abstained from voting. (4-0-1) Motion carried.

3. Delegations. There were no delegations.

4. Public Comments. There were no public comments.

B. Reports

1. Ex-officio Report. Mr. Nickerson welcomed Joan Bengtson as a new member of this
Commission.

He informed the Commission the Board of Selectmen has had discussion on the Samuel Smith
House.

The Annual Town Meeting will be held on May 14. The budget will then go to Referendum.

2. Chairman’s Report. Mr. Santino informed the Commission his work will require that he
spend time in another state and finds it necessary to resign after tonight’s meeting.

He welcomed Joan Bengtson as a new member of the Commission.
Commission member Mr. Shapiro joined the meeting.

3. Presentation of Bills. The Recording Secretary presented a bill for 3.50 hours of secretarial
work for the Economic Development Commission from April 10, 2012 to May 7, 2012.

MOTION:        Mr. Gada moved to pay the bill from the
               Recording Secretary for 3.50 hours of secretarial
               services from April 10, 2012 to May 7, 2012.
               Seconded by Mr. Shapiro. (5-0) Unanimous.

C. Old Business

1. Agri-Business Committee. Mark Christiansen reported the Agri-Business Committee is
working on its October 21 Trail Ride. The Trail Ride will be increased by 5 miles. He felt a
future Trail Ride should take place at Darrow’s Pond. Two 4H groups want to help with the Trail
Ride.

The Zoning Commission will rule on agricultural signs.

The Agri-Business Committee is working on a Small Farm Preservation Program. The Town
will need to qualify on six items. The due date for the town to send the application to the state is
May 31. Ms. Bengtson asked who will produce a letter from the Town? Mr. Christiansen
replied the Conservation of Natural Resources. Ms. Bengtson asked have you researched how
other towns have handled this? Mr. Christiansen replied they have not.

There is nothing new to report on chicken regulations.

2. Visitors Center. Mr. Gada reported the Visitors Center has a few volunteers. It plans to open
on Memorial Day Weekend and weekends throughout the summer. Niantic Main Street is
willing to give support to the Visitors Center. Mr. Jensen agreed to ask ELBOW to participate.
Mr. Nickerson suggested using the scroll to advertise the need for volunteers for the Visitors
Center. Mr. Shapiro suggested asking for volunteers from the Senior Center.

3. Next Steps for the EDC. Mr. Shapiro reported Gary Goeschel has agreed to attend the next
meeting of this Commission. He will need more input from this Commission on the Plan of
Conservation and Development chapter for the Economic Development Commission.

Mr. Santino asked if any commission member is willing to take over as Chair? Mr. Gada, Mr.
Jensen and Mr. Shapiro agreed to alternate during the summer months. There will be no meeting
in August.

MOTION:        Ms. Bengtson moved to appoint as Acting Chairman
               Richard Gada for the June meeting and John Jensen
               for the July meeting. Seconded by Mr. Shapiro
               (5-0) Unanimous.
D. New Business

      1. Open Discussion.

MOTION:      Mr. Shapiro moved to accept with regrets the
             resignation of our Chairman Steve Santino.
             Seconded by Mr. Gada. (5-0) Unanimous.

      Mr. Santino was presented with a gift from the Commission for his service.

E. Adjournment

MOTION:      Mr. Gada moved to adjourn the May 8, 2012
             Regular Meeting at 8:00 p.m. Seconded by
             Mr. Jensen. (5-0) Unanimous.

                                   Respectfully submitted,


                                   Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
