                                     EAST LYME BOARD OF FINANCE
                                   SPECIAL MEETING OF MAY 24,2022
                                              MINUTES

PRESENT: Denise Hall, John Birmingham, Peter DeRosa, Lauren McNamara and Paul
Maxfield
EXCUSED: Richard Steel
ALSO PRESENT: Finance Director Anna Johnson

Ms. Hall called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance

Ms. Hall asked for a moment of silence in honor of the tragic shooting at an elementary
school in Texas.

III. Set a mil rate for the 2022/2023 budget
DISCUSSION: Ms. Hall stated that the budget referendum was held on May 19th; 370
people voted yes and 228 people voted no for a total of 598 total votes out of -13,000
registered voters, Budget carried; tonight the Board will set the mil rate for the
2022/2023 budget season.

Ms. Johnson presented a report, attached hereto as Exhibit 1.

MOrroN (1)
Mr. Maxfield MOVED to set the mil rate of 23.84 for the Town of East Lyme 2022/2023
budget year.
Seconded by Ms. Hall. Motion passed 5-0-0.

IV. Board Comments
Mr. Maxfield thanked this Board for their hard work and collaboration during this
budget season, All Board member agreed and also thanked Anna Johnson for her
dedication and hard work.

V. Adjourn
MOrroN (2)
Mr. Birmingham MOVED to adjourn the May 24,2022, special meeting of the East
Lyme Board of Finance at 5:37 p.m.
Seconded by Mr, DeRosa. Motion passed 5-0-0.
