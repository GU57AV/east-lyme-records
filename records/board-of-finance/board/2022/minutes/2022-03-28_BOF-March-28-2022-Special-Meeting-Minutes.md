PRESENT: Denise Hall, Chairperson; John Birmingham, Vice Chairperson; Richard
Steel, Peter DeRosa, Paul Maxfield and Lauren McNamara

Ms. Hall called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Control Officer budget discussion to right after Police as Chief speaks on all of these
individual budgets.

IV. Budget Review - Public Safety

Emergency Management #274
. Chief Finkelstein is the Director of Emergency Management (EMD), and Julie
  Wilson is the Deputy Director of Emergency Management.
o Emergency Management is activated for every emergency; for example, the
    Deputy EMD will be in constant communication, twenty-four hours a day seven
    days as necessary during a weather event.
.   There has been an uptick in general duties of the emergency management
    department during the pandemic, which included distribution of PPE and test kits,
.   The communication services provided in budget line items 222 and 248 cover the
    communication needs of all town departments; police, fire, public works, school
    buses, etc.
.   They have only been in the new public safety building for a short time so they
    cannot give an accurate estimate of those expenses.
.   The line item for AED supplies is being moved out of this budget and put into the
    Capital budget (724).
.   NSEF and EMPG grants are not guaranteed but have been used to purchase items
    such as radios, the Everbridge program, electronic speed and information signs,

Police #2L6
. The Police Commission reviewed and approved the budgets being presented.
. Chief of Police Mike Finkelstein presented and referenced information in a
  PowerPoint presentation, which is attached hereto as Exhibit #1.
. The Police Department, the Chief and the ACOs and Dispatch all fall under the
  supervision of the Police Commission.
. The Commission supports hiring two full-time officers a year for the next six years
  in order to bring the department up to a standard staffing level.
o In 2O2L our police department's clearance rate was 40.57oloi cornpored to the
  statewide average of t6.75o/o.
. Mandatory training has increased since the inception of the Police Accountability
    law and includes an emphasis on crisis intervention. He reports that he envisions
    that the police and social workers would work together on certain calls to provide
    the best and most effective help in those situations. Chief reported that this is
    not new training, but that has been available but that is now mandatory.
.   Adding two officers in this budget will provide for quicker response time to an
    incident, will save money on overtime, and will allow for there to be three officers
    and one sergeant on each shift.
.   The money that is collected for the use of a police officer on a private road job is
    put into the vehicle acquisition account to cover the cost of new police vehicles.
    The purchase of new police vehicles no longer comes out of the general budget;
    they are all paid for with the money collected from these road jobs.

Dispatch #2t5

. Hiring two additional dispatchers at this time will not alleviate the need for
  overtime and that there is still a need for more full-time dispatchers.
. The uniform line item went up because they have not worn uniforms in the past;
    now that they are public facing in the new building, they will be outfitted with
    matching clothing.
