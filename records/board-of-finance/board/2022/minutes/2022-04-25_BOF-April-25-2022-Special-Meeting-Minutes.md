                          EAST LYME BOARD OF FINANCE
                   SPECIAL MEETING OF MONDAY, APRIL 25,2022
                                   MINUTES

PRESENT: Denise Hall, Chairperson; John Birmingham, Vice Chairperson; Richard
Steel, Peter DeRosa, Paul Maxfield and Lauren McNamara
ALSO PRESENT: First Selectman Kevin Seery, Finance Director Anna Johnson,
Superintendent Jeff Newton, Assistant Superintendent Annaliese Spaziano and BOE
Finance Director Maryanna Stevens

Ms, Hall called the special meeting to order at 8:01 p.m

II. Deliberation & Action - Proposed FY 22/23 Budget
DISCUSSION: The Board discussed the additional revenue realized from the recent
tax auction sale and how this will affect the proposed budget and subsequent mill
rate. It was agreed that the undesignated fund balance should remain as close to
15olo ?s possible to preserve the Town's credit rating. In addition to the $500,000 of
undesignated fund balance that the Board has already agreed to put towards this
budget, they debated putting in an additional $750,000 or $800,000 due to receipt of
the tax auction revenues. Ms. Hall called for a vote.

MOTTON ( 1)
Mr. Steel MOVED to use an additional $800,000 from Undesignated Fund Balance;
this is in addition to the $500,000 already appropriated from that fund in this budget,
and to use an assumption tax collection rate of 98.2o/o.
Seconded by Mr. DeRosa. IN FAVOR; Steel, DeRosa, McNamara and Maxfield.
OPPOSED; Hall and Birmingham. Motion passed 4-2-0.

Ms. Hall directed the Finance Director to make said changes to the budget

III. CIP
Approved at the last meeting; there was no additional discussion.

IV. Public Discussion
There was none.

V. Board Comments
The Board members agreed that this budget process has been respectful and
productive, and they all appreciate working together in the best interest of the
taxpayers. They discussed the possibility of combining future budget presentations
to the Board of Selectmen and Board of Finance to simplify the process.

VI. Adjourn
MOrroN (2)
Mr, Birmingham MOVED to adjourn the April 25, 2022, Special Meeting of the East
Lyme Board of Finance at 8:33 p.m.
Seconded by Mr. Maxfield. Motion passed 6-0,
