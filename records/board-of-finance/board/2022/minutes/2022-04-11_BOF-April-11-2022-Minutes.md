IV. New Business - Budget Deliberations

Board of Education
All of the Board members are in support of the Board of Education and the school system, and
they recognize the challenges that the pandemic has brought forth for both the students and
the administration. All agreed that although the added positions may be needed at the
moment, they are concerned that going forward these positions will be absorbed into the
budget instead of expiring when the ARPA funding runs out. Some points of interest:
         . All of the Board of Finance members agreed that this proposed budget is too high for
            the current fiscal situation that we are in.
         r Mr. Newton stated that their labor attorney ran a report of comparable districts to
            confirm that the salaries are in line with surrounding communities,
         o It was noted that although there is an increase in the budget with added staff, the
            population has decreased and is projected to continue to decrease in the next couple
            of years.
         . East Lyme's school system attracts young famllles who move to the communlty
            based highly on the education opportunities alone, but there is a large elderly
            population on the other end that are struggling due to the change in the economy;
            all of our residents need to be taken into consideration when adopting a budget.
         . We should be concentrating on keeping programs that affect all or at least a good
            portion of the student population; for instance, adding new sports at the middle
            school does not impact a majority of the students so maybe could have waited a
            couple of years to implement.
         . The Board of Education members were not all supportive of this budget and a couple
            of them voted in opposition of moving it forward.

Ms, Hall thanked the Board of Education members and the administration for their hard work
and noted the challenges of trying to balance the need to take care of the young and elderly
alike. She explained that in order to keep the increase to 1 Mil, the total budget would have to
come in at an approximate $2.7 million combined increase for both Town and Board of
Education; the Finance board members agreed that the optimal target is to keep the Mil rate
from increasing more than 1 Mil. Mr. Seery noted that for this year's budget, the Town has
received unexpected revenue for the sale of a commercial property, as well as the proceeds of
the tax auction sale of which some delinquencies have been paid in advance of the event. The
Board discussed possible use of the undesignated fund balance to bring the total down, but it
was ultimately agreed that this was not the right option.
