E. Reports

¢ First Selectman

Mr. Formica reported on the following:

= The Army corps permit and the DEEP permit have been received for the Boardwalk and 20 people
picked up the bid packets. They have $4.7M and are hoping to come in well under that figure. The
appropriation for this project should be coming before this Board on March 12, 2014 and then toa
Town Meeting on March 19, 2014. This would bring the bid award to April. He said that they are
making provisions for the Boardwalk to be open during construction. There will be a presentation on
this at the Board of Selectmen meeting on February 26, 2014 and they are all invited to attend.
* Regarding the initial Board of Ed increase request of 4.88% he said that the insurance figures are a
bit inflated in both budgets as they are hoping for adjustments. The Para-professionals insurance
has been put off for a year so $372,000 will be able to come out of the BOE budget. They are also
hoping to be able to modify the teacher evaluations cost of $250,000. Also, the BOE has asked for
more time on their budget and he suggested a date of March 10, 2014 as the Board of Selectmen
can only offer suggestions to the Board of Finance on the budget. He further suggested having a
potential joint Board of Selectmen/Board of Finance meeting to hear the Board of Ed presentation —
perhaps at the March 12, 2014 Board of Finance meeting.

* He noted that they have all been hearing about the issues of salt with all the storms and stated that
we are okay here.

* The Board of Ed has voted on and hired an architectural design firm at their last meeting at a cost of
$91,400. They are charged with looking into the construction/reconstruction of the elementary
schools. The original intent was to have this information for next year at the end of the fiscal year.
However; they will hold community outreach informational meetings and it would have to go to a
referendum vote and then it would depend on if or once it is passed as it would then have to go to
the State for a year review with them — so the time has been pushed out. Also — there is a $40,000
line item in their budget that would go towards the $91,400 with the rest coming from LEARN
monies. While they are proposing a 4.88% increase there are a number of items coming out that
can pare it down.

Ms. Picarazzi said that with reference to the $91,400 for the architect that this year they had received
three options for the schools and she wanted to know how this study would be different from that.

Mr. Formica said that it may be more detailed/specific and suggested that Ms. Picarazzi ask the
Committee that question.

Ms. Picarazzi said that she thought that they had received communication from the Attorney on how
non-academic recommendations for the Board of Ed are handled and asked if they have to submit
something back in writing if our recommendations are not accepted.

Mr. Formica said that he was not sure of the practicality of that here especially when their budget is
coming on March 10, 2014.

Mr. Kelley said that he believes that it said that they have to respond to our non-educational comments
only.

Ms. Hogan said that she thought that it does not really change anything except with the response in
writing.

Mr. Formica noted that we have a very good working relationship with the Board of Ed and that it
appears that communication was meant more for the municipalities where the two are in conflict with
each other.

Ms. Hogan asked about the teacher evaluations as she had thought that she heard that they were

delayed.
Mr. Formica said that the original plan would have called for 81 days of administrative time to comply

with the mandate. The ‘delay’ would have only reduced it by an 8 day net effect.

Ms. Hogan asked if that means that it has no effect on what the Board of Ed has said that they need to

hire to do this evaluation.
Mr. Formica said that it really does not change that. He added that he suspects that the Board of Ed will

come looking to hire retired professionals per diem to help with this.

Ms. Hogan asked what happens if this does not go forward and it is in the budget — could we get the
money back from the Board of Ed or does this make it difficult.
Ms. Johnson suggested that the money could be put in a contingency account and released only if they

find that the evaluations are going forward.
