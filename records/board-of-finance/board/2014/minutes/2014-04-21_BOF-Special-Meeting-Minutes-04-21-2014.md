A. Call Special Meeting to Order
Chairman Kelley called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Finance to order at 8:43 PM after the
previously scheduled Public Hearing on the Proposed FY-14/15 Budget.

B. Special Appropriation - Building Official - $7500
Mr. Kelley said that they would not be addressing this item as the Board of Selectmen has not acted on it yet.

there.

Mr. Formica said that with the $180,000 from the State for Town Aid Roads (TAR) that there may be other

on storm materials and supplies. He noted that he would hope that next winter is not as bad as this one has
been. He suggested that they could reduce line 440 — Storm Materials & Supplies by $45,000 as they would
offset it by the increased revenue in TAR funding.

Mr. Formica noted that they have discussed the teacher evaluation program and that he has worked on this
with Dr. Lombardo where there have been talks regarding giving communities such as ours a break on this
mandate. A compromise may come out as there are a number of performing districts such as ours involved
and the talks now are about going to the underperforming schools rather than the performing ones. The
legislative session closes May 7 — 8 which is a week before our Town Meeting.

Mr. Kelley said that he would agree that this may be helpful.

Ms. Alberti said that she had thought that she heard that if the teacher evaluations were not 100% required
that the extra funding would be returned.

Ms. Hogan said that they had discussed that they could perhaps put the money into a contingency account.

Mr. Kelley said that regardless of how they talk about it — even if it is in a contingency account for the BOE ~

**MOTION (14)
Ms. Hogan moved to open department 999 — Education

Vote: 6 — 0-0. Motion passed.

Ms. Hogan asked about the BOE reduction and how it ws reflected.

Mr. Kelley explained that due to the extension of time that the BOE asked for to turn over their budget to the
Board of Selectmen who then turned it over to us; the BOE cuts were not immediately reflected from the
original request. It came to us with a notation that the BOE had cut it.

Ms. Hogan said that her original cut suggestions were in the $275,000 - $400,000 range and that she does
agree that the burden of the common core is real.

**MOTION (12)
Ms. Hogan moved to increase the BOE budget 999 by $200,000.
Ms. Alberti seconded the motion.

Ms. Alberti said that she had seconded the motion for discussion. She said that she had originally supported
the BOE budget in its entirety. They have heard from people who moved here for the school system — she
said that she had also moved here originally for the school system — however that is not the reason that she
will stay here — she will stay here for the quality of life. She said that because they cannot cut specific areas
in the BOE budget that she apologizes if she offended anyone about the suggestion regarding the Niantic
Center School community however she feels that there are certain steps that can be done n the interim as
they cannot continue to fund at a significantly higher rate the education budget than that of the Town as they
have to meet the needs of all of our citizens and not just the school system. She said that they need to be
realistic and they need to look at areas where there is waste.

Mr. Kelley said once again that this has been a tough budget season. He noted that the $550,000 cut was
his suggestion and that he had looked at all aspects. This is a nice Town for many reasons and they have to
look at what the people can afford which includes a number of things that all the people in the Town expect
and deserve and that is how he reached his number. He said that he has to stand by it.

He called for further discussion or a vote on the motion.

Vote: 3-3-0. Motion failed.
