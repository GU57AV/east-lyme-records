¢ Capital (724) & CNRE — Paul Formica, First Selectman, Anna Johnson, Finance Director
Mr. Formica said that he would like to have Mr. Holyfield, Highway Superintendent and Fleet Manager review
the vehicle program and how they move in the system.

Chuck Holyfield, Fleet Manager said that they service over 100 vehicles with two and a half mechanics, one
of whom is 80 years old. This also includes two of the Board of Ed vehicles — they do all of the oil changes
and inspections. A few years ago they were being frugal with buying new vehicles and they devised a system
of handing down vehicles. The First Responders receive the new vehicles along with Public Works. He said
that when he first came here, they had old vehicles that were always breaking down — this past winter they
had only one storm with two trucks down and they managed to get one back on the road — which shows that
this process has paid off as they have been able to keep the vehicles on the road. He said that they fix
everything in the Town for the Water & Sewer department, Parks & Recreation, the two Board of Ed vehicles,
Fire Chiefs vehicles, Police, Public Works vehicles, and the two pick-ups that each Fire House has. He said
that he meets with the department heads and Mr. Formica on what to buy. Mr. Smith in the Building Office
received the handed down Fire Marshal vehicle which was painted under that program. He said that the
vehicles came out really well and they should provide the other departments with some good use. He noted
that he tries to have an old vehicle on hand in the event that something is in for repair and it is needed to fill
in for that vehicle in repair.
