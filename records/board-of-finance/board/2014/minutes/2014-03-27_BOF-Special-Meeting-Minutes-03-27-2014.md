sale, but the money goes into the booster's account. Senior graduation night goes into
their account. These are not funds that we house or draw off of. Mr. Reas stated if it is a
parents organization the parents manage it. In year's past at the High School there was
activity fees and lab fees. We are not allowed to use those fees. The Booster Club raises
money and then pays for lunches or team dinners from it. We have "pay to play" for
Freshman Lacrosse. Through the Booster Club we can afford to pay for the Freshman
Coach. For Hockey we coop with other schools, but the families pay a fee. The
philosophy behind this is we have 1100 at our school and 850 participate in some activity
at the High School. Six hundred students participate in more than one sport. Research
has been done that the more students are involved in sports there is higher attendance and
lower discipline rates. The same parents are also paying taxes. Ms. Picarazzi stated these
are hard choices, but we need to try to make improvements. Mr. Kelley reported last year
we spent $50,000 for equipment for the High School. Mr. Susi stated we have safety
procedures we need to follow. Helmets need to be reconditioned. We need to keep high
ropes safe. We have 1100 students using the equipment every year.

Mr. Harney noted under tuition there was a $223,000 decrease. Mr. Meltabarger
replied it is not all tuition. We received $105,000 in a security grant and $139,000 ina
technology grant which are one time grants.

Ms. Hogan asked if there are any grants coming from this year's budget? Mr.
Formica replied we have nothing in terms of grants only for matching capital
expenditures grant. We will receive $65,000 from the state, and we need to match it. Ms.
Hogan asked will we receive additional seed money for OPM? Mr. Formica replied the
Appropriations Committee was to forward a budget out of committee today. There is no
grant that will offset the new mandate. Mr. Reas added early childhood grants are given
to needy districts. We are not in that category.

Ms. Hogan asked if you have a Dean of Students at the Middle School would you
also need a monitor? Mr. Reas replied we could consider that. At times we need a
monitor. The new seats limit the driver's visibility. Ms. Alberti asked why doesn't school
busses have seat belts? Mr. Reas replied there is considerable costs for seat belts and the
current seats on the bus are made higher and the child's head will go into the seats in front.
Seat belts have become a safety issue. Mr. Meltabarger stated the children are safer
without a seat belt, e.g. if the bus was on fire or the bus driver was incapacitated. Mr.
Reas added on an annual basis we teach youngster safety on the bus. Ms. Alberti asked
have you found that cameras on the bus have helped? Mr. Reas replied we are now able
to investigate what has happened.

Ms. DeLeeuw, who has been a Middle School Principal for seven years, added we
have discussion with students on bus behavior at the Middle School. Every year we have
a program on positive behavior. Some of the busses are crowded, and we have to deal
with bus issues quite frequently. Much of it is loud inappropriate behavior. By using
cameras we are able to look at the incident. The Dean of Students could help the
administrators. We tend to have more incidents on crowded busses. Ms. Picarazzi asked
if any students lose their bus privileges? Ms. DeLeeuw replied some have been
suspended off the bus.

Ms. Picarazzi asked how many students are enrolled in Coastal Connections? Mr.
Susi replied there are currently 13. Last year there were 20. This year it was not well
advertised. Enrollment also fluxuates. We would like to get more tuition students. Ms.
Picarazzi asked if Coastal Connections is an alternative school? Mr. Susi replied it is a
program for students who were not successful in High School and have been sent to that
program. These students attend High School and have internships. Students are
interviewed before going into this program.

Mr. Harney asked how do you handle disciplinary problems? Mr. Susi replied we
are required to have ten hours of tutoring outside of school. Mr. Reas added a great job
has been done at the High School turning in-school suspension into something that
supports students and returns them back on track with academics. Ms. Hogan felt Coastal
Connections is a positive program. She asked if the two students from Old Lyme are in
the tuition line item? Mr. Meltabarger replied they are.

Mr. Kelley noted we pay the VNA for five school nurses at a cost of $564,262.
He asked if the VNA pays for their benefits? Mr. Reas replied they pay and supervise.
Mr. Meltabarger stated we moved the medically fragile and hired an aide. During the
summer we have a Special Education Program. Mr. Meltabarger stated we reviewed this
and found this was the best answer for our needs. We are paying an hourly rate.

Mr. Harney asked do you project any surplus being given back? Mr. Reas replied
we are tighter this year than last year. We have had a rough winter. Mr. Meltabarger
added this is the tightest year we've had in special education. We have had $97,000 in
extra expenses.

Ms. Alberti stated she would like to support this budget in its entirety. She noted
data that performance at East Lyme High School was rated at 12 in one article and 524 in
another. She felt these numbers need to be raised. Mr. Reas replied every year the Board
of Education sets high expectations; we are constantly trying to improve. Every year we
have improved in the state testing assessments. We are required to do teacher evaluations.
Ms. Alberti asked Mr. Hagen if there were any performance metrics? Mr. Hagen replied
Dr. Lombardo initiated a Five-Year Plan for the district. Coastal Connections brings
youngsters back for special education. Last year we were able to have all day
kindergarten. We are interested in a Five-Year Plan. Its important to conduct a search
for a new superintendent.

Ms. Alberti asked that while searching for a new Superintendent that you request
as part of the Strategic Plan that the High School move up to the top 10 rating. Mr.
Hagen stated we plan to have a Community Forum in gathering information for a new
Superintendent.

E. PUBLIC DISCUSSION

Ms. Senges-Murphy, a new member of the Board of Education, felt deliberation
on the Board is extremely thorough. Elementary language has been removed. We are
working on raising the level of education in East Lyme. The Board realizes finances are
tight. Fixed costs have increased. The Superintendent and staff did a good job of trying
to decrease the budget. Ms. Senges-Murphy has worked in Connecticut education for 40
years and know people from other areas of Connecticut. East Lyme has done well in
state scores. Young families would like to move to East Lyme because of the school
system. We do have to be concerned about our retired citizens. However, she asked that
the Board of Finance not cut this school budget. She felt the Board of Education and
Superintendent of Schools has done a good job of paring down this budget.

F. BOARD COMMENTS

Mr. Kelley stated the Board of Finance will meet at the Town Hall on March 31 at
6:30 p.m. to begin its deliberations.
