Chairman Kelley called this Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Finance to order at 7:00 PM.

The Pledge was observed.

C. Delegations
Mr. Kelley called for Delegations.

D. Approval of Minutes

= Wednesday, July 9, 2014 — Regular Meeting

Mr. Kelley called for a motion for approval or any changes to the Board of Finance Regular Meeting Minutes
of July 9, 2014.

**MOTION (1)

Ms. Hogan moved to approve the Board of Finance Regular Meeting Minutes of July 9, 2014 as presented.
Ms. Alberti seconded the motion.

Vote: 5-0-0. Motion passed.

E. Reports
¢ First Selectman
Mr. Formica reported on the following:
« The Boardwalk project has started and is moving forward on track.
= The capital Improvement project for the tennis courts at the school is underway. They found that the old
tennis court was built on what appears to be ‘shifting soils' which contributed to the constant cracking so
the soll is being replaced with suitable base material so that the problem will be solved for the new
courts. They will also have curtain drains.
= The STEAP Grant project for Hope Street Is going to start shortly. They did not get the STEAP Grant
that they had applied for to do the Cini Park bathrooms so they are applying for another grant for that
project.
* The bath area at vets is framed and almost done.
* He recalled that he had sent them the information on the beach pass activity which has improved in
numbers and dollars as was hoped and expected.
¢ Finance Director

Ms. Johnson said that she had provided them with a draft of the receivables this evening and explained that
her Accounts Receivable Admin has been out for a few weeks and that she will return this coming Monday.
While she has had others helping out, she said that it will be nice to have her back. She reported that
revenues are 1.5% higher than they were at this time last year. They also paid out approximately $3.7M in
debt payments in July. They paid out insurance waivers in July for those who elect not to be on our
insurance as they have other family members insurance that they are covered by. This was $1500 for seven
people and $500 for two people whose amount was pro-rated as they are newer employees. She explained
that while it may seem like a lot that it is not that much of a stipend when they consider that the insurance
would be in the $100,000 range. They also had bond sales and bonded projects and short-term financing for
some projects.

Mr. Kelley asked if they would have their auditor present at their next meting to synopsize the previous year.
Ms. Johnson said yes.

F, New Business

a. Special Appropriation - $20,000 Grant Award for Archaeological Reconnaissance Survey —
Samuel Smith Property

Mr. Formica explained that this is a completely reimbursable grant that will be used to survey the property.

Ms. Johnson noted that they could apply for one third of it to get started as it operates as a ‘draw down’.

Mr. Formica also noted that CIRMA had originally told them that they did not want people in the house until it
was more secured however they came to an agreement that people could go in through the back of the
house to the main room only and look around while a talk on the history of it was being given. Once it is more
secured, people will be able to more fully tour it.

**MOTION (2)

Ms. Hogan moved to approve a Special Appropriation for a grant in the amount of $20,000 from the State
Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) with the Department of Economic and Community Development (DECD)
in CNRE Fund 32 to a budget account titled, ‘Archaeological Reconnaissance Survey” with a corresponding
revenue account. The survey will be of the Samuel Smith property for the purpose of being recognized as a
Certified Local Government (CLG).

Mr. Harney seconded the motion.

Vote: 5-0-0. Motion passed.

Mr. Kelley noted that this also requires Town Meeting Approval.

b. Special Appropriation $280,000 - Acquisition Bayreuther Property

Mr. Formica explained that this is the same item as they had seen before just in a different package as they
found that the State money would only be turned over to the Town ($400,000) so they are asking for an
appropriation of $280,000 and the Town’s net figure to be contributed would stil! be the $25,000. This
procedure is to make sure that it is advertised correctly to the public.

Ms. Alberti asked about the Open Space Fund 59.
Mr. Formica said that it was set up around 1995 for the purpose of open space and that they had decided
that the $25,000 would come from it and it still would.

Mr. Kelley said that the open space fund has $908,000 in it and that it would have $25,000 less when this is
all said and done.
