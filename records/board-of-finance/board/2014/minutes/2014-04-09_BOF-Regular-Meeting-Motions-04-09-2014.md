E. Reports

¢ First Selectman

Mr. Formica reported that the money for the Boardwalk was appropriated at Town Meeting and that they
hope to finalize the permit process on the upcoming Tuesday. He said that he is also working with the Parks
& Recreation Chairman on not charging for the boardwalk. On the State front, they are working on the
reverse PILOT, minimum wage incremental increases, and teacher evaluations. They are also looking for
Dispatch language that they have submitted to the State. They are also looking to reduce the TO on the
Resident Trooper bill from 100% back to the 70%.
