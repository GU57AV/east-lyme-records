E. Reports

¢ First Selectman

Mr. Formica reported that the Regional Water project is winding down and is about to be turned on. As they
missed the original window for sending water back and forth prior to the summer season, they are looking to
do a side agreement with New London on sending water back and forth on Mondays, Tuesdays &
Wednesdays. This would also allow for thorough testing of the system. The line has been leak tested and is
full of water. The Department of Health has also visited and reviewed it.

He also reported on an Energize CT energy audit for homeowners who would pay $75 and receive all types
of energy savings measures within their homes. He said that there is also a ‘per resident’ program in
conjunction with CL & P that they will be rolling out shortly. This gives credit to the Clean Energy Program
and as the points mount up — a rebate is given that can be used within the community.

Ms. Picarazzi asked who conducts the energy audit.
Mr. Formica said that CL & P have authorized contractors that are used.

Mr. Kelley asked if there was an income limit on the $75 that would get you up to $700 worth of energy

savings measures installed in your home.
Mr. Formica said no. He added that they would also be doing another one with the seniors in mind.

Ms. Picarazzi asked when the boardwalk repairs would start.

Mr. Formica said it would be as soon as Amtrak gives the okay as they have all of the other necessary
permitting in place and a contractor who has been vetted and the money appropriated to do the work. Amtrak
has all of the information to review and has promised an answer before the May 21, 2014 which is the time
frame that the contractor has to hold the pricing. He noted that it is ridiculous that it has gone on for as long
as it has now, especially when considering that the Town cooperated to expedite the process for the bridge
repairs that Amtrak did. He expects to hear any day now.

¢@ Finance Director
Ms. Johnson said that they had their packets with all the information and that her monthly report was in their
packets. She had also passed out a copy of the Board of Ed budget report to them this evening.

Ms. Picarazzi asked if there was anything that they should be aware of regarding coming up on the end of

the year.

Ms. Johnson said that she was working with Mr. Bragaw to get all of the issues resolved with regard to the
winter that we had and that she does expect that they will come before the Board asking for $45,000 to cover
the heating costs for the extremely cold winter we had.
