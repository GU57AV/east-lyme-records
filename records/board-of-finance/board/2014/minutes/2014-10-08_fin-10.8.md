The Pledge was observed.

D. Approval of Minutes

= Regular Meeting - Wednesday, August 13, 2014

Ms. Picarazzi called for a motion for approval or any changes to the Board of Finance Regular Meeting
Minutes of August 13, 2014.

**MOTION (1)

Ms. Hogan moved to approve the Board of Finance Regular Meeting Minutes of August 13, 2014 as
presented.

Mr. Carpenteri seconded the motion.

Vote: 4-0-0. Motion passed.

E. Reports
¢ First Selectman
There was no report.

@ Finance Director

Ms. Johnson said that she had provided them with a copy of her report and reviewed the highlights. She
noted that they are concerned with the heating oil and propane budget of $115,000 as Mr. Bragaw has let
her know that they may need more to make it through the season. The telephone account is another area of
concern with the issue being with the cell phones. They are working on reviewing the plans that we have and
trying to cut back and update the plans. They also received a notice/report from unemployment looking for
$2400 that was from the last fiscal year but billed In this fiscal year. This makes this issue a bit more difficult.

Ms. Picarazzi asked about the detailed revenue account and if A = accrual and B= non-accrual.
Ms. Johnson said that was correct.

Ms. Johnson noted that they had also been provided with a Tuition Rate Study Calculation — Out-of-District
from the East Lyme Schools and that the FY 14/15 Proposed Tuition Rate is $14,522.

F. New Business
a. Final Budget Transfers — June 30, 2014 - $52,127.66
Ms. Johnson said that these are the final budget transfers to close out the fiscal year.

“*MOTION (2)

Mr. Carpenteri moved to ratify departmental transfers in the amount of $52,127.66 processed for fiscal year
end June 30, 2014.

Mr. Harney seconded the motion.

Vote: 4-0-0. Motion passed.

b. Special Appropriation — Use of funds from sale of NFD Apparatus - $9000

Ms. Johnson said that they recently sold an old Niantic Fire Department apparatus for $9000 and they are
looking to use the money for emergency services to link to the State of CT fiber optic closed public safety
network.

Ms. Hogan asked what they sold.

Mr. Morris said that they sold a 30 year old fire truck for $9000 to Kentucky. He said that he would like to
outfit his area with the fiber optics so that they can utilize the statewide network for free to better be able to
communicate together.

Ms. Picarazzi said that the total price is about $18,000 and they have $9000 from this; she asked where the
other $9000 is coming from.
Mr. Morris said that it is from the Nuclear Safety Fund.

**MOTION (3)

Mr. Hamey moved to approve a special appropriation in the amount of $9000 to an account to be established
titled, “Public Safety Data Network” to allow the Town to operate the IMC program over high speed fiber
optics for improved public safety communications.

Mr. Carpenteri seconded the motion.

Vote: 4-0-0. Motion passed.

c. Expenditure — contingency — Police Radios - $11,878.50

Ms. Johnson explained that most of the Police radios are old, failing, unable to be repaired and are not
supported by the system. This request is for three (3) portable radios that will be compatible with the State
radios.

**MOTION (4) -

Mr. Harney moved to approve the expenditure of $11,878.50 from account 01-01-120-200-500 (contingency)
to purchase three (3) portable radios for the Police Department along with the extended 4 year warranty to
maintain Town of East Lyme Public Safety. The cost per radio is $3,797.50 and the cost per unit for the
extended warranty is $162.

Mr. Carpenteri seconded the motion.

Vote: 4-0-0. Motion passed.

G. Old Business
