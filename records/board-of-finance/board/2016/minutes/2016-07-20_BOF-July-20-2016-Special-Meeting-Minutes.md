@ Sgt. Michael Macek Police Dept — Special Report - OT

Sgt. Macek thanked them for having him. He explained that there are several contractual obligations that
they have to meet and a lot of the items are lumped into one area. He said that he had implemented the
denial of last minute 1-day vacation day notice — they would have to take a personal or sick day — as doing
that helps with the last minute overtime. Also, as they have a three-man minimum shift — comp time can be
used if it will to create an overtime situation. They also have an MOU regarding the carry over of days
going forward — they have a time frame — personal leave days — there is a one time deal to roll them to use
them by December of this fiscal year or they will lose them. He said that he also institutes some call-out
shift changes. The Touch-A-Truck event has one paid officer and the rest are volunteers for the event. They
have mutual aid — other Towns helping — for the CEL event saves them around $16,000 which helps them
out. However, there have also been some unforeseen circumstances — recently two sergeants have issues
— one is out on medical and the other has some eye issues which has taken them off patrol — so he has had
to switch people around and shifted the schedule so 35 overtime shifts have been reduced to 11. He said
that he is trying to do the best with what they have. He has worked with the First Selectman on these issues
in good faith on both sides.

Ms. Picarazzi thanked Sgt. Macek for this effort as it is huge. She asked how the officers took it.

Sgt. Macek said that he told them that he was tasked with this by the Town Board of Finance and that the
goal was to not have to come back to them for more money for overtime. He added that they are also
working on a grant like the one that he came with that helps out considerably.

Mr. Nickerson applauded Sgt. Macek and said that he has done the lion’s share of the work here on this.

F. New Business
a. Special Appropriation — $969,436 — Turn-key Energy Efficient Street Light Project
Mr. Harney called upon Mr. Bragaw to present information on this item.

Mr. Bragaw said that he had presented this to them last year and that there are two purposes for doing this:
First, it is cheaper for us to own the lights and to put LED’s in them and second, it is the right thing to do. He
said that right now, they just react and pay bills whereas this gives them control over what they will pay. He
explained the process saying that we purchase the lights from CL&P and the rate structure drops some
80%. If we don't do anything it will continue to cost the Town more money. We currently pay $200,000/year
on streetlights. In the gn year of this project it is all savings. (See attachments) Montville is doing this now
as well as New London. There is also a rebate check once the lights are changed out of $130,000.

Ms. Picarazzi thanked Mr. Bragaw for doing the work on this. She said that it is a no-brainer and that she is
in support of it.

Mr. Pazzaglia said that right now CL&P handies the maintenance of the lights and poles — how will that

change.
Mr. Bragaw Said that Tanko has a request system where they have a local contractor come out and change
out any bulbs that go out. The utility company still owns the poles. This is for the light heads and lights.

Ms. Alberti asked about the lights on Rte. 161 which is a State road.
Mr. Bragaw said that we pay for them as they service this Town. It is the same for other Towns. The State
takes care of the major State highways, bridges etc.

b. Special Appropriation — $60,000 McCook’s Amphitheater

Mr. Nickerson noted that the band shell will be located on a swath of lawn near where the facilities are. The
space will back up to the RR and face out to the water and flag pole. The Lion’s, Rotary and EL Public Trust
all donated monies to this ($60,000) plus the $15,000 that we already have budgeted. Further, he said that

build it is coming from service organizations.

flat grassy area below the pavilion.

$52,000. This request is to move the $60,000 out of CNRE to add to the $15,000 so that they can move on
this project.

Ms. Alberti commended them for getting the donations and stepping up when we are under such budget
constraints. She asked about the potential to rent out this facility for events as that could cover any
maintenance cost.

Mr. Putnam said that the intent was to do just that and to rent it as they do the tent and pavilion, so it should
cover any maintenance.

d. Special Appropriation $480,000 FFD Class A Pumper Apparatus

William Rix, Asst. Chief, Flanders Fire Department said that they had not said that there was a crack in the

other costs and get the best possible price for their needs and money. (See attachment)

years and the other at 23 years of age.
