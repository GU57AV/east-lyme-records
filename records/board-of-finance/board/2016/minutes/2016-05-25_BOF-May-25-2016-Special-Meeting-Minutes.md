A. Call Special Meeting to Order
Chairman Harney called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Finance to order at 6:30 PM.

B. Pledge of Allegiance
The Pledge was observed.

Cc. New Business
o Set the Mil Rate for the FY 2016/17 Budget
Mr. Hamey noted that the budget had gone to referendum and had passed.

Ms. Johnson said that when the budget was set that they were looking to raise a certain amount however
since that time the Town is getting $259,118 less from the State. They had originally thought that they would
raise the mil rate by .59 however now in order to cover the$259,118 shortfall it would require a .71 mil rate
increase instead.

Mr. Harney called for discussion.

Ms. Alberti said that at the last regular meeting that there was hesitancy to take the new shortfall from fund
balance and that Ms. Johnson had indicated that it would affect the bond rating. She presented information
(attached) indicating that the February 2010 bond rating for the Town was an Aa? and that the current bond
rating for the Town is better at an Aa?. There has been a steady increase to the fund balance and while
$570,000 may be retumed to fund balance by the end of this fiscal year — that would be an 11% increase to
the fund balance over the last year. If they were to take the $259,000 from that, they would still return
$310,000 and the fund balance would increase by 6% for an overall 14% increase in the 2012-2016 time
frame. She said that she does not think it would be risking our good bond rating and noted that the increase
to the budget this year is less than it was last year. Also — last year the budget passed at referendum with a
74% approval and this year the approval rate was 62%. She said that she sees this as an indication that they
are reaching a saturation point and she cannot recommend increasing the mil rate over what was originally
suggested.

Ms. Hogan asked if they were to agree with Ms. Alberti — would the $310,000 still maintain the 8% fund
balance.

Ms. Johnson said that she does not think that it would and she also thinks that it would jeopardize our rating.
She also noted the current health insurance situation at present and the hope that it will get better in the next
fiscal year. $5.5M in fund balance would equal 8%.

Mr. Weber said that he still thinks that next year will be worse for them as the State will have a larger deficit.
He said that his opinion is to raise the mil rate to cover the added shortfall from the State.
(Note: 6:40 PM - Ms. Picarazzi joined the meeting)

Mr. Pazzaglia said that he sits somewhere in the middle on this and can see both sides. He suggested that
maybe they could consider splitting the $260,000 cut.

Mr. Nickerson offered the following suggestion — they could increase the mil rate to cover half of the
$260,000 and challenge him and Mr. Newton to find the rest during the year. Take half ($130,000) from the
budget and increase the mil rate by the other half ($130,000).

Mr. Harney noted that this would mean a .65 mil rate increase rather than the .71 and that the new mil rate
would be 25.36. He called for a motion.

**MOTION (1)

Ms. Hogan moved to set a mil rate of 25.36 for FY 2016/17 for the Town of East Lyme.
Mr. Weber seconded the motion.

Vote: 6-0-0. Motion passed.

o Discussion/Action Tax Collection Suspense List 2000-2014 in the amount of $44,905.08
John McCulloch, Tax Collector stated that the Suspense List is a compilation of past due motor vehicle and
personal property taxes that have been deemed uncollectible at this time for various reasons but are not
abated as a tax liability and are collectible. (Attachment) He noted that this year there was a $9000 increase
over last year.

Ms. Hogan asked how often they check on these to see if the people pop up.
Mr. McCulloch said that people re-entering the State cannot register without paying up.

**MOTION (2)

Mr. Weber moved to accept the 2000-2014 Suspense List as presented at $44,905.08.
Ms. Hogan seconded the motion.

Vote: 6-0-0. Motion passed.

o Audit RFP
Ms. Johnson explained that the Audit RFP was sent out on May 13, 2016. There were 19 firms on the State
list and she sent out to 11 with the return by date of Wednesday June 8, 2016. She noted that the last time
they reviewed the responses that they used a five member selection committee comprised of her, Mr.
Nickerson, the School Finance Officer and two members from this Board. She said that she would be looking
to do interviews the week of June 13, 2016.

D. Board Comments
Mr. Harney called for comments from the Board —
There were none.

E. Adjournment
**MOTION (3)
Ms. Picarazzi moved to adjourn this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Finance at 7:00 PM.

Ms. Alberti seconded the motion.
Vote: 6-0-0. Motion passed.
