♦ Harbor Management Commission (136) – Don Landers, Chairman, Steve Dinsmore, Treasurer
**MOTION (4)
Mr. Hart moved to open the budget review for Item 136 – Harbor Management Commission.
Mr. Kelley seconded the motion.
Vote: 6 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.

Mr. Formica noted that there is a bit of an increase to this budget due to the maintenance of the harbor from
the institution of the mooring permit program. They eventually expect more revenues from the permits and
that this will take care of itself as it becomes more stable.

Mr. Hart asked about line #415 as it looked like they were taking $3000 from it and moving it to a different
line item.
Mr. Dinsmore said that the warden retired and that a previous warden was volunteering some time to them.

Mr. Langan said that the actual expense from the previous year was significantly less than what they are
requesting this year ($3448 v. $12,074).
Mr. Dinsmore said that a lot had to do with the warden volunteering his time.
Mr. Formica said that the $1700 and $3000 increases have to do with the increase in the harbor
management due to the mooring program which is a significant program and to get it up and running so that
it can pay off.

Mr. Kleinhans said that prior to the mooring program that they spent $3448 and that to date this year they
have nearly $8000 left – he asked if they expect to spend it.
Mr. Landers said that is a valid question and that there are currently a number of moorings out there that are
unpermitted and he is not sure if they will have to have them hauled and pulled. He explained that they are
set up by State Statute and the money they collect is supposed to be put into an account and that he hopes
that they will eventually be self sustaining with this program. They previously brought in zero and now they
are bringing in some money.

♦ Building Maintenance (113) – Michael Giannattasio, Public Works Director
**MOTION (12)
Mr. Hart moved to open the budget review for Item 113 – Building Maintenance.
Mr. Harney seconded the motion.
Vote: 6 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.

Mr. Formica noted that Mr. Giannattasio has taken control of this over the last year and that he has been
certified in facilities maintenance. They have also reached out to the schools to work with them and have
seen efficiencies there.

Mr. Kleinhans asked Mr. Giannattasio to speak about the certification he received.
Mr. Giannattasio said that he went through the Building Operator Certification Series – Level I covers energy
efficiencies and green energy and Level II involves diagnostics and systems analysis. There are eight
classes of eight hours each and he attended under the State of CT certification program. He said that he was
recommended and accepted to the program and that they have recommended that people from the school
also take it and they have also been accepted.

Mr. Kelley asked about line #314 – Overtime as to date they have spent 39% of what was budgeted. He
asked if they expect that to go at an increased rate through the end of the year.
Mr. Giannattasio said that the figures that they are seeing are due to the fact that the highest paid person
has taken the extra time in comp time rather than pay – so he gets comp time and a half.

Mr. Kelley asked about the utilities line item as electricity seems tight for the rest of this year.
Mr. Giannattasio said that what they are seeing is all of the money encumbered up front. He noted that he
thinks that they will be okay.

budgeted.
Mr. Giannattasio said that his figures came in close also and added that there is an increase coming in
January from CL&P of around 5% plus the new building will be coming on line.

Mr. Harney asked about the building consolidation and some coming off line.
Mr. Giannattasio said that he thought that it would work out okay.

Mr. Larcen asked about the maintenance staff that was being added.
Mr. Giannattasio said that he felt that they would need 2 hours more per day to do the cleaning for the new
building. They have not yet figured out how that will be handled.
