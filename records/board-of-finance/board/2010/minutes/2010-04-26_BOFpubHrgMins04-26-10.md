                                  EAST LYME BOARD OF FINANCE
                                    PUBLIC HEARING MINUTES
                                 FY 2010-2011 PROPOSED BUDGET
                                     Monday, APRIL 26th, 2010

Members In Attendance:          Robert Kleinhans, Chairman
                                Steve Larcen, Secretary
                                Steve Harney
                                Raymond Hart
                                Steve Kelley
                                Jeff Langan

Also In Attendance:             Paul Formica, First Selectman & Ex-Officio
                                Tim Hagen, Chairman, Board of Ed

Absent:                         No One



A. Call Meeting to Order
Chairman Kleinhans called this Public Hearing on the FY 2010-2011 Proposed Budget of the East Lyme
Board of Finance to order at 7:05 PM.


B. Pledge of Allegiance
The Pledge was observed.

Mr. Kleinhans outlined the format of the meeting and introduced the members of the Board to the audience.

C. Board of Finance Presentation of Proposed Budget for FY 2010-2011
Mr. Kleinhans said that he would have Mr. Larcen, Secretary on the Board of Finance do the presentation.

Mr. Rando called Point of Order and asked why the Chairman was not doing the budget presentation.
Mr. Kleinhans said that as the Chair that he could appoint anyone to do the presentation and that he had
chosen Mr. Larcen to do it as he had done it last year.

Mr. Larcen explained the summary process that they followed: - the Board met nine (9) times with the
various departments and to review and cut some $822,000 from the budget that was presented to them. The
budget represents an overall 1.9% increase in the property tax mill rate. He noted that they had received
over $400,000 less in funding from the previous year. He said that they have a lack of revenue and that the
revenues are some $17,000 less than they were two years ago. The lack of revenues is due to the lower
interest rates, and less income from building and other fees. He said that they would not be using any money
from the fund balance this year. In a PowerPoint presentation he went over the major reasons for reduced
revenues in the FY10-11budget and the reductions that the Board of Finance had proposed from the budget,
as received from the Board of Selectmen. He said that the current budget is $60,368,873 and that the budget
being submitted this evening for comment is $60,770,731. He noted that people ask if the cuts proposed are
proportionate and the pie chart shows that Education is 65% of the total budget with General Government at
24%; Capital 2%; Contingency 0% and Debt Service, 9%. He said that each of the department heads was
helpful in working with them on this budget and there were wage concessions made by the Education
administrative staff and management and reductions in the overtime hours. Most significantly, within the
Public Works Department, the advent of single stream recycling should realize some savings and revenues.
The $60,770,731 proposed budget is a 1.9% increase from the current year. The mill rate would go from
19.186 to 19.551 with no money being used from the undesignated fund balance. There is a contingency
item set aside for unexpected items. He said that they had all worked together in these tough economic
times. The Town Meeting on the budget will be held on Monday, May 10, 2010 at 7 PM here in the High
School Auditorium and he said that the referendum on the budget would be on Thursday, May 20, 2010 at
the East Lyme Community Center.
Mr. Kleinhans said that Mr. Formica, First Selectman would say a few words regarding the budget.

D. Comments by First Selectman
First Selectman, Paul Formica thanked everyone for coming and for all of the hard work on the budget by
everyone. He said that this is the first time that they have done this under the new process of going to the
automatic referendum. The referendum voting will be on Thursday, May 20, 2010 from 8 AM to 8 PM at the
Community Center.
He said that the budget being proposed is for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2010 and running through
June 30, 2011. This six month budget process started with the department heads and then went to the Board
of Selectmen and to the Board of Finance. It will go to Town Meeting and Referendum after this. The
revenues come primarily from property taxes, licenses and fees and from the State. He said that they are all
affected by this economy and thanked the department heads for holding the line on spending. They have
heard him say that the Town needs to be run like a business and difficult times offer unique opportunities in
running the Town as a business as the Town will be around 100 years from now, (unlike many businesses)
and they have to invest in the infrastructure. He noted that they will be a four Town hub for Probate
beginning in January 2011 and that the Registrars will be moving to an office in the Community Center which
will put them closer to the voting process. They switched from CIRMA to MIRMA and were able to save
some $300,000 in that year. He noted that Emergency Management has not been as easy to regionalize as
he had hoped as people do not want to give up their own system so this budget represents an increase in
personnel for Emergency Management. He thinks that they will see a new type of model set-up for the Police
in the future. Parks & recreation represents a new type of program that will be tried in the upcoming year – a
Special Revenue Fund where revenues will be shared with the Town. He also noted that the debt service
payments would be reduced from $6.1M to $5.4M and reviewed the long range capital plan items. There is
also a vision committee that is looking at the needs of the Town for the future. He noted the recent $1.6M
Boardwalk settlement and said that Amtrak is expecting to be done with the bridge replacement in May of
2013 and that they are looking at the possibility of working on fixing the other part of the Boardwalk sooner
than that time. He cited the streetscape monies that they had received and the new sidewalks, trees, granite
curbing and streetlights that are going in downtown and the new sidewalks that had been put in at Cini Park
with grant money. He said that he feels that this is a good budget and thanked everyone for all of their hard
work and urged them to go out and vote at the referendum. He said that Tim Hagen, Board of Ed Chairman
and Dr. Lombardo, Superintendent of Schools would speak to them next.

E. Comments by Board of Education Chairman
Tim Hagen, Board of Ed Chairman noted the extent of the meetings that they held and said that the Board of
Ed budget has been looked at extensively. He acknowledged the great staff that they have and thanked the
community for their support in sustaining excellence in the education of the children. He thanked staff,
teachers, administrative personnel, custodians and all of the people who make things happen in the schools
each day. He especially thanked the teachers, paraprofessionals and administrative personnel who will
receive no wage increases and no step increases in the next fiscal year. He then introduced Dr. Lombardo
and thanked him for his objectivity in bringing forth new methodology.

Dr. Lombardo, Superintendent of Schools thanked everyone and said that he and his family very much enjoy
their new home in Saunders Point. He said that he would present the high points of the school district budget
but first explained his two daughters non-traditional educational paths noting that East Lyme has the same
type of students and that the goals and priorities for the year were crafted around educating the whole child
and making sure that no child is invisible and is supported by caring adults. He said that by the end of the
year they will have hired nine (9) new administrators and that 70% of the teachers have less than 10 years
experience. He went over a PowerPoint presentation noting that the FY 2010-2011 budget represents a
1.83% increase over the current year. The frozen salaries represent around an $850,000 savings and there
is a 15% reduction in supplies for $185,000 savings. One increase is $85,000 for a SPED coordinator K-12
which will be offset by stimulus funds and paraprofessional reductions (10 less by attrition). He said that they
know that this is a difficult year and he thanked Mr. Formica for all of his hard work and the Boards of
Selectmen and Finance and everyone who has worked tirelessly on this for many months now. He said that
as always, they put the children first and will continue to give them the best possible education.
