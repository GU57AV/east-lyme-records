C. Delegations

Mike Schulz, Lovers Lane, East Lyme said that he had a few things that he wanted to mention. First, he
has been tuning into Channel 22 to see the meetings televised, without success. He strongly
emphasized the importance of the meetings being televised and of the agendas being made available
on-line; posted on the website. A few other topics he said that he wondered if anyone on the Board
could explain the how or why they happened or at least something on them are: how a non-elected
individual was positioned as the Chairman of one of the most important Boards in the Town – the Board
of Finance and also how and why one of the people elected to the Board of Finance resigned.
Additionally he said that he would like to know why one of the most honored people elected to the
Board of Selectmen, with the most experience was not designated as the Deputy First Selectman and a
newcomer with no experience was put there instead. He said that he thinks that there is some political
foul play there and would like an explanation of why and how all of this came about.

Bob Gadbois, 358 Boston Post Road said that he feels the same as Mr. Schulz does as the Board of
Finance is an elected Board and he feels that someone elected should be the Chairman. He also said
that he attended the Board of Ed meeting the other night where they announced a 3.5% increase in
their budget. He said that if they do not sharpen their pencils that he thinks that the Board of Finance
needs to as the 3.5% increase in this economy with so many people out of work, is way out of hand. He
noted that Mr. Langan had said at the last meeting that it would be a tough budget year and that he
agreed with him that it would be a very tough year.

John McCulloch, Tax Collector gave his report on tax collections noting that as of 12/31/2009 they were
slightly ahead at a 97.97% collection rate versus the previous year of 97.62% collection.

E. Reports
a. Review of Draft 6/30/2009 Audit by Mr. Scott Bassett, McGladrey & Pullen LLP
Mr. Formica noted that Mr. Bassett has done quite a bit of extra work for them and worked hard on the
audit. He introduced Mr. Bassett, a partner with McGladrey & Pullen to present the draft audit report.

Mr. Bassett, partner with McGladrey & Pullen LLP spoke about how they had been working on
improving the processes and noted that Ms. Johnson and her staff have been working over the hurdles
that were noted last year. He said that he does not have the management letter for them yet as they do
not have it tonight and are scheduled to review it with Mr. Formica later this week. He said that there
would be some new items on it although a lot of the old items will have been taken care of. He would
have to come back at a later time to present it to them.

Mr. Kelley said that while many of the past findings have been corrected that it does not mean that all of
them have been taken care of; he asked which remained.
Mr. Bassett said that he would provide them with a list of the items although he thinks that the
significant findings regarding the bank reconciliations have been taken care of. He said that the list
would be forthcoming. He continued going over the draft noting that the balance sheet is a snapshot in
time and that collections on the tax levy were 98.65% versus 98.67% in the previous year. They also
came in spending roughly $1M less than budgeted for the year. It was noted that the annual required
contribution for those retirees and future retirees receiving health benefits from the Town was estimated
at $1.1M and with only $19,000 being contributed, it was suggested that this be better funded. Also
mentioned was the recovery of loss of $414,634 from the Board of Ed school lunch program, found on
page 72.

Mr. Hart asked Mr. Bassett what percentage he would recommend that they fund this obligation at.
Mr. Bassett recommended that they fund the $1M over a period of time, perhaps a 10-year time period
once the economy turns around.

Mr. Larcen said that he thought that it was a good idea to have Mr. Bassett come back with the
management letter and findings so that they could better discuss this.
Mr. Bassett said that he would come back with the letter.

Mr. Langan asked if on Page 14 of the draft that the undesignated fund balance was roughly at $3.5M.
Mr. Bassett said yes and added that in 2008 it was $3.382M so they had a pick-up of roughly $100,000
to this year.

Mr. Larcen noted that part of the problem with the fund balance in 2008 was the LoCIP monies were not
received.
Mr. Bassett noted that page 63 shows the pick up of $400,000 of LoCIP monies that were recovered.

Mr. Langan noted that revenues were nearly $187,000 less than anticipated.

c. Finance Director
• Monthly Report Review
Mr. Kleinhans asked that Ms. Johnson in the future please include her monthly report in their packets so
that they could review it prior to the meeting and be ready to ask questions about it at the meeting.

Ms. Johnson read her December 2009 month end report and noted that a portion of the ECS funding
was not coming directly to them and that accounted for the percentage difference.

Mr. Kelley said that they had gone from $443,000 in interest to $211,000 in interest to $40,000 interest
on investments and asked where they were going on investment rates.
Ms. Johnson said that the rates were higher in the other years and that they were higher last year and
started to decrease in the later part of last year.

Mr. Larcen asked if there are reasonable ways to invest money to get better returns – perhaps in short-
term bonds.
Mr. Formica said that he had just provided Ms. Johnson with information on other bank programs and
that he does agree that they have to look into other things for a better return.

Mr. Kleinhans asked what they had budgeted for interest income for the year.
Mr. Kelley said that they had budgeted for $200,000 and that they are only at $40,000 with half of the
year over.
Mr. Kleinhans said that they would run short of projection this year on revenues.
Mr. Formica noted that they have some higher one-time building fee monies from the AHEPA project
which hopefully will offset the projection. He further noted that in all of the meetings that he has been to
with the COG and CCM, etc. that they have indicated that in 2011 the funding monies will go away and
it will be extremely difficult as they will have to look for other opportunities to recoup those monies.

Mr. Harney asked if the actuary and investment advisor that they are engaging for the pension
committee could be consulted with regard to increasing their income.
Mr. Formica said that there is not really a lot that they can do without speculating.

Mr. Kleinhans noted that he was at a meeting regarding the State budget and that while it is not good
now, they have said that 2011 will be worse with a lot of non-recovery happening and the State does
not know where they will come up with the money.

Mr. Larcen asked Ms. Johnson about the projected revenue report.
Ms. Johnson explained how the report was devised and how the projections obtained. She also noted
that they had only received one-half of the Tax Relief on Boats.
Mr. Larcen asked if the $190,974 is favorable.

• Update on Outstanding Items
Mr. Larcen asked about the information that he had requested at the last meeting regarding the Youth
Services fees and if Ms. Johnson had researched it.

some storage space available at the Town garage for it.
