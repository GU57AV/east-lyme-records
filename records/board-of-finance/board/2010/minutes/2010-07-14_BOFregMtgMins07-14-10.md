F. New Business
a. Special Appropriation - $500,000 Repair and Replace Town Roads
Mr. Kleinhans noted that Mr. Giannattasio was present this evening to explain this and to answer any
questions they might have.

Mr. Formica said that this is the second phase of what is hoped to be a three phase project.

Mr. Giannattasio explained that they have now fully implemented the software program for road
repairing and that the cost for them for the work that would need to be done for everything is projected
at $12.5M. They now have to plan on how they want to do the work. There are various phases of
maintenance with regard to the roads – those that require no maintenance; those that require routine
maintenance such as crack sealing; those that require preventative maintenance such as sealing of the
pavement surfaces and cracks, those that require milling and overlay and lastly those that require total
reconstruction. The phases range in cost from the least to the most expensive. He explained that if they
were able to keep all of the roads up that they would not have so many of the ‘poor condition’ roads.
The cost per mile of road changes based upon the amount and type of work that needs to be done.
They are asking for $500,000 that would be bonded tonight and that it would be applied to all of the
categories. They will do some components of Walnut Hill Road and others throughout the Town.

Mr. Harney asked what component of work the Town employees are qualified to do.
Mr. Giannattasio said that they may provide help to the crack sealing process but that for the most part
they hire people who are set up for this type of work and who have all of the equipment necessary to do
it properly.

Mr. Langan asked if other Towns do it this way and if we are comparable to them.
Mr. Kelley said that he is looking forward to seeing the difference in the chart that they are looking at
this evening once the various areas have each had some work done when they come back for the next
phase.

Mr. Formica said that while Mr. Bialowans is present, that they would be happy to go up there to Walnut
Hill Road and meet with him and see what they can do to make the road better. He also noted that the
money that they are discussing is in the capital plan.

Mr. Kleinhans noted that Mr. Larcen had asked if this was going to be bonded separately or with other
items as he had thought there were other items.
Mr. Formica said that it would be with other items.

b. Special Appropriation - $225,000 – Revaluation 2011
Mr. Formica recapped that this is typically a $500,000 event and that they have joined together in a joint
bid process with Waterford. They received six (6) bids and they have come in at the $365,000-$370,000
range for the Town with the both Towns working together. This $225,000 comes from the CNRE fund.
He said that he thinks that this joint endeavor has turned out very positively for both Towns. They will
know in the near future who the company is that will be awarded the contract.

Mr. Kleinhans noted that he had thought that the bids would come in at the amounts cited. He asked if
they approve the $225,000 tonight, they would need the $130,000 - $140,000 more – when.

Donna Bekech, Town Assessor said that there is a 30-day billing cycle.

Mr. Formica said that they would also have some specialty items such as the prisons that would be
done and billed separately.

Mr. Kleinhans asked if, in reviewing the bids they found any newer technology that is beneficial to the
community.
Mr. Formica said that they have discussed community outreach and how the process has to be better
explained.

Mr. Kelley said that he thought that he heard that this would go to Town Meeting for approval and asked
why as it is coming from the CNRE and was already voted on.
Mr. Formica said that he has had the same conversation and that they would do it in the interest of
transparency.

**MOTION (4)
Mr. Kelley moved to approve an appropriation in the amount of $225,000 for the purpose of completing
a portion of the reappraisal and revaluation of all real property (taxable and exempt) for the Town of

available in the Capital and Non-Recurring (CNRE) Fund.
Mr. Harney seconded the motion.
Vote: 4 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.
