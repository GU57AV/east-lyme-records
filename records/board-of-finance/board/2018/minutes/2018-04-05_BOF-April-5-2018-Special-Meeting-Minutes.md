Ms. Picarazzi asked about building permit revenue.

¢ Police (216) — Chief Michael Finkelstein; Sgt. Mike Macek, East Lyme Police
Mr. Nickerson noted that Chief Finkelstein and Sgt. Macek would present their budget.

Chief Finkelstein said that they looked at areas to increase productivity and efficiencies. They also looked at
their IT needs. The part-time admin position shows a 20% increase as the full time admin has been with the
Town for almost 30 years now and has more time to take off. The part time person fills in when the full time
person is out or off. Line #513 — Foot Patrol/Parade Duty is used to cover events. It was increased as they
are now also covering football games, etc for the schools. Longevity is contractual; Training is to get the
officers up to their necessary level. They also utilize Star Computers for computer maintenance.
Miscellaneous Supplies is up as items that were not kept up need to be. He said that they have been lucky
that L & M has given them Narcan however, that is not always the case. Legal ads also fall under
miscellaneous supplies. They increased the canine line item as they have two dogs and they require vet
care. Line #326 — Training Supplies covers consumables such as cartridges, tasers and also training tuitions.
New items that were added are line #331 — Scheduling Software — their software has reached the end of its
useful life and is no longer supported. The new software would allow for better and faster scheduling under a
number of parameters. Line #332 — Towing is a cost that they incur and have to pay for towing vehicles.
They have always had to pay for it before — they just took the money for it from other line items in the past.

Ms. Picarazzi asked about line #519 — Grant Overtime (Reimbursement).

Chief Finkelstein said that this is a State DUI Grant and that the amount each year may be different. They
get grant revenue to offset this.

Sgt. Macek explained that it runs through September so the account is never negative. The grant comes
from the Feds and is 100% reimbursable.

Ms. Picarazzi asked about line #514 — Overtime and if they are over-estimating what that line should be.
Chief Finkelstein said that by increasing it, this gives them the ability to do narcotics and other pro-actives.
He said that he thinks that they are on-target with this figure.

Ms. Picarazzi asked if they have considered using other people to help with parade duty rather than the
officers at overtime.

Chief Finkelstein said that he does — they use the Explorers on a regular basis.

Mr. Nickerson explained that this is an attempt to put items in the proper areas. He added that they are
looking at the security for CEL and trying to determine a potential cost to vendors to try to recover some of
the security cost in the vendor fee.

Chief Finkelstein said that he realizes it is large however they utilize that amount.

Sgt. Macek explained that in the previous year $5000 was only part of what was necessary for the private
force and they ended up utilizing a lot more hours. This year they have more things coming up and have had
issues with things that do not talk to each other and not being able to do everything at the same computer
due to glitches.
