                                    EAST LYME BOARD OF FINANCE
                                     SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
                                   Thursday, SEPTEMBER 27th, 2012

Members In Attendance:          Raymond Hart, Chairman
                                Steve Harney, Secretary
                                Richard Bengtson
                                Steve Carpenteri
                                Steve Kelley

Also In Attendance:             Paul Formica, First Selectman
                                Anna Johnson, Finance Director

Absent:                         Lisa Picarazzi


A. Call Meeting to Order
Chairman Hart called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Finance to order at 6:00 PM.

B. Pledge of Allegiance
The Pledge was observed.

C. Delegations
Mr. Hart called for Delegations.

Matthew Fleischer, 38 White Birch Circle said that he is a member of the Sportsman’s Club and they
are part of this deal. Their part is a cash offer and he hopes that they will pass this and get it done.

Marvin Schutt, 39 North Edgewood Rd. said that he is part of the group that is forming to manage the
Samuel Smith House property.

D. Reports
♦ First Selectman
Mr. Formica said that he would report under New Business as his report is for the one item.

E. New Business
a. Discussion – Acquisition of Samuel Smith House
Mr. Hart asked Mr. Formica to make the presentation on this.

Mr. Formica said that this is something that has been hovering around for some time now and the
number of people interested in this property has grown. The parcel has historical significance to the
community as it has the oldest historical house on it and it has been the hope of Steve & Carol Huber
that it would be preserved. He passed out two smaller maps and explained the modification of the lot
line to allow for the affordable purchase of the property. It has been proposed for two parcels - the 8.1
acre parcel first lot and the 9.5 acre parcel second lot that is the subject of the agreement with the
Sportsman’s Club to purchase it for $125,000. The Sportsman’s Club is interested in the parcel as it
buffers their shooting ranges. They will allow and provide for the use of it for a nominal fee/lease (such
as $1). The property will operate like the Lee House as a 501c3 and will have the same arrangement as
the Lee House and will be maintained on its own. The purchase price is $425,000 minus the $125,000
from the Sportsman’s Club leaving $300,000. There is also a Historic Preservation Grant available for
up to one-half of the $300,000 price (potentially $150,000). They will know about the Historic
Preservation Grant award by January 15, 2013 and they have made arrangements with the Huber’s and
the Sportsman’s Club to close in March of 2013 as they have to wait on the grant. If they do not get the
grant, it could be $300,000 however they hope to get something in the grant award. He further said that
they had discussed bonding however there is approximately $1M in the CNRE fund with $700,000
earmarked and about $300,000 of available funding from savings on previous items that were saved
for. The suggestion was made to use this rather that to bond. If they get the grant – whatever they get
would go back into this fund. He acknowledged that there was some skepticism however it seems that
this is a home that needs to be preserved and the Sportsman’s Club participation makes it a win-win
situation. Once they find out what they get from the grant in January of 2013 they will be able to send it
to the Attorney General’s Office for review. Currently an attorney is drawing up both options. This item
will go to Town Meeting for approval. The Town Meeting would be held on October 3, 2012 at 7 PM. He
said that the urgency lies in the fact that they have to move this along in order to get the grant
application in by the October 26, 2012 deadline. This is also why they have opted not to go to
referendum with this. He said that he would be happy to answer any questions they might have.

Mr. Kelley said that he had heard while watching the Selectman’s meeting on Channel 22 that a
contractor had said that it would be about $30,000 to bring the house up to snuff. He asked where that
money would come from.
Mr. Formica said that the house was built in 1685 and that it would be their issue as a 501c3 to find the
means to do the work.

Mr. Hart asked if they had received any other appraisals on this property.
Mr. Formica said that they had and that they were greater.

Mr. Hart asked about the CNRE money and if there was any benefit to using it rather than the
contingency dollars.
Mr. Formica said if they used contingency that it would take away from what it was intended for – such
as cost over runs on severe storms, etc.

Mr. Carpenteri asked about the loss in tax dollars on the property.
Mr. Formica said it is in the $4,000 range annually. He added that the Sportsman’s Club does pay taxes
so roughly half of that amount would still be paid.

Mr. Hart asked Mr. Formica if he has been inside of the house.
Mr. Formica said that he has and that it is very ‘old.’

Mr. Kelley said that he toured the house and during the tour there were some elderly ladies who went
upstairs – which was difficult for them. He asked if the house would be outfitted with safety features
such as rails, etc. as they would like to use it for educational purposes and having people there may be
difficult under the current circumstances.
Mr. Formica said that he would think that it would have to be roped off and fixed.

Luane Lange, Chairman of the Historical Properties Commission said that the grant requires that certain
types of ADA features be installed and it also requires a historical preservation easement for 20 years.

Mr. Hart asked if they were ready to make a motion.

**MOTION (1)
Mr. Kelley moved that it be Resolved to appropriate the sum of $425,000 from the Capital Non-recurring
(CNRE) Fund 32 for the acquisition of the Samuel Smith property, said Fund to be reimbursed with the
proceeds from the sale of a portion of the property and with any grants received pertaining to said
property.
Mr. Carpenteri seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.

Mr. Hart noted that this item would be taken up at the Town Meeting on October 3, 2012 at 7 PM.

Mr. Formica thanked the Board for their consideration.
