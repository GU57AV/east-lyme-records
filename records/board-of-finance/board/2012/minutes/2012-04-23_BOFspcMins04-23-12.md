C. Discussion and Possible Action on the Capital Improvement Plan. $100,000 was
deleted from the CIP for town projects and funded through the CNRE account. Mr. Hart
reported the new amount for the Capital Improvement Plan is $796,722.

MOTION:       Mr. Harley moved to amend the Capital
