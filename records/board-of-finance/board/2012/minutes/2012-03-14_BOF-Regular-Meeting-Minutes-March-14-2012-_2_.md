Mr. Formica reported on the following:

they have a new attorney to handle contracts who is very mindful of the Town’s interests.

F. New Business
a. Presentation – Open Space Plan
Mr. Hart said that Art Carlson, Chairman of the Commission for Natural Resources would present this.

Mr. Carlson thanked them for having him and said that he would like them to think of open space in
terms of an investment in the future. He said that when he asks people about open space that they
think of green space and that there is either too much or too little. He noted the watershed areas in the
Town and how some 40+ years ago we put houses and roads over the watershed areas. The
watershed areas hold our sand and gravel aquifers. He said that the Town does not have a history of
investing in open space outside of a few areas such as McCook’s.

Ms. Picarazzi stated that we had just added the very large Darrow Pond area.

Mr. Carlson said that they wanted to minimize the future negative aspects on the water system and had
come up with attributes important to people such as having water access, passive recreation and

agricultural land. He noted that we are fortunate in that we have other blocks of land in our Town such
as Stones Ranch at around 2000 acres, Nehantic Forest at just under 2000 acres and the Yale Property
that has more than 2000 acres, all creating a 6000 acres block that will not be developed. They then
looked for large parcels of land within the aquifer system and tagged them and wrote letters to those
owners and asked that if they were going to sell their property that they consider giving the Town the
right of first refusal on it. They sent out 65 letters and noted that the land could be donated to the Town
to be kept as open space for public passive use.

Mr. Hart asked Mr. Carlson if out of the 65 letters they received any responses.
Mr. Carlson said yes – they had received six – one is for 100 acres and the owner wants to keep it;
another was for a small amount of a very few acres and was sent in error as they were looking for large
tracts of land only and another is the Samuel Smith property.

Mr. Carlson then passed out to the Board Members, copies of a survey that was done (a copy was not
submitted for the record) noting that the majority of the responses were in favor of spending money on
the purchase of open space. With this in mind, he said that they looked at what putting $500,000
towards open space does to the debt and it would only be about one-tenth of a percent increase and
would cost each taxpayer only an additional $4.08 per year.

Mr. Carlson lastly noted that they were recently granted permission to put 50 miles of trails in the
Nehantic Forest.

Mr. Hart thanked Mr. Carlson for the presentation.

b. Budget Transfer – Town Clerk Department
Mr. Hart asked Mr. Formica to explain this item.

Mr. Formica said that the Town Clerk is retiring on April 6, 2012 and that this is a necessary transfer to
pay out her vacation time. He noted that this is an elected position and that a policy established for
elected officials makes them ineligible for leave time accruals however Ms. Williams was grandfathered
due to her length of time already in service. Going forward, the next Town Clerk, just as with the current
Tax Collector will not accrue leave time. They do however; have to notify them of time that they are
taking off. He said that the next Town Clerk will come in at entry level as they will be a first time Town
Clerk. The funding for this vacation days payout is available in the Youth Services Director Salary
account due to the department re-structuring that was done this year.

Mr. Formica noted that Thursday April 5, 2012 is Ms. Williams last day of work and that day they are
inviting everyone to come and say good bye to her and wish her well. Many restaurants throughout the
Town have donated items for the event here at Town Hall.

Mr. Hart called for a motion.

**MOTION (3)
Mr. Kelley moved to approve a transfer in the amount of $6,309 from account 2-01-45-422-100-211 (YS
Director) to account 2-01-01-107-100-111 (Town Clerk) due to a retirement payment obligation.
Mr. Bengtson seconded the motion.
Vote: 6 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.


c. Presentation of 2012 – 2013 Proposed Budget & Overview by First Selectman
Mr. Formica noted the various pictures in the front of the budget book depicting important projects
within the Town. He said that Amtrak Beach would be greatly enlarged with the bridge replacement
project and that it is a one-half mile beach that when expanded will be like Rocky Neck and will require
more efforts from the Town. The completion of the bridge and beach area will be a significant event for
the Town. It is anticipated that it will bring in more traffic and that boaters will also come in for the day.
All of this will afford great economic opportunity for the community.

Ms. Picarazzi asked if Celebrate East Lyme and the OpSail event will be on the same weekend this
year.
Mr. Formica noted that the first Celebrate East Lyme event was started during Mr. Fraser’s time when
the tall ships first came here in 2000. 2012 represents the 200th anniversary of the War of 1812. The
OpSail event will be July 7 and 8 in New London and they expect close to 1M people to be coming here
for it. The ships will be here before that on July 6 so the Celebrate East Lyme event will be held on
Friday July 6, 2012 as a lead in event for the New London OpSail event. The fourth of July falls on
Wednesday so the Lions Lobsterfest and Craft Fair event will be held the weekend before. He noted
that they do have mutual aid agreements with neighboring Towns for additional police.

Mr. Formica synopsized the various changes that the Board of Selectmen had made to the budget
noting a Probate increase in shared costs, reductions to FICA/Medicare, Health Benefits/Town, Police,
Library, Parks & Recreation and Capital Outlays/Equipment. The Board of Education budget was
forwarded as approved by the Board of Ed showing their reduction of the Superintendent’s budget by
$385,875. He noted that the estimated value of a mill is just over $2M at this time.

d. Budget Presentation – Library – Department 421 – Wm. Deakyne, Library Director
Mr. Formica noted that Mr. Deakyne will be retiring as of July 1, 2012 and that it has been a joy to work
with him and that he has been with the Town for 50 years.

Mr. Deakyne, Library Director provided the members with a copy of the Library Annual Report and a
picture of the containers of books that come in each day to the library as well as the foundation formula.
He said that his budget represents a low increase as his successor will come in at a lower salary. The
salary savings is a one time event. He noted that Mr. Makiaris and Mr. Arnold were also present as well
as his successor, Mrs. Timothy and that they could answer any questions they might have.

Mr. Hart asked how many full time and part time employees they have.
Mr. Deakyne said that they have 18 part time that are regularly scheduled and 11 full time people. The
rest are hourly and they also have retired people who fill in as necessary when people are out.

Mr. Hart asked the total hours of operation per week.
Mr. Deakyne said that they are open 61 hours per week and that they shorten the Sunday hours in the
summer as the use falls off.

Mr. Kelley asked if they pay benefits for the part time people.
Mr. Deakyne said only the social security on part time employees.

Mr. Harney asked about the health insurance.
Mr. Deakyne said that the full time people are covered by it and that it comes through an arrangement
with the Town and is a segment of the Town’s Master Plan.
Mr. Formica explained that they originally thought that health insurance would increase by 9% however
it came in at 2% so an adjustment was made.

Mr. Kelley asked if the health insurance is $155,000 at the adjusted figure.
Ms. Johnson said yes.

Mr. Hart thanked Mr. Deakyne for his 50 years of service to the Town.
Mr. Deakyne said that he has loved his job.

e. Review of Projected 2012 – 2013 Revenues
Mr. Formica noted that the Governor understands the system of taxes and that if he decreases the
money that is given to the Towns that the Towns have to increase taxes and this creates more issues. It

increase as they have $36M more in value with the improvements to Camp Niantic and the regional
training center that they have built there.
