♦ Police (216) – Sgt. Bill Blanchette, Resident State Trooper
**MOTION (19)
Mr. Kelley moved to open the budget review for Item 216 – Police.
Mr. Carpenteri seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.

Mr. Formica said that Sgt. Blanchette is present this evening and thanked him for the excellent job that he
does noting that the Police Department had not been so well managed prior to his arrival. He explained that
they increased the Resident Trooper salary by $10,000 as they are billed in the arrears and that the Town
pays 70% plus other costs.

He said that he suggested increasing the department by one police officer and noted that they had
processed 11,000 pieces of evidence from the burglaries up and down the coast. The Board of Selectmen
however put off the hiring of a new officer to January. He noted that they had an officer who left and went to
Florida and that they have a new officer to replace him and they also have two new police dogs. He said that
the department is growing and the needs of it are growing. He said that Sgt. Blanchette has done an
excellent management job with personnel and the boat patrol. The larger beach at the Boardwalk (Amtrak
Beach) will require more help.

Mr. Kelley asked about line 514 – Overtime – and at what point it becomes better to increase staffing rather
than to pay out a quarter of a million dollars in overtime.
Sgt. Blanchette said that they are trying to increase staffing and that they have minimum staffing
requirements which mean that line item (514) starts out at $100,000 in the hole as the officers can work and
bank their holidays and then get paid for them at the end of the year.
Mr. Formica noted that there should be a separate line item for the contractual holiday pay versus overtime.

Mr. Hart asked about the patrol boat.
Sgt. Blanchette said that it is a 50/50 Waterford/East Lyme split and that it was purchased with a grant. It has
to be in the water 365 days on call 24/7 and can only be out for specific days for maintenance. They will have
one East Lyme and one Waterford officer working it and it should be out patrolling more. With the cost of
diesel fuel approaching $5/gallon they will pay the Town Garage out of their budgets for fuel as it will be
cheaper when purchased as fleet fuel.

Mr. Hart asked about line 412 – Part Time Clerical at $3000 and if they anticipated needing this as they have
not used much so far this year.
Sgt. Blanchette said that this is for when the Administrative Assistant is on vacation and they need to go to
others for coverage.

Mr. Hart asked about coverage and considerations with the upcoming large scale OpSail event and the large
amount of people coming into Town. He also asked about the need for another detective.
Sgt. Blanchette said that he has seven (7) Troopers committed to coming in to help during the OpSail/CEL
event on a ‘mutual aid’ agreement. He said that they would also help out New London for the event. He
explained that neighboring Towns frequently help each other out for such events. He said that he would love

but that they also have a great need for another person for traffic issues.

Mr. Hart asked if the new patrol boat is larger than the last one.
Sgt. Blanchette said that it is 27’ and substantially larger than the old one. It is aluminum, twin outboard
motors and they have a five (5) year maintenance contract on it.
