C. Delegations
Mr. Hart called for Delegations.

John Drabik, 18 Drabik Road said that the bond rating for us has dropped from double A+ to double A-
which means that we owe more than we are making. He said that he sees a lot on the Board of Ed
budget, Capital and Capital Improvements and that he wants them to reduce this budget to flat as they
have the power to do it. He asked why they are having budget deliberations before putting the budget
out to the people as he feels that if they ask the people first that they would come in with a better
budget. Also, around two years ago the Town had to have a new backhoe which cost $100,000 and
they said that the old backhoe was junk surplus equipment and that they would get around $18,000 for
it. They bought a new backhoe and he said that he has seen a backhoe on the Board of Ed property
and they recently said that they will put money into it to keep it going. He asked what the story is.

Karen Rak, 27 Black Point Road said that she wants to see a zero percent increase in spending. The
reason is the capability of the taxpayers to pay is not increasing at the rate of the Town spending. Over
an eight year period of 2001 – 2009 the per pupil spending increased by 57% and government
spending increased by 43% in a 10 year time. They need to go back and look at that percentage. She
said that she was surprised when going into the per pupil expenditures to find that in the past 10 years
grades PK thru 8 rose by 65.5% and grades 9 thru 12 rose by 75%. Also, tax freedom day is a day that
each State has where they have paid in all of the taxes for the year. In CT – May 5 is the day and once
again CT is #1 with how long it takes to pay in taxes for the year. Between the local property taxes,
State income tax and Federal taxes – we are getting hit pretty hard. With the CT individual income tax
system the capability of the taxpayer is less and less able to support government spending.

John Drabik, 18 Drabik Road said that in the proposed budget that there are plans for the new town
beach. He said that he is hesitant about that as it is not a necessity but rather a luxury. We already
have two Town beaches and the new beach needs time to naturalize with the elements. This is a bigger
budget with bigger spending and they have taken a lot of properties off the tax rolls such as Darrow
Pond and they are considering the purchase of even more and there are less people to support the tax
system.
