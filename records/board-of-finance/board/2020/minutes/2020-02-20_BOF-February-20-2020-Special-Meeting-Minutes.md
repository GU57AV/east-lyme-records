1. New Business
   .   lnformational Session with the East Lyme Library Foundation Board of Trustees along
       with a Tour of the Library Facilities

The Session and Tour commenced at 7 PM

Dave Jacobs opened the session and thanked the Board of Finance members for their
attendance at this Special Meeting. After introductions were made, Tara Borden led a tour of the
library facilities, explaining in detail the purpose of each room within the building and the
resources required for their operation and upkeep.

We heard moving testimonials regarding the impact the library programs have on the patrons.
We were also introduced to several rare collections housed in the facility, such as historical
documents and maps, of which many of us were unaware.

We then retreated to the East Lyme Room to hear about the library's recent successes with
special programs, its current operational challenges, and its vision for the future.

This informational session adjourned at 10 PM
