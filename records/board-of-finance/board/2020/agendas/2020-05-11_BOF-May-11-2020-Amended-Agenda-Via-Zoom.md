A Zoom Webinar is a live, recorded teleconferenced meeting with Panelists and Attendees.
Panelists consist of Commission Members, Staff, and invited participants. Attendees consist
of taxpayers and members of the public that would like to attend the meeting. Panelists and
attendees have different roles and abilities within the Zoom Webinar, similar to the structure
of an in-person Board of Finance Meeting.

Panelists: Board of Finance members, Staff, and invited participants will receive a unique
link e-mailed directly to them to attend the Zoom Webinar as a participating panelist.
