A. Call Special Meeting to Order
Chairman Hart called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Finance to order at 8:43 PM after the
previously scheduled Public Hearing on the Proposed FY-13/14 Budget.


B. Deliberations & Action to Send Proposed FY 13-14 Budget to Town Meeting on
    May 13, 2013
Mr. Hart said that they had completed the Public Hearing process and heard comments. He called for
discussion and asked Mr. Formica if he had any suggestions for them.

Ms. Picarazzi said that she would like to suggest that they reinstate the $16,000 to the Zoning Commission
based upon the input they heard at the public hearing.

Mr. Hart asked Mr. Formica if he had any other suggestions on areas to recoup the $16,000.

Mr. Formica suggested that they could take $500 from the First Selectman’s budget – under Ad Hoc
Committees; under the Assessors’ they would need to reinstate the $5973 that they had cut from Service
Contracts as that affects many other departments in the Town; in Building Maintenance they could reduce
line 213 – Propane by $2000; in General Govt. 114 they could reduce Legal Ads by $4000; in 120
Contingency they could reduce payroll contingency by another $2500; in Department 132 – Inland Wetlands
they could reduce the consultant by another $2000; in Department 224 – Emergency Management they
could reduce the Overtime line item by $1000 - $1500; in Public Works department 317 – they could reduce
the Tree Warden line item by $1500. This should offset the $16,000 and they still have the $5793 to make
up. He continued that they had already cut Health Insurance by $18,000 and that the Town may be able to
do an additional reduction of $17,000. They would know more once the Office of Policy & Management
comes back with other figures. He said that with approximately $500,000 being returned that they could use
more money from fund balance.

Mr. Hart asked if there would be any surplus coming back from the Board of Ed.
Mr. Hagen did not indicate that there would be any at this time.

Mr. Hart asked what the Board would like to do.

**MOTION (5)
Ms. Picarazzi moved to open department 420 – Library.
Mr. Kelley seconded the motion.
Vote: 6 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.

**MOTION (6)
Ms. Picarazzi moved to increase line 150-295 – Library budget by $10,000.
Mr. Carpenteri seconded the motion.

Ms. Picarazzi said that she saw this as middle ground as she would not want to see it closed for a full shift.

Mr. Kelley said that they are talking about reducing $15,000 out of a $1M budget and that still gives them
more money than they have for this year. He said that he would not support this.

Mr. Hart asked Ms. Timothy if she would like to comment.
Ms. Timothy, Library Director said that they would have to close by more than just one hour.

Mr. Harney asked about other options.
Ms. Timothy said that they could possibly cut some from materials if some of the money was restored.

Mr. Hart asked how many hours they are open during the week.
Ms. Timothy said 62.
Mr. Hart asked if they modified the cut to $7500 if she could work with that.
Ms. Timothy said yes.

Ms. Alberti asked why they had not looked at the possibility of reducing people during the less busy hours
instead of cutting the library hours in general. She asked that it be clarified why the only discussion revolved
around cutting the hours of operation and why they did not look into other areas to make up the cut.
Ms. Timothy said that they are stretched and during the hours that they are open they can be faced with
many things as there are a wide variety of things that everyone does while interfacing with the public. She
said that she could take some from supplies but not all of it.

Mr. Formica noted that they may also realize some savings in the Health insurance line.

Mr. Hart asked Ms. Picarazzi if she would amend her motion to $7500.

Ms. Picarazzi amended her motion to increase line 150-295 – Library budget by $7500.
Mr. Carpenteri seconded the amended motion.

Ms. Picarazzi asked about Road Reconstruction and where they stood.
Ms. Johnson said that they recently encumbered a lot of the money as the work starts now – during this
season.

Mr. Formica said that this is for the repairs to curbing and catch basins that were damaged during the storms
and that these repairs are rather extensive this year throughout the Town. He noted that the Governor has
proposed to potentially increase TAR (Town Aid Roads) and that they perhaps could cut $10,000 and they
could instead use the TAR funds to cover the difference.

Mr. Harney asked what type of effect this would have on their rating if they had to go to other means for this
or just in general used fund balance.
Ms. Johnson said that it would have an effect and that they do take a negative look on how much is used
from fund balance.
