                                   EAST LYME BOARD OF FINANCE
                                     PUBLIC HEARING MINUTES
                                  FY 2013-2014 PROPOSED BUDGET
                                     Thursday, APRIL 25th, 2013

Members In Attendance:           Raymond Hart, Chairman
                                 Steve Harney, Secretary
                                 Camille Alberti
                                 Steve Carpenteri
                                 Steve Kelley
                                 Lisa Picarazzi

Also In Attendance:              Paul Formica, First Selectman
                                 Tim Hagen, Chairman, Board of Ed
                                 James Lombardo, Superintendent of Schools

Absent:                          No One

A. Call Public Hearing to Order
Chairman Hart called this Public Hearing on the FY 2013-2014 Proposed Budget of the East Lyme Board of
Finance to order at 7:05 PM.

B. Pledge of Allegiance
The Pledge was observed.

Mr. Hart welcome everyone, introduced the members of the Board and said that they would first hear
comments from the Board of Education Chairman, Tim Hagen.

C. Comments by Board of Education Chairman
Tim Hagen, Board of Ed Chairman said that they felt that they had submitted a good budget. On Tuesday
evening at a Board of Ed meeting Dr. Lombardo walked them through the potential areas for the $650,000
cut. He said that everyone agreed that the FT Kindergarten must go forward so they would be cutting into
some of the ‘flesh’ which Dr. Lombardo will explain. He said that there are also some cost savings such as
with health insurance that will come into play. He acknowledged the great staff that they have and thanked
the community for their support in sustaining excellence in the education of the children. He thanked all of
the people who make things happen in the schools each day. He then introduced Dr. Lombardo,
Superintendent of Schools.

Dr. Lombardo, Superintendent of Schools said that the current 1.76% increase in the budget includes the FT
Kindergarten program and without it, it would have been the lowest budget increase ever. They strived to
keep the tech upgrades and maintaining the class sizes at research base levels as they are a recognized
school district and among the top 2% of school districts. He noted that the new athletic field should be ready
for use in the coming weeks. He said that as always, they put the children first and will continue to give them
the best possible education.

D. Comments by First Selectman
First Selectman, Paul Formica thanked everyone for coming. He said that there are a few items that he
would speak about. First – he said that he feels that the top 2% designation for the schools deserves a round
of applause. He also noted that in a State of CT on-line poll that Niantic is #1 and that if it remains so some
beneficial advertising would come their way.

He explained the budget process noting that it starts in December and culminates in May with the Annual
Town Meeting (May 13, 2013) where the vote on the budget is sent to Referendum (May 23, 2013). He said
that they receive approximately $8.5M from the State in several ways – the largest is the $7.1M to
Educational Cost Sharing (ECS). There is also Payment in Lieu of Taxes (PILOT) funding which is for State
properties to try to make Towns ‘whole’ and the PEQUOT funding that was $329,000; however they are not
sure of what it will be this year. He explained that the Governor proposed this year to add the $755,000 for
PILOT to the ECS and reduce the PILOT to zero – with ‘no strings’ - meaning that they could not get to that
money to use it. It does not look like this will happen however as a result of the Governor and the
appropriations budget – they are short by approximately $400,000 – but the State is still working on their
process and does not have to finish it before we need the numbers for our budget.
With regard to the car tax – while the idea is still out there – it does not look like it will happen this year. The
Town would lose $2.4M in tax revenue if that is adopted. He said that he proposed to the Board of Finance
that they put $500,000 towards the budget from fund balance as they return approximately $500,000 each
year to the general fund from the hard work of the department heads and staff. He suggested that they cap
the fund balance at 8%.

He lastly noted that the Amtrak portion of the Boardwalk is expected to be opened around the middle of
June. Our half is expected to be under construction towards the end of the summer.

E. Board of Finance Presentation of Proposed Budget for FY 2013-2014
Mr. Hart thanked everyone for coming explained the summary process that they followed: - the Board met
seven (7) times with the various departments and to review and cut some $978,607 from the budget that was
presented to them. The budget represents an overall 1.58% increase from FY 12/13 and an estimated 0.74
increase in the current property tax mill rate. This is still a ‘fluid’ number until more final numbers come in
from the State. He said that the Board of Finance will meet after tonight’s Public Hearing to further finalize
the budget that will go to a Town Meeting on May 13, 2013 and then to a referendum.

In a Power Point presentation he noted the key increases and decreases in Revenue in the FY 13/14 budget:
The Key Increases are:
     Tuition from other districts - $131,673
     Use of fund Balance - $500,000
     Building Permits - $30,000
     Town Fees - $8,000

The Key Reductions in revenue as compared to the prior year is from:
   Taxes – Current Grand List (Amended) - $319,755 less
   Fees, Transportation Subsidy, Salem Debt - $8,980 less
   Sewer Assessments - $439,006 less
   Conveyance Taxes due to economic climate - $25,000 less
   Tax Relief – Sales Tax and Room Occupancy Tax - $100,000 less
   Commercial Tipping Fees - $37,000 less
   Decrease in State Aid - $329,644 less

He said that the current budget is $62,215,721 and that the budget being submitted this evening for
comment is $63,199,865 – a 1.58% increase. He noted that people frequently ask if the cuts proposed are
proportionate and the pie chart shows that Education is 66% of the total budget with General Government at
24%; Capital 1%; Contingency 1% and Debt Service, 8%. He said that each of the department heads was
helpful in working with them on this budget. The acquisition program for vehicles spreads out the cost over
the life of the vehicle. There is also a contingency line item set aside for unexpected items. He explained that
the Fund Balance Policy for reserves of 7.9% of expenses preserves the credit rating and lowers borrowing
costs. They would like to have it at 8% and hold it at that percentage. The estimated returns from the Town
and Board of Ed for the current year allows for use of $600,000 while maintaining the 8%.
He said that they had all worked together and that economics are still lagging behind in this area of the State.
The Town Meeting on the budget will be held on Monday, May 13, 2013 at 7 PM here in the High School
Auditorium.


F. Public Discussion

Lisa Timothy, Library Director said that she would like to address the $15,000 reduction that they had made
to the library budget. She said that she was the Assistant Library Director for nine years before becoming the
