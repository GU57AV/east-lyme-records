a. Budget Reviews – FY 2013-2014
♦ Registrars (110) – Carol Marelli, Nancy Moran, Registrars
Mr. Harney called for a motion to open this budget review.
**MOTION (1)
Mr. Kelley moved to open the budget review for Item 110 – Registrars.
Ms. Picarazzi seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.

Mr. Formica noted that both Registrars were present and that the budget is the same as this last year except
for the salaries. The Referendum/Primary at $23,500 they are not sure about what would happen regarding
a special referendum/primary.

Ms. Picarazzi asked about the telephones noting that she was just asking regarding economies of scale.
Mr. Formica said that he thinks that this has to do with having to keep the lines open for polling places.
Ms. Marelli and Ms. Moran said that was true.

base pay due to the extra hours that they are working. She asked if they are salary or hourly.
Ms. Moran and Ms. Marelli said that they are salary.

Ms. Picarazzi asked why this department is in such a situation of working the extra hours.
Ms. Marelli and Ms. Moran said that it is due to more State mandates; more reporting and more mandated
hours before elections that they have to be open.

Ms. Picarazzi asked Mr. Formica about paying more.
Mr. Formica explained that part of it is due to not knowing what the revenue picture is; some of it is that it is a
State mandate and the State pushes this on the Towns to cover and there are other department heads that
are in the very same situation.

♦ General Government & Miscellaneous Benefits (114) – Paul Formica, First Selectman
**MOTION (7)
Mr. Kelley moved to open the budget review for Item 114 – General Government & Miscellaneous Benefits.
Ms. Picarazzi seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.

Mr. Formica explained that this area is a general government catch-all comprised of Personnel Services;
Legal Services; Services - Contracted Operations and Operating Expenses. He noted that with the
reorganization of the Pension system that it has been doing well. They are still in the process of getting new
numbers for Health/Dental and should know in a week or two what they are. He is expecting that the
increase in the overall budget will be a wash with the decrease in Health/Dental. They expect to be changing
companies for Town employees.

Ms. Picarazzi asked if that includes the Board of Ed.
Mr. Formica said yes.

Mr. Formica continued that under Legal Services that if the law is changed by the Legislature with respect to
the advertising of public notices in the newspaper that the necessity to post a legal notice with the entire
language of (for instance) an ordinance, etc. would be changed to just a small ad referring to a website or the
Town Clerk’s office to read/view the entire document. This would save some of the expense.
Regarding OPEB - post employment benefits – most is for the teachers and one Town employee. The
$500,000 will be addressed separately and is not in this budget.

Regarding #300 – Operating Expenses – he noted that the State has taken over control of SEAT due to a
recent large spill. SEAT is the only transportation operation where the Towns pay dues and ours is one of the
cheapest. These dues may or may not go away – the State has asked to keep them in for another year.
While there is a $55,000 overall increase in the bottom line for this department, most of that is expected to go
away with the anticipated Health reduction.

Ms. Picarazzi asked about labor negotiations.
Mr. Formica said that he would only have one contract to negotiate in the upcoming year so that has been
reduced.

**MOTION (8)
Ms. Picarazzi moved to close the budget review for Item 114 – General Government & Miscellaneous
Benefits.
Ms. Alberti seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.

♦ Contingency (120) – Paul Formica, First Selectman
**MOTION (9)
Ms. Picarazzi moved to open the budget review for Item 120 – Contingency.
Ms. Alberti seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.

Mr. Formica explained that this is a practice that they started some four to five years ago – one of these
items is #300 – Operating Contingency. They keep $145,000 in this line to make transfers out of and
whatever they do not use goes back to the general fund. Payroll changes come from line 500 under
Personnel Services and as there is only one contract up for negotiation that line item has been reduced.

Ms. Picarazzi asked how the $145,000 Contingency is different from the Fund Balance.
Mr. Formica explained that this $145,000 is in the operating budget whereas the Fund Balance usage has to
go through the various approval channels for transfer out for use. The $145,000 in this line item allows them
to keep a good hold on the use of this money.

Mr. Kelley said that they have only spent about $19,000 so far this year.
Mr. Formica said that the real value is in how the rating agencies see this and the value that it has in that
aspect to the Town. It is considered prudent fiscal management for municipalities and it keeps them from
having to go to Fund Balance for appropriations.

**MOTION (10)
Mr. Kelley moved to close the budget review for Item 120 – Contingency.
Ms. Picarazzi seconded the motion.
Vote: 5 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.
