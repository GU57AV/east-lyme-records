Note The discussion centered on the Town Clerk's budget, specifically the miscellaneous supplies line, which
increased from an actual of $2,239 in 2024 to a proposed $2,800. A potential $300 reduction was suggested if
the current year's spending hasn't significantly exceeded the budget.

 Note *Current year spending shows $1,500 spent and $158 encumbered, leaving around $250. However,
encumbrances represent anticipated future spending under blanket purchase orders, not necessarily actual
procurement to date.

 Note The Board's discussion then shifted to the line item that includes dog licenses and ballots. The potential
impact of increased early voting on the number of ballots needed was raised. It was clarified that the Town
Clerk, purchases absentee ballots, while the Registrars' office handles ballots for early voting, same-day
registration, and election day voting.

Note *The number of absentee ballots needed is uncertain due to the removal of excuse requirements for
absentee voting. Additionally, the town needs to switch ballot providers for their new voting machines, and the
cost of ballots is expected to increase with either of the two potential vendors.

increases due to early voting, and the minimum purchase requirements, there's reluctance to reduce the ballot
budget. It's also noted that the miscellaneous supplies budget appears to be on track. Therefore, the Board felt
it best that no reductions be made to either of these Town Clerk budget lines at this time.

   $1,231,860.

   consistent 5% increase across departments, he feels the library deserves some flexibility due to its past
   low increases and community value.

    Note Mr. McAllister asked the Library Director how she would implement a $26,000 budget reduction if that
   was the outcome, and Ms. Timothy offered the following response:

   (excluding e-books.)
   *If the cut necessitates further action at the start of the next year, reducing Sunday hours would likely be
   the first step to cover part of the shortfall, potentially involving half Sundays or every other Sunday.
   *The main areas for cuts are materials and operating hours, as most other expenses are contractual.

   a children's program. She acknowledged a specific population likely uses the library on Sundays but can't
