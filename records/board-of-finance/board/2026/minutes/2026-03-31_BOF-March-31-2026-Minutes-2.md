1. Call Meeting to Order

  Note Chair McCallister called the March 31st, 2026, Special Meeting of the Board of Finance to order at 5:30
  p.m., led those assembled in the Pledge of Allegiance; a quorum was present.
  Dr. McCallister delivered opening remarks before delegations. He thanked all attendees, especially members
  of the school community. He stated the Board values East Lyme's schools and the town deeply, noting East
  Lyme was ranked the number 10 school system in Connecticut. He acknowledged that success reflects the
  commitment of teachers, staff, administrators, students, and families. He congratulated Superintendent
  Newton on being named Superintendent of the Year.
  He emphasized the Board of Finance's broader responsibility for the entire town- schools, public safety,
  infrastructure, and financial stability of residents, and noted that financial stability matters more this year than
  in previous years. The Board's charge is to essentially balance competing responsibilities, not good versus
  bad, or education versus the town, but how to fairly allocate limited resources.
  He reminded attendees that all Board Members are volunteers doing their best with the information available
  and added that he looks forward to a thoughtful and respectful discussion.

    Note 1. Privatizing Fleet Maintenance- Joe surveyed the 169 municipalities via the state listserv. Response

   *Significantly raise transfer station fees (currently charging 9-10 cents for bulky waste while loader costs

   public works employees).

   budget.
   *Defer road line painting (37 miles of double yellow, stop bars, crosswalks- approximately $30,000/year, all
   contracted).
   *Defer repainting no-parking designations at crosswalks.
