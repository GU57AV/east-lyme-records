      Minutes of Board of Finance May 4th, 2026, Special Meeting - 05/04/26

Date and time:           05/04/26 5:30 PM to: 05/04/26 5:50 PM
                         Brooke Stevens, Rec Secretary (* Ms. Stevens was not in attendance), BOF Board:,
Present:                 Scott McCallister, Sandra Bullock, Lauren McNamara, Barry Sheckley, (Via Zoom),
                         Absent:, Kim Kalajainen, Richard Steel
CC:                      Kevin Gervais, Finance Director, Dan Cunningham, First Selectman
Location:                East Lyme High School & Via Zoom


Topics

1. Call Meeting to Order

   Note Chair McCallister called the Special Meeting of the Board of Finance to order at 5:00 p.m. and led those
  assembled in the Pledge of Allegiance; a quorum was present.

2. Old Business
   2-1. FY2027 Board of Finance Budget Deliberations

       Note Dr. McCallister explained that the Board has convened to address newly received state funding
      impacting the FY 2027 Board of Finance budget.

       Note He highlighted the following:
      *Over the weekend, the town learned it would receive additional Education Cost Sharing (ECS) funds and
      Mashantucket Pequot & Mohegan Fund (MPMG) grant dollars.
      *ECS funding is not discretionary revenue; it is tied to the Minimum Budget Requirement (MBR) under
      Connecticut law.
      *Any increase in ECS funding must be used for direct educational purposes, such as classroom instruction
      and student support.
      *Although ECS flows through the town’s general fund, it is functionally committed to education.
      *MPMG funding, by contrast, is flexible and may support broader town needs and help manage the tax
      burden.
      *It's the Board’s responsibility to balance legal education funding obligations with the needs of the entire
      town, and that tonight’s action ensures compliance.

       Note Mr. Gervais confirmed the town received $536,657 in new state revenue and recommended the
      Board allocate the funds to the following:
      *Account 43400 - Other Grants (Operating); this would allow the town to apply the revenue to offset the tax
      increase.

      Decision MOTION (1)
      Ms. McNamara moved to add $536,657 from the State of Connecticut to Revenue Account 43400.
      Ms. Bullock seconded the motion.
      Motion carried, 4-0-0.

       Note Mr. Gervais explained that the new mill rate is 29.49, which is an increase of 1.48 mills over last
      year’s 28.01, amounting to a 5.28% rise. He noted that earlier deliberations had placed the mill rate even
      higher at 29.68, so the final figure reflects a smaller increase than previously expected.

3. Public Comments

  Note No members of the public offered comment.

4. Board Comments

  Note Ms. Bullock said she's speaking as a new Board member and offered prepared remarks expressing
 appreciation for the public’s engagement and the Board’s work, while also stressing the need to maintain
 safety and essential services in a growing community.

  Note She highlighted concerns about communication gaps for first responders and the need for cameras in
 town facilities and cautioned that the town budget should not be reduced to compensate for the Board of
 Education budget, which already represents more than 60% of total spending.

  Note Ms. Bullock raised questions about early Chromebook and laptop use, student anxiety and one‑on‑one
 testing support, and whether pet‑therapy programs might offer a cost‑effective benefit. After reviewing the April
 28 Board of Education presentation and the Board of Finance’s deliberations, she noted that the Board
 restored $362,000 to the schools, increasing the mill rate impact from 1.24 to 1.67, and expressed concern
 that the Superintendent’s next‑day message to parents referenced a $514,000 shortfall.

  Note She emphasized that the Board of Finance did not cut staff and that future pressures stem from state
 and federal mandates, not Board actions. She requested quarterly joint meetings with the Board of Education
 to improve transparency and long‑term planning.

  Note Dr. McCallister thanked Ms. Bullock for her comments and agreed that a strong working relationship with
 the Board of Education is essential.
 He reported that he and Eric Bauman, Chair of the Board of Education, have already discussed establishing
 quarterly presentations between the two Boards.

  Note Mr. Sheckley echoed Ms. Bullock's comments and added that he was surprised that despite a 60%
 increase in funding over six years, student performance metrics presented by the Board of Education showed
 no measurable improvement. He wants more dialogue with the Board of Education regarding how funds are
 used to support student growth.

  Note Dr. McCallister agreed that continued dialogue benefits the town, the Board of Education, and students.
 He reminded everyone that the Town Meeting will be held May 11th, where the budget amount will be
 authorized to proceed to referendum at the end of May.
 He thanked Mr. Gervais, Mr. Cunningham, the Board of Selectmen, the Board of Finance, and members of the
 public for their work and participation.
