                                    EAST LYME BOARD OF FINANCE
                                      PUBLIC HEARING MINUTES
                                   FY 2011-2012 PROPOSED BUDGET
                                       Monday, APRIL 25th, 2011

Members In Attendance:           Robert Kleinhans, Chairman
                                 Steve Larcen, Secretary
                                 Steve Harney
                                 Raymond Hart
                                 Steve Kelley
                                 Lisa Picarazzi

Also In Attendance:              Paul Formica, First Selectman & Ex-Officio
                                 Tim Hagen, Chairman, Board of Ed
                                 James Lombardo, Superintendent of Schools

Absent:                          No One



A. Call Meeting to Order
Chairman Kleinhans called this Public Hearing on the FY 2011-2012 Proposed Budget of the East Lyme
Board of Finance to order at 7:05 PM.


B. Pledge of Allegiance
The Pledge was observed.

Mr. Kleinhans outlined the format of the meeting and introduced the members of the Board to the audience.

C. Board of Finance Presentation of Proposed Budget for FY 2011-2012
Mr. Kleinhans said that he would have Mr. Larcen, Secretary on the Board of Finance do the presentation.
He explained that the budget as crafted reflected a slight decrease of .05 of a mill.

Mr. Larcen thanked his colleagues on the Board of Finance for their confidence in him to make this
presentation. He also thanked the Board of Ed and Town Government officials and staff for their timeliness in
getting the information to the Board of Finance that they had requested.

He explained the summary process that they followed: - the Board met seven (7) times with the various
departments and to review and cut some $930,522 from the budget that was presented to them. The budget
represents an overall 1.22% increase from FY 10/11 and an estimated 0.24% decrease in the current
property tax mill rate which is about .05 of a mill. He said that the Board of Finance will meet after tonight’s
Public Hearing to further finalize the budget that will go to a Town Meeting on May 9, 2011 and then to a
referendum.

In a Power Point presentation he noted that they would have a net increase in revenues due to an increase
in State Aid to the Town – such as with a portion of the increased sales tax as well as a portion of the
increased hotel occupancy tax that would be returned to the Town. It is also anticipated that there will be an
increase in zoning and building permits of some $50,000 based upon current trends and a potential increase
of $63,000 from increased single stream recycling activity. He also went over the major reasons for reduced
revenues in the FY11-12 budget and the reductions that the Board of Finance had proposed from the
budget, as received from the Board of Selectmen. He noted that there is a reduction in debt service due to
April refinancing of Town debt.
He said that the current budget is $60,770,731 and that the budget being submitted this evening for
comment is $61,511,706. He noted that people frequently ask if the cuts proposed are proportionate and the
pie chart shows that Education is 67% of the total budget with General Government at 24%; Capital 1%;
Contingency 0% and Debt Service, 8%. He said that each of the department heads was helpful in working
with them on this budget. The acquisition program for vehicles spreads out the cost over the life of the
vehicle. There is also a contingency line item set aside for unexpected items. He explained that three (3)
years ago the Board of Finance adopted a policy of a 7% undesignated fund balance and that they should hit
the 7% by the end of this year and that it should allow for the use of $537,500 while achieving the 7% target.
He said that they had all worked together in these tough economic times. The Town Meeting on the budget
will be held on Monday, May 9, 2011 at 7 PM here in the High School Auditorium.

Mr. Kleinhans said that Mr. Formica, First Selectman would say a few words regarding the budget.

D. Comments by First Selectman
First Selectman, Paul Formica thanked everyone for coming; Mr. Larcen for his presentation and the Board
of finance and Board of Ed for the good working relationship they all had. He said that it was a true team
effort and thanked Mr. Giannattasio and Ms. Johnson for their leadership. He said that the referendum voting
will be on Thursday, May 19, 2011 from 8 AM to 8 PM at the East Lyme Community Center.

He said that they are all affected by this economy and thanked the department heads for holding the line on
spending. He thanked Dr. Lombardo for the good working relationship that they have and said that he look
forward to expanding the partnership to a facilities maintenance program. He noted the operating reduction
and cited the single stream recycling program. He explained that project implementation takes time and
noted the re-tooling that will take place with Public Works and the Parks & Recreation people moving to the
Field Services Building on Colton Road later this year. Care & Share will move into the Highway
Superintendent’s old office and some of the other nearby space and pay the costs to heat and cool the area
that they will be using. Other areas will be divided up for storage for many of the departments who need it.
He noted the various initiatives that will be brought forth in terms of roof and road repairs. The four Town
Probate district is in the cottage and the expenses are being shared. On the roof of that building there are
solar panels that were provided free under a federal program and for the first month or so; they used no
energy as it was supplied by the panels. There is also a new LCD flat screen in the reception area of Town
Hall that scrolls the meeting schedule and other upcoming information. It can also be used to show meetings
live on the TV screen should there be a large number of people in attendance at a meeting that overflows
into the lobby area. With thanks to Dick Morris, Fire Marshal, the Middle School has a generator through a
grant program. Streetscape III is also in progress and will provide many more parking spaces for the
downtown area. The vehicle acquisition program has enabled them to be able to replace severely outdated
vehicles and pay for them over the life of the vehicles. He said that he has also been working with the Op-
Sail Committee in New London as the tall ships will be returning in 2012 and New London has asked us to
again host them in the Bay the night before. This would be in July 2012 and he would like to coordinate the
Celebrate East Lyme event for that time. There will be a local committee and the public is invited to join it. He
thanked everyone for their support and hard work and urged them to go out and vote at the referendum.

He said that Tim Hagen, Board of Ed Chairman and Dr. Lombardo, Superintendent of Schools would speak
to them next.

E. Comments by Board of Education Chairman
Tim Hagen, Board of Ed Chairman noted the extent of the meetings that they held and acknowledged the
great staff that they have and thanked the community for their support in sustaining excellence in the
education of the children. He thanked staff, teachers, administrative personnel, custodians and all of the
people who make things happen in the schools each day. He then introduced Dr. Lombardo, Superintendent
of Schools.

Dr. Lombardo, Superintendent of Schools thanked everyone and said that he would present the high points
of the school district budget in a PowerPoint presentation. He noted that the FY 2011-2012 budget
represents a 1.78% increase over the current year. He said that they increased the repair budgets to try to
keep things up, especially with technology where they have experienced some breakdowns in computer
switches. They will be working on the $460,000 reduction to the budget that was made by the Board of
Finance and have not yet identified where it will come from. He explained that they are trying to develop a
business plan for programs and alternative education programs. They have commissioned a study which
showed that it is less costly and better for the child to be educated within the district. He noted that the new

programs that they developed with the stimulus monies for the current year were able to be absorbed into
the curriculum for the upcoming year. Future plans involve the continuation of extended day programming,
vocational programming and a new Coastal Connections program of alternative education where they hope

F. Public Discussion
Mr. Kleinhans said that the Board of Finance has held seven (7) budget meetings where they take public
comments in the beginning and again in the end of the meeting. The Board of Selectmen has also held many
budget meetings where they have taken comments. Those comments have been incorporated into this
budget. This budget also relies on the current State budget assumptions which should be passed sometime
next week.
He then called for public comments and asked that speakers state their name and address and spell their
last name if it might be unusual or subject to being misspelled.

Karen Rak, 27 Black Point Road said that as they have spoken about ‘assumptions’ with this budget – that is
her problem with it – the spending has increased and the budget is based upon assumptions. She asked that
they ask Dr. Lombardo about the 25% increase in legal fees –
Mr. Kleinhans asked Dr. Lombardo for clarification on that –
Dr. Lombardo said that it had actually decreased from FY 09-10 and that they have now increased it from
$80,000 to $100,000 as they have bargaining unit contracts and arbitration. He noted the $711,000 increase
in the Board of Ed budget with cost cuts of $930,000 and said that they need to have the taxpayer realize
these cuts.

John Drabik, 18 Drabik Road said that they are proposing a fine looking budget in terms of spending but the
revenue side is weak at best and is based on “mights.” While Malloy provides parts of the tax increases that
he is putting in place with the State, to the Town – the bottom line is that we will pay more in taxes to give
back to the State and then to ourselves in the Town. And – while the Town bills itself as a tourist Town, the
Hotel Tax will probably hurt more than it will help. The revenues are very fuzzy and this is a poor budget.
People’s fuel costs are going up as well as everything else and you are defending a budget. Also – how
many more employees is the Town hiring in this budget?
Mr. Formica said none.
Mr. Drabik said that there is a rumor that the Highway Superintendent is getting a Highway assistant. He
then asked about raises for non-union salary people and if that is in the budget.
Mr. Formica said that it is in the wage contingency and that he does not participate in that decision as he is
part of that class. That will not be decided until late fall once the committee meets.
Mr. Drabik said that last year there was a 2.75% increase and that he knows that they will say that people
deserve it.

Mr. Formica said that part of the fund balance - $537,000 is predicated on the Board of Ed returning
$250,000 from their budget this year and the Town returning $300,000.
Mr. Schwartz asked if the school was working on any pre-kindergarten programs.
Mr. Hagen said that is a good question as they have been looking at those programs.
Mr. Schwartz said that the indication from Washington is that there will be funding for those programs. He
suggested that they come up with some programs as they could probably get the funding for them.
