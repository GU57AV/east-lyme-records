C. Delegations
Mr. Kleinhans called for delegations.

Robert Gadbois, 358 Boston Post Road said that he had missed the opportunity to speak regarding
the Board of Ed at that budget presentation but wanted to ask why with the enrollment dropping in the
schools does their budget keep going up and also how come they have not taken care of their buildings
when they get the money to do it each year.

Mike Schulz, Lovers Lane thanked the Board of Finance and Board of Ed for all of their effort and
service. He said that he had a couple of things to bring up – on average each administrator will get an
increase of $3500 this year and as Mr. Gadbois has said, they have decreasing enrollment but
increasing dollars. The enrollment from Salem is also decreasing so Salem’s budget decreases but
what about East Lyme’s – this means that we get less revenue. He said that he thinks that Salem
should be hit harder if they want to use our school system and if not – then let them build their own and
see what it costs. Also, the school feasibility study is out and that cost will approach $23M and he does
no believe that the athletic complex figure is included in that $23M. Every Town employee is getting
COLA this year and that’s great; the teachers are getting a salary increase this year and that’s great –
but he does not agree with contracts that go out beyond one (1) year. He said that he remembers
health insurance being a 50-50 contribution and that people were happy to have it. It should be a 50-50
contribution now. Also, the administrators get 50% of their agreement if they opt out – which is absurd.
You have the $15M water project being bonded and a $2M sports complex and the list goes on and on
– you have a credit card with no limit and no one will be able to afford to live in this Town.

♦ First Selectman
Mr. Formica reported:
▪ That the additional parking area for downtown that is part of the Streetscape project is moving
    forward.
▪ A meeting with the Ambulance Association was held and they are coming to some accounting of
