a. Budget Deliberations

Ms. Alberti said that last year they had adopted a hybrid approach to budget deliberations. With regard to the
Board of Ed, they do not have line item control over their budget — they can make suggestions on where they
might like to see reductions but they would only be able to reduce the bottom line number. She noted that
State Senator Cathy Osten is proposing a bill that would allow Towns to make line item decisions to the
Board of Ed budget.

She said that she would first ask them for any motions that they would like to make on any line item or dept.
changes to specific budgets for any that they may feel particularly strongly about. Later on, they would also
make a general reduction on the budget where the First Selectman and Board of Ed would then determine
where the actual cuts would be made to meet the reductions.

Several factors are considered with regard to the Town when deciding how to come up with the amount of
the reduction with the last being that of what the Town can afford.

She said that she would call on each person with respect to their seniority on the Board. She called upon Ms.
Picarazzi first.

Ms. Picarazzi said that she had two line items that she wanted to cut that fall with the Fire Study that they
have been asking for. She said that she wants to keep both Fire Departments at a zero increase.

**MOTION (1)
Ms. Picarazzi moved to reduce the Niantic Fire Department proposed budget by $10,276.
Ms. Alberti seconded the motion.

Mr. Weber said that a plan is a plan until the plan changes — and until they get the study he does not think
that they should assume that there are savings that they can get.

know where they might be — she would rather make it at the bottom line level and let the First Selectman

implemented for this upcoming year.

they also will have to initially spend dollars to get dollars.
