      Note Mr. Gervais shared that Terri Dipallina, the AP clerk, has left, and Denise from the Tax Office has
     taken over the position. He added that Munis implementation for the accounting system is ongoing, with
     training scheduled for next month, and they're rolling out a revised 401 and 457 plan with no changes to
     benefits, just structural changes, on July 1st and 15th.

      Note Mr. Gervais reported some of the following:
     McCook’s Point bathroom suffered damage amounting to $7,000.
     The damage exceeds the $5,000 insurance threshold, and a claim will be filed.
     Year-end preparations and audits are ongoing.
     Finalizing the fiscal 22 audit with three remaining items for technical review.
     Fiscal 22 financial statement expected by the next regular meeting.
     Preparations for fiscal 23 audit are also underway.
     In terms of Employee Pension Plans, non-affiliated, non-union employees are in the defined contribution
     plan.
     Firefighters have transitioned to the defined contribution plan, except one who is grandfathered into the
     pension plan.
     Police and dispatch are still in the defined benefit plan.
     Fire contract negotiations are ongoing.

   5-2. Special Appropriation: $ 157,000, Eversource Reimbursement

      Note A special appropriation of $157,000 for an Eversource reimbursement was discussed.
           Eversource.pdf

      Note Mr. Gervais detailed the following:
     A couple years ago Eversource was paving three roads, Society Road, Riverview, and Roxbury.
     As part of that process, the engineering department required them to get a right-of-way permit.
     Instead of having them patch the road and having potholes and the like, the Public Works and Engineering
     Department made a deal with Eversource to release them of responsibility in exchange for a payment, in
     lieu of restoration, since we were already going to repave these roads in a couple years.
     We're now moving that, dropping it down from the balance sheet into our P&L, so we can pave those
     roads.

      Decision MOTION (6)
     Mr. Steel moved to approve a special appropriation in the amount of $157,000 to the Fiscal Year, 23-24
     Highway Road Reconstruction Account 01-30-317-200-224 to offset utility pavement restoration costs and
     forward to the Board of Finance for approval. Note this requires a town meeting.
     Mr. Birmingham seconded the motion.
     Motion carried, 4-0-0.

   5-4. Special Appropriation: $15,000 Grant from Community Foundation of Eastern CT

      Note see attachment.
           Brookside_Farm.pdf

      Note Mr. Gervais detailed how a $15,000 grant was awarded to the Brookside Farm Museum by the
     Community Foundation of Eastern Connecticut. The grant is intended to establish a home-grown national
     park to regenerate biodiversity in the community. The Brookside property will serve as a host site offering
     biodiversity training, book clubs, discussion groups, and will turn about 40 acres into a diverse ecosystem.

      Note Mr. Gervais added that the grant has a life expectancy until September 1st, 2029, and there is no
     clear estimate of the future maintenance costs once the grant runs out.

      Decision MOTION (9)

     of Eastern Connecticut.
