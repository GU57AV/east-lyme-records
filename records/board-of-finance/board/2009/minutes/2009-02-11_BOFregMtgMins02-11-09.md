 C. Delegations
 Mr. Larcen called for delegations.

 Kevin Seery, 25 Quailcrest Rd.; Chairman of the Board of Ed noted that they had supplied them with the
 updated information and that Tim Hagen is the budget committee chairman and that he will ask that he
 contacts them to speak with them regarding budget information coordination. He noted that from where
 they stand right now, that if everything continues as it is that they would end up at $69,000 to the positive.
 Individually by line items they are over in salaries due to unexpected resignations in the middle of the year
 and having to have a long-term substitute principal at Flanders School. He said that there is a freeze on
 hiring and on just about everything and that they have had a few positives in diesel fuel and heating oil. At
 this time, they will not know what the State’s reimbursement rate is until much later on in the year. They are
 going with an assumption of 75% even though it is supposed to be reimbursed at 100% after the 4X
 expenditure. They also plan on making the last payment of $197,000 for the OHIO Plan Early Retirement
 as by doing so; they will realize a savings. He also reported that they are hoping to be on-line with solar
 power in a few weeks time.

 Mr. Larcen asked about the timing of when they would be notified of what they will get from the State.
 Mr. Seery and Mr. Smotas said that they typically do not get notified until late in the year and that last year
 it was in May. They think that the State waist and looks at its pool of money before notifying them of what
 they are going to get.

 Mr. Taylor asked about the unexpected resignations.
 Mr. Seery said that Mr. Buck and another administrator retired in the middle of the year and they were due
 payment for accrued time that they had such as vacation. He noted that 64 other Towns within the State
 have so far submitted their budgets and that the budget that East Lyme Schools has submitted is lower
 than most of those.

 E. Reports
 a. First Selectman
  ♦    Update on Authorization, Issuance and Sale of not exceeding $16,000,000 Town of East Lyme
       General Obligation Refunding Bonds
  Mr. Formica said that they have a copy of the Board of Selectmen ‘refunding of $16M in bonds’ that was
  approved at a recent meeting. He said that they acted on the $5.3M and that the ratio of savings to cost is
  3:1.
  Mr. Taylor complimented Mr. Formica on following through on this as it had been discussed earlier.
  Mr. Formica said that Ms. Johnson and the bond advisor were also vigilant.

  Mr. Formica reported on these other items:
  ♦     The Governor’s initiatives have areas of cuts and some are in the Resident Trooper program. He
       said that while it is not a lot of money, it becomes equivalent to the bond re-financing savings which,
       while they are staying even – in this economy, staying even is not enough. So, he has asked the
       Charter Revision Commission to move forward in looking to add a clause that would allow them to
       change over to their own Police Department as an option should it become necessary and the
       Resident Trooper program faces more cutting as has been discussed. He said that he also asked for
       another Sergeant’s position in the budget so that they would have full-time coverage. This would
       represent a 3% increase from the top stipend and may require an entry level person coming in.
  ♦     The budget presented to the Board of Selectmen last week showed a $160,000 reduction on the
       Town side and they have since taken another $100,000 off. They also had a meeting with the Health
       Insurance people and were able to forecast a loss ratio at 32% from the previous 89% so they will not
       ask for no increase in that line item which would realize another $100,000 savings. They are also
       instituting a wellness program and are hoping that will realize more savings down the road.
  ♦     As previously stated, he asked the departments to hold back on 1.5% of their current budgets and
       with that they are expecting $200,000 to $250,000 this year to offset shortfalls.
  ♦     The grand list growth is just about 1%.
  ♦     Revenues from permits and conveyance fees are down and they are not sure of where the Governor
       stands yet.
 Mr. Larcen said that the Governor proposed a retirement program that she wants approved by the end of
 March so that she will be able to see where they stand.

 Mr. Taylor asked the total staff count for the police department.
 Mr. Formica said that for full time it is 21 and we are at 20; and for part time it is at 4 and we have 3.

 Mr. Langan said that in the Government Stimulus Package that there were monies for local education and
 asked if it would be in addition to the ECS money.
 Mr. Formica said that it is the money that is ‘at risk’ as was explained by Senator Dodd. It would also be a
 one-time ‘in addition to’ the ECS money.

 Mr. Larcen asked why the Charter bears on how we organize our Police Department.
 Mr. Formica said that it is driven by Charter and therefore he asked the Charter Revision Commission to
 research it. He noted that Ledyard is similar and that they had their Charter allow for the independent
 structure to happen. He said that he just wants something there so that, in the event that other things
 happen, they would not have to re-convene the Charter Revision Commission just to work on that one
 thing.

 Mr. Larcen asked about the PILOT TAR funds.
 Mr. Formica said that he thinks that they have not been adjusted for inflation for many, many years.

 c.   Discussion and possible action to amend A Resolution Appropriating $4,180,000 For Various
      Water System Improvements

 **MOTION (5)
 Mr. Jones moved to approve the “Resolution Amending A Resolution Appropriating $4,180,000 For Various
 Water System Improvements and Authorizing the Issuance of $4,180,000 Bonds of the Town to Meet Said
 Appropriation and Pending the Issuance thereof the making of Temporary Borrowings for such Purpose” .
 Mr. Taylor seconded the motion.

 Mr. Formica synopsized the projects that are being looked at and said that it is crucial in moving forward to
 solve the Town’s summertime water crises and that this is being repaid by the ratepayers of the water
 system. He said that the new wells will be able to help with the summertime crises and that they have a
 MOU that they have signed with the State Dept. of health regarding their concerns with solving the water
 supply problem. He noted that by the year 2040 that they expect there would be a 10M gallon per day
 water shortfall and that the projects they are undertaking now are intended to help with that. He said that
 some of our highest priorities are also high on the States’ list of priorities. Between Well 2A and Darrow
 Pond, they are hoping to pretty much mitigate the shortfall for this July. Some of the monies are also for the
 regional interconnection. He noted that the regional interconnection also allows water to flow both ways, in
 both directions and can be a source of income during our lower demand months. He said that they are also
 working with Camp Rell on some capped wells there as an option. He strongly urged that they adopt this,
 this evening as it will keep these projects moving forward.

 He said that Mr. Giannattasio and Mr. Kargl were present this evening to answer any questions they might
 have and that they also had a power point presentation ready to go should they like to see it.

 The consensus of the Board was that they did not need to see it but would like to have a hard copy of it if
 one was available.

 Mr. Kargl said that he had hard copies and would be happy to provide them with one. (Copy attached at
 end of minutes)

 Mr. Taylor said that, as they know, this was a bit of an issue to him as he was concerned with them having
 control over their water system. He said that he now realizes that this actually gives them additional control
 over their water rather than less and that he is in favor of it.

 Mr. Larcen asked how the funding works and the percentage.
 Mr. Formica said that it was 2% DWSRF funds.

 Mr. Larcen asked if there was any Federal Stimulus money available here.
 Mr. Formica said that he has pushed this forward to the Governor and Congressman for funding.

 Vote: 6 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.
