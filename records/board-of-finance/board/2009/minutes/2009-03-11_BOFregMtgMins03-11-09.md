 A. Call Meeting to Order
 Chairman Larcen called this Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Board of Finance to order at 7:00 PM. He
 noted that he had heard from Ms. Carini and that she was not feeling well and would not be present this
 evening.


 B. Pledge of Allegiance
 The Pledge was observed.


 C. Delegations
 Mr. Larcen called for delegations.

 Mike Schulz, Lovers Lane said that he had some Thank Yous to acknowledge this evening. First, to Bob
 Jones for his article in the Day regarding the 3.9% increase for the superintendent. He said that he hopes
 that they realize by now that is a small increase by comparison as the teachers at various levels get 4.5%,
 4.7% and at higher levels with a Masters’ Degree it also includes steps which mean increases of 7%, 11%
 or 16%. He said that he could show it to them as he had a copy of the contract and it is written in that
 contract. He also noted that it would be nice for the contract to be posted on the website for everyone to
 see instead of having to come and pay 50 cents per page to obtain a copy of it. It is a Town contract. He
 further said that the Board of Ed has taken to micro-managing and has become so dysfunctional that they
 have meetings to control the meetings, so to speak. He said that the Town Employees contract is also not
 available on-line however, he had purchased a copy of it and noted that a Town Highway Maintainer Level
 3 Step 5 gets a promotion that involves a 3.25% COLA and 2.5% step.
 He said that they should also understand that the waiver premium on health benefits for administrators not
 desiring health coverage states that they shall receive 50% of the Board’s cost for these premiums – and
 on the Town side, they are given $1000. On the positive side, he thanked Mr. Freeman for declining his
 4/7% raise in these difficult economic times. He also thanked Mr. Taylor for the great effort that he puts
 forth on Channel 22 explaining the budget to the people. He thanked Mr. Formica for sharpening his pencil
 and for trying to run the Town within the budget and added that this should be an expectation of the
 position no matter who is in the position. He also said that he gives credit to Dr. Smotas for saying that they
 should cut all programs that do not provide a school credit. If they would do this, they would realize nearly
 $1M in savings!
 Other increases, such as for Town non-union managerial are 3.5% across the board. For the First
 Selectman this is $3300. Add all of this up and then add another 8% for FICA and Medicare (teachers) and
 there is a considerable amount of money that is involved. He also asked why they are charging the Town of
 Salem $15,000 per student and not making any money off of this arrangement. He said that they should be
 making some money from this as if they had to build and staff their own school system; it would cost them a
 lot more. They need to get smart and tough as these are tough times.

 E. Reports
 •      Progress Report from Independent Auditor on FY 2008 Audit
 Scott Bassett from McGladrey & Pullen said that they made a lot of headway and that the Trial Balance,
 Capital fund and Enterprise Fund will be finished by 3/13/09 as Ms. Johnson has been working diligently on
 them this week. Pension and Debt Service are expected to be completed on 3/18/09 and they feel that they
 will be able to meet these targets.

 Mr. Taylor asked that they received their draft copies a few days before the meeting so that they will have
 the time to review them.

 Mr. Larcen said to Mr. Bassett that he is asked by many people if there are, and what the ramifications are
 for the Town for filing so late and asking for so many extensions.
 Mr. Bassett said that when things go beyond March 31, 2009 that the Federal Government gets involved so
 the date of the report would be a ramification and that it would be vital that they get back on track the next
 year as this could not be allowed to continue.

 Mr. Taylor said that they are preparing to meet with all of the departments on their budgets and that
 typically they would like to see the audit before they do this. As the Board of Ed budget comprises two-
 thirds of the Town budget, he said that they would like to see that if possible.
 Mr. Bassett said that it is still in progress in relation to tying the detail to the Town information.

 Mr. Langan asked how they were doing for this fiscal year with regard to the reconciliations and if they were
 still on track.
 Mr. Formica said that they have completed the reconciliation process through November 2008 and that
 they are keeping up with this. They have a 40 hour per month person working on this.

 The Board thanked Mr. Bassett for coming and updating them.

 c.    Presentation 2009/2010 Proposed Budget
 •     Review of Projected Revenues
 Mr. Formica announced that he will not accept his raise for this next fiscal year and said that he will be
 meeting with the entire department heads tomorrow regarding where they stand with the budget for the
 Town. He said that he will also be meeting with all of the union leaders to update them and where the
 budget stands and to get input from them. He additionally noted that he has coordinated supplies buying
 which should realize some savings and that he has trimmed supplies line items within departments
 accordingly. He noted that there is discussion at the State level about possibly allowing legal ads to be
 placed on websites with a blurb only in the paper. This is something that could save them money however it
 is only in discussion stage at the State level. He said that he had gone back through the budget and had
 further trimmed from the supplies, overtime and recording secretary line items and then synopsized the
 budget that was being presented to the Board of Finance for their further scrutiny.

 Mr. Larcen asked about Public Safety and the assumption on the cost of the Resident State Trooper.
 Mr. Formica said that we pay 70% of $120,000 and that the Governor is proposing that it go to 85% - if that
 is the case, he said that he thought (at this time) that they might get it from Contingency.

 Mr. Formica noted, in reviewing the projected revenues that they are only a snapshot in time and that the
 State will not be done by the time that we need to be done with our budget. The budget that he is passing
 to the Board of Finance for their review is $61,751,964.

 Mr. Larcen congratulated Mr. Formica and his team for the excellent presentation and document that he
 presented to them.

 Mr. Taylor asked about the percentage decrease (total) of the budget for General Government.
 Mr. Formica said that General Government came in at a 3.5% decrease.
 Mr. Taylor commented that they have given more than the 2.5% decrease that was requested.
