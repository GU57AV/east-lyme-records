She reported that the Board of Ed Forum that was originally scheduled for Monday, December 14, 2015 has

¢ Finance Director
Ms. Johnson noted that they had received the monthly reports in their packets and asked if they had any
questions.

Mr. Harney asked that Ms. Johnson explain the reports to the new members.
Ms. Johnson did.

Ms. Picarazzi asked about the revenue item — extra duty police and why it was on the revenue sheet.
Ms. Johnson explained that we bill developers/trades when they require police help for work that they are
doing in busy traffic areas. The cost we bill is for the people, vehicles, etc and whatever the difference is
between that and actual we post as revenue.

Ms. Hogan said that she noticed that the police boat usage was up over 50% and asked if that was
seasonal?
Ms. Johnson said yes.

Ms. Alberti asked about the timing of the sewer assessment revenue.
Ms. Johnson said that it is typically done two times per year.

Mr. Pazzaglia asked if there would be anymore revenue for beach/recreation.
Ms. Johnson said that they will take in more in May and June which are heavy months for the sale of beach
passes and programs.

Ms. Johnson reported that for November the Health Insurance claims were $480,000 but YTD is at $3.4M
when it was at $3.1M last year at this time. She said that she was hoping that the trend will be following the
November trend.

Ms. Picarazzi asked Ms. Johnson if she had any areas of concern.

Ms. Johnson said that she is watching the legal fees as they budgeted $20,000 and spent $15,000 so far.
Planning & Zoning is going through their legal money quickly. They could potentially transfer from some
other areas that have not used as much. She said that she is watching this. Another area she is watching is
the Police Overtime line item which is $20,000 over where they should be at this time.

c. NFD/FFD PT & OT Review

Ms. Johnson said that she had provided them with this information and explained that with Niantic Fire
Department the PT Firefighters will be over budget but regular overtime could possibly be under so they may
be able to meet their needs.

With the Flanders Fire Department it seems to be the same. However, they are just now reaching the half
way mark and many things could change between now and the end of the year.

Ms. Picarazzi said that this gives them a good idea of where they are but it does not give them a sense of
how to better manage this — so for her, something is still missing here with how to have better control over it.
Mr. Nickerson said that more will be forthcoming on this as he is working on how to make it more
accountable. He noted that this is a different relationship than say with the Director of Public Works who
comes in with a budget as requested. The Fire Departments are different in that they choose amongst
themselves who will lead and then the chosen leader finds that he has to do things that may typically not be
that easy for him to do.

Ms. Picarazzi asked if this could potentially be something that an outside consultant could review.
Mr. Nickerson said that maybe it could at some future point in time.

Mr. Pazzaglia asked if the overtime numbers are actual payroll numbers.
Ms. Johnson said that it is what they paid in wages and excludes the payroll taxes.

Mr. Pazzaglia asked if 10% more would be reasonable if including the payroll taxes.
Ms. Johnson said that it would be about that.

Mr. Nickerson noted that their shift hours are different and they frequently work nine hour shifts. Further,
when there is an injury that loads up on the payroll as they have to cover the time.
