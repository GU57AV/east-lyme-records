      1:::iff:Mr. Gervais reported the following:
      The wall at McCooks was cracked and after the last bad storm actually took the wall down.
      The wall collapsed and our parking lot is now being eroded with every wave.
      We've already lost 30% of the parking lot.
      Which translates into about 10 parking spaces.
      This $25,000 will be an emergency repair.
      We're going to spend 5 to 10 grand on giant concrete blocks.
      We're going to shore up where the wall was with these blocks and tie them together with steel.
      Labor will cost 10 to 15 grand.
      This is only a temporary fix to keep things from eroding further.
      The appropriation will be paid from ARPA per approval from Tracy Collins, Town Attorney.
      We have to look for a more permanent solution and the Engineering Department currently estimates a cost
      of $300,000.
      He has a call scheduled with Bond Counsel and our Financial Advisor for after the holidays.
      We can front the major project with cash and will follow the purchasing policy for this.
      We have $69,000 of unspent ARPA money.
