       Article IV Officers of the Advisory Council
              A. Election of Officers
              In odd years, the Advisory Council shall elect its Chair, Vice-Chair, Secretary, and Treasurer at the May regularly
              scheduled meeting (PURA Regulation 16-333-29).
              1. At the January meeting, the Chair will open the call for nominees.
              2. At the January meeting, March meeting, and May meeting, candidates may be nominated by a member or
              themselves.
              3.The election of officers shall be at the end of the May meeting or whenever there is a vacancy. Voting may be by
              a show of hands unless two or more members call for a secret ballot. The candidate receiving the majority of votes
              cast shall be selected. In the event no candidate has a majority, subsequent ballots shall be taken with the
              candidate receiving the fewest votes being eliminated after each subsequent ballot until a candidate receives a
              majority of the votes cast for selection. In the event the lowest vote-getter is tied, a special ballot will be cast
              between the two tied parties, and the candidate receiving the majority on this special ballot shall remain in the
              running, and all others shall be eliminated.
              4. If a secret ballot is taken, the franchise holder’s representative (if present) will tabulate the ballots and report
              the results. Otherwise, the Secretary, Treasurer, Vice-Chair or Chair (in that order) will do so.
