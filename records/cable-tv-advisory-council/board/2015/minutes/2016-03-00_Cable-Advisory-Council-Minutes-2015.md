                            November 18, 2015
The meeting was called to order by Chair Todd Pomazon at 5:34
In attendance were Todd Pomazon, Richard Gladue, Bob Vogel, Mike Meinz.
From Atlantic Broadband: Kathy Diaz, MaryJane Rickard, and Chap Hanley.

Minutes of the last meeting, September 16, were discussed and revised to
show that Mike Meinz and Bob Vogel were not only nominated and seconded,
but also (originally omitted) elected as Treasurer and Secretary
respectively. Mike Meinz moved the 9/16 minutes be approved, Richard
Gladue seconded and everyone agreed.

Since Treasurer, Brian Lynch, resigned there is a need to change the bank
account for new Treasurer Mike Meinz.

Correspondence from the Town of Waterford appointed David Campo to the
Cable Advisory with a term ending 6/30/2016.

Unfinished business: Chap Hanley contacted the Towns to let them know
that all PEG channels will be made available to all subscribers. As a
result, up to 8 additional PEG channels will be available to subscribers
with DVRs or set-top boxes. PURA will be notified of this change.

Bob Vogel remarked that the channel guide on the screen is far behind the
schedule provided by Connecticut Magazine for CPTV. A third party
provides the on-screen guide.
Chap Hanley and MaryJane Rickard provided overview of Atlantic's
Community Access facility including training, internships, and outreach.
Though much of the equipment is now capable of HD quality, the outgoing
content is not. There is a limited budget, so it is not clear what an
upgrade to HD would cost or when it might be done. Some of the existing
equipment still uses DOS. Slides shown (included in the packet mailed to
the Cable Advisory) mentioned that there will be an upgrade to Windows.

Mike Meinz asked if actual video content could be incorporated into the
PSAs. MaryJane thought it could. Mike also called for more communication
with other Public Access participants using periodic meetings. Bob Vogel
suggested creation of an email list for the same purpose.
Chip Hanley described upgrades to faster Internet speeds (up to a
gigabyte.) TIVO will be rolled out in the first quarter 2016. Email
migration will take place around February, but old Metrocast addresses
will be forwarded for a long time (TBD). No decision on personal web
pages.

The 2016 meeting schedule was included in the CA packet. Mike Meinz moved
it be approved, Richard Gladue seconded, and there was no dissent. Next
meeting will be January 20, 2016 at which, according to the By-Laws the
Chair will appoint a nominating committee and present the Annual Report
including the Treasurers Report for Advisory Council approval.

Meeting adjourned at 7:15. Motion by Richard Gladue, seconded by Mike
Meinz, and all agreed.
Respectfully   Submitted,
Bob Vogel
