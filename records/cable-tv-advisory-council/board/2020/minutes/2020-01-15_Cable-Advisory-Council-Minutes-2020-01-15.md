                              Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council

                                               Meeting Minutes
                                                   January 15, 2020

The meeting at Montville Townhall was called to order at 5:30PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Craig Merriman, Ed Berdick, Joseph Rice, James Barnie, Stephen
Marchesseault (By Phone)

Members Absent: Kathleen McCarty, Jeffrey Buchbinder

Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Chap Hanley

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made by Ed Berdick and seconded by Joseph Rice. The
motion passed with one abstentions.

Treasurer
The bank balance is $12,588.70.

Atlantic Broadband Information and Reports
   •   New Digital Access Controller at headend is installed in parallel and undergoing tests. Expect to cutover on
       January 29th. Some customers with older set-top boxes will need upgrades.
   •   Atlantic Broadband is in the process of purchasing Thames Valley Cable.

Unfinished Business
   Discussion: PSA Contest Update

   •   This year, only ads for the Advisory Council and Community Access TV will appear in the Killingly H.S.
       Quiet Corner Film Festival. Next year, there will be a Community Access TV PSA track in the Film
       Festival. The Advisory Council will sponsor the track and award prizes.
   Discussion: Town's web site

   •   No Action
   Discussion: Community TV Slide on town's Channel 22

   •   No Action

Motion to Adjourn
The meeting was adjourned at 5:57PM

Respectfully submitted,
Ed Berdick
