                               Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council
                                 Montville Town Hall – Room 203
                                         Meeting Minutes
                                                 September 16, 2020

The meeting was called to order at 5:30PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Ed Berdick, Joseph Rice,

Members Absent: Craig Merriman, Stephen Marchesseault, Patricia Lannon, James Barnie, Kathleen McCarty,
Christopher Steinbrick

Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Mary Jane Rickard

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made by Joseph Rice and seconded by Ed Berdick The
motion passed with no abstention.

Treasurer
The bank balance is $12,588.70. The annual stipend check was delivered at this meeting.

Atlantic Broadband Information and Reports
    •   Announcements
        Local senior management has been eliminated. A regional director maintains an office in New Hampshire.
    •   Customer Service Review
        Due to COVID-19, all customer service representatives are working at their homes with ABB computers.
        Productivity is an all-time high and absenteeism is at an all-time low.
    •   Annual Community Access Review and Goal Setting
        New employee Drew Wilson – lots of Community Access experience.
        In COVID era, five new shows including a show promoting the U.S. Census in multiple languages and
        story time show by an East Lyme librarian. Last year, Community Access had 2,236 hours of
        programming. This year was doing even better pre-COVID.
        Interest in streaming Community Access but unsure of platform, format or management approval.
    •   Mary Jane will forward to the Chair ABB’s response to a consumer letter about her Internet cable modem.
