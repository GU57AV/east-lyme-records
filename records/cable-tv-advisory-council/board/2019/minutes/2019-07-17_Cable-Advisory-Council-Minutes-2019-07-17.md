                              Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council

                                              Meeting Minutes
                                                    July 17, 2019

The meeting, at the Montville Townhall was called to order at 5:30PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Craig Merriman, Stephen Marchesseault, Ed Berdick, Kathleen McCarty
