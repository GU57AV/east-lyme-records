                               Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council

                                                Meeting Minutes
                                                  September 18, 2019

The meeting, at the Montville Townhall was called to order at 5:30PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Craig Merriman, Ed Berdick, Joseph Rice

Members Absent: Kathleen McCarty, Stephen Marchesseault, Jeffrey Buchbinder, James Barnie

Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Chap Hanley

Public Comments
A senior citizen from Waterford attended the meeting to speak on how the increasing cost of cable tv is a burden.

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made by Ed Berdick and seconded by Craig Merriman.
The motion passed unanimously with one abstention.

Treasurer
$106 was paid for one year of PO Box rental. The bank balance is $10,588.70.

The annual $2,000 stipend check was delivered and will be deposited this week.

Atlantic Broadband Information and Reports
            o   Approximately 500 Hitron Coda DOCSIS 3.1 cable modems were distributed for customer self-
                install to replace older DOCSIS 3.0 cable modems.
            o   The Internet enhancement project has been completed in Killingly and Putnam.

    Discussion: PSA Contest Update

    •   No update.

    •   Griswold redesigning their website. Ed Berdick will work with them to get Advisory Council page and
        Community Access page included.
    Discussion: Community TV Slide on town's Channel 22

    •   No progress.

Motion to Adjourn
The meeting was adjourned at 5:58PM

Respectfully submitted,
Ed Berdick
