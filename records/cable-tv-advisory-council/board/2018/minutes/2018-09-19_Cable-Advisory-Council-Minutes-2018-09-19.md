                               Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council

                                               Meeting Minutes
                                                  September 19, 2018

The meeting, at the Montville Town Hall, Room 102, 310 Norwich-New London Turnpike, Uncasville, was called
to order at 5:30PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Craig Merriman, Ed Berdick, John Kleinhans, Joseph Taraya

Members Absent: Monica Pomazon, Jeffrey Buchbinder, Kathleen McCarty, James Barnie, Denise Roireau,
Stephen Marchesseault

Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Kathy Diaz, Mary Jane Rickard

Public Comments
       None

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made by Craig Merriman and seconded by Michael
Meinz the motion passed unanimously with three abstentions.

Treasurer
In July, the annual PO Box rental fee of $96 was paid. The bank balance is $8,694.70.

Atlantic Broadband Information and Reports
       Kathy Diaz delivered the annual stipend check.
       Mary Jane Rickard presented the annual community access review.
       Michael Meinz reported that Frontier is now showing the EG channels. The channels on Frontier Vantage
        TV are:
        6112 East Lyme                           6115 Montville                       6118 Griswold
        6113 Waterford                           6116 Killingly                       6120 Plainfield
        6114 New London                          6117 Putnam


New Business
       Ed Berdick made a motion to elect Craig Merriman as Treasurer. John Kleinhans seconded the motion and
        it was unanimously approved.
Unfinished Business
Discussion: Scholarship Grant Process

      The members discussed a few minor modifications to the documents and the need to launch the contest
       soon.

Discussion: Advisory Council Membership

      The chair will send a letter to the chief elected officials soon.

Discussion: Town Web Sites

      Thanks to Craig Merriman’s efforts, Waterford’s web site now contains information about the Cable TV
       Advisory councils and Community Access TV.

Member Communications
      Ed Berdick is working on a PSA promoting Community Access.

Motion to Adjourn
The motion to adjourn was made by Ed Berdick and seconded Craig Merriman The meeting was adjourned at
6:12PM



Respectfully submitted,

Ed Berdick
