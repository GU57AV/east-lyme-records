                                 Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council

                                                Meeting Minutes
                                                      May 16, 2018

The meeting, at the Montville Town Hall, Room 102, 310 Norwich-New London Turnpike, Uncasville, was called
to order at 5:30PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Ed Berdick, Monica Pomazon, James Barnie, Denise Roireau, Stephen
Marchesseault, Craig Merriman

Members Absent: Jeffrey Buchbinder, Kevin Ide, John Kleinhans, Joseph Taraya, Kathleen McCarty

Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Kathy Diaz, Charles Hanley

Public Comments
A senior citizen customer from the Quaker Hill area of Waterford spoke on increases in cable tv rates and long customer
service wait times.

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made Monica Pomazon and seconded by Ed Berdick the
motion passed unanimously with four abstentions.

Treasurer
The bank balance is $8,790.70.

Atlantic Broadband Information and Reports
Charles Hanley and Kathy Diaz presented Atlantic Broadband Announcements.

       More Internet speed increases are planned.
       C-SPAN announced three StudentCam winners from East Lyme.

EG Channels 22.1 - 22.9 availability on Frontier Channel

       Mike Meinz stated that Frontier is continuing to work on getting the Atlantic Broadband
        Education/Government channels on to their system.
       Comcast has initiated contact with Atlantic Broadband to fulfill their requirement to carry the PEG
        channels in their overbuilt areas.

New Business
       No new business.

Unfinished Business
Discussion: Scholarship Grant Process

      Jeff Buchbinder submitted a proposal. Members were asked to review and provide feedback directly to Jeff
       so that we can discuss and finalize at our July meeting.

Discussion: Advisory Council Membership

      Three new members since the last meeting. One attended tonight. Mike Meinz reported that he is waiting to
       send a letter to the nine CEOs until Frontier has the EG channels available.

Discussion: Town Web Sites

      Putnam and Griswold have posted some of the information. Other towns are being approached by their
       respective members.

Discussion: Town Channel 22

      Putnam has no channel 22 connection. The Atlantic Broadband representatives were asked to look into this.

Member Communications
Members invited to attend the Killingly Film Festival on May 17.

Motion to Adjourn
The motion to adjourn was made by Stephen Marchesseault and seconded by Denise Roireau. The meeting was
adjourned at 6:12PM.



Respectfully submitted,

Ed Berdick
