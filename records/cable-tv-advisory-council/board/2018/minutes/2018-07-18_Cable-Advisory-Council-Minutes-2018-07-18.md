                                 Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council

                                             Meeting Minutes
                                                   July 18, 2018

The meeting, at the Montville Town Hall, Room 102, 310 Norwich-New London Turnpike, Uncasville, was called
to order at 5:30PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Monica Pomazon, Jeffrey Buchbinder, Kathleen McCarty, Craig Merriman

Members Absent: Ed Berdick, James Barnie, Denise Roireau, Stephen Marchesseault, Kevin Ide, John Kleinhans,
Joseph Taraya

Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Kathy Diaz, Charles Hanley

Public Comments
      None

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made by Craig Merriman and seconded by Monica
Pomazon the motion passed unanimously with two abstentions.

Treasurer
The bank balance is $8,790.70.

Atlantic Broadband Information and Reports
Charles Hanley and Kathy Diaz presented Atlantic Broadband Announcements.

      Announcement on availability of Voice-enabled Remote Control for no extra charge
      The Annual Customer Service Review covered call volume statistics, improved monitoring to prevent
       issues, improvements in scheduling service calls, improvements in on-line self-service.

EG Channels 22.1 - 22.9 availability on Frontier Channel

      Michael Meinz reported that Frontier is almost ready to begin showing the EG channels. The channels on
       Frontier Vantage TV will be:
       6112 East Lyme                           6115 Montville                        6118 Griswold
       6113 Waterford                           6116 Killingly                        6120 Plainfield
       6114 New London                          6117 Putnam


New Business
      None
Unfinished Business
Discussion: Scholarship Grant Process

      Jeff to apply suggestions received to-date and solicit additional suggestions so that documents are complete
       for the September meeting.

Discussion: Advisory Council Membership

      Letter to be sent to CEOs after Frontier implements EG channels

Discussion: Town Web Sites

      Craig Merriman has submitted request to Waterford.

Discussion: Town Channel 22

      If Putnam wants Channel 22 moved from the school to the town hall, they will need to make a formal
       request to ABB and pay the costs of doing so.

Member Communications
      Kathleen McCarty recognized ABB for their support of community activities including flags for Rope
       Ferry Road and much more.

Motion to Adjourn
The motion to adjourn was made by Kathleen McCarty and seconded by Jeff Buchbinder The meeting was
adjourned at 6:15PM



Respectfully submitted,

Michael Meinz
