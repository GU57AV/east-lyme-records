                              Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council
                                        Meeting Minutes
                                                   January 19, 2022

The meeting was called to order at 5:33PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Ed Berdick, Temosticles Valdes, Stephen Marchesseault, Jeffrey Robillard,
Catherine Steel

Members Absent: Patricia Lannon, William Garbati, James Barnie, Craig Merriman

Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Nadine Heinen

SEC-TV representative attending: Frank Facchini

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made Stephen Marchesseault and seconded by Jeffrey
Robillard The motion passed unanimously.

Treasurer
Chair Michael Meinz was reimbursed $113.71 for the WebEx subscription fee. The bank balance is $16,210.99.

Correspondence
   •   Three customers sent letters. The letters were forwarded to Breezeline for resolution.

Breezeline
   •   Company name changed from Atlantic Broadband to Breezeline.
   •   Marketing an IP-based cable television service in NH. Will be marketed in CT in the future.
   •   Nadine will investigate whether Breezeline will honor CT CGS 16-331 for their IP-based cable service.

SEC-TV
   •   Officially assumed accountability for Community Access on January 1, 2022.
   •   Continuing to work on a new location including researching if a lighting grid can be installed in the
       building at the proposed site. Frank indicated that the owner of the building was willing to consider it.
   •   Catherine Steel raised issue about poor sound on channel 24. Frank will investigate.
   •   Working on PEGPETIA application to be used to replace some of the equipment at the Waterford studio.


New Business
   •   Motion to approve 2021 Annual Report made by Ed Berdick and seconded Temosticles Valdes. Motion passed
       unopposed.
   •   Motion to change the Advisory Council name to Cable TV Advisory Council of Eastern Connecticut Report made
       by Ed Berdick and seconded by Catherine Steel Motion passed unopposed.
Advisory Council Member Communications
   •   Temosticles Valdes reported that he has seen improvement in Internet reliability. He wants to monitor it for
       a while and survey his neighbors on their experience.
   •   Ed Berdick asked to consult with Michael Meinz on Griswold’s PEGPETIA application.

Adjourn
The meeting was adjourned at 5:53PM

Respectfully submitted,
Ed Berdick
