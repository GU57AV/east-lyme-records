                              Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council
                                        Meeting Minutes
                                                    May 18, 2022

The meeting was called to order at 5:33PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Temosticles Valdes, Stephen Marchesseault, Jeffrey Robillard

Members Absent: Ed Berdick, Catherine Steel, Patricia Lannon, James Barnie, William Garbati, Craig Merriman

Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Nadine Heinen, Josh George

SEC-TV representative attending: Frank Facchini

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made Stephen Marchesseault and seconded by Jeffrey
Robillard. The motion passed 4-0-0.

Treasurer
Michael Meinz reported the bank balance is $16,210.99.

Correspondence
   •   No correspondence received

Breezeline
   •   Josh George, newly hire Technical Operations Director reporting to Nadine joined us for the first time.
   •   Nadine reported that TIVO has declared the currently deployed TIVO boxes to be End-of-Life so will not
       fix the issue where My Shows videos could not be played back on personal computers via a web browser.

SEC-TV
   •   Frank reported that he applied for $150,000 grant for the Waterford Studio and a $60,000 grant for the
       Groton studio. PURA granted $44,000 which will be used for new equipment for the Waterford studio. A
       used TriCaster from the Groton studio will be re-used in the Waterford studio.
   •   Plans for location of the new studio are still undecided. Frank expects to know more by the July meeting.


Advisory Council Member Communications
   •   Stephen Marchesseault is working to get Killingly’s PSA scroll to eliminate the use of channel 22 and
       replace with channel 193.

Adjourn
The meeting was adjourned at 5:53PM

Respectfully submitted,
Michael Meinz
