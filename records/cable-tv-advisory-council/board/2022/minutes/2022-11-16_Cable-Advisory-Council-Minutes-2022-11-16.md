                    Cable TV Advisory Council of Eastern Connecticut
                                   Meeting Minutes
                                                  November 16, 2022

The meeting was called to order at 5:30PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Temosticles Valdes, Stephen Marchesseault, Ed Berdick, Craig Merriman,
Jeffrey Robillard,

Members Absent: James Barnie, Catherine Steel, Patricia Lannon

Atlantic Broadband representative attending: None

SEC-TV representative attending: Frank Facchini

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made Edward Berdick and seconded by Temosticles
Valdes. The motion passed 4-0-1.

Treasurer
Craig Merriman reported the balance is $17,401.66.

Correspondence
   •   No correspondence received

Public Comment
   •   None

Breezeline
   •   None

SEC-TV
   •   New equipment purchased with the help of PEGPETIA grant and a reconditioned TriCaster from the
       Groton studio will be installed next week replacing the old MediaStar playback system. The TriCaster will
       have streaming capability.
   •   A rack card has been designed to publicize SEC-TV as the Community Access Provider.
   •   A proposal for developing a new SEC-TV website has been received. Need help with the funding.
   •   High probability that the studio will remain in the current building. Possible it could be moved to the front
       of the building.


New Business
   •   Motion to approve HDMI cable invoice for $52.86 made by Craig Merriman and seconded by Edward
       Berdick. Motion passed 5-0-0.

   •   Motion to approve PO Box Rental invoice for $182.00 made by Edward Berdick and seconded by
       Temosticles Valdes. Motion passed 5-0-0.

   •   State-wide Advisory Council representative
           o   Temosticles Valdes is appointed to be the representative from the Breezeline/Waterford cable TV
               region to the State-wide Video Advisory Council.
   •   Cable TV subscription alternatives
          o Tabled until next meeting
   •   Motion to approve a grant of $5,000 for SEC-TV to partially fund development of the new website made by
       Edward Berdick and seconded by Craig Merriman. Approved 5-0-1.

Old Business
   •   Killingly H.S. Quiet Corner Film Festival sponsorship
           o   Michael Meinz reported that he has been told that the film festival is going to include our Cable TV
               Advisory Council sponsorship for 2023 as was previously approved pre-COVID. The Quiet Corner
               Film Festival website has not yet been updated to solicit submissions for 2023.

Advisory Council Member Communications
   •   Documentation on Chrome/Edge browser restriction that broke viewing of TIVO My Shows videos on a
       Windows PC tells how to re-enable this capability. Michael Meinz reported that he has the information on
       how to re-enable the capability to view shows record on TIVO on a Windows PC.


Adjourn
The meeting was adjourned at 6:10PM

Respectfully submitted,
Ed Berdick
