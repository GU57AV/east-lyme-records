                   Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council
                                   Meeting Minutes
                                         July 20, 2016

The meeting was called to order by Treasurer Michael Meinz in the absence of Chairman
Todd Pomazon.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Robert Vogel, Michael Meinz, Charley Williams, Kathleen McCarty
Members Absent: Todd Pomazon, Jeffrey Buchbinder. David Campo, Grant Place, Richard
Gladue
Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Chap Hanley, Kathy Diaz.

Approval of Minutes
Charley Williams moved the 5/18/2016 minutes be approved, Robert Vogel seconded.
Motion passed.

Treasurer
Michael Meinz presented the Treasurer's Report. The balance is $6,145.52. There was no charge
for the Best Western Cristata room this evening, since we had to meet in the lobby.

New Business
Election of a new Vice President was deferred to the next meeting since no one stepped forward.

There was a discussion of donation of Advisory Council Funds that included the amount that
should be available, the process by which recipients should be identified, the scope of
requirements for eligibility. This has been done in the past, so there will be an attempt to
determine how it was previously done. Kathleen McCarty will research and present information
for discussion at a subsequent meeting.

Unfinished Business
Although Todd Pomazon sent out letters on June 1 to member Towns without
representation so far there has been no response.

Advisory Council Member Communications
Michael Meinz created a Cable Advisory web site hosted at the Town of East Lyme.
See cac.eltownhall.com. So far, only the Town of Montville has linked to it. There was
discussion of the possibility of replacing the paper mailing by enhancing the web site.
Issues for the agenda next time
Kathleen McCarty asked that a discussion on Cable Advisory grants be continued on the
agenda.

Atlantic Broadband Information and Reports
Email migration from Metrocast to Atlantic Broadband has occurred. Forwarding of
Metrocast email will not continue for long, but the cutoff date has not been announced.

For the Annual Customer Service Review, Chap presented Atlantic Broadband's Customer
Service Actions and Planning. He also discussed the plan for increased Internet speeds
including 1Gbit service.

Value customers will be able to access 9 new channels starting September 15.
MGM HD will no longer be available.

Motion to Adjourn
Michael Meinz moved to adjourn.

The next meeting of the ABB Advisory Council will be held at the usual location at
the Best Western Cristata Inn on Wednesday, September 21, 2016.

Respectfully Submitted,

Robert Vogel
