                   Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council
                                   Meeting Minutes
                                         May 18, 2016

The meeting was called to order by Chairman Todd Pomazon.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Todd Pomazon, Bob Vogel, Mike Meinz, Jeffrey Buchbinder, Charley
Williams, Richard Gladue
Members Absent: Grant Place, Kathleen McCarty, Dave Campo, Scott Garbini, David
Hersant
Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Kathy Diaz.
One member of the public also attended.

Approval of Minutes
Mike Meinz moved the March 16 minutes be approved with the amendment that Richard
Gladue was absent from that meeting, Jeff Buchbinder seconded. Motion passed.

Treasurer
Mike Meinz presented the Treasurer's Report. The balance is $6,145.52.
There was no charge for the Best Western Cristata room this evening since we had to meet
in the lobby.

New Business
Discussion: Donation of Advisory Council Funds: Mike Meinz moved to postpone this
discussion. Jeffrey Buchbinder seconded. Motion passed.

Unfinished Business
Discussion: Pending Bills: H.B. 5243 and S.B. 286 did not come up for a vote during the
legislative session.

Discussion: Advisory Council Membership: Chairman Todd Pomazon is working on a
letter to remind municipalities of advisory council vacancies. The letter will be mailed on
June 1. Thereafter, the advisory council will plan to mail a reminder letter in January of
each year. New London members (Scott Garbini and David Hersant) have not attended
meetings for a long time. Per PURA Regulation 16-333-26, they will be removed from the
member list.

Discussion: Advisory Council web site: Mike Meinz created a Cable Advisory Council
page hosted on the Town of East Lyme web site. See cac.eltownhall.com. Members were
asked to request their towns to link to this page. A slide of the 2016 Cable Advisory
meeting calendar now appears on the community access channels. A slide announcing
openings on the Cable Advisory Council now appears on the community access channels.
Bob Vogel mentioned that there is a regional calendar www.core-ect.com that could also
be used for public notice.


Advisory Council Member Communications
Chair, Todd Pomazon cannot attend the July 20, 2016, meeting.

Atlantic Broadband Information and Reports
Email migration from Metrocast to Atlantic has occurred, forwarding of Metrocast email
will continue, but the cutoff date has not been determined.
TIVO installations are proceeding rapidly.
Door-to-door sales people have been trained and deployed.
A new VOIP business phone service has been added. The E911 [extended 911] service can
determine caller’s location.

Motion to Adjourn
Mike Meinz moved to adjourn, Jeffrey Buchbinder seconded, and there was unanimous
agreement.

The next meeting of the Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council will be held at the Best
Western Cristata Inn on Wednesday, July 20, 2016.



Respectfully Submitted,

Bob Vogel
