                     Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council
                                   Meeting Minutes
                                     September 21, 2016

The meeting, at the Hyatt Place Mohegan Sun, 2049 Norwich-New London Turnpike
Uncasville, was called to order by Chairman Todd Pomazon.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Todd Pomazon, Robert Vogel, Michael Meinz, Charley Williams,
Jeffrey Buchbinder, Kevin Ide, Stephen Marchesseault
Members Absent: Kathleen McCarty, Dave Campo, Grant Place, Richard Gladue,
Michael Minarsky
Atlantic Broadband representative attending: Chap Hanley, Kathy Diaz, Mary Jane
Rickard.

Approval of Minutes
Charley Williams moved the 7/20/2016 minutes be approved, Mike Meinz seconded.
Motion passed with 4 abstentions.

Treasurer
Michael Meinz presented the Treasurer's Report. The balance is $6,145.52, which will be
reduced by $105.29 for today’s meeting room expense to $6,040.23.

New Business
We welcomed two new members: Kevin Ide and Stephen Marchesseault.

Michael Meinz is maintaining the Cable Advisory web site hosted at the Town of East Lyme
(See cac.eltownhall.com) where Cable Advisory documents are accessible to the public. There
was discussion of whether paper mailings still need to be made before each meeting. Jeffrey
Buchbinder made a motion to eliminate them. Robert Vogel seconded and was accepted by all.

Jeffrey Buchbinder was nominated for Vice President by Kevin Ide, Robert Vogel seconded and
Jeffrey was elected by acclaim.

Discussion of donation of Advisory Council. Since Kathleen was absent, this was postponed.

Michael Meinz moved to establish the 2017 Meeting Calendar, Charley Williams seconded and
the Calendar was approved. Meetings, as before, will be held on the third Wednesday of odd
numbered months at 5:30 PM. Dates are January 18, March 15, May 17, July 19, September 20,
and November 15. All will be held at the Fairfield Inn & Suites (formerly the Best Western
Cristata).
Unfinished Business
Todd Pomazon sent out letters on June 1 to member Towns without representation, so far
there has been incomplete response. Two new members were appointed from Killingly and
one new member was appointed from Griswold. There is no representation from New
London, Plainfield, Putnam or Sterling.

Advisory Council Member Communications.
Michael Meinz suggested that a notice of PEG channels and Community Access TV be
placed on each town’s website. See eltownhall.com/community/community-access-tv.

Issues for the agenda next time.
Discussion of Cable Advisory grants to be continued.

Email migration from Metrocast to Atlantic has occurred, forwarding of Metrocast email
will not continue for long, but the cutoff date has not been announced.

Atlantic Broadband Information and Reports
Chap presented Atlantic Broadband's plan for increased Internet speeds including 1Gbit
and commented on Customer Service Actions and Planning.

Mary Jane Rickard presented the Annual Community Access Review. (The Community
Access website which includes contact information and current program playback
schedules is at ctaccess.atlanticbb.com). There will be an assessment of cost to upgrade
access channels to high definition. The next producer's meeting will be held on October 10
at 6:00 PM. Slides for PSA's can be submitted as jpegs, full motion spots must be delivered
on DVDs.

Motion to Adjourn
Jeffrey Buchbinder moved to adjourn, Kevin Ide seconded, and all agreed.

The next meeting of the ABB Advisory will be held at the usual location at the
Fairfield Inn & Suites in Uncasville, formerly the Best Western Cristata on
Wednesday, November 16, 2016.

Respectfully Submitted,

Robert Vogel
