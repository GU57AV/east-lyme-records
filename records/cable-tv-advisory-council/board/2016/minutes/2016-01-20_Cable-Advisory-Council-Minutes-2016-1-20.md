                       Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council

                                     Meeting Minutes
                                        January 20, 2016

    The meeting was called to order by Chair Todd Pomazon at 5:32.

Roll Call of Members
    Members Attending: Todd Pomazon, Richard Gladue, Bob Vogel, Mike Meinz.
    Kathleen McCarty and Jeffrey Buchbinder.
    Members Absent: Dave Campo, Scott Garbini, David Hersant, Peggy Jackson, Debra
    McGlinton, Grant Place, Charley Williams.
    Atlantic Broadband representatives attending: Kathy Diaz, and Chap Hanley.

Approval of Minutes
    Mike Meinz moved the 11/18 minutes be approved, Richard Gladue seconded and everyone
    agreed.

Treasurer's Report
    Mike Meinz presented the Treasurer's Report. The 12/31/2015 balance is $7,452.28.

Report of Officers

    Motion was made by Mike Meinz to change the name of the Council to the Atlantic
    Broadband Advisory Council, seconded by Bob Vogel and all approved.

    Mike Meinz moved to accept the Annual Report, Bob Vogel seconded and approval was
    unanimous.

    The next meeting of the ABB Advisory (3/16) will be held at the Waterford office on
    Myrock Avenue so that Chap can demonstrate new offerings including TIVO. So moved by Bob
    Vogel and seconded by Richard Gladue and all agreed.

Old Business: Issues for the agenda next time.
    There was discussion of the lack of representation or attendance from some of the member
    towns. (By-laws note that a member with three absences will be deemed to have resigned.
    PURA Regulation 16-333-26) There are vacancies. Todd Pomazon has agreed to contact the
    towns to encourage their fuller participation.

    By-laws specify that the CA have a website, but it is not clear which ISP would host it.
    Chap said that ABB will no longer offer personal web sites. The issue was not resolved.




New Business

    Kathy and Chap presented a package from Atlantic Broadband. Highlights include:
    Email migration from Metrocast to Atlantic has started, but forwarding of Metrocast
    email will continue for at least six months.

    At the next CA meeting at the Waterford office, there will be a roll-out and
    demonstration of TIVO.

    Newer higher speeds need DOCSIS 3.0 compatible Modems. Such modems may be rented from
    ABB or purchased at retailers. A link has been suggested to test modem speed, but it
    does require downloading the Flash application.

    PEG channels from all nine towns will soon be visible to everyone with a set top box.
    Grants for equipment for PEG channels were formerly available from the State, but
    funding has been redirected to General Funding and is no longer available.


Motion to Adjourn

    Mike Meinz moved to adjourn at 7:10, Kathleen McCarty seconded, and there was unanimous
    agreement.

    Respectfully Submitted,

    Bob Vogel
