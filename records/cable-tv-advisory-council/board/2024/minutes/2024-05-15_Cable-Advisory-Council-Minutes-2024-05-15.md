                     Cable TV Advisory Council of Eastern Connecticut
                                    Meeting Minutes
                                                    May 15, 2024

The meeting was called to order at 5:30PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Serg St. Felix, Temosticles Valdes, Stephen Marchesseault, Dan Cleary

Members Absent: Craig Merriman, Ed Berdick, Jeffrey Robillard

Breezeline representatives attending: Jeffrey Gold (Technical Operations Supervisor), David Hennessy
(Operations Manager)

SEC-TV representative attending: Frank Facchini

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made by Stephen Marchesseault and seconded by Serg
St. Felix. The motion passed 5-0-0.

Treasurer
The current balance is $11,969.53.

Correspondence
    •   None

Public Comment
    •   None

Breezeline
    •   Breezeline is beginning to migrate customers to Stream TV. Older equipment is starting to break down.
        Replacements are hard to get. Working on Groton region first because of amount of older equipment. Wi-Fi
        moving to OpenSync Mesh network using cable modem as main antenna.
    •   Cell phone service now available in all state served by Breezeline.

SEC-TV
    •   PEG funding bill did not pass. Governor’s stance against the bill may be the main reason. Will try again
        next session.
    •   Town of Killingly meeting videos now being replayed on SEC-TV Breezeline 24, Comcast 12 and Frontier
        6041.
New Business
   •   Breezeline and SEC-TV review of Community Access TV promotion responsibilities as specified by
       PURA Regulations and State statute
                    i. CGS 16-331 [16-331a (d)]
                         1. May infer that Breezeline promote SEC-TV on basic channels and/or the
                             Guide channel. An ad inserts capability will be available on Stream TV at
                             sometime in the future.
                         2. SEC-TV working with Breezeline on possibility of including SEC-TV’s rack
                             card in an Annual mailing to current customers.

                   ii. PURA Regulation 16-331a-5- Annual Notice
                         1. Breezeline and SEC-TV are exploring methods to include SEC-TV donation
                            information on customer bills.
                         2. SEC-TV should compose a report on community involvement in community
                            access and Breezeline could include it in Annual mailing to customers.

                  iii. PURA Regulation 16-331a-11 (e) – Promotion
                         1. Breezeline could consider promotion of SEC-TV on the Guide channel.

Old Business
   •   None

Advisory Council Member Communications
   •   Montville video project – Vendor has been selected. Conversations started.
   •   Members and the public are invited to the Quiet Corner Film Festival at Killingly High School on May 29
       at 5:30PM.

Adjourn
The meeting was adjourned at 6:19PM

Respectfully submitted,
Michael Meinz
