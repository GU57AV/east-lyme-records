                    Cable TV Advisory Council of Eastern Connecticut
                                   Meeting Minutes
                                                September 18, 2024

The meeting was called to order at 5:30PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Roll Call of Members
Members Attending: Michael Meinz, Jeffrey Robillard, Temosticles Valdes, Stephen Marchesseault, Craig
Merriman, Jason Anderson

Members Absent: Dan Cleary, Serg St. Felix, Ed Berdick

Breezeline representatives attending: Jeffrey Gold

SEC-TV representative attending: Frank Facchini

Approval of Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes of the prior meeting was made by Stephen Marchesseault and seconded by Jeffrey
Robillard. The motion passed 5-0-1.

Treasurer
Balance is $11,797.24.

Correspondence
   •   None

Public Comment
   •   None

Breezeline
   •   Jeffrey Gold is now Technical Operations Manager for Breezeline’s Connecticut business. David Hennesy
       is now Regional Director for Breezeline’s East Coast businesses.
   •   The Stream TV product and Mesh Wi-Fi products are continuing to be installed and have both been very
       successful.
   •   Breezeline is undertaking a review of all nodes in the cable system to ensure and improve quality and
       performance.

SEC-TV
   •   25 Active Community Access shows.
   •   The SEC-TV landlord is requiring SEC-TV to move. A proposal to get grant funding through a program in
       which corporations donate to non-profits in exchange for a tax rebate has been approved. Currently,
       working on searching for one or more corporations that will participate.
New Business
   •   Annual Community Access Review and Goal Setting
          o Going to continue to look for other grant opportunities.
          o Continue and increase awareness, outreach and promotion of SEC-TV Community Access.
          o Seek alternative funding sources.

Old Business
   •   None

Advisory Council Member Communications
   •   Temosticles Valdes reported that many local Community Access organizations are concerned about
       funding – varying levels of concern due to different circumstances in each municipality.

Adjourn
The meeting was adjourned at 5:58PM

Respectfully submitted,
Mike Meinz
