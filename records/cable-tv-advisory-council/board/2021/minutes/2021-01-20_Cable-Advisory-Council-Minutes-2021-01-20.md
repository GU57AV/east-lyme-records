                               Atlantic Broadband Advisory Council
                                            Via WebEx
                                         Meeting Minutes

The meeting was called to order at 5:31PM by Chair Michael Meinz.

Atlantic Broadband Information and Reports
   •   Announcements
       ABB is installing an equipment dropbox for used equipment and a bill payment dropbox at the walk-up
       counter locations. No decision on when or if the walk-up locations will be reopened.

       New product Wi-Fi My Your Way based on the Plume WiFi product. Press Release. Provides whole-house
       coverage and availability scheduling options.
   •   Customer Service Issues
       Working on additional tools for handling the call volume. Implementing new customer call management
       software soon. Hired 21 of 40 new call center employees. Tools available on ABB website to reset cable
       modems and set-top boxes. Kathleen McCarty said that she heard two voices (languages) during football
       games on CBS and Fox channels.
   •   SNY
       Number of customers watching SNY did not justify the high cost of the channel. SNY ranked 97 of 200 of
       ABB’s channels.
       ABB intends to add the YES Network on February 1.
