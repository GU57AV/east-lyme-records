G. Goeschel said back in November 2016 he responded to a Jetter he received
from T. Harris in August and when he went through the files he found that the
original wetland permit was granted on February 5, 2007, subsequently on
February 9, 2007, the Commission approved a modification and called it permit
0705M and then on March 3, 2008, they approved another modification and
called it permit 0705M2. Going through this they did hold a public hearing in
2008, and it was as T. Harris said that an application was submitted and a fee
was paid. It is G. Goeschel’s opinion that based on the original permit of
February 5, 2007, that it expired on February 5, 2016. T. Harris then responded
to that and sent correspondence to the Town Attorney who replied with his own
correspondence which reiterated the same information from G. Goeschel that
based on the original permit granted on February 5, 2007, the permit expired 9
years later on February 5, 2016. He also said the minutes clearly indicate that
the permit holder never requested that the permit be renewed.

B. 41 Walnut Hill Road, Benjamin & Dorothy Mattos Owners, Town of East Lyme
Applicant. Application to conduct regulation activities within a 100-foot upland
watercourse associated with the re-armament of an embankment to correct and prevent
further erosion.

William Scheer, Deputy Director of Public Works, is proposing a plan to fix the
embankment on this property. In 1982 we had horrendous floods in East Lyme, CT
and many bridges washed out. This is one of the bridges, and the records are sketchy.
In May of 1992, the Engineering Department at the Town Hall came up with a new
design to replace the washed out bridge on the plan in front of you. At that time they
reconfigured the brook. It moved the whole structure over 50 ft and directed the
water at the embankment shown on the picture. Big flat rocks were put on the
embankment to fix the problem, and my experience working on other projects
including the Boardwalk big flat rocks are not the right way to fix this problem.
Dorothy Mattos has been complaining to the town for many years to fix this. We
have proposed a plan to fix the problem. It should take 1-2 days. We plan on fixing
it during the summer when there is a drought and when the water is at its lowest.

MOTION (6): P. Berger motioned to accept the plans — seconded — passed (7-0-0)
Unanimous.

C. Lozanov doesn’t think a public hearing is needed. The Commission agreed
