A. Giants Neck Rd, Giants Neck Improvement Club, Inc., Owner, Coastline Consulting
& Development, LLC Applicant. Application to conduct regulated activities within a
wetlands and watercourse associated with the hydro-raking of the Lower Nehantic Pond.
(Date of Receipt: 3/13/17; Deadline to open a public hearing 5/17/17).

J. Westermeyer of Coastline Consulting & Development is proposing maintenance hydro-
raking not dredging of the Nehantic Pond. He would like to address some of the questions
asked at the last meeting. The timeframe for the hydro-raking would be September 15 —
October 31. There would be no impact to the turtles and frogs because the spawning season
occurs in early spring to early summer and hibernation season is at the end of November. If

~~ the weather doesn’t cooperate and it appears that it cannot be completed in the timeframe~

provided a second hydro-rake may be used. The other option is that if it can’t be completed
in the timeframe provided it can be done over a number of years if necessary because the
permit is good for five years. The hydro-rake will enter from the southeast corner so as to
not disturb any other areas. The question was asked by the commission if the current
violation had been resolved. The violation in question is about vegetation that was cleared
by a neighbor in 2007. The tree has not been replaced. G. Goeschel mentioned that he sent
a notice back on July 15, 2016, regarding this issue. Mr. Beaulieu said the tree would be
replaced. K. Hall asked if these current plans were in existence when the email went out in
August 2016. J. Westermeyer said they were not — but the scope has not changed. K. Hall
said there is a discrepancy in quantity on plans. J. Westermeyer said 9,881 is the correct
number and he will fix the typo and resubmit the plans to the town.

K. Hall would like to see the changes make to plans fixing the typos of cubic square yards.
K. Hall asked J. Westermeyer’s if the tree needing to be replaced could be addressed as a
stipulation to approve this permit would he be in agreement. J. Westermeyer said he would
be in agreement.
