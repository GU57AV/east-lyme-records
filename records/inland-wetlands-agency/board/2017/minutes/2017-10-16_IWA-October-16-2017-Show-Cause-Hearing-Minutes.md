EAST LYME INLAND WETLANDS AGENCY

Mary Jane Gaudio, Recording Secretary

CALL TO ORDER: C. Lozanov called the East Lyme Inland Wetlands Agency Show Cause
Hearing Meeting of October 16, 2017, to order at 6:04 p.m.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE - The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

C. Lozanov introduced the members of the Commission, Recording Secretary, and Staff.

Cease, Desist, and Correct Order - 13 Green Valley Lakes Rd; for the installation of a dock
which encroaches approximately 20-feet into a watercourse located on an abutting property, and
the clearing, grading, removal and deposition of material on the land within 100 feet of a
wetlands (upland review area) has been conducted without an Inland Wetlands Permit, at property
located at 13 Green Valley Lakes Rd, Assessor’s Map #14.0, Lot #62, East Lyme, Connecticut.

G. Goeschel spoke and said that the Chantrell’s applied for a permit today 10/16/17 for land
clearing and a portion of the dock that is on their property (15 feet).

Kristen Chantrell introduced herself and apologized for any problems this has caused and will
comply with G. Goeschel, Enforcement Agent. She explained that the dock was built in 2013 and
the land was cleared in 2011. She spoke with Docko at that time before starting any work and
was told that a permit was not needed. She is requesting from the Commission an extension to
the cease and desists because thirty days is not enough time. The Chantrell’s have inquired about
purchasing the property that the dock is on from the property owner’s (Merrick’s). At this time
there is no environmental impact and everything is stable. The Chantrell’s have hired a lawyer
and are working with Bob Russo.

G. Goeschel explained that there are two separate issues. The dock is stable and the Merrick’s
have asked that it be removed. The 30-day notice to remove the dock was sent to the Chantrell’s
in May and their lawyers responded to the notice and have not heard back from the Merrick’s.
The Chantrell’s want to purchase the property the dock is on.

P. Berger wants to sce a copy of the correspondence from the Merrick’s and the response from
the Chantrell’s. The Chantrell’s showed current pictures of the property to the Commission.

K. Chantrell asked the Commission to review the application as two separate issues: 1) the dock
and land clearing as one matter and 2) Extension of the 36-foot dock. We want to have time to be

able to purchase the property. If we can’t purchase the property we will reduce the dock to
15 feet, removing 21 feet of the 36-foot dock.

G. Goeschel said there is a deal with Bob Fusari to purchase the property and the contract deal
does not include the dock.
