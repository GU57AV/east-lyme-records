Chesterfield, LLC, Owner, c/o Pat Quinn; Clearing, grubbing, grading, and filing on

G. Goeschel said that he walked the site with Bob Russo.

B. Russo introduced himself as the soil scientist for this site and explained that the plan is
drawn, signed and stamped. He said that the erosion and sedimentation are working
properly. Matts were installed and woodchips were placed on slopes. He said the
property has been stabilized.

B. Russo explained that the owner had originally removed some waste (tires) and then
later removed vegetation not realizing he was doing anything wrong. No wetland fill was
done ~ grading only.

3. He will provide the place and phone number of where the seed will be purchased.

4. The topsoil is in good condition. There are some topsoil piles on the property that

will be used for the spring planting (April timeframe),
