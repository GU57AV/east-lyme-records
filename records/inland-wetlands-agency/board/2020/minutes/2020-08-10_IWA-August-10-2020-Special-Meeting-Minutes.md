 I. PENDING APPLICATIONS
     I. Creek Road, Giants Neck Heights Club House, and 21 Marshfield Rd, Your Brothers Keeper
         LLC, Agent for Owner for a Permit to conduct regulated activity at Creek Road and 21 Marshfield
         Rd for the clean out of a culvert entrance and exit to maintain the natural flow of water. East Lyme
         Assessor's Map 04.7, Lot 18 and 19.


       G. Goeschel read his memo dated August 6,2020. He strongly recommended the removal of
       phragmites which are highly invasive and the short term impacts associated with the work should not
       create adverse impacts. G. Goeschel stated that phragmites sometimes need an herbicide and if the
       applicant decides to use a herbicide, a licensed applicator should be used.
       The applicant has done their due diligence and they want to clear the drainage ditch to restore the
       natural flow through the ditch.
       Brian Kennedy, applicant informed the agency that a landscaper has been contacted to plant the
       recommended vegetation where the phragmites have been removed. The tarp over the pulled
       phragmites will be regularly inspected for new shoots.
       MOTION: (Upton/Schmitt) to approve the application of Creek Roado Giants Neck Heights Club
       House and2l Marshfield Rd. to clean out culvert entrance and maintain the natural flow of
       water and to mitigate the phragmites. Vote: APPROVED. In favor-Upton, Ostfeld, Koch
       Chantrello Phimister, Schmitt. Opposed-Berger. Abstaining-none.

       The agency inquired about the area by the bridge which had been cleared. G. Goeschel informed the
       agency that he has referred the matter to the zoning official.
