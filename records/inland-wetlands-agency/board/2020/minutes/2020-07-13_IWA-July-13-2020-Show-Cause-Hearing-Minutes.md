I. CEASEO DESIST & RESTORE ORDER
  Cease, Desist & Restore Order-for the outside storage of equipment, construction materials, and the
  stockpiling of other earthen materials including but not limited to yard debris, mulch, woodchips,
  gravel, topsoil and other woody debris within the 100-foot Upland Review Area of a wetlands and
  watercourse (URA) without an Inland Wetlands Permit on the property located at297 Boston Post Rd'
  East Lyme Assessor's Map 30.L, Lot 57.
  G. Goeschel issued the Cease, Desist & Restore Order for 297 Boston Post Rd on June t0,2020 and it was
  sent certified mail on June 12,2020. He received a letter from Attorney Paul Geraghty representingPazz &
  ConstructionLLC, which he read into the record, stating that the Agency failed to afford his client the
  opportunity of a hearing within the required time frame and because the agency had no jurisdiction over his
  client and they do not intend to appear for the hearing.
  G. Goeschel informed the agency that according to Town Attorney, Mark Zamarka, the Agency can reissue
  the Cease, Desist & Restore Order.
  The Agency was informed that to enact the Governors 90-day extension (Directive 7M3) they had to make a
  motion and post the motion on the towns website in order to extend the timelines to 90 days.
  MOTION: (Schmitt/Ostfeld) move as an agency to accept the Governors directive (7M) to allow the 90-
  day extension due to the COVID crisis. Vote: Approved. In Favor-Upton, Bergero Ostfeld, Chantrell,
  Phimistero Schmitt, Rhein. Opposed-none. Abstaining-Koch
  MOTION: (UptonlBerger) to reissue the Cease, Desist & Restore Order to 297 Boston Post Road
  owned by Al Smith and used by Jason Pazzaglia. Vote: Approved Unanimously.
