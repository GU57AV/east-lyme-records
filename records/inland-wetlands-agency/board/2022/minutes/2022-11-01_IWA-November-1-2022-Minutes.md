         Public Comment
         Foster, 7 Peggy Lane, Niantic asked if the water quality retention basin would be wet or dry
         and how close will it be to the water table? What native plants will be installed?

         J. Schmitz responded that the basin would be dry most of the year. There is a liner in the pond
         so the first flush would not immediately infiltrate to the ground. The ground water is
         approximately 28 ft., the bottom of the pond will be above ground water.
