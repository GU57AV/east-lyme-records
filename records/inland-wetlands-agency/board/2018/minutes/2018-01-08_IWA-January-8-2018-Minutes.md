The Tom and Kristen Chantrell of 13 Green Valley Lakes Road, East Lyme presented
their application for the landscaping activities that occurred on their property
approximately 3-years ago which included clearing for a play scape and the
establishment of a new turf lawn area. In addition, the application included
approximately 10-feet of a dock which was constructed in the existing pond. Further,
the dock encroaches approximately 21-feet onto the neighboring parcel. T. Chantrell
indicated they have written a letter to the Meric’s (the abutting land owner) to
negotiate an easement of land conveyance so they may make application for the
dock. K. Chantrell indicated they cleared approximately 0.lacres for the play scape.

Mrs. Chantrell indicated they installed the dock about 3-years ago and the beach
area was in its current condition since the 1980's.

T. Chantrell said the dock was constructed using 4-inch pvc pipe driven 6-10-feet
down, filled with concrete and rebar. Four of the pilings are on their property and
four are on the adjacent parcel. He indicated the prospective developer cannot grant
them an easement as he does not own the property.

J. Baldwin suggested the Agency give the Chantrell’s some additional time to try and
address the issue.

Motion: J. Baldwin moved to approve the application for the after-the-fact
landscaping activities only.
Seconded by: Peter Derosa

Discussion: The Agency briefly discussed the fact that they did not include the dock
in their approval and that they would work with the Respondent by giving them
additional time to try and negotiate with the abutting land owner.
