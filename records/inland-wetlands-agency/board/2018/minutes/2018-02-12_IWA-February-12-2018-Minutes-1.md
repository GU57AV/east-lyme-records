C. Enforcement
1. Cease, Desist and Restore Order; 13 Green Valley Lakes Rd; Thomas & Kristen
Chantrell, Owner; Installation of a dock which encroaches approximately 20-feet
into a watercourse located on an abutting property and the clearing, grading,
removal and deposition of material on the land within 100 feet of a watercourse
without an Inland Wetlands Permit.

The respondents, Kristen and Tom Chantrell, gave an update to the Agency. They
indicated they sent a second certified letter to the Meric’s the last week which,
they know the finally received as it was signed for. Mrs. Chantrell indicated they
were questioning the property boundary as well as any ownership rights or
warfing rights they might have. She indicated they have few options; they may
obtain permission from the Meric’s or Fusari but due to the ongoing litigation it
appears unlikely that will happen. As such, they are looking into their warfing
rights and possible adverse possession. Therefore they requested an extension
of time until more definitive plans are in place for the Twin Valley application.
