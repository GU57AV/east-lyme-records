C. Enforcement
1. Cease, Desist and Restore Order; 13 Green Valley Lakes Rd; Thomas & Kristen
Chantrell, Owner; Installation of a dock which encroaches approximately 20-feet
into a watercourse located on an abutting property and the clearing, grading,
removal and deposition of material on the land within 100 feet of a watercourse
without an Inland Wetlands Permit.

The Agency received additional correspondence from the Meric’s, the abutting
land owner on whose property the dock encroaches, indicating that this is there
second request of the Chantrell’s to remove the dock immediately.

G. Goéschel indicated the Agency granted the Chantrell’s 90-days to resolve the
matter at their last regularly scheduled meeting in an effort to work with them in
resolving the matter. However, being that there are approximately 60-days
before the expiration of the 90-day grace period and upon receipt of the Meric’s
second letter, the Agency advised Mr. Goeschel to recommend the Chantrell’s
begin preparing a plan for the removal of the dock per Schedule A of the Cease
and Desist Order in an effort to have a plan the Agency finds acceptable and may
approve at the end of 90-day grace period should the Chantrell’s be unsuccessful
in negotiating a deal with either the Meric’s or the prospective developer.

2. 115 Roxbury Road — Discussion ensued regarding the stockpiling of material at
the subject property. G. Goeschel indicated he has been in contact with Mel
Weiss of Weiss Constrcution and was scheduled to meet with him on March 29"
to discuss the matter further. G. Goeschel also indicated the Mr. Weiss
submitted a plan depicting the location of the wetlands relative to the stockpile
of soil on-site which, were located approximately 120 +/- feet west of the
stockpile. Unfortunately, the plan was not stamped and signed by either the
Land Surveyor or Soil Scientist.
