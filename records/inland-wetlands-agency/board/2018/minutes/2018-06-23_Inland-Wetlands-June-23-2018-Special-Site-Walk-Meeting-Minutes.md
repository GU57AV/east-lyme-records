CALL TO ORDER:

Gary Upton called the Inland Wetlands Agency Special Site Walk Meeting of June 23, 2018 to
order at 9:04 a.m.

A. Twin Valley 23-Lot CDD Re-Subdivision, Green Valley Lakes Road and Spring Rock Road;
Frank & Rajko Maric Owners, Real Estate Service of CT, Inc., c/o Bob Fusari Jr., Applicant;
Application to conduct regulated activities within the 100-foot upland review area from
wetlands and watercourses associated with the construction of a road.

Members, Neighbors and the Applicant met on site and conducted a walk of the property. The
walk was led by the applicant Robert Fusari who led the group along the centerline of the proposed
road stopping at station stakes 7, 9, 11, 14, 15, 17, 18, and 19. Members also visited Test Pits 893,
982, 918, and 919. In addition, the group observed the portion of the Fourmile River behind
proposed lots 11, 12, and 13. Further, the group observed the wetlands adjacent to the proposed
emergency access drive from the intersection of Spring Rock Road and Green Valley Lakes Road.
Moreover, the group walked the group walked to lot number 25 observed an existing historic
cemetery on an adjacent property at 9 Green Valley Lakes Road as well as the onsite wetlands.
