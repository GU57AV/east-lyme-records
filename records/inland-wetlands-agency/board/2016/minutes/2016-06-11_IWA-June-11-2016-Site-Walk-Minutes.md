EAST LYME INLAND WETLANDS AGENCY
SITE WALK MINUTES
Saturday, JUNE 11, 2016

The East Lyme Inland Wetlands Agency conducted a site walk, on Saturday, June 11, 2016, commencing
at 9:00 AM.

PRESENT: Cheryl Lozanov, Chair; Chuck Reluga, Vice-Chair; Keith Hall, Secretary; Phyllis Berger,
Norm Bender, Gary Goeschel/sons

33 Great Wight Way — Niantic

The group met at the Town Hall lot at 9 AM and proceeded to the site arriving approximately 9:20 AM.
At the site, the group oriented themselves with the site plan. A few attendees walked toward the

westernmost boundary of the site before vegetation became too thick. The entire group then walked
along the road pausing to identify the two planned driveways onto the site and to note the wetland flags.

The site walk was then adjourned at 9:52 AM.

Respectfully submitted, FLED

Keith Hall, Secretary Tone? 20 | Lea
