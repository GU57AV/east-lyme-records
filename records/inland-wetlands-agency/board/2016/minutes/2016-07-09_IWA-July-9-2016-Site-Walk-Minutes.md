EAST LYME INLAND WETLANDS AGENCY
SITE WALK MINUTES
Saturday, JULY 9, 2016

The East Lyme Inland Wetlands Agency conducted a site walk, on Saturday, July 11, 2016, commencing
at 9:00 AM.

PRESENT: Cheryl Lozanov, Chair; Chuck Reluga, Vice-Chair; Keith Hall, Secretary; Phyllis Berger,
Norm Bender, Harry Clarke, Gary Goeschel/son, Jim Beaulieu

Mamacock Rd, Giants Neck Beach Association— Niantic

The majority of the group met at the Town Hall lot at 9 AM and proceeded to the site arriving
approximately 9:19 AM,

At the Lower Pond site, the group was joined by Harry Clarke and then oriented themselves with the site
plan. It was noted that the application seemed only to contain a diagram of the Upper Pond. A brief
discussion about the history of past visits to this location followed. Shortly thereafter, the group got back
In thelr cars and drove to parking spaces closer to the Upper Pond where we met Mr. Beaulieu. We then
walked down the access path on the east perimeter of the pond. The scope of work for the current
application was reviewed. After a few attendees walked out onto previous grassed mitigation work area
at north end of pond, the group then walked around to the west side of pond where field cutting was
proposed.

FILED
The site walk was then adjourned at 10:02 AM.
