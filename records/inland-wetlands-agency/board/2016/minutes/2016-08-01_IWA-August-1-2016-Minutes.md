= 57 Walnut Hill Road — Bond Release Request
Ms. Lozanov asked if there was any updated information.

Mr. Goeschel said that he was unable to get in touch with the site surveyor who did the original work to
obtain an estimate. He said that he would continue to pursue this. Additionally there is a plan proposed and
the onus is on the applicant to provide an ‘As-built’ survey and that has not been done. They also indicated
at their last meeting that they would like to see how well vegetated it is at the end of the second growing
season which is coming up.

Mr. Goeschel noted that there is new case law that would have allowed them to enter the property in
February to do the other work as the case law has changed.

Mr. Clarke said that his opinion is that the loam and seed is an important part of this and that without that he
could not support releasing the bond. The wood chips are only a temporary measure.
The other members expressed agreement.

Ms. Lozanov said that they would carry this over to their next meeting in September.
Mr. Goeschel noted that the applicant should request another bond release when the work is completed.

**MOTION (4)

Mr. Clarke moved not to release the bond for 57 Walnut Hill Road due to the E & S conditions not
being completed.

Mr. Bender seconded the motion.

Vote: 4-0-1. Motion passed.

Abstained: Mr. Pazzaglia
