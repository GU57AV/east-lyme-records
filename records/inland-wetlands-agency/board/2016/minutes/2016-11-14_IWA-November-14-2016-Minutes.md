VIII. REPORTS
A. Chairman's Report -

Chairwomen Lozanov reported she attended the CACIWIC conference on Saturday,
November 12" and there was very little in the way of any legislative update
regarding the regulation of inland wetlands. Also, she noted that gypsy moth
infestation in 2017 is predicted to be extreme and will have significant impact on
trees.
