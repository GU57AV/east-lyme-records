EAST LYME INLAND WETLANDS AGENCY
SITE WALK MINUTES
Saturday, MARCH 12, 2016

The East Lyme Inland Wetlands Agency conducted a site walk, on Saturday, March 12, 2016,
commencing at 9:00 AM,

PRESENT: Cheryl Lozanov, Chair; Chuck Reluga, Vice-Chair; Keith Hall, Secretary; Harry Clarke,
Norm Bender, Gary Goeschel/son, Property Owner Lance Hall

46 Scott Road ~ East Lyme
The group met at the Town Hall lot at 9 AM and proceeded to the site arriving approximately 9:20 AM.

At the site, the group met Mr. Hall, one of the property owners. We walked down a slope towards the
land cleared to ascertain proximity to wetlands. The four corners of the proposed garage were staked.
While no wetland flags were observed, it was noted that the majority of the garage fell within the 100
foot upland review area as depicted on Mr. Pfanner’s map. The group noted a section of partially buried
silt fencing before leaving the site.

The site walk was then adjourned at 9:45 AM, FILED IN EAST LYME
co

Respectfully submitted, eee:
Keith Hall, Secretary AST L
