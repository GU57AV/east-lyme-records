MEMO
TO: R. McNamara, T. Harris, M. Lombardi
FROM: Michael Klein
DATE: 4/29/2016
RE: Bialowans stream remediation

The stream remediation was completed on Wednesday, 4/27/16 by the crew from Centerplan. Don
Fortunato and John Bialowans observed the work. The site conditions were very similar to those
observed in 2015, which indicates that the completed stormwater management system at the Antares
site is functioning as designed. All seven sites identified in 2015 were addressed by removing sediment
and debris dams as specified. There was one field adjustment made from the remediation as previously
identified. An additional debris dam was removed near the confluence with Cranberry Meadow Brook,
because it was diverting the flow and a new channel was beginning to erode. The total volume of
materia! removed at any one location was typically <1 c.f, the largest was approximately 3-4 c.f. Both
Mr. Bialowans and Mr. Goeschel agreed that the work was completed as required.
