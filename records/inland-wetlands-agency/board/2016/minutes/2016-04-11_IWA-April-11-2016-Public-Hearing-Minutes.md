Mr. Harris stated there was a request for staff minutes. Written staff minutes are
not customary. The results of those discussions were the result of revisions of the plans.
Mr. Benni, the Town Engineer, addressed problems that need to be corrected. We
received revised plans and storm water report. As of April 1 Mr. Benni was satisfied that
his concerns were addressed. The revised plans met all of his comments.

Mr. Clarke asked about the storm water basin. Mr. Harris stated it was developed
and designed for the possibility of one more tie in.

Mr. Clarke stated there was discussion regarding shade along the basin. Mr.
Harris replied there are some trees in that area. Ms. Carlson added between the stream
line and the basin it has been shown on the Landscaping Plan. We responded to Judy
Rondeau's comments.

Mr. Clarke asked in the Pattagansett River how will warm water affect it? Ms.
Carlson replied we have used the Best Management Practices (BMPs). We are collecting
all of the storm water. It will be cooled prior to going into the basin. The basin will
provide for infiltration.

Mr. Clarke asked if there was an analysis for the quantitative of thermal impact?
Mr. Rabideau indicated there was no model developed or product that analyzed any
thermal pollution. He was informed it was not required. Mr. Rabideau indicated the used
Best Management Practices, stating the water is cooled prior to going into the basin and
they used the BMPs recommend by the state in the CT 2004 Stormwater Quality Manual
which are consistent with maintaining the temperature of the Pattagansett River.

Ms. Berger asked does the water from the roof also go into the basin? Ms.
Carlson replied it does. It is a white roof.
Ms. Lozanov asked how are the plants in the basin maintained? She was
informed there will be a maintenance schedule and if sediment comes in they will have to
be replenished. Ms. Lozanov asked how often will it be maintained? Ms. Carlson replied
as written in our Operational Maintenance Manual, its more often in the first year. Ms.
Lozanov asked who is responsible for the maintenance? She was informed Costco. Mr.
Lozanov asked to give this Commission contact information.

Ms. Lozanov asked how is water or soil tested to make sure there is no
contamination into the wetlands from the gas station? Ms. Carlson replied all of the
storm water is collected and goes into the storm water system. She was informed the
Water Department is requiring an upstream and downstream well. If there is any change
from the downstream sample, we will be able to know right away. We can add to that
testing in order that your concerns are addressed. Ms. Lozanov asked if there were
carcinogens that would cause toxicity or any that would get into ground water and could
they could build up. Ms. Carlson did not think so because of the maintenance required.
We will remove any sediment, oils at our central system and they have 24 hour coverage
at thier central station.

Mr. Montesaro replied we have fiberglass tanks that are warranted for 25 years
and designed for 40 years. After 30 years they can be re-certified. We will not take any
chances.

Mr. Harris stated they have submitted early on Exhibit BB. Mr. Geoschel stated
all concerns have been addressed. He referred to item 6 of Mr. Benni's memo
recommending a $68,000.00 erosion and sedimentation bond. Mr. Goeschel indicated in
discussion with Mr. Rabideau they worked up an estimate of $50,000 for vegetation
along the wetland boundary and shade trees.

Ms. Lozanov asked if there were different questions or answers that were not
addressed on March 29". Ms, Carlson replied they responded to Judy Rondeav's letter to
Mr. Goeschel, and her concerns were addressed.

Ms. Lozanov asked for clarification of the Storm Water Management Plan. Mr.
Harris replied this is activity in the upland.

Ms. Lozanov asked if there were any other comments from the public? There
were none.

MOTION (1): Mr. Bender moved to close the Public Hearing at 8:43 p.m. Seconded
by Ms. Berger. (6-0) Unanimous.
