       EAST LYME INLAND WETLANDS AGENCY
       Show Cause Hearing Special Meeting, July 8, 2025,5:30 PM
       CHAIRMAN: Kristen Chantrell                           WEO: Gary Goeschel II
       VICE CHAIRMAN: Don Phimister                          RECORDING SECRETARY: Sue Spang
       SECRETARY Phyliss Berger

       CALL TO ORDER: 5:49
I.      ROLL CALL:
                 Present: Ryan Williams, Don Phimister, Doreen Rhein (5:53), Phylis Berger, Carlo
                 Lombardo, Alt.,
                 Absent: Kristen Chantrell, Greg Mclntire, John Vilcheck, Alt., Leonard Saltet
                 Also Present: G. Goeschel, WEO, Sue Spang, Recording Secretary, Anne Cicchiello,
                 BOS
                 The Pledge was recited. C. Lombardo was seated
     il.   SHOW CAUSE HEARING-CCEASE AND DEISIST ORDER-Siddhartha S. Kalita,
           Owner, 78 Flanders Rd., Assessor's Map #21.3, Lot #150, Niantic, CT.
           G. Goeschel issued a Cease and Desist (C&D) due to removal of vegetation and trees within
           the regulated area of the pond. Mr. Kalita has since planted four sugar maples. He has
           submitted a restoration plan that includes other plantings.
           Siddhartha Kalita, 10 Palmer Rd, Easton CT., owner, introduced himself. He said he spoke to
           his neighbor about a tree hanging over the neighbor's property. The neighbor asked if he could
           chop three trees down on Mr. Kalita's property and was told he could. There were five trees cut
           down on his property, some were diseased. He left the stumps.
           Mr. Kalita misunderstood directions given to him from G. Goeschel when told he could take
           plantings according to the plan. He admits he read the plan and the limits of clearing wrong.
           He said his plan includes two more maple trees, blue berry bushes, hydrangeas and woodchips.
           G. Goeschel recommended a $5,000 planting bond to cover two growing seasons.
           MOTION:(Rhein/Berger) to allow work to continue on site and the proposed planting
           plan at 78 Flanders Rd.o with a $5,000 planting bond to be held for two full growing
           seasons and eligible for release upon 807o survival of plantings. Vote: APPROVED
           unanimously.
     m. Adjournment
           MOTION:(Rhein/Berger) motion to adjourn. Vote: APPROVED unanimously. 6:10 .
