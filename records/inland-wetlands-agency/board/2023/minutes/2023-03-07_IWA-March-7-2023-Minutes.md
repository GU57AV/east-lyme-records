IV. SHOW CAUSE
     A. Cease and Desist Order-Monaco Ford, Respondent;2181222, & 226 Flanders Road,
         Assessor's Map # 26.1Lot# 23024r 26, Niantic Connecticut
        G. Goeschel informed the Agency that there was extensive clear cutting of the understory at the
        property and clear cutting around the basin for maintenance.
        Mike Monaco, Monaco Ford said he was clearing the debris, old barb wire and broken fencing
        on the property. There is a dilapidated building structure that he would like to eventually take
        down. There are no plans currently to do anything else to the property. M. Monaco would like to
        spread the wood chips that are on the property and seed in the Spring.
        G. Goeschel said the wood chips on the property are adequate to stabilize the soil. The
        tree canopy over the river was maintained and he suggested keeping the status of the order in
        place until the site can be inspected before the next meeting.
        MOTION: (Phimister/Sims) to take no action until the April Meeting at which the issue
        will be revisited. Vote: APPROVED unanimously.
        A site walk will be scheduled before the next meeting.

      A. Inland Wetlands and Watercourse Regulations
         G. Goeschel supplied the draft regulations for review by the Agency. He stated the fees have not
         changed in ten years but are still competitive with surrounding towns.
         The commission discussed adding examples of minor permits, such as pools and shed. They
