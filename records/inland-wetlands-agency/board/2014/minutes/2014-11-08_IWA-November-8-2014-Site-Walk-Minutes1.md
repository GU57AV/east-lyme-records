TOWN CLERK |
The East Lyme Inland Wetlands Agency conducted a site walk, on Saturday, November 8, 2014,
commencing at 9:00 AM.

PRESENT: Chuck Reluga, Vice-Chair; Keith Hall, Secretary; Harry Clarke, Phyllis Berger, Kimberly
Bradley; Gary Goeschel

26 Industrial Park Rd — East Lyme

The group met at the High School parking lot at 9 AM and proceeded to the site arriving approximately
9:15 AM.

At the site, the group walked to the back of the subject parcel. Mr. Pfanner’s site plan was reviewed to
orient members as to the limits of the upland review area with respect to the paved parking lot and the
top of slope where previous disturbance of the soil was evident.

5 Liberty Way — East Lyme
The group then made its way to the second site of the walk.

At the site, the group met Mr. Matthew White of Angus McDonald, Gary Sharpe and Associates of the
development team.

Mr. White reviewed for the group the history of the site as he had been involved in previous Conservation
Commission approval when the parcel and building were much bigger many years previous. The grouop
then walked a portion of the eastern boundary noting flags and old silt fence. Agency members were
oriented to site plan by the various telephone and electric utility cabinets/pads already existing on site.

Mr. White answered a few basic questions about the intended use for the building and whether or not the
applicant intended to have floor drains in his building.

The site walk was then adjourned at 10:10 AM.
Respectfully submitted,

Keith Hall,
Secretary
