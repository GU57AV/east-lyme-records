SITE WALK MINUTES ‘
Saturday, JULY 7, 2014 BAST EY te WN GLERK

The East Lyme Inland Wetlands Agency conducted a site walk, on Saturday, July 12, 2014, commencing
at 9:00 AM.

PRESENT: Cheryl Lozanov, Chair; Chuck Reluga, Vice-Chair; Keith Hall, Secretary; Norman Bender,
Phyllis Berger, Kimberly Bradley; Gary Goeschel

ABSENT: Joe Mingo, Harry Clarke

249 Old Black Point Road- Niantic

The group met at the Town Hall parking lot at 9 AM and proceeded to the site arriving approximately
9:20 AM.

Upon arrival, the group walked along the seawall at the perimeter of the site and briefly discussed the
phragmites removal that had been the subject of a previous application.

The limits of the inland wetland were pointed out as well as the applicant's intent to move the
underground septic system from a location close to the wetland to a spot closer to the road away from
the wetland and Long Island Sound. Septic tank relocation will be performed as part of the demolition of
existing residence and construction of a new house on the parcel.

21 Pattagansett Drive — East Lyme
The group then proceeded to the second site arriving approximately 9:45 AM.

Upon arrival, the group observed the site where the 46’ x 120’ garage had been partially cleared. A few
of the wetland flags along the abutting northeast property line were easily observable but not all.

There were some vehicles and construction equipment that appeared to have been there for some time
and which were parked within the upland review area. Other areas of the site had stockpiles of downed
trees and brush as well as woodchipping equipment. Not all clearing that will be necessary to construct
garage had been done.

Mr. Bender left the site walk at approximately 10:10 to attend to a previous appointment.
The site walk was adjourned at 10:25 AM.
Respectfully submitted,

Keith Hall,
Secretary
