= Cease, Desist & Correct Order — 20 Farm Meadow Road, East Lyme, CT - Centerplan
Construction Company and GRE 314 East Lyme, LLC, Owner — Antares Solar Field on property
located at 20 Farm Meadow Road, East Lyme, CT. Assessor’s Map 52.0, Lot #126, East Lyme, CT.

Attomey Theodore Harris, representing Centerplan recapped that they have been before this Commission

many times and that the remediation has been completed and they will see that reported on tonight. He said

that of concem to everyone has been the issue of stabilization of the site and they work that has been done

appears to be working.

James Cowen, Soil Scientist, Professional Wetland Scientist, Environmental Planning Services, LLC said that
he was before them during the permitting process and has been back since then. He said that he has made a
number of site visits and revised the plan mid-summer and since that time there has been continued re-
vegetation and growth. He noted that he was on-site four times during the month of September as the major
concer was stabilization and the removal of sediment and the re-vegetation of areas. He reviewed his report
dated October 1, 2014 (copy attached). He said that with this years’ dry conditions that it would not have
worked to plant during the month of August and that September worked out much better as they did get some
precipitation. In one major area they planted over 1000 shade tolerant plants. In total they installed over 3100
plants and utilized a custom seed mix of wet and dry that would also continue to grow in the fall.

Ms. Lozanov asked that Mr. Cowen speak to the restoration area around the solar panels where there was
erosion and gullies.

Mr. Cowen said that they have been working with Centerplan on this and they have made improvements.
The critical areas have been addressed and it has been a progressive process.

Ms. Lozanov asked about preventing future erosion and about the product that was stock piled.
Mr. Cowen said that he has been in consult with them on all of the work.
