EAST LYME INLAND WETLANDS AGENCY
SHOW CAUSE HEARING
Monday, APRIL 7th, 2014
MINUTES

The East Lyme Inland Wetlands Agency held a Show Cause Hearing on the Cease, Desist & Correct Order
issued to GRE 314 East Lyme, LLC & Centerplan Construction Co. for property located at 20 Farm Meadow
Road, Assessor's Map #52.0, Lot #126, on April 7, 2014 at Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Ave., Niantic, CT.
Chairman Lozanov opened the Show Cause Hearing and called it to order at 7:04 PM.

“EAST LYME TOWN CLERK
Call to Order

Ms. Lozanov called this Show Cause Hearing to order at 7:04 PM and introduced the members seated,
Inland Wetlands Agent and Recording Secretary.

Pledge of Allegiance
The Pledge was observed.

Show Cause Hearing

1. Cease, Desist & Correct Order Issued to GRE 2314 East Lyme, LLC, Owner of 20 Farm Meadow
Road, East Lyme, CT and Centerplan Construction Company for the pollution of an onsite inland
wetlands and watercourse. More specifically, the deposition of sedimentation within an onsite
wetlands and watercourse as a result of storm water management system failures and failures of
erosion and sedimentation controls associated with the construction of a solar field/array on
property located at 20 Farm Meadow Road, Assessor's Map #52.0, Lot #126, East Lyme, CT.

Ms. Lozanov asked Mr. Goeschel for comment.
Mr. Goeschel first explained that the Cease & Desist record has been corrected to reflect the accurate dates
and a corrected copy has been sent to Centerplan. He said that Attorney Harris was present.

Attomey Harris, place of business 351 Main Street, Niantic, CT said that this is regarding the solar farm
located on Walnut Hill & Grassy Hill Roads and is a bit different as it was approved by the CT Siting Council.
A few weeks ago on the weekend there was a significant rain storm event with the ground still frozen which
caused failure of the system. The owners were aware of it on Sunday and were on the site on Monday. At
that time, they were also interfacing with the Town Hall to let them know what had happened. They also
contacted the Siting Council and an environmental professional also visited the site. Areas will be defined for
remediation and proposed mitigation will be presented to them.

He noted that he was the one who suggested the Cease & Desist route to this Commission to move the
process along faster. He said that he feels that they will be at the next meeting of this Commission with a full
remediation proposal. The bottom line is that both Construction Managers for the project are present this
evening and they will have more detail for them at the next meeting. In the meantime, they have put
additional silt fencing in the areas and they are ready to come back with a full mitigation plan.

Ms. Lozanov asked if anything had gone onto other people's property.

Mike Lombardi, P.E. , President Centerpian Construction said that there was a small amount of sediment
that did leak to a neighboring property and that he has personally spoken with those people.

Mr. Mingo asked if the Land Trust Property was untouched and also about the pond.

Mr. Lombardi said that the Land Trust Property was not touched however the pond was affected and they
have contacted professionals who are the best in the industry to work with them on this. They are not trying
to avoid this. The event was a convergence of unusual things and they are diligently looking to come up with
a plan to mitigate it.

Mr. Mingo said that he has trouble with the boundaries and the parcel owned by the Town and asked if they
built anything on it.

Mr. Lombardi said no, they did not build on it.

Ryan McNamara, Project Manager, Centerplan Construction said that they had professionals stake out the
boundaries.

Attomey Harris said that the property boundary extends into the conservation area and that it is independent
of the Land Trust. The area was staked out before they started any construction. He again said that they will
be back with a full plan for the next meeting.

Ms. Lozanov asked if the run-off had any chemicals or if it was only sand and silt.
Mr. Lombardi said that it was sand and silt only.

Ms. Lozanov asked if rainstorms in the future would cause more problems.

Mr. Lombardi said no - the area will be all grass and if they look at the other areas where there is grass
already — there are no issues. He said that they are shooting for May for this to be done.

Ms. Lozanov said that while grass does provide some good erosion control — she asked if they could also
consider other plants, etc.

Mr. Lombardi said that he would certainly mention it to their professionals to see if it can be considered.

Mr. Bender asked what is being done for further rain such as the 2” that is expected this evening.
Mr. Lombardi said that they have installed silt fencing and other items such as check dams, water bars, etc.
and they expect that they will work well.

Ms. Lozanov asked Mr. Goeschel if he has the information that he needs to contact the Centerplan people.
Mr. Goeschel said yes and added that he has spoken with all of them before.

Mr. Reluga asked if they knew how much rain fell in that event.
Mr. McNamara said that it was 5.45”.

Mr. Goeschel noted that he had asked to have the plans by April 30, 2014 so that he could review them
before the May 5, 2014 meeting of the Commission.

Ms. Lozanov said to Mr. Lombardi and Mr. McNamara that she appreciates their quick reply in this
unfortunate event.

Ms. Lozanov closed this Show Cause Hearing at 7:32 PM.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary

East Lyme Inland Wetlands Agency Show Cause Hearing Minutes — April 7, 2014
