Public Hearing |
5 Liberty Way — Wave construction Group, Applicant: Fairway Developers, LLC Owner
Application to conduct regulated activities within the 100 foot upland review area associated
with the construction of a 9500 sq. ft. building for light industrial use, together with 14 parking
Spaces and a loading area on an approved lot within an existing industrial park on property
located at 5 Liberty Way, Niantic, CT.

Ms. Lozanov introduced the members of the Commission, Recording Secretary and Staff. It was noted that
the Notice of Public Hearing was published in the Day on November 28, 2014 and December 5, 2014. She
then called upon the applicant or their representative to present their information.

Matthew White, Angus-MacDonald, Gary Sharpe, Assoc. explained that the lot is under the Light Industrial
zone and they are proposing a 9,442 sq. ft. building with front offices. The rest is for industrial use with
loading docks on the eastem side. There are also 14 parking spaces which are required by Zoning. This is
an approved lot within an existing industrial park. There are two entrances/exits to Liberty Way from the
property and a utility easement located in the center of the property. Storm water design is for a 25-year
storm. There was a previous application before this Commission in 2003 for this site and another that was
approved. This design is similar to that one just on one parcel instead of the two. He lastly noted that all of
the comments from the Town Engineer are acceptable to them.

Mr. Clarke asked about the loading dock and if any modifications were made from the previous meeting.
Mr. White said that it is excavated in that area and that they would put a snout hood on the catch basin.

Mr. Clarke said that he was looking for something to prevent things such as spills from getting in.
Mr. White said that a small oil/water separator could be installed and would be agreeable to them.

Ms. Lozanov asked who would be responsible for maintaining it.
Mr. White said that it would be the owner and that there is a maintenance plan.

Mr. Mingo asked what type of materials they would be using and if any liquids would be stored inside the
building.

Dave Keatley of Wave Construction said no — there would not be any liquids stored there. The type of
business that they are proposing for the building is an industrial service company that works at the
customer sites and this is to be used more for a warehousing of those items.

Ms. Bradley, Alternate asked when the delineation of the tidal waterway was done.
Mr. White said it was done in 2003 and there has been no erosion or change and no evidence of any
change to the wetlands.

Ms. Lozanov asked if they would be changing oil on the equipment or doing anything of that nature.
Mr. Keatley said that they work on power generating equipment — the nature of their work is stee! and does
not involve any hazardous materials or chemicals.

Ms. Lozanov said that she would like to see wetland tags installed.
Mr. White said that he did not see any problem with doing that.

Mr. Goeschel noted Mr. Benni, the Town Engineer’s comments about notes being added to the plan and
that the applicant has indicated that he has no issue with adding them.

Ms. Lozanov called for any comments from the public —
There were no comments from the public.

Ms. Lozanov asked if there were any further questions from the Commissioners.

Mr. Hall asked about the oil/water separator and who would review it prior to it being installed.

Mr. Goeschel said that Mr. Benni would review the specs for it. He said that they could add it as a condition
to the listing of items that Mr. Benni had in his letter.

**MOTION (4)

Mr. Mingo moved to close this Public Hearing.

Mr. Clarke seconded the motion.

Vote: 7-0-0.

This Public Hearing was closed at 7:20 PM.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary
