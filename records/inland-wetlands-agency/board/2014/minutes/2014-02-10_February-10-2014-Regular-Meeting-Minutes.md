V. Pending Applications
233 Upper Pattagansett Rd., East Lyme, Assessor’s Map 39.0, Lot 6, Pazz Construction. LLC,

Applicant/Owner — Application for grading within the 100 foot upland review area for the
construction of a detention basin to mitigate run-off from development of property associated
with an eight lot subdivision of land — Date of receipt — 1/13/2014.
Ms. Lozanov called for the presentation as they had determined that a public hearing was not necessary at
their last meeting.

Robert Pfanner, representing the applicant noted that they had revised the plans showing revisions on the
two basins in the front of the property. He noted the changes to Basin #1 and #2 on Pattagansett Road. The
outlets were re-done and they now mimic Basin #3 with the details appearing on Sheet #6 of the plans.

Ms. Lozanov asked what the reason was for the change.
Mr. Pfanner said it was to provide details on the plan for these basins and they were the only changes from
the last time.

Ms. Lozanov said that she wasted it clarified that one of the properties has wetlands in it.

Mr. Pfanner said that there are actually two — Lots 7 & 8 which will be part of the deed restriction that there is
a wetland easement.

Mr. Goeschel noted that they could do it as part of a condition of approval.

Ms. Lozanov asked who maintains the basins.
Mr. Pfanner said that they would be maintained by the property owners as they are on private property. Lots
3, 4 & 8 have basins.

Ms. Lozanov asked what would be the required maintenance.

Mr. Pfanner noted that was also a concern of the Town Engineer and said that there is minimal maintenance.
They would need to trim them and they won't have a lot of sediment going into them. It is very minimal as
they will be much like a rain garden and they are dry basins.

Mr. Goeschel noted that the existing stone wall would help with any ‘trash’ that might fall in. He added that
the plantings would act as a bio-filter.

Mr. Pfanner said that they are not proposing planting anything other than grass going up to the basins as the
grass is also a good filter. They could put in the maintenance program that there is to be minimal use of
fertilizers, etc.

Mr. Reluga asked if the pipes under Basins 1 and 2 carry any water under the road.
Mr. Pfanner said that the water goes to another catch basin.

Mr. Reluga asked Mr. Goeschel about the amounts of pervious and impervious surface area.

Mr. Goeschel said that they could indicate that.
