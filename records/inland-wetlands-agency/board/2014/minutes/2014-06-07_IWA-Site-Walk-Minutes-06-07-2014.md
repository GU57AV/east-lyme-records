SITE WALK MINUTES EAST LYME TOWN CLERK

Saturday, JUNE 7, 2014

The East Lyme Inland Wetlands Agency conducted a site walk, on Saturday, June 7, 2014, commencing
at 9:00 AM.

PRESENT: Chuck Reluga, Vice-Chair; Keith Hall, Secretary; Norman Bender, Phyllis Berger, Kimberly
Bradley; Joe Mingo; Gary Goeschel

ALSO PRESENT:John Lombardi, Lombardi, Sand and Gravel; Bill Fries, Centerplan Construction Co, LLC;
Barbara Johnston, Nick and Donnie Dombrowski, Bud Piecazio
Farm — 40-44 Grassy Hill Road/89 Walnu' = Lyme

The group met at the High School parking lot at 9 AM and proceeded to the site arriving approximately
9:20 AM.

At the site, the group met other interested members of the public along with representatives of the
development team.

The group walked the perimeter of the site to observe survey stakes that had been set since last month's
site walk.

Work to remove sediment from northernmost basin, believed to have been deposited during the rain
event on the morning of June 5, was temporarily stopped to allow safe passage of the group.

The group stopped at each of the basins on the easterly boundary of the site to note the status of
additional erosion and sedimentation measures installed since last month’s walk and observe current
conditions.

The site walk was adjourned at 10:50 AM.

Respectfully submitted,

Keith Hall,
Secretary
