EAST LYME INLAND WETLANDS AGENCY
SITE WALK MINUTES
Saturday, AUGUST 9, 2014

The East Lyme Inland Wetlands Agency conducted a site walk, on Saturday, August 9, 2014,
commencing at 9:00 AM.

PRESENT: Cheryl Lozanov, Chair; Chuck Reluga, Vice-Chair; Keith Hall, Secretary; Harry
Clarke, Phyllis Berger, Kimberly Bradley; Joe Mingo

ALSO PRESENT: Bud Piecazio, Cris Staubach

— 40-44 Gi i Walnut Hill Rd — East Lym

The group met at the High School parking lot at 9 AM and proceeded to the site arriving approximately
9:15 AM.

At the site, the group met other interested members of the public. No representatives of the
development team were present.

The group walked the perimeter of the site to observe installation of fencing, stone check dams and to
ascertain effectiveness of hydro seeding.

A member of the public noted that there appeared to be modifications to the side of the outlet control
structure in the northernmost basin. A similar observation was noted on the next basin to the south.

Keith Hall left the site walk at 9:45.

The group proceeded to the next basin on the southerly half of the farm before returning to parking
where they observed some recently completed excavation work,

The site walk was adjourned at 10:00 AM.
Respectfully submitted,

Keith Hall,
Secretary
