+ Application for Determination of Permitted/Non-Regulated Activity - 80 Grassy Hill Road, East

Lyme - Thomas and Nancy Kalal, Owner — reinforcement of earthen berm to allow the widening
of an existing pathway to allow haying equipment to cross on property located at 80 Grassy Hill
Road, East Lyme, Assessor's Map 51.0, Lot 9.
Mr. Goeschel explained that there was a boundary dispute and that the neighbor put up a fence that cut off
the crossing that they were using to get to their haying area in the back lot. They are asking to fill in a small
portion/area of the pond to create another crossing to get to that back lot haying area. Their determination
here is if it is a regulated activity or not. He noted that he had the mapping on the overhead for them to
visualize better.

Ms, Lozanov asked if any alternatives were considered.
Mr. Goeschel explained that he had suggested another area through a wooded wetland however that would
have been a more extensive disturbance.

Mr. Mingo asked if the pond is essential to the farming operation and how many acres the pond is.

Mr. Goeschel said that the applicant is present and can better answer that.

Nancy Kalal, 80 Grassy Hill Road, applicant said that the pond is only about 3000 sq. ft.

Mr. Clarke asked what the watercourse is that runs through there.

Mr. Goeschel said that they still want to cross over the concrete structure that is there but just move over as
the fence has been put there and the equipment can no longer pass as it is not wide enough with the fence
there.

Mr. Mingo said that is they are filling in anything then they need to come before them.

Mr. Hall asked how wide the piece of equipment is that they would be bringing across.
Ms. Kalal said that is it 6 feet in width and that the water flow would not be disturbed in doing this.

Mr. Hall asked Mr. Goeschel if he would have Victor, the Town Engineer look at this.

+ 11 Naomi Lane, Marlene Lord Owner; Scotland Hardwoods, LLC c/o Rod Burgess Applicant;
Application to conduct an “As of Right” timber harvest which includes stream crossings, on
approximately 8 of 20.73 acres on property located at 11 Naomi Lane, Assessor's Map #24, Lot
36, East Lyme, CT

Ron Burgess of Scotland Hardwoods LLC said that he is a State Certified Forester. He submitted revised

copies of the Exhibits A & B for the record. He explained that this is for the logging operation of 8 of

approximately 28 acres and is depicted by the yellow area on the map.

Mr. Mingo said that a farmer got stuck on that way in from Boston Post Road when they were cutting trees
there some time ago.

Mr. Burgess said that in going via Boston Post Road that it would eliminate one crossing. He added that
there were no visible signs of that road not being able to support the log trucks.

Ms. Berger asked how many loads they would be taking.
Mr. Burgess said that it would be 10 to 12 truck loads, as it is a small operation. The truck comes in empty
and goes out full.

Ms. Lozanov asked if the wood is selectively cut.

Mr. Burgess said yes — and added that it is a three man operation and they work from 7 AM to 6 PM Monday
thru Saturday. They have marked 150 trees with an estimated harvest yield of 35,000 board feet. The wood
is oak, tulip, hickory and poplar. They expect that it will take three to four weeks to complete the harvest and
would like to do it during the summer/fall time frame.
