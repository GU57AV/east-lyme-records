Vi. Old Business

= Cease, Desist & Correct Order - 20 Farm Meadow Road, East Lyme, CT - Centerplan
Construction Company and GRE 314 East Lyme, LLC, Owner; Antares Solar Field on property
located at 20 Farm Meadow Road, East Lyme, CT, Assessor’s Map 52.0, Lot 126, East Lyme, CT

Mr. Goeschel reported that he did try to get Centerplan and Mr. Bialowans together to walk the property

since the last meeting but that has not happened. They will hear from Mr. Fortunato this evening and forward

that information to Centerplan for their review and comment.

Don Fortunato, Certified Soil Scientist, 61 Plants Dam Road said that he walked the area and that it looks
pretty much the same as when they walked it in the Spring of this year — 2015. He said that he brought three
sets of photos for them to see (submitted to Mr. Goeschel and the members). He said that Mr. Bialowans
said that they could walk the property as long as they were with someone in case they fell — so Centerplan
could walk it with him. He also noted the letter from the USDA and said that the area is pretty much the same
as before.

Mr. Goeschel said that he would forward this information and the photos to Mr. Klein and a report could be
formalized.

Mr. Clarke asked Mr. Fortunato if the area has or has not gotten worse.

Mr. Fortunato said that log jams seem to need to be cleared, noting that some of it is Mr. Bialowans old log
stream crossing that is wider now. He said that the USDA letter has some technical things in it that go even
beyond his own expertise.

Mr. Clarke said that they can always find more people to have an opinion as they continue to have the work
dragged out by Mr. Bialowans refusal to allow them on his property to fix it. Further, he said that he does not
see it as being fair to Centerpian to add items when they agreed to fix the agreed upon items in the spring
and have not been allowed on the property to do so.
