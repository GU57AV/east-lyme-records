I. Pledge of Allegiance -
   The pledge was recited.

II. Show Cause Hearing - Cease and Desist Order; Andrezej Kowalski, Respondent, 14
   Park Lane, Assessor's l'lap #4.7rLot#230 Niantic, Connecticut

   Inland Wetlands Agent Gary Goeschel II addressed the commission regarding the reissuing
   of this Cease and Desist Order. Goeschel noted he had not yet heard from the Respondent,
   and described the activity occurring within 300ft of a watercourse located near 2-4 Park
   Lane. Goeschel added almost one year had passed since issuing the first Order. The greatest
   impact has been on the coastal area adjacent to the subject lot, and Goeschel has notified
   William Mulholland, the Coastal Area Enforcement Officer having jurisdiction over the
   marsh lands.

   If no action is taken to address the Order, Goeschel stated he would speak with the Town
   Attorney to then request an injunction.
