Attendees: Elbert Burr, Town of Salem; Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; John Jasper,
Town of East Lyme; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Doug

Excused: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management;
Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP.

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:01 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Chris Tomichek made a motion to seat East Lyme alternate Don
Danila for Don Landers, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of March 1, 2012 Meeting Minutes: Don Danila pointed out a
misspelling of Don Lander’s name and also that all the letters in DEEP should be
capitalized. Marvin Schutt commented that as a point of procedure, after a motion is
made and seconded, discussion may take place. Marvin also stated that East Lyme
First Selectman Paul Formica has agreed to allow the Committee to use the East Lyme
Town Hall as its official address. Elbert Burr thanked Ruth Savalle for taking the
March meeting minutes in the absence of the watershed coordinator. Elbert Burr
made a motion to approve the minutes as corrected, seconded by Marvin Schutt. All
in favor, motion passes.

4. Budget Review: Treasurer Dave Turner reviewed the budget. Don Danila requested
reimbursement for materials he has purchased for the water quality monitoring
program. Discussion regarding reimbursement was deferred to later in the agenda.

5. Coordinator Report: Judy reviewed her activities for the previous month. She has
prepared handbooks for the new Board members. She reported that the water
quality monitoring equipment we applied for in the 2012 EPA Equipment Loan
Program round has arrived. Planning for the Clark Pond Demonstration Riparian
Buffer project is coming along nicely. The NRWC website has been renewed for
another year, and has been updated to reflect recent activities. Judy wrote a support

letter on behalf of NRWC for Jamie Vaudrey, a researcher at UConn/Avery Point for a
grant application to the National Fish & Wildlife Foundation Long Island Sound
Futures Fund grant program. Jamie is proposing to conduct a review of research data
from the Niantic River similar to the data review NRWC had proposed in 2010; but on
a greater scale.

. Old Business:
a. Review/update Plan of Work: Chris Tomichek compiled the results of the Directors’
rankings of the Plan of Work goals. There was brief discussion about additional
goals. Marvin Schutt asked if sewering of Saunders Point was still included in the
Plan of Work (it is). Marvin quickly outlined the outcome of a discussion he and
John Jasper had with town officials regarding Saunders Point sewers. The Town (of
East Lyme) needs to conduct a feasibility study before it can proceed further, and
to date, funds have not been available to conduct that study. Marvin and John
Jasper agreed to draft a letter indicating NRWC support of the project and will
bring it to the Board for review and discussion. John Jasper shared the material he
presented to the Town officials at the sewer meeting. Dave Turner made a motion
to accept the rankings for the 2012 Plan of Work, seconded by Don Danila. The
motion passed unanimously. Judy will update the Plan of Work and distribute to
Directors.
b. Upcoming Grant Opportunities
© NFWF/LISFF: Judy suggested that NRWC might want to consider an oyster
seeding project for the 2013 NFWF/LISFF grant round. Recent research
indicates that oysters have been successfully used to reduce nutrients in other
estuaries. Doug Lawson stated oysters do well in many parts of the Niantic
River.

¢ Rivers Alliance Watershed Assistance Small Grant Program: Judy suggested
applying for funds for educational brochures.

7. Subcommittee Updates

a. 501c3: Dave Turner updated Directors on the status of the 501c3 application. The
subcommittee has been working on the Articles of Incorporation, which are the
first step in applying for non-profit status. Once a draft of the Articles is
completed, it will be submitted to Attorney Branse for his review and comments.

b. Monitoring Group: Judy reported that the equipment we had been waiting for (YSI
meter cable and sensors) has arrived. Don Danila reported that the Environmental
Lab at Dominion has agreed to assist with the set up and calibration of the new
equipment. There was brief discussion regarding where the collected data would
be permanently stored, and how it will be used. These will be topics for a future
the Monitoring subcommittee meeting.

members of the inactive outreach group to assess their willingness to become
involved. Marvin has also been in communication with Judy Benson of The Day
newspaper to get some publicity. Judy has expressed interest in attending a future
stream monitoring.

8. New Business:

a. Reimbursement: Dave Turner proposed the development of a request form that
would be submitted for reimbursement. Until such time as NRWC finances are
transferred to a NRWC account, all requests will go to ECCD for reimbursement.

b. Public Statements: Directors were urged to be conscientious when issuing
statements to news media on behalf of the organization.

c. Pot Luck Social: Marvin Schutt suggested a pot luck supper this coming summer to
allow Directors and spouses to socialize in an informal setting. Dave Turner
suggested tying the supper to a river outing.

9. Next meeting: June 7, 2012, 6 PM, 914 Hartford Tpk — 2nd Flr, Waterford, CT.

10. Adjourn: Marvin Schutt made a motion to adjourn at 7:43 PM, seconded by Don
Danila. Motion was approved unanimously.
