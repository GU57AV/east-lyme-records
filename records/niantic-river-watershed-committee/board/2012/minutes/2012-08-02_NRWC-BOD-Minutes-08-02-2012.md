FILED IN EAST LYME

Minutes
NRWC Board of Directors F, weer ore AMEND
August 2, 2012, 6:00pm

914 Hartford Tpk — 2" Fir, Waterford, CT AST LYME TOWN CLERK

Attendees: Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; John Jasper, Town
of East Lyme; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management; Ruth Savalle, Town of

Salem; Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP; Chris Tomichek, Town
of Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

Excused: Elbert Burr, Town of Salem; Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Don Danila, Town
of East Lyme; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Marc Lafrance,
Town of Montville (appt. pending); Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford.

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:02 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Don Landers made a motion to seat Waterford-East Lyme
Shellfish Commission alternate Peter Harris, seconded by Dave Turner. Allin favor,
motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of July 12, 2012 Meeting Minutes: Don Landers made a motion
to approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Marvin Schutt. Allin favor,
motion passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Treasurer Dave Turner reviewed the budget. Ruth Savalle made a
motion to accept the budget as presented, seconded by Marvin Schutt. All in favor,
budget is accepted. Discussion followed regarding disbursement of the Salem NRWC
contribution to the Eastern Connecticut Conservation District to pay for the
watershed coordinator’s staff time. Dave Turner made a motion to disburse funds to
ECCD to pay for the watershed coordinator’s staff time, seconded by Marvin Schutt.
Allin favor, motion passes.

Don Landers requested an accounting of the coordinator’s activities and hours to
demonstrate to the Towns when requesting funding support how the funding will be
used. Judy will include an accounting of expended time with her monthly
Coordinator’s Report.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy reviewed her activities for the previous month. Along with
several members of NRWC, Judy attended Celebrate East Lyme Day. The Millstone
Environmental Lab generously supplied aquatic critters and tanks for the event,
which were a hit with the public and a great draw for the booth. Five folks signed up

to participate in upcoming water quality monitoring programs. Judy continued to
work on organizing aquatic invertebrate surveys (RBV), and supporting the on-going
Latimer Brook water quality monitoring program. Judy also noted that an ECCD
stormwater implementation project on Colony Road in East Lyme is moving forward,
and is targeted for installation this fall. This project is included in the NRWC 2012
Plan of Work.

6. Old Business:

a. Establishment of bank account/transfer of funds: Treasurer Dave Turner reviewed
several banks as potential candidates to open a NRWC account. Dave queried the
Board as to whether we should open an account or wait until we have obtained
our 501c3 designation. Consensus was to wait until the 501c3 has been finalized.

b. Saunders Point Sewer letter: Marvin Schutt reported that he has been in
communication with Water & Sewer Commission staff person Brad Kargl. It is
Marvin’s impression that recent discussions have resulted in the proper
connections being made to move this topic forward with the appropriate Town
and State personnel and agencies. Marvin will work with Rick Kanter to revise a
letter of support for the sewering of Saunders Point from NRWC. Don Landers
stated he would draft a similar support letter from East Lyme Harbor
Management. Judy reported a phone call she had a from a resident of Morten
Street, who wanted to know about the possible sewering of her neighborhood.
Board members suggested Judy have her contact Brad Kargl at the Town of East
Lyme.

c. Salem Thank you Letter: Judy distributed a draft thank you letter to the Town of
Salem for Directors to review, and will distribute a digital copy as well. She has
requested Directors send any comments or revisions to her.

d. Waterford Appeal Letter: The Waterford directors reviewed the appeal letter and
sent comments to Judy. Judy will revise and send back to the Waterford reps for
discussion with the town.

7. Subcommittee Updates

a. 501c3: The draft Articles of Incorporation were sent out to the Board for review
and comment. As no comments were received, Judy will submit the draft Articles
to Attorney Branse for his review.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila conducted sampling on July 27". He emailed the
results to the group for review. The group discussed the recent algae bloom in the
Golden Spur. John Jasper suggested that East Lyme High School students should
be notified of the water quality monitoring program. Don Landers reported that
eelgrass populations in the river that were very sparse in June have completely
collapsed in July. The only surviving plants are near the causeway. He also
reported that CT DEEP has approved the Dominion Niantic River and Bay
Monitoring Plan, and that it was determined that funding the USGS Niantic stream

gages for the next 6 months was the highest priority. Water samples that have
been collected over the last year will not be analyzed until next year.

c. Education/Outreach Group: Judy reported that with the end of summer fast
approaching, she will finish work on the teacher loan kits and contact the teachers
who expressed interest in using them. Marvin said he would try to reconnect with
Stuart Mattison. Eric Thomas suggested the NRWC website could be used to post
seasonal information such as wise water, water conservation and so on. Don
Landers stated water quality monitoring results should be posted on the website.
John Jasper noted that any updated presentations should also be updated on the
website.

d. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Eric reported that the US EPA Clean
Water Act §319 grant program applications are due on September 15th

8. New Business:

a. Dave Turner announced that next month (September) will probably be the last
month we will be able to meet at the Chamber of Commerce. Brief discussion was
held regarding alternate locations.

9. Next meeting: September 6, 2012, 6 pm, at 914 Hartford Turnpike, Waterford, CT.
10. Adjourn: Dave Turner made a motion to adjourn at 7:20 PM, seconded by Ruth

Savalle. Allin favor, the meeting adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
