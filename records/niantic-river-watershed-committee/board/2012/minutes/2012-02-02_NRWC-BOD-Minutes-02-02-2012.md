Niantic River Watershed Committee

www.nianticriverwatershed.org

Minutes FILED IN EAST LYME

NRWC Board of Directors
February 2, 2012, 6 pm
914 Hartford Tpk — 2"! Flr, Waterford, CT

Attendees: Elbert Burr, Town of Salem; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Rick Kanter,
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management; Ruth
Savalle, Town of Salem; Dave Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed
Coordinator, and guests Peter Harris of East Lyme, and Marc LaFrance and Jeff Crouch, both of
Montville.

1. Welcome: Judy Rondeau opened the meeting at 6:02 pm. The Board welcomed Peter
Harris, who has agreed to fill the WELSCO alternate seat, Jeff Crouch, who has expressed
interest in the open Montville Director seat, and Marc Lafrance, who would like to learn
more about NRWC.

2. Review and Approval of January 5, 2012 Minutes: Don Landers made a motion to accept
the minutes as presented, seconded by Elbert Burr. All in favor, motion carries.

3. Watershed Coordinator’s Report: Judy reviewed her activities over the last month. She
reported that she will be working on wrapping up the Year 3 §319 grant over the remainder
of the month. She has been in contact with Mayor McDaniel of Montville, who has
identified a candidate for the second Montville Director seat (present this evening). Judy
reported that she needed to revise the Dominion Foundation grant to reflect specific grant
tasks. The riparian buffer implementation at Clark Pond is coming along well. A meeting is
scheduled with the landscape architect to review draft plans in the next week; then a
meeting will be scheduled with Town staff to review the plans and project. Judy shared
with Board members an article written by Judy Benson of The Day about the Watershed
Committee. Judy stated she would like to nominate the Watershed Committee for the
Aquarion Environmental Champion Award to acknowledge the great strides the Committee
has made in the last three years. Finally, The Niantic River Nitrogen Work Group will be
meeting on February 27". Several Committee and Board members will be attending.

Niantic River Watershed...

The Niantic River Watershed Coordinator is supported in part by funding from the CT DEEP
through a US EPA Clean Water Act $319 Nonpoint Source Program Grant.

Coordinator Funding Update: Board members provided updates regarding funding efforts.
Judy stated the Dominion grant, if awarded, will provide funding through June 2012. She
will continue to review grant opportunities, and will target them to specific tasks in order to
continue implementation of the Niantic River Watershed Protection Plan recommendations.

Review Plan of Work: The Plan of Work is due for review and revision. Directors are asked
to review and rank the goals, rate the activities as high, medium or low priority for
discussion at next month’s meeting, and otherwise update activities as needed.

Discussion Regarding 501c3: Dave Turner gave a brief overview of steps necessary to apply
for 501c3 status, including steps we have already taken (establishing a Board of
Directors, creating a Mission Statement, adopting By-Laws).

. Working Group Updates:

a. Monitoring Group: Board members discussed use of a flow meter to establish flow rates
necessary to calculate nitrogen loading to Latimer Brook.

b. Education/Outreach Group: Judy stated that she needs assistance to get the Teacher
Loan Kit ready for spring use by several interested teachers.

Next Meeting: Thursday, March 1, 2012 at 6 pm, 914 Hartford Tpk — 2" Flr, Waterford, CT.

Adjourn: The meeting was adjourned at 7:22 pm.
