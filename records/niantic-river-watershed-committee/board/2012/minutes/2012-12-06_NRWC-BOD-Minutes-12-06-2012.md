1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:17 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Don Landers made a motion to seat East Lyme alternate Don Danila,
seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of October 4, 2012 Meeting Minutes: Dave Turner made a motion to
approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Don Landers. All in favor, the motion
passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Treasurer Dave Turner reviewed the budget. Don Landers made a
motion to accept the treasurer’s report as presented, seconded by John Jasper. All in favor,
report is accepted.

5. Coordinator's Report: Judy reviewed her activities for the previous month. Judy submitted a
letter of support on behalf of NRWC to Don Landers for the FEMA Community Resilience
grant application to maintain the USGS stream gauge on Latimer Brook. She attended the
Niantic River Nitrogen Work Group meeting in October, and reported on water quality
monitoring in Latimer Brook. The opportunity also came up to discuss partnering with
Jamie Vaudrey of UConn and Fred Grimsey of Save-the-River-Save-the-Hills to conduct O2
monitoring in the upper Niantic River in 2013. Judy prepared and submitted a report to
EPOC per the grant requirements summarizing the results of the Latimer Brook water
quality sampling. Judy also updated the Board on ECCD grant projects in the Niantic River
watershed.

6. Old Business:
a. Saunders Point Sewer letter: Rick Kanter read a draft letter regarding the sewering
of Saunders Point to the Board. After brief discussion, Don Landers made a motion

to submit the letter to the Town of East Lyme, seconded by Dave Turner. All in
favor, the motion passes.

b. Town Appeals: Don Landers reported that Paul Formica is willing to support the
NRWC Coordinator in the 2013-2014 budget. Chris Tomichek and Doug Lawson will
meet with Dan Stewart to discuss Waterford’s contribution. Don Landers reported
that he submitted the FEMA Community Resiliency grant by the October deadline.
He has not heard back regarding the results. The grant application included funding
for the watershed coordinator. Don also commented that it is time to start
preparing the Dominion grant application.

c. Audit Committee: Don Landers and Rick Kanter have volunteered for the Audit
Committee (thank you!).

d. Watershed Brochure: Subsequent to the October Board meeting, Judy made some
tweaks to the draft brochure and sent it out to the Directors for final review. The
Grants/Funding Subcommittee will look for funding to print brochures for future
outreach and distribution.

e. Landmark Development Corp. Application: Directors briefly discussed the Landmark
Development Corp.’s application to the East Lyme Sewer and Water Commission.
Rick Kanter offered to draft a letter to the Town on behalf of the Watershed
Committee. Chris Tomichek suggested someone from the Board should attend the
public hearing.

7. Subcommittee Updates

a. 501c3: Dave Turner stated that there is no progress to report on this item.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila updated the Board on the water quality monitoring
and RBV sampling on Latimer Brook. Brief discussion took place regarding the need
to develop a QAPP for water quality sampling. Don Danila mentioned the upcoming
SeaGrant/LISS volunteer water quality monitoring meeting. One of the topics will be
a blanket QAPP for volunteer monitoring. Several board members plan to attend.

c. Education/Outreach Group: John Jasper presented a power point regarding the
results of his calculations on N flux in Latimer Brook based on stream temperature
and conductivity. Judy will post the presentation on the NRWC website. John
reported that he was unsuccessful in his attempts to get East Lyme High School
students interested in the water quality data. Dave Turner asked whether the
Monitoring subcommittee had considered conducting bacteria sampling on
Cranberry Meadow Brook as a check on livestock as the source of N. Don Danila
commented that N doubles between the dam and Colony Road, not surprisingly
since this is where housing density is greatest. There was brief discussion about
conductivity, and whether conductivity would go up in the winter.

d. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: This subcommittee has not yet met.
