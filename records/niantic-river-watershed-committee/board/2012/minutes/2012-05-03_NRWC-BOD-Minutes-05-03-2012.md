Minutes

NRWC Board of Directors

May 3, 2012, 6:00pm

914 Hartford Tpk — 2™ Fir, Waterford, CT

Attendees: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Rick Kanter,
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville (appt.
pending); Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management; Ruth Savalle, Town of Salem;
Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP; Chris Tomichek, Town of
Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

Excused: Elbert Burr, Town of Salem; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford.

Guest: Stuart Mattison.
1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:00 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Chris Tomichek made a motion to seat East Lyme alternate Don
Danila for Doug Lawson, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of April 15, 2012 Meeting Minutes: The “next meeting date”
was incorrectly listed as June 7" rather than May 3, Ruth Savalle made a motion to
approve the minutes as corrected, seconded by Don Danila. All in favor, motion
passes.

4. Budget Review: Treasurer Dave Turner reviewed the budget. The account has
approximately $2100, most of which is grant funds earmarked to specific projects.
Don Landers presented the grant award from Dominion Foundation in the amount of
$6667 to the Coordinator. The Coordinator read the award letter from Dominion
Foundation into the record. Discussion followed regarding the need to revisit the
watershed towns regarding our earlier request for funding for the Coordinator
Position, Ruth Savalle reported that Salem may have funds available in the Inland
Wetlands Commission budget. Don Landers noted that informing the towns that we
are pursuing non-profit status may be a selling point, in that our goal is to be self-
sustaining.

5. Coordinator Report: Judy reviewed her activities for the previous month. She
reported that the application sent to EPOC for funding for water quality testing

www.nianticriverwatershed.org

materials was approved. On April 13" the Monitoring subcommittee conducted its
first water quality testing of Latimer Brook. Sampling went well. The Clark Pond
riparian buffer project received approval from the East Lyme Inland Wetlands
Commission. The first planting will take place on May 22"4, >

6. Old Business:

a. Informational Brochures: Eric Thomas noted that at last month’s meeting we had
discussed possible funding sources to create informational brochures. He stated
that the Rivers Alliance might be having another grant round and it would be
reasonable to submit an application to fund brochure creation.

b. Long Island Sound Futures Fund Grant: Judy briefly summarized the grant
application submitted by Jamie Vaudrey for Niantic River data analysis. Discussion
ensued regarding the benefits of a unified data analysis to river managers, and to
NRWC for planning outreach and implementation activities.

7. Subcommittee Updates

a. 501c3: Dave Turner reported that a few decisions needed to be made regarding
the Articles of Incorporation, particularly concerning the status of CT DEEP asa
board member. Don Landers made a motion to revise the By-laws to list CT DEEP
as a non-voting, ex officio member of the Board of Directors, seconded by Dave
Turner. Allin favor, motion passes. Discussion followed regarding potential fund
raising activities.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila reported that the monitoring group conducted the
first sampling on April 13'". Despite an initial issue with the YSI meter (it locked
up), sampling went well. Several sites had to be changed due to accessibility
issues and the presence of multiple channels, and sampling flow was challenging
due to very low flow at several sampling sites. Flow sampling will be discussed at
the next Monitoring Group meeting. Don also reported that he was not able to
test for nitrogen at all the sites because the reagent ran out. Dave Turner asked if
it was possible to calculate flow based on the contributing watershed area,
geologic conditions, et cetera. Discussion ensued regarding the value of having
flow data to calculate nitrogen flux.

c. Education/Outreach Group: Marvin introduced guest Stuart Mattison, who was
attending this evening’s meeting. Stuart is an educator at the Sound School in
New Haven, CT. Marvin had contacted Stuart to see if he was interested in
participating on the Outreach/Education Committee. Don Landers announced that
Jim Marco has offered to seed the Niantic River with oysters. Marvin noted that
he will continue to contact potential Outreach/Education participants, and will
keep the Board updated on his efforts.

8. New Business:

a. Celebrate East Lyme Day: Discussion took place regarding possible fund-raising
activities, including stickers or bottled water to be sold at Celebrate East Lyme Day.
Directors discussed developing a trifold brochure to distribute at the event.
Marvin noted that we don’t want to be confused with Save-the-River, Save-the-
Hills. Don Landers asked if anyone had spoken with Fred Grimsey recently about
the NRWC activities. Marvin said he would touch base with Fred. Eric asked if we
had discussed establishing a liaison with STR-STH. Judy stated that Fred is still a
member of the Monitoring Group and receives all communications. Additional
discussion focused on what displays we should set up, including promoting the
water quality monitoring program. Directors agreed that “hands-on” exhibits are
best.

b. Potluck Supper: Marvin updated the group on a proposed potluck supper this
summer. Dave Turner suggested we have a trip on the river. Several directors
offered the use of their boats.

c. DEEP Water Quality Assessment: Eric Thomas reported that DEEP is preparing the
draft biannual Water Quality Assessment. The draft report should be available for
review and comment in the coming month or so. Eric also stated that NRWC
should keep an eye on any Rt. 11 developments, as Latimer Brook would be
significantly impacted if the project went forward.

9. Next meeting: June 7, 2012, 6 PM, 914 Hartford Tpk — 2nd Flr, Waterford, CT.

10. Adjourn: Marvin Schutt made a motion to adjourn at 7:59 PM, seconded by Ruth
Savalle. Allin favor, the motion passes.
