1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:03 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Ruth Savalle made a motion to seat East Lyme alternate Don Danila,

3. Review and Approval of September 6, 2012 Meeting Minutes: Don Landers made a motion

4. Treasurer’s Report: Treasurer Dave Turner reviewed the budget. Don Landers made a
motion to accept the treasurer’s report as presented, seconded by Marvin Schutt. All in
favor, report is accepted.

5. Coordinator's Report: Judy reviewed her activities for the previous month. A Rapid
Bioassessment for Volunteers (RBV) workshop was held on Sept. 15", Approximately 29
people attended. Judy scouted for RBV sites with the assistance of Maureen Fitzgerald,
the Waterford Environmental Planner. The Teacher Water Quality Loan Kit was finalized
and went out on Oct. 4" with a Waterford High School science class to sample Jordan
Brook. Judy updated the Board on ECCD grant projects in the Niantic River watershed.

Subsequent to the Coordinator's Report, discussion included potential funding sources
to maintain the Latimer Brook USGS stream gauge. It was decided that Don Landers
would speak with John Mullaney of USGS and Judy would speak with Jon Morrison of
USGS about methods to keep the stream gauge active.

501c3: Dave Turner reported that he spoke with Attorney Branse in September.
Attorney Branse confirmed that all the necessary components of the Articles of
Incorporation were included in the draft document, and advised that we should
proceed with filing for incorporation. Once we are incorporated we can proceed

Monitoring Group: Don Danila reported that RBV sampling has been started. Recent
rain has made scheduling difficult, as water levels at many of the sampling sites have
been too high to make sampling possible. Don stated that he would like to identify a
new water quality sampling site on Cranberry Meadow Brook above the horse farms
to compare to the lower sites for nitrogen content. John Jasper noted that Morgan
McGinley of Trout Unlimited had commented that stream quality near Rocco Drive is
not as high as it once was.

Education/Outreach Group: Judy reported in the Coordinator’s Report that the
Teacher Loan Kit had gone out to Waterford High School. John Jasper stated that his
daughter would like to do an article for her school newspaper.

Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: This subcommittee has not yet met.

Agriculture — Bureau of Aquaculture. Dave Turner noted that inadequate sampling
due to staff shortages could skew the assessment results. Eric suggested that NRWC

e. New Connecticut State Regulations: Eric Thomas briefly updated directors regarding
regulations regarding the new coastal jurisdiction line, and new phosphorus
regulations. The coastal jurisdiction line has been shifted from the mean low tide to
the mean high tide line. The new phosphorus regulation is part of a statewide
phosphorus reduction strategy tied to NPDES permitting and involves phosphorus
content in fertilizers and the application of fertilizers on established lawns.

f. Pratt & Whitney Grant Program: Judy is in the process of connecting with Jeff
Thompson of Pratt & Whitney to learn more about this program, and will report
back when contact is made. Don Landers commented that the Dominion
Foundation grant round is coming up and we should apply for additional funding
through that source.

g. Landmark Development Corp. Application: Marvin Schutt had previously updated

prepare a letter to be submitted to the Planning & Zoning Commission for the Public
