Minutes
NRWC Board of Directors Ju
September 6, 2012, 6:00pm
914 Hartford Tpk - 2" Fir, Waterford, CT

Attendees: Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Rick Kanter,
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville (appt. pending);
Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; Ruth Savalle,
Town of Salem; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; David Turner, Town
of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

Excused: Elbert Burr, Town of Salem; Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Peter Harris, Waterford-
East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme.

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:02 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: John Jasper made a motion to seat East Lyme alternate Don Danila,
seconded by Ruth Savalle. All in favor, motion passes.

2a. New Agenda Item: The Biogeochemical Circulation of the Niantic River Estuary with a Focus
on Nitrogen: An Update from the NRWC - Presentation by John Jasper. Don Danila made a
motion to add item 2a to the agenda, seconded by Ruth Savalle. All in favor, motion passes.
John Jasper shared with the Board a Powerpoint he had presented at the February 2012
Niantic River Nitrogen Working Group meeting in order to update Board members on
nitrogen in the Niantic River. Discussion followed regarding the status of eelgrass in the
river, including possible causes of the eelgrass dieback observed in the river this year.

3. Review and Approval of August 2, 2012 Meeting Minutes: Dave Turner made a motion to
approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Don Danila. All in favor, with one
abstention, the motion passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Treasurer Dave Turner reviewed the budget. Discussion followed
regarding the status of coordinator funding. Directors reported on efforts to secure
municipal funding. Doug Lawson reported that Pratt & Whitney has a foundation program
that provides support for non-profits that we should pursue once our non-profit status is
secured. Eric Thomas noted that such support can serve as local contributory match for
numerous state/federal grant programs. Don Landers noted the need for an updated
NRWC brochure to distribute for both outreach and funding opportunities. Eric Thomas
noted that CT SeaGrant has a small grant program that is suitable for NRWC activities.

Don Landers made a motion to accept the treasurer’s report as presented, seconded by
Doug Lawson. All in favor, report is accepted.

Don Landers suggested that the Board consider forming an audit committee to review the
financial reports. Several board members noted that other organizations they participate
with send their financial records to an independent auditor. Chair Chris Tomichek
requested this be put on the October agenda for further discussion.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy reviewed her activities for the previous month. In August,
Judy and Don Danila met with Cassie Devney of the Long Island Sound Embayment
Monitoring Project, which is compiling information regarding near-shore water quality
monitoring by volunteer groups. Judy sent the draft Articles of Incorporation to
Attorney Branse for his review. Judy notified watershed teachers that the Teacher
Water Quality Loan Kit is available. One Waterford teacher has reserved it for the
beginning of October. Judy scheduled Rapid Bioassessment workshops in partnership
with The Last Green Valley, and has been working to identify sampling sites throughout
the Niantic watershed. Judy has been looking into alternative meeting locations. Judy
has been evaluating possible CWA §319 projects, including culvert surveys, for the
upcoming grant round. Discussion followed. Dave Turner reported that he wasn’t sure
about the future availability of the current meeting space, but would keep the Board
informed. Don Landers suggested the meeting room at the Pequot Commercial building
right up the road. Ruth Savalle suggested the Chesterfield Fire House, also on Rt. 85.
There was brief discussion about culvert surveys as a grant funded project. It was
decided that because it is not a priority action item on our approved work plan, it should
not be pursued at this time. It was further suggested that we should review the Plan of
Work and craft a few action items into draft proposals for future funding opportunities

6. Old Business:

a. Establishment of bank account/transfer of funds: Treasurer Dave Turner recapped
discussion from the August meeting. Several grants were applied for by ECCD on
behalf of NRWC and need to stay with the Conservation District. The consensus was
to wait until the 501c3 has been finalized before opening an account.

b. Saunders Point Sewer letter: No action on this item at this time.

c. Town Appeals: This was previously discussed in agenda item 4.

7. Subcommittee Updates
a. 501c3: This item was discussed in the coordinator’s report.
b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila conducted sampling on August 20" and September
5th. The September sampling was a wet weather sampling. Don emailed the results
