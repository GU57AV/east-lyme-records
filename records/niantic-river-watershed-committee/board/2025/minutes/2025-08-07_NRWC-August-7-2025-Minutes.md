Minutes
NRWC Board of Directors Meeting
August 7,2025,6 PM

Attendees: Susan Gonzalez, Town of East Lyme; Nate Hermann, Town of Montville; Peter Harris,
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; Amy Caban'iss; Jamie
Vaudrey, NRWC Coordinator; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management/Shellfish Commission; Dave
Turner, Town of Montville

Excused:Jim Paganetti, Town of Waterford; Don Danila, Town of East Lyme


1". Call to Order: S. Gonzalez called the meeting to order at 6:05 PM.
2. Seating of Alternates: Motion by C. Tomichek to seat A. Cabaniss for the Town of Salem, seconded
   by D. Landers. Motion passes,
3. No welcomes and introductions
4. Review and Approval of July 10 minutes: Date in section 4, review and approval of minutes should
' be revised to June 5. C. Tomichek moves to approve as amended, P. Harris seconds. Motion passes.
5. Treasurer'sReport: D.Danilahadprovidedthetreasurer'sreportaheadoftimeandtherewereno
   questions or objections from the committee. C, Tomichek moves to approve the report as written, D,
   Landers seconds. Motion passes.
6. Coordinator's Report: J. Vaudrey provided a coordinator's report on the following topics.
            a. Celebrate East Lyme - Went very well, was much better attended than in previous years.
                Joe Courtney was among one of many visitors that came by to talk with Jamie.
            b. Enviroscape - was loaned out to Christy, has been returned successfully. lncluded a
                simple letter as the full agreement was not fully approved and that went well as a
                truncated version of what we had. Would get names and signatures moving forward but
                found the letter to be sufficient. Will also add the inventory and make checking their
                presence the borrower's responsibility both at the start and end of the loan.
            c. Bacteria Sampling - doing both bacteria tests, Entero and IDEXX. eDNA also at 8 stations
                with 8 more coming later. Ordered the water from Hardy and will receive hopefully on
                Monday to do sampling on Tuesday. Brooks are around base flow right now which has
                prevented other samples from being taken recently. lf water is too low to sample, then
                will come back after rain. However, base flow should be sufficient forthese tests.
            d. Report card - Nothing to report as new activity, Deb had a listening session with Save the
                River, Save the Hills and now we will hold one in September as the final piece for the
                report card.
            e. Hole in the Wall - no additional work, but still looking at 3 dates in November that will
                work for the towns. Will let CLEAR plan it after the date is set closer to November.

7. Town Matters: D, Landers reports that the bay (except forthe mooring area around the Yacht club) is
   almost all conditionally open except for when a huge amount of rain is taken which is a success story
   for shellfishing in the area. Looking to open around Giant's Neck, but need homeowners there willing
   to do some water sampling as there is not a public access point. Cecil the Seagull has been picked up
   on the Today show and coverage is intense. 36 Butlertown Road got 5500,000 from the state for
   Avalonia land trust which is a great piece of property to conserve including a heron rookery.
8. Old Business: None.
9. Subcommittee Updates:
   a. Monitoring Group: Nothing to report aside from bacterial sampling discussed earlier.
   b. Education/Outreach Group: The hats were successfully purchased and printed for all committee
      members, they are very popular.
LO. Eastern CT Watershed Network Updates: Kickoff meeting on July 1-6th, next meeting will be August
         20th. Have a set of themes out on a poll to decide upon for the 5 small events, One agenda item
         at the next meeting will be any report outs from the various watershed committees represented
         on the network. N. Hermann has been selected to represent the NRWC on the network pending
         approval by the committee. C. Tomichek moves to approve N. Hermann to serve on the ECTWN
         and D, Landers seconds. Motion carries.
11-. New Business: Catchafire is program which we gain access to as recipients of a grant from the
         Dominion Foundation. Jamie led the committee through the 3-minute needs survey they begin
         the program with to identify areas where the NRWC may be best aided by Catchafire. Notable
         areas of assistance are marketing, grant management, and operation.
12. Public Comments: None.
13. Next meeting: September 4,2025, 6 PM.
L4. Adjourn: C. Tomichek moves to adjourn, D. Landers seconds. Meeting adjourned at 6:30.
