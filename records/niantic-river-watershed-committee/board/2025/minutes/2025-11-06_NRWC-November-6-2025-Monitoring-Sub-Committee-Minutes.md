     3. Discussion of 2025 planned outfall bacterial water sampling:
    Jamie noted she would like to discuss further eDNA sampling with Maureen FitzGerald of the Town of
    Waterford. Based on previous findings, ourfuture focus might be on Stony Brook. However, Oil Mill Brook
     (OMB) also has high bacterial counts and work there may also be pursued. Winter sampling should be
     okay if there are no problems with weather or collecting, so we will move forward with it. For an
     upstream OMB site, Don suggested contacting Deb Moshier-Dunn to approach a homeowner whose land
     is nearwhere Willys Meadow Brook joins OMB. This is where he had a long-range watertemperature
     reorder placed and the homeowner was very cooperative.
    4. 2024 RBV Sampling Results:
     Don noted that he and the Hellers have decided to forego any RBV sampling this year and in the future. lt
     has been very difficult to schedule sampling due to personnel availability and varying stream flow
     conditions. This team has performed RBV sampling since 2012. Future work might be conducted by
    Ashley Hamilton and others at the National Estuarine Research Reserve. Jamie concurred that Ashley has
     an interest in this program. Required training provided by DEEP may be an issue,

    Don noted that there was a decreased need to hold separate Monitoring Subcommittee meetings as the
