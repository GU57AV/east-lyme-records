NRWC Board of Directors Meeting
February 6, 2025, 6 PM

Attendees: Jamie Vaudrey, NRWC Coordinator; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford (remote); Jim
Paganetti, Town of Waterford; Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Susan Gonzalez, Town of East Lyme;
Dave Turner, Town of Montville (remote); Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Nate
Hermann, Town of Montville (remote); Amy Cabaniss.


Excused: Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management/Shellfish Commission

L.        Call to Order: C. Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:00 PM.

3. Review and Approval of January 5 minutes: Jim Paganetti moved to approve the minutes as written,

     Treasurer's report as presented, seconded by Amy Cabaniss. All in favor, motion passed.
5, Coordinator's Report: J. Vaudrey presented the Coordinator's report.
     2. Dominion Grant requests are due on February 24. J. Vaudrey will draft a proposalfor where eDNA
        sampling can be extended, probably this weekend for people to review. Previously have request

       two places she knows of in Stony Brook. Don himself noticed that Scott's had what looked like pigs
       which he will investigate. Susan and Don Danila looked into certifying organization with Benevity
       (through Dominion) which would allow organization to accept donations.

       Outreach Goal section B "Organizational growth goal: Maintain and grow the NRWC." And for
       Section 44 make sure to lead with the types/methods of support. Under section 42 Expand social
       media presence, state that we will add lnstagram. Clean Marina program may no longer be active
       and should be eliminated, but boater education could be included regardless. Discussion on the

       rather than making too specific. Should invasives workshop fall under community outreach and be
       listed specifically to give credit for things already done. Getting names can serve both to identify a
