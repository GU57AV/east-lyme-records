            i. Website Update: Most pages are up and formatted. P. Harris will provide information to N.

         ii. Enviroscape Loan Agreement: A local teacher provided their thoughts on the loan

              potential costs for damage which, as they are so high, would almost certainly be passed up

              be invoiced and due within 45 days ratherthan immediately.

        month.
11. Public Comments: Save the River, Save the Hills had a meeting on Monday which brought up a
        question on studies of silting in the Niantic River as this has been identified in Latimer Brook at
        Banning Cove. Folks are hoping to get the channel dredged and want to know what it takes to
        do this, the opinion by the Committee is that there is nobody that would pay for it especially
        outside of the main channel in the Bay. However, a Coastal LIDAR dataset has just been made
        available in the past two weeks, may be able to compare data across time points. Deb Surabian
        at USDA-NRCS may be a point of contact for a recent sedimentation survey; however, this
        survey may not have gone north of Route 1.
