Monitoring Subcommittee Meeting

Conference Room 1- East Lyme Town Hall


Attendees: Don Danila, Jamie Vaudrey, Susan Gonzalez, Chris Tomichek (attending remotely)
   L. Don opened the meeting at 5:10 PM.
   2. Chris moved and Susan seconded to approve both the August 2023 Special Meeting
       Minutes and April 2024 Monitoring Subcommittee meeting minutes. Approved
       unanimously.
   3. Discussion of 2024 outfall bacterial water sampling:
   Jamie noted that the earliest this sampling could occur would be midJune, but a longer
   wait time would be better. Reagent expiration dates will not be an issue. As mentioned
   previously, these samples will be processed at UConn-Avery Point. Jamie will train
   volunteers in early June during two to three sessions as needed. Sample locations will be
   scouted during May, using the outfall map presented at the previous October
   subcommittee meeting. Alex Klose will help in choosing East Lyme locations and Maureen
   FitzGerald will be approached for Waterford, which needs to be done soon, since she is to
   retire this year. There is 56,600 of NRWC funding allocated for the bacterial sampling
   project, primarily for sample sampling. EDNA samples (-$1-75/sample)could possibly be
   collected in July for any locations found with high bacterial concentrations. This could help
   pinpoint sources of bacteria, for example, from humans, dogs, horses, cows, waterfowl, or
   chickens. Jamie also reported that Deb Moshier-Dunn of Save The River-Save The Hills
   reported that they have funding to perform bacterial track down sampling in Keeney Cove
   and Stony Brook. Jamie applied for an EPA equipment loan on behalf of STR-STH so routine
   water quality parameters can be measured as well as chlorine and surfactants. Eight upper
   river discharges in this area will be sampled using kayaks. This will also allow the NRWC to
   focus on other areas within the river. STR-STH will take most of their samples to Harbor
   Watch, which is a state certified laboratory, butJamie willalso analyze some at UConn. STR-
   STH will perform dry weather sampling, but if they find problems with bacteria they will also
   perform wet weather sampling in July or August. Harbor Watch will advise them on this.
   The NRWC sampling still needs to identify the people needed to perform our sampling.
   Jamie has the names of some potential volunteers from our Summit meeting and also has
   summer students available through the NERR. Most of our sampling will be done by land,
   although using a special pole sampler to reach the discharge to be sampled. However,
   kayaks might be needed for some locations.

   4. Partner Reports:
         a. Niantic River eelgrass study - Jamie reported that eelgrass plants were put into
                the study area last fall. However, all initial plants died, forcing Cornell University
                to re-plant. These plants will be viewed shortly. There was an anecdotal report
                that the addition of iron resulted in better growth of eelgrass receiving this
                treatment. Jamie also mentioned that in the Beebe Cove area of the Mystic River
                the Sixpenny Oyster Company in association with the UConn Marine Science
                Department will examine the interactions between eelgrass and oyster
                aquaculture gear. This 2-year study by has been funded by CT Sea Grant and will
                start this summer.
           b. Waterford Solar Farm water temperature monitoring - A decision still needs to
                be made on whether to continue monitoring once Don has analyzed the
                temperature data from Tidbits deployed near the solar farm. Don, Susan, and
                Deb Moshier-Dunn have not been able to download water temperature data due
                to frequent and considerable rainfall occurring this winter and early spring and
                other scheduling issues. Hopefully, they will be able to get out later this spring.
   5. New Business:
   There may be a need for a special Monitoring Subcommittee meeting prior to the initiation
   of the bacterial sampling training sessions. This will be decided in the near future.
