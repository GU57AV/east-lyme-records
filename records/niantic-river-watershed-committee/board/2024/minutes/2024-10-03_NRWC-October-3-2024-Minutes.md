5. Coordinator's Report: J. Vaudrey presented the Coordinator's report.
   a. Bacteria sampling - Hoping to do wet weather sampling in a few locations when water is flowing,
      but only had 0.7 inches last rain event. Samples collected in dry conditions may have inflated
      bacterial counts due to low water flow in the drains. Plan to also collect samples for eDNA
      analysis at select locations in the estuary and neighboring storm drains. Cost per eDNA sample is
      -S175/test + shipping. M. Fitzgerald can have someone pop lids on storm drains for easier access.
          i. D, Landers motioned to allocate up to 52500 to support eDNA sampling, seconded by D.
             Danila. All in favor, motion passed.
   b. Hole in the Wall-Jerry Lokken (EL Parks and Rec) felt that multiple teams in the region would be
      interested and benefit from a training workshop on maintenance of green infrastructure projects
      and invasive species identification and management. J. Vaudrey will send an email to Jerry and
      Dave Dickson (CLEAR) regarding the workshop planning. Jamie will also write an emailto
      Dominion with an outline of the updated plan for use of the grant funding.
   c. EnviroScape - CT NERR deliverables should be finished by the next NRWC meeting, then Jamie and
      Amy willtake over completion of the project. A dedicated EnviroScape page on the NRWC
      website might be helpful for providing lesson plans and links to relevant videos. This is also a good
       place to advertise EnviroScape availability. A QR code could be generated and posted at events to
       direct people to the webpage.
