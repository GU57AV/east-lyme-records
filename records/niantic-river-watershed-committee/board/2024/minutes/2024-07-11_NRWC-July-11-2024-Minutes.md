NRWC Board of Directors Meeting
July 11, 2024,6 PM

Attendees: Jamie Vaudrey, NRWC Coordinator; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; Don Danila, Town

       passed.

       development of Enviroscape lesson plan and accontpanying how-to video.

        i. Development of maintenance plan for the site
       ii. Workshop/event on BMP maintenance (potentially with UConn CLEAR) and
           identifying/treating invasive plants (M. Meekhoff has some contacts for this portion)
   d. Bacteria sampling update
      i. Site surveys on 6/tL and 6/!3, by CT NERR students - outfall data entered in Epicollect5 app.

           Enterococcus. Report shared with NRWC.
