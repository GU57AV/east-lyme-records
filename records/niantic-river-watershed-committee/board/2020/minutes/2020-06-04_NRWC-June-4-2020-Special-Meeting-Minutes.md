Minutes
Niantic River Watershed Committee
Special Meeting - Monitoring Group
June 4, 2020,5 - 5 pm
Via Zoom Online Conferencing

Attendees: Don Danila, Maureen Fitzgerald -Town of Waterford, Susan Gonzalez, Don Landers,
Chris Tomichek, Dave Turner, and Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

t. Welcome: Watershed Coordinator Judy Rondeau opened the meeting at 5:02 pm
2. Waterford Stream Temperature Logger Deployment: D. Danila reviewed streams flowing
    near and discharging from the proposed Waterford solar facility site plan. The group
    discussed potential locations for the placement of Hobo temperature loggers prior to the
    commencement of construction, to document stream water temperature, including the
    number of loggers needed. S. Gonzalez will check on Tidbit loggers at the Millstone
    Environmental Lab.

3. EPA Stormwater Toolbox Equipment Loan Program: Not discussed
4. Other Business: Members briefly discussed observations by John Jasper of thick mats of
    filamentous algae downstream of the fish lift on Latimer Brook.

6   Adjourn: the meeting adjourned at 5:45 pm by consensus of the attendees
