1. Call to Order: D. Landers called the meeting to order at 6:04 PM
2. Seating of Alternates: D. Danila motioned to sit E. Kanter for C. Tomichek, seconded by P. Harris.
   All in favor, motion passed.
3. Review and Approval of March 3,2022, meeting minutes: P. Harris motioned to approve March
    meeting minutes, seconded by D. Danila. All in favor, motion passed.
4. Treasurer's Report: D. Danila presented Treasurer's report. He noted that Bluehost issued a
   coupon to cover the website hosting renewal fee, so there will not be an expenditure for that
   service. There haven't been any applicants for water quality monitoring supplies funded through
   the EPOC grant, but a request is expected to come in from East Lyme High School. P. Harris
   motioned to approve Treasurer's report, seconded by E. Kanter. All in favor, motion passed.
5. Coordinator's Report: J. Vaudrey reviewed the coordinator's report. NRWC is registered for
   Celebrate East Lyme Day on July 16, more details to follow for coordinating volunteers. Discussion
   followed about potential NRWC participation in Earthfest CT 2022 on June LL, and outreach
   material/activities that would be appropriate for the event. J. Vaudrey will get estimates for
   printing additional outreach material for these and future activities. Additionally, J. Vaudrey will
   review the proposal for development of the watershed report card and put together a plan to move
   it along. The Education and Outreach subcommittee has been fairly inactive and there is interest in
   reengaging the group to help plan and prepare for outreach activities.

6. DEEP Liaison Report: E. Thomas reported that the biennial water quality statewide assessment is
    currently in draft format. DEEP Fisheries Division interested in conducting summer fish sampling in
    Latimer Brook and other tributaries possibly in conjunction with sampling performed by water
    monitoring staff.
