1. Callto Order: Chair C. Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:02 pM.
2. Seating of Alternates: No alternates were present to be seated.
3. Review and Approval of October 2021meeting minutes: Motion to approve December Meeting
    minutes by D. Danila, seconded by D. Landers, motion passed.
4. Treasurer's Report: D. Danila presented Treasurer's report. Our Bluehost website hosting was
    renewed for 3 years with officer approval. D. Landers moved to approve Treasurer's Report, second by
    S' Gonzalez, motion passed. D. Danila indicated that the annual audit is due. Audit Committee was
    selected to include Don Landers, Dave Turner, Rick Kanter, and pete Harris
5. NRWC Coordinator: D. Mullins reviewed the Coordinator's Report and summarized the month's
   activities. Pending approval of DEEP funding and subcontract arrangements, J. Vaudry will transition
   with support from D. Mullins into the new NRWC Coordinator role.
6. DEEP Liaison Report: E. Thomas followed up on grant funding for NRWC Coordinator for the
   transition of J. Vaudry. E. Thomas indicated that it was still going through the approval process.
7. Old Business:
       Town Matters:
                   . D. Danila, D. Landers, and C. Tomichek met with the new East Lyme First Selectman,
                     Kevin Seery and provided him a copy of NRWC Watershed Work Plan Update. Mr.
                     Seery indicated he is very interested in supporting initiatives to improve the
                     Watershed. Current initiatives include upgrading the kayak launch and improving
                     transient boating access to the Niantic River.
                   . Discussed Noble Gas Station project status. D. Moshier-Dunn coordinated a ZOOM
                     meeting with the property owner and Steve Trinkus. (Discussed further in Public
                     Comment)
                   . Discussed need to recruit new members for NRWC specifically to serve as alternates
                     in Salem, Montville, and East Lyme.
                   . Discussed pump-out boat funding issues. While Waterford and East Lyme agreed to
                     support project, a cash advance is needed to begin fabrication.
                   . Potluck for Preservationists have been postponed due to Covid-19.

       Subcommittee Updates:
                 . Monitoring Group: Q4 precipitation monitoring has been completed. This
                   precipitation event completes the 5-year term. Data from transducers deployed
                   near Waterford solar site has been downloaded. D. Danila and D. Landers shipped

8. New Business:
               . Downtown Niantic Watershed Museum: D. Mullins shared that he had received a
                     call from Dave Fairman (Former Waterford Selectman / Eastern CT Community
                     Garden) looking for the NRWC and ECCD to support a proposal for the creation of a
                     Watershed Museum to be developed in the former State Police Building on Main
                     Street (across from the Niantic Cinema). Following discussion on the matter, NRWC
                     had concerns about the location and the potential for the initiative to impact the
                     available funding opportunities that NRWC and STR-STH depend on.
                 .   Plaque Presentation: D. Turner obtained a plaque for presentation to Ralph Bates
                     for his service to the Committee as an East Lyme alternate. Consensus was to put
                     off getting together for a presentation until spring due to weather and ongoing
                     COVID issues.
9. Public Comments:
               . D. Moshier-Dunn provided summary of the meeting with the Noble Gas property
                     owner to discuss implementation of specific engineering / design improvements and
                     BMPs to mitigate impacts to Niantic River. The Property Owner is open to working
                     with D. Moshier-Dunn and Steve Trinkus to develop ideas as long as his existing
                     approvals are not jeopardized. E. Thomas suggested that the owner or Deb look
                     into grant funding opportunities for the research / development of BMPs /
                     engineering technology that was utilized for a similar project in Charlestown, Rl.
