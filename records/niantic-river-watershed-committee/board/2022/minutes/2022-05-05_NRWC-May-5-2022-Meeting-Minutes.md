                                                                                       EAST LYME TOWN CLERK
Via ZOOM Online Conferencing
Attendees: Jamie Vaudrey, NRWC Coordinator; Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Susan Gonzalez, Town
of East Lyme; Amy Cabaniss, Town of East Lyme (alternate); Dave Turner, Town of Montville; Chris
Tomichek, Town of Waterford; Karen Bishop, Town of Waterford (alternate); Don Landers, East Lyme

Excused: Melissa Bezanson, Town of Salem; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford

L Call to Order: C. Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:00 PM.
2. Seating of Alternates: D. Danila motioned to seat A. Cabaniss for vacant seat in Salem and K. Bishop
   for vacant seat in Montville, seconded by P. Harris. All in favor, motion was approved.
3. Review and Approval of April 7,2022 meeting minutes: D. Landers motioned to approve amended
    minutes, seconded by D. Danila. All in favor, motion was approved.
4. Treasurer's Report: D. Danila presented the Treasurer's report. D. Landers motioned to approve
   the Treasurer's report, seconded by P. Harris. All in favor, motion was approved.
5. Coordinator's Report: J. Vaudrey reviewed the Coordinator's report. NRWC will participate in
   Earthfest (June L1) and Celebrate East Lyme (July 16) and will need volunteers to help with those
   activities. J. Vaudrey will develop a watershed report card outline for NRWC review. Bacterial
   sampling will likely start in June and use a non-state certified lab for initial screening if funding
   sources approve. There was discussion of identifying projects in the Plan update with
   funding/partnership needs. E. Thomas suggested a subcommittee for that purpose. D. Danila
   mentioned that the ECCD could be a resource for ideas. Lastly, J. Vaudrey shared that there are
   funds still available for rain gardens and that money (S250/applicant) could be requested for plants,
   design assistance, or in-ground planting.
6. DEEP Liaison Report: E. Thomas reported on the East Lyme Land Trust acquisition of the Nehantic
   Nature Preserve. Discussed potentialfor a presentation or site visit with Art Carlson or perhaps a
   panel discussion with ELLT and other groups during the Watershed Summit. The state integrated
   water quality report is not available yet, but it should be out at the end of May, followed by a Zoom
   presentation.
7. Old Business:
       a. Town Matters: P. Harris mentioned that the responsible lawn care article was printed in the
           Lyme Times.

8. SubcommitteeUpdates:
      a. Monitorins Group: D. Danila still needs to collect the final quarterly wet weather samples.
                  The WELSCO weather station has been inoperable since April 1"4. P. Harris will contact
                  DA/BA for more information. Waterford stream temperature data download planned for
                  June. M. Fitzgerald reported that the solar panels are going up at the Waterford solar site.
                  E. Thomas will check with the DEEP fisheries group on fish sampling plan for the summer.

            b. Education/Outreach Group: E. Thomas mentioned that the -town bioretention and
                  biofiltration project is ongoing. Opportune time for additional community outreach
                  following installation of rain gardens, tree filters, and storm drain filters, especially in
                  Montville and Salem. Project needs to finish up by end of summer.
            c. NRWPP Update Summit Committee: Planning on hold due to Covid,
9. New Business: None
10. Public Comments: D. Moshier-Dunn shared the following information:
            a. Waterford POCD - Town of Waterford will be soliciting input for the POCD. The town asked
                  Waterford Land Trust to put together a list of their properties and the priority uses for them,
                  STR-STH and WLT will be asking for a line item in town budget for conservation (which it
                  doesn't cu rrently have).
            b. Boat shrink wrap recvcling program - D. Landers will contact Suzanne Thompson for more
                  information on the program and share with local marinas and yacht clubs. P. Harris
                  suggested The Day could advertise the initiative. E. Thomas mentioned the nip bottle
                  surcharge and pointed out that nonprofits can propose uses for the money received by the
                  towns (e.g., for recycling coordination),
            c. Stormwater District - D. Moshier-Dunn will be speaking with attorney Tom Londregan about
                  working with towns to adopt a stormwater district, and will update the NRWC. E, Thomas
                  encouraged towns to consider a stormwater utility because municipal budgets and
                  infrastructure are strained, and referenced the UCONN CLEAR webinar on establishment of
                  the South Burlington, VT stormwater utility. M. Fitzgerald mentioned that the COG received
                  funding for a study on community willingness, need, and benefits from a stormwater utility.
L1. Next meeting: June 2, 2022, 6 PM, via Zoom.
12. Adjournment: D. Landers motioned to adjourn at 7:00 PM, seconded by D. Danila. All in favor, the
    meeting was adjourned.
