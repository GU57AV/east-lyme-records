Attendees: Don Danila, Maureen Fitzgerald, Don Landers, Nancy Kalal, Dan Mullins, Chris
Tomichek, Eric Thomas, Dave Turner, Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

1. Welcome - Watershed Coordinator Judy Rondeau opened the meeting at 5:12 pm.

2. Review of January 7, 2016 Meeting Minutes — Subcommittee members reviewed the
January 7, 2016 meeting minutes. Chris Tomichek made a motion to approve the minutes as
presented, seconded by Don Danila. All in favor, the minutes were approved.

3. Water Quality Monitoring:

a. Latimer Brook Monitoring Program update - Don Danila reviewed recent water quality
data. Don suggested that we should continue for one more year, which would give us 5
years of data, and re-evaluate at that time how to proceed. There was brief discussion
about shifting toward wet weather sampling, with fewer core sites.

b. UConn student study update — John Jasper has requested that the student, William
Grant, present the results at an upcoming Board of Directors meeting.

c. DEEP Latimer Brook Fish Survey Discussion — Eric Thomas spoke with DEEP Inland
Fisheries and Anadromous Fish program staff about the results of the 2013 fish survey
of Latimer Brook. Fewer fish and fish species were found than anticipated, and species
tended to be warm water rather than cold water species.

d. River Bio-assessment for Volunteers (RBV) — Judy reviewed the results of the fall 2015
RBV sampling. NRWC collected RBV samples from three sites along Latimer Brook, and
the East Lyme Conservation of Natural Resources Commission collected samples from
Cranberry Meadow Brook. We did not collect samples from Oil Mill Brook or Stony
Brook this year because Waterford retained a contractor to conduct stream bio-
assessments. The 2016 RBV report has not been completed, but initial reports indicate 4
or more most wanted insects at the two lower Latimer Brook sites (Colony Road and
IGA), and 2 most wanted at St Mathias Church. One most wanted insect was identified
in Cranberry Meadow Brook. Maureen Fitzgerald reported that the Town of Waterford
has received the results from their consultant and will make the results available after
they have been internally reviewed.
