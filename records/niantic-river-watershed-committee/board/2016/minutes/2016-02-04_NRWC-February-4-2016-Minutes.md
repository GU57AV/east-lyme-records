* Niantic River Watershed Committee, Inc.

www.nianticriverwatershed. or’

FILED IN EAST LYME

Minutes Feo CONNECTICUT
NRWC Board of Directors TUE ee
February 4, 2016, 6 PM ST LYME TOWN OLE

East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, CT.

Attendees: Ralph Bates, Town of East Lyme; Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Peter Harris,
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management & Shellfish Commission; Doug
Lawson, Town of Waterford; Ruth Savalle, Town of Salem; Chris Tomichek, Town of
Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator; Dan
Mullins, ECCD/Waterford resident; Aaron Pauley, Niantic resident.

Excused: Tom Bowen, Town of Montville; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Eric Thomas, CT
DEEP; Fran Violante, Town of Waterford.

Absent: Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville.
1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:00 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Don Landers made a motion to seat board alternates Ralph Bates
and Peter Harris, seconded by Don Danila. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of January 7, 2016 Meeting Minutes: Don Landers made a motion
to approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion
passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Don Danila reviewed the financial report. Dave Turner made a
motion to accept the Treasurer’s report as presented, seconded by Ruth Savalle. All in
favor, motion passes. Rick Kanter requested that a second copy of the balance sheet be
printed so that the Directors have a review copy.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy provided a letter of support to Steve Harney for the New
England Forestry Foundation Open Space Land Acquisition Fund proposal to purchase
land in Waterford located near the Niantic River Headwater Preserve land. She
attended the Niantic River Nitrogen Work Group meeting at UConn-Avery Point on
February 1%. She received notification from the Society of Women Environmental
Professionals — Connecticut Chapter (SWEP-CT) that our application was not selected for
funding. EPOC has announced their 2016 grant round. Judy plans to apply to this
source for funding for water level logger; however, she spoke briefly with John Mullaney
at the Nitrogen work Group meeting and learned that the Latimer Brook gauging station
set-up may not be as straight forward as she thought. She will speak with John more to
determine if it is still suitable for our use. Judy has begun to prepare the CT DEEP
FY2016 CWA Section 319 grant proposal for the update of the NRWPP. Finally,
Dominion Foundation has announced their 2016 grant round. Judy will begin to draft a
proposal for that funding source. Regarding ECCD projects in the Niantic River
watershed, Judy and Dan Mullins met with Waterford Planner Abby Piersall and
Environmental Planner Maureen Fitzgerald to talk about the Mago Point tree filter
project. The project is still a go, but timing may be an issue. Judy provided a quick
rundown on the number of hours she is currently funded for. She suggested that her
ability to use her time most productively could be improved if members were willing to
assume some administrative duties, such as meeting minutes and website maintenance.

6. Old Business:
a. Town Matters: Rick Kanter reported that that shellfish beds were closed due to
recent rain, but that the scallop season was still open. Peter Harris reported that
NOAA collected approximately 100 scallops for spawning at other locations.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a. Monitoring Group: Don Danila reviewed the results of the February water
sampling.

b. Education/Outreach Group: Previously reported in the Coordinator’s Report.
Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Previously discussed in the
Coordinator’s Report. Rick Kanter asked if we had a spreadsheet or similar tool
to track upcoming grants, and suggested it would be beneficial to create one.
Judy stated that we do not, but one could easily be created.

d. Audit Committee: The Audit Committee, which is comprised of Don Landers, Rick
Kanter, and Ralph Bates, will meet this month.

e. Membership Subcommittee: Fran Violante was not able to attend this evening’s
meeting, but Aaron reported that Fran has been very busy compiling
membership materials. Discussion followed regarding how to engage new
members, what levels to establish for sponsorship, and the need for a semi-
annual newsletter to inform and update members on watershed activities.
Directors discussed engaging members through participation in watershed
activities.

8. New Business:
a. Save Oswegatchie Hills Coalition Discussion: Directors discussed whether to join
the Save Oswegatchie Hills Coalition. After discussion, it was decided that
directors should confirm with their respective municipalities before making a
decision. This item will be put on the March meeting agenda for further
discussion.

9. Public Comments: None

10. Next meeting: March 3, 2016, at 6 pm, at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108
Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic, CT.

11. Adjourn: Don Landers made a motion to adjourn at 7:21 PM, seconded by Dave
Turner. Allin favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
