      a. Water Quality Monitoring Program Review: D. Danila reported that he had collected the
         winter wet weather sample in March. He noted that the Town of East Lyme included our
         water quality data in their 2020-2021 MS4 report. Don reported that one of the loggers
         deployed at the Waterford solar facility site may need to be moved as it may not be
         upstream of stormwater discharge from the site.
      b. Data assessment/evaluation/report: D. Danila stated that we will have completed 5
         years of wet weather sampling in the fall of 202L. At that time, we should conduct a
         deep analysis of the data and prepare a water quality report.
      c. EPA Stormwater Toolbox Equipment Loan Program: Judy reported that she returned the
         toolkit to EPA this past week, per our agreement with EPA. She has applied for the kit
         for 202L

4. Partner Reports: None
5. Other Business: D. Landers raised the question of how we recruit new volunteers to
      continue water quality monitoring programs. S. Gonzalez suggested we prepare a sampling
      plan to help structure our WQM program. C. Tomichek suggested we begin thinking about
      potential monitoring objectives for our October Monitoring Group meeting.
