1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:00 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Don Landers made a motion to seat board alternates Peter Harris
and Ralph Bates, seconded by Don Danila. Allin favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of November 5, 2015 Meeting Minutes: Don Danila made a
motion to approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Peter Harris. All in favor,
motion passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Don Danila reviewed the financial report. There were no expenses
in November. Don will apply for a debit card to be used for NRWC business and
necessary purchases. Ruth Savalle made a motion to accept the report as presented,
seconded by John Jasper. Allin favor, motion passes.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy has updated content on the website, including revising the
partner page and updating several of the water quality improvement projects. Judy
accompanied East Lyme Cub Scout Pack 7 to Latimer Brook by St. Mathias Church on
November 14", RBV has wrapped up for the year. We collected three samples from
Latimer Brook. The East Lyme Conservation Commission collected two samples from
Cranberry Meadow Brook for us. Judy was notified that the application we submitted to
the Community Foundation of Eastern Connecticut for the watershed plan update has

6. Old Business:

a. Town Matters: Rick Kanter reported that the Niantic River was opened for scalloping
as of December 1*. Peter Harris added that shellfishing permits were selling well and
permit-holders were reporting good results.

b. Watershed Signs: There has been no activity on this item at this time.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a. Monitoring Group: Don Danila provided an overview of the water quality
monitoring program for guest Mark Nickerson, and reviewed the results of the
November water sampling. He noted that nitrate levels dropped considerably
from October levels. He reported that he replenished the fluid in the DO sensor
of the YSI meter prior to sampling. John Jasper reviewed the water quality data
relative to the study the UConn environmental engineering students are
conducting, and shared a draft of the report the students have been preparing.
He noted that while at the Latimer Brook/Cranberry Meadow Brook confluence,
Latimer Brook contributes approximately 2/3 of the stream volume, based on
data we have collected, Cranberry Meadow Brook appears to contribute
approximately 50% more of the nitrate load than Latimer Brook. Directors
discussed contacting the East Lyme Conservation Commission and Agriculture
Subcommittee to discuss addressing potential nitrate sources in the Cranberry
Meadow Brook watershed. Don Danila will try to get us on the Conservation
Commission’s agenda for the January or February 2016 meetings. Judy noted
that the Monitoring Subcommittee is due to meet, and proposed meeting prior
to the January 2016 Board of Directors meeting. Subcommittee members
concurred.

b. Education/Outreach Group: Judy previously reported her activities in the
Coordinator’s Report. Don Danila reported that he organized a clean-up of
Latimer Brook behind Flanders IGA, in conjunction with Save-the-River, Save-the-
Hills and Laura Ashburn and her East Lyme High School students. Rick Kanter
suggested that would be a good location for an outreach/awareness sign. Mark
Nickerson noted that protection of the wetlands at that site was a condition of
the zoning approval, and would be willing to help with installing signage. Peter
Harris commented that WELSCO has proposed to submit an article or Letter to
the Editor to The Day regarding the effects of spring fertilization on water
quality. John Jasper noted that we have observed spring and fall peaks in nitrate
levels that could be related to fertilizer use.
