Niantic River Watershed Committee, Inc.

www.nianticriverwatershed.org

Minutes FILED IN EAST LYME
NRWC Board of Directors VarS CONNECT cur i"

February 5, 2015, 6:00 PM tie a Joe MT
East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1 STCYME TOWN CLERK
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, East Lyme, CT.

Attendees: Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Rick Kanter,
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission;.Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management;
Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; Eric Thomas, CT
DEEP; David Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator; Ralph
Bates, Niantic resident; Dan Mullins, Waterford resident.

Excused: Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Ruth Savalle,
Town of Salem.

Absent: Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville.
1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:02 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: A motion was made by Don Landers to seat Peter Harris for John
Jasper, seconded by Rick Kanter. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of January 8, 2015 Meeting Minutes: Don Landers made a motion
to approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Doug Lawson. All in favor, motion
passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Dave Turner reviewed the financial report. A motion was made by
Don Landers to approve the report as presented, seconded by Doug Lawson. All in
favor, motion passes.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy emailed the teacher at the Dual Languages and Arts Middle
School in Waterford who had previously contacted her. She will report back after she
connects with this teacher. Judy contacted Sunny Ezete of CT DOT regarding the
watershed signs. She will report when she hears back from him. Judy has had the
opportunity to continue with updates on the recently reformatted NRWC website. Judy
contacted YS! regarding the issues Don D. has been having with the screen. The
technician made some suggestions that Don will try when he returns from his vacation.
Judy shared the Latimer Brook streamwalk data we recently collected with East Lyme
town staff. The town engineer was particularly interested in how the data could be
used to supplement stormwater system mapping required under the MS-4 permit.
Judy is in the process of preparing the Dominion Foundation to secure funding for 2015,
including funding for rain gardens or other stormwater implementation. To that end,
Judy met with East Lyme Town Engineer Victor Benni about a possible stormwater
management project at the high school. The next step will be to contact the school
superintendent to pitch the project. ECCD’s Waterford-East Lyme tree filter project is
slated to begin in 2015. The downtown Niantic redevelopment project, which will
include the installation of four tree filters along Pennsylvania Avenue, is scheduled to
begin spring 2015. ECCD met with BETA Group, which has been retained by the Town of
Waterford to design the Mago Point project. That project is slated to begin spring 2016.
Eric Thomas commented that stream crossing signs are installed through a different
program or division at CTDOT, and that perhaps we should investigate if we are making
our request through the correct channels. Don Landers commented that Dominion
Foundation is targeting large, “boots-on-the-ground” projects this year and perhaps we
should expand the scope of our 2015 proposal. Eric Thomas informed the group that CT
DEEP is funding the Town of East Lyme through the 319 program to retrofit the
Stormwater Classroom to treat water quality as well as quantity.

6. Old Business:
a. Election of Officers: Rick Kanter nominated the following slate of officers for 2016:
Chair — Chris Tomichek
Vice-chair — Don Landers
Secretary — Ruth Savalle
Treasurer — vacant
The motion was seconded by Peter Harris. All in favor, motion passes.

b. Town Matters: East Lyme Harbor Management has been asked by the selectmen
and finance committee to discuss the line item for the Latimer Brook stream gage.
Eric Thomas asked about the status of the Harbor Management Plan. Don Landers
stated it is in development but has not been drafted yet. Rick Kanter has arranged
for NRWC to be on the agenda for the February WLSCO meeting to talk about water
quality and the WQM program. Peter Harris stated that the new rain gauge at the
East Lyme Dispatch Center is running well. Details, including data availability, are
still being worked out. This rain gauge was installed to replace a USGS rain gauge
that is being discontinued.

c. Watershed Signs: Previously discussed in Coordinator’s Report.

d. Plan of Work Update: Directors reviewed updates to the Plan subsequent to last
month’s meeting. Approval will be tabled to the March meeting to allow
Directors time to review.

e. Board Vacancies: The “Members Wanted” letter was printed in The Day, and will be
printed in The Post Road Review in the next publication.

7. Subcommittee Updates:
a. 501c3: The application has been submitted. Dave will check to see if we have
received any notification from the IRS.
b. Monitoring Group: Don was not able to attend this evening’s meeting, but he
previously emailed members his water quality monitoring report for January.

c. Education/Outreach Group: Reviewed in the Coordinator’s Report.

d. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Reviewed in the Coordinator’s
Report.

8. New Business:

a. Don Landers presented a letter from Dominion Foundation, which Chris Tomichek
read into the record, regarding an additional award of $5000 to NRWC to be used
for water quality improvements in the watershed.

b. LIS Summit: Eric Thomas announced that the CT Fund for the Environment/Save
the Sound has announced the 24" Long Island Sound Citizens Summit, Friday,
April 10", 2015.

c. Local water /shellfish testing: Doug Lawson and Peter Harris discussed briefly the
need for a local lab for water and shellfish testing, so closed shellfish beds can be
evaluated and re-opened in a timely manner after rains. There is a great deal of
support for a local lab from eastern CT shellfish commissions. UConn at Avery
Point has been put forward in informal discussions as a possible location for such
a facility. There was additional discussion regarding sources of bacteria, including
septic systems and cesspools. Reference was made to a successful program
launched by HarborWatch in Westport, CT that tracked down and identified illicit
discharges through the stormdrain system in an effort to reduce bacterial
contamination.

9. Public Comments: None.

10. Next meeting: March 5, 2015, at 6 pm, at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108
Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic, CT.

11. Adjourn: Dave Turner made a motion to adjourn at 7:36 PM, seconded by Don
Landers. Allin favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
