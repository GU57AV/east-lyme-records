* Niantic River Watershed Committee, Inc.

www.nianticriverwatershed.org

Minutes FILED
NRWC Board of Directors so]
September 3, 2015, 6 PM

East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, CT.

IN EAST LYME
NNECTICUT

Attendees: Ralph Bates, Town of East Lyme; Tom Bowen, Town of Montville; Don Danila,
Town of East Lyme; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme
Shellfish Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management; Ruth Savalle, Town of
Salem; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; David Turner, Town of
Montville; Fran Violante, Town of Waterford; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator;
Maureen Fitzgerald, Town of Waterford; Dan Mullins; Waterford resident; Aaron Pauley,
Niantic resident.

Excused: Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Doug Lawson, Town of
Waterford.

Absent: Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville.
1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:01 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Don Danila made a motion to seat Fran Violante and Ralph Bates,
seconded by Don Landers. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of August 6, 2015 Meeting Minutes: Don Landers made a motion
to approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion
passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Don Danila reviewed the financial report. A balance of $17,028.92
is being managed by ECCD. A balance of $598.94 is in the NRWC account. Dave Turner
made a motion to accept the report as presented, seconded by John Jasper. All in favor,
motion passes. Don Danila stated that a request to Dominion Foundation to authorize
the transfer of the Dominion funds from ECCD to NRWC has not yet been approved.
Don Landers will follow up. Judy requested and received permission to use funds in the
water quality monitoring budget to purchase a banner for outreach events.

a. Audit Subcommittee: Don Danila commented that at last month’s meeting, the Board
voted to form an Audit Subcommittee. Ralph Bates and Don Landers have offered to
serve on the subcommittee. Rick Kanter offered to serve on the Audit Subcommittee
as well.
5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy was guest on Suzanne Thompson’s Connecticut Outdoors
radio program on August 11". Judy presented a program on stormwater management
practices to the East Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources on
August 12th. RBV workshops were held on Aug. 28" and 29". Judy will be reviewing
the monitoring sites over the next few weeks. She will also be contacting the East Lyme
cub scouts to see if they want to sample Latimer Brook at St Matthias Church again this
year. Judy met with school superintendent Jeff Newton, facilities manager Ted Hunt,
Victor Benni, East Lyme Town Engineer, and Lisa Vaudreille, an East Lyme elementary
school teacher, on August 17" to discuss the bio-retention garden at East Lyme High
School. The informational sign for the Niantic tree filter project in has been ordered and
should arrive by the end of September. Judy met with Abbie Piersall, the new
Waterford town planner, regarding the status of the Mago Point redevelopment project.
The Town is proposing to include the tree filters in the central parking lot design. A
spring 2016 installation is still proposed. Don Danila commented that, as a result of
changes in the RBV program, the East Lyme Conservation Commission may be available
to do RBV sampling at some different sites this year in the Niantic watershed. Don
Landers thanked Fran Violante, Ruth Savalle, Ralph Bates and Judy Rondeau for manning
the booth at this year’s Celebrate East Lyme Day.

6. Old Business:

a. Town Matters: Judy distributed drafts of a thank you letter to Welles Gilmartin and
an appeal letter for Latimer Brook stream gauge funding to Kraft Corp. for directors
to review. Judy will distribute the letter s via email for further discussion/ revision.
Dan Mullins commented that United Natural Foods, Inc. (UNFI) provides donations
to environmental organizations. Directors discussed the upcoming public meeting
being held by the Army National Guard regarding Stone Ranch and Camp Niantic,
particularly regarding a proposed 250 foot wide non-encroachment zone in the
Niantic River along the Camp Niantic river frontage. Directors are not in favor of any
restrictions that would limit public access for shellfishing and recreation or that
would disturb or destroy habitat, especially bottom habitat. Don Danila stated he
intends to attend the meeting and is willing to speak on behalf of NRWC. Rick
Kanter made a motion to authorize Don to speak on behalf of NRWC, seconded by
Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

b. Watershed Signs: Eric Thomas commented that the placement of signs seems to
depend on what DOT department handles the encroachment permit, and suggested
that discussion between town leaders and DOT might expedite the process.
Maureen Fitzgerald suggested that as part of mitigation requirements relating to
roadway improvements, DOT might include the installation of the watershed signs.
Don Landers stated that a sign has been installed at the entrance to the Niantic River
for boaters, with information on river navigation, the pump out boat, and so on.

c. Membership: Directors discussed the establishment of members and expressed the
need to organize and conduct a membership drive. Don Landers suggested the
formation of a membership subcommittee to research the development of
membership and develop a membership campaign. Fran Violante, Ruth Savalle and
Aaron Pauley offered to serve on the subcommittee.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a. Monitoring Group: Don Danila reviewed the August water sampling results.

With assistance from the Millstone Environmental Lab, he has resolved
calibration issues with the new pH probe. Don thanked Tom Bowen for
disposing of used nitrate solution at the Montville hazardous waste collection.

b. Education/Outreach Group: John Jasper commented on the growing body of
presentations NRWC members have prepared in the last few years, some of
which have been shared with colleagues at Yale, Connecticut College and
University of Connecticut. John stated that he continues to work with the UConn
Environmental Engineering department professor and student on the analysis of
Latimer Brook water quality data. This is a term project which should be
completed by the end of fall term. Directors discussed whether it was time to
plan another watershed summit. This item will be placed on next month’s
agenda for discussion. Dave Turner asked if the teacher loan kits were being
requested and suggested contacting the school superintendents in the four
towns to advertise among teachers. Judy stated that a Waterford High School
teacher has requested the kit in October.

c. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Rick Kanter suggested the Community
Foundation of Eastern Connecticut as a possible funding source through their
newly established environmental grant program. Rick has arranged to meet with
CFECT to discuss further. Don Landers and Chris Tomichek expressed interest in
meeting with them as well. CFECT has an open grant round and is accepting
letters of intent through September 15'". Directors discussed various possible
grant projects. Judy will review the RFP and prepare a letter of intent. Ralph
Bates discussed a CT DEEP grant for municipalities for the control of invasive
aquatic species.

8. New Business:
a. Watershed Plan update proposal for 2015 CWA §319 grant round: Directors
discussed the possibility of applying for a CWA §319 grant through DEEP to
update the NRWPP.

9. Public Comments: Maureen Fitzgerald complimented the Committee on recent
activities.

10. Next meeting: October 1, 2015, at 6 pm, at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108
Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic, CT.

11. Adjourn: Don Danila made a motion to adjourn at 7:22 PM, seconded by Dave
Turner. All in favor, the meeting was adjourned.
Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
