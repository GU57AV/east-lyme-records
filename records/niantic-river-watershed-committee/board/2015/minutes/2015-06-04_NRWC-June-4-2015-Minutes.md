East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1 S TOWN CLERIC

108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, CT.

Attendees: Ralph Bates, Town of East Lyme; Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Rick Kanter,
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management;
Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; Ruth Savalle, Town of Salem; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP; Chris
Tomichek, Town of Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed
Coordinator; Tom Bowen, Oakdale resident; Aaron Pauley, Niantic resident.

Excused: John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission.

Absent: Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville.
1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:01 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Don Danila made a motion to seat Ralph Bates for John Jasper,
seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of May 7, 2015 Meeting Minutes: Dave Turner made a motion to
approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Ruth Savalle. Allin favor, motion
passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Don Danila reviewed the financial report. Don Danila requested
reimbursement for chemicals purchased for the colorimeter. Dave Turner made a
motion to reimburse Don Danila, seconded by Rick Kanter. All in favor, motion passes.
Dave Turner commented that we had previously established an expenditure limit, and
that expenses under that limit do not need a motion for approval. Judy will review
meeting minutes to identify what the limit was, and this will be revisited at next month’s
meeting. Don Landers presented the Dominion Foundation notice of award to Judy.
Judy read the award letter into the record, and thanked Don and Dominion Foundation
for their continued support. Don Danila commented that he has begun to review area
banks in order to open a NRWC account and transition funds from ECCD to NRWC.

5. Coordinator's Report: Judy participated in the East Lyme Stormwater Classroom event at
the Hole in the Wall Outdoor Classroom on May 12", She has registered for Celebrate
East Lyme Day, which will be held on July 18". She gave a presentation on the East
Lyme Tree Filter project in May for the Adventures in Lifelong Learning program at
Three Rivers Community College in Norwich. She also gave a short presentation of the
same project at the Niantic River Nitrogen Work Group meeting on June 1%. Judy
downloaded data from the Latimer Brook/Cranberry Meadow Brook temperatures
loggers and sent it to Don D. and John J. for analysis. Judy will be meeting with East
Lyme Town Engineer Victor Benni to discuss the rain garden at the East Lyme High
School. Installation of tree filters in the East Lyme part of the Two Zone Tree Filter
project is complete.

6. Old Business:

a. Town Matters: There was brief discussion regarding preparing letters to the
watershed towns to request financial support for watershed programs. Eric Thomas.
asked if Towns had been notified that NRWC has obtained 501c3 status. Rick Kanter
suggested we prepare a general press release.

b. Watershed Signs: Judy will follow up with an alternate contact at CT DOT to discuss
what is necessary to move this project forward.

c. Latimer Brook Stream Gauge Funding: There is no funding for this item; the gauge
will be discontinued.

d. Celebrate East Lyme Day (July 18"): Judy, Ruth and Fran will head up the East
Lyme Day Committee. Don Landers confirmed that MEL will provide critters and
tanks again this year. Dave Turner stated that we can now put up a “Donations
Welcome” sign.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a. 501c3: Dave Turner confirmed that we have received approval of our 501c3
application. The confirmation letter was sent to the East Lyme Town Hall in
December 2014, but was misplaced. Dave noted that he has not heard back
from the State regarding efforts to correct our name on the incorporation
documents (“River” was dropped from our name). Dave also stated that he
submitted an annual report as required under the 501c3. Finally, directors
agreed we should send a letter of thanks to Welles for his assistance with the
501c3 process.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila reviewed the May water sampling results. Craig
Tobias of UConn will analyze the samples for nitrogen isotopes in order to
potentially identify the N source. Millstone will be collecting a sample at our
station #1. This will allow us to compare results to determine how accurate our
methodology (Lamotte colorimeter) is. The pH probe on the YS is still not
working properly. Don will troubleshoot or investigate ordering a new probe.
Don has converted all nitrate data results to nitrate-nitrogen so that our results
will be comparable to Nitrogen Work Group data. Finally, Don noted that the
tree filter trees along Colony Road looked good this year.

c. Education/Outreach Group: Reviewed in the Coordinator’s Report.

d. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Reviewed in the Coordinator’s
Report. Eric Thomas commented that the 2015 CWA §319 grant announcement
should be publicized in the next few week. There was brief discussion about
identifying an implementation project on Grand Avenue in East Lyme, which
discharges its stormwater directly to the Niantic River. Don Danila queried the ~
Board on its opinion regarding the installation of rain gardens on private
property using NRWC grant funds. Judy commented that she and Victor Benni
have discussed some sort of town-wide rain garden initiative to encourage East
Lyme residents to install rain gardens, possibly in conjunction with the municipal
MS-4 permit.

8. New Business:

a. Plaques: Dave Turner has solicited estimates from several award companies for
plaques for Marvin Schutt and Paul Formica. Don Landers made a motion to
authorize up to $75 each for plaques for Paul and Marvin, seconded by Ruth
Savalle. Allin favor, motion passes.

9. Public Comments: Rick Kanter commented on the proposed 250 foot no-go” zone
in the Niantic River in front of Camp Niantic. Don Danila read the letter NRWC sent
to the East Lyme Zoning Commission regarding the Oswegatchie Hill permit
application. Don also commented on minutes from the CT Council on Environmental
Quality in which the impacts to the Bialowans property from the Antares solar
installation were discussed. Eric Thomas commented that he noticed discussion
regarding the former Woodsman property in the East Lyme Board of Selectmen
(BOS) meeting minutes. The BOS has approved funds towards the purchase of the
property as open space provided a DEEP Open Space and Watershed Land
Acquisition grant application is approved.

10. Next meeting: July 2, 2015, at 6 pm, at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania
Ave, Niantic, CT. Brief discussion was held regarding rescheduling the meeting to
July 9" due to the holiday. Don Landers made a motion to reschedule the Board of
Directors meeting to July 9" 2015, seconded by Don Danila. All in favor, motion
passes.

11. Adjourn: Dave Turner made a motion to adjourn at 7:10 PM, seconded by Ralph
Bates. Allin favor, the meeting was adjourned.
