Niantic River Watershed Committee, Inc.

www. nianticriverwatershed.or}

FILED IN EAST LYME

Minutes CONNECTICUT
NRWC Board of Directors Qwad ahpnephe
August 6, 2015, 6 PM \ EB Guy T CLERK

East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, CT.

Attendees: Ralph Bates, Town of East Lyme; Tom Bowen, Town of Montville; Don Danila,
Town of East Lyme; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; John Jasper,
Town of East Lyme; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP; Chris
Tomichek, Town of Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Fran Violante, Town of
Waterford; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator; Aaron Pauley, Niantic resident.

Excused: Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme
Harbor Management; Ruth Savalle, Town of Salem.

Absent: Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville.
1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:03 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Doug Lawson made a motion to seat Ralph Bates and Peter Harris,
seconded by John Jasper. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of July 9, 2015 Meeting Minutes: Doug Lawson made a motion to
approve the minutes as presented, seconded by John Jasper. Allin favor, motion
passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Don Danila reviewed the financial report. Tom Bowen made a
motion to accept the report as presented, seconded by Ralph Bates. All in favor, motion
passes. Don reported that a NRWC checking account has been established, with Chris
Tomichek and him as signatories.

a. Formation of an Audit Sub-committee: Don Danila made a motion to form an Audit
Sub-committee to provide oversight of NRWC finances. The Vice Chair of the NRWC
Board of Directors will be an ex officio member and chair of this sub-committee,
which will have two or more additional members appointed by the Board. The Audit
Sub-committee should perform, at minimum, one audit per year and take on any
additional responsibilities as approved by the Board. The motion was seconded by
Dave Turner. Following brief discussion, the motion was amended such that the
Audit Sub-committee would perform one audit per year and report the results of
audit to the Board, and take on any additional responsibilities associated with
financial matters as requested by the Board. Dave Turner seconded the amended
motion. Allin favor, motion passes. Ralph Bates offered to serve on this sub-
committee.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy has been invited to appear on Suzanne Thompson’s
Connecticut Outdoors radio program on August 11" to talk about the Niantic River and
what property owners can do to reduce water pollution. Judy has also been asked to
present information on stormwater management practices to the East Lyme
Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources on August 12th. Judy, Don
Danila, and Aaron Pauley deployed three temperature loggers in Latimer and Cranberry
Meadow Brooks on July 22nd. Judy, Victor Benni, East Lyme Town Engineer, and Lisa
Vaudreille, an East Lyme elementary school teacher, will be meeting with the new
school superintendent on August 17" to discuss the bio-retention garden at East Lyme
High School. The tree filter project in Niantic is complete. Development of
informational signage is underway. Judy has spoken with the interim Waterford planner
regarding the status of the Mago Point redevelopment project, and the project is still
moving forward.

6. Old Business:
a. Town Matters: Per discussion at the July meeting, Judy will draft a letter to Rand
Whitney regarding potential funding for the Latimer Brook stream gauge.
b. Watershed Signs: Judy has not had an opportunity to follow up at this time.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a. Monitoring Group: Don Danila reviewed the July water sampling results. He
noted that flow has been very low, and nitrate levels have been relatively high.
He has purchased a new pH probe for the YSI, but has had difficulty with the
calibration. Don reviewed the stream temperature data retrieved from
Cranberry Meadow Brook and the unnamed tributary from the Antares solar
site. It appears that subsequent to the development of the site, the tributary has
warmed up. However, there does not appear to be a significant temperature
change in Cranberry Meadow Brook. Don also reviewed the Latimer Brook/
Cranberry Meadow Brook temperature data. Don ran the data through a
regression analysis and determined that based on data collected downstream of
the confluence, Cranberry Meadow Brook contributes about 1/3 of the water
volume, while Latimer Brook contributes about 2/3 of the water volume. This
correlates well with previous calculations made by John Jasper.

b. Education/Outreach Group: Reviewed in the Coordinator’s Report.

c. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Eric Thomas reported that CT DEEP
anticipates an announcement regarding the FY 2016 CWA §319 grant round in
the near future. Discussion followed regarding a possible application relating to
the updating of the Niantic River Watershed Protection Plan (NRWPP) to reflect
the many Plan implementations that have been completed since the Plan was
written in 2006. Doug Lawson reported that he has been following up ona
foundation grant program offered by Pratt & Whitney.
8. New Business:

a. Purchase of NRWC banner for outreach events — Ruth researched the cost of
banners, and determined that a 5 foot long by 2% foot high banner would be
$60-$70. Dave Turner made a motion to purchase a banner for outreach events,
seconded by John Jasper. All in favor, the motion passes.

b. Membership: Following the recent approval of our 501c3 application, do we
want to revisit the issue of membership to the watershed committee? Directors
will review the by-laws regarding membership. This item will be placed on the
September agenda for discussion.

9. Public Comments: Don Danila announced that he has been appointed to the East
Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources. Chris Tomichek noted
that East Lyme residents voted to approve the purchase of the Niantic River
Headwaters Preserve land. Eric Thomas commented on a pending DOT permit to
replace the bridge on Route 1 in East Lyme and Waterford.

10. Next meeting: September 3, 2015, at 6 pm, at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108
Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic, CT.

11. Adjourn: Dave Turner made a motion to adjourn at 7:50 PM, seconded by Don
Danila. All in favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
