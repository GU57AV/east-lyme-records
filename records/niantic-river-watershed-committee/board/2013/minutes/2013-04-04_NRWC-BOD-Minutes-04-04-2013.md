April 4, 2013 EAST LYME TOWN CLERK
East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1

108 Pennsylvania Avenue, East Lyme, CT.

Attendees: Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission; Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; Ruth
Savalle, Town of Salem; Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP; Chris
Tomichek, Town of Waterford; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

Excused: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management;
David Turner, Town of Montville.

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:10 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Rick Kanter made a motion to seat East Lyme alternate Don
Danila, seconded by Doug Lawson. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of February 7, 20132 Meeting Minutes: It was noted that under
New Business, agenda item 9b, “Proposed Solar Field, Grassy Hill and Walnut Hill Roads,
East Lyme,” no figures had been given for proposed acres of clearing and the name of
the location. The amount of proposed clearing was 35 acres and the location was
Pigeon Hill. Don Danila made a motion to approve the minutes as corrected, seconded
by Marvin Schutt. All in favor, the motion passes.

4, Treasurer’s Report: Treasurer Dave Turner was not present, so Coordinator Judy
Rondeau reviewed the budget. Marvin Schutt made a motion to accept the treasurer’s
report as presented, seconded by Don Danila. All in favor, report is accepted.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy reviewed her activities for the previous month. Judy
reported that our application to Dominion Foundation was approved. We did not
receive the full amount we requested, but were awarded adequate funds to go forward
with most of our proposed programs. Judy attended the Niantic River Nitrogen Work
Group meeting on Feb. 25", where she updated the group on NRWC activities and the
status of the Colony Road tree filter project. The manure management workshop to the
East Lyme Conservation Commission and Agriculture Committee has been postponed.
ECCD and sister conservation district Connecticut River Coastal Conservation District will

be holding an Equine Manure Management in Killingworth May 4" and East Lyme horse
owners have been invited. A flow gauge has been installed at Latimer Brook in
Montville, just upstream of our water quality sampling site, for a stream flow project
ECCD is conducting with USGS and CT DEEP. This gauge can be used to approximate
nitrate loading at the upper limit of our sampling area. ECCD will be partnering with the
towns of East Lyme and Waterford to install tree filters later this year in downtown
Niantic and on Mago Point in Waterford. Judy participated in an ERT review of the
Darrow Pond (East Lyme) property in March.

6. Old Business:
a. Town Appeals: FEMA has not yet announced grant awards.
b. Audit Committee: Judy distributed the requested materials to the audit
committee.
c. Landmark Development Corp. Application: The East Lyme Planning & Zoning
Committee Superior Court appeal was settled.

7. Subcommittee Updates

a. 501c3: With the approval of the Dominion Foundation grant application, we can
move forward with our 501c3 application.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila updated the Board on the water quality
monitoring on Latimer Brook. Don added a new station at Egret Road, which is
located between Colony Road and RT 1, to try to narrow down the noted
increase in nitrate between those two sites.

c. Education/Outreach Group: It was suggested that we pursue some form of
community outreach regarding nitrate levels in the Grassy Hill Road area,
possibly through the Day or other outlet. Judy noted that the next task on the
Colony Road tree filter project is the development of educational signage.

d. Grant Review/Fundraising Committee: It was previously reported that the
Dominion Foundation grant application was approved. Eric commented that the
Long Island Sound Futures Fund (LISFF) grant round was in swing, and NRWC
should think about possible projects for next year’s grant round. Judy stated she
has been in touch with a teacher at Tyl Middle School in Montville who would
like to do a rain garden project, and she is looking for funding for that project.

8. New Business:
a. Work Plan Review: Board members reviewed the 2012 NRWC Work Plan. The
goals were re-prioritized, based on work that has been completed. Judy will
update the Plan and distribute it for member review.

b. Salem Riparian Corridor Protection: Ruth Savalle informed members that the
Town of Salem is considering extending riparian corridor protection from
streams in the Eight Mile River watershed to all rivers and streams in town. Rick
Kanter suggested someone from NRWC should meet with the Salem Planning &
Zoning Commission to explain how the lower reaches are affected by activities in
the upper watershed. Rick further stated that many people from Montville and
Salem purchase permits to shellfish in the Niantic River. Ruth also said that the
recently vacated Town Planner position is temporarily being covered by Rich
Serra of SECCOG. Doug Lawson stated that Jordan Cove is subject to major
water quality problems, and wondered if NRWC could do anything to assist with
water quality improvements.

c. NRWC Publicity: Marvin suggested that that we should publicize
accomplishments we have made in the last few years. For instance, we could
pick the top two projects we have completed and publicize them. Judy
suggested we could submit regular articles for The Patch.

9. Next meeting: May 2, 2013, 6 pm, at the East Lyme Town Hall.
10. Adjourn: Marvin Schutt made a motion to adjourn at 7:51 PM, seconded by Rick

Kanter. All in favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
