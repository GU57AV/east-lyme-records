Niantic River Watershed Committee

www.nianticriverwatershed.org

Minutes
NRWC Board of Directors FILED IN EAST LYME
June 6, 2013 CONNECTICUT. an

East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 2
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, East Lyme, CT.

Attendees: Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission;
Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management; Ruth Savalle, Town of Salem; Marvin Schutt, Town of
East Lyme; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau,
Watershed Coordinator.

Excused: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission;
Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; John Jasper, Town of East
Lyme; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP.

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:05 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Don Landers made a motion to seat East Lyme alternate Don Danila,
seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of May 2, 2013 Meeting Minutes: A motion was made to approve the
minutes as presented by Marvin Schutt, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Treasurer Dave Turner reviewed the budget. Don Landers made a motion to
accept the treasurer's report as presented, seconded by Marvin Schutt. All in favor, report is
accepted.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy reviewed her activities for the previous month. She has contacted
Adam Welchel of The Nature Conservancy, who led a coastal risk workshop with several
coastal towns including Waterford and East Lyme. Judy and Don D. installed three temperature
loggers in Cranberry Meadow Brook and the tributary from the area where the solar array is
proposed. Judy and her ECCD coworker, Matt Snurkowski assisted with the riparian planting at
Clark Pond in East Lyme. Judy has reserved a booth at Celebrating East Lyme Day. ECCD will be
installing informational signage at Colony Road. Planning has begun for the Waterford & East
Lyme (Mago Point and downtown Niantic) tree filter projects.

6. Old Business:
a. Town Appeals: No new information.
b. Work Plan Review: Judy made revisions based on last month’s discussion. Board
members requested that a section be added to the end of the Plan to document
activities that have been completed.
7. Subcommittee Updates

a. 501c3: Dave Turner reported that prior to submitting the Articles of Incorporation, we
need to amend the By-Laws to reflect some information required in the Articles.
Members reviewed the proposed amendments. Dave Turner made a motion to amend
the By-Laws as presented, seconded by Don Danila. Brief discussion followed regarding
next steps to move the 501c3 process along, including submitting the Articles of
Incorporation and associated fees, and retaining an attorney to assist with the 501c3
application process.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila updated the Board on the water quality monitoring in
Latimer Brook. Don noted that he was able to dispose of used chemicals at the East
Lyme Hazardous Waste Day.

c. Education/Outreach Group: There was discussion about submitting articles to various
news outlets, including local weekly and quarterly newspapers and on-line resources.
Judy will determine the level of interest from the publishers and how often we could
submit articles.

Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: No new information.
Audit Committee: Dave Turner will coordinate a meeting of the committee.

8. New Business:

a. Watershed Progress Report: This agenda item was previously discussed under agenda
item 6b — Work Plan review.

b. Dominion grant task revisions: Judy distributed a revised task list for this grant to reflect
that we were not funded for the full amount we requested. Members approved the
revised task list by consensus.

c. Celebrate East Lyme Day —July 20": Members discussed informational material to be
included this year, including river and bay species for display tanks, water quality
monitoring program and results, horse manure management, tree filter projects, and
riparian buffers.

d. Niantic River Tour: Members discussed a gathering and tour of the river in the summer.
Judy will send out an email with some prospective dates.

e. Dominion Volunteers: Don Landers reported that the Dominion volunteers will be
looking for new volunteer opportunities in 2014 and suggested they could assist with
river bio-assessments (RBV).

9. Next meeting: June 6, 2013, 6 pm; location to be determined.
10. Adjourn: Marvin Schutt made a motion to adjourn at 7:43 PM, seconded by Don Danila. All

in favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
