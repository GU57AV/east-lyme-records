Attendees: Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Doug Lawson, Town of
Waterford; Ruth Savalle, Town of Salem; Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme; Eric Thomas,
CT DEEP; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Judy
Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

Excused: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; John Jasper, Town
of East Lyme; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Marc Lafrance, Town
of Montville; Don Landers, East lyme Harbor Management.

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:01 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Marvin Schutt made a motion to seat Waterford-East Lyme
Shellfish Commission alternate Peter Harris, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor,
motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of November 7, 2013 Meeting Minutes: Dave Turner made a
motion to approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Marvin Schutt. All in favor,
motion passes.

4. Treasurer's Report: Dave Turner reviewed the monthly financial report provided by
ECCD. Ruth Savalle made a motion to accept the report as presented, seconded by
Peter Harris. Allin favor, motion passes.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy prepared and distributed a draft annual summary for Board
review, subsequent to discussion at the November Board of Directors meeting. Judy
also revised the watershed sign location map to include town lines. Eric commented
that CT DOT has a signage requirement for stream crossings and may be a partner for
this project. Doug commented that the shellfish commission has had signs made by the
prison system sign shop. RBV sample collection was concluded in November. Seven
samples were collected from four streams throughout the watershed by 11 volunteers.
Don Danila, John Jasper and Judy re-installed Tidbit temperature loggers in Cranberry
Meadow Brook and the unnamed tributary from the solar power site, and at the
confluence of Latimer Brook and Cranberry Meadow Brook. Judy will be preparing a

new proposal for Dominion Foundation. She is also waiting to hear from CT DEEP
regarding the proposal to the America the Beautiful Forestry grant program for a
demonstration riparian buffer at Mago Point Park. ECCD will be initiating project
planning with the Towns of Waterford and East Lyme for the two-zone tree filter
installation project. Judy will be taking time off at the end of the year.

6. Old Business:

a.
b.

Town Matters: No news this month.

New Directors: Judy has contacted all directors whose terms are due to expire at the
end of December. She has also contacted the municipalities to let them know which

re-appointments are required.
Watershed Signs: This was previously discussed during the Coordinator’s report.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a. 501c3: Dave Turner updated the Board on the status of the 501c3
application. Welles Gilmartin, the CPA who is assisting us with the
application, has suggested some revisions to the Articles of Incorporation.
Dave will work with Welles to revise the language and will distribute the
revised language to Directors for review prior to the January 2014 meeting,
with the goal of having the revision of the Articles approved at the January
meeting, and the application for incorporation submitted immediately
thereafter. Dave Turner made a motion to approve the disbursement of
$200 to the State of Connecticut for the filing fee for the incorporation
application to the state, seconded by Ruth Savalle. Allin favor, motion
passes.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila was not able to attend this evening’s meeting,
but he previously emailed an update to directors of the past month’s
monitoring results.

c. Education/Outreach Group: Directors discussed the possibility of partnering
with CT DEEP to conduct an outreach activity at the Latimer Brook fishway
for World Fishway Day (May 24, 2014). Judy will contact DEEP Fisheries staff
to see if they have any events planned.

d. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Judy previously updated the Board
in the Coordinator’s Report.

8. New Business:

a.

NRWC draft annual report: Judy distributed a draft report for Directors’ review.
The report summarizes the past year’s activities, and is intended to inform
readers about Watershed Committee accomplishments in the past year. Several
suggestions were made, including adding a summary of water sampling results,
and including watershed facts and statistics. Judy will make some revisions and
redistribute the report among Directors.

Quarterly Coordinator payment to ECCD: Dave Turner made a motion to
authorize payment to the Eastern Connecticut Conservation District in the

amount of $1750 from the Dominion Foundation grant for the third quarter of
Coordinators services, seconded by Marvin Schutt. All in favor, motion passes.
c. Mago Point, Waterford: The Board had brief discussion regarding the proposed
land swap at Mago Point between the State (CT DEEP Fisheries Division) and the
Town of Waterford. Eric Thomas reported that an internal review of the
proposal was underway at DEEP, and a decision should be forthcoming.

9. Next meeting: January 2, 2014, 6 pm; East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Ave,
Niantic, CT. ,

10. Adjourn: Marvin Schutt made a motion to adjourn at 7:27 PM, seconded by Ruth
Savalle. Allin favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
