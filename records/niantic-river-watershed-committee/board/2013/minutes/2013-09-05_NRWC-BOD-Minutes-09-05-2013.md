Niantic River Watershed Committee

www.nianticriverwatershed. or;

Minutes
NRWC Board of Directors FILED IN EAST LYME
September 5, 2013 Fale CONNECTION AM
East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1 GL A.

EAST LYME TOWN CLERK

108 Pennsylvania Avenue, East Lyme, CT.

Attendees: Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Don Landers,
East Lyme Harbor Management; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; Ruth Savalle, Town of
Salem; Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford ; David
Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

Excused: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Marc Lafrance, Town
of Montville; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP.

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:03 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: John Jasper made a motion to seat East Lyme alternate Don
Danila, seconded by Marvin Schutt. Allin favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of August 1, 2013 Meeting Minutes: Don Danila made a motion to
approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Ruth Savalle. Allin favor, motion
passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Dave Turner reviewed the budget. Don Danila made a motion to
accept the budget as presented, seconded by Marvin Schutt. All in favor, motion
passes.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy is in the process of verifying RBV locations, getting property
owner permissions and assigning sites to volunteers. Upcoming grant opportunities
include CWA §319 NPS grant program, America the Beautiful (US Forest Service) and the
CT Dept. of Agriculture’s Agriculture Viability grant. Judy would like to submit an
America the Beautiful proposal for a riparian buffer demonstration project in Waterford,
and has approached the Town of Waterford to determine level of interest. Judy
suggested developing a proposal for the Ag Viability grant similar to a project conducted
by Harbor Watch in Westport regarding storm drain contamination in shellfish beds.
Judy has been invited to participate in a Mago Point planning charrette in September.
She will also be taking some water depth measurements in the storm drains at Mago
Point to determine if tidal influence will affect the proposed tree filters.
6. Old Business:
a. Town Matters: No updates.
b. New Directors: There are currently a few vacancies on the Board to fill, including
a director and an alternate for Salem and an alternate for Waterford. Ruth
Savalle spoke with the Montville first selectman, and will ask if any Inland
Wetland Commissioners are interested.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a. 501c3: Dave Turner updated the Board on the status of the 501c3 application.
Doug Lawson spoke with an acquaintance, who had a list of questions regarding
the application for the articles of incorporation. It appears we have addressed
all the major points. Dave and Doug will meet with Doug’s friend to discuss next
steps. A question was raised about the disposition of any balance in the
Committee’s accounts if NRWC dissolved. The Board will revisit this question in
the future.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila updated the Board on the water quality
monitoring in Latimer Brook. Water levels were generally low. Judy previously
updated the Board regarding the status of RBV.

c. Education/Outreach Group: Eric Thomas is preparing an article regarding the
Niantic River watershed for the upcoming issue of the Sound Outlook.

d. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Directors discussed potential grant
proposals , including identifying direct stormwater discharges, stormwater
management at the Grand Street (East Lyme) outlet, in conjunction with a boat
launch improvement project, and DNA tracking of bacteria from stormwater
outfalls to identify and target host species, especially in shellfish bed areas.

e. Audit Committee: The audit committee will try to meet with the treasurer this
month.

8. New Business:
a. CT Department of Agriculture’s Farm (Agriculture) Viability grant program:
Previously discussed.

9. Next meeting: October 3, 2013, 6 pm; East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Ave,
Niantic, CT.

10. Adjourn: Don Danila made a motion to adjourn at 7:09 PM, seconded by John
Jasper. Allin favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
