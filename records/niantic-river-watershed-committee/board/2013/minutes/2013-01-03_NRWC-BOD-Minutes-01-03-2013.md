January 3, 2013 RST LYME TOWN CLERK

914 Hartford Tpk — 2" Fir, Waterford, CT

Attendees: Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville; Don Landers,
East Lyme Harbor Management; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; Ruth Savalle, Town of
Salem; Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP; Chris Tomichek, Town of
Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator, Steve
Harney, Guest.

Excused: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission.

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:01 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Dave Turner made a motion to seat alternates Don Danila and Peter
Harris, seconded by Marvin Schutt. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Discussion —Woodmen’s Land Trust, Inc. Conservation Easement: Steve Harney was present
to discuss the Woodmen’s Land Trust Gurley Brook Preserve. Woodsmen’s Land Trust was
recently awarded $500,000 from CT DEEP’s Open Space Land Acquisition Program for 170
acres of land located just north of the Route 1, I-95 & I-395 intersection, and within the
2,000 foot Route 11 Greenway. Steve thanked the Board for the letter of support NRWC
provided in 2012 supporting the acquisition.

4, Review and Approval of December 6, 2012 Meeting Minutes: John Jasper noted that in item
7c. (Education/Outreach Group update), the minutes inaccurately reflected his report
regarding participation of East Lyme High School students in the water quality monitoring
program. Participation was hampered not by lack of interest but because teachers were not
able to fit our water quality program into their current curriculum. Don Landers made a
motion to approve the minutes as corrected, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, the
motion passes.

4. Treasurer's Report: No treasurer’s report was presented for review, due to illness of the
ECCD office administrator.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy reviewed her activities for the previous month. Subsequent to
discussion at last month’s meeting Judy reviewed and distributed the most current version
of the NRWC By-Laws. Judy and several Board members attended a Volunteer Water
Quality Monitoring meeting sponsored by CT Sea Grant and Long Island Sound Study (LISS).
Following up on water quality sampling results in the Cranberry Meadow Brook watershed,
Judy has been coordinating with the Town of East Lyme to conduct a manure management
workshop with livestock owners and land-use commissioners this winter. Judy also updated
the Board on several ECCD grant projects in the Niantic River watershed.

6. Old Business:

a. Saunders Point Sewer letter: The letter prepared by Rick Kanter in support of future
sewering of Saunders Point has been submitted to the Town of East Lyme.

b. Town Appeals: Don Landers reported that he checked with East Lyme First
Selectman Paul Formica regarding the status of the FEMA grant application. Paul
had no news to report. Eric Thomas noted that the FEMA website had a notice
saying that due to the extremely large number of applications received, the award
announcement had been delayed.

c. Audit Committee: Don Landers reported that he is waiting for a year-end statement
from ECCD.

d. Landmark Development Corp. Application: Marvin Schutt reported that the East
Lyme Sewer & Water Commission refused the requested sewer allocation for the
proposed Landmark development at Oswegatchie Hills.

7. Subcommittee Updates

a. 501c3: Dave Turner stated that there is no progress to report on this item. The next
step is to file the application for the Articles of Incorporation. Judy said she would
try to do that.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila updated the Board on the water quality monitoring
and RBV sampling on Latimer Brook. Don and John Jasper took measurements at a
few extra stations behind St. Matthias Church and the campground to collect
additional data for John’s flux calculations.

c. Education/Outreach Group: Marvin asked Judy how the manure management grant
came about, and how ECCD would go about recruiting horse owners to participate.
John Jasper mentioned that he has simplified his mixing model and is just looking at
water temperature. Eric Thomas stated that CT DEEP has hired a new volunteer
monitoring coordinator and also that DEEP has several temperature loggers that
may be available for loan.

d. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: This subcommittee will be meeting on
January 8", after the Monitoring subcommittee meeting.

8. New Business: Don Landers reported that this season has been the best scalloping
season in the river in recent years. Doug Lawson reported that the oyster yield at the

mouth of the river by the Niantic Bridge has been very good this year. There was brief
discussion about the listed shellfish impairment, and whether inadequate sampling due
to staffing shortages at the CT department of Agriculture Bureau of Aquaculture may be
compounding the problem. Marvin asked how impairments are determined. Eric briefly
described how CT DEEP evaluates surface waters.

9. Next meeting: February 7, 2013, 6 pm, at 914 Hartford Turnpike, Waterford, CT. "

10. Adjourn: Marvin Schutt made a motion to adjourn at 7:34 PM, seconded by Dave
Turner. Allin favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Watershed Coordinator
