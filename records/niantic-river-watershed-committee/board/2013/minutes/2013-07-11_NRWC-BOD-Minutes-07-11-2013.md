1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:01 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Marvin Schutt made a motion to seat East Lyme alternate Don
Danila and WELSCO alternate Peter Harris, seconded by Don Landers. All in favor,
motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of June 6, 2013 Meeting Minutes: Marvin Schutt made a motion to
approve the minutes as presented, seconded by John Jasper. Eric Thomas identified an
error in Item 9 - Next Meeting. The date should have read July 11", not June 6". The
motion on the table was amended to include the correction to Item 9. Allin favor,
motion passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Judy Rondeau reviewed the budget. Don Landers made a motion to
accept the treasurer’s report as presented, seconded by Marvin Schutt. All in favor,
report is accepted.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy reviewed her activities for the previous month. She has
contacted Adam Whelchel of The Nature Conservancy, who led a coastal risk workshop
with several coastal towns including Waterford and East Lyme. Judy will invite Mr.
Whelchel to attend a meeting later this year (September/October). Judy is preparing
materials for the upcoming Celebrate East Lyme Day event (July 20") and would
welcome volunteers to assist with outreach. Informational signage was installed at the
Colony Road tree filter site in June. Judy met with East Lyme and Waterford staff to
discuss the upcoming tree filter installations, also in June. ECCD continues to recruit
horse farms for the Coastal Equine Manure Management grant project.

Town Appeals: No new information.

Work Plan Review: Subsequent to discussion at the June meeting, Judy added a
section at the end of the work plan to document activities that have been
completed. Judy will send a copy of the updated work plan and the brochure to
the watershed municipalities.

Celebrate East Lyme Day: Eric Thomas provided Long Island Sound Study
literature for the event. Ruth Savalle and John Jasper offered to man the booth.
Watershed Progress Report: No new activity on this item.

. Subcommittee Updates

a.

501c3: Directors discussed retaining an attorney or CPA to assist with the 501c3
application review and filing. Doug Lawson will speak with an acquaintance
about assisting with the application.

Monitoring Group: Don Danila updated the Board on the water quality
monitoring in Latimer Brook. Don suggested that it might be beneficial to plot
nitrates versus rainfall to see if there is an association. John Jasper offered to
plot the data. Judy will send out some target dates in August for conducting
stream walks of Latimer Brook and Cranberry Meadow Brook.
Education/Outreach Group: Judy noted that Celebrate East Lyme Day will be a
major outreach opportunity for the Committee. She is planning to focus on
NRWC activities over the last year. Directors noted that The Day article about
the Latimer Brook stream gauge garnered much attention and provided a good
outreach and education.

Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Eric Thomas informed the Board that
CT DEEP has announced the upcoming §319 grant round. The Niantic River
watershed is a high priority due to the approved Niantic River Watershed
Protection Plan and projects that have already been completed in the
watershed. Eric urged the Committee to explore potential projects as identified
in the NRWC Work Plan. Don Landers suggested initiating discussion with East
Lyme engineer Bill Scheer regarding potential projects to address storm drainage
discharging to the Niantic River from the Grand Street boat launch area.

Audit Committee: No new information.

New Business:

a.

Nitrogen Work Group Updates: Members who attended the meeting updated
the Board. Don Landers stated that the lab at UConn/Avery Point has begun
processing water samples collected by Dominion/MEL last year; Jamie Vaudrey
of UConn is developing an eelgrass suitability index model based on data
collection throughout LIS; John Mullaney will update the USGS Niantic River
watershed study with an additional year’s data that he collected, as well as the
groundwater study at Pine Grove; Eric Thomas will be preparing an article on the

is beginning to recolonize.

b. NRWC Picnic — Sat. Aug. 3": Directors discussed details of the picnic, including
when to meet, boats, food and so forth. Judy will coordinate with Marvin and

c. Additional Directors: Salem needs one director and one alternate. Waterford
needs an alternate. Directors will brainstorm about possible candidates and
bring information back to the Board next month.
