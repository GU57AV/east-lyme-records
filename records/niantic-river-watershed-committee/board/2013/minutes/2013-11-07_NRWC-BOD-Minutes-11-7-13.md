          Niantic River Watershed Committee
                                www.nianticriverwatershed.org



Minutes
NRWC Board of Directors
November 7, 2013
East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, East Lyme, CT.

Attendees: Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Rick Kanter,
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management;
Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; Ruth Savalle, Town of Salem; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP;
Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau,
Watershed Coordinator.

Excused: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission; Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville; Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme.

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:02 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Don Landers made a motion to seat East Lyme alternate Don
   Danila, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of October 3, 2013 Meeting Minutes: Don Danila made a motion
   to approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion
   passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Dave Turner reviewed the monthly financial report provided by
   ECCD. Don Landers made a motion to accept the report as presented, seconded by Don
   Danila. All in favor, motion passes.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy and several NRWC members attended the Niantic River
   Nitrogen Work Group meeting on October 28th at UConn-Avery Point. Judy updated
   Work Group members on the status of NRWC water quality sampling. Judy has been
   communicating with Laura Ashburn at East Lyme High School regarding coordinating
   several activities with students, including a clean-up of Latimer Brook near the IGA
   Plaza, use of the Teacher Water Quality Loan Kit, and adoption of an RBV site within
   walking distance of the high school. RBV sampling is going well this year. Eight
   volunteers collected five samples from streams throughout the Niantic River watershed.
   Additionally, the East Lyme Conservation Commission collected samples from two sites
   on Cranberry Meadow Brook. Judy submitted an application to the America the
   Beautiful Urban Forestry grant program for a demonstration riparian buffer project at
   Mago Park on Mago Point in Waterford. The Waterford-East Lyme Tree Filter project
   contract has been signed and the grant is now active. Judy will work with the towns of
   East Lyme and Waterford to plan and install up to ten tree filters in downtown Niantic
   and Mago Point. The ECCD Coastal Manure Management grant is wrapping up.
   Unfortunately, ECCD was not able to recruit any horse owners in East Lyme to
   participate in the program.

   Eric Thomas commented that CT DEEP is interested in using RBV data to identify high
   quality or anticipated high quality stream segments. Don Danila requested we obtain
   verification from CT DEEP of the aquatic insect identification to see how accurate our
   identification from the field cards was.

6. Old Business:
   a. Town Matters: Directors discussed creation of an annual NRWC report identifying
      Committee activities that would be submitted to the member towns. Judy will
      identify a simple format and put together a draft for directors to review. Don
      Landers stated that discussion between East Lyme and USGS regarding the re-
      establishment of the Latimer Brook stream gauge is proceeding.
   b. New Directors: Directors reviewed the current term expirations to identify which
      terms are expiring soon. Judy will send letters to the respective municipal CEOs
      informing them that reappointments are upcoming, and are supported by the Board.
      Dave Turner offered to follow up with Montville land-use commissioners. Judy will
      also contact the Salem first selectman regarding filling board vacancies for that
      town.

7. Subcommittee Updates:
   a. 501c3: Dave Turner updated the Board on the status of the 501c3 application.
      All is proceeding well, and the application should be ready to be filed shortly.
   b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila updated the Board on the water quality
      monitoring in Latimer Brook. Don and Marvin visited the Aces High Campground
      on Rt. 161 and obtained permission to install temperature loggers in Cranberry
      Meadow and Latimer Brooks. This will allow John Jasper to calculate relative
      flow contribution from Cranberry Meadow Brook, and in turn estimate nitrate
      contribution from Cranberry Meadow Brook. Judy updated the group on the N-
      Sink presentation at the Nitrogen Work Group meeting. N-Sink is a decision
      support tool that predicts relative nitrogen removal from a waterbody based on
      adjacent land type (wetland, riparian buffer, etc.) that is being developed by URI
      and CLEAR. Don Danila submitted NRWC water quality data to the developers
      for use in calibrating the model. Directors discussed preparing a draft water
      quality monitoring report to share with municipalities and stakeholders.
   c. Education/Outreach Group: Judy previously discussed communication with Laura
      Ashburn at East Lyme High School. Eric Thomas informed directors about an
      upcoming webcast by EPA on engaging water quality monitor volunteers. Judy
      proposed rain garden and riparian buffer installation workshops for next spring,
      continuing on work already started by the Committee, and possibly engaging the
      assistance of local nurseries/landscapers.
   d. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Judy reviewed a few upcoming grant
      opportunities, including Dominion Foundation and EPOC. She also commented
      the CT Department of Agriculture Agricultural Viability grant deadline was
      extended to December. Eric Thomas commented on how to utilize/leverage
      raised funds, for instance, as match for other funding sources such as the CWA
      §319 program.
   e. Audit Committee: The audit committee met in October and reviewed financial
      information currently managed by ECCD. This information will be included in
      ECCD annual audit. Until NRWC has been awarded non-profit status and
      established its own bank accounts, no further action is needed. Don Landers
      noted that once we have received 501c3 status, we will need to establish a
      membership committee

8. New Business:
   a. Watershed Signs: John Jasper met with Bill Scheer, the East Lyme Town Engineer,
      to discuss the installation of watershed signs. Bill was generally supportive of
      the idea. Judy prepared a map showing potential sign locations at major road
      crossings. Eric shared some information from CT DOT regarding sign standards
      for stream crossings. We will continue to gather information regarding this
      topic, including sign size and cost, CT DOT stream crossing signs, and gauge
      support from the watershed towns for sign installation.

9. Next meeting: December 5, 2013, 6 pm; East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania
   Ave, Niantic, CT.

10. Adjourn: Dave Turner made a motion to adjourn at 7:38 PM, seconded by Don
    Danila. All in favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,



Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
