October 26, 2011 @ 7 pm
914 Hartford Tpk - 2" Fir, Waterford, CT

Attendees: Elbert Burr, Salem; Don Danila, East Lyme; John Jasper, East Lyme; Rick Kanter,
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management; Doug
Lawson, Waterford; Ruth Savalle, Salem; Marvin Schutt, East Lyme; Chris Tomichek, Waterford;
Dave Turner, Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator

1. Welcome: Judy Rondeau opened the meeting at 7:07 pm.

2. Watershed Coordinator’s Report: Judy welcomed and thanked all Directors for agreeing to
serve on the Board. In lieu of the Coordinator’s Report, Judy provided a brief summary of
the Watershed Committee activities from 2008 to the present.

3. Review and Acceptance:

a. By-laws: The Directors reviewed the draft By-laws (as approved by the Board
Development Committee on 11/9/10). Judy will revise the draft document per the
discussion and distribute to Directors for review and action at the next meeting. Several
procedural questions arose, including whether or not appointees need to be sworn in by
their CEO, how to determine which Director will be assigned the initial short term, in
order to stagger appointments, and how to deal with habitual absenteeism.

b. Mission Statement: Judy will revise the Mission Statement per Directors review and
comments.

c. Logo: There were no changes proposed to the logo.

4. Election of Officers: As several Directors are awaiting appointment, and elections cannot be
held until the By-laws have been adopted, it was the consensus to table this item until the
next meeting.

5. Review Plan of Work: Judy briefly described the Plan of Work. Directors will review the
Plan for discussion at the next meeting.

6. Discussion Regarding Coordinator Funding: Judy reviewed two grant opportunities she
recently received. The first, due November 15" is through the Community Foundation of
Eastern Connecticut, and could tie paid staff time to a specific task, such as one of the
outreach and education activities. The second, due December 1%, is offered through the
Office of Policy and Management (OPM), and could possibly directly fund the coordinator

position. Judy will draft applications for each grant. The Board also discussed the current
level of funding for the coordinator position, and approaching the watershed towns for

potential support.

7. Next Meeting: December 7, 2011 at 6 pm, at 914 Hartford Tpk — 2" Fir, Waterford, CT. Judy
will draft a list of 2012 meeting dates for review.

8. The meeting adjourned at 9:28 PM.
