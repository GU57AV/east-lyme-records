1. Call to Order: Chair Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:01pm.

3. Review and Approval of June 7, 2018 meeting minutes: Don Landers made a motion to approve

4. Treasurer’s Report: Presented by Don Danila. Ralph Bates made a motion to accept the
Treasurer’s Report as presented, seconded by Don Landers. All in favor, motion passes.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy Rondeau reviewed her activities for the past month. Don Danila
noted that Judy received the Lesa Meng Aquatic Conservation Award from the American
Fisheries Society — Southern New England Chapter at their summer meeting in June. Judy
thanked Don for the nomination. Chris Tomichek announced that her application to the
Kleinschmidt Foundation for $10,000 to support the watershed coordinator was approved.

6. DEEP Liaison Report: Eric provided the Board with an update of DEEP-related information,
including an update on the Connecticut Water Plan, a stormwater discharge complaint at the
Waterford Speed Bowl, a review of the water quality assessment as pertains to Niantic River
watershed waterbodies, and the status of the 2018 bi-annual statewide water quality
assessment.

Don Landers made a motion to enter into the MOU as presented, seconded by John Jasper.
