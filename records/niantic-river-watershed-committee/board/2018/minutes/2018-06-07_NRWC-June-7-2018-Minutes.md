1. Call to Order: Chair Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:03pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: No alternates were present. Introductions were made to Kerry Taylor and
Melissa Bezanson, the new representatives from the Town of Salem.

3. Discussion regarding the re-activation of the USGS streamflow gage on the Latimer Brook: This
item was postponed pending the arrival of all discussion participants. Upon arrival of invited
guests, John Jasper provided an overview of the USGS stream gauge operation. John stated that
continuation of the gauge is necessary for NRWC to determine nutrient loading to the Niantic
River from the Latimer Brook watershed. Tim Sargent discussed several options available for
operating the gauge, ranging from full operation by USGS to the installation of a staff gauge and
establishment of a rating curve. Discussion about potential funding schemes followed.

a. Pine Grove Rain Garden: Sean Slusarz, an Eagle Scout from East Lyme Troop 240 was
present to discuss the installation of a rain garden on Laurel Street at Pine Grove as part of
his Eagle Scout project. This rain garden is being installed in partnership with the Pine Grove
Association and ECCD, utilizing funds from the NRWC Rain Garden Initiative and ECCD’s 100
Rain Barrels/100 Rain Garden project (funded through the NFWF LISFF). Sean provided an
