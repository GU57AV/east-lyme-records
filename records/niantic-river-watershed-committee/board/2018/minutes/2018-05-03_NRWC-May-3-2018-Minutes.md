Niantic River Watershed Committee, Inc.

www.nianticriverwatershed. org

Minutes | +3: OAM
NRWC Board of Directors (VLA S{ 20 i_K S40 mn
May 3, 2018 6:00pm VAL
East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1 EAST Lou TOWN CLERK

108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, CT

Attendees: Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; Ralph Bates, Town of East Lyme; Don Danila, Town
of East Lyme; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission (WELSCO); Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Don
Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management and Shellfish Commission; David Bliven, Town of
Montville; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP; and Sam Alexander, Southeastern CT Council of Governments
(guest).

Excused: Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Judy Rondeau, ECCD.

Absent: None.
1. Call to Order: Chair Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:03pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Don Landers made a Motion to seat Ralph Bates for the vacant Salem
seat, seconded by Don Danila. All in favor, Motion passes. Introductions were made for the benefit
of two new meeting attendees. C. Tomichek welcomed new Committee member David Bliven who
is representing the Town of Montville (along with Committee member D. Turner). C. Tomichek
shared the resignation notice from Ruth Savalle, effective immediately. C. Tomichek requests that
J. Rondeau draft a letter to R. Savalle for his several years of service to the Committee. D. Turner
made a Motion to pursue a service recognition plaque for R. Savalle, using the previous plaque
template and under the expense cost threshold set previously by the Committee; seconded by R.
Bates. All in favor, Motion passes. C. Tomichek stated that with Ruth’s resignation, the Committee
has a vacant Secretary position that needs to be refilled and is seeking Committee member interest
to step up for consideration of that position. This will be added to the June, 2018 meeting agenda.

3. Review and Approval of the April 5, 2018 Meeting Minutes: D. Landers made a Motion to
accept the minutes as presented, seconded by D. Turner. All in favor, Motion passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Presented by Don Danila. Motion by R. Bates to accept the Treasurer’s
Report as presented, seconded by D. Turner. All in favor, Motion passes.

5. Coordinator’s Report: C. Tomichek read the monthly submitted by J. Rondeau for activities for
the past month. Discussion was held on the scheduled May 17" WELSCO meeting for aquaculture
operation policy under their jurisdiction including in the Niantic River. Committee members are
encouraged to attend the meeting at 6:30pm at Waterford Town Hall.
6. DEEP Liaison Report: E. Thomas provided brief comments about the recent MS4 municipal
stormwater training workshop held at East Lyme Town Hall that was well received by 40+ attendees
who toured several installed stormwater practices in the Niantic village area.

7. Old Business:

a. Town Matters: D. Turner stated that a Montville landowner within the Niantic River
watershed will be removing her horses from the property that has a chronic eutrophic small
pond discharging to Powers Brook before entering upper Latimer Brook. The landowner
indicated interest in the NRWC Rain Barrel/Rain Garden project and D. Turner will share
information with J. Rondeau to pursue further.

b. Review and Update of By-Laws: D. Danila reviewed the final draft updates to Committee
members. P. Harris made a Motion to approve the by-laws as revised; seconded by D.
Landers. All if favor, Motion passes. D. Danila will distribute clean copies of the revised by-
laws to each of the four Niantic River watershed town Chief Elected Officers with a cover
letter from the Committee’s Chair. D. Landers recommended action to update the NRWC
official letterhead to reflect current Committee members and affiliations; D. Landers to
pursue this action with J. Rondeau.

c. Niantic River Watershed Plan Update Contract: C. Tomichek reported her signing the DEEP
CWA Section 319 grant funded agreement and associated forms and that E. Thomas had
hand delivered them back to the DEEP Business office for processing. The grant agreement
may be fully approved by the NRWC June meeting to allow the project subcommittee to
start work on the project’s Request for Proposals (RFP) for qualified contractor services.

d. Land Conservation Update: E. Thomas recently communicated with Whit Beals of New
England Forestry Foundation (NEFF) about an update on their Niantic Headwaters Preserve
project that roughly straddles the East Lyme/Waterford/Montville area. The Headwaters of
the Niantic Community Forest Phase 1 property acquisition (200 ac) is now completed, and a
Phase 2 property acquisition (185+/- ac) is now pursuing a final acquisition phase. W. Beals
offered to update the Committee more regularly, as it may assist Committee discussions
about supporting a regional land conservation strategy with desirable watershed protection
objectives. E. Thomas distributed a NEFF Niantic Headwater Project area map indicating
active and proposal projects (Headwaters plus the proposed Silver Falls Community Forest)
within a 2,200 acre continuous forest block area that includes Willys Meadow Brook, Gurley
Brook and portions of Latimer Brook. Meeting guest S. Alexander, SCCOG Planner indicated
a preliminary interest by COG staff to consider some watershed basin planning support (and
future pursuit of funding assistance) that could include three main freshwater tributaries
within the Niantic River watershed.

8. Subcommittee Updates:
a. Monitoring Group: D. Danila stated that he will continue conducting wet weather nitrate

nutrient sampling event around the next big rain storm in June. D. Danila reported he and J.
Rondeau will be retrieving the previously installed stream water temperature loggers to
send to DEEP for downloading of valuable data. D. Danila also reported that this season’s
Latimer Brook fishway is passing a sizable increase in migrating alewife fish (12,500 to date)
and that DEEP has switched to an electronic counter to handle the large influx of passing
fish. J. Jasper provided attending Committee members with a graphic handout and verbal
interpretation of nutrient loading (and changes, or flux over time) into the Niantic River. J.
Jasper has been communicating with Tim Sargent of the U.S. Geological Survey New England
office about options to reactivate the USGS Latimer Brook stream gaging station. Valuable
water flow measurements could then be complementary to the nutrient water sampling
work being done by the Monitoring subcommittee — leading to useful outreach information
to local community land use decision makers and managers working to protect and restore
the water quality and resources of the Niantic River and Bay. Some funding support may be
offered through USGS, but a cost share match of approximately $11,000 is needed to cover
operational costs for one water year and formalize an agreement with USGS. Committee
members discussed possible grant or other funding sources to pursue.

b. Education/Outreach Group: D. Danila discussed the latest DEEP Sound Outlook newsletter
which contained several relevant short articles, most with focus in East Lyme, about the
Niantic River and Bay watershed. Committee members provided consensus for D. Landers
to take the modified newsletter digital file provided by DEEP to print color copies for local
distribution at the summer Celebrate East Lyme Day and at town hall counters, and maybe
through the NWRC website.

c. Grant Review/Fundraising: This item was reviewed in the Coordinator’s Report.

. Membership Subcommittee: Nothing to Report.
e. NRWPP Update/RFP Subcommittee: Activity will begin when the 319 contract is executed,
possibly in early June. A Plan Update subcommittee will then need to start up the RFP
development process for contractor services.

9. New Business:
a. Announcement was made for the Celebrate East Lyme Day event and anticipated NRWC
booth on Saturday, July 21 from 3:00-9:00pm. Committee volunteers are needed to staff the
booth at different periods during the event — contact J. Rondeau to coordinate scheduling.

10. Public Comments: S. Alexander indicated interest to participate as SCCOG staff in future
Committee members on a semi-regular business, which was well received by the Committee.

11. The next meeting will be held on June 7, 2018 at 7:00 PM in East Lyme Town Hall, 108
Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic, CT.

12. Adjournment: A Motion was made by D. Landers at 7:25 pm to adjourn, seconded by R. Bates.
Allin favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Eric Thomas
Filling in for the vacant Secretary position
