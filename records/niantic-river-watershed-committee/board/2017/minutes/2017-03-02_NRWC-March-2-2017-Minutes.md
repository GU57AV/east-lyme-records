108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, CT EAST LYME TOWN CLERK

Attendees: Ralph Bates, Town of East Lyme; Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; John Jasper, Town of
East Lyme; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Ruth Savalle, Town of Salem;
Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; David Turner, Town of Montville; Dan Mullins, Waterford; Eric
Thomas, CT DEEP and Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

Excused: Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor
Management; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford.

Absent: Tom Bowen, Town of Montville.
1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:04pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: Don Danila made a Motion to seat Ralph Bates for Don Landers and Rick
Kanter for Peter Harris, seconded by David Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Guest Speakers: Chris Bellucci and Ansel Aarrestad from DEEP presented an informative report
regarding the results of recent fish surveys of Latimer Brook and the resulting recommendation
for habitat impairment listing, including a Latimer Brook Stressor Map.

4, Review and Approval of January 5, 2017 and February 2, 2017 Meeting Minutes: Motion to
approve minutes as corrected by Don Danila, seconded by Rick Kanter. All in favor, motion

passes.

5. Treasurer’s Report: Presented by Don Danila. Motion to approve as presented by David Turner,
seconded by Ralph Bates. All in favor, motion passes.

6. Coordinator’s Report: Presented by Judy Rondeau and then discussed by Board.

7. DEEP Liaison Report: Presented by Eric Thomas and then discussed by Board. Eric noted that
Stony Brook and Latimer Brook have been proposed on the draft 2016 303d list for recreation
impairments due to bacteria. He and Judy will follow up regarding the data that led to these
recommendations.
8. Old Business:
a. Town Matters: Dave Turner and Dan Mullins will try to identify potential locations for rain
gardens in Montville.
b. Watershed Signs: Nothing to Report
c, Bi-annual Plan of Work Update: Motion to table the Plan of Work discussion to the April 6,
2017 meeting by Don Danila, seconded by Rick Kanter. All in favor motion passes.

9. Subcommittee Updates:
a. Monitoring Group: Don Danila presented the monthly water quality monitoring report. Don

will be continuing monthly testing until March of 2017. Then the focus will change to the
collection of wet weather samples in Latimer Brook.

b. Education/Outreach Group: Covered under Coordinator’s Report. It was suggested that
NRWC could approach the Town about placing educational watershed information at the
town-owned kiosk at Darrow Pond.

c. Grant Review/Fundraising: Covered-under Coordinator’s Report. —

. Membership Subcommittee: No report.

e. NRWPP Update/RFP Subcommittee: The subcommittee will not meet until the contract has
been executed. Eric reported that contract preparation is moving forward.

f. Audit Committee: Ralph Bates, Don Landers, David Turner met prior to the Board meeting.
Don Danila will provide necessary financial records. A summary will be prepared for the
April meeting.

10. New Business:

a. Discussion regarding the proposed high speed rail service: Discussed links in email. Don
Danila will write a letter, to go on the record about the devastation that would be caused by
the relocation of the Rail line North of the I-95. Dave Turner shared information about the
CT Wetland Fee In-Lieu Program (an Army Corps program administered by AudubonCT).

11. Public Comments: None.

12. The next meeting: April 6, 2017 PM, East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic, CT
13. Adjournment: Ruth Savalle made a Motion to adjourn at 7:48pm, seconded by David Turner.
Allin favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Ruth L. Savalle
Secretary
