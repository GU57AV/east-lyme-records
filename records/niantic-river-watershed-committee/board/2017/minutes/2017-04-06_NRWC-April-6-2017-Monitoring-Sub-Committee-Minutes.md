Attendees: Don Danila, Maureen Fitzgerald, John Jasper, Don Landers, Chris Tomichek, Judy
Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

1. Welcome - Watershed Coordinator Judy Rondeau opened the meeting at 5:08 pm.
2. Review of January 5, 2017 Meeting Minutes —-The minutes were approved by consensus.

3. Water Quality Monitoring:

a. Water Quality Monitoring Program review - Don Danila reviewed the March water
quality data collected from Latimer and Cranberry Meadow Brooks. As of April, we will
be switching to event-based sampling. Discussion followed regarding sample hold times,
collecting pre- and post-event samples to document potential rise and recession of
nitrate levels, and where to sample. Don D. will prepare a sampling plan and distribute it
to committee members for review.

b. DEEP Latimer Brook Fish Survey Discussion - Committee members reviewed and
discussed information shared by Chris Bellucci of DEEP in his presentation at the March
2™ Board of Directors meeting.

c. Temperature logger deployment — The Committee agreed to deploy temperature
loggers in Latimer Brook if it will assist DEEP with their habitat assessments. Judy will
contact DEEP staff to find out where to deploy the loggers.

d. Flow gauge deployment — Chris offered to loan NRWC Kleinschmidt monitoring
equipment to assist with flow monitoring. Brief discussion followed regarding the best
place to install a water level logger. It was suggested we contact USGS for guidance.

e. Data assessment/evaluation/report — Committee members will review Latimer Brook
water quality data and prepare a summary report. There was brief discussion about
whether and/or how our data was being incorporated into Dr. Jamie Vaudrey’s Niantic
River data synthesis.

4. Partner Reports: Maureen Fitzgerald, Town of Waterford, noted that she could adapt her
municipal monitoring program to complement NRWC’s monitoring program.

5. Other Business: None.

6. Next meeting: January 4, 2018, 5:00PM, East Lyme Town Hall.
7. Adjourn: The meeting adjourned at 5:56 pm.
