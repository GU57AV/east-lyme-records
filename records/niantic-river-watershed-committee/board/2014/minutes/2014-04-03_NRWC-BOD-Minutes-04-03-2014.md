Niantic River Watershed Committee

www.nianticriverwatershed.org

FILED IN EAST LYME

Minutes CONNECTICUT
NRWC Board of Directors Tolyto2o.tt aT ae!
April 3, 2014

East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 2
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, East Lyme, CT.

Attendees: Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme
Harbor Management; Ruth Savalle, Town of Salem; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; David
Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed Coordinator.

Excused: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Peter Harris,
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Marc Lafrance,
Town of Montville; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme; Eric
Thomas, CT DEEP.

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:11 pm.
2. Seating of Alternates: No alternates were seated.

3. Review and Approval of March 6, 2014 Meeting Minutes: Don Landers made a motion to
approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Ruth Savalle. Allin favor, motion passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Dave Turner reviewed the March financial report, and reported that he
had resolved some questions he had regarding the format of the report. ECCD has revised
the report format to reflect beginning and ending balances. Don Landers made a motion to
approve the treasurer’s report as presented, seconded by Ruth Savalle. Allin favor, motion
passes.

5. Coordinator’s Report: Judy reported that NRWC will be partnering with the Millstone
Environmental Lab to conduct a stream walk of Latimer Brook in the Flanders section of
East Lyme. She will also pursue a clean-up of Latimer Brook with East Lyme High School
students. She reported that the Monitoring Group will be meeting April 8", and will
evaluate the current Latimer Brook monitoring program. Judy has been informed that we
will be receiving funding from the Dominion Foundation again this year. She has not heard
about the status of the EPOC grant application that was submitted in February, but will
follow up with the EPOC program coordinator. She is looking for a small grant to install a
rain garden or similar BMP on the Flanders area as part of the lower Latimer Brook
streamwalk project. ECCD received the grant contract for the Mago Point riparian buffer
planting this month. Judy has contacted the New London Master Gardeners program to see
if any master gardeners would like to participate in the project. ECCD has begun planning
for the Waterford-East Lyme Tree Filters project. The Mago Point installation will be
conducted as part of the Mago Point redevelopment. Waterford has applied for STEAP
funding to conduct that project. Dave Turner asked if NRWC could propose a project in
Flanders and how it might be funded. Judy suggested that once we obtain our nonprofit
designation, we could apply for funding through the CWA §319 program.

6. Old Business:

a.
b.

Town Matters: There was no update on the Latimer Brook stream gauge.

Watershed Signs: Judy distributed several draft versions of the sign prepared by John
Jasper for review. Members discussed the inclusion of scallops and/or ospreys on the
signs. This will be revisited at the May meeting.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a.

501c3: Dave Turner provided an update on the status of our filings. We applied for
and received an Employer's Tax ID number; however there was an error in our name
(“River” omitted). A request for correction has been submitted. We also submitted
required documents to the CT Secretary of State to Incorporate as a Non-Stock
corporation and filed our first report. However, we missed one required signature
on the paperwork. That has been remedied, and we are now waiting for the final
notification. Once we receive that, we can file the application for 501c3 status.
Monitoring Group: Don Danila provided a review of the March water quality
sampling via email.

Education/Outreach Group: Reviewed in Coordinator’s Report. It was suggested we
reach out to local news media to cover the Millstone Lab work day.

Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Reviewed in Coordinator’s Report. There
was brief discussion about recent fines levied against Unilever for illegal discharges
at their (now closed) Clinton facility. The question was raised whether there might
be SEP funds available from that fine.

8. New Business:

a.

Quarterly Coordinator reimbursement to ECCD: Dave Turner made a motion to
reimburse ECCD for Coordinator services for the first quarter of 2014 (fourth and final
quarter of the 2013 Dominion Foundation grant), seconded by Don Landers. All in
favor, the motion passes.

9. Next meeting: May 1, 2014, 6 pm; East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic,
CT.

10. Adjourn: Ruth Savalle made a motion to adjourn at 6:52 PM, seconded by Dave Turner.
All in favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

apoy Corkhow.

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
