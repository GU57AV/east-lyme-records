Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management; Doug Lawson, Town of

1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:03 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: A motion was made by Don Landers to seat Don Danila for John
Jasper, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of June 5, 2014 Meeting Minutes: Don Landers made a motion to
approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Dave Turner. Allin favor, motion
passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Dave Turner reviewed the June financial report. Don Danila made a
motion to approve the financial report as presented, seconded by Rick Kanter. Allin
favor, motion passes.

5. Coordinator’s Report: This year’s Celebrate East Lyme Day is scheduled for July 19%,
from 3-9 PM. Anyone who would like to “man” the booth is welcome, as are ideas and
materials for displays. Don Landers confirmed that MEL will supply critters and tanks for
display. Judy has been participating on the steering committee for a Volunteer Water
Quality Monitoring Summit that will be held on Friday, July 25" at Goodwin College in
East Hartford. NRWC is being listed as a sponsor. Don Danila and John Jasper will both
be presenting on the results of water quality sampling in the Latimer Brook watershed.
Judy will be presenting on the “how-to’s” of bacteria sampling. The Niantic River Kayak
Regatta is scheduled for August 16"". The organizing committee has asked if NRWC
would like to set up an information board as part of the education/outreach
component. Judy has ordered three Hobo temperature loggers from Onset, Inc. and
requested three loggers from DEEP. DEEP will contact Judy when loggers are available.
Over the next month, Judy will begin to plan Latimer Brook stream walks. Don Landers
commented that the Dominion Millstone Environmental Stewardship team may be
interested in participating in streamwalks. Judy has not heard back from East Lyme High
School regarding a potential stormwater management project there. The Mago Park
riparian buffer planting was completed in June. Dan Mullins has been watering the
planting every few days, and everything looks good. Planning on the Waterford and
East Lyme tree filter installations is proceeding. Eric Thomas commented that CT DEEP
anticipates posting the 2014 CWA §319 RFP in the near future. Members reviewed the
draft newsletter and water quality monitoring summary and made a few suggestions/
revisions.

6. Old Business:

a. Town Matters: Don Landers noted that funding for the Latimer Brook stream gauge
has been approved. Dave Turner asked what the Committee can do to support the
Town of Montville.

b. Watershed Signs: Judy Rondeau reported that plans to install watershed signs at key
points along main roads throughout the watershed have come somewhat to a
standstill. Marybeth Hart of CT DEEP had contacted CT DOT to determine next
steps, and DOT appointed her the “point person” for the project. Judy has not
contacted Marybeth to determine if she is willing and able to take on this
unexpected charge.

c. Celebrate East Lyme Day: Previously discussed in the Coordinators’ report.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a. 501c3: Dave Turner reported that our filing with the Secretary of the State has
been accepted and we are now officially incorporated. There was a correction
needed on the tax payer identification number paperwork. Dave has submitted
the necessary paperwork, and we are waiting for the correction to be made. We
will continue to work with the accountant to prepare and submit the 501c3
application. There was brief discussion regarding transitioning accounts from
ECCD to NRWC. It was decided to continue to partner with ECCD until the 501c3
process has been completed. Several members suggested we prepare an
organizational guide with important dates and procedures that need to be
completed annually, such as the filing of annual reports with the State.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila reviewed the June water quality sampling results.
Water temperatures were generally only slightly warmer than in May. Dissolved
oxygen was at or near saturation levels. Nitrate concentrations increased
slightly at Station 1 on Latimer Brook and at Oil Mill Brook, and decreased
slightly or remained similar at all other Latimer Brook stations, Cranberry
Meadow Brook and Stony Brook. Don presented the powerpoint on Stream
Water Quality Monitoring that he has prepared for the WQM conference later
this month. Members shared comments and observations. Don also clicked
through John Jasper’s presentation on stream temperature mixing and nutrient
flux.

Education/Outreach Group: Reviewed in the Coordinator’s Report.

Report.
