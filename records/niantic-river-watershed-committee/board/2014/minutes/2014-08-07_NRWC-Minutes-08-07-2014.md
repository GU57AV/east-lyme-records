Minutes 292014 ATL YO) GPM

NRWC Board of Directors

August 7, 2014

East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, East Lyme, CT.

AST LYME TOWN GLERK

Attendees: Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management;
Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford; Chris Tomichek, Town of Waterford; Judy Rondeau,
Watershed Coordinator; Dan Mullins, Waterford resident.

Excused: Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP;
David Turner, Town of Montville; Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme; Ruth Savalle, Town of
Salem.

Absent: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville.
1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:02 pm.

2. Seating of Alternates: A motion was made by Doug Lawson to seat Don Danila for
Marvin Schutt, seconded by John Jasper. Allin favor, motion passes. A motion was
made by Don Landers to seat Peter Harris for Rick Kanter, seconded by Don Danila.
Allin favor, motion passes.

3. Review and Approval of July 10, 2014 Meeting Minutes: Don Landers made a motion to
approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Don Danila. All in favor, motion passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Judy Rondeau reviewed the July financial report. Don Landers made
a motion to approve the financial report as presented, seconded by John Jasper. Allin
favor, motion passes.

5. Coordinator's Report: Judy and Ruth Savalle attended Celebrate East Lyme Day on July
19", The Millstone Environmental Lab generously donated aquatic displays again this
year, which were very popular. Don Danila and John Jasper presented talks about our
water quality monitoring program at the Volunteer Water Quality Summit at Goodwin
College in East Hartford on July 25". Both talks were very well received. NRWC was
listed as a sponsor. The Niantic River Regatta is scheduled for August 16". The
organizers have asked NRWC to provide outreach material. Judy queried board
members as to whether they would be interested in ordering order NRWC logo-wear for
outreach or other events. Judy purchased Onset Hobo temperature loggers, which
arrived in July. We were also able to borrow three units from DEEP Marine Division and
three units from the DEEP water quality monitoring program. Judy and Don Danila
installed the nine units in the unnamed tributary from Pigeon Hill (the Antares solar
array site), Cranberry Meadow Brook, and Latimer Brook. Judy plans to schedule a
streamwalk training in the near future to get started on Latimer Brook streamwalks.
There will also be an RBV workshop scheduled through The Last Green Valley Volunteer
Water Quality Monitoring program towards the end of August. The RFP for the 2015
§319 grant program is expected to be announced soon. Judy will be meeting with Chris
Donnelly of the DEEP Forestry Division to get final approval of the Mago Park
Demonstration Coastal Riparian Buffer project. The informational signage arrived on
August 7", and was installed by Judy and Dan Mullins. Bill Scheer of the Town of East
Lyme has indicated to Judy that planning for the Niantic Streetscape is proceeding and
construction, which will include the installation of stormwater tree filters, will begin
after Labor Day. Dennis Goderre announced that the Town of Waterford has been
awarded STEAP grant funding, and that planning for the Mago Point Redevelopment,
including stormwater tree filters, will begin soon.

6. Old Business:
a. Town Matters: There were no updates regarding town matters.
b. Watershed Signs: There has been no action on the watershed signs.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a. 501c3: Dave Turner and Doug Lawson have had discussions about next steps
and will contact Welles Gilmartin to move the application process along.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila reviewed the July water quality sampling results.
He will be going out with Maureen Fitzgerald in the next week. Now that bridge
construction on Route 1 is complete, Don may move the Stony Brook sampling
site from Waterford Parkway North to Route 1. There was also brief discussion
about conducting isotopic analysis on the Cranberry Meadow Brook and Latimer
Brook nitrogen samples.

c. Education/Outreach Group: Reviewed in the Coordinator’s Report. A discussion
was held regarding the need for updates to the website. A suggestion was made
to contact local high schools to identify tech-savvy students who can assist with
the necessary updates.

d. Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Reviewed in the Coordinator’s
Report.

8. New Business:

a. Review of Statewide Bacteria TMDL — Estuary 14: East Lyme/Waterford draft
document. A request has been made by CT DEEP for a review of the draft TMDL
for East Lyme/Waterford. Members agreed that the document was not well
organized and difficult to read.
9. Next meeting: September 4, 2014, at 6 pm, at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108
Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic, CT.

10. Adjourn: Don Danila made a motion to adjourn at 7:14 PM, seconded by John

Jasper. Allin favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
