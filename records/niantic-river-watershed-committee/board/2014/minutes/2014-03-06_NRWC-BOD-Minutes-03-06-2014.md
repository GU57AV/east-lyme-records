1. Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:10 pm.
2. Seating of Alternates: No alternates were seated.

3. Review and Approval of February 6, 2014 Meeting Minutes: Marvin Schutt made a
motion to approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor,
motion passes.

4. Treasurer’s Report: Dave Turner reconciled the financial report from last month. Don
Landers made a motion to approve the treasurer’s report as presented, seconded by
Marvin Schutt. All in favor, motion passes.

5. Coordinator's Report: Chris Tomichek read the Coordinator’s Report into the record in
the coordinator’s absence.

6. Old Business:
a. Town Matters: No report
b. Watershed Signs: No report
c. New Directors: No Activity on appointment of new directors
7. Subcommittee Updates:

a. 501c3: Dave Turner provided an update on the status of our filings. We applied
for and received an Employer’s Tax ID number. However, they have the name
wrong. They listed us as Niantic Watershed Committee (left out River). Dave has
submitted a change of name request to the IRS but that could take several
months to process. We also submitted required documents to the CT Secretary
of State to Incorporate as a Non-Stock corporation and filed our first report.
We've not received a response from the Secretary of State office yet.

b. Monitoring Group: Don Danila provided a review of previous month’s water
quality sampling via email.

c. Education/Outreach Group: Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: No
Report.

8. New Business: Art Carlson (East Lyme Conservation Commission) provided an over-
view on the Commission’s initiatives and the importance of water in the Town of
East Lyme and requested the monitoring data that has been collected on Latimer
Brook. All in attendance agreed that water is a significant resource to preserve and
protect. The Board is happy to share its monitoring data with other Commissions
and suggested that Art reach out to Don Danila for the data.

9. Next meeting: April 3, 2014, 6 pm; East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Ave,
Niantic, CT.

10. Adjourn: Marvin Schutt made a motion to adjourn at 7:20 PM, seconded by Don
Landers. All in favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Don Landers
