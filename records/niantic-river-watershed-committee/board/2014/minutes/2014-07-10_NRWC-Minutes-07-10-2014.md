Minutes FILED IN EAST LYME
Niantic River Watershed Committee CONNECTICUT
Monitoring Subcommittee Meeting

July 10, 2014, 5-6 pm

East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 2
108 Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic, CT

Attendees: Don Danila, Don Landers, Eric Thomas, Chris Tomichek, Dave Turner, Judy Rondeau,
Watershed Coordinator.

1. Welcome - Watershed Coordinator Judy Rondeau opened the meeting at 5:07 pm.

2. Review of January 14 and April 8, 2014 Meeting Minutes — The minutes were approved by
consensus.

3. Water Quality Monitoring:

a. Latimer Brook Monitoring Program update/Year 2 - Don Danila reviewed the water
quality monitoring program. Attention is being focused on lower Latimer Brook and
Cranberry Meadow Brook where land uses appear to be contributing NPS to Latimer
Brook. After review of the existing sites with the Monitoring Committee, and in
consultation with Waterford Land Use Department staff, several sites in the upper
Latimer Brook watershed, where no water quality issues were noted, were
discontinued, and two sites in Waterford, on Oil Mill Brook and Stony Brook, were
added.

b. River Bioassessment for Volunteers (RBV) — Brief discussion regarding the upcoming RBV
season was held, including searching for different sorting pans without ribs in the base,
and scouting for a new site in the upper Latimer Brook watershed.

c. Data assessment/evaluation/report - Don Landers stated that it would be beneficial to
provide a summary report to the municipalities to keep them updated on our activities.
The subcommittee reviewed the draft outline suggested by John Jasper. A simple one-
page water quality monitoring summary was suggested as an outreach piece. Chris
Tomichek suggested we tie our data to data presented by the Niantic River Nitrogen
Work Group.

d. Draft QAPP — No progress has been made on the draft QAPP.
