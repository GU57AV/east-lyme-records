Minutes CONNECTICUT
NRWC Board of Directors Jul ,
June 5, 2014

Niantic River Watershed Committee

www.nianticriverwatershed. org

FILED IN EAST LYME

East Lyme Town Hall, Conference Room 1
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, East Lyme, CT.

Attendees: Don Danila, Town of East Lyme; Rick Kanter, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission; Don Landers, East Lyme Harbor Management; Doug Lawson, Town of Waterford;
Ruth Savalle, Town of Salem; Marvin Schutt, Town of East Lyme; Chris Tomichek, Town of
Waterford; Eric Thomas, CT DEEP; David Turner, Town of Montville; Judy Rondeau, Watershed
Coordinator, Dan Mullins, ECCD.

Excused: Jeff Crouch, Town of Montville; Peter Harris, Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish
Commission; John Jasper, Town of East Lyme; Marc Lafrance, Town of Montville.

1.

Call to Order: Chairperson Chris Tomichek called the meeting to order at 6:03 pm.

Seating of Alternates: A motion was made by Don Landers to seat Don Danila for John
Jasper, seconded by Dave Turner. Allin favor, motion passes.

Review and Approval of May 1, 2014 Meeting Minutes: Don Danila made a motion to
approve the minutes as presented, seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes.

Treasurer’s Report: Dave Turner reviewed the May financial report. Don Landers made a
motion to approve the treasurer's report as presented, seconded by Don Danila. All in
favor, motion passes.

Coordinator’s Report: Judy was invited to participate in the Stormwater Classroom event
for Town of East Lyme third-graders on May 29". She talked about the role of plants and
soil in removing pollutants from stormwater. Celebrate East Lyme Day in July 19"", Anyone
who would like to staff the booth is welcome. Judy has been involved in the planning of a
Volunteer Water Quality Monitoring Conference which is scheduled for July 29" in East
Hartford. Judy and several NRWC members attended the quarterly meeting of the Niantic
River Nitrogen Work Group at UConn/Avery Point on June gu Judy met with East Lyme
staff Victor Benni and Gary Goeschel to identify possible stormwater implementation sites
at the East Lyme High School. They identified a potential site near the baseball field and are
waiting to hear from the principal if the school is interesting in pursuing an implementation.
The Mago Park Riparian buffer planting is scheduled for June 16, Planning on the
Waterford and East Lyme tree filter installations is proceeding.
6. Old Business:

a.

on

Town Matters: East Lyme is moving forward with plans to contract with USGS to re-
activate the stream gauge on Latimer Brook.

Watershed Signs: Judy Rondeau reported that plans to install watershed signs at key
points along main roads throughout the watershed are moving along slowly. John
Jasper is trying to identify the correct person at DOT to assist with approvals. At this
point, we need information on the correct sign size so we can order the signs, and we
need to know if the State will install them on the state roads.

Celebrate East Lyme Day: Previously discussed in the Coordinators’ report.

7. Subcommittee Updates:

a.

501c3: Dave Turner reported that the process is moving forward slowly. He
encountered several issues with the incorporation application. The State reported
our name incorrectly on the first forms, which needed to be corrected. Secondly,
we missed some information on the application form, and thirdly, we submitted the
Articles of Incorporation in the wrong format. These have been corrected and
resubmitted by Dave. There was brief discussion about whether we should have
someone review the corrected application before we submit it.

Monitoring Group: Don Danila reviewed the May water quality sampling results.
Nitrate-nitrogen levels in Latimer Brook were highest in the Flanders area. Nitrate-
nitrogen was higher in Cranberry Meadow Brook than in Latimer Brook. Don noted
that Stony Brook and Oil Mill Brook in Waterford had fairly low conductivity, pH was
higher, and dissolved oxygen was at or near saturation. Stony Brook had a slightly
higher conductivity than Oil Mill Brook. Don noted the presence of iron bacteria and
questioned whether there was a link. We received the results of the most recent
temperature logger deployment from Meg Ruta at DEEP. The logger that was
placed in Latimer Brooks downstream of the Cranberry Meadow Brook confluence
did not record, so John was not able to use the data for his flux calculations. Don
noted that he attended the show-cause hearing for the sedimentation form the
Antares solar farm project, and will keep the Committee updated. Don and John
Jasper have offered to do presentations on NRWC’s water quality monitoring
program at the Volunteer WQM Conference in July. Rick Kanter noted that the
eelgrass in the Niantic River appears to be making a comeback this year.
Education/Outreach Group: Reviewed in Coordinator’s Report.

Grant Review/Fundraising Subcommittee: Eric commented that the2015 §319
grant round is coming up. He also suggested that NRWC support an application of
the Town of Waterford for the development of the green infrastructure tool box,
should the Town choose to pursue through the 319 program.

8. New Business:
a.

Mago Point Demonstration Riparian Buffer Planting Day: As previously reported, the
planting day is set for June 16", Anyone who would like to assist will be welcome.
Hobo Data Logger Purchase: Based on discussion with Meghan Ruta at DEEP about
the availability of DEEP’s supply of Hobo data loggers, Judy proposed that we use
funds available from the 2013 Dominion grant to buy our own loggers. Dave Turner
made a motion that Judy determine the cost for a minimum of three loggers, the
data transfer shuttle and the computer software, circulate this information by email
to all directors, and an e-vote be taken to approve or disapprove the purchase. The
motion was seconded Rick Kanter. All in favor, the motion passes.

c. Pine Grove Japanese Knotweed Workdays: The CT Invasive Plant Work Group has
scheduled a series of workdays to remove Japanese Knotweed growing in the Pine
Grove area. Judy shared a flyer with the work schedule for anyone interested in

participating.

9. Next meeting: July 3, 2014, 6 pm; East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic,
CT. Discussion was held regarding re-scheduling the July meeting due to its proximity to
the Fourth of July Holiday. Don Danila made a motion to reschedule the meeting date
from July 3 to July 10", seconded by Dave Turner. All in favor, motion passes. The July
meeting will be held on Thursday, July 10, 2014, at 6 pm, at the East Lyme Town Hall,
108 Pennsylvania Ave, Niantic, CT.

10. Adjourn: Dave Turner made a motion to adjourn at 7:32PM, seconded by Doug Lawson.
Allin favor, the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully Submitted,

Judy Rondeau
Watershed Coordinator
