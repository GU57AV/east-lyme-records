1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS

The Commission on Aging Regular Meeting of December 14, 2015 was called to
order at 5:00 p.m.

a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

b. Delegations. There were no delegations.

c. Approval of Meeting Minutes - November 9, 2015. Mr. Mountain asked for
additions, deletions or corrections to the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting Minutes of
November 9, 2015.

MOTION: _ Ilene Harris moved to approve the November 9,
2015 Commission on Aging Regular Meeting Minutes,
as presented. Seconded by Marge Caste. (5-0)
Unanimous.

d. Correspondence. No correspondence was received.

2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported our vehicles were sent for quarterly review.
One bus is at the Town Garage and will need to go to Dawley's for repair.

She informed the Commission that transportation continues to be very busy. There
are days that the driver is unable to return to the Senior Center by 4 p.m. Medical
appointments are also very busy. Ms. Harris asked whether or not Dial-a-Ride is being
used? Ms. Wilson replied we try to use it for out of town appointments only.

b. Senior Trips.
(1) Enrichment Trips. The following enrichment trips are planned: January 13 for
lunch at Mystic Diner in Mystic. The trip fee is $3; January 27 for lunch at the Old Lyme Inn
in Old Lyme. The trip fee is $3.

(2) Day/Overnight Trips. The following Day/Overnight trips have been planned:
April 7 with the Lyme Senior Center for a trip to Mohegan Sun. Participants will receive a
casino package. The trip fee is $20; April 30 to New York City on your own. The trip fee is
$48. October 9-11, 2016 to "Samson" at the Sight & Sound Millennium Theatre in
Lancaster, PA for a two night stay at the Double Tree by Hilton Hotel, with free time at
Longwood Gardens and Philadelphia included. The cost is $467 for a double, $429 for
triple and $557 for a single.

January 28-February 7, 2016 for a fully escorted 11 day-7 shore excursions
Hawaiian Cruise included in the price starting at $4,449pp.

The following three additional cruises are planned: an Alaskan Cruise on June 6-19,
2016, aboard the Holland American "Zaandam" starting at $4,434 per person with port and
government taxes included; August 27-September 13, 2016 for a Trans-Atlantic Cruise for
18 days with flight and motor coach included aboard the Royal Caribbean "Serenade of
the Seas" starting at $3136 pp with port and government taxes included. October 30-
November 11, 2016 for an escorted Caribbean Cruise aboard the Royal Caribbean
"Serenade of the Seas". Fly out of Boston and return from Fort Lauderdale for 12 nights
starting at $2,027 with port and government taxes included.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson reported we no longer have a Site
Server. Tim Grills who is Director of the Senior Program will be leaving.

Ms. Hardy asked how is attendance at the meals site? Ms. Wilson replied it is
growing. We now have eight people a day. Ms. Hardy asked do you lose people who are
going to Florida? Ms. Wilson replied we do not.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson reported ongoing programs are
doing well. We lose some participants who go south during the winter months. Between
Christmas and New Year we do not run programs.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. Kristen Caramanica has been very busy. She
has developed an intergenerational Book Club with third graders at Lillie B. Haynes. and it
has been well received. Ms. Caramancia also does health and wellness every month.

d. Budget - General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson reported all is going
well, except for the Program Instructors Line Item. She is reviewing programs that are not
doing well and plans to eliminate them. Trips are doing fine.

The Drama Club wants to work on more shows.

She will meet with First Selectman Mark Nickerson to discuss the budget.
e. Revenues - General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson reported revenues
and the revolving account are doing well.

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Senior Center Regional Blog. Ms. Wilson reported the blog will launch on
January 1. Ads may be sold and revenue received. She recommended that the
Commission to go to www.seniorcenterct.org for more information.

b. Budget Preparations for Fiscal Year 2016-2017. The Commission reviewed the
proposed budget request for fiscal year 2016-2017. She is requesting a 5.7% increase for
overtime for the full time bus driver. The Longevity and Recording Secretary accounts
have been reduced. The Program Instructor line item will remain the same. Three
departments will use Rec Trac. We are requesting transportation software that will locate
where transportation is needed and allow us to know what type of vehicle is needed. We
no longer will have cable TV. We would like to move program services, bridge lessons
and entertainment into the revolving account. Our vehicles need to be inspected every
year. DMV charges $300 for three vehicles.

Commission member John Whritner arrived.

In the Special Revenue Fund we are requesting $13,500 for programs that will pay
for themselves.

We anticipate revenues of $78,500 for 2016-2017.
We have been asked to show a budget with a 0% increase, which she will work on.

llene Harris felt in order for the Senior Center to run appropriately she
recommended that the Commission on Aging approve $264,770, and she did not
recommend a zero increase. B. B. Smith concurred with this analysis, because the senior
population in East Lyme is growing.

over 60 years old? There was a general consensus that a 10% discount on Senior Center
meals should be offered to all veterans.
