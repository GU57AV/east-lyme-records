2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation.

Ms. Wilson reported the vehicles will go in for quarterly reviews. She stated
transportation is very busy.

Ms. Wilson reported she received letter dated May 12, 2015 from George Myers
of Seaspray Condo regarding its no honk policy.
b. Senior Trips.

$18.

July 2 to see "My Fair Lady" at the Theatre by the Sea in Matunuck, RI. Lunch is

performance by John Timpanel. The trip fee is $94pp; August 4 for a guided "Corsets
and Top Hats" tour of the Canfield House and the Marble House in Newport, R.!. Mrs.
Astor will escort you and provide you with details of High Society living during the Gilded
Age. Lunch is included. The trip fee is $96pp; August 25 to Gloucester, MA on
Privateer IV for a seven seas Whale Watch narrated by their outboard naturalist. Before
the Whale Watch you'll eat in downtown Gloucester. The trip fee is $84 pp; September
40 to Boston on your own for a trip fee of $45pp; September 27 for a guided riding tour
of Harlem, NY with a special tour of the Apollo Theatre with lunch at Sylvia's
Restaurant. The trip fee is $119; December 2 for a trip to Radio City Music Hall for their
Christmas Spectacular for a fee of $145.

July 4-10 for an Atlantic Canada Tour of Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island
in a private stateroom on the "Nova Star". The cost is $1,637 pp for a double, $1567 pp

Millennium Theatre in Lancaster, PA for a two night stay at the Crown Plaza Hotel at a

2016 for a fully escorted Hawaiian Cruise 11 day-7 shore excursions starting at

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson reported the number of individuals
coming to the Senior Nutrition Program and meals being delivered have increased.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson sent an email to instructors
regarding new programs and explained the time, space and budget constraints. She
plans to review charges for programs.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. Ms. Wilson reported the Live Well Program
will discuss Diabetes Management. Nine individuals are interested in it. It will begin

next week for six weeks.

d. Budget - General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson reported some
transfers were needed, but we were under budget.
e. Revenues - General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson stated revenues
are doing better than anticipated.

3. OLD BUSINESS
a. Transportation Documents. Ms. Wilson sent the transportation documents to
the First Selectman, and it was reviewed by Town Attorney. The transportation

documents now state that transportation is offered for only those individuals who are 60
and over or disabled.

b. Update on Dial a Ride Grant. Ms. Wilson reported this year we are asking for
a $24 annual enrollment fee. Each individual is allowed twelve round trip rides. If only
mileage reimbursement is requested, there is no annual enrollment fee.

The total grant is $270,451 for ten towns.

c. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Caste asked for an update on the freezer. Ms.
Wilson replied the freezer has been received.

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Will Bequest - Ideas. Ms. Wilson reported $50,000 has been received, and
asked for suggestions on how this money is to be spent. Ms. Caste suggested a flat
screen TV.

b. Gail Glenn Retirement. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission July 24 is Ms.
Glenn's last day, and she would like to have a Open House on that day. Ms. Glenn has
worked for the Senior Center for twenty years.

She has been teaching a weight lifting class and would like to continue teaching

Ms. Wilson would like to have a panel for the interviews.
c. Other Pertinent Business. The Commission reviewed an incident report.

Ms. Bengtson reminded all the Senior Center will have its 25th anniversary in
August.
