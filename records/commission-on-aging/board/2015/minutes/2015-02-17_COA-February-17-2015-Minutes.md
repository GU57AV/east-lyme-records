2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation

Ms. Wilson reported that she is in the process of writing the grant for Dial-A-Ride and that they are
asking Preston to join them. The total would be for $275,000. The amount of the grant has not gone up
although ridership has gone up. Rather than sometimes utilizing Curtin Delivery they are looking to
broker through ECTC for a car and driver or to charge a $25 annual membership fee for the service.
They will be asking the DOT if they can do this as they have people who sign up and hold a spot but
who sometimes do not utilize it and they cannot give it to someone who may need it more and who
would use it as they have maxed out their numbers for members. While the Dial-A-Ride grant is not
specific as to how it can be used, they like to utilize it here for medical help as there are people who
really need it for medical appointments.

Ms. Caste asked about the number of rides per person.
Ms. Wilson said that there are 12 round trips or 24 one-way rides. She noted that the grant needs to be
submitted by March 27, 2015. She also reported that driving during the constant snow that we have
been getting has been rough but they have been doing what they can.
