(1) Enrichment Trips. The following enrichment trips are planned: May 13 for
lunch to the East Lyme High School Culinary Program with a trip afterwards to Old
Saybrook for shopping. The trip fee is $6 or with only lunch $3; May 27 to Groton for
lunch at the Par 4 Restaurant with a trip to Mystic afterwards for strolling and shopping.

(2) Day/Overnight Trips. The following Day/Overnight trips have been planned:
May 14 to The Port of Galilee in Narragansett, RI for a "Day Behind-the-Scenes Tour".
Lunch is included for a trip fee of $78pp; May 28 for a 90 minute Lobster Roll Cruise on
Cape Cod Bay, MA to Cod Creamery and Cape Cod Potato Chip Factory. The trip fee
is $86 pp; June 19 to the Newport Flower Show at the Rosecliff Mansion in Newport, RI
and on your own to the Wharf area. The trip fee is $42 pp; July 2 to see "My Fair Lady"
at the Theatre by the Sea in Matunuck, RI. Lunch is included for a trip fee of $80 pp;
July 9 All you Can Eat Lobster and musical performance by John Timpanel. The trip fee
is $94pp. August 4 for a guided "Corsets and Top Hats" tour of the Canfield House and
the Marble House in Newport, R.I. Mrs. Astor will escort you and provide you with
details of High Society living during the Gilded Age. Lunch is included. The trip fee is
$96pp; August 25 to Gloucester, MA on Privateer IV for a seven seas Whale Watch
narrated by their outboard naturalist. Before the Whale Watch you'll eat in downtown
Gloucester. The trip fee is $84 pp.

July 4-10 for an Atlantic Canada Tour of Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island
in a private stateroom on the "Nova Star". The cost is $1,637 pp for a double, $1567 pp
