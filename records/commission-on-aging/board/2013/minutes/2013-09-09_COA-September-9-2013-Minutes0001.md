Senior Nutrition Program — Ms. Wilson reported that they had received notice that the

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Reimbursement for Celebrate 90+ Tea Expenses

Ms. Wilson noted that the expenses for the 90+ Tea came to $462.97 and that she would like

b. 90+ Birthday Program

Ms. Wilson said that they have a resident turning 100 this year who will receive a letter from President
Obama. She said that Florence Sayre who is 100 will receive a birthday proclamation from our First
Selectman. She said that she would like to do this for those who are 90+ also and that she would like to
see if it could be done in conjunction with the culinary program at the High School and perhaps the
students could bake some cookies that could be frozen and they could wrap up a dozen to present to
the person along with the proclamation. Perhaps the students could deliver these and spend some time
with these members of our community.

1,896 and program attendance figures had gone up and were at 24,078.
