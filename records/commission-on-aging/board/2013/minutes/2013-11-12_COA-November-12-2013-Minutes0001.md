1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS

Chairman Mountain called the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting of
November 12, 2013 to order at 5:00 p.m.

a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

b. Delegations. Dr. Si Baker-Goodwin reported that her mother and father
both had dementia and she is their only child. Ms. Wilson and her office staff were
very helpful while she cared for her parents. It was crucial for her to have Ms.
Wilson's help.

Her Dad is now in a nursing home, and her Mom now also has cancer. The
Senior Center has been wonderful. She has joined the Weight Program at Gail
Glenn's suggestion. She is very thankful to have a place to go to seek help. The
Senior Center informed her about a Caregiver Support Group in Waterford which
she took advantage of.

c. Approval of Meeting Minutes — October 13, 2013. Mr. Mountain asked
for additions, deletions or corrections to the Commission on Aging Meeting
Minutes of October 13, 2013.

MOTION: Marge Caste moved to accept the October 13,
2013 Commission on Aging Minutes, as
presented. Seconded by Joe Palazzo.

(6-0) Unanimous.

d. Correspondence. There was no correspondence.

a. Transportation. All is well with the vehicles and drivers.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. A Meals-on-Wheels driver found a recipient
on the floor. After 911 was called the woman went to the hospital. Ms. Wilson felt
a daily check on the recipients of Meals-on-Wheels can save lives.

There are approximately eight individuals having lunch at the Senior
Center, and there are approximately 35 who have meals delivered to their home.
(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson reported the Chair

Yoga class is held on Tuesdays from 9:30 a.m. and on Friday at 10:30 a.m. We
are now doubling the class without necessarily doubling the revenue.

Pickleball has resumed.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New A trial PIYO (a combination of Pilates
and Yoga) is held on Tuesdays from 4:15 p.m. to 5:15 p.m. Line dancing will
begin tomorrow from 2 p.m. to 3 p.m. for six weeks. We are now at capacity in the
Activity Room.

d. Budget - General Fund, Revolving Account. We are having issues with
the budget because some classes are being expanded. Otherwise the budget is
doing well.

e. Revenues - General Fund, Revolving Account. Revenues are doing fine.

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Wilson asked the Commission to make
recommendations to her for a new Commission member.

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Budget Preparation. The Commission reviewed a draft budget
worksheet provided by Ms. Wilson. The budget is due on December 4. She
asked that Commission members review it and vote on it at the next regularly
scheduled meeting of this Commission.

b. Capital Request. The vehicles need to be five years old or have
100,000 miles on it to be eligible for the 5310 Grant. One vehicle will be placed in
the 2015-16 Capital Plan and one in the 2016-2017 Capital Plan.

Ms. Wilson reported the Senior Center kitchen is more than twenty years
old and needs to be updated. Mr. Palazzo stated the Lions are willing to purchase
a freezer for the Senior Center.

c. Goals Presentation. Ms. Wilson plans to reach out to the business
community to request that they sponsor a program at the Senior Center. Mr.
Whritner noted space is an issue.

Ms. Wilson suggested using the tables in the Community Center to sell
coffee and scones. We are looking at space to determine whether it can be
reused.
d. Annual Report. Ms. Wilson plans to present the Annual Report at the

next meeting of this Commission.

e. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Wilson reported our mileage has 1 1/2
times more than it has been. Ms. Caste felt the five senior complexes may cause
this. As residents age the need for transportation increases. Ms. Caste stated
seniors want to remain in their homes.
