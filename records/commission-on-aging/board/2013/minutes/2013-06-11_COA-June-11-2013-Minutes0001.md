1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS

Chairman Mountain called the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting of June
11, 2013 to order at 6:00 p.m.

a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

b. Delegations. There were no delegations.

c. Approval of Meeting Minutes — May 13, 2013. Mr. Mountain asked for
additions, deletions or corrections to the Commission on Aging Meeting Minutes of May
13, 2013.

MOTION: _ Ilene Harris moved to accept the May 13,
2013 Commission on Aging Minutes, as
presented. Seconded by Marge Caste.
(5-0) Unanimous

d. Correspondence. There was no correspondence received.

2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported the R-3 was brought to the Town
Garage. They found the problem was with the air conditioning and was under warranty.
The warranty work was done at Ford. The other two vehicles will go to the Town
Garage.

b. Senior Trips. July 10 for a Connecticut River cruise with RiverQuest CT River
Expeditions out of Haddam. The trip fee is $22 which includes admission on to the
boat. Attendees are asked to pay in cash. We will stop for ice cream afterwards. On
July 24 we will have a trip to Lavender Hill Farm Alpacas in Lisbon, with lunch on your
own at the Caddyshack Restaurant located at the Norwich Municipal Golf Course. The
fee is $4.

(1) Enrichment Trips. The following enrichment trips are scheduled: The July 7
Tanglewood trip has been canceled due to low registration. July 18 to Long Island Wine
Country with lunch on your own at Greenport. The trip fee is $77 per person. August 1
to "Little Italy" in the Belmont section of the Bronx. Lunch is included at Ann and Tony's.
The trip fee is $89 per person. August 13 to Pilgrim Belle Harbor Tour in Plymouth, MA
with lunch at John Carver Inn. The fee is $75 per person. August 22 to the Newport
Dinner Train with free time in Newport. The trip fee is $84. September 10 to Wines &
Spirts of Ulster County in Hudson Valley. Visit Tuthilltown Spirits Distillery, Whitecliff
Winery and have lunch at the Tuthill House at the Mill Restaurant. The fee is $83 per
person. September 18 to the Big E in Springfield, MA. On September 24 a 3.5 hour
guided tour of Martha's Vineyard with a 90 minute stop in Edgartown for lunch on your
own. The fee is $86 per person.

(2) Day/Overnight Trips. The following overnight trips are planned: August 26-
28 to Atlantic City and Cape May. This trip includes Resorts Casino Bonus Package,
Physick Estate and Cape May Trolley Tour. The cost is $277 pp for double occupancy;
September 9-10 to Ogunquit and Portland, ME to hear story teller and a 90 minute
cruise of Portland Harbor, with Kittery shopping. The cost is $259 for double
occupancy; October 4-9 to Charleston, Savannah and Jekyll Island. This trip includes
motor coach and airfare. It also includes passes to the 37th Annual Charleston Home
and Garden Tour. The cost begins at $2,229: December 10-12 to see a Miracle of
Christmas at the Sight and Sound Millennium Theater in Strasburg, PA. The cost is
$421 for double occupancy; January 9-19, 2014 for a Hawaii Cruise Tour on NCL's
Pride of America. A seven day cruise with seven shore excursions and two nights at
the Marriott Waikiki Beach. The fee starts at $3,999.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. The Senior Nutrition Program will serve salad
during the summer months. They require 72 hours notice for a reservation. They have
changed their summer bistro menu and the price has increased. The congregate meals
continue to decrease. The week of July 4 TVCCA will not serve congregate meals. The
meals-on-wheels drivers are willing to drive.

The Meals-on-Wheels Program is doing well. The number of people going on
the north route has decreased. The B and D routes have a substantial number of
individuals riding. Ms. Wilson plans to restructure these routes.

St. John's is piloting a Meals-on-Wheels Program that is not age appropriate.
Ms. Wilson has contacted TVCCA but to date has had no response.
The Waterford Senior Nutrition Program is offered on Tuesday, Wednesday and
Thursday. There is no food program on Monday. On Friday a town employee cooks.
Waterford's congregate numbers are also declining.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson reported at the request of
attendees, Chair Yoga is now being offered at 9:30 a.m. and 10:30 a.m. until the end of
June.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New There was nothing new to report on new
programs.

d. Budget - General Fund, Revolving Account. There are no issues with the
budget. A few transfers were made.

e. Revenues - General Fund, Revolving Account. Revenues are doing fine.

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Caste asked if a Coast Guard Concert is
scheduled for July? Ms. Wilson plans to schedule one or two Coast Guard concerts.

Ms. Caste felt Ms. Wilson should be commended on the tea for residents who
are 90 and older. The attendees loved it and she did a wonderful job.

4. NEW BUSINESS
a. Background Checks. Ms. Wilson reported on a new procedure for

background checks. It is now being done by SSCI. The cost is less and the procedure
is very easy.

Mr. Palazzo asked if bus drivers have random drug testing? Ms. Wilson replied
they do.

b. Annual Appeal Letter for Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported we have sent
an annual appeal letter for transportation annually for a few years.

c. Other Pertinent Business. Beginning in September our Commission meetings
will begin at 5 p.m.

Commission member John Whritner left the meeting.
5. PUBLIC DISCUSSION
There was no public discussion.
