2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation

Ms. Wilson reported that with the Dial-A-Ride program that they had asked for a $24 enrollment fee this
year and that the number of people who enrolled was dramatically reduced. This enabled them to clean
up their records as many of these people were not utilizing it but were holding spots that others would
have liked. Also — the people who are looking for mileage reimbursement and the money allotted for that
is not going as far as it once was so they are moving from 12 round trips (or 24 single trips) to 18 round
trips (or 36 single trips) and they will write the new grant for the local transportation in that way.

She said that she had explained the ridership numbers to the Board of Selectmen at her budget
presentation as they have gone up. In FY11-12 they had 261 riders per month; FY15/16 it went to 279
per month and so far this year to date it has been at 317 per month. She is working on the 5310 grant for
vehicle replacement (Summer 2017) and they are looking to keep the two buses that they have now and

give up the van to another area within the Town that can use it. Lastly, the quarterly work on the vehicles
is coming due.
