2. STATUS REPORT
a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported all vehicles will need quarterly review.
Her full time driver has been able to drive some afternoons.

b. Senior Trips.

(1) Enrichment Trips. The following enrichment trips are planned: January 11, 2017
for a lunch trip to Go Fish in Mystic, CT. The trip fee is $3; and January 25 for a lunch trip to
The Olive Garden in Waterford, CT. The trip fee is $3.

(2) Day/Overnight Trips. Ms. Wilson reported plans are being made for 2017
Day/Overnight trips. The following potential trips for 2017 are being considered: the NY
Botanical Garden, NY Museum of Natural History, Greenport, Ll; Plum Island Winter Solstice
Concert/Event; Rockefeller Estate; Boston Lighthouse Cruise; Brooklyn Botanical Gardens;
Perkins Cove, ME; Lake George Cruise, NY; Washington, DC; Finger Lakes, NY; Ocean City,
MD; Nantucket, MA; a trip to the Casino; a trip to the Boston Museum of Fine Arts and a free
day in New York.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. On Wednesdays and Fridays we have had as many as
15 individuals in the dining room. We are serving 25-35 meals-on-wheels. Ms. Harris asked if
the increase is due to programs prior to dining in? Ms. Wilson felt this was possible on
Fridays.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson reported ongoing programs are
doing well. She is considering price increases for Zumba, Chair and Yoga.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. No new programs are planned, because the
Senior Center is at capacity.

d. Budget - General Fund, Revolving Account. Payroll for part time drivers has
increased. ;

e. Revenues - General Fund, Revolving Account. Revenues are doing well.

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Open Door Program at Waterford Senior Services. Ms. Wilson received letter
dated December 6, 2016 from Waterford Senior Services acknowledging receipt of $1,000
from East Lyme towards the Open Door Program. Waterford is pleased with this Program.
They feel it is a positive service for caregivers. Ms. Caste stated there are twelve individuals
in the support group.

b. Budget Process for FY 17/18. Ms. Wilson reported she had a preliminary
discussion with Mark Nickerson, First Selectman. She will submit a budget removing her
instructor payroll account. She would like to upgrade Anna Hartung's classification and add a
few more part time bus drivers' hours. There will also be an increase in longevity. She
informed the Commission the First Selectman was very supportive of our budget.

MOTION: _ B. B. Smith moved to approve the proposed General
Fund budget for the Commission on Aging for
fiscal year 2017/18 in the amount of $235,111.
Seconded by Joan Bengtson. (6-0) Unanimous.

c. Other Pertinent Business. Mr. Palazzo asked if any fundraising has been done?
Ms. Wilson replied the Drama Group is fundraising.

Ms. Smith suggested a candlelight walk on the Boardwalk in spring, with vendors and
music. Ms. Wilson suggested having this event in May to celebrate Older American's month.

Ms. Hardy suggested distributing baskets to shut-ins.
4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Change in Bistro Program. Ms. Wilson reported TVCCA had a budgetary crisis and
cut congregate meals to four days per week.

We have been charging $5 for a bistro meal. To date we have lost $1500. TVCCA
has recently informed us they will charge $4, $6 or $8 depending on the amount of side
dishes and the size of the servings. We no longer have a Site Server. We are volunteer
based. Ms. Harris asked what happens if you don't have any volunteers? Ms. Wilson replied
she and her staff work in the kitchen. After discussion, Ms. Wilson stated in February she will
try the $4 menu which gives a choice of nine entrees and one side dish.

b. Meeting Schedule for 2017. Commission members reviewed the proposed meeting
