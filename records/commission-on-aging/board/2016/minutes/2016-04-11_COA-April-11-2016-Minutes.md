2. STATUS REPORT
a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported her part-time driver is away and her on-
call drivers are covering for him.

Ms. Wilson informed the Commission she has had discussion with the Board of
Selectmen and Board of Finance that the transportation requests are escalating. She
has needed to call in on-call drivers to help manage it.

She went back five years of transportation requests. Transportation requests
from fiscal year 2011-2012 to fiscal year 2014-2015 have increased by 26%.
Transportation requests from fiscal year 2014-2015 to current fiscal year 2015-2016
have gone up 18%. We are seeing older folks who are no longer able to drive. We
have had many requests for transportation for physical therapy. Ms. Wilson stated
medical appointments take priority. Depending on the need for other rides we may
move them to another day or time.

Vehicles are fine and drivers are fine. Transportation is just very busy.

4. NEW BUSINESS
a. Grant for Elderly & Disabled Demand Responsive Transportation. Ms. Wilson

reported Dial-a-Ride provides transportation for medical appointments, it allows for
caregiver mileage reimbursement. There is a $24 annual enrollment fee for 18 round
trips.

Ms. Harris asked is this transportation only available for those over 65 or is it also
available to the disabled? Ms. Wilson replied also for the disabled. Salem has also
asked to join in on this grant. This transportation is a huge benefit for senior centers.
The application has been submitted.

b. Federal Transit Administration Section 5310 Program Grant. Ms. Wilson is
working on this grant for vehicle replacement. She wishes to take the Ford van out of
the Senior Center because it is not handicapped accessible. This grant is due by April.
DOT would pay 80% for the vehicle and the Town would pay 20%. The earliest it can
be received is July 2017.

applications TVCCA sent a check for $700 which will go into the Parahus Account.

you tie in with another group it will be $30 per month.
