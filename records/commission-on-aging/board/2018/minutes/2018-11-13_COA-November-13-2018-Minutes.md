1, PRELIMINARY BUSINESS
a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.
b. Delegations. There were no guests.
c. Minutes of September 10, 2018. Chairman Palazzo asked for additions, corrections or
deletions to the September 10, 2018 Commission on Aging Minutes.

MOTION (1): Mr. Whritner moved to approve the September 10, 2018 Commission on
Aging Minutes, as presented. Seconded by Ms. Caste. (4-0) Unanimous.

d. Correspondence. No correspondence was received.

2. STATUS REPORT
a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported all three vehicles have been inspected at the Town
Garage. Annual lift inspections by a certified dealer are needed. Transportation is very busy.
We will schedule two drivers every day. We are checking into cell phones for the drivers.

b. Senior Trips.
1. Enrichment Trips. December 12 for a special concert and lunch at East Lyme High School.
The lunch fee is $5 pp.

2.Day/Overnight Trips. December 4 for the Radio City Christmas Spectacular with lunch
included for a trip fee of $167.

April 28-May 4 to Myrtle Beach, SC and Charleston, SC 7 days, 6 nights trip includes 4 night
stay in your ocean front room at Ocean Reef Resort. Prices start $1,219 pp double occupancy.
May 5-12, 2019 for an escorted Bermuda cruise on the Celebrity Summit for 8 days and 7
nights. Transportation to pier is included. Prices begin at $1,437pp for double occupancy.
August 22-September 1, 2019 for an escorted Trans-Atlantic cruise from London on the Queen
Mary 2 for 11 days and 10 nights with transportation and transfers included. Prices start at
$3,883pp. September 15-22, 2019 for a guided tour of America’s Cowboy Country to explore
the West for 8 days and 7 nights with transportation and transfers included. Prices start at
$3,599pp double occupancy. October 29-November 9 for a guided tour of Southern Italy and
Sicily for 12 days and 11 nights with transportation and transfers included. Prices start at
$3,799pp for double occupancy. November 9-24, 2019 for an escorted trip to NY for Panama
Canal cruise on NCL’s Norwegian Dawn w/partial transit of Canal for 16 days and 15 nights
with transportation and transfers included. Prices start at $2,484pp double occupancy.

c. Programs.

1. Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson reported the Senior Nutrition Program has hired a
new Director and a new Chef. Emergency packs were sent out last week. Meals-on-Wheels is
steady.

2. Senior Center Programs — Ongoing. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission attendance at
dances is declining because of many individual aging. She plans to book three dances, use
other bands and open it to the general public.

3. Senior Center Programs — New. Ms. Wilson will distribute questionnaires to determine
what programs are liked and what programs are not liked.

d. Budget — General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson reported the budget is doing fine.
She informed the Commission from the Special Revenue Fund refunds were given because some
trips were cancelled. The Day Trips are not doing well. The Overnight Trips have done well.
Individuals register at the Senior Center but payments for all overnight trips will now be processed
through the Travel Agent.

e. Revenues — General Fund, Revolving Account. Revenues are doing well.

FY 17/18 Annual Report. The Commission reviewed a draft of the Annual Report. There was
