(1) Senior Nutrition Program — Ms. Wilson reported that there have been no changes.

(3) Senior Center Programs - New — Ms. Wilson reported that they have started the Tax
Program and that it has been going well. There have been no issues with having to re-

regarding the Free credit report that people can get each year and how to avoid being
scammed into having to pay for it.

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Budget Review Request for FY 18/19
Ms. Wilson reported that the budget that she put through is still intact. She will see when she presents to
the Boards of Selectmen and Finance.

b. Other Pertinent Business

Ms. Hardy asked that Ms. Wilson explain to the Commission what has happened with the Rental Rebate
program through the State.

Ms. Wilson said that it was reduced by 7% last year and also another 7% for this year and they will have
to see how long this program survives with the State budget problems as they are.

Ms. Hardy said that they have withdrawn the 7% reduction for this year and that now the Town will have
to scrounge around to cover the program thru this June 30, 2018. The State sent the people to the Town
to look for the rebate dollars. This created chaos in the Town offices and here. The Board of Selectmen
during budget reviews will see if they can find enough money to cover it for this year. Right now it is not
an optional program.

Mr. Bekech said that there is also the ‘Homeowner's Program’ that is out on a limb and the local option
of the Town so they will have to decide what they will do.

Ms. Wilson said that the homeowners appreciate the break that they get on their taxes. She added that
the problem with all of this is that no advance notice is given to the local municipalities from the State.

from outside and the in-house person and in the end hired the in-house candidate. She has just begun
