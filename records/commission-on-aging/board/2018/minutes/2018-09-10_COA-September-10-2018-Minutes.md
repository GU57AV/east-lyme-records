COMMISSION ON AGING

REGULAR MEETING
SEPTEMBER 10, 2018
Present: Joe Palazzo
John Whritner
Marge Caste FILED IN EAST LYME

BB Smith CONNECTICUT
Joan Bengtson ~~ “EAST LYME TOWN CLERK
Ilene Harris

Also Present: Cathy Wilson, Senior Center Director

1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS
a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

b. Delegations. There were no guests.
c. Minutes of July 9, 2018. Chairman Palazzo asked for additions, corrections or deletions to
the July 9, 2018 Commission on Aging Minutes.

MOTION (1): BB Smith moved to approve the July 9, 2018 Commission on Aging Minutes,
as presented. Seconded by Ms. Bengtson. (7-0) Unanimous.

d. Correspondence. No correspondence was received.

2. STATUS REPORT
a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported reported the two older vehicles passed the state
vehicle registration. The registration information was sent to DMV. Transportation is very
busy. The R-3 was involved in an accident. It is now at the Town Garage.

b. Senior Trips.

1. Enrichment Trips. October 10 for lunch at Lenny & Joe’s Fish Tale in Westbrook with
shopping afterwards. The trip fee is $5. On October 24 to Pumpkintown, USA in East
Hampton for a thirty-minute hayride and a visit to their village. Lunch beforehand at Rossini’s
Restaurant in East Hampton. Admission to Pumpkintown is $13pp and the trip fee is $5.
October 10 for lunch at Lenny & Joe’s Fish Tale in Westbrook with shopping afterwards. The
trip fee is $5.

2.Day/Overnight Trips. October 23 to Roger Williams Zoo in Providence, RI for the Jack
O’Lantern Spectacular with time prior to the zoo at the Providence Place Mall for
dinner/shopping. The trip fee is $66pp. November 1 to Mohegan Sun Casino for a trip fee of
$22. November 4 to Warner Theatre in Torrington, CT to see “Newsies” with lunch included
for a trip fee of $100. November 12 to a USO Show at Aqua Turf in Plainville, Ct. with lunch
included for a trip fee of $72 pp. December 4 for the Radio City Christmas Spectacular with
lunch included for a trip fee of $167.

October 17-19, 2018 to Lancaster, PA to visit the Sight & Sound Millennial Theatre for the
production of “Jesus” for three days and two nights. The trip fee is $595/double, $545/triple,
$745/single. May 5-12, 2019 for an escorted Bermuda cruise on the Celebrity Summit for 8
days and 7 nights. Transportation to pier is included. Prices begin at $1,437pp for double
occupancy. August 22-September 1, 2019 for an escorted Trans-Atlantic cruise from London on
the Queen Mary 2 for 11 days and 10 nights with transportation and transfers included. Prices
start at $3,883pp. September 15-22, 2019 for a guided tour of America’s Cowboy Country to
explore the West for 8 days and 7 nights with transportation and transfers included. Prices start
at $3,599pp double occupancy. October 29-November 9 for a guided tour of Southern Italy and
Sicily for 12 days and 11 nights with transportation and transfers included. Prices start at
$3,799pp for double occupancy. November 9-24, 2019 for an escorted trip to NY for Panama
Canal cruise on NCL’s Norwegian Dawn w/partial transit of Canal for 16 days and 15 nights
with transportation and transfers included. Prices start at $2,484pp double occupancy.

c. Programs,
1. Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson reported the Senior Nutrition Program has hired a

new Director.
2. Senior Center Programs — Ongoing. Ongoing programs are doing fine.
3. Senior Center Programs — New. Ms. Wilson reported the foot clinic is very popular. Jam
sessions are enjoyed by all and Tech support with high school students is enjoyed by seniors
and students. BB Smith felt you may want the Jam sessions to continue even when classes are
in session. Mr. Palazzo suggested that they play during lunch. Ms. Wilson stated the Chorus
group has sung at Assisted Living and Convalescent homes.

d. Budget — General Fund, Revolving Account, Ms. Wilson reported the budget is doing fine.

e. Revenues — General Fund, Revolving Account. Revenues are doing fine.

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. New Registration Software. The Commission reviewed two quotes for new registration
software. Ms. Wilson will receive a third quote. BB Smith suggested determining what type of
software other senior centers are using.

b. Chair Yoga Issues — Pass Classses ys. Session Classes. Ms. Wilson has received complaints
that Chair Yoga is crowded. The participant is now asked what time they wish to attend.
Classes begin at 8:30 a.m. or 9:30 a.m.

c. Rebranding of the Senior Center. Ms. Wilson plans to obtain information on Nicholas
Parahus in order to recognize him at the Senior Center. She would like to name the center on
the outside of the building the East Lyme Senior Center to make it easier to find us.

d. Laptop Replacement for Director. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission she will receive a
new laptop. One half of the cost will be taken from the Commission on Aging budget and one
half will be taken from the General Fund.

e. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Caste asked if the drama group will have another program?
Ms. Wilson replied they will on the first weekend in December. The summer stock at
McCook’s went very well.

4. NEW BUSINESS
a. Vacation of Director. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission she will be on vacation
beginning tomorrow afternoon and returning on September 26.

b. Other Pertinent Business, Ms. Harris suggested considering hosting a Life Long Learning
session affiliated with a college.
5. PUBLIC DISCUSSION
There were no guests.

6. BOARD RESPONSE
No guests were present.

7. ADJOURNMENT

MOTION (2): Mr. Whritner moved to adjourn the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting
of September 10, 2018 at 5:40 pm. Seconded by Ms. Bengston. (7-0)
Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
