COMMISSION ON AGING

REGULAR MEETING

Also Present: Cathy Wilson, Senior Center Director

1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS

a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

b. Delegations. There were no guests.

c. Minutes of February 12, 2018. Chairman Palazzo asked for additions, corrections or
deletions to the February 12, 2018 Commission on Aging Minutes.

MOTION (1): Mr. Whritner moved to approve the February 12,
2018 Commission on Aging Minutes, as presented.
Seconded by Mr. Mr. Bekech. BB Smith abstained from
voting. (5-0-1) Motion carried.

2. STATUS REPORT
a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported all vehicles and drivers are fine. Next year she plans
to apply for the 5310 Grant. The R-2 has the most miles. The R-3 is newer and has less miles
but has body rust issues. Transportation is very busy.
b. Senior Trips.
1. Enrichment Trips. On April 11 for a lunch trip to Christy's in Madison with shopping to
follow for a trip fee of $5. On April 25 a lunch trip to Grasso Tech with shopping to follow for
a trip fee of $5. Lunch at Grasso Tech is $7.75 pp.
2. Day/Overnight Trips. On April 5 to Mohegan Sun Casino for a trip fee of $22. On April 24
for a guided tour of the Norman Rockwell Museum in Stockbridge, MA. Lunch at Michael's
Restaurant beforehand. The trip fee is $79 pp. On May 5 to New York City on your own for a
trip fee of $48 pp. Lunch is not included. On May 9 to see a production of “Sister Act” at the
Westchester Broadway Theatre in Elmsford, NY. Lunch is included for a trip fee of $99 pp. On
May 24 for a trip to visit the Berkshire Botanical Garden and lunch on your own at Barrington
Brewery and a tour of Chesterwood, the home, studio and gardens of Daniel Chester French.
The trip fee is $85 pp. On June 9 for an escorted tour of the New York Public Library and the
Morgan Library with lunch on your own at the Grand Central Terminal food court. The trip fee
is $94 pp. On June 22 for a trip to the Newport Flower Show at Rosecliff Mansion in Newport
with lunch/shopping on your own at the Wharf. The trip fee is $49 pp.

October 15-25, 2018 for an escorted tour of the Imperial Cities featuring Prague, Vienna and
Budapest. The cost is $3,649/double; $4,299/single; $3,619 triple (travel insurance is not
included). On October 17-19 for our annual trip to Lancaster, PA to visit the Sight & Sound
Millennial Theatre for the production of “Jesus” 3 days and 2 nights for a cost of $595/double;
$545/triple, $745/single.

c. Programs.
1. Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson reported that due to weather conditions, the Senior

Nutrition Program has been sending out food for several days when appropriate. The kitchen
volunteers and the Meals-on-Wheels volunteer drivers have been terrific dealing with the extra
work.
2. Senior Center Programs — Ongoing. Ms. Wilson reported attendance has been lower in the
winter months.
3. Senior Center Programs — New. Ms. Wilson stated there is a waiting list of ten individuals
for the Tax Program. She will offer a program of one-half hour of massage therapy.
d. Budget — General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson reported expenses are within budget.
e. Revenues — General Fund, Revolving Account, There has been a reduction in revenue due to
the weather and lower attendance.

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Budget Request Review for FY18/19. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission she and Mr.
Bekech went to the Board of Selectmen for the Commission on Aging budget hearing. On April 5
at 6:30 p.m. the Board of Finance will review the Commission on Aging budget. All Commission
members were invited to attend.

b. Other Pertinent Business. There was no other pertinent old business.

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Proposed Expenditures from the Parahus Account. Ms. Wilson ordered kitchen supplies last
month with the approval of funds from the Parahus Account. One item that was ordered was a
commercial can opener. However the model requested was no longer available—only a more
expensive model is available but that would have brought the order total over the $350 approved
amount. Upon discussion with the Finance Director, she suggested using the funds that were left
over from the kitchen appliance purchases. This would use up funds already approved on a
previous purchase order and keep the current purchase order intact.

b. Annual Report for FY 16/17. Ms. Wilson distributed copies of the Annual Report for review
by Commission members. No revisions were suggested.

c. Dial-a-Ride Grant. Ms. Wilson reported Dial-a-Ride is such a benefit to the nine towns
participating. An application will be submitted in the next few weeks.

d. New FDA Food Code for July 1, 2018. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission training is
needed on the new regulations regarding food. She and her staff will take training in the
summer.

e. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission the Town Hall will be closed

tomorrow due to weather conditions.

She will be on vacation the last two weeks in March.

Mr. Bekech reported during the budget meeting there was discussion on what will happen if the
$10,000 funding for SEAT transportation is not funded. It was felt that the individuals riding

the SEAT buses are not seniors.
