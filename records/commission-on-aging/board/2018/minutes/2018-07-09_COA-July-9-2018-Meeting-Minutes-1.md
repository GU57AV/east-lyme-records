reports. Transportation is very busy. All drivers are doing fine.

its daily use as well as the hours for each of the drivers. Ms. Hardy asked if the requests for
transportation are from individuals who own a home or live at senior housing. Ms. Wilson
replied most own homes. Ms. Hardy asked if transportation is a larger problem during the
summer months? Ms. Wilson replied most are year-round residents.

OLD BUSINESS

New Registration Software. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission she received two quotes
for new registration software. LPI was $5,000 and Schedule Plus was $1,000. She will be
obtaining one more quote. Ms. Caste suggested that Ms. Wilson contact the Old Lyme Senior
Center regarding their experience with software. The Commission will consider this purchase
at a special meeting in August.

Chair Yoga Issues — Pass Classses vs. Session Classes. Ms. Wilson reported in July the
Senior Center will transition from pass classes to a session class. Presently passes expire after
one year. When individuals purchase a pass, we have no way of knowing how many will attend
ona particular day. She has received complaints that it is crowded. She would like to change
the pass classes to session classes where those attending will be asked what time they wish to
attend. Ms. Harris suggested a drop-in fee. Ms. Wilson felt there was not enough room to offer
it.

c. Other Pertinent Business. There was no discussion of other pertinent business.
