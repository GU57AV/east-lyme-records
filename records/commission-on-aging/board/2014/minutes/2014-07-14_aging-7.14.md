2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported the three vehicles have been inspected.
One required emission testing.

She was informed this morning from her full time driver that he needs medical
attention. A gentleman who delivers mail to the High School and town is willing to fill in
as a substitute Senior Center driver. He has a CDL license.

The Commission reviewed an Incident Report. There were no injuries.
b. Senior Trips.
(1) Enrichment Trips. The following Enrichment Trips were planned:

; a. August 13 for a trip to the Creamery Brook Bison Farm in Brooklyn,
Connecticut, with lunch on your own at Hank's in Plainfield. The trip fee is $4.

b. August 27 for lunch at Lenny & Joe's Fishtale in Westbrook, with a stroll
around Old Saybrook Point after lunch. The trip fee is $4.

(2) Day/Overnight Trips. The following Day/Overnight Trips were planned:

a. August 12 to the Metropolitan Museum of Art and to The Cloisters in
NYC. Lunch on your own. Trip fee $86 pp.

b. September 7 for a Lighthouse Cruise of the Narragansett Bay in Rhode
Island, with brunch included at the Quidnesset Country Club. The trip fee is $88 pp.

c. September 23 to New York's Horticultural (High Line) Walk and New
York Botanical Garden. Lunch on your own at Chelsea Market. Trip fee $91 pp.

d. October 9 for a production of "South Pacific" at the Westchester
Broadway Theatre in Elmsford, NY. Lunch is included. Trip fee $104 pp.

e. October 23 for a trip to Mohegan Sun together with Lyme's Senior
Center. Those attending will receive a casino package. The trip fee is $20 pp.

f. November 6 to the Culinary Institute of America in Hyde Park, New
York for a tour of the kitchen and a few tips on how to become a chef. Lunch is

g. December 11 for a trip to New York City for "Holiday Sparkle", which
includes shopping at Byrant Park, lunch at Brazil and a two hour guided tour of the

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission only ten
meals are being ordered for the Bistro Program on Mondays.

On Tuesday through Friday the Senior Nutrition Program has from 4-6 people.

She is considering an ice cream social, with discussion on the Program, when she does
energy applications.

Meals-on-Wheels is doing fine.

_ _ (2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson reported she now has two
Chair Yoga classes on Friday. One is at 9:30 a.m. and one is at 10:30 a.m.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. A Drama Club began last Thursday. She is
considering dinner and play.

A retired broker is willing to do a Financial Health Program. Ms. Wilson is
considering having other senior centers be invited to attend.

There will be a Car Fit Technical Program on August 2 in the Community Center
parking lot from 9 a.m. to noon teaching how to adjust mirrors and seats to individuals.

Ms. Wilson would like to have a Pig Roast at McCook's Point Park at our Annual
Picnic.

d. Budget - General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson felt a few transfers

may be needed.

e. Revenues - General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson stated revenues
are higher than what was projected. The Revolving Account is better than what was

projected.
3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Expansion of the Senior Center. Ms. Wilson reported there is a plan to have a
medical piece for employees with expansion at the Senior Center in order for the Town
to get lower premiums. She would like the expansion for activities at the Senior Center.
This plan will go before the Board of Selectmen at their August meeting.

b. Transportation Services. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission transportation
services need changes. More disabled adults are using transportation services. We
have reduced the number of individuals to 6 and we have limited the number of bags.

The Monday New London run has been reduced to 6 individuals. We now have
a waiting list. She is considering two New London runs, one for medical and one for
shopping.

d. Other Pertinent Business. There was no discussion of other pertinent
business.

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Review of Day/Overnight trips for FY 13/14. Ms. Wilson reported from July 1,
2013 to June 30, 2014 we received $322.29 for Day Trips and $1,540.50 for Overnight
Trips.

b. Review of Enrichment Trips for Fiscal Year 13/14. Ms. Wilson informed the
Commission we lost $75 on Enrichment Trips.

c. Bistro Program in Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson stated we lost $333 on the

Bistro Program.

e. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Wilson will evaluate whether or not the second
run on Mondays is worthwhile.

The wheel chair lift is being used more.

Ms. Bengtson suggested a computer person who has offered to teach seniors
free of charge.

Ms. Harris suggested having a course on the use of new telephones.

Mr. Palazzo felt something should be done to stop scams. Ms. Harris suggested
a town mailing.

5. PUBLIC DISCUSSION
There was no public discussion.
