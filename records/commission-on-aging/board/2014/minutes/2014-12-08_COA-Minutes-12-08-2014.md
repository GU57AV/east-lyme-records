2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported all vehicles will go through a quarterly
inspection. All drivers are fine:

b. Senior Trips.
(1) Enrichment Trips. The following enrichment trips are planned: January 19 to
the Hideaway in Old Lyme and January 28 to the Spice Club in Niantic.

(2) Day/Overnight Trips. The following Day/Overnight Trips were planned:
February 27 - March 9, 2015 for a no fly cruise out of New York City to the sunny
Caribbean with Norwegian Gem. The cost starting at $1,104 per person including
taxes. March 27-29, 2015 to Washington, D. C. at cherry blossom time by motor coach,
for a two night hotel stay, two dinners, sightseeing and admission. The cost is $451 per
person for a double. On May 26-28, 2015 to Victorian Williamsport, Pennsylvania for a
tour of the Millionaires Row, Little League Baseball Museum and Hiawatha Paddlewheel
Riverboat. The cost per person for a twin/triple based on 40-50 is $411 and for single
$511. This trip includes deluxe motor coach, two nights in a hotel, 2 breakfasts, two
dinners, sightseeing and admissions. On July 4-10, 2015 to Atlantic Canada Cruise
Tour to Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island for seven days at a cost of $1,637 per
person for a double room, $1,567 for a triple room and $2,217 for a single room. This
trip includes 1 night in a private stateroom on board the ship, 5 nights in a hotel, taxes,
baggage handling and port taxes, 4 breakfast meals and 5 dinners, guided tours, and all
sightseeing and admission fees. On October 30-November 11, 2015 for transportation
to Baltimore, two nights in a hotel in Baltimore with a welcome dinner, a tour of the US
Naval Academy in Annapolis, a ghost tour on Halloween Night and a ten night cruise to
the Caribbean, all meals, daily activities, entertainment are included for a cost of
$1,749, plus taxes.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. We no longer have a TVCCA Site Server. A new
Site Server will start next week.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. All ongoing programs are doing well.

(3) Senior Center Programs—New. —_ Ukulele lessons and the Drama Club are
doing very well.

d. Budget - General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson reported the General
Fund, Revolving Account is doing well.

e. Revenues - General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson stated revenues
are doing well.

3. OLD BUSINESS
a. Budget Review for FY 15/16. Ms. Wilson reported Gail Glenn will be retiring

on July, 2015, and she would like to upgrade the new positions to Program Coordinator
and upgrade the Administrative Secretary to Administrative Assistant.
Ms: Wilson added we have been asked to increase our revenue stream. Ms.
Harris asked if other towns are fundraising? Ms. Wilson replied they are or they are
increasing fees.

This budget has an increase of 4.36%.

Ms. Hardy asked if the increase was due to added hours? Ms. Wilson replied the
increase is due to a change of duties.

She informed the Commission the other increase is due to an annual
maintenance fee for software expense. Senior Services' share is one-third of it. Ms.
Wilson would like to have this come out of the Revolving Account. The telephone
account has increased for the air card which was previously paid for from a grant that
has run out.

Vehicle inspections need to be done by a licensed inspector beginning in
2015/16.

MOTION: Ms. Harris moved to approve the 2015/16 Commission
on Aging Operating Budget in the amount of
$264,417. Seconded by Mr. Palazzo. (6-0) Unanimous.

a. Other Pertinent Business. There was no other pertinent old business.
4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Insert in Tax Bill. Ms. Wilson reported the Tax Collector had no problem with
inserting information from Senior Services with the tax bills. There was a general
consensus to include Senior Services inserts with tax bills.

b. Meeting Schedule for 2015. Ms. Wilson gave commission members a copy of
suggested meeting dates for 2015.

MOTION: John Whritner moved to approve the Commission
on Aging 2015 meeting dates, as submitted.
Seconded by Mr. Palazzo. (6-0) Unanimous.

c. Potential Upgrade of Registration Software. The Commission was given

information on WebTrac. The Parks and Recreation Commission is looking into online
registration. Senior Services can share the software with Parks and Recreation. It will
allow us to accept credit cards.

by Ms. Bengtson. (6-0) Unanimous.

d. Commission Members E-mail Addresses. Ms. Wilson was asked to provide
e-mail addresses for the Town Clerk.

and Commission members are invited to attend. You are asked to RSVP to Darlene
Stevens if you plan to attend.
