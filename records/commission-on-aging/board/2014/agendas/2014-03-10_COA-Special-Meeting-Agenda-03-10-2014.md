Chairman Mountain called the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting of March

January 13, 2014. This matter was delayed until a quorum is established.

d. Correspondence. There was no correspondence.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson one of our volunteer drivers has
relocated. Fourteen people regularly take advantage of the Senior Nutrition Program.
Ms. Smith suggested offering a program ending just before lunch is served and maybe
they will take advantage of the lunch.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson reported she will discuss

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Budget for FY 14/15. Ms. Wilson reported she will meet with Board of
Finance on March 17 at 6:30 p.m.

b. Other Pertinent Business. There was no discussion of other pertinent
business.

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Expansion of the Senior Center. Ms. Wilson has had discussion with First
Selectman Paul Formica and David Putnam, Director of Parks and Recreation, about
expansion of the Senior Center. Mr. Whritner felt each department head at the
Community Center needs to be included in discussions on the expansion of the
Community Center. Ms. Smith suggested determining how much space is available for
expansion.

b. Disbursement from the Parahus Account. Ms. Wilson was informed the cost
of an architectural study on expansion will need to be taken from the Parahus Account.
Ms. Wilson will get an estimate on the cost of an architectural study.

Ms. Wilson reported she received a donation of $279 for a ping pong table for the
Senior Center. The actual cost was $372.72. She asked for reimbursement of $93.17.

MOTION: Mr. Palazzo move to reimburse Cathy
Wilson $93.17 for the extra expense for
the ping pong table for the Senior
Center. Seconded by B. B. Smith.
(4-0) Unanimous).

c. Update on the Energy Assistance Program. East Lyme had 374 applications
of which Ms. Wilson submitted 170. East Lyme received $173,856.08 towards energy
assistance.

d. Municipal Grant for Medical Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported East Lyme
and eight other towns will submit an application for DIAL-a-ride medical transportation.
We are also looking to bring in other towns. There is no cost for the rides but the
number of rides are limited. The application is due on April 3.

e. 5310 Grant from DOT. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission we have
purchased one vehicle with this grant and would like to submit another grant application
in the future. They have expanded the program.

f. Bistro Program. The Bistro Program began in November of 2012. To date we
have a $180 deficit in this program. Ms. Wilson would like to move it out of the Supplies
Account and place it in the Revolving Account.

g. Mayors for Meals. Mr. Formica has agreed to deliver Meals on Wheels to the
homebound.

Ms. Wilson reported we had a recent inspection of our kitchen and received a
good score. Our new Meal Site Server will be QFO trained.

h.Smith Harris Request. Ms. Wilson reported Gary Lakowsky has asked to use
a Senior vehicle on June 14 from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. for individuals to visit each of the
three historical homes in East Lyme. They are willing to cover all costs.

i. Incident Report. The Commission reviewed an incident report.

j. Other Pertinent Business. TVCCA will have its annual fundraiser at the Port n
Starboard on April 12, 2014.
