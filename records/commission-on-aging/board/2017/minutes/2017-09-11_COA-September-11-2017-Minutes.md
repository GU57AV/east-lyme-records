4. NEW BUSINESS

a. New Senior Center Bus and Update of Title VI Policies. Ms. Wilson reported
Title VI is a requirement for any agency that receives federal funds. You cannot
discriminate, and you need to make sure they have a way to get information about
services provided. You can call AT&T and request an interpreter. Each bus will have
language cards so it can be identified what language is spoken. Ms. Wilson is
reviewing a letter to the Town, because we need to have a second person in the Town
to sign off. She plans to have this ready by next month.

MOTION (3): Ms. Bengston moved to authorize
Ms. Wilson to assure that Title VI
policies are adhered to.
Seconded by Mr. Bekech. (7-0)
Unanimous.

b. Registration issues with Enrichment Trips. Ms. Wilson informed the
Commission she has included in their packet what the Senior Center has been doing for
the last three months regarding registration. It now begins on the first business day of
each month at noon. The later registration time is so that people who ride the bus can
get into the Senior Center and register. Individuals are now arriving before 11:30 a.m.
and are in line before the bus arrives.

Consideration is being given to have a lottery.

We can allow individuals to register for one trip and be on a wait list for a second
trip.

We discussed offering the same trip twice a month, but there are not enough
people for it.
Ms. Smith suggested as they walk through the door have them draw a number.
It gives everybody in the building time to register.

Ms. Harris suggested first come, first serve. Ms. Smith felt this would not work,
because some individuals have to rely on transportation.

Mr. Palazzo suggested using the smaller van. Ms. Wilson stated then you would
have two drivers, plus a staff member.

Ms. Harris felt calling in to make a registration is not a good idea. Ms. Smith felt
this was too cumbersome. Ms. Wilson felt we could ask them to call at 8:30 a.m. to
register themselves and one other person. We would need to get the money within two
business days. We might be able to have a credit card system at some point.

Ms. Caste suggested signing up for one and then being on a waiting list for
another.

Ms. Hardy felt phone calls would be time consuming for the staff. She felt
people would understand the lottery. Ms. Caste asked if you use the lottery system how
does your name get put in for the second trip. Ms. Smith felt this could be for only those
individuals who come to the Senior Center. Ms. Wilson felt we could inform them you
can only sign yourself up. If a friend wants to also go they may not be on the same trip.

Ms. Harris suggested contacting other senior centers to see how they handle

this.

MOTION (4): Ms. Smith moved to table this discussion until
the next meeting of this Commission. Seconded
by Ms. Bengston. (7-0) Unanimous.

MOTION (5): Ms. Harris moved to have Ms. Wilson contact

other senior centers to determine how they
handle trips. Seconded by Ms. Bengston. (7-0)
Unanimous.
