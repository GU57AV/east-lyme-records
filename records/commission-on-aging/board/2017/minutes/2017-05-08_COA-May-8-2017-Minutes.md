1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS

Chairman Palazzo called the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting of May 8,
2017 to order at 5:00 p.m.

a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.
b. Delegations. Shirley Lan was present.
c. Approval of Meeting Minutes - April 10, 2017. Mr. Palazzo asked for additions,

corrections or deletions to the April 10, 2017 Commission on Aging Minutes.

MOTION (1): Ms. Smith moved to accept the April 10,
2017 Commission on Aging Regular Meeting
Minutes, as presented. Seconded by Ms. Caste.
Ms. Harris abstained from voting. Mr. Bekech

carried.

d. Correspondence. There was no correspondence.
2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported all vehicles are running well. All drivers
are ok.

Ms. Wilson reported our order for a 12 passenger new vehicle has been placed.
She stated when we submitted the 5310 grant we requested $60,000-$62,000. After
meeting with the garage and drivers specifications the cost was $1535 more.
Mr. Bekech arrived.

MOTION (2): Ms. Harris moved to approve the expenditure
of $1535 to be taken from the Parahus Account
to be able to add to the 5310 grant for a new
vehicle. Seconded by Ms. Caste. (6-0)

She plans to have the Dial-a-Ride paperwork submitted by the end of the week.
We hope to increase the rides to 22 round trips. There are twelve towns participating in
Dial-a-Ride.

The Town has eliminated the islands in the Community Center parking lot. Each
parking space needs to be striped. They have designated areas for our vehicles to
park. Mr. Whritner asked if there will be the same handicap spots? Ms. Wilson replied
they will.

b. Senior Trips.

(1) Enrichment Trips. The following enrichment trips are planned: June 14 for
lunch to the Marina at American Warf in Norwich, CT and June 28 for lunch at the Back
Porch in Old Saybrook, CT. Each are for a trip fee of $3.

(2) Day/Overnight Trips. The following day trips are planned: On June 19 a trip
to Boston Harbor for the return of the Tall Ships. Lunch is included for a trip fee of
$119. On June 23 to the Newport Flower Show at Rosecliff Mansion in Newport with
lunch and shopping on your own for a trip fee of $46. On July 11 to New Bedford, MA
for a guided walking tour of the New Bedford Whaling National Historical Park and in the
p.m. for a guided tour of the New Bedford Whaling Museum. On July 27 to the Gilded
Age of the Berkshires including a tour of Ventford Hall and Naumkeag with lunch at the
Mount for a trip fee of $107 On July 29 to the Bronx Zoo (all ages welcome) for a trip fee
of $70. On August 8 for a tour of Pindar Winery on Long Island and on August 24 to
see "The Producers" at Theater by the Sea.

The following overnight trips are planned: On June 29-30, 2017 to Ogunquit and
Portland, ME for one night and two days with a guided tour of Portland, Lighthouse
Cruise of Casco Bay included. $309/double, $379/single, $299 triple. On September 8-
10 for three days and two nights includes Potomac River Cruise, Museum of African
American History and more $523/double, $693/single, $473 triple. On October 1-11,
2017 for a Fall Foliage Cruise in New England, Canada and Newfoundland with Royal
Caribbean for 11 days and 10 nights. Trip fees from $1520 to $2620 with taxes
included. October 2-4 to see "Jonah" at the Sight and Sound Theatre in Lancaster,
Pennsylvania; November 4-15 for an escorted tour of Croatia, Slovenia and the Adriatic
Coast. The trip fees are from $3,369 to $3,899. Travel insurance is not included.
January 28-February 9, 2018 Southern Caribbean Cruise on Royal Caribbean Anthem
of the Seas for 13 days and 12 nights. No Fly - Get on board at NJ Pier. The trip fees
are from $1799 to $2,019.

Mr. Palazzo reported he has been approached by four individuals who feel the $3 fee
for trips should be increased to $5 to help defray the cost of gas and lunch for the driver
and staff member. Ms. Harris agreed to increasing it to $5. Ms. Wilson added that the
staff and driver are limited to $15 for lunch. Many of East Lyme residents do not have a
way to go out to lunch. She suggested reducing the number of trips. Ms. Smith asked if
Senior Services is also picking up the residents going on the trip? She was informed
they are picked up if they have no way to get to the Community Center.

MOTION (3): Mr. Whritner moved that as of July 1 the fee for Day
trips now costing $3 be increased to $5. Seconded
by Ms. Harris. (6-0) Unanimous.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson met with the Director of the Senior
Nutrition Program to request a stipend for the work of our staff. She was informed they
have no funding. Ms. Wilson plans to write up a proposal to request $50 per week for a
secretarial position, because Senior Service spends five hours per day on this program.
They are also looking at registration software to reduce the paperwork. She added
other parts of the state give restaurant gift certificates. Ms. Harris felt those without
transportation cannot participate. Ms. Harris asked if other senior centers have a similar
problem? Ms. Wilson replied most senior centers have a Town employee who runs the
program along with the Senior Center's own meal program. We would need to hire
someone for that purpose. Ms. Caste asked if Meals-on-Wheels will also be cut? Ms.
Wilson replied that it is targeted. We ended the Bistro Program in April. Our Cafe’
meals are now Monday through Friday. It will have a suggested donation of $3.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson reported the 90+ Tea will be
held on Friday May 19 at 1 p.m. Crescent Point and Bridebrook will be involved. Main
Street Grille will provide soup.

On June 9 and 10 the Drama group will perform in Neal Simon's Fools. If you
purchase a ticket in advance you will receive 20% off at Flanders Fish for dinner on the
same date.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. On June 12, 19 and 26 we will have
access to Youtube. The Senior Learning Program will be on Yellowstone Park. On
June 2 and July 2 we will have a program on winemaking. On June 28 Gladview will
have a cooking solo program on better breathing.

d. Budget - General Fund, Revolving Account. Ms. Wilson reported her
instructor line item will need a transfer. She has had discussions with the Finance

Director about the Special Revolving Account.

e. Revenues - General Fund, Revolving Account. The Commission reviewed
information on revenues. Ms. Wilson stated we have tracked what is deposited on a
monthly basis.

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Budget Process for FY 17/18. Ms. Wilson reported the Town Meeting on the
budget is tonight at 7 p.m. at the High School. The Board of Finance will present its
budget for fiscal year 2017-2018 to the Town. Comments may be made on its budget.
The Referendum vote will be held on May 18. The Town has no information from the
state.

b. Replacement of ELCC Kitchen Appliances. Ms. Wilson reported she has
received a quote from a plumber for natural gas to the new stove of $4,000. If we get
the same stove we now have the plumbing bill will be $800. Mr. Palazzo stated we
have had a couple of different companies check the stove. There is a 3/4" gas line
going into the stove. The stove is deteriorating. Ms. Caste felt this cost is for
maintenance of a town building.

Mr. Palazzo stated the ovens were working until they converted. Ms. Wilson asked if
we had a new stove piped correctly will it work? Mr. Palazzo felt we need a convection
oven and a regular oven. Mr. Whritner stated we had a plan for a new stove, but it
needs to be natural gas. Ms. Harris added the Lions are willing to give us $3,000
towards a new stove. Right now it is not efficient and since this building is owned by the
Town, it needs to be taken care of as soon as possible. Ms. Smith asked is there
another option that could accommodate the less expensive stove? Mr. Palazzo felt we
have funds in the Parahus Account. Ms. Smith felt donations will not be made to the
Senior Center if it is not used for seniors. Ms. Harris felt this expenditure will benefit the
entire town. Mr. Palazzo felt if an individual rented the Senior Center for an event, the
stove could be used. Mr. Whritner felt this is an immediate need.

MOTION (4): Mr. Whritner moved that the Commission on
Aging approve the expenditure of $4,000
from the Parahus Account for the exclusive
use of the plumbing to complete the
improvement of the kitchen for the entire
Senior Center. Seconded by Ms. Harris.
(6-0) Unanimous.

Ms. Wilson agreed to obtain additional quotes from plumbers.

e. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Wilson gave copies of the policy for the
Parahus Account approved in 2011 for their review. There was a general consensus not
to make any changes to it.

5. PUBLIC DISCUSSION

There was no further discussion from the public.

MOTION (5): Mr. Whritner moved to adjourn the April 10, 2017
