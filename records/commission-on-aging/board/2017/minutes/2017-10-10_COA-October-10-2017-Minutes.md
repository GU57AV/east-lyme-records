c. Registration Issues with Enrichment Trips

Ms. Wilson said that she had provided them with information that they had requested from the last
meeting. It was noted that many of the other senior centers wither do not offer any of these type of trips
or offer them only once per month. Those that do offer them have the drivers serve as the trip leaders or
a volunteer; those who go on a wait list are the first ones offered a seat on the next trip; no refunds are
given if you cancel and there is no one to take your place. Montville has people call in starting at 8 AM
and it is first come, first served. She said that there are also a variety of buses that are used and some
of the other centers can take more passengers. She said that she would just like to give everyone a fair
opportunity to register. She suggested that they can have people call on the first business day of the
month starting at 8 AM to register and that they would then have to pay by 4 PM the next business day
or their spot would go to someone on the waitlist. She said that she feels that everyone has a phone so
this would be fair to everyone who wants to go.

Ms. Smith said that she feels that the time in which the payment has to be made has to be very clear.
Ms. Wilson said that she would prefer that people pay the same day.
Ms. Smith said that she would agree as she would not like it to be extended out.

Ms. Caste said that she thinks that this is the fairest way for those people who do not drive.

After further discussion the Commissioners worked out the following details:
= Registration will be by phone call only starting at 8 AM on the first business day of the month.
= People can sign up for themselves or themselves with a caregiver only.
= You can register for one trip only and then be put on a waitlist for the second trip.
«= Payment is due by 4 PM on the next business day.
= The waitlist is opened once the 2 (two) day payment period has ended.
**MOTION (3)
Mr. Whritner moved the following registration policy for enrichment trips:
= Registration will be by phone call only starting at 8 AM on the first business day of the month.
= People can sign up for themselves or themselves with a caregiver only.
= You can register for one trip only and then be put on a waitlist for the second trip.
* Payment is due by 4 PM on the next business day.
= The waitlist is opened once the 2 (two) day payment period has ended.
Ms. Caste seconded the motion.
