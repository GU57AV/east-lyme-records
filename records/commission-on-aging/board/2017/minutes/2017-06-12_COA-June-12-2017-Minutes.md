4, NEW BUSINESS
a. Potential Changes in Pricing Strategy for Senior Center Programs. Ms. Wilson

reported session classes are easier than pass classes. With pass classes we don't
know how many people will come. We may need to have a waiting list. Ms. Harris
asked how do other senior centers handle this? Ms. Wilson replied they only have
session classes. Ms. Smith asked how many classes are they allowed for $60? Ms.
Wilson replied 30 classes for the entire year. She added if someone were to purchase
a session they are more likely to attend classes. Ms. Harris felt the cost is reasonable.

Ms. Wilson informed the Commission at the present time we allow those
individuals 55 and over to come to the Senior Center. Our transportation is available for
individuals 60 or older or disabled. If an individual is under 60, they can attend a Parks
and Recreation program. She also suggested that we consider a sur-charge for non-
residents on big trips.

b. Incident Review. The Commission reviewed an incident report.

c. Lions Club Lobsterfest. Ms. Wilson reported she spoke to the President of the
Lions Club who informed her they wish to use two Senior Center vehicles and two
drivers for the Lobsterfest. They agree to pay all costs associated with this request.

Mr. Bekech asked if we have any liability for this request? Ms. Wilson replied it is
covered under the Town's insurance.
