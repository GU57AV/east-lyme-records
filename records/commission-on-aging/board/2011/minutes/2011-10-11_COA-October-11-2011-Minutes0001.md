Commission on Aging Regular Meeting Minutes
October 11, 2011

Present: Philip Mountain Also Present: Cathy Wilson

B. B. Smith Senior Services Coordinator
Mary Broga Rose Ann Hardy, Ex Officio
Carrie Janus Flo Hurley

2:00 :
Absent: Jane Powers

TOWN CLERK

Chairman Mountain called the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting of October
11, 2011 to order at 6:00 p.m.

1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS

a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

b. Delegations. There was no discussion from guests.

c. Approval of Meeting Minutes — September 12, 2011. Mr. Mountain asked for
additions, deletions or corrections to the Commission on Aging Meeting Minutes of
September 12, 2011.

MOTION: Carrie Janus moved to accept the September
12, 2011 Commission on Aging Minutes, as
presented. Seconded by Carrie Janus.
Marge Caste abstained from voting. (4-0-1)
Motion carried.

d. Correspondence. The monthly status reports were made available to the
Commission.

2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported both vehicles are running well. All
drivers are doing well.

The appeal letter was sent out in September. To date $680 has been received.
B. B. Smith arrived.

(1) Enrichment Trips. The following Enrichment trips are planned: Wednesday,
November 9" to the Annual Greek Festival at St. Sophia Hellenic Wrthodox Church in

New London for a trip fee of $3; Wednesday November 23 to the West Farms Mall in
Farmington, Connecticut for a trip fee of $4.

(2) COA Sponsored Trips. No COA sponsored trips have been scheduled.

(3) Day/Overnight Trips. The following day trips are planned: Wednesday,
November 2 to Newport Playhouse & Cabaret in Newport, R.I. to see “Social Security”
for a trip fee of $83; Tuesday, November 29 to New York Botanical Garden Holiday
Train Show and a trip to Little Italy of the Bronx for lunch on your own. The trip fee is
$59; and on Monday, December 19" “Step into Christmas’ at the Aqua Turf Club in
Plantsville, Connecticut for a trip fee of $74.

The following overnight trips are planned: On November 9-16, 2011 to the
French Riviera, prices range from $2,749 to $3,179; December 8-10, 2011 to see
Niagara Falls and the Canadian Holiday Festival of Lights, prices range from $365 to
$474 per person; February 3-6, 2012 to Quebec City for Winter Carnival, prices range
from $649 to $919 per person; March 15-30, 2012 to see Treasurer of Angkor Wat and
Vietnam, with an optional two nights Hong Kong post tour extension for $5,699 to
$6,599 per person; April 7-20, 2012 to Holland and Belgium in Springtime River Cruise
with an optional 3 nights in Amsterdam, prices range from $3,550 to $3,950 per person;
and September 8-19, 2012 to National Parks of America with prices ranging from
$3,829 to $4,879 per person.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. There have been no changes to the Senior
Nutrition Program.

Rose Ann Hardy asked if the attendance does not pick up will there be budget
implications if meals are prepared at the Senior Center. Cathy Wilson felt there would
be.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson reported all ongoing
programs are doing well.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. Cathy Wilson is considering using a Power
Point Presentation for an Informational Meeting on Medicare Part D twice a year.

d. Budget. The budget is doing fine.
e. Revenues. Revenues are doing well.
3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Other Pertinent Business. Mary Broga asked if Ms. Wilson had discussion
with the gentlemen at AHEPA who asked if the Senior Center bus can be used to take

AHEPA residents to the Wadsworth Atheneum. Ms. Wilson informed him that he may
request a particular trip at the Senior Center, which would be offered to all senior
residents of East Lyme.

4. NEW BUSINESS
a. Budget Preparation FY 12/13. Ms. Wilson asked those present if there was

anything in particular the Commission would like to see in the upcoming budget. They
asked her what she would like.

Cathy Wilson would like full time coverage to replace the part-time coverage in
her office. The Medicare Part D discussions have been an hour long and the individual
must return in order to make their decision.

Ms. Hardy suggested that Ms. Wilson list her increased duti3s over the last two
years and the number of clients she is seeing. Also what duties this full time person
could assume.

Ms. Hardy asked what will happen if you do not get this person? Ms. Wilson
replied she is presently working some evenings and weekends.

Ms. Hardy asked Ms. Wilson if she would like to change how trips are handled?
Ms. Wilson replied she would like to eliminate the subsidized trips and subsidize some
programs instead.

b. Building Used as a Shelter Issues. Ms. Wilson reported we could not run
programs while the Community Center was used as a shelter. We were able to have
the congregate and meals-on-wheels programs. We assisted Waterford with its meals-
on-wheels program.

She felt department heads should be informed if a road is closed prior to drivers
leaving the building.

We were able te assist with communications.

We were able to help Emergency Management.

Larger individuals were unable to use a cot.

c. List of Repeating Rentals of Sr. Center During Off Hours. Ms. Wilson reported
we have six groups that meet at the Senior Center on a regular basis. B. B. Smith

asked what is the charge? Ms. Wilson replied if they are a non-profit local group there
is no charge. Otherwise they are charged $50 each time they meet.

B. B. Smith asked what are the hours that the Senior Center can be rented? Ms.
Wilson replied Monday through Friday from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m.
d. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission that last year

we received $130 million for energy assistance. This year we will receive $65 million.
As she is preparing energy assistance applications she is informing individuals they can
plan to receive one-half of what they received last year. The income levels have not
changed only the benefit levels.

Ms. Broga stated oil companies are waiting longer for payment. The companies
who do not participate are referring individuals to the companies that do participate.

Marge Caste visited the Groton Senior Center and highly recommends the
expenditure of a TV board.

5. PUBLIC DISCUSSION
There was no public discussion.
6. ADJOURNMENT
MOTION: Carrie Janus moved to adjourn the October 11,
2011 Commission on Aging Regular Meeting at
6:45 p.m. Seconded by Marge Caste. (6-0)

Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
