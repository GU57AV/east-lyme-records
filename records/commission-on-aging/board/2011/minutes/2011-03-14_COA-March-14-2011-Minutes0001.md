2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation

Ms. Wilson reported that the R-3 bus that is shared with the Youth Center has developed the same leak
that the R-2 bus had (heater coil) so it has been scheduled to go for repairs on Wednesday and will be
gone for a few days.

She also reported that her part time driver is going away for about five weeks starting the end of this
month and Mr. Collins will fill in during that time.

She is also in the process of putting in for grant funding for medical transportation with other Towns.
Salem will no longer be participating so the grant will be smaller as Salem represents $16,000 of the
grant. The grant paperwork is due by April 1st. From last July 1 through the end of February, 45 people
in East Lyme utilized this service although 100 have registered for it and keep it as a back up in the
event that they have no other transportation option. The program is doing well and they are estimating
around 4000 trips for this fiscal year.

c. Programs

(1)

Senior Nutrition Program — Ms. Wilson reported that the kitchen was recently inspected
and that they received a 96%. They have started the soup and sandwich Prime Time Café
program today and many people from within the building had lunch with them today. It runs
from 11:30-13:30 and you would place an order and pay for it in the AM and come in and eat
and socialize during the 11:30-12:30 time. They will continue with this and see how it goes.
Fresh Prep continues to be served during the week.

Mr. Mountain noted the great kitchen inspection report.
Ms. Wilson said that they were very pleased with it and that things were going well in the kitchen.

Senior Center Programs - Ongoing — Ms. Wilson reported that the on-going programs are
doing well. She noted that her Tuesday Chair Yoga instructor had to give up the class and
that she has one of the other instructors picking it up and has also spoken with another who
will fill in on a substitute basis if necessary. The Tax program has been very busy and
booked solid on both Mondays and Fridays. The Thrill Me Tuesday musical entertainment
has not really increased the counts for lunch as expected and will be reviewed for the
remainder of the programs that have been booked to see if it should be continued or not.
There is a TVCCA St. Patrick’s Day luncheon meal this Thursday for $3.50 and they have 45
people registered. This is a trial as these special meals are usually prepared by the staff
rather than TVCCA. While more people have indicated that they would like to attend, TVCCA
has a much earlier cut off date for registration than they would normally have if staff prepared
the meals. The Zumba Gold class is doing very well and they will do another starting in mid
April. In May they will be looking to do a Tues/Thurs class.

d. Budget
Ms. Wilson reported that the budget is doing well.

e. Revenues

Ms. Wilson reported that the revenues are a little off due to the bad winter weather and that she is
looking for it to pick up with the better weather. They received $440 for the Parahus account from the

3. OLD BUSINESS
a. Budget Request for FY 11/12

Ms. Wilson reported that she will present her budget before the Board of Finance this coming Thursday
at 6:30 PM at the High School INTV Room. She invited them to come.

b. Congregate Dining Room
Ms. Wilson said that she would like to use the $440 of donations in memory of Betty York to freshen up
the dining room area. Ideas will be forth coming.

c. Pickleball

Ms. Wilson said that this popular program ends this month. They will let it go for the summer as people
would rather be outside and start it back up in December. The schedule will be contingent upon interest
and there will be a nominal fee.

Ms. Janus asked Ms. Wilson if she keeps track of the time that she spends on this on Saturdays.
Ms. Wilson said that it is a couple of hours each Saturday.
Ms. Janus asked that Ms. Wilson present the total hours to them at the next meeting of the Commission.
