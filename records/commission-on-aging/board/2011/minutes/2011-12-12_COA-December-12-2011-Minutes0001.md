c. Possible Request from Waterford Senior Services. Sally Ritchie, Director of
Senior Services in Waterford, has been reviewing its Senior Nutrition Program.
Waterford has a Site Server who works 19 % hours and is a Town employee. Ms.
Ritchie would like to eliminate the dining room portion of the Senior Nutrition Program
on Fridays and asked if East Lyme would be willing to heat the meals at its Senior
Center. Meals need to be delivered within two hours. After discussion with First
Selectman Paul Formica it was felt that each Town needs to handle the Senior Nutrition
Program in its own way.

d. Energy Assistance for 2011/2012. Ms. Wilson reported as of today she has
completed 114 applications for energy assistance. She will work on applications at
AHEPHA tomorrow and next week. Last year 159 applications were completed.

Last year in East Lyme 348 households received $288,196.07 worth of
payments. She received an email informing her that funds and staff have been cut. All
electric customers will be processed in March. She will submit this information to the
Board of Selectmen.
