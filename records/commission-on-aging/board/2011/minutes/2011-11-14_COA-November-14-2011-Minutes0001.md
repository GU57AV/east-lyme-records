1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS TOWN CLERK

Chairman Mountain called the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting of
November 14, 2011 to order at 6:00 p.m.

a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

b. Delegations. There were no guests.

c. Approval of Meeting Minutes — October 11, 2011. Mr. Mountain asked for
additions, deletions or corrections to the Commission on Aging Meeting Minutes of
October 11, 2011.

The following corrections were noted: On page 1, under item c., the first motion
was seconded by Ilene Harris. On page 4, under item d., the first two sentences were
revised as follows: “Ms. Wilson informed the Commission that last year the State of
Connecticut received $130 million for energy assistance. This year the state will receive
$65 million.”

MOTION: Marge Caste moved to accept the October
11,2011 Commission on Aging Minutes, as
amended. Seconded by Mary Broga.

(6-0) Unanimous.

d. Correspondence. There was no correspondence received.

2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported there were no issues with transportation.

(1) Enrichment Trips. The following Enrichment trips are planned:
December 14 to the Saddler’s Restaurant and December 28 to Café Mangia.

(2) COA Sponsored Trips. No COA sponsored trips have been scheduled.

(3) Day/Overnight Trips. On December 19 a trip is planned to the Aqua Turf
Club in Plantsville, Connecticut for “Step into Christmas” The show: stars Cahal Dunne
and includes Aideen O Donnell (Harpist & Vocalist) and Irish Step Dancers. The trip fee
is $74.

The following overnight trips are planned: On December 8-10, 2011 to see
Niagara Falls and the Canadian Holiday Festival of Lights, prices range from $365 to
$474 per person; February 3-6, 2012 to Quebec City for Winter Carnival, prices range
from $649 to $919 per person; March 15-30, 2012 to see Treasurer of Angkor Wat and
Vietnam, with an optional two nights Hong Kong post tour extension for $5,699 to
$6,599 per person; April 7-20, 2012 to Holland and Belgium in Springtime River Cruise
with an optional 3 nights in Amsterdam, prices range from $3,550 to $3,950 per person;
and September 8-19, 2012 to National Parks of America with prices ranging from
$3,829 to $4,879 per person.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. There have been no changes to the Senior
Nutrition Program.

A kitchen inspection went well.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson reported all ongoing
programs are doing well. No programs will run the week between Christmas and New
Year.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. Cathy Wilson informed the Commission
Pickleball has resumed. There will be a Holiday luncheon on December 9. A Mocktail
party will be held on December 13 from 5-7 p.m. Hospice Bereavement will be held
December 14-28. On December 20 after lunch the East Lyme High School students will
give a concert.

d. Budget. The budget is doing fine.

e. Revenues. Revenues are doing well.

Fund guidelines (see attached).

a. Budget Preparation FY 12/13. Commission members reviewed the proposed
budget for 2012-2013.

MOTION: — Carrie Janus moved to approve the proposed
budget for the Senior Citizens Commission for
2012-2013 in the amount of $262,575. Seconded
by Ilene Harris. (6-0) Unanimous.

the Lions. Seconded by Marge Caste. Approved:

video board. Ms. Wilson had no update at this time.
