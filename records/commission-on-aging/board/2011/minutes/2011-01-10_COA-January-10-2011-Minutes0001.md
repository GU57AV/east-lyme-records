FILED IN EAST LYME TOWN

Marge Caste
Absent: Ilene Harris

1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS

Chairman Mountain called the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting of January
10, 2011 to order at 6:00 p.m.

a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

b. Delegations. There were no delegations.

c. Approval of Meeting Minutes — December 20, 2010. Mr. Mountain asked for
additions, deletions or corrections to the Commission on Aging Meeting Minutes of
December 20, 2010.

MOTION: B. B. Smith moved to accept the December
20, 2010 Commission on Aging Minutes, as
presented. Seconded by Marge Caste.
(4-0) Unanimous

d. Correspondence. Correspondence was made available.

e. Election of Officers. There was a consensus of the Commission to table this
matter to allow members absent to give their input.

2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation. Cathy Wilson reported one of the new vehicles is still leaking
coolant. Matthew Buses is working on it.

b. Senior Trips.

(1) Enrichment Trips. The following Enrichment trips are planned: Lunch at the
Parthenon Diner in Old Saybrook on February 9 and lunch at the Steak Loft in Mystic on
February 23. Each trip will cost $2.75.

(2) COA Sponsored Trips. There were no COA sponsored trips announced.

(3) Day/Overnight Trips. The following day trips are planned: February 24,
2011 to Newport Playhouse dinner theater for buffet and to see “Norman is that you?”
for $83; March 15, 2011 to Cornerstone Productions to Mohegan Sun and to see
“Shenanigan Street” for $50; April 2, 2011 to tour Grand Central Station, have lunch in
Times Square and visit Chelsea Market for $77; and April 26, 2011 to visit the lower
east side’s Tenement Museum with a stop at Katz’s Deli for $66.

The following overnight trips are planned: April 8-11, 2041 for a Cherry Blossom
Tour in Washington, D.C. The cost for a double room is $559, triple $519 and single
$729; September 2-12, 2011 for a Russian River cruise. Prices range from $4,279 to
$4,379 per person; and March 15-30, 2012 to Angkor Wat and Vietnam. Prices range
from $5,699 to $6,599.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission that musical
entertainment is provided on the third Tuesday of each month.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ongoing programs are doing well.
Participants in Zumba Gold have asked for a Friday class. It will be tried for six weeks.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. Pickle ball has been well received and will
be held on Saturdays from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. through March. Ms. Wilson will be present
through March. Ms. Hardy felt the Commission should consider compensation time for
Ms. Wilson.

Laughter Yoga will start on February 3 at 11:30 a.m. This program is free of
charge.

d. Budget. Ms. Wilson reported overall the budget is doing well. The Day Trip
Account is a little higher than it has been in the past.

e. Revenues. Revenues are doing fine.
3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Budget Request for Fiscal Year 2011/2012. Cathy Wilson will meet with First
Selectman Formica at the end of this month to discuss the upcoming budget. She will
inform the Commission of the dates the Commission on Aging will go before the Board
of Selectmen and the Board of Finance.

b. Other Pertinent Business. There was no discussion of other pertinent
business.

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Youth Center & Senior Center Sharing Vehicle. Cathy Wilson reported the R-3
vehicle was purchased with Town funds with the understanding that Youth Services can
also use it. Youth Services has planned ski trips, and equipment will be transported on
the R-3 bus to the ski area.

b. Use of Senior Center by Lions Low Vision Center. Ms. Wilson informed the
Commission that the Lions have asked to use space at the Senior Center for its Low
Vision Program beginning on Thursdays from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. in the Craft Room. It
would not conflict with any other activity at the Center.

c. Need for Additional On-Call Driver. Cathy Wilson reported she has had two
‘ on-call drivers in the past and one left for another position. She would like to hire a new
on-call driver and asked if the Commission would like to be invoived in the interview
process of an on-call driver. Ms. Smith felt Ms. Wilson should hire the type of individual
she needs. Ms. Janus stated she would defer to Ms. Wilson’s expertise.

d. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Hardy asked the Commission if they would be
willing to review one copy of the daily bus log at the meeting rather than have It mailed
to everyone, in order to save paper. There was no objection to her suggestion.

Ms. Caste asked how donations for riding the bus are handled. Ms. Wilson
stated donations are accepted in the office.

Ms. Hardy, on behalf of the Board of Selectmen, thanked Commission members
for their volunteer services and Ms. Wilson for her dedicated service.

5. PUBLIC DISCUSSION
There was no public discussion.
6. ADJOURNMENT

MOTION: Marge Caste moved to adjourn the January 10,
2011 Commission on Aging Regular Meeting at
6:45 p.m. Seconded by B. B. Smith. (4-0)
Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
