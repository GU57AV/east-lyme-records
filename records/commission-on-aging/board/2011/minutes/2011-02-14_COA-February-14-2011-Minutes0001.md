1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS EAST LYME |CWN CLERK

Chairman Mountain called the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting of
February 14, 2011 to order at 6:00 p.m.

a. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

b. Delegations. There were no delegations.

c. Approval of Meeting Minutes — January 10, 2011. Mr. Mountain asked for
additions, deletions or corrections to the Commission on Aging Meeting Minutes of
January 10, 2011.

MOTION: Carrie Janus moved to accept the January
10, 2011 Commission on Aging Minutes, as
presented. Seconded by Marge Caste.
Ilene Harris and Mary Broga abstained from
voting. (4-0-2) Motion carried.

d. Correspondence. Correspondence was made available.

e. Election of Officers. Commission members were asked for nominations of
officers of the Commission on Aging for 2011.

MOTION: Mary Broga moved that the following officers remain:
Philip Mountain as Chairman, Carrie Janus
as Vice Chair and Ilene Harris as Secretary
of the Commission on Aging for 2011.
Seconded by Marge Caste. (6-0) Unanimous.

2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation. Cathy Wilson reported both vehicles are back in operation
and her drivers are doing well.

Ms. Wilson is working on providing transportation for an individual who has
oxygen and a motorized scooter.

The Municipal Grant will be worked on during the month of March.

b. Senior Trips.

(1) Enrichment Trips. The following Enrichment trips are planned: March 9 for
lunch at the Caddy Shack Café 2 in Norwich. On March 23 for lunch at the Top of the
Hill restaurant located in the Ella Grasso Technical School in Groton, with shopping to
follow afterwards. The trip fee for each is $2.75.

(2) COA Sponsored Trips. There were no COA sponsored trips announced.

(3) Day/Overnight Trips. The following day trips are planned: March 15 for the
Cornerstone Holiday Production of “Shenanigan Street” with a morning side trip to the
Mohegan Sun. On April 2 a trip to New York City for a tour of Grand Central Station
and a visit to Chelsea Market. The trip fee is $77. April 26 a trip to the Tenement
Museum in New York City and a guided tour of the Lower East Side with lunch on your
own at Katz’s Deli. The trip fee is $66. On May 10 a trip to New York City to visit the
Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island with lunch included at SPQR. The trip fee is $98. On
May 25 a trip to Boston, Massachusetts to visit the Kennedy Library and the Gardner
Museum with lunch at Joe Tecce’s. The trip fee is $98. On June 12 a Lighthouse
Cruise in the Narragansett Bay, Rhode Island, with brunch at the Quidnesset Country
Club. The trip fee is $79. On June 30 to Theater by the Sea to see “Man of La
Mancha’.

The following overnight trips are planned: April 8-11, 2011 to see the Cherry
Blossom Special in Washington, D. C. The cost is $559 per person for double
occupancy. September 2-12, 2011 for a Russian River Cruise. Prices range from
$4,279 per person to $4,379 per person. November 9-16, 2011 to the French Riviera.
Prices range from $2,669 to $3,099. March 15-30, 2012 to Angkor Watt and Vietnam.
Prices range from $5,699 per person to $6,599.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission she is
considering rearranging tables so that the area used for the Senior Nutrition Program
can be better utilized.

Soup and sandwiches will be available on Mondays beginning in March.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. All ongoing programs are doing well.

On Mondays and Fridays AARP and IRS trained volunteers are assisting with
taxes.
The AARP Mature Driver Safety Classes have been very successful.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. A Matter of Balance Class will be held on
Saturday and Wednesday beginning in April.

On April 25 there will be discussions on what to shred and what to keep. May 7
will be the.next Shred Day.

The Laughter Yoga Class is well received.
d. Budget. Ms. Wilson reported overall the budget is doing well.

e. Revenues. Revenues have decreased because some trips have cancelled
due to weather conditions.

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Budget Request for Fiscal Year 2011/2012. Cathy Wilson met with First
Selectman Formica to discuss the upcoming budget. She will inform the Commission of
the dates the Commission on Aging will go before the Board of Selectmen and the
Board of Finance.

b. Other Pertinent Business. There was a general consensus of the Commission
to review one copy of the daily bus log at the meeting.

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Nurse Managed Clinics by VNASC. The VNA and UCONN School of Nursing
will hold a Nurse Managed Clinic on the second Wednesday from 1 p.m. to 3 p.m.
beginning this month. On the fourth Wednesday of the month a Blood Pressure Clinic
will be held.

b. Pickleball. Pickleball is very popular. It is held on Saturdays from 11 a.m. to
1 p.m. Ms. Wilson is planning Bocce and Pickleball tournaments during Celebrate East
Lyme.

d. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Wilson will be on vacation beginning February
23. She plans to be back at work on March 7.

She discussed a home visit with the Commission.
5. PUBLIC DISCUSSION

There was no public discussion.
6. ADJOURNMENT

MOTION: Ilene Harris moved to adjourn the February 14,
2011 Commission on Aging Regular Meeting at
6:50 p.m. Seconded by Marge Caste. (6-0)
Unanimous.
