(1) Senior Nutrition Program. Ms. Wilson reported she received a copy of a
letter addressed to Mr. Formica from Deborah Monahan and Tim Grills of TVCCA that
on May 8 TVCCA received notification on the final allocation of funding for the Senior
Nutrition Program, congregate and Meals on Wheels program that for fiscal year 2011-

2012 the funds were less than expected and there will be a reduction in services
beginning the week of June 4, 2012 through September 30, 2012. Services will be
provided four days per week. The program has been cancelled on Monday. Ms. Wilson
informed the Commission that we are able to use our budget to continue this service
until June 30. This has been discussed with Mr. Formica. Any voiunteer who delivers
Meals-on- Wheels on Monday will not be able to receive mileage reimbursement.
TVCCA has indicated that they expect to know its budget status by August. We are
serving a small number of people for congregate meals. Ms. Broga felt Meals-on-
Wheels will always be needed. Ms. Wilson stated senior centers are considering
eliminating congregate meals. Ms. Janus asked Ms. Wilson if it is possible to work with
the school system on the congregate meals. Ms. Wilson replied that is a possibility.

Ms. Wilson reported each Town has a different relationship with TVCCA. Most of
the Northeastern part of Connecticut accepted it. In Southeastern Connecticut it is
different in each Town. Mr. Grills has approached us to promote congregate meals.

Ms. Wilson invited AHEPHA, Twin Haven and churches on Wednesday June 13 at noon
to taste the quality of food and have discussion with Mr. Grills, TVCCA Director of
Nutrition Services.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson stated the Lions Low Vision
and the VNA nurses managed clinics continue to have a low attendance. The

bereavement program.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. Line dancing class is doing very well.
