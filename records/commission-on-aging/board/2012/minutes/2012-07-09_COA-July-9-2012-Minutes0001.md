2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation

Ms. Wilson reported that Youth Services would be using the R-1 bus for the next month. She said that
this should not present any problems. R-1 is the Ford van and it has passed the emissions testing. The
R-2 bus still has to go in for the recall item. She also reported on the letter that was sent to Mr. Mitchell
asking about the three (3) AHEPA’s and the potential of obtaining a vehicle. He wrote back about a grant
and she will help with the grant writing. She said that she was not sure if he has a funding source in
mind and/or other people to help with the writing — all details that still have to be worked out.

Ms. Hardy said that she thought that there could possibly be some AHEPA monies that George might
know of. She asked about how many people they are transporting from AHEPA.

Ms. Wilson said that the majority of the Tuesday shopping run is AHEPA people (8) and other days they
transport people for medical and other appointments. She also said that she was asked why they don’t
supply a Senior Center bus to the casinos. She said that this is not something that they have done.
Ms. Caste said that she thought that it was motor coach trips that took people to the casinos and that on
her visits there (to the casinos) she did not recall seeing buses from any of the senior centers.

Ms. Wilson said that she was also approached by the Clamshell Quilters about a Quilting show that is
being held at Connecticut College in August — they would like to have a bus offered to take people to it.
She said that she would look into it however, they would have to charge a fee for it and the people would
have to pay their own admission to the show. It would be like a senior trip. She said that she would see if
there is interest.

The Commissioners suggested that Ms. Wilson look into it and use her judgment on if there is enough
interest to make it feasible to go.

Ms. Hardy asked if there were any requests for a bus to OpSail.
Ms. Wilson said no.

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. September COA Meeting/Vacation of Director

Ms. Wilson said that she would be on vacation for two weeks and that her vacation time would cover the
date of their Regular September meeting. She asked if they would like to re-schedule it to a time before
she leaves on vacation or wait until the end of the month when she returns.

The consensus of the commissioners was that they would prefer that the meeting be re-scheduled to a
time before Ms. Wilson leaves on vacation. After discussion — it was agreed that the Regular Meeting
scheduled for September 10, 2012 would be cancelled and a Special Meeting of the Commission would
be scheduled for Wednesday, September 5, 2012 at 6 PM instead.

b. Potential Newsletter Issue

Ms. Wilson said that they have dealt with Senior Citizens Publishing for many years and that they were
recently purchased by Community Communications Inc. who do church bulletins and senior center
newsletters. This new company has been weaning some of the less profitable newsletters out due to the
lack of advertising from those areas. She said that she has been working with their sales representative
and has sent out a letter to various potential sponsors with little success. She said that she would hate to
lose this newsletter as it reaches at least 700 households not counting the number that are picked up at
the senior center by those who visit and have come to rely on it. She said that she will try to look into
other avenues for support.
