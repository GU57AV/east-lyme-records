2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation. Ms. Wilson reported her part-time driver has returned from
vacation. Senior Services is continuing to offer transportation to New London on the
first and third Wednesday of the month. There are few individuals taking advantage of
this transportation. The Monday New London run is usually a full bus. Ttransportation
to New London on Monday and twice a month on Wednesday will be continued.

We have a recall for the lift on both of our new vehicles. The R-3 bus is
scheduled to have the lift repaired on June 5 and it is expected to be repaired by June
7. The R-2 will be scheduled after June 7.

afterwards. The trip fee is $3. Admission to the train is separate.

(2) COA Sponsored Trips. There are no COA sponsored trips scheduled.

c. Programs.

(1) Senior Nutrition Program. Senior Services directors were invited on May 9
to discuss the Senior Nutrition Program at Senior Resources. This program is
constrained by federal regulations. Reservations need to be made 72 hours in
advance. East Lyme has five or six taking advantage of these meals.

Many towns are cooking their own food, because the counts are very low.
On Monday East Lyme serves soup and sandwiches and no reservations are needed.
Twelve to twenty individuals are served.

_ Mary Broga asked if it was possible to continue Meals-on-wheels without
the congregate meals?_ Ms. Wilson felt they can. Food programs may become a
revenue stream for senior centers.

(2) Senior Center Programs-Ongoing. Ms. Wilson stated the Lions Low Vision
and the VNA nurses managed clinics have a low attendance and since we switched the
blood pressure clinic to the 4" Wednesday of the month, we have very few individuals
coming.

The Celebrate 90 program was enjoyed by all who attended. One hundred and
twenty attended.

(3) Senior Center Programs — New. Line dancing class is doing well. The
class will end on June 20. The instructor would like it to be an ongoing class.

Monday night Bingo will start in June.
A dance was held last Friday and everyone seemed to enjoy it.
d. Budget. The budget is doing fine.

We began this month to work with the schools to order food and paper good.
This should save time and money.

e. Revenues. Revenues are doing well.
3. OLD BUSINESS
a. Budget Request FY 2013. Ms. Wilson reported the Commission on Aging

budget request for fiscal year 2013 has remained in tact. The Annual Town Meeting will
be held at 7 p.m. tonight. The Referendum will be held next Thursday.

b. Volunteer Recognition Event. The Volunteer Recognition will be on June 20 in
the evening.

c. Vacancy on the Board. Two individuals have expressed an interest on serving
on this Board. The Board of Selectmen will appoint one of them.

e. Other Pertinent Business. There was no other pertinent business.

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Possible Expenditures from the Parahus Account. Ms. Wilson would like to
purchase a room divider and office furniture, using the $4,000 from TVCCA for her

have furniture she could use. Ms. Wilson did not feel it would suit her needs.

b. Other Pertinent Business. Ms. Wilson informed the Commission there has
been discussion on a dental clinic to be held in a school during its April 2013 vacation.
Volunteers will be needed.
