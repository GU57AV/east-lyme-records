a. Transportation. Cathy Wilson reported we are preparing a grant application
for municipal transportation for medical appointments. The town of Lisbon has joined

on April 2.

Mondays are ccntinuing to be busy days.

Tne trip fee is $126.00; July 26 to Newport, RI for a cruise of Newport Harbor with lunch

$96.00.

The following overnight trips are planned: June 19-21 to Cape Cod/Nantucket
Island. The cost is $399 per person for double occupancy; August 9-10 to Lake George
and Saratoga Race Track. The cost is $275 per person for double occupancy;
