1. PRELIMINARY BUSINESS
Chairman Mountain called the Commission on Aging Regular Meeting of September 5, 2012 to order at
6:00 PM.

a. Pledge of Allegiance
The Pledge was observed.

b. Delegations
There were no delegations.

Meeting Minutes of August 6, 2012.

Mr. Whritner moved to approve the August 6, 2012 Special Meeting Minutes of the Commission on

2. STATUS REPORT

a. Transportation

Ms. Wilson reported that they had been offering another trip to New London during the week on
Wednesday on a trail basis for a couple of months and that the response to it has been lukewarm at best
so it has been suspended for the time being. She said that she is still considering re-instituting this extra
New London run for the fall if it becomes necessary but the Wednesday New London run has been
eliminated at this time.

The Youth Center used a vehicle twelve (12) times and put 200 miles on it. The inspections/registrations
have all been sent to the DMV.
b. Senior Trips

(1) Enrichment Trips — Ms. Wilson reported that on October 10, 2012 that they would go to the
‘Six Paca Farm’ in Bozrah to see the Alpacas with lunch on your own at Old Tymes in
Norwich; and on October 24, 2012 they would do a Leaf Peeping Trip to Northeast CT with
lunch at Hank’s Restaurant in Brooklyn.

(2) COA Sponsored Trips - Ms. Wilson reported that no COA sponsored trips have been
- scheduled at this time and that this item has been combined with the Parks & Recreation
Special Revenue program as of the new fiscal year.

Ms. Wilson said that they would be holding a trip subcommittee meeting to discuss ideas on
trips to offer for the upcoming year in early October once she returns from vacation. In the
meantime she said that she has emailed the people on the trip email list and asked if they
have any suggestions on the types of trips that they would like to go on.

(3) Day/Overnight Trips - Ms. Wilson reported that the Rockport /Gloucester trip had gone and
that it seemed that they would be going to New York to the 911 Memorial tomorrow with a full
bus. She also reported on the following upcoming day trips:

c. Programs
(1) Senior Nutrition Program — Ms. Wilson reported that this program requires that you have to
be at least 60 years old to participate in it. If not, then you are supposed to pay $7 for the
lunch. However, they were informed that the $7 fee was computed incorrectly and that the
actual cost for those less than 60 years of age should be $12.47. She said that they rarely
get people in that category so it should not affect them.

This summer they have been paying for the site servers to work on Mondays due to the re-
allocation of funds. They stopped serving Meals on Wheels and meals on Mondays but they
have been covering Jan thru September. She said that she has spoken with Timothy Grills
regarding the Mondays which has been soup & sandwich offerings only and as of October 1,
2012 they will take Jan back and pay for her and they will also take the proceeds from the
soup & sandwich program. She noted that the congregate and MOW’s operate together.

She lastly reported that a recent kitchen inspection received a score of 100.

(2) Senior Center Programs - Ongoing — Ms. Wilson reported that the on-going programs are
doing well and that there are no issues.

(3) Senior Center Programs - New — Ms. Wilson reported that flyers went out for the
September 29, 2012 Drug Take Back Day; and they would be holding Flu Clinics on October

d. Budget
Ms. Wilson reported that the new fiscal year has just begun and that they are on target.

3. OLD BUSINESS

a. Purchase of Office Furniture

Ms. Wilson reported that they have received their new office furniture and that it is all in place.
Everything matches and it affords them a bit more room and more efficient work stations. She asked that
they stop in the office on their way out and take a look at it.

b. FT Adm. Secretary Position
Ms. Wilson said that this position is filled and that everything is working out.

c. Potential Newsletter Issue
Ms. Wilson reported that she received a call form CPI Newsletters about a potential advertising plan and
has not heard anything since that call.

d. Other Pertinent Business
There was none.

4. NEW BUSINESS

a. Appeal Letter for Transportation

Ms. Wilson said that the transportation of people is not on a ‘fee based’ method so they send out letters
once a year asking for a donation. They enclose self-addressed envelopes for people to mail back
donations.

Ms. Harris asked how this has been working out.

Ms. Wilson said that she has had people asking when the letters would be going out.

b. Review of New Revolving Account

Ms. Wilson said that they started this new account for their trips on July 1, 2012 but they started
separating the monies out in June. So far they have collected $10,139.59 and paid out $3,300. She said
that the start up will take time to work out and that they would see two revenue sheets initially.

c. Annual Report

Ms. Wilson said that they used to get a notice each year to compile an annual report for their
department. While she has not received this notice for some time, she said that she compiles the report
for herself anyway as a means of keeping track of the events that they have offered and what has been
done during the year. She said that she would include a copy of it in their packets for the next meeting.

d. Other Pertinent Business
There was none.
