                              Public Hearing Minutes March 9, 2009
                              East Lyme Charter Revision Commission


 Present:      Leonard Goldberg
               Steven McCue
               Carole Green
               Lawrence White

 Also Present: Barbara White
               Bob Gadbois
               Michael and Karen Rak
               John Drabik
               Bob Kleinhans

 I. Call to Order. Chairman Goldberg called the Public Hearing of March 9, 2009 to order at 7 p.m.

 II. Pledge of Allegiance. The Pledge of Allegiance was observed.

 III. Presentations from the Public. Mr. Goldberg asked members of the public to address the
 Commission at the podium if they wished to speak.

      John Drabik of 18 Drabik Road, East Lyme, stated he agreed with most of the work of this
 Commission.

        He felt there should be further debate on Bifurcation of the General Government and
 Education budgets. He stated the Board of Education presents its budget separately from the General
 Government, and that each budget should be voted separately. This would give the Board of
 Selectmen and the Board of Finance a better idea of what residents want.

        He agreed with this Commission that the Town Budget should go to an automatic
 referendum.

        Robert Gadbois of 358 Boston Post Road, East Lyme, agreed with Mr. Drabik that the Board
 of Education and General Government budgets should be split. He stated many other towns split
 them.

        He felt any taxpayer should be allowed to vote in an election.

        Karen Rak, 27 Black Point Road, Niantic, would like to see bifurcation of the General
 Government and Education budgets, because it is a value statement for some individuals, and it gives
 the Town direction.

        John Drabik of 18 Drabik Road, East Lyme felt any individual with even $1 worth of
 assessment should have the right to vote on the budget.

        There were no other comments from the public. Mr. Goldberg thanked those present for their
 input. The Public Hearing was closed at 7:15 p.m.
