VI.    PUBLIC HEARINGS:
         A. Application of Jason Pazzaglia, for Pazz and Construction LLC, Owner; Application and
            waiver request from Section 23.5 B of the East Lyme Zoning Regulations for a 1-lot re-
            subdivision of 5.29 acres of land within the R-40 zoning district located at 24 Darrows Ridge
            Rd., East Lyme, Assessor’s map 44.0, Lot 8-31.
             K. Scott started the Public Hearing at 7:15. G. Goeschel stated exhibits A-H are available on the
             town website. The legal notice was published on July 15, 2020 and July 23,2020 in the Day paper.
             G. Goeschel informed the commission the application is the same as approved by the commission
             several years back. The previous application is invalid because the applicant failed to file Mylar’s
             in the 90-day period after the approval.
             Ted Harris, 351 Main St. Niantic, attorney for the applicant stated the materials are identical to the
             application the commission approved previously. He stated there were two nights of public
             hearings on the previous application in 2016 and the application was approved in January of 2017.
             T. Harris informed the members that there is case law that states if an application has been
             previously approved and there have been no material changes then the commission is obligated to
             approve the application unless there are regulatory, plan changes, etc. He stated there is an active
             and valid wetlands permit.
             Bob Pfanner, 37 Grand St. Niantic, engineer for the applicant gave background information on the
             proposed work. There will be one new lot created from one existing lot accessed by their own
             driveway. There is one common rain garden which will be in the deed restrictions. Each lot will
             have their own septic system.
             Public Comment-none
             MOTION: (Gordon/Davison) to close the public hearing at 7:30. Vote: Approved
             Unanimously.
         B. Application of Kristen T. Clarke, P.E., for English Harbour Asset Management LLC,
            Owner; Application for a 4-lot re-subdivision of approximately 3.8 acres of land zoned RU-
            40, located at 22 and 2 Upper Kensington Dr., East Lyme, Assessor’s Map 40.0, Lot 22 and
            23.
             K. Scott started the public hearing at 7:34.
             G. Goeschel read the legal notice for the public hearing which was in the July 15, 2020 and the
             July 23, 2020 editions of the Day paper. He stated exhibit items A-K are on the towns website for
             public review.
             Paul M. Geraghty, Esq., 38 Grand St. Niantic, attorney for English Harbour Asset Management
             LLC, stated there is a memo with exhibits AA-NN. The application is for a re-subdivision for two
             previously approved lots into three lots. A fourth lot will be donated to the East Lyme Land Trust.
             He informed the commission that there was a site walk with the East Lyme Wetlands Agency and
             the agency determined they had no jurisdiction because no activity was proposed in a regulated
             area. There is a rain garden in the lower edge of the property. The rain liters drain into the rain
             garden.
             The design reduces the amount of impervious surface by combining the driveways into one
             common driveway. There is a buffer of 15 feet on each side of the driveway.
             P. Geraghty addressed comments in a letter sent to the town concerning the application. He stated
             the subdivision is done in phases because the sanitarian and previous town planner preferred it be
             done in phases. The letter stated K. Clarke has a conflict of interest; P. Geraghty stated her work is
             reviewed by town officials and she is not going to jeopardize her P.E. license by putting false
             information on plans. According to the letter there is concern the proposed houses will not
