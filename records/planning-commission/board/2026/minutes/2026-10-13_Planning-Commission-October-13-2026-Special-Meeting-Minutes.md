        A. Application of The East Lyme Zoning Commission for a proposed Text

           -There is no proposed Data Center location in East Lyme

-Mr. LaDucer noted, in the POCD, Chapter 6 speaks to preservation of open
space.


-Mr. Menapace does not believe the POCD speaks to this specific topic, it
encourages commercial use, but necessarily large buildings


-Ms. Maitland thinks they are more complex than the proposed language reflects.
She noted in doing her own research there are four sizes: edge, enterprise,
colocation, and hyper scale. She believed the data center proposal that was in
Waterford fell under the hyper scale, but that East Lyme may not want to oppose
an edge or an enterprise sized data center. Additionally, last month, the Southern
Environmental Law Center issued a repoft regarding local approaches to data
center development, and there are green data centers. There are opportunities to
not unduly limit the development choices by giving more detail in the definition.


-There was discussion on where a data center would be allowed in East Lyme
now, such as in the Light Industrial (Lf Zone, which is a small portion of town


-Mr. LaDucer noted there wasn't anywhere in town to put a data center and
putting one up goes against the POCD, because most of the town has some form
of protected water in it. The environmental impact would be great.

-Mr. Goeschel read that prohibiting data centers is consistent with the POCD's
water, aquifer, sewer, and energy policies. (Prohibiting) Accessory data centers, as
drafted, is inconsistent with the POCD and is over broad and needs a threshold
and exemptions. CGS 8-2 generally allows the town to prohibit them, however,
the Zoning Commission's reasons should be stated on the record. In reading
further, he suggested regulate rather than prohibit.


-Mr. Menapace stated he would like to suggest the Zoning Commission have
more specificity as to what the text amendment does regulate as there are several
types ofdata centers.


-There was discussion on the nuances of the wording of the proposed text
amendment and whether the data centers should be prohibited and/or regulated
and if the definition should be expanded. There is pushback across the country
regarding the large-scale buildings and how adata center would serve the
residents of East Lyme.
