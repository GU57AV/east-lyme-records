IL Public Delegations

Public Delegations is the time when members of the public are invited to speak to the Commission
about certain matters. Issues or concerns related to approved subdivisions under construction (Item VD

It. Zoning Referral
1. Request of the Town of East Lyme Zoning Commission to amend the East Lyme Zoning
Regulations Section 20.27 Item #4 to expressly prohibit outdoor bars and services bars.

Mr. Goeschel noted that he had provided, for their review, a copy of an analysis of this request with
respect to the goals and objectives as outlined in the 2009 POCD. He noted that the proposed
regulation appears to be inconsistent as it proposes to prohibit a form of commercial activity (outdoor
bars and services bars) within the CA and CB Commercial Districts and as such would not enhance the
goods or services available for local residents. The proposed regulation also would not promote the
expansion of business and would not contribute to the strengthening of the overall tax base.

In addition the proposal does appear to be consistent with the objective to maintain the traditional New
England character of the community and enhance the village identities of East Lyme.
Discussion ensued regarding how this might inhibit economic development in areas that were just
showing signs of rebounding. It was further suggested that this might be better served by use of the
special permit process — as with the outdoor dining which renews annually.

**MOTION (1)

Mr. Schuch moved to find the Request of the Town of East Lyme Zoning Commission to amend
the East Lyme Zoning Regulations Section 20.27 Item #4 to expressly prohibit outdoor bars and
services bars INCONSISTENT with the goals and objectives of the 2009 POCD based on the
findings and memorandum from Gary Goeschel to the Planning Commission dated May 15,
2012.

Ms. Palazzo seconded the motion.

Vote: 6-0-0. Motion passed.
