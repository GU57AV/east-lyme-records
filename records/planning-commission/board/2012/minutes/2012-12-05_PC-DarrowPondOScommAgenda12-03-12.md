          Darrow’s Pond Open Space Planning Committee

                              The members of the Darrow’s Open Space
                              Planning Committee would like to invite fellow
                              towns members to our Dec. 3rd meeting for the
                              purpose of hearing two important presentations
                              by respective experts, regarding our beautiful
                              Darrow’s property.

Raymond (Dick) Sherwood, DEP Forester: Dick
has conducted a multi‐day onsite review of the
property and has prepared a report for our town
on his overview/review. He will cover prominent
property features and attributes, biodiversity,
problematic issues, and make recommendations
on the town’s path forward for managing the
parcel (i.e. stewardship).

                             James R. Cowen, M.A.L.D., Soil and Wetland
                             Scientist, Environmental Planning Services:
                             James has agreed to provide our town with an
                             early assessment of the property from the point of
                             view of invasive species management. According
                             to all experts the Darrow’s committee has worked
                             with, control of invasive species is the most urgent
and biggest challenge the town faces in managing the property. James will
provide an overview of the service they provide and make some initial
recommendations, based on a review of our property.
