Mr. Schuch noted that the issue here was for clarification with respect to property rights,

Mr. Goeschel said that he thought that they could possibly add a word to fix the issue of taking people’s
rights away. He added that he would prefer that they work on the ordinance first rather than the brochure that

was brought to them.

the road’ when you have plenty of them on a road that are State or private entities rather than regular

position in a State office sending a letter saying that they did not have any issue with it. The percentage of
landowners needs further delineation.
