Public Hearing II
1. Application of Fred Finn Jr., LS for Patrick Quinn, Owner for a subdivision of 2.96 acres of

property located at 285 Chesterfield Road, Assessor’s Map 44.0, Lot 13.

Mr. Bowers noted that they had continued this Public Hearing and called upon the applicant's representative
to present any further information.

Fred Finn, Land Surveyor, 15 Colton Road place of business said that he is representing Mr. Quinn and that
they had continued this Public Hearing as they were waiting for action on it from the Inland Wetlands
Agency. Mr. Goeschel has the approval from the Inland Wetlands Agency.

Mr. Goeschel noted that Exhibit O is the approval and that he also added Exhibit P to the listing as that is a
letter from him to Mr. Quinn stating that the Inland Wetlands Agency has approved his application and has
noted that any changes to the plan would require that they would have to go back before the agency with the
changes. Exhibit Q is the revised site plan from the last meeting — it is dated 1/3/2012 and depicts the swale
as was requested. Exhibit K is a memo from Mr. Calkins of Ledge Light Health District noting that both lots
are suitable for septicin their current condition. They are proposing a rain garden and drainage swale with
drywell to collect run-off. This has been reviewed by the Town Engineer and he has suggested a $4000 bond
for the grass swale and rain garden with a deed restriction to make sure that it will be maintained.

Mr. Finn said that the applicant will not be the person developing the property and that he will put it up for
sale. He requested that the bond be placed at the time that someone actually comes in to build on the lot so
the future owner would be the one to bond it. He said that he would put in a proviso that at the time it is sold
that the person purchasing the property and building on it would have to post the bond.

Mr. Bowers asked Mr. Goeschel if the Town Engineer felt it was okay to waive the storm water regulations as
requested.

Mr. Goeschel said that Mr. Scheer had provided a memo stating that he felt that the requirements for the
waiver had been met. He noted that the sections as published were correctly stated and that the correct

sections had been reviewed.
