East Lyme Planning Commission Special Meeting Minutes

SLERK

Tuesday May 17, 2016

Present: Peter Lynch, Brenda Henderson, Michael Hess, Kirk Scott
Also Present: Gary Goeschel, Planning Director

Absent: Brian Schuch, Rita Palazzo, Joan Bengtson, Anthony Arias — Alternate, and
Holly Cheeseman - Ex-Officio

Mr. Lynch called the meeting to order at 7:03PM and sat Alternates Kirk Scott and
Michael Hess.

I NEW BUSINESS
A Acceptance of East Society Road Extension; Gateway Commons

Mr. Goeschel reviewed the details of the proposed road acceptance of the East Society Road
extension and cul-de-sac. He indicated the Gateway Master Development Plan contemplates
the eventual extension of East Society Road to Flanders Road.

Motion (1): Mike Hess moved to approve for acceptance by the Board of Selectmen for public
highway purposes a certain roadway designated as a cul-de-sac at the eastern
terminus of East Society Road, as more particularly described on a map depicting
said road and in a deed conveying said road, and a drainage easement
appurtenant thereto.

Seconded by: Kirk Scott
There was no discussion.
Vote: 4-0-0. Motion Passed
Motion (2): Mike Hess moved to adjourn the meeting at 7:12PM
Seconded by: Kirk Scott
There was no discussion

Vote: 4-0-0. Motion Passed

Respectfully Submitted,

IZ,

Gary A. Goeschel II, Director of Planning
