Vi. Zoning Referrals (CGS 8-3a)

Proposed changes in zoning regulations or boundaries shall be referred to the Planning Commission for a
report that shall contain the findings of the planning commission on consistency of a proposed regulation
or boundary changes with the Plan of Development and any other recommendations the Planning
Commission deems relevant. Failure of the Planning Commission to report prior to or at the hearing shall
be taken as approval of such proposals. The report of the Planning Commission regarding such proposal
shall include the reasons for the commission’s vote and shall be incorporated into the records of any
public hearing held thereon by the Zoning Commission. A proposal disapproved by the Planning
Commission may be adopted by the Zoning Commission by a vote of not less than two-thirds of all the
members of Zoning Commission.

B. Budget Discussion

Mr. Goeschel explained that a change in employment status from part-time to full-time of the
Planning Recording Secretary, as well as the increase in the amount of meetings and the
lengthy duration of said meetings has led to Planning Commission exceeding their clerical
budget.

Mr. Goeschel added that the Board of Selectmen approved a $1,000 transfer from Contingency
to cover this expense for the rest of the year. He clarified that budget cuts will require this same
request to occur next year if the Commission continues with the same Recording Secretary.

The Commission agreed they were happy with the minutes taken and would like retain the
current Secretary. The Secretary and Mr. Goeschel discussed streamlining the minutes
emphasizing that meeting minutes should never be a transcript.
