Mr. Schuch called this Regular Meeting of the Planning Commission to order at 7:17p.m.

I. Additions to the Agenda
There were none.

Il. Public Delegations
Public Delegations is the time when members of the public are invited to speak to the Commission about
certain matters. Issues or concerns related to approve subdivisions under construction (Item VI) and in-
house proposals or general topics of discussion (Item Vil) are open to comment. Items, referrals, or
applications subject to a decision by the Commission, a public hearing, or in litigation may not_be
discussed. The members of the Commission will not directly answer questions or make comment during
delegations.

There were none.

Il. Reports
A. Communications

Mr. Goeschel detailed the proposal by American Towers, LLC and New Cingular Wireless PCS, LLC for a
cell tower at 2 Arbor Crossing; there is already an existing tower on this site and the silo being proposed
would actually be lower in height than the existing tower.

The Commission briefly discussed this referral.
A. Liaison to Zoning
Ms. Palazzo said the last Zoning Meeting was cancelled.
B. Ex-Officio: Holly Cheeseman
Ms. Cheeseman was not in attendance.
C. Town Planner Gary Goeschel

Mr. Goeschel said he is holding a community resilience building workshop with the Nature Conservatory
on November 2™, 2016. The workshop will primarily consist of Town Staff and he will be sending out a
“save the date” notice. \

Mr. Goeschel also briefly discussed the final draft of the JLUS Study.

D. Sub-Committees
a. Sustainable Development and Climate Adaptation

They have not met, but the workshop with Adam Welchel of the Nature Conservancy will take place on
November 2™, 2016.

b, Walkability

The Walkability Subcommittee met this evening at 6:00p.m. Land Use Intern Kevin presented a
proposed Sidewalk Plan. The Sub-Committee is looking to perform a suitability study to help with this
project. Mr. Schuch said they also want to measure the current walkability in Town.

Mr. Schuch had nothing to report.

A. Subdivision Application

Re-Subdivision 24 Darrows Ridge Road, East Lyme; Jason Pazzaglia, Applicant; Pazz and Construction

Vi. Zoning Referrals (CGS 8-3a)

Proposed changes in zoning regulations or boundaries shall be referred to the Planning Commission for a
report that shall contain the findings of the planning commission on consistency of a proposed regulation
or boundary changes with the Plan of Development and any other recommendations the Planning
Commission deems relevant. Failure of the Planning Commission to report prior to or at the hearing shall
be taken as approval of such proposals. The report of the Planning Commission regarding such proposal
shall include the reasons for the commission’s vote and shall be incorporated into the records of any
public hearing held thereon by the Zoning Commission. A proposal disapproved by the Planning
Commission may be adopted by the Zoning Commission by a vote of not less than two-thirds of all the
members of Zoning Commission.

There were no zoning referrals.

VIL. Municipal Referrals (CGS 8-24)

A. Town of East Lyme; Installation of 101 (+/-) FT wide main float with associated restraint
piles, and water/electric utilities accessed from an existing float and ramp and the
installation of a new ADA kayak/canoe launch float, all waterward of the Mean High
Water and CT Coastal Jurisdiction Lines (CJL.)

B. Town of East Lyme; Construct a new 5-FT wide x 9 (+/-) LF wood pile and timber ramp
landing with rails and associated hinged hinged ramp. Install an 8-FT wide float and 40
(+/-) LF of 6’FT wide float all with associated restraint piles and safety rails along the
shoreward edge of the floats, waterward of the Mean High Water and CT Coastal
Jurisdiction Lines (CJL.)

5|Page

Mr. Nielson, P.E. of DOCKO, INC was in attendance to present this proposal. Both of these projects have
been submitted to DEEP for permits.

The Grand Street Dock will provide boaters using the boat launch a loading zone on the north side of the
dock. The south side of the dock will provide dingy dockage space for visiting boats that are anchored or

moored. This will allow boaters to walk in Town and visit the local businesses.

The Cini Park Project will provide dingy dock space as well as space for a few smaller boats. There is a
handicapped kayak launch attached to the west side of the dock that will be one of the first in the State.

Overall, both projects will enhance public access and provide transient boater access to the beach, Cini
Park and the town. These projects have been approved and reviewed by the Shellfish Commission.

Mr. Goeschel said he provided plans of the projects, which are in the Member Packets (attached.)
The Commission discussed the projects in depth.
Mr. Neilson explained the dock at Cini Park will run parallel to the shore.

Ms. Palazzo asked about the electric utilities and Mr. Nielson said it was put in the initial application to
give the Town the option to add it in the future if they so chose.

Mr. Schuch asked about the handicapped access and asked if the current restroom facilities are suitable.

Mr. Goeschel detailed the STEEP Grant that was awarded and explained the plan is to bring in a portable
bathroom that could be removed in the event of a storm; this is the most viable solution to the flood
elevation issue. The money from the Grant will be utilized for the Water and Sewer infrastructure.

Mr. Schuch said it would be nice to have a master plan for Cini Park and that perhaps Planning could
help devise this.

The Commission discussed handicapped parking at Cini Park.

The Commission asked about the cost and Mr. Nielson replied that $50,000 to $60,000 is a fair estimate.

e **Motion (2)
Ms. Palazzo moved to approve the Referral by the Town of East Lyme for installation of 101 (+/-) FT
wide main float with associated restraint piles, and water/electric utilities accessed from an existing
float and ramp and the installation of a new ADA kayak/canoe launch float, all waterward of the
Mean High Water and CT Coastal Jurisdiction Lines (CJL.)
Mr. Lynch seconded the motion.
Vote: 6-0-0. Motion passed.

Mr. Lynch seconded the motion.

Re: CGS 8-24 Review for the Proposed Docks at Cini Park and Gran
Launch

This office is requesting a CGS 8-24 review by the Planning Commission for municipal
improvement for the projects referenced above. Both projects have been submitted to DEEP
for permits. Shovel ready projects are more likely to receive funding including grants, and
therefore we try to obtain necessary permitting (i.e. DEEP, 8-24) to ensure that the projects
are viable.

Grand Street Dock:

This proposal will serve two purposes. To provide boaters using the boat launch a
loading zone on the north side of the dock. Boaters can load or unload passengers and
supplies after the boat is launched or before the boat is removed from the water. Currently
passengers have to climb a vertical ladder on the bulkhead to enter or exit a boat. This
improvement will make the existing Grand Street Boat launch much more user friendly. The
south side of the dock will provide dingy dockage space for visiting boats that are anchored
or moored, Waterford or East Lyme residents that have a dingy may also visit this dock.
This will allow boaters to walk into town and visit the local businesses. If this project is
constructed, rules and regulation regarding use, time limitations, and oversight will have to
be developed.

Cini Park Dock:

This project will provide dingy dock space and space for a few smaller boats to allow
boaters to go into Town and visit the local businesses. There is also a handicapped kayak
launch attached to the west side of the dock that was spearheaded by a local Girl Scout. She
is currently seeking funding for that portion of the project. If this project is constructed, rules
and regulation regarding use, time limitations, and oversight will have to be developed.
