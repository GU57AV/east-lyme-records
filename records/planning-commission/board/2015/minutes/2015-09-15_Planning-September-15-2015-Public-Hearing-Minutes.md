Mr. Goeschel read Exhibit O into the record.

The Applicant, Wayne Fraser of 22 Gurley Road, came forward to detail the proposal.
The Commission discussed the request for the waiver from the open space requirement.

Mr. Fraser stated that if he gave 10% of his lawn towards open space, someone would have to mow it;
the land in question is a lawn and that will not change with the addition of a house.

The Commission discussed the open space requirement. Mr. Balantic stated that this is not an area
where we have to be concerned with open space; that is more of a cluster development issue which
concerns three or four lots.

Mr. Schuch observed that the waiver for the Archaeological Survey is irreverent since it has been

completed; no objections have been made regarding the waiver for the open space requirement and
Town staff are satisfied with the request for the waiver for storm water management. Mr. Benni’s letter
