Public Delegations is the time when members of the public are invited to speak to the Commission about
certain matters. Issues or concerns related to approved subdivisions under construction (Item Vi} and in-
house proposals or general topics of discussion (Item Vill) are open to comment. Items, referrals, or
applications subject to a decision by the Commission, a public hearing, or in litigation may not be
discussed. The members of the Commission will not directly answer questions or make comment during

delegations.

3. Zoning Representative
Ms. Palazzo attended the March 21, 2013 Meeting. She noted that the Meeting contained twenty
something items on the Zoning Agenda including CAM’s applications to repair the damage done by
Storm Sandy to Old Black Point Road and Grand Street. She also informed the Commission about the
application of Aces High to build a storage pavilion. Ms. Palazzo noted that much of the Meeting
concerned issues regarding outside dining, and that this is the time of year when everyone needs new
permits.

a. Liaison Schedule

The Commission took this time to review the attached Planning Commission Liaison to Zoning
Commission Schedule. Mr. Covino volunteered to fill in for Ms. Ladd’s absence from the August 1, 2013
Meeting while Ms. Palazzo opted to take Ms. Nee’s place at the September 5, 2013 Meeting.

4. Regional Planning Commission Representative- Brian Schuch, Luane Lange

Mr. Schuch stated that they did not meet.

5. Subcommittees

¢ Sustainable Development and Climate Adaptation- (Gary Goeschel, Francine Schwartz)
Ms. Schwartz stated that she had nothing to report at this time.
Mr. Goeschel included the attached Marsh Advancement Parcel Analysis in the Member Packet for the
Commissions perusal. Mr. Goeschel observed that the parcels along the coast owned by the Town may
be set back rather than rebuilt and added that the rise of the sea level will obviously impact this
decision. He also informed the Commission that on August 5, 2013 FEMA will be releasing new maps and
they will have to be reviewed to determine any differences. Mr. Goeschel also noted that a Sound
Summit would be occurring in New York on April 26, 2013.

6. Staff Communications

e Route 11 Greenway Authority Commission
The next Greenway Meeting is scheduled for May 15, 2013.

e State of Connecticut POCD
Mr. Goeschel noted that in addition to the work he has been doing on the Chapters, he just applied for a
$50,000 dollar Grant for Darrow Pond to improve its trails and formalize parking. The intention is to
obtain permission to pass the Yale School property since it connects to the State Forest. The long term
plan exists for a Regional connection of trails all the way to Marlborough, Connecticut. Mr. Goeschel
cited Vermont and New Hampshire as examples of areas which contain lengthy, extensive trails and how
this is a big draw for recreational activity and the avid hiker. He also noted that news regarding the
Grants for Cini Park and the Boardwalk should be known anytime now. Mr. Goeschel added that given
the large amount of Summer Activities planned, repair work on the Boardwalk would begin in Mid-

August or September.
