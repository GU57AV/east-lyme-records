IV Zoning Referrals (CGS 8-3a)

© Application of Theodore A. Harris, Esq., Agent for Benchmark Senior Living, to amend the
East Lyme Zoning Regulations Section 9 and Section 25.5 regarding additions to assisted

living facilities.

Mr. Goeschel explained that the zoning amendment which would allow the applicant to expand would
be pertinent to only existing facilities, in this case Crescent Point. He also noted that the amendment
would add to the town’s tax base. Mr. Goeschel informed the Commission that this item will go to
public hearing before the Zoning Commission on April 4, 2013.

Mr. Schuch expressed interest in learning the reasoning behind changing the zoning regulation back,
when it had previously allowed for such expansion to occur. He asked Mr. Goeschel to research the

history of this regulation.
Mr. Balantic expressed concern over whether such an expansion would be burdensome to the

downtown and other businesses. He also noted that he too would like to review when this regulation
was first changed. Mr. Balantic expressed concern over the parking impact as well.
