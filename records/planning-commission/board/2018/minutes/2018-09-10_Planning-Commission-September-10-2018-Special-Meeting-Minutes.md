Mr. Scott called the Special Planning Commission meeting to order at 7:30 p.m.
Mr. Scott sat Ms. Johnson as a Regular Member for the meeting.

1 Pledge and Roll Call
Mr. Scott led the Pledge. The Commission Members identified themselves for the audience.

ll. Call for Public Delegations
Marie St.Onge of 41 Spring Rock Road came forward to speak. She said the width of the road is
30 feet and recently she had to park in the road since her driveway was being resurfaced. She
also explained her neighbor had a graduation party and several people had to park on the road.

Ms. St.Onge said between the resurfacing of driveways which people do every 2 to 3 years, and
socials gatherings there will frequently be parking on the road. She said 24 feet is not keeping in
with the rest of the neighborhood and noted her mailbox gets plowed every year.

Mr. Scott explained people will have the option of speaking on this matter later in the evening
during the public hearing; this time is reserved for other matters.

lll. New Business
A. East Lyme Coastal Resilience Climate Adaptation and Sustainability Study Kick-
Off Presentation.

Mr. Goeschel reported on a grant the Town has received for a resilience study the Planning
Commission will be overseeing. The study will be conducted by Stantec Consulting Services
out of Boston and will look at the flood plain ordinance, climate change, and drought & flooding;
the intent is to create a new flood plain and give the Building Department greater control and
flexibility. He introduced Larissa Brown and Nels Nelson from Stantec.

Ms. Brown said they've had experience on resilience issues with other New England
communities. She said the risks of the future are not the risks of the past; FEMA has historically
relied on past regulations but weather is different now.
Mr. Nels Nelson introduced himself. He said he toured the Town today and the scope of the
project will be hazard analysis, compatibility analysis, infrastructure and land use strategies to
resolve issues; they will create a toolbox and plan to treat challenges.

Mr. Nelson said he saw some issues along the seacoast but is impressed with how the Town
manages itself; the Town is way ahead of some of the other communities they've dealt with.
He said one area of focus will be FEMA flood elevation and differences in Zoning and Building
in how to treat changes in FEMA regulations, particularly freeboard.

Mr. Nelson said they’re going to examine different options, a substantial damage rule and how
to administer it, design requirements, and structures that no longer meet requirements due to
FEMA changing their regulations. They will be having a Saturday workshop on October 20th,
2018 and are looking for input from community.

Mr. Fitzgerald asked him to elaborate on the living shoreline. Mr. Nelson explained it’s a policy
of the State and when trying to protect the shoreline they don’t want to see new seawalls;
they're looking for a combination of boulders and vegetation. Santec also wants to solicit public
for input regarding hazards at the October 20th workshop.

IV. Reports
A. Communications-

B. Zoning Representative

Vi. Public Hearing

A. Continuation of the Application of Robert Fusari, Real Estate Service of
Connecticut, Inc, Applicant/Owner; Application for a 23-Lot Re-subdivision of
approximately 97.3+ acres, Zoned RU-40 at Spring Rock Road and Green Valley Lakes
Road, East Lyme, Assessor’s Map # 14.0 Lot #45, together with a waiver request from
Section 6-10-11 Of the East Lyme Subdivision Regulations.

Mr. Scott explained this is a continuation of the public hearing. Mr. Goeschel read communication he
received from Attorney Ted Harris (Exhibit XX); their Engineer Joe Wren was unable to attend tonight’s
meeting so they would like to present either at a special meeting or the next regularly scheduled Planning
meeting.

Mr. Goeschel said they can take public comment.

Mr. Scott said the attorneys are not here tonight so they won’t hear what the public has to say, but anyone
who wishes to speak may do so.

Ms. St.Onge returned to the podium to speak and reiterated her previous comments. She added that the
plan is to save some of the trees along the road and asked what will be done about the roots which can
cause damage to the road and side walk. She asked about the noise ordinance and the noise that will no
longer be muffled because of the trees; currently she can already hear the guns at Stone Ranch going off.
Ms. St.Onge provided the Commission with a written copy of her comments (Exhibit YY.)

There was no further Public Comment.
The Commission discussed holding a special meeting as opposed to waiting for the next regular meeting.

The Commission decided to continue the Public Hearing at the regular planning meeting scheduled for
October 2nd, 2018.
