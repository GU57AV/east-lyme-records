A. Application of Theodore A. Harris, Esq., for a text amendment to Section 8.2.2
Mixed use Dwelling Units, of the East Lyme Zoning Regulations, Parking.

The Commission discussed the application.

Mr. Goeschel explained this would allow a developer to provide two calculations and would give
them more flexibility. Mr. Goeschel said in his opinion, this application promotes economic
growth and has an added environmental conservation component since it would reduce
impervious surfaces.
