IX. Old Business

1. POCD Chapter 4 - Economic Development and Chapter 8: Circulation and Transportation

Mr. Goeschel said that they have some comments from the Economic Development Commission noted in
the side margins of Chapter 4. Some are from Mr. Shapiro, some from Mr. Zoller who was part of one of the
very early review subcommittees for the POCD update and some are from a person who is new to the
Economic Development Commission.

Mr. Bowers said that he is glad to see a railroad stop mentioned in this although he has not heard anything

about a potential stop here in East Lyme.
Mr. Goeschel said that the First Selectman had sent correspondence to the former Governor about having a

railroad stop here in East Lyme.

The consensus of the Commissioners was that the newly noted comments were not really relevant to what
they were working with and were more personal in nature.

Mr. Bowers said that he has some comments that he would like Mr. Goeschel to review and that he also has
rearranged some of the areas so that they will flow better as the information seems to be disjointed. He
provided Mr. Goeschel with a copy of his recommendations.

Mr. Goeschel said that he had added Economic Gardening to this. This concept that he has been working on
would provide the Economic Development Commission with staff through the Planning Department to help
grow small businesses. He said that he knows of professionals who can help with this and who hopefully will
be able to be engaged as this moves forward. He suggested that they review this and try to wrap this section
up for the June 7, 2011 Regular Meeting and then begin working on Chapter 8 — Circulation and
Transportation. He asked that they have their comments to him by May 27, 2011.

He asked if they would like to put together a number of sections before going to Public Hearing as that would
help with the advertising costs,
