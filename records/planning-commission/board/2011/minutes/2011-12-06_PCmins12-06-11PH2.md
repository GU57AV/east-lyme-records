Public Hearing II
1. Application of Fred Finn Jr., LS for Patrick Quinn, Owner for a subdivi sion of 2.96 acre s of
   property located at 285 Chesterfield Road, Asse ssor’ s Map 44. 0, Lot 13.

Mr. Bowers noted that the Public Hearing Notice was published in The Day on 11/24/2011 and 12/2/2011.

Mr. Bowers then called for the applicant or his repres entative to present this application.

Fred Finn, Land Surveyor said that he is representing Mr. Quinn. This is for a 2-lot subdivision at 285
Chesterfield Road south of the campground and east of Hidden Acres. There is an existing house and out
structure which will remain. Lot 1 is 1.24 acres and has been tested and approved by Ledge Light. It has
underground utilities and perk tests have been completed. There are no landmark trees or unique or scenic
features to this property and it is in a non-s ensitive area. He said that this application is also gong before the
Inland Wetlands Agency at a Public Hearing scheduled for December 12. 2011. That would mean that this
public hearing would have to be continued to wait to hear from them.

Ms. Bengtson asked if this property was flooded over from Latimer B rook when it flooded some years ago.
Mr. Finn said no – as this is at elevation 90 which is quite high – the other lower areas may have flooded at
that time.

Ms. Bengtson asked about the driveway and where it would come out.
Mr. Finn said that it would be exiting to Chesterfield Road near the area where the gate is in the stone wall. It
would be a driveway for the one house only.

Ms. Bengtson asked what type of driveway it would be.
Mr. Finn said that is would have a paved apron, beyond that he could not say at this time. He noted that they
had also considered passive solar for this project.

Mr. Goeschel noted the Exhibit List and memo from Mr. Scheer, Town Engineer suggesting the granting of
the waiver of the storm water regulations that is being requested. He added that there is a letter from Ledge
Light indicating that bot h lots are suitable for sub-surface sewerage disposal and that water is available.
Mr. Finn not ed the bond for improvements and that Mr. Quinn had asked that any bonding for the rain garden
and storm water be held off pending other approvals and because he is offering the property up for sale and
not building on it himself.

Mr. Bowers said that Mr. Scheer had recommended a $4000 bond.
Mr. Goeschel said that they can work that out before the next meeting pending the Inland Wetlands Agency
result.

Mr. Bowers asked about dry well and storm water mitigation.
Mr. Finn said that the water coming off the roof leaders is not considered dirty wat er.

Ms. DeGrooth asked if the swale would hold up over time and continue to work.
Mr. Finn said that it would and that it would have to be mowed.
Mr. Goeschel asked that he add detail to the plan on the swale construction.
Mr. Finn said that he would do so.

Mr. Bowers called for anyone from the public who wis hed to comment on this application.
Hearing no one –
Mr. Bowers said that they would continue this Public Hearing to the January 3, 2012 meeting of the
Commission.

This Public Hearing was adjourned at 7:44 PM and continued to January 3, 2012.
