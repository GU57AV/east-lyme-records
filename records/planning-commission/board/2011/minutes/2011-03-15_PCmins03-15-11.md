3. Affordable Housing Webinar Report

Ms. Schwartz reported that she had watched this webinar presented by UConn CLEAR. In it they went
through the definition of Affordable Housing and noted that we are #47 in the nation versus where we should
be. Various myths such as higher density and more school children were discussed and dispelled with
information from the Rutgers Study and information on other types of housing such as incentive housing
zones that are now being looked at within Towns. She said that it was a good overview of Affordable
Housing but not necessarily new material that they did not already know or were not already addressing
within our Town.

IX. Old Business
1. Scenic Road Ordinance
Mr. Bowers said that they had copies of this in their packets.

Mr. Goeschel said that he has spoken with Attorney O’Connell on this and was informed that the Board of
Selectmen had wanted the certified return receipt mailing in the language. He said that they could insert ‘or
certificates of mailing’. He added that they could be more restrictive than the Statute with regard to Scenic
Roads however they could not be tess restrictive. He added that in fact the subdivision regulations are more
restrictive than the Statutes.

Jack Hogan, Ex-Officio offered the explanation that the Town Attorney had put the certified return receipt
mailing language in and that it appeared fine to them until now with the issue that has come up where they
could send notices cheaper than the $5.54 per mailing for the certified return receipt.

Mr. Balantic pointed out that this is people’s property that they are talking about and that a lot of the items
mentioned are a burden on the properly owner who may want to do something other than what the ordinance
allows with his property that borders the roadway. As they are tax paying property owners, he said that he
feels that they really have to engage them and let them know about this potential designation change as they
are affected by it. In that sense he feels that the ordinance should be strict and that they should make sure
that they notify all of the people. He said that he was not trying to be difficult but that as this affects the
property owners, they need to know what they would be subject to.

Mr. Goeschel said that if a stone wall is in the Right of Way then they would have to check with the Town on
what they could do with it.
Mr. Balantic said that paragraph 12 in the ordinance does not specifically say ‘Town Right of Way’.

Mr. Bowers suggested that Mr. Goeschel check with the Town Attorney on the new Public Act regarding the
certificates of mailing and discuss with him if they should just add that language or delete the certified return
receipt mailings all together.

Mr. Goeschel said that he would work on it and get back to them.
