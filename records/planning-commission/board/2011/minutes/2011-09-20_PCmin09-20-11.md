lll. POCD Chapter 8 — Circulation and Transportation’ .

Mr. Goeschel noted that they had received a copy of this Chapter with the last meeting packet and had
asked that they review it and submit any suggestions that they might have. He said that the suggestion
of the train stop at Rocky Neck State Park was made as it is an area that affords many parking spots,
has easy access and is close to the highway.

Mr. McPherson suggested that they put it in as a recommendation due to the proximity to I-95 and the
easy access.

Mr. McPherson said that the suggestion encouraging bike paths along the sides of the roads was a
good one although the biggest problem would be with the widening of the roads. He said that they could
recommend Routes 156 and 161 for this type of thing — 161 in particular to encourage such travel
connection between the both parts of Town. He said that it would be difficult near Gorton Pond as there
is virtually no road there and it is a State road.

Mr. Bohmbach asked if the recommendations have been prioritized.
Mr. Goeschel said that it is a guidance document and that they could certainly prioritize the
recommendations. He said that he could incorporate comments from Mr. Minuitti also.
Mr. Bohmbach said that he thought that it would be nice to have sidewalk access from one end of the
Town to the other.

Mr. Balantic noted that they would have to encroach even more to get a center lane plus sidewalks and
a bike lane. Also, regarding the train station at Rocky Neck, he said that while it is a nice area for it that
he is not sure that he sees the big need for a train station. While it would be nice to be able to get to NY
without having to go to Old Saybrook or New Haven to catch a train he is not sure that there is a big
need in this area. He said that this was only his opinion. He also mentioned the parking issue downtown
and said that he thought that the perception of what parking was available was less than what was
really there.

Mr. Goeschel said that there is a difference between the perceived parking and the real parking and
that the perception is basically less than what is actually available there. There are many more spaces
especiaily with the re-striping that will take place once the new parking area is finished.

Mr. Balantic suggested that the ‘dog bone’ design at the crosswalks was probably not practical and said
that it should be removed as the document should reflect feasible items that they would really like to
see happen.

Mr. Goeschel said that he thinks that the crosswalks that they have designed do work and that they
probably could take the ‘dog bone’ design out. He noted that he would suggest that they make sidewalk
connections with the primary arteries and would incorporate some of the ideas into the update.

Mr, Balantic said that overall he thinks that it is a good plan for 10 years although he does question the
suggestion of the bike tunnel underground and the train station and the need for them as he would
rather see items that are truly needed and more practical suggested so that they at least have a chance
of being funded and done.

Mr. Goeschel asked that they submit any other suggestions that they might have.
