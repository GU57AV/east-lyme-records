            A. Subdivision Regulations-Section 4: Application Submission Procedures:
                 G. Goeschel said that when they revised the Subdivision Regulations, they did not
                 address the timeline for when the Commission on Natural Resources had to
                 respond to a referral by the Planning Commission. He suggested they add
                 language for a time limit to respond as it may be interpreted that no response is an
                 approval.
                 G. Goeschel said the subdivision regulations call for an architectural review. He
                 said very few towns in Connecticut require this review. There is a concern that the
                 requirement may be so onerous that the developer would find it more
                 advantageous to submit an application for an affordable development than pay for
                 the architectural review; this would give the Commission less control over the
                 application.
                 G. Goeschel suggested changes to the Subdivision Regulations to reflect these
                 concerns.
            B. ByJaws:
                 B. Bohmbach suggested they review the section of the bylaws that discuss
                 appointments. He said the Commission should be bi-partisan and try to get the
                 best person with the most experience. He also suggested that another option is to
                 replacelappoint according to party affiliation to match the party of the resigning
                 member. The Commission decided to make no changes to the bylaws.
