             Zoning Regulations Section 5.3. 1 .3 for Floodplain Management Regulations.

             G. Goeschel stated the original application was looking for an extensive over hall of
             the regulations. After he met with the ZEO andthe applicant they streamlined the
             text amendments.
             The applicant would like the amendments to allow for hydraulic vents as opposed to
             breakaway walls. The regulations were reviewed by DEEP and they approve of the
             language in the amendment. G. Goeschel stated that the vents are allowed by the
             CT building codes and FEMA Flood Plain Requirements.
             (P. Lynch leaves at7:30)

             G. Goeschel finds the text amendments to the ZoningRegulations consistent with
             the POCD. He stated that the current regulations require that houses in a FEMA
             flood zone would have to be elevated one foot over base flood elevation. Allowing
             flood vents would eliminate the need for elevation.
             MOTION: (Fitzgerald/Phimister) the proposed text amendments to the Zoning
             Regulations are consistent with the town's POCD. Vote: Approved
             Unanimously.
