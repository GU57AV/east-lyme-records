           A. Affordable Housing Plan
               G. Goeschel informed the members that during the last legislative session there were a number of
               changes. Every town has to update their Affordable Housing Plan (AHP) ev€ry five years. The
               next one for East Lyme is due in2022. G. Goeschel said he has an intern that will update the
               housing inventory. He suggested the commission consider a subcommittee to update the plan.
               There are some requirements, such as towns will now have to allow accessory apartments as an
               as of right activity. There can be conditions attached to the accessory apartments. The town may
               opt out of the requirement with a recommendation from the Planning Commission and approved
               by the Board of Selectmen.
               The state is requiring mandatory education for land use commissions at four hours a year for
               each member.

               G. Goeschel informed the members that there are grants for helping towns to develop and
               research an affordable housing plan. The Commission stated they would like the town planner to
               pursue the $15,000 grant for the purpose of developing the Affordable Housing Plan.

               The members would like to have the AHP as a regular agenda item.
