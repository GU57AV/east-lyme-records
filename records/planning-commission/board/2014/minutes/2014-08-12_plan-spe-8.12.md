Proposed Zoning Regulation Text Amendment

Cluster Regulations Setbacks

As the commission may recall in 2010 the board deleted the cluster development zoning regulations
found in Section 23 and replaced them with Conservation Development by Design (CDD) regulations.
One of the unforeseen consequences of that action was the elimination of the setback requirements for
cluster design developments. The community has several existing subdivisions which were approved
over the years under the cluster regulations. The required setbacks for cluster developments were 20°
for front yards, 15’ for side yards, and 30’ for rear yards. The elimination of the clusters setbacks has
resulted in the imposition of the underlying zoning district setback requirements for the construction of
new additions and structures in developments approved under the cluster regulations.

For example, today, an addition proposed in an RU-40 zone would be held to a front yard setback
requirement of 50’ and 30’ for side and rear setbacks. If the subject property was originally approved as
a cluster the setbacks would be 20’ for front yard, 45’ for side yard, and 30’ rear. This discrepancy
creates an unusual hardship for property owners within the cluster subdivisions who wish to improve
their properties with additions or other improvements.

In order to rectify the problem, the following text amendment is proposed:
Add new Section 20.29 to Section 20 Generat Regulations

20.29 Cluster Subdivision Setbacks

Any lot which was set off under a former officially approved cluster subdivision , no building or
structure shall be placed less than 20’ from any street line or less than 15’ from any side yard or
less than 30’ from any rear line. Lot coverage shall be as prescribed in the underlying zone.

This regulation will essentially “grandfather” these setbacks in approved cluster subdivisions.
