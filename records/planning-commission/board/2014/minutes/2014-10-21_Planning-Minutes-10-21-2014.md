IV. ZONING REFERRALS
A. TOWN OF EAST LYME ZONING COMMISSION TEXT AMENDMENT; AMENDMENT TO THE EAST

LYME ZONING REGULATIONS, SECTION 20.20.8 LIQUOR, REGARDING THE PROXIMITY TO
LOCATIONS OF PLACES OF WORSHIP, TO REDUCE THE “200 FEET RADIUS” TO 100 FEET.

**Chairman Schuch recused himself for this item at 7:10 p.m.
Mr. Balantic sat Anne Thurlow as a regular member for this item.

Mr. Goeschel stated the Zoning Commission initiated this Regulation Amendment. The proposed
change would only apply to the CB zone, and would reduce the buffer to 100 feet.

Mr. Covino asked why the 200 foot buffer was implemented.

Mr. Goeschel stated at one time it was 500 feet.

Ms. Bengtson asked if there is a regulation regarding alcohol near schools.

Mr. Goeschel stated there are a number of restaurants that serve alcohol near schools. He believes the
whole section could use a review, but this Commission needs to find out if this amendment is consistent
with the POCD.

Ms. Thurlow asked if they could table this item until Zoning reviews the entire section.

Mr. Goeschel stated if they make no comment it would be construed as an approval. He recommended
they make a decision.

Mr. Balantic suggested they add a comment to the Motion that the Zoning Commission review Section
20.20.3 in its entirety.

Ms. Bengtson stated Crescent Point is a hospital setting, which could be another conflict.

Mr. Goeschel stated the Zoning Commission has a sub-committee that is reviewing the Regulations. It is
not up to this Commission to determine what the whole section means, but we can suggest they have

2
their sub-committee look at the section. This will encourage economic development but the Regulation
as a whole needs to be reworded.

Ms. Bengtson stated she is not comfortable with this. There are too many loopholes with the schools
and hospital and once this is in place we are stuck with it. She can’t give a recommendation on this.

Motion (2) Mr. Covino moved that the Town of East Lyme Zoning Commission Text Amendment
to the East Lyme Zoning Regulations, Section 20.20.8 Liquor, regarding the proximity to location of

places of worship, to reduce the “200 feet radius” to 100 feet is CONSISTENT with the POCD citing
Objective 2.1, Objective 2.2, and Chapter 4 Economic Development Section 4.4.

Seconded by Anne Thurlow.

Mr. Balantic stated we should strongly recommend the Zoning Commission review Section 20.20.3 to
ensure consistency with revisions of the Regulations.

Mr. Covino amended his Motion to read the following:
Mr. Covino moved that the Town of East Lyme Zoning Commission Text Amendment: Amendment to
the East Lyme Zoning Regulations, Section 20.20.8 Liquor, regarding the proximity to location of places
of worship, to reduce the “200 feet radius” to 100 feet is CONSISTENT with the POCD citing Objective
2.1, Objective 2.2, and Chapter 4 Economic Development Section 4.4. The Planning Commission
strongly recommends that the Zoning Commission review Section 20.20.3 to ensure consistency with
revisions of the Regulations specifically regarding distance descriptions.
The Amended Motion was seconded by Anne Thurlow.
Motion Passed 5-1 (Joan Bengtson — Nay)
