  The meeting was called to order by Chairwoman Michelle Williams at 6:30 p.m.
  The subcommittee discussed preparing for multiple workshops to foster a
  dialogue and showcase what each board and commission is doing toward POCD
  implementation. The group would like to use the matrix as an idea for creating
  relationships that haven't been built and identify the individuals doing the actual
  work and moving things forward.
  Workshops should include civic groups as well as boards and commissions. The
  goal of the workshops is to get groups, boards and commissions to discuss the
  recommendations of the POCD, which willtake about three to five meetings. The
  subcommittee would like to see a liaison from each board and commission
  attend the POCD meetings. Mr. Goeschel has drafted a letter and will work with
  staff to disseminate to the various Town boards and commissions.
  Gary Goeschel suggested using ArcGlS for tracking progress, including the use of
  lcons.
