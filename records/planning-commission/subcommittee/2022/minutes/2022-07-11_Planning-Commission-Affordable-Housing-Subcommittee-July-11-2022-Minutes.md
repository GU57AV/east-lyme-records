    A. Kickoff meeting with Donald Poland, Ph.D., AICP of Goma n+York
           Don Poland of Goman+York discussed the previous Affordable Housing Plan. He noted there
           were no significant impediments to Affordable Housing. He then presented a power point
           presentation (copy attached).

           Dr. Poland reviewed how Affordable Housing is defined by the Connectciut General Statutes
           (CGS, 126a Sec. 8-309). He the went on to describe how affordable housing is calculated and
           determining the housing need. He presented the spatial distribution of qualified affordable
           housing across the state and noted as of 2010, there were 8,456 housing units and
           approximately 5.15% of East Lyme's housing stock qualified as affordable housing. He also noted
           2020 estimates indicate East Lyme's total housing units will reach 26,72L bringing the percent
           affordable to 6.04%.

           There was brief discussion around some of the points such as whether the state would reduce
           the percentage of required affordable housing from LO%lo 5% or 6%. Mr. Poland didn't believe
           that would occur, as the fair share position is gaining momentum and could pass.
