TOWN OF EAST LYME
POLICE COMMISSION
REGULAR MEETING OF JANUARY 10, 2019
MINUTES

MEMBERS PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Joseph Perkins, Marc Powers and Lisa Pellegrini.
EXCUSED: Daniel Price, Tony Buglione and Giancarlo D’Angelo

Also in Attendance: Police Chief Mike Finkelstein and Administrative Sgt. Mike Macek
Mr. Nickerson called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

MOTION (1)
Motion by Mr. Perkins to elect a temporary Chairperson for tonight’s meeting, and to nominate Mark Powers.
Seconded by Ms. Pellegrini. Motion passed 4-0.

3. Delegations
There were none.

4. Approval of Minutes

MOTION (2)

Motion by Ms. Pellegrini to approve the Regular Meeting minutes of December 13, 2018, as submitted.
Seconded by Mr. Powers. Motion passed 4-0.

6a. New Business — 2019 O’Niantic 5K & Niantic Bay Triathlon — Legal Traffic Authority Approval
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reported that these events have been long standing, successful events in
our community for many years. There are some details that will need to be discussed with St. Agnes
Church regarding traffic flow and timing, but nothing of concern just convenience. Sgt. Macek reported
there will be more of a police presence in that area to help with traffic flow.

MOTION (3)

Motion by Mr, Perkins to recommend that Chief Finkelstein sign off on all necessary paperwork for the
2019 O’Niantic 5K and Niantic Bay Triathlon.

Seconded by Ms. Pellegrini. Motion passed 4-0.

7a. Chief’s Report

Chief Finkelstein summarized the monthly report for December 2018, which is attached hereto as
Exhibit 1. He reported that the active shooter training held for Town Hall employees was a success, and
many valuable lessons were learned through this exercise. He briefly discussed the DOTs plan to close
down the exit 74 on ramp on 95S around the same time as their planned bridge project which will close a
portion of Lovers Lane at Route 1, and stated that there will be more discussions with the DOT, The
Police Department received $10,000 JAG fund approval to be used in cases involving narcotics and
human trafficking. The Chief reported that we are running out of evidence space in Waterford, and this
could be a problem if additional evidence lockup space isn’t put in place.
