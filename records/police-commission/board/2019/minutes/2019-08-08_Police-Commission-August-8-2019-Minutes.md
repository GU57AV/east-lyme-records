5b. Revenues
     Chief Finkelstein reported that are looking into current revenue as well as future and anticipated revenue.
He estimates about $13,900 in revenues from vendor permits, golf cart registrations and reports/records; and
another approximately $6,000 for pistol permits. We currently do not charge for finger printing services but
this is something that should be looked into. Sgt. Macek reported that the state may make it mandatory to use
a state contractor for finger printing in the future, and will need to be revisited. Anticipated timeline for this
switchover with the State is within the next year, and our police department will be required to have a full
sized finger printing cabinet unit by that time. Mr. Nickerson noted that the IT expenses should fall under the
Town's main IT budget for general IT items such as desktop computers, monitors, etc, They will continue to
sort out revenues and report back at the next meeting.

     It was noted that the agenda stated "Fairview Road"; and the correct road is Fairhaven Road. Chief

landscaping equipment on the side of the road, and that he met with Public Works Director Joe Bragaw on this
parking issue. If the vehicles and equipment are being parked completely off of the road, a street parking
violation cannot be enforced, and It was agreed that this matter will be on next nronth's agenda, and that letters
will be sent out to residents in that immediate area inviting them to attend and speak at the meeting.
