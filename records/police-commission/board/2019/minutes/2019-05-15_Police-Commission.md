5a. Public Safety Building Vision Committee Update
DISCUSSION: Mr. Price stated that the next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, May 16tr'. It was reported that
since the last meeting, the closing has happened and the Town is now officially the owner of 277 West Main
Street. Chief Finkelstein reported that they are working on the scope of work and will discuss this at the next
Vision Committee meeting. The tours they have taken to other police departments have been a benefit to the
process. They have had the opportunity to see how departments do things differently and also have benefited
from the lessons learned communicated from staff, He reported that they have been performing initial inspections
with members of the Public Works department to identifli areas in which there can be potential savings in utilities.

6a. Part-time Animal Control Officer
DISCUSSION: Sgt. Macek stated that they received upwards of fifty applications for the open position, but that
they only ended up with two applicants agreeing to come in for an interview. In accordance with the existing
agreement that we have with Waterford, the part-time ACO works paid shifts on Friday, Saturday and Sunday,
and is also on unpaid call seven days a week. This combination of pay rate and schedule is making it difficult to
find qualified applicants. First step is to sit down with Waterford and see if we can negotiate an updated
agreement to address this issue. Another possible option is to have the full time ACO be on call Monday through
Thursday, and have the part-time person only be on call the days in which they work paid shifts. Sgt. Macek
stated that they would like to meet with Waterford and open this discussion.

MOrroN (2)

Control structure, both fulltime and part timc, as wcll as thc possibility of rcgionalizing, and report back to the

Seconded by Mr. D'Angelo. Motion passed 6-0.

6b. E-Citation Grant
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reported that the State is in the process of switching over to an E-Citation
system, and they are currently offering a grant to municipalities who sign up and start using this system now
before it officially goes into effect. Eventually all municipalities will be required by the State to use this
electronic ticketing system. The cost of the system is approximately $50,000 to install and get up and running,
and will come with ayearly maintenance fee of approximately $4,000. He reported that the electronic ticketing
system is too costly for the town to utilize currently, but that it may be an option in the future.

6c. Truck Traffic on Oswegatchie Hills Road
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reported that there have been problems with large trucks delivering to the
marina. Increasingly there seems to be an issue with the trucks going past the marina and then not being able to
turn around to get out of the neighborhood, The residents have requested that the Town put in signs alerting that
trucks should not be traveling past the entrance to the marina. Although this type of signage needs to be installed
by our Public Works department, the Chief wanted to make the Commission aware of the situation and let them
know that he will be working with Public Works to get the proper signage installed to try to prevent this from
happening in the future. The Commission members were in agreement with the Chief s plan of action.

7a. Chief s Report
     Chief Finkelstein summarized the monthly report for April 2019, which is attached hereto as Exhibit 1. The
Chief reported that the recent audit went very well with no problems found or changes made, and commended
Sgt, Macek on a job well done. He reported that Officer Lindsey Cutillo and Officer Matt Willett were among a
group of officers who were honored at the 33'o annual Law Enforcement Recognition Ceremony of the
Connecticut Chapter of Mothers Against Drunk Driving. In addition, Officer Cutillo received a five year award
for receiving this recognition for the past five years. Congratulations to our officers for working so hard to keep
our community safe. It was reported that they are currently applying for a Port Security grant to make necessary
repairs to our marine patrol boat. This is the smaller town-owned, rapid response vessel and not the regional boat
which is shared with Waterford. The hope is to receive the grant or they will have to ask the Town for funds to
get this boat fixed. Mr. Nickerson added that discussions are still ongoing with Waterford and New London
regarding a possible interlocal agreement.
