3. Acknowledgement of K9 Officers
    Chief Finkelstein did the honor of publicly acknowledging our two K9 Officers, Knox and Jimo, as well
as their Handlers, Officers Lindsay Cutillo and Michael Jezierski. Chief Finkelstein stated that these K9
Officers attended training together in 2012 and have supported the East Lyme Police Department and the
residents of East Lyme since that time. The Chief reported that they are working on creating an area of
acknowledgement in the Police Department to honor these and all future K9 Officers and their Handlers.
Mr. Nickerson thanked the Officers for their hard work, as well as a sincere thank you to their families; and
he communicated that he speaks for the Commission in stating that the K9 program will continue in the
Town of East Lyme. Chairman Price expressed condolences to the Handler and his family for Jimo's recent
passing and acknowledged that these K9 Officers are not only the Handlers partners, but a part of their
families.

6h. Fees for Fingerprinting
DISCUSSION: The Commission members agreed that fingerprinting should remain a free service to East
Lyme residents, but that the hours should be cut back. Currently fingerprinting is done Monday thru Friday
at 8am and 4pm. Chief Finkelstein stated that they will work on updating the hours that fingerprinting will
be available, and that he will update the Commission at a future meeting.

DISCUSSION: The proposed 202012021 budget and list of CIP items is attached hereto as Exhibit 1. Chief
Finkelstein reported that this budget is up only 5Yo from last year, and that includes contractual salary
increases as well as adding two additional officers. He stated that the line item for the part time officer has
increased to comply with the contractual agreement; he was previously out on leave but has since returned
to his contracted schedule of sixteen four-hour shifts per month. Overtime for this year is on target and the
increase for next year is due to contractual salary increases. He explained that the towing line item needs to
be increased due to escalating costs; this year's budgeted amount was expended by the end of September,
He noted that they have begun billing insurance companies for reimbursement of towing for certain
vehicular circumstances. The fees for the digital camera are required and were not budgeted for this current
year. He reported that the Military 1033 program could yield items such as vehicles, weapons, and even
generators, and that the Town should continue to participate in this program. Ms, Pellegrini inquired as to
where the Chief envisions the two new officers to be placed, and the Chief stated that one of the officers
will be assigned traffic duty while the other will be patrol. He noted that regarding the facility fee, the
contract with Waterford is up for renewal so that figure may change depending on the outcome of the
negotiations. Regarding the CIP items, it was explained that all items that are needed for the foreseeable
future are on the list, but that does not prevent the police department and/or the Commission from funding
these items in a different matter, If the item is funded in a different manner, it will simply come off of this
I   ist.
