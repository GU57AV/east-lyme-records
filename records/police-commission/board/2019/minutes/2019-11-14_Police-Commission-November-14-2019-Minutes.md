7a. Fees for Fingerprinting
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reported that a vast majority of police departments in Connecticut do not
charge for fingerprinting. Waterford PD does charge $15.00; and New London is charging $25.00 and they
use a private company to perform the fingerprinting. He reported that most other police depaftments are
limited in when they perform this service, usually only one or two days per week; currently our PD offers the
service every weekday, Monday - Friday, at 8am and 4pm, and requires that an on-duty officer be in the police
department and not out on the road. He reported that our PD has performed fingerprinting services for
residents 272 times so far in 2019. Chief Finkelstein stated that he doesn't feel that we should charge for
fingerprinting as residents come in either for pistol permits or for a job at one of the schools or at a daycare.
People applying for pistol permits already pay large fees to the State, and for teachers they are required as part
of the application process. It was agreed to postpone this matter to a future meeting.
