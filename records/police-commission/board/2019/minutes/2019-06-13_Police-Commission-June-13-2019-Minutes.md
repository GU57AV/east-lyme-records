6a. Walnut Hill Road
     Deputy Director of Public Works, Bill Scheer, spoke on this matter. Mr. Scheer explained that this
area of the retaining wall began to collapse about four years ago, and that this was State controlled land.
The Town has worked with the State to have a portion of that land deeded to the Town to enable us to go
ahead and repair this area. This has been done, and the Town is working closely with the State DOT for
their guidance in creating the temporary traffic pattern to be used, as well as signage. Mr. Scheer reported
that there will be a flagman during the day as the road will go down to one lane for the duration of the
project; at night there will be adequate signage instructing drivers on how to navigate the one lane
roadway. There will be notification to property owners in the vicinity, and the use of an electronic sign
will be considered.
MOTTON (2)
Motion by Mr. Perkins to approve the latest plans, entitled "Retaining Wall Repair Walnut Hill Road",
attached hereto as Exhibit 1.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 4-0.

6b. Full Time Dispatcher
     Chief Finkelstein reported that the interview process is complete and they have chosen a candidate to
extend an offer to. He stated that they have chosen Jeffrey Smith, who is curently a part time dispatcher
here in East Lyme. Mr, Smith holds a full time position with another municipality, and will be putting in
his two weeks' notice.
MOTTON (3)
Motion by Mr. Powers to approve the Chief s recommendation that we hire Jeffrey Smith as a full time
dispatcher for the Town of East Lyme.
Seconded by Mr. Perkins. Motion passed 4-0.

5a. Discussion - Public Safety Building Vision Committee
DISCUSSION: Mr. Price and Chief Finkelstein provided a brief update on the status of this project. It
was repofted that a bidder's conference will be held on June 20'l' for the final three firms chosen to submit
an RFQ. This will give them an opportunity to thoroughly inspect the building and ask questions before
submitting their RFQ. The Work Scope committee has been working diligently to put together a plan that
maximizes the space and resources that we currently have.

5b. Part Time ACO
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reported that they continue to work on trying to fill this position. They
are working closely with Human Resources and the Finance Director to try and work out a more
reasonable work and on-call schedule for the part time ACO, as well as increasing the pay rate. This
matter will be discussed at the next meeting.

7a, Chiels Report
    Chief Finkelstein summarized the monthly report for April2019, which is attached hereto as Exhibit
2. The Memorial Day parade and the Touch A Truck event both went well with a large turnout at each
event. He reported that the Police, Fire Fighters, Ambulance and Dispatch have been meeting to evaluate
Dispatch needs across all emergency management and how to better facilities those services. He shared
three thank you letters that came in to our officers and the Department; and he noted that he has received
very good feedback from the schools and the public regarding Officer Hull's involvement with the
schools and the students. The Chief reported that they have bumped up traffic enhancement patrols, and
also have been able to provide one hundred percent traffic complaint follow up, which has been very
appreciated by the residents. He reported that the vehicle replacement plan is on track and moving
forward. As we approach the end of the fiscal year, all three EOC budgets are tracking to come in within
budget. He reported that they are currently in the process of applying for a couple of different grants
which will assist in the cost of replacing the motors on the 20' regional boat that we share with Waterford.
Lastly he reported that a couple people participated in this year's Torch Run for the Special Olympics,
and that next year they plan to be better represented from our department. Mr. Powers made mention of a
recent article in The Day about false alarms, and the Chief agreed that this is something that we need to
address, and will include allEOC. Chief Finkelstein stated that we should be receiving our new tickets
soon, and that the officers will be equipped to write a ticket if the situation permits.

8. Chairman's Repoft

change for the part-time ACO, as well as a review of revenues in the department.
