                                     TOWN OF EAST LYME
                                     POLICE COMMISSION
                               REGULAR MEETING OF APRIL 11,2019
                                          MINUTES

MEMBERS PRESENT: Daniel Price, Mark Nickerson, Marc Powers, Lisa Pellegrini and Tony Buglione
EXCUSED: Joseph Perkins and Giancarlo D'Angelo
Also in Attendance: Police Chief Mike Finkelstein and Administrative Sgt. Mike Macek

Mr. Price called the meeting to order at 6:04 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

3, Delegations
There were none

4. Approval of Minutes
MOrroN (1)
Motion by Ms. Pellegrinito approve the Regular Meeting minutes of March 14,2019, as submitted.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 5-0.

5a. Discussion - Public Safety Building Vision Committee
DISCUSSION: Mr. Price reported that the Committee created two sub-committees; a Work Scope sub-
committee and a RFQ Review subcommittee. Chief Finkelstein reported that the Work Scope sub-
committee has met once and also toured the Stonington Police Department, with another tour scheduled
for Ledyard Police Department in the near future. Stonington Police l)epartment is -20 years old and the
Ledyard Police Department is fairly new, so both tours will provide valuable information for our Vision
Committee to work from, Mr. Price reported that the RFQ Review sub-committee is meeting tonight also
and is working to narrow the applicants down to three or four and send a finalrecommendation to the
Vision Committee by their next meeting on April 25tr'.

6a. Lyme Shore Tennis & Fitness Parking Modification Request
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reported that they have requested a modification in on-street parking
for October 5'l' and 6tl', Currently Colton Road is no parking on both sides, and they are requesting that
they be allowed to park on one side of the street for their event in October. Chief stated that he sees no
problem with suspending the no parking rule for this event only, and recommended that the Commission
approve the request. The logistics of how to set up temporary signage will be worked on after approval,
and all of our officers will be made aware of the temporary suspension of parking regulations in advance.

MOTTON (2)
Mr. Powers MOVED that the request for temporary modification of parking regulations on Colton Road
for an event held by Lyme Shore Tennis & Fitness on October 5tl'and 6tl'be approved.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 5-0,

7a. Chief s Report
     Chief Finkelstein summarized the monthly repoft for March 2019, which is attached hereto as Exhibit
 1. The Chief reported that the O'Niantic 5K went well and was well attended, despite the cold weather.
He reported that they have received a lot of applications for the part-time Animal Control Officer
position, but that none of the applicants hold the required certification. The next certification course is
scheduled in August, so for the time being the full-time ACO has agreed to cover the part-time shifts as
well as his own shifts. Sgt. Macek reported on the budget for payroll, and stated that because of a glitch
in the accounting software after a recent upgrade, some officer payroll was coming out of the foot and
patrol account instead of the salary account, so these numbers were wrong but have now been corrected.
Chief Finkelstein reported that the Niantic Motel has recently had a change in ownership, and the hope is
that a big hotel chain willtake over and renovate the property. Regarding false alarms, they are working

8. Chairman's Report
Mr. Price stated that they had covered everything that he wanted to cover, so no additional report was
necessary.

9a. Subcommittee Reports
There were none.

At this time, it was agreed that the Commission would hear public comment before they go into
Executive Session,

1 1.   Public Comment
Mr. Stephen Rebelowski,24 MacKimron Place, addressed the Commission and stated that he is the
President of the East Lyme Police Benevolent Association. He noted that the Town has not properly
represented at the wakes and funerals of retired East Lyme police officers that have passed. He asked that
this Commission work with the Chief of Police to make sure that going forward these fallen officers are
properly respected. Chief Finkelstein stated that he will make sure that going forward the Police
Deparlment properly represents at all future fall officers wakes and funerals,
