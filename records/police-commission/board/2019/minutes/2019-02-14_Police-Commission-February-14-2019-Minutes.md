5. Old Business - Budget
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reviewed the budget for the Commission, taking note that they had
originally requested that the budget include two new police officers, but that the number was cut that back
to one new hire. He stated that he will be presenting the budget to the Board of Selectmen on February
 l9tL, and he will emphasize that the average ratio oipolice tolesident is 2.1 police officers per 1,000
residents; we are current at 1.2 officers per 1,000 residents, The Police Commission members agreed that
they support the hiring of two new officers in the 201912020 fiscal year. Regarding adding the position of
Lieutenant, it was discussed that although there is an assignment given to the Administrative Sergeant,
there should be an actual supervisory position, such as Lieutenant, to oversee the other Sergeants. It was
decided that a subcommittee would be formed to explore this option further. It was noted that although
the description of the position is not outlined in the contract, there is reference to a Lieutenant position
and should be researched further.

MOrroN (2)
Motion by Mr. D'Angelo to approve the Chief s original 201912020 budget to include the addition of two
full time police officers, and amend line item 5l I (Police Officers) to include an additional $10,000 to

Seconded by Mr. Powers, Motion passed 5-0.
