5a. Part-Time Animal Control Officer
Chief Finkelstein stated that the part time position remains unfilled, and he believes that the pay rate
combined with the hours is deterring qualified candidates from applying. Currently the position offers
Friday - Sunday on duty hours at $14.35 per hour, and that person is also on call seven days a week.
Currently the fulltime ACO averages between l2-14 hours of overtime each week. A Motion was made
by Mr, Nickerson, seconded by Mr. D'Angelo, to raise the pay rate to $17.00 per hour and to direct the
Chief and Administrative Sergeant to work with Finance and Human Resources on the work schedule.
This motion was rescinded for further discussion of the matter. It was discussed that the set hours for the
part time ACO will be Friday, Saturday and Sunday, and that position will require them to be on call
Friday night, Saturday night and Sunday night. Sgt. Macek stated that hiring someone who is already
certified would be an ideal situation, and should be paid a higher hourly rate.

MOTTON (2)
Motion by Mr, Nickerson to set the hours of the part time Animal Control Officer to work day shifts and
be on call in the evenings on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays; and to authorize the Chiefand
Administrative Sergeant to hire a part time Animal Control Officer at the rate of pay of $ 1 7.00 up to
$18.50, depending on if the applicant holds the proper certification.
Seconded by Mr, Price. Motion passed 6-0,
