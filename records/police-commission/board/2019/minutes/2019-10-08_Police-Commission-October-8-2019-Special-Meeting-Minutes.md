3. Introduction of and Approval of Part-time ACO Candidate
Chief Finkelstein introduced Allison Ryan, and stated that through their interview process, Allison is the candidate that
has been selected for the position of Part-time ACO. Both Chief Finkelstein and Sgt. Macek stated that she is an
excellent candidate and they recommend that the Police Commission vote to approve hiring her. Current East Lyme
ACO, Robert Yuchniuk, addressed the Commission and stated that he supports the decision to hire Allison and is
looking forward to having her on board and to having some help. Ms. Allison Ryan addressed the Commission and
briefly reviewed her background and stated that she has a passion and love for helping animals and humans alike. Chief
Finkelstein commended ACO Yuchniuk on doing an outstanding job of supporting the community and working hard
and being on call 2417 while the process to find a part-time ACO played out.
MOTTON (l)
Mr. Nickerson MOVED to hire Allison Ryan as the East Lyme part-time ACO.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione, Motion passed 5-0.
