Report to the East Lyme Police Commission

Subiect; East Lyme Police Cadets Meeting - Wednesday, january 8

Commissioner's Report:

On Wednesday, fanuary B, I had the pleasure of attending a meeting of the East
Lyme Police Cadets, a program that has been an integral part of this department
since its inception in 2001. Over the years, the cadet program has cultivated many
future police officers, including notable graduates like Lindsey Cutillo, and has
prepared young individuals to serve in law enforcement agencies across the region"

During my visit, I saw Detective Turcotte and Sergeant Hallbauer as they led the
cadets through an exceptionally well-organized and meaningful training session.
The focus of this session was on handling an interpersonal conflict, a scenario
designed to immerse the cadets in realistic law enforcement challenges,

Detective Turcotte and Sergeant Hallbauer guided the cadets step-by-step through
the scenario, emphasizing policy and procedural adherence throughout the exercise.
The training also incorporated essential components such as:
  a   Proper completion of documentation.
  a   Identification of resources available to victims.
  a   Strategies to keep officer and civilian safety at every stage

The attention to detail and the methodical approach shown by Detective Turcotte
and Sergeant Hallbauer gave a powerful example to the cadets. Their ability to
model professionalism, leadership, and teamwork in a high-pressure situation was
inspiring, and it was clear that the cadets were engaged, learning, and growing as
individuals.

It was particularly impressive to see how this program develops not only technical
skills but also critical decision-making abilities, responsibility, and a sense of
teamwork, Furthermore, I want to recognize the East Lyme Police Cadets for their
dedication to the community. These young individuals consistently contribute their
skills and time, taking part in community service activities and events that make
East Lyme a better place to live. Their commitment reflects the values of this
program and proves the positive impact of investing in our youth.

As a commissioner, I was proud to witness the dedication and volunteerism of our
department members in mentoring the next generation. Programs like the East
Lyme Police Cadets are a testament to the department's commitment to community
engagement and leadership development.l commend Detective Turcotte, Sergeant
Hallbauer, the cadets, and all those involved in ensuring the success of this program.

I encourage all of us to seek out ways to better support this program, attend their
fundraisers and bolster their initiatives. This program is a worthy cause that reflects
positively on the department and reinforces our mission to foster trust,
collaboration, and excellence within our community.

Respectfully submitted,
Paul Vincenti
East Lyme Police Commission
