RE: Town of East Lyme request to relocate Memorial Park Drive where it
intersects Oswegatchie Hill Road

Dear Police Commission,

       The East Lyme Engineering Department is proposing to relocate the entrance to
Memorial Park Drive from its current location to a point on Oswegatchie Hills Road that will
provide a more appropriately designed intersection. The submitted plan includes many items
under the jurisdiction of the legal traffic Authority. The following list of the items shown on the
plan under the direct jurisdiction of the Police Commission.

   •   Relocation of stop sign on Memorial park Dr,
   •   Relocation of stop bar on memorial Park Dr.
   •   Revise centerline on Oswegatchie Hills Rd. (create a break at Memorial Park Dr.)
   •   Install “Do not Block Intersection” sign on Oswegatchie Hills Road.

The Department of Transportation has reviewed and approved the submitted plan. They have
jurisdiction over the intersection with Route 161.



    William A. Scheer Jr.
