6a. Crescent Beach - Traffic Authority
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein briefly reviewed the Guidance for Local Traffic Authorities posted by the
Office of the State Traffic Administration and noted that stop signs cannot be installed as a speed deterrent.
That being said, he stated that this area has had traffic issues and that these issues should be addressed. He
requested that the Commission, acting as the Local Traffic Authority, direct him to work with Public Works
to evaluate the neighborhood as a whole and determine some long-term solutions to the traffic problems
there. Mr, Perkins noted that there are many signs in that areathat have been posted by private citizens, and
he agrees that the area needs to be looked at.
MOrroN (s)

action.
Seconded by Mr. Perkins. Motion passed 7-0.

6b. Quarry Dock Road - Parking Issues
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reported that the parking lot at the end of the road which was put in to
provide parking for tlie nature trails has recently been closed. He stated that there has been an increase in
the public parking there and on the road in order to walk down to the swimming area. Mr. Nickerson stated
that this activity has increased substantially this year, maybe due to the pandemic and the state and local
beaches filling to capacity quickly. Mr. Nickerson also pointed out that the property where the rock and
swimming hole is located is on private property and the owner does not want people trespassing. He also
noted that when approached and told that they are trespassing on private property, the offenders have been
confrontational with the property owners. Chief Finkelstein stated that he would like the Commission,
acting as tl-re Local Traffic Authority, to designate that area as no parking. He noted that once they decided
to close the parking area they did put in boulders, which were subsequently moved by trespassers. The
parking area is secure now, but people continue to park on the street, creating ahazard. Mr. Perkins
inquired if the Public Works Director is aware of this issue, and the Chief stated that he is aware and they
are working together on this.

7. Chief s Report
Chief Finkelstein gave his report, which is attached hereto as Exhibit l. He reported that the State is

mandatory, so they have begun by reviewing the quotes that were received back in 2017 and begin
researching from there. The proposal will require body cams for all officers, as well as in-car cameras not
only for the police vehicles, but also for the ATVs, gator and bicycles. Chief Finkelstein reported that they
have starting fingerprinting again, by appointment only; and fingerprinting is being done for the schools.
He reported that they have received a number of tharrk you letters from the community, as well as a thank
you letter from Parks & Recreation for our support to their department. They also received a thank you
letter from the victirn and family of a recent accident. Hc notcd that wc do not just invcstigatc the accidents
and identify the offender; our officers support the victirn and their families and stay involved well after the
accident occurs. He stated that Mr. Nickerson has met with the DEEP regarding the walk-in issues that our
beacli neighborhoods have been experiencing due to overflow from Rocky Neck reaching capacity; things
seern to be going better since that meeting.
