4a. Memorial Park Drive Relocation
MOrroN (2)
DISCUSSION: Mr. Scheer addressed the Board and explained that the current intersection has been a
problem for a while and that they have been working on a solution to make it safer. The proposed plan will
allow for a clear sightline in both directions when pulling out of Oswegatchie Hill Road, as well as increase
the radius for vehicles turning onto Oswegatchie Hill Road. A map showing the proposed re-alignment is
attached hereto as Exhibit l Mr. Perkins inquired if there was an incident that brought this change about,
and Mr. Scheer stated that the intersection was designed this way and it has always been a problem and a
proactive move such as this re-alignment will fix the original design flaw. Mr. Powers inquired if the
telephone poles that currently block the sightline will be rnoved, and Mr. Scheer responded that they will
not need to be moved because re-aligning the intersection and adding the median forces drivers into a
position that will now have a clear sightline in both directions. This project will have minimal impact on
the overflow parking area for the park and will have minimal impact to the driveway of one property owner.

Motion by Mr. Powers to approve the recommendation of the Deputy Director of Public Works to relocate
MemorialPark Drive where it intersects with Oswegatchie Hill Road.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 6-0.

4c. General Discussion / COVID Update
Mr. Nickerson repofted that we all continue to hold business meetings viaZoom and other online seruices.
He stated that most of the Town's employees are back to work, but that all Town buildings remain closed to
the public, and no recreational activities have resumed yet. He reported that as of Saturday morning a
beach pass will be required to park and visit the beach, and that passes are currently being sold only to East
Lyme residents and that the beaches will operate with reduced capacity. Many local restaurants will be
offering outside dining, so please everyone do their best to suppoft our local businesses, He reported that
Niantic Main Street is holding a virlual ribbon cutting to commemorate businesses starting to upon up
again. Mr. Nickerson stated that our Police Department has done an outstanding job of maintaining peace
and safety, and he thanked each one ofthem for ajob well done.
      Chief Finkelstein reported that they have continued to operate on a fairly normal basis, with some
special lneasure being taken in their response due to the pandemic. He reports that no officers have fallen
ill, but that they are prepared and have coordinated with our neighboring communities should the need for
mtltualaid arise. He stated that they are receiving a lot of donations of masks and meals from the
community, and he is very grateful for the community's support. We continue to receive weekly PPE
supplies from the State, but it is unclear how long that will continue. As far as enforcement of social
distancing non-compliance in the community he stated that so far, all incidents have been resolved by
voluntary compliar-rce. He stated that as the beaches open up, they will have one officer at each park to
assist the Parks and Recreation staff, as well as an added patrol on Main Street. Ms. Pellegrini inquired as
to anticipated traffic issues at Rocky Neck, and Chief Finkelstein responded that they have communicated
with local areas where people historically park to walk onto the beach to communicate to them that there is
no parking in these areas and no walk-ins allowed onto the beach. The Chief stated that he has been in
communication with the State to make sure that they will be sending additional resources to police the area,
as well. He reporled that they have a plan in place where the DEEP will contact dispatch once the state
park is full, and dispatch will then be able to communicate that to Parks and Recreation so that they are
prepared.
