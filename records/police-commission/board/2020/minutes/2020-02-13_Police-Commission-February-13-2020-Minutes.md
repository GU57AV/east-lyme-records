Mr. Price called the meeting to order at 6:20 p.m, and led the pledge of Allegiance.

3. Delegations
Mr, Ken McCollum, 287 Chesterfield Road, East Lyme, addressed the Commission and spokc on agcnda
item # 5b. Mr. McCollum explained that he would like make a report to the Department of Transportation
regarding the stretch of route 161 by his house, and that he needs the Board of Police Commissioner's
backing, as the local traffic authority, in order for our Public Works Director to bring this matter to the
DOT. The main issue that he would like the DOT to consider is removing the passing zone in the
northbound lane of Route 161 south of Aces High RV park.

5a, CT Cycling Advancement Program
DISCUSSION: Mr. Brian Wolfe, Events Director for the Connecticut Cycling Advancement Program was
in attendance to speak on this matter. He explained that this event is not a race, so they are not asking for
police officers to monitor and direct traffic as this is not needed. There will be approximately 100
participates ranging in age from 9 years old to over l8 years old, who willbe broken up into groups of 2-3
people with a parent and/or a coach in each group. Depending on the age and skill level of each group,
there will be three different routes that riders will take, all of which start and finish in the same place and go
along with the flow of traffic.
MOTTON (3)
Motion by Mr. Powers to approve the request for Use of Local Roads for the CT Cycling Advancement
Program to be held on Sunday, May 3,2020.
Seconded by Mr. Perkins, Motion passed 6-0.
