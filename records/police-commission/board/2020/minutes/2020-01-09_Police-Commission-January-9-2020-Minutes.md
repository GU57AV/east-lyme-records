6. Chief s Report
     Chief Finkelstein briefly reviewed his report for December, which is attached hereto as Exhibit 1. He
reported that the annual light parade was a success and went very well. Even though the event was
postponed to the rain date, the Department received all of the mutual aid from other towns; thank you to
those towns for being flexible and being able to send their officers in spite of the date change. The Chief
reported that the police academy called and stated that the town became eligible for a seat in the class
starting on January 1Otr'. He announced that Philip Carrier was sworn in this week and will be attending the
academy on the 10tr' and that between the academy and the required 400 hours of field training is
anticipated to be on shift on his own in the fall or winter timeframe. He stated that the Town's other new
officer, which is a lateral move, will be starting on February 17tr'; and he reported that she will be replacing
an existing officer who is retiring. He reported that the part time ACO is doing a great job and is finished
with training and working her own hours, and that the full time ACO is back to working a normal work
schedule. The Chief reported that the Town did not receive the annual DUI grant this year, stating that the
state changed their parameters to quali$' for the grant and that our numbers did not meet those parameters.
He stated that they are asking for an additional officer in this year's budget, and that officer will be solely

the Town will be in better shape for the increase in incidents over the summer months. He noted that the
community was very positive and supportive last year with the increase in police presence, which was due
to the DUI grant monies. Chief reported that the Board of Selectmen approved the funding for a K-9, and
that it willgo before the Board of Finance next week for final approval. If approved they willbegin the
process to appoint a handler, then begin the search for a K-9. He stated that the Public Safety Building
Vision Committee is very close to finalizing the drawings for the new police station, and that it will not be
long before they hand the project over to the Town Building Committee. He stated that the Town recently
received a waiver from the State Building Official on reinforcing the building, and also that they are
working on a $75,000 grant from FEMA, These funds together could add approximately $300,000 to the
funds available for this project.
