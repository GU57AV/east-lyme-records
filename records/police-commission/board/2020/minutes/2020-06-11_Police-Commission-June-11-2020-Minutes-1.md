Chief Finkelstein gave his report, which is attached hereto as Exhibit I . He reported that the purchase of a
new CAD records management system was approved at last night's Board of Finance meeting, so they will
begin working with NEXGEN to get the system implemented. The Police Department continues to receive
many thank you notes, including a letter of appreciation from Broadway Kids. Chief Finkelstein reported
that Officer Matthew Roland has retired frorn the Departrnent as of May 31 ,2020, and that Sergeant Terry
Saffiotihas announced that he will retire as of July 1,2020. They have opened lateral positions on Police
App to fill these vacancies; there are now three positions total to be filled this year.
     Chief Finkelstein stated that they have begun reviewing our Police Departments policies, and he has
been working with State Representative Holly Cheeseman to discuss statewide use of force policies. We
have good Policemen and Women in our Police Department, but there is always room for training and
improvement. It was noted that use of force policies, as well as de-escalation methods, are part of almost
every other training that they are required to take on a yearly basis. Mr. Rebelowski mirrored that
sentiment by stating that over the last fbw years, training in de-escalation techniques has increased
significantly. Ms. Cheeseman stated that this is and will continue to be a collaborative process between
state and local agencies, and she looks forward to continuing the meetings and collaboration with the Chief.
Chief Finkelstein noted that Connecticut is already far above other states in training and policies. It was
discussed that the cost of body cams and the needed storage is very l-righ, and that remains something that
our policc dcpartmcnt will continue to look into. Chairman Price stated that the Polices and Procedures
Subcommittee will meet soolr for a review of our current policies.
