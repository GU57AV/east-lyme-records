6. New Business

a. Discuss proposed Fees Schedule
DISCUSSION: A brief discussion was held with some brainstorming on the types of things that we
might be able to charge fees for, such as fingerprinting. It was decided that a sub-committee would be
formed and that this subcommittee would be charged with doing the research and establishing a fee
schedule. This subcommittee will be formed and will report back to the Commission at a future
meeting.
MOTION (3)
Motion by Mr. Buglione to create a Fees sub-committee that would be responsible for researching and
devising a proposed fee schedule
Seconded by Mr. D’Angelo. Motion passed 5-0.

MOTION (4)
Motion by Mr. Price to appoint Tony Buglione and Giancarlo D’ Angelo to the fees sub-committee.
Seconded by Mr. Perkins. Motion passed 5-0.
