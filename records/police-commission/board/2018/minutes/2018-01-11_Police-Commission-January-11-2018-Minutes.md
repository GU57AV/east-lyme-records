a. Receive information and take possible action regarding a crosswalk request for Joanne
Street and Pennsylvania Avenue.
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein stated that he understands completely the need for a crosswalk at
this location, but explained that lack of money in the budget for this project is going to be the biggest
hurdle. This was discussed and it is the understanding of the Chief that the State may approve the
crosswalk but that they will not fund the project; the Town would need to come up with the money.
Mr. Nickerson stated that it is not the Town’s jurisdiction to make a decision on putting the actual
cross walk in, but simply to endorse the possibility and direct the Public Works Director to send a
letter to the State DOT. Since everyone is in agreement that it would be a good idea to put a crosswalk
at that intersection, it was decided to move forward and see what the options are.
MOTION (2)
Motion by Mr. Buglione to support the possible installation of a crosswalk at the intersection of
Joanne Street and Pennsylvania Avenue, and to direct the Public Works Director to send a letter of
request to the State of Connecticut DOT.
Seconded by Mr. Gardner. Motion passed 6-0.

6. New Business
b. Receive information and take possible action regarding a request for no parking signs
on Fairhaven Road.
DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson reported that Fairhaven Road is the road that connects and is used as a
cut through from West Main Street to the Crescent Beach area. This situation has arisen because a
homeowner on that road has been using the Town’s right-of-way to park their large landscaping
equipment, and it is causing dangerous sightline issues. Mr. Nickerson stated that this
homeowner/business owner has been the subject of blight issues in the past. During this past storm,
the plows had a difficult time maneuvering around the equipment. It is requested that no parking signs
be put in on the Town piece of property to prevent non-Town equipment and vehicles from being
parked there.
MOTION (3)
Motion by Mr. Gardner to approve the installation of parking signs on a piece of Town property on
Fairhaven Road. The property will be surveyed by the Chief of Police and the Public Works Director,
and the number of signs necessary and the locations of such signs will be determined and installed per
their recommendation.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 6-0.

c. Update on upcoming Police Union negotiation process by First Selectman.
DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson gave a brief summary of what the Commission members should expect
from the contract negotiation process. He stated that the Commission should bring any
questions/issues they might have to him, and he will in turn take it to negotiations if appropriate.

d. Discuss staff needs.
DISCUSSION: Mr. Gardner stated that he has done a risk threat assessment and is asking that the
Commission approve two additional dispatchers plus an increase in police officers based on a staffing
analysis based on population versus number of officers. Mr. Price noted that the upcoming budget
proposal does include two additional dispatchers, and that we should hold off on adding officers until
next budget cycle. Everyone agreed that adding officers would be good, and it was decided that doing
a.thorough analysis taking all factors into account should be done; Mr. Gardner agreed to put together

that report for a future meeting.

East Lyme Police Department Monthly Report

Section 1: Noteworthy Incidents/Information

The East Lyme Police Department App & Tips411 are live and fully operational. This was covered
by a pre rollout spot on NBC30, New London Day article on release and social media
announcement. Will be working with Superintendent on increasing school awareness.

The Chief continues to meet regularly with the Superintendent of Schools to review and improve
upon the relationship between our two organizations. This has included recent steps to
streamline Officers access into schools in both emergent and non-emergent situations.

One of the two new East Lyme Police Cruisers is nearing completion at the PWD facility and
should be on the road within a few weeks. The second will be undergoing equipment install
immediately following the completion of the first. In December, one vehicle was involved in a
minor car vs deer accident and is currently out of service while undergoing repair. Another
unmarked SUV has also been removed from service. The PWD continues to work with a vehicle
wholesaler to liquidate several of the Asset forfeiture vehicles.

The East Lyme Police light parade was held with great success. East Lyme Officers, along with
Officers from 8 Southeastern Connecticut Police Departments, provided event security and
traffic control.

Notes of thanks

The East Lyme Police Department received a tremendous outpouring of notes of thanks from
the community during the holiday season. We are extremely grateful for our partnership with
our residents and look forward to improving upon that in 2018.
