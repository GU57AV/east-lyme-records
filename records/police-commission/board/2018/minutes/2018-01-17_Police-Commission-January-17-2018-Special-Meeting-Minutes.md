TOWN OF EAST LYME
POLICE COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING OF JANUARY 17, 2018

MEMBERS PRESENT: Daniel Price, Thomas Gardner, Joseph Perkins and Giancarlo D'Angelo.
Also in attendance: E.L. Police Sergeant Mike Macek
Mr. Price called the meeting to order at 5:59 PM and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

During the meeting, Mr. Price, Mr. Gardner, Mr. Perkins and Mr. D’Angelo requested Sgt. Macek
to provide justification for many items contained in the ELPD proposed FY2018-2019 budget
as prepared by Chief Finkelstein.

Specifically, Sgt. Macek was asked questions pertaining to:

Account 513-foot patrol/parade duty- Mr. Price questioned whether any of these costs could
be offset by charging a fee to vendors of each event, such as EL Day, the Light Parade, the
Holiday Stroll and the Memorial Day Parade. In regards to beach patrols, should any of the cost
be offset by a fee to the Beach Associations. Sgt. Macek was requested to determine the EL
Parks and Recreation costs and reimbursements schedule.

Account 514- Police overtime: Sgt. Macek explained that a majority of this cost is contractural
and that the other non-contractural cost is projected based on historical data from-year-over-
year actual expenses. Mr. Price snd Mr. Perkins both suggested that the Board of Education
should be incurring the overtime expense for the officers assigned to home football games.

Account 515- Overtime- Boat Duty: Set. Macek explained that 95% of this line item is for the
regional boat, shared with the Town of Waterford, and the obligation is based on the 2012
Homeland Security Grant that purchased the boat requiring both towns man and operate the
boat.

Account 517- Training: Sgt. Macek explained that all officers are required to attend training in
order to maintain their State certification as police officers. Some of the increases relate to not
having a local range to qualify as well as new costs imposed by POST. Due to staffing
shortage, all training is on overtime.

Account 330- Server replacement: The current server is over is over 6 years old, has exceed its
useful life and needs to be replaced immediately to prevent catastrophic failure. Mr. Gardner
requested that the Town IT Director be informed and request if she could assist in developing a
long term strategy to protect the ELPD IT environment.

Mr. Price asked about the status of the AFIS (Automated Fingerprint Identification System).
Sgt. Macek indicated we received a grant from Dominion Energy for $6000 towards the
purchase cost of approximately $10,000. If we do not purchase this equipment rather quickly,
we stand the chance of losing the grant money. This equipment is critical to the successful
fingerprinting of residents requesting pistol permits as well as all persons seeking employment
to work with children whether it be in the public or private sector. The current “inked” print
process is archaic and is extremely unreliable.
