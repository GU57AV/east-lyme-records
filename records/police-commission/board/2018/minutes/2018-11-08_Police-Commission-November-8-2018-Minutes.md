TOWN OF EAST LYME
POLICE COMMISSION
REGULAR MEETING OF NOVEMBER 8, 2018
MINUTES

MEMBERS PRESENT: Daniel Price, Mark Nickerson, Joseph Perkins, Tony Buglione, Marc Powers

and Lisa Pellegrini. Giancarlo D’Angelo arrived at 6:08 p.m.

Also in Attendance: Police Chief Mike Finkelstein, Sgt. Mike Macek and ACO Robert Yuchniuk.

Mr. Price called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance. He introduced Mr. Robert

Yuchniuk as the newly hired Animal Control Officers. Mr. Yuchniuk introduced himself to the Commission
members and gave a brief overview of his qualifications.

4. Approval of Minutes EAST LYME TOWN CLERK

MOTION (1)

Motion by Mr. Nickerson to approve the Regular Meeting minutes of October 11, 2018, as submitted.
Seconded by Mr. Powers. In Favor; Price, Nickerson, Perkins, D’Angelo, Powers and Buglione.
Abstained; Pellegrini. Motion passed 6-0-1.

5a. Old Business — Corey Lane

DISCUSSION: Mr. Price stated that the Commission has not had enough time to fully review the data for
this matter. Chief Finkelstein reported that there were no major and only one minor accident on this
stretch of road, so from that safety point there doesn’t seem to be an issue.

6a. New Business — 2019 Meeting Schedule

MOTION (2)

Motion by Mr. Nickerson to approve the 2019 Police Commission meeting schedule, as submitted. The
scheduled is attached hereto as Exhibit 1.

Seconded by Mr. Powers. Motion passed 7-0.

7. Chiefs Report

Chief Finkelstein summarized the monthly report for October 2018, which is attached hereto as
Exhibit 2. He reported that the ELPD is participating in “No Shave November” to raise money for cancer
patients. Please go to the ELPD Facebook page to make a donation; their team has raised $1,500 to date
for this cause. He welcomed Robert Yuchniuk, the Town’s new Animal Control Officer. He reported
that we continue to work together with Waterford with our ACO’s, and that Caitlyn will remain the part-
time ACO.

MOTION (3)

Motion by Mr. Nickerson to add an agenda item to discuss the possibility of creating a Lieutenant
position.

Seconded by Mr. Powers. Motion passed 7-0.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Price stated that they have recognized the need to create a lieutenant position within
the force, and he envisions that this would take the place of the Administrative Sergeant position,
eliminating the need to back fill this position down the line. There was discussion as to whether this
newly created position would be a union position.
MOTION (4)

Motion by Mr. Nickerson to direct the Chief of Police to meet with the Union Representative to open
discussions about adding a Lieutenant position within the union; and to also work on a job description for
this position.

Seconded by Mr. Powers. Motion passed 7-0.

8. Chairman’s Report
Mr. Price had nothing to report for this meeting.

9. Subcommittee Reports
There were none.

10. Communications
There were none.

11. Public Comment
There was none.

12. Commission Response
There was none.

13. Executive Session

MOTION (5)

Motion by Mr. Nickerson to enter into Executive Session for the purpose of discussion personnel matters,
and to include Chief Finkelstein and Sgt. Macek.

Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 7-0.

They entered at 6:28 p.m. They returned at 7:36 p.m. and Mr. Price stated that no formal votes were
taken in executive session.

MOTION (6)
Motion by Ms. Pellegrini to offer the position of East Lyme Certified Police Officer to Brianna Santello.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 7-0.

14, Adjournment

MOTION (7)

Mr. Perkins Moved to adjourn the November 8, 2018, regular meeting of the Police Commission at
7:38 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Powers. Motion passed 7-0.

Respectfully submitted,

Sandra Anderson
