b. Discussion — Chief Review Procedure
MOTION (5)
DISCUSSION: It has been a year since Chief Finkelstein came on board, and things are going
great. In reviewing examples of employee review forms from Waterford and Stonington, it was
agreed that the East Lyme Police Commission would use the Stonington form as a template for
our Police Chief yearly review form, and that we should meet again before the next meeting to
review.
Motion by Mr. Nickerson to create a new East Lyme Chief of Police Evaluation Form using the
Town of Stonington’s form as a template.
Seconded by Mr. Price. Motion passed 6-0.

c. Review and possible action on the Parking and Traffic Regulations Ordinance.
MOTION (6)
DISCUSSION: The Commission reviewed a draft prepared using the Stonington PD’s ordinance
as a template, and made a couple of changes to make it specific to East Lyme. Mr. Nickerson
noted that this Commission has the authority to choose and appoint the civilian board that will
address any appeals. It was discussed that the funds received from these tickets will go into a
fund that will be put in place for the purchase of future police vehicles; right now the funds will
go into the general fund or CNRE account.
Motion by Mr. D’Angelo to approve the Parking and Traffic Regulations Ordinance, as
amended; and to direct the Chairman to send the ordinance to the Board of Selectmen for review.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 6-0.

East Lyme Police Department Monthly Report

Section 1: Noteworthy Incidents/Information
The applicant AFIS system was installed and is ready for operation. Thank you again to
Dominion Energy for the generous grant.

The East Lyme Police Department participated in an informational session at St. Agnes
Church with members of St Agnes, St John’s and Niantic Baptist. Our Department was
invited to assist in discussion of Active Shooter and Disaster Preparedness for the
parishes. A follow up letter to this event praised the assistance of Officer Levandoski
with this event.

The O’Niantic 5K and the Boardwalk 5K both were held successfully on beautiful
weather days, with no issues/problems occurring.

The Chief has continued meeting with Chief’s of the Waterford and New London Police
Department's to establish a regional Accident Reconstruction Team, as well as other
regional cooperative efforts.

On April 13, 2018 at 1239 East Lyme Police received 911 calls indicating that a
pedestrian had been struck on Main Street near the entrance to the Hole in the Wall
Beach. Additional callers indicated that the vehicle which had struck the pedestrian was
fleeing the scene. Witnessed followed the vehicle, updating Police regarding the
vehicles location until Officer(s) were able to catch up. The victim sustained minor injury
and the vehicle operator was cited for multiple motor vehicle violations related to the
accident.

On April 24, 2018 a vehicle at the Gorton Pond Boat Launch rolled into the pond just as
the operator exited. An ELPD Patrol Officer entered the water and assisted in ensuring
the vehicle was unoccupied and assisted with the vehicles removal. Due to being a State
Boat Launch, DEEP investigated the incident.
