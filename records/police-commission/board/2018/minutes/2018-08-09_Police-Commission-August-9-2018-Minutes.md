TOWN OF EAST LYME
POLICE COMMISSION
REGULAR MEETING OF AUGUST 9, 2018
MINUTES

MEMBERS PRESENT: Daniel Price, Mark Nickerson, Joseph Perkins, Giancarlo D’ Angelo, Tony
Buglione and Lisa Pellegrini. Marc Powers arrived at 6:08 p.m.

Also in Attendance: Police Chief Mike Finkelstein and Sgt. Mike Macek

Mr. Price called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance. He introduced Lisa
Pellegrini, newly appointed Police Commissioner. Lisa is the Director of Developmental Services in Westerly,
Rhode Island, and a proud resident of East Lyme since May of 2017. Lisa served as the First Selectman of
Somers, Connecticut for almost four terms before taking her position in Westerly. In Somers they operate
under the State Resident Trooper program, so she brings with her a great deal of experience in running a town
and running a police department.

3. Delegations
There were none.

4. Approval of Minutes

MOTION (1)

Motion by Mr. Nickerson to approve the Regular Meeting minutes of June 14, 2018 and Special Meeting
minutes of June 28, 2018, as submitted.

Seconded by Mr. Buglione. In Favor; Price, Nickerson, Perkins, D’Angelo and Buglione. Abstained;
Pellegrini. Motion passed 5-0-1.

5. Old Business

Mr. D’Angelo inquired as to why the parking ordinance had changed slightly from the version that the
Police Commission sent to the Board of Selectmen. Mr. Nickerson explained that the attorney’s made
minor changes to put it in line with the Town’s standard ordinances. Also the Board of Selectmen made
the decision to get rid of the triple fine if not paid, so the most the fine will go up is double. The fine was
also raised to $50 as the $25 fine in the end is lower than paying to park at the beach and they didn’t feel
that was enough of a deterrent to keep people from parking on Main Street if they are going to the
beaches. Attorney Ronald Stevens is the Appeals Officer.

MOTION (2)

Motion by Mr. Price to include in the Executive Session that they will also be discussing
building/property matters.

Seconded by Mr. Perkins. Motion passed 6-0.

6. New Business

a. Discussion and possible action — request for stop sign at Shore Road and South Drive
Mr. Price reported that he, Chief Finkelstein and Public Works Director Joe Bragaw went and
surveyed the area and there are some concerns with the placement of a stop sign there. It was decided
that this matter should go to the Oak Grove Beach Association for their consideration and if they decide
as an association that a stop sign is needed they will bring that forth to the Police Commission. This
matter will be discussed at a future meeting. FILED

b1) Parking Study

Mr, Price explained that a subcommittee should be created to look at parking regulations and
parking signage around town. Mr. Nickerson stated that this subcommittee will partner with Niantic
Main Street and a merchant or two, possibly Holly Cheeseman on behalf of the Children’s Museum, and
work together to get some regulations in place for Main Street. Chief Finkelstein reported that the DOT
is scheduled to pave in September, but that the lines won’t be painted until springtime so there is time to
really evaluate this and have a good plan in place before it’s time to paint lines. It was agreed that a
parking subcommittee would be created and will consist of Mark Nickerson, Lisa Pellegrini, Joe Bragaw,
East Lyme Police Department, Niantic Main Street, and a merchant or two to be determined.

b2) Building Study
The inception of this possible committee will be discussed at a future meeting.

b3) Staffing Study
Mr. Price stated that this is a very important committee to organize as we have some staffing
concerns coming up in the future and we need to get on top of a long-term plan sooner than later. This
committee will address all staffing needs, not just the upcoming retirements. This committee will consist
of Tony Buglione, Joe Perkins and the Chief or his designee.

East Lyme Police Department Monthly Report

Section 1: Noteworthy Incidents/Information

Celebrate East Lyme was a very successful event which resulted from months of
strategic planning between agencies. The event which brought thousands to Niantic,
was kept save with the assistance of 16 mutual aid Police Officers, supplementing the
full complement of East Lyme Officers. During the event, only a single medical call and
one verbal disturbance occurred.

The Fourth of July was another busy day in the Town of East Lyme. While the East Lyme
Police Department responded to 100 calls for service, all were minor in nature.

The Chief and First Selectman met with the East Lyme Counsel of Beaches on June 27,
2018 to discuss Policing for the upcoming summer. Outstanding dialogue occurred and
positive feedback has been received to date. To address concerns with unregistered golf
carts in Black Point, letters were proactively sent out to owners of past registered carts.
This resulted in increased registrations and Officers assigned to Black Point have made
enforcement/education a priority.

On July 19, 2018 the East Lyme Police Department took part in “Operation Praying
Mantis”, a statewide warrant operation. In total, 324 arrests occurred with the East
Lyme/Waterford/Parole team serving multiple warrants. A letter from Waterford Police
Department Lieutenant Tim Silva to Sgt Joseph San Juan commending him on his
excellent work in this operation was received.

On July 24, 2018 a letter was received from Groton City Police Department Chief
Michael Spellman thanking this agency for the work of Officer Holmes who was assigned
to the Thames River Fireworks

On July 23, 2018 a letter was received from Superintendent Jeff Newton thanking the
East Lyme Police Department for the wonderful partnership that has developed and the
“spectacular” work of Officer Don Hull, who is assigned to the schools as Challenge
Officer.

On July 25, 2018 a letter was received from the New London Police Department
thanking us for our assistance with their 2018 Sailfest Fireworks.
