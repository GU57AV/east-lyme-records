Mr. Price directed the Commission to the photographs in the Member Packet.

Victor Benni, Town Engineer came forward and referenced his memorandum to Chief
Finkelstein dated September 18th, 2018; this was in response to Mr. DeSantis’ letter to Mr.
Nickerson.

Mr. Benni shared the following:

e Corey Lane was sealed and paved.

The Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices for Streets and Highways indicates
centerlines should be utilized on 18 ft width roads that have an average daily traffic count
of 3,000 or more vehicles per day.

Counts of Corey Lane over a 6 day period average 270 cars per day.

© The 4 speed limit signs will be updated with new signs to meet the retroreflection
requirements.

2 additional speed limit signs will be added in the northbound direction.

A400 ft long curbed grass island separates north and sound bound traffic at the end of
Corey Lane and prior to its intersection with Roxbury Road.

The MUTCD advises diverging markings on the roadway to guide vehicles along the
island edge; Public Works has prepared an island marking design for Corey Lane.

e He was told that remnants of yellow center lines still visible on Corey Lane are due to
previous error made by the Town.

Mr. Powers asked if there was a double line before the road was resurfaced and Mr. Benni said
a 150 ft line was on the beginning section of route 156.

Mr. D'Angelo asked about the hit and run accidents referred to by Mr. DeSantis. Mr. Benni said
he can check with the Police Department.

Chief Finkelstein said there have been no accidents in the last year and a half.
Mr. Perkins said there have been double lines on Corey Lane since the 1960s and that no one
pays attention to speed limit signs. Mr. Benni said he doesn't see that in the aerial photograph.

Mr. Perkins said he doesn't remember not seeing the yellow lines there.

Mr. Nickerson said the yellow lines should be utilized if they're going to save lives and said the
question is, will it?

Chief Finkelstein said he will investigate the accident history.
Mr. Benni said he will price out the cost for putting the yellow lines back in, in case they opt to.

He pointed out there are other roads in Town that meet this same criteria and to keep in mind
they will be setting a precedent if they choose to do this.
Mr. Powers said he is uncomfortable making a decision without more information and a road
piecemeal policy is not a good policy to adopt.

Mr. Price said let's focus only on Corey Lane right now.

Mr. Powes seconded the motion.
Motion carried, 4-1-0.
Nay: Mr. Perkins
