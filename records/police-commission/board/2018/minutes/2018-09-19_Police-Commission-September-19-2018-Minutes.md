TOWN OF EAST LYME
POLICE COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING OF SEPTEMBER 19, 2018
MINUTES

MEMBERS PRESENT: Daniel Price, Mark Nickerson, Joseph Perkins and Lisa Pellegrini.
Mr. Price called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

3. Mike’s Famous Gift Run — Sunday, November 4th

MOTION (1)

Mr. Perkins MOVED to authorize the Chairman to sign off on the East Lyme Police Road Closure
Request and the State of CT DOT Application for the Use of State Highways for Motorcycle Event for
Mike’s Famous Harley-Davidson’s 29" Annual Gift Run on Sunday, November 4, 2018.

Seconded by Ms. Pellegrini. In Favor; Price, Nickerson, Perkins and Pellegrini. Motion passed 4-0.

4. Executive Session
The Commission did not enter into Executive Session.

5. Adjournment

MOTION (2)

Mr. Perkins MOVED to adjourn the September 19, 2018, special meeting of the Police Commission at
6:05 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Price. Motion passed 4-0.

Respectfully submitted,

Wauslro Cucloa
