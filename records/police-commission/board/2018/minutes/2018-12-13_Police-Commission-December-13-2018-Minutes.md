TOWN OF EAST LYME
POLICE COMMISSION
REGULAR MEETING OF DECEMBER 13, 2018
MINUTES

MEMBERS PRESENT: Daniel Price, Mark Nickerson, Joseph Perkins, Tony Buglione, Mare Powers
and Lisa Pellegrini.

EXCUSED: Giancarlo D’Angelo

Also in Attendance: Police Chief Mike Finkelstein and Sgt. Mike Macek

Mr. Price called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

3. Police Chief Mike Finkelstein officially swore in new Police Officer Brianna Santello.

4, Delegations
There were none.

5. Approval of Minutes

MOTION (1)

Motion by Mr. Nickerson to approve the Regular Meeting minutes of November 8, 2018, as submitted.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 6-0.

6. Old Business
There was no Old Business.

7a. New Business — Proposed Public Safety Complex

It was reported that the first public forum held on December 10" went well and the public came out with a
lot of good questions and concerns. The next forum is being planned for January 15, 2019. The main
concerns appear to be possible increase in taxes and the location of the proposed facility.

MOTION (2)

Motion by Ms. Pellegrini to deem the current building unsuitable for police activity and work on a plan to
move the police department as soon as possible.

Seconded by Mr. Nickerson. Motion passed 6-0.

MOTION (3)

Motion by Mr. Powers to support the plan as presented by the task force to convert the Honeywell
building, if purchased by the Town, for the purpose of a public safety complex.

Seconded by Mr. Perkins. Motion passed 6-0.

8a. Chief's Report

Chief Finkelstein summarized the monthly report for November 2018, which is attached hereto as
Exhibit 1. He reported that there have been a number of serious accidents in the past few months, one
fatality, and the department and officers have received numerous thank you notes from residents and

8b. Budget Discussion

Chief Finkelstein and Sgt. Macek briefly went over the budget as it is early in the process. Sgt.
Macek noted that we have very limited space for evidence holding at the Waterford Police Department;
and due to the recent murder in town, we have reached that limit and that could cause issues if we do not
find a solution for the current police building problems. The budget is currently on target, but they cannot
predict how the recent police activities in town will affect this trend. Chief stated that three officers, two
of which are supervisors, are expected to retire in the near future; and that the Town should really begin
the hiring process sooner than later to avoid not having enough officers and having to pay overtime to
staff shifts.
