4. Delegations
     Howard Hale, 100 S, Beechwood Road, Niantic, spoke in favor of the Police Commission
approving the use of golf cards in the Giants Neck Heights Association (GNHA) neighborhood.
He stated that the GNHA Board of Directors is in support of this request, and he noted that the
President, Shari Whittaker, attended this meeting. He appreciates the concern with the two
steep hills in the neighborhood but stated that the board of directors considered this and feels
that these are coming in and out of the neighborhood itself and is not cause for concern.
     Shari Whittaker, 82 Spring Glen Road and President of the Giants Neck Heights Association,
stated that their Board of Directors met and requested that she and Mr. Hale attend tonight's
meeting on behalf of their group and neighborhood. The Association as a whole is in favor of
allowing golf carts in GNHA. She noted that there is some opposition from within her
association, but the positive support outweighs this opposition. It was also made clear that the
Giants Neck Beach Association, which is a separate neighborhood, does not want the use of golf
carts on their roads. Currently golf carts are not allowed in Giants Neck Beach or Giants Neck
Heights.
     Rich Coan, 10 Woodbridge Road, Niantic, stated that the question regarding use of golf carts
is the number one asked question to the association board members from existing and new
members.
     Lt, Macek stated that the biggest concern is the safety of the roads, specifically Dell and
Griswold which are both very steep. He stated that there is already a big concern with parking
at the beaches; he requested that the GHNA prepare a plan for parking and bring that to a
future meeting for review and discussion.
