6.     Chief's Report
Chief Finkelstein reviewed his monthly report, attached hereto as Exhibit #1. He reported
that they have taken in four prisoners in the new facility, and that all is going well as they
finish up minor details. He reported on the recent accident where one of our Sergeants was
rear-ended while turning into Cini Park; he stated that this accident was caused primarily
by the bright sun restricting the other driver's vision. We received one of our new vehicles
this week, but it is unclear if the vehicle involved in the accident will be totaled so we may
not actually be up a vehicle; he will report back at the next meeting. The two new officers
are both just about ready to come off of field training and into the regular schedule; both
are doing great, and he has already received a compliment from someone who dealt
directly with Officer Desjardins, The Chief reported that we have two sergeants who are
injured; one who is currently out after fracturing his ankle in training, and the second who
injured her knee and is at work currently but will require surgery which is coming up
shortly. He stated that he requested two police officers be hired in the upcoming budget;
this was reduced to I-L/2 officers by the First Selectman but put back to two full officers by
the Board of Selectmen. The budget will now go to the Board of Finance. Chief reported
that the new communications tower up on Mostowy Road will be coming online in April, at
which point the temporary tower at the solar field on Grassy Hill Road will be abandoned.
He reported that the project to put cameras throughout town is slowly moving forward, with
the Senior Center cameras to be installed soon, This project has moved along slowly due to
backordered equipment. They will be replacing all of the computers for the police and
dispatch using a $25,000 grant they received from OPM. He reported that he sat down and
did a questions and answers session with members of the SCORE group; he will forward
that interview when it is published, Chief reported that they have already begun meeting
with the DOT regarding the upcoming Exit74 construction scheduled to begin in the Spring
of 2023, There will be two major road closures during construction that will significantly
impact traffic; he will keep this commission abreast of the situation as it progresses. The
commission briefly discussed the possibility of increasing fees charged for road jobs;
currently the charge is $25 per hour for use of an officer and vehicle. Chief indicated that
he is in discussions with the Zoning Official to include the Police Commission on future
projects, beginning with the exit 75 project which will require traffic control by our
department. He reported that the uniforms subcommittee has field tested a new uniform
and it did not wear as well as anticipated; they will continue to test wear. The goal is to
have less options than are available now to make it more uniform; there will be two
unforms, one for everyday use and one for formal events. The new uniforms are priced to
fit within the current uniform allowance given to each officer.
7.     Chairman's Report
Mr. Price reported that it is time for the Chief's review; this year's evaluation form was
distributed, and Mr. Price indicated that a special meeting will be scheduled for March 24th
at 6pm when all members are available and can be in attendance.
