                                      TOWN OF EAST LYME
                               BOARD OF POLICE COMMISSIONERS
                             REGULAR MEETING OF SEPTEMBER B, 2022

                                              MINUTES

MEMBERS PRESENT: Dan Price, Giancarlo D'Angelo, Mark Powers, Joseph Perkins, Regina Hitchery,
Stephen Rebelowski and Tony Buglione

ALSO IN ATTENDANCE: Chief Mike Finkelstein, Lt. Mike Macek, Lt. Dana Jezierski, and First
Selectman Kevin Seery

Chairman Price called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

2.     Delegations
Eileen Birk, 839 West Side Road, Block Island, Rhode Island, stated that her family owns and
operates Birk Manufacturing, as well as other businesses in our town, She wanted to express her
concern with the lack of staffing on our Police Department. Her experience with a recent matter
which required police involvement, she stated that our officers and detectives are great, and she
knows that they are doing the best that they can, but she feels that it is clear that we need more
police officers, She inquired as to the best way to support the police in asking for more staff, and a
discussion was held around the budget process and that the public should bring their concerns before
the Board of Finance meeting where said budget will be discussed,

4.    Lieutenants'Reports

Lt, Jezierski reported on the following:
    o Two new dispatchers have started and there are now two dispatchers on every shift; she noted
        that one of the new dispatchers is bilingual.
    . New platform call Prepared 9-1-1, which is a program that will allow the usage of facetime and
        text messaging to take in emergency calls and will allow for recording and easy uploading of
        said communications. Anticipated mid-October timeframe for rollout.
    . Continued work on Quality Assurance programs.
    . Officers are being encouraged to cross train in Dispatch so all staff members are familiar with
        the new EOC and all of the new equipment.
    . Update on the accreditation process; there are only four policies left to review, and there are
        four policies ready to publish.
    . Received two new body cameras; each officer now has a body camera and there are two spare
        cameras.

5. Chief's Report
Chief Finkelstein reviewed his monthly report, attached hereto as Exhibit #1. He reported on the
following:

   a   Recent power supply issues for both radios and towers, and noted the processes allowed for
        good training and lessons learned. The Town's new microwaves are scheduled to be rolled
        out within a couple of months.
   a   They have been working closely with the schools on security and access; will be meeting
        quarterly.
   a   First National Night Out event was a successful event. Held at the Miracle League Field,
        thirteen officers were in attendance and the community enjoyed it; plans are being made for
        next year's event.
       Pink patches will be available in October in honor of Breast Cancer Awareness month.
   a   Tip-A-Cop, held at Family Pizza, was a fun evening and raised $1,500.
   a   Citizens Police Academy will be starting in October; currently eight people from the community
        have signed up. This is a 9-week event; please contact the police department for more
        information.
       Working on organizing a regional traffic team, which would bring together police forces to work
        together to pinpoint areas to saturate within each town,
   a   Three key positions at Town Hall need to be filled that directly affect the Police Department; IT
        Director, Finance Director and Human Resources Director.
   a   With the most recent new hire of a lateral officer, we now have twenty-seven officers; he
        stated that it is possible we could have three retirements in 2023.
   a   Two seats reserved for the January 2023 POST Academy.
   a   Many minor accidents are happening, the majority of which are on Flanders Road; trouble
        spots are intersections at Costco, Starbucks and Flanders four corners.
   a   Three officers are trained in infant car seat installation; Officers Forbes, Hanna and Desjardins,
        The recently held installation event was well received and went well.
   a   Two officers have been identified and are now taking Peer Support training. There is a number
        of training events going on now and in the near future.
