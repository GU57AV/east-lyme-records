                                      TOWN OF EAST LYME
                                BOARD OF POLICE COMMISSIONERS
                              REGULAR MEETING OF OCTOBER 13, 2022

                                                MINUTES

MEMBERS PRESENT: Dan Price, Giancarlo D'Angelo, Mark Powers, Joseph Perkins, Regina Hitchery
and Stephen Rebelowski
MEMBERS EXCUSED: Tony Buglione
ALSO IN ATTENDANCE: Chief Mike Finkelstein, Lt. Mike Macek, Lt. Dana Jezierski, and First
Selectman Kevin Seery

Chairman Price called the meeting to order at 6:00 p,m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance. The
Chairman called for a moment of silence in honor of the police officers killed in the line of duty in
Bristol.

2.     Delegations
Barbara Booth, 34 Riverview Road, addressed the Commission and stated that she would like to see
more sidewalks in town to connect the shore to Flanders. She was given information on the DOT
public presentation to be held on October 27th at the EL high school to discuss the Rte, 161 Corridor
Study currently ongoing and relates to this question.

3.    Approval of Minutes
MOrroN (1)
Ms. Hitchery MOVED to approve the regular meeting minutes of September B, 2022, as submitted
Seconded by Mr. Rebelowski. Motion passed 6-0.

4. New Business - Pine Grove speed hump request
This item is tabled.

5.    Lieutenants'Reports

Lt. Jezierski reported on the following:
    r Have completed five additional policies since the last meeting; there are seventy-five total to
        be published.
    . Officer agility test is scheduled to take place on Sunday, October 16th; the oral examination
        will be conducted on October 21st,
    . Have seats confirmed for both the Law Enforcement Council academy and POST for January
        2023.
    . Dispatch has been training and preparing for the implementation of Prepared 9-1-1, which is
        an application which will allow for the use of text and facetime by callers, police and dispatch

Lt. Macek Reported on the following:
    . Two new vehicles are expected to be on the road by the end of the month.
    . Met with State of Connecticut Department of Transportation regarding our eligibility for future
       grants, such as the DUI grant, with the hopes of working towards receiving more of those
       types of grants going forward.
    o Investigating the use of e-ticket application with printers to be installed in vehicles, which will
       replace the use of handwritten tickets.
