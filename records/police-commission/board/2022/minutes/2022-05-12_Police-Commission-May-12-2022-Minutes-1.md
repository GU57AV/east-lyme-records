2.    Delegations
Mr. Michael Neal, 12 Kevin Road, Niantic, spoke in favor of the Commission following up
with the Local Traffic Authority request for Kevin Road / Gorton Pond boat launch. Mr. Neal
would like to see more lights and more police presence at the boat launch after dark and
during the overnight hours. He reported that he thinks that boaters may be coming to this
boat launch to flush and rinse off the salt water. Mr. Neal would also like to request that
the speed limit be reduced to 15 mph on Kevin Road.

4a. Local Traffic Authority - Kevin Road / State Boat Launch
Chief Finkelstein reported that these are valid concerns and that it would be appropriate for
the Town to follow up with the DEEP. In the meantime, the overnight shift has been made
aware of this complaint and they will be patrolling more often during the overnight hours.
He reported that he spoke with Public Works regarding the speed limit, and that 25 mph is
the standard speed limit for this road, and that they have been asked to put a speed limit
sign on that road. Communication from the Town to the State DEEP will be made; an
update will follow at a future meeting.

4b. Local Traffic Authority - Exit 75 to Rt 1
Chief Finkelstein stated that he understands the applicant's concerns, but that it is a State
road and their jurisdiction. He noted that the State is currently working on a traffic study of
this area, so we will review that once it is complete. Overall, Chief stated that there is
really not an inordinate amount of issues in that area, but that it might be worth a letter to
the DEEP expressing these concerns. Communication from the Town to the State DEEP will
be made; an update will follow at a future meeting.

4d. Animal Control Officers
Chief Finkelstein explained that ACO Robert Yuchniuk and Assistant ACO Allison Ryan came
forward with this request, attached hereto as Exhibit 1, but too late into the budget process
so it did not make it into the budget for the upcoming fiscal year. ACO Yuchniuk explained

and that they are very dedicated to the wellbeing and safety of the animals and the public.

5.    Lieutenants'Reports
   Lt. Jezierski reported that there are only 30 policies left to review and approve for CALEA
Accreditation. That being said, the law has changed in that a department need only achieve
State accreditation. CALEA is more expensive, but it is what they have been striving for
and add a lot to the training of our officers. No action will be taken; continue on the
current path to CALEA accreditation.
   Lt. Jezierski reported that three of our officers participated in a warrant sweep with a
neighboring town, yielding 225 arrests. Lt. Jezierski reported that everyone now has new
uniforms and patches; the patches now match the Chief's, LTs, and Sgts badges with the
scallop shell,

    Lt. Macek reported that we have the two new vehicles in our possession, and that the
one vehicle that was involved in a recent accident is still off the road with no current return
date. The large, shared marine vessel is still out of service, waiting on the engine to arrive.
The East Lyme and Waterford Marine Patrol will use the skiff until the larger vessel is fixed.
    Lt. Macek reported that everyone is getting settled in at the new public safety complex.
Upcoming events are the O'Niantic 5K; the national DEA drug take back is scheduled for
this weekend; the Vice President is scheduled to attend the Coast Guard Academy
graduation next week; this year's Memorial Day parade is Monday, May 30th 2 p,m. start;
Touch A Truck is May l8th at McCooks; and this year Celebrate East Lyme is being held on
July 16th, The Niantic bridge will be closed tonight for cleaning and repairs, and is
scheduled to be completed and reopened by 8:00 a.m.

Mr. Perkins left the meeting at 6:45 p.m.
