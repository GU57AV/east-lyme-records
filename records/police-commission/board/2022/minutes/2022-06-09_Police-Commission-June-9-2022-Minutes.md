4.a. Request to close Fairhaven Road between Brainard and Gada Roads
Public Works DirectorJ. Bragaw presented a request for the Commission to approve a two-to-
three-week closure of Fairhaven Road to accommodate the repair of the bridge spanning the
Pattagansett River. The Request for Action bythe East Lyme Police Commission/Traffic
Authority Re: Fairhaven Road Closure Request is attached hereto as Exhibit 1. Director Bragaw
said the department's goal is to send out the competitive bid package by the end of June. He
expected the work to be completed by a contractor in Fall 2022 after the summer traffic peak
to minimize the inconvenience to residents. J. Perkins requested that letters be sent to
affected residents advising them of the timing of the closure and the alternate traffic plans as
soon as a contractor is selected.
