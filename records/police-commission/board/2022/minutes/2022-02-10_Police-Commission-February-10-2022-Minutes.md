5. Lieutenants' Reports
     Lt. Jezierski reported that the policy review continues to move forward and that they are currently reviewing
Chapter 7, She reported that the two new officers should be coming off of field training, one around the middle of
March and the other at the end of March, so she will begin adding them to the weekly schedule at that time. She
confirmed that we have received the 30% reimbursement from the State for the body cameras; that was originally
supposed to be a 50% reimbursement. Lt, Jezierski reported that they currently have an intern in house and expect a
second intern to come on board at the end of the month. She reported that the town has received the 30o/o
reimbursement from the State for body cameras. Lt. Jezierski stated that the dispatchers took a total of 58 calls in a
very short period of time the afternoon of the fire at Midway Plaza; they did a great job and were as thorough and
respectful on the very last call as they were for the first call that afternoon. The MOUs for re-certification have
been signed off on and are being sent over to POST.
     Lt. Macek reported that both finger printing machines are up and running at the new building, and the Draeger
system is set up and ready to go irr the prisorrer processing area, which is pretfy close to being complete. He stated
that Waterford has agreed to go month-to-month until we can fully move out of their facility and into our own; Mr.

6. Chiefls Report
Chief Finkelstein reviewed his monthly reporl, attached hereto as Exhibit # l. He reported that the first two steps of
the move into the new public safety building are complete, both dispatch and the police have successfully moved,
and he thanked Star Computer for their IT support as this was a major part of the move. The final piece will be for
the holding cells and evidence space to be ready. Chief Finkelstein reported that our two newest officers graduated
the Academy at the end of December and are both doing well and currently satisfying their field training
requirements, He reported that there are four officers eligible to retire, three of which are sergeants, and that there
are two additional officers who are close. The Chief reporled that one of the sergeants was injured during training
and willrequire surgery so will be out for approximately six months. He reported that crime is up 135olo since
January 2021, and that the criminal arrests in 2021 were 200/o higher than in 2020; our police force continues to do
more with less. He reported that the overall closure rate for the State is l6Vo, and the closure rate for our officers in
our town is 400h, so we are much higher than the state average. Chief Finkelstein stated that we are eligible for the
yearly JAG grant through OPM for $25,000, and he plans to use that grant money to start replacing the desktop
computers.

7. Chairman's Report
Mr. Price reported that both the Board of Selectmen and the Board of Finance have taken tours of the new building;
and he thanked the Chief and the Lieutenants for their hard work in making this move happen.
