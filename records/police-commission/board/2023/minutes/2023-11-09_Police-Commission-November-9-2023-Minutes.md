These residents spoke regarding recent break-ins in the Darrows Ridge neighborhood, as well as
an uptick in break-ins in general. They communicated their concern for their safety and the
safety of the general public and requested that the police department provide all available
resources to solve these crimes. Chief Finkelstein reported that Detective Comeau is actively
working on this matter, and that the evidence collected has been sent out for forensics and that
sometimes takes some time. The neighbors are going to look into organizing a Neighborhood
Watch program.
