                               REGULAR MEETING OF JUNE B, 2023
                                          MINUTES

MEMBERS PRESENT: Dan Price, Regina Hitchery, Mark Powers, Joseph Perkins and Stephen
Rebelowski
MEMBERS EXCUSED: Tony Buglione and Giancarlo D'Angelo
ALSO IN ATTENDANCE: First Selectman Kevin Seery, Chief Mike Finkelstein and Lt. Mike Macek

Chairman Price called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

2. Delegations
There were none.

3. Approval of Minutes
MOTTON (1)
Ms. Hitchery MOVED to approve the regular meeting minutes of May LL,2023, as submitted.
Seconded by Mr. Perkins. IN FAVOR; Price, Hitchery, Powers and Perkins. ABSTAINED;
Rebelowski. Motion passed 4-0-1,

4a.Golf Carts in Giants Neck Heights
DISCUSSION: Mr. Price explained a little history of this matter, stating that prior to the
inception of the Police Commission, the Board of Selectmen were the Legal Traffic Authority for
the town. As a regulation that covers public safety, the golf cart regulation should now be
removed from the book of town ordinances and will now be maintained by the Local Traffic
Authority, which is the Police Commission. The existing maps have been redrawn, with no
changes, and the new map for Giants Neck Heights Association has been created and added.
Although the golf carts are not allowed to freely drive under the train trestle into Giants Neck,
they are permitted to use the drop off at the beach, just as vehicles use it. The Commission
discussed the possibility of putting up temporary signage alerting traffic that golf carts are in use
and agreed that it would be acceptable as long as it was not permanent signage. The final
regulation along with the approved maps are attached as Exhibit #1,

MOTTON (2)
Ms. Hitchery MOVED the following:
RESOLVED, the Police Commission, as the duly authorized traffic authority of the Town of East
Lyme, adopts Golf Cart Regulations which govern the use of golf carts on those roads in certain
beach communities in town as set forth in the attached Regulation, This regulation takes effect
immediately upon its adoption this Bth day of June, 2023 and supersedes East Lyme Golf Cart
Ordinance Section 74.01 and 74.99 by operation of state law,
Seconded by Ms. Hitchery. Motion passed 5-0.

5. Lieutenants' Reports
Lt. Macek reported:
    . The bids are out for the new police cruisers; he noted that the base price for said vehicle
       is up approximately $9,000 from last year's cost based on the bids that have been
       received,
   .   Marine patrol season is underway. The regional boat will be having some minor
       maintenance work next week, then it should be ready for the season.

6. Chief's Report
Chief Finkelstein reported that OSTA (Office of the State Traffic Authority) approved the speed
limit reduction to 20 MPH in Attawan Beach, except for West End Avenue, which will remain 25
MPH, OSTA is requiring the town to complete the following actions:
   a   Install, on stop signs with multiple direction stops, a sign that states "stop Always".
   a   Where appropriate add a sign that indicates that "Traffic to the Right Does Not Stop
   a   Slow children sign should be removed,
   a   Add pedestrian warning signs at crosswalks.
   a   One "Curve Ahead" sign installed.

If the Commission has no comments or issues, the Chief will work with Public Works to complete
the items outlined by OSTA in their final report,

Chief reviewed his monthly report, attached hereto as Exhibit #2, He reported:
   a  The Exit 74 project is going well and communication between the Town, the DOT and the
        contractors has been great, Very minimal complaints have been received from the public.
   a  The State is trying out new speed radar enforcement in areas of construction, so there will
        be speed cameras in our area; recently they were positioned between exits 74 and 75.
   a The Memorial Day Parade went well with lots of people downtown. Will be putting more

        enforcement downtown during the summer season.
   a   Officers Chen and Kelly are doing really well at the Academy and are scheduled to
        graduate mid-July.
   a  There is an open Officer position after receiving a resignation; hiring is difficult at the
        moment.

7. Communications
There was none.

B. Chairman's Report
Mr. Price commended Officer VanOverloop on how he handled a recent incident. Chief echoed
the sentiment and reported that he concurs and that the officer continues to work with parents
and the buslness closely.

9. Public Comment
There was none.

10. Commission Response
Mr. Rebelowski reported on the following:
    . He inquired if the digital speed signs can be put on any of the State roads; the answer is
       no, they are allowed on town roads only.
    . East Lyme PBA has discussed disbanding due to lack of participation. This youth group
       holds numerous fund raisers that benefit the community and would be missed. They will
       look at options.
    . Would like to plan an awards ceremony for the department; everyone was in favor of this
       and Mr. Price stated that he would work on planning something for the Fin the Fall.

11. Adjournment
MOrroN (3)
Mr. Perkins MOVED to adjourn the June 8,2023, regular meeting of the East Lyme Board of
Police Commissioners at 6:36 p.m.
Seconded by Mr, Rebelowski, Motion passed 5-0.

Respectfully submitted,



San dra Anderson

EAST LYME POLICE COMMISSION GOLF CART REGULATIONS

 tA) Pursuant to the provisions of Conn. Gen. Stat. $ 14-300g, as amended, the Police
Commission, acting herein as the duly constituted traffic authority of the town, hereby
permits the operation of golf carts within the town in accordance with the terms of this
regulation.
  (B) Gas or electric powered golf carts permitted on town roads must be equipped with a
minimum of four wheels, capable of a maximum speed of no more than 25 miles per hour,
capable of transporting no more than four persons and comply with the following
standards:

      [1) Equipped with a locking brake, main power switch, head lamp(s), tail/brake lamps,
operable horn as defined in Conn. Gen. Stat. $ 14-80(e), rear view mirror and
seatbelts/passenger safety restraints for rear-facing passengers.

   (2) A flag with a minimum height of 72 inches from the ground and at least one foot
above any installed roofshall be placed on the left rear ofthe golfcart to assist pedestrians
and motor vehicle operators in observing the location and operation of a golf cart.

      [3) A valid registration sticker from the Police Department placed in a visible location
on the rear ofthe golf cart.

  tC) Prior to operation of a golf cart the owner shall register it with the Police
Department, The Police Department shall prepare a registration form and registration
sticker for the registration of golf carts. The registration shall expire on May 31 of each
calendar year, The owner of a golf cart shall at all times meet the following standards to
obtain and maintain a valid golf cart registration.
     (1) The owner must submit proof of ownership, such as a certificate of title or bill of
sale, and a certificate of insurance covering the golf cart that meets the State of Connecticut
minimum liability insurance requirements for a motor vehicle or insurance requirements
that are established by the Connecticut Department of Motor Vehicle Regulations
concerning golf carts upon adoption of same.

     (2) The golf cart must satisfy the standards of division (B) above.
   (3) The payment of a fee in the amount of $35 is due for each registration period. From
time to time the Police Commission may amend the amount of such fee to an amount
sufficient to cover all costs associated with the implementation and enforcement of the
requirements of this regulation.
   (D) The following conditions shall apply to the operation of a golf cart:
    [1) Operators must possess a valid Connecticut motor vehicle operator's license, proof
of insurance and a valid golf cart registration.
