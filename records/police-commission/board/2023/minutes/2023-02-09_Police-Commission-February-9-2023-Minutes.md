Chairman Price called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance, Mr.
D'Angelo arrived at 6:05 p.m,

2. Delegations
     Mr. Anthony Cotoia, 14 Broadway in the Pine Grove neighborhood, spoke in favor of the
Police Commission recommending speed humps be installed in that neighborhood for the
following reasons, including but not limited to; there is a constant flow of people traveling
through the neighborhood that don't reside there, and speed humps have been effective in
places like New London and Hartford. Over the years the residents have tried everything that
the town has recommended, including working with the police for increased patrols, signs have
been put up, the former First Selectman has even been down to walk the neighborhood.
     Ms. Carol Ann Gordan, 26 Broadway, Niantic, stated that she understands the reasoning of
why the speed humps might not be recommended by the Town, but she is hopeful that the
Commission with work with the neighborhood to find a resolution. She suggested more stop
signs.

4a, Pine Grove - Speed Hump Request
DISCUSSION: Mr. Price stated that this Commission did rule against this request at the last
meeting, but wanted to give the applicant an opportunity to come back as they were not
available to attend the meeting in December. He stated that the decision was based upon
recommendations from both the Town Engineer and the Chief of Police, and that nothing
significant was brought forth tonight to change that previous decision, Stop signs in small, tight
neighborhoods such as Pine Grove often increase speed as people speed from one intersection to
the next, and they are not recommended by the State to be used as speed control. The
concerns regarding the safety and wear and tear on emergency vehicles remains at the forefront
as well, It was noted that there are posted speed limits, but that some of them are not legal
signs and have posted speed limits of lower that the State allows. Chairman Price stated that
the previous decision to not recommend speed humps stands, but that this Commission will
consider that something might be done to improve the speeding issues in that neighborhood.
