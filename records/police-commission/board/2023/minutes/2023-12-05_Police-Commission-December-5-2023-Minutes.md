                                    TOWN OF EAST LYME
                             BOARD OF POLICE COMMISSIONERS
                           SPECIAL MEETING OF DECEMBER 5, 2023
                                         MINUTES

MEMBERS PRESENT: Dan Price, Regina Hitchery, Joseph Perkins and Stephen Rebelowski.
Giancarlo D'Angelo arrived at 6:05 p.m,
MEMBERS EXCUSED: Mark Powers and Tony Buglione
ALSO IN ATTENDANCE: Chief Mike Finkelstein, Lt. Mike Macek and Lt. Dana Jezierski

Chairman Price called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

3. 2024 Meeting Calendar
MOTTON (1)
Mr. Rebelowski MOVED to approve the 2024 Police Commission Meeting Schedule, as presented
Seconded by Ms. Hitchery. Motion passed 4-0.

4. Executive Session
MOTTON (2)
Mr. Rebelowski MOVED to enter into executive session for the purpose of conducting an
interview for a Police Officer.
Seconded by Ms. Hitchery. Motion passed 4-0.

The Police Commission entered into executive session at 6:01 p.m. They returned at 6:26 p,m.
and Mr. Priced stated that no formal votes were taken while in executive session.

MOTTON (3)
Mr. D'Angelo MOVED to hire Candidate A.
Seconded by Mr, Rebelowski. Motion passed 5-0

6. Adjournment
MOTTON (4)
Mr. D'Angelo MOVED to adjourn the December 5, 2023, special meeting of the East Lyme Board
of Police Commissioners at 6:27 p.m.
Seconded by Ms. Hitchery. Motion passed 5-0.

Respectfully submitted
