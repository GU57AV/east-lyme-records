ln 2025 the East Lyme Police Department continued its efforts to maintain Tier 3 State
Accreditation compliance, the highest level of state compliance a depar"tment can achieve.
This process requires the ongoing review and updating of policies and procedures to ensure
they align with current state statutes and mandates, as well as providing proof of compliance
with all 322 accreditation standards.


Police accreditation is multi-faceted, focusing on enhancing professionalism, accountability,
and community relations. Accreditation promotes best practices, guarantees adherence to
standards, and enhances overall effectiveness. lt also leads to improved operational
efficiency, reduced liability risks, and better officer safety and professionalism. Additionally,
accreditation fosters transparency and trust within the community, which is essentialfor
effective policing.

                         Drug Recognition Expert (DRE)




The Los Angeles Police Department originated the DRE program in the early
1970's when LAPD officers noticed that many of the individuals arrested for
driving under the influence had very low or zero alcohol concentrations. The
officers reasbnably suspected'that the arrestees were uncier the influence of
drugs but lacked the knowledge and skills to support their suspicions. ln
response, the LAPD worked with various medical doctors, research
psychologists, and other medical professionals to develop a simple,
standardized procedure for recognizing drug influence and impairment. The
lnternational Chiefs of Police coordinates the lnternational Drug Evaluation and
Classification program with support from the National Highway Traffic Safety
Administration of the U.S. Department of Transportation. The East Lyme Police
Department has one DRE, Officer Hanna. Officer Hanna has undergone
extensive training to recognize impairment in drivers under the influence of
drugs other than, or in addition to, alcohol. ln 2025 our DRE was utilized eleven
(1 1) times.
