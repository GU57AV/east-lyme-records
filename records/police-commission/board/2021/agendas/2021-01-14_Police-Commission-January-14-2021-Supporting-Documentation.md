4a. Signage Oswegatchie Hills Road

MOTION (3)

DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reported that after speaking with the owner of Three Belles Marina, he
indicated that the GPS system consistently tells drivers to continue past the entrance to the marina, which
appears to be what is causing all of the problems. Fixing how GPS instructs these drivers to get to the
marina would solve the problem, but that signage would certainly help. In addition to a sign located at the
entrance to Oswegatchie Hills Road indicating narrow roads ahead, he suggests that an additional sign be
installed just before the entrance to the marina signaling truck deliveries to enter there.

Mr. Powers MOVED to approve a “narrow roads ahead” sign as recommended by the Public Works
Director, to be installed by the entrance of Oswegatchie Hills Road, and to install an additional sign prior to
the entrance of Three Belles Marina which directs delivery trucks to turn right into the driveway of the
marina,

Seconded by Mr. D’Angelo, Motion passed 5-0.

5b. Budget Review

MOTION (5)

DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein reviewed the FY21/22 proposed budget and outlined all of the increases
and new line items due to the recently passed Police Accountability Law. It was explained that the
implementation of the Police Accountability Bill included mandates to provide Psychological testing and
drug testing for Officers, as well as mandating that each department acquire CALEA accreditation; the
CALEA initial cost to enroll is $11,450. There is a slight increase in the uniform line item which is related
to the contractual uniform allowance increase. Chief stated that regarding the facility rental line iter, the
agreement with the Town of Waterford was renegotiated and we are budgeting for an estimated two months
of service. Once the new Public Safety Facility is occupied, this contractual service will be discontinued, as
well as the need for alarm monitoring. Chief reported that they continue to struggle with covering dispatch
shifts 24/7, and he reports that this is consistent with what other towns are experiencing. The overtime line
item for Dispatch is being increased to reflect this past year’s overtime numbers, and he reported that they
will cut back on the use of part time dispatchers and rely more on our full-time personnel. He reported that
the new body cameras and in-car cameras and equipment have been ordered, The Chief reported that
overall there is a $51,000 increase, as well as the contractual salary increases.

Chief Finkelstein reported that the Office of Legislative Research published a report on December 8" of
this year entitled “Police Officers in Connecticut Statistics”, and that report shows that East Lyme is the 5"
lowest staffed police department in Connecticut. It was discussed that this commission will re-convene the
subcommittee to review the staffing plan after the holidays.

Mr. Powers MOVED to endorse the budget as presented by Chief Finkelstein.
Seconded by Mr. Perkins. Motion passed 5-0,

6. Lieutenants’ Report

Lt. Macek reported that he and Lt. Jezierski have been working well together and he reviewed how
responsibilities have been divided between them and how they have modified how they conduct some of the
day-to-date activities. He reported that they received the new motor, which was paid for by a grant, for the
skiff and that it has been winterized and stored for the season. The regional boat needs some work after
being damaged by hot metal coming from the work being done on the bridge, and this is currently out to
bid. He reported that the AFIS fingerprinting system will be discontinued once the new equipment is
received; they expect a February 2021 launch of this new equipment.

finished with their training periods.

7, Chiefs Report

Chief Finkelstein gave his report, which is attached hereto as Exhibit |. He reported that vehicle break ins
continue to be a problem all over the state and advised the public to please lock your vehicles and remove
the keys and all valuables, He reported that all local and State Police are working together on this problem.
The body cameras have been ordered and they are working closely with AXON to prepare for their arrival.
He reported that election day went smoothly with no notable incidents to report. A new pedestrian walk
signal has been installed at the intersection of Pennsylvania Avenue and Main Street.

The Chief reported that the communications tower on Mostowy Road is complete and ready for our
equipment to be moved into place from the temporary tower; not only will this improve emergency
communication in the north end of town, it will also provide better service to the Giants Neck Heights
neighborhood,

Chief Finkelstein congratulated Commission Member Rebelowski on his retirement from the Law
Enforcement Council of Connecticut and thanked him for his many years of support to law enforcement.
