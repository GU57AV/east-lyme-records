6. Chief s Report
     Chief Finkelstein's report is attached hereto as Exhibit l. He reported that the work at the new public safety
building is on target for completion in August. They are currently working primarily on the technical projects, which
have priority now. Chief Finkelstein reported that he is very pleased with the Commission's decision a few months
ago to promote both Sergeants to Lieutenant and stated that they are doing a great job and working together well. He
reported that there will be a Presidential visit on Wednesday,May lgth and that our officers will be providing mutual
aid for that event. The Chief reported that Officer Hanna has completed FTO training and is on his own and doing a
great job; Officer Baldino started this past week. For the budget, Chief reported that they are at about 78Yo for the
Police, 84o/o for Dispatch, and 73Yo for the ACO; he noted that benchmark is 84%). Overtime is at 94%o for the PD and
80% for Dispatch. The Asset Forfeiture balance is $19,974. Chief Finkelstein stated that they are going to begin the
additional, roving traffic support that they implemented last year and found that it was very effective, as well as some
additional DUI shifts on the weekends. He reported that crime in our area continues to increase.

7. Lieutenants' Report
     Lt. Mike Macek reported that the Animal Control Officers van is just about complete and should be back
on the road soon; and he reported that the regional boat is in the water. The NexGen data transfer project is
in progress and on target for the May 24th "go-live" date. Three of the dispatchers have been certified as
trainers to assist in the training process for the switchover. He reported that the DRAEGER has not yet
arrived as their systems are down for a few more days; work will resume after that time. Lt. Macek stated
that car 107 is back on the road so the entire fleet is up and running. They are planning all the details for the
upcoming Memorial Day parade to take place on Monday, May 3l't at2pm; the parade route will be the
same as it has been every year.

    Lt. Jezierski reported that as well has her and Lt. Macek, Officers Cutillo and Priest are now all trained to
be trainers on the new NexGen system. She reported that AXON was on site doing installations in the
remaining vehicles and stated that all in care cameras are in and fully operational. Lt. Jezierski stated that
they are finalizing the policy with the union regarding body worm cameras, and she expects that those
negotiations will go well as the proposed policy change mimics the State policy for a change in work
conditions. They are working on FBI and Collect audit training in preparation for an August audit. Lt.
Jezierski reported that the State project to provide emails and get the State system in place for the
Dispatchers to utilize is complete and in place.
