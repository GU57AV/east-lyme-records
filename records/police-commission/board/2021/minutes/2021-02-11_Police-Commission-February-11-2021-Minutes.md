4. Chief s Report
     Chief Finkelstein gave his report, which is attached hereto as Exhibit l. He reported that the work at the public
safety building is going well and even a little ahead of schedule. He stated that we had applied for two separate
grants but that we became ineligible for the FEMA one due to a change in criteria; the second grant is moving
forward. Chief Finkelstein thanked Julie Wilson for her work on preparing these grant applications. He reported
that most officers have gotten their second COVID vaccination, and that the couple remaining have theirs
scheduled. He stated that they are close to awarding the RFP for the upgraded, state of the art phone system for
dispatch, and that they are working with the State to integrate their system into this new system. He stated that this
year's nuclear safety grant which is received through Emergency Management will be used to supply the
Emergency Operations Center training room. Chief Finkelstein reported that he is looking into a community wide
information notification system called Everbridge, currently being used by the State. He stated that he is
considering the soft version for East Lyme, which would push out notifications such as parking bans, They will be
going to select the first K-9 officer at the end of this month, and he anticipates that they will work on getting the
second K-9 officer in the 202112022 fiscal year. Training for a K-9 officer is eight weeks to get certified. He
reported that accidents were down last month with only nine occurring in town and only one on Chesterfield Road;
five were at intersections, two were because of the snow and three involved injuries.

5. Lieutenants'Report
     Lt. Jezierski reported that the body cameras have been received and she is working on that policy, as well as
continuing to review existing policies. She stated that the Daigle Law Group is a firm that specializes in law
enforcement policies, and that she may look to them for assistance on our policies;the average cost of a complete
policy revamp is in the $15,000 - $25,000 range. Mr. D'Angelo inquired as to the timeline for the completion of the
body camera policy, and Chief Finkelstein stated that the camera system cannot be installed into the vehicles until
the May so there is still plenty of time left for Lt. Jezierski to finalize that policy. She stated that there are two
lateral positions posted and will remain open until the end of the month, and that it is confirmed that East Lyme has
a seat at the Academy for the June 4tl' class. Lt. Jezierski reported that we now have two officers that have received
the Seizure Recognition and First Aid Training certification, She reported that the sergeants are doing well and are
meeting regularly. She is working with the State to get all our officers certified in the Connecticut Information
Sharing System (CISS) which is a state-wide information technology system used by Connecticut Criminal Justice
