TOWN OF EAST LYME
POLICE COMMISSION
MEETING OF JANUARY 26, 2017
MINUTES

Members Present: Daniel Price, Joseph Perkins, Tony Buglione and Mark Powers
Members Excused: Mark Nickerson, Thomas Gardner and Giancarlo D’ Angelo
Mr. Price called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

3. Approval of Minutes, Meeting of January 12, 2017

MOTION (1)

Mr. Powers moved to approve the minutes of the meeting of January 12, 2017, as amended
below.

DISCUSSION: Item 4.a.1 — last sentence should read, “Mark Powers is going to reach out to the
Police Commissioners Association of Connecticut, and Mark Nickerson will work with the CT
Police Chiefs Association”.

Seconded by Mr. Perkins. Motion passed 4-0.

4. Discuss Future Meeting Dates

Mr. Price reported that after further review, Thursday evenings at 6pm will be the best
time for.this Commission to meet. The meeting schedule for the Police Commission will be the
2" and 4" Thursday of each month at 6pm in the Upper Meeting Room at Town Hall.
MOTION (2)
Mr. Price moved to approve the amended Police Commission 2017 meeting schedule, as
amended and attached hereto as Exhibit 1.
Seconded by Mr. Powers. Motion passed 4-0.

d. Communications: Nothing to report.

e. Budget: Nothing to report.
