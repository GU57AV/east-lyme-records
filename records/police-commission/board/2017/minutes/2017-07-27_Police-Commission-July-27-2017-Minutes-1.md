East Lyme Police Department Monthly Report

Section 1: Noteworthy Incidents

On July 1, 2017 at 0000 hrs the East Lyme Police Department made the successful transition
from the Resident State Trooper program to an independent Police Department.

Fourth of July —

This year’s Fourth of July Holiday arrived with beautiful weather and large amounts of visitors to
the Niantic section of Town. In all, we had 18 Officers scheduled to work portions of the day.
This included Patrol, Beach, Traffic, neighborhood, and Marine Patrol(s). In all, the Department
handled 94 Calls for service that Day/Eve. This includes a drug overdose with narcan save and
water rescue just outside of East Lyme waters.

During the Fourth of July weekend members of the East Lyme Fire Marshal's Office, the State
Fire & Explosion Investigation Unit and the East Lyme Police Department seized approximately
200 pieces of fireworks from multiple locations.

Celebrate East Lyme Day —

This year’s celebrate East Lyme day was a very successful event. Tens of thousands of people
came to Niantic for your celebration and fireworks. This year we incorporated increased security
to guard against vehicle access to Main St. We also utilized significant mutual aid assets to help
make the event successful and secure. This included 12 mutual aid Officers from Law
Enforcement Council Departments (Waterford, Montville, New London, Norwich, Groton City, ,
Ledyard, Stonington, and Amtrak) . We also received assistance from 2 New Haven Police
Officers and their Mobile Communications Vehicle. This, combined with our regions vehicle,
provided an enhanced level of monitoring during the event. We also established a forward
command post at the East Lyme Police Department to run direct dispatch and logistical
functions, while also providing a walk in area for citizens. In all, the large scale event resulted in
two criminal incidents (Assaults) with one arrest.

Notes of thanks

The City of New London Acting Police Chief send letter of thanks for the mutual aid East Lyme
Officer assigned to the City for the Sailfest Fireworks.
