Mr. Chris Karg, 20 Hill Road, East Lyme, spoke on the matter regarding making a portion of Calkins
Road a one-way street. Mr. Karg is in favor of doing this, and asked that the Town consider putting up

Ms. Wanda Czelusniak, 41 Bayview Avenue, Niantic, spoke on the matter of no parking signs on

would like to see that go all the way up to Columbus Avenue.

Mr. Mike Nebelung, Niantic Fire Department, spoke on the matter of speed limit signs in Pine Grove,
and recommended that we also look into no parking on Broadway as it is difficult for emergency
vehicles to navigate.

6. New Business

a. Review and possible action regarding No Parking signs on Crescent Avenue
MOTION (3)
DISCUSSION: Mr. Bragaw stated that the President of the Crescent Beach Association reached out to
him with this issue. Upon review it is recommended that a no parking from here to corner sign be
posted on the north side of Crescent Avenue from Bayview up to Columbus Avenue. Mr. Bragaw
stated that he sent out the proper notice to all of the residents in that area, and he received no negative
comments or resistance in putting up this sign. Mr. Nickerson noted that bringing back a parking
ticket program should be addressed at a future Police Commission meeting. Mr. Nickerson stated that
there has been an increase in people parking on the street this summer because of the new restaurateur
that has moved into the little hamburger and hot dog stand; this new endeavor has been successful and
has brought in a lot of people who are parking there just for the restaurant. He noted that another
solution to cut back on the over parking in that area is for them to go back to their small hamburger
and hot dog eatery that catered to the beach crowd only.
Motion by Mr. Gardner to approve a “No Parking Here to the Corner” sign on the north side of
Crescent Avenue as detailed in the attached documents, attached hereto as Exhibit 1.
Seconded by Mr. Nickerson. Motion passed 5-0.

b. Review and possible action regarding making a portion of Calkins Road a one-way street.
MOTION (4)
DISCUSSION: Mr. Bragaw stated that there is a very big sightline problem when people exit Calkins
Road onto Route 1, where the sightline is very obscured and the vehicles traveling on Route 1 are
going fast. He indicated that he sent proper notice to all of the residents involved, and that no one was
opposed to this plan. Mr. Nickerson noted that instead of using a large No Outlet sign, it should only
be necessary to use the smaller signs that attach onto an existing signpost so as not to add additional
signage into this small area.
Motion by Mr. Nickerson to approve the One Way Plan for Calkins Road, as detailed in the attached
documents, attached hereto as Exhibit 2; and to possibly add one additional no outlet sign, to be
determined by the Public Works Director.
Seconded by Mr. Gardner. Motion passed 5-0.

c. Review and possible action regarding speed limit recommendations for Pine Grove
MOTION (5)
DISCUSSION: Mr. Bragaw stated that the Town does not set any speed limits, and that it is the
responsibility of the State of Connecticut Office of the State Traffic Administration (hereinafter
referred to as OSTA). The standard speed limit on residential roads in Connecticut is 25 MPH unless
otherwise posted with an approved state sign. It was agreed that a meeting would be set up between
the Police Commission, Public Works and OSTA to discuss all of this further. Mr. Bragaw stated that
in the meantime he would get the proper 20 MPH speed limit sign posted on Broadway, and the 15

2
MPH speed limit sign on Cherry Street. He asked Mr. Allen Steiger, President of the Pine Grove
Niantic Association, to take down the 15 MPH speed limit signs that are not approved (Cherry Street
is the only road approved at 15 MPH), as well as the sign that states “no on street parking as per the
Board of Governors”, as the Board of Governors has no jurisdiction over these matters. Mr. Price
noted that the Police Commission will support Pine Grove in its initial appeal to OSTA, but that the
Commission will honor the State’s decision and will not pursue the matter further. It was agreed to
take this matter up at the next meeting of the Police Commission to allow time for the conference call
to be arranged with OSTA.

Motion by Mr. Gardner to table this matter to the next scheduled meeting of the Police Commission.
Seconded by Mr. Nickerson. Motion passed 5-0.

d. Review State of Connecticut reimbursement program for body cameras.
MOTION (6)
DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that this is a great program being offered by the State Office &
Policy Management (OPM), but noted that the State has not yet finalized a budget, and there are quite
a few towns that have participated in this program that have yet to be reimbursed. Mr. Gardner stated
that OPM has taken the position that they are committed to following through with this program, and
that the funds will be released once the State budget is finalized. It was agreed that this matter should
be postponed to a future meeting to give the Commission and Sgt. Macek more time to review the
policy.
Motion by Mr. Gardner to postpone this matter to a future meeting.
Seconded by Mr. Nickerson. Motion passed 5-0.

Mr. Nickerson left the meeting at 7:05 p.m.

e. Review “Tip411” software
DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson agrees that this is a great tool, but just does not think that it is a good
time to be asking for additional funds, and we should let the Police Commission and the new
independent police force settle in before adding resources. It was agreed that further research into this
matter would be done and brought back before the Commission at a future time if feasible.

f. Discussion regarding roles, responsibilities and scope of the following subcommittees;

SUBJECT: Request for One Way on Calkins Rd

Summary of Agenda Item:

Presently, vehicles are allowed to exit on Calkins Road onto Boston Post Rd in the Golden spur area of Town.
The sight line as you come out of Calkins Road looking west is extremely limited due to a large stone wall so it
is very difficult for cars to pull out knowing that vehicles on Boston Post Rd could come up on them quickly
going up to 45-50 MPH. Because of this, I sent a letter to the residents of Calkins, Hill and River Roads back in
March of 2017 telling them that we have come up with a draft plan to make a portion of Calkins Rd one way to
eliminate this safety issue. I received some responses from residents. The letter that I sent in March 2017 and
the attachments along with the response letters are attached with this memo. I would like to bring this issue
before the Board of Police Commissioners as the Legal Traffic Authority to move this issue forward. I will be
sending a letter to all of the residents of this neighborhood letting them know that it will be on the BOPC

time.
