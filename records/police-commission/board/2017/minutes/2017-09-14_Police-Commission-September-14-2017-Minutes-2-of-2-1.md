Section 1: Noteworthy Incidents

On Sunday August 5, 2017 the Town of East Lyme hosted the Hartford Marathon Association
lronman event. Over 600 participants took part in the event, with a full complement of Police
Officers from East Lyme and our mutual aid partner agencies providing Police Services. This
event was highly successful with no incidents occurring.

CO detectors have been installed in Ford Interceptor Utility vehicles. No positive readings to
date.

Met with superintendent of schools to discuss the enhanced relationship between the East
Lyme Police Department and the East Lyme Public Schools. The CHALLENGE program will begin
September 25, 2017. The East Lyme Police Department maintained a highly visible presence at
all East Lyme Schools on the first day of classes.

One Officer has been activated for his role in the US Coast Guard Reserves effective August 30,
2017 to Corpus Christi Texas. Orders place his return on September 29, 2017, though it may
legally be extended an additional 30 days.

One Supervisor who has been a long term light duty is scheduled to return to full duty on
Monday September 25, 2017.

**Mutli-Jurisdictional Pursuit Leads to Stolen Vehicle, Armed Robbery Arrest***

On August 28, 2017 at approximately 5:30 p.m., Troop F was notified by the New Haven Police Department that
officers were in pursuit of an armed robbery suspect operating a stolen motor vehicle.

After taking a stationary position I-95 northbound near exit 63 in Clinton, troopers saw the suspect vehicle travel past
them at a high rate of speed. Troopers pulled out behind the suspect vehicle and attempted to stop it; however the
operator/suspect refused to stop.

An Old Saybrook police officer, waiting at the exit 67 off ramp, successfully deployed stop sticks deflating the left rear
tire of the suspect vehicle. Despite the flat tire, the operator/suspect continued traveling on I-95 before exiting the

highway at exit 70 in Old Lyme.
Troopers waiting at the intersection of Four Mile River Road and Route 156 in the town of Old Lyme successfully

deployed stop sticks deflating both right side tires. Again, despite the flat tires, the operator/suspect continued
traveling on Route 156, into East Lyme, until the vehicle eventually became disabled due to the three flat tires.

The operator/suspect, George D. Lopez (dob 1/8/94) of 240 Tyler Street, East Haven, was taken into custody with the
assistance of the East Lyme Police Department. Upon his arrest, troopers found Lopez in possession of crack cocaine
and drug paraphernalia. .
