5. New Business

a. Approve a $25.00 fee per Police vehicle used for external road jobs
MOTION (5)
DISCUSSION: Chief Finkelstein stated that we current charge $5.00 for our police vehicles used on
external road jobs. He checked with the surrounding towns on how they charge, and $25.00 is a very
reasonable fee and in line with our region. Mr. Nickerson stated that the Motion should be amended to
include that this should go to the Board of Selectmen for approval and that it should take effect as soon
as possible.
Motion by Mr. Nickerson to approve a $25.00 fee per Police vehicle used for external road jobs and
send this to the Board of Selectmen for their approval. This policy will take effect as soon as possible.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 4-0.
6. Chief's Report

Chief Finkelstein reported that Sunday’s annual Hartford Marathon when well and was an overall
success with no injuries and small items that may be adjusted for next year’s race to help it all go even
smoother. He reported that statistically the number of accidents that occurred last month was higher
than normal, but that there were no serious injuries. He will be taking a look and keeping track of hot
spots in Town that we might add increased police presence in an attempt to get these issues under
control. Mr. Gardner inquired if we have K-9 insurance for our police dogs; Chief Finkelstein stated
that we do not and that he would inquire as to the cost and coverage and report back at a future
meeting. He reported that we will be back to full staff of all 23 officers shortly and that will reduce
the current overtime expenses.
