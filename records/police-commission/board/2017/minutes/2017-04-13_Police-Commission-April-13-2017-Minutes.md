TOWN OF EAST LYME
POLICE COMMISSION

Also Present: Sgt. Mike Macek, Mr. Michael Finkelstein and Sgt. Paul Renshaw
Members Excused: Thomas Gardner
Mr. Price called the regular meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

MOTION (1)
Motion by Mr. Powers to approve all five meeting minutes. Seconded by Mr. Nickerson.
Motion passed 6-0.

Mr. Price announced that the first ever Police Chief contract for the Town of East Lyme is ready
to be signed this evening, and invited Mr. Finkelstein forward for the signing. In the presence of
the Police Commission members, Mr. Price and Mr. Finkelstein fully executed the contract; and
Mr. Finkelstein was administered his oath of office, with official start date of May 1, 2017, as the
Town of East Lyme’s first Chief of Police.

4, New Business

a. Dispatch Communications

Sgt. Macek reported that there is a lot of training that will need to be done in order to
bring all of the dispatchers up to speed with adding the Police Department to the dispatch;
currently the dispatchers primarily field fire and EMS 911 calls. The police 911 calls currently
go through State Police dispatch. He reported that Waterford has a certified trainer on their
dispatch staff, and she will be assisting in the training process. Sgt. Macek stated that there is
online training, Total Response Law Enforcement Dispatch training, taking place in June that has
not been budgeted but that the dispatchers will need to complete the training. For less than 20
people the cost is $369 per person; over 20 people and the price drops to $299 per person or
approximately $6,000 total.

Chief Finkelstein noted that our dispatchers come with varying levels of experience and
training, and not all of them will require the same amount of training. It was reported that the
Police Department will officially take over dispatch once the Collect System goes live; our
dispatchers will be trained and ready for that to happen, and Waterford is ready to assist our
dispatchers will this transition.

Sgt. Macek reported that they are in the process of reviewing Waterford’s SOPs for
dispatch, and noted that the SOPs in place for our Emergency Management team contains
detailed instruction on entering information into IMC, but does not contain enough information
on actual dispatch procedures that will apply to the police department.

Mr. Buglione inquired as to if there will be a workstation set up for the on duty Sergeant
to do paperwork and be accessible to dispatch, and it is confirmed that there is a workstation set
up for this purpose, and that there will also be appropriate filing space for active warrants to be
stored and accessible to dispatch.

b. Priority List
Mr. Price noted that the two top items on the priority list are dispatch and radios. Sgt.
Macek is working on compiling all of the hard copy and electronic forms necessary in an
independent police department scenario. Mr. Price reported that replacement of the security
window and bricking as a security measure has been completed at the Police Department. IMC
received Board of Finance approval, and is now headed to Town Meeting on April 19" at 7:00
p.m. Records storage will be housed at the police department in East Lyme, and provisions are
being put in place and proper equipment procured. Dispatch training will be forthcoming with
assistance from both Waterford and Ledyard Police Department dispatch training personnel.
c. Ongoing CI Plan
Mr. Nickerson stated that because of the state of the current economy, this budget is,
and needs to remain, minimal. He confirmed that approved in this year’s budget are the
replacement of two police vehicles, as well as the purchase of a vehicle for the new Chief.

5. Subcommittee Reports

a. Chief of Police
Mr. Price noted that the contract was signed at tonight’s meeting and oath administered,

with an official start date of May 1, 2017.

b. Procedures
Mr. Perkins reviewed briefly the changes that are being proposed for the Policies and
Procedures manual, and reported that few of the major changes will be switching from “First
Selectman” to “Chief of Police”, the chapter on possessed property, arrest procedures, and
evidence procedures.
c. Police Department Building / Operations
Mr. Buglione reported that he has been working with Sgt. Macek to go over the
Operations of the Police Department, including staffing, administrative duties, patrol shifts and
areas, summer and special events, marine patrol and regional patrol with Waterford, to name a
few. Regarding the building, Mr. Buglione reported that the existing building needs some work,
but that he has been working with Joe Bragaw who has been able to provide all maintenance and
the dates that work was performed.
d. Communications
Mr. Perkins shared reports outlining the subscriber list and town frequencies list.
e. Budget
Mr. Nickerson stated that Sgt. Macek and Ms. Johnson worked very hard to keep this

budget efficient and at the bare minimum. With that in mind, he reminded everyone that
depending on what happens with the final State budget, this budget may change even though it is
bare minimum. He reported that the Dispatch budget has been removed from the Emergency
Management budget line by line and moved over to the Police Department budget. It was
discussed that an Audit/Inventory subcommittee should be formed to oversee this process.
MOTION (2)
Motion by Mr. D’Angelo to create an Audit & Inventory Subcommittee; the members of which
will be Tony Buglione, Giancarlo D’ Angelo and Tom Gardner.
Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 6-0.

6. Communications
There were none.

7. Public Comment

Sgt. Paul Renshaw congratulated Mr. Finkelstein on his appointment as Chief of Police, and
stated that the men and women of our police department are supportive of this choice and
looking forward to the transition to an independent police force.

Sgt. Renshaw reported that they have been conducting weekly sergeant meetings to keep on
top of what needs to be done before the switchover. He noted that there are three main things
that need to be accomplished before June 1", First is dispatch, second is communications, and
third is IMC reporting. Currently we do not have the IMC reporting piece in place, but an
appropriation of $17,796 for this module is on the agenda to be approved at the April 19" town
meeting. Once this module is in place, they will come to us to perform all the training necessary,
which is included in the cost of this module.

Chief Finkelstein noted that switching over from Nexgen to IMC will require additional
labor. Sgt Macek, Chief Finkelstein and our current Resident State Trooper, Sgt. Jeffrey
McDermott, will be meeting in the near future to discuss this process.

Mr, Perkins inquired as to how we will be handling open cases, and Chief Finkelstein stated
that those open cases are technically owned by the State Police, and this is a piece of the puzzle
that Sgt. McDermott has a good handle on.

8. Adjournment
MOTION (3)

Commission at 7:05 p.m.
