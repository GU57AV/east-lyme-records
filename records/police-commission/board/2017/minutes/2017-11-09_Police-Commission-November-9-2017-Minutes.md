East Lyme Police Department Monthly Report

Section 1: Noteworthy Incidents/Information

During the storm in late October, the East Lyme Police Department sustained water intrusion
into the building in multiple locations. This includes water entering along the entire south side,
through the ceilings in several locations and through the floor in one Detectives Office.

The East Lyme Police Department remained deployed throughout the late October storm,
working closely with the Department of Public Works, Emergency Management and the Fire
Departments to ensure the safety of residents.

The East Lyme Police Department deployed extra Officers on patrol to ensure a safe Trick of
Treating event on Halloween. Officers handed out glow sticks to Trick or Treaters with a large
amount of positive feedback.

The East Lyme Police Department took part in a Multi Agency Warrant Sweep and assisted the
Norwich Police Department with a “shots fired” incident involving a assault suspect.

The East Lyme Police Department received approval through the Department of Transportation
of a DUI Enhanced Enforcement Grant in the amount of $79,700 for the period of Oct 2017 —
September 2018.

The Chief has been working with "Citizens Observer” (TIPS 411) & Apple to develop the East
Lyme Police Department smart phone app. This work continues. When completed the app will
allow individuals to provide direct communication with the ELPD, as well as to receive alerts,
News Releases, and eventually crime mapping features.

The Chief has also been working with “Carfax” to develop a partnership with the East Lyme
Police Department. This will allow us to greatly enhance our investigative resources involving
vehicles, while also providing citizens an online portal to obtain accident reports. In doing this
the ELPD receives 100% of the fee charged for each report.

The East Lyme Police Department has made an alteration to its phone lines/system. Individuals
who now call 860-739-5900 will reach the dispatch center 24/7, rather than the PD building 8a-
7p. The 860-739-7007 number, which used to roll to the State Police Barracks, will connect
callers to the Police Department.

Notes of thanks

Citizens Bank recognized the East Lyme Police Department as part of their “Community Spirit
Day” for our work in the community. .

The Terri Brodeur Cancer Foundation sent a card of thanks to the East Lyme Police Department
for our support of the Terry Brodeur Breast Cancer Walk Across SECT.
