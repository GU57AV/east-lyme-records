POLICE COMMISSION
EAST LYME TOWN CLERK tE-TING OF FEBRUARY 16, 2017

MINUTES

Members Present: Daniel Price, Mark Nickerson, Giancarlo D’ Angelo, Tony Buglione and
Mark Powers

Members Excused: Thomas Gardner and Joseph Perkins
Mr. Price called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

4, Approval of Minutes, Meeting of January 26, 2017

MOTION (1)

Mr. Powers moved to approve the minutes of the meeting of January 26, 2017, as submitted
Seconded by Mr. Buglione.

IN FAVOR: Daniel Price, Tony Buglione and Mark Powers

ABSTAINED: Mark Nickerson and Giancarlo D’ Angelo

Motion passed 3-0-2.

5. New Business
a. Overview of meeting with Ledyard Police Department on February 2nd.

DISCUSSION: Mr. Price reported that he met with Chief John Rich of the Ledyard Police
Department and took a tour of their new facility. Chief Rich outlined things that need to be done
during the process of instituting an independent Police Department, and Mr. Price reports that so
far this Commission is on track in the process. Once the Chief has been hired and has a POST
number, the Department will then apply to the State for our ORI (Originating Reporting
Identifier) number. Mr. Price will return for another meeting with Chief Rich once we get more
into the process; and also once our Chief has been hired we will start scheduling meetings with
the individual subcommittees to go over to Ledyard.

6. Subcommittee Reports

a. Procedures: Mr. Buglione reported that they met on Monday, February 13" at the
Police Station, and all of the PD Supervisors were present for the meeting. Mr. Buglione reports
that in reviewing all of the procedures that are in place already, he has found that our existing
policies are in good shape. The biggest concern right now is how communications/dispatch is
going to work.

b. Police Department Building / Operations: Mr. Buglione reported that this is a work
in progress, and that he has reached out to Joe Bragaw, Public Works regarding questions he has
after his recent tour of the building. Mr. Buglione stated that he has asked for a list of
maintenance that has been performed and when, as well as a list of what needs to be done. Mr.
Price reminded everyone that we need to be mindful of the budget and not get ahead of ourselves
in the process. Mr. Buglione acknowledged the budget, but stated that we should start really
thinking about this because the building is not in good shape and will need work.

Ms. Kathy Miller came on board as part time/evening reception at the Police Department,
so the lobby will now be accessible to the public until 8 p.m. Monday through Friday. She
brings a lot of experience with her to this role and will be an asset as we move into the
independent police force.

c. Communications: Mr. D’Angelo reported that Collect Training is in the works, Mr.
Buglione requested that he be allowed to participate on this Subcommittee with Mr. D’Angelo
and Mr. Perkins; he would like to starting specifically gathering information on Dispatch. It was
noted that Dispatch is currently overseen by Mr. Richard Morris, but that it will move under the
supervision of the new Chief; Mr. Morris will still oversee the technology piece of the
department. Our situation is unique in that our Dispatch Center is not located with the Police
Department. Mr. Buglione looks forward to contributing to this Subcommittee.

d. Budget: Nothing to report.

Mr. Price reminded everyone that the deadline for Police Chief applications is Thursday,
February 23", after which time we will begin the review process. The plan is to review all of the
applications and narrow the applicants to the top 6, with a tentative date for interviews to be set
the second week in March.

7. Public Comment
There was none.

8. Adjournment

MOTION (2)
Mr, Price moved to adjourn the February 16, 2017, meeting of the Police Commission at

6:40 p.m.
Seconded by Mr. Nickerson. Motion passed 5-0.

Respectfully submitted,

Sandra A. Anderson, Recording Secretary
