TOWN OF EAST LYME
POLICE COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING OF MARCH 28, 2017
MINUTES

Members Present: Mark Nickerson, Daniel Price, Joseph Perkins, Thomas Gardner, Tony
Buglione, Giancarlo D’ Angelo. Mark Powers arrived at 5:10 p.m. and joined the Commission in
Executive Session.

Mr. Price called the special meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.

2. Enter into Executive Session for the Purpose of Discussing Personnel.

MOTION (1)

Mr. Gardner moved to entered into Executive Session, Seconded by Mr. Nickerson. Motion
Passed 7-0. The Police Commission entered into Executive Session at 5:01 p.m.

3. Return to Regular Session

MOTION (2)

Mr. Gardner moved to return to the regular session, Seconded by Mr. D’Angelo. Motion Passed
7-0. They returned at 5:58 p.m. and Mr. Price called the special meeting back to order.

4, Discussion and Possible Action on the Selection of the Police Chief
DISCUSSION: Mr. Nickerson stated that the Town received 19 applications, which were
reviewed and a reduced to a handful of applicants. These applicants each went through two
panel interviews, as well lengthy oral exams.

Mr. Nickerson thanked all of the applicants for taking the time to apply for this position,
and noted that all of the candidates were great and highly qualified.

MOTION (3)
Mr. Nickerson moved that the Town of East Lyme Police Commission select Mr. Michael
Finkelstein of Gales Ferry to be our Police Chief, subject to background check and appropriate

examinations.
Seconded by Mr. Gardner. Motion Passed 7-0.

5. Public Comment
There was none.

6. Adjournment

MOTION (4)

Mr. Powers moved to adjourn the March 28, 2017, special meeting of the Police Commission at
6:00 p.m,

Seconded by Mr. Perkins. Motion passed 7-0. FILED
