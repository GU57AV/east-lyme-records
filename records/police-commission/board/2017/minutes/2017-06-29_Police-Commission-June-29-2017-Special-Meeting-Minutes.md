TOWN OF EAST LYME
POLICE COMMISSION —- EAST LYME TOWN CLERK

SPECIAL MEETING OF JUNE 29, 2017
MINUTES

Members Present: Daniel Price, Mark Nickerson, Joseph Perkins, Mark Powers, Thomas
Gardner and Giancarlo D’Angelo.

Members Excused: Tony Buglione

Also Present: Police Chief Mike Finkelstein. Chief Finkelstein left the meeting at 6:25 p.m.
Mr. Price called the special meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.
3. New Business

a. Priority List Update
Chief Finkelstein gave a final update on the Priority List that has been in place to
monitor things that needed to be complete before the Department goes independent on July 1,
2017. The Chief reported that all tasks are complete and that we are ready to make the move to
an independent police force on July 1. Priority List is attached hereto as Exhibit 1.

b. Discussion and possible action on subcommittees
MOTION (1)

Mr. Nickerson moved to establish three subcommittees; Administrative, Finance and
Operations.
Seconded by Mr. Gardner. Motion passed 6-0.

i. Administrative Subcommittee - Members will be Mark Nickerson and Giancarlo
ii. Finance Subcommittee - Members will be Dan Price and Tom Gardner
iii. | Operations Subcommittee - Members will be Joe Perkins and Tony Buglione
MOTION (2)
Mr. Powers moved to appoint the above members to the newly formed subcommittees.
Seconded by Mr. D’Angelo. Motion passed 6-0.

Mr. Nickerson thanked all the members of the Police Commission and congratulated them on
their hard work and dedication during the process of moving to an independent police
department. This Commission worked tirelessly to interview and hire a Police Chief, and to
prepare the police department and the officers for the transition to an independent police force,
and their efforts are to be commended.

c. Review and possible changes to the approved meeting schedule

DISCUSSION: Mr. Price stated that since we are on the eve of the police department
going independent, the Police Commission no longer needs to meet twice a month. The official
meeting schedule for the remainder of 2017 is attached hereto as Exhibit 2, and will be filed with

1
Town Clerk. It was also noted that a special meeting will be scheduled for July 13, 2017, in
order for the Commission members to go to the next scheduled meeting of the Stonington Police
Commission.
MOTION (3)

Mr, Perkins moved to approve the amended regular meeting schedule for the Police
Commission.
Seconded by Mr. Powers. Motion passed 6-0.

4. Adjournment

MOTION (4)

Mr. Nickerson moved to adjourn the June 29, 2017, special meeting of the Police Commission at
6:45 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Price. Motion passed 6-0.

Respectfully submitted,
Sandra Anderson,
Recording Secretary
