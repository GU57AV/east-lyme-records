East Lyme Police Department Monthly Report

Section 1: Noteworthy Incidents/Information

On September 22 & 23" the Town of East Lyme hosted the East Coast Watercross Jet Ski event
off Amtrak Beach. This event brought competitors from around the country and was supported
by the East Lyme/Waterford Police Regional Marine Patrol. The event was extremely successful
and resulted in no incidents.

The East Lyme Police Department initiated the Challenge Program in the ELMS on September
25, 2017. Officer Hull, who is assigned to this program, will be available/responsible for
response to all school related incidents. This includes planning, problem solving, prevention,
education, liaison, and investigation. Also have meet with Superintendent of Schools ona
regular basis and he is very happy with the enhanced relationship between the East Lyme Police
Department and our schools.

The Officer deployed as a member of the US Coast Guard Reserves has returned to duty after
being released from Texas. A second Officer, a member of the US Naval reserves as a Criminal
Investigator, was deployed for a week of training by his unit.

With the return of the Patrol Sergeant who was on light duty assignment, the East Lyme Police
Department is now at full strength.

On October 3, 2017 | took part in a press conference at Yale New Haven Health Lawrence &

Memorial Hospital to announce partnership with area Police Departments to provide narcan.
ELPD was the first agency provided the new narcan in the programs second year, with higher
dosage narcan to be received shortly. The Town of East Lyme has had 8 narcan saves in 2017.

As part of rebranding, working with local vendor to re-design cruisers with traditional “Black &
White style”. This also results in cost savings.

Human Trafficking report as required by PA 17-29 has been completed and submitted.

Planning meeting has been conducted for the Community Enhancement Task Force. Officer
assignment should take place in the very near future.

As directed by Commissioners, 2 Police Body Camera vendors were hosted by the ELPD to
demonstrate their product(s). Awaiting quotes and possibly demo units.

Tips 411 continues to be examined. Quote received and agency references have been checked,
all with very positive feedback.

The new “Viper” 911 system was installed at dispatch and is up and running with no issues. This
system provides increased functionality.

Movie shoot held in Town and at the ELPD lobby October 4" & 5°".
