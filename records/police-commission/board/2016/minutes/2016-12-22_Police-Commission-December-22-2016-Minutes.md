5: Commission Responsibilities
The top priority of the Commission will be to hire a Chief of Police.

6. Police Chief Hiring Discussion
The Members of the Commission reviewed a draft copy of the job description, which was
compiled using information from several surrounding Towns. This document is a draft
and has not yet, as of this meeting, been posted to the public.

The Members agreed that the job will be posted to the public the week of January 3, 2017, witha
submission deadline of February 3, 2017.

Mr. Nickerson noted that the State Police have a subcommittee in place to assist Town Police
Departments in the hiring process. The Commission will work with this subcommittee to
review resumes, and they will also participate in the first round of interviews.

Mr. Price moved to create two subcommittees;

Seconded by Mr. Perkins. Motion passed 5-0.

The first Subcommittee will consist of Joseph Perkins and Tony Buglione, and they have been
charged with compiling a list of interview questions.

The second Subcommittee will consist of Daniel Price and Giancarlo D’Angelo, and they have
been charged with creating the job description.

7. Police Building Upgrade Discussion

The Members discussed that Public Works is reviewing the possibility of refurbishing the
existing building, and will bring their findings to the Commission when complete. Mr.
Nickerson noted that the Commission should consider alternate sites should the existing
building not be sufficient to refurbish. Before the next scheduled meeting, the
Commission will attempt to make arrangements with various surrounding Towns to tour
their Police Departments.

8. Other Tasks Discussion

Sgt. Macek noted that the dispatcher will be attending Collect Certified Dispatch Training as
soon as possible, which is mandatory training for a dispatcher working in an independent
Police Department scenario.

Mr. Nickerson noted that the MOU with the Town of Waterford being worked on and will be
submitted by January 1, 2017.

He reported that the current Police contracts are being reviewed in anticipation of changes that
will need to be made with the inception of an independent Police Department.

Mr. Nickerson noted that the task of researching the build out of cages will be done by Sgt.
Macek; the Board of Selectmen will begin looking at the budget.

9. Adjournment

MOTION (4)

Mr. Price moved to adjourn the December 22, 2016, meeting of the Police Commission at 7:56
p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Buglione. Motion passed 5-0.
