1. Call to order
e The meeting was called to order at 7 PM by First Selectman Mark Nickerson

2. Introduction of Members
e Members of the Commission introduced themselves and provided brief
biographical backgrounds on themselves.

3. Election of Chairman
e There was discussion about interest in the position of permanent Chair. The
Commission decided to table the election of the Chair until the next scheduled
meeting. Motion made by Mark Powers, seconded by Joseph Perkins.
Unanimous approval.

4. Election of Secretary
e Similarly, the Commission decided to table the election of the Secretary until the
next scheduled meeting. Motion made by Mark Powers, seconded by Joseph
Perkins. Unanimous approval.

5. Scheduling of 2017 Meetings
e The members scheduled the initial four meetings of the Commission. The
scheduled meetings will be on January 12 and 26, 2017, and February 9 and 23,
2017. The meetings will be at 7 PM in conference room | at Town Hall. Motion
made by Giancarlo D’ Angelo, seconded by Daniel Price. Unanimous approval.
e A full schedule of meetings for 2017 will be adopted at the January 12, 2017
meeting.

6. Introduction of Sgt Macek
e Sgt Macek was introduced to members of the Commission. He will serve as the
initial primary liaison between the PD and the Commission
¢ Sgt Macek provided an Action Items List, dated 12-01-2016 (attached), to the
Commission. The list outlines steps that need to be addressed as the PD moves
toward independence. He briefly reviewed, and discussed, with the Commission
the items on the list.

7. Commission Responsibilities
e The members discussed Commission responsibilities. It was felt that the
members needed time to review the Action Items List, the ordinance establishing
the Police Commission, The Town Charter, and other applicable documents prior
to establishing a specific list of responsibilities. This Commission’s
responsibilities will be addressed and determined at the next meeting.

8. Sub-Committee Assignments
e Similar to the Commission responsibilities, it was decided to address the
formation of sub-committees and assignments at the January 12, 2017 meeting.

9, Adjournment
e Daniel Price made a motion to adjourn at 9:07 PM. Seconded by Mark Powers.
Unanimous vote to adjourn.

Respectfully submitted,
