Discussion and Possible Action - Attawan Beach Association use of golf cafts:
Chairman Price asked Chief Finkelstein for concerns he may have regarding the
issue. Chief Finkelstein does not have any specific concerns. Chief Finkelstein
referred to the study done by OSTA regarding the speed limits in Attawan Beach.
Board members discussed concerns for parking if being used to go to the beach. A
resident of Attawan who was present and spoke, stated the purpose is more for
visiting neighbors and specifically in his case transporting a handicapped family
member within the community. He would be using driveways to park the golf cart.
Chairman Price asked Chief Finkelstein if golf caft parking complaints had been an
issue in other beach communities, Chief Finkelstein has not been made aware of
any parking issues, S. Rebelowski asked if the police department should be posting
any signage regarding golf cart usage. Chief Finkelstein feels the specific
association should handle any signage; it is not currently a town function. Chief
Finkelstein advised the board members to consult with the town attorney before
any phanges are made.

Motion: Motion made by S. Rebelowski, seconded by J. Perkinsto approve golf
carts in Attawan Beach, update town website and map after consulting with the
town attorney.
