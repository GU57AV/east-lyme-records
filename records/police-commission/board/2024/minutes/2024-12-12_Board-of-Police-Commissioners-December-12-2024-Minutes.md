            EAST POLICE DEPARTMENT MISSION STATEMENT

  The mission of the East Lyme Police Department is to serve the public in a professional
manner. We take on the responsibility of making our community safe for all those that live,
work or pass through here. We also strive to make our roads safe for motorists and pedestrians
alike. lt is our responsibility to work with other agencies and with the public we serve and treat
everyone with respect and dignity.
