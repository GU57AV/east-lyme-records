Acting Chairman Seery called the Public Hearing to order at 6:42 PM immediately following the
previously scheduled Public Hearing on the Water rates. He noted that the notice had been published in
the local newspaper, The Day with the Publisher's Certificate dated October 16, 2015 attached.

Mr. Bragaw, Public Works Director noted that the proposed rates represent a 22% increase and
recapped that last year they had an 8% increase and that in years prior to that the increases also were
quite substantial as they were doing poorly. This proposed increase indicates that they are putting
themselves in a better position.

Mr. Seery called for any members of the public who wished to comment —
There were none.

Mr. Mingo asked about the past borrowing and if it had been paid back.
Mr. Bragaw explained the borrowing and that it had not all been paid back however they now had it under
control and on a program.

Ms. Russell asked about the sewer revenues and how the most recent billing had come in.
Mr. Bragaw said that they were actually higher than projected as the flows coming out were high.

Mr. Seery called for a motion to close this Public Hearing.
**MOTION (1)

Mr. Mingo moved to close this Public Hearing.

Mr. Zoller seconded the motion.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

Mr. Seery closed this Public Hearing at 6:46 PM.
