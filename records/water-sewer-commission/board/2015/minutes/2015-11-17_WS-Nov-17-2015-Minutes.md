« Meter Replacement Project
Mr. Kargl said that he had contacted the DPH about ‘sole-source’ and noted that for some 10 to 15
years that they have been with Sensus. He would like to formally request that they go with Sensus with
the DPH as they have been with them for so many years now.

Mr. Jacques asked how many meters would have to be replaced if they went to competitive bid and
another vendor.

Mr. Kargl said 6500. He added that with what they have now that they would only have to put the
touchpad in and then be able to do the radio read while with another vendor they would have to replace
the entire assembly.
