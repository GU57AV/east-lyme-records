EAST LYME WATER & SEWER COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
Thursday, OCTOBER 8th, 2015

Present: Mark Nickerson, Chairman, Steve DiGiovanna, Joe Mingo, Dave Murphy, Carol
Russell, Roger Spencer, Dave Zoller

Also Present: Joe Bragaw, Public Works Director FILED IN EAST LYME

Brad Kargl, Municipal Utility Engineer Feige ECT ay rap)

Kevin Seery, Ex-Officio Board of Selectmen

Anna Johnson, Finance Director EASFLYME TOWN CLERK

Absent: Dave Bond, David Jacques

1. Call to Order
Chairman Nickerson called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Water & Sewer Commission to
order at 6:12 PM and led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

2. Park Place Sewer Extension Update

Mr. Kargl reported that staff has gone out and staked all of the lateral locations and that they were
trying to work with the residents to put the laterals in places where they would want them, if at all
possible. They also requested that the developer re-locate the sewer main further up the road by
about 75 feet. They will see how this works out. The signed easements are expected any day now
as they are with the attorney.

Mr. Bragaw said that they have also asked for an inspection fee from the developer.

3. Clarks Hollow Blow-off Installation

Mr. Kargl said that he is looking for the Commission to authorize spending of up to $7000 from the
capital projects budget to excavate the Williams Way end and put in a 2” blow-off as that area is
currently difficult to clean out. He suggested that they could take it from the Shore Road remaining
balance and transfer it to Water Main Improvements. He noted that he had provided them with the
information on the $52,000 left in the Shore Road project which is 100% complete and the Water
Main improvements which has $2,300 in it. He said that this project most aligns with Water Main
Improvements so it would be appropriate to put it there.

**MOTION (1)

Mr. Murphy moved to approve up to $7,000 from the Water Construction Account to pay for the
installation of a 2-inch blow-off on the Williams Way water main in Clarks Hollow to improve the
serviceability of the water main during flushing by the transfer of up to $7,000 from Account #62-
70-006-500-002 (Shore Road Water Main Extension) to Account #62-70-006-500-017 (Water Main
Improvements).

Mr. DiGiovanna seconded the motion.

Vote: 7-0-0. Motion passed.

4. Discussion - Water and Sewer Rates

Mr. Nickerson said that Mr. Bragaw had prepared sheets on the revenues/expenditures for the water
and sewer proposed rate increases necessary for the budget that had been adopted for the fiscal
year. He suggested that they discuss each individually and that they start with the water side first.

= Water Rates

Mr. Bragaw reviewed the water packet starting where they are and noting that the current figures
seem to be on track with projections. He noted that the department recently lost a long term person
due to retirement and that they have done a great job getting on track and that they have read every
meter in Town for the upcoming billing period.

The trend has been more positive on the water side with the loss becoming less over the years. He
said that he would propose a 1.25% rate increase which would put them where they need to be and
help with regard to the new meter system. He added that it is hard to try to project water
usage/billable water but does not think that it would be lower than what it is now. He said that they
do not yet know what the revenues will be for the November billing and that he is still working on that
component. He does feel that what has been projected is fairly close. He explained the graph noting
that the revenues and expenditures are in much better alignment.

Mr. Bragaw also explained the suggested increases for the miscellaneous water charges noting that
the attempt was to have the fees be more in line with what it actually costs to provide the service as
they should not be losing money on the services.

Discussion amongst the Commissioners ensued regarding having a 1% increase rather than a
1.25% increase or perhaps a 1.5% increase in the event that the billing does not meet projections.

Mr. Spencer noted that there has been a 32% increase in the water rates just over the past 5 years
and that they should consider that here.

Ms. Johnson said that if they did want to propose a 1.5% increase to be safe in light of the
information still coming in on the November billing and the fact that they have to advertise the Public
Hearing for the October 27, 2015 meeting date; that they while they could not increase the proposed
increase that they could always reduce the proposed increase at the Public Hearing when they had
more information.

Mr. Nickerson and Mr. Bragaw said that they could also hold a short Special Meeting on Thursday
October 15, 2015 to change the percentage going to Public Hearing should it be absolutely
necessary to do so.

**MOTION (2)

Mr. DiGiovanna moved to propose for Public Hearing a 1.5% water rate increase and various
miscellaneous charges as outlined and proposed.

Mr. Murphy seconded the motion.

Vote: 7-0-0. Motion passed.

= Sewer Rates

Mr. Bragaw explained that this is set up in the same way as the water is. He noted that the sewer
side is doing very well. He said that they would see that he is proposing a 2.5% increase which is
less than what the increases have been. He added that they are carrying money to cover past issues
and that he feels that the 2.5% is fine. He recalled that they have had some larger increases in the
past and that it has helped them.

Mr. Mingo said that he is concemed that they have dollars for infrastructure and that they should be
looking for a 3% increase instead.

Mr. Bragaw noted the graph that he had created and the fact that they have been closing the gap
and better managing themselves.

Mr. Nickerson said that he would like to give people a break and that now is the time to do it as they
have great management and things are under control.

Mr. Spencer noted that there has been a 36% increase in the sewer rates just over the past 5 years
and that should be considered here.

Mr. Bragaw said that he would ideally like to keep it at a 2.5% increase or less.

*MOTION (3)
Mr. DiGiovanna moved to propose for Public Hearing a 2.5% sewer usage rate increase.
Mr. Zoller seconded the motion.

Mr. Murphy said that he would publish the increase at 3%.
Mr. Zoller said that there is a $25,000 contingency in the budget and that 2.5% is good.

Mr. Nickerson said that he would agree with Mr. Zoller that they have the contingency and that 2.5%
is good. Further they have good management and things are under control.
He called for a vote on the motion.

Vote: 6-1-0. Motion passed.
Against: Mr. Murphy

5. Adjournment

**MOTION (4)

Mr. Mingo moved to adjourn this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Water & Sewer
Commission at 7:41 PM.

Mr. Spencer seconded the motion.

Vote: 7~ 0-0. Motion passed.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary
