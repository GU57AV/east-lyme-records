Public Hearing |
¢ Proposed Sewer main Extension for 11 Industrial Park Road, East Lyme, CT

Chairman Nickerson called the Public Hearing to order at 6:30 PM. He noted that the notice of Public
Hearing had been published in the local newspaper, The Day on April 10, 2015. (Copy attached) He
called upon Mr. Kargl to provide them with an outline of the proposal and noted that Mr. Haubert, P.E.
with CLA Engineers was present to represent the applicant should they have any questions.

Mr. Kargl entered Exhibit A into the record — a map of the area provided by Mr. Haubert of CLA

Engineers dated 4/28/2015. He synopsized the following:
The intent is to extend the sewers from its terminus on Industrial Park Road approximately 340’ to 11
Industrial Park Road.

¢ The extension will consist of a 2-inch force main that will be constructed by the developer and then
turned over to the East Lyme Sewer Department.

¢ The developer will install a duplex grinder pump station on their property that will be owned and
maintained by the owner.

¢ There will be a flushing manhole installed at the property line/or in the Town right-of-way that will be
used by East Lyme to flush/clean and maintain the force main.

¢ Once the building complex is completed, a sewer assessment will be levied, the amount to be
determined in accordance with the commercial/industrial sewer assessment formula.

¢ The property is within the sewer shed and a favorable 8-24 consistency review by Planning was
completed.

Mr. Murphy asked what the estimated discharge was for the building.
Mr. Kargl said that it is around 3500gpd.

Attorney O'Connell noted that the statutes require that when a sewer extension occurs that a Public
Hearing is required — separate and distinct from any assessment hearing.

Mr. DiGiovanna asked if this is the only one that has been a force main.
Mr. Kargl said yes and added that there is no other property here to connect in. He noted that the
Orchards have the same type of system for six homes.

Mr. Mingo said that they have no way of knowing if it will leak.
Mr. Karg! and Mr. Haubert said that it is a continuous pipe and would not leak.
Mr. Bond said that if it is fuse-sealed that they would not have a problem with it.

Mr. Nickerson called for any comments from the public —
Hearing none —
He called for a motion to close this Public Hearing.

**MOTION (1)

Mr. Mingo moved to close this Public Hearing.
Mr. Murphy seconded the motion.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

Mr. Nickerson closed this Public Hearing at 6:50 PM.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary
