1. Call to Order

3. Review of Draft Water & Sewer Operating Budgets for FY 2016

Mr. Nickerson said that they would review the drafts of the Water & Sewer operating budgets for FY
2016 and that they would begin with the Water side first. He said that Mr. Bragaw had prepared the
drafts and would present them.

= Water Budget Draft - FY-2016
Mr. Bragaw explained the draft information that he had prepared noting that they are making gains
and that there are potential opportunities for revenues, He noted the following:
= The information provided reflects a 2% proposed rate increase based on 4% more water
usage) which is less than one-half of the rate increase from the last three fiscal years.
= With regard to next year’s water usage there is a lot of development being proposed right now
in Town that should drive up billable water usage and revenues. The assumption is flat usage
for the prisons but a 4% increase in the rest of the Town (due to many projects that are being
built coming on-line).
* Additional revenues for inspection services for developers and a backflow preventer annual
inspection fee are being looked at.
= Fire sprinkler charge from $150 to $200 would provide some additional revenue.
= On the expense side there are three large increases - $45,000 for the rest of the Gateway
meters; $37,200 for the interconnection (first year that we purchase the mandatory 14.8M
gallons of water from NL) and $24,000 electricity increase due to the two new pumping
stations for the interconnect along with the increase in electricity rates that everyone is seeing
now.
= We were able to save over $11,000 by locking in unleaded and diesel rates for the next fiscal

year.

Mr. Kargl noted that Gateway would be paying to the sewer side some $1.3M and they thought it
feasible to move $40,000 of that over to water to complete paying for the meters.

Mr. Bond concurred and said that they have been through this discussion before and you can’t keep
nickel and diming people over everything when you have a business to run and that you have to
work to be as fair as possible.

Mr. Mingo disagreed and said that it is no way to run a business.

Mr. Nickerson and Mr. Bragaw said that as this is the first time that this is being addressed that they
would obtain a legal opinion on utilizing $45,000 from the sewer assessment for the rest of the
meters.

Mr. Bond asked if they would use the same configuration down the road.

Mr. Bragaw and Mr. Karg! said that was the thought.

Mr. Bragaw said that he would propose paying more back to sewer as more projects come on line
and went on to explain the rest of the items.

Ms. Russell asked if they had a moratorium on fluoride what they would save.
Mr. Kargl said that it would be about $12,000.

Ms. Russell said that she knows where they are in the budget cycle and that they can’t do anything
about the hydrants now — but she wants to seriously have a committee set up to discuss this for the

next budget cycle.
Mr. Nickerson noted that when they have a short agenda over the next 8 to 9 months prior to the

new budget sessions that they will put this item on for discussion.
Mr. Nickerson noted that Mr. Poola and Mr. Pape are present this evening.

Ms. Russell asked for a breakdown on the people and the salaries that are paid for by the water &
sewer department.

= Sewer Budget Draft - FY 2016
Mr. Bragaw said that he had done the same thing with this budget as he had with the sewer draft
budget. As he explained the sheets, he noted the following:
* The information presented shows a 4% proposed rate increase which is one-half of the rate
increase from the last three fiscal years.
= There is a lot of development in progress that should drive up sewer billable flows and
revenues. He is assuming flat usage for the prisons but a 2.6% increase in billable sewer
flows for the rest of the Town.
= Included is $4000 in projected revenue for FEMA anticipated to be received in the next fiscal
year from the January 29, 2015 blizzard Juno.
= He has included the first payment of $35,000 to pay back the loan from the Sewer
assessment fund for running in the red for fiscal years 11/12, 12/13 & 13/14, this amount will
need to increase to $50,000 in FY 16/17 but was too difficult to absorb at this time.
= Chemicals (to contro! odors) cost has increased by $28,000 so that line item is showing an
increase over last year by 35%.

Mr. Bragaw said that he feels that they are showing the ratepayers that they are moving in the right
direction.

Mr. Murphy asked about money for infrastructure repairs and said that he would be just as happy to

Mr. DiGiovanna said that he would think that they would need it mainly on the water side.
