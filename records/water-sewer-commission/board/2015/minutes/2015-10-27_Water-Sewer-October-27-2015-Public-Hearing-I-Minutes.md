@ Proposed Revisions to rates and charges for connection to and the use of the East Lyme
Water System

Acting Chairman Seery called the Public Hearing to order at 6:32 PM. He noted that the notice had been
published in the local newspaper, The Day with the Publisher's Certificate dated October 16, 2015
attached. They would hold the public hearing on the Sewer rate changes separately after the close of this
public hearing.

Mr. Bragaw, Public Works Director noted that the proposed rates represent a 12% increase and
recapped that last year they had an 5% increase and that in years prior to that the increases also were
quite substantial as they were trying to get back on track. He said that the main items here are the cost of
electricity, wages and benefits and a new truck. He noted that the differences from the current rates are
minimal.

Mr. Seery asked if there were any members of the public who wished to comment -
There were none.

Ms. Russell asked about the current billing period and how they came out regarding revenues,

Mr. Bragaw said that they came in a bit lower than expected. He noted that March was a very difficult
month and they had for the first time in a long time read all of the meters. As they had previously
estimated those that they could not read, this is sometimes the outcome as the adjustment is made when
the meter is actually read. He said that this puts them on a good footing to have all of the meters read
going forward.

Mr. Seery asked if there were any other comments —
Hearing none —
Mr. Seery called for a motion to close this Public Hearing.
