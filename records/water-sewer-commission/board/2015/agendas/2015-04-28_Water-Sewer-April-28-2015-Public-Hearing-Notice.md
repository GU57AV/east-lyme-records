TOWN OF EAST LYME
WATER AND SEWER COMMISSION
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING FOR SEWER EXTENSION

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the East Lyme Water and Sewer Commission will
hold a public hearing on Tuesday, April 28, 2015 at 6:30 P.M. at the East Lyme Town
Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut regarding the construction of a
proposed extension of the Town’s existing sanitary sewer system. The proposed
extension will begin at the northerly terminus of the existing sanitary sewer line in
Industrial Park Road and run in a general northerly direction in and along Industrial Park
Road a distance of approximately 340 feet to the property line of the premises known
generally as 11 Industrial Park Road. Interested persons are invited to attend and be
heard.

TOWN OF EAST LYME
WATER AND SEWER COMMISSION
