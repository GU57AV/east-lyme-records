                     EAST LYME WATER AND SEWER COMMISSION

                  Tri-town Sewer Negotiation Gommittee Meeting

                                        Minutes

                                   February 21,2020

                                East Lyme Town Hall
                               Conference Room No. I
                                   Time: 9:30 AM



The Tri-town Sewer Negotiation Committee met on Friday, February 21,2020 at the
East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, CT.

Present: Chairman Mark C. Nickerson, David Jacques, Steve DiGiovanna, and Joe
Mingo

Also Present: Joe Bragaw, Public Works Director
              Brad Kargl, Utility Engineer
              Ben North, Assistant Utility Engineer
              Ed O'Connell, Town Attorney


Chairman, Mark Nickerson called the meeting to order at 9:30 AM

Mr. Mingo moved to go into executive session to discuss the Tri-town Sewer Agreement
negotiations and engineering estimates. Mr. DiGiovanna seconded the motion. The
motion passed with a vote of 4 - 0. The Committee went into executive session at 9:31
AM.

Mr. Mingo moved to go out of executive session at 10:53 AM with no action taken. Mr
DiGiovanna seconded the motion. The motion passed with a vote of 4 - 0.

Mr. DiGiovanna moved to adjourn the Committee Meeting at 10:53 AM. Mr. Mingo
seconded the motion. The motion passed with a vote of 4 - 0.
