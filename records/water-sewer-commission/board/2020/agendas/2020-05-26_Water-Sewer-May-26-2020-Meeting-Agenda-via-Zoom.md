The East Lyme Water and Sewer Commission will be holding their regular
May 2020 Meeting via a Zoom Webinar.


A Zoom Webinar is a live, recorded teleconferenced meeting with Panelists
and Attendees. The Panelists consist of the Commission Members, Staff,
and invited participants. The attendees consist of rate payers and
members of the public that would like to attend the meeting. Panelists and
attendees have different roles and abilities within the Zoom Webinar,
similar to the structure of an in-person Water and Sewer Meeting.


When: May 26, 2020 07:00 PM Eastern Time (US and Canada)
Topic: Water and Sewer Meeting (Regular) via ZOOM
