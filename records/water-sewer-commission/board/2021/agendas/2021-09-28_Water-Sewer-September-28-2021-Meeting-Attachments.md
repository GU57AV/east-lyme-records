Re: Sodium Levels in Drinking Water Wells
Data Summary 2005-2021


East Lyme Water and Sewer Commission:
As requested, please find attached an analysis that was performed that summarizes the annual
average concentration of sodium in milligrams per liter (mg/l) for each well in the East Lyme
Water System. Lab analyses were recorded from each well dating from 2005 to 202I from at
least two testing events per year when data was available.
The overall concentration of sodium in the wells appears to have risen modestly over the initial
l2-yeat period of this analysis but appears to have stabilized in concentration over the last four
years, while some wells have even seen a decrease in sodium concentration. It is uncertain
whether the current trend will continue based on the data available at this time but should be
closely monitored and used as an important tool to drive future policy-making decisions
regarding de-icing operations in aquifer areas.

SUBJECT: Discussion on Strategies for Collecting Delinquent Water & Sewer Bills
As of September 27,2021, we had a combined 541 delinquent water and/or sewer accounts totaling $174k owed. This is
a little more than usual at this time of year. We sent out a message from the Water Department on the EL Public Safety
Facebook and Instagram pages along with posting the message on the Town's website which I have included below.

To ltgure out what best to do, we started by printing out a report of all customers that owed more than $300. This query
showed that the 117 customers owing more than $300 totaled approximately $124k of the total owed. I did a further
query and dropped out all customers that were current in their payments until this past May billing. That reduced the
number of accounts to 98 totaling $114k. The Department is trying to get these delinquent accounts paid; however, there
is a lot of manpower needed to do shutoffs. Because of this, my recommendation is ai follows;

       Make the criteria for the shut off list, bills over $1,000 due with a prior past balance that drops the number to 36
                                                                                               -
       accounts which is a manageable amount of accounts if we had to shut them all off.
   o   With these accounts, I would ask our staff to print out the bills due for these 36 accounts and attach them to a
       door hanger at the service location (see a copy of our door hanger attached). The bill would need to include that
       it is our intention to shut their service off in 14 calendar days from the date of the bill unless the bill is paid or
       other arrangements have been made. The Water Department staff should take a picture of the door hangei on the
       doorknob for our records.


Delinquent water and sewer usage bills
We have a lot of delinquent water and sewer usage bills at this time. lt is not fair to the other customers in the East Lyme
Water and Sewer System to have people that don't pay their bills in a timely fashion. Therefore, it is the Department's
intention to shut off cus_tomers for non-payment in the next few weeks if their bilts have not been paid. We have not done
shutoffs since before COVID, so we wanted to send out this message in advance to give customers every opportunity to pay
their bills.

lf a customer is struggling and can't pay the full amount of their bill, they need to call the Department at 860-6g1-4104 or they
can stop by the Water Department in ihe lower level of Town Hall at 108 Pennsylvania Avenue. The last water and sewer bills

notices in August that stated that they needed to pay the bill by September 1,2021or we reserve the right to turn off services

lf a customer is shut off, they would have to pay a turn on fee to have our staff go back out and turn the water on after the bill
has been paid in full, so it is in the custome/s best interests to contact the Department as soon as possible.
