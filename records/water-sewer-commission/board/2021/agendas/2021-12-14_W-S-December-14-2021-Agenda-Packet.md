RE,: East Lyme FEMA Hazard Mitigation Grant Assessment (HMGA) Application

Dear Mr. Alexander

I am pleased to submit the Town of East Lyme's FEMA HazardMitigation Grant Assessment
(HMGA) Application to the Connecticut Division of Emergency Management & Homeland
Security and to the Federal Emergency Management Agency.

Through this application, East Lyme is addressing climate change vulnerabilities, more
specifically the anticipated increase in coastal flooding on the Niantic Sewer Pumping Station.
All residents in East Lyme and Old Lyme that are connected to sewers are served by this
pumping station, in addition to the York Correctional Institution as well as the Camp Nett
National Guard Facility. A flooding event of either a 100 year or 500 return period would result
in significant harm to our served residents and to the environment. We support efforts to design
and install flood-proofing measures and devices at this facility to extend its useful life and
resiliency. We also agree to participate in engagement and outreach meetings related to this
project to inform our residents of the importance of this pumping station and the need to
incorporate climate adaptation features to make this infrastructure more resilient.

Our total project cost is $856,526.00. We are requesting a HMGA grant of $642,192.00 and are
committed to providing a match of $214,131.50, or 25Yo of total cost. The matching funds are in
cash from our Sewer Assessment Fund and are available immediately upon our approval by our
Water and Sewer Commission. The Commission endorsed support of this application at its
December 14, 2021 meeting.
