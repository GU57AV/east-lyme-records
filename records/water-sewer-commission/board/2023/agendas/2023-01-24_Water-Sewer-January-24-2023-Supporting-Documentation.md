"Applications for Determination of Adequacy of Sewer Capacity Pursuant to General Statutes
$7-246a(a)(r)".

applications for sewer treatment capacity pursuant to General Statutes S7-2aOa(aX1).

                more than 20 residential units or (b) require more than 5,000 gallons per day

                $7-246a(a)(1), for determination of adequacy of sewer capacity related to a

SUBJECT:       Water Shutoff Procedure

Summary of Agenda ltem:

This item is meant to provide a synopsis of the procedure that the water department seeks to
continue and to ensure adherence to the objectives of the East Lyme Water and Sewer
Commission. The water department is required to ensure that bills are received in a timely
manner. Water bills are considered late after 30 calendar days from which the bills are mailed.
The procedure that the water department has used and would like to confirm with the Water and
Sewer Commission entails sending a delinquent notice after 60 calendar days from which the
bills were mailed. State law requires that customers be given thirteen days' notice before the
shutoff can occur. The notice must be in writing and include the date that service will be shut off
and what the customer must do to get service turned back on. Additionally, service cannot be
shut off on a Friday, Saturday, Sunday, legal holiday, the day before a legal holiday, or less than
an hour before the department's office closes for the day.
Once the delinquent notice is mailed to customers and at least thirteen days have passed, door
tags are hung on customer's residences to let them know that water shutoff is imminent and to
call the office immediately to enroll in a payment plan and / or make a payment. Additionally,
new language will be appearing to let customers know that the water department can accept
payments through TVCCA if they qualiS'for a hardship.

SUBJECT:       Meter Installation Project at East Lyme High School

Summarv of         da Item:

Over the most recent holiday season, a main break occurred at the East Lyme High School. The
break, which appeared to have been leaking for some time, was located underneath the bleachers
at the baseball field in front of the school. This incident brought up an older issue that parts of

to detect and repair water leaks. The High School water system is not looped, but is fed from
both Chesterfield Rd and Boston Post Rd. The water department has determined that the best
course of action to remedy this situation is to install a meter at each entrance of the facility. In
the past, the Board of Education has made it clear that this is too large of an expense for them to
incur in any given year. Therefore, it is the opinion of water department staff that Sewer
Assessment money is used to cover the up-front costs of the installation of these meters and to
facilitate the project. The Board of Education would be billed over a period of time to be
 determined at an interest rate equal to the interest that the Sewer Assessment is currently
 enjoying in the State of Connecticut STIFF account.

Move to appropriate up to $150,000 out of funds available in the Sewer Assessment Fund to an
account titled "East Lyme High School Meter Installation Project" for the purchase and
installation of two water meters and associated appurtenances at the East Lyme High School.
