8. Discussion and Possible Action on Charges for Delinquent Interest

Mr. Bragaw presented that he would like to change the monthly delinquent rates from 1% to
1.5% to be consistent with the Town's Tax Collector's rates (change from 12% to 18% a year).
Attorney O’Connell mentioned that he had come up with 2 sets of solutions. Handouts were
shared (Reference attached).

The First Resolution was pertaining to the Water and contained two provisions.

Attorney O'Connell read the First Resolution. Mr. Bragaw mentioned that the Public Hearing
should probably take place at the next meeting. Mr. Roberts questioned if the next meeting is a
feasible timeline for the public hearing and haw the public would be notified. Mr. Seery and
Attorney O'Connell responded that the notice should be published in the newspaper at least 10
days prior to the meeting. Mr. Seery mentioned that the Town Clerk would also have to post it.
Mr. Bragaw mentioned that it should not be hard to pass such changes since it is just a
technicality on having the Water delinquent rates match the town’s tax rates. Mr. Bragaw also
mentioned that this increase in rates would increase revenue.

Mrs. Russell mentioned that the language about new rates should be about standardization of
the Water rates to match the states rates. Mr. O'Connell mentioned that it would also match the
town’s Tax Collector rates of 18%.

**MIOTION (3)
Mr. Bond moved to adopt the First Resolution. Mr. Murphy seconded the motion.
Vote 8- 0 - 0. Motion passed.

The Second Resolution was pertaining to Sewer that also contained two provisions.

Attorney O'Connell read the Second Resolution and mentioned that these solutions are right out
of the statute and that they keep the consistency of using the same 18% rate that the state
uses. Mr. Seery asked if there were any questions on the Second Resolution. Mrs. Russell
asked if a lien is to be applied on a property, do we have to notify the public ahead of time. Mr.
Seery answered that it is an everyday process in the Sewer Department, and it would not be

Vote 8- 0 - 0. Motion passed.

9. Project Updates:

a. Meter Replacement Project
Mr. Garneau reported that they changed out 236 meters in July for an 86% completion rate. He
also mentioned that meter replacements will continue throughout the meter reading process for
the upcoming billing. There are at least 300 appointments scheduled throughout the next couple
of months, making the meter reading process much easier. Mr. Garneau mentioned that we will,
in the end, have 5% to 8% of customers that will be a challenge to replace the meters and that
we should probably start looking into implementing the meter replacement fee on the May 2024
bill.
Mr. DiGiovanna asked if the meters that are left to be replaced are spread out throughout town.
Mr, Garneau answered yes. Mr. Murphy asked how the beach areas are doing since it is going
to be more challenging once we lose accessibility after summer residents leave. Mt. Garneau
mentioned that we still have a lot of meter pits to be replaced in those areas and that we would
be targeting them to get completed.
Mr. Bond asked what the plan is for quarterly bills. Mr. Bragaw said that maybe fall of 2024. Mr.
Garneau mentioned that we would need a 98% to 99% completion of meter replacements in
order to implement quarterly billing. Mr. Bragaw mentioned that the quarterly billing would
improve cash flow and that if the rates are going to change before next fall, then now is the time
to start talking about quarterly bill rates.

b. Niantic Pump Station Rehabilitation Project
Mr. Garneau reported that this project is progressing along in a timely manner.

c. Well 2A Raw Water Transmission Main Project
Mr. Garneau reported that we started the execution of this project last week and that will
probably take 4-5 weeks to finish the main installation.

contractor.

entitled “Water Rates Generally” to provide that any rates for use of the town’s water
system, if not paid when due shall constitute a lien upon the premises served and a

rate as would unpaid taxes.

remainder of said section shall be in full force and effect.

RESOLVED, that the Commission hereby authorizes the Chair to execute a
notice of a public hearing to consider the amendment of Section 53.103(B) of the
“Resolution Regarding Adoption of Sewer Use Charges; Sewer Rates and Charges
Generally” to provide that any charge for use of the town’s sewerage system, not paid
within thirty days of the due date, shall thereupon become delinquent and shall bear
interest from the due date at the rate and in the manner provided by the general
statutes for delinquent property taxes. Each addition of interest shall be collectible as a
part of such use charge.

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Commission hereby authorized the Chair to
execute a notice of public hearing to consider the amendment of Section 53.103(C) of
said Resolution to provide that any such unpaid charge shall constitute a lien upon the
real estate against which such charge was levied from the date it became delinquent.
Each such lien may be continued, recorded and released in the manner provided by the
general statutes for continuing, recording and releasing property tax liens. Each such
lien shall take precedence over all other liens and encumbrances except taxes and may
be foreclosed in the same manner as a lien for property taxes.

EXPLANATION OF BILLING TERMS

METER FEE: A meter service fee is being charged to all customers for the service, maintenance, and replacement of the meters.
The fee is divided into two equal payments that coincide with the twice annual May and November billing periods. The charge is
designed to recover costs that do not vary with consumption. These costs include those of meter reading, maintenance of the
meters, and a portion of the debt service costs for meter replacement.

OTHER FEES: These fees are added services based on individual needs. For example, Seasonal Turn On/Off fee and,
Frozen Meter Install/Replacement fee. Contact the office for more information on these or other charges that appear on your bill.

WATER AND SEWER CHARGES: Water charges are calculated based on meter readings at six months intervals. Sewer
charges are calculated based on water consumption. For more detailed information, please visit www.eltownhall.com and search
for WATER RATES or call the office.

ESTIMATED READING; An “E” letter after the meter reading identifies an estimated reading. There are several reasons the
reading was estimated: If the meter is located inside your home, the outside reading equipment is not working; your meter pit
may be blocked or covered and/or the meter may be damaged. If you have a bill based on an estimated reading, please contact us
for a resolution. After an ELWS employee reads your meter, your bill will be adjusted to reflect your actual usage.

SUBJECT: Water and Sewer Department Request for Skid Steer

Summary of Agenda Item:

The Water and Sewer Departments had traditionally used a backhoe for handling materials and
excavation work in the field. As the backhoe aged and newer technologies emerged that
facilitated field work, a mini excavator was purchased to replace most of the tasks that were
previously performed by the backhoe. The backhoe was originally purchased 1984 and currently
is in need of a major overhaul. Staff decided that a new machine with increased versatility was
needed to replace the old machine. A skid steer was selected to fill the current role of the
backhoe and to allow for increased versatility in the field by assisting with manhole repairs,
water main breaks, loading and unloading equipment, snow removal, and mowing. It can be
fitted with numerous attachments that will perform various tasks for the water and sewer
departments for decades.

Staff proposes that this cost be split evenly between water and sewer departments, whereas the
sewer department would pay their share from the sewer assessment fund, and the water
department will pay their share from a five-year loan from the sewer assessment fund at an APR
of 5% interest, with the first payment due on July 1, 2024.

Action Needed:

Motion to approve and request up to $94,762 out of funds available in the sewer assessment fund
and create an account titled “John Deere 333G Skid Steer” to purchase a John Deere 333G Skid
Steer and related attachments. Additionally, the Water Department will pay back $47,381 or 50%
of the purchase price back to said account over a period of five years at an interest rate of 5% per
year by making annual payments. The first payment will be due on August 1, 2024 and the last
payment will be due on August 1, 2028.
