3. Significant Items

1.) Precipitation was 8.37 inches for the month of July, about twice the average for a typical July. The
Bridebrook wellfield (Wells 2A, 3A, 3B), were under stream flow restriction for 4 days, July 4, 5, 17 &18.
This resulted in a limited withdrawal from Wells 3A & 3B during this time period. Well 6 exited it's "dry
period release" monitoring requirements on 7/14, pumping was not required to cease due to measurements
remaining above required levels.

2.) A total of 166 work orders were completed in July; 75 Call Before You DIG (CBYD) work orders were
completed. 12 Shutoff/Turm On requests were completed either for service or seasonal residents. 23
inspections were conducted, 17 of which were annual cross-connection inspections/surveys. 42 Meter work

orders were completed to address communication issues, troubleshooting, or installing new meters. 8 work
orders for miscelaneous items were completed.

3.) Staff have been working to identify and replace the remaining older-style meters (1" and below) in the
system . Office staff have been diligently scheduling these replacements. As of August Ist, There are in total,
25 of these meters left in the system. Of which, 9 are known to be located in vacant/demolished properties.

4.) Due to the significant rain received in July, Staff were able to complete the planned hydrant flushing
program for 2026. Staff flushed 108 hydrants in the month of June. The areas they flushed include; Black Point
Road, Old Black Point, Black Point, & Crescent Beach areas. This brings the total for 2026 flushing to 446

hydrants, covering the entire southern portion of town. Nearly every hydrant was flushed between 2025 &
2026.
