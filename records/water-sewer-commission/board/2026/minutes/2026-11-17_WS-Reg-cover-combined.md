  Note Mr. Bragaw explained that quarterly billing had previously been discussed as a way to address cash flow
 concerns and provide customers with smaller, more frequent bills. He noted that while the Advanced Metering
 Infrastructure (AMI) system has reduced some operational workload, quarterly billing would significantly
 increase administrative work.

  Note Discussion also included customer portal options, usage notifications and the costs associated with
 implementing those services.

  Note Mrs. Royce Williams suggested that the Finance Subcommittee review the issue and prepare a business
 case outlining the costs, benefits, staffing impacts and customer considerations associated with quarterly
 billing for 2028.

10. Project Updates
  10-1. Discussion and Possible Action on the Purchase of a new Pump for Niantic Sewer Pump
  Station

      Note see attachment.
             Sewer_pump.pdf

      Note Mr. North discussed the need to purchase a replacement pump for the Niantic Sewer Pump Station.
     He explained that this was the first time since he has been with the Town that a pump required a complete
     replacement rather than a rebuild.

      Note Mr. North noted that the station operates with four pumps and that staff currently has a spare unit
     installed. The proposed purchase would replace that spare.

      Note Mr. Bragaw noted that the pumps have been in service for approximately ten years and stated that
     the Commission has set aside funds specifically for major equipment replacement projects such as this.

      Decision MOTION (5)
     Mr. Murphy moved to approve up to $69,895 from the Sewer Construction Fund for the purchase of a new
     pump.
     Mr. Zoller seconded the motion.
     Motion carried, 6-0-0.
