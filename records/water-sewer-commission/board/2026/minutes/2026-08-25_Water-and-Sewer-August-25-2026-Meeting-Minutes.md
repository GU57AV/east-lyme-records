  Note Mr. North briefly reviewed Mr. Gervais' report and noted that Finances remain essentially unchanged

8. Chairman's Report

  Note Mr. Cunningham reported that work continues regarding the former police station site and that Phase I
 analysis has been completed. Findings will help guide the Phase II engineering investigation, including
 underground conditions around the building.
 It was also noted that the Route 161 bridge area work is nearing completion, though temporary traffic and
 transportation impacts are expected over the next few weeks.

9. Staff Updates
  9-1. Water Department Monthly Report

      Note see attachment.
             Water_Monthly_Report.pdf

      Note Mr. North highlighted some of the following:
     *Water usage reached its seasonal peak.
     *August demand is projected to be around 60 million gallons, similar to previous peak periods.
     *Approximately 8.37 inches of rainfall have been received, helping maintain water supplies and reducing
     drought concerns.
     *A customer portal replacement is expected before next year to improve customer awareness of water use.

     Note Commission Members noted significantly greener conditions compared to previous years and Mr.
     North and Mr. Bragaw discussed the benefits of some recent conservation measures.

  9-2. Sewer Department Monthly Report

      Note see attachment.
             Sewer_Monthly_Report.pdf

      Note Mr. North reported that increased flow rates are being monitored and are largely attributed to rainfall.
     Staff are installing additional flow meters in targeted areas to identify and address infiltration and inflow
     sources. More data collection and analysis will continue through August and beyond.

     Note Planning for future water and sewer projects is ongoing.
     A Capital Projects Subcommittee meeting will be scheduled soon to discuss project priorities and funding.

      Note Mr. North briefly discussed the Wastewater Treatment & Force Main Projects noting that they
     received four proposals for wastewater treatment plant planning and two proposals for the force main
     project between Evergreen and the wastewater treatment plant. Staff will review the proposals over the
     next few weeks and recommendations are expected in October.

      Note Mr. North added that planning for future wastewater system improvements is expected to take about
     18 months. The project will likely use Clean Water Fund financing, which could provide both
     financial assistance/grants and low-interest loans. Because New London qualifies for certain funding
     programs, additional financial assistance may be available.
