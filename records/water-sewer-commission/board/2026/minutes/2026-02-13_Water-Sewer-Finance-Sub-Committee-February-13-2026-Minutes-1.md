    it seemed that the cost should be borne by the customer, as they would stand to benefit by moving to
    the deduct system. Ben North shared a cost comparison that showed a return on investment for
    customers on sewer who wished to install the deduct system of 2-4 years based on parts and
    installation. Michelle Williams asked how leak alerts work and wondered if l-10 policy should be
    adjusted. Ben North explained that continuous consumption is currently monitored weekly with admin
    reaching out to anyone on the report and following up with a mailer. He also mentioned that irrigation
    is excluded from the l-10 policy. Dave Bond suggested a seasonal automatic shut off to irrigation
    systems by the department. Ben North noted that this is not an adequate way to winterize an irrigation
    system and would create an improper expectation for the homeowner. Ben preferred to have vendors
    winterize systems and shut off instead so it is done properly without the liability.
 ' Joe Bragaw commented that the customer portal would be a great resource for property owners to self-
    check usage and he was hoping to work that into the budget. Since the rate structure would affect the
    budget and potentially add revenue, it was decided by the Subcommittee that the public would need to
    be informed of these changes by the March Commission meeting so that it could be voted on at the
    April Commission meeting. Michelle Williams asked for the use of pictures or graphs to,make it easier
    for the public to understand. Chairman Cunningham felt the sooner this was rolled out, the better. Ben
