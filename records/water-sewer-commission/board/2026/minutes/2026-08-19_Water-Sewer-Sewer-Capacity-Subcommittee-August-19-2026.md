Dan Cummingham called the meeting to order. Ben North clarified that the primary purpose of the
meeting was to review Weston & Sampson's initial draft report. He emphasized that the document was
strictly a preliminary draft and not an official record, noting that all physical copies would be collected
prior to adjournment.

The committee expressed widespread concern that the draft report was fundamentally flawed due to its
methodology. The primary issue discussed was Weston & Sampson's decision to calculate theoretical
sewer flows based on metering data of individual accounts, rather than utilizing the actual observed
flow data provided by ELWS. Members also pointed out several notable omissions in the draft,
including the requested multi-year look-back data, infiltration versus exfiltration metrics, and
considerations for specific customer categories such as irrigation users and unconnected assessment
holders. The committee agreed that sufficient supporting documentation for the repoft's assumptions
was lacking. During the discussion, alternative data presentation fonnats were reviewed, and members
suggested providing the consultant with a previous Sewer Capacity Allocation Table to guide their
revisions.

Action Items and Next Steps

The committee concluded that Weston & Sampson must recalculate their findings, starting with actual
historical data. Ben North was tasked with emailing the consultant to formally outline the committee's
concerns. He will request a revised draft that uses total observed flow as a baseline, correctly
categorizes property types using verified ELWS data, and provides comprehensive backups for all
calculations. Ben North will request a two-week turnaround for the revisions to ensure the committee
can meet their late-October reporting deadline to the Board of Selectmen.

Adjournment

The date for the next meeting remains to be determined and will be scheduled by Ben North upon
receipt of the revised draft. The meeting was officially adjourned at2:07 p.m., at which time all copies
of the draft report were given back to Ben North.

Submitted by Heather Larson, Water and Sewer Utility Administrative Assistant
