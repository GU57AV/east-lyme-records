Prepared for the W&S Commission Rate Hearing on 10/24/17

By Joe Bragaw, Director of Public Works

TALKING POINTS

1,

The operating budget (revenue and expenditures) is approved in April and any proposed
changes to rate structure are voted on at the October W&S meeting.

At the April 2017 W&S meeting, the FY 17-18 budget was approved with a 1.0% increase over
the adopted FY 16/17 budget. At that time, we had the following assumptions;

a. We indicated to the W&S Commission that even with the minimal 1% operating budget
increase; we may be looking at a rate increase of around 6% based on declining flows
and revenues.

b. We assumed flat water usage. The November 2017 billing was 12.76% less than the
November 2016 billing.

c. We did set aside money in the FY 17-18 budget to begin moving forward with a meter
replacement program that is essential to our future operations and the technology that
we should be going too.

d. In our FY 17-18 approved budget, we did increase the town fire hydrant fee by 10% to
help offset the service the Water Department provides the town for town-wide fire
protection

The largest water customer in our system, the State prisons has seen water usage drop by a
significant amount over the last few years. The revenue from the prisons has gone from $381k
in FY 14-15, to $314k in FY 15-16 to $242k in FY 16-17 as their prison population has
decreased. This drop of over $140k in 3 fiscal years is putting pressure on the rest of our
operating budget. Even though there is less water needed, we still have to keep open the
interconnection and keep the system available to provide for peak demand, so the largest
customers add more cost to our operations. Last November we created a top tier user (over
2,500,000 gallons per 6 months) and we are proposing to increase this top tier by 15%.

Even with the 5% rate increase, we will need to cut the existing operating budget by $53k to
balance projected revenue with expenditures. This will be difficult to do but is necessary to
help contain rising rate costs for our customers. If we had the same rate increase for all
customers and didn’t cut the existing budget, it would take a 10.9% rate increase across the
board to balance the budget.

Besides water usage, we are proposing 5% rate increases to the application fees and 5% rate
increases to all service fees that are tied to increases we have in labor and benefits.
