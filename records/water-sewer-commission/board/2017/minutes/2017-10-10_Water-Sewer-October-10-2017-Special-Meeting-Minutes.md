1. Call to Order
Acting Chairman Seery called this Special Meeting of the Water & Sewer Commission to
order at 6 PM.

2. NIANTIC PUMP STATION - Action to Authorize Pump Replacement Work

Mr. Kargl provided an update to the Commission regarding the replacement of the pumps at
the Niantic Pump Station indicating that Weston and Sampson had arranged visits of the
HOMA and Sulzer pump facilities for Friday, October 13, 2017. He indicated that he had
confirmed with Weston and Sampson that the lead time for the pumps still was in the order of
one to three weeks depending on the pump manufacturer that was selected. The lead time
on the VFD’s still needed to be confirmed.

He also noted that by the next regular meeting he expected to have a firm proposal from
Weston and Sampson regarding the material and labor cost associated with the pump
replacement work. There was some discussion about the installation cost that would be part
of Weston and Sampson’s work and the need for a cost comparison from a third party.

Due to this, no action was taken at this time.

3. PROPOSED WATER & SEWER RATES

= Water Rates
Mr. Bragaw reviewed the water rates information. (Attached) He indicated that, in general,
with declining water usage and revenues, a 5% increase in the water rates was
recommended for all class of users except for the highest volume users, in which case, a
15% increase was recommended. Even with the proposed increase, a shortfall of
approximately $53,000 in the FY18 operating budget was anticipated, and would be offset by
a reduction in expenditures primarily from the meter replacement line item. After some
discussion, it was the consensus of the Commission to also increase the connection permit
fees and labor related miscellaneous charges by 5%.

**MOTION (1)

Mr. Murphy moved to approve the proposed 5% water rate increase with some other
modifications to be presented at Public Hearing.

Mr. DiGiovanna seconded the motion.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

=" Sewer Rates
Mr. Bragaw then reviewed the sewer rates information. (Attached) He indicated that similar
to the water budget, there was a decline in flows and revenue and that a 3% increase was
recommended for all users except for the highest volume users, in which case, a 10%
increase was recommended. With the proposed increase, a shortfall of approximately
$62,000 in the FY18 budget was still expected, and would be offset by a reduction in
expenditures primarily from the Treatment Plant line item (flows are directly related to cost).
Similar to the proposed water rates, it was agreed to also increase the connection permit
fees and labor related miscellaneous charges by 5%.

**MOTION (2)

Mr. DiGiovanna moved to approve the proposed sewer rate increases with the
modifications as proposed to be presented at public hearing.

Mr. Murphy seconded the motion.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

4. ADJOURNMENT
Mr. Seery called for a motion to adjourn.

**MOTION (3)

Mr. DiGiovanna moved to adjourn this October 10, 2017 Special Meeting of the Water
& Sewer Commission at 7 PM.

Mr. Spencer seconded the motion.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
