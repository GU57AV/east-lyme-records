6. Finance Director Report
Mr. Seery noted that they had received Ms. Johnson’s report and that she was unable to be here as she

7. Water and Sewer Operating Budgets

= Status Reports

Mr. Bragaw explained the spreadsheets that he had provided them with in the meeting packets. He
recalled that they had reduced the Sewer budget by $135,106 due to less revenue from the prisons and
the Water budget by $86,225 for the same reason.

Ms. Russell asked with respect to bonding payments if they would be decreasing at some point fairly
soon.

Mr. Bragaw said that they would however they are looking at the Well treatment plant modifications and
other work that they had discussed coming on line at the time that the reduction would happen. He
added that he is looking for the new water meters to be able to be funded on their own.

Mr. Kargl also noted that when the debt is reduced that the Well 1A & 6 work would take up that slack.

Ms. Russell recalled that they had mentioned that the sewer side was going to help with the new meters
as they also benefit from them.
Mr. Bragaw said that would come up at the budget workshop meeting as the water meters are the ‘cash
register for the sewer charges.

= Set Date for Budget Workshop
After some discussion, the consensus was to hold their Budget Workshop Meeting on Thursday April 6,

2017 at6 PM.

Mr. Bragaw asked that discussion on the new meters be a specific agenda item for the workshop
meeting.

8. Water Project Updates

o Well 1A and 6 Treatment Plant Modifications and Upgrades

Mr. Kargl reported that he had received the loan agreement from the DOH. It took awhile however it is a
2% loan paid back over 20 years. Other than this, they are a bit behind schedule — by a few weeks.

o Water Meter Replacement

Mr. Seery said that they would discuss this at the Budget workshop as well as hydrants and the other
items that they had touched upon this evening.

9. Correspondence Log
Mr. Murphy asked about the letter they had received regarding a violation on monitoring and reporting.

Mr. Kargl explained that results on monitoring are sent from the lab and that they had gone in late so
they received a violation. The results are sent electronically. They received the notice and will have to
make noted of it in their annual water report. They were not fined.

Ms. Russell asked if they could get some money back from the lab for the error that caused the results
to be sent in late.

Mr. Kargl said that this does not usually happen and that lots of data transfers electronically and there
has not been an issue before this.

10. Chairman’s Report
Mr. Seery said that he would address any questions that they might have.

Mr. Murphy asked about an update on the Old Lyme project.
Mr. Seery and Mr. Bragaw said that they had put it back in their court for a retainer in order to have our
Attorney get involved and so far they have not heard back on this.

Ms. Russell asked about the master meters at the High School and how that was coming along.
Mr. Kargl said that they are working on them.

11. Staff Updates

a. Water Department Monthly Report
Mr. Kargl reported that they have made a lot of progress on water going to New London and that they
have sent over 71% so far. They are in fairly good shape as the demands are low at this time of the
year.

b. Sewer Department Monthly Report
Mr. DiGiovanna asked if they still plan to add restrooms at Cini Park.
Mr. Bragaw and Mr. Kargl said that they are still looking at this as there are a lot of issues with it being a
State road and the infrastructure that is already there.

Mr. Kargl noted an incident with the Pattagansett River Bridge project where a contractor knifed through
the sewer main there. A by-pass pump was set up and a temporary repair made so far with a
permanent pipe repair to follow. The contractor will be paying for the repair.

Mr. Russell asked if it had any impact on the water.
Mr. Kargl said no.

12. Future Agenda Items

Mr. Seery said that they would be discussing the meters and budgetary items at the budget workshop
next week. He also noted that once the Costco permit comes in and the project gets underway that they
would have an update. At this time the permit has not come in yet.

Sewer Commission at 7:50 PM.
