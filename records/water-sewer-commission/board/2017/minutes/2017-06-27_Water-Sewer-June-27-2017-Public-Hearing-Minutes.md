Chairman Nickerson called the Public Hearing to order at 6:30 PM. He read the Notice of Public Hearing
that had been published in the local newspaper, The Day on June 15, 2017.
Mr. Kargl submitted a publisher's certificate (Copy attached)

Mr. Nickerson noted that the applicant has been in contact with Mr. Kargl concerning this process.

Mr. Karg! explained that in 2014 they had assessed for the land portions and then the units were
constructed consisting of 10 buildings with 28 units each for a total of 280 units and a clubhouse. This
was considered Phase | of the project and it is now completed and considered closed. The benefit
resolution rate per unit follows the 2013 rate as they were on an existing sewer line.

Mr. Nickerson asked with the applicant not present how they should proceed as Mr. Kargl has indicated
that he has been in touch with them regarding this.

Mr. Kargl said that they did not reach out to him about keeping this open, even as late as yesterday when
they were in touch with one another. He said that they are aware of the fees.

Mr. Nickerson called for comments from the Commission as no one was present in the audience.

**MOTION (1)
Mr. Mingo moved to close the Public Hearing.
Mr. Spencer seconded the motion.

Mr. Mingo asked Attorney Zamarka if there was any reason to keep it open.
Attommey Zamarka said no, as it has been noticed and they also have the appeal period once a decision
is made.

Mr. Nickerson called for a vote on the motion.
Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

Mr. Nickerson closed this Public Hearing at 6:40 PM.

Respectfully submitted,
