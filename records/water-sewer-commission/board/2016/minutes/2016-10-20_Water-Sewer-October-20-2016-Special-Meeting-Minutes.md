EAST LYME WATER & SEWER COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
Thursday, OCTOBER 20th, 2016

Present: Mark Nickerson, Chairman, Dave Bond, Steve DiGiovanna, Dave Jacques, Joe
Mingo, Dave Murphy, Carol Russell, Roger Spencer,

Also Present: Joe Bragaw, Public Works Director
Brad Kargl, Municipal Utility Engineer
Attorney Edward O'Connell, Attorney Mark Zamarka, Town Counsel

Absent: Dave Zoller

1. Call to Order
Chairman Nickerson called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Water & Sewer Commission to
order at 6:00 PM and led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

2. Payment to New London for Treatment

Mr. Bragaw explained that they received invoices from New London for treatment costs at half year
intervals. The most recent invoice that they received was dated in August 2016 for $359,169.40.
Traditionally at this time of the fiscal year they do not have a lot of funds in their account as they do
not bill until November. After that time they will receive the revenue from the billing period and will
have enough in assets to pay off the invoice. He asked that rather than stall payment to New London
until late November that they borrow from the sewer assessment fund account so that they can pay
the invoice as they have the finances available there. They would then return that borrowed monéy
to the sewer assessment fund account once they receive revenue from the first billing cycle of this
fiscal year.

**MOTION (1)

Mr. Murphy moved to authorize a short duration cash flow loan from the Sewer Assessment
Fund to Sewer Operations in the amount of $359,169.46 in order to pay the New London
Sewer Treatment Invoice in a timely manner. This will be paid back to the Sewer Assessment
Fund in the month of November as we begin to receive semi-annual payments.

Mr. DiGiovanna seconded the motion.

Mr. Bond said that he wanted to be sure that they retum the funds and asked if they should make
another motion to retum the funds as soon as they received them from the billing revenue.

Mr. Bragaw said that this one motion would take care of that and that Ms. Johnson would be
watching to make sure that the borrowed funds are returned.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

and sewer proposed rate increases necessary for the budget that had been adopted for the fiscal
year. He suggested that they discuss each individually and that they start with the water side first.

2.5% increase. However the prison numbers and usage is down significantly and is now tracking at a
44.3% decrease in billing income. He said that they are now proposing a 5% increase. (Copy

= Sewer Rates

Mr. Bragaw reviewed the sewer packet noting that they had projected in April that they would need a
1.5% increase. However a lot of the issues that plague the water side also affect the sewer side. The
sewer side is also losing with the prison decline and they are down by 38% on flows from the
prisons. He said that they are now proposing an 8% increase. (Copy attached)

Mr. Bragaw explained the $135,106 in cuts that he had made to the current fiscal year budget in
order for it to work in light of the reduction in revenue due to the reduced flows and billing from the
prison decline.

Mr. Bragaw said that he would have liked to do a tiered billing system however, due to their Sewer
Use Charges Resolution that was not possible at this time. He said that Attomey O’Connell would
explain this.

Attorney O'Connell said that due to the regulations on the sewer use charges resolution — they are
prohibited from changing to a tier system unless they decide to change their resolution. The
resolution that they operate under stated that they have to bill on the ‘actual gallons of water used’. It
also states that they are to be billed semi-annually.

Mr. Bragaw noted the changes in charges that he had made — noting that the inspection fees had
increased a bit so that they are recovering their costs. He lastly noted that in comparison to other
Towns that they are on the higher side for sewer rates.

Mr. Murphy asked Attorney O’Connell if it would involve a lot of work to update their sewer
resolution.

Attorney O’Connell said that they would have to meet and review the changes that they would want
to make. He added that they would need to incorporate the quarterly readings if that is something
that they want to do in the future.

4. Adjournment
Mr. Nickerson called for a motion to adjourn.

**MOTION (2)

Mr. Murphy moved to adjourn this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Water & Sewer ‘
Commission at 7:20 PM.

Mr. Spencer seconded the motion.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
