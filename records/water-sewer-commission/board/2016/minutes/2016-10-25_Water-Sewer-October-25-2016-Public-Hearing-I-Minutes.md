What are the proposed changes?

1.

2.

3.

We are proposing an 8% rate increase for all accounts up to 2,500,000 gallons per six
month period

We are proposing an additional usage category for over 2,500,000 gallons per six
months for our highest users, which is being proposed for a 10% rate increase.
Inspection Services are proposed to go up from $65/$97.50/hr (Reg/OT) to $70/105/hr.

Why the increases are needed?

1.

We are seeing a 38% decrease in flows from the prisons since March of this year
causing a reduction in revenue from FY 15-16 of $177k. The prisons are by far our
largest customer.

The Commission approved a 1.5% operating budget increase in April for this fiscal
year. Because of the reduced revenues, we are making mid-year cuts in the amoynt of
$135k from our current year budget. This will make this year’s budget $104k less than
last year’s (4.93% reduction)

There will be no new equipment purchased in this fiscal year.

The sewer system is now over 20 years old and we are seeing more needed repairs
throughout the entire system. Some of the major pump station repairs can be over

$20k at any one time.
We need this rate increase to provide the revenue to maintain our aging system.
