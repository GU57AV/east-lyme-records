12. Water Utility Coordinating Committee (WUCC)

Mr. Kargl noted that he had provided information on this as he attends these meetings representing us.
This is what is going on at the State level and is meant to establish ‘Exclusive Service Areas’ (ESA’s) It
is a coordinated plan for connections based on the ability to service customers.

Ms. Russell asked the purpose of this.
Mr. Kargl said that it is meant to facilitate coordination between areas with regard to development
should it become necessary when something falls between two areas.

13. Correspondence Log

been put on the current agenda which was not possible due to the number of items on it and the late
request. He noted that they also had a response from our legal staff.

Mr. Mingo noted that there was a memo on the fire hydrants and said that he feels that it is the
responsibility of the Fire Department to shovel out the hydrants and to paint/maintain them.

Mr. Nickerson said that he would sit down with Mr. Bragaw, Mr. Kargl and the Niantic and Flanders Fire
Chiefs and discuss this.
