What are the proposed changes?

1. We are proposing a 5% rate increase across the board thru all usage categories

2. We are dropping the first category from 50,000 to 40,000 as 81% of our customers fall
within 40,000 usage over 6 months

3. We are proposing an additional usage category for over 2,500,000 gallons per six
months for our highest users

4. We are proposing a $5 fee on the minimum charge
Min charge = (($50.33* 1.05) + $5) = $57.85

5. Meter repair work is going up from $75/$120 (Reg/OT) to $100$/150, respectively.

6. Installing/disconnecting meters during normal work hours was at no charge before but
is proposed to go up to $100.

7. Installing/disconnecting meters after normal work hours is proposed to go up from
$120 to $150.

8. All meter tests are now proposed at $75.

9. Inspection Services are proposed to go up from $65/$97.50/hr (Reg/OT) to $70/ 105/hr.

10.Seasonal customers used to be allowed one free turn on-turn off. As proposed, al tuyn

on-turn offs would be $75

Why the increases are needed?

1. We are seeing a 44% decrease in usage from the prisons since March of this year
causing a reduction in revenue from FY 15-16 and FY 14-15 of $97k and $143k,
respectively. The prisons are by far our largest customer.

2. Because of this loss of revenue, we can’t afford to give away from services that our
staff is providing to the customers.

3. The Commission approved a 2.5% operating budget increase in April for this fiscal
year. Because of the reduced revenues, we are making mid-year cuts in the amount of
$86k from our current year budget. This will make this year’s budget $18k less than
last year’s (0.6% reduction)

4. Our payroll will be approx $33k less than last year as we have not filled the position of

Water Supt upon that person’s retirement in 1/16.

There was no new equipment purchased in this fiscal year.

6. The water system is now over 20 years old and we are seeing more needed repairs and
water main breaks. We have had 6 water main breaks in the last 2 weeks alone.

7. We need this rate increase to provide the revenue to maintain our aging system.
