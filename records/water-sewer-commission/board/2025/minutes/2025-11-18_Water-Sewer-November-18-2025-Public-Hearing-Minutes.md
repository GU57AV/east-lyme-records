   Note Mr. Cunningham announced that all future Water and Sewer Commission meetings, including public
  hearings and regular meetings, will be recorded in the interest of greater transparency and openness with the
  public.

Development on 202 Pennsylvania Ave

   Note Mr. Trakas was present, and a letter was read into the record requesting a continuance of the public
  hearing since the applicant is still in the process of obtaining and assimilating information for the presentation.

  Note Mr. Cunningham stated for the record that this would be the last extension granted, as the Public Hearing
  must be closed and a decision made at the December 16th, 2025, meeting.

   Note Tracy Collins, Town Attorney, pointed out that Mr. Trakas' application still does not comply with
  Commission regulations, specifically concerning proof that the applicant owns the property or has the right to
  develop it.

  Note It was confirmed that the continuance could still be granted, but the non-compliance issue must be
  cleared up prior to the December 16th Public Hearing for the application to proceed.


3. Public Comment

  Note Mr. Cunningham called for public comment and there was none.
