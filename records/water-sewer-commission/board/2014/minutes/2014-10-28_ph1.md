Public Hearing |
+ Proposed Revisions to rates and charges for connection to and the use of the East Lyme
Sewer System

Acting Chairman Nickerson called the Public Hearing to order at 6:30 PM. He noted that the notice had
been published in the local newspaper, The Day and that they would hold the public hearing on the
Water rate changes separately after the close of this public hearing. He noted that the Publisher's
Certificate dated October 15, 2014 is entered as Exhibit A of the hearing record. (Copy attached)

It was noted that the proposed rates represent an 8% increase and that Mr. Bragaw; Public Works
Director would review how they had come up with that figure.

Mr, Bragaw reviewed in an overhead presentation what was provided at Town Meeting on the budget on
May 12, 2014 with updates showing how they had arrived at the 8%. (Copy attached) He noted that at
that time they were looking at a 10.8% rate increase and that they were able to lower that to the 8%. A
large part of the increase is related to the costs that they have for the New London treatment plant as we
do not have our own plant. He said that the revenues were about $60,000 less than projected which
necessitates the increase. It would not be wise to have the expenditure line higher than the revenue line
or to underfund the budget (graph on Page 5). The 8% increase allows for considerable maintenance
which will reduce costs. He said that they are doing all of the things necessary to try to bring expenses
down. He noted that they only have 3,360 customers on the sewer side.

Mr. Nickerson asked if there were any members of the public who wished to comment —

Karen Rak, 27 Black Point Road said that she opposes the 8% increase. Further, when they have a
budget where expenses exceed revenues they need to do what everyone does when they work and have
a home and the costs exceed what you make — you look at your expenditures and you cut back to make
ends meet. So — here you have salaries and some other line items where you can cut back. She said that
while she does believe that they do need an increase that 8% is not the figure — it is too high. She added

that it is very telling when you see all of the increases over the years and there is still a problem. She
lastly said that she did go out today and speak with some of her neighbors and that she has a survey
from them — there are 20 who are also opposed to the rate increase. They have indicated if they have
public water or sewer or both on the survey. She said that due to the time of the public hearing that many
people cannot get to it. She added that she would present it after the next public hearing on the water
rates as she will speak there also.

Mr. Nickerson called for any further comments from the public —
Hearing none —

He asked if the Commissioners had any questions or comments —
Hearing none -

He called for a motion to close this Public Hearing.

*MOTION (1)
Mr. Mingo moved to close this Public Hearing.

Mr. Murphy seconded the motion.
Vote: 6-0-0. Motion passed.

Mr. Nickerson closed this Public Hearing at 6:55 PM.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
