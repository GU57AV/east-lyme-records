EAST LYME WATER & SEWER COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
Tuesday, JANUARY 28th, 2014

Present: Mark Nickerson, Acting Chairman, Steve DiGiovanna, Joe Mingo, Dave Murphy,
Carol Russell, Roger Spencer, Dave Zoller

Also Present: Attorney Edward O'Connell, Town Counsel
Attorney Mark Zamarka, Town Counsel
Brad Kargl, Municipal Utility Engineer
Joe Bragaw, Public Works Director

Absent: Paul Formica, Dave Bond

1. Call to Order
Acting Chairman Nickerson called this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Water & Sewer
Commission to order at 6:35 PM.

2. Enter into Executive Session for the purpose of reviewing and discussing
pending litigation regarding the Landmark Sewer Capacity Appeal
Mr. Nickerson called for a motion.

**MOTION (1)

Mr. Mingo moved to enter into Executive Session for the purpose of reviewing and
discussing pending litigation regarding the Landmark Sewer Capacity Appeal inviting
Attorney Edward O’Connell, Attorney Mark Zamarka, Brad Kargl and Joe Bragaw to join them.

Mr. DiGiovanna seconded the motion.
Vote: 7-0-0. Motion passed.

3. Return to Regular Session and
4. Adjournment

**MOTION (2)

Mr. Mingo moved to return to Regular Session with no action taken in Executive Session and
to adjourn the Special Meeting at 7:13 PM.

Mr. DiGiovanna seconded the motion.

Vote: 7-0-0. Motion passed.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary
