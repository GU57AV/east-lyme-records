8. a. Discussion of Interest Rates - Ordinance Concerning Water Rates

Mr. Bragaw explained that Mr. Scheer had brought this up to him originally. We have been charging 1%
interest on unpaid fees while the going rate for quite some time has been 1.5%; also our tax office
charges 1.5% on unpaid taxes. The suggestion is to raise the water interest rate on unpaid fees to 1.5%
to be in line with the tax department. A list of other water companies was provided showing that 1.5% or
18% per annum is the typical rate.

Attorney O’Connell noted that the 1% interest rate is scattered throughout the ordinances so he would
suggest that if they want to change them that they would make a request to the Board of Selectmen to
amend the ordinances to raise to 1.5% the interest rate (18% per annum).

Mr. DiGiovanna asked if they would have to hold a public hearing on this.

Attorney O'Connell said that the Board of Selectmen would hold the public hearing.

Mr. Jacques asked what the dollar figure on the delinquent accounts could be.

Mr. Bragaw said that he would guess in the $6,000 range or more. He added that as they are working
with the new meters that he would suggest holding this as they may find other items that would need to
also be changed to be consistent and they could do it all at the same time.

Ms. Russell said that she is wondering if there would be an encouragement of some sort — where for
instance people would pay up and have a time frame in which to do it before paying the higher interest
rate.

Mr. Jacques said that he would be interested in knowing all the areas of the ordinance that would be
affected by the change.

Mr. Mingo asked about the sewer rate.

Attorney O'Connell said that it is the same as the unpaid taxes — 18% / year or 1.5% / month.

Mr. Nickerson asked if they could enact it in the same manner as the sewer rate which aligns to any
unpaid taxes.

Attorney O’Connell said that they could.

8. b. Adjusting Sewer Manholes for the DOT Paving of Rte 156

Mr. Bragaw explained that the Dot is paving Rte 156 from the Niantic River Bridge to the rocky Neck
connector this September to October. They will be milling from the Niantic River Bridge to the
intersection of East Pattagansett and doing a 2” overlay from there to the Rocky Neck Connector. Due
to this, we need to adjust all the sewer manhole covers. The DOT compensates around $300 each
however it will cost around $800-$900 per structure due to the size and many of the frames need to be
replaced. Mr. Pape has said that there are 40 manhole covers there and has estimated that there are
approximately 30 manholes that need to be replaced in that section of the road. They need to move on
this as the date of the paving is fast approaching. They are estimating it at costing up to $60,000.

Mr. Jacques asked if they could firm up the actual number of frames needed.

Mr. Bragaw said that they were comfortable with the 40.

Ms. Russell asked about the history on the $300 each that the DOT compensates.

Mr. Bragaw said that it has always been that amount. He noted that the reimbursement that they
receive should go back to the same account.

*™MOTION (5)

Mr. DiGiovanna moved to approve up to $60,000 from the sewer assessment fund to purchase
new manhole structures and to cover the cost of labor to adjust structures on Rte 156 from East
Pattagansett Road to the Rocky Neck Connector.

Mr. Jacques seconded the motion.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.
