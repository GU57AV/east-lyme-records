5. Billing Adjustments — 239 Old Black Point Road

Mr. Bragaw noted that Mr. Adams of 239 Old Black Point Road is present this evening regarding his bill.
He synopsized that a letter was sent to Mr. Adams in October of 2017 regarding his outstanding bills
from past billing cycles in the amount of $3,441.57. He said that the property has an irrigation system
and that the billing review from November of 2015 reflects the cycle of larger billing for the summer
period and smaller during the winter. The one larger reading during one summer was due to an
unusually hot and dry extended summer where they also took water from New London for a longer
period due to the extended dry spell. As they did not hear from Mr. Adams in October, another letter
was sent and he requested to come before the Commission this evening regarding this dispute. They
have been out to check the meter and have not found any issues with it.

Mr. Seery asked that the Commissioners ask any questions that they might have.
Mr. Zoller asked if the irrigation system is turned off for the winter and then back on for the summer as
the billing does show a consistent pattern. He suggested that the irrigation system might have a leak.

Mr. Adams said that he had the system turned off for the winter and has had the system checked by the
people who installed it and they said that there is no leak; the billing does not make sense to him.
Neither he nor his family spend a lot of time there and as such do not feel that they use a lot of water.
He passed out a timeline to the Commissioners of his calls to the office and results from those calls.

= Niantic Pump Station Emergency Upgrades
Mr. Kargl reported that the bypass pumps are still out there.

Mr. Jacques asked if the control valves are here yet.

are concerned with the delay. FW Webb Co. said that the pipe company had a manufacturing delay and
while they were supposed to be here in 14 — 16 weeks, it was 22 weeks. They scheduled construction
to coordinate with the 14-16 weeks and when they did not arrive they had to go on shutdown - it is
unexpected that there was almost an 8 week delay. Currently the week of June 3, 2018 is the
