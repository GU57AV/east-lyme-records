EAST LYME WATER & SEWER COMMISSION
PUBLIC HEARING I
Tuesday, OCTOBER 23rd, 2018
MINUTES

The East Lyme Water & Sewer Commission held a Public Hearing on October 23, 2018 at Town Hall,
108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut to hear comments regarding proposed revisions to rates
and charges for connection to and the use of the East Lyme Water System.

PRESENT: Kevin Seery, Acting Chairman, Dave Bond, Steve DiGiovanna, Dave
Murphy, Joe Mingo, Carol Russell, Roger Spencer, Dave Zoller

ALSO PRESENT: Joe Bragaw, Public Works Director
Brad Kargl, Municipal Utility Engineer
Anna Johnson, Finance Director

ABSENT: Mark Nickerson, Chairman, Dave Jacques

Pledge of Allegiance
The Pledge was observed.

Acting Chairman Seery called this Public Hearing on the proposed Water rates to order at 6:48 PM. He
noted that the notice had been published in the local newspaper, The Day on October 12, 2018. (Copy
Attached)

Mr. Seery asked if there were any members of the public who wished to comment —

Harold Storrs, 70 Giants Neck Road asked about the $25 meter fee and if it was a flat fee and, if itis a
flat fee, why not put it into the rate structure as opposed to being a separate fee. He also asked if the fee
is the same for all size meters.

Mr. Bragaw said that it is a fixed cost and the same for each customer. They did it this way as the $25 is
a meter fee. It is charged two times per year at $12.50 per billing period.

Mr. Seery asked if there were any other comments —
Hearing none —
