1. Call to Order
Chairman Nickerson called this Special Meeting of the Water & Sewer Commission to order

at 6 PM.

2. Executive Session — Pending Litigation — Landmark Sewer Appeal

**MOTION (1)

Mr. DiGiovanna moved to enter Executive Session at 6:05 PM to discuss Pending
Litigation on the Landmark Sewer appeal inviting Attorney O’Connell, Attorney
Zamarka, Joe Bragaw, Brad Kargl and Kevin Seery to join them.

Mr. Murphy seconded the motion.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

**MOTION (2)

Mr. DiGiovanna moved to exit Executive Session at 7:04 PM and return to Special

Session with no action taken in Executive Session.
Mr. Spencer seconded the motion. |
Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

3. Approval of Capital Funds for Valve Replacement Work on Rte 156 in Advance of
DOT Paving |

Mr. Kargl provided handouts including the project budget, site map, and capital projects

spreadsheet, (copy attached) He discussed the need for the valve replacement work to be

done prior to the DOT paving of Rte 156 which has already begun. There was discussion

regarding the project, the funding for it and the necessity to do it now as the State has

already milled that section of Rte 156.

** MOTION (3)

Ms. Russell moved to authorize up to $53,000 from the Water Construction Fund for
water valve replacement work on Route 156.

Mr. DiGiovanna seconded the motion.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

4. Set Special Meeting Date For Water and Sewer Rates Discussion

Water & Sewer Commission Special Meeting Minutes September 18, 2018 4
Mr. Bragaw recalled that they are coming upon the time for the Public Hearings to be held on
the rates for the upcoming year. He suggested two potential dates to have the Special
Meeting to discuss what rate increases would be necessary. It was the consensus of the
Commissioners to set a Special Meeting date of October 9, 2018 at 6 PM at the Town Hall to
discuss the water and sewer rates.

§. ADJOURNMENT
Mr. Nickerson called for a motion to adjourn.

**MOTION (4)

Mr. DiGiovanna moved to adjourn the September 18, 2018 Special Meeting of the
Water & Sewer Commission at 7:16 PM.

Mr. Jacques seconded the motion.

Vote: 8-0-0. Motion passed.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary
