1. Call to Order/ Pledge of Allegiance

Mr. North called the meeting to order at 1.0:00AM. The Pledge was observed

2. Discussion on Sewer Capacity

Mr. North outlined the current data on remaining sewer capacity. He noted that there has been an increase
in sewer usage throughout town that does not have a seasonal correlation. Due to new developments in
town, the average sewer daily flow from the town and state facilities is roughly 1 million gallons per day
(MGD), out of the total 1.5 MGD allotted to the town and state per the agreement with New London and
Waterford. Mrs. Russell pointed out that the typical five - year lookback period on sewer flows should be
discontinued in favor of using a two -year lookback, which would give more insight into flow trends that
the sewer department is experiencing.
Mrs. Russell and Mr. North outlined the sewer projects approved for development that have not yet been
constructed and identified that it looks like the Town will need to secure additional sewer capacity at the
New London WWTF.

3. Discussion on Point O'Woods Agreement
A review of the Point O'Woods Agreement was conducted to identify the sewerflows from Point O'Woods
and how they factored into the remaining sewer capacity. Mr. North commented that the agreement
identifies State of Connecticut flows that were loaned to East Lyme to allow for Point O' Woods sewer
flows. Additionally, any request for additional capacity should included provisions for taking Point O'
Woods flows back from the state, as the agreement stipulates that any additional capacity that East Lyme
receives could allow the state to have its loaned capacity back.

4. Discussion on Options for Obtaining Additional Capacity
Mr. Bragaw and Mr. North laid out some scenarios of the logistics of obtaining capacity. lt was discussed
that East Lyme should look for capacity from both Towns and the State.

5. Discussion on Timetables for Recommended Action
Mr. Bragaw recommended that this subcommittee set a goal of the end of the year to determine which
avenue makes the most sense for obtaining capacity.
