1. Application of North Road Properties/Wave Construction Group for a Special Permit, for a
contractor/trade service facility at property identified in the application as 7 Capital Drive,
East Lyme Assessor’s Map 09.0 lot 22.
Mr. Walker noted for the record that a legal advertisement was run on this item.

Mr. McPherson read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

The applicant, David Keatley of 54 Niantic River Road, Waterford came forward to answer any questions
the Commission had. He stated that the Application directly reflects their intended use for the property.

Mr. Donovan asked what would be stored in the building and Mr. Keatley said that it will mostly be
industrial welding equipment and clarified that no hazardous waste will be stored on the premises.

Mr. Walker stated that the application looks straightforward.
Mr. McPherson asked if there would be any stockpiling of material outside and Mr. Keatley said no.
There were no public comments in favor, against, or neutral.
e Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.
Mr. Donovan seconded the motion.
Vote: 6-0-0. Motion passed.

Mr. Peck rejoined the Commission. Mr. Lukas returned to the audience.

2. Application of the East Lyme Parks & Recreation Department for placement of a 32’x32’
gazebo at Bride Brook Park, Niantic.

Mr. Walker noted for the record that a legal advertisement was run on this item.
Mr. McPherson read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Parks & Rec employee Mike McDowell of 114 Giants Neck Road, came forward to speak on behalf of
Parks & Rec Director Dave Putnam.

Mr. McDowell pointed out the lightning protection kit that will accompany the gazebo.

Mr. McPherson asked if the gazebo will have lights, Mr. McDowell said not initially but if desired, they
could be added at a later date.

2|Page
Mr. Kane asked if there will be picnic tables and Mr. McDowell said yes.

Mr. Terrance asked what the gazebo will be made of and Mr. McDowell explained that it will be a steel
construction maroon in color with grey asphalt shingles over a concrete pad foundation.

Mr. Peck expressed concern over wind velocity questioning what the gazebo is rated for. Mr. McDowell
said that he could check with Mr. Putnam. Mr. McPherson observed that if the gazebo was anchored
properly it should be fine. Mr. Peck stated that the gazebo appears light and the roof looks like it could
be a “wing” given the right wind conditions.

There were no public comments in favor, against, or neutral.

e Motion (2) Mr. Peck moved to close the Public Hearing.
Mr. Dwyer seconded the motion.
Vote: 6-0-0. Motion passed.

3. Application of Steve Carpenteri for Niantic Bay Inn, Inc, (aka Lyme Tavern), for renewal of a
Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at 229 W Main Street, Niantic.

Mr. Walker noted for the record that a legal advertisement was run on this item.

Mr. Walker stated that he did not think it was necessary for Mr. Carpenteri to do a presentation since it
is a renewal. Mr. Carpenteri said nothing has changed.

There were no public comments in favor, against, or neutral.

Mr. Walker stated that Mr. Mulholland told him that there have been no complaints or issues on this
permit.

Mr. Kane stated that he wanted to go on the record that is the one application for outside dining that he
always sees a sign for; whatever conditions the Commission requires of the applicant they are always
met. This is a good example for all.

e Motion (3) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.
Mr. Dwyer seconded the motion.
Vote: 6-0-0. Motion passed.
