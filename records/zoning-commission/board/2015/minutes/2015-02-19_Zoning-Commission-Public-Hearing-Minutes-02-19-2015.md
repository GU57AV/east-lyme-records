CALL TO ORDER

Chairman Walker called the Public Hearing to order at 7:32 p.m.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

Chairman Walker led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS

There were no public delegations.

1, APPLICATION OF NORTH ROAD PROPERTIES/WAVE CONSTRUCTION GROUP FOR A SPECIAL

PERMIT APPLICATION, SITE PLAN AND STORM WATER REVIEW FOR CONSTRUCTION OF A
9,500 SF+ BUILDING FOR OFFICE AND INDUSTRIAL USE AND ASSOCIATED FACILITIES AT

PROPERTY IDENTIFIED IN THE APPLICATION AS 5 LIBERTY WAY, EAST LYME ASSESSOR'S MAP
09.4, LOT 11.

Mr. Mulholland stated he received an email this afternoon from this applicant that his application has
been withdrawn.

2. CONTINUATION OF THE EAST LYME ZONING COMMISSION PROPOSAL TO AMEND THE EAST
LYME ZONING REGULATIONS SECTIONS 1 THROUGH 33, INDEX, APPENDICES AND REVISION
RECORD FOR MISCELLANEOUS HOUSEKEEPING ITEMS AND RE-NUMBERING.

Chairman Walker confirmed the legal advertisement ran on this item.

Mr. McPherson read a letter from the East Lyme Planning Commission.

Mr. McPherson read a letter from James Rabbitt of the SCCOG Regional Planning Commission.
Mr. McPherson read a letter from Marcy Balint of DEEP.

Mr. Mulholland stated the Commission has reviewed the revisions to the Regulations. The sub-
committee was Mr. Peck, Mr. Donovan, Chairman Walker, and Mr. Salerno.

Mr. Mulholland reviewed all of the revisions to the Regulations.

During Mr. Mulholland’s review the only change that the Commission asked for was in Section 24, it
should read 6 feet, and not “free”.

There was no public comment.

Mr. McPherson stated the sub-committee did a good job.

Chairman Walker commended the sub-committee, it was a time consuming job and they used a lot of
attention to detail while reviewing the Regulations. He also commended Jennifer in the Zoning
Department for doing a great job.

Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.

Seconded by Mr. Donovan.

Motion Passed 6-0.
