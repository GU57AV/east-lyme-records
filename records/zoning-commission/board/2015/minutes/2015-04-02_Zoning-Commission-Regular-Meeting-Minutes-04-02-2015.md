1. REQUEST OF CELLCO PARTNERSHIP DBA VERIZON WIRELESS FOR A SITE PLAN REVIEW FORA
SMALL CELL ANTENNA AT 314 FLANDERS ROAD, EAST LYME

Mr. McPherson read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.
Joey Lee Miranda, of Robinson and Cole, 280 Trumbull Street, Hartford stated they are seeking to install
one antenna in the cupola of the Liberty Bank building. They are proposing to increase the height of the
cupola by one foot. It will blend in with the existing architecture.

Doug Drosk of Centek Engineering, 63-2 North Branford Road, Branford stated the radio equipment will
be in the basement, the antenna will be in the cupola. The cupola will have the same architecture and
appearance as the existing one.

Mr. McPherson asked if this is for the bank’s use, or for all of Verizon customers.
Ms. Miranda stated this will increase capacity for Verizon customers.

Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to approve the Request of Cellco Partnership dba Verizon
Wireless for a Site Plan Review for a Small Cell Antenna at 314 Flanders Road, East Lyme.

Part One: Raise the Cupola on 314 Flanders Road
Part Two: Install a small cell wireless telecommunications antenna within the cupola.

Seconded by Mr. Dwyer.
Motion Passed 6-0.

2. REQUEST OF KYLE HAUBERT, PE OF CLA ENGINEERS, LLC FOR NORWICH ORTHOPEDIC GROUP,
PC FOR A SITE PLAN REVIEW FOR CONSTRUCTION OF A MEDICAL OFFICE COMPLEX AND
ASSOCIATED PARKING LOT AT PROPERTY LOCATED AT 11 INDUSTRIAL PARK ROAD, EAST LYME
ASSESSOR’S MAP 26.1, LOT 3

Mr. McPherson read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.
Mr. McPherson read a memo from Joe Smith, Building Official.

Kyle Haubert, of CLA Engineers, 317 Main Street, Norwich presented the request on behalf of Norwich
Orthopedic Group.

Mr. Haubert stated that they are proposing a 30,477 square foot office building at 11 Industrial Park
Road. Itis a light industrial zone. They are proposing 135 parking spaces, and the requirement is 122.
Norwich Orthopedic Group will occupy half of the building. Almost the whole perimeter of the building
has ADA spaces, and there will be patient drop off in the front. There will be rain gardens around the
building. The surface water will drain to a basin at the lowest spot on the site. They will use silt fence,
hay bales, and anti-tracking pads. They provided a pollution prevention plan. They are requesting a
waiver of the frontage sidewalk. The crossing at the nearby bridge over the river is very narrow, based
on safety they are asking for the waiver. If the bridge is rebuilt or pedestrian access is built on the
bridge they then would build a sidewalk.

Bill Chapman, of BBL Medical Facilities or 302 Washington Avenue Extension, Albany, New York is the
architect on the project. They will use manufactured stone at the base, and above that will be brick.

Mr. McPherson asked how far they are from the highway.

2
Mr. Haubert stated they are approximately 20 feet from the right of way.

Mr. Donovan asked about fire protection.

Mr. Chapman stated they would use a sprinkler system.

Mr. McPherson asked about the placement of the generator.

Mr. Haubert stated it has been relocated and will be screened with evergreen.
Mr. Peck stated he would like to see 2 Norway Spruce added to the plantings.
Chairman Walker stated it is located in the aquifer protection area.

Mr. Haubert stated all water will be treated in the retention basin. They are providing water quality
measures. They also use a contractor for disposal of waste.

Mr. Donovan stated they should only use regular salt for plowing, they can’t use chemicals.
Mr. Dwyer stated this is not an area that people walk around.

Mr. Donovan stated they are willing to add a sidewalk if they do rebuild the bridge or make a pedestrian
walkway on the bridge.

Mr. Dwyer asked if there was just one entrance and exit for parking lot.

Mr. Haubert stated yes.

Chairman Walker stated it is a great looking building and an excellent addition to the Town.
Mr. Dwyer asked about their signs.

Mr. Chapman stated they have discussed signs with Mr. Mulholland. They have discussed a monument
sign at the driveway.

Motion (2) Mr. Peck moved to approve the request of Kyle Haubert, PE of CLA Engineers, LLC for
Norwich Orthopedic Group, PC for a Site Plan Review for construction of a Medical Office Complex

and associated parking lot at property located at 11 Industrial Park Road, East Lyme Assessor’s Map
26.1, Lot 3. With the following conditions:

1. Sidewalk waiver approved;
2. Two Additional Norway Spruce between the building and I-95.

Seconded by Mr. McPherson.

Motion Passed 6-0.
