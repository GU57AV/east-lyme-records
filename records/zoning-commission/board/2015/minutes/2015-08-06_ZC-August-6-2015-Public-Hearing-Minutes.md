1. APPLICATION OF THE TOWN OF EAST LYME PARKS AND RECREATION FOR AN ELECTRONIC

MESSAGE BOARD AT THE EAST LYME TOWN HALL, 108 PENNSYLVANIA AVENUE, NIANTIC.

Chairman Walker confirmed the legal advertisement ran on this item.

Mr. McPherson read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Are
Dave Putnam, Director of Parks and Recreation and a member of the Niantic Rotary stated the Rotary
has donated $15,000 toward this electronic message board. They thought the Town Hall would be an
appropriate location. He has worked with John Wilson of Sign Craft with the design.

Mr. Mulholland stated the location is staked in front of the Town Hall.

Mr. Putnam stated they would like to move the State of Connecticut sign to the island in the parking lot.
The electronic message board would be to the right of the sidewalk as you leave the First Selectman’s
Office.

Mr. Mulholland stated they are welcome to go out and look at the location.

Chairman Walker stated he didn’t feel they needed to go out, and the Commission concurred.

Mr. Mulholland stated the center of the message board will be the electronic board, the top will be a
fixed sign, and the only active lighting will be in the center. The Regulations address the frequency of
the brightness level, and the changeable copy.

Chairman Walker asked Mr. Mulholland if this conforms will all of the Regulation’s criteria.

Mr. Putnam stated it will be to advertise for non-profit events. The Parks and Recreation Commission
would like to get rid of the sandwich boards on the Town Green.

Chairman Walker asked about the words per message.

Mr. Putnam stated each screen has to stay up at least 10 seconds. There will be no scrolling or flashing.
They would still do banners on the Town Green, but no sandwich boards. This could also be used for
emergencies and road closures.

Chairman Walker stated this is an impressive donation by the Rotary.

John Wilson of Sign Craft, 5 Black Point Road, Niantic, stated this will fit in with the aesthetics of the
Town and will get rid of the sandwich boards on the green. The top section will be curved. The
electronic display will have a burgundy background. The changeable copy can be made to sleep.

Mr. Donovan asked if it can be dimmed at night.

Mr. Wilson stated there is an automatic dimming feature. The electronic display will be burgundy with
white letters. Everything is adjustable, it can be controlled from the 911 center, and they can wake up
the sign for an emergency.

Mr. Donovan asked if there would be lights on it.

Mr. Wilson stated it could potentially be lit, but they don’t think they need that from the get go.

Mr. Dwyer asked if it is two sided.

Mr. Wilson stated yes, and time and temperature can also be displayed.

2
Mr. Donovan suggested putting the Town Hall sign on top of the electronic sign instead of Community
Events.

Mr. Wilson stated the Town Hall sign is 7 feet wide and 3 feet tall. It would not fit within the
regulations.

Mr. Mulholland stated we can work on it.

Mr. Wilson stated they discussed putting pavers and benches on the island where the State sign is being
moved to.

Chairman Walker stated he thinks it would be ok with the Town Hall sign separate from this one, but he
wouldn’t be adverse to them combining them.

Mr. McPherson asked if they have coordinated the location with the Lions regarding the Art Show. It
looks like it will take up a bit of their space.

Public Comment:

Richard Kushman of 107 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, stated he lives directly across from the Town
Hall. He is not against this sign, but it is a complicated corner in the summer. Smith Street is used as a
cut through, his concern is safety. He believes there is an argument for putting it on the Town Green.
People would stop at the light and have plenty of time to read it.

Richard Gada of 77 West Main Street, Niantic, stated he is a business owner and a member of the
Rotary. They worked very hard raising this money. He thinks this will update the town, the signs can be
removed from the green, and possibly traffic may slow down.

Kristen Foster stated this will keep people informed, this will add pizazz to the town and she thinks we
need it.
