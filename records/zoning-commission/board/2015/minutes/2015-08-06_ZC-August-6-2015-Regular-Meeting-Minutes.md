2. APPLICATION OF THE TOWN OF EAST LYME PARKS AND RECREATION FOR AN ELECTRONIC
MESSAGE BOARD AT THE EAST LYME TOWN HALL, 108 PENNSYLVANIA AVENUE, NIANTIC.

Chairman Walker stated he is confident any quirks can be ironed out with this. He is not a huge
supporter of electronic signs, but he is excited that this will be tasteful.

Mr. Donovan asked about it being dimmed.
Mr. Wilson stated it can sleep at certain hours and be turned back on for emergencies. It auto dims at
sunset.

Motion (1) Chairman Walker moved to approve the application of the Town of East Lyme Parks
and Recreation for an electronic message board at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania
Avenue, Niantic. With the following condition:

1. Sleep midnight to 5:00 a.m. except for emergencies and auto dim from dusk to midnight.

4. REQUEST OF KEITH NEILSEN, FOR DOCKO, FOR COLIN DOWLING FOR A COASTAL AREA
MANAGEMENT REVIEW FOR THE RAISING AND RE-ORIENTATION OF A HISTORIC HOME TO

MEET FLOOD STANDARDS, INCLUDING RAISING THE GRADE AT PROPERTY IDENTIFIED IN THE
APPLICATION AS 235 OLD BLACK POINT ROAD, NIANTIC, CT.

Keith Neilsen of Docko, Inc stated he represents Mr. Dowling. They would like to re-orient the house
and raise the grade on the property so waves have energy taken out of them. It is clear of the coastal
jurisdiction line. They will use stone, gravel fill, topsoil, and switch grass. It will appear as a dune. The
grass has an aggressive root system. It is clear of DEEP jurisdiction. This will be a significant
improvement of the usefulness of the property. There is quite a bit of wave action at this location. This
will not change the drainage.

Mr. Donovan asked about the property on the side of this.

Mr. Neilsen stated the drainage will be diverted from other property. He also said that page 4 of his
application he should have checked “coastal hazard area on site”.

Motion (3) Mr. Donovan moved to approve the request of Keith Neilsen, for Docko, for Colin
Dowling for a Coastal Area Management Review for the raising and re-orientation of a historic home
to meet flood standards, including raising the grade, at property identified in the application as 235
Old Black Point Road, Niantic CT.

Seconded by Mr. Peck.

Motion Passed 6-0.
