1. APPLICATION OF THEODORE A. HARRIS FOR A PROPOSED REGULATION CHANGE FORA
PROPOSED BREW PUB REGULATION (LI ZONE)

Chairman Walker confirmed the legal advertisement ran on this item.

1
Mr. McPherson read a letter from James Rabbitt of SECCOG Regional Planning Commission.
Mr. McPherson read a memo from Marcy Balint of DEEP.

Chairman Walker sat Mr. Liska as a regular member for the Public Hearing and the Regular Meeting
immediately following.

Mr. McPherson read a letter from the East Lyme Planning Commission.

Attorney Theodore Harris stated this is a national trend and this Commission recently enacted a
Regulation for the Commercial Zone. This application is for the LI Zone. Groton has a brew pub
similar to what is proposed. They have proposed a new definition in the definition section of the
Regulations. This would primarily be a manufacturing facility. The parking proposal is a standard for
brew pubs. There are limited hours. A lot of the business would involve buying and consumption
off site. It is low impact in terms of the number of people. Also, this doesn’t propose to relax the
distance, so there won’t be a multitude of these. There are restrictions on quantities in the State
Liquor Control Regulations. If the Commission feels service of anything other than beer or wine is
no appropriate they would not have anything against them limiting that.

Mr. Donovan asked if there is a limit on what they can manufacture.

Attorney Harris stated there is no specific limit, but if they exceed 5000 gallons they would need to
apply for a more expensive permit through the State.

Mr. Donovan asked how this differs from a tap room.
Attorney Harris stated this would allow the service of food. They would have other beers as well.

Mr. Mulholland stated the LI Zones are in the area of Liberty Way, Camp Niantic, Colton Road,
Industrial Park Road, and a small piece of Boston Post Road.

Mr. Mulholland suggested the members read the State Liquor Laws.

Attorney Harris handed the members subsections of the Liquor Laws. He stated they have no
problem if they want to limit this to beer and wine. He stated he can come back to the next meeting
with a revision.

Mr. Donovan stated it is too broad to approve it as written now.

Attorney Harris stated he is happy to come back with a re-draft.

Mr. Donovan stated he suggests no liquor.

Chairman Walker stated having this in the LI Zone has worked out well in Groton.

Attorney Harris stated he will sit down with his client.
Mr. Mulholland suggested they continue this without taking public comment as the new
amendment will be different.

Chairman Walker stated this item will be continued.
