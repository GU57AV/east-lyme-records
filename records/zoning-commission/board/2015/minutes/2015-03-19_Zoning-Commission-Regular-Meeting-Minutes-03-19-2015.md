Ms. Cheeseman stated there was a Special Town Meeting to approve an appropriation for WEB TRAC
Software for Parks and Recreation and the Senior Center, which will enable online registration. They

approved the Consent Calendar, they approved a Grant for the Hole in the Wall. They tabled the Zoning
Commission Alternate appointment as they had two members absent. That will be on their next
agenda. They also approved an appropriation for a new freezer at the Senior Center.
