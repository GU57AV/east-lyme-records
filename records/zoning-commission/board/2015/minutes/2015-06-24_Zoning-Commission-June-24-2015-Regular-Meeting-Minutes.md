1. APPLICATION OF TODD SAKOWSKI, TO AMEND THE EAST LYME ZONING REGULATIONS
SECTION 1.79, 9.2.10, 20.20.8 AND 25.5 TO PERMIT THE OPERATION OF A TAPROOM
BREWERY

Mr. Dwyer suggested we investigate other towns that have breweries, he did research online and there
was some information about obnoxious odors. He would like to find out more.

Mr. Donovan stated the applicant mentioned the odor from spent grain, and that would be taken away
daily from Cranberry Meadow Farm.

Mr. Dwyer stated he would like to look at the zoning regulations in other towns.
Mr. Donovan stated they would have to come for a Special Permit anyway.

Mr. Mulholland stated the next meeting is July 9", the applicant can provide us with additional
information, and the Commission could possibly go visit other breweries.

Mr. Peck stated the alternate would be a one year permit.

Mr. Mulholland stated the one year permit creates anxiety and risk to the applicant. This is a big
financial commitment.
Mr. Donovan stated he understands the concerns about odor. He would like to get more information
and see the final re-wording of the proposed amendment.

Mr. Mulholland stated he can sit down with the applicant and this can be continued to the July 9"
meeting. He wants to move forward in September. We could possibly have a Special Meeting to help
him out.

Chairman Walker stated this is a unique application and we want to do our due diligence. We will
continue this to the July 9" meeting.

2. APPLICATION OF JULIE SMART, FOR SMARTY’S, FOR A SPECIAL PERMIT FOR OUTDOOR DINING
AT PROPERTY IDENTIFIED IN THE APPLICATION AS 215 MAIN STREET, NIANTIC

Chairman Walker stated this is an inviting atmosphere.
Mr. Donovan stated the landlord won't let him go past 10:00 p.m.

Motion (1) Mr. Donovan moved to approve the application of Julie Smart, for Smarty’s, for a
Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at property identified in the application as 215 Main Street, Niantic
with the following conditions:

1. Light amplification music;
2. Entertainment not to exceed 10:00 p.m. on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays of long holiday
weekends.
Seconded by Mr. McPherson.
Motion Passed 5-0.
