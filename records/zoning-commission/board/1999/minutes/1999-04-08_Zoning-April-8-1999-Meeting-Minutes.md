                    2. Oswegathchie Hills 3:5 Acre Zoning
                            Mr. Formica introduced and recognized Meg Parulis, Land Use Coordinator.
                    Mrs. Parulis reviewed in detail the informational packet distributed to the Commission.
                    Included was a basic outline and summary of what the regulations were intended to
                    accomplish. She noted that there are three groups of regulation changes being
                    examined, including the creation of the Greenway Conservation District - up-zoning of
                    what is presently now zoned as RU120, cluster development regulation changes and
                    minimal area buildable land.
                            It was the consensus of the Commission to continue discussion at the next
                    Regular Meeting allowing the members the opportunity to examine the material in more
                    detail.
