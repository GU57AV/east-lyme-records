                  6. Liability of Members
                     As a general rule, municipal officers are protected from personal liability when acting in
                     their official capacity as members of a Board or Commission by the concept of
                     governmental immunity. If the member exercises good faith and reasonable one cannot be
                     subject to any damages. The exceptions are willful misconduct and malice.

           Mr. Weber inquired as to the role of the Alternates during Public Hearings

           Mr. O'Connell indicated that the Alternates sitting in the audience cannot ask questions during the
           Public Hearing. The altemates can listen and consider but not comment or be active participants.
           During the decision making process, only the decision-makers may discuss. An Alternate or
           Member not present at a Public Hearing must review the evidence and listen to the tapes of the
           Public Hearing in order to be familiar with it and must declare to that fact.
