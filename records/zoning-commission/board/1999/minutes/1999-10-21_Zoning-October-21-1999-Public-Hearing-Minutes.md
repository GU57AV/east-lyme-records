The East Lyme Zonng Commission held a Public Hearing and Regular Meeting on September 9,1999 at
Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, Connecticut 06357. Paul Formica, Chairman, called the
Public Hearing to order at 7 :31 PM and the Regular Meeting to order at 7 :43 PM.

PRESENT: Paul Formica, Chairman, Norman Peck, Daniel Price, Shawn Mclaughlin, William Weber,
and Alternates Robert Bulmer and William Dwyer.
Also Present: William Mulholland, Zoning Offrcer.
Absent: Athena Cone, Rose Ann Hardy, ex-officio

After opening the meeting, Mr. Formica introduced the Commission Members andZoningOfficer. He
indicated that Mr. Bulmer and Mr. Dwyer will sit this evening for Mrs. Cone and Mr. Peck who is siuing
out due to a conflict. Mr. Formica lead the meeting in the Pledge of Allegiance.

PUBLIC HEARING

Application of Robert D. Tobin, agent for West Hill, LLC, for a Special Permit to construct 88 units
of Elderly Housing at237 F'landers Road (rear).

 Mr. Formica noted the Legal Ad was published in the New London Day on October 8th and October 19th,
       t999.
 Mr. Mulholland noted that a sign was posted as required by the Regulations.

 Mr. Formica reviewed the Public Hearing Procedure. He indicated that a Public Hearing can be held open
       for a period of 30 days and the Commission has 65 days to make a decision.

Robert Tobin, attorney representing West Hills, LLC and Benjamin Orvedal addressed the Commission.
      The application is for a Special Permit to construct 88 units of Elderly Housing. Mr. Tobin
      indicated that the applicant has met with Mr. Mulholland and Staff. As a result of those meetings,
      there are going to be some technical modifications to the plans. Mr. Tobin requested the Public
      Hearing be continued to November 4,1999.

Mr. Mulholland concurred.
Mr. Formica indicated that upon request of the applicant and Staff, the Public Hearing is continued to
      November 4,1999.
