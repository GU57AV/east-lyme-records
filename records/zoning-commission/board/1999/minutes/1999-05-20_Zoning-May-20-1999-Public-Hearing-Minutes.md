Mr. Formica introduced Attorney Robert Tobin who addressed the Commission.

Attorney Robert Tobin, representing in the applicant West Hill, LLC, addressed the Commission and public.
The project is a 15 unit, first phase of the development of this particular site. Mr. Tobin introduced Joanne
Carol who presented a slide review of similar developments including Chapman Farms and the status of
Elderly Housing. She indicated that 22.5% of the population if over age 55 years and the housing options are
limited. The homes designed for elderly produce tax revenues yet have no impact on the schools. The homes
are designed for single level living.

Matthew White, P.E., Angus McDonaldlGary Sharpe & Associates then addressed the Commission and
public. He presented an aerial photo (exhibit 1) of the l0-acre parcel that has an 80 foot frontage on Rte. 161
and is accessible via the road to the Best Westem Motel which is located north of the subject site. He pointed
out the 2}-acre parcel east of the site for eventual build out.

Mr. White presented the Site Layout Plan (exhibit 2) revised May 6,1999. The development consists of 2
clusters; 7 units on the north and 8 units on the south side of the proposed road. The homes will be Cape-
style, 3-4 units attached with a storage/utility connector unit. Unit separation will be 15" (Regulation requires
24') and the applicant is requesting a waiver. Each unit has garage space some having2-bays. Parking spaces
would be approximately 3 per unit. Public water and public sewer will serve the project. The applicant has a
20-foot easement down to Rte. 161 and will construct a sewer line to the existing sewer line. There will be a
constructed wetland biofilter/detention pond on the site. This will serve as a filtration system for storm water
run-off.

Mr. White then presented the Site Development Plan (exhibit 3). Sidewalks will be oonstruoted within the 80'
frontage on Rte. 161. The proposed sidewalk will be connected to the site by traversing the daycare center
property. The sidewalk will cross the motel access road and continue into the development. The clusters,
however, will not have sidewalks. Conservation Commission has approved the sidewalk, however, Mr.
Orvedal does have an application before the Commission to do some additional landscaping associated with
the sidewalk. Also proposed are walking trails through the wooded areas on the site. In response to Mr.
Dwyer's questions, Mr. White indicated that the power lines from Millstone runs west to east along the south
boundary of the site and demonstrated on the Site Development Plan. He added they were not high power
tension lines, but rather wooden poles similar to street poles.

Mr. Tobin introduced David Sullivan of Barkin & Mess Associates who had conducted atrafftc survey. Mr.
Tobin did point out that the applicant is not required to do a traffic survey as part of the application process.
Mr. Sullivan studied the existing conditions at the subject intersection on Rrte. 161, updated traffic counts, site
lines and other safety related issues of the access. Also studied was the proposed impact of the development
on traffic and finally estimated traffic conditions during the summer. On a per unit basis it was estimated that
this development could generate on average .2-.4 trips per unit during a peak-hour (one of the hours between
7-9AM and 4-6PM). A single-family development would generate 1.0 trips during a peak hour. This project

Mr. Tobin introduced Mr. Benjamin Orvedal who reviewed the housing design and landscaping. Houses will
be Cape-style (7 optional designs), some having 2-bay garages, and one-level living. All homes will have full

between the buildings are in compliance with the Zoning Regulations for CA District-Elderly Housing and
will serve as common storage/utility area. He displayed drawings of the various designs of the connectors and
homes.

Mr. Orvedal described the proposed landscaping indicating that the majority of vegetation will remain. He

modifications to the Architectural Plans previously submitted - the patios have been enlarged fuom 12 x 12 to

years.

encourage saving trees on the site and aplanned planting program. The program provides national recognition
to the developer and the Town.
