     wetlands that are 50 ft. northeast of the proposed dwelling. The site does not contain any

     indirectly impact the coastal resource wetlands to the northeast of the site.           b
     Mr. Mulholland expressed his opinion that the applicant has demonstrated compliance
     with all the applicable policies and goals of CAM.

     Mr. Mulholland added that typically single family houses are exempt from Coastal
     Management review unless they are within 100 feet of certain coastal resources. This
     property is within that 100-foot limit.

     Mr. David Coonrod, Land Surveyor presented the plans to the Uommission. The
     property has municipal sewer and water to the site. He pointed out the tidal wetlands
     location on the plans. Due to the fact that a portion of the foundation falls within the
     flood plan area, there will be no grading in the rear of the house and there will not be a
     full cellar, but a slab with no crawl space. Silk barriers will be in place during and for a
     time after construction to maintain and stabilize the area. Construction should be
     completed in approximately 6 months. Excess material will be removed from the site.
     All disturbed areas will be re-seeded and mulched. There is no impact on coastal
     resources with the proposed plan.

     Mr. Mullaney inquired if the building was above the 12 foot above high water level.

     Mr. Coonrod stated that the flood plan area is l0 ft. and all utilities have to be above 10
     ft. and the finished floor will be at 12 ft.
