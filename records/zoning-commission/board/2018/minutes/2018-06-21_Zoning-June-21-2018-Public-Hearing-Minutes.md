Topic A Frames for advertising
Richard Waterman, President ELPPI,
1am here by the request of a selectman and a member of the Zoning Board
| wish to talk to you about the use of A frames for advertising.

The ELPPI is a non-for profit organization which brings puppet shows for the
people of East Lyme and surrounding towns. Our shows are family oriented and
attract not only families but people of all ages and handicaps. At present our
shows attract at least 100 people. Shows are held at the East Lyme Community
Center. Our expenses are rental fees, advertising, insurance, and the cost of
shows. Our income comes from ticket sales, grants , and donations from
businesses, individuals and other community organizations such as the Rotary .

We have done over 12 shows in the past 4 years, conducted workshops in East
Lyme Elementary schools and after some shows. We have a planned workshop for
this coming fall. Our latest large activity will be the production of our first puppet
show, “We the People”, a story of the American Revolutionary War
andConnecticut men such as David Bushnell of Saybrook and Ezra Lee of Lyme.
This is the story of America’s first submarine the “Turtle”. It is a fascinating story
of the attack upon the British fleet in 1776 and an episode of the sinking of a
privateer ship in Niantic Bay in 1777. Its location us presently unkown. We have
received a $2500 grant to start up this project and we are preparing a puppetry
show to support its development.

Now, our interest in the “A Frame” . We have 2 A frames for our present show.
One is in the foyer of the East Lyme Library. The other is in the Chelsea Groton
Bank. Your ordinance on A Frames make it illegal to display them in public spaces.
Yet they are found all over town. Some are put up by non-profits and others by
businesses who want to bring customers into their stores. You are not enforcing
your ordinance. It is my opinion that either you enforce it or get rid of it. A
frames are important for advertising. They bring customers into stores and to
various town activities sponsored by non-profits such as the Lions Club and the
East Lyme Historical Society. | believe you should take action on your present
ordinance. Be a friend to businesses and non-for profit organizations who work to
make us a better town.

Pass out flyers.
