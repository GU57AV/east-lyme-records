2. Application of Timothy O’Reilly, Owner, East Lyme Cafe, LLC dba
Smokey O’Grady’s, for a Renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining
‘at property identified in the application as 306 Flanders Road, Niantic.

MOTION (1)
Mr. McPherson moved to approve the Application of Timothy O’Reilly, Owner, East Lyme
Cafe, LLC dba Smokey O’Grady’s, for a Renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining
at property identified in the application as 306 Flanders Road, Niantic

Ms. Kalajainen seconded the motion.

Motion carried, 6-0-0.

Ms. Kalajainen began a brief discussion regarding consistency verses viewing sites individually
when make choices regarding outside dining. Mr. Peck pointed out the Village is a sensitive
area as opposed to Flanders Road, and has different concerns. Mr. Mulholland noted he has no
complaints regarding outside dining on Flanders Road; he has however, received complaints

about outside dining in the Village. Mr. Mulholland stated that sites are unique and should be
considered as such.

Ms. Kalajainen said it sounds like each application should be addressed separately. Mr.
Donovan said they already approved the applications from last month as a group. Mr. Manning
asked if the same conditions exist for Smarty’s and the Black Sheep and was informed that the
same conditions do not exist. Mr. Walker clarified that the applications from last month were
taken together merely because no one had any issues with the renewals. Ms. Kalajainen
explained she meant looking at each permit individually. Mr. Mulholland said referrals can be
taken together when there are no issues.

3. Application of Leo Roche, for Strive LLC, for a Renewal of a Special Permit
for Outdoor Dining at property identified in the application as 247-2 Main Street,
Niantic.

Mr. Peck said the Applicant is doing his best to rectify the wall situation. Mr. Donovan said he is
opposed to reinstating the sitting at a table condition. Mr. Manning said he agrees; if the
condition doesn't exist for Smarty’s, it shouldn't for the Black Sheep. Ms. Kalajainen suggested
amending the condition so it said “seated at a table or for a special event.” Mr. Mulholland said
he would like input regarding the fence around the wall, and Mr. Roche said he would have no
problem with that. Mr. McPherson suggested including the comment that this condition doesn't
apply during special events.

MOTION (2)

Mr. McPherson moved to approve the Application of Strive LLC, for a Renewal of a
Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at property identified in the application as 247-2 Main
Street,

Niantic, with the previously approved conditions, and with the additional condition that
patrons must be seated at a table when consuming alcoholic beverages; this condition
does not apply during special events.

Ms. Kalajainen seconded the motion.

Motion carried, 5-0-1.

Mr. Manning abstained from the vote.
