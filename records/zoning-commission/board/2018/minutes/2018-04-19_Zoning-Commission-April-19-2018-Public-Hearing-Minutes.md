Public Hearing:

Chairman Walker suggested opening items 1-6 all at once since they are all renewals
for outside dining.

1. Application of Steve Carpenteri for Niantic bay Inn, Inc. (aka Lyme Tavern), for
renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at 229 W Main Street, Niantic.

2. Application of Julie Smart, for Smarty's, for renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor
Dining at property identified in the application as 215 Main Street, Niantic.

6. Application of Lionheart for a Renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at
property identified in the application as 267-283 Main Street, (aka 13 Hope Street),

Chairman Walker read each public hearing and opened them all together.

Mr. Mulholland noted for the record that there are no zoning issues with any of these renewals
and there have been no complaints. He further noted that all required legal advertisements
were published accordingly.

Chairman Walker invited the applicants present to speak if they chose. There were none.
Chairman Walker invited the commission members to ask questions. There were none.
Chairman Walker invited the public to speak for, against or neutral and no one chose to speak.

Chairman Walker called for a motion.

MOTION (1)
George McPherson moved to close the Public Hearings for these applications.

Kimberly Kalajainen seconded the motion.

Motion passed 6-0-0.

The public hearing was closed at 7:34 p.m.
