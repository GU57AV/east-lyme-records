Chairman Walker called the Zoning Commission meeting to order at 7:38 p.m.

The Commission sat Mr. Dwyer and Mr. Liska as Regular Members for the evening.

Pledge of Allegiance
Mr. Walker led the Pledge.

Public Hearing-
1. Application of Mostowy Towers LLC, for a Special Permit for the construction and

operation of a communications tower facility at 16 Mostowy Road, East Lyme,
Connecticut. East Lyme Assessor’s Map 40.0 Lot 5.

Mr. Walker noted the legal ad published, for the record.

Mr. Mulholland explained this is a public/private project and the Town will be leasing antenna
tower; the tower will be on Town land. He said he has also discussed the project with the Town
Attorney who has worked on the leasing agreement.

Chris Hack of Marcus Communications and Fire Marshall Chris Taylor came forward to discuss
the Application and reviewed the following with the Commission:

« The tower will run completely on solar power with no electricity or telephone poles.

e Solar panels will be 63 which is the standard size.

« The tower will be galvanized.

« There will be a concrete structure for securing the equipment.
Ms. Kalajainen seconded the motion.
Motion carried, 6-0-0.

Regular Meeting-
1. Application of Mostowy Towers LLC, for a Special Permit for the construction and

operation of a communications tower facility at 16 Mostowy Road, East Lyme,
Connecticut. East Lyme Assessor’s Map 40.0 Lot 5.

Mr. Donovan said he would only be concerned with this application if they didn’t have to come
before Zoning again for the addition of cellular.

Mr. Walker said this is a unique application because of the combination of a public and private
partnership. He said the fact that there are no neighbors here tonight is compelling.

Mr. Donovan observed this is a much needed safety measure.
Mr. Walker agreed there should not be dead zones in the rural areas of our Town.

MOTION (1)

Mr. Liska moved to approve the Application of Mostowy Towers LLC, for a Special Permit
for the construction and operation of a communications tower facility at 16 Mostowy
Road, East Lyme, Connecticut. East Lyme Assessor’s Map 40.0 Lot 5.

Mr. Dwyer seconded the motion.

Motion carried, 6-0-0.

2. 2019 Zoning Commission Meeting Schedule
Mr. Mulholland suggested the Commission review the schedule and rule on it at the next
meeting.

3. Approval of Minutes of October 18th, 2018.
Mr. Peck said towards the bottom of page 2 it says:
“Mr. Peck said after Bob’s Discount Furniture went in that zone was eliminated- there is no more
shopping centre in the industrial area and that in and of itself will reduce traffic.”
It should read “Mr. Peck said after Bob’s Discount Furniture went in, that use was eliminated-
shopping centres are no longer allowed in the industrial zone.”

MOTION (2)
Mr. Liska moved to approve the Meeting Minutes of October 18th, 2018 as amended.
Mr. Donovan seconded the motion.

Motion carried, 5-0-1.

Ms. Kalajainen abstained from the vote due to her absence from the October 18th, 2018
meeting.

Old Business-

1. Subcommittee-Mixed Use in CB Zone
Mr. Mulholland said he is working on this.
3. Zoning Official
Mr. Mulholland said the Midway Mall Plaza has been bought and will be getting a
facelift, the new Hope Street condos are almost done, and he’s been battling with
Dollar General about their catch basin.

4. Comments from Ex-Officio
Ms. Hardy was not in attendance.

5. Comments from Zoning Board Liaison to Planning Commission
Mr. Liska gave his report at the previous Zoning Commission meeting.
Mr. Donovan said the meeting was cancelled. Mr. Liska filled in for Ms. Kalajainen and
attended the October 2nd meeting which he briefly outlined for the Commission; the
meeting consisted of the public hearing for the Twin Valley Subdivision.

6. Comments from Chairman
There were none.

Adjournment

MOTION (3)

Mr. Donovan moved to adjourn the Zoning Commission Meeting at
8:51 p.m.

Ms. Kalajainen seconded the motion.

Motion passed, 6-0-0.

Respectfully Submitted,

Brooke Stevens
Recording Secretary
