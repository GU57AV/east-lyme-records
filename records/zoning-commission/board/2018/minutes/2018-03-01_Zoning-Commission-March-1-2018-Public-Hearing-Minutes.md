IC HEARINGS

1. Request of the Town of East Lyme for a Special Permit for the
construction of approximately 101 LF of 8’ wide transient docks connected
to the current Kayak launch floating dock, and the installation of an EZ
Kayak/Canoe launch structure at 22 Main Street, East Lyme. East Lyme
Assessor’s Map 12.2, Lot 3.

Mr. McPherson noted the legal ad published regarding this application and Substitute Secretary
Mr. Donovan read Mr. Mulholland’s memorandum dated March 1, 2018 into the record.

Mr. Mulholland said that no CAM review would be necessary; after speaking with DEEP it was
determined this is outside the purview and jurisdiction of Zoning.

Deputy Director of Public Works Bill Scheer came forward to detail this application and provided
the Commission with a mock photo of the launch.

e Mr. Scheer said this application is very straightforward and we have received the full
DEEP permit for the docks.

e The dock will be pre-built elsewhere and floated in; it should take a day or two to install
and is designed not to be removed during the winter months.

e The dock is solely for launching and there will be no storage.

Mr. Peck asked#f there would. be a.charge for.utilizing the launch. Mr. Scheer said Parks and
Recreation will have to establish rules and regulations, but initially there should be no charge.

Mr. McPherson asked about the depth and Mr. Scheer said it is 7 feet deep at the dock location.

Mr. Peck suggested they might reconsider taking the dock out during the winter and Mr.
Donovan concurred. Mr. Scheer said that is definitely a possibility since the Town owns the land
right by Boats, Inc.

MOTION (1)

Ms. Kalajainen moved to close the Public Hearing.
Mr. Donovan seconded the motion.

Motion passed, 5-0-0.

Respectfully Submitted,

Brooke Stevens
