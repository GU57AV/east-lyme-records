RE:   Mixed use development CA and CB zones

        Over the last several months I have received a number of inquiries from parties
interested in mixed-use development in our CA and CB CommercialZones. During
discussions on the subject it became apparent that the limitations of the existing
regulations were an obstacle to this type of development. Specifically, Secflon 2S.5
"Table of Minimum Controls for Specific Special Permit" provides additional regulatory
controls, which requires all mixed uses to have residential units above the business
component, which is restricted to office, and retail uses. The restriction to office and
retail can be limiting. For example you could have a small business in a stand-alone
building, which is not retail or office use, which could benefit from having second floor
apartments. Such uses as restaurants, personal service establishments, educational
uses and other uses would seem to fall into the mixed use business category.
        I would suggest that the Commission modify the controls governing mixed uses
in Section 25.5 by eliminating the restrictions to office and retail use.
        Currently this section states:

 Residential Uoits in CA ard CB      l. Residential units above first floor.
     Commercial Structure            2. Required floor area of dwellings shall not be less than
                                     MINIMUM FLOOR ARE+                  BEDROOMS
                                               EFFICIENCY                      500 square feet
                                                 ONE                           650 square feet
                                                 TWO                           800 square feet
                                     3. The business use shall be resticted to offices or retail.

       I would propose that you delete item number 3.
      Should the Commission endorse this modffication the proposal could be
scheduled for a hearing in the near future.
