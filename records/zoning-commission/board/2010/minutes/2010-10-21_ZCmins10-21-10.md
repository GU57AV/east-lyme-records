Chairman Salerno led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Brief Presentation — Incentive Housing Zones

Mr. Salerno stated that the sub-committee has been working on this. The Council of
Governments has written the regulations. They picked zones in Town they thought
would be appropriate for this Incentive Housing. They are now waiting for State
approval.

Mr. Formica presented the Incentive Housing information to the Zoning Commission,
This is a way for communities to identify parcels that make sense to locate as affordable
housing. This really makes a lot of sense for the Town. He believes these types of zones
will work. They have discussed this idea with the owner of the Midway Mall, and there
are also two other properties in Niantic, This is a very forward thinking proposal.
Mr. Bulmer asked if this was in agreement with the POCD, and he wanted to know if the
Gateway project would be included, and if this would just be an option or mandatory.

Mr. Formica stated they are trying to include locations we can get done quickly. At the
time this was being planned Gateway was on somewhat of a hiatus. Since then Gateway
has started moving along. Also, this would only be an option to developers, not
mandatory.

Mr. Mulholland asked the members to take the information home and review it and they
can discuss at the next meeting.

Public Hearing

1. Application of Pauline Lord dba White Gate Farm, for a Special Permit
for a Farm Store at property identified in the application as 83 Upper
Pattagansett Road, East Lyme, Connecticut.

Mr. Salerno stated the legal ad had run on this Public Hearing.

Mr. Bulmer read a memo from Mr. Mulholland to the Zoning Commission, and he read a
letter of support from Tom and Nancy Kalal.

Pauline Lord the owner of White Gate Farm stated the store will be in two existing
rooms, there will not be structural changes to the outside of the building or the inside,
The store will be 920 square feet.

Patrick Gaffey of 2 Pepperidge Lane voiced his support for White Gate Farm.
Motion (1) Mr. Gada moved to close the Public Hearing.

Seconded by Mr. Bulmer.

Motion Passed 6-0-0.
