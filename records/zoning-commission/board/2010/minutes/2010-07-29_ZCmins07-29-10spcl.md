1. Application of KSM Enterprises Store Two LLC dba Five Guys Burgers and
Fries, for a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining/Patio per East Lyme Zoning
Regulation 20.27, at property identified in the application as 295 Flanders
Road, East Lyme, Connecticut.

Marc Salerno stated this does not involve alcohol, and it is a three year permit. They will
have a fence, low intensity lighting, no outdoor entertainment except for low volume
speakers.

Motion (1) James Liska moved to Approve the Application of KSM Enterprises
Store Two LLC dba Five Guys Burger and Fries, for a Special Permit for Outdoor
Dining/Patio per East Lyme Zoning Regulation 20.27 at property identified in the
application as 295 Flanders Road, East Lyme CT and to allow for low volume
speakers.

Seconded by William Dwyer.
Motion Passed 5-0-0

Chairman Salerno stated this would be published on August 5, 2010 and would take
effect on August 6, 2010,

2. Application of Fred Finn, Agent, for Maximum Inc dba Burke’s Tavern, for

3. Application of Christopher & Amy Leigh for a Special Permit to operate
String Theory School of Music as property identified at 11 Freedom Way,
Units D3 and D4, Niantic, Connecticut.

Mr. Walker stated this is an existing business, the plan looks good and the presentation
addressed parking issues.

Mr. Salerno agreed that parking was adequately covered.

Motion (2) Matthew Walker moved to approve the Application of Christopher
and Amy Leigh for a Special Permit to operate String Theory School of Music at
property identified as 11 Freedom Way, Unit D3 and D4, Niantic, CT.

Seconded by James Liska.

they have also requested speakers with low volume music and live acoustic music.

Motion (3) William Dwyer moved to approve the Application of Nick & Rita
Fokaidis for a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining/Patio East Lyme Zoning
Regulations 20.27 at property identified in the application as 53 West Main Street,

amplification.
