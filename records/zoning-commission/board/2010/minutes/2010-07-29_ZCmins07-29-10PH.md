Public Hearing

L Application of KSM Enterprises Store Two LLC dba Five Guys Burgers and
Fries, for a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining/Patio per East Lyme Zoning
Regulation 20.27, at property identified in the application as 295 Flanders
Road, East Lyme, Connecticut.

Chairman Salerno stated the legal ad had run for this application.
Matt Walker read a memo from Zoning Officer, William Mulholland into the record.

Kevin Glazier presented the plan for KSM Enterprises Store Two LLC. He handed out
copies of his plan (attached as Exhibit 1). The patio would be a little over 500 square feet
and will be in front of the restaurant. They do not serve alcohol so this is a three year
application. They will put a fence up around the patio. They will have a trash receptacle
outside. Their hours are 1] a.m. to 10 p.m. They will have low intensity outside lighting,
and if more is needed they will work with the town on that. They already have
substantial parking, there are no problems with setbacks, they will have no outdoor
entertainment, and they will have plain solid color umbrellas. They will have the same
counter service as inside. They do want to have a speaker outside with low volume
music. There will not be a calling system to the outside patio. The tables will be small
bistro sized tables.

Mr. Dwyer stated he does not see a problem considering the area this business is in, it is
not a residential area.

There were not public comments.

Motion (1) Mr. Gada moved to close the public hearing.

Seconded by William Dwyer.

Motion Passed 5-0-0.

3. Application of Christopher & Amy Leigh for a Special Permit to operate
String Theory School of Music as property identified at 11 Freedom Way,
Units D3 and D4, Niantic, Connecticut.

Chairman Salerno stated the legal ad had run for this application.

Matthew Walker read a memo from William Mulholland into the record.

Amy Leigh presented the plan for the String Theory School of Music (attached as Exhibit

1) and discussed the parking spaces that are available to them. They will not be in huge

competition to get parking spaces. They are presently in business in East Lyme and have

had no complaints from any of their neighbors. They do have a “jam session” once a

month, usually on Sundays from 2 to 4 and that gives the students an opportunity to play

with their teachers.

William Dwyer stated he did not see any safety concerns.

Motion (2) Edward Gada moved to close the public hearing.

Seconded by William Dwyer.

Motion Passed 5-0-0,
4. Application of Nick & Rita Fokaidis for a Special Permit for Outdoor
Dining/Patio per East Lyme Zoning Regulation 20.27 at property identified
as 53 West Main Street, Niantic, Connecticut.

Chairman Salerno stated the legal ad had run for this application.

Rita Fokaidis presented the plans for the patio (attached as Exhibit 1.) This patio was
supposed to be 20x30 but it may end up being smaller as the builder has found many
boulders in the area, They do serve beer and wine. People will only be able to get to the
patio from the inside of the restaurant. They have low intensity lighting right now and if
that is not sufficient they will work with the town on that. This is a one year application
because of the alcohol service, and the umbrellas will also be plain solid color. There
will be a fence around the patio. Their hours are 10:30 to 9 Sunday through Thursday
and 10:30 to 10:00 on Friday and Saturday. This will be a full service patio, and they
would like low volume music through speakers and would like to have live acoustic
guitar as well,

There was not public comment.

Chairman Salerno asked how many table they plan on having. Mrs. Fokaidis stated right
now they are planning on 10, but it will depend on how big the patio ends of being.

Motion (3) Edward Gada moved to close the public hearing.
Seconded by William Dwyer.

Motion Passed 5-0-0.

Dear Zoning Board Members,

My name is Rita Fokaidis. My husband Nick and I own Village Pizza and
Grille, 53 West Main Street, Niantic. We would like to request a special permit to
allow for a deck to be constructed on the east side of our restaurant. We believe this
would help maintain and grow our business. Being that we are in a shoreline
community, our town offers many outdoor activities and part of that is outside
dining.

Please find included a site plan with the dimensions of 20 x 30 for the deck. It
would be constructed of burgundy Trex decking. It is a combination of wood and
plastic, preventing rotting and splitting, resisting moisture and insects with
excellent traction. This would allow for optimum appearance.

The height of the rails would be 36 inches around the perimeter of the deck.
Access on and off the deck would only be through the restaurant. We will be serving
alcohol however the fencing would protect the public space area. Tables and chairs
would be of wrought iron construction. The umbrellas would be 7-foot burgundy
market umbrellas over each table, with no lettering. We currently have low intensity
lighting off the building and also off the light post to the east of the building. At this
time we are also requesting that speakers be permitted for music and occasional
light live music.

Thank you for your time,

Rita Fokaidis
