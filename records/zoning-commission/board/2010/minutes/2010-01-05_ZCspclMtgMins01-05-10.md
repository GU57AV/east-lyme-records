                       EAST LYME ZONING COMMISSION
                             SPECIAL MEETING
                            Tuesday, January 5, 2010
                                  MINUTES

The East Lyme Zoning Commission held a Special Meeting on Tuesday, January 5, 2010
at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, CT.

PRESENT:              Marc Salerno, Steve Carpenteri, Norm Peck, Matt Walker, Bob
                      Bulmer, Alternate

ALSO PRESENT:         William Dwyer, Alternate
                      Rose Ann Hardy, Ex-Officio, Board of Selectman

ABSENT:               Rosanna Carabelas

1. Call to Order
Chairman of the Zoning Commission, Marc Salerno called this Special Meeting of the
Zoning Commission to order at 6:00 PM. He noted that William Dwyer was seated at the
table as alternate this evening.

2. Pledge of Allegiance
The Pledge was observed.

3. Filling the vacancy of a Regular Member (Mark C. Nickerson)
Mr. Salerno asked the commission for nominations for the vacancy of the Regular
Member position vacated by Mr. Nickerson.

MOTION (1)
Mr. Carpenteri moved to elect Edward P. Gada of 3 Whitney Circle, East Lyme, CT
06333 as a Regular Member.
Seconded by Mr. Peck.
Mr. Salerno asked Mr. Gada if he would like to say anything to the commission. Mr.
Gada then took the microphone and stated that he felt he was very qualified to fill this
position, and thanked the board for there consideration.
Mr. Salerno then asked if there were any further nominations or comments from the
public.
There were none.
Mr. Salerno called for a vote on the nomination of Mr. Edward Gada for the position of
Regular Member on the Zoning Commission.
Vote: 4-0-2. Motion passed.
Mr. Salerno congratulated Mr. Gada and welcomed him back to the board.
4. Brief Discussion regarding SBA Towers II LLC proposed Telecommunications
Facility at 49 Brainerd Road, Niantic, CT.
Mr. Salerno announced that per recommendation of the First Selectman there would be a
brief discussion regarding the proposed Telecommunications Tower Facility at Brainerd
Road, Niantic, CT. Mr. Salerno stated that the Town of East Lyme does not have
jurisdiction over this matter. That there were regulations sited 10 years ago regarding
cell towers, however they are not binding. Mr. Salerno stated that control has been given
to the Ct. Siting Council over this matter. Mr. Salerno recommended that the
commission send a letter to the Ct. Siting Council to express the concerns of the Zoning
Commission and the residents of Brainerd Road, and request that the Ct. Siting Council
meet the Towns regulations.
Mr. Dwyer expressed his concern that the discussion was premature due to the fact that
there is a Regular Meeting scheduled on Thursday, January 7, 2010 and that by having
this discussion the commission would be predetermining there position.
Mr. Salerno reassured Mr. Dwyer that this was not an application put before the board
and not a public hearing, therefore there was no need for concern.
Mr. Salerno reiterated that the most the Zoning Commission could accomplish would be
to write a letter to the Ct. Siting Council stating the recommendations of the Commission.
Mr. Bulmer stated that he had prior experience with the Ct. Siting Council and that the
Town and the Zoning Commission in particular does not have any say over the building
of this facility, however, he feels that they do have influence. He felt that what would be
effective would be to present the Ct. Siting Council with an alternate site.
Mr. Dwyer stated that he was under the impression the Tower was being built primarily
for use by the railroad. (Amtrak)
Mr. Peck stated that he has seen a Telecommunications tower that was made to look like
a tree and felt that this would be appropriate if placed in a wooded area.
Mr. Salerno agreed that he has also seen this type of tower and that it would be more
appealing.
Mr. Carpenteri asked for clarification as to how the location of these towers is decided,
was it strictly a state decision, and that the town does not in fact have any jurisdiction.
Mrs. Hardy as Ex-Officio stated that we could choose alternate sites for recommendation.
Mrs. Hardy stated that another property owner located on Route 156 has stepped forward
and offered the use of there property. Mrs. Hardy mentioned the reason both property
owners are interested in offering the use of there property is due to the fact that SBC will
be paying them a monthly stipend for the use of there property.
Mr. Salerno concluded that the Commission was in agreement to write a letter and that he
would coordinate the appropriate staff to have this letter written with an attachment of the
Town regulations.

Adjournment
Mr. Salerno called for a motion to adjourn.
MOTION (2)
Mr. Peck moved to adjourn this Special Meeting of the East Lyme Zoning
Commission at 6:30 PM.
Mr. Carpenteri seconded the motion.
Vote: 6-0-0. Motion Passed

Respectfully Submitted,


Zoe Zrakas, Recording Secretary
