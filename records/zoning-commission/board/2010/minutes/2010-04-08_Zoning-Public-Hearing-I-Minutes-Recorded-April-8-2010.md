Public Hearing |
1. Application of Leo Roche for Strive LLC for a renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at
property identified in the application as 247-2 Main Street, Niantic, CT

Mr. Salerno noted that he had seated Bob Bulmer, Alternate at the table this evening. He said that the ad for
this Public Hearing had been run in the New London Day. He then caifed upon the applicant for his
presentation.

Leo Roche, Mystic, CT said that he is applying for a renewal of the outdoor dining permit for his patio at the
Black Sheep, the same as he has now.

Mr. Bulmer asked how many seats he had and what does the ‘same as last year’ mean.
Mr. Roche said that he has 24 seats o the patio — 6 tables with 4 seats each. He serves liquor up until
midnight; there is no sitting on the wall, no pets, etc.

Mr. Bulmer asked if they allow people to order a drink without food.

Mr. Roche said yes.

Mr. Bulmer asked if it is a requirement to order food in order to get hard liquor.

Mr. Salerno and Ms. Carabelas said that in the Minutes of February 9, 2009 that they allowed people to order
liquor as long as they were seated at a table.

Mr. Roche noted that they have a tate menu and serve until midnight.

Mr. Walker said that he admires the outdoor patio area, the stonework is absolutely beautiful and he hopes
that he is there for a long, tong time.

Ms. Garabelas echoed the same sentiments and added that she and her daughter enjoy sitting out there and
having a drink in the nice weather.
Mr. Roche said that there is a need for outdoor seating as it says that the Town is open fo business.

Mr. Salerno called for any other comments from the public —

Hearing none —

Mr. Salerno asked Mr. Roche if he keeps the chairs out all year.

Mr. Roche said no and added that he just put them out now so that they could be tightened up and prepared
for the season.

Mr. Salerno asked if they allow people to carry drinks outside.
Mr, Roche said that typically they do not and that he does have people watching at the door although some
people do slip through. He said that they will monitor it more carefully.

Mr. Salerno asked if he had planters last year.
Mr. Roche said that he did have them on the top of the stone wall and that some were damaged and others
were stolen.

Harry Loginidis, owner of Family Pizza noted that all of his planters were broken in one night last year and
that he did not replace them as they are very expensive.

Mr. Roche said that they will put them back out this year but they have to bring them in each night as
otherwise they get damaged.

Ms. Carabelas asked if they could be secured to the top of the wall.

Mr. Roche said no as he does not want to damage the bluestone top. They will take them in each night.

Mr. Salerno asked about the speakers and the music outside and the volume.
Mr. Roche said that there is a speaker for outside music and that he was not aware of any complaints and
will monitor it carefully.

Ms. Carabelas asked if they have cigarette receptacles.
Mr. Roche said that he has them and that people would still put them on the ground regardless of how many
are put out there.

Mr. Salerno asked if there were any other questions or comments.
Hearing none —

Mr. Salerno called for a motion to close this Public Hearing.
**MOTION (1)

Ms. Carabelas moved that this Public Hearing be closed.

Mr. Bulmer seconded the motion.

Vote: 6-0-0. Motion passed.

Mr. Salerno closed this Public Hearing at 7:50 PM.

Respectfully submitted,

Karen Zmitruk,
Recording Secretary
