PUBLIC HEARING

1, Application of the Town of East Lyme Zoning Commission to amend the
East Lyme Zoning Regulations Section 1.38 Definitions and Section 23
Conservation Design Development which are being modified in support
of the new Subdivision Regulations promoting cluster development in our
community.

Chairman Salerno stated the legal ad had run on this Public Hearing.

Secretary Carabelas read a letter from Marcy Balint of the DEP dated November 16,
2010 into the record.

Ms. Carabelas also read a letter from Gary Goeschel dated November 17, 2010, and a
Letter from the Southeastern CT Council of Governments dated November 16, 2010.

Mr. Salerno stated this Commission has sponsored this zone change application. It has
been a sub-committee for the last two years.

Mr. Peck who was the chairman of this sub-committee stated the idea behind this zone
change is to do away with “cookie cutter” type development. It also gives more
flexibility to the Zoning Commission and the Planning Commission. They have worked
hard in this to preserve the values of the parcels, They did receive input from
Developers on this Application, This will look at all of the features of the land. Houses
will be placed strategically to maintain those features of the land, and then the roads are
designed. All to preserve and have the nicest neighborhood that can be made, Open
Space requirements will remain the same.

Mr. Mulholland stated this will take advantage of the land to make the subdivisions more
attractive. They may need to tweak some things once everything is in place, as they have
had to do in the past.

Mr. Peck stated Planning Commission has had two public hearings on this, They are
waiting for this Commission’s decision before they close their Public Hearing.

There was no Public Comment.
Mr. Dwyer stated he was satisfied with this application.

Ms. Carabelas stated this is excellent for the town, and a step forward. She also
commended Mr. Peck and the other members of the subcommittee for their hard work.

Motion (1) Mr. Bulmer moved to close the Public Hearing at 8:10 p.m.
Seconded by Mr. Carpenteri.

Motion Passed 6-0-0.

2. Application of Peter Balanger, Applicant, for Food Bag, for a special
permit to replace all the fuel dispensers and piping for gasoline service
system on the premises of 202 Flanders Road, Niantic, Connecticut.

Mr. Salerno stated the Legal Ad had run on this Public Hearing.
Ms, Carabelas read a memo from Mr. Mulholland. They are asking for a sidewalk waiver
on this application.

Mr. Belanger represents General Equities, the owner of the property. They want to
upgrade the dispensers and pipes, These are normal upgrades, They will take about two
and a half weeks to complete the work. There will be minor excavation done,

Mr. Dwyer asked if they would be touching the tanks.

Mr. Belanger stated they will have to dig down to the tank and screw a new top on the
tank. The tanks are 30 year tanks and have 13 years left in them.

Mr. Bulmer asked if they have their own independent inspection system.

Mr. Belanger stated all line and vapor testing is done by the state. If there are any
environmental issues DEP will get involved. The Fire Marshai will also be there.

Mr. Mulholland stated they will also have to get a permit from the Building Inspector.
Motion (2) Mr. Bulmer moved to close the Public Hearing at 8:20 p.m.
Seconded by Ms. Carabelas.
Motion Passed 6-0-0.
