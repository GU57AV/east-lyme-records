       Public Comment
       James McClure, 5 Faulkner Dr. stated although he is on good terms with the applicant, he
       opposes the application. He believes the application is in direct conflict with the zoning
       regulations mission and specific conditions. J. McClure stated there will be decline in property
       value and the proposed structure is 3 times the size of any structure on Faulkner Dr. The Special
       Use Permit is designed to be a safeguard to protect the surrounding properties. The proposed
       building is not in harmony with the surrounding homes.
       He is concerned about noise, light pollution, and hours of operation. He stated the lights from the
       building and vehicles exiting the garage will shine directly into his bedroom.
       He pointed out there is no definition of, "landscaping business" in the regulations.
       J. McClure said he would be happy to discuss options for more privacy along the driveway and
       his properly line and asked for the Commission to extend the public hearing.
       Brenna McClure, 5 Faulkner Dr. stated her concern about the lights and vehicle headlights
       shining into the two bedrooms on the side of the house facing the proposed building. She wanted
       to know the hours of operation and was concemed that during a snow event, vehicles would be
       going out during the night.
       Amanda Smith, 7 Faulkner Dr. is opposed to the application. She and her family will see the
       operation from her back porch and be significantly impacted.
       Chris Manwaring, 23 & 25 Black Point Rd., property owner, clarified that the proposed building
       is approximately 2800 sq. ft which is not triple the size of his house or some of the commercial
       buildings near the proposed building. The building across the street is also large. He stated that
       the property is zoned commercially, and many types of business could go there.
       The building will be used mainly for storage for seasonal turnover of equipment.
       His landscaping business consist of landscaping and homecare. There will be lawnmowers, front
       loader and skid steer, and low level maintenance vehicles stored at the site. There will be no
       large sign advertising the business. C. Manwaring stated he would consider planting along the
       south side of the driveway to give more privacy to the neighbors. He will submit revised site
       plans showing additional plantings.
       James McClure, 5 Faulkner Dr. said his main concern is privacy and noise.
       Amanda Smith, 7 Faulkner Dr. asked how many people would be working in the office and
       building and what are the hours of operation.
       C. Manwaring stated the hours of operation will be 8-4:30. There would be 2 people in the office
       and approximately 5-10 people in and out a day with no weekend activity.
