CALL TO ORDER

Chairman Salerno called the Public Hearing to order at 7:33 p.m.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

Chairman Salerno led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Mr. Dwyer sat as a Regular Member for Item 1 in the place of Mr. Donovan.
PUBLIC DELEGATIONS

There were no Public Delegations.

1. THE TOWN OF EAST LYME ZONING COMMISSION PROPOSAL TO AMEND THE EAST LYME
ZONING REGULATIONS SECTION 22, REGARDING PARKING

Mr. Salerno confirmed that the legal advertisement ran on this Item.

Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Mr. Walker read a letter from Frank Balantic of the East Lyme Planning Commission.
Mr. Walker read a letter from James Rabbitt of SCCOG.

Mr. Walker read a letter from Nancy Balint of DEEP.

Mr. Salerno stated this is a Commission sponsored application. They want to encourage business
growth downtown.
Melinda Grauss of Gumdrops & Lollipops commended the Commission for this application.
Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.

Seconded by Mr. Walker.

Motion Passed 6-0.

**Mr. Donovan entered the meeting at 7:40 p.m. He sat for the remainder of the Public Hearing and
Mr. Dwyer stepped down.

2. THE TOWN OF EAST LYME ZONING COMMISSION PROPOSAL TO AMEND THE EAST LYME
ZONING REGULATIONS SECTION 15, “FLOOD HAZARD AREAS” TO MAINTAIN COMPLIANCE
WITH THE MINIMUM STANDARDS OF THE NATIONAL FLOOD INSURANCE PROGRAM.

Mr. Salerno confirmed that the legal advertisement ran on this Item.
Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.
Mr. Walker read a letter from Frank Balantic of the East Lyme Planning Commission.
Mr. Walker read a letter from James Rabbitt of SCCOG.
Mr. Walker read a letter from Nancy Balint of DEEP.
Mr. Salerno stated this is a housekeeping item. He reviewed the amended section.
Mr. McPherson stated where it says 7/18/11 it should be corrected to be 8/5/13.
Motion (2) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.
Seconded by Mr. Donovan.
Motion Passed 6-0.
