1. Application of Thomas and Terri Yuhas for a Special Permit for agricultural use to keep/house
various farm animals at property identified in the application as 36 Giants Neck Road, Niantic,

CT. East Lyme Assessor’s map 7.1, Lot 1.

2. Continuation of Application of Keith Neilson, Docko, Inc., Agent for a Coastal Area
Management Site Plan Review to reconstruct and restore the coastal bluff at Crescent Beach
as property identified in the application as Crescent Beach from Crescent Avenue to South

Washington, Niantic, Connecticut.

This item was continued with no discussion.

3. Meeting Schedule for Calendar year 2014.

Motion (1) Mr. Donovan moved to approve the 2014 Regular Meeting Schedule (attached).

Seconded by Mr. Kane.

Motion Passed 6-0.
4. Approval of Minutes of October 3, 2013.

Motion (2) Mr. McPherson moved to approve the Minutes of October 3, 2013.
Seconded by Mr. Kane.
Motion Passed 5-0-1 (Mr. Walker Abstained)
Old Business
1. Sign Sub-Committee
Mr. Walker stated they met tonight. They discussed digital signs, and their uses (municipal vs. private
business). They discussed whether that is something this Commission would want in town, and in what
locations they would be suitable. It is worthy of significant discussion.

Mr. McPherson stated they also discussed farm signs and directional signs.

Mr. Walker stated they want to move this along on an aggressive schedule. They would like the town
attorney to review what we can and can’t do.

Mr. McPherson asked if Mr. Mulholland can have something for the Commission at the next meeting.

Mr. Mulholland stated he will try to get something to the town attorney tomorrow, but his response
may take a few weeks.

Mr. Walker stated he will coordinate with Mr. Mulholland, and if we do hear back from the town
attorney before the next meeting they will schedule a sub-committee meeting.

2. Outdoor Dining Bars Sub-Committee
There was no report.
New Business

1, Request of Cynthia and Williams Stevens for a text amendment to the East Lyme Zoning
Regulations Section 1, 9.2 and 25.5 regarding Pet Daycare and Boarding.

There was no objection to this item being brought to the floor, and Chairman Salerno asked Mr.
Mulholland to schedule this for a Public Hearing.

2. Any business on the floor, if any, by the majority vote of the Commission

Mr. Peck requested another sub-committee be formed to review the Zoning Regulations with a goal
toward simplifying as it has been quite some time since they have been reviewed.

There was no objection to this item being brought to the floor.
Mr. Mulholland agreed with Mr. Peck that this would be a good item to review.

It was agreed that Mr. Walker, Mr. Peck, Mr. Donovan, and Chairman Salerno would be on the sub-
committee. They are aware that they will have to file an Agenda and Minutes for their meetings.

Chairman Salerno stated they will meet the Second Thursday in December, and they will start with
definitions.

3. Zoning Official
Mr. Mulholland stated they have been very busy, the phone has been ringing off the hook, there are
several projects moving along, Henny Penny, the plans for three buildings across the street from Henny
Penny have been presented to him, the Crescent Point addition is going forward, the sidewalk on West
Main Street looks good, Gumdrops and Lollipops addition is moving along, the architect for Gary Smith’s
building said the plans have been completed, the building across from the Midway Mall is moving along.
He spent two hours yesterday with Gateway.

4, Comments from Ex-Officio
Ms. Cheeseman stated the Board of Selectmen met last night, and the majority of action was
appropriations, the Town has received some FEMA funds, there will be a Special Town Meeting on
November 20", they had discussion of a potential Public Safety Committee looking at what other towns
are doing. They also set their meeting schedule for 2014.

5. Comments from Zoning Board Liaison to Planning Commission
Mr. McPherson did not attend the meeting.

6. Comments from Chairman
Chairman Salerno congratulated everyone who was re-elected. This has been a very pro-active
Commission, everyone has gotten involved. The new sub-committee will be important, and he is glad
Mr. Peck brought that up.

7. Adjournment
Motion (3) Mr. Donovan moved to adjourn the meeting at 8:00 p.m.
Seconded by Mr. Walker.
Motion Passed 6-0.
