PUBLIC DELEGATIONS

1. APPLICATION OF HARRY AND TRIFONAS LOGINIDIS DBA FAMILY PIZZA FOR A RENEWAL OF A

SPECIAL PERMIT FOR OUTDOOR DINING AT PROPERTY IDENTIFIED IN THE APPLICATION AS
233 MAIN STREET, NIANTIC,

Mr. Salerno confirmed the legal advertisement ran on this item.
Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

The applicant was not present. Mr. Salerno stated he had not heard of any issues with this applicant’s
outdoor dining area.

Mr. Dwyer stated it is a well-run business.
Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.
Seconded by Mr. Walker.

Motion Passed 6-0.

2. APPLICATION OF LEO ROCHE FOR STRIVE LLC DBA BLACK SHEEP FOR A RENEWAL OF A SPECIAL
PERMIT FOR OUTDOOR DINING AT PROPERTY IDENTIFIED IN THE APPLICATION AS 247-22

MAIN STREET, NIANTIC,

Mr. Salerno confirmed the legal advertisement ran on this item.

Leo Roche, the applicant was present. He stated everything works great, and his only issue is the people
sitting on the wall. They had flower boxes there and they were vandalized. He has reported it to the
police. He stated he always has a manager on duty to overlook the area.

Mr. Donovan asked if he would be willing to do something else instead of flower boxes.

Mr. Roche stated they are going to try the flower boxes again this year. If that doesn’t work he could try
some iron fence, but that would be a big expense.

Mr. McPherson suggested masonry flower pots that are permanent.

Mr. Roche stated another issue is the customers at Dunkin Donuts using his patio in the morning.
People after hours are a problem.

Mr. Salerno stated the business looks great, and it adds a lot to the town. He suggested roping off a
walkway when they are done serving on the patio, and to keep it roped off until morning. He feels very
positive about this business.

Motion (2) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.
Seconded by Mr. Walker.

Motion Passed 6-0.

5. APPLICATION OF THEODORE A. HARRIS, ESQ., AGENT FOR BENCHMARK SENIOR LIVING, TO
AMEND THE EAST LYME ZONING REGULATION SECTION 9 AND SECTION 25.5 REGARDING
ADDITIONS TO ASSISTED LIVING FACILITIES,

Mr. Salerno confirmed the legal ad ran on this item.

Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Mr. Walker read a letter from Gary Goeschel.

Mr. Walker read an email from Marcy Balint at DEEP.

There was no response from Regional Planning.

Attorney Harris presented the application. They would like to add to the assisted living facilities. This is
for individuals that can live independently but need some assistance. This proposal compliments the
existing regulations. This facility benefits downtown. They are using the land to the maximum
potential. They are only adding to existing facilities. Any expansion can’t exceed 25%, they cannot

expand on additional land, and they must meet all criteria in 25.5.

Motion (5) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.

Seconded by Mr. Kane.
Motion Passed 6-0.
