1. Application of Mirabell, LLC, for a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining to include the serving of

alcohol, at property identified in the application as 344 Main Street, Niantic, Connecticut.

Mr. Mulholland confirmed the legal advertisement had run on this item.

Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Brian Bellmore, 25 Marsh Hill Road presented the application. They will surround the existing patio with
fence. They are applying for outdoor dining, and the State Liquor Control Board for a beer and wine
license to serve on the patio.

The patio is now surrounded by gardens, now there is no fence, except for the west side. If it is required
they will enclose the patio.

The following exhibit was submitted:

Exhibit A — Fence Options
All of the options were acceptable to the applicant; he prefers the black powder coated steel. The patio

will stay the same size. Currently they are not open at night. They have no plans to be open past
sunlight hours. They are not planning outdoor entertainment, but they may have a speaker out there.

Public Comment:

Gary Smith, 369 Main Street, Niantic recommended this application, it is a great operation.
Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.

Seconded by Mr. Walker,

Motion Passed 6-0.

2. Application of Bill Wild, for Wild-Froyo, LLC dba Peachwave Frozen Yogurt East Lyme,

Owner/Applicant, for a Special Permit to operate a Fast Food Restaurant at prope

identified in the application as 170 Flanders Road, East Lyme, Connecticut, Assessor’s Map

26.3, lot 11,

Mr. Peck recused himself, and Mr. Dwyer sat for Mr. Peck.

Mr. Mulholland confirmed the legal advertisement had run on this item.

Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Bill Wild, 16 Town Road, Niantic presented the application.

The following exhibit was submitted:

Exhibit A — Overview

Mr. Wild stated this is a fast moving concept around the country. They will sell healthy frozen desserts,
with no and low fat options. It will be similar to one in the Crystal Mall, but larger. They will be doing
large improvements to the interior. There will be 1100 square feet of public space. Hopefully in the
future they will have outdoor seating. They are working with the landiord on exterior improvements to
the building, and improved exterior lighting. There is a lot of available parking. Their peak hours will be
in the evening. This is not fast food, there is substantially less traffic, no drive thru, no stoves, no

cooking, no soda fountain service. They will have a cooler with bottled drinks.

Mr. Mulhoiland stated he called the City of Danbury. That city recognized the difference between this
type of business and a fast food restaurant.

Mr. Wild stated the tenants are encouraging the landiord to improve the parking lot lighting, and the
signage lighting on the building.

There was no Public Comment.

Motion (2) Mr. Walker moved to close the Public Hearing.
Seconded by Mr. McPherson.

Motion Passed 6-0.

Mr. Peck returned, and Mr. Dwyer stepped down.
