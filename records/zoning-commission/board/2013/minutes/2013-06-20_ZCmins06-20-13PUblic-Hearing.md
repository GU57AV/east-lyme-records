3. EAST LYME ZONING COMMISSION PROPOSAL TO AMEND THE EAST LYME ZONING
REGULATIONS BY DELETING SECTION 13 AQUIFER AND PRIMARY RECHARGE DISTRICT AND
SECONDARY RECHARGE DISTRICT, AND SECTION 30, AQUIFER PROTECTION DISTRICTS FROM
THE EAST LYME ZONING REGULATIONS, AND TO AMEND THE ZONING MAP BY REMOVING THE

AQUIFER PROTECTION ZONES. JURISDICTION AND RESPONSIBILITY HAS BEEN TRANSFERRED
TO THE TOWN’S AQUIFER PROTECTION AGENCY.

Mr. Muiholland confirmed the legal advertisement had run on this item.
Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Mr. Walker read a letter from the East Lyme Planning Commission.

Mr. Walker read a letter from James Rabbitt of SCCOG.

Mr. Walker read a memo from Marcy Balint of DEEP.

Mr. Salerno stated in the past year the state has requested towns to appoint an Aquifer Protection
Agency, the Zoning Commission serves in that capacity. The Town was required to perform A1 surveys
to determine the boundaries of the aquifers.

Mr. Mulholland stated those were established through drilling and modeling.

Mr. Salerno stated they determined those areas that could damage the aquifers, since they don’t have a
reservoir in this town. Aquifer protection regulations are in the zoning regulations. The State suggests
they have one set of regulations. They had held off on this. The Town’s Water Engineer explained how
they got the maps in the 70’s and they are based on topography and are not very scientific. They now
have two maps, and one isa little less restrictive. Any business within the aquifer would have to apply

for a permit.

Mr. Mulholland stated if a use is considered to possibly have adverse impacts they must register with
him. They must have a best management plan.

Mr. Salerno stated the State carefully designed the regulations to balance with economic development.
Mr. Peck asked where the Planning Commission was coming from in their determination that this was
Inconsistent with the POCD.

Mr. Donovan stated he was at that meeting and they didn’t have all of the information that was
presented to us. The information that was explained to us was not explained to them.

Mr. Peck asked if any engineer or professional staff was consulted.
Mr. Donovan stated not in that meeting.
Mr. Donovan stated they were concerned with future drilling of wells.

Mr. Mulholland stated the State would have to give a permit for new wells and they would need new
maps for that.

There were no public comments.

Motion (3) Mr. Donovan moved to close the Public Hearing.
Seconded by Mr. Walker.

Motion Passed 6-0.
