CALL TO ORDER

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

Chairman Salerno led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS

Larry Winkler of 38 Roxbury Road stated there is a problem at 45 Roxbury Road. It is zoned residential;
there is a folder in the zoning office three inches thick on that property. He has lived in town for 20
years and he doesn’t understand why nothing can be done. Also at 133 Roxbury Road there is a
gentleman running a business out of his garage. He is repairing lawn movers and scooters, and that is
not allowed either. He would like to see something done.

Mr. Mulholland stated there is a file three inches thick on 45 Roxbury Road. He has sent him two or
three letters, and they are trying to get a court order to go on the property. They believe he is running a
processing plant of some sort. They have had to engage the town attorney. This is an ongoing battle.
He also received a letter recently on the other property. He will look into that.
1. THE TOWN OF EAST LYME ZONING COMMISSION PROPOSAL TO AMEND THE EAST LYME
ZONING REGULATIONS SECTION 4.2 REGARDING AGRICULTURE OR FARM USE, INCLUDING
THE RAISING OF LIVESTOCK OR POULTRY, AND SECTION 25.5, TABLE OF MINIMUM CONTROLS.
This was continued from the last meeting. All letters have previously been read into the record.

Chairman Salerno read the proposed amendment to Section 4.

Mr. Mulholland stated the way it is worded it is still a special permit activity. If they want to not have a
special permit required it should be called Section 4.1.3.1. It has to be added to permitted uses outright.

Mr. Salerno stated that is the intent, to not have to require a special permit.

There was discussion of including RU80 and RU120 under District. The objective is to allow it by right
and not require a special permit.

There was no public comment.

Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.
Seconded by Mr. Dwyer.

Motion Passed 6-0.

Respectfully Submitted,

Karen Miller Galbo
Recording Secretary
