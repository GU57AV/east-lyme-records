Public Hearing —

1. Application of Keith B. Neilson, P.E., of Docko, Inc., Agent for Alastair John Hunt and Pauline B.
Hunt, Owners, for a Coastal Site Plan Review, at 243 Old Black Point Road, East Lyme,
Assessor's Map 02.1, Lot 4. The proposal calls for re-grading of the shoreline property to create
a living structure, to create a uniform, gently graded sloping lawn to respond to flooding and
wave. The proposal is to place 400 (+/-) CY of new armor stone over 2000 (+/-) SF, 400 (+/-) CY
of gravel fill and top soil over 2,500 SF along the shore, landward of the high tide line and CT
Coastal Jurisdiction line and then planting native, non-invasive, salt-tolerant, drought resistant
grassy vegetation that will help to act like a dune of uniform elevation.
