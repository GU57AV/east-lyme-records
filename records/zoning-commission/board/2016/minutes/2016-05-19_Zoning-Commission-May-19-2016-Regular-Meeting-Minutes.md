3. STOP & SHOP TREE DISCUSSION/SOLUTION ETC

Mr. Donovan stated he did not know an arborist was there when the trees were cut.

Mr. McPherson stated if any die they need to be replaced.

Mr. Peck stated approval was given and was clear, the reasons were discussed. The applicant accepted
them in good faith. We wanted to hide the back of the grocery store, now it is not hidden. A letter was
sent reminding them not to touch the trees. If we let this happen we ought to not even have a Zoning
Commission.

Attorney Harris stated maintenance was a part of the approval.

Mr. Mulholland stated trimming, but not massive cutting.

Attorney Harris stated there will be no further massive cutting, minor pruning maybe.

Mr. Peck suggested he send a letter to the Commission saying it won’t be done in the future.

Mr. Mulholland asked if that was possible.

Attorney Harris stated he will consult with his client.

Mr. Mulholland stated they received notice 13 months ago about the trees.

Attorney Harris suggested continuing this to the next meeting so he can talk to his client.

Mr. Peck stated they understand exposure, that exit is a gateway to East Lyme; it is a very busy exit.
They tried to make the gateway look better than a regular exit. Now it looks typical.

Mr. Donovan stated if they get away with this what happens if someone else does this.
Mr. McPherson suggested the letter say the present or future owners.
