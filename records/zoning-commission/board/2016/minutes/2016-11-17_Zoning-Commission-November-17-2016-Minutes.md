1, ESTABLISHMENT OF NEW SUB-COMMITTEE (RESIDENTIAL USES IN LI ZONES)

Chairman Walker stated we discussed this at our last meeting; it was facilitated by Mr. Dwyer. He has a
legitimate concern regarding dormitories in LI zones. It is a collective concern worthy of a sub-
committee. He would like them to report back within 30 days.

Mr. Dwyer stated it seems like that would be using up time.

Mr. Mulholland stated he has had a recent inquiry about multi-family in the LI zone, but nobody has
asked about a school.

Mr. Donovan asked if we have the hearing scheduled for convalescent homes can we modify it at the
hearing.

Mr. Mulholland stated we cannot.

Mr. Peck stated we also may have to worry about health spas that could have overnight guests. He used
Norwich Inn & Spa as a comparison.

Mr. Mulholland stated the spa is a piece of the Norwich Inn and Spa, but it’s a hotel.
Mr. Peck asked how we would know how a judge is going to interpret hotels.
Mr. Mulholland stated it is clear in the definition that hotels are not long term.

Mr. Mulholland stated we can move forward with the piece in front of us and he will go back and look at
the motel aspect and report back to them at the next meeting.

Mr. Dwyer, Mr. Peck and Mr. Manning were appointed to the Sub-Committee. They will meet at 5:00
p.m. on Monday November 21 in Mr. Mulholland’s office.
