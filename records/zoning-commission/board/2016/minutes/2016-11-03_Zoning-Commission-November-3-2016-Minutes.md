NEW BUSINESS

1. Request of the Town of East Lyme Zoning Commission for a Text Amendment to delete
from the East Lyme Zoning Regulations, Section 11.2.7 Convalescent Homes from
Section 11 “LI Light Industrial District.” Convalescent Homes are presently permitted by
Special Permit.

Mr. Dwyer suggested amending the language of the proposal; he is concerned about residences
be permitted in the Light Industrial Zone. He added that in the case of a Convalescent Home, if
you live there, eat there and sleep there it’s a residence.

Mr. Donovan said the Commission can address the Convalescent Home aspect of the
Amendment first.

Mr. Dwyer would like everything to be addressed at once.
Mr. Liska and Walker said any form of residence is inappropriate for the LI District.
Mr. Manning suggested obtaining guidance from the Town Attorney.

The Commission will consult Mr. Mulholland for clarification regarding this issue and suspend
the conversation until the next meeting.

Mr. McPherson said LI issues should be an agenda item for the next meeting.

2. Application of Robert S. Foster (Old Orchard Farm) for a Text Amendment to amend
Section 25 of the East Lyme Zoning Regulations to permit Farms of 5+ acres to obtain a
Special Permit in order to conduct Farm Events and Agritourism Events (activities
accessory to farm/agriculture use of the property.)
