5. APPLICATION OF STEVE TURNER, FOR LILLIAN’S FOR A RENEWAL OF A SPECIAL PERMIT FOR
OUTDOOR DINING AT 374 MAIN STREET, NIANTIC

Mr. Mulholland confirmed the legal advertisement ran on this item and the sign was posted. He also
stated that there have been no issues with this permit.

Mr. Turner was present, he stated that the size of the sign that must be posted is alarming. He has had
to answer questions on numerous occasions about what is going on. He asked that they
reconsider the size requirement.

Chairman Walker stated we do need to reconsider that, he drove by at night and saw the sign and did a
double take.

Mr. Turner also stated his permit expired in January, he asked if it could possibly expire around this time
of year.

Mr. Mulholland stated he has no issue with that, he is absolutely right about the sign, we can try to
make this easier for the restaurant owners.

6. APPLICATION OF EDWARDO MARTONE, FOR A SPECIAL PERMIT FOR OUTDOOR DINING AT 11
EAST PATTAGANSETT ROAD, NIANTIC

Mr. Mulholland confirmed the legal advertisement ran on this item.

Mr. McPherson read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Stanley Martone, 11 East Pattagansett Road, stated they are new in town, they own one other
restaurant in Meriden. They are a family restaurant with authentic Italian meals. They will respect all of
the neighbors. Their electrician will use very soft lighting. They would like to have speakers for soft
background music. There will be no late parties.

Mr. Mulholland asked if they can survive without the speakers.

Mr. Martone stated he thinks it will provide nice ambience. They would like to have it.

Mr. Mulholland stated if his office got a call he would have to contact them. Would they have a
microphone?

Mr. Martone stated no.
Mr. Mulholland asked the hours of operation of the deck.

Mr. Martone stated until 9:00 during the week and 10:00 on weekends, although they haven’t decided
on definite hours. Winter hours may be different.

Mr. Donovan asked if the patio would be open until an hour before close.

Mr. Martone stated every waitress will check every ID. They have a scanning device to scan IDs, they
hope to have a soft opening on April 28", and grand opening on May st

Mr. Manning asked if they would have live music on the patio.

Mr. Martone stated no.
Mark Datum, 9 East Pattagansett Road stated they have done a wonderful job with the building, but he
is not really happy about the patio. His main concern is parking. He doesn’t think it’s fair, with the
limited parking they already have to add extra seating. It is now a beautiful building.

Mr. Mulholland stated it is commercially zoned, people do a lot of walking, and there is a school across
the street. He had the parking discussion with the applicant regarding past parking issues.

Mr. Peck asked if they have attempted to find other parking.

Mr. Martone stated yes, they have also discussed employees parking down the street. He himself will
bike to work. Right now they have 14 spaces and one is handicapped.

Mr. Peck asked if they have approached the school to ask if their patrons can park there.
Mr. Martone stated he will reach out to them. He doesn’t want to disturb the neighbors.
Motion (3) Mr. Dwyer moved to close the Public Hearing.

Seconded by Mr. McPherson.

Motion Passed 6-0.

7. APPLICATION OF JEFFREY MCNAMARA FOR TUCK PROPERTIES, LLC FOR A SPECIAL PERMIT FOR
COMMERCIAL RECREATION AT 27 HOPE STREET, NIANTIC

Mr. Mulholland confirmed the legal advertisement ran on this item.

Mr. McPherson read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Jeffrey McNamara, 100 Pennsylvania Avenue stated this is the old New London Tape building. Robert
Marelli purchased it. He is the owner of SECONN Fabrications and another business in Waterford, his
daughter is a softball player. He is familiar with commercial sites. This will be a safe clean environment
for teams and individuals to practice. There is a need for it in Southeastern Connecticut. He envisions
families dropping their kids off for practice and staying in town and visiting our restaurants and stores.
The property abuts the railroad tracks.

Mr. Dwyer asked where the entrance is.

Attorney McNamara stated it is off of Main Street.

Mr. Donovan stated he would like to see this building being used.

Chairman Walker stated it will be a great resource.

Motion (4) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.

Seconded by Mr. Donovan.
Motion Passed 6-0.
