3. RESPONSE/DEEP HEARING OFFICER REGARDING 235 OLD BLACK POINT ROAD PERMIT

This item was heard prior to the previous Public Hearing to make efficient use of the Town Attorney’s
time. Mr. Liska and Mr. Mcllhenney did not sit for this item.

Chairman Walker stated he only wanted members seated who were at the meeting when the Coastal
Site Plan was approved for 235 Old Black Point Road.

Attorney O’Connell stated this pertains to a hearing pending before DEEP. The permit was issued and
approved by the Zoning Commission for 235 Old Black Point Road. An order was issued by the hearing
officer pertaining to what the zoning Commission considered it was doing when it approved the Dowling
Coastal Site Plan. The Zoning Commission is not an official party to this action, the dispute is between
neighbors. He gave them sample resolutions to adopt. They do not need to give a reason as to why
they adopt a particular one.

Mr. Mulholland stated this was about a year ago. He distributed copies of the application and narrative.
Chairman Walker stated the hearing officer wants clarification from us.

Mr. Donovan stated a structure would be a concrete wall or the such. He didn’t think it was a structure.

Chairman Walker recalled regarding it as a living shoreline. In reviewing the application and the notes
that is his conclusion.

Mr. McPherson concurred with Chairman Walker.
Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved the following:

When approving the Dowling Coastal Site Plan, the East Lyme Zoning Commission considered the
approval to be of a living shoreline activity requiring coastal site plan approval.

Seconded by Mr. Donovan.
Motion Passed 4-0
