 Public Hearing I
 1. Application of the Town of East Lyme Parks & Recreation Dept. for a Special Permit to install
    lighting at the Smith Harris little league field located on Society Road, Niantic, CT

 Acting Chairman Carabelas said that the Legal Ad for this application had run in The Day on February 20,
 2009 and March 2, 2009.

 She then asked Marc Salerno, Acting Secretary to read the correspondence into the record.
 Mr. Salerno read the following correspondence:

 ♦   Memo dated 3/5/09 to the East Lyme Zoning Commission from Wm. Mulholland, Zoning Official – Re:
     Special Permit Application of Town of East Lyme Parks & Recreation to install lighting at Smith Harris
     Little League – noting it is an ancillary use to the existing ball field and is regulated under Section 20.1-
     F of the regulations and under the Special Permit regulations in Section 25.

 Ms. Carabelas called upon the applicant or their representative for a presentation of this application.
 Dave Putnam, Director of Parks & Recreation said that this is Field #3 which is located behind the Middle
 School on Society Road. The Little League wants to light this field and all of the funding to do so is coming
 from money raised by the Little League. He submitted Exhibit 1 & 1A – color pictures of the lighting fixtures
 and Exhibit 2 – picture of cut-off lighting (spill & glare) for the record. He explained that the lighting shines
 directly down and does not spill out to neighboring areas.

 Mr. Peck asked if this lighting has less light spill than the other fields.
 Mr. Putnam said yes, much less.
 Mr. Mulholland asked if this field would be used solely by the little leagues.
 Mr. Putnam said that it would be used mostly for little league baseball.

 Ms. Carabelas called for comments from the public, in favor of this application –
