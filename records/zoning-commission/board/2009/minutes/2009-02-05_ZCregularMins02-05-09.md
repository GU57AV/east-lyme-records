 1. Call to Order
 Chairman Nickerson called this Regular Meeting of the Zoning Commission to order at 8:01 PM after the
 previously scheduled Public Hearings. He noted that he had seated William Dwyer, Alternate at the
 table this evening.

 Pledge of Allegiance
 The Pledge was previously observed.

 Public Delegations
 Mr. Nickerson called for anyone from the public who wished to address the Commission on subject matters
 not on the Agenda.

 There were no delegations.


                                               Regular Meeting

 1. Application of Greg Dubrule for Blackhawk Sport fishing LLC for a Special Permit for a sign
    advertising/locating Blackhawk Sport fishing LLC at the western entrance of property identified
    in the Application as Main Street (Cini Park) Niantic, CT.
 Mr. Nickerson said that they had just closed the Public Hearing on this and called for discussion.

 Mr. Salerno said that he thinks that a sign here is good for business and good for the Town and that it is a
 tricky area to see without a sign letting you know where it is.

 **MOTION (1)
 Mr. Salerno moved to approve the Application of Greg Dubrule for Blackhawk Sport fishing LLC for
 a Special Permit for a sign advertising/locating Blackhawk Sport fishing LLC at the western
 entrance of property identified in the Application as Main Street (Cini Park) Niantic, CT.
 Mr. Carpenteri seconded the motion.
 Vote: 6 – 0 – 0. Motion passed.

 Mr. Mulholland said that this would publish on February 12, 2009 and become effective on February 13,
 2009.

 2. Application of Leo Roche for Strive LLC for a renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at
    property identified in the application as 247-2 Main Street, Niantic, CT.

 Mr. Nickerson said that they had also just closed the Public Hearing on this application and called for
 discussion. He stressed that this is a renewal of a special permit for outdoor dining and suggested they
 move forward in their discussion.

 Mr. Salerno said that he has no problem with this although pushing the time on the patio to 12:30 AM may
 be pushing it a bit much. He noted that no other outdoor dining in the area is in operation at that time and
 said that he would like to keep it to 11:30 PM and see how it goes for the actual first year.

 Mr. Peck said that he has no problem with the 12:30 AM time as the music is off at that time and people
 would have to be yelling pretty loud for noise to be a problem. He added that they spent $4M on a
 Boardwalk and $25,000 on the Yale Charrette study on how to bring business and people downtown and
 nothing works – but they put a bar/restaurant in downtown and all of a sudden they have a parking problem
 – he said that he has no trouble with the 12:30 AM closing time for the patio for the year.

 Mr. Dwyer noted that the police station is across the street and that they would certainly know if there is a
 problem and a lot of noise.

 Mr. Gada suggested that as they have endorsements for 11:30 PM and 12:30 AM closing times for the
 patio that they compromise at midnight for the first year and see how it goes.

 Mr. Nickerson said that sounds reasonable since the kitchen closes at 11 PM.

 Mr. Mulholland asked that they be specific on the terms of the renewal as they had been previously.
 The Commissioners discussed the terms/conditions and said that they have limited the patio to six (6)
 tables and that they would want to keep it that way and not have 50 people standing out on the patio. They
 were also interested in not having people standing out there consuming alcohol. They agreed that they
 would add a condition that would address this concern.

 Mr. Gada suggested that with regard to smoking that they could have three tables that would allow smoking
 and three that would to.
 Mr. Nickerson and Mr. Salerno said that while they might not have a problem with that, that Mr. Roche has
 testified that patrons would smoke at the sidewalk.

 Mr. Mulholland asked if speakers should be off during the winter and if they would want to allow them or
 not.
 Mr. Nickerson said that they have not put seasonal on the permit and that it seems to be fairly self-
 regulating.

 **MOTION (2)
 Mr. Salerno moved to approve the Application of Leo Roche for Strive LLC for a renewal of a
 Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at property identified in the application as 247-2 Main Street,
 Niantic, CT with the following conditions:
 a. The outside live acoustic music will not have an amp on the guitar, only the microphone and
     the microphone will be cut off at 8 PM.
 b. The soft, background, outside dinner music from the speakers will be shut off at 10 PM.
 c. The patio shall close one hour after the kitchen closes but no later than 12 midnight.
 d. The setback requirements will be waived to zero as per the site plan.
 e. A sign will be posted that there is “No Sitting on the Wall’ outside in the patio area.
 f. Patrons shall not use the patio area for consumption of alcohol unless seated at a table.
 Mr. Dwyer seconded the motion.

 Mr. Nickerson asked if they wanted anything about the front door being open as the inside noise could spill
 to the outside.
 Mr. Dwyer said that he has not heard of any of that happening when he has been by there and that they
 would have the AC on and not want the doors open.
 It was also noted that there is a double set of doors and that while the outer one may be open, there is
 another set that is closed.
 Mr. Nickerson called for a vote on the motion.

 Vote: 5 – 0 – 1. Motion passed.
