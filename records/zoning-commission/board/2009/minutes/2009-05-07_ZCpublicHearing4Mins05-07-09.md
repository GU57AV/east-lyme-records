Public Hearing I
1. Application of Melinda Graus for a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at property identified in the
Application as 334 Main Street, Niantic, CT.

Chairman Nickerson noted that the Legal Ad for this application had run in The Day on April 24, 2009 and
May 4, 2009.
He then asked Rosanna Carabelas, Secretary to read the correspondence into the record.

Ms. Carabelas read the following correspondence into the record:

♦ Letter dated 5/7/09 to EL Zoning Commission from William Mulholland, Zoning Official - Re: Application
of Melinda Graus for outdoor dining at Ice Cream Shop located at 334 Main Street – noting that this business
was recently approved a Special Permit to sell ice cream from a service window; they would like to place four
tables out on the lawn to allow customers to be seated while enjoying their ice cream.

Mr. Nickerson then called for the applicant or their representative to make their presentation.

Melinda Graus, 334 Main St., applicant; said that she is the owner of Lollipops & Gumdrops and that she just
opened an outdoor ice cream window and would like to put out a few tables and chairs for people to sit out
and enjoy their ice cream on the lawn. She said that she does know the regulations as she has gone over
them with Mr. Mulholland.

Mr. Salerno asked Mr. Mulholland if this would fall under the new regulations.
Mr. Mulholland said that those are not in effect yet so this permit would be for a one year term.

Mr. Nickerson explained the protocol for public comment and called for those from the public who wished to
speak in favor of the application.

Elaine Koziatek, 56 White Birch Circle said that she fully supports this application and feels that the addition
of outdoor tables would not be detrimental in any way.

Mr. Nickerson called for anyone wishing to speak against or neutrally on this application.
