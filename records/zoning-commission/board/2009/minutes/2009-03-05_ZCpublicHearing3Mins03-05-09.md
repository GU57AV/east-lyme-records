 Public Hearing I
 1. Application of Melinda Graus for a Special Permit for an Ice Cream window at the side and rear
    of her store located at 334 Main Street, Niantic, CT

 Acting Chairman Carabelas said that the Legal Ad for this application had run in The Day on February 20,
 2009 and March 2, 2009.

 She then asked Marc Salerno, Acting Secretary to read the correspondence into the record.
 Mr. Salerno read the following correspondence:

 ♦    Memo dated 3/5/09 to the East Lyme Zoning Commission from Wm. Mulholland, Zoning Official – Re:
     Special Permit for an Ice Cream Window at candy shop at 334 Main Street – noting that this falls under
     a classification of fast food restaurant and that at present there is no plan to install tables or outdoor
     seating although this could be applied for at a later time. It was also noted that the Commission may
     fully credit existing plantings in lieu of planting evergreen along the property line (Section 24.6 E-1).

 Ms. Carabelas called upon the applicant or their representative for a presentation of this application.
 Melinda Graus, 27 East Shore Drive said that she wants the special permit for an outdoor side window for
 ice cream and that there would be a new window in the front along Baptist Lane next to the sidewalk with a
 gravel base put down around the window area.

 Ms. Carabelas asked if there would be a walkway from the sidewalk to the window that would be of gravel.
 Ms. Graus said yes.

 Mr. Mulholland explained that Ms. Graus wants to be able to order equipment should she be allowed to do
 this and that at a later time she may come back and apply for a patio that would hold a few tables with
 umbrellas for people to eat their ice cream at. He noted also that this falls under fast food restaurant.

 Ms. Graus said that patrons would only be able to purchase ice cream from the outside window and not

 it from inside the candy shop. Perhaps, if this was successful, she said that she might also offer milk

 Ms. Carabelas called for comments from the public against or neutrally on this application –
 Hearing none –

 **MOTION (1)
 Mr. Salerno moved that this Public Hearing be closed.
