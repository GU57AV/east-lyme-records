           1. Chapman Farms Violation Update
Gary Goeschel, the East Lyme Planning Director, came forward and reminded the
Commission that he handled this item due to Mr. Mulholland's conflict; Mr. Mulholland lives
at Chapman Farms. He said he is here to follow up on this matter and provided the
Commission with his memorandum dated January 2nd,2020 (attached.)

Mr. Goeschel said he is satisfied with the new plantings and believes that the Association
is now in compliance. He observed that the downside is that Chapman Farms failed to
maintain the original plantings which actually won some landscaping awards when
created. Mr. Goeschel said an entire Management Plan has been formulated based on
Chapman Woods' own. He recommends that the violation be lifted.

Mr. Peck asked about the trees with the canopy row and Mr. Goeschel explained that they
were originally planted too close together.

MOTTON (3)
Mr. Donovan moved to lift the Chapman Farms Violation.
Ms. Thurlow seconded the motion.
Motion carried, 6-0-0.
