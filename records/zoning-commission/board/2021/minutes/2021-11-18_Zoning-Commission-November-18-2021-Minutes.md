Mr. Harris detailed his parking calculation (see attachment) and noted there is a lot of open
space not being used that has an immense impact on calculation in terms of area versus
parking. The approach he used is based on what they anticipate for actual use on the site
itself and the suggested total would be 90 spaces, which is actually the same total that
would result from the area calculation formula. They have a total of 120 spaces so they
have more spaces than are required.

Mr. Harris briefly discussed their sidewalk waiver request and explained that neither of the
previous businesses in this location had a sidewalk and there is a stretch of lawn in front of
the building that would meet this need. He further explained that given that this is light
industrial, this isn't a location where you want to encourage or attract pedestrian traffic.

able to utilize this facility for practice. He added that the military is currently using similar
technology and this technology is in fact military grade.
