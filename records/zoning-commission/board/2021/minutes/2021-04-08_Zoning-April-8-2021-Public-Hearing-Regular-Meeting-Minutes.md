Mr. Mulholland added that these are routine renewals and has had no problems with any of
these applicants and that in the case of La Llarona, they've had an existing permit for outside
dining and the new operator would like to expand it to the right side of the building to
accommodate three additional tables.

Mr. Donovan said the new operator cleaned up the outside dining area and it looks ten times
better than it did before. Mr. Mulholland said they also replaced the old beat up fence and Mr,
Goeschel showed the Commission a rendering of the area in question.

Mr. Mulholland said they're asking that this additional area to the right be a permanent part of
their outside dining, and he noted a new black iron fence surrounds the area. He added that in
his opinion this is somewhat of a housekeeping item but since it's an expansion, it requires the
Commission's endorsement.

Mr. Donovan noted the legal ad for these items have been run and is posted on the Town
website along with all the applications as per executive orders; all applications are outside
dining renewals with the exception of La Llarona, who is asking to expand the existing outside
dining permit along with renewing it.

   2. Comments from Zoning board liaison to Planning Commission
       a. Kalajainen - March gth,2021
       b. Peck- April 13th,2021

Ms. Kalajainen reported that she attended the March gth, 2021 Planning Commission
meeting and that the Commission wasn't happy that the Zoning Commission didn't honor
their request to wait for their review prior to voting on the FEMA text amendment.

Ms. Kalajainen said she told them if they're going to request that Zoning put off voting they
should include the reason why in their correspondence. She said the item in her mind was
cut and dry, but that going fonvard we should be sensitive to the requests from other
Commissions.

Mr. Donovan noted that when a Zoning Commission member attends a Planning
Commission meeting they're there as a liaison and shouldn't comment on the
proceedings.

Mr. Mulholland said text amendments and zone changes are referred by Statute to various
agencies including Planning, and they have a certain amount of days to consider it. He
said with all due respect to our friends in Planning, they had adequate time to consider the
amendment. Mr. Mulholland said perhaps the timing was off due to the Planning
Commission meeting dates.

Mr. Donovan reminded Mr. Peck that he is scheduled to attend the April 13tn,2021
Planning Commission meeting.
