           CAM-Memo-26-Bayview-Ave.pdf

    Note Craig Caulkins of Caulkins Homes explained that his firm has applied to construct a new home for
   Mr. and Mrs. Flanagan. He characterized the application as largely a housekeeping matter, noting that the
   project site is outside Flood Zone X.

    Note Silt fencing will be installed as shown on the plans, and the work is expected to have minimal impact
   on coastal area management. He directed Commissioners to the narrative and materials in the report,
   emphasizing that the proposal is routine and that no adverse impacts are anticipated. Standard mitigation
   measures will be in place from the start.

    Note Mr. Caulkins added that the builders were prepared to begin work quickly. He also acknowledged that
   the initial review identified a missing label on the map, which will be corrected.

    Decision MOTION (4)
   Ms. Jett-Harris moved to approve the application of Kevin B. and Kimberly Flanagan for a Coastal Area
   Management Site Plan Review to construct a single‑family dwelling at 26 Bay View Avenue because the
   application is consistent with all applicable goals and conditions of the CAM Act, and the applicants have
   taken all reasonable measures to mitigate any potential adverse impacts on coastal resources and future
   water‑dependent uses.
   Ms. Edwards seconded the motion.
   Motion carried, 6-0-0.
