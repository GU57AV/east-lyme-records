                As the Commission is aware, coastal site plan reviews are a state mandated program authorized
        in Chapter 444 of the Connecticut General Statutes. This statute delegates legal authority to Zoning
        Commissions to adopt regulations and regulate activities, not otherwise exempted, occurring above the
        CJL or coastal jurisdiction line as identified by the Connecticut DEEP and within the defined coastal
        boundary of their respective communities to protect coastal resources and ensure compliance with the
        CAM Act's coastal goals and policies. The Board is charged with the review of coastal site plans and
        may approve, modify, or deny activities proposed after evaluating the specific site, and considering the
        potential effects, both beneficial and adverse, ofthe activities on coastal resources and their consistency
        with applicable state coastal goals and policies. In acting on a coastal site plan, the Commission must
        state its Frndings and reasons lor the decision. To approve, the Board must find that the proposed activity
        is consistent with all applicable coastal policies and standards and whether or not any potential adverse
        impacts of the proposed activity on both coastal resources and future water development are acceptable.
                 In this application, Lucky Nine, LLC, is seeking CAM approval to repair an existing stone
        shoreline erosion control structure which has suffered minor deterioration over a number of years. The
        degradation of the structure is essentially from the natural and constant assault of the tbrce of nature.
        This project is viewed as a housekeeping matter to repair and manage the existing armored shoreline.
        The Connecticut DEEP and the New England Army Corps of Engineers have already issued permits for
        the project under their jurisdictions.
                The Applicant will give a brief presentation and will document how the proposed repairs comply
        with the CAM criteria. I note that our DEEP CAM contact has retired and has been replaced by Karen
        Michaels. Ms. Michaels has provided a coastal site plan review comment checklist, which is in the
        Commissioner's packets.
                In conclusion, the Board should consider the characteristics of the site including the location and
