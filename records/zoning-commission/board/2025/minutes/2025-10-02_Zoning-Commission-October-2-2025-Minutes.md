-The following is Q&A between the Commission and Mr. Lokken:

-Mulholland: What are hours of operation? Would there be cameras? Would there be staffonsite?
How is the waste handled?

-Lokken: Hours would be sunrise to sunset. No Cameras at first but if the need arose, they could be
installed. The park would not be staffed. The maintenance crew would come to cut grass, empty
trash etc. but not to staff the park. There would be "Mutt Mitts" available and people would be
responsible to clean up after their dog and throw into the trash which would be emptied be Parks &
Rec (P&R) staff regularly.

-Kalal: Reminder that it is responsibility of the Commission to think about the health and welfare
of all residents. Why was the right side of the driveway, which is not level, chosen as this slope is
at the bottom of the pond which feeds into Latimer Brook and into Long Island Sound. How will
you stop any fecal problems from going into Darrows Pond since I gram of feces contain 23
million coliform bacteria? How can you count on feces being policed if there isn't staff on site?
Will the picnic table currently there stay?
-Lokken: The dog park would be over 300 feet from the water source. The area would be confined
by fences, and any feces would be policed. He is available by phone at any time, and he would be
notified and would have it taken care of. Any pet owner would be contacted in the event of
aggressive dogs or persistent feces left. The owner would be asked to comply with the rules. Most
dog owners will be responsible users. The picnic table will stay but whether it will be in or out of
the fence hasn't been determined yet. A group of Girl Scouts are constructing agility equipment.
The right side of the driveway was chosen because of the noted presence of eastern cottontail on
the left side in an environmentally sensitive area and the right side is already mowed and
maintained.

-Markovitz: Will there be signage to include P&R contact information?

-Lokken: There is a list of suggested rules in Exhibit 2 but these rules could be amended

-Jett-Harris: Are nonresidents allowed to use the park? Will 10 parking spaces be enough? Have
you done a study on the traffic as it is frequently a full lot? Is there a plan to do any spraying?
Have you spoken to any veterinarians about their opinion on a dog park? Who will be in charge of
making sure dogs are vaccinated?

-Lokken: The park is open to nonresidents just like any other park in town. The usage now plus the
dog park should be fine with regard to parking. There are many other towns with dog parks and
East Lyme's would be one of many. Lokken was unsure what type of spraying Ms. Jett-Hanis was
asking about but a dog park would be pretty low maintenance and the area inside the fence where
the grass likely wouldn't thrive anyway. They did not survey local veterinarians. Best Practices
would be to make sure the dog is vaccinated when it goes to a dog park. The Town Clerk handles

-Jett-Harris: Are there any shade trees there? Will staff be going up daily to ensure feces inside and
outside the fence will be picked up?

-Lokken: There are pitch pines, and a legacy oak. There could be a shade structure at some point.
Staff will go up daily just like the other town park.

-Kalal: Will you tear out the current parking lot to put more spaces in? Who will lock and unlock
the fence? How did you decide on 6 feet for fence? This will not keep a deer out. Would water
bowls be provided?

-Lokken: The engineer was talking about putting in a pavement cut and putting in additional
spaces. There will be no lock, just posted hours of operation. If it was found that a lock needed to
be added that could happen. If a deer got in it would have to get itself out. There isn't city water or
a well nearby so owners would need to bring their own water.

-Mulholland: Would dogs be on leashes?
-Lokken: The ordinance is written that you need to be in control of your dog so that could be open
to interpretation, but generally speaking, dogs would be on leashes to get out of the car and then go
to the outer fence area which would be like a sally porl. Then you could unleash your dog and let
them into the park, off leash.

-Peck: Did you consider any other locations? Was McCook's Point considered, as many dog parks
are located in denser areas.

-Lokken: Vets Field, Roxbury Rd & Romagna Dr, Roxbury Rd & Roxbury Ct. McCook's was not
considered as the area is too heavily used for other things and taking away an acre for a dog park
wouldn't make sense.
-Liska: In traveling overseas, he noticed dogs are everywhere, he wondered if more and more
stores in East Lyme were allowing dogs in.

-Lokken: had no data on that.
