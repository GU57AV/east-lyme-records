                                   TOWN OF EAST LYME
                                   ZONING COMMISSION
                                     Notice of Decision

The East Lyme Zoning Commission took the following actions onApril 24,2025, at7:30 p.m. at
the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 PennsylvaniaAve, Niantic, CT:

       1. APPROVED: The East Lyme Zoning Commission proposal to rezone the existing RU
       80 Zoning District northerly of Interstate I-95, as identified on the official ZoningMap
       dated 21712024 on file with the Town Clerk, from its RU-80 designation to an RU-200
       designation.

       2. APPROVED: Accompanying the Zone Change is a proposed Text Amendment to
       create a new zoning district identified as Section 2A entitled RU-200. This section would
       regulate uses and dimensi,onal requirements in the new zone. The uses and dimensional
       requirements are the same as in the RU-80 district except for lot size.

       3. APPROVED: Application of Eric S. Parket Esq for Proposed Text Amendment to
       Section 20.26, by adding Subsection M, of the East Lyme ZoningRegulations.

All actions shall be effective May 2,2025.

Minutes can be viewed at eltownhall.com
Nancy Kalal,
Secretary
