             -Shhin Development owns property at the intersection of Main St and Haigh Ave.
             -The proposal seeks to permit mixed use development within new builds and is currently not a
             permitted use in the CB Zone, subject to Special Permit and certain minimum controls.
             -The Commission must consider the proposal's consistency with the POCD and the
             Comprehensive Plan for Zoning, which are the Regulations and the ZoningMap. The

             -There had been discussion among his client and with Mr. Mulholland regarding doing a

             -9.2.3: Special Permit Uses, would remove the "Existing Buildings Only" an open the door to

 -9.3.1: lot size requirements changed to simpli$ them and to make sure sufficient lot area to
 support mixed use. The proposal would allow existing buildings to have a minimum lot size of
 10,000 square feet and new buildings to have a minimum lot size of 20,000 square feet.
 -Because this proposal affects the entire CB Zone, the smaller lot size could benefit from a
 smaller requirement for development.
-25.5: The purpose, submission requirements, controls. The most important piece being the
design controls which, when submitted, must depict the building, the elevations, the floor plan,
the materials in a design and a building massing which reflects New England Village character.
It must be compatible with or enhance the surrounding neighborhood. If it does not, the
application would have to be denied. The submission would also require all existing
requirements under the current Site Plan and Special Permit regulations, as well as any other
information that the Commission may reasonably require to evaluate the proposal.
-11 additional specific controls (proposal 3 A-K) would be required for mixed use development
for new buildings.
-Minimum lot size:20,000 square feet.
- Residential density: 2,000 square feet.
-Number of bedrooms:maximum 2.
- First floor residential units would be allowed if there is no street access to them. This would
provide for ADA units and not require elevators.
-Parking in accordance with Section 22.
-Building height:minimum 2 stories, maximum 30 feet with potentially up to 10 additional
feet to allow for an attractive architectural feature (i.e. cupola, roofline, etc.).
-Municipal water & sewer must be provided
-Landscaping & lighting consistent with New England Village character
-All mixed use shall be designed as walkable and pedestrian oriented
-No building in excess of 20,000 square feet.
-Gross floor area of commercial use shall be 30% of the total gross floor area. For new
construction, residential use shall not occupy more than 50% of the first floor of any building.
