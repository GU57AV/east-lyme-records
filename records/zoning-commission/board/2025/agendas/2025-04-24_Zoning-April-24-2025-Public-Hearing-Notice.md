                             TOWN OF EAST LYME
                             ZONING COMMISSION
                             Notice of Public Hearing

The East Lyme Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on April 24,2025, at7:30
p.m. at the East Lyme Town Hall 108 Pennsylvania Ave Niantic to consider the
following:

l.     The East Lyme Zoning Commission proposal to rezone the existing
       RU 80 Zoning District northerly of Interstate I-95, as identified on the
       official Zoning Map dated 21712024 on file with the Town Clerk, from its RU-80
       designation to an RU- 200 designation.

2.     Accompanying the Zone Change is a proposed Text Amendment to create
       a new zoning district identified as Section 2A entitled RU-200. This
       section would regulate uses and dimensional requirements in the
       new zone. The uses and dimensional requirements are the same as in the
       RU-80 district except for lot size.

3.     Application of Eric S. Parker, Esq for Proposed TextAmendment to Section
       20.26, by adding Subsection M, of the East Lyme ZoningRegulations.

Copies of the proposals are available for review in the Town Clerk's Office and the
Land Use Office.
