Pauline Lord of 83 Upper Pattagansett Road, East Lyme said that she and her husband David Harlow own

area. They also intend to keep the noise under the decibel limit.
Mr. Gada asked if there would be any tents erected.

cook tent and a portolet tent.
Mr. Gada asked if any flooring or carpeting of some of the grass area would be done.
Ms. Lord said yes, a floor for dancing would be installed and taken up when done.

Mr. Mulholland said that he wants to make sure that the Commission focuses on this particular amendment
that is being presented with this application as should it be approved, it applies throughout the Town and at
this point in time, it is not site specific. lt would become site specific upon the request for a special permit for
this type of use and at that time these type of issues would be addressed on an individual basis.

the icehouse.
