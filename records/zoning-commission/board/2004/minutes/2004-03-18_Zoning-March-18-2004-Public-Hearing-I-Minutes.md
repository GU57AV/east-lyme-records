Public Hearing I
1. Application of Nevil C. Petrini for a Special Permit to locate a 2nd floor dwelling unit (apartment)
   over the existing marine office at {99 Oswegatchie Hills Road, Niantic, GT, Tax Map No 22.3, Lots
   33 and 27.

Mr. Gada seated Alternate memberWilliam Dwyer at the table.
Mr. Gada also noted that Ms. Carabelas would be the Acting Secretary forthe evening. He then asked her to
read the correspondence into the record for this application.

Ms. Carabelas, Acting Secretary read the following correspondence into the record:
I   Letter dated 3/16104 to EL Zoning Commission from Wm. Mulholland ZO - Re: Mixed Use Special
    Permit Application, 1 19 Oswegatchie Hills Road, Niantic, CT - finding that the current use of the property
    as a commercial madna with typicalancillary uses is conforming and given the existing on-site
    conditions, offering that the requested waivers might be appropriate.

Mr. Gada asked Mr. Petrini, the Applicant to give his presentation.
Nevil Petrini, applicant said that he and his wife Gloria were present this evening. He proceeded to hand out
to the Commission members a bound presentation folder entitled "special Permit Application Table of
Contents" containing 22 pages of information pertinent to the application. (This was later entered into the
record as Exhibit 1 and is aftached at the end of these minutes.)

Mr. Petrini explained the contents of the folder to the Commission. This included: an A-2 survey of the
property, certification that signs were placed and displayed on the property from 312104 announcing the
public hearing and application, warranty deed to the property, legal notice from the New London Day and
detailed explanation of the compliance with the Zoning regulations. He noted that the two-bedroom
apartment would not change any lines of the building and that it would be occupying area that has been used
to store documents. lt falls within the lot requirements and the space requirement. With respect to parking
spaces, there are two businesses there already and 125 parking spaces. He explained the reason for the
vvaiver request on the sidewalks, buffers and interior landscaping, noting that nothing is being altered or
changed.

Mr. Gada thanked Mr. Petrini for the great presentation and asked the Commission members if they had any
questions.
Ms. Carabelas asked what he would do for trash, are there dumpsters and would it be picked up.
Mr. Petrini said that he already has a S-yard hopper on site due to the people traffic and businesses there.
This is emptied weekly on Tuesdays. He would notify his tenant of this and when it is picked up.

Ms. Byrnes said that he had said that the boats are hauled up in the winter and stored and that she drives by
there everyday and it looks crowded. She asked how many parking spaces he has available during the
winter when the boats are being stored.
Mr. Petrini said that he has parking for 30-40 cars in front of the shop and that this is more than enough as
they do not have customer influx during the winter like they do in the summer. Further pages 18 and 19 of his
presentation are pictures that show the available parking spaces near the buildings.
Ms. Byrnes suggested that it would probably get pretty jammed up when the people come in the spring to
work on their boats and those spaces would not be enough.
Mr. Petrinisaid that he has not had that problem as he starts launching boats on April 1 and once he has
cleared two rows of boats, there are plenty of spaces for people to park. (Page 17 diagrams 240 available
parking spaces)
