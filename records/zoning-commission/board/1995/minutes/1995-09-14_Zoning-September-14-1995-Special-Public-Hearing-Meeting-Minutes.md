Chair Wayne L. Fraser called the meeting to order at7:33 p.m. Board members
present: Wayne L. Fraser (Chair), Paul Formica (Secretary), Sean Mclaughlin,
Christopher Mullaney, William Dwyer, Athena Cone; Alternates present: Norm
Peck, Kent Presley. William Mulholland, present.

Public Delegations: None

Public Hearing:

1     Continuation of the application of Robert T. Tobin, agent for Chapman Farms
      L.L.C., for a change of zone from RU-40 to S.U. (Special Use) for the
      pulpose of developing 65 units of elderly housing at 211, Pennsylvania Avenue,
      East Lyme Tax Assessor's Map 16.2, Lot 17.

      Correspondence: Secretary Paul Formica read into the minutes correspondence
      received.

      Attorney Robert Tobin and Angus McDonald (surveyor) made their
      presentation on this project covering issues of the access or emergency road,
      grading, roads within the project; Ben Orvedal (builder) discussed the phasing
      of the project and presented the actual lay-out of proposed homes. David
      Sullivan reviewed the fact that DOT requirements are met. Joanne Carroll
      (Long Island marketing firm rep) made a comparison to the successful Heritage
      Village in Southbury.

      The Board's questions covered stockpiling during construction (where and how
      much); noise from rock crushing; hours of operation; time frame.

      Questions from the public included the amount of ledge present throughout this
      site; access road usage; noise from the rock crushing; denseness of homes;
      traffic; elderly population in the area; opposition to the zone change rather than
      opposition to homes for the elderly; property value and actual land use; damage
      to nearby homes from the blasting; whether any state or federal money involved
      in this project.

There being no further discussion, the first section of the public meeting was closed at
9:06 p.m.
