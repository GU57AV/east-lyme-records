PUBLIC DELEGATION
Wayne Fraser, l't Selectman stated that the following review and discussion, staff is recommending
changes to the SU-E District and the Gateway Planned Development District.(GPDD). Mr. Fraser stated
that the GPDD was added approximately three years ago and he has been working with several potential
developers since that time. He indicated that if the Zoning Regulations as they stand present an obstacle
to the development of that District. He also indicated that the service road and the new exit off I-95, a
huge infrastructure endeavor and cost of approximately $3-4 million. The only way that this is going to
be supported and paid for by a developer is if retail, as a major component, helps pay for the
infrastructure. With respect to the GPDD District, the staff is recommending amending Section IlA.2.l
by deleting "No one store over 20,000 square feet." And adding to Section ll\.6.7 as indicated in the
handout to the members.

With respect to recommended amendment to the SU-E Elderly Housing Regulation, Mr. Fraser pointed
out the recommended deletions and additions to, Section I2A.2.l (Change lot size to l0 acres in area
from 20 acres and delete the rest of the paragraph), Section I2A.2.2 (Replace 500 feet frontage with 50
foot frontage), Section 12A.2.3 (replace existing text with "setbacks: No new building or structure shall
be placed less that 50 feet from a street line. A 50-foot buffer shall be required along all SU-E district
zone lines") changes to Section 12A.3.2(c),

Mr. Fraser stated that there is a lot of interest in smaller elderly housing developments. Additionally, he
indicated that the 500-foot frontage requirement precludes elderly housing developments on many tracts
of land that do not have the frontage, thus losing an opportunity for a new development in various areas
of town.

Mr. Peck stated that perhaps the existing setbacks should remain as part of the Regulation and allow for
a waiver of that requirement on a case by case basis.


Mr. Bulmer indicated that the Commission would review the recommendations and he would request
that Mr. Mulholland schedule a Public Hearing.
