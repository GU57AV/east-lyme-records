Mr. Mulholland stated the phrase "whenever possible" is encouraging the developers to locate

Mr. Nickerson stated the Commission wants to write regulations with language that does not severely
restrict developers to specific sites, as this would be counterproductive. The Commission wants to
encourage affordable housing and where possible in proximity to or in commercial districts and along
public transportation.
