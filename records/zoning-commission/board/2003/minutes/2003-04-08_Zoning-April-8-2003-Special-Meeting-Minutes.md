The East Lyme Zoning Commission held a SPECIAL MEETING on Thursday APRIL 8, 2003 at the

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

SPECIAL MEETING

Mr. Nickerson called the meeting to order. He indicated that the purpose of the Special Meeting was to
elect a member to fill the vacancy resulting from Athena Cone's resignation. At the Regular Meeting of
April 3, 2003 Mr. Dwyer and Mr. Bulmer were nominated, nominations were closed and the vote was
(2-2) tied.

Mr. Nickerson stated that Mr. Dwyer has withdrawn as a nominee.

Mr. Nickerson called for the vote for Mr. Bulmer to fill the vacancy on the Commission.

The vote was unanimous for Mr. Bulmer

Mr. Nickerson stated that Mr. Bulmer will resign as Alternate and be sworn in as a fill member of the
Commission after which the Commission will need to elect an Alternate to fill Mr. Bulmer's position
now vacated.
