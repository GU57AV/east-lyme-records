   Mr. Peck stated he thought the project would be an asset to the Town of East Lyme. He
   stated that the Zoning Commission may or may not have jurisdiction with respect to
   archaeological sites but noted that historically there has been a lot of activity around or on
   that site which has been documented. He stated he was concerned with the preservation of
   any historic site.


   Mr. Chamberlain agreed with Mr. Peck with respect to the possible archaeological
   importance of the area and inquired as to language, which could be used in a motion.
   Mr. Mulholland stated that the Commission could encourage the applicant, but not require,
   to be mindful of the possibility of historic artifacts on the site.

   Mr. Mclaughlin noted that the application conformed to Zoning Regulations.

   Mrs. Cone noted that the applicant is also requesting a waiver of the requirements for
   sidewalks and she supported the request.
