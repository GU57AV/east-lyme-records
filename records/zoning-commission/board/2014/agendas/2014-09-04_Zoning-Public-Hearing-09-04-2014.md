TOWN OF EAST LYME
ZONING COMMISSION
Notice of Public Hearing

The East Lyme Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on September 4, 2014 at 7:30
p.m., at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, CT, to consider the
following:

1. East Lyme Zoning Commission proposal to amend Section 20 General Regulations of the
East Lyme Zoning Regulations to add Section 20.29 Cluster Subdivision Setbacks.

2. East Lyme Zoning Commission proposal to amend Section 18 Signs, by removing
“Community Bulletin Boards” from Section 18.1.10, and adding Section 18.1.12 (subject
to renumbering of the entire section) “Community Bulletin Boards”.

Copies of the specific proposals are available for public viewing in the offices of the Town Clerk

and the Zoning Official.
