OLD BUSINESS
1. ZONING REGULATIONS REVIEW

They have not met. They did get through all of the Regulations and they will try to meet in October to
review all of the changes. They tentatively scheduled a meeting for October 20" at 7:00 p.m.

NEW BUSINESS

1. ANY BUSINESS ON THE FLOOR, IF ANY BY THE MAJORITY VOTE OF THE COMMISSION
There was no business on the floor.

2. ZONING OFFICIAL

Chairman Salerno stated Mr. Mulholland was not here tonight because his mother passed away. He
sent the Board’s sympathy to him.

3. COMMENTS FROM EX-OFFICIO
Ms. Cheeseman stated the Board of Selectmen met last night and had an Executive Session. They then
had a Regular Meeting where they authorized Mr. Formica to sign on with CCM for an electric rate if it is
in the best interest of the Town. They performed final budget transfers, they approved an appropriation
for a fiber optic network, and approved money for additional police radios, and they authorized the
Board of Education to apply for a Grant to re-roof the Central Office Building. The water
interconnection is just about finished. The steel retaining walls have started to be placed at the
Boardwalk.

4. COMMENTS FROM ZONING BOARD LIAISON TO PLANNING COMMISSION
Mr. Lukas did not attend. Mr. Dwyer will attend on the 7°.

5. COMMENTS FROM CHAIRMAN
Chairman Salerno had no comments.

Motion (3) Mr. Donovan moved to adjourn the meeting at 7:55 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Dwyer.

Motion Passed 6-0.
