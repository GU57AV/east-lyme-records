Mr. Mullholland will work on this item and will come up with some language they may want to use, they

(Special Permit Uses) and then 2.A.2 will become 2.A.3 (Dimensional Requirements).

Section 2.A.1.4 Small Animal Veterinarian Clinic should replace Veterinarian.

It was decided that they will replace ALL Veterinarian and Veterinarian Hospital, with “Small Animal
Veterinarian Clinic” throughout all of the Regulations.
