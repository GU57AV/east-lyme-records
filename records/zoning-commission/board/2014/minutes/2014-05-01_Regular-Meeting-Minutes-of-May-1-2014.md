9. ZONING OFFICIAL
Mr. Mulholland stated that Henny Penny is moving along, they are getting ready for the tanks, and the
outdoor canopy. They are working on cleaning up the landscaping across from the Midway Mall. 38
Hope Street has applied for the main center building. The owners across from 38 Hope street are in

discussions for a new buyer. Gateway is moving along, there is no action on the commercial side yet.
The Zoning Department has taken in $90,000 so far this year.

Mr. Donovan asked about the Habitat house.

Mr. Mulholland stated something may have happened with the family they signed up. They have a year
to build based on the building permit.

Chairman Salerno stated he has heard complaints about a business downtown selling furniture outside.
Mr. Mulholland stated he was there the other day and discussed it with him.
Mr. Donovan asked about Crest Ford having a permit to park their vehicles on the lot next door.

Mr. Mulholland stated they do not have a permit, and he has sent letters and met with them. The next
step would be a Cease and Desist Order.

10. COMMENTS FROM EX-OFFICIO

Ms. Cheeseman stated the Board of Selectmen had a Special Meeting last week; most of the meeting
was taken up with discussion of golf carts. They did approve golf carts, with the exclusion of Giants Neck
Beach, Giants Neck Heights, Pine Grove, and Attawan Beach. There were a number of people who came
up after the meeting that weren’t in favor of the golf carts, despite what their Board of Governors said.
They are still waiting to hear from Amtrak on the boardwalk revetment. The boat ramp has been fixed.

Mr. Donovan stated they have started to do work on an emergency access road from Giants Neck to
Rocky Neck.
