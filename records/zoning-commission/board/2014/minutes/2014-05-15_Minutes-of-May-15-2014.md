1. Application of Leo Roche, for Strive LLC, for a renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining
at property identified in the application as 247-2 Main Street, Niantic.

Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to approve the Application of Leo Roche for Strive LLC for a
renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at property identified in the application as 247-2 Main
Street, Niantic CT with the following conditions:

a. The outside live acoustic music will not have an amp on the guitar; only the microphone and
the microphone will be cut off at 8:00 p.m.

The soft, background, outside dinner music from the speakers will be shut off at 10:00 p.m.
The patio shall close one hour after the kitchen closes but no later than 12:30 a.m.

The setback requirements will be waived to zero as per the site plan.

A sign will be posted that there is “No Sitting on the Wall” outside in the patio area.

Patrons shall not use the patio area for consumption of alcohol unless seated at a table.

4. Comments from Ex-Officio

Ms. Cheeseman stated the Board of Selectmen met last week. They signed the Call for the Town
Meeting. Ms. Hardy talked about putting together a business listing, and she is working with the
Assessor’s office on that. The Referendum to vote on the budget is next Thursday from 8:00 a.m. to
8:00 p.m. They are still waiting on Amtrak for the Boardwalk.

Ms. Cheeseman will find out for their next meeting.

5. Comments from Zoning Board Liaison to Planning Commission

Mr. McPherson went to the April 1* meeting, and they discussed a subdivision based on the
Conservation by Design Regulations, which never got Town Attorney approval.

Mr. Mulholland stated the Town Attorney doesn’t need to approve it. This application is the first one
under the Regulation. When you write a complex Regulation you try to analyze it best you can. Some
questions have arisen. They have decided to ask him because the question has to do with the Zoning
Regulations. He is attempting to assist Planning.
