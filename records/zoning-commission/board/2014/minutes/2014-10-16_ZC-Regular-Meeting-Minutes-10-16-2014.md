1. APPLICATION OF THE TOWN OF EAST LYME, BOARD OF EDUCATION, FOR A SPECIAL PERMIT
FOR A COMMUNITY BULLETIN BOARD AT PROPERTY IDENTIFIED IN THE APPLICATION AS 171
BOSTON POST ROAD, EAST LYME, KNOWN AS EAST LYME HIGH SCHOOL, 30 CHESTERFIELD

Chairman Salerno stated he would like movement being blocked out and the minimum 10 second run
time on text being part of the Motion, and blocking out graphics.

Motion (1) Mr. Donovan moved to approve the application of the Town of East Lyme, Board of
Education, for a Special Permit for a Community Bulletin Board at property identified in the
application as 171 Boston Post Road, East Lyme, known as East Lyme High School, 30 Chesterfield
Road, East Lyme. With the following conditions:

- The changeable copy shall have a minimum duration of ten seconds and must be a static
display. No portion of the message may flash, scroll, twirl, change color, fade in or out, or in
any manner imitate movement

- The background portion of the bulletin board’s display shall not contain any illumination that
moves or changes in intensity

- The bulletin board shall display only written text.

Seconded by Mr. McPherson.
Motion Passed 6-0.
