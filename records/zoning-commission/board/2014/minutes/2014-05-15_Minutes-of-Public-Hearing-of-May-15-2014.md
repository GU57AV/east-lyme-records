Public Delegations

There were no Public Delegations.

1. Application of Leo Roche, for Strive LLC, for a renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining
at property identified in the application as 247-2 Main Street, Niantic.

Mr. Mulholland confirmed the legal advertisement had run on this item.

Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Mr. Roche was present. He stated he would like all of the conditions to stay the same. Last year they
added more flower boxes to the wall, and the situation improved as far as keeping people off of the
wall. He spoke to Mr. Mulholland and Chris from the Fire Marshal's office about roping an area off, and
it could become an egress situation.

Chairman Salerno asked that he and his managers keep their vigilance up.

Mr. Mulholland stated there were no complaints on this Permit.

There was no public comment.
Motion (1) Mr. Donovan moved to close the Public Hearing.
