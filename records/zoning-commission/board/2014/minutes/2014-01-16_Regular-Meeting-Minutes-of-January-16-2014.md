2. Application of the Town of East Lyme, for a Coastal Area Management Site Plan Review for
creation of a lay down area for construction equipment and materials and a proposed 12’

wide construction access way at McCook’s, and the reconstruction of approximately 2800’
feet of Niantic Boardwalk; aka Boardwalk Reconstruction Project.

Mr. Walker read a letter from Mr. Mulholland.
Victor Benni, Town Engineer presented Exhibit A: Aerial View of Site.

Victor Benni represented the applicant. They are applying to make improvements to the Boardwalk and
build an access way through McCook’s. The access will be from Columbus Avenue through McCooks to
the Boardwalk improvement area. The work will be adjacent to the Shoreline and coastal hazard areas,
near shore waters and beaches and dune areas. The relocating the Boardwalk will include relocating
stone, driving steel piles, and building a new walkway. The new design will be 2.5 feet higher than the
original boardwalk.

Mr. Benni presented Exhibit B: Comparative Cross Section
The steel sheet pile will be put in and there will be a concrete cap over the concrete walkway. It will be
11 feet wide. There will be a chain link fence between the boardwalk and the train track. The steel

sheet piles will be 30 feet long.

Mr. Benni presented Exhibit C: Typical Cross Section Sheet 1 of 5

1
The steel sheet pile and the concrete will take wave forces.

Bill Scheer, the Assistant Director of Public Works stated this is designed to meet 100 year storm
standards, this will also provide better handicap access.

Mr. Benni stated the access from McCooks will remain in place after the construction is done. The
continued path from under the tracks at Hole in the Wall to McCooks will be a better use. There is
American Beach Grass that will be replanted after construction is done. They did have a soil scientist
look at this and he instructed them on what should be done. The work will be from this March to
December. The Hole in the Wall Beach will remain open, and will be gated with a Town Employee
leading people across when they are working on the Hole in the Wall end; they are hoping to keep it
open throughout the season. All work will be done from the shore. The sheet piles will be the barrier
between the work and the water, they will not need any back fill, the builders will utilize a system for
removing and stockpiling what we need, on the front side of the sheet piles they will put stone in the
holes and cover them with sand.

Mr. Scheer stated the only thing removed will be construction and demolition garbage left over. There
is a provision in the contract that the contractor will relinquish the area during Celebrate East Lyme and
give the town full access.

Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to approve the application of the Town of East Lyme, for a
Coastal Area Management Site Plan Review for creation of a lay down are for construction equipment

and materials and a proposed 12’ wide construction access way at McCook’s, and the reconstruction
of approximately 2800’ feet of Niantic Boardwalk; aka Boardwalk Reconstruction Project.

REASONS:
1. Application is consistent with all applicable goals and conditions of the CAM Act.
2. Applicant has taken all reasonable measures to mitigate any adverse impacts of the proposed
activity on both coastal resources and future water dependent uses.
Seconded by Mr. Dwyer.

Motion Passed 6-0.

This item will be published on Thursday, January 23 and be effective Friday, January 24m.

3. Application of Keith Neilson, Docko, Inc., Agent for a Coastal Area Management Site Plan

Review for shoreline protection improvements at 279 Old Black Point Road, Niantic,
Connecticut.

Mr. Walker read a letter from Mr. Mulholland.

Keith Neilson presented the application. This property is in the southeast corner of Old Black Point. It
does have large stone in front of it. The smaller stone has dislodged and scattered. He has
recommended 7 to 8 ton stones, the work will be landward of the CT Coastal Jurisdiction line, DEEP and

the Army Corps of Engineers have issued permits. The work will be done from land, a truck will deposit

2
the stones from land, and an excavator will place them from land. The land will be re-turfed in spring
when growing conditions are suitable. A lot of work will be done quickly when the ground freezes. If it
doesn’t freeze they will build a gravel drive. There are no tidal wetlands on site, it is stony shore front
and the character of the beach will not change.
Motion (2) Mr. Donovan moved to approve the application of Keith B. Neilson, PE, of Docko Inc.,
for Coastal Area Management Review for shoreline protection improvements at property identified in
the application as 279 Old Black Point Road, Niantic, CT.
REASONS:

1. Application is consistent with all applicable goals and conditions of the CAM Act.

2. Applicant has taken all reasonable measures to mitigate any adverse impacts of the proposed

activity on both coastal resources and future water dependent uses.

Seconded by Mr. McPherson.
Motion Passed 6-0.
