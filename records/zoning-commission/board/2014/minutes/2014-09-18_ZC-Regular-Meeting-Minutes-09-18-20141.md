1, REMAND ORDER DATED JUNE 26, 2014, IN THE MATTER OF JAG CAPITAL DRIVE, LLC v. EAST

LYME ZONING COMMISSION DOCKET NUMBER HHD LND CV 13-6044284S, FOR THE LIMITED
PURPOSE OF A FACTUAL HEARING ON THE DAY TO DAY OPERATIONS OF THE BRIDE BROOK

NURSING AND REHABILITATION CENTER.

Mr. Dwyer stated the Zoning Commission sets the ground rules. The Regulations state what
convalescent homes are, nowhere is residential mentioned in the definition or permit.

Attorney Zamarka stated as noted in his opening comments during the Public Hearing, the Order is not
specific regarding the factual hearing. The Zoning Commission is the finder of fact.

Mr. Dwyer stated he would not be allowed to go live there; he would need a doctor recommendation to
get in.

Chairman Salerno stated he agreed and people can’t come and go as they please.

Mr. McPherson stated it is locked down, there are no interior locks, they applied as a convalescent
home, and it is not designated as residential. It is a convalescent and rehabilitation center.

Chairman Salerno stated the units don’t have kitchens or showers. He recommended a Resolution.
Mr. Peck stated this is an affordable housing application. Affordable housing does not have 24 hour
nursing. There is heavy use, visitation, large staff and a large parking lot.

Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to approve the following Resolution:

Be it therefore resolved that based on evidence presented pursuant to the Court’s Remand Order
dated June 26, 2014 the East Lyme Zoning Commission hereby determines that the Bride Brook
Nursing and Rehabilitation Center is not a residential use pursuant to Section 8-30g(g)(2)(A) of the
General Statutes.

Seconded by Mr. Peck.

Motion Passed 6-0.
