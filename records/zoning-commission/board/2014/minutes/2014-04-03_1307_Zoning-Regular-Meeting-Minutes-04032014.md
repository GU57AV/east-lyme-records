REGULAR MEETING

1. Discussion regarding Town of East Lyme Zoning Commission potential proposal for a Zoning
Text Amendment to Section 20.27 Outdoor Dining.

Mr. Donovan stated he, Mr. Dwyer, and Mr. McPherson were on the outdoor dining subcommittee, they
prepared several drafts, each member was given a new draft tonight with all changes highlighted.
The members reviewed the draft presented to them for this meeting.
The following revisions were made to the draft:

- The opening paragraph’s last sentence should read “Such dining shall be subject to a Special
Permit for establishments that serve alcoholic beverages. (see Section 25 “Special Permits”).

- Paragraph 4, there should be a period after restaurant, and the word and should be removed.

- Paragraph 6, the last sentence the words on to should be changed to onto.

- Paragraph 7 should read: The Zoning Commission may designate closing times for outdoor
dining areas. The commission may permit entertainment and shall limit such entertainment,
including hours and operation. Outdoor public address system and outdoor speakers may be
permitted during designated hours as approved by the Zoning Commission.

- Paragraph 10 should read: Not withstanding Section 9.3.3 the Zoning Commission may waive
the setback requirements for outdoor dining areas. (See Section 20.27.13A) A waiver of
setbacks for establishments where alcohol is not served may be granted as an administrative
action and shall not require a special permit or public hearing. (See Section 20.27.13B)

- Paragraph 11 was removed so any subsequent paragraph will need to be renumbered.

- Paragraph 11 should have a comma after areas.

- Paragraph 13 will be renamed Permitting Process

- Paragraph 13a should read: The special permit for establishments that serve alcoholic beverages
under this section shall be valid for one year from the date of approval and may be renewed for
a period not to exceed one (1) year subject to an approval of a special permit.

- Paragraph 13b should read: Permit for establishments that do not serve alcoholic beverages
shall be valid for three years from the date of approval and may be renewed subject to an
approval of a permit by the Zoning Official.

- The final section that was submitted by Mr. Dwyer regarding Suspension shall not be added.

- The word liquor should be replaced by alcoholic beverages throughout the Section

- The word patio shall be changed to outdoor dining areas throughout the section.

Chairman Salerno will have this scheduled for a public hearing as amended.
