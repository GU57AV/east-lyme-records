1. APPLICATION OF THE TOWN OF EAST LYME ZONING COMMISSION FOR AN AMENDMENT TO
THE EAST LYME ZONING REGULATIONS, SECTION 20.20.8 LIQUOR, REGARDING THE
PROXIMITY TO LOCATION OF PLACES OF WORSHIP, TO REDUCE THE “200 FEET RADIUS” TO
100 FEET.

Chairman Salerno stated this will change Section 20.20.8, Mr. McPherson’s comments will be addressed
by the Zoning Regulations Review Sub-Committee.

Mr. Walker stated this is an excellent modification and it doesn’t make sense to restrict restaurants.

Motion (1) Mr. Donovan moved to approve the following amendment to Section 20.20.8 of the
East Lyme Zoning Regulations:

PROXIMITY TO PLACES OF WORSHIP; NOTWITHSTANDING SECTION 20.20.6 AND 20.20.7 ABOVE, FOR
CB ZONES, NO PREMISES CAN BE USED FOR THE SALE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES IF THE BUILDING OR

PREMISES IS WITHIN 100 FEET OF A CHURCH, SYNAGOGUE, OR OTHER BUILDING USED AS A REGULAR
PLACE OF RELIGIOUS WORSHIP.

Seconded by Mr. Walker.

Motion Passed 5-1-0 (Mr. McPherson — Nay)
Mr. McPherson stated he is not against reducing the proximity to 100 feet, he just feels the entire
section should be amended together.

This will be published on November 13, 2014 and will become effective on November 14, 2014.

2. REQUEST OF J. ROBERT PFANNER TO AMEND THE PREVIOUSLY APPROVED SITE PLAN FOR 23
CHESTERFIELD ROAD, EAST LYME, CONNECTICUT, SUBWAY RESTAURANT, TO PERMIT THE
CONSTRUCTION OF A DECK.

Chairman Salerno stated this was originally approved for a patio and not a deck.
Mr. Pfanner submitted Exhibit A — Site Plan.

Mr. Pfanner stated they have a handicapped ramp from the back and stairs in the front. They still have
the same plantings that were approved.

Chairman Salerno stated the original plan had a patio, which is why Mr. Pfanner had to come for a
modification request.

Motion (2) Mr. McPherson moved to approve the request of J. Robert Pfanner to amend the
previously approved site plan for 23 Chesterfield Road, East Lyme, Connecticut, Subway Restaurant,
to permit the construction of a deck.

Seconded by Mr. Donovan.

Motion Passed 6-0.
