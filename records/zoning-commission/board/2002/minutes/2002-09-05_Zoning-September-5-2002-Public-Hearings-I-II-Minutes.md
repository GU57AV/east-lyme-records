Thomas Londregan, representing John Lombardi addressed the Commission. He submitted a
photograph (Exhibit 1A) of the sign posted on the property for fifteen days. He added that the applicant
has no objection to the requirement that the bond be held separately and he submitted the bond estimate

Mrs. Cone read Exhibit 1C into the record at Mr. Dwyer's request.

Mr. Londregan stated that this permit goes back several years. The applicant currently wishes to
stabilize the property and make it a productive subdivision. The Planning Commission has approved
nineteen lots subdivision of varying sizc lots (2-70 acres lots with 30% open space). Much of the
material ( approx. 12,000 cy) on the property can be used for the subdivision (approx. 20,000 cy total).
The excess of approximately 8,000 cy would be moved off-site. The applicant is requesting 1-year
extension of his permit or until the first certificates of occupancy is issued for the site. No gasoline will
be stored on-site.

Ms. Bartlett, Principle Engineer, CLA Engineering stated that the site consisted of 232 acres and the
excavation site consists of approximately 3.5 acres. The excavation site will be finished to blend with
the contours of the existing terrain. She presented drawings of the site and grading of the excavation
site. The applicant is requesting exceptions to the landscape buffer (Section 24.68) and sidewalks
(Section 24.6C) along Route 161 due to the proximity of Latimer Brook.

Ms. Bartlett indicated that a new bridge is being constructed over Latimer Brook. The subdivision
provides for 30Yo open space.

There be no further speakers in favor or opposed to the applicaton.
