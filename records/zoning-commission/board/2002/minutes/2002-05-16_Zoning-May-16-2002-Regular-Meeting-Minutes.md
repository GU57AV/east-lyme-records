4. Application of Landmark Development Group, LLC to amend the East Lyme Zoning
   Regulations by creating a new Section 32 entitled "Affordable Housing".

Mr. Nickerson stated that the Commission has not received the written charge by Atty. E.
O'Connell as yet. Mr. Mulholland stated that Mr. O'Connell has not had the opportunity in the past
week to complete the written charge, but will get it to the Commission as soon as possible.

Mr. Nickerson inquired if the transcript would be available to the Commission of the Public
Hearings. Mr. Mulholland stated the transcript is in process and would get copies to the
Commission as soon as possible.

Mr. Peck inquired if there is a denial, reasons must be given and how much detail must be included
with the reasons, i.e. referencing the transcript of the Public Hearing or general reasons. Mr.
Mulholland stated that Mr. O'Connell would address this issue and others in his written charge. Mr.
Mulholland will be meeting with Atty. O'Connell in a week to clarify several issues.

Mr. Nickerson suggested the Commission discuss in general how to proceed and organize material
for deliberation without specifically addressing either application at this time. He noted that
members of the Commission might raise questions that Mr. Mulholland can convey to Mr.
O'Connell.

Mr. Mulholland recommended that the Commission be in possession of the written charge and
invite Mr. O'Connell to be present in order to put questions directly to him for his legal advice. Mr
Nickerson requested comment from the members on this matter.

Mr. Bulmer stated that in his opinion, there are very complex issues in this matter and that the
Commission would need the written charge from Atty. O'Connell, as well as his presence during
deliberations to respond to questions in provide interpretation as needed. He also noted that there
are 45 Exhibits, which the Commission should have before them when deliberating, as well as the
transcript, which the Commission does not have yet.
