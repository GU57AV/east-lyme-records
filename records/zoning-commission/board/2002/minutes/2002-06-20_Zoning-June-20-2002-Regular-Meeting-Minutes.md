Mr. Nickerson indicated that the Zoning Commissions responsibility in a CAM site plan review
application is to assess whether the applicant has demonstrated compliance with policies and goals
of the act and whether all reasonable measures to minimize any adverse impact from the proposed
development have been taken. Local shoreline towns must review certain types of activities and
development and assess their impact on coastal resources such as tidal rivers, wetlands, beaches,
dunes, bluffs, escarpments, etc.

 Mrs. Cone stated that the public, as well as herself, raised questions during the public hearing that
did not relate directly to the CAM Report, but were general information questions or are the
jurisdiction of other Commissions and Agencies.

Mr. Peck stated that he opposed approving the application because now and in the future, beaches
are more valuable than boardwalks. He stated he is also opposing the application based on
reduction ofbeach size and thus public access to the beach.




MOTION (3): Mrs. Cone moved to approve the application of the Town of East Lyme for a
coastal site plan review to construct the Niantic Bay Overlook Walkway and Boardwalk on property
along the Niantic waterfront from the eastern edge of McCook's Point approximately 5,475 feet
easterly to the Amtak Railway Bridge.
Mr. Gada seconded the motion.
Vote in favor: (5-1), Mr. Peck voted in opposition to the motion. Votes in favor: Mr. Nickerson,
Mr. Mclaughlin, Mr. Gada, Mrs. Cone and Mr. Salerno
REASONSL 1. Application is consistent with all applicable goals and policies of the CAM act and
                   incorporates all reasonable measures which would mitigate the adverse impacts of
                   the proposed activity on both coastal resources and future water-dependent
                   development uses. Complies with policies and goals of CAM.
                  2. Project provides public access.
Mr. Nickerson stated for the record that the Zoning Commission, although not required, had decided
to go to a Public Hearing on this application.
