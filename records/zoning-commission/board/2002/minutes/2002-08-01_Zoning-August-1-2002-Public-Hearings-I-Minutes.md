                    EAST L




The East Lyme Zoning Commission held a PUBLIC HEARING I on Thursday AUGUST 1,2002 at the
East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Ave., Niantic, CT.

Mr. Mark Nickerson, Chairman, opened the Meeting at 7:30 PM.

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Ed Gada, Norman Peck, Athena Cone, Alternate Marc Salerno,
              Alternate William Dwyer and Alternate Robert Bulmer

Absent:       David Chamberlain, Shawn Mclaughlin.

Also present: William Mulholland,Zoning Official; Meg Parulis, Planning Director

Panel: Mr. Nickerson, Mr. Peck, Mrs. Cone, Mr. Gada, Alternate Dwyer and Alternate Salerno.

PUBLIC HEARING I AND II

PUBLIC HEARING I
Continufltion of the Public Henring on the applicution of Theodore A. Huruis to amend the East
LJtme Zoning Reeulations to permit "landscaping"facilities in RU40 and RU80 Residential Zoning
Districts.

Panel: Mr. Nickerson, Mr. Peck, Mr. Gada, Mr. Salerno and Mr. Dwyer

Mr. Nickerson opened the Public Hearing and noted that this is a continuation of the Public Hearing of
July 11,2002. He invited Mr. Harris to address the Commission.

Mr. Harris stated that he has made modifications to the existing proposal and distributed those changes
to the Commission members. First, he noted the existing Special Permit Uses in RtI40 and RU80
Zoning Districts. Second, Mr. Harris reviewed the modifications.
         1. Earth products and quantity: 1000 cu. yd. maximum, no more than 200 cu. yds. per pile and
            no more than 50-ft in height. Subject to further setback or screening restrictions by the
            Commission.
        2. Building: All equipment stored in the building. Maximum of 5,000 sf building with the
            ability to increase up to 10,000 sf in total at the rate of 1,000 sf per acre. Commission may
            limit the size depending on neighborhood, topography or other circumstances.
        These safeguards are not in the other Special Permit Uses currently allowed in the zones.

Mr. Harris stated that "landscaping facility" is not intended to be a earth materials processing plant and
no crushing is allowed and no box trailers are allowed. The facility would have the potential for
growing plants, storing earth materials and equipment storage building.

Mr. Dwyer indicated that these modifications address his previous concerns. He added that the Planning
Commission comments do not, however, apply to these modifications and recommended that these
modifications be referred to Planning Commission for comment.

Mr. Nickerson noted that the Planning Commission had recommended denial of the application as stated
in their letter of June 5,2002.

Mr. Hanis stated that he would grant an extension if the Commission decided to refer the modifications
to the Planning Commission.

Following a two minute recess, Mr. Mulholland indicated that the next Planning Commission meeting
was scheduled for August 13,2002.

MOTION (1):             Mr. Gada moved to continue the Public Hearing on the application of Theodore
          A. Harris to amend the East Lyme Zoning Regulations to permit o'landscaping" facilities in
          RU40 and RU80 Residential Zoning Districts in order to refer to the East Lyme Planning
          Commission for their opinion modifications to the original application.
          Motion #1 seconded by Mr. Dwyer.
          Vote in favor: (5-0), Mr. Nickerson, Mr. Peck, Mr. Gada, Mr. Salerno and Mr. Dwyer.
          (Unanimous)

Mr. Peck, in referencing Proposed Regulation Section 25.5.3A, suggested "Zoning Commission may
require that all equipment be stored in the building." Mr. Harris stated he had no objection to this
change.

Mr. Peck also stated his concern regarding the building appearance. He suggested adding, " Building
design shall be in harmony with the surrounding area."

Mr. Dwyer inquired how the Zoning Commission could establish and require a particular building size.

Mr. Salerno inquired if manure was considered o'earth materials". Mr. Harris stated that it was
landscaping materials.

Mr. Peck read from the East Lyme ZoningRegulations, Section 25.4.I, Special Permit. "The location
and size of the use, the nature and entity of the operation involved, the size of the site in relation to it
and the location of the site with respect to the existing and future streets giving access to it, shall be such
that it will be in harmony with the orderly development of the District and the location, nature, and
height of building, walls and fences will not discourage the appropriate development and use of adjacent
land and buildings or impair the value thereof."
