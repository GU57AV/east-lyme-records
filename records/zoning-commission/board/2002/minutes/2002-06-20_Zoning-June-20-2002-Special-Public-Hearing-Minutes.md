The East Lyme Zoning Commission held a PUBLIC HEARING on Thursday JUNE 20,2002 at the
East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Ave., Niantic, CT.

Mr. Mark Nickerson, Chairman, opened the Meeting at 7:30 PM.

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Athena Cone, Shawn Mclaughlin, Ed Gada, Norman Peck, Alternate
              Robert Bulmer and Alternate Marc Salerno, Alternate William Dwyer.
Absent:       David Chamberlain, William Mulholland, Zoning Official
Also present: Rose Ann Hardy, Ex officio; Meg Parulis, Planning Director

Panel: Mr. Nickerson, Mr. Mclaughlin, Mr. Peck, Mr. Gada, Mrs. Cone and Alternate Mr. Salerno.

PUBLIC HEARING

The application of the Town of East Lyme for a coastal site plan review to construct the Niantic
Bay Overlook Walkway and Boardwalk on property along the Niantic waterfront from the
eastern edge of McCook's Point approximately 51475 feet easterly to the Amtrak Railway Bridge.

Mr. Nickerson opened the Public Hearing at 7:30 PM, read the call and noted the Legal Notice had
been duly published.

Mrs. Cone read into the record:
       1. Memo from Zoning Official William Mulholland to the Zoning Commission dated June 20,
          2002.
      2. Letter from DEP, Marcy Balint, Sr. Coastal Planner, Department of Environmental
          Protection, State of Connecticut dated Jun 17,2002.

Mr. Nickerson invited Planning Director Meg Parulis to present the CAM Report.

Ms. Parulis distributed a copy of the report to the Commission. She stated that she has been involved
with the boardwalk project for several years. She stated that Don Gerwick, PE was hired to prepare the
CAM report, but due to unforeseen scheduling conflicts is unable to be at the meeting this evening.

Ms. Parulis described the project utilizing a number of drawings and photographs. She indicated that
the least environmental impact involved a combination of stabilized granular walkway (ADA
approved) and wooden boardwalk. The boardwalk, l4-ft. wide, was designed so if or when Amtrak

Ms. Parulis noted that the DEP letter from Marcy Balint, Sr. Coastal Planner touched on many of the
major issues. She added that any activity 100-ft. landward of the high is anareanot within the DEP
jurisdiction for review. She noted that this is the prime area for mitigation. DEP did note that the
beach sandwort is a species of concern and will advise the Town on how to relocate and plant so as to
preserve and protect the species.

The wooden porlion of the boardwalk is above the flood elevation and a plan will be in effect if there is
a weather event that poses a safety issue. Visually, the boardwalk from a distance will blend into the
stone embankment.

The application was referred to the Harbor Management Commission. The only concern in terms of
impact on shellfish is in the area of the temporary fill and was not considered a major impact.

As a precautionary measure during construction, there will be floating erosion control in the water.

Mr. Nickerson opened the hearing for public comment.

Marvin Schutt, 29 Edgewood No., Niantic stated that he served on the design committee and was
pleased with the presentation. He added that some of the questions that he came into the hearing with
have been answered. He supported approval of the application.

Mr. Nickerson invited comment in favor of the application. There being none, he invited those in
opposition to the application to address the hearing. There being none, he invited a motion to close the
public hearing.

MOTION (1): Mrs. Cone to close the Public Hearing on the application of the Town of East Lyme
       for a coastal site plan review to construct the Niantic Bay Overlook Walkway and Boardwalk
       on property along the Niantic waterfront from the eastern edge of McCook's Point
       approximately 5,475 feet easterly to the Amtrak Railway Bridge.
       Mr. Gada seconded the motion.

Vote in favor: (6-0), Unanimous.
