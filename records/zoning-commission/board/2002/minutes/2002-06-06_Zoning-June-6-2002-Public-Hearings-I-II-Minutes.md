PUBLIC HEARING I
Application of Joseph E. Smith for a Special Permit to construct and operate a
commercial greenhouse at 415 Boston Post Road, East Lyme, CT, with the waiver of
exception to Section 24,6c-Sidewalks and Section24.6e3 Landscape granted as requestedn
property further identified as Assessor Map 24.0,Lot30.

Mr. Nickerson opened the Public Hearing and read the call. Mrs. Cone read into the record a
June 6,2002 memorandum from William Mulholland,Zoning Official to the Zoning

Mr. Nickerson invited the applicant to present his request to the Commission

Joseph E. Smith, 415 Boston Post Rd., East Lyme stated that he proposed to construct a
permanent, glass topped greenhouse on a cement foundation at 415 Boston Post Rd. The site
has been used for farming in the past. He indicated that commercial sales will not take place at
the site, but the product will be sold at the existing 4-6 West Main St., Niantic. He stated that
he has been working with the Zoning Office to ensure compliance with setback and other
regulations. He stated that the greenhouse will be energy effrcient and cost efficient.

Mr. Nickerson invited members of the public to make comments in favor of the application.

PUBLIC HEARING II
Application of of Cuddy and Feder and Worby, LLP, agent for AT&T Wireless PCS
LLC, for a Special Permit to mount six panel antennae to the fagade of the building at 15
Liberty Wuy, Niantic, CT, property further identified on Assessor Map 9.4,Lot 8.
Reasons: In compliance with Standards and Regulation.

Mr. Nickerson opened the public hearing and read the call. Mrs. Cone rcad a memorandum
from William Mulholland, Zoning Official to the Zoning Commission dated June 6, 2002. Mr.
Nickerson invited the applicant to address the Commission.

Lucia Chiocchio, of Cuddy and Feder and Worby, LLP stated that the facility proposed consists
of six panel antennae mounted to the fagade of the existing building just below the cupola. An
equipment cabinet will be located to the rear of the building within an existing wood fence
compound. The gate will be locked and the cables to the panels will be underground. The
antennae panels are designed to blend with the fagade of the existing building. She distributed
photo-simulations to demonstrate what the building would look like before and after the
antennae are installed. She presented a drawing indicating desired level of signal coverage.

Ms. Chiocchio reviewed the Regulation and pointed out how the applicant has met the
requirements. She indicated that the Siting Council would not be involved in this application as
there is not a new tower. She stated the proposed facility will not interfere with any existing
TV, radio or emergency communications systems.
