OLD BUSINESS

1. Affordable Housing
        Mr. Nickerson recommended that the Commission table discussion for this evening as input
may be required from Mr. Mulholland and the Commission would be more productive at the next
meeting.

        Mr. Peck stated that in his opinion the reason there is an Affordable Housing 'crunch' is that
Towns throughout the State and Nation have passed Zoning and Planning Regulations and specifications
on construction that have made it unprofitable for developers to construct housing units that are
affordable to a lot of people of low and moderate income. He noted that in Stonington, the regulations
for example require granite curbs, which are highly expensive. He stated that to invite Affordable
Housing, the town must make it affordable to the developer first. He indicated that some adjustments
could be made in the Regulations that do not diminish the quality and aesthetics of the buildings and
surrounding open space but will contribute to cost savings to the developer.
