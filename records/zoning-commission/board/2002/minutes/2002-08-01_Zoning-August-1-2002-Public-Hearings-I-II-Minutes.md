Mr. Bulmer stated his concern for the term ooexisting street", noting that Ancient Highway is an existing
street, but part of the street is a dirt pathway.

Mr. Nickerson indicated that Planning Commission recommended revision of Section 20.8.4 to read
"No back lots shall be created on the following streets: Route 1, Route 156 and Route 161 unless access
is combined with a driveway serving a lot which fronts on the street." The Commission concurred.

There being no further comment from the Commission, Mr. Nickerson opened the hearing to those
wishing to speak in favor of the proposed amendment. There being none, he opened the hearing to
those wishing to speak in opposition. There being none, he opened the hearing to general public
comment.

C. Egelhoff, 28 Church La.,E. Lyme, CT stated that the abutting property owners should be notified
when an application has been made under Section 20.8.

Mr. Mulholland stated that there are State Statutes that address this item. However, when it is a one-
time free split, there is no public forum. If a subdivision is being created, abutting property owners are
notified via a sign posted. He added that the Zoning Regulations address land use and not the
notification process.

Mr. Mulholland requested Mrs. Parulis inform the Commission the Planning Commission thinking on
Section 20,8.3.

Mrs. Parulis stated that the Planning Commission was in discussion regarding the length of an
accessway from the street line to the front building line of the back lot in an RU40 District. The
Planning Commission is considering reducing the distance to 500-ft. due to hydrants and fire protection.
Zoning Commission amended Section 20.8.3 places this distance at 1,000-ft. She indicated that the
Zoning Commission has an option of (1) waiting on this amendment until the Planning Commission has
made its final decision, or (2) proceed with the amendment at 1000-ft. and revisit the item after the
Planning Commission has acted.
