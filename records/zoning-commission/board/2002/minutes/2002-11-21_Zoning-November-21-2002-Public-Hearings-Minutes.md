                      EAST LYME ZONING COMMISSION

                                     MINUTES
The East Lyme ZoningCommission held a PUBLIC HEARING on Thursday NOVEMBER 21 ,2002 at
the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Ave., Niantic, CT. Mr. Mark Nickerson, Chairman,
opened the PUBLIC HEARING at 7:30 PM.

PRESENT: Mark Nickerson, Ed Gada, Norman Peck, Shawn Mclaughlin, Alternate Marc Salerno,
              Alternate William Dwyer.

require buidings with over 20,000 square feet in area to obtain a Special Permit. - Withdrawn

                Construction for a Special Permit to excavate gravel from property identified as 80
                Quailcrest Rd., East Lyrne Assessor Map 41, Lot 1.
                Mr. Peck seconded the motion.
