2. Fast Food               Subcommittee
        Mr. Peck indicated that the problem to be addressed is the traffic around fast-food
        restaurants with drive-thru windows. He stated that fast food restaurants cannot be singled
        out as a cause for traffic problems. He presented suggested language as follows:
                "Adding Section 20.26 to the Regulations: oono restaurant or food service
                establishment having a drive-thru facility shall be built or established upon a lot or
                premises within a radius of 1,000 feet of any other lot or premises used for such
                pu{pose. Existing premises used for such purpose with a drive-thru prior to this
                regulation shall not be deemed nonconforming."


Discussion followed regarding the exclusion of other types of businesses with drive-thru windows.
        Mr. Peck stated that banks, drug stores, post offrce should not be targeted in his opinion.
        He stated that fast food restaurants depend on volume and this creates to concern regarding
        increased traffic.

Mr. Weber stated that if the goal was to reduce the number of cars entering and exiting traffic what
       other, if any, drive-thru facilities should be considered.



Mr. Mulholland suggested obtaining definitions of drive-thru and drive-in. He will pursue this.
      He noted that a drive-thru is frequently considered an accessory use to a restaurant.

Mr. Peck stated that he did not believe banks or gas stations should be included, but rather focus
      on food service.

Mr. Chamberlain stated that he supports the concept, but the choice of language would need some
      examination. He noted that Rte 161, in particular, needs to have the traffic situation
        addressed and alleviated.

It was the consensus that a definition of 'drive-thru facility' needed to be obtained for
        clarification.

Mr. Mulholland will review the language, obtain a definition of 'drive-thru facility', discuss it
      with Town Attomey and report back to the Commission at the next meeting.
