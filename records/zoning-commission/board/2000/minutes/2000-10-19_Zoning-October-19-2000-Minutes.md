                of the Aces High Recreational Park,301 Chesterfield Road, twelve (12) months
                per year.

            Mrs. Cone opened discussion by suggesting discussion and deliberation, and reminding the
            Commission that they are not required to vote this evening.

            Mr. Chamberlain stated that the 60-day limitation does not appear to be consistent with
            other privately owned campgrounds in the State and the zoning regulations would need to

        Mr. Nickerson noted that the application request to permit 12-month occupancy and the

        Mrs. Cone stated that currently the RV Park is operational from April I to October 31. All
        trailers are to be off site unless being stored. Fuel cannot be stored over winter on the
        premises.

        Mr. Peck stated he is in favor of winter camping, but in order to allow winter camping, the
        regulations would have to be amended. The Commission is legally bound to comply with
        the Regulations as written. He noted that Mr. Quinn had stated at the Public Hearing that
        during the summer months, the RV Park is not complying with the 60-day limitation. Mr.
        Peck stated that he had no problem with a 6-month stay.

        Mr. Chamberlain stated that he understood Mr. Quinn's urgency, and that he had no
        problem amending the length of stay regulation.

        Mrs. Orefice noted that Section20.l7 of the ZoningRegulations also states that the
        Regulations also limit use to oorecreational uses" and the applicant is requesting temporary
        residence, not recreational use.

         Mrs. Cone stated that the Commission has the option of holding discussion and vote over
         to the next meeting, thus providing an opportunity to discuss these concerns with Mr.
         Mulholland.

         Mr. Nickerson noted that the Commission could end up with a 2-3 year occupant unless
         there are limitations placed on length of stay. In that event, the RV Park becomes a trailer
         park or mobile home park with temporary hook-ups rather than permanent hook-ups. He
         stressed the concept of temporary residence, i.e. months not years.

         Mr. Nickerson stated he would like to review the Regulations and application with Mr.
         Mulholland, Zoning Offi cial.

         Mrs. Cone noted that the request is for "occupancy twelve months per year"

         Mr. Peck stated that if the Commission voted, and approved the application, the
         Regulations as written apply. He would encourage someone to propose an amendment to
         the regulations.
