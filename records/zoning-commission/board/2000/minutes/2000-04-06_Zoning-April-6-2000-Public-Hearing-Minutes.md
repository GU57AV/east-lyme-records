The East Lyme Zoning Commission held a Public Hearing on April 6,2000 at the Town Hall, 108
Pennsylvania Ave., Niantic, CT. Mr. William Weber chaired the meeting. Mr. Weber called the
meeting to order at7:32PM.

PRESENT: Chairman William Weber, Athena Cone, Norman Peck, Ed Gada, David
               Chamberlain, and Shawn Mclaughlin, Altemates Robert Bulmer

Also Present: William Mulholland,ZoningOfficer

PUBLIC HEARING 1
Application of Ted Harris to amend the East Lyme ZoningRegulations by adding a new
Section 18.1.5.1 to permit the illumination of Theater Marquee signs in the CB Commercial
ZoningDistrict.

Mr. Weber opened the Public Hearing and introduced the Commission members.

Mr. Theodore Hanis addressed the Commission. He noted that the ZoningRegulations prohibit
maf,quee lighting and signage. IIe stated that theaters are unique in the use of marquees and that
the marquee seryes two functions. First, it provides shelter to theatergoers and second it serves an
aesthetic function. Mr. Harris indicated that the proposed amendment is limited to theater
marquees in CB ZoningDistrict.

Mr. Harris noted that the Planning Commission approved the proposed amendment following
addressing their concerns. He added that in order to allow theater marquees in the Zoning
Regulations, vatious sections would have to be amended. Therefore, the format as a proposed
amendment is to provide a specific exception in a CB CommercialZoningDistrict.

The lights may give the appearance of movement, but may not blink in total or in large groups.
They shall not exceed 15 watts and shall be white. The marquee signage may only identiff the
name of the theater and shall only be illuminated during the time of theater operation.
