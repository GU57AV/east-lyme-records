5.   East Lyme ZoningCommission proposal to amend Section 18.1.10 of the zoning
     regulations to permit'cCommunify Bulletin Boards".

     Amend Section 18.1.10 by adding the following language:

     Community Bulletin Boards - The Town of East Lyme may have three (3) community bulletin boards
     for the purpose of advertising community events, which are sponsored by any civic or registered non-
     profit organization recognized as having its permanent address within the Town of East Lyme. These
     signs may be erected on public or private land and be externally lit, but such lighting must be shielded
     from all adjunct properties. Any qualifiing group shall obtain a permit from the Zoning Department
     prior to use of the bulletin board.

     The bulletin board signs shall not exceed atotalareaof80 square feet. Individual advertising signs
     placed on the board shall not exceed 2 feetby 3 feet and shall have 3-inch high black letters on white
     background.

     Applications shall be submitted at least 30 days prior to an event and no more than 90 days in
     advance. The display of signboards shall not exceed l0 days and shall be removed immediately upon
     conclusion of the event.

     The final design of the Community Bulletin Boards shall be reviewed and approved by the Zoning
     Commission.
