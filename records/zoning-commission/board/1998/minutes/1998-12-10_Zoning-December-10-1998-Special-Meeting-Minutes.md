I.      Election of Officers

        Mr. Formica called the meeting to order at 6:05 PM.

        Mr. Dwyer will sit in Mr. Mullaney's absence.

        Mrs. Hardy chaired the meeting for the election of officers. She inquired as to the
        Commission members voting this evening and if the Commission had a preference for
        paper ballot or show of hands. The Commission consensus was for a show of hands.
        Voting members of the Commission will be Mr. Formica, Mrs. Cone, Mr. Peck,
        Mr. Mclaughlin, Mr. Price and Mr. Dwyer.

        Mrs. Hardy opened the floor for nominations for the position of Chairman of the East
        Lyme Zoning Commission.

     Motion (1): Mr. Dwyer nominated Paul Formica to be Chairman stating he has done an
     tenific job as Chairman and has the experience. Mrs. Cone seconded the nomination.

        Hearing no other nomination, Mrs. Hardy closed the nominations.

        Vote in favor of Mr. Formica as Chairman of the East Lyme ZoningCommission:
        (6-0), Unanimous.
