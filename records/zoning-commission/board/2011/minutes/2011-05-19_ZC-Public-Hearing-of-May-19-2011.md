PUBLIC HEARING
1. APPLICATION OF THE TOWN OF EAST LYME TO AMEND THE
EAST LYME ZONING REGULATIONS SECTIONS 15 FOR

COMPLIANCE WITH 44 CFR 60.3 AND NEW STATE
REQUIREMENTS FOR FLOODPLAINS.
Mr. Mulholland stated the legal ad had run on this Application.

Mr. Carpenteri read a letter from Gary Goeschel dated May 18, 2011.
Mr. Carpenteri read a letter from Mr. Mulholland.

Mr. Carpenteri read a letter from Marcy Balint, of the Connecticut DEP.

Mr, Carpenteri read a letter from James D. Rabbitt of the Southeastern Connecticut
Council of Governments.

Mr. Mulholland stated this is a two year project. FEMA sent new flood maps to the
town. If the maps are adopted they will be effective July 18", FEMA has updated their
maps nationally.

Mr. Mulholland reviewed the text amendment with the members.

Mr. Dwyer asked who this may affect.

Mr. Mulholland stated many people have been in to look at the maps. It has been
advertised that they are in the Town Hall. The maps show who is affected. It is not the
town’s obligation to determine if someone is in or out of a flood zone.

Mr. Dwyer asked if the onus is on the citizens.

Mr. Mulholland stated it always has been.

There were no public comments.

Motion (1) Mr. Carpenteri moved to close the Public Hearing.

Seconded by Mr. Dwyer.

Motion Passed 4-0.
