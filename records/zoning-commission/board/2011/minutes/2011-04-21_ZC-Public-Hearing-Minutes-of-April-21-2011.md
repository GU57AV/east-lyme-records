The regular members of the Zoning Commission asked the alternate members to join
them as regular voting members for the Public Hearing, and the alternate members all
agreed to do so.

Mr. Peck nominated Bob Bulmer to act as Chairman for the Public Hearing and
Regular Meeting of April 21, 2011.

Seconded by Mr. Gada.
Nomination Passed 5-0,

Mr. Gada nominated Mathew Walker to act as Secretary for the Public Hearing
and Regular Meeting of April 21, 2011.

Seconded by Mr. Bulmer.
Nomination Passed 5-0,
Chairman Bulmer called the Public Hearing to order at 7:31 p.m.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

Chairman Bulmer led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS

There were no public delegations,
PUBLIC HEARING
1, Application of Tim Yuhas for Yuhas Performance Training for a Special

Permit for Indoor Recreation for a fitness club for adults at property
identified in the application as 239-2 Main Street (aka 14 Pennsylvania
Avenue,) Assessor’s Map 12.1, Lot 109.

Mr. Walker read the memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Mr. Peck recused himself for Item 1.

Mr. Mulholland stated the legal ad has run.

Tim Yuhas, 55 Crescent Avenue, Niantic presented the application.

Mr. Mulholland asked Mr. Yuhas to explain what 14 Pennsylvania Avenue is.

Mr. Yuhas stated it is the old Mitchell’s Market.

Mr. Mulholland explained that the property has been split into 4 spaces. Mr, Yuhas will
only be using one of them.

Mr. Dwyer stated he doesn’t see any concerns with this.

Mr. Walker did not have any concerns; this will be a welcome addition to town. He
knows Mr. Yuhas and he does tremendous work.

Mr. Gada stated it seems like it will pass.
There was no public comment.
Mr. Bulmer closed the Public Hearing,
2. Application of Robert P. Sassi, for a Special Permit to operate a fast food
restaurant at property identified in the application as 170 Flanders Road,
Niantic, Connecticut. Assessor’s Map 26.3, Lot 11.

Mr. Peck returned to the meeting,

Mr. Walker read a memo from Bill Mulholland.
Mr. Mulholland stated the legal ad had run.

Robert Sassi, 234 North Parker Hill Road, Killingworth, Connecticut presented the
application.

Mr. Gada stated it seems like everything is in order.
Mr. Walker stated everything looks like it is in order. Parking shouldn’t be an issue.

Mr, Dwyer asked if the applicant was aware that the Zoning Commission made that an
affordable housing area.

Mr. Sassi stated he is aware.

Mr. Dwyer stated he doesn’t see a problem as far as zoning.
Mr. Peck asked if there would be outdoor dining.

Mr. Sassi stated there would not be.

There were no public comments.

Mr. Bulmer closed the Public Hearing,

3. Application of Louis Torelli, for Best of Everything Country Gourmet,
for a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at property identified in the
application as 214 Flanders Road, Niantic, Connecticut. Assessor’s Map
26.3, Lot 18,

Mr. Peck recused himself for Item 3.

Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland,

Mr. Mulholland stated the legal ad had run.

Louis Torelli, 310 Baker Lane, Lyme, Connecticut presented the application. This will
be a takeaway gourmet establishment, they are applying to put in 5 or 6 outside tables,
Their hours will be 8:30 to 6:00 p.m. 6 days per week. The patio will be open during
business hours only. They will not be serving on the patio.

Mr. Dwyer did not have any lighting concerns.

Mr. Torelli stated the establishment already does have outdoor lighting on timers, There
wiil be no entertainment, and no alcohol.
Mr. Walker stated he drove by and it will look nice, he likes this idea.

Mr. Gada stated this will be an asset to the Town.

Mr. Mulholland stated they are going to paint the building when weather permits.
Mr. Torelli stated they have already started painting.

There were no public comments.

Mr. Bulmer closed the public hearing,
