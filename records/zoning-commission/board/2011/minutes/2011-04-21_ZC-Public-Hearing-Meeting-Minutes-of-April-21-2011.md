1, Application of the Town of East Lyme for a Special Permit to modify and

Map 12.1, Lot 94, by adding an additional 59 parking spaces.

The legal ad was run on this Public Hearing.
Mr. Carpenteri read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Mr. Scheer presented this Application for the Town of East Lyme. The goal is to add
more spaces for downtown, and to link Hope Street and Main Street. There will be a net
gain of 55 parking spaces. They are working with the Mitchells and Dunkin Donuts.
They are also proposing to add more trees than they will be taking down. It will have a
boulevard effect and they will be installing LED lighting similar to the Niantic Rotary
Park, He has been working with a pervious concrete vendor, who may donate materials
for the project.

There was no public comment.

Mr, Salerno stated they will need a waiver on the Southerly and the Westerly line.
