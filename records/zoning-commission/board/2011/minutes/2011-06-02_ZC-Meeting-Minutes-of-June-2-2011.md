PUBLIC HEARING
1. Application of Leo Roche for Strive, LLC for a renewal of a Special
Permit for Outdoor Dining at property identified in the application as
247-2 Main Street, Niantic, CT.
Mr. Mulholland stated the legal ad had run on the application.

Leo Roche, 247 Main Street, Niantic, stated this is a renewal for the patio at The Black
Sheep. He does not want to change anything, except he would like for the patio to be
open until 12:30. They are really pushing it some nights by the time people get out of the
late movie, and trying to get them fed and out by 12:00. It just cuts it close.

Ms, Carabelas asked if someone could come in just to order a glass of wine or would they
have to order food on the patio.

Mr. Salerno stated they did not have to order food; they just have to be seated at a table.
Mr. Mulholland stated there have been no complaints to his office at all.

Mr. Roche stated he feels the patio is very important. It shows that the Town is open for
business.

Mr. Mulholland stated they are good for business, There was a recent amendment to the
patio regulations, now if a business just wants two tables and chairs, with no alcohol, they
do not need to come to this Commission.

Mr. Dwyer stated this is a well established owner, and there has never been a problem.
He is in favor of this.

There were no more comments from Commission members,
Frank Balantic of the Planning Commission spoke in favor of this application.

2. Application of Harry and Trifonas Loginidis dba Family Pizza for a
renewal of a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining at property identified in
the application as 233 Main Street, Niantic, CT.

Mr. Mulholland stated the legal ad had run. He stated the applicant is not here this
evening, but this is a renewal, and they have had no issues at all. There have been no

complaints.

Mr. Liska asked if we granted the 12:30 closing time to one, would that be across the
board.

Mr. Salerno stated it is a case by case basis.

There were no more comments.
Motion (1) Ms. Carabelas moved to close the Public Hearing.
Seconded by Mr. Walker.
Motion Passed 6-0.
