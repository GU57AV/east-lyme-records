PUBLIC HEARING

1 Application of the Town of East Lyme Parks & Recreation Department
for a Special Permit and Coastal Area Management Site Plan Review to
construct a new beach support building, with bathrooms, at property
identified in the application as 8 Atlantic Street (aka McCook’s Park)
Assessor’s Map 11.4, Lot 175,

Mr. Mulholland confirmed that the legal advertisement had run on Item 1.
Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland, and a letter from Marcy Balint,

Mr, Mulholland reviewed the CAM Act application.
Chairman Salerno stated this will be going in the same spot as the existing bathroom.
Mr. Kane asked if it will be the exact same as the Hole in the Wall,

Mr, Scheer, Town Engineer, stated it would be very similar, but they have added a
window in the front, He also stated that sand would render a pervious surface ineffective.
So they would not want to have a pervious surface around the new building, as suggested

by Marcy Balint.
There were no public comments,

Mr. Mulholland stated they have asked for a waiver of the buffer, and a waiver of the
landscaping,

Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing,
Seconded by Mr. Donovan.

Motion Passed 6-0.

2. Application of Gerry Karpuska, for a Special Permit for Outdoor Dining
at property identified in the application as 26 W Main Street, Niantic,
Connecticut. Assessor’s Map 11.2, Lot 17.

**Mr. Peck recused himself and Mr. Dwyer sat in his place,
Mr, Mulholland confirmed the legal advertisement had run on Item 2,
Mr, Walker read a memo from Mr, Mulholland,

Gerry Karpuska, Canty Lane, Old Lyme represented the Niantic Diner for the
application, The outdoor dining would be a 918 square foot deck to the rear of the
building. He stated the owner of the property manages the restaurant as well, He will be
putting flower boxes around the deck to screen it, They will be moving a small dumpster
up to the area, They will be closing at 9:00 p.m. from Sunday through Thursday, and at
10:00 p.m, on Friday and Saturday. They will be installing down lighting, and it will be
low intensity. There will be access from the inside only. There is no parking in that area
of the lot so they will not be losing any parking spaces,

Mr. Mulholland stated the property owners recently acquired the property to the West of
this property. They meet all setbacks, and do not need a waiver,

Mr, Karpuska stated they are asking for small speakers to play very soft background
music. Nobody other than the owner will control the volume on those speakers, They
can turn off the speakers in bad weather and in the winter, They will have umbrellas over
the tables, but there will be no writing on them. There is no roof over this area,
There was discussion of Mr. Mulholland’s memo, as he referenced some of the old
regulations,

Kostas Anastasiou of 13 Sapia Drive, Niantic stated that 95% of his customers are there
for dinner. They are not there just to drink.

Mr. Donovan asked if there was to be parking under the deck.
Mr, Anastasiou stated there is no parking back there.

Mr, Dwyer asked about handicapped access,

Mr. Anastasio stated he will put a ramp in,

Public Comment ~

Patricia Avery, 32 West Main Street, Niantic stated the owners of the Diner have done a
wonderful job and they run a great business. Her home is to the rear, and sound does
travel across the field. They a considering subdividing in the future behind the restaurant.
She requested that they prohibit outside speakers and entertainment, and that they allow
minimal overhead lighting turned off by 10:00 or table lamps only,

Chairman Salerno asked the applicant if he could live without speakers, and if the lights
would be turned off when they close.

Mr. Anastasio stated he could live without the speakers and the lights will go off when
they close, other than a motion sensor light in the back,

Motion (2) Mr. Donovan moved to close the Public Hearing,
Seconded by Mr. Walker.

Motion Passed 6-0,

“Mr, Peck returned to his seat, and Mr. Dwyer stepped down,

3. Application of Stephanie Torlone, DVM for a Special Permit to operate

Stephanie Torlone, 43 Button Road, North Stonington stated she would have one exam
