1. APPLICATION OF PETER T. TYTLA, OWNER FOR A ZONE CHANGE

FROM CA TO RU-40 FOR PROPERTY IDENTIFIED IN THE APPLICATION
AS 91 BOSTON POST ROAD, ASSESSOR’S MAP 31.0, LOT 2.

Mr. McPherson stated this is a no brainer.

Mr. Donovan concurred with Mr. McPherson.

Mr. Peck stated it is certainly consistent with the POCD to preserve Open Space.
Mr. Kane stated with that end of town being so commercial this is a good thing.

Chairman Salerno stated they have to be cautious when converting zones, but this lot
does seem inappropriate for commercial use.

Motion (1) Mr. Donovan moved to approve the application of Peter T. Tytla,
Owner for a Zone Change from CA to RU-40 for property identified in the
application as 91 Boston Post Road, Assessor’s Map 31.0, Lot 2.
Seconded by Mr. McPherson.

TO CONSTRUCT A CHICKEN COOP, 7’X3’, FOR UP TO TEN CHICKENS
FOR PERSONAL USE AT PROPERTY IDENTIFIED IN THE APPLICATION
AS 52 CARDINAL ROAD, EAST LYME, EAST LYME ASSESSOR’S MAP

This item was moved up to Item 2.
Mr. Kane asked if they should put in that no roosters are allowed.

3. APPLICATION OF THE TOWN OF EAST LYME ZONING COMMISSION TO
AMEND THE EAST LYME ZONING REGULATIONS SECTION 20.27 ITEM
NUMBER 2 TO ALLOW ADDITIONAL 450 SQUARE FEET FOR OUTDOOR
DINING ADJACENT TO A PUBLIC SIDEWALK.

Chairman Salerno stated it should specifically say outdoor dining and not outdoor bars,
he agrees with Mr. McPherson on that. People can still drink at the tables, they just
can’t have bars.

Mr. Walker stated the language is pretty explicit. They could add the word “only” for
outdoor dining.

Mr. Mulholland stated they can just add “outdoor bars shall not be permitted in these
areas.”
Mr. Peck stated if they change the wording to no outdoor bars, that is a substantive
change. There would be an FOl issue. He will vote against any additional wording.

Mr. McPherson stated he will not approve it without the additional wording.

Motion (3) Mr. Peck moved to approve the application of the Town of East Lyme
Zoning Commission to amend the East Lyme Zoning regulations Section 20.27
item number 2 to allow additional 450 square feet for outdoor dining adjacent to a

public sidewalk.

Seconded by Mr. Donovan.

Motion Passed 4-2-0 (Nay — Chairman Salerno, Mr. McPherson)

Mr. Mulholland stated this will be published on the 19" and effective on the 20".

4. REQUEST OF THEODORE A HARRIS FOR EXCEPTIONS TO SECTION 24
SITE PLAN REVIEW REQUIREMENTS (SPECIFICALLY SECTION 24.6C
9(SIDEWALK) AND SECTION 24.6 E(3)(BUFFERS) FOR PROPERTY
LOCATED AT 30 WEST MAIN STREET, EAST LYME ASSESSOR’S MAP

11.1, LOT 29.

*Mr. Peck recused himself for this item.

waived. They will be putting a split rail fence facing the restaurant, and they will be
replacing an existing stone wall on the East side of the property. In the back there are

arborvitaes and a very large maple tree.

Mr. Peck requested a recess to speak to Mr. Mulholland and Attorney Harris.
