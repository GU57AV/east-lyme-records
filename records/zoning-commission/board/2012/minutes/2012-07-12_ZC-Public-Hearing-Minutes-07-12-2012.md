PUBLIC DELEGATIONS

PUBLIC HEARING

1. APPLICATION OF PETER T. TYTLA, OWNER FOR A ZONE CHANGE FROM
CA TO RU-40 FOR PROPERTY IDENTIFIED IN THE APPLICATION AS 91
BOSTON POST ROAD, ASSESSOR’S MAP 31.0, LOT 2.

a
Mr. Mulholland confirmed that the legal ad had run on this item.

Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Mr. Walker read a letter from Mr. Goeschel.

Mr. Walker read a letter from Marcy Balint.

Mr. Walker read a letter from the Water and Sewer Commission.

Peter Tytla, of 91 Boston Post Road, East Lyme stated he has a unique property, and
he would like to leave it to the town or state or have the town buy the property. There is
a pond, Latimer Brook, and there are three levels on the property, which are the high
rock level, the house and the outbuildings, and the wetlands and pond. He has 23
acres, and he wants to leave something for the animals. The front end of the property
by the road is zoned commercial. It is not a good place to build commercially.

Chairman Salerno entered a map of the property as Exhibit 1.

Mark Christensen of 66 Grassy Hill Road stated he supports Mr. Tytla’s application.
The area by the Brook should not be zoned commercial.

Mr. Tytla stated it has been in his family since late 1930s. He does not want to develop
the property; he wants to have it residential for the animals.

Motion (1) Mr. Donovan moved to close the Public Hearing.
Seconded by Mr. Walker.

Motion Passed 6-0.

Mr. Walker read a letter from the Regional Planning Commission.

Chairman Salerno stated they have a sub-committee studying outdoor bars. He
questioned whether the language would allow outdoor bars.

Mr. Mulholland stated they could request a bar under a special permit. Someone could
