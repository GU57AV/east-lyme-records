1. APPLICATION OF THEODORE A. HARRIS, ESQ FOR EAST LYME CAFE,
LLC DBA SMOKEY O’GRADY’S FOR A SPECIAL PERMIT FOR OUTDOOR
DINING AT PROPERTY IDENTIFIED IN THE APPLICATION AS 306
FLANDERS ROAD, EAST LYME.

Mr. Peck stated there should be no speakers, no electric amplification, the bar outside
will be shut down at 10:00 p.m., the last seating will be at 10:00 and service will end at
11:00. He also wants to ensure that lighting is permanently installed as presented in
Exhibit 5. He also wants them to stipulate that they will turn the music off if it is
requested of them by the cemetery.

Mr. McPherson stated he doesn’t like the way the bar was put there, that it was
originally introduced as storage.

Mr. Mulholland stated they can order it removed.

Motion (1) Mr. Donovan moved to approve the Application of Theodore A.
Harris, Esq., for East Lyme Café, LLC dba Smokey O’Grady’s for a Special Permit
for Outdoor Dining at property identified in the application as 306 Flanders Road,
East Lyme with the following conditions:

1. Entertainment will be allowed with acoustic guitar without amplification,
and a low level amplifier for the microphone only. Entertainment will be
allowed until 10:00 p.m.

2. Service will be from 11:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., the last seating will be
allowed at 10:00 p.m. All service shall cease at 11:00 p.m.

3. Upon request of the East Lyme Cemetery Association, any entertainment
outside will be halted for burial services.

4, There are to be 9 fixed seats around the service counter and 5 fixed seats
around the tabletop and that will be serviced by wait staff.

Seconded by Mr. Walker.

Motion Passed 5-0-1 (Mr. McPherson — Nay)
