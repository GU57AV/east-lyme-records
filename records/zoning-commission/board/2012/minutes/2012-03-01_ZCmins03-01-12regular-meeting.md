1. Application of Francis G. Grace for Main Street Grille, Niantic for a Special Permit

Chairman Salerno stated the applicant stated the last seating will be at midnight. There wilt
be no entertainment, but they will have light background music with a few speakers.

Mr. Kane asked if the speakers would be on the top deck.
