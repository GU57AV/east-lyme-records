4. COMMENTS FROM EX-OFFICIO

Rose Ann Hardy stated Lew Mostowy has resigned from the Zoning Board of Appeals.
At the present time they are in good shape with Water, the Board of Selectmen has
approved an Ordinance for water conservation in advance if it is needed. They are still
in a holding pattern for the Samuel Smith House. Joe Smith has been re-appointed as
Building Official. At a Town meeting they approved $95,000.00 for temporary repairs of
the Boardwalk. The town has received a STEAP grant in the amount of $400,000.00 for
Phase 3 of downtown to come around the corner of Pennsylvania Avenue, for
sidewalks, street lamps extending to State Road, and that will include plantings also.

Mr. Mulholland asked if they had any discussion on the bath house at McCooks. The
public has been asking questions as to why the work is being done now.

Mrs. Hardy stated there were reasons it was not done in March. They approved at
Town meeting an allocation of $50,000.00 for downtown sidewalk general repair. They

also approved an appropriation of $400,000.00 for improvements on Walnut Hill Road
and anything left will be used to upgrade existing roads.

Mr. Mulholland asked if they have discussed extending the sidewalk around Gorton
Pond.

Mrs. Hardy stated they have not had discussion on that.
Chairman Salerno asked about the football field at the high school.

Mrs. Hardy stated it was supposed to start the day after graduation but she drove by the
other day and it had not started.
