CALL TO ORDER
Chairman Salerno called the meeting to order at 7:31 p.m.
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Chairman Salerno led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.
PUBLIC DELEGATIONS
There were no public delegations.
PUBLIC HEARING
1, Application of Donna Criscuolo for Niantic Yoga Center for a Special
permit for Indoor Recreation for a yoga studio at property identified in

the application as 11 Liberty Way, Units 3 & 4, Niantic CT.

Mr. Mulholland stated the legal advertisement has run.
**Mr. Peck recused himself for the Public Hearing.
**Chairman Salerno sat Mr. Liska as a regular member for the Public Hearing.
Mr. Walker read a memo from Mr, Mulholland.

Donna Criscuolo representing Niantic Yoga Center stated they will be moving from 6
Liberty Way to 11 Liberty Way.

Exhibit 1 was entered into the record by Ms. Criscuolo.

Chairman Salerno stated there are 22 parking spots available, and they are now across the
street from where they were.

Chairman Salerno stated Liberty Way is unique in the parking situation. They have to
show there are enough spaces for the tenants in the building, and a lot of the tenants have

different hours.

Mr. Mulholland stated the industrial park was originally industrial and office. Now it is
more commercial with many activities. The other business in this building closes at 5:00

p.m,

Chairman Salerno asked Mr. Mulholland if there have been any issues with any
businesses out there.

Mr. Mulholland stated they have approved similar businesses out there and to date they
have had no complaints.

There was no public comment.
Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing,
Seconded by Mr. Walker.

Motion Passed 6-0.

AY ob UAC ALAAL
(( Miller Galbo
