2. LANDMARK II IMPLEMENTATIONS

Attorney O’Connell gave a history of the Landmark applications that have come before
the Zoning Commission, and he discussed the appeal process that had taken place for
each application. He discussed the process of affordable housing application and their
subsequent appeals where the burden of proof is reversed and is up to the Zoning
Commission to prove to the Court why the denial was proper.

There is a 104 page decision where the Court ordered text amendments, and the Court
gave the Zoning Commission discretion in determining what consideration to employ.
The Zoning Commission has to start drafting new regulations and they will have to have
public hearings, input from the public, and they would want the stakeholders engaged to
some degree. Several of these regulations will affect the whole town and will affect all
affordable housing to come before us. When the regulations are amended then Landmark
is to submit the conceptual site plan in accordance with those amended regulations. The
Department of Transportation will have to approve the improvements by Landmark’s
traffic engineer and Landmark must bear the whole cost of the improvements.

They will need to keep this on the agenda and at some point would have to start drafting

the amended regulations. They have to show that they are complying with this order, He
suggested they keep this on the agenda as old business.
