FIRST SELECTMAN

FILED IN EAST LYME
Sept 24, 2062 AT_3-35AM

EAST LYME TOWN CLERK

Mr. Formica stated he had recently met with a Committee that is part of Niantic Main Street about more
directional signage downtown. He also stated that Rotary has contributed $15,000.00 for a digital
welcome sign in front of Rite-Aid. This may alleviate the illegal sandwich signs, and non-profits may be
able to advertise on the digital sign. It would take a little modification in the Regulations. The
Community Bulletin Boards are presently permitted, but not in digital. There is also going to be a need
for beach signs. They are hoping to have a trolley system put in place. The trolleys would need signs.
He also discussed restaurants using outdoor patios, and a lot of people want to sit under an umbrella at
those patios. It is a chance for tasteful advertising, which may need to be considered. Also, “Open”
neon signs are allowed in one zone, but not the CB zone. The Town is trying to move forward. There
will be a new treescape up Pennsylvania Avenue, going left onto Hope Street.

REGULAR MEETING

1, LANDMARK II] REMAND PROCESS
Attorney O’Connell stated the first stage in the process is to adopt regulations consistent with the
Judge’s decision. The second stage will be to entertain an application from Landmark consistent with
those regulations. The question came up at the last meeting as to whether they should review the
entire Section 32, since the addition of 32.9 may affect the Section as a whole. They passed out the
entire Section 32 and provisions. He hopes to have a public hearing scheduled.
Attorney Zamarka presented the Proposed Amendments to the East Lyme Zoning Regulations.
There was discussion as to how often a Public Hearing would take place in the Affordable Housing
Application process. The Zoning Commission recommended Section 32.9 state that the Commission
shall hold a Public Hearing on the Preliminary Site Plan and, at the Zoning Commission’s discretion a
Public Hearing at the Final Site Plan.
Mr. Mulholland stated he will get it scheduled for a Public Hearing.

NEW BUSINESS
1. ANY BUSINESS ON THE FLOOR, IF ANY BY THE MAJORITY VOTE OF THE COMMISSION
Mr. Dwyer asked about the bus downtown with the illuminated signs.
Mr. Peck felt that the Town holds business owners to certain standards, and they are not following
those standards at the new Town Garage. He suggested Mr. Mulholland sent a letter to Public Works,
with a copy to Mr. Formica to express that opinion.
Mr. Mulholland stated he will generate a letter and he will talk to Mr. Scheer.
Mr. Peck stated the letter should be signed by the Chairman of the Zoning Commission.
2. ZONING OFFICIAL
Mr. Mulholland stated he is working with a group working on developing a property across from the
Midway Mall, he is anticipating an application for a rebuild of Henny Penny on Route 156. He has
discussed a mixed use on Main Street, and met with folks regarding a take-out restaurant in the north
end of town, and another person looking to re-open the old Frank’s Gourmet Grill. In response to Mr.
Dwyer’s question about the bus with the illuminated lights, he was informed by Town Counsel that it is
under the jurisdiction of the DPUC of the State of Connecticut and the Zoning Commission has no
jurisdiction. Next time the Town Attorney is here he recommended that Mr. Dwyer ask him about that.
3. COMMENTS FROM EX-OFFICIO
Ms. Hardy stated the Board of Selectmen has approved the purchase of the Samuel Smith House. It has
been forwarded to the Board of Finance. The Sportsmen’s Club has offered to buy a portion of the land.
There will be a likely expenditure of $125,000.00 for the taxpayers after anticipated grants are

approved. She concurs with Mr. Peck’s comments about the Town Garage.
