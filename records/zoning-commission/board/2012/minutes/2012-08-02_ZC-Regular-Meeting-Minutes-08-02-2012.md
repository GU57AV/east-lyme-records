PUBLIC DELEGATIONS

Mark Christensen, Grassy Hill Road and Tom Kalal of Grassy Hill Road representing East Lyme Agri-
Business. They feel that agriculture was being neglected in East Lyme. They are a sub-committee of the
Economic Development Commission. Recently they did an Open Farm Day, they had a spot at East Lyme
Day, and they will be doing a trail ride in the Fall. They are here because they have suggestions
regarding Chicken Regulations. East Lyme is lacking in the chicken department. The most recent
applicant for chickens will take three years to get her money back after the costs of applying for a
permit. It is onerous and doesn’t seem right.

Mr. Kalal stated he is the operator of Cranberry Meadow Farm. They raise beef and pork and they keep
bees. The people who recently applied for a chicken coop paid $433.00 in fees for 6 chickens. They
provided members with a copy of the Guilford CT Regulations and the New Haven regulations.

REGULAR MEETING
1. Request of Theodore A. Harris for exceptions to Section 24 Site Plan Review Requirements
(specifically Section 24.6C (sidewalk) and Section 24.6e(3)(buffers) for property located at 30
West Main Street, East Lyme Assessor’s Map 11.2, Lot 29.

**Mr. Peck recused himself for Item 1.

Mr. Harris stated this is a continuation from the last meeting. There are buffer issues and sidewalk
issues. They have reviewed their plans and they are able to get the driveway between the two trees and
all existing trees will remain.

Chairman Salerno asked about the height of the arborvitaes in the back.

Mr. Harris stated they would be 10 to 12 feet tall at planting. He recognized the desire for a sidewalk in
that area, but questions the practicality of it. In order for that sidewalk to connect with downtown they
would need a new bridge. It also diminishes what can be done in the front yard. It would have to jog

around a telephone pole and a fire hydrant. The regulations allow them to take physical difficulty into
consideration.

Mr. Donovan stated they can put a sidewalk there, it is not as major as it sounds.

Chairman Salerno stated it is a tricky spot.

Mr. Hogan stated the use of the property is no longer residential but is commercial.

Mr. Donovan asked where the access to the building will be.

Mr. Harris stated in the back.

Mr. Walker stated if the intent is for a business a sidewalk would be appropriate.

Motion (1) Mr. Donovan moved to approve the request of Theodore A. Harris for exception to
Section 24 Site Plan Review Requirements (specifically Section 24.6e(3)(buffers) only and to deny the
exception to Section 24.6C (sidewalk) for property located at 30 West Main Street, East Lyme
Assessor's Map 11.2, Lot 29.

Seconded by Mr. Hogan.

Motion Passed 5-0.
