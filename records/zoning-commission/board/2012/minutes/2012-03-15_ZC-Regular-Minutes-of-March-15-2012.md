2. Application of Keith B. Neilson, P.E., of Docko, Inc. for Giants Neck Improvement Association
for a Coastal Area Management (CAM) Site Plan Review for the restoration of the Giants Neck
Beach due to damage from Tropical Storm Irene at property identified in the application as
Giants Neck Road, Niantic, Assessor’s map 04.2, Lot 3.
Mr. Walker read into the record a letter from Mr. Mulholland regarding the above referenced
application,
Mr. Bill Cotter of 3- 8" Avenue Waterford, CT representing Docko Inc. Operating at 14 Holmes Street
Mystic, CT addressed the commission.
Mr. Cotter stated that the project is for restoration of Giants Neck Beach due to damage from Tropical
Storm [rene. Mr. Mulholland asked if the footing was exposed at the seawalls. Mr. Cotter explained
that the intent of the proposal was to excavate in front of the sea wall and to place large stone to
protect the walls from exposure in the event that this were to occur again.
Mr. Cotter explained that another part of the project was to repair damage from overflow of the pond
that eroded part of the asphalt. Mr. Mulholland asked Mr. Cotter to clarify this part of the project to the
commission. Mr. Mulholland asked what type of equipment would be used and the necessity for a
permit from DEP for activity below mean high water. Mr. Cotter explained there would be a small
excavator and a loader to move the sand. The contractor feels that the project would take just a couple
of days. Mr. Mulholland asked that any refueling of vehicles would take place off the beach. Mr.
Mulholland asked for notification upon commencement of the project. Chairman Salerno asked that all
sand being dredged would be form the area and would not be trucked in from off site. The rock that
was necessary would be trucked in, there was discussion about this process. Mr. Donavan asked if any
of the steps would be buried. Mr. Mulholland asked if it was the opinion of the engineers that there
wouid not be any impact to the coastal resources, It was agreed there would not be any impact.
Chairman Salerno asked if the DEP had reviewed the permit, Mr. Cotter said that it was out for signature
and the DEP review process could take a couple of weeks. It was concluded the project would take one

week to ten days in total.
Chairman Salerno asked if there were any further questions from the commission. Hearing none he

asked for a motion.

3. Motion 2: Mr. Donovan moved to approve the Application of Keith B. Neilson, P.E., of Docko,

Irene at property identified in the application as Giants Neck Road, Niantic, Assessor’s map
04,2, Lot 3. For the following reasons:
1. Application is consistent with all applicable goals and conditions of the CAM act
2. Applicant has taken all reasonable measures to mitigate any adverse impacts of the
