A. Call to Order

B. Pledge of Allegiance
Chairman Salerno led the assembly in the Pledge of Allegiance.

C. Public Delegations
Frank Grace, the owner of Main Street Grill stated there is a scheduling conflict with this
meeting. He requested they keep the Public Hearing open after this meeting because a

lot of business owners who would like to attend this meeting are also at a Niantic Main
Street meeting that the First Selectman is speaking at.

1. Town of East Lyme Zoning Commission proposal to amend the East Lyme
Zoning Regulations Section 20.27 item number 4 to expressly prohibit
outdoor bars and service bars.

Mr. Muiholland confirmed that the legal ad had run on this item.

“Mr. Liska entered the meeting at 7:36 p.m., and was seated as a regular member for
the meeting.

Mr. McPherson read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.
Mr. McPherson read a memo from Marcy Balint of the CT DEEP.
Mr. McPherson read a letter from Mr. Goeschel.

Mr. McPherson read a letter from Southeastern Connecticut Council of Governments
Regional Planning Commission.

Chairman Salerno stated the Zoning Commission initiated the outdoor dining
regulations about 10 years ago. It was intended for dining, not bars. They want to now
be more specific with the language. It is not targeted at any specific business.

Mr. Dwyer stated from what he has observed there is no problem existing. It costs
these businesses thousands of dollars to make these. He likes the way it is.

Mr. Mulholland stated outdoor dining was intended to let restaurants have an accessory
to allow outdoor dining. The Zoning Commission has asked for clarification regarding
outdoor dining. If the Regulations don’t say you can, you can't do it.

Chairman Salerno stated the Regulations were written for outdoor dining not outdoor
bars.

Mr. Donovan stated we can’t take away their business. The way it is written now it is
harsh.

Frank Grace, the owner of the Main Street Grill stated the new words expressly prohibit
him from facilitating added seats. He understands they don’t want night club
atmosphere. He is opposed to prohibiting this because it is an integral part of
restaurants.

Craig Taft, the owner of Burke’s Tavern stated his bar has been open for 15 years.
There have been no issues. There are no problems. The Town is lucky to have
responsible business owners. They are here to generate revenue for the Town. This
makes no sense.

Mr. Mulholland stated they are going to get a legal opinion as to whether present
owners would be “grandfathered” in.

Chairman Salerno stated it is his opinion that they would be.

Mr. Mulholland stated he doesn’t feel the Zoning Commission is targeting any one
business. They have chosen to take a look at it more closely and most likely present
business owners would be grandfathered in.

Fred Finn, stated he can’t believe the Zoning Commission wants to diminish the ability
of businesses to bring in customers.

Mr. Peck stated the Zoning Commission is thinking of downtown. It is an effort to clean
up Main Street. If they aren’t pro-active with the Regulations they can envision bars up
and down Main Street.

Mr. Mulholland stated this is a family community. The Commission has done a good job
over the year. There needs to be a balance. They are very pro-business.

Frank Balantic, Laurel Hill Drive, stated this Regulation would stifle businesses. The
one year renewal keeps it in check and as a resident he is concerned.

Steve Carpenteri, 25 Harvest Glen, stated he understands what they are trying to do,
but they should look at the Regulations and control how many they allow.

Ms. Hardy entered the meeting at 8:20 p.m.
Mr. Grace stated the business owner needs to monitor the clientele.
Mr. Taft stated there is no middle ground in the language, it is stifling.

Mr. Peck suggested continuing the Public Hearing but in the meantime revisit the
wording and trim it down.

Mr. Muiholland stated if the language is more forgiving and less restrictive they would
not need to re-advertise. They can withdraw it or continue it.

Mr. McPherson stated as a Point of Order members of the Planning Commission are
not allowed to speak to the Zoning Commission.

Motion (1) Mr. Liska moved to withdraw Town of East Lyme Zoning Commission
proposal to amend the East Lyme Zoning Regulations Section 20.27 item number
4 to expressly prohibit outdoor bars and service bars.

Seconded by Mr. McPherson.

Motion Passed 6-0.

Chairman Salerno stated they have to come to the Zoning Commission for parking, just
to make sure there is an adequate amount.

Mr. Mulholland stated there are 57 spaces.

Pastor Rivera stated right now they have about 40-45 people who attend.

Mr. Mulholland stated there is quite a bit of frontage and there will be no problem with

Chairman Salerno stated there is a church in that area now and there has been no
