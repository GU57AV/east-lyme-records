New Business

Mr. Mulholland stated the office has been very busy.

3.. Comments from the Ex-Officio

Ms. Hardy stated Esther Williams will be serving as an alternate on the Zoning Board of
Appeals. Lew Mostowy had resigned his regular position, which was filled by John
Smith, who was the alternate. Care and Share will be leasing the old Town Garage.
The Children’s Museum will be leasing the old carpentry shop. They have bonded
$900,000.00 for road repairs, mostly on Walnut Hill. The Town Hall will have a $50,000
roof replacement. They will be doing sidewalk repairs, and borrowing money from the
Boardwalk lawsuit money to make temporary repairs to the Boardwalk. The Town has
received a donation of a triangular portion of land on Boston Post Road from Mr. Rando.

Mr. Dwyer asked if the Boardwalk lawsuits are done.
Ms. Hardy stated the money for the repairs, which are temporary, is being borrowed
from a lawsuit and that particular suit was settled, however there is another one
pending.

4. Comments from Zoning Board Liaison to Planning Commission
Mr. Donovan stated the Planning Commission approved an application for a
resubdivision on Black Point Road. They discussed the Zoning Commission referrals
and an application for Open Space.

There were no comments.
