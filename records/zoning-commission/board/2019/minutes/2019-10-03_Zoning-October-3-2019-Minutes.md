                     Sustainable CT Program - Summary of Goals

   a   An online framework for logistical support to CT town officials looking to implement a
       range of economic, environmental and cultural improvements and attractions.
   a   Program provides grant opportunities to fund infrastructure and transportation
       projects, protect the environment, and create efficiencies in housing and public services.
   a   Program is coordinated by Eastern Connecticut State University lnstitute for Sustainable
       Energy, and funded by three CT nonprofits: the Emily Hall Tremaine Foundation,
       Common Sense Fund, and Hampshire Foundation (providing more than 5250,000 for
       small grants to towns for climate and energy-related projects).
   a   Each participating town becomes certified when at least one of many listed actions is
       completed in each of nine different program aspects (see list below). Higher certification
       levels are earned when more actions are completed.
   a   To date more than 34 towns, including Waterford and Groton, have signed on to the
       program; fewer have completed their certification process'
   a   Goals of the program are to save towns money by helping to improve energy efficiency,
       promote partnerships within and among towns, and assist with planning tools that help
       avoid expensive infrastructure damage during storms and power outages,
