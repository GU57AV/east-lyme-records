       5. Request of the Town of East Lyme Zoning Gommission for a text amendment
       to the East Lyme Zoning Regulations to add Section 1.48.1 Definitions Medical
       Marijuana Dispensary and add a new Section 8.2.16 Medical Marijuana
       Dispensary by Special Permit.

Mr. Walker invited the Town Attorney to speak.

Mark Zamarka from Waller, Smith and Palmer, the Town Attorney's office, came fonrvard to
review some of the legal basis of a medical marijuana dispensary. He explained our concern is
whether and to what extent a dispensary can be regulated on a municipal level. He discussed
State preemption and whether State law preempts local regulation. Mr. Zamarka discussed the
liquor control act and how in some areas it gives the towns the right to prohibit the sale of
alcohol; in the medical marijuana regulations there is no corresponding provision to prohibit the
sale of medical marijuana.

Mr. Zamarka briefly discussed towns that have chosen to ban medical marijuana production and
dispensary facilities outright:
   .   Monroe
   .   Ridgefield
   .   Guilford
   .   Bethel
He noted there are no cases that have challenged such bans and if the Town chose to ban they
shouldn't discount the possibility of a legal challenge. He observed that the majority of
municipalities that dealwith this issue do this through regulations and discussed some
examples that allow this use in industrial zones with separation requirements.

Mr. Manning said to his understanding they could choose to ban package stores and bars but
we can't ban dispensaries.

The Commission discussed their options with the Town Attorney.

The Commission had an in-depth discussion of the proposal itself
