             bees with a water source. He stated bees prefer dirty brackish water and the

             is dry and hot. They try to educate beekeepers to supply water during those
             months.
             M. Creighton stated he gets 2-3 calls a year from local zoning officials about
             conflicts with neighbors over bees; he will go with the ZEO and talk to the
             neighbors to educate and diffuse the situation. He stated most people that keep
             bees are usually on smaller lots.
