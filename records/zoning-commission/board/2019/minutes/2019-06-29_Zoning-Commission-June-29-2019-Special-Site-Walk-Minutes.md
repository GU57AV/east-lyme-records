 The Zoning Commission met at the East Lyme Town Hall on June 29th, 2019 al9:00 a.m. and
then proceeded to Ancient Highway for a site walk of the property referenced in the application
of Eric J. Garofano:
        -Application of Eric J. Garofano, Esq., Agent for John H. Drabik, Owner, for a Special
        Permit for construction of a 175' tall amateur radio tower to be located on property
        identified in the application as Ancient Highway, East Lyme Assesso/s Map 30, Lots 1
        And 2.

The Commission arrived at the site at 9:15 a.mr and Mr. Garofano and Mr. Drabik gave a brief
tour of the property. Around 15 neighbors were also present for the site visit.

Mr. Peck inquired about the base of the tower and Mr. Garofano said it would be 30 X 30 square
feet.

Mr. Donovan and Mr. Peck discussed the clearing of the site with Mr. Drabik; 85 X 85 square
feet is currently cleared and will be 100 X 100 square feet fenced in if the tower is approved.

Mr. Dwyer asked about the ceremonial landscape and Mr. Walker clarified this visit is to see
where the site is in relation to the neighbors; he added that he would have to rely on someone
with more expertise since he wouldn't know what he's looking at.
