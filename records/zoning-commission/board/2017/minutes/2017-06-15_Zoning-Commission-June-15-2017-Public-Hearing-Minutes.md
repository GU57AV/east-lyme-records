1. APPLICATION OF EUGENE EMINI, OWNER, BLACK POINT PIZZA, FOR A SPECIAL PERMIT FOR

OUTDOOR DINING AT 44 BLACK POINT ROAD, NIANTIC WITH NO ENTERTAINMENT

Chairman Walker confirmed the legal advertisement ran on this item.

Mr. McPherson read a memo from Mr. Mulholland.

Eugene Emini stated they are applying for outdoor dining; they will have planter boxes facing the street
and fence along the side. They are applying to the State for a beer and wine permit. They have done a
full renovation inside and have done an exterior facelift, they think this will mix in with the community

and fit in with the neighborhood.

Mr. Mulholland asked if the fence will be vertical rail or split rail.

Mr. Emini stated it will be vertical. They will also be using landscape timbers. They have not decided on
the color of the fence. They will do plantings in the flower boxes prior to placing them. They will be
open from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. on weekdays and 11 a.m. to 11 p.m. on weekends. The patio will be open
those same hours; there will be waitress service on the patio. They will have umbrellas with no
advertising. There will not be an outdoor bar. There is existing lighting there now, if there is any added
it would be added for décor only.

Mr. Donovan asked if they would be willing to close the patio earlier, they need to worry about the
neighbors.

Mr. Emini stated if 8:00 p.m. is the last seating there shouldn’t be anybody on the patio past 10:00.
Mr. Donovan asked if they will have speakers with radio music on the patio.

Mr. Emini stated with low amplification, they want a relaxed environment.

Mr. Donovan suggested last seating on patio on weekdays at 8:00, and 9:00 on weekends.

Mr. McPherson suggested they have a condition that the approval of the lighting and fencing will be
given by Mr. Mulholland.

Mr. Donovan stated the outdoor speakers should be separate from the interior speakers.
PUBLIC COMMENT
George Mitchell stated he lives in that neighborhood and he thinks this is a good thing.

Terry Womack, 47 Black Point Road stated it is good to have business in that area, it is good for the town
and the neighborhood.

Virginia Zitzkat of 35 Central Avenue stated she plans to be there at least once a week, she is very
excited,

MOTION (1) — Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.
Seconded by Mr. Donovan.
Motion Passed 6-0.

Respectfully Submitted,
