4. Application of Leo Roche, for Strive LLC, for a Renewal of a Special Permit for
Outdoor Dining at property identified in the application as 247-2 Main Street,
Niantic.

2[Page
Mr. Walker noted that the Legal Ad had been published.

Mr. McPherson noted that he concerned about this renewal. He explained that the Wednesday
before Thanksgiving he went by the property and witnessed the doors jammed open and drinks
and people everywhere; the patio was over capacity and people were sitting on the wall and
drinking without being seated at tables.

Mr. McPherson noted that he went to the Police Station and that when he returned to the area
an hour or so later, it was better but still a problem.

Mr. Donovan said at that time of year every business is packed solid and it’s added that it’s one
night, during the year. He said it would be extreme to deny approval based on one evening.

Mr. Donovan added that larger flower boxes could be installed and that he doesn’t want to hurt

Mr. Roche’s business.

Mr. Walker said the main areas of concern seem to be sitting on the wall and people drinking 2
without being seated at a table. He said it’s not easy to police a busy establishment every

second.

Mr. Walker observed if flowers were put in completely around the bar, no one would be able to
sit on the wall. He added that it’s up to the Manager to ensure that people are seated when
drinking, but it will never be perfect.

Mr. Mulholland said the Black Sheep is an asset to downtown and suggested hearing from the
operator since it is a public hearing; perhaps staff could be told to police the establishment
more.

Mr. Donovan asked why Smokey O’Grady’s doesn’t have the same condition (sitting at a table
while drinking on the patio), tied to their approval.

Mr. Mulholland explained that the village is a different zone and therefore subject to different
conditions.

Mr. Donovan said he doesn’t believe it’s fair to single one business out.

Mr. Roche of 247 Main Street came forward to speak. He said the S+P Restaurant in Mystic
allows you to sit on the wall and there hasn’t been a problem. He detailed his businesses in
Groton/Stonington and Westerly, nothing these towns do not carry any of the outside dining
conditions that East Lyme has.

Mr. Roche said he tries his best to follow any conditions the Board requires but feels the rules in
East Lyme should be less strict and the same for every party. He added that technically no one in

3[Page
Town is supposed to be served alcohol without food, so no one in Town or on the Board is
following that rule.

Mr. Roche said that he and his Manager cannot be outside 24 hours a day, but will continue to
do the best they can that the conditions are met. He said that the Police have never mentioned
the incident before Thanksgiving that Mr. McPherson is referring to.

Mr. Walker thanked Mr. Roche and said his feelings have been noted and if the Commission sees

fit, conditions can be tweaked.

Mr. Peck gave a brief history of the resident surveys conducted several years ago and explained
that the surveys shaped the rules we have now, which is why there are annual renewals.

There were no public comments.

MOTION (2)

Mr. Donovan moved to close the Public Hearing.
Mr. McPherson seconded the motion.

Motion passed, 6-0-0.

9. Application of Carla Steele, for Elements Flowers, for a Special Permit for Office and
Retail Sales at property identified in the application as 5 Freedom Way, Building C Unit 8,
Niantic.

Mr. Peck recused himself this item and Mr. Walker sat Mr. Mclhenney in his stead.
Mr. Walker noted the legal ad which was published.

Mr. Mulholland referred the Commission to his memorandum dated May 4", 2017 in the
member packet; the business is located in the light industrial zone and a permitted use.

had.

Ms. Steele said the shop will handle big events such as weddings by request. She has no plan to
do regular retail outside of holidays; it doesn’t make sense to do so when most orders are done
via the internet.
