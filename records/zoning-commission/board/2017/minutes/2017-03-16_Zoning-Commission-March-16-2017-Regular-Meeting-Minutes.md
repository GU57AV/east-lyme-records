15. COMMENTS FROM EX-OFFICIO

Ms. Hardy stated the Commission on Aging is very busy with a small staff. There is a tremendous
demand on them for transportation, when age restricted developments come before this Commission
they need to think about program needs. Transportation is provided for doctor appointments,
pharmacies, grocery shopping. They are also serving 15 to 20 meals a day. There are 2 full time drivers
and one part time driver. In the past when developments have come into town they have given gifts to
the Town. It is something to think about. The Elementary School Project Referendum was postponed
due to weather. The Town had to obtain considerable legal advice since a postponement had never
been done before. The Board of Selectmen has been busy with budget reviews. The Police Commission
has been reviewing applications for the Police Chief position. They are down to 5 candidates. The initial
screening will be done by three current Police Chiefs. The Town is going to be renting lockup space from
the Town of Waterford. The Board of Selectmen approved a communications tower at the rear of the
Darrow’s Pond property.

Mr. Donovan stated when he first heard of the tower it was described as an emergency tower, but then
the First Selectman said the agreement has potential for cellular.

Ms. Hardy stated when it was first presented it was for town use for emergency dispatch. Then during
last night’s discussion it was stated that in the future the company building the tower may wish to put
cellular on the tower. The company is paying for all of it, at no cost to the town.

Mr. Donovan stated if they sublet to a cellular company the town gets half the money.

Ms. Hardy stated they floated balloons and it is not in a highly visible area, it is at the back of the
property. Ms. Hardy stated the company is bearing all of the costs of construction and the tower.
