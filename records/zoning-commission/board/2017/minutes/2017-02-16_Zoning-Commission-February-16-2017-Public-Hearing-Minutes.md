Attorney Erin Cabelus representing Mr. Foster stated they have revised their proposed text amendment;
she reminded them this is a supplement and not intended to replace the current section. This will give a
level playing field to smaller farms. This is consistent with the POCD. This will only apply to lots that
have agricultural uses. This would enable the smaller farms to obtain an annual Special Permit for
Dinners at the Farm for 5 consecutive nights one time per calendar year. This would enable the smaller
farms to have amplified sound events up to 5 times in one calendar year. There would be a one-time
special permit for agri-tourism events such as indoor music, fruit picking, trail rides, indoor farm to table
dinners, etc. They took into consideration the comments from the last hearing. They do not want to
add an administrative burden to the Town. All outdoor music would cease by 10 p.m.

Mr. Peck asked about the language 150 feet to the property or 350 feet to the dwelling. He thought it
should be “and” instead of “or”.

Robert Foster, 22A Scott Road, East Lyme thanked the members and respectfully wished to keep the
comments brief and on point. He asked his supporters to be here, and he asked their comments to only
be pertinent to the text amendment. This entire process is based on suggestions by Mr. Mulholland in a
letter he received last year. He believes they have addressed their concerns. The East Lyme Planning
Commission outlined the parcels of land that could be affected. However, this would only concern the
50 active farms, not land parcels, but active farms. The Planning Commission comments are in conflict
with the POCD. They do not wish to have all of New London County at their farm functions, they are
mostly by invitation.

Chairman Walker asked that comments by related to the text amendment.

Mark Christensen, 66 Grassy Hill Road, East Lyme stated he is the Chair of the Agri-Business Sub-
Committee; he was also a member of the 2009 POCD Sub-Committee, and a former member of the

2
Planning Commission. He was dismayed to read the Planning Commission’s letter saying that this would
be inconsistent with the POCD. What could be more “New England” than a small farm? They
overreacted with stating 283 parcels would be affected. People talked about what kind of farming can
be done at 10 p.m., but often times it is imperative to work late at night.

Attorney Cabelus entered the following exhibits:

Exhibit AA — POCD Sections;
Exhibit BB — Special Permit application for McCook’s.

Mr. Donovan asked if they are trying to replace the current section.

Attorney Cabelus stated they are just supplementing it.

Mr. Manning stated they should add a heading stating it is for farms of 5 to 49 acres.

Mr. McPherson stated this allows amplification, but for 50 plus acres it is not allowed.

Attorney Cabelus stated that theirs would have to be amended then.

Mr. Mulholland stated they should weigh this text amendment and discuss it during deliberations.
Attorney Cabelus stated she hopes they clarified that this doesn’t affect 283 parcels.

Mr. Donovan stated White Gate went under the same scrutiny.

Mr. Peck stated as to the amplified music and in fairness to everyone, he has been told the Special
Permit for McCook’s the music stops at 8:00 p.m. except for Celebrate East Lyme when the music has to

stop at 10:15 p.m. Is 8:00 acceptable to the applicant for amplified sound events?

Mr. Foster stated they would defer to the existing Noise Ordinance that is in place, 8:00 may be
considered not practical.

Mr. Donovan asked if that was a no?
Mr. Foster stated they can take that as a no.

Attorney Cabelus stated they had presented them with sample ordinances from other towns, there are
some interesting points in Salem’s and Griswold’s ordinances.

Chairman Walker stated he struggles with the outdoor amplified music. We have an obligation to help
small farms, but we also have an obligation to all residents.

There were no comments for or against this item from the Public in attendance.
Motion (1) Mr. McPherson moved to close the Public Hearing.

Seconded by Mr. Donovan.
