5 Application of Leo Roche, for Strive LLC, for a Renewal of a Special Permit for

Niantic.
Mr. Peck asked if we wanted to conditions of all these applications.
The Commission discussed downtown properties.

Mr. Donovan said that everyone’s conditions should be the same or the condition should be
removed altogether.

Mr. Mulholland said this can be changed for next year’s approvals if the commission so
wishes.

be policed anyway. He said no real problems have occurred and he thinks we can loosen up
on the rules.

there are problems or fights, that businesses owner can be denied their renewal next
summer.

Mr. Dwyer and Mr. Manning both said they agreed and the no alcohol unless you’re having
food rule is unrealistic.
