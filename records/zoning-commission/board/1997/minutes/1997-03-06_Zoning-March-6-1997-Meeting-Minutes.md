                           EAST LYME ZONING Commission
                                 REGULAR MEETING
                            THURSDAY, MARCH 6,1997
                              EAST LYME TOWN HALL

CALLED TO ORDER

     Wayne Fraser called the Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Zoning Commission to order
     at 8:21 p.m. on March 6, 1997 at the East Lyme Town Hall in Niantic, Connecticut.

ATTENDANCE

     Five regular members were in attendance: Wayne Fraser, Athena Cone, Norman Peck,
     Chris Mullaney and Shawn McLaughlin and one alternate member was in attendance:
     Donn Jourdan.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS

     Jim Logateri asked about the future of the Boardwalk. Bill Mulholland indicated that
     he was unsure about the jurisdiction of the Zoning Commission but that it was being
     discussed.

     Joe Kowsnuski, 67 Walnut Hill Road, indicated that he was curious why there was
     such a big secret with regards to the Boardwalk. He also discussed the "Sidewalks
     to Nowhere."

MINUTES OF FEBRUARY 6, 1997

     Athena Cone moved to accept the minutes of the February 6, 1997 regular meeting.
     Chris Mullaney seconded the motion. Five members were in favor of the motion. One
     member abstained from the vote. No one was opposed. The motion carried.

REGULAR MEETING

     APPLICATION OF PAUL AND DONNA FORMICA

     Chris Mullaney moved to approve the Application Paul and Donna Formica for a Special
     Permit to establish a fast food restaurant at 10 Chesterfield Road, further identified on
     Lot #42 East Lyme Assessor Map 31.1. Donn Jourdan seconded the motion. Six
     members were in favor of the motion. None were opposed. The motion carried.
     Effective upon publication, for the following reason: The Application complies with all
     applicable Zoning Regulations and meets the Special Permit criteria.


     APPLICATION OF NORMAN P. PECK, III

     Norman P. Peck, lll stepped down during the vote of this Application. Chris Mullaney
     moved to approve the Application of Norman P. Peck, lll, 32 West Main Street,
     Niantic, Connecticut for a Special Permit to keep livestock (1-2 Horses, 2-3 Sheep and
     less than a dozen fowl), property further identified as Lot #30 on East Lyme Assessor
     Map 11.1. Athena Cone seconded the motion. Five members were in favor of the
     motion. None were opposed. The motion carried. Effective upon publication, for the
     following reason: The Application agrees with East Lyme Zoning Commission
     Regulations.

     APPLICATION OF JOHN T. PARKOS

     Chris Mullaney moved to approve the Application of John T. Parkos for a Special
     Permit to operate a fast food restaurant (Chica go Pizzal at 1 80 Flanders Road, Niantic,
     Connecticut, property further identified as lot #12on East Lyme Assessor Map 26.3.
     lncluded within the scope of the approval are the following exceptions: 34.6E 2 -
     Front Landscaping; and 34.6E 3 - Buffer Strips. Athena Cone seconded the motion.
     Five members were in favor of the motion. None were opposed. The motion carried.
     Effective upon publication, for the following reason: The exceptions are granted due
     to the nature of previously existing on site physical conditions.


OLD BUSINESS

     SUBCOMMITTEES

     Wayne Fraser discussed the little items that need "housekeeping" that he would like
     to get taken care of in one Public Hearing.

     Wayne discussed the Woods place. He said he was authorizing the clean up of the
     place. He indicated that he had corresponded with them and given them ample time
     to respond, which they have not. Chris Mullaney has how long it would take before
     the project was completed once started. Bill Mulholland indicated that it would
     probably only take a week. Bill Mulholland also stated that the project should be done
     the end of March or early April. Wayne Fraser said that after several meetings with
     no results his patience had ended.

     ZONING ENFORCEMENT OFFICER

     Bill Mulholland stated that the budget had been approved by The Board of Selectman
     and was going forward to Finance. He also said that this is a busy time of year of for
     Zoning.
NEW BUSINESS

      NIANTIC SPORTSMEN CLUB

      Ted Harris discussed the Nazarko vs. East Lyme Zoning Commission and the Niantic
      Sportsmen club. He indicated that the Zoning Commission would have to formally
      authorize the Town Council to appeal the decision. He spoke about the problems of
      the case that lead to the decision. Athena Cone moved to authorize the Town Counsel
      to appeal the court decision in the case of Nazarko vs. East Lyme Zoning Commission
      and the Niantic Sportsmen club. Chris Mullaney seconded the motion. Five members
      were in favor of the motion. One member abstained. The motion carried.

      The Commission spoke about the budget for a part-time person and computer system.
      Athena Cone stated that it would be a good idea to look into software now. Chris
      Mullaney asked if the Commission would be able to show a savings.

      BUSINESS ON THE FLOOR

      Athena Cone motioned to put three Applications on the agenda. Shawn McLaughlin
      seconded the motion. Six member were in favor. None were opposed. The motion
      carried. The following Applications were put on the agenda to be scheduled for the
      next available Public Hearing:

      1.     The Application of Robert Reardon for a change of zone from RU-40 to CA-
      Commercial for properties known as 435, 436,441, and 442 Boston Post Road, East
      Lyme, Connecticut.

      2. The Application of Theodore Harris to amend Section 18.1.3 - Definitions and
      Section 18.1.10 to allow on site directional signs.

      3.    The Application of J. Robert Pfanner & Associates, P.C. for a Special Permit to
      construct and operate a recreational (RV) Park on property of Chesterfield Road, East
      Lyme, Connecticut, further identified as East Lyme Assessor Map 48, Lot 123.

      CORRESPONDENCE

      Wayne Fraser discussed a letter that the Commissioners should have received inviting
      them to the Kick-Off Supper for the Yale Study.

      Athena Cone said that it was nice to see Mrs. Hardy's students attending meetings
      regularly.

      ADJOURNMENT

      Athena Cone motioned to adjourn the meeting at 9:07 p.m. Norman Peck seconded
      the motion. The meeting was adjourned.



Heather S. Paul, Recording Secretary
