EAST LYME ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS
REGULAR MEETING

A Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Zoning Board of Appeals was held on Monday, May 26, 2015 at 6:00
PM at the East Lyme Town Hall, 108 Pennsylvania Avenue, Niantic, CT.

PRESENT: William Mountzoures, Chairman, Tom Boguszewski, Craig Mason,

Executive Session — Pending Litigation - Maluccio v. East Lyme Zoning Board of Appeals

Mr. Mountzoures called for a motion to enter Executive Session for the purpose of discussion on pending

**MOTION (3)

Mr. Mason moved to adjourn this East Lyme Zoning Board of Appeals meeting at 6:51 PM.
