RE: Variance Application for 14 Orchard Street
To whom it may concern,

1 am filing this variance application on behalf of my client, Karen Nickerson, property owner of 14
Orchard Street. She wishes to convert the existing three season cottage into a year-round dwelling
with a small accessible bedroom on the first floor.

The existing residence is a small cottage with one designated sleeping area on the 2" floor. The
purpose of the variance is to construct a small 126 sf bedroom on the first floor to allow my client
adequate space for sleeping as her age starts to hinder her ability to navigate stairs safely.

In looking at the existing neighborhood, I believe the town imposed a hardship on 14 Orchard Street
as well as many other landowners in the area by imposing regulations that do not match the existing
fabric of the neighborhood and therefore creating pre-existing nonconforming lots throughout the
district. Ms Nickerson’s lot is a narrow lot of roughly 25’ in width and about 100° in depth. By
applying the 12’ required side yard setback it renders the entire lot nonconforming by allowing a
roughly 12” strip of buildable land. The lot coverage is also stringent in that the lot area is almost
25% of the required size and the existing residence is nonconforming. Visual inspection of the bird’s
eye view of the area shows a vast number of lots in the neighborhood exceed the 20% building
coverage requirement.

The proposed design will show the bedroom does not increase any existing side yard setback
nonconformity on either side and it adheres to the existing front yard setback from Pleasant Street. It
also stays consistent with the fabric of the neighborhood and does not deviate from the general
purpose or intent of the regulations.

Sincerely,
