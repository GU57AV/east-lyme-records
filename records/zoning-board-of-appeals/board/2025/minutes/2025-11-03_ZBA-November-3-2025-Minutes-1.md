    2. Public Hearing
          East Lyme ZoningBoard of Appeals Case 3-2025: Application of Matthew H. Greene Esq for a variance
          to the East Lyme Zoning Regulations Section 18.1.5, Table I .5C Freestanding Sign Heights, from 15
          feet to 20.6 feet at top of the sign at the southeasterly corner of the property located at 300 Flanders Rd,
          Niantic. Assessor Map 31.3 Lot 6

          -Mr. Carpenteri noted that the legal ads had been run and were in the file. He invited the Applicant to
           speak.


           -Attorney Greene of 300 State St, New London, Agent for Cash Home Center, spoke on behalf of the
           application. He made the following comments:
           -Seeking height variance for 1 freestanding sign closest to Flanders Road.
           -He noted that the State had taken land along Flanders Rd and while the location of the sign was not part
           of the land taken, it was removed for the work to be done adjacent to it and then reinstalled by the State.
           -Due to the elevation of the new Flanders Rd, a retaining wall was built with a six (6) foot fence on top
           of it.
           -For the reinstalled sign to be seen, it had to be elevated 16 feet.
           -Photos in the record show the fence, retaining wall, and reinstalled sign.
           -The reinstalled sign was placed in same location as had been removed, it was present for decades before
           removal.
           -The sign has the same dimensions (4 feet x 12 feet) same internal illumination, the only difference is
           the 16 feet from the ground to the bottom of the sign.
           -The Regulations only allow for 15 feet, hence the reason for the variance request,
           -The top of the sign would reach 20.6 feet as there is a 6" gap between the end of the poles and the
           bottom of the sign.
           -The hardship is due to the unique circumstances of the State project which changed the topography of
           the land, elevated Flanders Rd and the building of a retaining wall with a fence on top of it.
           -Without the variance the sign would not be visible.
           -Through no fault of Cash Home Center, a sign which had been in place for decades was removed and
           reinstalled in the same location due to a State project..

           -Mr. Clapp noted a typo in Exhibit 2,Paragraph 3, where it stated that the sign would be20'6'but
           should rcad2j'6"
