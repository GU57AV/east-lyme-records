L. The office staff cannot and will not state what the ZBA Board may decide.

2. The office staff cannot and will not deny anyone the right to apply for a variance.

3. The office staff cannot be held responsible for the information disseminated to Mr. Wilson from Mrs.
Wilson,

4. The office staff can and does provide an opinion as to whether something qualifies as a legal hardship,
however, the staff will not deny anyone the right to go before the Board.

5. The office staff provides information from a legal land use primer entitled "Connecticut Practice Series,
Connecticut Land Use Law and Practice" by Attorney Robert Fuller which describes legal hardships as
well as ZBA powers, authorities, and duties.
