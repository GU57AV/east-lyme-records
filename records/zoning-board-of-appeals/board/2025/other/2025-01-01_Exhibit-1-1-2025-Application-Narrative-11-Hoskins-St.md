Dear Members of the Zoning Board of Appeals,

Subject: Variance Request for 75-Foot Front Setback Requirement at 11 Hoskins Street, Niantic, CT
06357

t respectfully request a variance from the 75-foot front setback requirement for sheds in the R10 zoning
district to allow placement of an 8x10 shed 17 feet from the front property line at my property, 11
Hoskins Street, Niantic, CT 06357.

My property has a unique physical characteristic that justifies this variance: the front property line
abuts another private property rather than a public road, unlike most lots in the R10 district. This
configuration renders the 75-foot setback requirement disproportionately restrictive, as it consumes a
significant portion of my lot’s usable space and prevents me from placing a shed in a practical location.
Due to existing structures that comply with the setback, it is impossible or impractical to install a shed
—a permitted accessory structure—without this variance, effectively denying me reasonable use of my
property.

This hardship is not self-created, as it arises from the lot's pre-existing configuration. The requested
variance, reducing the setback to 17 feet, is the minimum necessary to alleviate this hardship. The
proposed shed will not impact public health, safety, or welfare, as it will not be visible from any public
road and can be screened with landscaping to minimize any visuat impact on the abutting property.
Granting this variance aligns with the comprehensive zoning plan by allowing a permitted use while
preserving the character of the neighborhood. | respectfully request your approval of this variance to
enable reasonable use of my property.

Thank you for your consideration. | am available to provide additional information or attend a hearing to
discuss this request.
