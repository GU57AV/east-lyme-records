December 15, 2015 MEETING MINUTES

Members Present: Steve Dinsmore, Chairman; Barbara Johnston; Treasurer Don Landers; and

Rick Kanter
Members Absent: Bill Mountzoures, Greg Murin, Joe Hitchery

1. CALL TO ORDER. Chairman Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:35 p.m.

2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF NOVEMBER 17,
2015. Minutes were approved 3-0-1.

3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS.

Keith Nielsen of Docko, Inc. Presented a proposal for the additional of floating transient
docks and a dingy dock at Cini Park. Mr. Landers made a motion to find it consistent with our
Harbor Management Plan. Mr. Kanter seconded. After a lengthy discussion, the motion was
tabled as a consensus could not be reached (2-2, with Ms. Johnston and Mr. Kanter dissenting).
Mr. Neilsen will investigate some commission concerns and the topic will be placed on next

month’s agenda.

4. REPORTS

A. Shellfish.

Mr. Kanter reported that it was a very good year for scallops. Over 200 permits sold.
B. Harbor No Report.

C. Treasurer.

Mr. Landers presented a budget with an 11% budget increase. After discussion, with our
ex-officio, the town will be looking to hold costs. Mr. Landers will reallocate funds from other
departments and will hold the line at the current spending level.

D. Ex-Officio Mr. Salerno reported on current proposals in the board of selectman’s
meeting.

5. OLD BUSINESS

A. Harbor Management Plan—
QY EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

6. NEW BUSINESS

A. Election of officers. Mr. Dinsmore was re-elected Chairman, Mr. Landers was re-
elected Treasurer, Mr. Hitchery was re-elected secretary.

B. Meeting dates were submitted for the 2016 calendar year. Corrected calendar will be
submitted to the town clerk.

C. Correspondence — None.

7. FINAL COMMENTS - None
8. ADJOURNMENT

The meeting was adjourned at 8:53 PM
