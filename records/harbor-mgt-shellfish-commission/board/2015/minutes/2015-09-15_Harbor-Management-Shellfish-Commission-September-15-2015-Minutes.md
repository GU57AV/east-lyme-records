3. Public Delegations

A. 249 Old Black Point Application - John Lust, who is charge of guiding this Application
through the DEEP process, stated that plans were sent for a residential pier, ramp, and float. The
applicant is Davies and Kramer. He distributed the plans to the Commissioners for their review.
He went on to state that there are new owners from Redding at this property, and the original
house that was built in 1935 was taken down, and they are now constructing a new home on the
property. He inquired regarding any comments/concerns from the Commissioners. DEEP and the
Army Corps have both okayed the design. One concern that Mr. Lust has is that it's very shallow
over there, so the pier has to go out quite a ways to get in water that's deep enough to
accommodate any kind of a boat. The design is as follows: it's a 90' pier (5' wide) with 23' ramp
and an 8x20 dock; there will be a boat lift on the property and a few tie-off pilings.

Commissioner Johnston inquired regarding the size of the boat planned to be docked there. Mr.
Lust wasn't sure, but believed the boats would be somewhere in the 30' to 35' range. Apparently,
they haven't purchased a boat yet, but have plans to do so, as they are avid boaters.

Commissioner Kanter inquired regarding the size of the floaters. Mr. Lust stated that DEEP
realized that the wave action in this area deems more square footage necessary for stability,
therefore they allow up to 160 SF with a wind/wave analysis, which was performed.

Commissioner Landers wanted to know if this was an individual Permit, to which Mr. Lust
affirmed that it is. Commissioner Landers further instructed Mr. Lust to provide the
Commissioners with Attachment E and D to the Application, in order for the Commissioner to
proceed on it. Commissioner Landers also wanted to verify that this design plan was akin to the
two other docks that are to the north and south of this property, to which Mr. Lust confirmed that
itis. Commissioner Landers also weighed in with respect to the shellfishing in the area, which is
all pretty strict relay. There are now active shellfishing activities there, with no Leases in the area.
So he has no issues with the Proposal, with respect to its impact on shellfishing.

MOTION: Commissioner Landers moved to find the subject Dock Application for Kramer
and Davies, as presented by John Lust, consistent with the Shellfish Management Plan. It
was seconded by Commissioner Johnston. The motion carried. (5-0-0)

MOTION: Commissioner Johnston moved to find the subject Dock Application for Kramer
and Davies, as presented by John Lust, consistent with the Harbor Management Plan. It
was seconded by Commissioner Murin. The motion carried. (5-0-0)

"While the East Lyme Harbor Management Shellfish Commission finds the Application
consistent with the Harbor Management Plan, Commission feels it is not the best location
for a floating dock, due to its proximity to southwest exposure."

4. Reports

A. Shellfish. Commissioner Kanter said the river's been closed due to the 2.5" to 3" received and
that the rain gauge was not reading correctly. He went on to explain that other rain gauges
indicated 2.5" to 3" and the local rain gauge only showed 0.75". He stated that Peter Harris spoke
to someone in David regarding the calibration.

Mr. Morris chimed in to indicate that the rain gauge was full of berries, which is not a new
problem. So, the ladder truck from Niantic went and cleaned it out. The gauge currently is at 100
feet. Commissioner Kanter queried regarding the height. Mr. Morris replied that it was to keep it
away from squirrels, etc. He continued on to state that Niantic Fire put one on their building, as
well, and it's been fine up there for a while. Yet, every few years, this berry issue happens. More
discussion ensued regarding the maintenance of the rain gauge.

Commissioner Johnston brought up the issue of wardens enforcing rules during poor weather.
Commissioner Kanter replied to state that the wardens and the DEEP authorities would go at low
time on a nice day, when there's going to be more clammers out there, although they do have a
random schedule during bad weather, when the river is closed, as there could be poachers out
there.

Discussion took place regarding water drainage in the town.

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Morris declared that the moorings are completed. The money collected
was approximately 97% of all of the fees. Mr. Morris and Mr. Berger intend on searching the bay
a bit more within the next few weeks. The desire is to have Jaxson Vibber during the outing to be
able to identify moorings that were marked last year that have yet to be pulled this year, as well as
to identify some of the newer ones that nobody's used, i.e. two off of Crescent Beach that haven't
been used in years. Mr. Morris suggested that the Commissioner should discuss setting money
aside every year to use for the services of mooring haulers, so that moorings could be pulled as
they are discovered, rather than being delayed until a meeting is had on the topic.

Chairman Dinsmore indicated that there is money set aside every year for maintenance of the
harbor. Commissioner Landers cautioned that although the money is available, outreach and
communication should take place prior to any pulling, as to not create a disturbance among the
mooring permit holders, in terms of trying to locate their moorings after they've been removed.

Discussion ensued regarding the inability to contact certain permit holders, due to death or
relocation.
