3. Public Delegations

Allen Steiger, from East Lyme, off of Pine Grove, questioned the speed limit on the Niantic Channel. He
was curious as to how many speeding tickets have been issued on the Niantic River in the past, and
whether or not the Commission has any plans and/or strategies in order to address the speed limit issues
going on. His main concern was it was a safety hazard for kayakers and people maneuvering small
sailboats in the area. He also mentioned that the high speeds caused damaging wake, which did disrupt
the marine life in the area. He made suggestions to increase awareness, education, and communication
with the public about the issue, and requested if the Commission could publish in their Meeting Minutes
monthly the number of speeding tickets issued on the Niantic River in that month, just to keep the public
informed.

Chairman Dinsmore addressed his concerns stating that the East Lyme and Waterford Police do monitor
the speed zones on the River. Commissioner Landers added that it's unfortunate that the monitoring is not
consistent, and that the days that they need to be out patrolling, which, during the summer, are from
Friday afternoon until] Sunday evening, tend to be the most sparse times when the Police are out on the
water. He did go on to say that whilst it is a busy time on the water, it also is the busiest season on the
streets, as well, and so staffing may be an issue.

Chairman Dinsmore stated that if Mr. Steiger wanted to, he should approach Law Enforcement to request
more surveillance. This comment was echoed by Commissioner Landers. Chairman Dinsmore also said
that, if need be, the Commission can approach Law Enforcement, as well.

Commissioner Landers commented that Fred Grimsby is working on getting informational signs attached
to the RR Bridge, although the regulations are quite specific. He agreed that a communication plan
should be developed, alongside Law Enforcement Officials.

Mr. Salerno suggested that, since he receives a letter every month from Police Department regarding the
number of street tickets issued, he will see if there's one regarding boating, and, if not, he will request it
from the Police Department.

5. Old Business

A. Harbor Management Plan - Update. Chairman Dinsmore spoke with Mr. Steadman last week
on Friday night. Mr. Steadman discussed during that meeting that he had plans to charter a plane
from Chester airport, in order to take aerial photographs of the area. Mr. Steadman is going to try
and incorporate the photographs into the Plan. He inquired of Chairman Dinsmore regarding to
whom he should circulate the current revised Plan, without said photographs. The Commissioners
came up with the following names to contact: Dave Putnam, Parks and Rec; Bill Mulholland,
Zoning; Gary Goeschel, Planning; Art Carlson, Conservation; Peter Harris, WELSCO; Friends;
Save the River.

Chairman Dinsmore questioned whether a 30-day window was enough time in order to get
feedback from all of these groups. Mr. Salerno said it's not enough time for the Zoning
Commission, but he doesn't foresee a problem with the Planning Commission. Chairman
Dinsmore stated that he plans to send a letter to those listed above, that will instruct them that the
Commission will be accepting comments from them through either the next Regular Meeting, or
the end of July, whichever would accommodate more feedback. He goes on to say that DEEP is
planning to give some final comments from Marcy Balint, who is currently on vacation. .

Commissioner Landers is concerned that there may be some issues once the boardwalk goes in, so
he stated he's curious as to what Park and Rec has to say about the Plan. Chairman Dinsmore
stated that after the comments are received and incorporated into the Plan, the final Plan will go to
Board of Selectman, as well as the Town Attorney, along with the Ordinance. Mr. Salerno
commented that the Ordinance looks mostly benign.

B. Port Authority Act - Chairman Dinsmore spoke with Mr. Steadman about its status. Right now,
it's basically on fast track being pushed by the Governor. Mr. Steadman has reached out to many
of the Agencies that are providing input, yet nobody has responded, to-date. Chairman Dinsmore
suggested that Mr. Steadman call the Governor. Mr. Pinto is trying to call the Governor's office to
see if we can provide some input into the ramifications of all of the major ports being under Port
Authority and minor ports under Economic Development, yet it's indeterminate to where it's going
to fall.

Commissioner Hitchery said he heard from Mr. Steadman that they were going to transfer Harbor
Masters to DEEP. Transportation was to get out of harbor management entirely, with plans of
transferring it to the Port Authority, who would manage all of the harbors. However, the Port
Authority is not a State Agency. This topic could come up in Special Session, and would be in
limbo until a final decision was made. Commissioner Landers said that the Feds need to get
involved. The State Senator and the State Representative were contacted with no response.
Chairman Dinsmore stated he will pass on any information he receives to the Commission.
