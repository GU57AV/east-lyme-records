3. Public Delegations

Bob Doane had a Permit Application. He also provided two full-sized plans and an exhibit. The
proposal was a dock for 2 Elizabeth Street, just north of Smith Cove, which was a dock that
existed 30 years ago. John Petrillo would like to rebuild it. It hasn't been in the water for three or
four years, because there was an issue regarding the ownership of the shoreline. An application
had been submitted to DEEP, and they wanted to wait until that's resolved, which it has been
currently. He went on to explain that they have a dock on the north side and a dock on the south
side, and he projected a line across. It's proposed to go 110 feet out from the coastal jurisdiction
line. The review process had been started with the State. He stated he will get the signature form
to the Commissioners, as it seems to be missing from the packet. WELSCO liked the proposal, as
long as some conditions were met, i.e. chain stops on the float to keep it off the bottom.
Therefore, this would be a floating dock. Decking material would be pressure-treated.

The Commissioners asked a couple of logistical questions regarding the dock, as well as discussed
their thoughts and opinions on the proposal. Mr. Doane went on to say that Michael Guswinski
(?), from DEEP, suggested increasing the size of the float, due to the fetch and the waves' buildup
as it comes across the shallow. Currently, the proposal is 100 square feet, being 4.8 feet by 24.4
feet long. But Mr. Guswinski suggested to Mr. Doane to increase it up to 200 feet, being 8 feet
wide and 25 feet long. Commissioner Kanter gave an alternate approach, rather than increasing
size, which would be to put crisscross lines on it, in order to hold it in place in the event of any
wake. Commissioner Landers queried whether the float was coming out in the wintertime, to
which the response was it would either come out, or it would be raised up. Commissioner
Johnston would like to see the size stay as it is.

MOTION: Commissioner Murin moved to approve the dock, as drawn, and to affirm that it meets
the Guidelines in the Harbor Management Plan. Commissioner Kanter seconded the motion. The
motion carried. (6-0-0).

Commissioner Hitchery questioned regarding the fixed portion length. Mr. Doane responded that
it was 67 feet, not including the 25-foot ramp.

4. Reports

A. Shellfish. Commissioner Kanter declared the river is open. And it was a long battle, but all of
Sandy Point is open to shell fishing. Finally DEEP agreed with WELSCO, so now the floating
demarcation sign's across on Quarry Dock Road, west from Sandy Point. He also announced that
there will be a scallop season this year, starting November 1| 5th, but possibly December Ist. He
went on further to state that there's an unknown quantity of scallops, so scallop at your own risk.

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Morris discussed his nice conversation with Congressman Joe Courtney.
Congressman Courtney had indicated to him that on Friday the Admiral of the Navy sent in a
letter of support with keeping the spoils area open off of Fisher's Island, and to refute anything that
New York was doing, in hopes that because of National Security and dredging for the sub-base
that that has to be dealt with. He went on to state that Congressman Courtney would be very
happy to support and help with the dredging concept, along with Senator Formica, and
Representatives, and First Selectman Nickerson. So as soon as the dumping area was finished, a
conversation will be held regarding how to dredge in the River.

Mr. Morris brought up the fact that there were three boats: (1) abandoned; (1) sank out in the
center of the River, owned by Gary Smith; (1) 21' Aqua Sport that swamped in storm that
occurred. Mike W. (?) went out and retrieved the latter two boats, and has them in his possession.
Mr. Morris went on to say that Mike W. was very accommodating in the process.

Mr. Morris expressed that he noticed today they're starting to dredge over in 4 Mile River with a
good-sized tugboat, good barge, and good crane. And he went on to state that delivery is going to
be taken on the new boat on Friday. He continued on that the boat is going to help in the process
of taking GPS readings on all of the moorings out there to easily map, which should take place in
the next couple of weeks.

Chairman Dinsmore requested of Mr. Morris to provide the Commission a list of the moorings at
the end of the year, to include owners and locations. Commissioner Landers inquired regarding
O&M for the new boat for 2016. Mr. Morris affirmed that it would have to be a line item on the
budget.
