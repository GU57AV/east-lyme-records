1. Call to Order. Chairman Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:31 p.m.
2. Approval of Minutes of the Regular Meeting of October 20, 2015.

MOTION: Commissioner Landers moved to approve the October 20, 2015 Regular Meeting
Minutes, as presented. Commissioner Kanter seconded the motion. The motion carried. (4-0-0).

3. Public Delegations

Timothy Londregan attended the meeting in hopes of paying for the Lease. He thought it was
four, but apparently previously it had been five, so he was seeking clarification on that issue, and
write a check for the Lease. Chairman Dinsmore indicated that the Lease is done in five-year
increments. Commissioner Landers concurred. Chairman Dinsmore went on to say that it's
$5/acre, paid annually.

Mr. Londregan went on to say that he was pretty much ready to go. He was talking to the people
down at the Bureau about starting in April. There's one application that they've been trying to get
from the DEEP, which is not an issue, but just taking time. He stated he's toying with ideas of a
nursery area, around Watts Island, yet no testing has been done to see if it's viable, or somewhere
in the River, in prohibited areas, or over to Waterford on the west side of Jordan Cove. He just
wanted to put the Commission on notice to his thought process.

Mr. Londregan was curious regarding the inclusion zone in front of the Military Base in the River.
Is it still going through as initially planned? And if so, are they still planning on opening it up on
whatever basis for shellfishing? Chairman Dinsmore responded that he believed it is moving
forward, although it is supposed to be open for recreational shellfishing, which is handled by
Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission. Commissioner Kanter added that WELSCO is
meeting on Thursday. As far he knows, they only want to preclude vessels from mooring

overnight in front of the camp.

Commissioner Landers inquired regarding plans with the upweller in the area of Watts Island. Mr.
Londregan said it would be more ideal for surface gear for juveniles above 3/4". More discussion
was had regarding the upweller and its details. Commissioner Landers also questioned regarding
first steps that Mr. Londregan planned on taking in April. Mr. Londregan responded that they
would have to first deploy the marking buoys, one every 300 feet, in a square.

4, Reports

A. Shellfish. Commissioner Kanter declared the river is open. Scallop season is going to open
December Ist throughout the entire River. Commissioner Landers did note that there are still
some areas with opening up Area B because of the number of boats at the Marinas for clams. Mr.
Salerno queried if scalloping was going to be monitored this year to see the outcome.
Commissioner Kanter answered by saying in the past there were surveys done, although in the
most recent history it has not been done. He said that there's not evidence of a gigantic population
this year, so he would not expect it to be a banner season. He would expect people who know
what they're doing to get their quota, although greenhorns may not do as well.

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Morris distributed a map that extends over to the Hole in the Wall, which
displayed the plotted moorings between Wigwam Rock and the breakwater at the Yacht Club. It
shows that it is a fairly congested area. He indicated his hopes are that, as we progress back
during the summer with the Excel sheet that Joe started, all the data will be inputted into the
marks, cross-referenced with the boat name and photograph of the boat on the mooring, which will
show a real-time diagram of the moorings' location, through the GIS. Data input will probably be
the longest. Further discussion regarding the map was had.

Chairman Dinsmore wanted to know if Mr. Morris had taken delivery of the new boat, to which
Mr. Morris responded that, yes, they have, and a dozen hours have been put on it. It's being taken
to Whalen to put the emergency lights on it this week. It'll be put back in for a while to check
some other stuff. The Commissioners continued to inquire regarding the new boat.

C. Treasurer - Budget. Commissioner Landers stated that the state of the treasury is we have an
additional $320 tonight, thanks to Tim Londregan. That will be offset by some significant Warden
Patrols by WELSCO, $31.25 (October), 1.25 hours.

Commissioner Landers indicated that a budget needs to be prepared. He distributed a spreadsheet
of expenses in 2015, so that the Commission can work on the budget, and would like to be able to
hold a discussion in December. He went on to say that recognizing that we may have some
additional expenses related to the operation of the new patrol boat, we may want to consider
bumping the Maintenance of Harbor line item to accommodate that. Commissioner Landers
requested of Mr. Morris to put together a projection on the spending for the new boat, with the
caveat that it's a 50% split with WELSCO. Preliminary discussions took place regarding the
budget.

A. Harbor Management Plan - Update. Chairman Dinsmore stated that he spoke with Geoff
