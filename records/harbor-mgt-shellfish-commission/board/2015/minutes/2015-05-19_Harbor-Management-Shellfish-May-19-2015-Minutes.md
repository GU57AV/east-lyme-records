3. Public Delegations

Lt. Col. Danao and Major Castelucci provided information and explained the proposed Camp
Niantic security buffer zone. It would extend 250’ out from the high tide line and is being
requested for future enforcement of possible security events when the Camp might want to
exclude people from the area. This is very shallow water and access to shellfish beds and the
passage of small vessels, kayaks, and canoes would be discouraged but not prohibited. The 24/7
contracted security personnel would not have “eyes on” the zone constantly, but people observed
within the zone would probably be identified and asked to leave. Presence within the zone would
not be an “arrestable” offense.

This is a National Guard request similar to the Stone Ranch security arrangement and is not a
Federal requirement.

The placement and marking of the buoys delineating the zone will be determined by the DEEP as
part of the required permitting process.
No Commission action was requested and none was taken. The officers were thanked for their
presentation and departed.

4. Reports
A. Shellfish: No report.
B. Harbor Master: No report.
C. Treasurer: No report.

D. Ex Officio: Selectman Salerno reported on the proposed Goldfinch Terrace property
acquisition which is part of the Niantic River headwater. The Town has been asked to contribute
to the purchase of this property in order to prevent commercial development. Commissioner
Johnston pointed out that proposed RT. #11 goes through this area and private ownership might be
a problem. Value of the land also is dependent on development possibilities. The issue will require
a referendum.

5. Old Business

A. Harbor Management Plan — Update: Commissioner Hitchery reported DEEP is still reviewing
the final draft revision. No particular problem is expected and approval is anticipated within a
week. As mentioned in the Special Meeting, the Plan should be circulated to various EL
department heads for review.

B. Rain Gauge: Since funding is not available and no action taken in several months, this item is
removed from future agendas without objection.

6. New Business

A. Landmark proposal for Oswegatchie Hills: The proposed letter of May 13, 2015 to the Zoning
Commission urging disapproval of the request for a zoning change was discussed. It was
agreed a copy of the letter will be placed in the Zoning mail slot and one will be hand
delivered to Chairman Walker by Commissioner Hitchery prior to the public meeting at Camp
Niantic.

MOTION: Commissioner Murin moved to approve the letter of May 13, 2015 to the Zoning
Commission concerning Zoning Regulation Amendment and zone change requested by Landmark
Development Group LLC regarding Oswegatchie Hills. Seconded by Commissioner Mountzoures.
Motion carried (4-0).
