3. Public Delegations

Lee Deer, GM of Three Belles Marina, inquired as to why the Commission was interested in
Three Belles' moorings. Chairman Dinsmore gave background regarding the pending question as
to whether the existing moorings should be registered and subject to inspection. Mr. Morris and
Mr. Berger gave extra background on the situation, whilst Chairman Dinsmore distributed a
packet that he received from Mr. Morris, which discussed a dredging and filling permit issued in
the past that also included a detailed explanation of where the moorings are.

Mr. Deer stated that his concern is that he has a field capable of holding 28 moorings and that he
doesn't want anyone encroaching on the area. Mr. Berger requested some clarification as to which
moorings are supposed to be permitted and which ones are not supposed to be there. Further
discussion took place, where the Commissioner advised Three Belles to ensure that all of their
moorings are well documented, so this issue won't come up in the future. Mr. Deer stated that he
is not opposed to paying the required mooring fees.

4. Reports

A. Shellfish. Mr. Harris stated that the river has been closed as of Saturday, due to an indication
of 1.9" on the rain gauge.

B. Harbor Master.

I. Mr. Morris discussed the 160 mooring stickers that were sent out. He stated that
the next round would be inspections, estimated to take place in July, which would
verify the moorings. According to him, there have been slight delays in terms of
transferring moorings, but he indicated the instructions that he had given to those
wishing to transfer, and feels that any issues in that regard will be alleviated. Mr.
Morris went on to state that he hopes to get some time with Mr. Berger to be able to
ensure that all moorings have stickers on them.

Mr. Salerno questioned the inspection rate, to which Mr. Morris replied that
moorings are to be inspected every three years. Commissioner Kanter questioned
the issue of illegal moorings, and Mr. Morris replied that he wasn't 100% sure
which moorings were illegal or not, due to the deterioration of the stickers, But he
assured the Commission that, with proper charting of the moorings, as well as new
stickers, he should have no problem verifying them going forward. There is a plan
to eventually get all the moorings into GIS, although they are not in there as of yet.

Il. Mr. Morris stated that the Harbor Master classes began last Wednesday. Last
week's topic was the history on the makeup of the Coast Guard. He indicated that
anybody on the Commission can attend whichever classes they wish, but that the
only way to receive certification was to do the entire eight weeks. Classes are at
6:00 p.m. on Wednesdays at the Waterford Library.

C. Treasurer. Commissioner Landers was absent. Commissioner Johnston questioned the
Commission's budget, in comparison to other Commissions, and a discussion ensued regarding
that topic, highlighting the expenditures of the USGS stream gauge and the pump-out boat.

D. Ex Officio. Mr. Salerno apologized for being absent at the last meeting. He discussed the
budget changes, and stated there are a couple of things on the agenda for the First Selectman to
vote on:

I. Increasing the part-time Building Assistant Official's hours to give them three-
quarters time.

II. Park and Rec to be allowing online registration.

5. Old Business

A. Harbor Management Plan - Update. Commissioner Hitchery indicated that he spoke with
Geoff Steadman today and hopes to have the final copies next week, which would incorporate all
the comments made by the DEEP Rep. Once received, he will distribute them to the other
Commissioners. Comments were requested from the Commission regarding the proposed revision
to the Ordinance, although none were made. Yet there will be a special meeting scheduled in the
future to consider any such comments, and any changes that need to be made to both the
Ordinance and the final draft of the Plan. Chairman Dinsmore indicated that June 30 is the outside
date to have everything finalized with Mr. Steadman's contract.

B. Rain Gauge Discussion. Mr. Harris stated that the USGS gauge went offline two weeks ago. A
week ago, MOUs were updated by DABA. Last Saturday, there was over 1" of rainfall, and the
WeatherLink gauge worked perfectly when it hit the 1" threshold.. He went on to say that, at the
end of the 24-hour period, WeatherLink gave a second alert, indicating total measurement once
again of 1.9", Although there was a slight hiccup and it went offline briefly, Chris Taylor was
able to promptly put it back online.

6. New Business
A. Correspondence.

I. Chairman Dinsmore received a Certification of Notice Form for Dock at 239 Old
Black Point Road, for which the DEEP Permit Consultation Form was discussed at
the previous meeting.

II. Commissioner Johnston inquired regarding any discussions with John Pinto, in
terms of the surveys discussed at the last meeting. Chairman Dinsmore indicated
that he had spoken to Mr. Pinto, and was informed that no additional input was
needed from the Commission. The conclusion was made that the Harbor Masters
were to stay with the DOT until next year.

7. Final Comments.

Commissioner Hitchery inquired about the status of the dingy dock at Cini Park and also the one
proposed at Grand St. Mr. Morris reiterated that the grant was refused, and that it was all
predicated over the water sewer going in and building a bathroom at Cini. Everything was tied to
the same grant.
