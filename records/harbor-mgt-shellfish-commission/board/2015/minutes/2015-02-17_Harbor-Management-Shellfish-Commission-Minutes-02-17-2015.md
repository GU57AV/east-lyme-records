1. Call to Order the Public Hearing. Chairman Dinsmore called the public hearing to order at 7:05 p.m.
2. Public Hearing on Timothy Londregan III's Lease Proposal

Mr. Londregan made a brief presentation regarding his lease proposal and distributed a short
handout to the Commissioners to aid in the description of his plans.

A. Public Comments:

I. Eric Fortin, 17 Cherry Street, Niantic commented, “If it helps the bay, that's what
we're looking for.”

II. Chairman Dinsmore described the benefits of having shellfish and how they
improve the water quality.

II. Commissioner Landers questioned why there was a recommended reduction from 7
acres to 2 acres, as per preliminary discussions that took place between Mr.
Londregan and the Army Corps of Engineers. Mr. Londregan showed the
Commissioners a letter he had received from the Corps in regard to this issue
indicating that 2-acre plots are typical in an application. His plan is to accept an
initial 2 acres if approved, cultivate it, and then go back to the Army Corps to
request more acreage.

IV. Commissioner Hitchery asked Mr. Londregan what his plans are, in terms of
removal of his equipment, should the venture fail. Mr. Londregan ensured
Commissioner Hitchery that he desires to make as minimal an impact as possible,
and he would clean up all of his equipment from the site.

V. Chairman Dinsmore inquired regarding the remaining 56 acres that are not part of
the proposed 7 acres for Type II aquiculture. Mr. Londregan stated that his
intentions are to do a technically Type I aquiculture, with diving being the form of
cultivation on the property. He doesn't want to dredge it, and would rather keep the
natural ground intact, sow seeds, and dive on it.

VI. Commissioner Landers was curious regarding the meeting that Mr. Londregan has
scheduled next week, and who would be included. Mr. Londregan indicated that
DEEP, DABA, Shannon (?), and Dave Carey will participate.

VII. Commissioner Kanter questioned if Mr. Londregan had spoken to the Boating
Division. Mr. Londregan says that he spoke to Marc a year ago, but not recently.

VIII. Commissioner Landers explained Mr. Londregan's next steps, and indicated that
this was the very first step in his venture. He also announced that the public
hearing was properly noticed in The Day. Mr. Londregan submitted a check for the
application/filing fee.

3. Adjournment of Public Hearing. Chairman Dinsmore closed the public hearing at 7:25 p.m.

6. Public Delegations

Eric Fortin requested more information on Millstone's efforts in the Niantic River. Commissioner
Landers indicated that it's part of a very broad ecological program, in which three tows are taken,
about 0.37 nautical miles, which enumerate and measure the dominant fish species and
vertebrates, as required by the Water Discharge Permit from the State of Connecticut. He stated
that a report is issued annually on this subject.

calculated.
