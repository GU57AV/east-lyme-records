MINUTES

Special meeting of May 12, 2015

* Call to order at 7pm

* Establish quorum: Members present Barbara Johnston, Greg Murin, Joe Hitchery, Don
Landers; Exofficio Marc Salerno, Consultant Geoff Steadman

* Purpose of meeting was to review comments on the latest draft of the updated Harbor
Management Plan. A page turn of each chapter was accomplished and members
provided specific comments on the updated Plan. Geoff Steadman will incorporate the
comments and provide an updated plan to the Commission. Members recommended that
the final draft be circulated to various EL department heads and Waterford Harbor
Management for review. Members discussed schedule for submitting the updated plan to
CTDEEP for approval and final implementation with the BOS.

* Adjournment: Barbara Johnston moved to adjourn at 10pm, seconded by Greg Murin,
motion carried unanimously.
