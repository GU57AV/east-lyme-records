1. CALL TO ORDER. Chairman Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m.
2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF July 16, 2013.

MOTION (1): Mr. Mountzoures moved to approve the Regular
Meeting Minutes of July 16, 2013, as presented.
Seconded by Mr. Schutt. (4-0-2) Motion carried.

3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS.

A. Cummings Application. Mr. Westermeyer of Coast Line Consulting
discussed the Cummings Application for a dock. He stated CTDEEP and Army Corps had no
concerns with navigation.

During the following discussion, the commission reviewed that riparian rights were being
preserved and that neighbors were informed of the plans.

This area is under the jurisdiction of the Waterford/East Lyme Shellfish Commission for
shellfishing. WELSCO had previously determined that this project is consistent with their
Shellfish Management Plan.

MOTION (2): Mr. Murin moved that the Cummings
Application is consistent with our
Harbor Management Plan. Seconded
by Mr. Mountzoures. (6-0) Unanimous

4. REPORTS

A. Shellfish Mr. Landers reported he has received emails from DA/BA with respect to
sampling deficiencies. Clamming is open. There is not a lot of activity in the bay.

Mr. Dinsmore reported he received a letter from Christopher Brown, Chief, Aquaculture
& Enhancement Division inviting this Commission to attend an Open House at the National
Marine Fisheries Service, Northeast Fisheries Science Center at the Milford Laboratory on
October 19, 2013 from 9 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. RSVP to Carletta Wright Sipes of Sylvia Feeney at
(203) 882-6541/6509 no later than October 10 if you plan to attend.

Mr. Landers reported bacteria has been found in clams because of warmer waters.

Mr. Schutt asked for an update on scallops and eelgrass. Mr. Landers replied we have

B. Harbor Master
Recognition of Mr. Hitchery's Service. The Commission thanked Mr. Hitchery
for his exemplary job of implementing the Mooring Program. His wife and he were thanked for
all their hard work.

Mr. Hitchery replied he has found his work rewarding and enjoyable. If there is an
opening on the Commission, he would like to be considered.

Review of Possible Replacement. Mr. Adolbo thanked Mr. Hitchery for his
work and recommended Richard Morris.

Mr. Morris noticed the improvements since Mr. Hitchery took over as Harbor Master.
He felt the river and bay are enjoyable. He feels many things can be done in the river. He
attended a Forum, and it was felt we could rebuild the area and attract more boaters.

Ms. Johnston gave Commission members copies of the Black Pointer which gave thanks
to the Public Works Department for removing a giant tree 200' off our shoreline. Mr. Morris
stated we were fortunate enough to have the equipment to remove it.

MOTION (3): Mr. Landers moved to submit the names
of Ron Johnson, Frank Skewes and
Richard Morris to the state to replace
Joe Hitchery as Harbor Master and
recommend Richard Morris. Seconded
by Ms. Johnston. (6-0) Unanimous

C. Treasurer. Mr. Landers received an invoice dated August 1 from Save the River
Save the Hills for our annual contribution to its Pumpout Program in the amount of $3,000. We
received a bill from the Recording Secretary for 2.75 hours.

MOTION (4): Mr. Murin moved to approve $3,000 for
the Pumpout Program. Seconded by
Ms. Johnston. (6-0) Unanimous

MOTION (5): Mr. Mountzoures moved to approve the Treasurer’s

D. Ex-Officio Mr. Seery reported the Selectmen have discussed the invasion of
bamboo. The state has passed a law that it should be removed. It will push us asphalt and
concrete.

Mr. Formica has been reviewing plans for the completion of the Boardwalk. This
summer we had a successful season for the Boardwalk and Main Street.

Mr. Seery informed the Commission that the police boat has been repaired and is in the
water.

Saturday night there was a concert at McCook's Park and a birthday party for Paul
Formica.

He thanked Mr. Hitchery for all he has done.
