3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS.

A. Cummings Application. Mr. Westermeyer of Coast Line Consulting
discussed the Cummings Application for a dock. It will need approval from CTDEEP and Army
Corps. Mr. Landers felt any issues relating to shellfish were outside the jurisdiction of this
Commission. He referred Mr. Westermeyer to WELSCO which will meet on July 18.

Mr. Westermeyer stated the concrete bulkhead and footing needs to be repaired. They
would like to install a fixed pier ramp and have a floating dock.

Mr. Murin felt the area was very congested. He believed the Town of East Lyme has a
right of way in that area. It also crosses the riparian line. He would like to be assured the
neighbors would receive notice. Mr. Murin added that the proposed dock could prevent
adjoining property owners ability to install a dock and that may be an issue. Mr. Westermeyer
stated they are trying to avoid any conflicts with neighbors. Mr. Schutt asked has there been
communication between neighbors? Mr. Westermeyer replied there has not been. Mr. Murin
felt the neighbor’s point of view needs to be taken into consideration. Mr. Mountzoures felt the
ramp angle could interfere with navigation.

This matter was tabled until the Commission has had an opportunity to visit the area and
clarification on who owns the adjoining right of way is determined.
