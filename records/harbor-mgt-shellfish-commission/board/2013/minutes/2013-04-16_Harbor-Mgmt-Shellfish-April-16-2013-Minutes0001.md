3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. George Sisson of Troop 7 stated he is working towards
his Eagle Scout Award.

4. REPORTS

A. Shellfish. Mr. Kanter reported scallop season will end on March 31. Individuals are
now clamming.

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Hitchery would like estimates for repair of the Smith Cove
channel markers. He stated we are charged with the responsibility for upkeep and maintenance
of them. He had a preliminary estimate from Jaxson Vibber. Mr. Vibber stated there were
changes in the specifications and material.

Mr. Kanter asked if there is some material that would last longer? Mr. Hitchery replied
plywood or aluminum can be used. Mr. Landers suggested investigating whether the
Department of Corrections or the Town Garage are able to do this work.

Mr. Hitchery suggested that we fund the installation of the mooring for the Harbor
Master. There is no place to attach any along the river. He recommended a 500 pound mooring.
Mr. Landers felt a RFQ is needed.

Ms. Johnston suggested that Mr. Hitchery update his form to add design of the moorings.
He felt this was a good idea.

Mr. Hitchery attended a meeting in New Haven where representatives from DEEP, DOT
and Home Land Security discussed issuing ID badges for harbor masters and their deputies for
Region 1. He also attended a meeting in Waterford and discussed the Act regarding abandoned
boats. The Harbor Master's boat should be available by the end of this month.

He received 236 mooring renewals, thirteen no responses, eight individuals relinquished
their moorings and he received eight requests for new moorings.

Mr. Dinsmore stated he is in favor of establishing a mooring for the Harbor Master. Mr.
Landers supported funding of a mooring for the Harbor Master.

C. Treasurer. Mr. Landers reported our budget was approved by the Board of
Selectmen but the Board of Finance recommended reducing it to $14,650. Our Capital Request
in the amount of $15,000 was approved over a two year funding cycle. We will have $7500 next
year to fund an outside consultant to update the Harbor Management Plan. He has spoken to Mr.
Steadman about working on the Harbor Management Plan.

MOTION (2): Mr. Kanter moved to approve the Treasurer’s Report. Seconded by Mr.
Mountzoures. (5-0) Unanimous.

D. Ex-Officio. Mr. Seery reported the Capital Plan will be appropriated later in the year.
The Board of Finance reduced the General Government and Board of Education budgets by
$978,607. If we receive the same amount from the state, residents will have a .5 increase in
taxes. The Board of Finance Budget Hearing will be held on April 25 at 7 p.m. at the High
School.

The Board of Selectmen approved the noise ordinance.
The Board of Selectmen have cancelled its meeting for tomorrow night.

Ms. Johnston asked if we will purchase a meter? Mr. Seery replied Old Lyme and
Waterford have one.

A. Harbor Management Plan—Reports from Commission Members. Ms. Johnston

B. Discussion of Niantic River No Wake Zone/Speed Limits. Mr. Kanter showed

6. NEW BUSINESS

A. Correspondence.

1. Article in the Lyme Times regarding our book donation Shellfish Aquaculture in the
Environment to the East Lyme Library.

2. Copy of letter dated April 6, 2013 from Don Landers and Rick Kanter to the National
Fish & Wildlife Foundation regarding support for funding to the Eastern Connecticut
Conservation District.

7. COMMENTS FROM OTHER COMMISSION MEMBERS

Mr. Landers reported at the Connecticut Harbor Management Association Meeting there
was discussion on training for harbor masters. No Wake Zone/Speed Limits and how to handle
abandoned boats was also discussed. Mr. Landers drafted a response letter.

Mr. Hitchery recommended that the bill regarding abandoned boats be supported.

Mr. Landers spoke to Jeff Steadman, and he is very interested in updating the Harbor
Management Plan.

Ms. Johnston welcomed the boy scout and applauded his efforts toward becoming an
Eagle Scout.

Mr. Kanter expressed concern about ospreys nesting in an area close to swimming and jet
skis. He observed last year that the osprey left its nest and babies after the area became busy.
Mr. Landers asked that Mr. Kanter give a report during breeding season.

Mr. Hitchery observed eagles in the river as well.
