East Lyme Harbor Management/Shellfish Commission
Special Meeting of June 27, 2013

Present: Steve Dinsmore, Chairman; Barbara Johnston, Secretary; Rick Kanter; Greg
Murin; Bill Montzoures

Also Present: Joe Hitchery, Harbor Master
Geoff Steadman, Consultant

1. Call to Order. Chairman Dinsmore called the East Lyme Harbor Management/Shellfish
Commission Special Meeting to order at 7 p.m.

2. Review Harbor Management Plan with Consultant. Members and Harbor Master were
introduced to Mr. Steadman.

The Harbor Management Plan is twenty-two years old and needs updating. Amendments
will need to be approved by the Commission of Transportation, DEEP and Army Corps of
Engineers. Then it would go to the legislative body in Town.

Mr. Steadman would like to have an overview of important changes this Commission feels
are needed since the last twenty-two years. He will contact DEEP and Army Corps to obtain
their input.

The budget available for updating the HMP was reviewed. Mr. Steadman felt that it would
be sufficient to complete the job.

MOTION: Mr. Murin moved to adjourn the June 27, 2013 Special
Meeting of the Harbor Management/Shellfish
Commission at 9:15 p.m. Seconded by Mr. Mountzoures.

(4-0) Unanimous

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
