HARBOR MANAGEMENT SHELLFISH COMMISSION
September 17, 2013 Informal Discussion

Members Present: Steve Dinsmore, Chairman; Treasurer Don Landers; Secretary Barbara
Johnston

Also Present: Joseph Hitchery, Harbor Master; Marc Berger, Deputy Harbor Master; Ex officio
Kevin Seery; Mike Theiler and T. J. Londregan

Members Absent; Rick Kanter; Bill Mountzoures; and Greg Murin

1. CALL TO ORDER. Chairman Dinsmore called the informal discussion to order at 7:35
p.m.

2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF August 29, 2013. This
matter was tabled.

3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. Mr. Theiler thanked Mr. Hitchery for the terrific job he has
done as Harbor Master.

Mr. Theiler submitted a letter that due to the present market conditions, he is unable to
renew his lease with the Town of East Lyme.

Mr. Landers stated he appreciated Mr. Theiler's efforts.

4. REPORTS

A. Shellfish There was no report on shellfish.

B. Harbor Master Mr. Hitchery reported there are 266 paid moorings and $6,900 in
fees have been collected. There are three individuals whose moorings he recommends not
renewing. He thanked Mr. Theiler for his help.

Status of Harbor Master Replacement Mr. Formica sent an email to Mr. Dinsmore
regarding the appointment of Dick Morris as Harbor Master.

C. Treasurer. Mr. Landers reported we have a bill from Jeanette T. Fisheries in the
amount of $200 for mooring removal, two bills from WELSCO in the amount of $118.75 for the
months of May and June and $156.25 for patrolling hours for the months of July and August and
a bill from the Recording Secretary for 2.50 for the August Meeting.

D. Ex-Officio . Mr. Seery reported at 7 p.m. tomorrow night there will be a Town
Meeting on appropriations specified in the Capital Plan.

The Town was awarded $500,000 in a STEEP Grant towards the Boardwalk. We hope to
begin repairs in October or November from Hole-in-the-Wall to Amtrak Beach.

There was discussion on some species of invasive bamboo. The Conservation of Natural
Resources was asked to look into it to determine whether or not an ordinance is needed.

Mr. Morris asked if they will erect bath houses at Cini Park? Mr. Seery replied they do
not know yet.

Mr. Landers stated two individuals were injured at the pump station. Mr. Seery reported
they are now fine.

Mr. Morris reported pump 2 and 3 are on line. The insurance company will have an
electrical engineer to test the breaker. Its thirty years old.

5. OLD BUSINESS
WY EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

A. Harbor Management Plan—Reports from Commission Members Mr. Dinsmore
had discussion with Geoff Steadman. He will begin working on the regulation section of the
Plan. A Memo will be sent to First Selectman Paul Formica in order for funds to be allocated
from the budget. Mr. Formica is willing to provide an intern if needed. Mr. Hitchery asked if
before it is finalized will it be presented to this Commission. Mr. Landers replied it will need to
go to DEEP and a Public Hearing will be needed. Mr. Hitchery would like to have input.

B. Discussion of Niantic River No Wake Zone/Speed Limits There was no discussion
on this matter.

6. NEW BUSINESS

A. Correspondence.

1. Memo from First Selectman Paul Formica thanking Mr. Hitchery for his work
as Harbor Master.

2. Certificates of Permission from the state for Angela Thompson at 257 Old
Black Point Road; Dowling for a dock at 287 Old Black Point Road and Jane Calkins at 160 Old
Black Point Road, a COP for dredging at Rocky Neck, and for Thomas Sena for a dock at 41
Oswegachie Road.

Mr. Hitchery reported Camp Niantic is requesting a waiver of its $25 annual fee.

B. Renewal of USGS Rain Gage Lease. We have renewed the Rain Gage Lease at a
cost of $7,000 for two years. We are responsible for one-half of that amount. Ms. Johnston felt
WELSCO should share in this cost. Mr. Dinsmore agreed to speak to the Chairman of
WELSCO. Mr. Landers felt the gage has been reliable and is a great tool for emergency
managers to gage how much rainfall we have. Mr. Morris felt it has been used for six to seven
years with great success. In 2008 we had a storm in April and that gage was a lifesaver. We
asked people to self evacuate. Some individuals had houses on an island. Ms. Johnston stated
she would like Ledge Light to pay part of this cost. The four towns in the watershed should
share equally in the cost.

7. COMMENTS FROM OTHER COMMISSION MEMBERS Mr. Landers reported
Crescent Beach has approved funding to authorize repairs following storm Sandy.

Mr. Landers informed the Commission we need demarcation signs. The Department of
Corrections will make signs for $90 each. Ms. Johnston asked how many signs do we need?
Mr. Landers replied we need at least four.

Mr. Hitchery stated we need a phone number to field questions. Mr. Dinsmore agreed to
provide it in October.

Ms. Johnston felt the list of mooring installers and inspectors needs to be revised.

8. ADJOURNMENT
The September 17, 2013 informal discussion was adjourned at 8:30 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
