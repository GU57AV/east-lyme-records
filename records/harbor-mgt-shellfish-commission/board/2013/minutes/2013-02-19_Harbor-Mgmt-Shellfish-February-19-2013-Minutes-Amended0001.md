4. Reports.
A. Shellfish. People are out scalloping. Rick went to the State Shellfish Commission
Meeting in New Haven. Each area representative spoke about what is occurring in their area.
There was good communication between the various commissions.

B. Harbor Master. There has been a 48% renewal rate of mooring fees.

C. Treasurer. Don Landers was excused (out of Town). Stephen Dinsmore indicated a
corrected bill of $1,750 had been approved for the rain gauge for one year.

D. Ex officio. Town budget is under review. The town was at 81% without power after the
blizzard of February 9th.

5. Old Business

Management Plan revision. Town is looking ata UCONN graduate student to act as a
consultant in revising the plan. Request was made to have Jeff Steadman give an
approximate quote to review and update the regulatory section.

B. No Wake Zone There was no discussion of the no wake zone.
