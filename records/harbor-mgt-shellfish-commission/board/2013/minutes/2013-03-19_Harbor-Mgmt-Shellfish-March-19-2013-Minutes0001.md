4. REPORTS

A. Shellfish. Mr. Kanter reported scallop season will be over by the end of March.
WELSCO has put in 18,000 first year scallops.

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Hitchery reported he has received 188 paid moorings. Seventy-
one are outstanding. He has sent reminder notices to each of them via email or regular mail for
those individuals who do not have an email address. He has also telephoned some of these
individuals. Mr. Landers asked how this compares to previous years? Mr. Hitchery stated last
year we had 20% by March 1. We are now at 72% this year. He felt by adding late fees there is
an incentive. Ms. Johnston felt Mr. Hitchery was doing a wonderful job.

Mr. Hitchery stated he has made it clear to those individuals who needed inspections to
send the renewal in before March 1 and he would hold the check until he receives the inspection
form.

Mr. Landers stated he was informed by the Chief that the boat will be available in early
April.

The Chief has called for a meeting of Southeastern Connecticut harbor masters to
establish a training program and to share information on how each are doing their job. Mr.
Berger added there will be discussion on how the chain of command is handled in different
areas.

Mr. Landers informed the Commission the Harbor Management Association Meeting
will be held tomorrow night to discuss three bills that are proposed: 5182 for roles, duties,
training and powers of harbor masters, 5846 concerning the Harbor Master Program and 5198
concerning No Wake Zone.

Mr. Hitchery felt training of harbor masters is a good idea, as well as sharing of
procedures and information. He preferred that law enforcement issue a citation in the No Wake
Zone. Mr. Landers agreed to share this information. Mr. Landers stated there will be questions
and answers concerning abandonment of vessels. Ms. Johnston noted the minutes of the Chief

speak to the Chief. Mr. Vibber stated he has General Marine Liability Insurance which has been
approved.

Ms. Johnston noted the boat will have an hour meter. Mr. Hitchery stated that is for the
time we have the motor running. He also has a log book. Mr. Landers stated we anticipate the

Mr. Hitchery has had inquiries regarding different types of moorings. All of our
moorings are not mushroom moorings. It can be mushroom moorings or its equivalent. Mr.
Landers felt this Commission should support helical anchors.

C. Treasurer. Mr. Landers reported as of the end of February we have expended
$6,180.04 and our remaining budget is $6,189.04. We have 53% remaining in our budget. Mr.
Dinsmore suggested purchasing shellfish area demarcation signs. Mr. Hitchery suggested pilings
on the Smith Cove channel.

We have received fees from Michael Theiler, one in the amount of $525 and one in the
amount of $884 to satisfy leases on EL7, 8 and 5.

D. Ex-Officio. Mr. Seery reported the Board of Selectmen have forwarded the budget to
the Board of Finance. The Board of Selectmen approved $15,000 for the Harbor Management
Plan. Mr. Formica has contacted a UCONN graduate student on this matter. The budget
forwarded to the Board of Finance has a $1.9 million increase, which would be a 1 mill increase.
Mr. Formica has saved on costs for Health Care. We will not receive pilot money for the prison,
Rocky Neck and the National Guard. We will receive one-tenth for the slots.

Tomorrow night the Selectmen will discuss a noise ordinance.

The Boardwalk and Cini Park will have 150 parking spots. The AMTRAK bridge is
scheduled to be completed in May. The Boardwalk should be completed by mid-November.

The Police Department will have a Jet Ski. Grant money was received for it and 3 ATVs.

It is possible we will have an overflow from Rocky Neck. Parks and Recreation will
need six lifeguards per day.

Ms. Johnston asked if this Commission could see the Plan for Cini Park? Mr. Seery
agreed to provide it for the next meeting of this Commission.

Mr. Landers asked if the same contractor that AMTRAK used will be working on the
Boardwalk? Mr. Seery replied the Boardwalk project will go out to bid.
