7. COMMENTS FROM OTHER COMMISSION MEMBERS

Ms. Johnston received an email from the Connecticut Shellfish Management requesting
comments.

Mr. Dinsmore reported Sea Grant has formed a panel to develop Shellfish Management
Regulations. They are requesting representatives from local shellfish commissions to attend.

Ms. Johnston noted that most town agencies have signed the MOU, except the Waterford
Shellfish Commission.

Mr. Landers did an eelgrass survey and found it has recolonized in a number of areas in
the Niantic River. He also found many scallops in the Niantic Bay.

Mr. Hitchery asked for guidance on specific moorings. Mr. Dinsmore suggested
allowing sixty days after an individual who has been deployed returns to make a decision on
whether or not they want to keep the mooring.
