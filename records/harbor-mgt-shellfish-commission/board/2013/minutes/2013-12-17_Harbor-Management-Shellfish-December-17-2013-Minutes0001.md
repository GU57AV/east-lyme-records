1. Call to Order. The December 17, 2013 Regular Meeting of the Harbor
Management/Shellfish Commission was called to order at 7:33 p.m. by Chairman Steven
Dinsmore.

2. Approval of Minutes of October 15, 2013. Mr. Dinsmore asked for additions, deletions or
corrections to the October 15, 2013 Minutes of the Harbor Management/Shellfish
Commission.

MOTION: Don Landers moved to approve the October 15, 2013 Regular
Meeting Minutes, as presented. Seconded by William
Mountzoures. (5-0) Unanimous.

3. Public Delegation. Ms. Brunza reported the Environmental Office of the Military
Department wishes to do work at Camp Niantic to repair damage during Hurricane Sandy.
The only section permitted by DEEP is rip rap. Army Corps has given its approval.

Mr. Dinsmore circulated its permit to Commission members. Mr. Landers asked if they
were trying to block the wall with rock. Ms. Brunza replied unless we get funds to replace
the wall, we are requesting this temporary work. She showed Commission members where
the rip wrap would be placed.

MOTION: Mr. Landers moved to find that the placement of rip

4. Reports.

A. Shellfish. Mr. Kanter introduced Peter Harris, the new Chairman of WELSCO. He
reported there is not much activity in the river because of inclement weather. Prior to the bad
weather there was much activity.

Mr. Landers reported the Annual Gathering of shellfish commissions will be held on J anuary
11, 2014 from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. in New Hampshire.

Mr. Kanter informed the Commission that WELSCO will meet on Thursday December 19 at
Waterford Town Hall.

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Morris was congratulated on his appointment as Harbor Master for
two years.

Mr. Morris is preparing to send out 270 renewal applications. Seventy require inspections in
2014.

C. Treasurer. Commission members reviewed the draft 2014/15 proposed Harbor
Management/Shellfish Commission Operating Budget in the amount of $14,650. Mr.
Landers reported we have used 28% of our 2013/14 Operating Budget.

We have received a bill from the Recording Secretary for 4 1/3 hours of secretarial work for
this Commission, Invoice 345, dated November 18, 2013 from WELSCO for October
patrolling hours in the amount of $6.25; Invoice 344 from WELSCO for the September
warden patrol hours in the amount of $12.50; Reimbursement to Mr. Hitchery for Invoice
170896 dated October 14, 2013 from Newton Manufacturing Co. for decals in the amount of

.$524.59; and reimbursement to Mr. Hitchery for Invoice 100882, dated April 16, 2013 for
East Lyme Harbor Master Patch in the amount of $174.41.

MOTION: Mr. Kanter moved to approve the East Lyme
Harbor Management/Shellfish Commission
proposed budget request for 2014/15 in the
amount of $14,650. Seconded by Mr. Mountzoures.
Mr. Hitchery abstained from voting. (4-0-1) Motion
carried.

Mr. Landers asked that this Commission authorize one-half of the cost towards maintaining
the rain gauge for shellfish closers in the amount of $1500 per year. East Lyme and
Waterford pay one-half of the cost.

The USGS group will give a presentation at WELSCO's meeting on Thursday, December 19.

On December 7 the Town of East Lyme had its Light Parade which was a fantastic event.

Tomorrow night the Board of Selectmen will reappoint board and commission members.

Mr. Morris reported Zoning approved the bluff specifications prepared by Mr. Mulholland.
5. Old Business

A. Harbor Management Plan. This Commission requested $15,000 in the Capital Plan for
updating the Harbor Management Plan. Mr. Dinsmore has spoken to Jeff Steadman, and he
would like to get together to discuss items we want updated to current statutes.

Mr. Morris asked how would this Commission feel about dredging and putting in transient
moorings. Mr. Landers replied this Commission has discussed transient moorings. Mr.
Morris would like to invite Rep. Ed Jutilla to discuss funding for it. Mr. Dinsmore felt the
shellfishing beds would be impacted. Mr. Landers felt it would be a very challenging
permitting process. Mr. Kanter felt a dingy dock is needed. He suggested publicizing it in
marine publications. Mr. Hitchery felt the number of moorings would have to be limited.
Mr. Dinsmore suggested seeking WELSCO's input on shellfish beds.

B. Renewal of USGA Rain Gauge Lease.

The term of the lease is for two years. East Lyme and Waterford pay one-half each. East
Lyme's contribution would be $1,850 for two years.

Mr. Kanter suggesting that we determine if we would be allowed to renew it for one year,
with the option to renew it after one year.

Mr. Morris felt the rain gauge is a terrific value.

Mr. Landers agreed it is expensive and there may be an alternative that suits our needs but
DA/BA needs a reliable source.

MOTION: Mr. Landers moved that the East Lyme Harbor
Management/Shellfish Commission supports
cost sharing of the USGS rain gauge for a term
of one year, with the option for a second year
at a price to be determined based on WELSCO's
December 19 Meeting. Seconded by
Mr. Mountzoures. (5-0) Unanimous.
