TOWN OF EAST LYME

EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

Chairman Treasurer Secretary
Stephen Dinsmore Donald F. Landers Jr. Joseph L. Hitchery

Not official until approved by the Commission at its next regular meeting
September 16, 2014 Regular Meeting Minutes; East Lyme Town Hall

Commissioners Present: Stephen Dinsmore; Barbara Johnston; Donald Landers; Joseph Hitchery and Rick
Kanter

FILED IN EAST LYME

Absent: William Mountzoures and Greg Murin CONNECTICUT

Others Present: Jaxson Vibber and Michael Theiler
1. Call to Order. Chairman Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:34 PM.
2. Approval of Minutes of August 19, 2014

MOTION: Mr. Kanter moved to approve the August 19, 2014 Regular Meeting Minutes, as presented.
Seconded by Mr. Landers. Ms. Johnston abstained from voting. Motion carried. (4-0-1)

3. Public Delegation — There were no comments from the public at this time..

4. Reports
A. Shellfish. Mr. Kanter reported the river has been open. There were not many scallops.

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Morris reported he and Mark have been using the boat shared by Waterford.
They tagged 15 moorings. The majority of the tagged moorings were old. We would like permission to
have them pulled. Many are in the bay that need to be pulled. We removed two anchors. We have 288
moorings. We spoke to those individuals who did not renew their moorings. Several need to be inspected.
Twenty moorings didn't have stickers. Some were washed off.

Mr. Morris submitted a bill for $1,590 to repair steering on the boat. Mr. Berger stated the police boat will
become available.

Mr. Berger reported 3 Belles Marina had quite a few boats with no markings and have since been marked by
3 Belles. The federal government has given permission for 25 moorings. We witnessed seven with no
stickers. 3 Belles has paid for two. He asked for guidance on how to proceed. Mr. Landers replied the
regulations are specific that you need permission from the local Harbor Commission or the State of
Connecticut. He suggested requesting that they produce paperwork from Army Corps or the state.

Mr. Berger stated 3 Blackpoint Road had nothing on it. It had a red sticker, with no registration and he
asked that it be hauled. Mr. Landers suggested first contacting the owner. Mr. Berger added there are three
with no compliance. Mr. Morris stated many do not have numbers on the mooring.

MOTION: Ms. Johnston moved to pull the three moorings
at Black Point Road. No second.

Mr. Landers felt they should not be pulled until doing the proper outreach. Mr. Kanter felt it is enough to
put a red sticker on them. He agreed there should be more public outreach. Mr. Landers suggested that this
Commission put a notice in the Lyme Times. Mr. Kanter agreed to write an article. Mr. Morris will have it
placed on the Town website.

C. Treasurer. Mr. Landers reported he received a clerical bill for 3 hours and a bill for Warden
patrolling from June to August in the amount of $87.50. Mr. Landers suggested tabling the bill for repair of
the boat until he had discussions with Mr. Formica and Chief Pendleton. There were no objections to the
Treasurer's Report, and it was accepted as presented.

D. Ex Officio. Mr. Seery was unable to be present and there was no report.

5. Old Business

A. Harbor Management Plan. Mr. Hitchery distributed email dated September 16, 2014 from
Geoffrey Steadman to Mr. Hitchery giving his progress report. Mr. Landers asked if Mr. Steadman felt there
may be any issues with DEEP on the updated plan? Mr. Hitchery replied he did not. Mr. Dinsmore agreed to
contact Mr. Steadman.

6. New Business
A. Correspondence. There was no new correspondence.

B. RT. 1 Bridge Replacement Impact Determination. Mr. Dinsmore provided an update from the
CT DOT on the storm water mitigation plan for the RT. 1 project.

MOTION: Mr. Landers moved to find State Project #044-147
to rebuild a bridge on US-1 over the Niantic
River bridge is consistent with the Harbor Management
Plan. Seconded by Ms. Johnston. (5-0)
Unanimous.

7. Final Comments. The sign with the camera for the no wake zone has been removed.
It was observed that a dock is being built in Black Point.
Ms. Johnston asked if the rain gauge was purchased. Mr. Morris stated we have asked for permission.
MOTION: Mr. Kanter moved to purchase an additional
module for the Town rain gauge. Seconded
by Ms. Johnston. (5-0) Unanimous.
Mr. Vibber reported he understood that in August or September there was discussion about proposed transient

moorings on Grand Street. Mr. Morris replied the Town was hoping for a STEEP Grant for bathrooms and
water and sewer. The Town did not receive the grant.
8. Adjournment
MOTION: Mr. Landers moved to adjourn the September 16, 2014 Regular Meeting of the Harbor
Management/Shellfish Commission at 8:30 p.m. Seconded by Mr. Kanter. Motion carried. (5-0)

Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
