FILED IN EAST LYME
CONNECTICUT

jy 2014 At 10:
TOWN OF EASTLYME *

EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

Chairman Treasurer Secretary
Stephen Dinsmore Donald F. Landers Jr. Joseph L. Hitchery

Not official until approved by the Commission at its next regular meeting
July 15, 2014 Regular Meeting Minutes; East Lyme Town Hall

Commissioners Present: Joseph Hitchery; Barbara Johnston; Donald Landers; William
Mountzoures and Greg Murin
Absent Commissioner: Stephen Dinsmore and Rick Kanter

Ex Officios Present: Richard Morris, Harbor Master; and Kevin Seery, Selectman
Others Present: Richard Caulkins; Edgar Riozzi; Frank Gillig

1. Call to Order. Acting Chairman Hitchery called the meeting to order at 7:32 PM.
2. Approval of Minutes of June 17, 2014

The following correction was noted under Reports (4) (B): “Messrs. Morris, Hitchery, and Riozzi
went on Hitchery’s boat to check on two reported hazardous winter stakes off Saunders Point. Mr.
Vibber was working in the area and was asked by the Harbor Master to pull one of the hazardous
winter stakes and non-compliant mooring #321 off Pine Grove; the remaining winter stake problem
was restified by Mr. Vibber.”

MOTION: Ms. Johnston moved to approve the June 17, 2014 Minutes, as amended; seconded by
Mr. Mountzoures. Motion carried (unanimous).

The following clarifications were noted for the May 27" meeting: (2) Mooring permit fees in the
amount of $6,950 have been collected; (4) (B) A Homeland Security Grant has provided funding
for the purchase of radios for the New London Marine Group at a cost of $8,000 each.

3. Public Delegation — Members from the public requested that the Commission evaluate
additional speed limit signs and extra marine patrols in the Niantic River to enforce boating
regulations and protect public property. This topic will be added to the August agenda.

4. Reports

A. Shellfish. No Report

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Morris reviewed a proposal for floating restaurant/bar in the area off Cini
Park, all moorings (except ~12-15) are permitted, PFD’s were purchased for the HM boat, and he

is working with Town staff on conceptual plans for additional transient boating oppurtunities.

C. Treasurer. Mr. Landers received an invoice for 3 PFD’s ($275.45) and reported that $4,535 had
been encumbered from the 2013-14 budget to support re-establishment of the Latimer Brook stream
gauge in 2014-15.

D. Ex Officio. Mr. Seery reported that the BoS and BoF acted on our request to encumber funds, a
special meeting is scheduled for Aug 6" to discuss funding for Scott air packs and land purchase
near Oswegatchie Hills. Celebrate East Lyme is this Saturday; boardwalk repairs are being staged
at McCook Pt and a successful fundraiser was held in memory of former Town employee Mike G.
E. Rain Gauge Subcommittee. It was recommended to move forward and purchase required
equipment and remove this agenda item.

5. Old Business

A. Harbor Management Plan. Commission members were asked to refer to Mr. Steadman’s recent
email on chapter reviews. Discussion followed on a recommendation to get a complete re-write of
all sections to review at subsequent special meetings.

6. New Business
A. Correspondence. A Certificate of Permission from the CTDEEP OLISP for water-related work
related at the Rebecca Davies residence, Old Black Pt. Rd.

7. Comments from Commission Members. Following discussion on the merits of adding docks
at the Grand St. launch, it was suggested that the Commission request funding from the Town to
move forward with the proposal. A request was suggested to have members of the Niantic River
Watershed Committee attend one of our meetings to discuss their activities.

8. Adjournment
MOTION: Mr. Mountzoures moved to adjourn at 9:35 p.m; seconded by Ms. Johnston. Motion
carried (Unanimous).

Respectfully submitted,

Don Landers, Acting Recording Secretary
