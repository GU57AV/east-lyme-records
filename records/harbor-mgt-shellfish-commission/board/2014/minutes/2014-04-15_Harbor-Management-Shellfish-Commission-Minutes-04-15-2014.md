FILED IN EAST LYME

TOWN OF EAST LYME

EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

Chairman Treasurer Secretary
Steven Dinsmore Donald F. Landers Jr. Joseph L. Hitchery

Not official until approved by the Commission at its next regular meeting
April 15, 2014 Regular Meeting Minutes; East Lyme Town Hall

Commissioners Present: Steven Dinsmore; J oseph Hitchery; Barbara Johnston; Rick Kanter;
Donald Landers; William Mountzoures
Absent Commissioner: Greg Murin

Ex Officios Present: Mare Berger, Deputy Harbor Master; Kevin Seery, Selectman
Others Present: Jaxson Vibber and Michael Theiler (Approved Mooring Installers)
1. Call to Order. Chairman Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:30 PM.

2. Approval of Minutes of March 18, 2014
MOTION: Ms. Johnston moved to approve the March18, 2014 Regular Meeting Minutes,as
presented. Seconded by Mr. Landers. Mr. Mountzoures abstained. Motion carried. (5-0-1)

3. Public Delegation — Mr. Vibber reported he had conversations with Mr. Hitchery, former
Harbor Master, and had reviewed the Harbor Management Plan regarding the types of
approved moorings. He presented the Commission with information regarding alternatives to
mushroom moorings and stated he has four previously permitted pyramid anchors in Niantic
Bay. Two were hauled and inspected last year and two are due for inspection this year. He
presented information to the Commission which he felt showed that pyramid anchors exceed
the minimum standards of East Lyme. He spoke to Richard Morris, Harbor Master, and his
response was to remove them when they come up for inspection. Mr. Vibber asked that these
moorings be grandfathered.

Mr. Hitchery confirmed the conversations took place last year and the specifications
(provided to him by the Dor Mor pyramid manufacturer) presented to this Commission
tonight were the specifications he previously gave to Mr. Vibber. It is true that last year this
Commission approved the use of pyramid moorings in the Niantic River, but not in the
Niantic Bay. Mr. Berger agreed with this because it had to do with the different conditions
of the bottom. Mr. Landers felt pyramid anchors should not be used in the Niantic Bay
because of the potential of high seas lifting them off the bottom. Ms. Johnston asked where
are they located? She was informed in front of the Yacht Club. Mr. Landers added the
minimum standards table was created before these anchors were used. Mr. Theiler felt it was
more important to look at the large bottom chain used since quite a bit of heavy chain adds to
the stability of a mooring. Mr. Vibber stated mushroom shanks can bend while pyramids
bury themselves. He is willing to have discussions with other harbor masters to determine
how they feel about this matter. Mr. Hitchery stated the inspection form asks for the weight
of the anchor and suggested in the future we should also ask for the design or type of anchor.
Ms. Johnston asked that this matter be placed on the June Agenda of this Commission.

Mr. Landers agreed to contact the Harbor Master and ask him to not do anything for sixty
days in order for the Commission to get more information. Mr. Thieler also stated there are
individuals who use railroad wheels for mooring anchors and this Commission needs to
decide what is appropriate. Mr. Vibber added he has approximately thirty customers in other
towns that use them, and he never had a problem with any of them. He noted that this
Commission allows 3/8" and 5/16" chains in the harbor. He showed photos of how a 3/8"
chain deteriorated after three years. Mr. Hitchery stated he had increased the minimum
requirements for the Bay area last year, but agreed it could be improved upon.

Mr. Theiler stated the mooring tackle for the Harbor Master boat is on his property and he
would like to know where to place it.

4, Reports
A. Shellfish. Mr. Kanter reported the river is open and there has been clamming.

He and Mr. Dinsmore and representatives from Waterford, Groton and Stonington attended a
meeting with DEEP regarding high level State employees taking samples to Milford. Mr.
Landers asked if anyone has considered Avery Point taking over these tasks. Mr. Dinsmore
recommended coordinating it with Ledge Light.

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Dinsmore reported the Harbor Master has had ten requests for
new moorings. He will send second notices to approximately fifty late responders. Mr.
Goeschel will apply for a DEEP Program Grant.

C. Treasurer. Mr. Landers reported we received a bill for 3.5 hours of clerical support. He
received a $25 check, presumably for a mooring, and an invoice from WELSCO for our 50%
share of the rain gauge.

MOTION: Mr. Landers moved to authorize payment of $1,625 for this Commission's 50%
share of the rain gauge. Seconded by Mr. Kanter. Ms. Johnston abstained. Motion carried.
(5-0-1)

MOTION: Mr. Mountzoures moved to accept the Treasurer’s Report, as presented.
Seconded by Mr. Kanter. Ms. Johnston abstained. Motion carried. (5-0-1)

D. Ex Officio. Mr. Seery reported the Board of Finance has approved this Commission's
budget. They have made cuts to the Board of Education and Town Government budgets.

The Board of Selectmen will discuss permitting the use of golf carts on the roads at Black
Point, Giants Neck, Pine Grove, and Saunders Point at its next meeting. Many communities
have had success with them. A driver needs to have a Connecticut driver's license, a horn, a
red flag and a registration certificate from the Town.

The Boardwalk contract has been awarded. Construction has not begun because of
AMTRAK. Mr. Formica and Congressman Courtney are having discussions with them.
There will be some disruption at Hole-in-the-Wall beach when construction begins. The Cini
beach area will have full-time attendants. Mr. Hitchery asked if there will be a parking fee for
those individuals who only wish to walk the boardwalk. Mr. Seery replied there will be no
charge for that.

Mr. Landers asked if any consideration has been given on reconstruction of the bluff? Mr.
Seery replied there was nothing on that. He agreed to get information for this Commission's
May Meeting.

E. Rain Gauge Subcommittee. Ms. Johnston asked if Mr. Morris was contacted
regarding adding the rain gauge to the Town's system. Mr. Landers suggested looking into
this for next year. Mr. Dinsmore was unable to reach Ledge Light regarding who receives
information. Mr. Landers suggested tabling this matter until next month. Mr. Landers
agreed to contact Mr. Morris to request that he put together a quote on a complete rain gauge.

5. Old Business

A. Harbor Management Plan. Messrs. Hitchery and Dinsmore met with Mr. Steadman
and received a final draft of the proposed revisions to the goals and objectives section. Mr.
Hitchery agreed to e-mail it to Ms. Ghersi for forwarding to Commission members for
comments. Mr. Hitchery plans to meet with Mr. Steadman on April 27 to review a draft of
the next revised section.

6. New Business

A. Correspondence. A COP was received from Docko for bulkhead repair for the Town
of East Lyme. The repairs have already been completed.

B. Niantic River Dinghy Dock Project. Ms. Johnston asked for an update. Mr. Landers
informed her that Mr. Morris is working with the Town Planner and Mark Kramer on
applying for a BIG grant to provide for transient boating opportunities in the Niantic River.
Ms. Johnston asked how do you dredge in an area with many clams? Mr. Landers replied it
is in the conceptual phase. We are trying to improve boating opportunities in the river. Ms.
Johnston asked that a plan be presented to this Commission. Mr. Landers stated the Bar
Study already refers to that area. Mr. Dinsmore will ask Mr. Morris to give a report on this at
our next regularly scheduled meeting.

C. Raised Bill No. 5289, An Act Establishing the Connecticut Port Authority. E-mail
was received asking for comments. Mr. Hitchery commented it should be engaged with
commercial ports only, and not recreational ports. Mr. Theiler stated the language refers
only to commercial traffic in New London and New Haven. Mr. Hitchery added it is also
connected with Homeland Security issues. Mr. Landers stated it is trying to drive economic
development to the major ports.

7, Final Comments There were none.
8. Adjournment
MOTION: Mr. Mountzoures moved to adjourn at 8:45 p.m. Seconded by Mr. Landers.
Motion carried. (6-0)

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
