3. Public Delegation — Mr. Vibber distributed information on pyramid mooring anchors, which
states that the Dor-Mor pyramid mooring anchor rapidly penetrates the bottom due to the high point
loading of its compact size and shape and that the pyramid shape has a high suction effect which
increases the deeper it sets. He was instructed to remove two pyramid moorings in the Niantic Bay.
He referred to the Foundation Findings which states that a 650 pound Dor-Mor mooring anchor
type has a resisting force of 4,500 at breakout. He felt all his mooring anchors exceed East Lyme's
regulations and the Harbor Management Plan. He asked that the ruling regarding the removal of
the pyramid moorings be revisited.

Mr. Landers was asked what was his objection to pyramid moorings? He stated he had no
problem with them in the river, but in the bay the bottom is like asphalt and he felt you could
not dig in it. Ms. Johnston asked where are they? Mr. Vibber replied in the Niantic Bay
Yacht Club area. In his experience he does scuba diving and the pyramid anchors are all
buried 100% and the one inch chain exceeds the minimum requirements for mushroom
anchors. He felt the Harbor Management Plan does not say not to use them.
