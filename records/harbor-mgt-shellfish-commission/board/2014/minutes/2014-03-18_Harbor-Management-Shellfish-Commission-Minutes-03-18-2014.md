E. Rain Gauge Subcommittee

The subcommittee met and discussed two options for the rain gauge: 1) continue the
present arrangement at the State highway garage, which is expensive; 2) use the 911
tower which could be enhanced to required standards for an approximate cost of $400.
Mr. Morris commented the tower rain gauge is not much different from the State gauge.
Mr. Dinsmore felt Waterford and WELSCO should be included in the discussion, since
they are part of the message stream. He agreed to contact Ledge Light to determine who
receives messages. Ms. Johnston stated there is no reason that WELSCO, with over
$62,000 in funds, cannot pay one-third of the cost for the rain gauge that it also uses.
Mr. Kanter stated he cannot speak for all members of WELSCO, but it is possible they
would be willing to share in the cost of the rain gauge. Mr. Hitchery asked who
established each of these commissions? If all three are benefiting from the rain gauge,
why are they not contributing equally to its cost? Mr. Kanter responded the Waterford
Commission is responsible for Waterford town coastal waters and the East Lyme
Commission is responsible for its town coastal waters. WELSCO is responsible for the
Niantic River waters by State statute. WELSCO is a State commission and as such is not
eligible for town funding, nor does it receive any State funding. He said WELSCO’s
finances were built up over the years from money received from shellfish permit fees and
the reserve is needed to pay boat expenses, wardens, legal fees, and audit costs. He said
when the last WELSCO boat sunk he personally drove to Virginia to pick up the
replacement boat and trailered it back to Connecticut in order to save money.
The Subcommittee was thanked for its hard work.
