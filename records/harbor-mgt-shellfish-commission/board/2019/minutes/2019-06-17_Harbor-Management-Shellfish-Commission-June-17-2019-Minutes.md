3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS:
   A. Public Delegations:
       Mike Theiler, Waterford, stated that after talking to the Harbor Master on Block lsland about the
       various jobs and responsibilities he has, Theiler believes the East Lyme Harbor Master and Deputy
       Harbor Master should get paid more so they can do the job as intended. He stated there is more
       that can be done but the position should be considered a "volunteer" position considering the low
       stipend paid by the State.
       The difficulty of placing moorings was discussed and the need for a program to do a grid layout of
       moorings.
       The members discussed the State statute, Town ordinance, and the Harbor Management Plan, all of
       which require mooring fees collected by the Harbor Management Commission be placed into a
       special fund to be used only for harbor improvements and harbor master expenses. S. Dinsmore
       discussed the issue with previous First Selectman Formica and was told that the operating budget
       the Commission receives is more than they take in for fees.
       D. Landers stated the Commission takes in between $7,000 and 58,000. Discussion took place on
       raising the fee for moorings. J. Hitchery stated no mooring fee increase should be considered until
       the specialfund is established as required by law.
       P. Dagle will bring the issue to the BOS for discussion and clarification.


       Mike Johnson, 45 Bellaire Ave. wanted to know what you get for the fee paid to the Town for a
       mooring spot.

   B. Presentation by Keith Neilson on the Blue Plan. No presentation as Neilson was absent.
       The members discussed the Blue Plan. Because the plan is over 400 pages, members have not read
       the whole document. Based on the information they have received about the plan, it was the
       consensus of the commission to direct the chairman to write a letter to the DEEP stating their
       concerns of the autonomy and authority of the commission being usurped by the plan.
