   A. Harbor Management Plan-Update
       Geof Steadman submitted a three-page summary of changes to DEEP. DEEP has verbally stated the

   B. Future Shellfish Restoration Projects
       An email from Allisa Dragan-DABA, stated she was in the area conducting surveys but there are still
       areas to survey. She stated that the area between 4 Mile River and Black Point could be considered for
       seasonal shell fishing. The members discussed the added cost of water sampling if these areas were
       opened up. R. Kanter suggested opening up the area on atrial basis.
       The email mentioned the expanding mooring at the Niantic Bay Yacht Club. Also mentioned were
       expired mooring stickers. Harbor Master R. Johnson stated that he is working on resolving the expired
       stickers.
      R. Kanter informed the members that there is a possible plan in place to seed the Niantic River with
      juvenile oysters. The oysters have the ability to improve the water quality of the river.
