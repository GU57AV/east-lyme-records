3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS:
   A. Public Delegations:
   Fred Wise, L38 Niantic River Rd., asked who would have jurisdiction if someone wanted to take
   water out of the Niantic River for a hatchery and return the water to the River. S. Dinsmore stated
   currently it would be WELSCO.

  Keith Neilson of Docko asked the Commissioners to read the DEEP Blue Plan very carefully as it is
  complicated and in his opinion, a bad plan. He stated it will preempt all local regulatory agencies,
  such as Planning and Zoning and Harbor Management, as well as the Army Corps of Engineers.
  There is a public comment period which ends on June 2L, 2Ot9. He stated this issue is very serious
  and covers Long lsland sound up to the first highway/road/railroad bridge on all rivers.

  Mark Berger stated the proposal is dangerous and it appears they are trying to stymie everything. lt
  includes all policies for development in the Sound and gives final authority to New York State to
  deny any proposals. He stated all of Niantic Bay would be in the Blue Plan territory.

  Michael Theiler, a Waterford commercial fisherman, stated that they are trying to get a provision
  into the plan that if a proposal affects commercial fishing then the commercial fishing industry must
  be consulted for comment. He stated the Nature Conservancy played a large role in developing the
  plan.

  Sgt. Macek, and Officer Willet, East Lyme Police and John Dwire, Niantic Fire Dept. addressed the
  Commission about the possibility of sharing the harbor management boat. The boat they share with
  Waterford is aging and they are in the process of applying for a port security grant to replace the
  motor on their 21'skiff. They stated they have more access to funding and would pick up the cost of
