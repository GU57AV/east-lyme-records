A. Sea Grant Restoration Project

S. Dinsmore drafted a letter concerning the Sea Grant Restoration Project and
submitted it to the members. The members agreed it was well written and

Commission’s gathering on January 26, 2019.
S. Dinsmore stated that he would like to talk to Dave Kerry about he possibility of

B. Correspondence
The members discussed the letter which was submitted by the Town Attorney at
the December meeting. J. Hitchery stated the letter was correct but that the
information is not what was asked for. The Commission wants an opinion on
who has the jurisdiction for granting leases in the Niantic River. He stated the
East Lyme attorney should be made aware of the Waterford town attorney’s
opinion on the subject.
S. Dinsmore informed the members of his discussion with the First Selectman.
The First Selectman reiterated his request that the Commission hold off on
taking any actions related to leases on the Niantic River until after the Public
Safety Complex issue has been resolved. Until the issue of leases is legally
settled, it was the First Selectman’s opinion that nobody has jurisdiction to issue
leases on the Niantic River.
