Dear SiriMadam,

Thank you for the opportunity to provide cornment on the Long Island Sound Blue Plan.

Our commission became aware of the Blue Plan during the inventory phase. We supported this
effort by taking stock of the resources within East Lyme waters of Long Island Sound. At that
time, the plan was described to be focused on prcserving state resources, while coordinating and
integrating rnanagement of statc resources with local regulatory authorities.

Our commission has concerns that the scope of the Blue Plan will infringe on our local
regulatory authority and jurisdiction. We have spent countless hours working to develop, update,
and maintain our Harbor Management Plan, which is approved by DEEP and by the Town of
East Lyrne through local ordinances. We have also developed a Shellfish Management Plan,
approved by the State ancl Town, for managing iocal shellfishing activities. We believe, as Town
appointed subject matter experts, we have greater insight into the local resources and water-
related issues within our Town waters. We also have concerns with aspects of the Blue Plan
infringing Town waters, particularly as dbfined by the 'Offshore waters' definition, and the
impact that may have on local fisheries and marinas,

We respectfully request that these issues be considered before the Blue Plan is finalized.
