5. OLD BUSINESS
A. Harbor Management Plan-Update

Dinsmore reported that the Plan is with Marci at DEEP waiting for her approval.

6. NEW BUSINESS
A. Sea Grant Restoration Project

Dinsmore drafted a letter concerning the Sea Grant Restoration Project and
submitted it to the members. The members agreed it was well written and
reflected their previous discussion. Dinsmore will submit it at the Shellfish
Commission’s gathering on January 26, 2019. Dinsmore stated that he would
like to talk to Dave Kerry about the possibility of opening the remainder of
Niantic Bay for shell fishing. He will ask what needs to be done to get the whole
Bay open ata 1” level.

B. Correspondence

The members discussed the letter presented from the Town Attorney at the
December meeting. Hitchery stated the letter confirmed what was already
known by the Commission but did not address the question of whether or not
WELSCO has leasing authority in the Niantic River. The Commission wants an
opinion on who has jurisdiction for granting leases specifically in the Niantic
River. He stated the East Lyme attorney should be made aware of the Waterford
town attorney’s opinion on the subject.

Dinsmore informed the members of his discussion with the First Selectman who
reiterated his request that the Commission hold off on taking any action related
to leases on the Niantic River until after the Public Safety Complex issue has
been resolved. Until the issue of leases is legally settled, it was the First
Selectman’s opinion that nobody has jurisdiction to issue leases in the Niantic
River.
