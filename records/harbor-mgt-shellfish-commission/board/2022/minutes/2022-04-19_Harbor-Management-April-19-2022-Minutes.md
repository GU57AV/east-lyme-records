3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS:
   A. Delegations from the Public:
      Bill Hurtle,41 Sterling Hill Rd, Lyme, presented the Commission with an application for an
      aquaculture lease in Niantic Bay. (See File Copy). He plans on conducting Type II aquaculture
      with approximately 100,000 seed oysters per year. He will be using a tumbler on the boat. B.
      Hurtle has been working with the state and they have picked out the co-ordinates and will put
      buoys in each corner ofthe designated area.
      The Commission did a minor review of the application and asked B. Hurtle to come back with a
      complete application which would include a registration I.D. and a certification from the East
      Lyme Town Clerk as to the suitability/availability of the proposed location. He was asked to look
      at the Plan and provide all the items on the check list in his updated application. The members
      pointed out that he could do an experimental lease and to consider that as a starting point. This
      item will be on the next agenda.
   B. Docko-repair of retaining wall next to Grand Street
      Keith Neilson, Docko Inc. described the process he will be using to repair/refurbish the retaining
      wall owned by Boats Inc. The members asked about the boundary of the project abutting the town
      and why there was not a 25-foot setback. K. Neilson stated it was not uncommon for the
      boundaries to come right up to another. This is a COP and does not need Commission approval or
      comment and has no impact to the town.
