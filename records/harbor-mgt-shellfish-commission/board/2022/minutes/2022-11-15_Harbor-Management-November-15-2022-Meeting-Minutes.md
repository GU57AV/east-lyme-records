      Discussion of Harbormaster Boat
      The boat is shared with Waterford. When the grant was approved the remaining cost of the grant
      was shared with Waterford at $12,000 each. It was determined that the FEMA contract has been
      satisfied and the Town can swap the Harbor Master boat for the police boat.
      The members discussed limiting Waterford to a schedule of possibly one day a week and for
      emergencies. They were confident a suitable arrangement could be worked out between both
      towns. Once the exchange of the boats is completed with the East Lyme police, both will be stored
      during the winter at the new Public Safety Building.

   A. Shellfish Lease Renewal Process/ Londregan Lease Renewal (EL3)

      D. MacKenzie questioned whether it was a conflict for the applicant to verifu location depths for
