  M. Berger discussed with a few towns concerning their policy and pricing for moorings. He spoke to
  the company who makes the Helix moorings concerning pricing. The towns he spoke to who have
  switched over to town owned Helix moorings informed him they converted from privately owned
  moorings to town owned moorings over the course of three years. Typically they would offer the
  private owner an option to have the first year of the new mooring free and the town would either sell
  the old mooring or sell it for scrap. The towns he spoke to then charged $300 for each mooring,
  making the money invested, back on the third or fourth year. The Helix moorings are the equivalent
  of a 400 lb. mushroom mooring. The Helix moorings are considered to be more environmentally
  friendly and less maintenance.
  MOTION: (Mason/ Landers) to form a subcommittee to establish standardization of mooringsl
