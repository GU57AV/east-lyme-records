January 19, 2016 MEETING MINUTES

Members Present: Steve Dinsmore; Barbara Johnston; Don Landers; Rick Kanter; Joe Hitchery;
Bill Mountzoures

Members Absent: Greg Murin
1. CALL TO ORDER. Chairman Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:31 p.m.

2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF DECEMBER 15, 2015.
Minutes were approved unanimously.

3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS.

Keith Nielsen of Docko, Inc. presented a revised proposal for the addition of floating
transient docks and a dingy dock at Cini Park. Mr. Landers made a motion to find the
Docko application revised 1/19/2016 consistent with harbor management plan. Mr.
Mountzoures seconded. After a lengthy discussion, the motion was passed 4-1-1 with Ms.
Johnston opposing and Mr. Kanter dissenting.

Mr. Nielsen also presented a proposal for a dinghy dock at Grand St. This was just a
review and presented as a discussion item. The Commission was not voting on this
item at this time. He reviewed the plan and explained the scope of the plan. This
proposal will be added to next month’s meeting.

4, REPORTS
A. Shellfish. Mr. Kanter reported that shell fishing had been closed due to rain.
B. Harbor Master. No report.

C. Treasurer. Mr. Landers submitted the budget, but has not received any feedback to
date.

D. Ex-Officio. Mr. Salerno reported on current proposals in the board of selectman’s
meeting.

5. OLD BUSINESS
