HARBOR MANAGEMENT SHELLFISH COMMISSION
May 17, 2016 MEETING MINUTES

Members Present: Steve Dinsmore, Don Landers, Joe Hitchery, Barbara Johnston, Bill
Mountzoures, Greg Murin.
Absent: Rick Kanter

Ex Officos Present: Harbor Master Dick Morris, Selectman Marc Salerno
Absent: Deputy Harbor Master Marc Berger

CALL TO ORDER. Chairman Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:34 pm.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF APRIL 19, 2016.
Landers corrected Section 6.b. by substituting “DABA” (Department of Agriculture
Bureau of Aquaculture) for “agricultural” permit.

Motion: Johnston moved acceptance of corrected minutes. Seconded. Passed 6-0
PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. None

REPORTS.
Shellfish: Landers reported the two seasonally restricted areas were closed May 15.

Harbor Master:
e 202 mooring renewals are complete
e 34 renewals paid & are pending inspection report
e 36 have not responded
e = 13 cancelled
. 6 new
A mooring in shallow water near Niantic River marker #7 requested by Mr.
Londregan for a 40’ barge used in his oyster cultivation was approved.
Harbor Master Morris has been subpoenaed to testify in a probation violation charge
against the owner of a boat abandoned on Crescent Beach last October. The owner
has not paid the Town $3,000 due for cost of removal.
A boat on mooring #256 in the Niantic River has an expired registration and is due
for mooring inspection this year. The owner has not renewed or responded to phone
calls. There was discussion on how the Harbor Master should proceed.

Treasurer: The fiscal year ends on July 29 and any outstanding bills or expenses should
be submitted by then. Invoice received from WELSCO for Warden fees, $75 for March

and $93.75 for April.
Q EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

Selectman: Salerno updated the Commission and advised the budget referendum is
scheduled for this Thursday.

OLD BUSINESS.

Harbor Management Plan: Geoff Steadman reported to Dinsmore that wording was
added to the Plan on request of DEEP stating any Camp Niantic exclusion zone in public
trust waters must be ratified by DEEP.

HM Plan now submitted for final approval by DEEP within 30 days, then to Town for
review by Board of Selectmen and public hearing.

NEW BUSINESS.

Camp Niantic Exclusive Zone: Any exclusion of the public from public trust waters by
Camp Niantic must be in accordance with DEEP regulations, and any related boundary
markers also must have DEEP approval, per DEEP.

Correspondence: Two COP applications submitted by Docko, Inc. for review only: one
on behalf of John Ranelli for stone wall repair at Giants Neck, and one on behalf of Mary
Sybasky for pier repair at 223 Old Black Pt. Rd.

Invitation received to the Save The Hills Coalition Meeting on Wednesday, June 9, from
5-7:30 pm at East Lyme Public Library.

FINAL COMMENTS: Niantic River Watershed Committee report given by Landers to
Johnston with an invitation to attend their meetings.

ADJOURNMENT.

Motion: Mountzoures moved to adjourn at 8:30 pm. Seconded. Passed 6-0.

Submitted by
Joseph Hitchery, Secretary
