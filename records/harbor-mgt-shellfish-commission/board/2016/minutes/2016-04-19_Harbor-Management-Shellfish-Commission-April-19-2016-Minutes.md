HARBOR MANAGEMENT SHELLFISH COMMISSION
April 19, 2016 MEETING MINUTES

Members Present: Steve Dinsmore, Don Landers, Joe Hitchery, Barbara Johnston, Rick Kanter,
and Greg Murin

Members Absent: Bill Mountzoures
Ex Officos Present: Harbor Master Dick Morris, Deputy Harbor Master Mare Berger

Ex Officios Absent: Selectman Marc Salerno

1. CALL TO ORDER. Chairman Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:31 pm.

Motion: Landers moved to add to New Business the Camp Niantic Exclusion Zone.
It was seconded by Kanter. Approved 6-0.

2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF MARCH 15, 2016.
Minutes were approved 6-0.

3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS.
a. Bob Conegliaro, Gerwick Mereen LLC, presented a COP for a dock at 28 Bishops

Bay Road (Jeff and Barbara Steckler) for information purposes.

b. In addition, Conegliaro presented a new dock application for 26 Bishops Bay
Road (Ellen and Bruce Bellucci). Dock consists of 4’ x 33’ fixed pier with 3’ x
26’ ramp and 10’ x 16’ float with boatlift, and retains existing bulkhead in place
since 1995 with 7’ clearance at base of bulkhead. He stated the proposal is
consistent with current DEEP policy.

Motion: Hitchery moved to find the application consistent with Harbor
Management Plan. Seconded by Johnston. Approved 6-0.

4. REPORTS.
a. Shellfish: Kanter reported good results.

b. Harbor Master:
e Renewals had 6% no response
e 68 paid, pending inspection report
e 135 paid and renewed
Q EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

e 13 cancelled

e 284 total moorings
Discussion ensued in regard to publication of mooring regulations. Harbor
Master boat to be launched Friday, April 22, 2016. Fuel to be provided from EL
town pump and shared cost-wise 50/50 with Waterford.

c. Treasurer: No report.
d. Ex Officio: Absent, no report.

5. OLD BUSINESS.

a. Harbor Management Plan: Dinsmore spoke with Geoff Steadman and HM plan
is still in review status with DEEP. Marcie Balint of DEEP should have the
results by 23 or 26 April and then to this Commission to review and pass on to the
Board of Selectman. Proposed plan previously sent to the other EL Town
Commissions for review and comments but none received to date.

6. NEW BUSINESS.
a. Camp Niantic Exclusive Zone: Signs and buoys apparently are not consistent
with DEEP regulations and permit per Marcie Balint. Dinsmore to contact Camp
Niantic and invite a representative to May meeting for discussion.

b. Correspondence: Landers reported Timothy Londregan received an agricultural
permit for his proposed oyster farming operation in Niantic Bay.

7. FINAL COMMENTS.
Further discussion of mooring regulations and Harbor Management Plan was conducted.
Conclusion was that wording in the Plan should be: “Moorings shall be maintained in
accordance with mooring regulations established by the Harbor Management
Commission”.

8. ADJOURNMENT: Motion to adjourn by Murin. Seconded by Kanter. Passed 6-0.
Meeting adjourned at 8:38 pm.

Respectfully submitted by
Joe Hitchery, Secretary
