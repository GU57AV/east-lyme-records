REPORTS.
Shellfish: None

Harbor Master: As of June 21, 2016, the current mooring status is listed below:

* 241 mooring renewals are complete

+ 30 mooring renewals are pending inspection (3 of which are new)

* 2 moorings on hold

+ 4 waiting to receive forms/fees

* Total moorings are 277
A mooring in shallow water near Niantic River marker #7 requested by Mr. Londregan
for a 40' barge used in his oyster cultivation was discussed. Dinsmore suggested Mr.
Londregan set up a Kiosk on the Boardwalk to address any questions and/or problems the
townspeople may have regarding the oyster cultivation. Mr. Londregan said his oyster
harvesting should be ready in September. Dinsmore stated WELSCO should be involved
regarding any shellfish activity on a barge. Mr. Londregon said he is currently working
with WELSCO.
Dave Marriott from the USCG contacted Morris regarding day beacons in Smith Cove,
channel marker #4. Marker #8 was apparently off-site, and new coordinates were
requested. Hitchery volunteered that he has been in contact in the past with Steve
Pothier, the USCG Administrator for PATONs and that he would be of assistance in
resolving the reporting discrepancies, as we know the pilings have never moved.
Morris said he has two interns for the summer that are GIS wizards, and they will be
assigned to work on these issues. Marriott also requested the removal of an
unauthorized “No Wake Zone” sign on marker #4.
