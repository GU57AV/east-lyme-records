1. CALL TO ORDER. Chairman Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:35 p.m.
2, APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF JANUARY 19, 2015.
Minutes were approved unanimously.
3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS.

A. Mr. Neilson presented a revised plan for Grand Street. There was discussion on dredging
and the disposal of dredging material. The floats are wood and should be taken out in the
winter. This needs to be on list of winter preparations.

Motion by Mr. Kanter to find the revised project for the Grand Street boat launch ramp
with straight dock going down to the east is consistent with the Harbor Management
Plan. Second by Mr. Landers.

Motion was approved unanimously.

B. Black Point application. Mr. Fountain appeared as the Black Point representative. Keith
gave an overview of the project. This is a COP (certificate of permission) to rebuild
launch ramp on Seaview Avenue. This is for information only as the Harbor

Management Commission does not have any official capacity with a COP. DEEP asked
for additional information. There are no objections to this project.

Harbor Management Commission will write a letter in support of the project and send it
to the contact person in Hartford. Keith will send contact information to Steve.

5. REPORTS

A. Shellfish. Mr. Kanter reported that shell fishing is open. Scallops going well. Closes at

B. Harbor Master. Received Phone call and email from Coast Guard regarding a
complaint they received about number 8 pylon in Smith Cove. Complaint stated that the
pylon had been moved; however, it is a pylon and it has not been moved. Mr. Morris
looked at it and it seems to be fine. There was discussion on who is responsible to
maintaining the pylons and markers.

Sent out mooring renewal forms. Getting some responses. $25 fee. Took in
approximately $7600 for moorings last year. Discussion on moorings in Waterford, they
do not have enough control on where moorings are placed.

C. Treasurer. Board of select cut our budget by $850. 200 from recording secretary, 600
from maintenance of harbor, 50 supplies. $3000 goes to save the river. 5000 left. Not
called to appear to defend the budget. How do we budget the fill ups? 4 fills a year at
town pumps. Split between EL and Waterford.

D. Ex-Officio. Mr. Salerno reported on current proposals in the board of selectman’s
meeting. The budget will be voted on tomorrow night. Ratified leases in Cini park.

Complaints about unused lobster pots under the bridge. There is room for another dock if
town wants to put it in.

5. OLD BUSINESS
A. Harbor Management Plan. Spoke with Mr. Steadman. Comments from Marcy Blint

(DEEP) and has incorporated those and sent back to her for final approval. When it gets

final approval from DEEP it will need to be presented to Board of Selectmen for

approval. Then there will be a public approval.
