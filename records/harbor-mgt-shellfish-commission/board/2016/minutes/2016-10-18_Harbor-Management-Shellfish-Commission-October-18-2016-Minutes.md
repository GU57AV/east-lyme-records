REPORTS.
Shellfish: Kanter reported that the River is now open. He also reported that scallop season is

Dec 1 —Jan 31.

Harbor Master: Deputy Harbor Master Berger said there is nothing new for this month. He did
mention that Sea Grant is responsible for the Long Island Sound Blue Plan. This "Blue Plan"
legislation establishes a process by which Connecticut will develop an inventory of Long Island
Sound's natural resources and uses and, ultimately, a spatial plan to guide future use of the Sound's
waters and submerged lands. Berger mentioned that they were having a meeting in Norwalk at
6:00 p.m. on November 16, which Commissioners might want to attend.

Landers spoke about the Community Resilience Workshop being held at Camp Niantic (Gary
Goeschel) on November 2". This meeting will discuss what the town can do to make sure assets
along the shoreline will continue to stay safe, considering the sea level rise and possible storm
damage, etc. Contact Gary if interested in attending.

FINAL COMMENTS: Kanter mentioned there appears to be conflicting information on the depths
of dredging in the Federal channel and at Smith Cove. He has reviewed several Boater Guides and
official charts in regard to these areas and although 5-6 feet is probably above average, an official
survey should be done as it has been many years since the late one.

Kanter also suggested that when we have a new dingy dock at Grand Street the publishers should be
notified so their guides can be updated. This would attract more people to our area and get them to spend
money in our town.

Londregan provided a status update on the oysters. He said that mortality was at 7%. He is going to
start selling the oysters this week.
