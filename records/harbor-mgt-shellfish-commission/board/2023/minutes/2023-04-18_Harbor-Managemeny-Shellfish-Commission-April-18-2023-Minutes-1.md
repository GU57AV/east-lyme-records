     Renewal of mooring permits-extension
     Due to the launch of the new mooring system and the late start of notification, the late fee for year
     2023 has been extended to May 12,2023.
     Consideration of Harbormaster salary adjustment
     The Commission discussed a salary increase for the Harbormaster. The State provides $450 per
     year for salary, the town does not supplement the salary. It was the consensus of the Commission
     that the salary should be more but they would wait until the special revenue account was set up to
     evaluate the Commissions expenses. The salary adjustment will be in future agendas.

   A. Shellfish Lease / Londregan Lease Renewal:
      S. Dinsmore has been reviewing the Shellfish Management Plan. He said sections I and2 weren't

   D. Expanding open areas to west side of Black Point
      S. Dinsmore said that if they were to expand into the west side of Black Point, they would need to
      take additional samples at seven locations. They would need to have access to the islands as well.
