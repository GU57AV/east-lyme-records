                               TOWN OF EAST LYME
                 EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

        MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING - JANUARY 17, 2012, 7:30 P.M.
    EAST LYME TOWN HALL 108 PENNSYLVANIA AVENUE NIANTIC CT 06357

Present: Chairman Don Landers, Secretary Barbara Johnston, Rick Kanter, Marvin Schutt, and
Joseph Hitchery (Harbor Master).
Absent: Treasurer Steve Dinsmore, William Mountzoures and Greg Murin.

1. Call to Order
The Meeting was called to order by Chairman Landers at 7:35 P.M.

2. Approval of Minutes of Regular Meeting of December 20, 2011

MOTION (1): Barbara Johnston moved to table approval of the December 20, 2011
Minutes until the next regularly scheduled meeting. Seconded by Marvin Schutt.
(4-0) Unanimous.

3. Public Delegations
There were no public delegations.

4. Reports
A. Shellfish
Mr. Kanter attended the “Gathering of Shellfish Commission’s Meeting” in New Haven. The
Guilford Town Sanitarian discussed how upland pollution can affect shellfish. There has been a
lot of shellfishing activity in the river due to mild weather. The scallop season has reportedly
been very successful, but concern was expressed on taking young, immature scallops. Mr.
Kanter reported that discussions with the Wardens indicated WELSCO should be cautious with
respect to the size of scallops being harvested since the scallop populations are just beginning to
come back in the Niantic River. WELSCO will be meeting on Thursday 1/20 to discuss the
scallop season and management options to sustain the scallop resources in the river.

B. Harbor Master
Mr. Hitchery attended a tour of the Amtrak bridge reconstruction project, received a phone call
from the Coast Guard regarding NavAid No. 9 in Smith Cove; no problem was identified with
NavAids in Smith Cove, Niantic and suggested to them that it may be the other Smith Cove on
the Thames River in Waterford. Mooring permit renewal applications and informational
packages were sent out to 234 applicants early in January; since then, 45 renewals have been
received (plus another 14 this evening); 2012 mooring stickers will be issued after reviewing the
applications. Discussion followed on the expectation for installing winter stakes, cut off dates
for winter stake removal, and requirements for numbering (owner identification) winter stakes.
It was agreed that the Harbor Master under the direction of the Commission would take all
necessary measures to ensure the harbor is safe and all moorings or winter stakes do not pose a
hazard to navigation and recreation in the harbor management area.

C. Treasurer
Mr. Dinsmore was unable to be present, and Mr. Landers gave the Treasurer’s Report. Invoices

5. Old Business
A. Harbor Management Plan (HMP) – Reports from Commission members. Ms. Johnston
agreed to develop a plan and schedule for Commission members to work on updates to various
chapters.

6. New Business
A. Correspondence
       1. Dowling dock proposal at Old Black Point, Niantic.
.      2. Kruse seawall maintenance and dock proposal at 51 Quarry Dock Road, Niantic.
B. Discussion of Niantic River No Wake Zone/Speed Limits. This matter was tabled until the

C. Discussion of Shellfish MOU. Mr. Landers reported that the three shellfish commissions met

the comments. Mr. Landers will discuss this further at Thursday’s WELSCO meeting.
D. Presentation by Capt. David Robinson (OnLine Mooring). Capt. Robinson did not attend.

7. Comments from other Commission Members
Ms. Johnston mentioned the article in the Day that a Board of Directors was established for the
Niantic River Watershed Committee. Mr. Schutt and John Jasper were appointed as directors by
Paul Formica and Mr. Landers and Mr. Kanter serve on the Board as representatives of East
Lyme Harbor Management and WELSCO, respectively. Ms. Johnston asked how much the four
towns are contributing to the Watershed Committee; Mr. Landers replied that presently none of
the four towns are contributing. Ms. Johnston congratulated Marvin Schutt who will be honored
for his community efforts on February 11 at the Rotary Club’s annual fund raiser in Mystic.

8. Adjournment

Management/Shellfish Commission Meeting at 9:00 P.M. Seconded by Mr. Schutt.
(4-0) Unanimous.
