CALLTO ORDER. Secretary Hitchery (Dinsmore absent) called the meeting to order at 7:31 PM.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF MAY 16, 2017.
Correction to Public Delegation — lines 7 and 9 ~ should show Dinsmore “suggested” a motion, not
“made”.
Under “New Business — Proposed Website Change”, Johnston questions the accuracy of
Dinsmore’s comment: “Dinsmore said that the opening/closing is controlled by WELSCO”.
Landers explained that the DABA in Milford makes the decision for opening/closing and it is
passed down to WELSCO who controls the opening/closing. Since Dinsmore is absent this may be
discussed at the next scheduled meeting.
Motion: Landers moved to accept minutes as corrected. Johnston seconded. Motion passed.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. None.

REPORTS.
Shellfish: Landers reported that the river is open with no issues to report.

Harbor Master: No report as Harbor Master Morris is absent.

Treasurer:

Our budget was approved at last year’s level ($10,000). Murin asked how much money was left
over from last year’s budget. Johnston showed Murin a spreadsheet from February 12017, which
showed we had spent $5,938 of the $10,000 budget at that time. Landers said he believes we have
spent close to the entire budget but will look into it and will report at the next meeting.

Two invoices were received from WELSCO for May and June in the amounts of $50.00 and
$68.15 for the warden who does the water sampling.

Ex-Officio: The Board of Selectmen is waiting to hear what happens with the State budget.

OLD BUSINESS.
Harbor Management Plan: Dinsmore reported by email that Marci Blint of DEEP has approved
the Harbor Management Plan. Comments to DEEP are due from the Army Corps of Engineers by
07/14/17. If they have no comments, we will have the document next month. Dinsmore will provide
an update when available.

Johnston wants to know why she has to read WELSCO’s minutes to find out what is going on.

Dinsmore reported by email that the new rain gage is operational at the Waterford boat

FINAL COMMENTS.

Landers reported that before the last meeting he was contacted by people at Golden Spur on
Boston Post Road in Waterford, CT. Since the bridge was raised water craft are now able to
get under when it is closed and are going at higher speed than they should. It was suggested
that we put a 6 mph sign under the bridge because the water is very low there and they could
run aground. Landers said he would pass this information on to Harbor Master Morris.
Johnston asked Murin if he had renewed his commission position as it expired in 2016.
Murin responded that when he came back from vacation a few months ago he did renew his
commission and has been sworn in.

Paul Dagle asked if Riverhead Lane is regulated by the Commission. Dagle mentioned that
he just moved into a house next to this road and noticed that it doesn’t appear to be
maintained by the town. He would like to be able to use this road to access the water and
wanted to make sure it was okay with the Commission. Landers said that he doesn’t see a
problem using the access road to get to the water using a trailer. Johnston asked if the road is
town approved. Dagle said he spoke with his lawyer who said it was okay to use.

Johnston said she attended the Waterford Harbor Commission and found they increased their
mooring fees to $75. Landers thought waterfront owners paid less. Johnston would like to
add to next month’s agenda the discussion of mooring fees.

Hitchery requested all Commissioners to please give prior notice to the Chairman if they are
unable to attend a scheduled meeting. Such notice can prevent unnecessary inconvenience to
other attendees if they know before the meeting that a quorum will not be present.

ADJOURNMENT.

Landers moved to adjourn. Montzoures seconded. Meeting adjourned at 8:10 PM.
