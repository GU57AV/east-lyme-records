MAY 16, 2017, MEETING MINUTES

Members Present: Steve Dinsmore, Joe Hitchery, Greg Murin, Barbara Johnston

Ex Officios Present: Harbor Master Dick Morris, Deputy Harbor Master Marc Berger
Absent: Selectman Paul Dagle

CALLTO ORDER. Steve Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:36 p.m.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF APRIL 18, 2017.
Motion: Murin moved acceptance of minutes. Johnston seconded. Passed 4-0-0.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. Docko Dowling Dock. Dinsmore gave a brief summary of the 2017 COP
brought before the Commission. The proposal is shortening the pier by 4 feet and changing the
width to 5 feet and adding lower stairs and walkway. Johnston mentioned that in 2015 on the
original COP it was 235 Old Black Point Road. She said going back to the paperwork it appears
the Commission did not sign off on it. Dinsmore said it had to have been signed because the town
engineer would have caught it if it wasn’t.

Dinsmore made a motion to find this COP consistent with the Harbor Management Commission.
Murin moved and Hitchery seconded. Johnston abstained. 3-1-0.

Dinsmore made a motion to find this COP consistent with the Harbor Management Shellfish Plan.
Murin moved and Hitchery seconded. Johnston opposed. 3-0-1.

Dinsmore made note that there is no recreational shell fishing allowed in this area.

* 50 Moorings await responses.

* 1 Mooring was canceled (E-472 Daniel Kaplan)

+ 3 new mooring applications were received.

+ Total Moorings are 286

funds that may be available for dredging the Niantic River. Formica, Nickerson, and
Courtney, and the Town of Waterford all support it. Dinsmore was contacted by someone on
the Waterford Harbor Management Commission asking if we are going the dredge the
channel. Dinsmore said at this time it is in discussion, but funding may be an issue.
