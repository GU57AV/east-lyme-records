HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION
JANUARY 17, 2017, MEETING MINUTES

Members Present: Steve Dinsmore, Joe Hitchery, Don Landers, Rick Kanter
Absent: Greg Murin, Barbara Johnston, Bill Mountzoures

Ex Officios Present: None

CALLTO ORDER. Steve Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m.
Because of late filing of this meeting agenda, no motions were accepted.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF DECEMBER 20, 2016.
Dinsmore deferred approval of December 20, 2016 meeting minutes until February meeting.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. None.

REPORTS.
Shellfish: Kanter reported that scallop season is still open, but there are not as many scallops as
last year. Kanter will be attending the Westport CT Shellfish Commission meeting about
commercial seed oystering and permit program.
Treasurer: Received an invoice for mooring stickers in the amount of $599.68, but we had
received an invoice at our December meeting in the amount of $569.72. Landers will follow-up to
clarify the amount and make sure it was paid.

OLD BUSINESS.
Harbor Management Plan: Dinsmore reported that he spoke with Geoff Steadman a few times this
past month. Marci Blint of DEEP has been out of the office for a few weeks, and has not been able to
complete her review of the revised Plan. Steadman has received inputs from Gary Goeschel and is
hoping to submit final draft tomorrow for Marci’s approval.

NEW BUSINESS.
Correspondence:
¢ Letter received from Westport CT Shellfish Commission in re commercial seed oystering and
permit program.

ADJOURNMENT.
Dinsmore adjourned the meeting at 7:41 p.m.

Submitted by
Mary Jane Gaudio, Recording Secretary
