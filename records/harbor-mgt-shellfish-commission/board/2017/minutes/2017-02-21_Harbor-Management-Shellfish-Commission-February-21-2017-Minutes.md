HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION
FEBRUARY 21, 2017, MEETING MINUTES

Members Present: Steve Dinsmore, Joe Hitchery, Don Landers, Rick Kanter, Bill Mountzoures
Absent: Greg Murin, Barbara Johnston

Ex Officios Present: Harbor Master Dick Morris, Selectman Marc Salerno
Absent: Deputy Harbor Master Marc Berger

CALLTO ORDER. Steve Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:32 p.m.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF DECEMBER 20, 2016.
Motion: Kanter moved acceptance of minutes. Montzoures Seconded. Passed.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF INFORMATIONAL MEETING OF JANUARY 17, 2017.
Motion: Hitchery moved acceptance of minutes. Kanter Seconded. Passed.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. None.

REPORTS.
Shellfish: Kanter reported that scallop season is still open, but there are not as many scallops as
last year. Some people have expressed an interest in oyster restoration because they do filter
feeding.

Harbor Master: Mooring status as of February 21, 2017:

* 284 Mooring Renewals were mailed out at the end of January 2017.

+ 99 Mooring responses have been received.

* 184 Moorings await responses.

* 1 Mooring was cancelled (E-472 Daniel Kaplan)

* Total moorings are 283
Morris reported that WELSCO doesn’t want to use our weather station because one day it
didn’t work which upset them. Kanter said that DEEP has a grant that will pay for the use of
the WELSCO weather station and recommends us to use it as the official one. Morris said
we pay $33 a year for our current weather station and Dinsmore suggested for the small
price we pay for this service we should keep it in place as a backup.
Morris also reported there is an abandoned boat on a mooring in the middle of the river
which has not been moved for over three years. Morris has spoken with the owner many
times about removing it but no action has been taken. The owner does not want the boat and
at this point it and the mooring are there for anyone who wants them free of charge. If not
removed in the next week Morris will have someone haul them away for disposal.
Landers stated if the Commission would have to pay someone to have them removed there is
about $1,400 available in the budget if needed.

Treasurer: The Commission has spent $6,000 of the $10,000 budgeted to date. Landers asked

decision is made.

Three invoices were received from WELSCO in the amounts of $31.25 (2/12/17) and $50.00
