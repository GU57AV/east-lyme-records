TOWN OF EAST LYME
EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

Chairman Treasurer Secretary
Stephen Dinsmore Donald F. Landers Jr. Joseph Hitchery

HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION
MARCH 21, 2017, MEETING MINUTES

Members Present: Joe Hitchery, Don Landers, Barbara Johnston
Absent: Steve Dinsmore, Greg Murin, Bill Mountzoures, Rick Kanter

Ex Officios Present: Harbor Master Dick Morris, Selectman Paul Dagel
Absent: Deputy Harbor Master Marc Berger

CALL TO ORDER. In the absence of Chairman Dinsmore, Secretary Hitchery called the meeting to

order at 7:35 p.m.
Because no quorum was present no motions were accepted.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF FEBRUARY 21, 2017.
Deferred until April meeting.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. None.

REPORTS.
Shellfish: River was closed because of a blizzard.

Treasurer: Landers submitted the three invoices received at last meeting for payment. Deposited
check for lease fee from Tim Londregan. Landers confirmed the mooring sticker invoice was paid.

Harbor Master: Mooring status as of March 21, 2017:

* 284 Mooring renewals were mailed out at the end of January 2017.

* 191 Mooring responses have been received (146 completed, 45 awaiting inspections)

* 94 Moorings await responses.

* 1 Mooring was cancelled (E-472 Daniel Kaplan)

* 2 New moorings were approved..

* Total moorings are 286.
Morris reported that he emailed Congressman Courtney asking for $20 million to dredge the
Niantic River as federal grant money may be available from Army Corp of Engineers. He
received a response that they would look into it. Morris said he has a quote for $500-
$600,000 to dredge in front of Cheney Park and a person was coming out to look and discuss
options. He was told it would cost $40,000 for sediment samples required before dredging.

Ex-Officio: Selectman Paul Dagel introduced himself. He is replacing Selectman Salerno as our
ex-officio Board of Selectmen representative.

OLD BUSINESS. FILED
Harbor Management Plan: No new updates.
MOV 23 9917 at 4.10 Gem

EAST LYME TOWN CLERK

East Shore Drive.
A replacement rain gauge is to be located near the shellfish warden shack at the State boat launch
in Waterford.

ADJOURNMENT.
Hitchery adjourned the meeting at 8:05 p.m.
