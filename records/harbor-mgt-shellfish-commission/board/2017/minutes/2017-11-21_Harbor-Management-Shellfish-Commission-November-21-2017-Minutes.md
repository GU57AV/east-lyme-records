3. Public Delegations

Peter Harris, WELSCO Commissioner informed the members the meeting with
Army Corps of Engineers (ACOE) has been postponed from December 7, 2017 for
60 days. There will be a tri-commission meeting arranged which will include East
Lyme Harbor Management, WELSCO, and Waterford Harbor Management.
Invited guest will include the First Selectmen from East Lyme and Waterford as
well as town planners from each town. The purpose is to come together to find
areas in the Niantic River which are suitable for aquaculture. There is no date for

the meeting at this point. The meeting will have to be noticed by all commissions
involved.

FINAL COMMENTS
Landers suggested being prepared for the joint meeting (discussed previously) between
the commissions. Various areas were discussed for possible aquaculture.
T. Londregan (applicant) commented on some of the areas and discussed the various
types of shellfish farms and equipment.
The issue of who has the authority to approve leasing of State waters was discussed and
also the question of whether the Board of Selectmen can override the statutorily
established commissions decision. Ex-Officio Dagle stated there was nothing in the
statutes which give the commission the authority to issue leases.

G. Murin stated he discussed the proposed dinghy dock with Boats Inc. and said there
may be another solution in addition to the one discussed at the October meeting.
Instead of finger pier sticking out in the middle of the channel and 8’ to 10’ wide float
(with ramp) could be built parallel to the bulk head. This would provide the full length of
the town park bulk head-approximately 30 feet-for dingy space, and would be
acceptable to Boats Inc. S. Dinsmore stated Don McKenzie from Boats Inc. will be at the
next months meeting.
