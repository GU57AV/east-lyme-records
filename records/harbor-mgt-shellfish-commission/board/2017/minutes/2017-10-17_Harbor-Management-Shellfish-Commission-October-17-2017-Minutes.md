East Lyme Harbor Management and Shellfish Commission,

Niantic Bay Shellfish Farm (NBSF) strives to better the community and the environment in
which it operates. Consequently, the benefits of NBSFs' endeavors are not strictly tied to one
geographical location. The charge of this Commission primarily revolves around water usage and the
protection/proliferation of natural resources contained within East Lyme waters. While this
Commission may feel that it is not their responsibility, nor right, to act in any shape, form or manner
regarding activities outside of their immediate jurisdictional boundaries, it seems that this is short
sighted and inconsistent with the Commissions charge. With that being said, NBSF respects this
Commissions decisions regardless.

An aquaculture project located outside of the immediate jurisdictional boundaries of this
Commission does not lessen the net positive environmental impacts of such a project to any waterbody
of which is within the jurisdictional boundaries of this Commission. In other words, if this
Commission is tasked with “Maintaining exceptional water quality... [shellfish] restoration and
enhancement...and mitigate competing uses...” than would it not be prudent for this Commission to
weigh in on an aquaculture project, or any project, which would effect, negatively or positively, their
set goals even when outside of their immediate jurisdictional boundaries? Restoring and enhancing
shellfish within the Niantic River, by way of a venture pursued by Waterford East Lyme Shellfish
Commission (WELSCO) and NBSF, would disseminate shellfish within Waterford and East Lyme
waters. Not only is stock enhancement an achievable goal, but so too is the sequestration of carbon and
the filtration of excess nitrogen from the Niantic River, which, as noted earlier, is located within
Waterford and East Lyme; consequently, this is of interest to this Commission given its’ charge.

Furthermore, while NBSF is not requesting an endorsement, NBSF is looking for individuals
with knowledge to speak from a position of expertize. For example, the shellfish management plan for
East Lyme clearly indicates the need for aquaculture as well as the fact that over 60 acres were set aside
in the Niantic River for cultivated shellfishing grounds between 1894 and 1931. This key fact indicates
a precedent that aquaculture has, in fact, been a long standing tradition within East Lyme and Waterford
waters, Perhaps the Commission could indicate why gear is used and why certain locations are
selected based off of predation, environmental conditions, competing uses etc., as is detailed in the
Management Plan. If the Commission feels under-informed on issues outside of their immediate
jurisdictional boundaries, it may be helpful, and in the Commissions best interest, to establish dialogue
with WELSCO as there are WELSCO aquaculture areas within this Commissions immediate
jurisdictional boundaries. Lastly, NBSF would like to remind the members of this Commission that in
the plan submitted to this body on the 18" of November 2014, NBSF expressed the interest and
necessity of a nursery up-river so as to not only achieve the goals laid out by NBSF but to also enable
this Commission strive to achieve its' charge.

Timothy A. Londregan III
Owner and Operator of NBSF

‘The Niantic River {s a small shallow estuary with a restricted outlat to Long Island Sound. It is a mostly
residential area and is heavily used for a variety of marine recreational activities. The Niantic River has few
identified areas open for aquaculture projects, which would not encroach on these activities. One of these
activities is shellfishing and it is the mission of the Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission to sustain and
enhance recreational shellfishing.

Therefore the Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission has deemed that any aquaculture projects be on an
experimental basis only, limited in their size, scope and duration. Due to the restricted area available for
aquaculture projects in the Niantic River, the Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission shall favor
experiments of a scientific nature with emphasis on restoration of native shellfish. Aquaculture projects shall
be defined as any experiment requiring any structure to be placed in the river, such as buoys, floats, nets,
cages, lines, anchors, etc.

The Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission will review applications for aquaculture projects on a case-by-
case basis and approval is based solely on the discretion of the Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission. If
approved, it is incumbent on the applicant to adhere to all Federal, State, Harbor Management, Local and other
tegulations that may be In effect, as to placement, size, construction, etc., 0 as not to infringe on any
navigable water, private property, marine grasses or in any other way to create a hazard or lessen the use of
the Niantic River. Projects shall be in areas identified by the Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission so as
not to adversely effect areas open to recreational shelifishing, as determined by the Waterford-East Lyme
Shellfish Commission. All applications shall show consistency with all applicable laws, including filing of public
notice,

Projects must be maintained in good order. If for any reason the project is not maintained to the satisfaction of
the Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission, the Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission reserves the
fight to have the applicant remove the structure prior to the experiment’s projected duration.

If aquaculture projects are successful, the applicant understands no ongoing operation will be allowed at the
conclusion of the experiment. During the project's life, the Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission
reserves the right to ask for a portion of shellfish produced to be distributed in the River in order to enhance the
river's recreational shellfishing.

If the applicant fails to abide by this agreement in any way, as determined by the Waterford-East Lyme

Shellfish Commission, the Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission reserves the right to terminate the
experiment with the onus on the applicant to promptly remove the project from the Niantic River. Any renewals
shail be solely at the discretion of the Waterford-East Lyme Shellfish Commission.
