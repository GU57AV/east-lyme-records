PUBLIC DELEGATIONS. Tim Londregan provided an annual review of his Oyster Cultivation Farm.
He said it is going well this year. He said of the 7 acres approved by the Commission he is
currently using 2 acres that the federal and state agencies felt was sufficient for beginning the
business. He will then get 2 more acres and then 1 more acre in the next year. Londregan said the
Stonington hatcheries have 80 million approaching larvae stages. He said that 50% would set —
but he is expecting 20% to 25% which he estimates will be 18-20 million oysters.

REPORTS.
Shellfish: Kanter reported that the river is open.

Deputy Harbor Master: Berger reported that everything is going well and that we are at about
three-quarters full. He reported that the derelict boat in the middle of the river had been removed.

Treasurer:

Two invoices were received from WELSCO in the amounts of $37.50 (2/17) and $137.50 (317)
which Landers will submit for payment.

Dinsmore mentioned that it is almost the end of the fiscal year so if we need to get anything done —
now would be the time to do it. Landers mentioned that in the past the Commission had posted the
demarcation signs. Dinsmore said he would try to find a weekend when he could get this done.

Ex-Officio: Selectman Dagle reported on items approved at the last Board meeting.

OLD BUSINESS.
Harbor Management Plan: Dinsmore reported that he spoke with Geoff Steadman. Marci Blint of
DEEP has approved the plan and has forwarded it to other state agencies for their review, requesting
all comments/inputs be submitted to her by May 25, 2017.
