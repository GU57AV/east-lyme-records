HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION
AUGUST 15, 2017, MEETING MINUTES

Members Present: Steve Dinsmore, Don Landers, Greg Murin, Barbara Johnston, Bill Mountzoures
Absent: Rick Kanter and Joe Hitchery

Ex Officios Present: None
Absent: Harbor Master Dick Morris, Deputy Harbor Master Mare Berger, Selectman Paul Dagle

CALLTO ORDER. Steve Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:37 PM.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF TUESDAY, JULY 18, 2017.
Montzoures moved to accept minutes of July 18, 2017. Murin seconded. Steve Dinsmore
abstained. Motion passed 4-0-1.

PUBLIC DELEGATIONS.

A. Dave LaBrie of the Inn at Harbor Hill, Grand Street, Niantic, CT has a few questions and was
looking to learn more about the Public Notice of Tim Londregan’s Niantic Bay Shellfish Farm.
Dinsmore responded that he has no comment as he hasn’t seen it. Landers said he heard about
it yesterday and it is a Type 2 Shellfish Agriculture in the River located in Area B on the
Waterford side. This would open after the winter when all of the boats are gone. Landers said
our Commission can comment on it in accordance with the Harbor Management side but not
on the Shellfish. He thinks it would be good for the river as it will clean out the river.

It was asked if there was a way the Commission could submit a letter on behalf of the Public
Notice. Protrusion of equipment may not be aesthetically pleasing. LaBrie asked “What if
something negative happens — who takes care of it? Landers said it is a long way from being
approved. WELSCO may require a public hearing, not sure. DEEP, Army Corps would be
voting in on this also.

It was decided to add this topic to the Agenda for the next scheduled meeting.
REPORTS.
Shellfish: Landers reported that there have been no notices of closure or rainfall. He noted that
Londregan does not have additional buoys yet.
Harbor Master: No report as Harbor Master Morris is absent.
Treasurer:

Landers had nothing to report at this time. He did email out last year’s budget numbers, money
spent and remaining funds to all members after the last meeting.

OLD BUSINESS.
Harbor Management Plan: Dinsmore spoke with Geoff Stedman and he reported they received
late comments from the federal agency that are being incorporated. The plan has been tentatively
approved. Marci Blint is out with family issues and when she returns and the plan has been
approved Steve will distribute copies to all of the members. Landers asked if he could get a copy of
the redline version of the plan to match it up against the final version of the plan to see what changes
were made. Dinsmore said he has two copies of the redline version and would give him one to use.

Johnston wants to know why Board of Selectman Dagle reported at the Board of Selection meeting
that the Harbor Management Plan was approved by the Harbor Management Commission. This is
not true. Dinsmore said he would contact Dagle and ask him to change that in the minutes.

NEW BUSINESS.

Correspondence:

© Dinsmore will forward a copy of the Public Notice of Tim Londregan’s Niantic Bay Shellfish
Farm to all members in the next few days.

e Murin moved to add discussion of increasing the mooring fees to the agenda. Landers
seconded. Motion passed 5-0-0.

¢ Johnston mentioned that she has asked to have this added to the agenda because she found out
that Waterford Harbor Management Commission (WHMC) is raising their fees. Murin
corrected her and said that WHMC is only increasing the application fee of the moorings, not
the renewals. He doesn’t feel we will make as much money as Johnston thinks because most
of the moorings are renewals.

e Murin suggested charging a $5 Slip Assessment (River Maintenance Fee) for the 300 to 400
boats that are currently in slips now that doesn’t pay us. We will definitely make money by
doing this if we can do it. Landers said that we can adopt an ordinance and the marinas will
enforce it. This is a topic that we will table and discuss at a later time.

e Johnston has asked the Harbor Master in the past to think about limiting the number of
moorings we have available to 300. Murin said that he believes we currently are limiting the
mooring applications. For instance, Giants Neck has a waiting list, the upper river is closed to
new mooring unless the person has waterfront property, and the lower river is not at capacity
yet so there are still some available.

¢ Murin mentioned that he noticed a couple of mooring in the river that shouldn’t be there. He
thinks he knows who own them. He will speak with Harbor Master about it in the next few
weeks as he is unavailable.

FINAL COMMENTS.
e Murin noticed that Waterford has added six new buoys at a cost of $7,000 and they are on the
east side of the Golden Spur. He also noticed there is a new sign at the entrance of Smith
Cove that the speed limit is 6 miles an hour within 100 feet of the shoreline.
