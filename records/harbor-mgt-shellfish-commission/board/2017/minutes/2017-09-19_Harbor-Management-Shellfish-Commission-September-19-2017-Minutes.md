Hitchery requested unanimous consent to move New Business Item 6 A on the agenda -
“Approval of Letter to the Army Corps of Engineers” - as the first order of business to be

the Niantic Bay Shellfish Farm (NBSF) application. There was no objection.

Hitchery then stated he had reviewed documents submitted by people opposing the NBSF
application at the Special Meeting last week, and meeting minutes from WELSCO and
the Waterford Harbor Management Commission (WHMC). He noted that WHMC voted
against it and WELSCO voted for it, and it appears our own Commission is divided on
this. He also noted the proposed site is on the Waterford side of the River where this
Commission has no jurisdiction, and therefore is not required to take any action.

Motion: Hitchery moved the Commission take no action on the proposed letter

of support. Murin seconded.

One of the attendees asked about the boundaries of East Lyme and Waterford waters.
Dinsmore explained that the East Lyme HM/SC is a joint Commission and the Shellfish
part of the Commission is responsible for Niantic Bay to Waterford town line and west
over to Old Lyme town line. We have two approved areas for shell fishing. The Harbor
Management part of the Commission has jurisdiction over the River from the shore to
mid-channel up to Golden Spur. East of mid-channel is Waterford waters. Shellfishing
activities in the River are controlled by WELSCO, which is a  state-appointed
Commission. East Lyme and Waterford each have their own Shellfish Warden. Also East
Lyme and Waterford each have a Harbor Master. Waterford waters include Smith Cove on
the Thames River.

B. Gerwick Mereen LLC Application. Bob Conigliaro presented an application on
behalf of Regina Hitchery to install a fixed pier, ramp, and float with a boat lift, and
to retain the existing bulkhead at 27 Saunders Drive. Landers asked if there would
be stops installed and if the float will be elevated during the winter. The answer was
yes. Landers asked if the neighbors were okay with this application. The required
notification will be sent to neighbors in Saunders Point. This is a structures
application only.

Motion: Murin moved that the Commission find this application consistent with the
Harbor Management Plan. Montzoures seconded. Motion passed, 5-0-1. (Hitchery
abstained)

REPORTS
Shellfish: Landers reported that the river is open with no issues to report.

Harbor Master: No report as Harbor Master Morris is absent.

Treasurer: Three invoices were received. Two were from WELSCO for July and August
in the amounts of $31.25 and $18.75 for the warden patrol. The third invoice for over
$700 was for mooring maintenance for the Harbor Master boat mooring in Niantic Bay.
Landers will contact Harbor Master Morris to ask him when and how the mooring was

Ex-Officio: The Board of Selectmen is waiting to hear what happens with the State

OLD BUSINESS
Harbor Management Plan: Dinsmore spoke with Geoff Stedman and was told that the
Harbor Management Plan was signed off today by DEEP. Dinsmore will pick up the Jetter
this week from the State. Landers asked how we proceed for Town approval. Dagle said
he believes the plan needs to go to the Board of Selectman for their review and then a
public hearing will be set up. Dagle will verify exact steps needed and report back to the
Commission.

Johnston wants the Commission to review the plan and the changes made to it by DEEP
before anyone else looks at it. Dinsmore said that once we have the approval letter from
DEEP he will distribute copies to Commissioners to review. Dinsmore mentioned that if
there are any changes that need to be made the plan will have to go back into the review
cycle and could take another year or longer to get it back. He stated for the record that
before the plan went to the State, Town officials had it to review and didn’t have any
comments. Dinsmore said that Geoff Stedman has worked really hard with no pay in two
years on this plan and would like to do something for him. Landers suggested writing a
letter of thanks and a letter of recommendation. The Commission agreed.
