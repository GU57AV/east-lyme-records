East Lyme Harbor Management/Shellfish Commission
Regular Meeting Minutes
July 19, 2011

Present: Don Landers, Chairman; Barbara Johnston, Secretary; Greg Murin, Bill
Mountzoures and Rick Kanter

Absent: Marvin Schutt and Steve Dinsmore

Also Present: Joseph Hitchery, Harbor Master; Marc Berger, Deputy Harbor Master and
John Birmingham, Ex Officio

1. Call to Order. Chairman Landers called the East Lyme Harbor Management/Shellfish
Commission Regular Meeting to order at 7:30 p.m.

2. Approval of Minutes of Regular Meeting of June 21, 2011. Mr. Landers asked for
additions, deletions or corrections to the Regular Meeting Minutes of June 21, 2011.

MOTION (1): Ms. Johnston moved to approve the Regular Meeting
Minutes of June 21, 2011, as presented. Seconded by

Mr. Murin. Mr. Mountzoures abstained from voting.
(3-0-1) Motion carried.

3. Public Delegations. There were no public delegations.
4. Reports
A. Warden. Mr. Landers reported the river is open for clamming.

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Hitchery reported he received a Notice to Mariners that
emergency repairs are needed on the existing Amtrak Railroad Bridge. The bridge will open to
vessel traffic upon two hour advance notice between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m. from July 18 to July 22,
July 25 to July 29 and August 1 to August 5, 2011. Mr. Berger stated the chain and chain drive
sprocket need to be replaced.

Mr. Hitchery reported a total of 14 new permits have been issued and five are pending.
He received a total of 199 renewals and has seven pending. Two partially submerged 4x4
wooden winter-sticks in the Niantic River previously identified as hazardous, were corrected; the
owner took care of one and Mr. Tyler removed one. Final notices were sent out via email to
previous mooring permit holders that did not renew permits in 2011. Some individuals
responded that they are relinquishing their mooring permits and some notices were returned as
undeliverable. Discussion followed on development of policies with respect to delinquent and
Wy, EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

unpermitted moorings. Some indicated that if an individual doesn’t pay the mooring permit fee,
the mooring should get pulled; others noted that extenuating circumstances should be considered
before pulling unpermitted moorings. Mr. Landers suggested a subcommittee of this
Commission and the Harbor Master/Deputy Harbor Master develop a policy to address
corrective actions associated with unpermitted/delinquent moorings.

Mr. Hitchery asked this Commission to consider purchasing a used boat for the Harbor
Master and Commission. Mr. Landers asked the Harbor Master/Deputy Harbor Master to

explore the availability and costs of a boat and present a proposal to the Commission for
consideration.

C. Treasurer. Mr. Landers reported this Commission received a bill from The Day in
the amount of $63.80 for a Legal Notice on 2011 Mooring Permit expectations in the Town of
East Lyme. Mr. Hitchery presented a receipt for mooring permit fees in the amount of $350.
MOTION (2): Mr. Mountzoures moved to accept the Treasurer’s

Report as presented. Seconded by Mr. Murin.
(4-0) Unanimous.
D. Ex Officio. Mr. Birmingham was delayed due to his work schedule.

5. Old Business

A. Harbor Management Plan—Report from Subcommittee. This matter was tabled.

B. Discussion of Dowling Dock Application at 285 Black Point. Ms. Johnston was
informed by Mr. Neilson that he is waiting for Mr. Dowling to obtain approval from neighbors
before proceeding any further.
6. New Business

A. Correspondence. No correspondence was received.

B. Discussion of Niantic River No Wake Zone/Speed Limits.

Commission member Mr. Kanter arrived.

(See attached memo from Rick Kanter dated July 15, 2011)

C. Discussion of Shellfish MOU and July 6 Special Meeting of WELSCO. Mr.
Landers felt the July 6 Special meeting of WELSCO was very positive. Most of the meeting

was spent discussing roles and responsibilities for shellfish closure/reopening, and revising the

MOU. This Commission will need to work on validating the location of demarcation signs once
the MOU is finalized.

Ex officio John Birmingham arrived.

7. Comments from Other Commission Members. Mr. Birmingham asked for an update on
commercial clamming operations in the river. Mr. Kanter (WELSCO co-chair) responded that
on a second trial, about 65 bushels of clams were harvested.

Mr. Birmingham asked if this Commission had a booth during Celebrate East Lyme? Mr.
Landers replied that the Commission indirectly provided support to a booth hosted by the Niantic
River Watershed Association.
