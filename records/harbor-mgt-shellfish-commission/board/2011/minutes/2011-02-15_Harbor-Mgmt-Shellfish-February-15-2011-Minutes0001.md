2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING OF JANUARY 18, 2011

MOTION (1): Barbara Johnston moved to approve the Minutes
of the Regular Meeting of January 18, 2011, as

MOTION (2): Barbara Johnson moved to add to this evening’s
Agenda item 6 c. Presentation by Gary D. Smith,
P.E. for Bayview Landing Proposal. Seconded
by Bill Mountzoures. (4-0) Unanimous.

Mr. Smith, Civil Engineer and resident of East Lyme, reported he has been working on the
Bayview Landing Marina project, located at 111 Main Street.

He showed a composite showing four plans of the proposed dock layout and dredge area. He
also showed a composite showing three plans of a profile dredge area and profile new dredge vs.
the past permitted with uplands improvements. The existing Bathymetric Site Survey showed
the mean low water and high tide lines. In 1987 this area was dredged by Boats, Inc. The
proposed dock layout would allow 144 slips. If open for transient boaters it would have a fuel
dock, dingy dock, pump out station and lift. The property is presently owned by the Morgillos.
Carmine Marino leases the marina.

Mr. Hitchery asked how far away from the channel is the last slip? Ms. Johnson asked would
there be a separate area for transient boats? Mr. Smith stated if we obtain grants they require
transient be available. There are no transient boats in this area.
Mr. Berger asked is there a requirement from the grant to set aside an area for commercial boats?
Mr. Smith replied there is not.

Ms. Johnston did not feel there would be sufficient parking. Mr. Smith replied parking is based
on the number of slips. The Town requires 1 4 slips per parking space or 0.6 parking spaces per
slip.

Mr. Smith stated we are in communication with AMTRAK and the analysis of the proposed
dredge material is all good. AMTRAK expressed an interest in the past to replenish the beach.
There is also a project in Old Lyme that may be interested in the dredge material.

Mr. Kanter asked if the testing found no contamination? Mr. Smith replied one area has a metal
composite. Millstone had data on eelgrass. Eelgrass is anchored at the beach. It grows every
summer. The state requires a 25’ buffer from the eelgrass.

Mr. Landers asked if it would be a new dock piling? Mr. Smith replied everything is new. The
wooden bulkhead will be rebuilt.

Mr. Smith referred to profiles of the dredge area showing the area where the dredging will be. It
shows the old and new dredge areas. This is important to the state. The area will be landscaped.
Mr. Smith plans to request Zoning to try to keep the parking areas open without trees to allow for
storage. Mr. Landers suggested considering pervious materials like those used at ‘Hole in the
Wall’ parking area rather than asphalt.

Ms. Johnston expressed concern with flooding? Mr. Smith replied it would be graded so that it
will not flood. There are catch basins along Rt. 156 or there can be on-site catch basins. Mr.
Landers asked that consideration be given to a rain garden or swale instead of raised curbing
spacers between parking areas. Ms. Johnston suggested using salt water friendly bushes.

Mr. Landers asked if there would be any improvements to the existing buildings. Mr. Smith
replied it would be handicapped accessible.

Mr. Berger asked when do you anticipate obtaining permission from the state? Mr. Smith
replied we would like to have it ready by spring of 2012. A tenant has a long-term lease on it.

Mr. Berger referred to a fuel dock vs. a bulkhead and asked wouldn’t you want deep water for a
fuel dock? Mr. Smith replied it is located in the 7’ dredged area.

Mr. Landers asked about underground fuel tanks? Mr. Smith replied we would need to be in
compliance with DEP.

Mr. Smith informed the Commission we would like to see a picnic area for the marina, pubic

parking, a sitting area, sidewalk and a landscaped area along the roadway. There will also be
handicap accessible bathrooms.
Ms. Johnston asked do you have a second entrance from the street? Mr. Smith replied it could.
Ms. Johnston suggested that Mr. Smith be in contact with the Fire Marshal.

Mr. Landers asked how many boats could you store in the winter season? Mr. Smith replied he
does not know yet.

Mr. Smith informed the Commission that in 1993 a plan was submitted to the Long Island Sound
Program, a cover letter was sent to the state, a chemical analysis was done. In 2010 everything
was found acceptable by the state’s standards. A benthic analysis was done, and it was found the
dredging would not result in any issue to the benthic community. The state’s concern is with
protecting the eelgrass. He referred to Millstone data, correspondence with the state, and a report
from Ms. Priscilla Bailey on SAV (submerged aquatic vegetation or in this case eelgrass). It was
found that there is no problem in the dredge channel. Mr. Landers recommended that this
Commission review all information received by Mr. Smith. Since this is a significant project he
felt it deserves a Special Meeting. There was a consensus of the Commission to hold a Special
Meeting on this matter. Mr. Kanter asked if Mr. Smith would attend a Waterford/East Lyme
Shellfish Commission Meeting and make a presentation.

Commission members were asked to send any questions they have to Mr. Landers who will
forward them to Mr. Smith. Mr. Landers stated he is very excited about this project. He felt
transient boating is important and that it will be an improvement to that part of Town. Ms.
Johnston agreed. Mr. Berger felt there was no down side to this project. Mr. Kanter felt it would
be an upgrade.
