Dear Board Members:

The Waterford/East Lyme Shellfish Commission (WELSCO) was asked by our ex-officio, Jack Hogan, to
comment on the possible withdrawal of East Lyme from the Ledge Light Health District.

WELSCO is responsible for shellfishing in the Niantic River and as such must work closely with the
Sanitarian.

Our primary concerns with regard to a change from our current situation are as follows:

e Since WELSCO covers both the Waterford and East Lyme jurisdictions of the river, it may
become problematic if one town stays with Ledge Light and the other does not.

e WELSCO is concerned that a new district with inland towns may not have the expertise in
dealing with estuarine issues as does a shoreline town.

Ledge Light has been satisfactory for the most part, but there are areas that can be improved.
Communication has been a problem, and better links need to be established to enhance effectiveness.

It is WELSCO’s understanding that East Lyme may stay with Ledge Light, may partner with other towns
to form a new district, or may return to a town department. It is also our understanding that Ledge Light
has offered to reduce prices going forward to be more competitive. While we support lowering costs and
increasing efficiencies, it is imperative that fee reductions cannot come at the expense of performance.

Sincerely,

Eric Kanter
Co-chairman
Waterford/East Lyme Shellfish Commission
