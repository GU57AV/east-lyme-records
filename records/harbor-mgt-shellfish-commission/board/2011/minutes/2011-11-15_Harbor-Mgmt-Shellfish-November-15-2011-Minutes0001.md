Mr. Kanter reported there will be discussion on the shellfish season for scallops in the Niantic

Mr. Dinsmore reported he received a bill from the Recording Secretary for 2.50 hours, bills from

River and removal of a dock section from Bishops Bay in the amount of $760.00. This work was
authorized by Harbor Master Joseph Hitchery. A check was received from WELSCO for $20 for
a Niantic Bay area shellfish permit. Invoices for annual commercial shellfish leases in Niantic
Bay will be issued to Mike Theiler and Robert Russell.

Mr. Landers reported members of the public have expressed concerns and there have been

7. Comments from other Commission Members

Mr. Kanter reported he received an inquiry from the Coast Guard regarding inconsistences
between the placement and chart location of navigational aids in the federal channel of the
Niantic River. Mr. Kanter through his service with the Coast Guard Auxiliary validated the
location of existing navigation aids in the federal channel and supplied that information to the
Coast Guard. The Coast Guard expects to include these findings in the next navigational chart
revision.
