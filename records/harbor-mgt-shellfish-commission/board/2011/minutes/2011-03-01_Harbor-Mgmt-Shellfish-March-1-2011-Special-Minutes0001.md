East Lyme Harbor Management/Shellfish Commission
Special Meeting Minutes

Marvin Schutt, and William Montzoures

Also Present: Joseph Hitchery, Harbor Master, Gary D. Smith, P.E., Keith Neilson of
Docko, Inc. and Cliff Kramm

1. Call to Order. Chairman Landers called the Harbor Management/Shellfish Commission
Special Meeting of March 1, 2011 to order at 7:32 p.m.

2. Discussion Pending Application for Bayview Landing, East Main Street. Chairman
Landers reclused himself from discussion on this application because he was involved with
providing information on the project.

Ms. Johnston asked will there be dingys on both sides of the dingy dock? Mr. Smith
replied there will be. Mr. Kanter asked if the dock will be free and available to the public?
Mr. Smith replied it will be. Mr. Montzoures asked if there will be a sign stating that the
dingy area is available? Mr. Smith replied there will be.

Mr. Smith was asked if 111 Main Street is the address of the property? Mr. Smith replied
it is.

Mr. Smith was asked if there are any plans for the building other than as it is? He replied
there were no other plans other than new bathrooms.

Ms. Johnston asked if the store will remain? Mr. Smith replied it will.

Ms. Johnston asked on the bulkhead when you face northeast will there be any boats tied
to it? Mr. Smith replied there will not.

Ms. Johnston asked if the bulkhead will be replaced with steel? Mr. Smith replied the
applicant is considering steel.

Ms. Johnston asked if the center will be curbed? Mr. Smith replied the plan is to curb it.
Mr. Mulholland will have the final say.

Ms. Johnston recommends a second emergency exit, even if it is temporarily opened in
the winter to enable emergency equipment in.

Ms. Johnston asked if there will be electrical outlets for each pier? Mr. Smith replied
there will be. She felt concrete was sturdier and easier to walk on. Mr. Smith agreed to
check into it. Ms. Johnston asked if there will be lights at night. Mr. Smith replied each
stanchion will have a light. Ms. Johnston asked if there will space for larger boats? Mr.
Smith referred to the plan. Ms. Johnston felt commercial space should remain. Mr. Smith
replied commercial boats can rent there. Ms. Johnson asked if there will be space for a little
troller? Mr. Smith replied there will be. Ms. Johnson asked if the height of the original
building will be the same? Mr. Smith replied it will.

Ms. Johnston asked if Boats, Inc. has been contacted about this project? Mr. Smith
replied the applicant has discussed it with them. Mr. Smith will also have discussions with
them.

Ms. Johnston asked who owns the area to the bridge? Mr. Smith replied it is a state road
and the Town owns Cini Park.

Ms. Johnston asked if there will be two free spaces for loading and unloading? Mr.
Smith replied it has not been outlined but he will look into it.

Ms. Johnston asked if there will be a special area for transient boaters? Mr. Smith replied
they will be mixed throughout the marina.

Ms. Johnston asked if there will be any pump out system on land? Mr. Smith replied
there will be a pump out system on the fill dock.

Ms. Johnston asked if the DEP permit in 1967 could still be used? Mr. Smith replied it
will have to be revised.

Ms. Johnston asked for an explanation of the dotted lines on the plan. Mr. Smith showed
the outline of the slip area.

Mr. Mountzoures asked if an environmental impact study will be required for this
project? Mr. Smith replied it will not.

MOTION (1): Ms. Johnston moved that this Commission accepts
Bayview Landing Marina to be consistent with
the Harbor Management Plan. The Commission
has determined that the work described in Part 1
of this form and attachment is consistent with the
Harbor Management Plan. Seconded by Mr.
Schutt. Mr. Landers reclused himself from voting.
(4-0-0) Motion carried.

Discussion on Pending Application of Vincent Dowling, 295 Black Point Road,
Niantic. Keith Neilson of Docko, Inc. presented revisions to the new fixed pier at this
WW EAST LYME HARBOR MANAGEMENT/SHELLFISH COMMISSION

location. He showed areas where there are boulders. Mr. Landers asked if the piling is 90°
from the high tide line? Mr. Neilson replied at the top of the revetment it is 40’ back.
Reveted 25’ from the toe to the top. 60’ from the mean low water line. 80° from the high
tide line.

Mr. Landers asked if the two adjoining property owners have been notified? Mr. Neilson
agreed to approach the neighbors. He also recommended that the DEP Boating Division be
consulted with because of its location. Mr. Mountzoures stated DEP requires that adjacent
neighbors be notified and given an opportunity for comments. Mr. Kanter felt a hurricane
will come from the Southeast. He also felt there will be very little opportunity for the
applicant to use his boat there.

Mr. Schutt felt Mr. Neilson as done everything possible to have this dock survive but
ultimately something serious could happen because of its location. It would be in the
applicant’s best interests not to have his dock at this location. Mr. Neilson agreed to speak to
the applicant regarding concerns of this Commission.

Ms. Johnston asked what is the size of the pilings? Mr. Neilson replied 30’ Class B. The
soil will probably be 8”.

Ms. Johnston asked where will the seasonal decking be moved to? Mr. Neilson it will be
stored on site. Ms. Johnston suggested contacting the neighbors regarding this matter.

Ms. Johnston asked that DEP Boating Division comment on this project. She did not feel
a dock should be placed at that location. Mr. Neilson stated it is consistent with DEP
standards and has been reviewed by them. She would like to be assured that the Town of
East Lyme and the Harbor Management Commission will be liability free.

Mr. Landers referred to page 4-6 of the Harbor Management Plan. He suggested that the
Black Point Beach Association be made aware of this project prior to a determination of this
Commission.

Mr. Landers referred to page 4-8, 6b, page 4-14, a. and b., and p.4-20 of the Harbor
Management Plan.

Mr. Kanter stated it is up to the applicant to contact neighbors and report back to this
Commission. Mr. Neilson agreed to provide letters.

Mr. Kanter felt the structure should not be allowed to be derelict. The property owner
will need to remove it.

Mr. Hitchery felt prior to any approval it should be stated that the applicant is responsible
to take immediate steps to clear the area.

Mr. Kanter felt that anyone with experience on the water would understand a dock cannot
be built at that location. Mr. Mountzoures referred to the damage of Hurricane Carol.

Mr. Landers stated based on review of this plan we feel the applicant and his consultant
need to talk with the neighbors and the Black Point Beach Association and give them
information on this proposal. We also recommend that DEP consult with its Boating
Division regarding navigational hazards. If approved and DEP approves, the DEP should be
held responsible in the event of a storm.

Mr. Neilson stated at the end of the pier there is 42” internal wiring for a light mounted to
the post. Mr. Hitchery felt it should not be a bright light. Mr. Neilson replied it is generally
in the range of 25 watts.

Mr. Mountzoures asked if the location of this will be discussed with the Coast Guard.
Mr. Neilson stated it will be coordinated with the Coast Guard and Army Corps of Engineers.

Mr. Schutt felt fog would be a major hazard.

The Commission will wait for additional information from Mr. Neilson before any action
is taken on this application.

MOTION (2): Mr. Mountzoures moved to table any decision
on this application until such time as Mr. Neilson
provides additional information to this
Commission. Seconded by Ms. Johnston. (5-0)
Unanimous.

3. Adjournment

MOTION (3): Mr. Mountzoures moved to adjourn the Harbor Management/
Shellfish Commission Special Meeting of March 1,
2011 at 8:40 p.m. Seconded by Mr. Schutt. (5-0)

Unanimous.
