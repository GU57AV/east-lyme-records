3. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS
There were no public delegations.
4. REPORTS
A. Warden. There was no report.

B. Harbor Master. Mr. Hitchery reported over one-half of the 2010 mooring
permits have been renewed and new decals sent out. The letter sent to Thomas Faber
requesting that he remove his boat from Camp Niantic Shoreline was returned as
undeliverable and unable to forward.

Commission member Barbara Johnston arrived.
Mr. Kanter agreed to deliver the letter to Mr. Faber’s home.

C. Treasurer. Mr. Dinsmore reported we have received $2700 for mooring
permit fees. The Recording Secretary submitted a bill for 6.75 hours of secretarial work
from February 15 to March 2, 2011. Invoices have been received from the
Waterford/East Lyme Shellfish Commission (WELSCO).

A Board of Finance Hearing on this Commission’s budget will be held on March
23 at 6:30 p.m.

Mr. Landers reported that Public Works would like to dispose of the abandoned
27° boat stored at the Town Garage since October 2009.

The Harbor Master said that he spoke to the owner of record in 2009, and she has
no interest in the boat. Mr. Kanter stated in the future the Commission should have a
procedure to follow in regard to abandoned boats. Mr. Hitchery agreed and suggested
that Town Counsel be consulted.

MOTION (3): Mr. Kanter moved to approve the bill dated March
14, 2011 from Watermark Marine Services, Inc.
in the amount of $350 for hauling the abandoned
boat to the Town garage in October 2009 and
an additional $250 for the cost of hauling the abandoned boat to the
transfer station for disposal; total authorized funding
not to exceed $600. Seconded by Mr. Mountzoures.
In Favor: Landers, Dinsmore, Kanter, Schutt, Mountzoures.
In Opposition: Johnston. (5-1-0) Motion carried.

John Birmingham, ex officio, arrived.

MOTION (4): Mr. Schutt moved to approve the Treasurer’s
Report. Seconded by Mr. Mountzoures.
Mr. Dinsmore abstained from voting. (5-0-1)
Motion carried.

D. Ex-Officio. Mr. Birmingham reported the Board of Selectmen decided to stay
with Ledge Light Health District for one year. Ledge Light Health District has reduced
its cost and it will try to have better communication.

Mr. Kanter reported the Waterford/East Lyme Shellfish Commission sent a letter
to the Board of Selectmen on February 23, 2011, a copy of which is attached to the
Minutes. Mr. Calkins asked WELSCO to remain with Ledge Light Health District.

Mr. Landers did not feel at this time there was enough information to make a
change.

A. Correspondence.

1. March 3, 2011 letter from DEP to Jack Sanga of 4 Hill Road approving
his permit application.

2. March 13, 2011 letter from DEP to Knut Aarsand tentatively approving
his application.

Aquaculture and Laboratory, Management Plan and Memorandum of Understanding and

Kanter and Mr. Landers will schedule a meeting to review the MOU.

4. An email was received from Pat Kelley, Co-Chairman of WELSCO,
informing this Commission that WELSCO is considering a proposal to allow commercial
harvesting of shellfish in the Niantic River.

B. Discussion of Dowling Dock Application at 285 Black Point Road. Ms.
Johnston distributed photos of Black Point during last Thursday’s storm.

She spoke to a neighbor who was not aware of plans for a dock. She indicated
that the Black Point Beach Association was not aware of plans for a dock.

MOTION (5): Mr. Dinsmore moved to table discussion of the Dowling
Dock Application at 285 Black Point Road until this
Commission hears back from the Consulting
Engineer. Seconded by Mr. Schutt. (6-0-0) Unanimous.

7. COMMENTS FROM OTHER COMMISSION MEMBERS.
Mr. Dinsmore requested an update of the Contact List.

Association and report on it at the next regularly scheduled meeting of this Commission.
