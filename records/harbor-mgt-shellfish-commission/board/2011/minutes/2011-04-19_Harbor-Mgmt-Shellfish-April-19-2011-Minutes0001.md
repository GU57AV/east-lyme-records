Mr. Birmingham arrived. Mr, Birmingham was asked to give his report.

Mr. Birmingham asked for an update on commercial clamming in the Niantic River. Mr.
Landers informed him that a_ proposal has been submitted to the Waterford/East Lyme Shellfish
Commission (WELSCO), which has exclusive jurisdiction for shellfishing matters within the
Niantic River (both Waterford and East Lyme). Mr. Birmingham asked if this will involve
leasing bottom areas for shellfishing?_ Mr. Kanter (co-chair of WELSCO) replied it will not. R
& B wants to harvest clams in the river. They will dredge for clams mostly in closed areas of the
river and as a benefit the towns will receive one-third of all harvested clams to go into
recreational areas. The next meeting of WELSCO is Thursday night, Mr, Pat Kelly (co-chair of
WELSCO) has asked East Lyme and Waterford town counsels for input. The agreement can be
renegotiated annually by WELSCO.

Mr. Birmingham reported that he listened to a program which alluded that the Niantic
River is cleaner than it has ever been. Mr. Schutt asked what is their definition of clean? Mr,
Kanter stated in some ways the river is cleaner. Mr. Landers added that according to the State
the river is impaired because in some areas shellfish cannot be harvested for human
