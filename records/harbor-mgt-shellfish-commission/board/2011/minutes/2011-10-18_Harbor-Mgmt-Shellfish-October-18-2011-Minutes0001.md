D. Ex-officio .

Mr. Birmingham asked for an update on the Dowling application. Mr. Landers stated this item
was removed from the agenda since we have not heard from the applicant or his agent. They
were asked to canvas the neighbors to determine whether or not they were agreeable to it. If they
come back with the necessary responses we asked of them, this matter will again be reviewed.

Mr. Birmingham stated after Hurricane Irene there was a lot of silt in the Connecticut River. He
asked for an update on the condition of the Niantic River after the storm. There were reports of
some boats dragging froin moorings or anchors and some minor dock damage, but overall,
damage to property and the environment was minimal. It could have been a lot worse. Mr.
Murin did not notice any signs of silt.

B. Discussion of Niantic River NO WAKE ZONE/SPEED LIMITS

Discussion focused on increased patrols and additional outreach and education. Plans are in
place for Waterford and East Lyme to perform additional patrols during peak hours of
recreational use on the river. Mr. Murin suggested putting up a sign (see attached sample) to
educate the boating comraunity. Appropriate locations for posting such signs will be explored
with the Coast Guard and Amtrak.

C. Discussion of Shellfish MOU. Mr. Landers had nothing new to report on the Shellfish MOU.
Ledge Light is working on it, and as soon as he hears more the Commission will be informed.
