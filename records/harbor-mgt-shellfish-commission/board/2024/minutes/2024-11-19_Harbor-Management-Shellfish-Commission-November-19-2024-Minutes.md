   2. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS
      A. DOCKO-Quarry Dock Road proposal
         Tim DeBartolomeo, P.E., DOCKO for the applicants, described the proposal for a dock as
         attached to five properties with four applicants. The property the dock is located on is owned
         by Barbara Ezikovich with a shared right of way to other properties. He described the
         proposed dock as a4' x48' access, ramp of 5'xl0', access ramp 4'x30' and al0'x 40' float.
         The float will accommodate 2 boats and a rowing shell. Pillions will be able to accommodate
         two more boats if needed in the future. DEEP has approved the project.
         Robert Linden, 82 Quarry Dock Rd, applicant, said all the neighbors are fine with the
         proposed dock and that the required notices went out to abutters within 500 ft of the project.
         MOTION: (Allen/iVlanwaring) the Commission finds the project consistent with the
         Harbor Management Plan. Vote: APPROVED unanimously.
      B. Delegations from the public
         Peter Harris, 5 S. Ridge Rd., WELSCO informed the Commission that they are down one
         warden and inquired about the possibility of the Harbor Master accompanying the Warden on
         the water quality checks during the winter. J. Spang, R. Johnson and J. Allen volunteered,
         S. Dinsmore asked if WELSCO has decided to combine the river and bay shellfish permits.
         P. Hanis said WELSCO had concerns about the MOU's not being up to date, lack of signage
         and the fact that there are so few permits issued for the bay there is no benefit for WELSCO.
         He said WELSCO does not patrol the bay.
         John Hughs, 52 Niantic River Rd, Waterford, says he cleans out the fishing line retainers in
         Waterford and East Lyme. He said the ones in East Lyme are cumbersome because people
         put their garbage in them due to lack of garbage disposals nearby. He wants East Lyme to
         take over cleaning them out. J. Deeble will discuss with Park and Rec and the Public Works
          Dept.
