      B. Docko Application-Chris Manwaring
         Keith Nielson, Docko, presented an application for Chris Manwaring, Old Black Point Rd.
         The plan calls for a crossing of approximately 200ft of marsh. There will be a walkway
         across the marsh. It will be 2' wide, 2' above the marsh sediments. The posts will be
         augured, 5' apart with cross beams across the posts. The grading will be either galvanized
         steel or fiber glass. His preference is the fiber glass as it does not rust, and the fiberglass can
         be in colors and would be easier to see at night. The walkway will have a single rail, but the
         drawings show two, it depends on the preference of DEEP. At the end of the walkway is a
         general permit dock. At the end of the walkway will be a pier I0-I2' long with a24'ramp
         landing on a 30 sq ft landing float with a 100 sq ft floating dock for boating access. There are
         tie up pilings around the floating dock. The plans include a solar pack and battery to allow
         for operation of a boat lift. Utilities will be strung to the underside of the walkway, both
         electric and possibly water.
         Construction will be in the winter so there will be no osprey nest disttrbance. The owner has
         a couple of small boats and a24' motorboat.
         MOTION: (Landers/Allen) to accept the Manwaring proposal with a preference for the
         fiberglass grading. Vote: APPROVED unanimous. S. Dinsmore signed the document.
