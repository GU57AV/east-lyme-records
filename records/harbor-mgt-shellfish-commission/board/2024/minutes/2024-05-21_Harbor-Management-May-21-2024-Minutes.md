  5. OLD BUSINESS
      A. Small Harbor Improvement Projects Program (SHIPP Grants)
         S. Dinsmore informed the members that S. Spang spoke to the Town Planner and the only
         grant he had was for the upgrade of the boat launch at Grand Street but it may not have been
         for a SHIP Grant.
         S. Dinsmore spoke to Steve Turner, previous owner of Lillians, about a transient mooring at
         the Hole in the Wall Beach. He was informed that they had a grant writer who could write a
         grant for a feasibility study of a transient dingy dock.
         S. Dinsmore suggested that the groin at the Hole in the Wall could be covered and used as a
         dingy dock. R. Kanter said he would be opposed to a dock in the bay as it is not a good
         anchorage due to the swells. He was concerned about the maintenance and who would be
         responsible. He said people who use transient moorings would have to be aware they exist in
         the town and the information should be available to the boating public.
         Permission from the town would have to be given before the grant was applied for.
         D. Landers said there should be a study done to see if this is something the town wants or the
         economic benefit of a dingy dock for transient boaters.
         S. Dinsmore will continue discussing a feasibility study with Main Street Niantic.
      B. Shellfish Management Plan-no report
      C. Expanding Open Areas to West side of Black Point
         R. Kanter said the subject of opening the west side of Black Point was discussed at a
         WELSCO meeting. It was reported that Alyssa Dragan DABA, said there are too many
         people bathing and boating to open that area for shell fishing during the summer. The
         problem with allowing shell fishing in the winter is that the Shellfish Wardens do not want to
         go around the point due to adverse weather conditions. This leaves the shoulder seasons, Fall
         and Spring. He said that if it was to open during the Winter it is possible that a volunteer in
         the area could do the testing and coordinate with WELSCO as they are the ones who send the
         samples to the lab.
         Paul Grady says he can see people shell fishing in the area now without it being formally
         open. R. Kanter said if it is an issue for residents, they should call DEEP.
         M. Berger said there is very limited access to the area.
         R. Kanter said there is more to the approval of opening the area than the water testing
         quality. The number of moorings, swimmers etc. affect whether shell fishing is allowed by
          DABA
          S. Dinsmore informed the public that besides the moorings and swimmers, there are homes
          on the islands which have septic systems that are leaching into the area.
          If volunteers are allowed to take water samples, should they be insured? Can shoulder
          seasons be allowed for shell fishing? Both are questions that need to be answered.
