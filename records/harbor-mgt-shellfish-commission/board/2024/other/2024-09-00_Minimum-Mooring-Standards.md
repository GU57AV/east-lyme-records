          In a penetrable bottom using 3:1 scope based upon high water depth and winds to 64 knots
                           Boat       Pyramid      Bottom      Top       Pendant
                           Length     Anchor       Chain       Chain

                          <13 feet   70 lbs         1/2 inch    3/8 inch   1/2 inch
                          13-18      135            1/2         3/8        1/2
                          19-21      200            1/2         3/8        5/8

                          28-32      500            5/8         1/2        1
                          33-39      700            3/4         1/2        1
                          40-50      1000           3/4         5/8        1

           1. Bottom chain length: at least 1 1/2 times high tide depth
           2. Top chain length: equal to high tide depth.
           3. Pendant: 2 1/2 times the distance from the bow chock to the water, plus the distance from the chock
                to the mooring cleat. Adequate chafe guards should be used.
           4. All shackles and swivels should be of equal or larger size than the chain used, properly seized with
           nylon ties. Do not use dissimilar metals such as stainless steel wire because of galvanic action.
           5. If your buoy has a galvanized rod through it, the pendant should be shackled into the top chain below the
           buoy so the strain is not carried by the rod.
           6. Buoys must be white with a blue band around the circumference and display the annual permit decal and
           weight of the mooring anchor. Name of vessel or permittee on the buoy is optional.
           7. Moorings must be pulled and visually inspected every third year by an approved inspector.
           8. Winter sticks must be white, marked with permit number, visible at least two feet above the surface at all
           times, and should be removed by May 31. No wooden winter sticks allowed.

           These are minimum requirements and cannot ensure vessel security, especially during severe coastal storms.
           Heavier tackle than the minimum is recommended. Neither the Harbormaster nor the Town of East Lyme
           assumes liability for personal injury or property damage caused by the use of mooring tackle which meet or
           exceed these standards. Mooring owners are responsible for damages caused by failure of their mooring.

revised August 2024
