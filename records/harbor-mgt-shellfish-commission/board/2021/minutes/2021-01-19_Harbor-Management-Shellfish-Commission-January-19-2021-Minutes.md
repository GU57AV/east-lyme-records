6. NEW BUSINESS
  A. Habitat Restoration-Habitat Studies
       S. Dinsmore referenced the paper he sent to members by Tim Visel concerning oyster
      restoration. Dinsmore said that before the Pattagansett River became silted in it was a prolific
      oyster area. It was his opinion that the Fairhaven Bridge and the train bridge restricted water flow
      which contributes to the, "black mayonnaise" of the river bottom. S. Dinsmore said there have
      been many studies of the Niantic River and the Commission should make the effort to gather all
      the information and store it in a central place. He has spoken to the head librarian and she will
      make room at the library. R. Kanter has information he will gather to submit. D. Landers stated
      that Dominion publishes a report of the river every year which could be included in the file.
       S. Dinsmore would like to see the Commission work towards improving the water quality in the
      Niantic River. The use of helical moorings was suggested. S. Dinsmore suggested there may be
      support and grants for dredging some of the black mayonnaise from the Pattagansett River to
      make conditions more favorable for oysters.
