      J. Hitchery reported that he met with the First Selectman and the disputed moorings will not be

      He had conversation with Rob Avena, Waterford town attorney and they discussed the 1839 act
      creating East Lyme, portions of Waterford and Lyme, which has the boundary between East
      Lyme and Waterford as the main channel of the river. They agreed that the main channel of the
      upper river from Sandy Point to the Route 1 bridge is in the middle of the river. They also
      discussed the difficulty of identifuing the main channel in the shallower lower river. For 182
      years the boundary in the Niantic River is in the center of the river, not the channel.

      The deputy harbor Master asked the Commission to discuss the need for boats to carry insurance

    The Commission discussed the pros and cons of a requirement. R. Kanter thought the insurance
