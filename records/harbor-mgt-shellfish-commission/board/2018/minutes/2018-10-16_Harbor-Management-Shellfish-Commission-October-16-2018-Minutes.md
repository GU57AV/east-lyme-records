B. WELSCO Aquaculture Policy Statement
S. Dinsmore reminded the members that J. Hitchery and D. MacKenzie will be
representing the Commission on the WELSCO subcommittee. He stated the
policy as written is not acceptable to the Commission.
Peter Harris, chairman of WELSCO informed the Commission that so far he is the
only representative from WELSCO to the subcommittee. He has invited the
Waterford Harbor Management Commission to be part of the subcommittee but
they have declined the offer.
S. Dinsmore explained the Commission’s process and responsibility when an
aquaculture application comes before the Commission:

1. WELSCO approves the application
2. The application is sent to Army Corps of Engineers for review
3. The application is sent to DEEP
4. The application is sent to Bureau of Aquaculture
5. A form is sent to the East Lyme Harbor Management to
determine if the application is/is not consistent with their
Harbor Management Plan

Dear Commissioners:

It has come to our attention that WELSCO is under the false impression that they are not
required to provide your commission with the opportunity to review and comment on
policies and/or proposals that affect the harbor management area under your

purview. This impression comes from an email that WELSCO Chair Peter Harris sent to
East Lyme legal counsel asking the following question:

“Does East Lyme have a similar statute that states the EL Harbor Management
must approve or deny any revisions to the WELSCO Aquaculture Policy Statement?”
Emphasis added.

While it is true that the harbor management commission does not have to “approve or
deny,” the harbor commission does have the authority to review, comment and issue a
determination of consistency with the harbor management plan. Any commission
proposing to affect property in, on or contiguous to the harbor must forward their proposal
to your commission and you have 35 days to render an opinion. Should you not render an
opinion within that 35 day window, your lack of response will be considered concurrence.

Please see EL Town Ordinances 9 and 10 on pages 198-200 https://eltownhall.com/wp-
content/uploads/2008/09/CHARTER-ORDINANCES-AND-SPECIAL-LAWS-2014. pdf
