1. Call to Order: S. Dinsmore called the meeting to order at 7:30 PM

2. Approval of Minutes of Meeting of June 18, 2018
MOTION: (Landers/Kanter) to approve the June 18, 2018 minutes as presented:

Vote: Approved

3. Public Delegations
Terry Lineburger, (Waterford) expressed concern about the amount of aquaculture gear

that would be visible in area 2 marked out by oysterman Londregan. Chairman
Dinsmore informed her that the Commission can comment on applications only in
regard to consistency with our Harbor Management Plan. R. Kanter stated that there
was not yet an official application submitted to WELSCO, which has jurisdiction over

permitting.
4. Reports

A. Shellfish
R. Kanter reported the Niantic River is open for shell fishing.

B. Harbor Master
Harbor Master Dick Morris informed the Commission that the navigation system

on the boat was being repaired.

Deputy Harbor Master Berger, introduced Mr. Ron Johnson to the Commission
as a candidate for Harbor Master. He is a retired Wethersfield police officer and

has served as a shellfish warden and marine patrol officer.
Three nominees must be submitted to the Governor with a recommendation for

appointment.

East Lyme Harbor Management and Shellfish Commission. July 16, 2018
MOTION: (Kanter/Landers) to submit the following three nominees for
consideration to the Governor for the Harbor Master position; Joe Hitchery,
Marc Berger, and Ron Johnson with recommendation for appointment of Ron
Johnson.

Vote: Approved Unanimously

R. Kanter reported a winter stick on the East Lyme side of the Niantic River
opposite Sandy Point that should be moved closer to shore as it is a navigation
hazard.

J. Hitchery suggested pulling moorings #250 and #221 in the Saunders Point area
as they are no longer being used.

C. Treasurer
D. Landers informed the members of a $2141.70 invoice from Geoff Steadman
for Harbor Management Plan revision services in the previous fiscal year.

D. Ex-Officio :
P. Dagle informed the Commission of a parking ordinance passed by the Board of
Selectman. He also stated there were a number of resignations from various
Boards and Commissions.

E. Niantic River Aquaculture
R. Kanter informed the members that WELSCO’s scheduled meeting for this
Thursday has been rescheduled for the following Thursday (July 26, 2018) at the
Waterford Town Hall. It will be a joint meeting of WELSCO and Waterford Harbor
Management.

5. OLD BUSINESS

A. Harbor Management Plan-Update
S. Dinsmore stated copies of the final Plan was delivered to the First Selectman
for distribution. The Plan will be discussed by the Board of Selectmen at their
September 5, 2018 meeting. G. Steadman and S. Dinsmore will be attending the
meeting to answer any questions.

6. NEW BUSINESS

A. Correspondence:
A COP for a dock at Quarry Dock Road was received.

7. FINAL COMMENTS

Terry Lineburger (Waterford) informed the Commissioners of a case which was
decided in contradiction of state statute.
