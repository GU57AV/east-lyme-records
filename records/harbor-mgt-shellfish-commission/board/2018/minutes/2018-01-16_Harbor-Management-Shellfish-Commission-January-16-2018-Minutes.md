A. Shellfish

Kantor reported the shellfish areas are now open but had been closed
due to a heavy rain event. He spoke to a few shell fishermen and they
stated there are plenty of clams and oysters.

Kantor attended the annual State shellfish meeting. He stated it was very
productive with a lot of good information provided on aquaculture.

B. Harbor Master - no report

The members asked the Chairman to remind the Harbor Master to
provide the moorings spreadsheet.

C. Treasurer

D. Landers informed the members that there have been no bills paid or
submitted. He presented the Commission’s budget of $10,000 to the
Town.

D. Ex-Officio

P. Dagle stated the Board of Selectmen approved a STEAP Grant of
$500,000 for Cini Park upgrades which include portable bathrooms and
possibly a kayak dock for people who may have difficulty getting into and
out of their kayaks.

Funding in the amount of $70,000 from STEAP and an additional
$139,000 left over from the brown field funds for the Mobil Station
property was approved. There will be a Public Hearing at 7:00 PM on
January 17, 2018.

E. Niantic River Aquaculture Meeting-mediation meeting

OLD BUSINESS

There is a mediation meeting on February 2, 2018 at the Old Lyme
Marine Fisheries. D. Landers will represent the Commission.

A. Harbor Management Plan-Update

NEW BUSINESS

There has been no activity on the Plan in the last month. D. MacKenzie
will read the plan and provide his comments to the Chairman.
Chairman Dinsmore explained the process for the Plan to be
implemented.

P. Dagle strongly suggested the Commission review the proposed
ordinance and provide any comment associated with the Plan.
