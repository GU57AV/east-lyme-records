M. Berger stated that Ron Johnson has shown interest in the position and would
be a good candidate for Harbor Master. The Town would need to send three
names for consideration to the Governor’s office for an official appointment.

C. Treasurer
D. Landers stated that there are a couple of weeks remaining in the 2018 fiscal
year and any outstanding invoices need to be submitted before June 30, 2018. It
was suggested that funds be made available in the future for a part-time Harbor
Master administrative assistant.

D. Ex-Officio
P. Dagle informed the Commission of a Public Hearing and Town Meeting to
establish a tax abatement for emergency responders.

E. Niantic River Aquaculture
R. Kanter informed the members that WELSCO will be discussing and possibly
voting on their updated aquaculture policy at their next meeting. They will also
be discussing the Londregan application.

5. OLD BUSINESS

A. Harbor Management Plan-Update
S. Dinsmore stated the final Plan was at the printers and would be distributed to
the Board of Selectmen and the Town attorney. Any changes would come back
to the Commission and then a final copy would be printed and distributed. The
ordinance would go to a Public Hearing and Town Meeting.

6. NEW BUSINESS

A. Correspondence:
Wracklines magazine, published by UConn Sea Grant Program, received.

None.
