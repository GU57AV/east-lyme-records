NEW BUSINESS
A. Suitable Areas for Aquaculture in the Niantic River

A 2002 WELSCO map was presented of the Niantic River with the following areas
identified for possible aquaculture:

e Area #1: near Camp Niantic (East Lyme)

e Area #2: along bar near RT 156 west of the kayak launch float (East Lyme)

e Area #3: Circle northeast of the original proposed area (Waterford)

© Area #4: Off Avenues 2 through 7 (Waterford)

e Area #5: Near Benvenuti’s north of Avenue 9 (Waterford)

e Area #6: Off Oswegatchie Hills across from Comfort Point (East Lyme)

P. Harris stated the WELSCO plan would have to be revised but they are looking
for areas that could be used for experimental aquaculture.

Chairman Dinsmore relayed for the record Commissioner Murin’s opinion that
the Niantic River was not suitable for aquaculture.

D. Landers suggested the possibility of using the upper Niantic River during the
winter months, moving down to the lower river and then out in the bay during
summer months, and then reverse the process in the fall. The upper river has
less recreational activity that lends itself to aquaculture. Commissioners agreed
it would be a good idea to go out on the Niantic River in the next few weeks to
look at the proposed areas.

P. Harris stated that the purpose of the request is that in the future all three
commissions (WESLSCO, ELHM/SC and Waterford HMSC) will meet with
Waterford Town Attorney to consider the areas recommended as being most
suitable for aquaculture. He stated it would be good for the Chairman to come to
the joint meeting with an area(s) that the Commission agrees on.

P. Harris stated that their attorney has drafted a WELSCO application form for

possible aquaculture. Once filled out the Town Clerk would certify and terms of
any lease agreement would come after submittal of the application.
