D. Sea Grant restoration project discussion
The Commission reviewed and discussed the communication from UCONN Sea
Grant. The goal of this project is to identify and prioritize areas in Long Island
Sound that are most suitable for shellfish restoration. The project will involve an
analysis of environmental and human use data, as well as expert input review by
many individuals and organizations. The letter went to all shellfish commissions
to identify such areas in Long Island Sound. A map of the waters in and around
the East Lyme shore and rivers was sent to the Commission.
The members discussed various areas that may be suitable for restoration. It was
the consensus of the members that an area in the Pattagansett Estuary,
specifically, south of Griswold Island and extending eastward up towards the
entry of the Pattagansett River would be suitable. The members wanted to add
restoration of scallops in the Niantic River as a priority. The response needs to be
consistent with the Commission’s Shell Fish Management Plan. S. Dinsmore was
given direction to create a response to the request from Sea Grant.

E. Correspondence
The members discussed the letter from the Town Attorney dated 12/18/2018 in
re authority for issuing leases for shell fishing in EL waters. Hitchery noted the
letter did not address the critical issue of municipal v. WELSCO jurisdiction to
lease areas specifically in the Niantic River and he requested that a legal opinion
regarding the Niantic River jurisdiction be provided. Chairman Dinsmore
informed the members that the First Selectman has asked that the Commission
hold off on any actions related to leases in the Niantic River until after the Public
Safety Building issue has been resolved.
