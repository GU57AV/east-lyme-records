B. Niantic River Watershed Letter on Waterford Solar Farm
D. Landers, member of the Niantic River Watershed Committee informed the
Commission that the letter to the State Siting Council, addressing the proposed
Solar Farm, will not be sent from the Niantic River Watershed Committee but
instead will be submitted by a member of the Committee as a private citizen. D.
Landers stated the deadline to comment was today (8/20/2018). He informed
the members that the site chosen was a 100-acre triangle between Interstate
395 and 95. The site will be clear cut of all trees. The Niantic River Watershed
Committee is concerned because the area is at the headwaters of the Niantic
River and could have a negative impact on the river. Letters have been sent to
State Senator Formica, State Representative Holly Cheeseman and State
Representative Kathleen McCurdy. The Town of Waterford has also sent a letter

7. FINAL COMMENTS
Terry Lineburger (Waterford) informed the Commissioners that in May 2018, a
Public Hearing on the “Policy” was held in Waterford and the public
overwhelmingly was opposed to type II aquaculture gear in ten acres of the
river. She informed the Commission that there was a traffic study of the river
but it only counted traffic going under the bridge. It did not take into
consideration the volume of recreational traffic that stays in the river and does
not go through the bridge.
