E. Niantic River Aquaculture
D. Landers updated the Commissioners on the mediation meeting hosted by
Janice Duchane in Old Lyme by Marine Fisheries. In attendance were:
e Army Corps of Engineers
e Sea Grant
e Representatives from East Lyme and Waterford
Dept. of Aquaculture
e T.Londregan
It was the consensus of the meeting that a small group of interested residents
should form a group to look at the Niantic River in a holistic way. They discussed
commercial aquaculture in the Niantic River, suitable areas for aquaculture, and
the permitting process. D. Landers thought it was a productive meeting.

The Commission then discussed the proposed revised application submitted to
WELSCO by T. Londregan. The proposed application is for areas designated 2 and
5. Only area 2 is in East Lyme. The Chairman reminded the members that the
only input the Commission has is to say is if the application is consistent or not
consistent with our Harbor Management Plan.

WELSCO will eventually hold a public hearing on the application. They will not
make a decision until after their review of the aquaculture plan is finalized.

The proposed area 2 runs parallel to Route #156 east from the Cini Park entrance
(about 630’ west of the existing kayak dock) south of marker 7, and at a
minimum 125’ from Route #156 and 125’ from the federal channel.

Londregan stated there would be no gear outside the proposed area. He
described the gear he would be using and that he may scatter seed on the
bottom outside of the proposed area. He will mainly be producing seed product
in this area.

The Commission stated it would be helpful if the applicant could place marker

buoys in the water at the corners of the proposed area so they can get a better
visual idea of where the aquaculture will be taking place.
