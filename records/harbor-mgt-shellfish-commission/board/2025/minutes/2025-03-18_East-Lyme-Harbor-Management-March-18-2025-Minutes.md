      Note Tim DeBartolomeo of Docko, Inc., who is representing Bob Emery, detailed an application for a
     structure on the west side of the Niantic River. He noted some of the following:
     The proposal is for a 83-foot-long pier.
     A 30-foot ramp.
     A 20-by-8-foot floating dock.
     The boat ramp will be situated between two existing docks.
     The design maintains separation from property boundaries; the dock is oriented to come up on one side
     then reaching the middle of the property.
     Remains within Army Corps guidelines (25 feet from the default).
     Awaiting consultation form approval to proceed with filing.
     They did receive a request from northern neighbors to reposition boat lifts to the south side. Determined
     impractical due to spatial requirements (25 feet separation not possible).

      Note Mr. Dinsmore observed that the documents are fairly standard, and Mr. Landers noted they can't sign
     off on this for WELSCO.

      Note The Commission inquired about the presence of eel grass and Mr. DeBartolomeo explained there is
     none and that they had an eel grass survey done.

      Decision MOTION (2)
     Mr. Kanter moved that the Emery Dock Project is consistent with the Harbor Management Plan.
     Mr. Allen seconded the motion.
     Motion carried, approved unanimously.

4. Reports
  4-1. Treasurer

      Note Mr. Manwaring had very little to report but questions were raised about missing invoices related to
     boat management. He inquired about the Harbor Master boat and the Commission discussed the boat and
     some of the following key points were highlighted:
     The potential swap of the current Harbor Master’s boat with the police boat.
     The current boat is larger, part of FEMA condition.
     Police prefer larger, more durable boat.
     Harbor Master prefers smaller, more manageable boat.
     WELSCO Warden (Rich Miller) has been conducting checks.
     Whether the boat should be available to the warden.
     First Selectman to be involved in decision.
     Harbor Master boat currently at police station awaiting recommissioning.
     Billing for boat maintenance to be reviewed.

      Note Mr. Dinsmore provided a detailed account of the Harbor Master boat acquisition, and Mr. Hughes
     agreed that although an excellent boat, it is not suitable for its current application.

     Task
     The Commission discussed how the Police want the boat since it is faster, and Mr. Hughes said he will
     broach this topic with his group (the Waterford Harbor Management Commission) as well as the
     Waterford First Selectman.
