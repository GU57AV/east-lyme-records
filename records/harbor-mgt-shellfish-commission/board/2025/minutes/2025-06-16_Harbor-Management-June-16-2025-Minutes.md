   B. Black Point Beach Club Association SHIPP Proposal
         Bill Bayne, 2 Osprey Rd., Black Point Beach Club is looking for directions on applying for a
         SHIPP Grant.
         He said there are two areas of waterfront: eastern and western. The eastern waterfront has
         existing assets which are in need of repair or replacement. The western waterfront, the club is
