D. River Aquaculture Determination (discussed earlier)
   S. Dinsmore said he asked J. Deeble to get him on the agenda for the BOS to discuss and ask the
   BOS to make a determination concerning who has the authority to approve aquaculture leases in the
   Niantic River. It was his opinion that WELSCO has that authority.
   S. Dinsmore said a few years ago T. Londregan wanted to put Type2 Aquaculture in various places
   in the river. Word came down that WELSCO did not have the authority to issue leases.
   R. Kanter has been on WESLSCO for 27 years, prior to Londregan issue there was never any doubt
   that WELSCO had the authority to issue permits. He does not believe the BOS has the right to
   deeide who has the authority to permit aquaculture in the river. He said the determination needs to be
   made by the courts when an applicant applies and was told no, then it would go to the courts.
   D. Landers said their Harbor Management plan is clear with respect to structures in the river on the
   EL side. He said there is a long history of WELSCO being involved in shell fishing in the river.
   John Huges, 52 Niantic River Road, Waterford Harbor Management Commission Chairman, said at
   one time WELSCO tried to give a lease to Londregan and it was determined by lawyers that
   WELSCO is not a leasing agency. R. Kanter disagrees with the opinion.
   S. Dinsmore informed members that there were a few people in the past that had aquaculture permits
   with gear in the river.
   Richard Miller, Deputy Harbor Master, said he understands that both WELSCO and Waterford
   Harbor Management Commission would have the authority to permit shell fishing for the bottom of
   the river but when it came to structures, they had to get a permit from the Office of Long Island
   Sound as well as various local and state commissions.
