   2. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS
         Melissa Root, Port Authority Liaison
      ^. M. Root introduced herself and said she has been with the Port Authority for 10 years. The
         SHIP Grant is in its 5th round and has appropriated 5 million annually by the state legislature.
         Last year they expanded who is eligible for grants which now includes private entities and
         large harbors. Some of the things the grant can be used for are docks, infrastructure,
         dredging, ramps, etc.
         The grant is a reimbursement grant and requires a20o/o match.
         She said the best-case scenario for the next round to be approved will be in January.
         Representatives from the Black Point Beach Association said they are working on two
         projects for submittal-repair and fortification of a groin and pier and an access way with a
         dock and kayak landing.
         M. Root suggested they make an appointment for a pre meeting. She confirmed that if a
         SHIP grant is awarded for a project, then it has to allow public access because the grant is
         taxpayer funded. Also, if a project is awarded then the applicant would have a better chance
         for a follow up grant award so the project can move forward.
         She said there is a rubric and municipalities get priority over private entities
