EAST LYME PUBLIC LIBRARY Sept at 2014 AT Z.YE AM(i

MINUTES of the BOARD of TRUSTEES MEETING
September 12, 2016 at 7:30 p.m. EAST LYME TOWN CLERK

Members Present: T. Attanasio, T. Borden, P. Carey, H. Cheeseman, M. Dunphy, A. Fisher, M. Helms, J.
Hoye, L. Mcllhenny, A. Mishra, R. Morrill, S. Moss, R. Patmore, J. Peters, C. Peterson, B. Skinner, L.
Timothy, and M. Walsh. Also present was guest, Richard Waterman.

Call to Order: President Holly Cheeseman called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m. Patricia Carey was
welcomed as a new member to the board.

Delegations from the Public: Richard Waterman, retired East Lyme teacher and current member of the East
Lyme Library Foundation Board, presented a Strategic Plan for the Expansion of the East Lyme Town Library.
Mr. Waterman prepared the plan himself, explained the various components to reconfigure the existing space,
and passed out his plan to each member. Part of his handout was a letter to Holly Cheeseman stating that he
would like to become a member of the Board of Trustees. No action was taken on the Strategic Plan nor on his
request to become part of the Board. The Strategic Plan will be kept as reference to peruse when the time is
expand is eminent.

Secretary’s Minutes: Minutes from the meeting of July 11, 2016 were mailed to each trustee. Creig Peterson
moved that the minutes be accepted. Seconded by Marilyn Dunphy. Motion passed with abstentions from
Patricia Carey and Mary Walsh.

Treasurer’s Report: The Treasurer’s Report (for two months ending August 31, 2016) was mailed prior to
tonight’s meeting. Lisa Timothy said that the percentage of the budget figures are where they should be at this
time, even the line item for Materials. When the Materials line item was cut, it was decided that any overage
would be picked up by monies from the Foundation and Annual Fund. Loretta McIlhenny moved that the
Treasurer’s Report be accepted. Seconded by Rosemary Patmore. Motion passed. Lisa stated that it is
becoming more evident that the current accounting software, Peachtree, has reached its limits and it is time to
switch to Quick Books. Quick Books has been purchased and Lisa will be attending a Quick Books workshop.

Director’s Report: Lisa received two thank you notes from Dave Jacobs and Lois Hobby which she passed
around the table for all to read.
Lisa gave her report as follows:

e The 37" Annual Fund Drive is in progress with $17,000 in donations so far. Our new web page has the
capacity to accept donations online via PayPal. The Annual Report of the library is currently being
printed.

e The Circulation Desk will soon accept credit and debit cards to pay for fines and other fees, This will
cost the library $35 per month, but the patron appeal and increase in payment rates will offset this fee.

¢ As of September 1%, the library has two new databases. The first is Lynda.com, which is a website that
offers thousands of instructional videos and classes on technology and business skills. The other new
database, JobsNow, offers employment assistance and help for those seeking to change their careers.
These were announced in the latest newsletter.

e A flyer on the Association of Connecticut Library Boards conference was sent out with the mailing.
This will be held Friday, November 4 at the University of Hartford. Lisa asked that anyone interested
contact her or Holly.

e There is now a new 10 minute parking spot in front of the building designated for dropping off books at
the library. An enthusiastic round of applause was given to Marilyn Dunphy who is the person
Tesponsible for this new change. Marilyn will look into having the handicapped icon removed from this

spot=~

Lisa announced that the first Open House which was held on August 13" was a success with about 30
people who came for the “behind the scenes” tour of the library. A highlight of that day is that a new
family to this area was issued library cards that day. There was also a well received reception to thank
the library volunteers in August.

Safety notebooks which were developed by the town safety committee and the library have been
distributed to and reviewed by all departments in the library.

One chronic homeless and mentally ill person is seen at the library most days. He has been evaluated by
social workers from the local homeless shelter and it has been determined that he is not a risk to himself
or others, so no action will be taken.

Lisa is considering a grant application to the Eastern Connecticut Community Foundation to hire a
strategic planner.

The Storywalk at Peretz Park is scheduled to open in one week.

President’s Update: Holly mentioned that she will be attending the conference in November and that either
Lisa or Tara will attend as well.

Committee Reports:

Administrative/policy — Nothing to report.

Personnel: - Nothing to report

Public Relations: - June Hoye is going to pursue two articles for inclusion in the Lyme Times. One will
be on the two new databases and the other will focus on the museum passes that the library offers.
Nominating: - Nothing to report.

Budget: - Nothing to report.

Publications Committee — Nothing to report about the new book on the history of the Correctional
Institute in Niantic at this time.

Executive: - Nothing to report.

Qld Business: C-Car delivery system continues to recover. There is no backlog of materials at the East Lyme
Library, but several librarys still have backlogs. All librarians are keeping an eye on the State Library and the
delivery system. The state has been paying for this delivery system. Lisa said we are doing “the best that we

can

”

New Business. Regarding the application for e-rate refunds for internet access - the filtering of computers, if
we went ahead with it, that would mean that all computers (including those used by staff) would be filtered.
This would not be productive.

Date of next meeting: Monday, November 14, 2016, 7:30 p.m.

There being no further business, Creig Peterson moved that the meeting be adjourned at 8:38 pm. June Hoye
seconded the motion. Motion passed.

Respectfully submitted,

Molly A. Helms, Secretary
