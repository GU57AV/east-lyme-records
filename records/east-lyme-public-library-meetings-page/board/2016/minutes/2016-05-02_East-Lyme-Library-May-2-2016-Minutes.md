EAST LYME PUBLIC LIBRARY
MINUTES of the BOARD of TRUSTEES MEETING

May 2, 2016 at 7:30 p.m. “a FILED IN EAST LYME

CONNEC TIENT

Present: T. Attanasio, T. Borden, H. Cheeseman, A. Doak, M. Dunphy, M. Helms, L. Hobby, J : Hoye,
D. Jacobs, L. McIlhenny, R. Morrill, S. Moss, R. Patmore, J. Peters, B. Skinner, L. Timothy, and

Corinne Weber.

Call to Order: President Holly Cheeseman called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m.

Delegations from the Public: None present

Secretary’s Minutes: Minutes from the meeting of March 29, 2016 were mailed to each trustee. June Hoye
moved that the minutes be accepted. Seconded by Dave Jacobs. Motion passed.

Treasurer’s Report: The Treasurer’s Report (for ten months ending April 30, 2016) was mailed prior to
tonight’s meeting and the Annual Fund Drive Report through April, 2016 was distributed at the meeting. Lisa
Timothy said that all budget figures are where they should be at this time. Marilyn Dunphy asked for an
explanation of the Transient Gift Fund. Lisa explained that every year the interest from the Eastern Connecticut
Endowment accrues and can either be reinvested or spent. During last year’s trustee meetings, there was
discussion that this money should be spent, rather than reinvested. Therefore, this year that interest appears as
Transient Gift Fund and was used toward the purchase of computers. Dave Jacobs moved that the Treasurer’s
Report be accepted. Seconded by Loretta McIlhenny. Motion passed.

Director’s Report: Lisa gave her report as follows:

¢ Since our last meeting, Lisa discussed the cuts made to the 2017 Library Budget at a hearing of the
Town’s Finance Committee. There have been no further cuts made to this budget. Since the original
cuts made to the budget could impact Sunday openings, Lisa mentioned that she is working very hard to
insure that we do not close on Sundays, and that she will use the Annual Fund for materials. Since the
health insurance coverage has not been finalized; when it is, the costs could impact the projected budget.

e The interlibrary book transit system still has issues to be worked out. All libraries in the state use this
method of transporting material, and several of those libraries still have bins waiting for delivery. Lisa
said that the library directors are in communication on this problem and hope to have it resolved in the
not too distant future.

e The trustees will have five vacancies as of the July meeting. Both Dave Jacobs and Lois Hobby are
scheduled to rotate off the Board in July. Our three high school members are all expected to return next
year. The Board plans to offer one of the positions to Abe Fisher (as discussed at the last meeting), so
that will leave the Board with four vacancies.

e Lisa and Tara are beginning a more intensive evaluation process for the eleven full time staff and
nineteen part time staff. This process is in preparation for a strategic planning process.

¢ The 36" Annual Fund Drive continues with good results. This year’s fund drive has received $1,000
more since the last report. The mailers for the 37" Annual Fund Drive will feature the library’s new
logo and it will be mailed July 1°, 2016.

e The iMovie makers (funded by our LSTA grant) held a successful flip book animation workshop for
middle school students. These workshops will continue into the summer.

e The library has purchased 12 new computers with grant money from the Eastern Connecticut
Foundation. All computers that predate 2010 have been replaced.

e Online payments for library fees continues to be a successful venture.
e The reception honoring Nancy Collins and the purchase of children’s books has been cancelled. Over
$3,000 was given in Nancy Collins’ memory. The family appreciated the thought of a reception, but
asked that we not have one.

e The library is partnering with the Parks and Rec Department to show outdoor movies on Friday nights at
McCooks this September. A screening of the movie Jaws will be shown this summer.

e The East Lyme Library Foundation is sponsoring a bus trip to the New Britain Museum of American Art
Thursday, June 23". Tickets may be purchased at the Circulation Desk for $65.

¢ The library is planning to set up a booth for the East Lyme Day celebration on July 16" from 3-9 p.m.
Volunteers are encouraged to sign up for this popular event.

President’s Update: Holly mentioned that she received an e-mail from Molly Helms regarding the
consideration of adding Patricia Carey to the Board. Holly said she would give Patricia a call. Holly welcomes
other suggestions of names for the Board membership positions.

Committee Reports:

Administrative/policy — Nothing to report.

Personnel: - Nothing to report

Public Relations: - Nothing to report.

Nominating: - Holly asked for a motion to reinstate Abe Fisher to the board. Dave Jacobs moved that
Abe Fisher be reinstated to the Board in July. Corinne Weber seconded the motion. Motion passed.
Budget: Nothing further to report.

Publications Committee — Nothing to report, however, Corinne Weber is planning to meet with her
committee to brainstorm ideas.

Old Business:_ Regarding the topic of a parking space designated for patrons to easily drop off or pick up
materials from the library, Marilyn Dunphy met with Bill Scheer and Victor Benni to discuss this possibility
and to also discuss the traffic pattern. They explained to her that nothing could be done without the consent of
the state as the state sets traffic jurisdiction and the number of handicapped spaces that any business/office
should have. The number of handicapped spaces is determined by the size of the parking lot and the number of
people that come and go on a daily basis. They told her they would ask the state. Marilyn said she would get
back to them and report at the July meeting.

New Business. Tara Borden and Ashley Doak have been creating a new web page using Square Space, and
plan to have the transition to the new web page for the library ready for Memorial Day. They gave a
demonstration to the Board on their work which makes the navigation to the different sections much more user

friendly and easier to follow. They have done a superb job given their daunting task. They asked the Board for
any suggestions they might have.

Date of next meeting: Monday, July 11, 2016, 7:00 p.m. Annual Meeting

There being no further business, Loretta McIlhenny moved that the meeting be adjourned at 8:35 pm. Marilyn
Dunphy seconded the motion. Motion passed.

Respectfully submitted,

Molly A. Helms, Secretary
