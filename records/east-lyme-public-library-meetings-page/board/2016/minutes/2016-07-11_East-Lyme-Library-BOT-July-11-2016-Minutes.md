Director’s State of the Library Report:

Library highlights for the past year include:

Many of the computers in the reading room have been replaced with Dell computers that run Windows
10. This uniformity is a welcome upgrade.

Tara Borden and Ashley Doak have updated the library’s web page which now includes a special
children’s section. This has improved navigation of the system.

The children’s department secured a major state LSTA Grant to offer filmmaking classes to middle
school students. As a result of these classes, two students screened their work at the 2016 New Haven
Film Festival. To assist children with literary skills, an AWE computer was purchased. The number of
participants in the summer reading program increased last summer and Rebecca Scotka, the children’s
librarian, continues to publish articles on the national ALA Official Blog of the Association for Library
Service to Children. The children’s room storage closets underwent a major renovation this year.

This year’s annual fund set a new record.

Lisa and Tara spent considerable time this spring speaking with each employee. When each one was
asked what they liked most about working in the East Lyme Library, their response was the same. They
all felt that the biggest strength was their fellow staff members. Lisa and Tara are now working on
improving staff training and initiatives.

All staff have e-mails and are expected to check them at least once during each shift. This has led to
improved communication between administration and staff.

A new auditor was hired. This year’s audit resulted in changes to update the process and a
recommendation to phase in a new software program over the next two years.

An adult summer reading program has been put into place over the last two years. This program was
recently the front page article of the Lyme Times.

The East Lyme Foundation began a new corporate giving program which has resulted in two local
businesses who have committed to giving annual donations of at least $500 to the Foundation’s
endowment. This program is called the Bookie Program.

The library has begun online payment for lost materials and late fees through the LION consortium.
Our partnership with the Parks and Recreation Department has resulted in a joint screening of a number
of movies at the beach this summer and fall and also a joint partnership in the installation of a Storywalk
(outlined in goals).

Last year we participated in Celebrate East Lyme Day and plan to make this an annual event.
Goals for the coming year include:

e Changes to staff include:
Several staff will be trained to assist patrons with technical support, e.g., ereaders, iphones.

One staff member will learn to better identify rare books.

One staff member will write a manual on the magazine and serial operations in the library, and complete
a technical services manual.

Two staff members will be trained to advertise the library’s services using Publisher software.

e The library will pursue a grant to fund a strategic planner.

e The new auditor has recommended the phase out of Sage/Peachtree software and replace with
Quickbooks. This change will be make it easier for more people to understand the library’s accounting
system and will also save on licensing fees.

e Anew beach outreach program begins this summer with showing beachgoers how to download ebooks
from the library and will continue to support a used book sale at the two town beaches.

¢ Inconjunction with Parks and Recreation, a storywalk with approximately 20 stations displaying a few
pages from children’s books along a mile perimeter at Peretz Park will be completed later this year.
This should assist children in their reading skills.

¢ The Library’s publication committee will commence work on a new publication about the history of the
women’s prison in Niantic.

The Foundation hopes to recruit more corporate members for the Bookie Program.
The Library will host its first open house in August to introduce the public to all of the resources that the
library provides. Invitations will be sent to members of the Boards of Selectman and Finance.
