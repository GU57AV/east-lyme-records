mentioned that a children's library workshop article on cartoons was published in The Day
newspaper. She also added that Rebecca received a statewide award for mentoring other libraries

ARP funds to support the funding that the library has requested. Lisa briefly described the
architectural changes in the library. Lisa updated the board members with the change in format
for the newsletter. The new Storybook trail has been installed in Bridebrook park. She said that
the Connecticut Library Association conference will be held in-person and she will be attending
it with few other library employees.
