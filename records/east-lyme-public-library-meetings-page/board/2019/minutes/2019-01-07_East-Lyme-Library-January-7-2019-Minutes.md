State also received close to $400 million as late payment of medical

February. Holly will keep us posted.

teacher's pensions, the indication the governor made was he would like to
keep municipal aid on hold but there are other things to take care.

how to get to those jobs is difficult so this pipeline will help. CT lost 12,000
residents since 2016 and this took away $2.6 billion revenue. We have lost
the 23-43 age group. Last year all the taxes came out well.
