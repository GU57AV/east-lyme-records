A. AED Funding:
Julie shared with the committee members that she filled out and submitted an application
she received from Stephen Pendl for CIRMA’s 2018 Excellence in Risk Management Award.
Four applicants will be selected from the entities that have demonstrated outstanding risk
management leadership results. Recipients will receive a $2,500 grant to be used for future
risk management purposes, they will be featured in an issue of CT&C and honored at
CIRMA’s Annual Meeting of members.

> Past Issues
Julie stated that she has spoken to Chief Finkelstein regarding the concern from our last
meeting with the Dunbar employee that was charged with threatening a Federal Agent
while in uniform. The Chief recommended reaching out to Dunbar for more information.
Julie spoke with Rob Myer, Regional Vice President of Operations for Dunbar, who
explained the entire vetting process they hold for each employee which includes an
extensive Federal background check. He explained the incident that had occurred involving
their employee in detail. He further explained that the incident went to court and there
were no charges brought against the employee. Because of his exemplary history with the
company, it was decided that the employee would remain on staff in an “unarmed” position
until his carrying permit is reinstated, Mr. Myer explained that Dunbar has a “Zero
Tolerance Policy” to which all employees must abide, and that violence of any type is
grounds for immediate dismissal. Julie will draft a memo to distribute to employees ready
for review at our next meeting.

Injury Reports - 6/2/2017 - 9/28/2017:

The committee discussed in detail ten injuries, seven of which were deemed “unavoidable”, one to
be referred to Joe Bragaw, one to be investigated by Ron Bence and one that resulted ina
unanimous agreement by committee members that a HAZWOPER training by Steve from CIRMA
would be beneficial. This would also be brought to Joe’s attention for scheduling. Additionally, Ron
Bence will discuss with Dave Putnam, Director of Parks & Recreation, about holding a HAZWOPER
training for Part-Time summer help in an effort to prevent a reoccurrence.

Julie reported on a Cyber Security Seminar that was recently organized by the Health & Safety
Committee with the assistance of Carmen Ames from IT and presented by Steve Pendl from CIRMA.
The session took place on September 21, 2017 at the Town Hall. Although attendance was light (12
in the a.m. and 12 in the p.m. sessions), the feedback from those in attendance was overwhelmingly
favorable. Many people contacted Julie after the training that had heard positive reviews asking if
we would be holding another session in the future, as they would like to attend. Julie will talk with
Mr. Nickerson on the possibility of holding another sitting in the spring that would incorporate the
latest update on Cyber Security. Chris Taylor suggested holding a future training at a different
facility, suggesting the Community Center or the Field Services building as options.
Upcoming Trainings:

> Julie asked for ideas for the annual 2018 employee training day. This training will be based
