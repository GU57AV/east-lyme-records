Town of East Lyme
Health & Safety Committee
Minutes for the July 14, 2016 Meeting

Douglas Pesce from the Department of Homeland Security was present to discuss training opportunities
available at no cost to the town. Mr. Pesce discussed:

Infragard: An alliance between the private sector, the FBI and other public sector entities with the goal
and intent of guarding the nation’s critical infrastructure through public and private partnerships and
information sharing.

CBAT (Computer-Based Assessment Tool) — Technology and services that help DHS, owners/operators,
local law enforcement, and emergency personnel prepare for, respond to, and manage critical Infrastructure,

key resources and special events.

HSIN — CS (Homeland Security Information Network — Critical Sector's) primary objective is to
generate effective risk management decisions, and to encourage collaboration and coordination on plans,
strategies, protective measures, and response/recovery efforts between government, operations, and owners
in the public and private sectors.

Infrastructure Survey Tool — (IST) — A multi-use, web-based platform used in conjunction with Enhanced
Critical Infrastructure Protection (ECIP) visits to enable effective data collection, analysis, sector and cross-
sector comparison, dependency and interdependency identification, and cascading impacts and effects,

Counter-lmprovised Explosive Device & Risk Mitigation Training — This course offers education for

our local law enforcement and other emergency services, municipal officials and emergency managers
strategies to prevent, protect against, respond to and mitigate bombing incidents.

At the conclusion of the presentation, Julie Wilson called the regular meeting to order at 3:20 pm

A discussion was held on the Employee Training Day held on June 1". Julie reported that 91 employees
signed into the training. There were 44 present in the a.m. session and 47 present in the p.m, session. Many
of the committee members she had spoken to regarding feedback from employees mentioned that everything
they had heard from those in attendance was very positive. Julie noted that she would be speaking with
Carmen on the editing process for the two sessions that were recorded and the process to create a training
DVD to provide to all of the Department Heads.

A discussion was held on implementing the annual drills referenced in the new Building Emergency
Procedures manual that was handed out to all employees at the training, It was decided that a fire alarm
drill and a lockdown drill would be the first two performed in the coming months. Julie suggested
contacting Chris Olsen, Director of Safety and Security at East Lyme Schools and having him at our next
meeting to discuss planning the “Lock Down” Drill. The committee concurred this would be beneficial, and
Julie will make contact with Chris Olsen.

Meeting was adjourned at 3:50 p.m.
