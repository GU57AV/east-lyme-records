April 2", a Water Department employee twisted his ankle exiting his town truck at a private residence, Ice was

morning meeting. Julie will create a signup sheet for Tailgate talks and have at the next meeting. The signup
sheets can be distributed with a link to the CIRMA site with all of the trainings available.

Upcoming Trainings:

Hazard Assessment form filled out. If OSHA comes in, they will request a Hazard Analysis for each position.
Victor will talk to Mark about distributing these at a Department Head meeting. Steve will research to see if he
can find examples of additional positions that we can use as a guide. Steve will also provide us with the list of 7
notices that must be posted on the employee bulletin boards.
