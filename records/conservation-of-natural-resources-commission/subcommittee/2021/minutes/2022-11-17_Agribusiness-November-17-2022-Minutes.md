   Minutes of Nov. L7, 2021Agribusiness Sub-committee Meeting
              East Lyme High School Commons (cafeteria)

  The meeting began at 6:35pm. Those in attendance were Tom Kalal,
Thomas Hall, Sally Uden, Kyle Corey, Mark Christensen. There were no
DELEGATIONS. The minutes from the Oct meeting were unanimously
approved 5-0. The Resilient Agriculture at Home educational series was
discussed. Tom K will teach a class on composting this winter, Sally will
present a class on beekeeping for beginners, Mark will be responsible
for a class regarding a home orchard, and another class maybe offered
regarding organic vegetable gardening. Kyle offered a class on keeping
backyard chickens. These are planned for Jan-April.

   The mini-orchard of no more than 15 trees at Samuel Smith Property
was discussed. Mark reported that we were given the ok to plant fruit
trees near the upper stone wall near the beehives and walking path.
We are planning on getting the trees from Dave Fairman who is
affiliated with a Memorial Tree Foundation. Mark reported that the
Town Public Works crew will be clearing the area, which currently has
some vines and brambles.

  There was nothing new on the Agricultural portion of the Sustainable
CT initiative, headed by Nancy Kalal. Nancy has or will meet with Penny
Heller, who is the Sustainable CT contact for E. Lyme

  Tom Kalal will continue to do occasional Facebook posts on East
Lyme Agriculture. We agreed to write + submit a letter to Selectman
regarding our efforts regariling Agriculture for Dec Selectman meeting.
The "Potluck Party for Preservationists" was discussed and tentatively
planned for January. lt was feared turnout could be low, due to the
Covid issue. The meeting was Adjourned at 7:25pm. Submitted by M.C.
