Discussion on the tour of Ledyard Vo-Ag.
The tour was attended by Mark Christensen, Terri and Tom Yuhas. The agriculture program is
taught to Ledyard and other area town students interested in agriculture as additional courses
on top of their regular courses of study. The program included options such as; aquaculture,
livestock, plant science, use and repair of shop and farm equipment required to maintain a
working farm. The sub-committee asked how we can help share information about the school to
potential students interested in agriculture. The sub-committee was provided with pamphlets to
distribute to interested students or parents during presentations, events, or other.

Discussion on potential partnerships with Brookside Farm Museum
Annah Perch, the curator for Brookside Farm Museum, discussed renderings put together by
UCONNs Landscape Design group to demonstrate potential recommendations for the farm
grounds based on the Farm Museum future goals.

Annah also discussed the enrollment of the farm museum into the "Homegrown National Parks"
program. Discussion groups were scheduled at the farm museum on June 5th, July 3, August 7th
with a special meeting with Doug Tallamy (potentially virtual attendance) on September 9th .
