Terri Yuhas discussed information learned from the presentation on the New Bear Regulations
in Conn. Put on by both the Connecticut Farm Bureau and the Conn DEEP. Besides field crops, it

rabbits. > 85% of livestock conflicts involved chickens. Concerns were raised that the agricultural

situation where they are attracting bears. Recommendations for electric fencing to help protect
livestock from bears as well as other information could be found on the website bearwise.org.
