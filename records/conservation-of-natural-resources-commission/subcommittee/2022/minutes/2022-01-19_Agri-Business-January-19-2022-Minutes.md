              Minutes of Jan 19th Agribusiness sub-committee
               Meeting at E. Lyme High School Commons


-The meeting Opened at 6:37pm.

-attendees included Kyle and Samantha Corey, Nancy Kalal, Mark
Christensen

-Educational efforts: Composting class by Tom K was postponed until
May. Sally will handle beekeeping class, Kyle will talk about backyard
chickens. Dates are being coordinated with Penny Heller of
Conservation Comm.

-mini orchard at Samuel Smith House: we need to look for a different
grant for trees. Memorial Tree Foundation is unable to help. We are
still planning on 5 or 6 trees for planting in April.
-Sustainable CT: issues of concern for farmers. We discussed at length
various issues that local farmers face, and Nancy K will compile these,
along with any other ideas from local farmers and submit to Penny
Heller of SustainableCT. Some of these issues are: Runoff; Groundwater
quality; larger farmstands; the need for the town to work better wlth
Agricultural enterprises; invasive species control; Land use; renting out
town owned open space for hay, etc.; actively encourage use of PA
 490; encourage shellfishing in local waters.
