East Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources
Regular Meeting Minutes
April 13, 2016

Present: Arthur Carlson, Chairman FILED IN EAST LYME
Penny Heller, Member CONNECTICUT
Marvin Schutt, Member LTD 2 1b NAb ty
Mark Christensen, Member EAST ZYME TOWN CLERK

Ron Nichols, Alternate
Laura Ashburn, Alternate

Absent: Don Danila, Member

CALL TO ORDER. Chairman Carlson called the April 13, 2016 Regular Meeting of the East
Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources to order at 7:00 p.m. Mr. Nichols was
asked to be seated.

I. Public Delegations. Judy Rondeau, Tom, Nancy and Kevin Kalal were present.

II Minutes,

A. Minutes of February 10, 2016. Mr. Carlson asked for additions, deletions or corrections to
the February 10, 2016 Regular Meeting Minutes.

MOTION (1): Ms. Heller moved to approve the February 10,
2016 Regular Meeting Minutes, as presented.
Seconded by Mr. Christensen. Mr. Schutt
abstained from voting. (4-0-1) Motion
carried.

Minutes of March 9, 2016. Mr. Carlson asked for additions, deletions or corrections to the
March 9, 2016 Regular Meeting Minutes.

MOTION (2): Mr. Christensen moved to approve the March 9,
2016 Regular Meeting Minutes, as presented.
Seconded by Ms. Heller. Mr. Schutt abstained
from voting. (4-0-1) Motion carried.

II. NEW BUSINESS
A. Judy Rondeau of ECCD, Coordinator for the Niantic River Watershed Committee,

to propose a Joint Manure Management Project Between the Commission and
NRWC
Ms. Rondeau reported they have decided to look at farm run off. Programs are available through
the Connecticut Department of Agriculture. Last month they applied for funding through the Dominion
Foundation for Small Farm Best Management Practice for manure. They will be working with the four
watershed towns. She asked if this Commission wishes to partner with the Niantic River Watershed
Committee.

MOTION (3): Mr. Nichols moved that this Commission supports
a partnership with the Niantic River Watershed
Committee's efforts regarding manure management.
Seconded by Mr. Christensen. (5-0) Unanimous.
Ms. Rondeau agreed to inform this Commission if the grant is received.

B. Summary of Our Accomplishments. Mr. Carlson reviewed the accomplishments of this
Commission since it was established. The entire Commission will work on the final product.

IV. OLD BUSINESS.

A. Continuation of Water Study. Mr. Carlson and Ron Nicols will meet with Mr. Williams of
DEEP. Mr. Carlson plans to meet with the Town Engineer next Thursday at 9:30 a.m.

V. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS,

A. Communications. There were no new communications.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported we met last month with
representatives of the state and Connecticut Farm Bureau. The First Selectman was in attendance.

Mr. Christensen reported he spoke at the Zoning Commission meeting in favor of the application
for White Gate Farm to hold agricultural programs at their farm.

He stated we are planning for the Memorial Day Parade and discussing Open Farm Day. Nancy
Kalal met with the First Selectman regarding Celebrate East Lyme.

Mr. Christensen spoke with the Garden Club regarding partnering with them.
We will have a Bike and Barbeque event on October 1 from 10 a.m. to 12 noon.

C. RBA Report--Penny Heller. We have found water is better in the north end than in the south
end of Town.

D. Chairman's Report. Mr. Carlson volunteered this Commission to lead hikes on Trails Day
to be held June 4 and 5. The Commission created this trail and also negotiated a twenty year lease to hike
on the Yale property, which property is part of the trail between Darrow Pond and the Nehantic State
Forest. Tomorrow he will finish all markers. All maps will be prepared.
Ms. Heller informed Mr. Carlson she is only available on June 5.
E. EX-OFFICIO. Ms. Hardy was unable to be present.

VI. ADJOURNMENT.

MOTION (4): Mr. Christensen moved to adjourn the April 13,
2016 Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources
at 8:40 p.m. Seconded by Mr. Nichols. (5-0)
Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
