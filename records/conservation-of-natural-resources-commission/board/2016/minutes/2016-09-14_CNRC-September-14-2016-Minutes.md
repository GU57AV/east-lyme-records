B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen informed the Commission the Bike and
Barbeque event will beheld on October | from 10 a.m. to 12 noon. There will be a short and long ride.
He stated approximately $4,000 has been raised from business sponsorships. The sponsorships will pay
for shirts, the barbeque and water bottles for the bikers. To date thirty-six individuals have registered.

Ms. Heller and Mr. Christensen have distributed flyers for this event at various biking events.

The short ride will visit the three historical houses in Town. The Thomas Lee House will be open
and Butch Foster will be playing with his band. The Samuel Smith House will have a woodworker
demonstrating his work using old tools. There will also be a musket display. The Smith- Harris House
will have an open house, refreshments and a model railroad demonstration in the barn. Two DEEP
conservation officers will be at the event. Bike teams are marking the routes. Each ride will be a
different color code. Betty Mahan is in charge of publicity.

The Agribusiness Subcommittee had a Beekeepers' Meeting with the Zoning Commission. The
purpose of the meeting was to allowed bees in East Lyme. Zoning withdrew the application, because the
Connecticut Beekeepers put forth guidelines for beekeeping in municipalities. The beekeepers plan to
write regulations to allow for it. The Zoning Commission spoke positively about having bees in East
Lyme.
