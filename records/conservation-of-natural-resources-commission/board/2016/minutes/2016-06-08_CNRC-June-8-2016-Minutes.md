East Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources
Regular Meeting Minutes
June 8, 2016

Present: Arthur Carlson, Chairman
Penny Heller, Member nD
Marvin Schutt, Member

Mark Christensen, Member TMelS roland CD)
Don Danila, Member
Laura Ashburn, Alternate EAST LYME TOWN CI

Absent: Ron Nichols, Alternate

CALL TO ORDER. Chairman Carlson called the June 8, 2016 Regular Meeting of the East
Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources to order at 7:00 p.m.

I. Public Delegations. There were no guests.
Tl Minutes.

A. Minutes of May 11, 2016. Mr. Carlson asked for additions, deletions or corrections to the
May 11, 2016 Regular Meeting Minutes.

Mr. Danila asked that it be noted in the May 11, 2016 Minutes that he was absent.

MOTION (1): Mr. Christensen moved to approve the May 11,
2016 Regular Meeting Minutes, as amended.
Seconded by Ms. Heller. Messrs. Schutt and
Danila abstained from voting. (5-0-2) Motion
carried.

Ill. NEW BUSINESS
Mr. Carlson suggested that this Commission not meet in July or August.

MOTION (2): Mr. Danila moved that the Conservation of
Natural Resources Commission not meet
in July or August, unless there is pressing
business as decided by the Chairman.
Seconded by Mr. Christensen. (6-0)
Unanimous.

Ms. Heller reported Travis Goddie called her regarding the cottontail bunnies. He trapped the
bunnies in order to count them. She received a telephone call from a reporter of Planning Magazine. The

reporter wanted to know how the Town felt about threatened species so the reporter could write an article.
Ms. Heller informed the reporter that the Town is concerned about threatened species.

IV. OLD BUSINESS.

A. Continuation of Water Study. Mr. Carlson showed Commission members well locations on
maps. All contained sodium. Ms. Heller suggested bringing the maps to this Commission's booth during
Celebrate East Lyme.

V. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS.

A. Communications. Mr. Carlson read letter he wrote to USFWS on May 18 supporting an
Open Space grant on a piece of property in the Open Space Plan.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported the Memorial Day Parade was
cancelled.

He informed the Commission the Agribusiness Subcommittee now has a Beekeeping
Subcommittee. Steve Dinsmore, the President of the Beekeeping Association is involved. Zoning has
agreed to it in a two acre zone. The state is considering a Pollination Bill. Regulations have been passed
regarding pesticides.

Mr. Christensen reported Open Farm Day will be held on July 17 from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. The
Kalal, the Morgan and the Gear farms will be open. He asked that this Commission fund the cost of the
brochure for Open Farm Day.

MOTION (3): Ms. Heller moved that this Commission pay
the cost of the brochure for Open Farm
Day for an amount not to exceed $100.
Seconded by Mr. Schutt. (6-0) Unanimous.

He informed the Commission a Bike and Barbeque event will be held on October 1 from 10 a.m.
to 12 noon. There will be a short and long ride.

C. RBA Report--Penny Heller. Mr. Carlson reported he has received Ms. Heller's RBA Report.

D. Chairman's Report. Mr. Carlson reported he attended the Niantic Headwater's fundraiser at
White Gate Farms. On Wednesday morning he walked on 33 acres of property of the Niantic
Headwaters.

The Samuel Smith House community garden has been planted. Three people have signed up for
three plots. At the Open House on Saturday, the Rotary will have a planting. Mr. Schutt has arranged for
publicity.

Mr. Carlson plans to put on the Town website a map of Darrow Pond and a map of Harmon Park.

He asked the Parks and Recreation Commission to have signs indicating that no motorized
vehicles are allowed.

E. EX-OFFICIO. Ms. Hardy was unable to be present.
VI. ADJOURNMENT.
MOTION (4): Mr. Christensen moved to adjourn the June 8,
2016 Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources
at 7:50 p.m. Seconded by Ms. Heller. (6-0)

Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
