Ill. NEW BUSINESS

A. Goodwin Trail. One of the kiosks was vandalized.

Road debris fill is being placed on top of the old J. C. Penny foundation. The plan is to
grade it.

B. Schedule of 2017 CNR Meeting Dates. Commission members agreed to meet at East
Lyme Town Hall on the second Wednesday of each month in 2017.

MOTION (2): Ms. Ashburn moved to approve the 2017
meeting dates for the Conservation of

of each month at 7 p.m. Seconded by

Mr. Christensen. (5-0) Unanimous.

IV. OLD BUSINESS.

A. Continuation of Water Study. The Water Study is continuing to be developed.

V. ‘REPORTS AND‘COMMUNICATIONS. _

- A. Communications.—Mr; Nichols xeported when COSTCO was approved they agreed
to redo the Exit 74 interchange. Zoning approved COSTCO and the gas station. He suggested
that this Commission write a letter to Planning and refer to the POCD. He felt it would be
advisable that the Town get together with DOT when they work on Exit 74. Mr. Christensen
suggested notifying Mr. Mulholland. Mr. Nichols suggested that this Commission work together
with Planning, Zoning and DOT on the reconfiguration of I-95.

Chairman Carlson arrived. He informed the Commission a trail map will be ready after
the parking lot on Whistletown Road is completed. Trees will be removed on Grassy Hill Road
for a parking lot. Volunteers will be needed for care and maintenance of the Goodwin Trail. He
agreed to have a trail map placed on the Town website.

It was felt Planning needs to be in contact with DOT on the design of I-95. The POCD
should be noted.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported the subcommittee is working
on beekeeping ordinances and are planning to have a Beekeeping Seminar. Commission

members attended a talk with a UCONN scientist on how an aquifer is protected. Fifty-four
individuals attended.

C. RBV Report--Penny Heller. Ms. Heller and Mr. Danila worked on Latimer and
Cranberry and found three or four of the most wanted species.

D. Chairman's Report. Mr. Carlson reported sixty individuals attended a program at
the Niantic River Head Water's Preserve. A hike was led around the property. There was much
enthusiasm.

Ms. Heller stated the New England Forestry is planning a full survey of the area.

E. Ex-officio. Ms. Hardy was unable to be present.
