A. Communications.
There was no communications received.
B. Agribusiness Subcommittee.

Mr. Christensen informed the Commission the Agribusiness Subcommittee received a grant that
was used for its brochures.

He stated we are planning a Celebration of Agriculture at the Town Hall on March 16 at 6 p.m.
Steve Reviczky, Connecticut Agricultural Commissioner will attend,

Mr. Christensen reported the Bike Ride will be held on October 1. We plan to have a short ride
for youngsters, as well as a 10, 25 and 62 mile ride. At the end of the ride there will be food and a free tee
shirt for participants, We are planning on the shorter distance riders to visit the three historical houses in
Town. Mr. Nickerson has volunteered his band to play at Rocky Neck. Parks and Recreation has agreed
to loan seed money for the Bike Ride. The next meeting will be held on March 15 at 6 p.m. in the Parks
and Recreation meeting room.
At the urging of the Agribusiness Subcommittee, the Zoning Commission agreed to signage
concerning farm tractors on public roads. Also they plan to meet with Zoning on the need for honey bee
regulations.
