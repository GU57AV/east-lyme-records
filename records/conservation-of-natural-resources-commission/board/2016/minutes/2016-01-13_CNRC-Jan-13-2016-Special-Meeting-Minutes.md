East Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources
Special Meeting Minutes
January 13, 2016

Present: Arthur Carlson, Chairman
Penny Heller, Member
Don Danila, Member
Mark Christensen, Member
Laura Ashburn, Alternate

FILED IN EAST LYME

Absent: Marvin Schutt, Member
Ron Nichols, Alternate

Also Present: Rose Ann Hardy, ex officio

Call to Order. Chairman Carlson called the January 13, 2016 Special Meeting of the East Lyme
Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources to order at 7:00 p.m. Ms. Ashburn was asked to
be seated.

I. Public Delegations. There were no guests.

II Minutes.

A. Minutes of November 10, 2015. Mr. Carlson asked for additions, deletions or corrections to
the November 10, 2015 Regular Meeting Minutes.

The following corrections were noted: On page 1, the first motion was made by Mr. Danila and
the last motion was made by Mr. Christensen.

MOTION (1): Mr. Christensen moved to approve the November 10,
2015 Regular Meeting Minutes, as amended.
Seconded by Ms. Danila. (5-0) Unanimous.

III. NEW BUSINESS
A. Eastern Connecticut Conservation District Request for Funding Support.

Mr. Carlson reported he received a letter from the Eastern Connecticut Conservation District
requesting $1500 to help support the Eastern Connecticut Conservation District. This organization runs
training, holds workshops and sends this Commission its monthly newsletter. The Commission will
consider this request and take action on it at the February meeting.

Mr. Danila informed the Commission Judy Rando is interested in a Manure Management project.
She would like to come to this Commission's February meeting to discuss it. Commission members felt
she should be invited to the February meeting.
B. Schedule of 2016 Meeting Dates.

The Commission reviewed the Conservation of Natural Resources proposed schedule of meeting
dates.

MOTION (2): Mr. Christensen moved to approve the 2016
meeting dates, as proposed. Seconded by
Mr. Danila. (5-0) Unanimous.

IV. OLD BUSINESS.
A. Continuation of Water Study.

Mr. Carlson attended a Planning Commission Meeting and discussed the Conservation of Natural
Resources water study. This study is nearing completion. The Planning Commission requested a briefing
at this time.

VI. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS.
A. Communications.
Mr. Carlson reported on communications he received.
B. Agribusiness Subcommittee.

Mr. Christensen reported the Potluck Dinner had 57 attendees. There were members from the
Land Trust, Conservation and Historical groups, as well as four Selectmen and members of the general
public in attendance.

Betty Murphy spent $83.52 for various items for the Potluck, and Mr. Christensen asked that she
be reimbursed.

MOTION (3): Mr. Christensen moved to reimburse Betty
Murphy $83.52 for supplies for the Potluck
Dinner. Seconded by Ms. Heller. (5-0)
Unanimous.

Mr. Christensen reported there will be a Bike Ride planning meeting on January 19, 2016 in the
Parks and Recreation Office at 6 p.m. The ride will be held on October 1.

Mr. Christensen informed the Commission part of the grant the Agribusiness Subcommittee
received was used to develop a better link to the Town website. Brochures were also developed.

Mr. Christensen reported 41 people attended a Beekeeper Seminar.
C. RBA--Ms. Heller.

Mr. Carlson asked Ms. Heller for a written report on the Rapid Bio Assessment. Ms. Heller
agreed to write it.

D. EX-OFFICIO. Ms. Hardy reported the Board of Selectmen will be reviewing budgets.
VII. ADJOURNMENT.
MOTION (4): Mr. Christensen moved to adjourn the January 13,
2016 Special Meeting of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources
at 8:36 p.m. Seconded by Mr. Danila. (5-0)
Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
