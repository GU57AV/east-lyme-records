b. North Bride Brook Development Update
  Per Commissioner Danila's report regarding a CT DEEP (Connecticut
  Department of Energy and Environmental Protection) report regarding the
  runoff of water with sediment from the Nofth Bride Brook Development
  worksite, Chairman Carlson reviewed the project area with a Contractor and,
  later, the Planning Director. No significant evidence of run-off from the
  development was found on either occasion. Silt fences and hay rolls were
  placed on the site and the issue appears to have been remedied.
  Regardless, Commissioner Danila expressed his disappointment with the lack
  of acknowledgment of the letter they sent to the Inland Wetland Agency
  (cc'd to the Zoning Commission and Board of Selectmen). The
  Commissioners agreed that the letter should have been documented,
  accepted, and acknowledged by the Agency.
  Chairman Carlson will follow up with the Planning Director regarding the
  letter and Municipal Utility Engineer Ben North regarding the well-specific
  salt data, which has been requested numerous times. Commissioner Heller
  will also speak with the Planning Director regarding the status of the spill
  mitigation letter to CTDOT (Connecticut Department of Transportation).
c. Liaisons to Other Commissions and Agencies
  Chairman Carlson will speak with the First Selectman regarding the best
  method to work with the other boards/commissions.
d. Update on Noble Gas Project
  Commissioner Danila reported the receipt of an e-mail regarding the lack of
  silt fences around the Noble Gas Project. Upon investigating the site, he
  discovered that, while silt fences were installed along the Route l/Boston
  Post Road-side of the property, there were no silt fences installed along the
  side with the moderately steep road leading the cemetery. He sent an e-mail
  to the Planning Director (cc'd to Selectwoman Rose Ann Hardy) regarding his
  findings. Chairman Carlson will provide the Commission with a copy of the
  Erosion and Sedimentation Ordinance that was adopted by the Town
  approximately 30 years ago.
