    c. Chairman's Report
      Chairman Carlson reported that the Commission's submission for the
      town's Annual Report will be forwarded to the Board of Finance
      Chairperson.
      In an effort to quell the issues with the motorcycles and ATVs riding along
      the Goodwin Trail, signage describing the impact of their activities was
      created and presented to the Commission. An Eagle Scout project, the
      18" x 22" signs will be posted along the trail. It is hoped that hikers will
      help monitor and enforce the activities,
      Two washouts have occurred at the Goodwin Trail. One of the washouts is
      on Town property and located at the beginning of the trail (Darrow Pond)
      and the other, larger washout is located on State property, south of
      Grassy Hill Road. While water bars were installed several years ago to
      protect the area, the recent heavy rainfall overwhelmed the area causing
      the washout. After inspecting the property with the Planning Director and
      Retired CTDEEP Forester Dick Raymond, it was estimated that the cost to
      repair the area would be approximately $50,000.00. With the help of
      State Forester Gary Nestaika, an application requesting funding for the
      project was submitted to CTDEEP. The Town has been informed regarding
      the washout at Darrow Pond.
      He recently learned of two limitations on Darrow Pond: (1) only residents
      who reside in the area are allowed to utilize the Pond for boating, p€F
      their Deed. The public is allowed to fish on the Pond. (2) the topsoil in the
      open fields located at the top of Darrow Pond was previously stripped off
      to eliminate the weed seeds in preparation for a proposed golf course.
      The area was not re-filled as the company went out of business. A request
      has been submitted to review the four-acre field for the possible location
      of a pollinator garden,
      In addition, a map of a 300-acre parcel, named Oakwoods, was presented
