    il.       MINUTES OF MARCH 9,2022 REGUTAR MEETING. Chairman Carlson asked for additions,

MOTION      (1): Mr. Christensen moved to accept the Regular Meeting Minutes of March 9,2022, as

              NEW BUSINESS. Mr. Danila informed the Commission of a Winter Salt Watch Program and
              asked if anyone wishes to participate to contact him. He will send an email with a link to this
              program to Commission members.

    IV        OLD BUSINESS.
              a. SustainabilityCT Program: Start of 2022 Recertification Process. Ms. Heller reported we
                  have approximately 15 of the needed 35 actions written up. We need photos, etc. She
                  added we have been invited to participate in a Solarize program through SustainableCT,
                  but the Town Planner Mr. Goeschel needs to initiate it. The program is designed to
                  educate people on ways to add solar panels to their home or business. A first step could
                  be a town policy promoting a clean energy alternative. Mr. Carlson recognized Mr.
                  Beeman. Mr. Beeman added the last time we had an intern to help complete the
                  certification process and wondered if we would get one this time. Ms. Heller plans to
                  contact SustainableCT staff to see if they will supply an intern and to clarify the status of
                  the current intern from Tufts University working with Mr. Goeschel.

                  Ms. Heller asked do we have money in the budget for printing handouts for two
                  upcoming workshops at the Samuel Smith Farmstead. Mr. Carlson replied we do.
               Ms. Ashburn informed the Commission EarthFest will be held on June l-1 at McCook's.
               Mr. Christensen will inform those attending the next Agribusiness subcommittee
               meeting and ask if they want to participate. This event conflicts with the 'Open House'
               events at the three historical houses in town on the same day.

  V        REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS.
           a. Communications. There were no new communications.
           b. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported we have planted apple trees at
               the SamuelSmith Farmstead. The Parks and Recreation Department received a donation
               of the apple trees. The Samuel Smith House needs a new roof and re-siding of the barn.

               Mr. Christensen reported an agribusiness member is moving and the committee may
               have someone who is willing to replace him. However, membership is still small and
               they are seeking new members who have experience or interest in agriculture.

               He informed the Commission the subcommittee has put compost in the Samuelsmith
               Community Garden.

               The subcommittee does not plan to be in the Memorial Day Parade because parade
               officials have blocked the participation of including farm animals or riding horses.

           c   Chairman's Report. Mr. Carlson reported as of Wednesday the Land Trust has
               completed acquisition of 301 acres of property now called 'Oak Woods' off Grassy Hill
               and Holmes Road. Ms. Heller asked what is the value of the property? Mr. Carlson
               replied S1,650,000.

               Mr. Carlson recognized Ms. Meekhoff. She stated she contacted Parks and Recreation,
               Mr. Goeschel and Public Works to suggest an inventory of trees that are on town
               property. Ms. Meekhoff stated some trees that are being planted by the town are not
               native and only native species should be considered.

           d. Ex-Officio Report. Ms. Hardy informed the Commission that the Board of Selectmen has
               already approved American Rescue Funds to various town groups providing the Board of
               Finance approves them.

  VI.      ADJOURNMENT.

MOTION   (2): Ms. Ashburn moved to adjourn the April L3,2022 Meeting of the
               Conservation of Natural Resources at 8:30 p.m. Seconded by
               Ms. Heller. (5-0) Unanimous.
