           insects. The problem with sampling is you need a specific flow and substrate. Maybe we

           generally lacking where we have been sampling. Despite the 202L results, Mr. Danila did

           water flows. Latimer Brook now is discharging about one-half cubic feet of water (1/16
           of the normal flow for this time of the year). He stated he took a UConn graduate
           student to Bride Brook and they looked at this stream at the North Bride Brook Road
           overpass and there was no water. Mr. Danila felt we should forego RVB sampling unless
           we get considerable rain in the coming weeks.
