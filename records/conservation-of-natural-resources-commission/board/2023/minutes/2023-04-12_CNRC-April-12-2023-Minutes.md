    ilt.    NEW BUSINESS
           Discussion of Trail Maintenance lssues: Mr. Carlson met with Parks and Recreation Director
           Jerry Lokken and Mike McDowell in March, 2023. Mr. Carlson said Mr. Lokken offered the
           assistance of Parks maintenance staff and machinery to repair damage on the Goodwin Trail
           due to erosion and heavy use. Ms. Ashburn emphasized that this was an excellent time to
           engage the new director in developing a comprehensive Trail Maintenance training program
           for the Parks staff so needed repairs can be done effectively with minimum staff time and
           expense. Mr. Carlson agreed with this suggestion and said he will look for published trail
           maintenance guides that can be used to initiate a training program, and report back to the
           Commission in May.

           Ms. Ashburn also suggested that the Commission invite a knowledgeable speaker to talk about
           trail maintenance and extend the invitation to Parks staff and the First Selectman. She also
           noted that this should be the first of many invited experts to inform Commission members as
           well as other interested town officials.
