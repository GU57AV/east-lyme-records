Minutes of Conservation of Natural Resources Commission

June 10" 2015 Meeting

Members Present: AST LYME TOWN CLER

Penny Heller, Marv Schutt, Mark Christensen

Members Absent:
Art Carlson, Laura Ashburn, Ron Nichols

Public Delegation/Niantic Watershed member: Don Danila,
Webster Rd., East Lyme

The meeting was called to order at 7:09p.m. as there was a
quorum. It was noted that no Agenda had been received,
however the schedule of meetings voted on in Dec., 2014
included a meeting for this evening and the town Bulletin
Board indicated there was a meeting scheduled and no
cancelation of this scheduled meeting was posted or received
by members in attendance. Secretary Penny Heller was acting
Chairman, Mark Christensen acting recording secretary.

-Approval of Minutes of May meeting were tabled, as no copies
minutes were received by members.

-REPORTS: Penny Heller reported that the newspaper had
reported that the Board Of Selectman approved the idea of
contributing money towards the purchase of the Harney
property (Niantic Headwaters land). The issue will ultimately be
decided at a referendum later this year. There is cost sharing
from other groups for this proposed purchase.

-Mark Christensen reported that he sent an e-mail to Kevin
Nursick of ConnDOT regarding spill mitigation piping/systems
along I-95/exit 74. The response from Mr. Nursick (from
January, 2015) stated that the DOT would not retro-fit any spill
mitigation piping in or along the highway. They would,
however, include such a system design in a re-design project. In
a subsequent (Feb., 2015) phone call between Mark
Christensen and Kevin Nursick, Mr. Nursick stated that to his
knowledge, there had been no conversations between DOT and
the developers of the Gateway project regarding the exit 74
proposed re-design since 2008, and that any re-design of exit
74 would be very costly because the power substation must be
relocated. The DOT did not have funds for this potential re-
design or relocation, according to Mr. Nursick. Additionally,
Mr. Nursick stated that a highway design would take about 5
years to complete, and that process had not yet begun, and
there was no projected date to begin the re-design of the
highway. Copies of the e-mails were forwarded to Commission
members earlier. Mark has sent another e-mail to Mr. Nursick
in June, to inquire about any updates regarding I-95/exit 74
work. Mr. Nursick tried to call, but was unable to reach Mark.
Penny Heller reported that some changes are needed to the
RGB study. The DEEP advised that we not bother testing sites
that continually test sub-standard (the worst sites). We should
find another brook, or move upstream. We should continue
testing intermediate sites. We may wish to sample an un-
named Cranberry meadow tributary near the Bialowans
property, near the Solar power station.

Don Danila, Niantic Watershed Group reported that there are
thermal and erosion issues along an un-named tributary of
Cranberry meadow Brook. The temperature of at least part of
this un-named brook is/was cold water that supports trout.

Agribusiness Report: Mark Christensen reported that the
Agribusiness sub-committee entered a beekeeping float as well
as 3 John Deere tractors in the Memorial Day Parade. The
purpose was to highlight the need and value of honeybees in
agriculture as well as in town and it was well received by the
public. Also, Open Farm Day will be held on Sunday, July 19°",
from 11-3pm. The 3 farms included this year are: Brubaker’s
Victory Garden (Rickety Pickets Garden, near Dairy Queen
Parking lot); Samuel Smith Farmstead (82 Plants Dam Rd.) with
Missy Miko’s milk goats, and old fashioned Narragansett
turkeys; McTurk’s Donkey herd, (252 Whistletown Rd.)
featuring their miniature Donkey’s. Brochures to these venues
will be handed out the day before, at Celebrate East Lyme,
where there will be an Agriculture/Conservation booth(s). Also,
a biking event is being planned for fall 2016 with help from
other groups to highlight farms, open space, water bodies and
trails in East Lyme. The next biking meeting will be July 28 at
the Olive Chendali Room, 6:30pm. Parks and Rec will offer
some assistance.

DISCUSSION, Item 1: Don Danila reported that although he
was asked quite some time ago if he would be willing to serve
on this commission, he has received no official notice from this
commission, town planner or selectman. He is becoming
frustrated and will soon move onto other venues rather than
wait. We need to get the appointment formalized, and get Don
sworn in so he can participate. Don is a Marine Biologist, and
would be a valuable member of this commission.

DISCUSSION, Item 2: Mark will sign up for an Agriculture
booth and a Conservation booth for Celebrate East Lyme Day.
A wetland display is planned for the Conservation booth, Penny
Heller will try to get the display from the DEEP.

Motion to adjourn made by Mark Christensen, seconded by
Marv Schutt. Vote was 3-0 to adjourn. Meeting adjourned at
8:09 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,

Mark Christensen
