East Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources
Regular Meeting Minutes
September 9, 2015

> CONNECTIGU

Also Present: Rose Ann Hardy, ex officio

Call to Order. Acting Chairlady Heller called the September 9, 2015 Regular Meeting of the East
Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources to order at 7:05 p.m.

I. Minutes.

A. Minutes of May 13, 2015. Ms. Heller asked for additions, deletions or corrections to the May
13, 2015 Regular Meeting Minutes.

The following corrections were noted: On page 1 Mr. Christensen was not present and Mr. Danila
was not sworn in. The vote on motion 3 was 2-0-1.

MOTION (1): Ms. Heller moved to approve the May 13, 2015
Regular Meeting Minutes, as amended. Seconded
by Mr. Christensen. Messrs. Christensen and
Danila were not present but read the minutes.
(3-0) Unanimous.

B. Minutes of August 12, 2015. Ms. Heller asked for additions, deletions or corrections to the
August 12, 2015 Regular Meeting Minutes.

The following correction was noted: On page 1, under A. Minutes of June 10, 2015, the first
sentence to be revised as follows: "There was not an official meeting because the Agenda was not posted
in a timely manner."

MOTION (2): Ms. Heller moved to approve the August 12, 2015
Regular Meeting Minutes, as amended. Seconded
by Mr. Danila. (3-0) Unanimous.

IIL. NEW BUSINESS
Mr. Danila reported on September 14, 2015 beginning at 6 p.m. there will be a public meeting on
Stone's Ranch and Camp Niantic Land Use Study. There are a number of town officials interested in
attending, as well as DEEP. They would like to keep out permanent moorings and large vessels in or
around Camp Niantic. Mr. Danila plans to attend representing the Niantic Watershed Committee. Ms.
Heller expressed interest in attending.

Mr. Danila informed the Commission of a DEEP grant for the control of aquatic species. This
grant will need the support of a municipality. It requires 50% of the cost. The deadline for submitting the

grant is November 1.
IV. OLD BUSINESS.

A. Water Study: Gary Goeschel, Director of Planning to Discuss CT Stormwater
Guidelines. Mr. Goeschel was not present.

Mr. Christensen read Mr. Carlson's notes on the Watershed Study. He wished to emphasize to the
Town the need for a Aquifer Protection Committee. The state has suggested that an Aquifer Protection
Committee be formed. The state has taken out the Aquifer Protection Zone and reduced it to 600' of the
well. Mr. Carlson felt most of the wells have been polluted by road salt. Mr. Danila referred to the
Town's Annual Water Quality Report which states that when the sodium concentration exceeds 28 ppm.
people who have been placed on a sodium-restricted diet should inform their physician. Ms. Heller
expressed concern that our Watershed Protection Area is in the middle of the Gateway Project parking lot.
Ms. Hardy informed the Commission Gateway would like to increase the apartments to 400. Mr.
Christiansen reported that Mr. Carlson wishes to stress that aquifer and watershed go hand in hand.

VI. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS.

A. Communications. Mr. Christensen read his email to Kevin Nursick DOT spokesman
and his reply regarding exit 74. Mr. Christensen asked if a hazardous spill containment/mitigation system
to protect our aquifer has been designed into the exit 74 reconstruction plans. Mr. Nursick informed him
there is only a concept design, and that the design is 0% complete.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported Ms. Smith will apply for the
Farmer's Market Grant.

C. Bio-Rapid Assessment Report. Ms. Heller reported we are unable to sample without
sufficient water.

D. Chairman’s Report. Mr. Carlson was not present, and there was no report.

E. Ex-officio. Mr. Christensen asked if the Selectmen have any input on exit 74? Ms.
Hardy replied this is under the jurisdiction of the state.

VII. Adjournment.

MOTION (3): Mr. Christensen moved to adjourn the September 9, 2015
Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources
at 8:20 p.m. Seconded by Mr. Danila. (3-0) Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
