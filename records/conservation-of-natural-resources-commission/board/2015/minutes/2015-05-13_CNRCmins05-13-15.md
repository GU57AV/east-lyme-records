IV. OLD BUSINESS,

A. Continuation of Water Study. Ms. Heller has done research but requested more
guidance. Messrs. Carlson and Nichols had discussion with Mr. Kargl regarding aquifer protection.

Vv. NEW BUSINESS.

A. Cell Tower. Ms. Heller reported residents of Ancient Highway asked her to discuss
the cell tower with this Commission and report back to them. Mr. Carlson discussed software that would
help. Mr. Danila stated the Citing Council is obligated to have a hearing. Ms. Heller stated they will not

have a hearing, because it is supposed to be a temporary tower.

VI. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS.
A. Communications. There were no new communications.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported via email that the
Agribusiness Subcommittee plans to enter a float, maybe a tractor as well. The theme will be
"Beekeeping". Their reasoning is that we want to promote beekeeping.

We will be hosting “Open Farm Day” on July 19. Two venues are confirmed thus far, Brubaker
Victory Garden, the Samuel Smith House and possibly McTurk's Donkey Herd.

C. Rapid-Bio Assessment Report. Ms. Heller will write up a report on the findings.

D. Chairman’s Report. Mr. Carlson informed the Commission a report has been
received from the state.

E. Ex-officio, Ms. Hardy was unable to be present, and there was no report.

MOTION (3): Mr. Nichols moved to adjourn the May 13, 2015
Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources
at 8:15 p.m. Seconded by Ms. Heller.
