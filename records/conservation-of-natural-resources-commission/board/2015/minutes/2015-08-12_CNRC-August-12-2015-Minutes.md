A. Judy Rondeau of the Eastern Connecticut Conservation District Presentation and
Discussion on manmade wetlands.
Ms. Rondeau reviewed Best Management Practices for Storm Water Management and detailed various
examples of Non-Point Source Pollution (NPS.)

The goal of these Best Management Practices is long term water supply protection. Mr. Carlson noted
that industrial grade protection is desired. Ms. Rondeau’s presentation included:

¢ Tree Filters

A. Water Study

The Commission discussed the Storm Water Vulnerability Assessment of the Niantic River Watershed.
Mr. Carlson suggested having Kleinschmidt Associates look at the other watersheds that the Commission
is concerned about.

V. Reports and Communications
A. Communications

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee
Mr. Christensen reported on the good turnout for “Open Farm Day” on July 19, 2015. Between the
Victory Garden, the Samuel Smith House and McTurk’s Donkey Herd, there were 350 guests.

Mr. Christensen also shared details regarding the bicycle race fundraiser; the idea is to showcase the
northern areas of Town. Profit is not anticipated for the first few years.
