attendees. Tilapia aquaponics equipment overwintered, to be built.

East Lyme Middle School Garden: Student garden plans complete. Award-winning
design to be determined.

Harbor Management Report: T. Londregan to seed shellfish beds in June 2015.
Memorial Day Parade: Monday, May 25, 2015. Possible goat float planning, bee
theme, tractors.

Bride Brook Tree Nursery: 103 trees, 50% died over winter. Need guidance on care
and irrigation of remaining trees. Mark available to go on-site.

Honeybee Education: Resident told incorrectly that she was not allowed to keep bees.

Need for representation and education. Tom and Steve to research and address.
Mark A. Hightower. CPA, P.C.

East Lyme Agriculture in Photography: B. DeSantos took more photos, more
scheduled. Favorites to be chosen and provided for town website and print.
State-wide Issues: Steve Dinsmore attended CT Ag Day, got a photo and autograph
from the 2015 Honey Queen. Governor and St. Senator Formica attended. CT New
Farmer Alliance resource available, Miko joined as a member. Proposal to ban all
pesticides on CT State-owned land. Contraindications include Rentschler Field,

benzenes emitted from AstroTurf, and UCONN’s fields where pesticides are tested.
