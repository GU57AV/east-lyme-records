Call to Order. Chairman Carlson called the March 11, 2015 Regular Meeting of the East Lyme
Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources to order at 7:02 p.m.

I. Additions to the Agenda.

MOTION (1): Mr. Nichols moved to add Under New Business
item A. Interaction with Other Commissions and
B. Forest Management. Seconded by Ms. Heller.
(4-0) Unanimous.

II. Public Delegations. There were no guests.
Commission member Mark Christensen arrived.
TIT. Minutes.

A. Minutes of February 11, 2015. Mr. Carlson asked for additions, deletions or
corrections to the February 11, 2015 Regular Meeting Minutes.

The following corrections were noted: On page 3, the ninth line from the top, the second sentence
should read: "The NEC require large habitat patches comprised of field shrubs between the ages of 3 and
15 years." It was also felt that the email from Mark Christensen to DOT and response from Kevin
Nursick should be attached to the February Minutes.

MOTION (2): Mr. Christensen moved to accept the February 11,
2015 Regular Meeting Minutes of the East Lyme
Commission for the Conservation of Natural
Resources, as amended. Seconded by Mr. Nichols.
(4-0) Unanimous.

IV. NEW BUSINESS.
A. Interaction with Other Commissions. Mr. Carlson spoke to the Chairman of the

Planning Commission about this Commission meeting with Planning. There was a general consensus that
this was a good idea. Mr. Carlson agreed to set up a meeting with them.
B. Forest Management. The Commission reviewed the plan of DEEP Foresters.

V. OLD BUSINESS.

A. Continuation of Water Study Results to Date. Mr. Carlson reported Zoning has
limited aquifer protection rules, The State of Connecticut has determined the size of the aquifer
protection zone. Buildings and houses have a negative affect on our water quality and quantity. Open
space does not have a negative affect. The Tylerville section of East Haddam and the Town of Moodus
have to use bottled water due to contamination of their water supply.

VI. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS.

A. Communications. Tom Kalal notified this Commission of his resignation. A thank
you letter for his service will be sent.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported we received a grant and are
now able to prepare a brochure advertising agriculture as well as develop a website.

C. Rapid-Bio Assessment Report. Ms. Heller reported we received the results of the
samples which she shared with the Commission.

D. Chairman’s Report. Mr. Carlson reported we need outhouses for the Goodwin trail
and a table with a gable roof. He suggested moldering outhouses. He has contacted Ledge Light
concerning the outhouses. He received a plan.

E. Ex-officio. Mr. Christensen reported Ms. Hardy recommended lobbying.
VII. Adjournment.
MOTION (3): Ms. Heller moved to adjourn the March 11,
2015 Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources

at 8:30 p.m. Seconded by Mr. Nichols. (4-0)
Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
