East Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources
Regular Meeting Minutes
January 11, 2012

Present: Arthur Carlson, Chairman
Marvin Schutt, Member
Tom Kalal, Member
Mark Christiansen, Member
Penny Heller, Member

Absent: Ron Nichols, Alternate

Also Present: Gary Goeschel, Planning Director
Rose Ann Hardy

Call te Order. Chairman Carlson called the January 11, 2012 Regular Meeting
of the East Lyme Conservation of Natural Resources to order at 7:00 p.m.

Mr. Carlson asked for nominations for Chairman of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources for 2012.

MOTION (1): Mr. Christiansen moved to nominate Art Carlson
as Chairman of the East Lyme Commission for the
Conservation of Natural Resources for 2012.
Seconded by Mr. Schutt. (4-0) Unanimous.

There were no other nominations.

MOTION (2): Mr. Christiansen moved to close nominations
for Chairman of the East Lyme Commission for the
Conservation of Natural Resources for 2012.
Seconded by Mr. Schutt. (4-0) Unanimous.

A vote was taxen and Mr. Carlson was unanimously elected Chairman of the East
Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources for 2012.

Mr. Carlson asked for nominations for Secretary of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources for 2012.

MOTION (3): Mr. Christiansen moved to nominate Penny Heller
as Secretary of the East Lyme Commission for the
Conservation of Natural Resources for 2012.
Seconded by Mr. Schutt. (4-0) Unanimous.

There were no other nominations.
MOTION (4): Mr. Christiansen moved to close nominations
for Secretary of the East Lyme Commission for
the Conservation of Natural Resources for 2012.
Seconded by Mr. Schutt. (4-0) Unanimous.

A vote was taken and Penny Heller was unanimously elected Secretary of the East
Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources for 2012.

I. Additions to the Agenda.

MOTION (5): Mr. Carlson moved to add to this
evening’s Agenda under Old Business
Foot Paths (Yale Update). Seconded by
Mr. Schutt. (4-0) Unanimous.

II. Public Delegations. There were no public delegations.
Ill. Minutes.

A. Minutes of December 14, 2011. Mr. Carlson asked for additions,
deletions or corrections to the December 14, 2011 East Lyme Commission for the
Conservation of Natural Resources Regular Meeting Minutes.

The following correction was noted: On page 2, under item A. 1. Mr. Migdalski’s
name was incorrectly spelled.

MOTION (6): Mr. Schutt moved to accept the
December 14, 2011 East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources
Regular Meeting, as amended. Seconded
by Mr. Christensen. (4-0) Unanimous.

IV. Old Business.
A. Nature of East Lyme Programs.

i. Foot Paths (Niantic Forest Update). Mr. Carlson discussed the
method to be used to blaze the Niantic Forest with the DEEP Park Ranger. He agreed.
with the method we plan to use. Mr. Christiansen reported an individual is willing to use
his excavator at no charge for new trails. Mr. Carlson felt the Town should take

advantage of his offer.

Yale Property. Mr. Carlson reported he met with Mr. Migdalski
who supports foot paths in the Yale outdoor recreation area,

Town Attorney is reviewing a draft lease.
ii. Forest Management. Mr. Carlson reported a State Forester
will walk Darrow Pond with this Commission and inform it of the health of the forest.
He read from the Eastern Connecticut Forest Landowners Association Newsletter 2003.

Rose Ann Hardy arrived.

iii, Rapid Bio-Assessment. Mr. Carlson reported the Water
Department has been measuring water run off in East Lyme for several years and ten
years of data is available.

The Commission reviewed a chart of Natural Resource Elements at locations in
East Lyme and a map of the Open Space Plan.

Mr. Goeschel reported the Nature Conservancy is concerned about sea level
rising, East Lyme is trying to determine the Town’s risks and what it should do long
term, The Planning Commission has Climate Adaptation and Sustainability
Subcommittee. It would be helpful to have a liaison from this Commission to work
together on it. Ms. Heller agreed to work with them.

Mr. Christiansen reported there is a new program for small farms. The Town
would need to partner with them.

B. Open Space Plan Updates and Corrections. Mr. Goeschel will
finalize and republish the Open Space Plan.

C. Uses of Darrow Pond. Mr. Christiansen felt hay would be the best
use on the Darrow Pond property.

Mr. Carlson reported Mr. Gephard of DEEP felt there was great potential for
Alewife living in Darrow Pond. Penny Heller informed the Commission that Mr.
Gephard stated to her he will put in a fishway when the Town works on the road.

D. Forest Forum Workshop. Mr. Carlson recommended having the
State Forester come to East Lyme in March and have a Forest Forum Workshop at the
Community Center.

V. New Business

A. Budget. Mr. Goeschel presented a draft budget for this Commission.
Mr. Carlson suggested reducing Meetings/Conferences/Training/Dues line item by $200
and adding $200 to the Printing and Brochures line item.

MOTION (7): Mr. Christiansen move to approved the proposed
Operating Budget for the Conservation of
Natural Resources Commission for 2012/2013
in the amount of $2,100, after reducing Meeting/

Conferences/Training/Dues line item by $200 and
adding $200 to Printing and Brochures line item.
Seconded by Mr. Schutt. (4-0) Unanimous.

Commission members would like the Town to hire a staff person to assist with
grant writing and for open space consulting. It was felt that this Commission should
submit an addendum to its budget in order to accomplish what it needs that it is unable to
do as volunteers.

B. Public Communication/Outreach. Mr. Christiansen reported a
Solar Park in an open space area is being reviewed. He visited a Solar Park in
Massachusetts. He did not feel this was a good idea if a zone change to commercial is
made. Rose Ann Hardy asked if solar panels can be placed in an open space area? Mr.
Goeschel agreed to check on the use of the land.

Tom Kalal arrived.
VI. Reports and Communications

B. Staff/Communications. Mr. Schutt asked Mr. Goeschel if money has
been made available for the purchase of open space? Mr. Goeschel replied $30,000 has
been added to the Capital Improvement Plan.

MOTION (8): Mr. Kalal meved to authorize the Chairman to
add an addendum to the budget outlining other
fiscal requests to support this Commission.
Seconded by Penny Heller. (5-0) Unanimous.

C, Chairman’s Report. Mr. Carlson recommended that this
Commission advertise upcoming events on Channel 22 or Channel 24.

Mr. Carlson read from Connecticut Statues §7-13 1a.

Mr. Carlson suggested participating in the Connecticut Forest and Park
Association Connecticut Trails Day celebration on June 2-3. There was a general
consensus that this was a good idea.

D. —_ Ex-officio. Rose Ann Hardy reported Penny Heller is now a full
time member of this Commission. Ron Nichols has been reappointed as an Alternate. A
new alternate has also been appointed.

Marvin Schutt will be honored by the Rotary on February 11 at the Marriott for
his community efforts.

VIL. Adjournment.
MOTION (9): Mr. Schutt move to adjourn the January 11, 2012,
Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources
at 9:25 p.m. Seconded by Mr. Christiansen, (5-0)
Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
