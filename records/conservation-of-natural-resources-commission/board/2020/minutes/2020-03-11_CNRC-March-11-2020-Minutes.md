      Waterford Town Hall, Large Conference Room, on March 31 at 3:00 p.m. GRE GACRUX,
      LLC, the Town of Waterford, and STR-STH will each have the opportunity to introduce
      exhibits and witnesses and conduct cross-examinations. After a short break, the hearing will
      be followed by a brief presentation by the petitioner and a public comment session at 6:30
      p.m. Public officials will be provided the opportunity to speak prior to the individuals.
      Should a large crowd be expected, the meeting will be moved to the Waterford High School
      Auditorium. Should a second hearing be necessary, it will be held at the CT Siting Council
      offices in New Britain, where a live audio feed will be available.
      Depending upon the progress of the Coronavirus, the March 3l public meeting may be
      canceled. The deadline for the submission of questions, pre-filed testimony and responses
      will not change. By Statute, the final decision must be made on August 31.
      Because the STR-STH is the intervenor, the members may not speak on behalf of the
      organization, but may make comments pro se, i.e., "in one's own behalf'. As such, they are
      in the process ofengaging the services ofan attorney to speak on their behalf.

        The Committee is also in the early planning stages of starting a communify garden in
