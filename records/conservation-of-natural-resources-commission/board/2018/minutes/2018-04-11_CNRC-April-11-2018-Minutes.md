Seconded by Ms. Ashburn. (5-0) Unanimous.

III. NEW BUSINESS

A. Pitch Pine Tree Farm. Ms. Heller reported the Friends of Oswegachie Hills are
requesting that this Commission endorse the development of the north end of Veterans
Memorial Park as a tree nursery for pitch pine trees. The President of Oswegachie Hills asked
for our endorsement.
Mr. Danila felt they would need to go to Inland Wetlands for approval.

Ms. Heller stated they would need to chop down the weeds, put black plastic down and add
soil as needed. They will build raised beds. The Town will only need to mow the area. All
equipment will be donated at no cost to the Town.

Judy Roundeau has a grant to build 100 rain gardens.

MOTION (3): Ms. Heller moved that the Conservation
of Natural Resources endorse the
development of a tree nursery for the
imperiled pitch pine and an adjacent
rain garden on the northern portion of
Veteran's Memorial Park overflow
parking area. Seconded by
Mr. Christiansen. (5-0) Unanimous.

IV. OLD BUSINESS

A. Progress in Putting Water Study Report Together. Mr. Carlson reported sections 1,
2 and 3 have been completed. He gave Ms. Heller section 4. Mr. Nichols asked if the

Commission will be able to see a draft of the Water Study Report? Mr. Carlson informed him
they will be involved in all aspects of assembling the final report.

B. Work on the Goodwin Trail. Mr. Carlson reported one-half a mile of Forest Road
south of Grassy Hill Road has been rehabilitated as the first one-fourth mile of the trail from
the Darrow Pond parking lot.

C. Farm Text Amendments. Messrs. Christiansen, Carlson, Mulholland and Walker
discussed the text amendment for honey bees.

There was discussion on music for special events at farms. The original proposal
called for farms of five acres, and it has been increased to ten acres. Messrs. Mulholland and
Walker will review the proposal.

D. SustainabilityCT.org. Ms. Ashburn asked that this item be kept on future agendas.

E. Website Development. Ms. Ashburn felt that information on Backyard Stream and
Pond Buffers can be placed on our website. Ms. Heller suggested a link to the Nature
Conservancy, because it has a lot of mapping. She suggested writing an explanation to make it
easier for residents to understand it. Coastal Resistance at Avery Point can assist the Town.

V. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS
A. Communications. Mr. Danila reported the Connecticut State Water Plan is under
consideration by a joint hearing by the Environment, Planning and Development, Public
Health and Energy and Technology committees on April 17. Comments on the State Water
Plan will be accepted until 2 p.m. on Friday April 13.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christiansen reported the Agribusiness
Subcommittee plans to be in the Memorial Day Parade. They would like to feature
acquaculture. The Agribusiness Subcommittee will also be involved on Celebrate East Lyme
and Open Farm Day.

He attended a Agribusiness meeting in Canterbury with several other agribusiness groups.
They all want to preserve acquaculture. The Agribusiness Subcommittee would like to have a
speaker at its November meeting on Conservation Options and Land Preservation.

C. Chairman's Report. Mr. Carlson reported he will meet with State Forester Dick
Raymond on April 13 to finish the report of the Forest Management Plan for Darrow Pond.
This report is part of the scientific Forest Management Plan that the Commission has started.
Messrs. Carlson and Goeschel will lead a hike on June 2 at Darrow Pond.

D. Ex-officio Report. Ms. Hardy will take the Commission's letter to the Board of
Selectmen regarding I-95 Exit 74 spill mitigation/containment.

VI. ADJOURNMENT
MOTION (4): Ms. Heller moved to adjourn the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources at 8:22 p.m.

Seconded by Mr. Danila. (5-0) Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
