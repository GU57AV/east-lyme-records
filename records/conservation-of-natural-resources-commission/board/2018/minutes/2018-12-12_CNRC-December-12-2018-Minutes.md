NEW BUSINESS

A. Follow up with Zoning on Status of Beekeeping Ordinance and Music Ordinance.

Mr. Carlson asked for an update on the status of the beekeeping and music ordinances. Mr.

The Music Ordinance is on hold. Mr. Christensen will meet with a Zoning Subcommittee on

A. Progress in Putting Water Study Report Together. The Water Study Report is almost

B. Work on the Goodwin Trail. Mr. Carlson reported the Engineering Department has two

C. SustainabilityCT.org
Ms. Heller agreed to contact Ms. Hardy regarding this matter.

e Update to this Commission on Solar Energy Project Development in Waterford. Mr.
Danila attended the December 6 meeting to reconsider the Siting Council’s ruling on
Greenskies’ Petition for Reconsideration. The applicant did not have a proper wildlife
survey as noted by CT DEEP. Save the River, Save the Hills also submitted comments. A
geotechnical plan on storm water was not submitted. The Siting Council denied the
request without prejudice so that the applicant can continue the process but would
need to complete a geotechnical plan and wildlife survey. The Siting Council previously
toured the area and spotted a ribbon snake, which is state threatened. Mr. Danila felt
they are not using the most modern methods for correctly handing stormwater.

Mr. Christensen reported about Mr. Bialowans, whose land abuts the area Greenskies
previously developed in East Lyme, had massive erosion and large gullies after the solar
field next to his property was clear cut.

Mr. Danila reported there will be a Special Water & Sewer meeting on December 18
with respect to the sewer capacity for the housing project proposed in the Oswegatchie
Hills.
