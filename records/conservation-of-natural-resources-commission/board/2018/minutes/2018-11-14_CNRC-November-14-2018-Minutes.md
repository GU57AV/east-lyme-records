D. Update to this Commission on Solar Energy Project Development in Waterford. Mr.
Danila attended a meeting of the Connecticut Siting Council on October 25 regarding
the ruling of the Council on the proposed Waterford solar energy project. A number of
Council members were concerned about this project. As noted by CT DEEP there was no
wildlife survey conducted for this project. Some wanted to see a geotechnical plan on
storm water. There was concern that the present owner performed logging throughout
the site before the Council made its ruling. Some Council members observed the cutting
of smaller trees than had been mentioned by the applicant in its petition. As a result of
these reasons, the Council voted to deny the Certificate for a Declaratory Ruling which is
needed before they can proceed with this project. On October 26 a letter was made
public denying the petition because of a lack of a wildlife survey. The applicant’s
attorney submitted a request to overturn this ruling. This will be undertaken by the
Council on December 6. Save the River-Save the Hills will be submitting comments on
this action. Mr. Danila is helping Save the River, Save the Hills in this matter. They also
have a very good engineering consultant with a lot of experience. Mr. Danila plans to
attend the December Council meeting to reconsider the ruling.
