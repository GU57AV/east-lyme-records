B. Agribusiness Subcommittee
Commissioner Christiansen recited pertinent portions of a letter written by CT Beekeepers
Association President, East Lyme Resident, and 20-year Beekeeper Stephen Dinsmore
regarding the institution of a honeybee regulation. The Commission reviewed the
regulation, which requires beekeepers to take a basic beekeeping course and pledge of
adherence to the best management practices. Terms included in the regulation will be
referenced to the Connecticut Beekeepers Association’s Best Beekeeping Practices for
Connecticut. Chairman Carlson will investigate the process to have the regulation added to
the Town’s Zoning Regulations.
Regarding East Lyme’s Fair Day, Commissioner Christiansen reported on plans to host a
Tractor Ride with an aquaculture theme, highlighting the Blackhawk Fishing Vessel. Plans
also include a presentation dealing with oysters by Tim Londregan and Rickety Pickets
Community Garden. The Friends of Samuel Smith will also be hosting a Farm Day,
highlighting farming equipment.

D. Chairman’s Report
Chairman Carlson reported that nine (9) water bars were placed at Darrell Pond two weeks
ago and is hoping to begin placing 18 (eighteen) additional water bars at the Niantic Forest.
