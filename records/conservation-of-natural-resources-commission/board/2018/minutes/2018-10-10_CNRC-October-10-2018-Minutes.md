Ms. Heller reported she received an email from Judy Rondeau regarding a presentation

Watershed Committee and Save the River, Save the Hills have commented on it. He
suggested that the Commission review the Connecticut Siting Council Petition No. 1347.
Mr. Christensen stated the logger will need a wetlands permit.

D. Ex-Officio Report. Ms. Hardy reported the Zoning Commission was going to come in
with a plan for COSTCO. After the issue was reviewed they did not go forward.
Corrections are needed. The focus was on the building itself and the fuel tanks. She felt
the parking lot needs to be reviewed. They are requesting 700 parking spaces.

Ms. Heller asked what is the status of the driving range? Ms. Hardy replied the owner is
withdrawing his contract. She added expansion of the apartments had a run off issue.
Ms. Hardy added the other concern is in the initial proposal there was to be only two-
bedroom units. The expansion requests that one-half of the units will be three
bedrooms and/or stand-alone units.

Mr. Beeman reported he has attended planning and wetlands meetings recently.
Oswegatchie Nature Reserve pitch pine went to Inland Wetlands and was accepted on
September 17.

At the Planning Commission Special Meeting on September 10, there was a
representative that discussed the Sustainability Program. They had a consultant from
Boston. They plan to have a Rain Barrel Workshop on Saturday October 20 at Perennial
Harmony on Boston Post Road and are looking for input from the community.

Mr. Danila reviewed the Master Plain for Stones Ranch and Camp Niantic and
distributed it to members of this Commission. He suggests that they review the
stormwater plan.

Ms. Ashburn reported last month we discussed the I-95 exit ramp memo and hazardous
mitigation. Ms. Hardy agreed to speak to Mark Nickerson regarding this matter.
