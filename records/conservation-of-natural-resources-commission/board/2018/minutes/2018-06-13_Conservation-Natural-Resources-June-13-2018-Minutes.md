EAST LYME COMMISSION FOR THE CONSERVATION OF NATURAL RESOURCES REGULAR MEETING OF
JUNE 13, 2018

Present: Arthur Carlson, Chairman
Don Danila
Penny Heller, Secretary
Mark Christiansen

Laura Ashburn
FILED IN EAST LYME

CONNECTICUT
Alternate: Harvey Beeman FJunrS20 18 aTILSS Gale

Absent: Ron Nichols, Alternate EAST LYM EWN CLERK

CALL TO ORDER
Chairman Carlson called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m.

I.PUBLIC DELEGATIONS
There were no guests.

H.MINUTES
Commission member Mark Christiansen arrived.

A. Minutes of May 9, 2018. Mr. Carlson asked for additions, deletions or corrections to the May 9,
2018 Regular Meeting Minutes of the East Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural
Resources.

The following corrections were noted: On page 2 under item C. the paragraph is to be rewritten
as follows: “Ms. Heller reported there are three grant organizations through Eastern Connecticut
State College this Commission can apply for following certification. In order to receive these
grants and certification, we will need a consensus from other town commissions.”

On page 2 the first sentence of the second paragraph in item B. to be rewritten as follows: “The
Agribusiness Subcommittee will have a booth on July 21 at Celebrate East Lyme promoting shell
fishing”.

MOTION (1): Mr. Danila moved to accept the May 9, 2018 East Lyme Commission for the
Conservation of Natural Resources Minutes, as amended. Seconded by Ms. Ashburn. (5-0)
Unanimous.

Ill, NEW BUSINESS
MOTION (2): Mr. Christiansen moved to add Discussion of paying for Zoning change as
item 1, Under New Business. Seconded by Ms. Heller. (5-0) Unanimous.

1. Paying for Zoning Change. Mr. Carlson and Mr. Christiansen received conflicting information
regarding whether or not this Commission needs to pay a fee for a zoning change. Ms. Hardy agreed
to look into this matter and suggested encumbering funds for this fee.

MOTION (3): Ms. Ashburn moved that if it is determined that this Commission must pay a fee for
the Beekeeping Zoning Regulation change that we determine if we have sufficient funds remaining
in this Commission’s budget. (5) Unanimous.

Mr. Christiansen reported the suggested amendment for music at a farm will be held at the July Zoning
Commission Meeting.

IV. OLD BUSINESS

A. Progress in Putting Water Study Report Together. Ms. Heller distributed copies of the work
thus far on the Water Study Report for this Commission’s review.

B. Work on the Goodwin Trail. Mr. Carlson reported much has been accomplished on the
Goodwin Trail. Invasive species were removed from four acres. This work was
recommended by the State Forester and was approved by DEEP. Semi-decayed wood chips
were spread from the parking lot to the field. Twenty-nine water bars were put in. Mr.
Christiansen felt the Goodwin Trail is getting much use.

C. SustainabilityCT.org. Mr. Carlson was informed by 2050 the sea level is expected to rise 18”
to 20” higher. Mr. Danila informed the Commission Sen. Murphy will have a workshop in
this area. He agreed to provide information to any Commission member who wished to
attend.

Ms. Heller reported the Nature Conservancy has a sustainability kit to guide the Town or a
landowner on good practices. Mr. Carlson added this will affect Zoning Regulations.

V. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS

A. Communications. There were no communications.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christiansen reported the Agribusiness Subcommittee
were in the Memorial Day Parade. The theme was farming in the sea. The crowd seemed to
enjoy it. The subcommittee would like to send another letter to WELSCO. Mr. Danila has
attended WELSCO meetings and stated they are revising their Management Plan. Mr.
Carlson stated shell fishing cleans the water and provides more shellfish for recreational
activities. Mr. Danila felt it was good for cleaner water, recreational shell fishing, the
economy and it is a non-polluting industry. There was a general consensus of the
Commission to support this type of shell fishing in this environment and to send a letter to
WELSCO, as well as a letter to the editor.

Mr. Christiansen reported some members of the Agribusiness Subcommittee will be
involved in Celebrate East Lyme. We will also have three open farms on Open Farm Day and
Blackhawk Fishing. Mr. Londregan has agreed to demonstrate how oysters are harvested.
Rickety Pickets gardens will also be involved.

Mr. Christiansen stated the Agribusiness Subcommittee will be charged $40 for a booth at
Celebrate East Lyme. Mr. Kahal had discussion with Chief Finkelstein regarding hay rides and
the Chief had no objection to them. Mr. Nickerson would prefer no hay rides. Mr.
Christiansen will attend a Police Commission Meeting at 6 p.m. on June 14. He has been
informed a state permit is needed in order to have hay rides. The senior bus or a trolley was
suggested to shuttle individuals.

Historical Properties will have a booth for the three historical houses in town and will also
pay $40 for the booth.

C. Chairman’s Report. Mr. Carlson discussed Water for Hartford about the early 1800s drinking
water supply from the stream and that after houses were built upstream they had to find
another source of water.

D. Ex-Officio Report. Ms. Hardy outlined a letter regarding Hazard Litigation for Mr.
Nickerson’s review and suggested that all Selectmen sign it. Ms. Hardy received a telephone

call from a reporter who was interested in a story on this matter. There is concern in the
area of I-95 and major business coming in over the aquifer.

Vi. ADJOURNMENT.
MOTION (4): Mr. Danila moved to adjourn the June 13, 2018 East Lyme Commission for the
Conservation of Natural Resources at 8:25 p.m. Seconded by Ms. Heller. (5-0) Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
