EAST LYME COMMISSION FOR THE CONSERVATION OF NATURAL RESOURCES

Also Present: Rose Ann Hardy, Ex-officio

CALL TO ORDER
Chairman Carlson called the meeting to order at 7:05 p.m.

I. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS
There were no guests.

Il. MINUTES

A. Minutes of December 13, 2017. Mr. Carlson asked for additions, deletions or
corrections to the December 13, 2017 Regular Meeting Minutes of the East Lyme
Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources.

MOTION (1): Ms. Ashburn moved to approve the December 13, 2017
Regular Meeting Minutes of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources. Seconded
by Ms. Heller. Mr. Christensen abstained from voting.
(4-0-1) Motion carried.

II. NEW BUSINESS

A. Election of Officers for 2018. The Commission was asked for nominations for
Chairperson of the East Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources for
2018.

MOTION (2): Mr. Christensen moved to nominate Art Carlson as
Chairman of the East Lyme Commission for the
Conservation of Natural Resources for 2018.

There were no other nominations.

A vote was taken and Mr. Carlson was unanimously elected Chairman of the East Lyme
Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources for 2018.

The Commission was asked for nominations for Secretary of the East Lyme Commission for
the Conservation of Natural Resources for 2018.

MOTION (3): Mr. Christensen moved to nominate Penny Heller
as Secretary of the East Lyme Commission for the
Conservation of Natural Resources for 2018.

There were no other nominations.

A vote was taken and Penny Heller was unanimously elected Secretary of the East Lyme
Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources for 2018.

B. New Initiatives. Mr. Carlson asked the Commission for comments on new
initiatives suggested by Ron Nichols.

After much discussion, it was decided to add information on invasive species, surface water
quality, sea level rise problems, to the Town's website, as this is the most central access point
for the Town boards, commissions and citizens.

Ms. Ashburn drafted a letter to Ms. Cori Rose of the Army Corps of Engineers regarding our
position on oyster farming in the Niantic River in which we are in favor of. The Commission
made edits and Ms. Ashburn agreed to revise it.

IV. OLD BUSINESS
A. Outline of Water Study Report. Mr. Carlson distributed copies of a draft outline of
the Water Study Report.

V. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS

A. Communications. There were no communications received.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported Mr. Dinsmore, President of
Beekeeping Association, discussed an ordinance for beekeeping. Beekeeping should be an
accessory use on residential property. The smaller lot would be allowed fewer hives. Lots
greater than one acre would not be limited. He asked that this Commission sponsor this
amendment as accessory use for beekeepers. Ms. Hardy suggested that a letter be written by
this Commission requesting an amendment to the Town's regulations. Mr. Danila was in favor
of it. He asked that colony and nucleus colony be defined and that the Connecticut's Best
Management Practice be included. Mr. Christensen replied Connecticut's Best Management
Practice would also be submitted.

Mr. Christensen felt there is a need to have another Open Forum on improvements to the
interstate. He felt the state must put in a spill mitigation containment. He suggested that this
Commission write a letter to the Board of Selectmen and/or DOT concerning this matter. Ms.
Hardy agreed to bring the matter up to the Selectmen. The Commission agreed to write a
letter to the Board of Selectmen. Mr. Christensen referred to letter dated December 4, 2008 to
Gregory Leonard, General Manager, Southeastern Connecticut Water Authority, regarding
Miller Pond Public Water Supply Stainability.

Ms. Heller and Ms. Ashburn agreed to draft a letter.
C. Chairman's Report. Mr. Carlson referred to Section II of the outline of the Water
Study Report which referred to an article written for the Nature Conservancy summer 2017
issue entitled "Liquid Assets". It states by maintaining the upstream land in as natural a state
as possible you minimize the chance of bringing contaminates into the aquifer and/or your
house.
D. Ex-officio Report. There was no report.
VI. ADJOURNMENT
MOTION (4): Mr. Danila moved to adjourn the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources at 8:45 p.m.
Seconded by Ms. Heller. (5-0) Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
