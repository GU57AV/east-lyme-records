Ili. NEW BUSINESS
There was no discussion of new business.

IV. OLD BUSINESS

A. Progress in Putting Water Study Report Together. Mr. Carlson and Ms. Heller
have been making progress on the Water Study Report. Input was received from members of
this Commission.

B. Work on the Goodwin Trail. Mr. Carlson reported with $10,000 remaining for work
on the Goodwin Trail, the Forester suggested that we rehabilitate the four acres of field that
was formerly a pasture. DEEP agreed with this suggestion.

Mr. Carlson reported he met with the Eagle Scout who will put in an outhouse at the campsite.

C. SustainabilityCT.org. Ms. Heller reported there are three grant organizations
through Eastern Connecticut State College this Commission can apply for. In order to receive
these grants, we will need a consensus from other town commissions.

V. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS
A. Communications. Mr. Carlson informed the Commission on communications

received.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christiansen reported the Agribusiness
Subcommittee will enter a float on Tim Londregan's oyster boat in the Memorial Day Parade.
They will also have several tractors. Mr. Kalal will take care of advertising in local

newspapers.

The Agribusiness Subcommittee will have a booth on July 21 at Celebrate East Lyme
promoting oystermen. On July 22 we will have Open Farm Day including the Black Hawk
and the Community Garden by Rickey Pickets. First Selectman Mark Nickerson and the
Police Chief will allow hayrides.

Messrs. Carlson and Christiansen met with the Zoning Commission regarding regulations on
bees and music at farms.

C. Chairman's Report. Mr. Carlson had nothing further to report.
D. Ex-officio Report. Ms. Hardy was unable to be present and there was no report.
VI. ADJOURNMENT
MOTION (2): Ms. Heller moved to adjourn the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources of May 9, 2018
at 8:30 p.m. Seconded by Ms. Ashburn. (5-0) Unanimous.
