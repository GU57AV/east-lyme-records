Iv. OLD BUSINESS

A. Progress in Putting Water Study Report Together. Ms. Heller distributed copies of
the work thus far on the Water Study Report for this Commission’s review.

B. Work on the Goodwin Trail. Mr. Carlson reported much has been accomplished on
the Goodwin Trail. Invasive species were removed from four acres. This work was
recommended by the State Forester and was approved by DEEP. Semi-decayed
wood chips were spread from the parking lot to the field. Twenty-nine water bars
were put in. Mr. Christiansen felt the Goodwin Trail is getting much use.

C. SustainabilityCT.org. Mr. Carlson was informed by 2050 the sea level is expected to
rise 18” to 20” higher. Mr. Danila informed the Commission Sen. Murphy will have a
workshop in Essex on June 15. He agreed to provide information to any Commission
member who wished to attend.

Ms. Heller reported the Nature Conservancy has a sustainability kit to guide the
Town or a landowner on good practices. Mr. Carlson added this will affect Zoning
Regulations.
V. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS

A. Communications. There were no communications.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christiansen reported the members of the
Agribusiness Subcommittee were in the Memorial Day Parade. The theme was
farming in the sea. The crowd seemed to enjoy it. The subcommittee would like to
send another letter to WELSCO. Mr. Danila has attended WELSCO meetings and
stated they are revising their Management Plan. Mr. Carlson stated shell fishing
cleans the water and provides more shellfish for recreational activities. Mr. Danila
felt it was good for cleaner water, recreational shell fishing, the economy and it is a
non-polluting industry. There was a general consensus of the Commission to support
this type of shell fishing in this environment and to send a letter to WELSCO, as well
as a letter to the editor of the New London Day.

Mr. Christiansen reported some members of the Agribusiness Subcommittee will be
involved in Celebrate East Lyme. We will also have three open farms (Niantic Bay
Shellfishing, Black Hawk fishing and Rickety Pickets, all in Niantic) on Open Farm Day.
Mr. Londregan has agreed to demonstrate how oysters are harvested. Rickety
Pickets gardens will also be involved.

Mr. Christiansen stated the Agribusiness Subcommittee will be charged $40 for a
booth at Celebrate East Lyme. Mr. Kahal had discussion with Chief Finkelstein
regarding hay rides and the Chief had no objection to them. Mr. Nickerson would
prefer no hay rides. Mr. Christiansen will attend a Police Commission Meeting at 6
p.m. on June 14. He has been informed a state permit is needed in order to have
hay rides. The senior bus or a trolley was suggested to shuttle individuals.

Historical Properties will have a booth for the three historical houses in town and
will also pay $40 for the booth.

C. Chairman’s Report. Mr. Carlson discussed Water for Hartford about the early 1800s
drinking water supply from the Connecticut River, and that after houses were built
upstream they had to find another source of water.

D. Ex-Officio Report. Ms. Hardy outlined a letter regarding Hazard Litigation for Mr.
Nickerson’s review and suggested that all Selectmen sign it. Ms. Hardy received a
telephone call from a reporter who was interested in a story on this matter. There is
concern in the area of I-95 and major business coming in over the aquifer.
