 Surface water runoff and inadequate draitrage were identified as major contributors to soil erosion across the

 other engineered green infrastructure is crucial to stabilizing the landscape.

 Vegetative Cover:

 Maintaining and enhancing the vegetative cover, especially with native species, remains essential for
 controlling soil erosion as well as the uptake of nutrients that will prevent harmful algae blooms in the Lake.
 Vegetation helps stabilize soil, reduce surface runoff velocity, and promote infiltration.

 Conclusion:

 The erosion concems identified during the 2024 site visit to the Pattagansett Art Center closely align with the
 historical challenges noted in the 1985 Environmental Review Team (BnD report. The continuedissues with
 soil erosion, hydrology, and vegetation stability indicate a need for compreheniiu" and sustainable mitigation
 strategies. The observations from the site visit highlight the importance of implementing targeted erosion
 control measures, including the use of permeable pavers, rain gardens, bioswales, and native ptantings, to
 stabilize the landscape and protect the local ecosystem.

pavers to mitigate surface runoff and maintain the integrity of the adjacent wetland areas. Expanding the
                                                                                                             use of

management efforts. Retaining the original parking lot location is ideal, as the existing soil c-ompaction in that
