     a. Updating Salt Report. Ms. Heller reported this Commission has over several years been
     gathering information that Public Works takes once a year on wells in town. We use

     water.

      Mr. Beeman reported he has been in contact with Mr. Lund of East Lyme Public Schools.
      They calibrate how much salt is applied by town trucks so it has lowered effect on school
      property. Ms. Maitland asked if there is a wayto tell if sodium is from road salt or brackish

     b. Update on the March 20 EiJC Public Hearing regarding 2-to-S-acre Zone Change. Ms.
      Heller reported the Zoning Commission voted to approve the zoning change for an area
      north of Grassy Hill Road, a change that this Commission had advocated for. Mr.
      Christensen added an additional need is impervious surface regulations. There was a good
      response from residents at the hearing. There was concern about the land values and
      native plants. Ms. Heller stated there are data that indicate when there is over LO% of

      the lot limiting impervious surface to L5%. Even if road salt is no longer used, it would take
      1-0 years to see improvement.
