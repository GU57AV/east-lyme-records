 VI. New Business
     a. 2026 Budget
        Discussion ensued regarding the Commission's current FY2025126 and proposed
        FY2026127 budgets. Chairperson Heller reported that Director of Planning Gary
        Goeschel requested that the Commission discuss the possibility of adding a line item
        for Trail Development. Discussion ensued regarding Darrow Pond, including its recent
        inappropriate mowing. The Commissioners agreed that site maintenance does not fall
        under the Commission's purview. Commissioner Dinsmore recommended the
        Commission draft a Conservation Plan, outlining the Town's vulnerable/sensitive areas,
        watersheds, areas of concern, conservation easements, open space, etc., and volunteered
        to review the Town's POCD (Plan of Conservation and Development), which includes
        the qualifications for deeming a land valuable and properties that may be eligible for
        open space and the Conservations Plans of other, similar towns. The Plan could be used
        to assist the Commission and support its zoning recommendations. Commissioner
        Beeman concurred, adding that, while the Commission has investigated the Town's
        water management issues and makes recommendations to other municipal agencies
        regarding proposed land use changes, it has lacked in other areas, including educational
        outreach and proposing greenway plans. The item will be added to the Commission's
        future agendas.

        Commissioner Beeman provided highlights of Parks & Recreation Director Jerry
        Lokken's response to their letter regarding the vehicle damage. A survey was

        sensitive to the damage caused by their vehicles and is doing its best to minimize any
