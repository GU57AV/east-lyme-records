RE: Protection of Niantic Bay Beach Sand Dunes

Mr. Greg Oecker of Niantic recently brought to the attention of the East Lyrne Commission for the
Conseruation of Natural Resources that wheeled vehlcles have been traveling over the sand dunes
located just off the boardwalk at Niantic Bay Beach. This damage to the dunes from vehicular traffic ha
also been observed by several Commission members. We understand that Mr. Decker also mentioned
this to you directly. Nevertheless, our Commission members wish to convey our concerns about this
issue as it is an enyironmental matter- As you rnay know, sand dunes serve many important functions,
including protecting more inland areas such as residences, businesses, and infrastructure, and providinl
habitat for a distinct assemblage of plants and animals. Due to many factors, sand dunes are not
commonly found along Connecticut's shoreline and should be protected. Also, the vegetation on these
East Lyme sand dunes was planted at great expense and labor. We strongly urge that town staff be
prohibited from driving vehicles on the sand dunes at this location or on 6ny others that may exist in th
town. We understand that the vehicles in use at Niantic Bay Beach might have some difficulty when
operating on the flatter sandy beach areas sea\^rard of the dunes. We suggest that your department
invest in tires that are more appropriate for this type of surface, which would enable their use on the
open beach ratherthan on the dunes. Should you need a special appropriation from the Board of
Selectmen for this purchase, our Commission would wholeheartedly support your effofts in obtaining
the funding. Thank you for your attention to this matter.
