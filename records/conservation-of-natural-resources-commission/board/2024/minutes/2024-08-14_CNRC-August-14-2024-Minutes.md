    a. Zoning lssue. Mr. Christensen reported he filed for a zoning change. The reason he has is
        because the sodium levels in East Lyme's drinking water have been climbing with development.
        As far back as the year 2000 the POCD suggested raising the zoning requirements to three or
        four acres. The area that would be changed is Stones Ranch, Yale, Aunt Ruth's Highway, Walnut
        Hill and Grassy Hill and much of the state forest. He would like to have the least impact on
        landowners as possible. To have two of our wells in a heavily developed area and expect clean
        water is not possible. He stated we need to do something to preserve the Four Mile River
        Aquifer. He added we asked DOT for a spill mitigation system. The town pumps water during the
        winter to New London so that it can be pumped back during the summer when our population
        increases. We need to give them potable water or we may not be able to honor our agreement
        with New London. Mr. Danila added fewer houses result in fewer septic systems. Mr.
        Christensen met with Mr. Mulholland who asked him to identify the property numbers.
        Commission members reviewed a map of the area. Mr. Christensen stated Mr. Goeschel has
        agreed to identifythe parcels. Mr. Christensen stated we have the Drinking Water Report, an
        updated Niantic River Study and 2020 POCD report which provide supporting information for
        this zoning change.

    VII. OLD BUSINESS
    a. Update on proposed development of 8 Brookfield Drive. Mr. Danila reported that the August
         lnland Wetlands Agency meeting on this single-house development was cancelled. He plans to
         go to the September meeting. There has been opposition in the neighborhood. There is road
         flooding that could occur from the small stream on this site that empties into Latimer Brook. Mr
         Danila did not feel this proposed development should go forward. The applicant doesn't know
         where the neighbor's well is located, which is necessary for placing a septic tank. Mr.
         Christensen added the septic reserve area is too close to the brook. The stamp on the deed says
         it is not a developable lot.

    b. Update on Pattagansett Lake herbicide treatment and potential threat of hydrilla introduction.
       Mr. Danila informed the Commission that he is on the Pattagansett Lake Association (PLA)
       Board. Eighteen acres of Pattagansett Lake were treated on July 19 with an herbicide with good
       results. The PLA's NDDB Determination limits what can be done for herbicide applications. The
       Determination requires a wetland plant survey for certain herbicide usage. The PLA received one
       survey quote for 59,000. He added that at last Monday's PLA Board Meeting, they were
       informed they will receive a monetary donation of 57,500 from a local charitable trust. More
       than three-quarters of the lakefront does not have adjacent wetlands. There was a recent article
       in the Dav newspaper on Gardners Lake showing a sign that DEEP put up about hydrilla. He

    c   2023 RBV results. DEEP sent a recent email indicating that the 2023 RBV report was now
        available. Mr. Danila stated the results in Cranberry Meadow Brook: three of the most wanted
        insects were collected; in Latimer Brook by IGA there were three most wanted and at Colony
        Road there were two most wanted; and in Four Mile River there were two most wanted. He
        stated four or more most wanted insect types indicated excellent water quality with about half
        of the reports in the state showing that level. Results of zero to two indicates more information
        is needed. He added these insects prefer to be in cool water streams. This year the water has
        been in the 8Os. He felt the RBV team may try a new Four Mile River location to see if the results
        would be different. He stated we have many years of data, which can show the effects of
        variable annual conditions.
