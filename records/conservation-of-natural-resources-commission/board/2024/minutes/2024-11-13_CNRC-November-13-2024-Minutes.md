     b. Update on Proposed Text Amendment and Zone Change. Mr. Christensen
        reported he withdrew his application because the fee is S10,000. Every landowner
        within 500'of the zone change needs to receive a certified mailing. The Town of
        East Lyme staff would need to send documents to DEEP and neighboring towns. Mr.
        Christensen suggested that the Zoning Commission form a subcommittee on this
        matter because of our concern of our drinking water and the Niantic River
        Watershed. A subcommittee of Ms. Kahl and Ms. Harris was formed. Ms. Heller will
        request a meeting with them. Ms. Heller has given information on the increasing
        levels of sodium in the Town's drinking water to the Zoning Commission. More than
        one individual's private well has run dry. Additionally, legal limits for PFAS in
        municipal drinking water will change in the next few years and it is in some of the
        Town's wells. They will likely be over the new state limit

    c. Update of Pattagansett Lake-Association State Listed Wetlands Plant Survey. Mr.
        Danila reported a very good survey was done by James Cowen for the Pattagansett
        Lake Association. The issue was to look for four state-listed wetlands plant species
        located along the shoreline. Mr. Cowen found none of the state-listed plants,
        although there were two species congeners. These two plants were not listed by
        the state.

        Mr. Danila will send this report to any member of the Commission that would like
        it.

       on October 28 and 29th. The streamflow was extremely low. A number of desired
