                    collected and wants four or more of the most desired insects as an indication of
                    good quality water.

                    Mr. Danila and Ms. Heller sampled in Cranberry Meadow Brook on October L8,
                    2019. The results were very good with 6 most wanted, 6 moderately wanted, 1
                    least wanted, and 3 other macroinvertebrate organisms collected.
