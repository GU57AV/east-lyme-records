               E. SOLAR FARM
             Mr. Danila reported that the Niantic River Watershed Committee met on September
             5 and again discussed placing water temperature and turbidity recorders on Oil Mill
             Road and Stony Brooks to determine any adverse environmental effects of this
             proposed project.

             The Waterford Town Planner noted potential difficulties in meter placement due to
             stream characteristics and site access. As an intervener in this proceeding Save the
             River-Save the Hills is in contact with the developers. They will seek site access from
             the developers. The organization remains opposed to this project, but is willing to
             work with the developers to design the best possible stormwater handling system
             should it go forward.
