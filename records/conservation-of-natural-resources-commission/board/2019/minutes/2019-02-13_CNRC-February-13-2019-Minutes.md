    D. Solar Update
       Commissioner Danila reported that he had no new updates regarding the development
       following the denial of the certificate. He, along with Engineer Steve Trinkaus, PE, attended
       a meeting with CT DEEP Director of Water Permitting and Enforcement Division Oswald
       Inglese and discussed their concerns regarding the solar projects throughout the State,
       including the cutting down of core forest and prime agricultural lands and the resulting
       stormwater issues due to inadequate plans andlor lack of follow-through. CT DEEP is in the
       process of drafting and instituting tighter regulations.

    E. Follow up with Zoning on Status of Beekeeping Ordinance & Music Ordinance
       Commissioner Christensen reported that a Public Hearing to discuss the Beekeeping
       Ordinance has been scheduled for March 21,2019,7:00 p.m., at Town Hall.
       The proposalto establish a Music Ordinance is currently on hold and may be included as
       part of a proposed Event Ordinance and will be discussed at the February 21 Agri-business
       Subcommittee Meeting.

       The Subcommittee hosted a successful Potluck Dinner and positive feedback and interest
       was received. The Subcommittee will be hosting a Seminar on Wednesday, February 27,

       Library regarding Purchase and Development Rights (PDR) and PA490 (Classification of
       Land as Forest Land). Guest speakers will include Joan Nichols from the CT Farm Bureau

       April with Judy Preston from CT Sea Grant who will be discussing ocean and river water,

       website.
