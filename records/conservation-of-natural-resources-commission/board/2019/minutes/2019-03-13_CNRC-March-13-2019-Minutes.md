           l) Many residents continue to include items that cannot be recycled in their recycling
               bins and increased awareness regarding what items can/cannot be recycled is
               necessary. He was pleased to see a recent advertisement in The Lyme Times for the
               Southeastern Connecticut Regional Resource Recovery Authority (SCRRRA) which
               provided a list of accepted recyclable items.
          2) Plastic bags have become a major issue as recyclers continue to include plastic bags
               among their recyclables rather than placing them in the recycling bins designated for
               plastic bags at local markets. The plastic bags clog the sorting machines wreaking
               havoc and adding unnecessary processing/sorting time. One possible solution would
               be to institute a ban on plastic bags is one possible solution.
       The Commissioners discussed speaking with the Public Works Department and SCRRRA to
       determine how recyclables are handled in an effort to generate possible solutions andlor ways
       in which they can inform and educate the public, including creating an informative webpage
       and producing labels that can be placed on the bins reminding recyclers of the acceptable
       materials. The information can be compiled and included in a brief report that can be
       presented to the Board of Selectmen for review and action. Chairman Carlson and
       Commissioner Beeman will schedule a meeting with the town engineer to discuss the main
       issues and ways in which they might be able to resolve the issue(s).
