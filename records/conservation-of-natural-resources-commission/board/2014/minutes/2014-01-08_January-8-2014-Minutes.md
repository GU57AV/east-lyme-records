IV. NEW BUSINESS

A. Discussion regarding Woodsmen's Trust open space proposal. Mr.
Carlson reported that this Commission developed an Open Space Plan based on open space being
valuable. He showed the Open Space Plan proposed footpaths; major watershed and aquifers
and natural resources elements considered.

Mr. Ellis, Chairman of the Woodsmen's Trust asked this Commission for support on the
Darrow 47 affordable housing units. He stated we will request a grant based on the appraisal of
the 47 units. The property is located above the Darrow Pond property and is located in the
Pattagansett watershed.

Mr. Harney gave Commission members copy of Memo dated September 15, 2005 to
Wayne Fraser from Meg Parulis regarding acceptance of open space lands associated with the
Nottingham Hills and Darrow's Ridge Subdivisions, as well as map of Route 11 Greenway
Planning Area property status.

Mr. Harney reported we asked for a letter of support one year ago for the Gurley Brook
piece in the Niantic River watershed, and we were awarded a grant. The state has another grant
application that needs to be submitted in March. The new parcel is the 47 acres of the Darrow
Ridge parcel. We do not want to miss submitting the grant application.

Mr. Ellis stated we can help manage forest and wildlife. He would like to apply for a
grant for this property. Mr. Carlson asked what is the state of your easement on the property?
Mr. Ellis replied there is a Woodsmen Land Conservation Easement on it. Ms. Heller asked
what is your plan for the 47 acres? Mr. Ellis replied to be all open space. When the state awards
an application it cannot be developed. It needs to be used for passive recreation. He was asked
who owns the 47 acres? Mr. Ellis replied NE National. Mr. Kalal asked do you plan to do forest
management? Mr. Ellis replied the Woodsmen Land Trust says it cannot do forestry. Mr.
Carlson referred to the Rt. 11 map the Commission was given and asked who were the owners
of the properties identified? Mr. Ellis replied he owns 400 and Mr. Harney owns 200.

Mr. Torrence agreed if you look at the aquifer map it is in the Pattagansett watershed. It
was called Smith Hill which was all farm land. Mr. Kalal stated it is good farmland. We have
done a number of test holes with Ledge Light on the property. Mr. Kalal asked why are they
joined together? Mr. Ellis replied I have spent 20 years on protecting land. Mr. Haney stated
each stands on its own.

Ms. Ashburn felt the Commission needs to determine if the 47 acres meet our Open
Space Plan?
V. OLD BUSINESS
A. Nature of East Lyme Programs.

i. Foot Paths (Update). Mr. Carlson informed the Commission Yale signed off
on the Trail Conservation License Agreement.

On December 13 Mr. Carlson walked with the Eight Mile River organization on the
middle third of the trail.

ii. Water Quality Town Wide Report. Ms. Heller received a copy of the USGS
November 22, 2013 estimated nitrogen list. The Town is in watershed #62. There are 26
watersheds that feed into the sound. Of the 26 we are considered West Thames drainage area of
twenty-six square miles. We are the seventh lowest for agricultural land. We are the seventh
highest for forest wetland. Seven percent is impervious cover.

Commission member Marvin Schutt arrived.

Ms. Heller stated our nitrogen level is lower. Surface water quality decreases as we go
north to south.

Mr. Kalal asked if she had the results from the rapid bio? She replied she received an
email that they would like photos of the sites.

Mr. Kalal stated we have had two dry years in a row.

iii, Samuel Smith House Status. Mr. Schutt reported the three historical
entities in Town and farmers met at the Niantic Community Church to exchange ideas at a
potluck supper.

iv. Open Space Plan-map & text updates. Mr. Carlson reviewed the new Open
Space Plan with Commission members.

Commission member Mr. Christiansen arrived.

y. Agriculture Business Subcommittee. Mr. Christiansen reported they did not
meet in December. We had a potluck dinner with all preservation groups and exchanged ideas.
