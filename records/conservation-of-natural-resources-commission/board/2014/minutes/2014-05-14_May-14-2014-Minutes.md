East Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources

Regular Meeting Minutes
May 14, 2014

Present: Arthur Carlson, Chai

Penny Heller, Member _ FILED IN nee

Marvin Schutt, Member or AtTLO, M

Mark Christansen, Membe:

Laura Ashburn, Alternate : EAST AME TOWN CLERK
Absent: Tom Kalal, Member

Ron Nichols, Alternate

Call to Order. Chairman Carlson called the May 14, 2014 Regular Meeting of the East
Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources to order at 7:03 p.m. Alternate
Laura Ashburn was asked to be seated.

I. Additions to the Agenda. There were no additions to the Agenda.
II. Public Delegations. There were no guests.

IIE. Minutes.

A. Minutes of April 9, 2014. Mr. Carlson asked for additions, deletions or
corrections to the April 9, 2014 Regular Meeting Minutes.

The following correction was noted: On page 2 the last sentence of the first paragraph of
item C to be revised as follows: "He was the President of the first Connecticut Chapter of the
Nature Conservancy and Professor of Connecticut College."

MOTION (1): Mr. Schutt moved to accept the April 9,
2014 Regular Meeting Minutes of the East Lyme
Commission for the Conservation of Natural
Resources, as amended. Seconded by Ms. Heller.
Ms. Ashburn abstained from voting. (4-0-1)
Motion carried.

IV. NEW BUSINESS

Ms. Heller reported 10 bunnies were caught during the rabbit testing and one was a New
England Cottontail. It was suggested to have briar up the two stone walls and down the slope.
Mr. Carlson stated the review team and forester more or less agreed.
V. OLD BUSINESS. Ms. Heller stated she will be receiving a spreadsheet from the
Niantic River Group monthly.

She was invited to a rapid bio workshop in July. Ms. Ashburn would like to also attend.
Mr. Christiansen arrived.

Ms. Ashburn plans to talk to the Zoning Enforcement Officer, regarding the acquifer
protection regulations.

Mr. Christiansen reported Steve Gephart informed him the Town owns the road at
Darrow Pond. He suggested building the road up, and he will run a fish ladder under the road.
The state has determined the dam is not safe.

VI. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS.
A. Communications. There was no communications.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christansen reported the agricultural group
will be in the Memorial Day Parade.

He added three farms can be visited on Open Farm Day. There will also be an event at
the Samuel Smith House.

C. Chairman’s Report. Mr. Carlson reported Mr. Harney wishes to put 180
acres in open space. He has received a $500,000 grant as a 50/50 share from the state and Town.
Commission members were invited to express their support for this at the June 4 Selectmen's
Meeting.

Two Eagle Scouts are working with him on projects at Darrow Pond.
D. Ex-officio. Ms. Hardy was unable to be present.
VII. Adjournment.
MOTION (2): Mr. Christiansen moved to adjourn the May 14,
2014 Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources
at 7:45 p.m. Seconded by Mr. Schutt. (5-0)

Unanimous.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
