V. Old Business
A. Continuation of Water Study
Discussion ensued regarding the issues, goals, and actions the Commission should take to
protect the quality of the water in the Town. Chairman Carlson presented maps depicting the
open spaces and major watersheds and aquifers located throughout the Town. He proposed
making a distinction between drinking and non-drinking water. The following possibilities

were discussed:
1. Speaking with the Department of Transportation (DOT) regarding their plans and

timeframes for the redesigning of I-95 and, possibly, suggesting the installation of a catch
basin so as to protect their water resources should an oil spill or any other such catastrophic

event occur.
Page| 2

2. Recommend that the Town make an investment in protecting their drinking water by
obtaining and securing certain available open spaces in an effort to protect and ensure that
the water entering into and flowing out of the watersheds and aquifers is sound and their
drinking water is safe from contaminants.

3. Discuss a policy requiring the installation of catch basins and purification system(s) for
commercial buildings.

4. Determine how the current aquifer protection zone protects the quality and quantity of
water.

5. Begin discussions with the Planning Commission, Zoning Commission, Water & Sewer
Commission, Stones Ranch, Wetlands Enforcement Officer, and Town Engineer.

6. Create an executive summary-type report with supporting documentation, including a map
depicting the important watersheds & their depths, well locations, depth of the aquifers,
road overlays, and open spaces and with recommendations on how to improve protections
of our water.

Other issues of concern include climate change and its resulting effects on the sea level and
tidal wetlands, possible contamination from catastrophic event(s) resulting in the contamination
of the Pattagansett and Bride Brook Watersheds, which contain water that is currently being
consumed. The possibility of holding a future presentation on water recovery, interceptor
wells, and how to properly clean a site was also introduced.

Motion (2): Motion made by Mr. Carlson, seconded by Mr. Christiansen, to create a
working group to discuss the methods of protecting the Town’s existing
water resources with other Boards and Commissions that will meet between
the scheduled regular meetings. Voice vote, 5-0, all in favor. Meeting
adjourned.
