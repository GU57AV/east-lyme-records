Chairman Carlson reported on Ron Nichol’s conversation with Brad Karg], Municipal Utility

quality of the Town’s drinking water supply. Following Ms. Ashburn and Ms. Heller’s

discussion with Bill Mulholland to discuss aquifer protection, the Commission will enter into
further discussions.

Mark Christiansen joined the meeting.

Ms. Heller reported on a presentation she attended with Ex-officio Rose Ann Hardy by Adam
Winchell of the Nature Conservancy. Mr. Winchell discussed the necessity of obtaining
and/or setting aside open space in an effort to save the drowning of the saltwater marshes
from the rising sea level. Due to the large percentage of salvageable salt marshes in the
Town, the Nature Conservancy created an analysis of the possibility of directing the marshes
landward. In response to her suggestion of adding this to the Commission’s criteria,
Chairman Carlson stated that it can be added to their Open Space Plan. Ms. Hardy added
that their services are offered free of charge and suggested that the Commission sponsor an
Open Forum.

A. Communications — None.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee
Mr. Christiansen reported that Celebrate East Lyme is scheduled for Saturday, July 19. Two
booths for Conservation and Agriculture are planned. Unfortunately, they will not be allowed
to have sheep at either of their booths at the proposed location. Chickens and honeybees
provided by Mr. Kalal. Handouts will be available. Three farms will be taking part: Five C’s
Horsefarm, the McTurk’s Mini-Donkey Farm, and the Samuel Smith Farmstead. Four
beehives have been set up and oxen will be featured at the Farmstead. In addition,
entertainment and refreshments will be offered, parking will be available across the street, and
the bathroom in the house will be available. Mr. Christiansen requested funding for the
printing of signage to be posted at key areas in Town inviting visitors to the farms.

MOTION (2): Motion made by Laura Ashburn, seconded by Penny Heller, to allocate
$100.00 to cover the printing costs for 1,000 promotional flyers. Voice
vote, 5-0, all in favor. Motion passed.

At the Request of Ms. Heller, Chairman Carlson will speak with Gary Goeschel regarding
obtaining a map of the public hiking trails that can be posted for East Lyme Day. Ms. Heller’s
daughter will be creating a self-running slideshow depicting some of the hiking areas. Two
tents, one provided by the Commission and the other by Mr. Christiansen, along with a table,
provided by Ms. Heller, will be available to house the laptop for optimal viewing.

With regards to the hiking trails, Chairman Carlson has requested to institute a cutting
schedule for the walking trails in an effort to keep the trails maintained, control the ticks, and
eliminate and/or control invasive species, e.g., Japanese switchgrass.

C. Chairman’s Report
Chairman Carlson reported on the progress at the Samuel Smith House. Fully funded, the
Community Garden, comprised of 36 beds measuring 4' x 12' each and two 4' deer fences
placed 5' apart is being planned. The Rotary Club has requested 75% of the beds to grow
produce for the local Food Banks, e.g., Care and Share. The well has been repaired making
water available to the area. A wood shed behind which a tool shed will be made available as
well as a bathroom.

Approximately $1,430.00 worth of materials were sold from the exterior of the Tinker
House. In addition, beams, floorboard, and paneling have been salvaged and lintels and
granite will also be set aside. He will be engaging in discussions with the Historical Society
regarding the insurance coverage they have in an effort to protect the historical houses in the
Town. In addition, they will be taking photographs and measurements of the structures
should something happen and the buildings need to be re-built. Chairman Carlson spoke of
the good relationship with the State’s Historical Trust Office and an archaeological survey,
completely funded by the State, will be conducted of the property. The prisoners have been
diligently working with them. While Mr. Christiansen noted that this would make a good
story for the paper.

Ms. Heller noted that she has obtained information regarding the solar composting toilets
which the State is utilizes and will forward the information to Chairman Carlson.

D. Ex-Officio Report
Ex-Officio Hardy encouraged the Commissioners to attend the Zoning Commission's Special
Meeting on Thursday, July 10 at 7:00 p.m., during which Costco will be presenting their
plans. While the public will not have an opportunity to speak at the Special Meeting, they
will be able to gather the necessary information to which they may respond via other
avenues. Included in their plans is a parking lot for 1,760 parking spaces located atop the
aquifer. Also of concern is the location of the entrance(s)/exit(s) of the proposed parking lot.
