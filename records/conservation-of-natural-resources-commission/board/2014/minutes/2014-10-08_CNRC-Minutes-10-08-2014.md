East Lyme Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources

Call to Order. Chairman Carlson called the October 8, 2014 Regular Meeting of the East Lyme
Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources to order at 7:05 p.m.

I. Additions to the Agenda. There were no additions to the Agenda.
II. Public Delegations. There were no guests.
III. Minutes.

A. Minutes of September 10, 2014, Mr. Carlson asked for additions, deletions or
corrections to the September 10, 2014 Regular Meeting Minutes.

MOTION (1): Mr. Schutt moved to accept the September 10,
2014 Regular Meeting Minutes of the East Lyme
Commission for the Conservation of Natural
Resources, as presented. Seconded by Mr. Kalal.
Ms. Ashburn and Mr. Nichols abstained from voting.
(5-0-2) Motion carried.

IV. NEW BUSINESS. There was no discussion of new business.
V. OLD BUSINESS.
A. Continuation of Water Study Results to Date.

1. Develop a report on the state of water quality and quantity in East Lyme. Mr.
Carlson stated he would like to write a report on the status of drinkable and non-drinkable water in the
Town of East Lyme.

There was discussion on the ideal watersheds and the watersheds in East Lyme. It was felt
the report should include aquifers within each watershed, flow and pumps, well locations and capacity
and usage. It should give impact scenarios from build out and the maximum consequences of oil spills,
big box development over the aquifer and run-off from parking lots. It should include the consequences
of drought and not sending water to New London, as well as future modifications to I-95. The report
should include the responsibility of the Town, DEEP and/or the state. It should note the costs for catching
and purifying water, a desalination plant and costs to the Town and businesses desiring to build in East

Lyme.
VI. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS.
A. Communications. There were no communications.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported the Niantic Farmers Market
has received $8,000 in grant money. The Farmers Market has been very successful.

A grant is being sought to promote agriculture in Town. The First Selectman has signed it.

C. Chairman’s Report. Mr. Carlson reported he ordered 25 reports made by the
Environmental Review Team. He agreed to have copies made of the Forester's Report for this
Commission.

D. Ex-officio. Ms. Hardy was unable to be present.
VII. Adjournment.

MOTION (2): Mr. Christensen moved to adjourn the October 8,
2014 Regular Meeting of the East Lyme Commission
for the Conservation of Natural Resources
at 8:20 p.m. Seconded by Ms. Heller. (5-0)
Unanimous.

Frances Ghersi, Recording Secretary
