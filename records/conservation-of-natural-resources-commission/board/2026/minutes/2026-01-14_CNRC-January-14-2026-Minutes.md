VI.   New Business
      a. CommissionReappointments
         Allthe Commissioners, with the exception of Commissioner Meekhoff, who was not
         present, confirmed that they were officially reappointed and sworn in.

      b. Plan for Conservation
         Commissioner Dinsmore suggested drafting a Conservation Plan that includes the
         Town's environmentally sensitive areas and provides guidance as to the maintenance
         of these areas. Chairperson Heller proposed highlighting, designating, and prioritizing
         the Town's resources by color, outlining their characteristics, and stating the
         reasoning for their preservation, specifically highlighting the area around the Four-
         Mile River Aquifer. The Town's open space, zoning changes, easements, aquifers,
         and native plants should also be outlined on the map. The Planning Director will be
         contacted regarding the possibility of engaging the intern to draft the map(s) for the
         Commission.

         The Commission's tasks, as outlined in the Town's (POCD) Plan of Conseruation &
         Development, were reviewed and the Commission agreed to draft a list of its
         priorities and objectives to provide the Town with the necessary teeth to make
         decisions and assure, for example, that open space remains open space.

      c. Town Open Space Issues
         Discussion ensued regarding the development of properties that were previously
         designated as open space and the lack of communication between the Town
         Departments. Part of the issue lies in the Land Trust's acquisition and selling of open
         space and the importance of including a statement in the Deed deeming the property
         as open space. Selectperson Hardy stated that the existing map should be updated and
         referred to, as necessary. She also informed the Commissioners that the matter has
         been brought to the First Selectman's attention and the issue will be addressed with
         the Board of Selectmen. Integrating departments, remembering agreements, and
         maintaining oBen space should be a fundamental goal of the Town. Commissioner
         Christensen proposed the Commission draft a leffer stating that open spaces created
         through the subdivision of a property should be signed over to the Town. The
         Commission agreed to draft a list of priorities upon which a Plan of Conservation will
         be based.
