        She gave an update on the following municipal PPEL projects:
        L. Betty Murphy Memorial Garden at the Public Safety Complex is looking fabulous and is
            100% native plants providing an ecosystem and wildlife service.
        2. The Public Works Department landscape is thriving and providing ecosystem service.
        3. Renovation on the left side of the front garden at the Community Center is coming along

        Newtown and Torrington Conservation Commissions and Ms. Meekhoff continue to network
        to establish the rodenticide ban. Lisa Stark of Newtown is offering to speak with us at one of
        our monthly meetings to educate us on what Newtown has done.

        Ms. Meekhoff presented information at the June 13 Zoning Commission meeting against the
        Parks and Recreation proposal for a Dog Park and future athletic fields speaking as a member
        of the Commission for the Conservation of Natural Resources, the Darrow Pond
        Subcommittee and President of PPEL. She voiced concern that development of any kind would
        clearly not benefit the eagle, heron and egret nesting grounds at the marsh area in a lower
        elevation of this parcel. The Zoning Commission unanimously vetoed Parks and Recreation's
        request, and member Norm Peck stated that this property should be left as open space in
        perpetuity. Ms. Meekhoff spoke with several DEEP staff who also felt the space would be
        protected and managed more effectively if it was under DEEP's jurisdiction. Bill Mulholland
        may need to be consulted if a transfer to the state is considered.

        PPEL's two-acre meadow at 4 Church Street Flanders that is now six years old is having an

        maintenance.
