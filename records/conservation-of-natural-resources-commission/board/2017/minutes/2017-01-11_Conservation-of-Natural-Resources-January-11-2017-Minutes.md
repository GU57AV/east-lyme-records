C. Federal Railroad Administration New England Rail Proposal. Mr. Danila showed a
map of the plan. He felt it would greatly affect the Town in the Flanders area of East Lyme. It will
cross our major aquifers and streams and areas developed for businesses and housing. It will cut
through lands in the Niantic River headwaters that we thought had been preserved. He reviewed the
Environment Impact Statement. The Federal Administration heard comments from residents of Old
Lyme concerning a tunnel in Old Lyme. He felt East Lyme residents missed an opportunity at that
time to give comments. The deadline for comments is January 31. There is concern about the impact
to water. Gov. Malloy felt repairs should be made to the railroad first.
