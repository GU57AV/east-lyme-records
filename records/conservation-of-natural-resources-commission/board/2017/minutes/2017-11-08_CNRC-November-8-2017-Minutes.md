CALL TO ORDER
Chairman Carlson called the November 8, 2017 Conservation of Natural Resources
Regular Meeting to order at 7 p.m.

Mr. Nichols and Ms. Ashburn were asked to be seated.

I. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS
Mr. Londregan invited Commission Members to a meeting on December 7 at 6 p.m. at
Waterford Town Hall at which Army Corps will attend.

Mr. Nichols asked Mr. Londregan to describe his work. Mr. Londregan replied he
currently runs the only commercial hatchery in the State of Connecticut. He stated at the
present time there are 22 oyster producers in Connecticut. The concept is to take 3/8 seed
and grow it to 1 1/2 in the river. The majority will be sold in Connecticut. Mr.
Londregan stated he pays for the area by giving a percentage of what he produces to
WELSCO. The more shellfish there is the more people will buy permits.

Mr. Londregan stated it is very low density shellfishing. If all the water disappears, then
they will see something is in the water. He added when he had to prepare his sounding
map, it had to be zero tide which happens seven times a month.

Mr. Christensen added half of the time it is night and doesn't last long.

Mr. Londregan stated he can't touch the mud area. Waterford and East Lyme have put
moorings in that area. Mr. Nichols asked if Mr. Londregan is using public trust lands for
private use. It is a water dependant use which qualifies for the use of public lands. Mr.
Carlson felt it was a natural resources use. He asked if Mr. Londregan was doing
anything to damage our resources? Mr. Londregan felt it is a net gain for the
environment. All shellfish have to be tested.

Mr. Nichols asked what species do you use? Mr. Londregan replied Niantic Bay
scallops.
