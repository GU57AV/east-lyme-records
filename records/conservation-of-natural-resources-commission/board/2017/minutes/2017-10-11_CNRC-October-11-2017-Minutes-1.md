Dear Ms. Rose,

The East Lyme Agribusiness sub-commission of the Conservation of ,
Natural Resources Commission would like to submit a letter of SUPPORT
for the Niantic Bay Shellfish Farm LLC (NAE-2017-00205) application.
The Agribusiness sub-commission has no jurisdiction and was not solicited
to comment on the matter. Nevertheless, we felt it was important to offer our —
advice.

We believe that this project will contribute positively to the restoration of
shellfish and the overall ecology of the Niantic River. Shellfish, acting as
filter feeders will remove large quantities of Nitrogen and phosphorus from
the river. We know that these levels are at increased levels from storm water
runoff, due to development in the Niantic River watershed. Additionally, the
spawning of oysters and scallops will help restore the stocks throughout the
river for recreational harvest.

We believe that the value of this project in helping clean the river far
outweighs any detriments and our sub-commission unanimously supports
this application. We also realize that the original comment period was
closed on the 20" of September, but understand that you may extend the
period for accepting comments. We did not meet until September 20", at ”
