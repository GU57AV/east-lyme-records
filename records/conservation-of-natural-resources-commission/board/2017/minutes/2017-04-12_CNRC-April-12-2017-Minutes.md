CALL TO ORDER
Chairman Carlson called the April 12, 2017 Conservation of Natural Resources Regular
Meeting to order at 7 p.m.

Mr. Nichols was asked to be seated.

I. PUBLIC DELEGATIONS
There were no guests.

II. MINUTES

Mr. Carlson asked for additions, deletions or corrections to the March 8, 2017 Regular
Meeting Minutes.

MOTION (1): Mr. Christensen moved to approve the March 8.
2017 Regular Meeting Minutes of the
Conservation of Natural Resources, as presented.
(5-0) Unanimous

II. NEW BUSINESS

A. Goodwin Trail. Mr. Carlson reported the history of the Goodwin Trail is being
developed by the Eight Mile River group.

Maps of the trail will be available. A hike will take place on June 3, State Trails Day,
rain or shine, leaving from Darrow Pond parking lot, five miles to Grassy Hill Road.

IV. OLD BUSINESS

A. Continuation of Water Study. Mr. Carlson read draft of letter to the East
Lyme Board of Selectmen suggesting a catch basin system along I-95. The Commission
will forward a finished copy by Thursday April 13, 2017, to the Board of Selectmen.
Mr. Christiensen stated the revision of exit 74 will go through the aquifer protection zone.
He asked Mr. Mulholland to consider a spill mitigation system along the highway.

There will be a presentation on the restructuring of I-95 on April 18 at 6 p.m. at Camp
Niantic.

V. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS

A. Cotnmunicaitons. There were no communications received.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported a Beekeeping
Meeting was held on March 17. There was discussion on sustaining our bees and other
pollinators. Mr. Dinsmore received information on Beekeeping Regulations from the
Town of Wallingford. He plans to meet with Mr. Mulholland.

Open Farm Day will be held.on July 16. The Samuel Smith House and Old Orchards
farms will be open to the public. The Connecticut Farm Bureau would like to contribute
and will bring a donkey to the Samuel Smith House.

Music at the Old Orchards farm has been denied. A rework is in progress.

The Agribusiness Subcommittee plans to have a float in the Memorial Day Parade and
will participate on East Lyme Day on Methodist Street.

Mr. Nichols reported that the Save the River Save the Hills group will have a Science
Walk on the Boardwalk.

On April 29 there will be a Walk for Climate Change legislation in Washington, D.C.
C. Chairman's Report. Mr. Carlson had nothing new to discuss.
D. Ex-Officio Report. Ms. Hardy was unable to be present.
