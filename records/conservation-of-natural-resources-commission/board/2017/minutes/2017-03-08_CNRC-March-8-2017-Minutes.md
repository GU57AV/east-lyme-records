A. Goodwin Trail. Mr. Carlson reported he received an email from Pat Young
informing him that the Goodwin Trail Committee of which Art Carlson (representing the
Commission) is a member of, will receive an award from the Connecticut Land
Conservation Council for Excellence in Conservation Organization.

B. Niantic River Behavior Change Campaign. Mr. Danila reported the Niantic
River Behavior Change Campaign has to do with the use of fertilizer in the Niantic River
Watershed. A door to door survey will take place to determine what type of fertilizer the
homeowners use, and provide information on its affect on water. They are requesting
help with the door to door campaign. The Commission was anxious to help. Various
suggestions were made to possibly involve Boy Scouts, the National Guard, beach areas,
and the Land Trust. More discussion is needed.

V. OLD BUSINESS

A. Continuation of Water Study. Mr. Carlson distributed to Commission
members information on Stormwater Management - Small Municipal Separate Storm
Sewer Systems issued by the State of Connecticut and discussed the stringent rules for
public use of the Massachusetts Quabbin Reservoir system.

Ms. Hardy arrived.

1. Review of Open Space Criteria. The Open Space Plan will be
updated and will include Water Study information. Several special meetings are
envisioned.

VI. REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS

A. Communications. Mr. Carlson reported on a Federal Government Wildlife
Fish and Game Project to purchase 3500 acres along the Connecticut and Rhode Island
border to create thickets for birds and mammals. Perhaps the 2200 contiguous acres
north of the recently purchased Niantic River Headwaters could be a player. There will
be more to come.

B. Agribusiness Subcommittee. Mr. Christensen reported a Beekeeping
Meeting will be held on March 17 from 6 p.m. to 8:30 p.m. in the Olive Chendali Room.
Three speakers will discuss pollinators. There are plants that can encourage it. All
Commission members were invited to attend.

The Agribusiness Subcommittee invited the Zoning commissioners for an
informal meeting together. Mr. Dinsmore plans to schedule a meeting with Mr.
Mulholland.
The Zoning Commission denied the text amendment to amend Section 25 of the
Zoning Regulations for farm events to occur on five acres as opposed to the now 50 acre
lot. A complaint was received regarding music in the barn of Old Orchards Farm.
Planning determined it was inconsistent with Planning. Mr. Christensen referred to
Section 1.1 of the POCD. Mr. Carlson asked Mr. Christensen to write up the
Agribusiness Subcommittee's position on this matter. Mr. Nichols stated this
Commission is in favor of supporting farmers.

C. Chairman's Report. Mr. Carlson reported we will lead hikes on the
Goodwin Trail during Trail Day. A few helpers will be needed.
