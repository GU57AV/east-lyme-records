### SALE OF ALCOHOLIC LIQUOR

#### § 111.01 SALE OF ALCOHOLIC LIQUOR.

   The sale of alcoholic liquor is allowed on Sunday in the town as permitted by the General Statutes of the state in all places operated under hotel, restaurant and club permits as issued by the Liquor Control Commission of the state.

(Ord. passed 12-12-1938)
