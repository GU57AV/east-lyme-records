   114.01   Adoption of state law

#### § 114.01 ADOPTION OF STATE LAW.

   The provisions of Conn. Gen. Stat. §§ 7-170 to 7-186, inclusive, as amended, providing for the operation of bazaars and raffles in the town, are adopted.
