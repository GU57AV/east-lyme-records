   73.01   Operation of snow mobiles and all-terrain vehicles on frozen surface of public bodies of water

 

   73.99   Penalty

#### § 73.01 OPERATION OF SNOW MOBILES AND ALL-TERRAIN VEHICLES ON FROZEN SURFACE OF PUBLIC BODIES OF WATER.

   No person shall operate a snowmobile or all-terrain vehicle, as defined in Conn. Gen. Stat. § 14-379, at any time on the frozen surface of the following public bodies of water within the town:

   (A)   Dodge Pond;

   (B)   Gorton Pond;

   (C)   Niantic River;

   (D)   Pattagansett Lake; and

   (E)   Pattagansett River.

(Ord. passed - -) Penalty, see § 73.99

Editor’s note:

   TM Volume 11, page 103

#### § 73.99 PENALTY.

   Any person violating the provisions of § 73.01 shall be fined not more than \$100.
