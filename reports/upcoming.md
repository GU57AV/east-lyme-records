# Upcoming posting deadlines

_Updated Nov 24, 2026 6:19 PM ET. Unofficial and automatic._

Meetings come from the Town calendar (including special meetings, once they are on it) and from each board's published schedule of meetings. Agendas and special-meeting notices are due at least 24 hours before the meeting; minutes are due within seven days after a meeting (Connecticut FOI Act, CGS § 1-225). A special meeting not yet on the calendar can't be predicted, and a scheduled meeting may be cancelled. Schedules are often scanned, so a date can occasionally be misread or missed.
