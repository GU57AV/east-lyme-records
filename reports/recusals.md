# Recusals

_Updated Nov 25, 2026 1:45 PM ET. Unofficial and automatic._

Each time the minutes record a named member stepping aside from a matter: recusing, leaving the room during a vote, or abstaining for a stated conflict-type reason. Recusal is what East Lyme's Ethics Code (Town Code § 35.18) and state law (for example CGS § 8-11 for zoning boards) call for when a member has an interest in a matter, so these entries show the rules being followed, not wrongdoing.

**Limits.** Names are as written in the minutes and read by OCR, so spellings vary and some may be misread. Minutes don't always record recusals, and a member who quietly doesn't vote won't appear. The matter shown is the nearest application or motion before the recusal in the minutes, which is usually, but not always, the one it applies to. Abstentions because a member missed an earlier meeting are left out. The full data is in [recusals.json](recusals.json).
